Realms Caged by Law

古神话之法则囚笼的地界

A volume on sacred mountains, hidden realms, courts, underworlds, and mythic landscapes governed by structure, taboo, boundary, and law. These places are not settings alone; they are active powers.

Realms Caged by Law is a book of places that think, constrain, bless, punish, divide, and remember. In Eastern mythology, landscapes are never neutral. Mountains hold lineages. Caverns preserve dynasties of exile. Courts of the dead sort moral consequence into geography. Sacred peaks, sealed regions, and hidden domains are shaped not only by beauty or terror, but by the laws that bind what may enter, what may leave, and what must remain trapped forever. This volume follows mythic space as a living structure. Every realm carries an order: ritual order, political order, karmic order, elemental order, or the cruel order of imprisonment itself. To cross a boundary in these stories is never only movement. It is trespass, invitation, initiation, sentencing, or return. If the beings of myth reveal what a world believes, its realms reveal what that world is willing to contain.
