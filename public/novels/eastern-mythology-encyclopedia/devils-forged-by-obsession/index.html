Devils Forged by Obsession

古神话之执念成劫的魔

A volume on beings warped by fixation, resentment, hunger, ambition, and unresolved ruin. Here evil is rarely pure; it is forged from obsession hard enough to survive its own destruction.

Devils Forged by Obsession enters the territories where desire curdles into catastrophe. The figures collected here are not simply villains in the moral sense. They are beings shaped by fixation so intense that it remakes body, soul, and destiny around a single wound, hunger, vow, or refusal. Some pursue power until power hollows them out. Some cling to grief until grief becomes identity. Some are born from corruption; others become monstrous by refusing to release what should have ended long ago. This volume is concerned with the mythic logic of spiritual distortion: how will becomes curse, how passion becomes engine, how persistence becomes doom. If other volumes ask how beings rise, rule, or survive, this one asks what remains when the self is consumed by one overwhelming necessity. The answer is rarely noble, often magnificent, and always dangerous.
