## Chapter Overview

Li Huowang is riding with the Supervisory Heavenly Office’s task force to hunt down a Heart-Turbid—a twisted counterpart to his own Heart-Element condition. The Chief Recorder briefs the team on the Heart-Turbid’s terrifying ability: to hide anything or anyone using its inner karmic affliction, and to erase its victims from existence itself. But as they move into an ivy-choked, silent village, the hunt comes to a sudden halt when Li Huowang spots a familiar pair of embroidered shoes and a red veil—the exact same ones worn by Bai Lingmiao.

## Key Plot Points

- The Chief Recorder (a eunuch official) openly briefs the team on the Heart-Turbid, explicitly distinguishing it from the Heart-Element: it is a “black cloth in the night” that hides everything, including itself and others.
- He warns that *any* eye contact with a Heart-Turbid risks having one’s souls and spirits defiled, leading to permanent erasure from the world.
- The plan: Liu Zongyuan encircles the village with fog, the Chief Recorder and Shentu Gang flush the target out, and Erjiu (an Ao-Jing Sect member) delivers the killing blow.
- Li Huowang feels a grim, internal sympathy for the Heart-Turbid, recognizing their shared fate as hunted anomalies.
- The village is unnervingly perfect—walls, roofs, and ground entirely covered in ivy, with no sound at all.
- The tension breaks when they find a woman behind a low wall, but instead of a monster, Li Huowang identifies the embroidered shoes and red veil as Bai Lingmiao’s.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Heart-Element, Heart-Turbid, Heart-Pan (心素, 心浊, 心蟠)** – The “Heart-Type” people in this novel are a class of beings whose internal spiritual composition is fundamentally out of balance with the world. A Heart-Element is a bright beacon, easy to find and valuable as a rare alchemical ingredient. A Heart-Turbid is the opposite: its karmic affliction acts as a *negative space*, an anti-presence that can hide itself and others in plain sight. The cosmological symmetry between “bright” and “dark” Heart-types is a recurring horror logic in Dao-Twisted: the rules of the world aren’t fair, they are *systemic*.

**Karmic Affliction / Ye Zhang (业障)** – In Buddhist cosmology, *karma* isn’t just a reward system; the afflictive karma (业障) is the residue of past misdeeds that clouds the mind and causes suffering. Here, it is literalized into a tangible, contagious force that can physically “hide” a person’s very existence. The horror lies in treating a spiritual blemish as a weapon.

**Red Bridal Veil (红盖头)** – A traditional Chinese bridal veil worn during wedding ceremonies, signifying purity and the beginning of a new life. Its appearance on a target in the Dao-Twisted World instantly subverts that meaning: it becomes a symbol of a stolen life, a corrupted bride, or a ghost-marriage. Li Huowang’s recognition of the same veil worn by Bai Lingmiao makes it a sharply personal horror.

**White Wuchang (白无常)** – A spirit from Chinese folk religion, part of the “Black and White Impermanence” duo who escort souls to the underworld. The white-robed, long-tongued figure is a death omen. In this chapter, Liu Zongyuan’s fog produces a White Wuchang apparition as a tool for spiritual containment.

## Reading Guide

Get ready, because this chapter delivers a masterclass in **genre cognitive dissonance**. The Chief Recorder’s briefing sounds like a tactical Xianxia debrief—rules, countermeasures, team roles. There’s almost a professional heist-movie rhythm to it. But then, *it works too well*. You learn the rules, you feel the tension build, and just when the action is about to start—the author pulls the rug out from under you. That pair of embroidered shoes isn’t the Heart-Turbid’s. It’s Bai Lingmiao’s.

This is the signature move of Dao-Twisted: the horror that comes *not* from the unknown, but from the known being in the wrong place. Li Huowang’s pity for the Heart-Turbid feels earned, but the last three words of the chapter rewrite everything. Is Bai Lingmiao the Heart-Turbid? Has she been replaced? Or is the Heart-Turbid using her appearance as a weapon against Li Huowang’s perception? Either way, the team’s plan just collapsed—because *their own member is the trap*.
