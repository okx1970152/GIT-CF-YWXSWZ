## Chapter Overview
We finally meet the hidden Heart-Pan that Doumu has been shielding—and the answer is as twisted as the world itself. Instead of a single powerful opponent, Li Huowang walks into a nightmare of five enslaved Heart-Elements, treated like disposable tools by a voice in the dark. The chapter is a brutal inversion: the very ability that made Li Huowang special has been weaponized against him, mass-produced and chained. He doesn't just fight a person; he fights a system that has turned people like him into ammunition. Get ready for self-mutilation, tactical improvisation, and a stark reminder that in this world, having power doesn't mean you're free—it means someone might want to use you.

## Key Plot Points
1. **The Eight Immortals are neutralized**: After the initial underwater struggle, Li Huowang decisively destroys the wooden puppets, shredding them into shavings that litter the sea.
2. **Direct confrontation begins**: Abandoning stealth, Li Huowang sets the ship on fire and demands the hidden Heart-Pan show itself. But the flames mysteriously reverse, and the damaged bronze tripod restores itself.
3. **The five Heart-Elements appear**: Five men with double pupils crawl out, chained and enslaved. They repeat commands from a hidden voice, and their words have the power to "speak things into existence"—Peng Longteng's legs are instantly severed on command.
4. **Li Huowang understands the threat**: Realizing all five are Heart-Elements, he grasps that their ability to repair the ship operates on the same "believe it into reality" principle he possesses.
5. **Tactical self-mutilation**: Li Huowang pierces his own eardrums and closes his eyes to nullify the Heart-Elements' command-based attacks, then charges through a surreal landscape of collapsing and liquidating surfaces.
6. **Interruption and breakthrough**: Using a rusty Daoist bell to disrupt the voice's commands, Li Huowang seizes the opening and launches himself into the dark cabin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Heart-Element (心素) vs. Heart-Pan (心蟠)**: These terms represent two sides of the same dangerous coin. A *Heart-Element* is a rare constitution capable of making beliefs manifest as reality—a cognitive pollution that bends the world. A *Heart-Pan* is a specific Heart-Element who has been bound to a *Siming* (a high-dimensional deity), serving as its causal anchor in the mortal realm. Li Huowang is a Heart-Pan; these five slaves are "raw" Heart-Elements, used as living tools. The distinction is crucial: the slaves have the dangerous power but none of the agency.
- **Doumu (斗姥)**: The Mother of the Big Dipper, a high-tier cosmic entity in this novel's pantheon. Her full title is *Yin-Yang Doumu*, and she is associated with life, healing, and astral balance—but in the twisted world of this story, even a mother of stars can harbor hidden weapons like enslaved Heart-Elements.
- **The "speak-into-existence" ability**: This is the core horror of Heart-Element cultivation. It's not magic; it's a form of reality editing where a sufficiently strong belief forces the universe to comply. The five slaves speak in unison because their individuality has been erased—they are a collective mouthpiece for the hidden operator.
- **Self-mutilation as tactical counter**: Li Huowang piercing his own eardrums is a brutal but logical response to an enemy who uses spoken commands. It echoes real-world Daoist practices of "sealing the senses" to resist external influence, but here the horror is literal: he has to damage his own body to stay in the fight.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a hard pivot from "Li Huowang as special protagonist" to "Li Huowang as a product of a system that mass-produces his kind." The five enslaved Heart-Elements are a mirror—they show him what he could have been if he had fallen into the wrong hands. Pay close attention to his reaction: he's not just disgusted, he's *tactical*. Every move he makes—destroying his hearing, closing his eyes, using the bell to interrupt commands—is him applying knowledge gained from his own suffering. He fights these five like he's fighting a previous version of himself. The cliffhanger of him launching into the cabin isn't just action; it's a choice to face the source of this nightmare directly. And remember: the person behind all this is Doumu's Heart-Pan. That means the Mother of the Dipper herself is implicated. Nothing in this world is safe, not even the stars.
