## Chapter Overview

Hold onto your wits, fellow travelers—this chapter is a brutal, breakneck tunnel crawl through the absolute depths. Li Huowang just barely survives a back-alley ambush, only to find himself dragged back down into a nightmare made of living white tassels. The fight is over in seconds, but the horror is only beginning. What starts as a quick kill turns into a frantic escape, a monstrous transformation, and a final image that will stick with you long after you close the page.

## Key Plot Points

- Li Huowang dispatches Han Fu, a would-be thief and White Lotus remnant, with surprising ease—but the kill feels hollow and suspiciously simple.
- He loots the corpse, finding talismans and, crucially, nineteen Yang-Life Pills, which he consumes immediately for a quick boost.
- As he climbs out of the cave on a chain, Han Fu's corpse reanimates, emitting a piercing childlike scream that attracts a massive, white serpent formed from the tassels of the earlier Lion-Mask hybrids.
- The serpent drags Li Huowang back down into the pit as his companions watch in despair.
- Bai Lingmiao attempts to jump in after him, but is physically restrained by Gao Zhijian and slapped by Chun Xiaoman to bring her back to reason.
- The chain begins to move again, and the group pulls Li Huowang out—only to reveal he has been grotesquely transformed, his hand now a twisted, fleshy claw fused with the white tassels.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Yang-Life Pills (阳寿丹)**: These are condensed years of a person's lifespan. In the Dao-Twisted World, they are a literal, tradable resource—consuming them extends your life force, but harvesting them requires draining another's existence. Their presence here is a grim reminder of how cheap life can be.
- **Bronze Coin Sword (铜钱剑)**: A classic Daoist ritual weapon made from old Chinese coins strung on red thread. Traditionally used for exorcism and warding off evil spirits, in this world it's a practical, combat-ready blade that can also scatter and reform, making it a versatile tool of last resort.
- **Lion-Masks (舞狮)**: A corrupted cult that fuses human flesh with the lion-dance costumes used in traditional festivals. The tassels on these costumes are not decorative—they are the living, sentient heart of the creature. The "white snake" is actually a swarm of these tassels, revealing that the masks themselves were just puppets. This inverts the protective, auspicious symbolism of the lion dance into a hive-mind horror.

### Bronze Coin Sword (铜钱剑)

A Daoist ritual sword made from old coins strung on red thread, used for exorcism and combat. In the Dao-Twisted World, it can scatter and reform as a tactical weapon.

### Yang-Life Pills (阳寿丹)

Condensed years of a person's lifespan. Consuming them extends one's life force, but harvesting them requires draining the vitality of another.

### Lion-Masks (舞狮)

A corrupted cult that fuses human flesh with lion-dance costumes. The tassels on the costumes are the living, sentient true bodies; the masks and inner flesh are mere puppets.

### White Tassel Serpent

A massive, living swarm of white tassels from the Lion-Mask costumes, capable of forming a serpentine body and attacking as a single, predatory entity.

### Fleshy Claw (肉爪)

Li Huowang's transformed hand after being dragged back up from the pit. It is a twisted, fleshy appendage fused with white tassels, no longer fully human.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in pacing—from a near-instantaneous kill to a slow, agonizing climb, then a sudden, visceral descent into worse horror. Notice how Li Huowang's internal monologue is flat and pragmatic even when cutting off his own fingers; he's no longer a panicked survivor, but a grimly experienced operator. The real gut-punch, however, is the final transformation. The "Li Huowang" that emerges is no longer fully human, at least not in form. This moment marks a permanent physical scar, a visible corruption that the group can no longer ignore. If you're here for body horror and the slow erosion of the protagonist's humanity, this is where the line truly begins to blur.
