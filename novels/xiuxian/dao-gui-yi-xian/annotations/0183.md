## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow travelers—this chapter is a masterclass in Li Huowang refusing to be played. Fresh off his dealings with the Anci Nunnery's rot, our favorite paranoid Heart-Element is done being a passive question-answerer. When the Rice Divination spits out a suspiciously convenient solution to his "missing Five Phases" problem, his Zuowandao-honed instincts flare up. He's not buying any of it. What follows is a tense, one-on-one psychic cage match where Li Huowang, armed with a blade and a few bitter lessons, decides to stop asking nicely and start threatening deities. The chapter ends with him securing a side hustle as an escort guard, a pragmatic move that shows he's learning to work the system instead of just surviving it.

## Key Plot Points
- Li Huowang interrogates the entity possessing Rice Woman Cui about the cessation of decay, hoping to understand the Abbess's fate. The entity's answer—attributing it to a merciful Bodhisattva—immediately triggers his BS detector. He calls it out for what he now knows is a lie: the heavens above hold no benevolent gods, only horrors he desperately tries not to think about.
- Struggling against a wave of existential terror triggered by those thoughts, Bai Lingmiao helps him ground himself. He resolves to stop worrying about the cosmic scale and just focus on his immediate survival.
- When Li Huowang asks the true nature of a "Heart-Element," the entity threatens him with instant death if it reveals the answer. Li Huowang, now fully convinced this is another con, tells the entity to get lost and physically smashes the ritual props, driving Rice Woman Cui out of the house.
- Realizing his identity is a constant target, Li Huowang dons his copper coin mask and decides to keep it on permanently for concealment. The group hurriedly leaves the area at dawn.
- Arriving at the border town of Siqi, which has a noticeable ceasefire atmosphere, Li Huowang secures a side contract with the Tiger Head Escort Agency as a traveling guard, leveraging Zhao Wu's letter of recommendation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**"Interrogating the Rice" (问米/Wen Mi):** A classic form of Chinese folk divination, still practiced in some rural communities. The medium uses uncooked rice as a conduit to summon a spirit to speak through them. The theatrical "hiccup" (嗝) the entity makes before nearly revealing the secret of the Heart-Element is a classic signal of possession—a detail that grounds the horror in a genuine folk practice.

**The "Missing Five Phases":** In Chinese cosmology, the Five Phases (五行, Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) are the building blocks of all physical existence. A person who lacks all Five is an ontological impossibility—like a book with no pages. This isn't just a curse; it suggests Li Huowang's very existence is a violation of the operating system of reality.

**Jin Zao Mu (金灶沐):** The three characters the entity suggests as a new name literally mean "Metal," "Stove," and "To Bathe." "Zao" (灶) is the character for the Stove God, a household deity. The entity's solution is absurdly simplistic—just *have* the Five Elements in your name to fix your deficiency. It's the supernatural equivalent of wearing a tinfoil hat, and Li Huowang correctly identifies it as a scam.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a major milestone in Li Huowang's character development. He's no longer the confused, desperate newbie. The lessons from Zhengde Temple and the Zuowandao have hardened him. When the Rice Divination entity tries its spooky routine, his first instinct is aggression, not fear. The key moment is his mental pivot: "No matter what those things are, this world is already mad. I just need to mind my own business." This is the birth of his pragmatic survival philosophy. He's still terrified of the cosmic horrors (the "those... those..." he can't finish saying), but he's learning to compartmentalize that terror. Watch how he deploys the copper coin mask not out of paranoia, but as a calculated tool. The side quest with the escort agency at the end is a small but telling victory: he's found a way to turn his dangerous journey into a source of income.
