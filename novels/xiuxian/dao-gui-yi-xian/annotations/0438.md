## Chapter Overview

Get ready, because this chapter is pure, uncut cosmic horror served on a golden platter. The chaos escalates in breathtaking fashion as the Grand Liang Emperor unveils his ultimate trump card—the Spirit Mountain Ghost Eye—only for the Zuowandao to reveal *they've been playing a completely different game this entire time*. It's a thunderous payoff of long-gestating plot threads: the mysterious stone slab from Danyangzi's failed ascension finally gets its full, devastating explanation, and the Dice prove once again that they don't just lie to others—they lie to heaven itself. Amidst the collapsing golden palace and the shrieking chants of a dead Siming's eye, Li Huowang and Zhuge Yuan are caught in the crossfire of a battle between empires and cosmic forces far beyond their weight class. The chapter ends on a gut-punch callback to Ox-Heart Village, reminding us that no one—not even a sleeping Gao Zhijian—is safe from the reach of the Zuowandao's cons.

## Key Plot Points

- Li Huowang, despite having no pain sensation and his chest wide open, hoists his own body and Zhuge Yuan, trying to flee the supernatural battlefield. His internal Black Tai Sui parasites physically drag him forward against an overwhelming suction force.
- The sphere of Great Qi history that should always surround Zhuge Yuan suddenly disappears, leaving both of them exposed and stranded in the middle of the Grand Liang Emperor's ritual.
- The Grand Liang Emperor reveals his decade-long scheme: he has retrieved the eye of a Siming that died over a thousand years ago. The golden temple and bricks melt and flow into a colossal golden mask, identical to the one above the Supervisory Heavenly Office gate, with the dead Siming's eye crammed into the empty right socket.
- The four Dice (Zuowandao) face the impending doom with unsettling glee, claiming they've been preparing even longer than the Emperor.
- The Deputy Director of the Supervisory Heavenly Office performs a rite with a stone slab—the same kind Danyangzi used. The Zuowandao's chant reaches him, and the slab cracks open, revealing a mocking laugh that declares the immortality method was a complete lie.
- The slab shatters into a pile of broken mahjong and domino tiles, and a purple light is sucked from the Deputy Director's brow into the Dice.
- The Zuowandao's chant reaches even further, all the way to Ox-Heart Village, where Gao Zhijian wakes to find the stone slab armor Li Huowang gave him has also split in two, with the same mocking voice declaring Danyangzi's entire ascension method was a fraud.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Slab is a Lie — Danyangzi's Posthumous Humiliation**: The stone slab Li Huowang retrieved from Qingfeng Temple was originally Danyangzi's supposed "immortality method." This chapter delivers the brutal punchline: the method was always fake, deliberately planted by the Zuowandao to waste Danyangzi's life and, by extension, anyone who trusted it. The crack in the slab releasing a mocking laugh is a classic Zuowandao move—not just murder, but *mockery* as a weapon. The fact that the same slab reached the Grand Liang court (via the Deputy Director) and Gao Zhijian (as armor) shows how deeply the Zuowandao's traps are woven into the world's infrastructure. No one is immune to their cons, not even a dead man.

**The Golden Mask & The Spirit Mountain Ghost (神山鬼)**: The Grand Liang Emperor's ultimate ritual weapon, the "Spirit Mountain Ghost Eye," involves reanimating the eye of a long-dead Siming. The golden mask that forms is a direct mirror of the mask at the Supervisory Heavenly Office's gate—except one eye socket is empty, and the other holds the dead Siming's vertical pupil. This suggests the mask is a long-standing icon of state-level supernatural authority, and the Emperor has been trying to complete it with a genuine divine artifact. The Zuowandao's goal, as usual, is to ruin that completion.

**The Zuowandao's Chant**: The incantation chanted by the four Dice ("True Void, Wondrous Marks, Dharma King and Teacher...") is a corrupted, quasi-Buddhist/Daoist hymn. It sounds sacred, but its function is distinctly profane: to channel or transmit a message over vast distances, overriding spatial boundaries to deliver their punchline (the slab cracking) across the entire region, from the capital to a remote village. Their power is not just in magic, but in *information warfare*—they weaponize revelation itself.

### Bang Bing Jue (帮兵决, Spirit-Summoning Verse)

A traditional northeastern Chinese chuma ritual chant with an urgent, rhythmic beat, used to compel spirits into presence or action.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in tonal escalation. The visual of the golden palace melting into a mountain-sized smiling mask with a dead god's eye stuffed into it is pure, unforgettable body-horror architecture. And the Zuowandao's response? Pure contemptuous comedy. They laugh in the face of a decade-long imperial plot treated with absolute seriousness. The humor is dark, disorienting, and deeply satisfying.

Pay attention to the callback to Gao Zhijian. His stone slab armor was a warm, protective gift from Li Huowang. Now it's revealed to be another piece of the Zuowandao's global prank. The line "Hahaha! You can't even read a single character, and you thought you could become an immortal?!" is Danyangzi's final, undignified epitaph—a man who was illiterate, obsessed, and completely played for a fool. The Zuowandao don't just kill you; they make sure your life's work is a joke.

Also, keep an eye on the "new legs" Li Huowang's chest worms form. The Black Tai Sui inside him is becoming increasingly adaptive and autonomous, turning his own body into a living tool without his conscious consent. That's not a power-up—it's a symptom of his ongoing dissolution.
