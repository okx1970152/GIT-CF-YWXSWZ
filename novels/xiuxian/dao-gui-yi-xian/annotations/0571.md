## Chapter Overview
Alright, buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is a straight-up lore bomb that will completely rewire your understanding of the entire story. Li Huowang, while desperately trying to gather intel about the missing Little Emperor of Great Qi, drops a dime on a thirty-year-old mystery and stumbles onto a revelation so massive it recontextualizes everything. That timeline-breaking, paradoxical child he sent through a fissure? Yeah, he didn't just go to another place. He went to another *time*. And the implications? They're sitting right under Li Huowang's nose, have been this entire journey, and we're only just now connecting the dots. Get your almanac ready because the calendar just got turned upside down.

## Key Plot Points
*   Li Huowang interrogates a villager who witnessed the arrival of a wounded infant thirty years ago—the Little Emperor of Great Qi, sent back in time by Li Huowang's own actions.
*   The villager reveals the baby was raised by a childless couple and grew up to be a "simpleton" with a crooked mouth and a slow mind.
*   Li Huowang's acute perception, using his ability to see the "Ten Emotions and Eight Sufferings", catches the villager lying about the child's fate. He drags the man to Great Qi for a more aggressive interrogation.
*   The truth is extracted: the "simpleton" was not lost in the woods but was taken by an "immortal"—the description of which perfectly matches Danyangzi.
*   Li Huowang connects the dots with chilling precision. The only simpleton Danyangzi ever took that fits the timeline is Gao Zhijian.
*   The chapter ends with the crushing realization that Gao Zhijian, his loyal and seemingly simple friend, is the grown-up Little Emperor of Great Qi. The proper recipient of the Heavenly Scripture has been with him from the very start.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Timeline Shenanigans & the Void Nian (虚年)**: The reveal hinges on the abilities of the "Void Nian," a creature previously introduced. The traditional Nian beast is a monster that appears on the lunar New Year, but here its ability is twisted into something far stranger. Li Huowang theorizes that it doesn't just drain *yang life* (a person's allotted lifespan), but can actually alter a being's *position in time*. This is our first major glimpse into the Dao-Twisted World's cosmic rules being far more flexible and terrifying than a simple "level-up" system. It's not just power; it's a fundamental manipulation of causality.

**The Burden of a "Simpleton"**: The story of the "simpleton" reflects a grim reality of historical Chinese village life. A child with a severe cognitive disability was often seen as an ill omen or a "spirit creature" (精怪), a burden that tested a family's capacity for love and survival. The narrative weaponizes this social reality, making the reveal that this marginalized, unlucky figure is actually the legitimate ruler of a dying empire a masterstroke of tragic irony. His low status was the perfect camouflage.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in how *Dao Gui Yi Xian* pays off its long-running threads. Did you ever wonder why Gao Zhijian, a character who seems so simple and out of his depth, survived impossible odds and felt drawn to the group? Well, now you know. His survival instincts weren't just luck; they were the survival instincts of a being who had already survived being stabbed through the heart as an infant. The chapter also beautifully demonstrates Li Huowang's growth as a detective of the bizarre. He doesn't just accept the first story he hears; he actively uses his supernatural senses to detect the lie, forcefully extracts the truth, and then engages in rigorous, logical deduction. He's no longer a man drowning in confusion; he's a man learning to swim through the lies and paradoxes this world throws at him. The final, chilling realization that he has been carrying the Emperor of Great Qi around like a pet rock is a gut punch that will echo through the next arcs. The question now isn't just *where* is Gao Zhijian, but *who* is he, and what will he become when he learns the truth?
