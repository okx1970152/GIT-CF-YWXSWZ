## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists, because the sky just went full *void mode* on us. Another Heavenly Calamity has struck, not even two years after the last one—and that’s the real problem. This isn’t just a freaky eclipse; it’s a screaming alarm from the cosmology itself. Li Huowang is forced to play town mayor again, corralling a terrified village while trying to make sense of a world where the sun is a black hole. But the spookiest part? It’s not the darkness outside—it’s the whisper from Zhuge Yuan that something is *very wrong* in the White Jade Capital, and that these back-to-back disasters are a sign nobody wants to read.

## Key Plot Points

- The chapter opens with Niuxin Village plunged into absolute darkness as a “heavenly dog eating the sun” panic erupts. Lü Zhuangyuan rallies the performers to beat gongs and drums, a folk remedy Li Huowang immediately shuts down with his signature blunt authority.
- Li Huowang confirms with Zhuge Yuan that this is indeed a recorded Heavenly Calamity. The key detail: these calamities are supposed to be decades apart, but the last one (the disappearance of decay) was just last year, making this deeply abnormal.
- Zhuge Yuan drops a heavy implication: the cause may lie within the White Jade Capital itself. He warns Li Huowang, as a Heart-Element now cultivating the true, not to even speak of such things, hinting at a form of taboo or attracting unwanted attention.
- Li Huowang leads the villagers to shelter in the Bai family compound, takes stock of supplies (enough grain for years, a well), and uses a commanding bell-ringing to restore order. He distracts the crowd by ordering an ox to be slaughtered for meat—a classic pragmatic move.
- A discussion on the Supervisory Heavenly Office reveals Li Huowang’s bitter irony: he used to hate their casual brutality, but now he finds himself hoping they can function well enough to stabilize this situation before it gets worse.
- Zhuge Yuan expresses distrust toward Great Liang’s Office for mixing with the imperial family, violating ancestral mandates—a seed of political corruption in the supernatural order.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Heavenly Calamity (天灾, Tiān Zāi):** In standard xianxia, a “heavenly tribulation” is often an individualized punishment or ascension trial for cultivators. *Dao Gui Yi Xian* bends this trope into something far more existential: a global event caused by the Simings fighting over the *Dao* itself. These aren’t karmic tests—they are symptoms of a diseased heavenly bureaucracy. The last one broke the law of decay; this one has swallowed the sun.

**“Heavenly Dog Eating the Sun” (天狗食日):** This is a classic Chinese folk explanation for solar eclipses. The myth says a celestial dog chases and bites the sun, and commoners traditionally made loud noises (beating gongs, drums, pots) to scare it away. Lü Zhuangyuan’s panicked reaction is culturally authentic—his “third uncle” story mirrors actual historical practices. Li Huowang dismissing it shows his transition from a modern skeptic to someone who knows the *real* horror is far beyond a folk remedy.

**Ancestral Mandate of the Supervisory Heavenly Office:** The text introduces a crucial piece of world-building: the Office is forbidden by ancient law from colluding with secular powers or aiding imperial conquests. Zhuge Yuan’s suspicion that Great Liang’s branch has violated this is a major political red flag, implying the Office may be compromised or even weaponized by a false dynasty.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in *atmospheric dread before the monster arrives*. Nothing overtly violent happens, but the weight of the world’s machinery starting to grind in the wrong direction is suffocating. Pay attention to the pacing: Li Huowang’s reaction is calm and managerial, which only highlights how much Zhuge Yuan’s unease signals a coming storm. Also, take note of the “false Great Liang” comment—it’s easy to gloss over, but it ties directly into the layered reality problem at the heart of the story. If the empire itself might be a lie, everything built on top of it is suspect.
