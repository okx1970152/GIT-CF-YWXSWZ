## Chapter Overview

Alright fellow Daoists, brace yourselves. After the non-stop body horror and existential dread of the last few chapters, we get a brief pocket of… almost hope? That’s right, Li Huowang takes a break from fighting for his life to have a therapy session, and it’s one of the most mind-bending scenes yet. Our favorite walking disaster uses scientific logic to *double down* on his madness, coming to the utterly unshakable conclusion that both worlds are real. The sheer manic energy he exudes as he rewrites the doctor’s reasoning into a new, personal cosmology is both exhilarating and deeply unsettling. But the chapter isn’t just mental gymnastics. We get a sweet (and paranoid) reunion with Bai Lingmiao and Li Sui, a final, heart-wrenching farewell to Zhuge Yuan, and a blood oath that cements their bond. Get ready for a rollercoaster of emotions, from frantic elation to quiet sorrow. This is the soul-crushing break your heart needed.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Therapy Session Twist:** Li Huowang, instead of being swayed by the doctor’s rational arguments, weaponizes them. He agrees that cause and effect are real but twists the logic, claiming his Innate Pneuma (a Daoist concept he introduces on the fly) was the *reason* his subconscious woke him. Using Zhuge Yuan as proof, he “proves” both worlds are real.
*   **A Desperate Hug:** In a moment of raw, manic triumph, Li Huowang hugs the bewildered doctor. The scene is immediately shattered as the doctor’s face melts away, revealing the flayed dog form of the hallucination, snapping us back to the Dao-Twisted World.
*   **The Family Reunion:** Li Huowang finds that Bai Lingmiao and Li Sui have secretly followed him. Bai Lingmiao’s reasoning is pure Li Huowang logic: she was afraid a Zuowandao had replaced him. Her quiet confession and his gentle embrace provide the chapter’s moment of tender humanity.
*   **The Final Burial (That Isn’t):** Li Huowang tries to bury Zhuge Yuan’s corpse in a beautiful spot, but the stranded soul stops him. Zhuge Yuan insists his spine-sword is too useful a tool to waste, urging Li Huowang to think of the bigger picture.
*   **The Sworn Brotherhood:** Moved by his friend’s sacrifice, Li Huowang performs a blood oath ritual. He dips Zhuge Yuan’s spine-sword into bowls of his own blood and declares a sworn brotherhood, vowing to find any way to resurrect him. The Zuowandao’s mocking echo of the final line undercuts the sacred moment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

*   **Innate Pneuma (先天一炁 / Xiāntiān Yī Qì):** You heard the madman’s new favorite term! This isn’t just a random phrase Li Huowang brought up. In Daoist internal alchemy, it’s a core concept—the primordial, undifferentiated energy of the universe that existed *before* Heaven and Earth. It’s the pure, original life force that a cultivator seeks to understand and refine. Li Huowang appropriating this ultra-prestigious term to justify his personal multi-dimensional chaos is a stroke of both genius and madness. It’s like a mechanic saying his car runs on “the fundamental essence of creation” to justify why it has three wheels and a fire-breathing engine.
*   **Dialectics (辩证 / Biànzhèng):** The doctor throws this word around, which is amusingly formal for a hospital visit. In a Dao-Twisted World context, it’s a cheap weapon. Li Huowang, a master of survival, picks it up and turns it right back on the doctor, using the tool of “objective reason” to create a highly subjective, self-serving truth. This is pure *修假为真*—cultivating the false into reality.
*   **Sworn Brotherhood (结拜 / Jiébài):** This is a massive deal in Chinese culture, especially in wuxia, xianxia, and historical fiction. It’s not just “best friends forever.” It’s a sacred, ritualized bond that transcends blood. By dipping a weapon in their shared blood and swearing to die on the same day, they create a karmic and spiritual link. It’s a promise made to Heaven, one with serious consequences if broken. The Zuowandao mocking the oath’s final line is a deliberate act of ritual desecration.

### Dialectics (辩证)

A method of argument involving contradictory viewpoints to establish truth. The doctor uses it as a tool of reason; Li Huowang weaponizes it to build a new, insane truth.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for some serious emotional whiplash. The chapter starts with the fastest, most manic *Confidence in Your Own Delusion* speedrun you’ll ever see. Li Huowang is over the moon, positively glowing with the certainty that he’s finally figured it all out. Savor that rare moment of joy—it never lasts long in this world. The middle section is a quiet, character-driven breather with Bai Lingmiao and Li Sui, which serves as the calm before the final storm of the chapter. The farewell to Zhuge Yuan is one of the most poignant scenes in the entire first volume. It’s a masterclass in bittersweet sacrifice and friendship. Keep a tissue handy for the blood oath, because this is where the story’s emotional stakes lock into place. Welcome to the brotherhood, Li Huowang. It’s going to hurt.
