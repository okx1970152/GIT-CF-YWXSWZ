## Chapter Overview

Li Huowang leads his crew up the mountain to Anci Nunnery, hoping to finally cash in on Layue Shiba. What they find is a temple drowning in its own filth—moldy mountains of food, vomit-gorging nuns, and a stench so thick you can taste it. This chapter is a masterclass in *greed as spiritual rot*. The nuns aren’t evil in a scheming way; they’re just *hungry*, in the grossest, most animalistic sense possible. And Li Huowang? He’s standing at the edge of a deal that could finally set him free.

## Key Plot Points

- Li Huowang decides to bring Bai Lingmiao and the others along after Gouwa argues he might need help if he has another psychotic break. He entrusts Baolu to stable the cart at an inn before the climb.
- The group reaches Anci Nunnery before dusk. It looks exactly as dilapidated as before—weeds, crumbling roofs, and an oppressive stench.
- Inside the first temple hall, they find the same fat nun sleeping with a flatbread helmet, covered in her own filth. Li Huowang bypasses her without waking her.
- The inner grounds are a horror show: rotting food covers every surface, forming moldy mountains swarming with flies and maggots. Yet it’s all vegetarian—the gold Li Huowang brought earlier was spent entirely on food.
- Yang Xiaohai, the former beggar, is furious at the waste and confronts nun Miaoyin, who responds by vomiting and eating her own puke with a straight face. Her logic: “If we don’t eat it now, it’ll rot.”
- They finally reach Venerable Jingxin’s secluded room. When Li Huowang opens the box with Layue Shiba’s eye, the blind abbess immediately senses it, her body trembling with greedy excitement.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Anci Nunnery** is a distinct breed of corruption compared to the polished evil of Zhengde Temple. This isn’t a genteel perversion of Buddhism—it’s a *vulgar* one. The nuns are fat, gluttonous, and have abandoned all pretense of spiritual duty. The grotesque physicality (obesity, vomiting, rolling in filth) drives home a key theme of the Dao-Twisted World: the “sacred” is always a mask for something base. In real Chinese Buddhist monastic codes, gluttony and hoarding are serious offenses, making this scene a deliberate inversion of monastic ideals.

Why is everything vegetarian but rotting? It mirrors a real-world tension in famine-era Buddhism: alms and charity rice were often distributed, but hoarding and spoilage happened when institutions became greedy. The nuns here represent a faith that has collapsed into pure consumption—eating, vomiting, eating again, with no thought for the starving world outside.

The **female script** (女书) on the gate couplets is a real historical script from Hunan province, used exclusively by women. Its presence here deepens the nunnery’s isolation and hints at a hidden layer of women’s culture—twisted into something decayed and insular.

## Reading Guide

**Li Huowang’s character voice** is crucial here: he’s no longer the confused victim. He’s the *leader*, briefing his team, guiding them through hell, and making cold decisions. But the chapter also shows his vulnerability—the Black Tai Sui’s suppression is fading, and he knows he could slip into a hallucination at any moment. That tension between *active agent* and *fragile man* is the heartbeat of the story.

**Don’t get distracted by the gross-out.** This isn’t just shock value. The rotting food, the vomit, the filth—it’s all a *visual argument* about greed. These nuns got gold and immediately bought quantities of food they couldn’t possibly eat. The waste isn’t stupidity; it’s *the shape of their desire*. They don’t want to be full; they want to consume endlessly, even if it means eating their own puke.

**Watch the dynamic with Yang Xiaohai.** The former beggar speaking up is a big character beat—he’s been quiet and passive, but seeing food wasted hits a nerve. It shows he’s still holding onto a moral core, and it contrasts with Li Huowang’s more pragmatic survival mindset.

**Setup alert:** Jingxin’s reaction to the box is pure predatory hunger. This deal is *not* going to be clean. The chapter ends with her fleshy bulk shifting toward Li Huowang like a glacier of greed. Whatever happens next, the price is going to be higher than he expects.
