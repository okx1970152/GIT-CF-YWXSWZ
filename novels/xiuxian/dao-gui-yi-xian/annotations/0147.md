## Chapter Overview
For fifteen days, Li Huowang becomes a pupil again—not of alchemy or forbidden scriptures, but of the blade. In this breather chapter, he picks up a sword manual, lets Zhao Qin (the escort agency chief) teach him and Chun Xiaoman the basics of the Duan Family Sword Technique, and discovers that Bashe's gaze sharpens more than just his pain. It makes him a frighteningly fast learner, turning a complete novice into a passable swordsman in half a month. But this is no triumphant power-up; it's a pragmatic step toward self-sufficiency. The chapter closes with a quiet trade: a letter of recommendation for escort work, a promise of future travel, and the silent acknowledgment that the road ahead is still long and bloody.

## Key Plot Points
- Li Huowang tries and fails to re-learn literacy from the stuttering Gao Zhijian, realizing he's back to square one with the written word. The True Sutra of the Fire Vestments remains inaccessible.
- Zhao Qin delivers a sword manual: the *Duan Family Sword Technique*, characterized by grand, sweeping motions rather than tricky finesse. Most of the manual is illustrations, which Li Huowang can memorize.
- Under Zhao Qin's guidance, Li Huowang and Chun Xiaoman begin daily sword practice. Li Huowang's progress is startlingly fast—he defeats Chun Xiaoman in a spar after only fifteen days.
- Li Huowang acknowledges that Bashe's enhanced perception (beyond just pain) is the reason for his rapid learning—a rare silver lining to his monstrous connection.
- He decides it's time to leave the escort agency. Zhao Qin writes him a letter of recommendation, granting him access to escort contracts at any agency in the future. The chapter ends on a note of professional courtesy and quiet farewell.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**"Wind Returns, Falling Geese" as Sword Technique Naming** – In wuxia and xianxia, move names are often poetic, evoking natural phenomena or classical imagery. *Hui Feng Luo Yan* ("Wind Returns, Falling Geese") suggests a counter-attack that follows the opponent's momentum—turning their retreat into your opening. It's not just flavor text; it signals a philosophy of combat where defense and offense are one continuous motion.

**The Duan Family Sword Technique (段氏剑法)** – Named after its creator, Duan Si, this fictional technique is described as "grand and sweeping" (大开大合), meaning it prioritizes power and reach over subtlety. In Chinese martial arts, such styles are associated with battlefield swordsmanship rather than dueling or performance art. It fits Li Huowang's current state: no foundation, no elegance, just raw, practical aggression.

**Escort Agencies and the Letter of Recommendation (担保信)** – In pre-modern Chinese fiction and history, escort agencies (镖局) relied heavily on personal reputation and networks. A letter of recommendation from a trusted chief was worth more than gold—it was a portable credential that could open doors across the entire trade. Zhao Qin's gift is a practical, long-term investment in Li Huowang's survival, not just a polite gesture.

## Reading Guide
**A Skill Earned, Not Given.** This chapter is a quiet victory after a long stretch of trauma. Li Huowang finally learns something useful *without* paying for it in blood, tears, or soul debt. But the chapter also plants a subtle seed: Bashe's gaze is a double-edged boon. Right now it helps him learn swordplay; later it might help him sense something he shouldn't. Keep an eye on how his "gifts" keep escalating.

**Chun Xiaoman's Presence.** She's not just a training partner. Li Huowang calling her to join the practice shows he's thinking beyond himself—building up his crew's combat ability. Every extra sword in the group is extra insurance against the next horror.

**The Farewell's Finality.** Zhao Qin says "if fate brings you back"—not "when." He knows the road Li Huowang walks is dangerous. The letter of recommendation isn't just a job aid; it's a quiet acknowledgment that Li Huowang might need to disappear into the escort network one day. That's the kind of gift a man gives when he suspects he won't see the recipient again.
