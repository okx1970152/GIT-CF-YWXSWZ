## Chapter Overview

Chapter 8 marks a turning point in Li Huowang's dual-world existence. Caught between Danyangzi's command to deliver the albino girl Bai Lingmiao for alchemy and his own conscience, he escapes to the modern hospital — only to find his mother Sun Xiaoqin arriving for a visit. Her fierce protectiveness momentarily banishes his anxiety, but the reprieve is short-lived. Shunted back to the cave world, he finds Danyangzi has pivoted: instead of the girl, the master displays a wriggling black mass called Black Tai Sui, signaling a shift in the chapter's stakes from human sacrifice to a deeper, more grotesque alchemical horror.

## Key Plot Points

- Li Huowang acquires a gold anklet from Bai Lingmiao, establishing a token of connection and responsibility.
- He asserts authority by appointing her to oversee the ingredient room, showing his growing willingness to shape the cave world's hierarchy.
- Danyangzi demonstrates a pill-forming divine ability (御物), sparking Li Huowang's greed to learn powers he could take back to reality.
- Danyangzi orders Li Huowang to bring Bai Lingmiao as a drug ingredient, forcing Li Huowang to confront the moral weight of his actions if the cave world is real.
- Li Huowang escapes to the hospital, where his mother's visit brings a rare moment of familial warmth and a reminder of his civilian identity.
- His mother's fierce defense and threat of legal action contrast sharply with the helplessness he feels in the cave world.
- Upon returning, Li Huowang discovers Danyangzi has "turned the page": instead of demanding Bai Lingmiao, he presents a wriggling Black Tai Sui from his palm, implying the immediate crisis is diverted but replaced by something more ominous.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Tai Sui (太岁)** is a concept from Chinese folk religion and alchemy. In traditional beliefs, Tai Sui is a deity associated with the year and fortune; offending one's personal Tai Sui brings misfortune. However, in alchemical lore, Tai Sui can also refer to a mysterious, flesh-like substance that grows underground — often called "flesh Tai Sui" — and is believed to confer immortality or great power when consumed. In this novel, Black Tai Sui (黑太岁) is a grotesque, writhing mass, visually and conceptually corrupted from its origins into a visceral horror that underscores the Dao-Twisted World's perversion of sacred practices.
- The title "turning the page" (翻篇) is a colloquial Chinese expression meaning to move on from previous events. Here it carries a weight of volatile power: Danyangzi chooses to spare Bai Lingmiao for now, but the display of Black Tai Sui hints at an even darker agenda.

## Reading Guide

- Observe how Li Huowang's two worlds mirror each other: a mother's absolute protection versus a master's absolute demand. The chapter's structure — escape, comfort, forced return — reinforces the trap he cannot break.
- Li Huowang's greed for the divine ability (御物) and his later relief when Danyangzi shifts targets reveal his adaptive pragmatism. He is neither a pure victim nor a hero, but a survivor constantly recalibrating.
- The Black Tai Sui is introduced without explanation, setting up a new source of horror. Readers should note its visceral description ("sticky and writhing", "sound like a toad licking an eyeball") — a hallmark of the novel's macabre aesthetic.

## The Nature of Black Tai Sui {#black-tai-sui}

Black Tai Sui is a corrupted alchemical entity that blurs the line between ingredient and parasite. In Chinese folk and Daoist tradition, Tai Sui is both a star god and a mythical "flesh" that grows in the earth; ingesting it was said to grant longevity. In the Dao-Twisted World, this myth is literalized into a wriggling, fist-sized lump of black slime that makes wet, disgusting sounds. It likely acts as a raw material for Danyangzi's twisted alchemy, and its sudden appearance suggests that the old master has moved on from human ingredients to something even more aberrant. Li Huowang's curiosity — he asks "What is that?" — shows he is still learning the rules of this world, which rewards greed with danger.
