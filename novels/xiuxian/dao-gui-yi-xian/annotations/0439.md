## Chapter Overview
Ding-ding-ding! The Zuowandao break out their nuclear option and summon their Siming—Doumu, the Celestial Venerable of the Dipper Mother—directly into the heart of Shangjing’s battlefield. And they do it with a lie, laughing all the way. This isn’t a desperate last stand; it’s a performance. For Li Huowang, who’s just trying to find one slippery Heart-Element conman, this turns into a front-row seat to cosmic horror. He witnesses a Siming in the flesh for the first time, and his brain nearly melts trying to process it. Only Zhuge Yuan’s steady hand and a boat made of painted silk get him moving again. But just as escape feels possible, a familiar eye decides it’s very, very interested in him. Fun times!

## Key Plot Points
- The illusion-deceived people across several kingdoms release purple light, which the four Dice collect to fuel their ritual. Their cube-shaped heads swell and their bodies become transparent.
- The Great Liang Emperor, seeing the threat escalate, joins the fight with his inverse-scale arms, backed by other factions. The remaining Zuowandao launch suicidal charges to buy time.
- The Dice complete their chant to “Yuanming Doumu Celestial Venerable” (圆明斗姥天尊) and their pips detach to form the ritual anchor.
- The Zuowandao collectively scream for Doumu, claiming the Great Nuo is waking—then the Dice immediately cackle that they were joking, revealing the whole thing was a setup to lure her out.
- Reality itself destabilizes in Shangjing: the ground turns liquid, things appear and disappear, and a yin-yang taiji pattern reveals itself as two interlocking oceans.
- Li Huowang, even in his Intercalating the Five Phases form, nearly collapses from the sight of the Siming; his seven orifices bleed and his head swells. The pain from his wounds keeps him conscious.
- Zhuge Yuan saves him by navigating a painting scroll like a boat across the liquefied terrain, writing “Swift” with his Judge’s Brush to speed their escape.
- The golden-masked giant eye is dragged into the white pupil of the black sea, losing the struggle. Li Huowang spots the city gate and thinks only of interrogating Bei Feng.
- In the final twist, the vertical eye from the golden mask is now floating in the sky, watching Li Huowang personally.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**斗姥 (Doumu, The Dipper Mother):** Don’t let the pleasant name fool you. Doumu, or Yuanming Doumu Celestial Venerable (圆明斗姥天尊), is a high-ranking deity in Daoist cosmology, often syncretized with the Buddhist goddess Marici (摩利攴天). She is the mother of the Big Dipper (the “Dou” in her name), associated with light, magic, and fate. Traditional Doumu has multiple arms and eyes and sits on a lotus throne—an image of benevolent, awe-inspiring power. In *Dao Gui Yi Xian*, the Zuowandao have taken this sacred figure and twisted it into a **genuine Siming-entity** that manifests as an infinite yin-yang ocean. The “sacred” became the “ontologically terrifying.” Seeing her isn’t an act of worship; it’s a psychic contamination event. Li Huowang’s reaction—seven-orifice bleeding, mental splitting, physical dissolution—is the novel’s way of saying: this is what happens when a mortal stares at a god that doesn’t want to be seen.

**The Dice’s Chant:** The incantation uses formal Daoist “Precious Declaration” (宝诰) structure, a liturgical form normally used to praise deities in a respectful, supplicating tone. But here it’s being used as a summoning trigger by criminals who immediately mock the entity they called. This is peak Zuowandao: weaponizing sacred language for a punchline.

**The Nine-Tiered Chessboard Returns:** Earlier in the arc, the Great Liang Emperor and others were playing a “nine-tiered chessboard” game (九重棋局), a reference to celestial politics and power distribution. Doumu’s arrival blows that board apart—she operates at a level that makes all prior scheming look like a children’s card game.

## Reading Guide
**First contact with a Siming:** This chapter is your first real sense of what a Siming actually *feels* like. It’s not a monster you fight. It’s a law of the universe that just decides to show up. The fact that Doumu is summoned by a lie, and that her arrival turns reality into stretchy, liquid nonsense, is the novel’s way of saying: the rules you thought applied? They don’t apply here. Cherish that brief moment of Zhuge Yuan’s calm competence, because it’s the last sane thing in the room.

**Why does the eye stare at Li Huowang?** That’s the million-tael question. The golden-masked eye was the Zuowandao’s trophy, and it was just absorbed into Doumu’s “white pupil.” If Doumu now sees through that eye, then Li Huowang—who is a walking Heart-Element, a reality-bending anomaly, and currently coated in Five-Element parasites—has just painted a giant neon target on his back. The chapter ends on a brilliant dread note: Li Huowang thinks he’s escaping, but the Siming is looking right at him. This is not a coincidence, and it’s not going to be resolved politely.

**Doumu’s entrance is a genre shift warning.** Up until now, the Shangjing arc has been “xianxia politics with extra suffering.” Doumu’s arrival announces the start of the “cosmic horror all-you-can-eat buffet.” If you were attached to the idea that the power ceiling was the Nascent Soul stage, you are now hilariously, tragically wrong.
