## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers one of the most devastating turning points so far. Li Huowang, attempting to escape a desperate, frozen situation, unwittingly triggers the *Thousand Greats Record* ritual by sacrificing his own skin—and in the process, ignites a conflagration that kills the entire Bai family. By the time he returns to himself, the world around him is charred earth, and he is a blackened, suffering statue. He meets Liu Zongyuan of the Moon Gate, who patches his ruined body with paper skin and a paper robe. But no amount of patchwork can fix what's broken inside: Li Huowang is now the author of the very horror he swore to prevent—and he has to go back and tell Bai Lingmiao that her family is ash. Buckle up, fellow readers—this is bleak, and it stays bleak.

## Key Plot Points
- The cold and damp are driving Li Huowang toward hypothermia; he burns his shoes for warmth, but the small fire becomes a trap.
- As he gets hotter, black smoke and embers rise from his body, signaling an involuntary ritual activation: he has sacrificed his entire skin to the *Thousand Greats Record*.
- In a single psychic flash, the fire explodes outward, consuming the entire reed field and everyone in the Bai family. Li Huowang sees them burning, collapsing, dying.
- Running toward them only makes the flames worse—he becomes a vector of death, not a rescuer.
- He wakes to a desolate wasteland of ashes. Liu Zongyuan of the Moon Gate finds him, mistakes him for an Ao-Jing Sect member, and casually helps him.
- Liu Zongyuan repairs Li Huowang's skinless body using the Moon Gate's "Sticking Technique," covering him with pink paper skin and a paper robe.
- Li Huowang reveals that the tentacled entity in his gut is his Nascent Soul—more body-horror evidence that his cultivation has gone terrifyingly off-track.
- The chapter ends with Li Huowang's hollow realization: he has killed Bai Lingmiao's entire family, and he has no idea how to face her.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Moon Gate (月亮门) and the "Sticking Technique" (黏字诀)**: Liu Zongyuan belongs to a sect that uses a mysterious paper-based technique to repair the body. This draws on a long Chinese folk tradition of using paper effigies (纸扎) in funerary and protective rituals—the idea that paper can serve as a surrogate for flesh. The Moon Gate's craft is eerily literal: they paste paper *over* a living person as if the person were already a paper offering. The eight techniques Liu lists (Sticking, Setting, Joining, etc.) likely correspond to a secret martial or ritual lexicon, each word a miniature Dao; their deliberate obscurity is itself a form of sect mystique.

**Nascent Soul (元婴)**: In standard Chinese cultivation (内丹, Internal Alchemy), the Nascent Soul is a condensed, higher self formed inside the dantian—a pure, ethereal embryo of the cultivator's true being. Conventionally, it "hovers" or "sits" in the chest or abdomen. Characters usually achieve it as a sign of profound mastery. Li Huowang's Nascent Soul, however, is a grotesque, tentacled thing with a heterochromatic eye and a double pupil—a creature that lives in his guts and *reacts* to external stimuli. Liu Zongyuan's crude joke about it turning into feces is not just dark humor; it underscores how far Li Huowang's cultivation has strayed from any orthodox path.

**Aftermath horror**: This chapter executes a classic *Dao Gui Yi Xian* move: the protagonist's every survival instinct compounds the tragedy. Li Huowang tried to hold back; he didn't want escalation. But the *Thousand Greats Record* does not care about intent. The ritual consumed him, and now the consequences are irreversible. This is not empowerment—it's entropy of the soul.

## Reading Guide
Get ready to sit with Li Huowang in the ash for a while. This chapter doesn't offer catharsis; it offers a quiet, excruciating aftermath. The reunion with Bai Lingmiao is coming, and it's going to be the emotional knife-twist of the arc. Key emotional beats to watch: Li Huowang's earlier delusional plea to Bai Sai ("I'm family") is now a corpse-laden irony. The paper skin Liu Zongyuan gives him is a fragile stopgap—stay away from water. And that tentacle-eye in his belly? Keep watching it. In the Dao-Twisted World, nothing inside you stays friendly forever.
