## Chapter Overview
Well, well, well. Who knew the silent giant had been secretly leveling up while Li Huowang was busy getting his own existence tangled in knots? This chapter is a delightful palate cleanser of pure, uncut *action* and a much-needed lore dump about the Zuowandao’s grand, multi-generational scam. We get to see Li Huowang struggling with his new cultivation method (it’s a lot harder than the Dice made it look!), a major discovery about the Pentagon War’s background, and a jaw-dropping display of power from our favorite simpleton. It’s a classic “the quiet one is actually the secret MVP” moment, and it hits just right.

## Key Plot Points
- Li Huowang wakes up late and rushes to practice the True Cultivation Method, but finds it incredibly difficult to push the Primordial Unity through his chakras.
- The practice session causes a localized reality warp, turning the room’s floor into the ceiling, demonstrating the method’s dangerous side effects.
- Gao Zhijian arrives, and Li Huowang finally deduces that the Heavenly Scripture was a long-term Zuowandao plot, masterminded by the Dice.
- The chapter explains the Dice’s true goal: using lies gathered over centuries from victims like Danyangzi to summon Doumu’s incarnation for the purpose of stealing the Divine Mountain’s Eye, which holds a Way of Chaos. Li Huowang questions if the Dice themselves were tricked by Doumu.
- Gao Zhijian reveals he has a Military Path cultivation manual from Peng Longteng and has been practicing it. He demonstrates his newfound strength by lifting a giant halberd and smashing the ground, unleashing a wave of killing intent that awakens the headless Peng Longteng’s spirit.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Military Path (兵家) 101:** In traditional xianxia, the “Military Path” is a classic cultivation branch focused on warfare, martial might, and leadership. It’s all about building up *shāqì* (煞气 / killing intent), a tangible, often visible aura of murderous will that can be used as a weapon or a shield. Gao Zhijian’s sudden skyrocketing power is a perfect example of how this path works—it rewards raw strength and a simple, direct nature. It’s also a stark contrast to Li Huowang’s True Cultivation Method, which is more about internal alchemy and cosmic enlightenment. One makes you punch a hole in a mountain; the other makes you question if the mountain was ever real.

**The Ten Emotions and Eight Sufferings (十情八苦):** This is a corruption or expansion of the standard Buddhist concept of the “Eight Sufferings” (Birth, Aging, Sickness, Death, Parting from what we love, Meeting with what we hate, Unattained goals, and the Five Aggregates). Li Huowang’s “Ten Emotions” add two more categories, likely related to the Heart-Element’s unique nature of belief and perception. The point of the True Cultivation Method he’s learning is to “filter” all these mundane, worldly pains and passions through the Primordial Unity (先天一炁), which is the cosmic, formless energy of creation. The goal? To turn these messy human feelings into fuel for enlightenment, or maybe just to burn them away.

## Reading Guide
Alright, new Daoists! Buckle up, because this chapter is a treasure trove. First, pay close attention to Li Huowang’s deduction about the Dice. This is the first time we get a clear picture of just how *big* the Zuowandao’s game is. It’s not just about messing with Li Huowang or Danyangzi; it’s a plot that was centuries in the making, targeting the throne of Great Liang itself. The fact that Li Huowang even questions if the Dice got out-tricked by Doumu is *chef’s kiss*. Never trust a trickster god, folks.

And then there’s our boy, Gao Zhijian. His sudden power-up is not just a cool moment; it’s a crucial reminder that in this world, there are different ladders to power. Li Huowang is stuck on the philosophical, reality-bending one. Gao Zhijian just needed a sword manual and a good stomp. His simple, direct nature is a perfect fit for the brutal, no-nonsense Military Path. Watch how the headless Peng Longteng reacts to his killing intent—she’s practically *sensing* the reincarnation of her own legacy. It’s a beautifully morbid moment of connection between a mute giant and a headless corpse.
