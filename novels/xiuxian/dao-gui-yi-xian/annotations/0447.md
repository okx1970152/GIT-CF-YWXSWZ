## Chapter Overview
Our boy’s back, folks—and he’s doing *great*. Chapter 446 sees Li Huowang doing what he does best: talking to trees while his personal ghost entourage critiques his life choices. But beneath the surface-level delirium, there’s a sharp, cold calculation running through every decision he makes. He’s analyzed his new Hong Zhong identity, weighed Doumu’s gift against its obvious strings, and come to a grim conclusion: time to go home, find a quiet corner, and start grinding that sweet, sweet True Cultivation. This chapter is a breather—a quiet moment of assembly before the next storm. But in the Dao-Twisted World, *quiet* is just another word for “something’s about to go terribly wrong.”

## Key Plot Points
- **The tree gets an earful**: Li Huowang hallucinates his mother and tries to explain his impending regression into madness with a mix of gentle reassurance and frantic denial. His ghostly audience (Zhuge Yuan, Red Center, Monk, Jin Shan Zhao) observes from above.
- **Zhuge Yuan gets the download**: Red Center and the Monk fill Zhuge Yuan in on the *full* hallucination roster—how they died, how many there used to be (seven total, with Pockmarked Head and a woman already gone), and the general mess of Li Huowang’s inner world.
- **Doumu’s gambit analyzed**: Li Huowang breaks down the pros and cons of having his past rewritten by Doumu. Net gain: a free Zuowandao identity + techniques + a True Cultivation manual. Hidden cost: *it’s Doumu*. The whole thing stinks of a setup.
- **The Dice-shaped shadow**: Li Huowang briefly toys with the idea of seeking out Dice for answers. Zhuge Yuan immediately shuts that down—Zuowandao don’t fight fair, and Dice is the worst of them.
- **Heading home**: Decision made. Niuxin Village. Quiet, remote, and Bai Lingmiao is waiting. But as he mounts up, he realizes he’s missing two things: Li Sui and the dog Mantou. A quick search reveals Li Sui has been “playing with the dog” in the most horrifying way possible—tentacle-wrapped and dangling in midair.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Doumu (斗姥) – The Mother of the Big Dipper and the Goddess of Falsehood**  
Doumu is a real, high-ranking Daoist deity, often portrayed with four faces and eight arms, seated on a lotus throne. She’s the mother of the Big Dipper stars and wields power over life, death, and cosmic destiny. In *Dao Gui Yi Xian*, she’s twisted into something far more unsettling: the Siming who *owns* all falsehood in the world. Every lie ever told is a tiny piece of her domain, and the Zuowandao’s entire power structure is built on defrauding those pieces from her. The catch? That might *also* be part of her plan. She’s as much a cosmic predator as she is a deity.

**The Real vs. The False (修真 vs. 修假)**  
This chapter quietly reasserts the novel’s core philosophical war: cultivation of the *True* vs. cultivation of the *False*. Li Huowang has stumbled into a Zuowandao cultivation method that lets him make falsehoods real—a terrifying power in a world where everyone is lying. But cultivating the True is the straight path: it’s about grounding yourself in what *actually is*, even when reality keeps shifting under your feet. The tension between these two paths is *the* central conflict of the entire story, and Li Huowang is now straddling both.

**The Seven Hallucinations – A Roster**  
Zhuge Yuan learns that Li Huowang has had *seven* hallucinations total: the current gang (Zhuge Yuan, Red Center, Monk, Jin Shan Zhao, No-Face), plus two more who have already faded—a baldy (Pockmarked Head) and an unnamed woman. In the Dao-Twisted World, the number seven often carries cosmological weight (seven stars, seven apertures, seven emotions), but here it just means Li Huowang has been collecting ghostly companions like Pokémon. And some of them are worse than others.

## Reading Guide
Buckle up, ‘cause after this chapter, the pace is about to shift. Li Huowang is heading back to Niuxin Village, and that means a reunion with Bai Lingmiao, Chun Xiaoman, and the rest of the weird little family he’s assembled. But don’t get too comfortable—every time Li Huowang makes a plan, the universe laughs and throws another pile of rotting Flesh Buddhas at him. The real tension here is the Doumu-shaped elephant in the room. That free cultivation method isn’t free. Doumu *wants* him to use it. The question is *why*. For now, Li Huowang is playing along, but in this world, playing along with a Siming is usually the first step toward becoming a puppet. Watch for the strings.
