## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists — this chapter pulls no punches. Fresh off his escape from the underground nightmare, Li Huowang is riding high on success. He got the Black Tai Sui! The cure for his hallucinations! But joy turns to sheer, visceral horror when he sees his own body in the moonlight. The white panicles from that abyss have completely *remapped* his flesh. Organs are flipped, misplaced, fused. He’s a walking mass of chaotic meat. With incredible force of will — and a whole lot of self-surgery — Li Huowang refuses to surrender to his monstrous form. It’s a chapter of body horror, stubborn defiance, and the first real test of whether he can stay human when his own body no longer agrees.

## Key Plot Points

- Li Huowang escapes from the underground lair with the Black Tai Sui in hand, feeling triumphant after his grueling ordeal.
- The joy is short-lived. When he tries to stand and ask for help, everyone recoils in terror. They don’t recognize him.
- In the moonlight, Li Huowang sees his own reflection: a chaotic tangle of displaced organs, fused limbs, and dangling white panicles. His body has been grotesquely rearranged by the white panicle entity.
- Realizing the panicles caused the damage, he deduces they might also be able to *reverse* it. He begins a brutal process of self-surgery — cutting, tearing, and repositioning his own misplaced features.
- The process is agonizing and imperfect. Gouwa drops Li Huowang’s ear in panic, but Bai Lingmiao steps up to sew it back on with a needle and thread.
- By dawn, the team has managed to roughly reassemble him into a human shape, but his screams haven’t stopped. The torture is far from over.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Black Tai Sui**: In Chinese folk religion and traditional medicine, *Tai Sui* refers to a mysterious, jelly-like substance believed to have life-extending and restorative properties. It’s often described as the “flesh of the earth” and is associated with the god of the year. In this novel, however, the Black Tai Sui is a living, parasitic entity — a grotesque inversion of that mythical panacea. Eating it comes with terrifying side effects, and Li Huowang’s plan to “cut it out and re-swallow it later” shows both his desperation and his terrifying pragmatism.

**White Panicles (白穗)**: These pale, swaying tassels are the weapon of the underground creature that dragged Li Huowang down. They seem to have the power to *rearrange* biological structure — changing the position of organs, fusing limbs, and displacing sensory organs. It’s a form of organic mutability that is different from healing or cursing. In Daoist-influenced body horror, such powers often represent the loss of one’s *fixed nature* — the thing that makes you *you* in the cosmos.

**Self-Surgery as Cultivation**: The idea of cutting and reshaping one’s own body to achieve a desired state is a recurring motif in xianxia and horror. While traditional Daoist cultivation focuses on inner alchemy — refining qi and spirit — the Dao-Twisted World demands *literal* flesh-crafting. Li Huowang’s willingness to slice open his own face and reposition his own eyeballs is a testament to his transformation: he will sacrifice any part of himself, as long as it keeps him alive and human.

### Self-surgery

In the Dao-Twisted World, extreme self-mutilation is sometimes the only way to fix supernatural damage; Li Huowang's method is blunt, painful, and brutally effective.

## Reading Guide

This is one of the most visually extreme body-horror chapters in the early volumes. The descriptions are intentionally grotesque — fused fingers, misplaced eyeballs, an ear falling to the ground and being *spat on* before being reattached. If you have a weak stomach, take a breath. But beyond the gore, this chapter is about a question Li Huowang can’t stop asking: *Am I still human?* His answer is brutal but clear: he will fight to keep his shape, even if he has to carve himself back together piece by piece. Bai Lingmiao’s quiet act of sewing his ear back on — not with magic, but with a needle and thread — is a small, tender moment in the middle of the bloodbath. Pay attention to her role here. She is no longer just the frightened girl from earlier chapters.
