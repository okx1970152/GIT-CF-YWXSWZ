## Chapter Overview
Get ready, folks. The big con is on, and Li Huowang has just rolled the dice with his own life. In a moment of brilliant, suicidal scheming, he feeds Danyangzi a batch of pills that are less "immortal elixir" and more "certified death sentence." But the Dao-Twisted World doesn't play by Li Huowang's rules, not even when he's dying. The chapter delivers one of the most gloriously horrifying punchlines in early *Dao Gui Yi Xian*: Danyangzi's fervent belief in a lie and his desperate, pathological obsession with immortality spark a transformation that is less "ascension" and more "biological collapse into a meat-fan of screaming mouths." This is where our protagonist learns—in the most visceral way possible—that in this world, faith can make things real, even if the thing being made real is a nightmare.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Setting of the Trap**: Li Huowang prepares six grotesque pills: metallic reds, writhing fleshes, and eye-watering blacks. He has dumped every poisonous, corrosive thing he can find into them, creating what he believes is a foolproof suicide cocktail. He knows he must eat them first to make Danyangzi trust the poison.
- **The Countdown and the Act**: As a stick of incense marks the final moments of the year, Li Huowang swallows the pills. He goes into a full-on method-acting performance, faking a transcendent "I see the light" moment, complete with sweating, trembling, and a crowning achievement: thin white smoke rising from his head.
- **The Bait is Taken**: Danyangzi, driven by his greed to achieve immortality and not to be outdone, finally consumes his own set of pills. The reader is already aware that the "method" behind the pills is a complete fabrication, copied from modern xianxia novels. The tension is in waiting for the trap to snap shut.
- **Li Huowang's Confession**: As the pain hits, Li Huowang lets out a manic, hysterical laugh. He taunts Danyangzi, slaps him, punches his face in, and finally reveals that everything—*everything*—was made up. The script was scribbled from novels. There is no immortal method.
- **The Horrifying Failure**: Danyangzi does not die. He shoves Li Huowang away, screaming about "heart-demons," and doggedly continues his mad meditation. His will to believe literally overrides reality, causing a grotesque, cancerous metamorphosis. His head splits open into three overlapping mouths with nested teeth, and his body erupts in pitch-covered feathers. He explodes, announcing his "comprehension" just before showering the room in his remains.
- **The Survivor**: Li Huowang, seemingly doomed, violently vomits the poison and survives. He is left staring in shock at the explosive, bloody aftermath of Danyangzi's "ascension."

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**1. "Three Flowers Crowned at the Summit" (三花举顶):** This is a real Daoist cultivation term, describing the pinnacle of gongfu where one's jing (essence), qi (vital energy), and shen (spirit) converge at the crown of the head. In this novel, it's turned into a horrifyingly literal and surgical event. Danyangzi doesn't refine his spirit; his flesh literally and pathologically splits open to mimic the metaphor.

**2. "Feather Transformation and Ascension" (羽化登仙):** A classic euphemism for an immortal's departure, often involving becoming a crane or simply shedding one's mortal coil "garment" (尸解). Here, the shedding is violently, physically real. The feathers are not the elegant plumage of a crane but spiky, tar-caked blades that tear out of the skin. The novel systematically undermines every graceful trope of Daoist transcendence.

**3. The Mechanics of Belief:** This chapter perfectly illustrates a core rule of the Dao-Twisted World: "Cultivate the False into Reality." Li Huowang's poison pills and fake mantras *should* kill Danyangzi. But Danyangzi's absolute, fanatical belief "cultivates" the false Heavenly Scripture into a real (but monstrous) path, causing a horrifying, chaotic transformation. This is not "the power of positive thinking"; it's a catastrophic interaction between corrupted faith and a reality that punishes certainty.

**4. Heart-Magic vs. Alchemy:** Danyangzi's power in this scene is almost entirely driven by the psychological and emotional force of his obsession, not by correct external alchemy. This is a key divergence from standard xianxia, where alchemy is a craft with fixed rules. Here, the internal state of the cultivator—their delusion and desperation—can override the physical reality of the poison, turning a fatal formula into a catalyst for mutation.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in tension and payoff. The slow ritual of the pills, the countdown of the incense, and Li Huowang's terrifyingly calm performance are all set against the explosive, grotesque climax. Don't expect a clean victory. The punchline is messy, confusing, and leaves Li Huowang—and the reader—wondering what the hell just happened. This is the moment when Li Huowang truly realizes that the rules of this world are not set in stone; they are fluid, dangerous, and dictated by the sheer weight of a person's conviction. For context, stick with the arc. The consequences of this "ascension" are far from over, and the mess it leaves behind is going to attract a lot of unwanted attention.
