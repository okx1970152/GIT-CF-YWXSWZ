## Chapter Overview

Alright, buckle up, fellow transcendents. Chapter 366, "Pain," is one of those chapters where you put the phone down, stare at the wall for a solid minute, and then pick it back up to read it again. This is Li Huowang at his absolute breaking point—forced to choose between his own salvation and the unbearable reality of his attachments. He has to *terminate* a hallucination of the person he cares about most just to escape a recursive trap, and in doing so, he triggers a direct, visceral connection with Bashe. The chapter starts with a brutal, desperate slaughter in the mental ward and ends with Li Huowang becoming a living conduit for divine agony. It’s less a fight scene and more an existential vivisection.

## Key Plot Points

Li Huowang, still trapped inside the Heart-Turbid fabricated layer, finds himself in the mental-stairwell hallucination. He enacts a bloody, near-suicidal assault on the thugs who "killed" Yang Na, moving from victim to predator with terrifying speed. The violence is shocking, raw, and pointed—he is not fighting for survival, but for controlled catharsis. The pivotal moment comes when he cornered the final hallucination of Yang Na. Unlike the fake projections he has faced before, this Yang Na speaks with a perfect, heart-wrenching verisimilitude, questioning why he came to save her when he is already “crazy.” To escape the trap (and the real Bai Lingmiao's danger inside the Outer Dao Heart-Turbid), he is forced to stab this Yang Na. The emotional impact nearly destroys him, but the failure—the inability to follow through—triggers the Black Tai Sui in his throat, shattering the illusion. He is catapulted back to the physical Dao-Twisted forest. There, the *Thousand Greats Record* has become fully animate, its tongue lolling out like a living creature. This triggers a complete sensory fusion with Bashe (巴虺). We get the clearest vision yet of the pain-god's nature and of Li Huowang’s new, terrifying role: he can harvest all pain within his domain and control it.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Heart-Turbid (心浊) vs The Mental Ward Illusion:** The text hints that Li Huowang *needed* to shed the illusion layer to progress. This is a brutal corruption of the xin culturing (心性修炼) trope—normally, a cultivator masters their heart-mind to achieve clarity. Here, Li Huowang had to *shatter* his heart-mind’s deepest attachment to pass through the Heart-Turbid’s trap. The horror is that the "test" of the illusion is not to resist, but to complete an act of horrific violence against your own simulated love. The chapter deliberately weaponizes the reader's emotional investment in the Yang Na arc. It’s a meta-fictional trap: the novel is *using* the tenderest part of its own story to hurt both the protagonist and the audience.

**The "Opening of the Senses" to Bashe (巴虺):** This is a profound moment in the *Thousand Greats Record* pathway. The text describes the *Record* as a living entity composed of red maggots—a grotesque inversion of the "golden text" trope. When Li Huowang’s senses merge, he connects to Bashe, but the hierarchy is critical: he first sees a vast, misty eye (likely a Siming or a ward of the White Jade Capital), and *then* reaches Bashe beyond it. This implies that Bashe’s domain is still, paradoxically, *inside* the "lies" of the White Jade Capital. Li Huowang’s power is an act of theft—he takes control of all pain within his vicinity and pours it into the black and white spheres. The yin-yang (黑一白) description of these spheres suggests he is binding pain to the very structure of duality, making him a fulcrum of suffering that balances reality itself.

### Two-Layer Heart-Turbid Trap (一生一死心浊)

A containment paradox where a dead Heart-Turbid conceals a living one; victims are trapped in a fabricated karmic obstacle layer (the mental ward hallucination) and cannot perceive the real threat.

### Siming (司命)

The high-level Arbiter of Fate entities residing in the White Jade Capital. The misty eye Li Huowang sees before reaching Bashe is likely a Siming's projection acting as a barrier or warden.

### Inner Dao (里道)

The paradoxical space 'inside' the Dao. The chapter reveals that the captured Bai Lingmiao was not inside the outer layer of the Heart-Turbid, but 'even more inside' it, creating a nested, unsolvable problem.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a crucible. If you were feeling any hope that Li Huowang could "get better" or reconcile his two worlds, this chapter is the narrative’s firm answer: the only way forward is through more suffering. The key takeaway isn't the power-up (though it is significant), but the *price*. Li Huowang had to become a functional psychopath inside the hallucination to survive it, and the emotional scarring from stabbing Yang Na is not something that can be healed with a cultivation pill. Also, pay close attention to the "breathing pain" at the end—Li Huowang isn't just causing pain; he is *managing a resource*. This is the new economy of his existence. He’s no longer just a Heart-Element ingredient; he’s becoming a local manager for a god’s domain. The big question is: what happens when he runs out of living things to derive that pain from?
