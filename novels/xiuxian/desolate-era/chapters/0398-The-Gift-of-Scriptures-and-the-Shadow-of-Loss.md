Before long, Ji Ning was granted an audience with the Diancai Immortal.

They sat beneath a quiet, secluded pavilion. A jug of immortal wine rested on the table between two cups, as master and disciple drank and conversed.

“Master, I hope I haven’t disturbed your secluded meditation,” Ji Ning said.

“I was merely sitting in quiet contemplation to settle my heart, not in the throes of some sudden enlightenment. There’s no harm in being disturbed.” The Diancai Immortal looked at Ji Ning with a satisfied nod. “Ji Ning, you’ve grown far more powerful than you were back then. When you first entered my tutelage, you were but a rough piece of jade—young and inexperienced. Now, your divine sense is even stronger than my own. You truly are a peerless genius who could claim first place in the Immortal Fortunes Assembly after only thirty-odd years of cultivation.”

“Master, stop your praise, or your disciple’s tail will be sticking straight up in the air,” Ji Ning said with a laugh.

“You rascal…” The Diancai Immortal shook his head with a smile.

Ji Ning’s expression grew serious. “Master, are you truly going to undergo your tribulation soon? You must be cautious with this Heavenly Tribulation. There is no room for the slightest carelessness.”

“Of course I know. There are countless Earth Immortals and Loose Immortals, yet it is rare for even one to become a Celestial Immortal from the entire Grand Xia world in a span of a million years.” The Diancai Immortal said, “But things are different now. Ji Ning was startled.

However, he did not share the same feeling of urgency to undergo his tribulation immediately. Clearly, thanks to his mastery of the Eight-Nine Arcane Art, his chances of survival were far greater than the Diancai Immortal’s.

“Master.” Ji Ning suddenly waved his hand. Instantly, a pile of books as tall as a man appeared beside him.

“These are…?” The Diancai Immortal was puzzled.

“Daoist scriptures,” Ji Ning replied. He took three thick volumes from the pile. “Master, you should take a look at these three first.”

“Excellent sword arts, excellent sword arts!” The Diancai Immortal’s eyes lit up. He quickly flipped through another book and grew excited once more. “Directly pointing to the Dao, directly pointing to the Dao!”

Ji Ning smiled.

One must understand that the Qi Kingdom had opened up a million li of territory in a mere tens of thousands of years; how could their scriptures be insignificant? The twelve great demon kings and the evil patriarchs were all beings comparable to Celestial Immortals. The combined accumulation of their scriptures was astonishing. Although it could not compare to Mount Innerheart, it was a hundred times richer than the Black-White College’s collection!

“Ji Ning, these scriptures…” The Diancai Immortal looked at Ji Ning.

“Rest assured, Master. Your disciple obtained them legitimately, and there are no lingering troubles. They are prepared for you and for the Black-White College,” Ji Ning said. The supreme techniques of the Mount Innerheart lineage could not be passed on due to the Patriarch’s rules. However, the scriptures acquired by killing demon kings and evil patriarchs had no such restrictions.

The Black-White College had over ten Loose Immortals and Earth Immortals, each one no ordinary cultivator. With these new scriptures, their strength could reach another level.

As for the Ji Clan? Ji Ning had already prepared a set of these scriptures for them using the Watermark Art. He believed that as his own power grew, he would be able to obtain even more impressive scriptures. To fulfill his father’s wish, to honor the hopes of the old Patriarchs, and as a member of the Ji Clan himself, it was only right that he make the clan flourish.

“Good, good, good. These scriptures are truly remarkable.” The Diancai Immortal was deeply moved. “These three sword art manuals you selected have inspired me. I must immediately go into seclusion to ponder them. It has significantly increased my chances of surviving the tribulation.”

Ji Ning nodded.

These three manuals were no less profound than the complete ‘Three-Foot Sword’. They were all Dao of the Sword classics! Within the Black-White College’s Retuning Void Earth Immortals, there were only two Sword Immortals: the Diancai Immortal and Ji Ning himself, so no one would compete with the Diancai Immortal for them.

He could only give the sect the scriptures he had taken from the demon kings and the evil patriarch, and yet this alone had sent them into a frenzy.

“Ji Ning, scriptures are the very foundation of a sect. With these, the Black-White College will certainly flourish, and in the future, it might even be able to rival the most top-tier sects in the Grand Xia Dynasty.” The Diancai Immortal looked at Ji Ning. “All of this is because of you. Your contribution this time is immense.”

It was rare for a disciple to give so many powerful scriptures to their sect rather than hoarding them for their own tribe or family.

“This is nothing,” Ji Ning said, shaking his head.

“The Anchan Commandery is in chaos, and even the entire Grand Xia world is seething with undercurrents and unrest.” The Diancai Immortal said, “With these scriptures, the strength of our Black-White College’s most elite Loose Immortals and Earth Immortals can ascend to another level. This will allow more of our people to survive the coming calamity.” He looked at Ji Ning. “At such a critical juncture, a single set of scriptures like this is far more important than ten or a hundred times as many after the calamity has passed.”

Ji Ning was puzzled. “Master, what chaos is there in the Anchan Commandery?”

“You don’t know?” The Diancai Immortal was taken aback.

“I have only just returned to the Grand Xia. I didn’t have much time to chat with Fifth Crazy and the others before you emerged from seclusion,” Ji Ning explained.

The Grand Xia’s cities forbade murder.

That was the law!

When the Shaoyin clan had sent the Flowing Cloud Immortal before, that had been a willing suicide strike.

Over a hundred assassinations within Anchan City. It was madness.

The Anchan Northmont clan ruled this entire region. They were a marquis!

The Blood God Church was merely one of eight great powers.

In theory, if the Anchan Northmont clan truly set their mind to destroying the Blood God Church, it shouldn’t have been a difficult task.

Whether it’s the clashes of Loose Immortals or the battles of ordinary disciples, the Anchan Northmont clan hasn’t been able to gain the upper hand. You could say it’s been a stalemate.”

Ji Ning listened in disbelief.

A marquis, the rightful ruler of the region, couldn’t eliminate one of the eight great powers within their own territory?

“The Blood God Church’s leader wasn’t this strong in the past.” The Diancai Immortal shook his head. “I’ve even crossed blows with ‘Blood God Zi,’ the leader of the Blood God Church. Only a short time has passed, and his power has risen to be on par with a Celestial Immortal.”

Ji Ning nodded.

They want to recruit the other seven great powers to join them in dealing with the Blood God Church,” the Diancai Immortal said. “But it’s not just our Anchan Commandery that’s in turmoil. The entire Grand Xia world is in chaos. How could any of our sects or tribes act carelessly?”

The entire Grand Xia world was in chaos. It felt as though the old order had been shattered.

It seemed that an incredibly terrifying hand was manipulating everything from the shadows.
