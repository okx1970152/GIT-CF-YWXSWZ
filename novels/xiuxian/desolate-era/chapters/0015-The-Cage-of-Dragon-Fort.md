Three days later, in the early morning, Ji Ning and his parents arrived at Dragon Fort.

Dragon Fort was a hundred zhang wide and three hundred zhang long. It was divided into two sections: the Cage and the Beast Lair. The Cage was where life-and-death battles took place, while the Beast Lair was where demon beasts were normally held in captivity. Because Dragon Fort housed many dangerous demon beasts, it had originally been built in a remote corner of the inner city.

...

Inside the Cage of Dragon Fort.

After entering the Cage, Ji Ning looked around. It was an open space about two or three hundred meters in diameter. The surrounding walls were made entirely of black metal, while overhead, a dense network of black iron chains was woven together, forming an inescapable cage.

"This is completely sealed off," said Ji Yichuan, his voice carrying a faint chill. "The walls are forged from Blackwater Iron. Although Blackwater Iron isn't considered a precious material, even an ordinary early-stage Xiantian expert would find it difficult to damage such a thick wall of it. As for the thick chains above, they are also made of Blackwater Iron. Given your strength... if you were to use the Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens at full power, you should be able to sever the chains. But with so many chains forming a net... you'd need at least ten breaths of time to cut through enough to escape."

Ji Ning nodded.

"Look." Ji Yichuan pointed above the chain net. There was actually a viewing platform up there.

"Your mother and I, along with your Uncle White, will be watching you from up there," Ji Yichuan said coldly.

"Be careful," Yuchi Snow encouraged her son.

"Growl!" The massive snow-white hound also looked at Ji Ning, its eyes filled with encouragement and expectation. This snow-white hound was his father Ji Yichuan's sworn brother. His father had tamed two great demons: one was the massive black serpent, Uncle Black; the other was this snow-white hound, Uncle White.

When beasts gained intelligence, they would begin to absorb the natural energy of Heaven and Earth, becoming demon beasts!

If a demon beast underwent a difficult transformation and crossed into the Xiantian stage, becoming a Xiantian lifeform, it was then called a great demon. Most great demons could take human form. Uncle Black was an example of this. But among demon beasts, there was a special type—Godbeasts.

Godbeasts were demon beasts that possessed the bloodline of ancient Fiendgods. They were born with intelligence, possessed extremely powerful talents, and were far stronger than ordinary demon beasts. However, it was also far more difficult for them to transform into human form, and the difficulty varied depending on the Godbeast.

Some Godbeasts needed to reach the 'Zifu' stage before they could take human form, some needed the Wanxiang stage, and some even higher...

Uncle White was a Godbeast, a 'Whitewater Hound'. He would need to reach the Zifu stage to take human form and be able to speak.

Ji Ning had a very close bond with this Whitewater Hound. Ever since his archery teacher, Meng Yu, had finished his lessons, Ji Ning would go with the Whitewater Hound every morning to the forests outside Westprefecture City to practice his archery. Ji Yichuan was not comfortable letting his son leave the city alone, so he had the Xiantian lifeform 'Whitewater Hound' protect him.

The strength of a Xiantian-level Godbeast was terrifying! Ji Yichuan's transcendent status within the Ji Clan's Western Prefecture was partly due to this Whitewater Hound.

"Just watch," Ji Ning said with a grin.

"Growl." The Whitewater Hound gave a low growl, then followed Ji Yichuan and his wife out through a narrow passage, soon appearing on the viewing platform above.

Ji Ning let out a soft breath to calm himself, then looked around.

"It feels just like a Roman arena," Ji Ning muttered to himself. "A fighting area and a viewing platform." He knew that those who were brought to the Cage to fight demon beasts were typically key members of the clan undergoing special training. Their elders and relatives would watch from the platform.

"Clatter..." A series of chain-rattling sounds came from a passage in the far wall. Ji Ning looked over. From the pitch-black passage came a low, guttural roar that shook the entire Cage, causing the massive chain net above to tremble and clang.

A huge, silver-furred head slowly emerged from the passage.

"That is..." Ji Ning observed carefully.

"Ji Ning." From the viewing platform above, Ji Yichuan said coldly, "You are training in the number one Fiendgod Body Refining method, so I specially selected an extremely powerful demon beast for you—a Howling Moon Wolf with the bloodline of a Fiendgod."

Ji Ning's eyes went wide as he stared up at his father, stunned.

A Fiendgod bloodline?

That meant it was a Godbeast! Of course, Godbeasts were also a type of demon beast. There were still quite a few Godbeasts at the Houtian stage scattered across the boundless lands—creatures like 'Earth-Armor Dragons', 'Mountain-Shaking Rhinoceros', 'Howling Moon Wolves', 'Redclaw Golden Crows', 'Thundersea Shrikes', 'Whitewater Hounds', and many others. Only one in several hundred or a thousand Houtian-level Godbeasts could undergo the difficult breakthrough to become a Xiantian lifeform.

"Clatter..." The sound of chains continued.

Ji Ning stopped thinking and focused on the massive passage in the wall. He knew they were now unlocking the chains. Once the chains were completely released, the Howling Moon Wolf would be set free.

"Clang!" The sound of chains hitting the ground.

"Aoow!" A joyful wolf howl rang out. Above, Ji Yichuan, Yuchi Snow, and the Whitewater Hound all watched the scene below intently.

Ji Ning held his breath.

A massive creature slowly emerged from the passage. It was covered in beautiful silver fur, standing about two zhang tall, and it walked out with a graceful, leisurely stride. It looked down at the tiny figure below—a human youth. As a special type of demon beast known as a Godbeast, its intelligence was no less than a human's.

It understood that when it entered this Cage, there were only two possible outcomes. One: it would kill the human and continue living. Two: it would be killed by the human youth.

"Choosing a Godbeast for my very first trial," Ji Ning said, reaching his right hand out. A sword materialized in his hand, its blade gleaming coldly. "Then let's fight."

The Howling Moon Wolf's massive body weighed nearly ten thousand jin, yet its four hooves moved lightly as it advanced, its narrow eyes fixed on the human youth.

Holding his longsword, Ji Ning walked forward step by step.

The distance between them closed rapidly.

"Boom!" The Howling Moon Wolf transformed from its previous elegance into pure savagery. It lunged forward, becoming a blur as it sprang directly at Ji Ning. Its soft paws flashed like unsheathed blades as its sharp claws extended.

Swoosh!

At the critical moment, Ji Ning moved his feet, flowing like the wind as he barely evaded the leaping attack. At the same time, his longsword shot forward in a precise thrust! The thrust was decisive and lightning-fast. If it could pierce the Howling Moon Wolf's body, the creature's own momentum would rip the wound wide open!

"Hm?" The moment he thrust, Ji Ning's expression changed. The tip of his sword met tremendous resistance. The Howling Moon Wolf's hide was incredibly tough, stopping the sword's point cold and preventing it from penetrating.

At the same time, the Howling Moon Wolf's tail whipped around. Unable to dodge in time, Ji Ning hurriedly raised his sword to block.

Boom!

The Howling Moon Wolf's tail struck the sword like a heavy steel whip. The immense force sent Ji Ning flying, his body slamming heavily into the distant Blackwater Iron wall. Boom! The thick wall shuddered violently.

"Roar!" The Howling Moon Wolf followed up with another lunge, its claws reaching for Ji Ning.

Ji Ning quickly dodged, leaping away.

Sss! Sss! Sss! Deep claw marks were gouged into the Blackwater Iron wall. The Howling Moon Wolf landed and turned its head to look at the distant human youth. Its tail whip was one of its lethal techniques. That single sweep had sent the human youth crashing into the wall. Yet the youth had instantly escaped to a safe distance. It understood now—this human youth was truly powerful, strong enough to fight it on equal terms.

"An ordinary Nine-Fang Warrior would likely have had their internal organs shattered by that tail whip and died," Ji Ning frowned, looking at the distant Howling Moon Wolf. "It seems I'll have to rely on the power of my Fiendgod body."

He had also been training in Ki Refining over the years and had reached the Houtian perfection stage. But a sword strike infused with Houtian perfection Ki couldn't even pierce its fur. How could he fight like this?

"Hmph!"

Ji Ning let out a low snort, and two visible streams of air shot from his nostrils, causing the very air to tremble. In that moment, the terrifying power lurking deep within his seemingly slender frame was fully unleashed. The Taiyin and Sunstar power hidden within his body also surged, causing a faint reddish tint to spread across his skin.

Ji Ning lightly waved his longsword. The air current created by the blade cutting through the air directly carved a deep furrow into the thick, packed earth.

The distant Howling Moon Wolf let out a low growl, its eyes locked onto the youth.

...

"Finally, the boy is using the Crimsonbright Diagram," Yuchi Snow said with a smile. "He was stubbornly refusing to use it before."

Ji Yichuan nodded. "The Howling Moon Wolf possesses a Fiendgod bloodline. Its hide is incredibly tough. An ordinary Houtian perfection warrior couldn't pierce it at all. I specifically chose the Howling Moon Wolf... to force Ji Ning to unleash all his strength, to see how well he could truly perform in a life-or-death battle."

"And now? What do you think of his performance?" Yuchi Snow pressed.

"Average," Ji Yichuan said, his eyes fixed on the scene below. "He's still calm, at least."

...

Ji Ning, holding his longsword, circled the Howling Moon Wolf, taking large steps around it.

The Howling Moon Wolf kept its eyes locked on Ji Ning, not launching an attack lightly. Because once it attacked... it would also reveal openings. In battles like this, life and death were often decided in a single, instantaneous moment.

"Hmph." Without any warning, Ji Ning turned into a blurry shadow, closing in on the Howling Moon Wolf.

The Howling Moon Wolf immediately roared and lunged, baring its fangs and extending its claws.

Swish!

A flash of sword-light!

As the sword shadow cut through the air, it shifted direction nine times in quick succession, accelerating to a terrifying speed. It was as if the very air was being torn apart. Sssssss! The sword shadow slashed across the Howling Moon Wolf's chest, carving a huge wound. Blood sprayed out.

"Clang." After delivering the sword strike, Ji Ning immediately turned his sword, blocking one of the Howling Moon Wolf's claws, and used the momentum to leap back.

The Howling Moon Wolf bared its fangs and roared at Ji Ning, its chest wound clenching as the bleeding slowed. But blood was still slowly seeping out. It was clear that the wound was simply too large.

The Howling Moon Wolf could truly feel death approaching!

"That 'Lightning Flash' technique from the Thunderfire Sword Fragments is truly impressive," Ji Ning said softly to himself.
