These past few days, writing has felt a little exhausting. A mental exhaustion, a numbness. I no longer have the same burning passion and drive I once did.

I've been trying to adjust these past few days, but every day, I keep procrastinating, putting it off until very late before I update.

I think it's because I've written over a million words, and I've hit a bottleneck. This feeling of exhaustion is really unpleasant. I've already planned out the story's direction, and I think it's full of passion—after all, the great calamity has already begun; the protagonist's repeated crises, great battles, and opportunities are all about to start... But I just can't seem to write it well. This state of mind simply isn't suitable for good writing.

After thinking it over, I've decided to take three days of rest. To completely relax and unwind.

My goal is to write a truly good, exciting, and *uninterrupted* story.

I hope everyone can understand.
