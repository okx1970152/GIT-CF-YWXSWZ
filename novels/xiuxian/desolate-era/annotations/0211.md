## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists—this chapter delivers one of the most terrifying power-gap moments in the entire Desolate Era saga so far. Ji Ning has just gone toe-to-toe with the Dragon Whale King, a newly minted Primal great demon who's throwing around Primalfire like it's pocket change. And for a hot second, Ning is actually holding his own with the Starseizing Hand and the Water-Fire Lotus. But then the Dragon Whale reveals his true form—a three-thousand-zhang leviathan—and the tables flip hard. Just when it looks like our heroes are about to get crushed, something *else* wakes up. Something *far* older and *far* more terrifying. A giant, fiery hand bursts from the earth, crushes the Dragon Whale's head like a grape, and a thousand-zhang-tall Fiendgod emerges from the lava. The message is clear: in this world, there is always a bigger fish. And sometimes, that fish is a primordial nightmare from the Fiendgod Era.

## Key Plot Points

• The Dragon Whale King, having broken through to the Primal Daoist stage, reveals his true form—a colossal dragon-whale three thousand zhang long—and easily overwhelms Ji Ning in raw power.
• Ji Ning commands Mu Northson and the Skygreen Serpent to flee toward the sealing formation and attempt to break it, intending to buy them time by engaging the Dragon Whale in a delaying battle.
• In the midst of the chaos, an absolutely overwhelming force descends, freezing everything in place: lava, rubble, water droplets, and even the Dragon Whale himself, who is rendered completely immobile.
• A massive, fiery-red hand, wreathed in magma, emerges from the cracked earth and crushes the Dragon Whale King's head like an egg, annihilating not just his body but his soul and truesoul entirely.
• A colossal, single-eyed Fiendgod—its head alone over a thousand zhang tall—rises from the earth, stating that the "tiny earthworm" had disturbed its sleep and deserved death.
• The Fiendgod scans Ji Ning, Mu Northson, and Qingqing with utter indifference, treating them as insignificant as ants.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Primalfire (元火):** This is the signature flame of a Primal Daoist (元神道人). When a cultivator breaks through to the Primal stage, their soul undergoes a qualitative transformation, and their Ki refines into a higher-grade energy that can manifest as Primalfire. It's distinct from mundane fire or even Earthfire—it carries the authority of the cultivator's own soul and Dao comprehension, making it far more destructive to both physical and magical defenses.

**The Starseizing Hand (摘星手):** Ji Ning's signature divine ability from Daoist Threelives, ranked among the top ten of the entire Three Realms since Pangu. It essentially turns his hands into living magic treasures. The fact that he could even parry the Dragon Whale King's attacks while using it—despite the massive realm gap—is a testament to how absurdly overpowered this technique is. But raw power can only bridge so much when the enemy reveals their true form and brings out their lifeblood treasures.

**The Fiendgod Era (神魔时代):** This is a crucial piece of worldbuilding. Before the current age of Immortals and schools, the world was ruled by Fiendgods—beings born directly from Heaven and Earth, often of colossal size, with innate divine patterns and terrifying power. They were the original overlords. The fact that one is waking up *here*, deep beneath the earth, speaks to how ancient and layered this world's history is. It also raises the terrifying question: if this one is still alive, how many more are sleeping out there?

## Reading Guide

• Watch Ji Ning's tactical shift in this chapter. The moment he realizes he can't overpower the Dragon Whale, he doesn't panic—he immediately pivots to a delaying strategy, buying time for his allies to break the sealing formation. That's a sign of a mature Dao-heart, not just raw combat instinct.
• The freezing of the entire battlefield is a masterclass in power scaling. The author doesn't just *tell* us the Fiendgod is strong—he *shows* it by making every other character, including a Primal great demon, completely helpless. This is the "absoluteness of power" that defines Xianxia at its highest levels.
• Pay close attention to the way the Fiendgod speaks. He doesn't threaten, he doesn't gloat—he simply *states* facts. "A tiny earthworm dared to destroy my cave." That casual, cosmic indifference is what makes ancient Fiendgods so terrifying. They don't hate you; they just find you inconvenient.

## Fiendgod Lore

**Fiendgods (神魔):** In the Desolate Era universe, Fiendgods are primordial beings spontaneously born from the fundamental forces of Heaven and Earth. Unlike cultivators who must *train* to gain power, Fiendgods are innately powerful, often possessing divine abilities and innate divine patterns etched into their very flesh. They were the dominant race during the Fiendgod Era, a time before the rise of Immortals and the great schools. They are defined by their gargantuan size, raw physical might, and a certain elemental or conceptual nature—this one, for example, seems to be a being of lava and earth.
