## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists! This chapter is pure, uncut cultivation *porn* in the best sense of the word—a deep, satisfying dive into Ji Ning’s power-up mechanics. We watch him efficiently top off his Zifu cultivation, learn the *exact* cost and reward matrix for building a Celestial Immortal foundation, and then get lost in a new sword manual that makes him feel like a kid again. Meanwhile, the plot machinery is quietly assembling at the Dao Debate Palace. We see Yinxue Wenjian nursing his wounded pride after a certain grotto incident and old disciples scheming to put the new geniuses in their place. Mu Northson, innocent and eager, gets his invitation and marches straight into the trap with his eyes full of stars. The calm before the storm is filled with the sweet sound of pages turning and power levels rising.

## Key Plot Points

1. **Zifu Perfection Achieved**: Ji Ning uses roughly 80 jin of Primordial Liquid to push his Ki Refining cultivation from early Zifu to the Zifu Perfection stage in a single intensive session. His Yuan power lake reaches maximum quality.
2. **The Foundation Blueprint**: The chapter delivers a crucial systems lecture on the Wanxiang stage. The strength of one’s projection (stars only, stars+moon, or stars+moon+sun) determines future potential. The ultimate prize—Moon Rabbit and Golden Crow manifestations—requires 800 jin of Primordial Liquid to be consumed at the breakthrough threshold.
3. **The *Little Five Elements Sword Manual***: Ji Ning starts studying his first major sect sword art. The narration emphasizes his childlike fascination and the sheer depth of a manual that is valuable enough to compete with divine abilities in cost.
4. **The Grudge Summons**: A blue-robed senior disciple visits Yinxue Wenjian to remind him of his embarrassment during the trials. Yinxue Wenjian hears that Ji Ning has been taken as a personal disciple by the Diancai Immortal, which stokes his competitive fire.
5. **The Unspoken Rule of the Dao Debate Palace**: The senior disciple reveals the college’s tradition of “humbling” new geniuses in the safe sparring grounds. Yinxue Wenjian accepts the invitation, but only on the condition that he fights Ji Ning.
6. **Mu Northson’s Invitation**: The blue-robed maiden Donglin visits Mu Northson. He eagerly accepts the invitation to the Dao Debate Palace, seeing it as a chance to test himself against worthy opponents, completely unaware of the political undertones.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Wanxiang Investment Strategy**: This chapter is a masterclass in the Xianxia trope of “resource-gated power.” The jump from Zifu to Wanxiang isn’t just about breaking a bottleneck; it’s about how *richly* you break it. The quality of your projection (and thus your potential to become a Celestial Immortal) is directly tied to the sheer volume of energy you can stomach during the breakthrough. Five hundred jin of Primordial Liquid gets you a Sun-Moon projection. Eight hundred jin gets you the divine beasts inside them. This is the cold, hard math of a cultivation world where talent is the raw material, but resources and sect guidance are the forging hammer. A rogue cultivator with a poor manual would be forever stuck with a Starlight projection, their path to Immortality blocked not by a lack of will, but by a lack of *currency*.

**The Dao Debate Palace**: This institution is a brilliant narrative tool. It allows for high-stakes ideological and skill-based duels without the risk of permanent death or crippling injury, which would be a disaster for a sect’s investment in its geniuses. It is a space for safe conflict, a release valve for internal rivalry, and a mechanism for maintaining the social hierarchy. The “unspoken rule” of humbling new disciples is a classic Xianxia trope: the upperclassmen’s prank or hazing ritual that serves to shock the protagonist out of their provincial arrogance and into the reality of a far more competitive playground.

### Daodebate Palace (论道殿)

A special facility within the Black-White College where formal disciples can spar safely. The battles are purely about one's comprehension and application of the Dao, completely independent of raw Yuan power or magic treasure tiers.

## Reading Guide

Prepare for some of the most satisfying “menu browsing” we’ve seen so far. If you love the granular details of a cultivation system, this chapter is a feast. Watch Ji Ning do the math on his future power—this kind of strategic planning is a core part of his character, separating him from the blindly reckless. The chapter also sets up a classic undercurrent of pride and manipulation. Yinxue Wenjian is an honorable rival, not a cartoon villain. His anger is understandable, and his desire for a rematch feels earned. Meanwhile, Mu Northson’s infectious enthusiasm makes him a prime target for the older disciples. The stage is set for a major sparring session, and it’s going to be the first real test of how Ji Ning and Mu Northson’s talents stack up against the established disciple corps.
