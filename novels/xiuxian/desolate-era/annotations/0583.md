## Chapter Overview

Ji Ning walks the razor’s edge of a cosmic gambit—forcing the Seamless Gate to sue for peace by painting a target on his own back. After receiving the master’s reluctant blessing, he retreats to Starseizing Manor and burns through a mountain of rare materials in a single day, pushing both the Eight-Nine Arcane Art and the Starseizing Hand to their next thresholds. The real payoff comes when he finally breaches Daofather Threelives’s final prohibition, unlocking three Innate Supreme-grade Spirit-Treasures whose purposes are as distinct as their personalities: one for unmatched escape, one for flawless decoy, and one for pure, savage murder.

## Key Plot Points

- Subhuti warns Ji Ning that his plan to force the Seamless Gate into a negotiated peace is akin to walking a tightrope over an abyss of death; but after hearing Ji Ning’s resolve, he agrees to help locate the Seamless Gate’s hidden strongholds.
- Ji Ning returns to the aquatic palace and completes the ninth cycle of the Eight-Nine Arcane Art and the fifth cycle of the Starseizing Hand in a single day, exhausting the Immortal pills granted by Daofather Crimsonbright.
- The seven Empyrean Gods of Starseizing Manor—Redjade, Yuanguang, Snow Scorpion, and the rest—enviously joke about how Ji Ning and Redjade both got to learn the legendary Arcane Art under Patriarch Subhuti.
- Ji Ning opens the final formation restriction with the enhanced Starseizing Hand and enters the hidden chamber, where Daofather Threelives’s remnant will leaves a heartfelt plea to treat the loyal guardians well.
- Three treasures are revealed: the Void Boat (supreme speed escape), the Nine-Life Tower (nine indistinguishable avatars for distraction), and a nameless cluster of beads (a murder-focused supreme treasure whose spirit warns Ji Ning he may not be able to control it yet).

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Eight-Nine Arcane Art (八九玄功)** is the undisputed number one divine ability below the True God level in the Three Realms. It grants the practitioner an almost indestructible body—enemies need to surpass the cultivator’s realm by a vast margin just to land a meaningful blow. Its ninth cycle is notoriously expensive, costing a Daofather real heartache to fund, which is why even the seven veteran Empyrean Gods of Starseizing Manor are openly jealous.

**Innate Supreme-grade Spirit-Treasures (先天极致灵宝)** are treasures born directly from the primordial chaos or the pure essences of creation, rather than being forged by any craftsman. They possess intelligent artifact spirits and often have unique, irreplaceable powers. The Void Boat was born from a wisp of pure qi over 84,000 years; the Nine-Life Tower was refined from chaotic materials; the murderous bead cluster may have an even more ancient origin. In Xianxia logic, such treasures are always tiered above human-made artifacts and often carry restrictions on who can wield them—especially the ones meant for killing.

**The Trope of the Last Testament:** Daofather Threelives’s dying words to Ji Ning reflect a deep Xianxia theme—a master who failed to achieve his ultimate goal (becoming a Daofather) leaves behind not only techniques and treasures but also a moral charge: protect the loyal followers. This echoes the Confucian virtue of faithfulness (忠义) and gives the inheritance emotional weight beyond mere power-ups.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a classic “power upgrade + loot reveal” beat, but with an emotional twist. The three treasures form a deliberate tactical suite—two for escape and misdirection, one for pure offense. Ji Ning’s biggest takeaway isn’t the power itself but the *responsibility* that comes with it. Pay attention to the artifact spirits’ personalities: the blunt, impatient killing bead; the patient, farmer-like Void Boat spirit; and the cheerful, informative Nine-Life Tower monk. Their introductions foreshadow how Ji Ning will (or won’t) use them in the coming storm.

The thematic contrast between Daofather Threelives’s reckless courage (charging into the Dawn War despite knowing the odds) and Ji Ning’s cold, calculated strategy highlights two different versions of Xianxia heroism. Which path will Ji Ning walk? The answer may define his survival in the upcoming confrontation with the Seamless Gate.
