## Chapter Overview
Get ready, because Ji Ning is about to uncover a massive family secret that puts even the Ji Clan’s history to shame! The *Windwing Evasion* manual his mother left isn’t just any movement technique—it’s a full-blown Divine Ability passed down by a freaking Celestial Immortal. That’s right, the Yuchi Clan has an origin story straight out of a legendary myth: a slave-turned ancestor saves a dying Immortal child, gets mentored for a century, and inherits one of the most coveted mobility arts in the Grand Xia. Ji Ning spends a month in the wilderness devouring the Roc Diagrams, not just training but *comprehending* the Dao within them. The result? A movement speed that makes Tie Muzhan’s talisman-boosted dash look like a crawl. But the real treat is the payoff: now that he’s a Xiantian lifeform, it’s time to make good on his promise and head straight for Winged Serpent Lake. The hunt is on.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Yuchi Clan’s Shocking Origin**: Ji Ning reads the *Windwing Evasion* manual for the first time, learning its true origin. The Yuchi Clan was founded by a slave, Yuchi Antong, who rescued a dying Celestial Immortal, Su Huan. After a century of personal tutelage, Su Huan granted the clan this Divine Ability and left.
- **Divine Ability vs. Ordinary Technique**: The chapter draws a clear line between mere techniques and true Divine Abilities. The *Windwing Evasion* isn’t something you can just learn from a book—it’s a profound, ancestral art that requires Fiendgod Body Refining and the Zifu stage to fully master. Ji Ning realizes how rare and dangerous this inheritance is.
- **The Eighty-One Roc Diagrams**: Ji Ning dives into the core of the manual: eighty-one drawings of a Roc in flight, each imbued with starlight points showing the flow of divine power. He instantly recognizes their profundity, matching the feeling of comprehending the True Meaning of the Dao, and begins absorbing their insights.
- **A Month of Breakthrough-Level Training**: Ji Ning spends over a month in the wilderness, repeatedly practicing the *Windwing Evasion*. The result is a dramatic leap in speed and grace—he can cross over a li in a single bound, faster even than a talisman-boosted Xiantian cultivator.
- **The Return and the Hunt**: Qiuye and Mowu have been waiting anxiously at a Black Armored Guard outpost. When Ji Ning returns, his aura has changed—more refined, more attuned to heaven and earth. He gives the order: “To Winged Serpent Lake.” The showdown between Ji Ning and the Winged Serpent is finally on the horizon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of a Divine Ability (神通) is crucial in high-level Xianxia. Unlike ordinary martial techniques or spells, a Divine Ability is an *innate law* or *primordial talent* of a powerful being, often a Fiendgod or an ancient Immortal, that has been written down and transmitted. Think of it as a pre-installed cheat code for reality. Famous examples include the *Heavenly Transformation* (gigantification), *Three Heads, Six Arms*, and *Houyi Shoots the Suns*—all listed here. Training in a Divine Ability requires specific physical prerequisites; the *Windwing Evasion* needs Fiendgod Body Refining and the Zifu stage, which is why Ji Ning’s mother forbade him from reading it until he reached Xiantian.

The story of Yuchi Antong and Celestial Immortal Su Huan is a classic Xianxia trope: the hidden expert meets the kind-hearted mortal. It’s pure serendipity—a luck-based origin story that explains why a seemingly small clan like the Yuchi possesses a treasure that even the Grand Xia’s top tribes would covet. The Roc Diagrams also echo a common theme in cultivation fiction: the use of animal imagery to convey the flow of energy and the ‘shape’ of a technique. The Great Roc isn’t just a bird; it’s a symbol of ultimate freedom, speed, and the Dao of the Wind.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is all about *setup* and *reward*. It’s a beautiful example of the “hidden inheritance” trope done right. Ji Ning has been slowly uncovering his mother’s secrets since the beginning, and now we get the payoff: the *Windwing Evasion* is a genuine Divine Ability with a legendary backstory. Pay attention to how the Eighty-One Roc Diagrams are described—this is a classic Xianxia moment where the protagonist gains a breakthrough just by *looking* at the right image. The profundity isn't in the text, but in the art itself.

Also, note the deliberate contrast: Tie Muzhan had to use talismans to boost his speed, while Ji Ning’s unaided movement is now vastly superior. This isn’t just a power-up; it’s a show of the difference between cheap tricks (talismans) and the true, inherited path of cultivation. And finally, the last line—*the hunt is on*—is a delicious promise. The Winged Serpent has been built up as a major threat. Now Ji Ning, armed with his newly mastered techniques and a legendary movement art, is confident enough to go head-to-head with it. Buckle up; the first major boss fight of the arc is coming.
