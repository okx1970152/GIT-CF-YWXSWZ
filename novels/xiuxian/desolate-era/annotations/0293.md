## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists! The treasure-bartering arc hits its climax in this chapter, and let me tell you—it’s not just about the coin. Ji Ning steps into the Heavenly Treasures Mountain HQ in the Grand Xia capital to liquidate his jaw-dropping loot from the Wu Jiang Immortal Estate. But what starts as a cold-eyed negotiation over mountains of Primordial Liquid quickly sets the stage for something far bigger. We get a masterclass in Xianxia economics, a hint of a monthly auction that could hold game-changing treasures, and—most importantly—a long-lost family member finally catching wind of our protagonist. Yes, the emotional bombshell is real. Someone’s about to crash the party, and she’s bringing the Gold-Armored Imperial Guard.

## Key Plot Points
- **Treasure Appraisal Frenzy**: Two purple-robed treasures buyers from Heavenly Treasures Mountain (Yu Qi and Ying Hua) show up to bid on Ji Ning’s massive haul. The sheer scale shocks even these veteran appraisers: over 100,000 Human-ranked, 10,000 Earth-ranked, and 100+ Heaven-ranked magic treasures are laid out, alongside rare herbs, pills, and life-saving oddities.
- **Cold-Eyed Bargaining**: Ji Ning demonstrates his upgraded Dao-heart. Refusing to be swayed by flattery or pressure, he pits the two bidders against each other. The short, fat old man bows out when Yu Qi makes a borderline reckless bid of 2.6 million catties of Primordial Liquid—a 1 million jumping increase from their initial estimate.
- **The Xia Medallion Earned**: With a total transaction above the 500,000-catty threshold, Ji Ning secures the coveted ‘Xia’-character medallion, granting him permanent 40% discounts at Heavenly Treasures Mountain, putting him on par with Loose Immortals and Earth Immortals.
- **The Monthly Treasure Competition Assembly**: Yu Qi tips Ji Ning about the midnight auction, where Immortal-ranked treasures occasionally surface. Ji Ning’s interest piques when he learns the Shaoyan clan will be present, hinting at future confrontation.
- **The Secret Sister Rises**: In the Yan Estate, a green-robed woman—a Xiamang clan princess—receives the intelligence report. Her reaction is explosive: tears, trembling laughter, and an immediate order to mobilize the Gold-Armored Imperial Guard. She swears to protect her “only little brother” from the Shaoyan clan.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Primordial Liquid (元液) Economy**: This chapter is a crash course in Xianxia high finance. Primordial Liquid is the universal high-end currency, and 2.6 million catties is a fortune that can bankroll a small sect’s operations for centuries. The 60% acquisition price standard reveals how middlemen like Heavenly Treasures Mountain take a massive cut, but for bulk or rare items, the profit margins can flip.
- **The ‘Xia’ Medallion (夏字令牌)**: This isn’t just a VIP card. In the Grand Xia Dynasty’s hierarchical system, the medallion signifies that its bearer is recognized as a major player—on par with Earth Immortals and Loose Immortals in commercial privilege. It’s a form of soft power, tying high-value cultivators closer to the imperial clan’s economic ecosystem.
- **Gold-Armored Imperial Guard (金甲禁军)**: These are not ordinary soldiers. As the personal guard of a Xiamang clan king’s daughter, they represent the absolute pinnacle of mortal and immortal military might within the dynasty’s capital. Their mobilization signals that the green-robed woman holds significant political weight and is willing to throw it around.
- **Family Bonds in Xianxia**: The emotional weight of the sister’s reveal hits hard because Xianxia often treats family as a sacred but distant anchor. Reuniting with a blood relative, especially one who holds power and has been searching for you, is a classic trope that can shift the entire power balance of a plot arc.

## Reading Guide
- **Emotional Payoff Incoming**: This chapter is the calm before a very loud storm. The treasure sale is excellent world-building, but the real treat is the setup. That green-robed princess is clearly Ji Ning’s maternal aunt’s child—his *only* remaining close kin in this world. Brace yourself for a reunion that will rock Ji Ning’s social standing in the capital.
- **Watch the Power Dynamics**: Ji Ning is now rich, but in the capital, wealth alone is not enough. The Shaoyan clan is still a threat. The arrival of a royal relative with military power fundamentally changes the calculus. Will this sister be a shield, a sword, or a new source of political complications?
- **The Auction Hook**: The midnight Treasure Competition Assembly is a classic Xianxia plot device. Expect rare items, rivalries, and probably a bidding war that escalates into a confrontation. Ji Ning’s interest in Five Elements Essence for his cultivation hints at a focused shopping list.
