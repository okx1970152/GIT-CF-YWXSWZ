## Chapter Overview

Alright, fellow cultivators, buckle up. This chapter is a masterclass in the quiet, methodical side of Xianxia progression that makes the explosive breakthroughs so satisfying. Ji Ning is done with the chaos of the Dao Debate Hall and is now a full-fledged Black-White College disciple. But being a 'monster genius' doesn't mean you get to coast. Our boy has to make some brutally pragmatic decisions about his development path. He questions his freakishly huge Zifu Lake (a classic sign of supreme talent), goes on a shopping spree in the Dao Repository Hall, and then makes a choice that will define his combat style for ages. He forgoes the immediate flashiness of a Divine Ability for a stealthy, soul-based killer move: the Soul-Shattering Art. This chapter is a perfect example of the "inside the dojo" montage that shows a genius isn't just born; they are forged through deep thought, strategic sacrifice, and intense, solitary training.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Freakish Zifu Question:** Ji Ning finally asks his master, the Diancai Immortal, why his Zifu Lake seems to have no bottom. The answer is a flex: his supreme Fiendgod body from the Crimsonbright Diagram, combined with the *Waterdrop Manual*, creates a foundation so solid it’s terrifying. The master estimates he needs a staggering 1,600–2,000 jin of Primordial Liquid.
*   **The Master's Tailored Curriculum:** The Diancai Immortal proves his worth as a teacher. He gives Ji Ning a list of 36 specific sword technique books to read. This isn't random; they are personally chosen to shore up his weaknesses and build his style.
*   **The Crossroads of Power:** Ji Ning faces a classic resource dilemma. He has enough Black-White Pearls for either a Divine Ability or a Soul-Nurturing Art, but not both. The choice is agonizing.
*   **Pragmatic Sacrifice:** Ji Ning’s Dao-heart shines. Despite the immense temptation of flashy Divine Abilities like the *Blazingheart Eye* or *Divine Lightning Eye*, he coldly calculates that his existing *Starseizing Hand* is superior. He chooses the *Soul-Shattering Art* as a new, versatile killer move.
*   **The Three-Year Time Skip:** The chapter closes with a classic Xianxia narrative device. The story jumps forward three years, establishing a period of focused, peaceful cultivation. Ji Ning is now twenty years old, and the real adventures are about to begin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Economics of Immortality: The Black-White Pearl**

This chapter gives us a fantastic look at the internal economy of a Xianxia sect. Forget gold; the real currency among cultivators is the **Black-White Pearl (黑白丹)**. Think of it as a universal credit system for immortal resources. You earn them by competing in sect tournaments (like the Dao Debate Hall) and receiving official rewards for major breakthroughs (like entering the Dao of the Sword). You spend them on everything—techniques, treasures, medicine. This introduces a brilliant layer of strategic resource management. Even a top genius like Ji Ning can't have everything; he has to ruthlessly prioritize, choosing between a soul art and a cool eye-beam technique. This economic tension makes every choice matter and grounds the otherwise lofty world of immortals in relatable, practical problems.

**The Soul-Shattering Art: A Sneaky Killer Move**

The **Soul-Shattering Art (撼神术)** is a perfect example of the esoteric side of Xianxia combat. It’s not a flashy fireball or a metal-shattering punch. It’s a soul-technique. By creating a complex, runic *Soul-Shattering Seal*, Ji Ning can weaponize his divine sense into a mental shockwave. This is devastating because it bypasses most physical defenses. Only a cultivator with a strong soul can resist it. Ji Ning’s genius here is twofold: First, he recognizes that his soul is abnormally powerful (Primal-level divine sense), making this art a perfect fit. Second, he chooses the *potential* of hidden, direct-damage power over the *certainty* of a visible, but less effective, Divine Ability. This shows a deep, strategic understanding of his own strengths and weaknesses.

**The "Monster Genius" Trope**

The Diancai Immortal's estimation that Ji Ning needs 1,600 to 2,000 jin of Primordial Liquid is a classic 'monster genius' flex. In Xianxia, a larger foundation (Zifu Lake) is always better. It means you can store more power, fight longer, and your 'explosion' when you breakthrough will be exponentially more powerful. A normal genius hits the 800-jin mark and is considered to have a Celestial Immortal foundation. Ji Ning's body, however, is demanding twice that. This isn't a weakness; it’s a testament to the sheer, terrifying potential of his combined Crimsonbright Diagram and *Waterdrop Manual* cultivation. The higher the ceiling, the more resources it takes to fill, but the payoff is legendary.

## Reading Guide

This is a chapter that rewards the patient reader. It's the "calm before the storm." If you're here for non-stop action, hold on—it’s coming. But for now, get inside Ji Ning’s head. Watch him think. Observe how he makes his choices. This is where the character is truly built. Pay close attention to the value of a master teacher like the Diancai Immortal. He isn't just giving Ji Ning power; he's giving him *direction*. Also, keep an eye on that *Soul-Shattering Art*. It’s a quiet investment that is going to pay off big when Ji Ning inevitably runs into an enemy he can't just overpower with his swords. The three-year time skip is your signal that the initial training arc is complete. Get ready. The world of the Grand Xia is about to get a lot bigger, and Ji Ning is about to step out of the college and into the wild.
