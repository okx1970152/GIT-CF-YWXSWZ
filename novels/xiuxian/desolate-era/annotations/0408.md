## Chapter Overview
Get ready, because this chapter is a full-on emotional gut-punch wrapped in the most spectacular lightning show you’ll ever see in Xianxia. Diancai Immortal is standing at the precipice of his Dao, his entire life’s cultivation hinging on whether he can survive the Heavenly Tribulation. Armed with his five newly acquired top-grade Immortal-ranked flying swords, the Plowshares Immortal swords, he begins the ascent. What starts as a triumphant display of power—casually swatting away lightning bolts—quickly spirals into a desperate, bloody struggle against the heavens. He pushes through the four sets of nine bolts with a forbidden technique, only to be confronted with a fifth set… and then a sixth. The chapter is a masterclass in dramatic tension, where the reader’s hope is built up and shattered in waves, culminating in a final, heart-wrenching cry of defiance that perfectly captures the brutal, indifferent cruelty of a Xianxia tribulation.

## Key Plot Points
* Diancai Immortal begins his tribulation with supreme confidence, using a single Plowshares Immortal sword to easily block the first 18 bolts, showcasing the immense power of his new treasures.
* He switches to a Three Talents Sword Formation for the third set of nine bolts, demonstrating his mastery over multiple Daos and his signature combat style.
* The fourth set of nine bolts (the Four-Nine Tribulation) forces him to unleash all five swords, forming the mighty Five Elements Sword Formation, which he manages to hold.
* Just as success seems assured, the tribulation extends to a fifth set of nine bolts (a Forty-Five Bolt Tribulation), pushing Diancai to his absolute limit.
* In a desperate gamble, Diancai activates a forbidden technique, burning his Golden Lotus Primal to boost his power, barely surviving each subsequent blast.
* The climactic forty-fifth bolt of lightning breaks his formation, but he barely blocks it with a last-second deployment of his original five flying swords, taking a grievous wound in the process.
* The crushing twist: the lightning vortex does not dissipate. A sixth set of nine bolts, a full Six-Nine Tribulation, is revealed to be waiting for Diancai Immortal, snatching away the readers’ joy and plunging everyone into utter despair.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Heavenly Tribulation (天劫)** is the ultimate bottleneck for any Ki Refiner in Desolate Era. It's a mandatory "exit exam" designed by the Heavenly Dao itself to cull the weak. The lightning is not just energy; it's a manifestation of cosmic law punishing a mortal for trying to ascend to immortality. The bolt counts are critical: sets of nine represent increasing tiers of difficulty. A Four-Nine Tribulation (36 bolts) is considered the standard threshold, within which a prepared cultivator has a real chance. Anything beyond that, like the Five-Nine (45 bolts) or the Six-Nine (54 bolts), is a death sentence that usually appears only for monstrous talents or disciples of the highest powers like Daofathers. This makes the *unfairness* of Diancai’s tribulation all the more tragic. The myth of Prometheus’s eagle consuming his liver is grim; the Xianxia Heavenly Tribulation is a grim, bureaucratic, and seemingly arbitrary executioner.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the ultimate test of a cultivator's Dao-heart—not just for the character, but for the reader as well. Brace yourself for a relentless escalation of tension. Pay close attention to the *rhythm* of the tribulation; the author deliberately builds hope after each set of nine, only to shatter it with the announcement of the next. The dialogue between Ji Ning and Wufeng is your emotional barometer—they voice the hope and fear you should be feeling. The real payoff isn't just the spectacle of the lightning, but the raw, desperate character moment when Diancai laughs after surviving the forty-fifth, only to see the vortex still churning. That single moment of silence is more devastating than any thunderclap. If you’ve ever felt the unfairness of a system, you’ll feel a kinship with Ji Ning’s final, furious roar at the heavens.

## Character Spotlight: Wufeng Immortal's Burden
Wufeng Immortal is a walking monument to survival and failure. He is the oldest Loose Immortal of the Black-White College, a man who has seen *everyone* fail. His master, his senior brothers, his junior brothers—all of them fell to their own tribulations. He himself fell to the twenty-fifth bolt. In this chapter, he acts as the terrified commentator, his dialogue laced with the trauma of witnessing countless deaths. His line, “He cannot fail, he cannot fail,” is less a cheer and more a desperate prayer repeated over the graves of his past. He represents the brutal reality that most cultivators don't succeed; they just die trying, leaving their friends to watch and hope for the next one.
