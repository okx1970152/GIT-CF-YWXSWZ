## Chapter Overview
The chase is on! After Ji Ning’s devastating ambush, the Seamless Gate is *pissed*. A formation of nearly three hundred Empyrean Gods and True Immortals, spearheaded by a roaring Skeletal Fiendgod, is hot on his tail with a Heaven-Witch Shuttle. It’s a frantic, nail-biting race through the Void, where a single misstep means total encirclement and death. But the Nuwa Alliance isn't a bunch of sitting ducks. Just when the walls are closing in, a massive reinforcement force—led by the fierce female Empyrean God Wozi, sent by none other than the most ancient and supreme Human Sovereign, Suiren—appears to block the pursuit. The chapter ends in a tense standoff, a magnificent display of cosmic-scale backing reminiscent of a gangster boss calling in his heaviest hitters. It’s a huge flex from the human race’s ultimate granddaddy.

## Key Plot Points
- The chapter opens with an awe-inspiring description of the ‘Blazing Sun Major World’, the personal domain of Suiren, the oldest and most powerful human. We get a legendary exposition dump on his history: his discovery of fire, his unification of early humanity, his peerless combat record (even beating the primordial Fire God Zhurong).
- The perspective shifts to Suiren himself, a majestic figure on a mountain of flame. Seeing Ji Ning’s peril, he issues a single, absolute command to one of his adopted daughters, the Empyrean God Wozi, to take 365 Empyrean Gods and True Immortals and save Ji Ning.
- The core tension is the chase. Ji Ning’s Void Boat and the Seamless Gate’s Heavenly Witch Shuttle are perfectly matched in speed. The Skeletal Fiendgod is gnashing his teeth in frustration, having missed Ji Ning by a literal split-second.
- The rescue arrives in the nick of time. Ji Ning’s pursuers pull up short, seeing the massive force of human Immortals blocking their path. A tense, wordless standoff ensues, followed by an ignominious retreat for the Seamless Gate.
- The Godking and Daofather Bixuan watch the failed hunt from afar. The Godking seethes, realizing Ji Ning’s mobility is a massive strategic problem. They debate the possibility of using a Chaos Treasure to hunt him, but the notoriously troublesome Daofather Demonhand is the only owner of a suitable one, making the idea nearly impossible.
- Back on the Blazing Sun army’s side, Ji Ning thanks Wozi, who humbly deflects all praise to Suiren.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Three Sovereigns (三皇) of Humanity:** This chapter introduces one of the most hallowed figures in ancient Chinese mythology: **Suiren (燧人氏)**. In classic myth, Suiren is the ‘Fire-Driller’ who taught humanity how to create fire, bringing civilization out of the dark age. In Desolate Era, he’s elevated to a cosmic level, becoming the *oldest and most powerful* human. He’s the first of the **Three Sovereigns (Suiren, Fuxi, Shennong)**, a trinity of sage-kings who founded human civilization. Fuxi taught hunting, fishing, and the Eight Trigrams, while Shennong (the Divine Farmer) taught agriculture and herbal medicine. In the novel, these aren't just legendary figures; they are top-tier players whose actions shape the fate of the entire Three Realms.

**The ‘Ancestor’ Trope:** In Xianxia, having a powerful ‘ancestor’ or backing is a huge deal. Here, the strongest ‘ancestor’ of the human race himself, Suiren, has personally taken an interest in Ji Ning. This is a massive flex. It demonstrates that Ji Ning’s actions for the Nuwa Alliance have caught the attention of the most legendary beings, and that he’s now part of the top-tier strategic calculus of the war. The 365 Empyrean God escort isn’t just a rescue; it’s an announcement.

## Reading Guide
Get ready to have your mind blown by the sheer scale of this universe, fellow Daoists. This chapter is a masterclass in showing, not telling. Instead of saying ‘Ji Ning is important’, it brings the *oldest human alive* into the story. The description of Suiren is perfect—a quiet, low-key power that makes the entire Seamless Gate tremble. His ‘slice of life’ moment, ordering his adopted daughter around from a throne of flame, is the kind of casual, ultimate power trip we live for.

The chase scene is pure tension, but the real meat is the politicking. The Skeletal Fiendgod’s rage and the Godking’s quiet fury give us a peek into the enemy’s command structure. They are scrambling. And the fact that their absolutely best solution is a ‘maybe we can ask the psychotic demon lady with the cool gloves’ is hilarious and terrifying. It shows that Ji Ning, while still weak individually, has become a queen piece on the board that the enemy is actively trying to checkmate. This is a victory, but it’s a victory that heavily relies on Suiren’s supreme power. The hunt will continue, and the stakes are now cosmic.

## Deep Dive: The Suiren Legend
The name ‘Suirenshi’ (燧人氏) literally translates to ‘the man who drilled wood to make fire’. In Chinese folk history, he is considered the first of the Three August Ones (Sovereigns), the progenitor of Chinese civilization. The story states that before his time, humans did not yet know how to make fire and ate raw food. Suiren, a sage-king, observed a woodpecker drilling into a tree and sparking a flame. He mimicked the action, inventing the drill-stick that has been a symbol of Chinese civilization ever since. The Desolate Era adaptation brilliantly scales this up: his invention isn't just a physical flame, but a metaphorical spark of progress. He fires up the *will* of a weak race, unites them, and leads them to master their world, eventually becoming a figure whose physical strength is so great that even the primeval God of Fire acknowledges his superiority.
