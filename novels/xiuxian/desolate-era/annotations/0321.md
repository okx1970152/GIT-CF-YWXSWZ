## Chapter Overview
Welcome to the opening salvo of the Immortal Fortunes Assembly’s first round! This chapter flips the script from "who has the biggest sword" to "who has the sharpest mind." Ji Ning quickly realizes that with 100,000+ geniuses crammed into the Brightmoon Landscape Painting, raw power alone is a recipe for disaster. When a tempest of over a hundred Wanxiang Adepts collectively decides to hunt him down, he pulls a tactical nope and *bolts*, proving the old Xianxia adage: he who fights and runs away lives to fight another day. But the real fireworks aren’t in the painting—they’re in the viewing hall. The Grand Xia Emperor’s quiet viewing party is crashed by a crew of legendary beings: Lü Dongbin, Great Strength Bodhisattva, Emperor Di Hao, and more. These are Pure Yang True Immortals and major players from across the Three Realms, here to scout future talent. The power ceiling just got a *lot* higher.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning is dropped alone into the Brightmoon Landscape Painting’s survival trial. The rules are simple: gather tokens, stay alive, and be one of the last 96 standing. There are over 100,000 participants.
- A skirmish between eight geniuses draws a hidden crowd of 103 onlookers, showing how quickly information spreads in this closed arena. Everyone is cautious and spying on everyone else.
- Ji Ning is identified by the crowd as a top-100 threat (thanks to his kill of the Loose Immortal). Over a hundred Wanxiang Adepts agree to hunt him down together, forcing Ji Ning to flee via treasure ship.
- The chapter establishes that individual combat power is capped—even the strongest can be overwhelmed by a hundred geniuses working in formation. Survival demands cunning, ruthlessness, and smart positioning, not just brute strength.
- Back in the real world, eight legendary figures unexpectedly arrive at the Grand Xia Emperor’s viewing hall, including the famous Lü Dongbin, Great Strength Bodhisattva, and Emperor Di Hao. The Emperor is gracious but wary—these beings are here to poach disciples.
- The scene establishes the cosmic stakes: the Daofather himself is watching this assembly, and the winners will be courted by the most powerful entities in the Three Realms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Eight Immortals and Lü Dongbin’s Thick Skin**  
Lü Dongbin (吕洞宾) is the leader of the legendary Eight Immortals (八仙), a group of Daoist demigods from Chinese folklore. He’s usually depicted as a scholarly swordsman with a magical sword that slays demons. In *Desolate Era*, he’s bumped up to a Pure Yang True Immortal who studied under two great powers and merged their teachings into his Sword Dao. His personality is spot-on: he’s infamous in myth for being a bit of a trickster and a shameless freeloader. Him barging into the Grand Xia’s assembly “just to watch” is pure Lü Dongbin energy.

**Great Strength Bodhisattva (大势至菩萨)**  
In Buddhism, Mahāsthāmaprāpta (Great Strength Bodhisattva) is one of the attendants of Amitābha Buddha, representing infinite wisdom and power. In this novel, he’s a Pure Yang-level Buddhist cultivator with a famously gentle smile and unfailingly good temper—so nice that even the Daoist-leaning Grand Xia Emperor can’t bring himself to kick him out. His presence signals that even the Buddhist faction is scouting for talent.

**The Pure Yang True Immortal Power Tier**  
The figures arriving at the hall are all *Pure Yang True Immortals* (纯阳真仙), which is the highest combat tier below Daofather. For context, a regular Celestial Immortal is already an immortal being who has transcended the mortal world. A Pure Yang True Immortal has mastered a Grand Dao and is considered a pillar of the Three Realms. Having a room full of them all staring at the same competition is like having a conference of Olympic gold medalists watching a high school track meet.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the **mindset reset** for the Immortal Fortunes Assembly arc. The endless killing and scheming rules we learned in the Cage Battle and War God Hall now apply on a *massive* scale, with 100,000 participants who are all nearly equal in raw power. Pay close attention to how Ji Ning adapts: he *immediately* learns the lesson that fleeing is not cowardice—it’s strategy. The real treat, though, is the VIP section. Seeing Lü Dongbin, Great Strength Bodhisattva, and the others show up is a massive lore drop. It tells you that this is not just a tournament for a dynasty’s pride—it’s a **Three Realms-level scouting event** for the upcoming great chaos. If you’re a lore junkie, memorize the names of these eight visitors. Some of them will become very important later.
