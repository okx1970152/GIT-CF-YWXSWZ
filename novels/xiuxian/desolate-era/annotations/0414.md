## Chapter Overview

Holy *carp*, fellow Daoists. We knew the Bloodcloud Hall was bad news, but they've just escalated to a whole new level of "oh crap, that's a problem." They've dragged Ji Ning's real body—through a torn void—into the territory of a *Celestial Immortal* general working for some shadowy organization. That silver-armored general looks at the chaos, scoffs at Bloodcloud Hall's incompetence, and then casually orders a full sect master and a terrifying "General Han" to finish Ji Ning off. And he's heading to a "Forbidden Zone" that's been carved out of the sect's own mountain? The stakes have just been *cranked*. This isn't a fight anymore; it's a full-scale gang-up in the enemy's backyard.

## Key Plot Points

1.  **The Viper Prison Fails:** The twelve Bloodcloud Hall assassins are stunned. Their ultimate forbidden art, the ‘Viper Prison,’ is powerful enough to kill returned-void Fiendgods and even Celestial Immortals, but Ji Ning’s third-cycle Arcane Art gives him a body comparable to an Immortal-ranked treasure, making him immune to its corrosion.
2.  **Tactical Desperation:** Realizing their main attack won't work, and unwilling to burn their souls again with another forbidden art, the assassins activate their “backup plan.” They tear open the void and drag Ji Ning to a pre-arranged location, sharing the kill credit with a more powerful ally.
3.  **Masters in a Panic:** Back in the minor world, the Whitewater Hound and the Wufeng Immortal can only watch helplessly as the serpent disappears into the Grand Xia World. Ji Ning’s Primaltwin remains calm, vowing to protect the Diancai Immortal at all costs, even at the risk of exposing his most secret treasures.
4.  **A New Threat Arrives:** The serpent drags Ji Ning into the “Eastwood Mountain Range,” the headquarters of the Eastwood Sect. But the sect is not its own master; it's been brainwashed by a silver-armored Celestial Immortal and his forces.
5.  **Power Play and a Forbidden Zone:** The silver-armored general dismisses the Bloodcloud Hall’s failure, orders his own men to join the kill, and then departs for a mysterious “Forbidden Zone” within the sect’s own territory, hinting at a much larger scheme.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The ‘Gear-Check’ and Protective Item Tropes:**
This chapter is a masterclass in how Xianxia often deconstructs defenses. The Bloodcloud Hall prepared an attack that would destroy any opponent *their own level* could face—a classic “one-hit-kill” special move. But Ji Ning’s defense isn’t just more of the same; it’s a *different tier* of existence. The Arcane Art making his body “comparable to an Immortal-ranked treasure” means he’s functionally ignoring an entire category of attack. The assassins, being of lower cultivation, can only rationalize this as a “protective treasure” from a master, a very common trope. They can’t comprehend the possibility of a mortal achieving such a qualitative physical transformation through a divine ability.

**The Forbidden Zone and Secret Bases:**
The “Eastwood Sect” being secretly taken over by a higher power is a classic Xianxia scenario. The mention of a "Samsara Talisman" (轮回符) being used to brainwash the sect leader is a direct callback to one of the most terrifying control methods in the setting. A Samsara Talisman, a legendary Pure Yang treasure, can forcibly reincarnate a target's soul, imprinting loyalty into their very being in their next life, while leaving their cultivation intact. Having such an item used on a mere sect leader shows the incredible wealth and ruthlessness of the organization behind the silver-armored general. The “Forbidden Zone” within the sect’s own mountain range is also a classic trope—the invaders are building *something* big, and they need a secret, secure space to do it.

### Third-cycle Arcane Art (玄功第三转)

A state of the Eight-Nine Arcane Art divine ability. At the third cycle, the cultivator's physical body becomes comparable to an Immortal-ranked magic treasure in toughness.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is all about escalation. The fight has moved from a desperate ambush in a pocket world to a full-blown raid on a corrupted sect’s headquarters. Pay close attention to how Ji Ning’s Primaltwin handles the situation versus his true body. The Primaltwin is a strategist, prioritizing his master’s safety above all else. The true body is the unstoppable engine of destruction, trapped but unyielding. The silver-armored general is the real new variable here. He’s a Celestial Immortal, which puts him in an entirely different league from the assassination squad. And he’s clearly not the top of the food chain—he has a “master” who resides in that Forbidden Zone. This isn't just a random ambush; it's a coordinated plan to drag Ji Ning into the heart of a powerful enemy's territory. The question now isn't *if* Ji Ning can break free, but *who* and *what* will be waiting for him when he does.
