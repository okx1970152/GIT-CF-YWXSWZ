## Chapter Overview

The battle for the Grand Xia world has reached a terrifying stalemate. The Seamless Gate, having been thoroughly outclassed by the Seven Radiance Empyrean God, is not retreating—they’re reloading. Daofather Ink Bamboo unveils a devastating new war asset: a Daofather Puppet, a spider-like mechanical god that hits at the Daofather level and is virtually indestructible. Meanwhile, the Crimsonbright side is thrown into despair as Daofather Crimsonbright forbids engagement, knowing his troops would be butchered. But just as all seems lost, a mysterious youth named Yun Chen descends from a spatial rift, bearing aid from no less a figure than Human Sovereign Shennong. Get ready, fellow Daoists—the power scales are about to be violently recalibrated.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Survival Vote**: The Seamless Gate’s remaining sixty-one Empyrean Gods and True Immortals learn that—despite their massive losses—Daofather Ink Bamboo refuses to surrender. They’ve chosen to escalate rather than retreat.
- **The Daofather Puppet ‘Armor-Shredder’**: Ink Bamboo produces a spider-like Daofather Puppet made from war spoils of the Crimsonbright Realmverse. It boasts Daofather-level strength, extreme toughness, and eight sword-like legs. It is, bluntly, a game-changer.
- **Internal Tournament**: A sparring match is held among the Seamless Gate’s top fighters to choose the puppet’s pilot. After a tight contest, Evergreen Sword Immortal beats the Nine-Headed Lion by a hair, due to his superior sword-control compatibility with the puppet’s eight limbs.
- **Diplomatic Coldness**: Evergreen Sword Immortal humbly praises his opponent, but the Nine-Headed Lion’s sour reaction plants a seed of resentment. Evergreen’s internal thoughts are colder than his words—this is a man who will remember a slight.
- **The Crimsonbright Side’s Panic**: The Seamless Gate issues a full-scale challenge. Daofather Crimsonbright—to everyone’s shock—forbids any engagement. He coldly explains that a single Daofather Puppet is stronger than all their combined forces (the Seven Radiance Empyrean God plus Ji Ning’s Karmic Punishment Deity).
- **The Savior Arrives**: A handsome, red-birthmarked youth named Yun Chen steps through a spatial rift, delivering a message from Human Sovereign Shennong. He states with calm confidence that he has been sent to turn the tide. Hope, it seems, has a name.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Daofather Puppets (道祖傀儡)**: This is the big brother of the Empyrean God Puppet introduced earlier. In Xianxia, *puppets* are not mere toys—they are supreme military assets, often forged from immortal materials and imbued with divine power. A Daofather Puppet is so physically robust that even a genuine Daofather (the top-tier god of this realm) cannot easily shatter it. Its power is defined by brute force and invulnerability, not sophisticated techniques—a classic Xianxia “unblockable wall” problem. The Seamless Gate using a puppet here, rather than a living Daofather, implies either a shortage of high-end manpower or a strategic desire for a disposable, fearless weapon.

**The Psychological Weight of a ‘Pilot’ Selection**: The Seamless Gate’s decision to run an internal tournament for who gets to pilot the Daofather Puppet is deliciously Machiavellian. Evergreen Sword Immortal’s humble victory speech—and the Nine-Headed Lion’s sullen loss—are classic Xianxia social dynamics. The loser loses face, the winner gains a powerful tool but also a dangerous rival. This isn’t just a power-up; it’s a plot hook for internal conflict.

**Human Sovereign Shennong (人皇神农氏)**: In Chinese myth, Shennong is the legendary Emperor of the Five Grains and a master of herbal medicine—the divine farmer. In *Desolate Era*, he occupies a different role: a major power of the Three Realms. His sending aid directly to Daofather Crimsonbright signals that this local Realm War has caught the attention of the highest cosmic authorities.

## Reading Guide

- **Watch the Power Creep**: If a Daofather Puppet is this dominant now, the only way to survive is to either acquire an even stronger weapon or to circumvent the puppet’s weakness (poor control, reliance on a single pilot). Yun Chen’s arrival teases the former, but don't rule out a clever trick.
- **Character Watch—Evergreen Sword Immortal**: Pay attention to his inner thoughts. He publicly praises the Nine-Headed Lion, but privately vows revenge for the slight. This is a textbook ‘smiling villain’ setup—a character whose courtesy is a weapon and whose patience is a form of cruelty.
- **The Red Birthmark Clue**: The description of the newcomer, Yun Chen, as a “handsome youth with a red birthmark between his eyebrows” is an iconic description for the character Wuxiao Celestial Immortal, a disciple of the Lord of All Demons. His arrival, supposedly on Shennong’s behalf, is either a genuine alliance shift or a devastating trap. Read closely.
