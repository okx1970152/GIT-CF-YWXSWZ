## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia *deal-making*. Ji Ning, fresh off his looting spree and reeling from the Shaoyan Clan threat, executes a lightning-fast pivot from escape artist to strategic alliance-builder. He doesn’t negotiate—he presents a proposition so absurdly favorable that the trapped demons of the Immortal Estate World can’t say no. In exchange for a thousand years of guarding his Ji Clan in Ten Thousand Swords City, he offers them the entire Immortal Estate Key and their freedom. What follows is the thrilling, bureaucratic climax of a side quest: the mass swearing of Heavenly Oaths and the first, triumphant step of a demon army into the wider Grand Xia world. It’s a perfect payoff—the sword wasn’t the only treasure Ji Ning found in that estate.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning reappears before the gathered demon army, openly holding the Immortal Estate Key. He immediately opens a passageway to the outside world, demonstrating his absolute control and eliminating any chance of a surprise attack.
- He lays out a brutal, honest summary of his situation: the Shaoyan Clan is coming for his tribe. He doesn’t sugarcoat the danger.
- Ji Ning proposes a stunning trade: 1,000 years of protection for the Ji Clan in Ten Thousand Swords City, in exchange for the Immortal Estate Key and complete freedom. He explains *why* the deal is fair—Celestial Immortals are too valuable to waste on revenge, making attacks from top-tier foes unlikely.
- The demon leaders, Immortal Wu Sui and Immortal Duo He, quickly realize this is the best offer they will ever get. They accept.
- Both Ji Ning and the entire demon army (30,000+ cultivators) swear a Heavenly Oath to bind the contract.
- The chapter climaxes with the evacuation. Duo He teleports the senior leaders directly to the outskirts of Ten Thousand Swords City, and Ji Ning points the way home.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Heavenly Oaths (天道誓言)** are the Xianxia equivalent of a nuclear-proof contract. Unlike a mortal’s promise that can be broken, a cultivator’s oath is *overheard* by the Heavenly Dao itself. If a Xian cultivator (Zifu and above) breaks their oath, the next Heavenly Tribulation they face will have an automatic, catastrophic amplification. For Loose Immortals and Celestial Immortals, this is an effective death sentence. This is why Ji Ning trusts the deal—the demons’ self-preservation instinct is now his strongest ally. The system works because the universe in Xianxia is *alive* and *judgmental*.

Also noteworthy is Ji Ning's understanding of the **Great Clan’s calculus of power**. He explicitly dismisses the idea of a Celestial Immortal attacking Ten Thousand Swords City because a Celestial Immortal is a clan’s ultimate strategic asset. Using one on a suicide mission for *revenge* against a minor tribe is wasteful. This cold, resource-based logic is a core pillar of Xianxia political science. Personal grudges matter, but institutional survival matters more.

## Reading Guide
Get ready to celebrate, because this is a rare, unqualified victory! For the first time in a while, Ji Ning has properly outmaneuvered his enemies. He didn't just get a magical technique—he got a private army. Pay attention to Ji Ning's speech here; he has fully grown into the role of a leader and diplomat. He doesn't posture or threaten. He presents the problem, offers the solution, and lets the other party’s logic and self-interest do the rest. This is a huge step up from the reactive, vengeance-driven kid we met. Also, the sight of tens of thousands of elite demon soldiers swarming out of that canyon is a fantastic image to hold onto—it’s the Ji Clan’s new security detail coming to town. The dynasty is about to have some very unexpected new residents.
