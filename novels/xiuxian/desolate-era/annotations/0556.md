## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists, because this chapter delivers the kind of tactical crescendo that makes Realm War arcs so addictive. The tide has turned, and it’s all thanks to the sudden, glorious intervention of the Seven Radiance Empyrean God—a seven-in-one fusion of Empyrean Gods under the legendary Chaotic True God Threelives. The previously dominant Three-Eyed Fiendgod (the merged form of the Devouring Mountain’s three demon gods) finds himself hopelessly outclassed. The Red Snow Empyrean God’s spearplay is a masterclass in applying fused Time and Space Daos, completely suppressing raw brute force with sublime elegance. We see the tactical domino effect of this shift, the frantic scrambling of Daofather Ink Bamboo, and the absolute desperation of the Nine-Headed Lion as his pride crashes against an immovable wall. It’s a glorious payoff for a formation we’ve been hearing about, and it changes the entire complexion of the war.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Shift in Dominance**: The chapter opens with the Seven Radiance Empyrean God effortlessly matching and then overwhelming the Three-Eyed Fiendgod. The Red Snow Empyrean God’s fighting style is highlighted as a fusion of the Grand Dao of Snow (Water) and Time, making his spear-play both fluid and unpredictable.
- **The Nine-Headed Lion’s Desperation**: Despite his immense pride and ambition, the Nine-Headed Lion (the core of the Three-Eyed Fiendgod) is forced to admit defeat. His combat techniques are completely countered by Red Snow, forcing him to beg Daofather Ink Bamboo for reinforcements.
- **The Counter-Offensive**: Freed from the threat of the Three-Eyed Fiendgod, Ji Ning, the Xia Emperor, and the other Brightflame combat units turn their fury on the rest of the Seamless Gate forces, taking the advantage.
- **The Evergreen Sword Immortal’s Pragmatism**: Seeing the situation sour, the Evergreen Sword Immortal immediately retreats to a safe distance, refusing to engage in a losing battle.
- **Dongzhe’s Exhaustion**: The chapter also notes the archer Dongzhe has fully expended his eight Chaos Daofruits and his Heartforce, a fatal blow to the Brightflame side’s long-range superiority.
- **Ink Bamboo’s Counter-Gambit**: Daofather Ink Bamboo, unwilling to concede, throws in eighteen more Empyrean Gods and True Immortals to support the Devouring Mountain trio, hoping to overwhelm the Seven Radiance Empyrean God through sheer numbers.
- **Total Domination by Red Snow**: The seven-in-one formation completely crushes this combined force, with the Red Snow Empyrean God even shattering their formation’s golden pagoda with a single spear thrust. The Brightflame side erupts in cheers.
- **The Cosmic Observer**: The chapter ends with a cut to Patriarch Subhuti and his disciple Ji Dian on Mount Innerheart, who recognize the Red Snow Empyrean God’s achievement as the true fusion of Time and Space into Spacetime—a rare and powerful breakthrough.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a feast of Xianxia war tropes, and the key to understanding it lies in the formations. The **Seven Radiance Empyrean God** (七曜天神) and the **Three-Eyed Fiendgod** (三眼魔神) aren't single entities, but combined battle avatars formed by multiple Empyrean Gods or Fiendgods merging their power. This is a step up from simpler formations like the Karmic Punishment Deities (刑天神) we've seen before. The key difference here is *quality of fusion*—the seven Empyrean Gods under Threelives are perfectly coordinated, making their avatar far more coherent and powerful. The **White-Faced Flood Dragon** (白脸蛟) mentioned earlier is another of these massive war formations, requiring a thousand Celestial Immortals and a hundred thousand Loose Immortals to manifest as a colossal dragon. These are not mere spells; they are the ultimate expression of military discipline in the Three Realms, turning an army into a single, devastating combat unit. The cost? Resources like **Chaos Daofruits** (混沌道果)—fragments of a Daofather's own Dao comprehension—that Dongzhe burns to fuel his supreme arrows. This is the high-stakes economy of a Realm War: priceless treasures and super-formations are the only things keeping the galaxy from falling.

## Reading Guide
Pay close attention to the Red Snow Empyrean God’s fighting style. The author specifically contrasts it with the typical brute force of a Fiendgod. Red Snow uses elegance, fluidity, and the fusion of Time and Water Daos to perfectly counter the Three-Eyed Fiendgod’s raw power. It’s a beautiful example of how, in Xianxia, a superior Dao-heart and comprehension can defeat a physically superior enemy. Also, note Daofather Ink Bamboo’s exhaustion of his tactical options. After Dongzhe’s arrows run out, he thinks he has won. The Seven Radiance Empyrean God’s arrival is a true “X-factor”—an unpredictable element he failed to account for. The final scene on Mount Innerheart sets up a deeper question: what does it mean for a cultivator to truly grasp the power of Spacetime? This is not just a battle victory; it’s a hint at the highest levels of the Dao.

## Spacetime: The Ultimate Fusion Dao
The glowing recognition from Patriarch Subhuti at the chapter’s end is a major lore drop. The Grand Daos of Time and Space are considered two of the most profound and difficult Heavenly Daos. Fusing them into a single, unified realm of **Spacetime** (时空) is a legendary achievement, one that allows a cultivator to twist reality itself. This is what makes the Red Snow Empyrean God’s spearplay so terrifying—he’s not just moving fast or striking hard; he’s bending the very stage upon which the battle is being fought. For a mortal, time is a river you swim in. For a Spacetime cultivator, time and space are a ball of clay to be shaped. This marks Red Snow as a being of extraordinary potential, and Subhuti’s interest signals that this power is far more significant than a simple battlefield advantage.
