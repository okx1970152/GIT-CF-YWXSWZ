## Chapter Overview

Ah, you thought it was an ambush of one? Surprise! It’s a twelve-on-one party! Talk about a plot twist. Our boy Ji Ning, fresh off his Ki Refining upgrade and sitting pretty on a cliff, was expecting to solo-lure the Firewing Demon King into a nice, neat ambush. Instead, he gets the entire Eastflow Twelve Demon Kings showing up for a barbecue. This chapter is a masterclass in “be careful what you fish for” – Ji Ning baited a Demon King and hooked the entire school. The chapter sets a deliciously tense stage: the calm before a very, very bloody storm. Get ready, because the odds just shifted from “tough but winnable” to “time to rethink your entire life plan.”

## Key Plot Points

- **The King’s Gambit:** The Firewing Demon King calls a conclave of the Eastflow Twelve. He reveals he destroyed the Kingdom of Qi to obtain a **secret treasure map**—a map to a place so dangerous, even he was trapped for over twenty years and almost died. He needs all twelve brothers to crack it.
- **The Feast of Predators:** The scene in the Qi palace masterfully establishes the Twelve as a pack of ancient, cruel, and casually murderous apex predators. One minor mistake by a servant means getting eaten alive. Their brotherhood is based on mutual benefit and shared ferocity, not sentiment.
- **The Rip Current:** As the Kings plan the treasure hunt, news arrives: all three teams of Firewing Guards sent to hunt down the Qi remnants have been **completely wiped out** (over 300 elite soldiers + 3 Loose Immortals). The Kings are intrigued, not scared.
- **The Fishing Trip Goes Wrong:** The Twelve, in a show of brotherly support and predatory curiosity, decide to personally go deal with the problem. They fly their army straight to the Qi remnants’ hiding spot, encountering Ji Ning sitting alone on a mountain peak.
- **The Final Reel:** Ji Ning, expecting to psych out one King, finds himself facing all twelve. The chapter ends with his internal curse: *Why did all of them have to show up?* The hook is set, and Ji Ning is the one who just got baited.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Beast Assembly (群妖会)：** This chapter is a classic example of the “Demon King Banquet” trope in Xianxia. It’s not just a feast; it’s a power display. The casual cruelty (eating a maidservant for a spill) isn't just shock value—it establishes a core rule of the demon world: **power is the only law.** For these twelve, centuries of life have made them utterly callous. Their unity is a fragile pact of convenience, but also a terrifyingly efficient killing machine. The way they instantly rally around a common threat (“disrespecting Third Brother is disrespecting us”) shows a primitive, honor-bound code that makes them far more dangerous than a lone villain.

**The Star Continent & Its Powers:**
The chapter name-drops “Star Continent” (星洲). We are officially beyond the scope of the Grand Xia Dynasty now. Here, Celestial Immortals are the top dogs. The fact that the Twelve Demon Kings claim to be unafraid of a Celestial Immortal is a massive power statement. They are essentially a sovereign nation of apex predators. This completely recalibrates the threat level Ji Ning is facing. He just tried to assassinate the president, and the entire Joint Chiefs of Staff showed up for the meeting.

**The Treasures of a Fallen Kingdom:**
The treasure map is a classic Xianxia MacGuffin. The Firewing King didn’t just casually destroy a kingdom; he did it for a very specific, high-reward reason. The Kingdom of Qi’s founding emperor only scratched the *outer edge* of this treasure ground and got supreme techniques from it. This establishes the treasure as being on a completely different tier—something that could potentially elevate the Kings to the True Immortal / True God level. It’s their ticket to the big leagues.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is the **calm before the storm / the storm’s opening act.** We’re doing a hard pivot from “Ji Ning dominates” to “Ji Ning is in deep, deep trouble.”

- **Don’t Panic:** Yes, the odds are terrifying. But remember who our protagonist is. Ji Ning is a master of tactics (Dao-heart and sword). He didn’t succeed by being reckless. This is a test of his most powerful asset: his ability to adapt and think on his feet.
- **Power Scaling Re-Calibration:** This chapter serves as a check on Ji Ning’s ego after his recent power-ups. He just advanced to the early Void stage, but the world is showing him that a single level-up doesn’t make him invincible. The gap between a single Primal Daoist and a coalition of twelve apex demon kings is still a chasm.
- **Question the Brotherhood:** Watch for cracks in the Eastflow Twelve’s unity. They are a coalition of convenience. The treasure map is the real prize. If Ji Ning can survive the initial clash, he might find a way to exploit these cracks. Divide and conquer is a classic strategy for a reason.
- **The Real Adventure Begins:** The treasure map subplot is the key to the next major arc. Ji Ning is about to be thrust into an adventure far bigger than his personal revenge against one Demon King. This is no longer a simple assassination mission; it’s a survival scenario on a battlefield filled with legendary monsters.
