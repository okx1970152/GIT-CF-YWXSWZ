## Chapter Overview

Welcome, fellow Daoists, to the grand opening of the Immortal Fortunes Assembly! After all the buildup, the pressure-cooker tension, and Ji Ning’s frantic preparation, the stage is finally set. This chapter is the epic prelude: the gathering of over a hundred thousand of the Grand Xia Dynasty’s most monstrous talents. Our protagonist, flanked by Yu Wei and the clueless-but-lovable slovenly True Man, navigates this overwhelming spectacle. We get our first taste of imperial might—from earth-shaking divine demons to a plaza-spanning formation powerful enough to make an artifact spirit like the Yellow-Maned Bear sit up and take notice. But beneath the pageantry, there’s a heavy undercurrent. This is not just a tournament; it’s a life-or-death hunger game with no exit. And in the crowd, a familiar pair of eyes meets Ji Ning’s for a fleeting, loaded moment, hinting at tangled fates yet to unfold.

## Key Plot Points

*   **A Sea of Geniuses**: The chapter opens with the chaos of over a hundred thousand cultivators surging into the imperial capital. Ji Ning explains the three-tier entry system before the city gates open to the sound of the Nine Caldera Bells.
*   **The World-Protecting Formation**: Once inside, the scale of the capital’s central plaza is revealed. The Yellow-Maned Bear drops a lore bomb: the plaza’s runic array is a world-protecting formation powered by thousands of Immortal and Pure Yang treasures, strong enough to fend off even top-tier powers of the Three Realms. It’s a serious flex of the Grand Xia Emperor’s backing.
*   **The Anchan Commandery Procession**: The orderly formations of the three thousand six hundred commanderies arrive. Ji Ning spots the Marquis of Anchan, his friend Northmont Baiwei, and his fellow Black-White College disciples.
*   **A Glimpse of the Power Structure**: King Yan personally brings Yuchi Xiyue to the Celestial Immortals’ upper hall. Seeing this reinforces the Xianxia social ladder—Duchess Xiyue’s favor is a colossal political asset for the Ji Clan.
*   **An Unexpected Reunion**: As Celestial Immortals ascend to the main hall, Nine Lotos flies by. Her eyes lock with Ji Ning’s. The chapter closes on that charged moment, leaving readers to wonder what this tangled thread means for the brutal battles ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Immortal Fortunes Assembly**: This is the Grand Xia Dynasty’s Super Bowl. It’s a once-in-a-blue-moon tournament where young geniuses from every corner of the empire throw down. Winning isn’t just about glory—it’s about catching the eye of a Celestial Immortal master or even the Emperor himself. In the ruthless world of Xianxia, these assemblies are the ultimate social ladder and the ultimate massacre rolled into one.
**World-Protecting Formations**: The Grand Xia Emperor isn’t just a bully in a palace; his throne is rooted in a massive, ancient formation known as a ‘world-protecting formation.’ These are not mere traps; they are the metaphysical equivalent of a M.A.D. (Mutually Assured Destruction) doctrine. Backed by Pure Yang treasures and powered by a top-tier Celestial Immortal, they act as both a shield and an absolute deterrent against any wandering True God or Daofather looking to cause trouble. This explains why the dynasty has stood for so long.
**The Nine Caldera Bells**: In traditional Chinese palace hierarchy, bells and drums are used to mark time and announce major proceedings. These nine bells aren’t just for show; they are ancient Immortal-ranked treasures that resonate with the heavenly laws. The sound isn’t just loud; it’s an announcement to the very foundations of the world that a momentous event is underway.

### Cousin-brother / Cousin-sister (表弟/表姐)

In Chinese family hierarchy, a 'cousin-brother' or 'cousin-sister' denotes a maternal relationship, distinct from a paternal cousin. This chapter showcases this specific bond between Ji Ning and Yuchi Xiyue.

## Reading Guide

*   **Watch for the Background Power Play**: The entire sequence is a masterclass in the Xianxia world-building law of ‘delegated awe.’ We don’t see the Emperor’s power directly, but through the scale of the city, the size of the plaza, and the terrifying formation protecting it. This is how you make a ruler feel godlike without them throwing a single punch.
*   **Don’t Miss the Emotional Stakes**: Amidst the spectacle, pay attention to Yuchi Xiyue’s position with King Yan. She’s not just a cute face in the crowd; her royal favor is a huge deal for Ji Ning’s far-away clan. Also, keep that glance between Ji Ning and Nine Lotos in your head—it’s a dangling thread that the author is deliberately setting up.
*   **Prepare for the Onslaught**: The slovenly True Man acts as the audience surrogate here, our “just woke up” guide to the chaos. But his surprise at the sheer number of competitors is meant to hit *you*. The bracket is going to be a slaughterhouse. If there are 100,000 geniuses, the early rounds will be a brutal culling where even a marginal edge can mean life or death. Buckle up.
