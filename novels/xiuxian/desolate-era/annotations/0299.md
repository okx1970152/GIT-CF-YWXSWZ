## Chapter Overview
The Treasure Competition Assembly reaches its climax—and Ji Ning walks away with the Peacock Plume after a tense bidding war against Shaoyan Xiushui. But winning the auction is only the beginning. As Ji Ning proudly secures his prize and then splurges on a mountain of Heaven-ranked flying swords for his Primaltwin, enemies are already sharpening their knives outside the Heavenly Treasures Mountain. Floating Cloud Immortal waits in the shadows, ready to strike the moment Ji Ning steps out. Meanwhile, a mysterious figure—Xiyue Commandery Princess—moves behind the scenes, desperate for a private meeting. The powder keg is lit, and the explosion is coming.

## Key Plot Points
1. **The Peacock Plume is won**: After a heated back-and-forth, Ji Ning clinches the Peacock Plume for five million jin of Primordial Liquid, frustrating Shaoyan Xiushui, who wanted it for his tribulation.
2. **Shaoyan Xiushui’s calculation**: He explains to his son why going above five million would be foolish—practical Xianxia economics at play, prioritizing long-term wealth over tribal pride.
3. **Floating Cloud Immortal’s ambush**: He stakes out the Heavenly Treasures Mountain entrance, determined to kill Ji Ning for the Peacock Plume—a classic “treasure draws death” setup.
4. **Ji Ning’s shopping spree**: He sells his Black Dragon carriage and buys 396 top-grade Heaven-ranked flying swords at a discount, preparing his Primaltwin’s Lesser Thousand Sword Formation.
5. **Xiyue Commandery Princess’s summons**: She requests a private meeting with Ji Ning through Yu Qi, hinting at a deeper personal connection that will soon be revealed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Treasure Competition Assembly (争宝会)**: Unlike a standard auction, this monthly event at Heavenly Treasures Mountain’s headquarters is a high-stakes, invitation-only affair. Bidders compete openly, often mixing financial muscle with political calculation. The “six‑fold discount” policy for standard-issue treasures reveals the Grand Xia royal clan’s behind-the-scenes industrial operation—they mass‑produce magic weapons at a fraction of the listed price, making the discount a marketing lure rather than a loss.

**Peacock Plume (孔雀羽)**: A rare material that can significantly increase a cultivator’s chances of surviving heavenly tribulation. Such treasure is “consumable”—once used, it is gone—which is why Shaoyan Xiushui values it so highly but balks at the price. In Xianxia logic, tribulation‑survival gear is the most precious asset for any cultivator below Celestial Immortal.

**Golden Crow (金乌)**: In Chinese mythology, the Golden Crow is the solar bird that dwells in the Sunstar, symbolizing the sun itself. Mentioning “the Golden Crow had just risen” sets the time as dawn, a classic literary device that also reinforces the novel’s cosmic symbolism.

## Reading Guide
**The calm before the storm**: This chapter is a classic setup—the hero wins a treasure, the enemies line up, and a mysterious ally appears. Ji Ning’s triumphant mood contrasts sharply with the lurking danger, building tension beautifully. Pay attention to Floating Cloud Immortal’s quiet resolve: he is not a hot-blooded young master but a seasoned Loose Immortal making a calculated, desperate gamble.

**The “Primaltwin + flying swords” strategy**: Ji Ning’s massive sword purchase underlines his unique dual‑cultivation approach. His true body focuses on divine abilities while his Primaltwin becomes a sword‑formation powerhouse. This is the engine that will drive many of his iconic victories ahead—so mark this moment as the ignition of that engine.

**Who is Xiyue Commandery Princess?**: Her nervousness before meeting Ji Ning hints at a deep personal connection. If you’ve been reading closely, you might guess her identity. For new readers, this is a tantalizing hook that will reshape the political web of Grand Xia. Get ready, fellow Daoists—the stage is set for a reunion that will shake the Imperial Capital!
