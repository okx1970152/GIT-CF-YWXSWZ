## Chapter Overview

This foundational chapter establishes the entire cultivation system of Desolate Era through a direct father-son lesson. Ji Yichuan delivers a cold, matter-of-fact explanation of the seven major stages from Houtian to Celestial Immortal, emphasizing the immense difficulty of each breakthrough and the perpetual threat of the Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations that haunt every cultivator below Celestial Immortal. Rather than being intimidated, Ji Ning calmly accepts these harsh truths and immediately requests to study Fiendgod Body Refining—showing his pragmatic, forward-thinking nature. The chapter ends with the retrieval of nineteen abridged manuals, setting up the crucial choice that will shape Ji Ning's early power.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Yichuan explains the seven cultivation stages: Houtian (100 yrs), Xiantian (200 yrs), Zifu Disciple (500 yrs), Wanxiang Adept (800 yrs), Primal Daoist, Void stage (Earth Immortal / Loose Immortal), and Celestial Immortal.
- He emphasizes that mortals must become Xiantian lifeforms to even begin true cultivation—a barrier that ninety-nine out of ten thousand fail to cross.
- The Zifu stage is introduced as the true beginning of the immortal path, requiring a thousand Xiantian experts to produce a single Zifu cultivator.
- Ji Yichuan reveals the Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations: a disaster every 300 years, a calamity every 900 years, forever until one becomes a Celestial Immortal.
- Despite the warnings, Ji Ning shows no fear, citing that the distant future does not concern him now. Ji Yichuan's cold approval is hinted.
- Ji Ning surprises his parents by asking for Fiendgod Body Refining instead of Ki Refining, arguing it is safer for his young age and can be practiced alongside Ki Refining later.
- Uncle Black, a Xiantian great serpent in human form, retrieves all nineteen abridged manuals from the clan vault. Ji Ning begins to review them.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **The Seven Stages**: This chapter presents the classic xianxia ladder. After Celestial Immortal, there are higher realms (True Immortal, Ancestral Immortal, etc.), but they are not yet relevant. The progression follows a Daoist cosmology where each stage represents a fundamental change in life vitality and connection to the Dao.
- **Ki Refining vs. Fiendgod Body Refining**: Ki Refining (炼气) focuses on cultivating internal energy (qi) and controlling external artifacts, formations, and spells. Fiendgod Body Refining (神魔炼体) mimics the primordial Fiendgods, granting superhuman physical resilience, regeneration, and brute strength. In xianxia tradition, Body Refining is vastly harder but yields crushing superiority at the same tier.
- **Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations (三灾九劫)**: A standard tribulation system in Chinese mythology and cultivation fiction, rooted in the idea that defying Heaven incurs periodic punishment. Every 300 years a ‘disaster’ (灾) and every 900 years a ‘calamity’ (劫). The phrase 'disasters can be dodged, calamities are inescapable' underscores the fatalistic pressure on cultivators.
- **Transcending the Five Elements (不在五行中)**: Becoming a Celestial Immortal frees one from the constraints of the basic material elements (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth) that govern mortal life, implying near-immortality and freedom from reincarnation within the Three Realms.
- **Uncle Black as Xiantian Demon**: In xianxia lore, beasts that achieve Xiantian are called 'great demons' and gain the power to take human form. This reflects the Daoist principle that all living beings can cultivate to higher forms through sufficient accumulation.

## Reading Guide

Ji Ning's reaction to his father's terrifying revelations is a masterclass in pragmatic xianxia protagonist mentality. He does not flinch—instead, he immediately calculates and chooses the path that best suits his current limitations (young age, no Ki Refining guidance) while maximizing future potential (Fiendgod's survival edge). His request to practice both paths later is a hallmark of the 'progression fantasy' genre: the protagonist refuses to lock himself into a single option. Notice Ji Yichuan's subtle shift from cold lesson-giver to supportive father when Ji Ning displays reasoning well beyond his years. The retrieval of the nineteen manuals foreshadows a crucial decision—readers should pay attention to the specific technique Ji Ning will select, as it will define his early combat identity.

## Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations {#three-calamities}

In xianxia cosmology, tribulations are the universe's mechanism for enforcing karmic balance against those who defy natural lifespan limits. The Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations are unique because they are *periodic* and *inescapable*—unlike a one-time tribulation at a breakthrough. This system creates a constant sword of Damocles over cultivators, forcing them to either advance to Celestial Immortal (which removes the threat) or perish eventually. The phrase 'disasters can be dodged, calamities are inescapable' reflects folk wisdom: minor disasters (三灾) might be evaded through preparation, but major calamities (九劫) inevitably strike. This chapter marks the first time Ji Ning hears the full scale of the immortal path's dangers, testing his Dao Heart before he has even chosen a technique.
