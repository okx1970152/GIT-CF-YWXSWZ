## Chapter Overview

Get ready for a tension-filled chapter where the calm before the storm is shattered by cold, calculating ambushes. Our focus shifts from the internal trials of the Diancai Immortal’s tribulation to the external machinations of his enemies. The mysterious force supplying the Blood God Church with top-tier intelligence makes another move, proving its ability to know the unknowable. The Bloodshadow God, armed with a new treasure, gathers his powerhouses for a strike. But he’s not the only predator in these waters. An elite squad from Bloodcry Hall, the legendary assassin organization, lurks in the shadows with their own agenda. It’s a deadly convergence on a remote volcanic island, and the only question is who will strike first—and who will survive the fallout.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Informant Network**: The chapter opens with a white-robed envoy of a mysterious force delivering intelligence to the Bloodshadow God of the Blood God Church. The information is incredibly precise, detailing the Diancai Immortal’s secluded tribulation site in a minor world, a location so secret even the protectors don’t know it. This demonstrates the terrifying reach of this shadowy organization, which operates with impunity even against the Grand Xia Dynasty.
- **The Predator’s Greed**: The Bloodshadow God immediately recognizes the opportunity. The Diancai Immortal is a core expert of the Black-White College, the most loyal ally of the Anchan Beishan clan. Eliminating him is a strategic blow. He reveals a new treasure—a small black boat—that gives him the confidence to move against Ji Ning and the Wufeng Immortal alongside his seven Law-Kings.
- **The Real Hunters**: Unbeknownst to the Bloodshadow God, he is not the mastermind. A squad of twelve assassins from Bloodcry Hall emerges from stealth. Their target is Ji Ning. They intend to use the Blood God Church’s attack as a distraction, a method they call “using their power to make the assassination more certain.” Their only fear is the unknown might of Ji Ning’s master.
- **The Calm Vigil**: Inside the small world, Ji Ning and the Wufeng Immortal stand guard over a sealing formation protecting the tribulating Diancai Immortal. Ji Ning’s instincts flare with an acute sense of danger, far stronger than it should be for a cultivator of his defenses. He activates the Candle Dragon’s Gaze to pierce the stealth of any approaching enemies, sensing that an overwhelming threat is bearing down on them.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Tribulation Secrecy**: In Xianxia, a cultivator’s life depends on the complete secrecy of their tribulation site. Any interference—even accidental—can trigger a Divine Punishment, massively escalating the tribulation’s power. The fact that this mysterious force has such perfect intelligence on three separate tribulations proves they have either an impossibly high-level spy network or a divination method that transcends the current chaos of the Heavenly Secrets. This is a core geopolitical threat, not just a personal one.
- **Heavenly Secrets in Chaos**: The Bloodshadow God notes that the “heavenly secrets are chaotic.” This is a staple Xianxia trope signaling the approach of a great calamity. Under normal conditions, supreme experts can use the Karma threads and cosmic laws to deduce future events or locations with a snap of their fingers. The chaos is a form of divine interference, preventing even the most powerful from easily seeing what’s to come. It’s a sign that the Three Realms are entering a period of immense upheaval.
- **The Candle Dragon’s Gaze**: Ji Ning activates his divine ability, the “Candle Dragon’s Gaze.” In Chinese mythology, the Zhulong (Candle Dragon) is a primordial deity whose closed eyes create night and open eyes create day. It represents absolute, unyielding vision. As a divine ability, the Candle Dragon’s Gaze allows Ji Ning to see through illusions, stealth, and spatial distortions, making him a terrifyingly hard target to ambush.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in setting up a multi-layered ambush scenario. The tension isn’t in a single fight, but in the *layering* of threats. We have the open threat (Blood God Church), the hidden threat (Bloodcry Hall), and the counter-countermeasure (Ji Ning’s instinct). The pacing is brisk, shifting from a cold political transaction in a cult’s mountain lair to a predator's council, and finally to the quiet, foreboding vigil on a desolate island. The true genius of the chapter is that it makes us feel the *scale* of the conspiracy without showing a single blow. The key takeaway is the power of information: the mysterious force’s intelligence network is so terrifying that it makes the Bloodshadow God, a major villain, feel like a mere pawn. All eyes are now on that sealing formation, and the reader knows a perfect storm is about to break.

## Geography of the Pendulum Falls

For those keeping track of the Desolate Era world map, the "Pendulum Falls" marks a significant addition. Unlike the common "waterfall falling from a peak" trope, this is a **spatial anomaly**: a river that literally falls from a hole in the void above, strikes a lower cliff, and vanishes into another lacuna below. This is a classic sign of a region where the fabric of space itself is damaged or unstable—likely a remnant of the ancient war that split the primordial world. Such places are often hunting grounds for rare spatial-treasures, or, as we see here, ideal shadow grounds for powerful cultivators to hide in plain sight. The "underworld" space beneath the cliff face is a pocket dimension, a bubble of reality clinging to the edge of a broken world. It’s a perfect hideout because it exists in a blind spot of normal perception, yet is physically anchored to the main world.
