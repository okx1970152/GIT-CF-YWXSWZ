## Chapter Overview
The trap is sprung, the blade is drawn, and the betrayal is absolute. Daofather Crimsonbright is forced to make a heart-wrenching strategic sacrifice as the Evergreen Sword Immortal reveals his true, cold-blooded colors in the most devastating way possible—killing his own junior sister and crippling the Crimsonbright Realm's defensive line. Ji Ning, now fully unleashed after hiding his heartforce progress, rips through Heavenly God Puppets and Pure Yang True Immortals alike, but finds himself completely outmatched by the fused Three-Eyed Fiendgod. The fight for the Crimsonbright Realm reaches its most desperate hour, with Ji Ning forced to bow and ask the slumbering gods of the Starseizing Mansion for help. This is the kind of chapter where every page feels like a punch to the gut—and the excitement is only just beginning.

## Key Plot Points
- Daofather Crimsonbright, watching the battlefield from afar, makes a cold strategic decision to abandon Ji Ning and the Xia Emperor's region, dedicating the archer Dongzhe's chaos-fruit arrows to the other seven battlefronts where the advantage can be pressed.
- The traitor Changqing's backstory is explored: Daofather Crimsonbright reflects on how the Evergreen Sword Immortal was the disciple he trusted most—a reclusive, single-minded Dao-seeker who seemed destined to become a Daofather. The betrayal cuts deeper than any wound.
- Ji Ning, seeing the disaster unfolding, unleashes his long-hidden heartforce and soul arts (nearing minor mastery) and goes on a devastating rampage, destroying a Bi Fang legion and killing a Pure Yang True Immortal with two shield slams.
- The three great demon gods of Devouring Mountain finally complete their unity formation and become the terrifying Three-Eyed Fiendgod, whose power has crossed the Daofather threshold.
- In a single clash, Ji Ning is sent flying backward by the Three-Eyed Fiendgod, confirming the immense gap in strength. The enemy has its orders: kill Ji Ning at all costs.
- Ji Ning's true body, inside the Water Mansion, bows before the seven ancient True Gods—Red Snow, Yuan Guang, and the others—and formally requests their aid.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Heartforce Mastery & Soul Arts (心力神魂法门)**—In Xianxia, these represent the most difficult but most rewarding path to power. Ji Ning's heartforce has reached "near minor mastery" (近乎小成), which is a huge milestone. Heartforce bypasses physical defenses; it allows one to strike at the enemy's mind and soul directly. Combined with a Karmic Punishment Deity's raw strength, it's a terrifying force multiplier.

**Karmic Punishment Deities (刑天神)** are the pinnacle of immortal war formations. They're essentially giant battle avatars formed by dozens or even hundreds of Celestial Immortals and Loose Immortals working in harmony. A well-piloted Karmic Punishment Deity is a level of combat power that can rival a True God or Daofather—which is exactly why Ji Ning, even vastly outmatched, is still a threat.

**"A person can pretend for a time, but it is difficult to pretend for an entire lifetime"** —This classic Daoist saying is wielded here to highlight the terrifying nature of the Evergreen Sword Immortal's betrayal. He didn't break under pressure; he was *always* a traitor. His Dao-heart was never conflicted. In the world of Xianxia, a cultivator's long lifespan makes betrayal all the more devastating—you entrust centuries of trust, and it can be undone in an instant.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, because this chapter is a masterclass in "the knife twist." The Evergreen Sword Immortal is revealed not as a weak-willed turncoat, but as a true, ice-cold demon whose Dao-heart is *stronger* than most righteous cultivators. That's what makes him so dangerous—and what makes Daofather Crimsonbright's realization so bitter. Meanwhile, Ji Ning's full-power display is utterly savage, showing that his time cultivating heartforce was not wasted. But the real headline here is the Three-Eyed Fiendgod. Ji Ning's defeat is instantaneous and decisive. This is a classic Xianxia "overwhelming enemy" setup—the hero must either level up, use a trump card, or call in reinforcements. And Ji Ning? He just chose option three. The party is about to get *loud*.

## Deep Dive: The Three-Eyed Fiendgod & The Unity Formation
The Unity Formation (合体阵法) is a staple of Xianxia war epics. It's not just about three experts standing next to each other; it's about fusing their Dao-hearts, divine power, and even their soul energy into a single entity. The resulting being is a new, gestalt intelligence that commands power far beyond the sum of its parts. The three Devouring Mountain Demon Gods are ancient, prideful monsters—their willingness to merge is a testament to how much they want Ji Ning dead. And in Xianxia, when your enemy stops caring about pride and just wants you erased, you know you're in real trouble.
