## Chapter Overview

Fellow Daoists, get ready for the most brutal training montage in the Yan Mountains! Chapter 11 strips away all illusions of instant mastery and plants Ji Ning firmly on the long, grinding road of sword cultivation. Ji Yichuan delivers a masterclass in Xianxia pedagogy: first the body must learn to obey, then the heart must engrave every strike a million times. By the end of the chapter, a year of relentless practice has turned our young protagonist into a crack shot who can nail four thrown stones with a single draw. It’s slow, it’s painful, and it’s absolutely glorious.

## Key Plot Points

- **The sword is an extension of the body**: Ji Yichuan explains that raw talent means nothing without the basics. He assigns Ji Ning a sixteen-form fist set to teach full-body power generation before even touching a real sword.
- **Thirteen fundamental forms**: Ji Ning learns that every sword technique is built from thirteen basic moves—six offensive and seven defensive. He must practice each form a million times on a stationary metal puppet.
- **The puppet awakens**: After mastering static practice, the puppet moves and attacks, forcing Ji Ning into defensive drills, then free sparring. It’s essentially a robot personal trainer from hell that never gets tired.
- **Swordsmanship’s three realms**: Ji Yichuan lays out the path: Basic → Subtlety → Unity of Man and Sky. He reveals it took him twelve years to reach Unity of Man and Sword (the Subtlety realm), a humbling benchmark for his son.
- **Father’s hidden pride**: Watching Ji Ning train without complaint despite the pain, Ji Yichuan admits to Yuchi Snow that his son’s willpower exceeds even his own expectations—a rare moment of warmth from the ice-cold Sword Immortal.
- **One year later – archery master**: Under Meng Yu’s guidance, Ji Ning masters the heart-guided arrow and the four-arrow simultaneous shot. He’s told the training ground is too small and must move to longer ranges.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Thirteen Fundamental Sword Forms (基础十三剑式)** – In traditional Chinese martial arts, almost every weapon skill is built from a small set of repeated actions. The novel elevates this into a strict cultivation method where quantity becomes quality: a million thrusts forge muscle memory that no theory can replace. This reflects the real-world Chinese saying “practice makes perfect” (熟能生巧).

**Three Realms of the Sword (剑法三重境界)** – Basic (基础) means the moves are executed correctly but mechanically. Subtlety (入微) means the body and sword become one, and control is so fine that even a hair’s breadth deviation is eliminated. Unity of Man and Sky (天人合一) is a transcendent state where the swordsman harmonizes with the surrounding natural laws, enabling superhuman reflexes and instincts.

**Metal puppet training** – Puppets (傀儡) are a common trope in Xianxia, often built by Ki Refining specialists as servants, guards, or training partners. This simple metal puppet has peak Houtian strength and indestructible body, making it the perfect tireless drill instructor—it can swing its saber for hours without needing to rest, drink, or yell at the trainee.

**Heart-guided arrow (心箭)** – Archery in Xianxia is not purely physical; it requires the archer’s intent and soul to guide the arrow. “Heart” here refers to spiritual focus and unwavering will. When Ji Ning’s heart-arrow is complete, his aim becomes a direct extension of his mind, bypassing slow mental calculations.

## Reading Guide

Chapter 11 is a deliberate slowdown after the explosive breakthrough of the Crimsonbright Diagram. The author is building the foundation for Ji Ning’s combat style: raw Fiendgod strength on one side, perfect sword and archery technique on the other. Savour the father-son dynamics here—Ji Yichuan’s cold exterior conceals an almost trembling pride. Also note the mention of twelve years to reach Unity of Man and Sword; this sets a brutal truth: even geniuses must crawl before they walk. The time skip at the end signals that the story is about to leap forward, so hold tight—the teenage years will bring bigger dangers and bolder enemies.
