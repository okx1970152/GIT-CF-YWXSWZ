## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is a masterclass in the classic Xianxia gear-check-and-upside-down-mood-beat structure. We start in the cozy loot cave with Ji Ning, the Yellow Bear, and the Black Ox, sifting through the belongings of the fallen Shaoyan Nong and Xiangliu Fang. It’s a treasure hunter’s dream—poisons, pills, Primordial Liquid, and the piece de resistance: a Unity Water-Pearl that can power up a Primaltwin. Our boy Ji Ning is about to pull off one of the rarest flexes in the cultivation world—making a second body while still a Wanxiang Adept. But just as we’re vibing with the victory lap, the tone whiplashes hard. A Celestial Immortal from the Shaoyan clan has arrived at Winged Serpent Lake, and he is *not* in a forgiving mood. The chapter ends with a chilling display of casual cruelty, a brutal reminder that in this world, the strong don’t just win—they make sure everyone pays.

## Key Plot Points

- The Yellow Bear takes charge of Shaoyan Nong’s inventory, using crazy artifact-spirit powers to auto-sort everything. Most of the common pills and poisons are dismissed as useless for a Fiendgod Body Refiner.
- The haul includes a massive 10,000 catties of Primordial Liquid from Shaoyan Nong—a nice emergency fund for an aspiring Primal Daoist, but the Yellow Bear forbids Ji Ning from using it, as he must stay below the Primal stage for the Immortal Feast.
- A spare Greater Teleportation Dao Talisman is found, giving Ji Ning *two* of these life-saving escape items. A brutal reminder that Shaoyan Nong died before he could even use his.
- The Black Ox spots a glowing Unity Water-Pearl in the pile. The Yellow Bear identifies it as a top-tier Primaltwin carrier, way better than the famous Mysterious Female Pearl.
- Ji Ning gets the green light to split his soul—his divine sense covering 1,000 li means his soul is strong enough to survive the split—setting up his Primaltwin cultivation at the Wanxiang stage.
- The mood abruptly shifts as the Yellow Bear senses a Celestial Immortal arriving at Winged Serpent Lake. Ancestor Xuanji of the Shaoyan clan has come for revenge.
- Unable to find Ji Ning or break into Ten Thousand Swords City, Ancestor Xuanji coldly orders the massacre of mortal civilians on the lake and sets up a surveillance net across Ji Ning’s homeland.
- The chapter closes with a command to the Heavenly Treasures Mountain to tip off the Shaoyan clan the moment Ji Ning is spotted anywhere in the Grand Xia.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Primaltwin (第二元神) & Soul-Splitting**: The Primaltwin is one of Xianxia’s most satisfying power-ups. A cultivator’s Primal is normally the fusion of their Soul and the Tortoise-Serpent that forms during breakthrough. To create a Primaltwin, you need a rare “carrier” (like this Unity Water-Pearl) and a soul strong enough to survive the split—half stays home, half goes to the carrier to build a second, independent body. Think of it as buying a second life, but the down payment is your own spiritual integrity.
- **Unity Water-Pearl vs Mysterious Female Pearl (玄牝珠 vs 一气元水珠)**: The Mysterious Female Pearl is the “standard luxury” carrier in most Xianxia—it works, but it’s a blank canvas. The Unity Water-Pearl is a specialty item: it forms the Primaltwin’s body from “water quintessence,” giving the twin an innate affinity for all Water-element Daos. For Ji Ning, whose Rainwater Dao is a branch of Water, this is like giving a fish a swimming scholarship.
- **The Immortal Feast Competition (仙缘大会) & Stage Lock**: The competition is strictly for cultivators “below the Primal stage.” Any Primal Daoist is out. But the Primaltwin rule is a famous loophole: as long as your true body stays Wanxiang, you’re eligible. The upper-tier geniuses of the Grand Xia are expected to show up with one, so it’s more of a “status flex” than a cheat.
- **Karmic Sin & Civilians**: Ancestor Xuanji’s massacre of the mortals on the lake is a major villain moment. In Xianxia cosmology, the Heavenly Dao actively punishes Immortals who slaughter ordinary people—it clings to you as karmic sin, reducing your luck and making you accident-prone. That a Celestial Immortal is willing to accept *any* sin shows how deep his vendetta runs.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a beautiful switch-up. You go from the cozy, satisfying feel of a loot-hall scene to the icy dread of an enemy *at the door* who can’t find you but is making sure your people can’t find peace either. Enjoy the gear-porn and the cool Primaltwin lore in the first half—seriously, the Yellow Bear’s deadpan “you can sell this poison” energy is chef’s kiss. But pay attention to the second half. Ancestor Xuanji doesn’t just fail and go home; he sets up a permanent watch, kills civilians out of spite, and bribes the Heavenly Treasures Mountain to be his informant network. This is Ji Ning’s world now: a hunted genius whose very existence makes his clan a target. The Primaltwin setup is huge, but the ominous shadow looming over his home is the real takeaway.
