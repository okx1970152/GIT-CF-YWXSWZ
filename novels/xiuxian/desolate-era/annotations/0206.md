## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists—this chapter is a short but *satisfying* palette cleanser before the storm. We follow Dongyi (remember that cold-sha-wielding Wanxiang Adept?) as he stumbles upon a vein of Goldflame Earthfire—a treasure he can’t use but can certainly sell. Then, we rejoin our favorite genius Ji Ning and his junior apprentice-brother Mu ‘Log’ Zishuo on their quest for the mysterious cold-sha Qingqing promised. What they find is a vein of the legendary Snow-Soul Cold-sha, a top-tier treasure with the potential to be nurtured all the way to first grade. But the real treat here isn’t the loot—it’s the sheer warmth of the brotherly banter between Ji Ning, the ever-put-upon Zishuo, and the snarky but loyal Skygreen Serpent. It’s a slice-of-cultivation-life moment, a pause to let us breathe and bond before the stakes ratchet up again.

## Key Plot Points
- **Dongyi’s Discovery**: The chapter opens with Dongyi, a veteran Wanxiang Adept, discovering a vein of Goldflame Earthfire seeping from a fissure. He immediately assesses its value but realizes that his own cold-attribute cultivation art makes it impossible for him to use.
- **The Treasure’s Fate**: Rather than letting it go to waste, Dongyi decides to trade it—a classic Xianxia moment where one cultivator’s bad luck becomes another’s opportunity.
- **The Lake Expedition**: Back with Ji Ning, Qingqing (in her cute snake form) guides the dragon-headed warboat to a massive lake, revealing the hiding spot of her own cold-sha vein.
- **Underwater Gorge**: The trio dives deep into an icy underwater gorge, bypassing lesser demons through Qingqing’s intimidating aura.
- **Discovery of Snow-Soul Cold-sha**: They find a sentient, ten-zhang-long vein of Snow-Soul Cold-sha. Ji Ning and Zishuo are overjoyed—this is a top-tier cold-sha with the potential to reach first grade.
- **Preparation and Risk**: Ji Ning explains the nine-tier grading of cold-shas, highlighting the rarity of first-grade potential. He sets up a maze formation and entrusts Qingqing with guarding while he and Zishuo prepare to plant the cold-sha into their Violet Palaces—a dangerous process that could be fatal for Zishuo if interrupted.
- **The Cultivation Begins**: Ji Ning takes the lead, inhaling a strand of the icy sha energy and guiding it into his Violet Palace to begin the planting process. The chapter ends with him successfully anchoring the cold-sha into a transparent ‘island’ within his Violet Palace—the first step of a long and delicate process.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Nine Grades of Cold-Sha (寒煞九品)**: In Xianxia world-building, natural elemental energies like cold-sha (ice-attribute lethal energy) and earthfire are classified into nine grades. The system is straightforward: grades 9–7 are low-grade, 6–4 are mid-grade, and 3–1 are high-grade. Most cultivators can only nurture their collected sha or fire to the top three grades. Reaching the *second* grade is a rare achievement, and reaching the *first* grade is legendary. Ji Ning’s excitement over Snow-Soul Cold-sha is because it’s one of the few types with the innate potential to reach first grade—a truly elite resource.

**“Planting” a Cold-Sha (采种寒煞)**: This isn’t as simple as just absorbing energy. Cultivators must draw a strand of wild, aggressive natural cold-sha into their Violet Palace (the internal dimensional space housing their power) and *anchor* it there, like planting a tree in soil. If interrupted, the cold-sha can rampage and destroy the palace, potentially crippling or killing the cultivator (especially for Ki Refiners who lack the resilience of Fiendgod Body Refiners like Ji Ning). This is why they need a guard—Qingqing’s role is crucial.

**The Economic Angle**: Dongyi’s discovery of the Goldflame Earthfire is a classic reminder of Xianxia economics. Not every treasure is for *you*. Sometimes, a cultivator finds a resource perfectly aligned with another path, and the smart play is to trade it for something you *can* use. The natural rarity of such veins (a “once-in-a-blue-moon” find) means they command a high price.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a perfect example of why pacing matters in Xianxia. After the intensity of sect trials and face-smashing duels, we get a straightforward exploration scene that does three things beautifully: 1) It deepens our bond with the main trio through playful dialogue (Qingqing calling Mu Zishuo ‘Log’ is peak sibling energy), 2) It delivers a concrete power boost via a well-described resource hunt, and 3) It sets up future narrative fuel—that Earthfire vein Dongyi found will almost certainly come back into play. Pay attention to the care Ji Ning takes with Zishuo’s safety; it reinforces his pragmatic-but-protective Dao-heart. The planting process, while currently just beginning, has high stakes. Will they be interrupted? What’s lurking in the lake? The calm before the storm is always the best time to tighten your seatbelt.
