## Chapter Overview
Ji Ning steps into the heart of the treasure ground and faces his most challenging trial yet—not a monster or a rival cultivator, but the terrifying residual will of an ancient Fiendgod. The sheer killing intent radiating from a long-dead corpse forces him to rely on his soul defenses, and he soon discovers that the greatest obstacle isn't the corpse itself, but the bow at its side. A broken, stringless bow gathers the energy of heaven and earth and begins firing arrows with mindless, relentless fury. Ji Ning must use every ounce of his Eight-Nine Arcane Art and sword mastery just to close the distance. The chapter sets up a classic Xianxia treasure-grabbing scenario with a twist—the treasure fights back, and the real test is yet to come.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning enters the massive gorge and is immediately assaulted by the killing intent from a huge Fiendgod corpse. He uses the Nuwa Diagram to stabilize his soul, showcasing the high-level soul-protection technique he learned at Mount Innerheart.
- He encounters a black-robed youth sitting in a cliff cranny. The youth reveals that Ji Ning has passed two of the three forbidden zones and now faces the third. He also comments on Ji Ning’s freakish durability, hinting that his Eight-Nine Arcane Art has made him virtually indestructible to physical attacks at this level.
- The black-robed youth confirms he is not from Mount Innerheart, establishing him as an independent Celestial Immortal or a rival treasure hunter.
- As Ji Ning approaches the corpse, the broken black bow acts autonomously, gathering natural forces and firing arrows at him. Even without a master, the bow’s power is immense, sending Ji Ning flying.
- Ji Ning analyzes the bow’s grade, comparing it to his own Thousandbull Sword and concluding it must be at least a top-grade Pure Yang treasure or even a Protocosmic spirit-treasure.
- He weathers the storm of arrows using the Water Heavens sword art and his Fiendgod body, finally reaching within ten zhang of the corpse. The black-robed youth warns him that the third forbidden zone—which tests something beyond physical toughness—is about to begin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Nuwa Diagram**: A visualization technique from the lineage of the goddess Nuwa, the creator of humanity in Chinese mythology. In the Xianxia context, such techniques are used to fortify the soul against external attacks, mental demon assaults, or the sheer pressure of a higher being’s residual will. It’s notable that Ji Ning learned this at Mount Innerheart, tying him to one of the most mysterious and powerful sects in the setting.

**Fiendgod Corpse & Residual Will**: In the Desolate Era cosmology, Fiendgods at a high enough level have their souls interwoven with every shred of their flesh. A being of this caliber can leave behind a lingering, lethal will that can kill lesser cultivators just by proximity. The fact that Ji Ning can withstand it at the early Void stage is a testament to both his soul fortification and his formidable Eight-Nine Arcane Art.

**Pure Yang Treasures & Protocosmic Spirit-Treasures**: The hierarchy of magic treasures in this world is strict: Mortal, Earth, Heaven, Immortal, Pure Yang, and then Protocosmic. The bow’s ability to fight autonomously with the power of a Celestial Immortal—even with a broken string—places it firmly in the upper echelons of treasure classification. This is a massive power-up potential for Ji Ning if he can claim it.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia treasure-hunting tension. The pacing is excellent: the initial oppressive atmosphere, then the introduction of the mysterious black-robed youth (who is clearly more than he seems), and finally the visceral action of Ji Ning getting punched and blasted across the canyon. The bow’s autonomous attacks are a brilliant way to showcase its power without a wielder, and Ji Ning’s clinical analysis of its grade gives lore nerds exactly what they want. The “third forbidden zone” cliffhanger is perfectly timed—it raises the stakes by hinting that Ji Ning’s trump card (his near-indestructible body) will be useless. Readers should pay close attention to the black-robed youth’s dialogue; he’s not just comic relief, but a potential source of crucial information or a future complication. This is a pivotal moment for Ji Ning’s power progression, setting the stage for what could be his greatest treasure yet.
