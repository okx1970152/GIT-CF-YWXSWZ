## Chapter Overview

Eighteen years after the Shaoyin fiasco, the sword of Damocles finally swings. Ji Ning and Yu Wei stand watch over Anchan City, but the narrator passes the conn to the *other side* of the chessboard—the Seamless Gate's war room. We see the sheer industrial scale of their operation: 2,000 squads, 10,000+ troops, dispatched simultaneously across the entire Grand Xia world—an earth-shattering probing strike before the real invasion. And among those thousands of squads, ONE is special: the eighteen-man elite squad targeting Anchan City itself. The Uniter Sword Immortal's stubborn refusal to bend has made him a marked man, and the Seamless Gate has rolled out the red carpet of death.

## Key Plot Points

- **Time Check**: 18 years have passed since Ji Ning's exile. Anchan City remains in a state of paranoid siege, rotating Celestial Immortal mental domains 24/7.
- **Yu Wei's Worry**: Yu Wei, the most pessimistic of the three defenders, repeatedly warns Ji Ning against underestimating the Seamless Gate. Her fear stems from the Shaoyin incident—she's terrified of losing him again.
- **Ji Ning's Growth**: Offscreen, he has nearly completed the Heartforce Sword Immortal technique and his sword-Dao comprehension has accelerated. He's no longer the same fighter who survived nine Celestial Immortals; he's now a *measurably* harder target.
- **The Fifth World Revealed**: Through the dialogue of two low-ranking defectors, we glimpse the Seamless Gate's home base—a "Fifth World" teeming with Celestial Immortals and True Gods. The scale is terrifying: comparable to Daofather Crimsonbright's entire roster.
- **Feint & Real**: Most of the 2,000 squads are either reconnaissance or *staged attacks* (e.g., 2 defectors sent to "fight" Fireshan Female Immortal to preserve her cover). Only a handful are real elimination squads.
- **The Elite Eighteen**: Purplegrass Celestial Immortal leads 17 peak Celestial Immortals, all trained in a Godking-created formation. Their explicit target: Kill the Uniter Sword Immortal and the Northming Sword Immortal. They are described as far more dangerous than 100 ordinary Celestial Immortals.
- **Simultaneous Strike**: All squads move at once, signaled by talisman shattering. The tension is palpable—the storm has begun.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### "A Million Celestial Immortals Under a Daofather"
The dialogue mentions that Daofather Crimsonbright commands roughly a million Celestial Immortals. In Xianxia hierarchy, a Daofather (Ancestral Immortal) is the supreme ruler of a major faction, controlling a vast number of major worlds. "A million Celestial Immortals" is not hyperbole but a genuine scale marker—it shows the cosmic-level resources and manpower a top-tier Daofather can marshal. The Seamless Gate's Fifth World, matching that number, signals an *equal-level* threat, not a minor skirmish.

### The "Feint Operation" (虚则实之)
The classic military stratagem described by the defector—"In the void, feign substance; in substance, feign void" (实则虚之，虚则实之)—is a hallmark of Chinese warfare philosophy, famously analyzed in *The Art of War*. Here, the Seamless Gate uses a staged attack on Fireshan Commandery to protect the Fireshan Female Immortal's cover. In Xianxia politics, "losing face" by being attacked is sometimes *strategically beneficial* to the victim: it provides plausible deniability for future defection.

### The Small Cycle of Reincarnation
This chapter references the broader cosmic aftermath: after the war that shattered the original Six Paths of Reincarnation, Daofather Crimsonbright established a *Small Cycle*—a localized, functional recreation. Its Ten Kings of Hell are said to be more politically accessible than the ancient, inscrutable ones of the original cycle. This frames the Seamless Gate's next move as operating within a *politically navigable* underworld, not a wild chaos.

### Small Cycle of Reincarnation (小型轮回)

A localized, functional recreation of the original Six Paths of Reincarnation, established by Daofather Crimsonbright after the cosmic war shattered the original system; its Ten Kings of Hell are more politically accessible to major powers.

### Human-ranked treasure (人阶法宝)

The lowest classified grade of magic treasure, suited for Zifu and Wanxiang-level cultivators.

## Reading Guide

**DIAL THE PRESSURE UP.** This entire chapter is a masterful *chessboard switch*—we spend the first half with Ji Ning's quiet tension, then the narrator flips to the enemy's POV. The genius of this structure is that we learn *everything* the protagonists don't know: the scale (2,000 squads), the deception (staged attacks to protect sleeper agents), and the personalized threat (18 elite Celestial Immortals, formation-trained, specifically aiming for *them*). Purplegrass Celestial Immortal's briefing is essentially a *death sentence* being read aloud. The Uniter Sword Immortal's stubborn refusal to join the Seamless Gate marks him—and by extension, Ji Ning—as high-value targets. Watch how the author uses the low-ranking defectors' casual gossip to deliver worldbuilding (Fifth World, Daofather-scale forces) without a boring info-dump. The payoff? That final "Crack" of 18 talismans shattering simultaneously. No trumpets. No speeches. Just a sound of glass breaking—and the annihilation protocol begins.
