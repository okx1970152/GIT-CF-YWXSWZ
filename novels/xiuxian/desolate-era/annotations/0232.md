## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists! Ji Ning is back in the Treasure Palace, and the stakes just got real. After breezing through the Wanxiang stage, he’s now facing a choice that will define his power for years to come: the selection of his first Earth-ranked treasures. The Yellow-Haired Bear presents the menu, and it’s a gourmet feast of primal power—supreme-grade Earth-ranked curios and weapons that would make any Grand Xia noble weep with envy. But with only three picks for his entire Wanxiang-to-Primal career arc, every decision is a sword-biting conflict between immediate power, long-term potential, and the hunger for a perfect combat suite. This chapter is a pure, delicious gear-porn deep dive into Xianxia resource management, strategic temptation, and the growing maturity of a monster genius who knows a quick high can become a long-term trap.

## Key Plot Points
- The Yellow-Haired Bear explains the crucial difference: Human-ranked treasures are short-term tools, but Earth-ranked selections can define a cultivator for centuries. Ji Ning must treat this choice with the gravity it deserves.
- Ji Ning is dazzled by the catalogue of supreme-grade Earth-ranked items, realizing that any single one could be the centerpiece treasure of a commandery’s Heavenly Treasures Mountain branch.
- First temptation: **The Black Tortoise Golden Scale Boat**. A flying treasure with Immortal-level speed and unmatched defense. Ji Ning rejects it, deciding a flying treasure is not worth a limited pick.
- Second temptation: **The Earthfire Heart Lamp**. A curio that can raise his Earthfire to the first grade in ten years—a genuine threat to Primal Daoists. Ji Ning agonizes but ultimately passes, reasoning that his sword-arts and Fiendgod cultivation will outscale it in that timeframe.
- Ji Ning discovers the synergy trap: raising Earthfire alone would unbalance his Water-Fire Lotus technique, which requires equal power from both Fire and Coldsnow.
- **The Curio Crisis**: Ji Ning tries to bargain, asking if the Earthfire Heart Lamp and Coldsnow Mirror can count as *one* selection. The Yellow-Haired Bear shoots him down with a blunt, angry lecture on their rarity.
- The chapter’s climax: the Yellow-Haired Bear reveals the hidden third option—**The Cold Abyss Sword Formation**, a set of nine supreme-grade Earth-ranked flying swords. This was meant for his third selection, but the Bear pushes it forward to keep him alive on the dangerous path ahead.
- Ji Ning is left staring at three devastatingly good options, forced to commit to a single choice.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Selection Tiers & The Gear Curve**: In Xianxia, the progression of magical treasures mirrors the cultivation ladder. Human-ranked items are starter gear—effective during early Zifu, but quickly outgrown. Earth-ranked items, especially supreme-grade ones, represent a major power plateau that can last through the Wanxiang and early Primal stages. The fact that Daoist Threelives’ ‘supreme-grade’ outclasses the Grand Xia Dynasty’s own classification system underscores just how far above the mortal realm this inheritance is. Ji Ning isn’t just shopping; he’s pulling from a god’s personal armory.
- **The Curio Trap**: The Earthfire Heart Lamp is a beautiful example of a ‘long-investment item’ in Xianxia. It promises an enormous payoff—a first-grade Earthfire that can threaten Primal Daoists—but only after a decade of nurturing. The tension is classic: take the guarantee of eventual power, or bet on a faster growth curve through techniques and combat? Ji Ning’s rejection shows his growing strategic confidence in his own talent, a hallmark of a peak genius protagonist.
- **Balance in the Dao**: Ji Ning’s rationale for rejecting the Earthfire Heart Lamp is a subtle nod to Daoist philosophy. A practitioner who lets one element overrun the other loses harmony, which in this novel directly translates to a weaker Water-Fire Lotus. It’s a small but deep moment of cultivation wisdom: strength is not just about the highest spike, but about the cohesion of one’s entire foundation.
- **Why Senior is Grumpy**: The Yellow-Haired Bear’s anger at Ji Ning’s bartering attempt is a classic ‘ancient senior exasperated by cheeky junior’ moment. In Xianxia, ancient artifact spirits have seen empires rise and fall; they are not to be haggled with. The Bear’s frustration comes from a place of genuine care—he’s already bending the rules to show the Cold Abyss Sword Formation early. Ji Ning’s attempt to bargain for two treasures as one is seen not as clever, but as ungrateful ignorance of the immense favor being done.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the calm before the storm—a gear-select interlude that, while containing no combat, carries massive weight for the upcoming conflict with Snowdragon Mountain. Keep your eyes on Ji Ning’s decision-making process: every rejection is a window into his Dao-heart. He’s passed up raw convenience and predictable power in favor of raw killing potential. That’s a swordsman’s choice. Also, take note of the Bear’s line about not wanting him to ‘die out there too early’. It’s a quiet confirmation that the road ahead is going to be brutal, and Ji Ning will need every edge he can get. Get ready, because the next selection is going to determine how the revenge arc unfolds.
