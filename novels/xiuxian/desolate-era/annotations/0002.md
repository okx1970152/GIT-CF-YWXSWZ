## Chapter Overview

This chapter begins Ji Ning's journey through the Netherworld after his death. Guided by a purple-robed emissary of Lord Cui, the First Judge, Ji Ning walks the Yellow Springs Path, crosses the Naihe Bridge, and approaches Meng Po's station to drink the soup of forgetfulness. Just as he faces the prospect of losing his memories, the underworld is violently attacked by the dreaded Two-Realm Calamity Dragon Birth-Annihilation Formation. In the chaos, Ji Ning seizes the opportunity to leap into the Human Path of reincarnation, successfully reborn into a new mortal life.

## Key Plot Points

*   Ji Ning learns about Lord Cui Jue's status as the First Judge, who personally judged his karmic merit and arranged a favorable rebirth.
*   Ji Ning observes the processes of the Netherworld: the Yellow Springs Path, the Naihe Bridge, the River of Forgetfulness, the Stone of Three Lives, and the Home Viewing Terrace.
*   As Ji Ning approaches Meng Po, she suddenly looks up and cries out in anger as the sky is torn apart by countless black dragons.
*   The Two-Realm Calamity Dragon Birth-Annihilation Formation unleashes billions of black lightning bolts, destroying the Naihe Bridge, annihilating countless souls, and threatening the Six Paths of Reincarnation.
*   With the invisible restraints lifted, Ji Ning dashes toward the nearest reincarnation passage—the Human Path—and jumps in just as lightning sweeps away the remaining ghosts.
*   Ji Ning is reborn as a human infant, crying out for the first time, signaling his successful reincarnation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the classic Chinese underworld bureaucracy and its iconic figures. **Lord Cui Jue** (崔珏, also known as Judge Cui) is a real figure from Chinese folk religion—the first judge of the Ten Kings of Hell, wielding the Book of Life and Death to determine the fates of all souls. His authority is second only to the Ten Kings themselves. **Meng Po** (孟婆, “Old Lady Meng”) is the goddess of forgetfulness, who serves a special soup (Meng Po Soup) at the Naihe Bridge to erase memories before reincarnation. The **Six Paths of Reincarnation** (六道轮回) originate from Buddhist cosmology, depicting the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth across six realms: Deva, Asura, Human, Animal, Hungry Ghost, and Hell. The **Two-Realm Calamity Dragon Birth-Annihilation Formation** is a devastating formation that breaches the boundary between worlds, marking the first major intrusion of the outside conflict into the underworld. This event foreshadows the impending Great Tribulation that will shake the Three Realms.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is critical for understanding the metaphysical structure of the Desolate Era universe—particularly the mechanism of reincarnation and the karmic system. Pay attention to the description of the Book of Life and Death and Lord Cui’s role: Ji Ning’s past-life karmic merit (from his charitable deeds on Earth) directly influenced his favorable treatment. The attack on the Six Paths of Reincarnation is a major plot event; it explains why Ji Ning was able to retain his memories despite drinking (or skipping) the Meng Po Soup. The chaos allows him to jump into the Human Path rather than his designated path, setting up his future cultivation advantage. The chapter also establishes the Netherworld as a vulnerable node in the cosmic order, a weakness that the Seamless Gate will later exploit. Readers new to xianxia should note that time flows differently in different realms—a concept that recurs throughout the series.
