## Chapter Overview

Get ready, because this is the chapter where Ji Ning's luck finally turns from "bitter disappointment" into "unimaginable jackpot." Hot on the heels of being passed over by Daofather Crimsonbright, a mysterious Pure Yang True Immortal named Jiang Jun drops in unannounced and drops a bombshell: his master, none other than the most enigmatic and legendary teacher in all the Three Realms, wants Ji Ning as his own disciple! This isn't just any offer; it's the universe's biggest "I got you, fam." The chapter is a masterclass in tension and release, from Ji Ning's initial, understandable paranoia to the earth-shattering reveal of the name "Patriarch Subhuti," sending our favorite bear into a frenzy of excitement. All the while, a delicious subplot brews as the Grand Xia royalty arrives with their own, far less impressive, offer for Ji Ning, setting up a classic Xianxia clash of destinies.

## Key Plot Points

*   A Pure Yang True Immortal, calling himself Jiang Jun, appears before Ji Ning without warning. Ji Ning is immediately on edge, understanding the power gap and the fact that a being of this level wouldn't care about mere 'geniuses.'
*   The immortal reveals he's there on his master's orders to fetch Ji Ning, telling him to address him as 'Brother Jiang,' a clear sign that a Daofather intends to take him as a junior disciple.
*   The aquatic palace's artifact spirit immediately sounds the alarm, warning Ji Ning to identify the Daofather first, as some were mortal enemies of his previous master, Daoist Threelives. Ji Ning's joy is instantly tempered with cold caution.
*   Jiang Jun recites a series of clues about his master: a being born from chaos, a friend of Pangu and Nuwa, a slayer of Fiendgod Daofathers, and the most mysterious figure in the Three Realms.
*   The reveal comes: the master is **Patriarch Subhuti** of Mount Innerheart's Tristar Crescent Abode! The artifact spirit goes wild with excitement, calling Ji Ning the luckiest man alive.
*   The artifact spirit explains the full weight of Subhuti's legend: his supreme teaching ability, his inviolable rule of secrecy, his unparalleled life-preservation skills, and, most importantly, his deep, sworn friendship with Daoist Threelives.
*   Subhuti's message to Ji Ning—"How is the little yellow bear doing these days?"—is the final, irrefutable proof that he knows all about Ji Ning's secret and is extending a helping hand out of friendship.
*   Meanwhile, in a parallel scene full of dramatic irony, the Prince of Qi and Prince of Yan arrive at Ji Ning's mansion, carrying a token from the Eternal Sword Immortal of Changqing, the Pure Yang Sword Immortal they had 'settled' for Ji Ning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a treasure trove of Xianxia tropes and deep mythological lore, centered around the legendary figure of **Patriarch Subhuti**. In Chinese folklore, most famously from *Journey to the West*, Patriarch Subhuti is the mysterious, reclusive master of Sun Wukong, the Monkey King. He taught the Monkey his most powerful divine abilities in his cave, the 'Tristar Crescent Abode' (a name with deep esoteric meaning), and then swore him to absolute secrecy, banishing him if he ever revealed his master's identity. This novel borrows that mythology wholesale, casting him as the ultimate hidden master – a being who operates from the shadows, knows everything (literally, through his 'One Dream of the Three Realms' ability), and whose power is so vast he once slew Fiendgod Daofathers. His friendship with Daoist Threelives is a crucial plot point, establishing him as a trustworthy ally rather than a potential threat. The concept of a teacher choosing a student from afar, based on a dream or divine observation, is a classic Xianxia "fate" trope. It underscores that Ji Ning's destiny is being guided and that his talents were being watched by beings far beyond his comprehension.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to the emotional whiplash Ji Ning experiences in this chapter. He goes from disappointed, to paranoid, to stunned disbelief, and finally to pure elation. Contrast this with the calm, casual authority of Jiang Jun, who treats the entire affair as a foregone conclusion. Also, don't miss the delicious dramatic irony of the final scene. While Ji Ning is having his fate changed forever by a being of cosmic legend, the two Princes are strolling in with their "good enough" consolation prize. This setup creates a fantastic cliffhanger. What will happen when the official Grand Xia delegation and Subhuti's envoy converge? The chapter masterfully sets the stage for a major choice: does Ji Ning join the well-trodden path of the Grand Xia's elite, or does he disappear into the pure mystery of the world's most secretive master?
