## Chapter Overview
Ji Ning lays down a brutal ultimatum to the Qi Kingdom’s last royals: release Qi Xiaoyu as his disciple or die on the spot. After a tense, knife-edge negotiation, the prince caves, and Ji Ning formally accepts his very first disciple—a girl burdened by karmic merit and a suspicious past. Meanwhile, hundreds of thousands of li away, the Firewing Fiend King convenes his fellow demon lords in a shadowy palace, revealing the true motive behind the kingdom’s destruction: a legendary, soul-shaking treasure trove that not even he could plunder alone.

## Key Plot Points
- Qi Rufeng tries to bargain for a century of protection, citing the karmic bond between the royal clan and Qi Xiaoyu; Ji Ning shuts it down with cold finality, offering a binary choice: immediate death for them all or his one-year/slay-the-Fiend-King deal.
- Ji Ning refuses to issue a Heavenly Dao oath, trusting only his own overwhelming power to enforce the agreement; the prince submits without the oath, and the karmic contract binding Qi Xiaoyu to the royal clan is dissolved by the Heavenly Dao itself.
- Qi Xiaoyu formally kowtows as Ji Ning’s first disciple; he makes clear he disdains force in this matter, offering her a genuine choice—a sign of his pride and respect that wins her genuine loyalty.
- A brief but sharp narrative cut shifts the scene to the Firewing Fiend King’s stronghold, where he summons his sworn “Seventh Brother” and reveals the secret that drove him to massacre an entire kingdom: an ancient, untouchable treasure vault that only the combined strength of all Twelve Fiend Kings of the Eastern Stream can hope to penetrate.
- The chapter closes on an ominous note: three squads of Firewing Guard, led by Loose Immortals, are dispatched to investigate the ambush that killed their fellow soldiers—a direct vector for conflict to find Ji Ning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Heavenly Dao Oaths (天道誓言):** In Xianxia metaphysics, a Heavenly Dao oath is the highest form of promise—binding under the universal laws of heaven. Breaking one invites immediate karmic backlash, tribulation, or even spontaneous annihilation. Cultivators of Ji Ning’s caliber treat them as nuclear-level commitments: they never swear one lightly, and demanding one is implicitly an insult to one’s trustworthiness. Ji Ning’s refusal to swear here is not petty stubbornness—it is an assertion of his absolute authority. The prince’s silent retreat is the correct survival response.
- **Karmic Merit & Servitude (功德与奴隶):** Qi Xiaoyu’s immense karmic merit makes her a prize in the Xianxia world—a naturally blessed foundation that any sect would fight to recruit. The royal clan’s “binding” of her through oaths and servitude is a dark, pragmatic strategy: use her virtue as the clan’s shield while controlling her with soul-level contracts. Ji Ning’s suspicion that the massacre of her childhood tribe was engineered by the very clan that “saved” her is a razor-sharp Xianxia trope: the rescuer is often the kidnapper, staging a tragedy to create a weapon.
- **The Twelve Fiend Kings of the Eastern Stream (东流十二妖王):** In the Star Continent’s demon hierarchy, these twelve represent the apex of regional power. Each commands legions of lesser demons, has carved out a vast territory, and answers only to their own ambition. The Firewing Fiend King convening them for a treasure expedition is not cooperation—it is a delicate alliance between predators, each one capable of betraying the others the moment a better prize appears.

## Reading Guide
Ji Ning’s pact is a masterclass in the “Daoist stick-and-carrot” negotiation style: he offers exactly enough hope (one year of shelter or a demon king’s head) to prevent the royals from choosing suicidal defiance, yet leaves them no illusion that they control the timeline. Watch how quickly his posture shifts from cold threat to quiet warmth once Qi Xiaoyu kneels—the mask of the killer drops the instant he has what he truly wants. The treasure-trove reveal is the story’s real ignition point: it retroactively justifies the kingdom’s destruction, introduces a looming alliance of top-tier demon lords, and sets the stage for Ji Ning’s next test. The chapter’s structural tension is delicious: we see the firepower preparing to converge on our protagonist before he even suspects it.
