## Chapter Overview

Fresh off a desperate, bloody battle, the Ji Clan breathes a sigh of relief as they formalize their life-saving pact with the Marquis of Anchan. But the predator is already circling. An Yinglong Guard from Snowdragon Mountain arrives, armed with a flawless military order, ready to dismantle the clan by the book. It's a tense standoff that pits raw imperial authority against the calculating might of a local superpower, and it all hinges on a single, charismatic variable: Beishan Baiwei. Get ready for a masterclass in political survival, where a signature on a scroll holds more weight than a thousand flying swords.

## Key Plot Points

*   **Political Counter-Play:** The chapter opens with the Ji Clan on high alert as an Yinglong Guard, Xue Hongyi, arrives to investigate the death of Xu Li, using a legitimate warrant for a fugitive as his cover. This is a classic Xianxia power play—using the system’s own rules to crush a weaker opponent.
*   **The Enforcer's Gambit:** Beishan Baiwei, the envoy from the Marquis of Anchan, immediately confronts Xue Hongyi. He dismantles the Guard’s legal threat not with brute force, but by asserting the political weight of the Marquis’ estate, revealing that his new contract makes the Ji Clan a protected asset.
*   **A Clash of Authorities:** Xue Hongyi, proud and arrogant in his Yinglong Guard status, is forced to back down. The arrival of a high-ranking member of the Marquis’s household, acting in an official capacity, supersedes even a direct military order from the Imperial Guard’s local command.
*   **The Pact Sealed:** In the safety of the warship, the formal transfer contract is signed. The deal is struck, cementing the Marquis of Anchan’s direct interest in the Ji Clan’s survival and paving the way for a very unequal resource-sharing agreement.
*   **A Friendship Forged in Power:** Beishan Baiwei declines the Ji Clan patriarch’s bribe, openly declaring Ji Ning a "friend." This personal bond, forged in this crisis, is far more valuable than any amount of prime essence stones.
*   **The Hidden Injury:** As the tension dissolves, Ji Yichuan’s body betrays him. The patriarch’s forced vitality crumbles, revealing a serious wound—a ticking time bomb that threatens the clan’s future leadership right when they need it most.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Yinglong Guard: The Emperor’s Sword & The Sect’s Shield.** The Yinglong Guard is a classic Xianxia institution: a state-sponsored, elite corps of cultivators that serves as a direct extension of the imperial will. To be a member is to wear the "imperial skin" (huang pi), granting one tremendous authority over the mortal and cultivator worlds alike. This makes them a perfect tool for major sects like Snowdragon Mountain, who seed their disciples into the Guard to use imperial power for personal vendettas—a perfect example of how the world of Xianxia blends politics and cultivation into one seamless, brutal system.

**The "Transfer Contract" – Power, Not Paper.** In the world of *Desolate Era*, legal documents like the transfer contract are not just pieces of paper; they are physical manifestations of a power hierarchy. A simple scroll becomes an immutable contract enforced by the terrifying prestige of a Marquis. By signing it, the Ji Clan isn't just giving up 70% of a mineral vein—they are swapping their sovereignty for the direct, untouchable protection of the strongest warlord in the region. It’s a realistic look at how "soft power" (political alliance) can be a far more effective shield than "hard power" (cultivation base) when facing an empire-backed enemy.

**Delegated Awe.** This chapter is a perfect example of this literary device. We don't need to see the terrifying power of the Marquis of Anchan. We see it through Xue Hongyi’s instant deference, the Ji Clan’s collective relief, and Beishan Baiwei’s casual dismissal of an entire sect. The power is felt through the reactions of those below it, making it feel vast and absolute without needing a single explosion or flying sword.

### Delegated Awe

A narrative device where the immense power of an unseen character or faction is conveyed through the awed or fearful reactions of present characters.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a palate cleanser and a setup after the frenetic action of the previous ones. Savor the shift in pace. Watch how Beishan Baiwei plays the role of the "cool older brother" power fantasy figure—the ultimate ally who solves problems with a smirk and a signature. But also pay close attention to Ji Yichuan. His collapse isn't just a shocking moment; it’s a narrative promise. A core pillar of Ji Ning's world is beginning to crack, and the emotional fallout will be immense. The safety bought by the Marquis is temporary, and the real, personal stakes are being quietly laid at the protagonist's feet. Enjoy the calm, fellow Daoists, because the storm is far from over.
