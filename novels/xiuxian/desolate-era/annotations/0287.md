## Chapter Overview
Hold onto your flying swords, fellow Daoists, because Ji Ning has just pulled off one of the most outrageous back-to-back power spikes in Xianxia history. Hot off the heels of snatching the One-Origin Water Pearl from a certain smug heir, our boy *immediately* converts that heavenly treasure into a fully operational second Primal—rocketing his clone from nothing all the way to the early Primal Daoist stage in a single chapter. No skipped beat, no grinding. Just raw, uninterrupted cultivation power. This isn't just a level up; it's a wholesale addition of a second, entirely independent combat body to Ji Ning's order of battle. The chapter is a masterclass in resource-to-power conversion, showing exactly why those rare heavenly earthly treasures cause such frenzy in the cultivation world. You'll also get a front-row seat to the birth of a Black Tortoise-Serpent Primal, a detailed look at the resulting soul upgrade, and a sobering reality check when the newly minted Primal tries to flex with his spell formation and hits an unexpected ceiling. If you've been waiting for Ji Ning's Ki Refining side to catch up to his Fiendgod body, *buckle up*.

## Key Plot Points
- **One-Origin Water Pearl Refined:** Ji Ning immediately puts the stolen treasure to use, discovering its perfect soul compatibility and vast internal energy reserves, making it the ideal vessel for a second Primal.
- **Uninterrupted Breakthrough Rush:** The second Primal cultivates from scratch directly to early *Primal Daoist* level, passing through Xiantian, Zifu, and Wanxiang in one smooth, unbroken surge, using only the treasure's own pent-up essence.
- **Birth of the Primal:** The chapter gives a vivid, step-by-step account of the Black Tortoise-Serpent Primal's formation, describing the falling stellar light, the merging of Yin and Yang, and the ignition of the golden Primordial Fire.
- **Soul Amplification:** Once housed within the tortoise-serpent, Ji Ning’s soul grows at an incredible rate, faster even than using the Nuwa Diagram, explaining why reaching the Primal stage grants cultivators their first Divine Sense.
- **Lesser Thousand Sword Formation Limitation:** Upgraded to Earth-ranked flying swords, the new second Primal struggles to control even the seventh level of the formation, highlighting the vastly increased difficulty of commanding better-grade weapons.
- **Strategy for the Future:** The yellow-furred bear reveals the hundred thousand jin of Primordial Liquid and hints that refining it will push Ji Ning’s clone to higher sub-realms, unlocking greater formation power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Black Tortoise-Serpent Primal (龟蛇元神):** You might recognize this beast—the *Xuanwu* (玄武) is one of the Four Symbols of Chinese constellations, a legendary creature embodying pure Yin and Water. In Daoist internal alchemy, the tortoise represents the True Essence of Water and the serpent the True Essence of Fire; their coiling symbolizes the perfect union of Yin and Yang energies. In the novel's cultivation system, this is the mandatory 'final form' that every Primal-level Ki Refiner's soul must take, and its formation is a rite of passage that massively amplifies spiritual perception and strength. The 'Primordial Fire' (元火) that ignites on its shell? That's a foundational power all Primal Daoists wield, born from the very act of creating the Primal itself.

**The Hell of Stepping from Wanxiang to Primal:** While Ji Ning made it look easy with a perfect vessel and a brick-like Dao-heart, let's not kid ourselves—in standard Xianxia, bridging that gap normally takes *centuries* of meditation, tribulation-surfing, and demon-plex scouring. The One-Origin Water Pearl is a cheat code of the highest order. Also noteworthy: breaking through to the Primal stage is the moment a cultivator truly earns the name ‘Daoist’ (真人/道人). Before that, they're still water fleas trying to jump across a river. After that? They can walk on it.

## Reading Guide
Fresh off the nail-biting Whitewater Hound breakthrough chapter, this one feels like a victory lap—and deservedly so. But don't let the festivity fool you; there's a quiet, masterful piece of storytelling happening here. Ji Ning's second Primal is now a *pure* Ki Refiner. No Fiendgod body, no divine abilities. This is a high-risk, high-reward bet: one soul, two bodies, two completely different combat toolkits. The chapter explicitly sets up the challenge of mastering the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation at higher grades, which plants a seed for future tactical depth. Keep an eye on that hundred thousand jin of Primordial Liquid. Our resident wise-cracking yellow-furred bear didn't bring it up just for show—that's the fuel for Ji Ning's next big push. Also, a tip for eagle-eyed readers: ever since the soul-twins formation, the Primaltwin's soul has been described as 'slightly weaker.' That detail may well matter when the clone faces its first serious threat.
