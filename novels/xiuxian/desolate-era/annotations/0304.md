## Chapter Overview

This chapter is pure Xianxia comfort food—half cultivation montage, half heartfelt backstage support. Ji Ning has just settled into the Yan Prince’s manor with his cousin-sister Yuchi Xiyue, and while he busies himself breaking through to the second cycle of the *Starseizing Hand* using the Peacock Plumes he bought at auction, an unexpected visitor arrives: the Five Loaths Immortal, his grandmaster from the Black-White College, who has traveled thousands of miles to hand-deliver a full package of secret manuals, techniques, and divine abilities. Meanwhile, lurking in the shadows, a desperate assassin is watching the gate and counting down the hours of his own life. Get ready for some heartwarming sect loyalty, a peek into the inner workings of the *Starseizing Hand*, and a quiet reminder that Ji Ning’s real support network runs deeper than any political alliance.

## Key Plot Points

1. **Cousin-Brother Bond**: Ji Ning reflects on the identity of Yuchi Xiyue as his cousin-sister, a secret so sensitive he hasn’t even shared it with his own spirit-beasts, mindful of the Yuchi clan’s past soul-scrying tragedy.
2. **Heartforce Over Divine Sense**: The yellow-furred bear explains that the Yan Prince used his Heartforce (a Celestial Immortal’s post-Tribulation ability) to sweep the manor but chose not to pry into Ji Ning’s immortal estate, earning the bear’s approval.
3. **Starseizing Hand Breakthrough**: Ji Ning completes the second cycle of the *Six Cycles of Starseizing Hand*, absorbing the Five Elements essence from the Peacock Plumes. His hands now rival Immortal-ranked treasures in power.
4. **The Assassin’s Clock**: Floating Cloud Immortal, bound by a Heavenly Dao oath to kill Ji Ning within three days, anxiously waits outside the manor, realizing his target isn’t coming out anytime soon.
5. **Grandmaster’s Gift**: Five Loaths Immortal arrives from Anchan Commandery with a treasure trove of cultivation manuals—the complete *Water Element Source*, the third volume of *Crimsonbright Diagram*, and a library of divine abilities and secret arts—a farewell gesture from the Black-White College before the assembly.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Heartforce (心识) vs. Divine Sense (神识)**: This is a major Xianxia power-tier mechanic. Before the Heavenly Tribulation, a cultivator’s soul is still ‘fragmented’—it can even be split to create a second Primaltwin. After surviving the Tribulation, the soul and the Primal merge into one, unlocking Heartforce, which is described as a power that can “vaguely touch upon the mysterious forces of fate.” Think of it as an upgrade from radar to precognition.
- **Starseizing Hand (摘星手) & The ‘Hand as Weapon’ Philosophy**: One of the top ten divine abilities since Pangu, created by Daoist Threelives. The second cycle is a massive leap because it turns the user’s hands into something qualitatively different—they now rival Immortal-ranked treasures. The lore note here is that Daoist Threelives never used weapons; his hands *were* the weapon. Ji Ning still uses a sword, which the system treats as normal but is technically a deviation from the original style.
- **The Black-White College’s Gift System**: Normally, disciples earn techniques through ‘Black-White Pearls’ (merit points), a deliberate system to temper them. The fact that Five Loaths Immortal is simply handing everything over is a massive exception—it signals that the college views Ji Ning’s situation (the Shaoyin clan threat, the approaching Immortal Fortunes Assembly) as serious enough to bypass the usual rules. It’s a quiet but powerful display of sect loyalty.
- **Cousin-brother (表弟) in Chinese Kinship**: In Chinese family hierarchy, there is a critical distinction between paternal cousins (堂兄弟/姐妹, *tang* siblings) and maternal cousins (表兄弟/姐妹, *biao* siblings). The ‘biao’ prefix here marks Ji Ning’s relation to Yuchi Xiyue through his mother, Yuchi Snow. This isn’t just naming trivia—it reinforces the deep, blood-bound loyalty between them, distinct from the more political ties of a paternal clan relationship.

A large lake in the Yan Mountains; Ji Ning’s secluded home where his parents’ ashes were scattered.

## Reading Guide

This is one of those deceptively quiet chapters where nothing “happens” plot-wise but the *story engine* is being quietly refueled. The Starseizing Hand breakthrough is a huge power spike—Ji Ning’s hands are now Immortal-tier weapons—and the arrival of the Black-White College’s full manual library is essentially an XP-pack delivery right before the big tournament arc. The real tension here is supplied by Floating Cloud Immortal, who is literally watching the clock tick down on his own life while Ji Ning blissfully cultivates indoors. It’s a darkly comedic contrast that Xianxia does beautifully: the protagonist’s quiet growth moment is someone else’s doom timer. If you love scenes where a mentor figure shows up with a “we believe in you” care package, this chapter will hit you right in the feels.
