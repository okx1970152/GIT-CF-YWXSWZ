## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists—the war arc is officially here, and it kicks off with a spectacle that would make even the ancient Fiendgods nod in approval. The Great Xia has finally arrived at the gates of the Seamless City, and the Xia Emperor wastes no time. Without informing even his own eight generals, he has already deployed the Tonghe Army as a Xingtian divine spirit—a thousand-zhang-tall, headless colossus modeled after the legendary True God of War—to serve as the vanguard and provoke the first true clash between the two powers. From the walls of the Seamless City, the White Extreme True Immortal and his gathered Empyrean Gods watch this titan stride across a thousand-kilometer lake. They respond by deploying their own ancient formation-beast: the White-Faced Jiao, a凶兽 born with the strength of an Empyrean God. Two mythic entities, one headless and one serpent-tailed, face off in a primordial lake. This chapter is all setup—a deliberate, breath-held moment of atmospheric tension before the storm. The Xia Emperor watches from his command hall, a master strategist who sees ten moves ahead. Ji Ning watches too, humbled by the scale, realizing his own power is still that of a single soldier, not yet a general commanding armies. It’s a quiet but overwhelming reminder of just how vast the chessboard of the Three Realms truly is.

## Key Plot Points

- The Great Xia’s main force has arrived at White Extreme Commandery. The Xia Emperor secretly sealed off a million-kilometer zone around White Extreme City with a grand formation, blocking all Greater Teleportation and mental perception.
- Without informing even his eight generals (Ji Ning included), the Xia Emperor dispatched the Tonghe Celestial Immortal’s army, transformed into the Xingtian divine spirit, to serve as the vanguard and test the Seamless Gate’s strength.
- The Xingtian divine spirit is a headless, ten-thousand-zhang-tall Fiendgod formed by a war formation. It runs barefoot across the wilderness, eventually stopping in a vast lake ten thousand kilometers from the Seamless City.
- On the Seamless City walls, the White Extreme True Immortal, the Foxwarden of Azure Fox, and other Empyrean Gods and True Immortals witness the Xingtian spirit’s approach. They decide to counter with their own formation-beast: the White-Faced Jiao.
- The White-Faced Jiao, a ten-thousand-zhang-tall ancient凶兽 with a white human face, black scales, and a three-thousand-zhang-long dragon-serpent tail, charges into the lake to confront the Xingtian spirit.
- The chapter ends with the two colossal beings locked in a tense standoff. The White-Faced Jiao initiates the attack with a lightning-fast lunge, claws extended toward the Xingtian spirit’s abdomen. The Xingtian spirit responds by raising its arm-blade, channeling the legendary war-god’s own battle stance.
- The Xia Emperor’s hall provides key context: the Xingtian formation is fueled by a thousand Celestial Immortals and a hundred thousand Loose Immortals. The East Eternal True Immortal and Tiannong True Immortal comment that the scene feels like a return to the primordial age.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Xingtian (刑天): The Beheaded God of War** — This chapter throws us headfirst into one of the most badass figures in Chinese mythology: Xingtian. In the ancient classic *Shanhaijing* (Classic of Mountains and Seas), Xingtian was a powerful Fiendgod who fought the supreme deity, the Yellow Emperor (黄帝). After losing his head in battle, Xingtian did not die. Instead, he grew nipples as eyes and a navel as a mouth, and continued to brandish his shield and axe in undying defiance. That’s the template for the Xingtian Grand Formation used here. The formation’s headless, chest-faced appearance isn’t just shock value—it’s a direct homage to this primordial rebel who literally would not stay down. In Desolate Era, the Xia Emperor’s use of this formation is a deliberate flex: it’s not just a powerful weapon, it’s a symbol of unyielding, heaven-defying war spirit.

**Ancient凶兽 vs. Divine Beasts** — The term 凶兽 (xiōngshòu) is distinct from 神兽 (shénshòu, divine beast). Both are types of ancient Fiendgods that took animal forms. The key difference is temperament: divine beasts were powerful but not inherently evil, while凶兽 were creatures of pure, savage bloodlust. The White-Faced Jiao referenced here falls firmly into the latter category—born with the combat power of an Empyrean God and a reputation for relentless savagery. Deploying it as a formation-beast is the Seamless Gate’s way of saying, “We have our own ancient horrors.”

**The Qi of Fortune (气运)** — Though not explicitly mentioned in this chapter’s action, the concept of ‘Qi of Fortune’ underpins the entire war. In Xianxia, a realm’s 气运 is its accumulated destiny and luck. Losing it makes the realm vulnerable to conquest. The Great Xia’s invasion of the Seamless Gate isn’t just about territory—it’s about breaking the enemy’s fortune. This war is as much a metaphysical contest as a physical one.

## Reading Guide

Welcome to the beginning of the endgame. If you’ve been following for the grind, the upgrades, and the one-on-one duels, this chapter is your extended blink-and-you’ll-miss-it transition into something much bigger. The power scale has officially shifted from “peak genius” to “war engine.” Ji Ning, our invincible sword-wielding protagonist, spends this chapter sitting in a chair watching a giant headless god and a giant snake-lizard monster prepare to throw down. That’s not a demotion—it’s a reality check. The world of the Three Realms is huge, and commanding armies of thousands of immortals is a different game from executing a perfect sword stance.

The tone here is deliberately slow and atmospheric. The Xia Emperor moves in silence, making tactical decisions behind a veil of secrecy. The gathering on the Seamless City walls is tense, pragmatic, and analytical. Even the battle itself hasn’t truly started—this is the moment of equipoise before the first blow. Treat this chapter like the deep breath before a storm surge. It’s a masterclass in establishing scale: the lake is a thousand kilometers wide, but it only comes up to the Xingtian spirit’s calves. The space between the two titans is hundreds of kilometers, but the first lunge covers it in an instant. Every number here is designed to make you feel small and awed.

Get ready. The next phase of *Desolate Era* is about to redefine what “epic” means.
