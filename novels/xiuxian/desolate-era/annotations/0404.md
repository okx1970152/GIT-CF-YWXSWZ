## Chapter Overview

Ji Ning says a tender goodbye to his cousin, Commandery Princess Xiyue, and makes a beeline for his hometown of Yan Mountain. His plan? To turn the entire ten-thousand-li region into a fortress that even Celestial Immortals would struggle to breach. While he and Uncle White tirelessly layer formation upon formation—transforming Yan Mountain into a death-trap for uninvited guests—the Bloodcloud Hall’s assassins watch from the shadows, grinding their teeth in frustration. But just as the first of the Three Great Lethal Formations clicks into place, news arrives that shatters the calm: Ji Ning’s master, the Diancai Immortal, has emerged from seclusion and intends to undergo his Heavenly Tribulation in nine days. The clock is ticking.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning declines his cousin Xiyue’s plea to stay longer in the Grand Xia capital, citing the growing chaos in the world and his need to fortify Yan Mountain.
- He hints at his own strength, claiming it is harder to kill him than a Celestial Immortal Patriarch, which leaves Xiyue skeptical but relieved.
- In Yan Mountain, Ji Ning convenes the clan’s leaders and announces his plan to layer multiple grand formations over their territory. The clan unanimously agrees, seeing it as a chance to establish a truly safe haven.
- Uncle White tirelessly sets up the complex formations over forty-nine days, starting with the core Mind-Mazing Eighteen Illusion Formation and then the first of the Three Great Lethal Formations.
- The Bloodcloud Hall attempts to scout Yan Mountain through insects, merchant caravans, and even possession arts, but all fail as the formations render the region an impenetrable labyrinth.
- The Bloodcloud Hall’s leaders conclude that assassination within Yan Mountain is impossible, and order a waiting game.
- The chapter closes with a bombshell: the Immortal Wufeng arrives to announce that the Diancai Immortal will undergo his tribulation in nine days.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The ‘Fortress Formation’ Strategy – A Classic Xianxia Trope
In the world of Xianxia, a sect or clan’s defensive formations are not just a luxury—they are the bedrock of survival. The concept of layering multiple grand formations over a single territory, each reinforcing the other, is known as a ‘Fortress Formation’ (堡垒大阵). The older a clan is, the more layers they accumulate, making their home exponentially harder to breach. Ji Ning’s ambition to encircle Yan Mountain with the legendary Three Great Lethal Formations is a textbook move: turn your home base into a fortress that only beings with True Immortal or Empyrean God-level power can dare to challenge. In the Grand Xia world, where the apex of power is the Celestial Immortal Patriarch, such a fortress means near-absolute safety.

### What Are the Three Great Lethal Formations?
Ji Ning mentions these as the crown jewels of his defensive plan. Among them, the ‘Sealing’ type is particularly interesting: Sealing formations cannot be bypassed through cleverness or technique. You cannot find a hidden escape route or exploit a flaw. You must use pure, overwhelming force to smash through them. This is a classic Xianxia design principle—forcing an enemy to match raw power with raw power, which is exactly what makes a stronghold truly unassailable.

### The Bloodcloud Hall – A Persistent Shadow
The Bloodcloud Hall is a professional assassination organization that operates across the Grand Xia world. Their methods are chillingly systematic: they use everything from ordinary insects (linked to a cultivator’s heart-connection), to bewitching techniques, to full possession arts just to gather intelligence. Their inability to even confirm Ji Ning’s presence on Brightheart Island is a testament to the power of the formations Uncle White has set up. In Xianxia, an assassin’s guild that cannot find its target is a guild that has already lost the battle.

### Fortress Formation

The cumulative process of layering multiple grand formations over a single territory, each reinforcing the other, making it exponentially harder for intruders to breach. A classic sect-protection strategy in Xianxia where age equals power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in the ‘preparation arc’—the quiet, methodical breath before the storm. While Ji Ning layers his home with formations, the tension is palpable. The Bloodcloud Hall is watching, waiting, and adapting. The fact that they immediately pivot to a “waiting game” shows just how seriously they take Ji Ning. Meanwhile, the chapter’s final reveal—the Diancai Immortal’s impending tribulation—is a perfect cliffhanger. Tribulation arcs in Xianxia are always high-stakes, emotional rollercoasters. Ji Ning has just finished one kind of preparation; now he must brace for another. This is the calm before a very violent storm.
