## Chapter Overview
Our boy Ji Ning finally gets a taste of real big-city luxury—and we're not talking about a new sword or spirit beast. Beishan Baiwei takes him to the Wuyou Grotto, the most exclusive pleasure palace in Anchan City, where the decor alone could bankrupt a Celestial Immortal. But more importantly, the chapter sets Ji Ning's path forward: he settles on a sect to join, thanks to a timely encounter. Meanwhile, Meng Jun stews in his own sour grapes, reminding us that not everyone is rooting for our hero.

## Key Plot Points
1. **Meng Jun's bitter envy**: The three Meng siblings witness Ji Ning's honored reception. Meng Yan and Meng Xin are impressed; Meng Jun is furious, rationalizing his earlier cold-shoulder as justified and vowing to surpass Ji Ning someday.
2. **The Wuyou Grotto's jaw-dropping scale**: Ji Ning is guided through a thirty-li corridor inlaid with luminous pearls worth Earth-rank treasures. The main hall alone is several li across, decked out with deep-sea coral and magical decor like a scene from legend.
3. **Meeting Miss Ziyi**: A fox-tailed lady with natural charm tests Ji Ning's Dao-heart. He recovers quickly, earning her surprised respect. Beishan Baiwei teases her about it.
4. **The Celestial Immortal Hall**: Beishan Baiwei reveals that the grotto has super-exclusive zones for Celestial Immortals, hinting at the political weight of the true powers behind Anchan City.
5. **Sect selection resolved**: Ji Ning states his requirements (divine abilities, top-tier ki refining, sword specialization). Beishan Baiwei recommends the **Liettian Sword Sect**, one of the top three sects in the commandery, with at least three Celestial Immortals and tens of thousands of Zifu disciples.
6. **Chance meeting with Zhou Li**: A Wanxiang Adept of the Liettian Sword Sect happens to be at the grotto. Beishan Baiwei introduces Ji Ning, and Zhou Li confirms he can secure Ji Ning's entry for the twelfth-month recruitment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**"Xianxia High Society" — The Wuyou Grotto as a power symbol.** In many xianxia, the truly wealthy and powerful don't just hoard spirit stones—they build pleasure palaces that double as political neutral zones. The Wuyou Grotto is one such place: owned by three Celestial Fox Immortals and backed by the Marquis of Anchan's estate, it's designed to rival the Heavenly Treasures Mountain (the most famous trading hub across the Grand Xia). Even Celestial Immortals dare not cause trouble here. This reflects a recurring xianxia trope: the "safe zone" where all grudges are set aside, allowing cultivators from warring clans to network, scheme, and enjoy themselves within a fragile but absolute truce.

**The Liettian Sword Sect (裂天剑派).** The name literally means "Sword Sect That Splits the Heavens." Its founder, the Liettian Ancestor, was a Loose Immortal who lived for hundreds of thousands of years. The sect has a history of eighty million years (yes, 80,000,000—that's written into the lore), which is relatively young by Chaosworld standards but ancient by mortal reckoning. The Zhou clan, to which Zhou Li belongs, has deep roots in the sect, showing how xianxia sects often become entangled with noble families over time. Ji Ning's choice to join here is strategic: he needs the resources and protection of a massive organization while still having room to grow his own Dao.

## Reading Guide
This is a classic "scene-setting and networking" chapter that serious xianxia readers will appreciate. It's not about explosions or breakthroughs—it's about worldbuilding through luxury. The Wuyou Grotto serves as a stage to show you exactly how far above the Ji clan's meager prefecture the Anchan Commandery's elite truly live. Every luminous pearl, every fox-tailed server, every whispered mention of the Celestial Immortal Hall is a reminder: Ji Ning is still small fish in an ocean of monsters. But the setup here pays off—the Liettian Sword Sect connection is his ticket to the next major arc. Also, savor Meng Jun's seething jealousy; it's a delicious reminder that for every friend Ji Ning makes, he's also collecting haters.
