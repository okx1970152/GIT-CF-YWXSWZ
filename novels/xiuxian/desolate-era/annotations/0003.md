## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Ji Ning is reborn into the Ji Clan of the Western Prefecture in a strange new world. He quickly discovers that his parents are extraordinary cultivators, his father the renowned 'Drip Sword' Ji Yichuan and his mother the caring Yuchi Snow. The family keeps powerful beast companions: a snow-white hound named Uncle White and a colossal black serpent named Uncle Black, both capable of assuming human form. At the celebration of his birth, the silver-haired Prefectural Lord declares his intention to make Ji Ning the next Prefectural Lord. However, the viper-like elder Ji Lie vehemently opposes, citing Ji Ning's mediocre aptitude. He challenges Ji Yichuan to kill a hundred great demons to prove the child's worthiness, setting up the first major conflict for the infant's future.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning awakens as an infant, using his past-life experiences to assess his new surroundings and family.
- He learns that he was injured in the womb, which diminished his innate aptitude despite his mother's consumption of celestial treasures.
- The family has two sworn beasts: the white hound Uncle White, who once saved Ji Ning's life, and the black serpent Uncle Black, a mighty demon who can transform into a human.
- The Prefectural Lord (the silver-haired elder) announces his plan to make the newborn Ji Ning the next Prefectural Lord.
- The viper-like elder Ji Lie challenges this decision, arguing that a weak Prefectural Lord would harm the clan's prestige.
- Ji Yichuan angrily claims he will personally slay any great demon that threatens the clan, but Ji Lie counters by setting a nearly impossible goal: kill a hundred great demons.
- The Prefectural Lord defuses the situation, ordering a feast and dismissing the crowd, but the conflict over succession is set in motion.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces several core xianxia concepts. 'Cultivation' (修仙) is the path to power, and aptitude determines potential. A child's innate quality can be improved through spirit treasures taken by the mother. The family's 'sworn beasts' demonstrate the common practice of taming and forming blood brotherhoods with powerful demon beasts, who can take human form after reaching certain cultivation levels. The Prefectural Lord system reflects the clan-based feudal structure: the Western Prefecture governs many tribes, and the Lord must have enough combat strength to command respect. The 'Drip Sword' title indicates Ji Yichuan has reached a high realm of sword mastery, beyond the Unity of Man and Heaven stage. The great demons (大妖) mentioned are Xiantian-level or above beast cultivators, formidable opponents.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to Ji Ning's rational, observant nature as he adapts to this brutal world. His father's cold exterior begins to show cracks of emotion. The challenge of killing a hundred great demons may seem impossible now, but it foreshadows Ji Ning's eventual rise in power. Note the political dynamics within the clan—the conflict between the elder generation (Prefectural Lord and Ji Yichuan) and the conservative faction led by Ji Lie. The beast companions Uncle White and Uncle Black will become important allies. This chapter establishes the foundation of family loyalty and the constant threat from demon beasts in the wilderness, setting the stage for the training that lies ahead.

## The Whitewater Hound and the Serpent Godbeast {#uncle-white-black}

Uncle White is a Whitewater Hound, a type of godbeast. In this world, demonic beasts can reach high cultivation levels and take human form, forming sworn relationships with cultivators. Uncle Black is an ancient serpent whose true body stretches hundreds of meters. Their loyalty to Ji Yichuan shows his reputation and strength. These beast companions will play important roles in Ji Ning's upbringing.

## The Drip Sword {#drip-sword}

Drip Sword is the honored title of Ji Yichuan, earned through his mastery of the Drip Sword Art (滴水经), a supreme technique surpassing the Unity of Man and Heaven realm. The name suggests his sword strikes are as precise and relentless as dripping water, capable of penetrating any defense. Ji Yichuan is one of the most feared fighters in the Western Prefecture, a reputation that casts a long shadow over Ji Ning's childhood.
