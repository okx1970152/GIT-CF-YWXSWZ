## Chapter Overview

The hunt for Ji Ning intensifies as the Seamless Gate’s top operatives gather in frustration—until a golden piece of intel falls into their laps: Ji Ning’s Primaltwin is about to undergo the Heavenly Tribulation. This chapter sets up the perfect storm for an ambush, as everyone from the Wuxiao Celestial Immortal to the Poison Ugly Immortal schemes to strike during Ji Ning’s most vulnerable moment. But Ji Ning isn’t sitting idle; he boldly walks into the Xia Emperor’s palace and requests the ultimate protection: a Daofather’s Dao-field. This is classic Xianxia tension-building—the hunter and the hunted both preparing for a clash that could reshape the Grand Xia.

## Key Plot Points

- The Azurefox Sect Master, Wuxiao, and Withered Grass Immortal lament their inability to kill Ji Ning while he’s under the Xia Emperor’s protection.
- Wuxiao’s rage over Purplegrass’s death is palpable, but even he can’t justify a full-scale assault.
- Breaking news arrives: Ji Ning’s Primaltwin will undergo its tribulation in nine days. This is the window they’ve been waiting for—during the Heart Demon Tribulation, Ji Ning’s true body will also be incapacitated, making him a sitting duck.
- The Seamless Gate camp rapidly assembles a strike team: avatars, doppelgangers, Hell Death Warriors, a golem, and the terrifying Yin-Yang Mysterious Qi Bottle.
- Meanwhile, Ji Ning seeks audience with the Xia Emperor, fully aware that his tribulation site is likely compromised. He asks for protection.
- The Xia Emperor reveals his trump card: he will take Ji Ning to his master Daofather Crimsonbright’s Dao-field, or failing that, to his sworn brother Daofather Yinglong.
- Ji Ning is both thrilled and wistful—his own master, the mysterious Subhuti, never makes things this easy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Heart Demon Tribulation (心魔劫) & Shared Memory Vulnerability**  
In Xianxia, the Heart Demon Tribulation is the final and most dangerous phase of crossing to immortality. It plunges the cultivator into an illusory world of their own inner demons, and **the true body and Primaltwin share memories and consciousness**. This means if one body enters the heart demon world, the other body automatically follows—making the cultivator completely defenseless in reality. This is a classic plot device used to create a vulnerability window for enemies.

**Avatars, Doppelgangers, and True Bodies**  
This chapter showcases how high-level immortals risk their lives: they send avatars (化身) or doppelgangers (分身) that carry only a fraction of their power (10% or so). If destroyed, the true body is unharmed. The exception is the Poison Ugly Immortal, who has no single true body—each clone is a “true body,” making him incredibly hard to kill. This contrasts with the Hell Death Warriors and golems used by the Bloodcloud Tower and the Ten Thousand Demons Cave, which are expendable assets.

**Daofather Subhuti’s Secrecy vs. Daofather Crimsonbright’s Open Rule**  
Patriarch Subhuti is the most mysterious Daofather in the Three Realms—his Tristar Crescent Major World is hidden from all other major powers, and he never summons his disciples unless he chooses. This makes him an elusive protector. In contrast, Daofather Crimsonbright openly rules dozens of major worlds and can be petitioned through his disciples like the Xia Emperor. The chapter highlights the different styles of “backing” in Xianxia: one offers ultimate secrecy but no control, the other offers accessible shelter but exposes your location.

## Reading Guide

This is a classic “calm before the storm” chapter where both sides finalize their battle plans. Pay attention to the tactical details of the ambush—especially the Yin-Yang Mysterious Qi Bottle, which was once used to nearly kill an Empyrean God of the Heavenly Court. The fact that Ji Ning is willing to undergo a Heavenly Tribulation outside Subhuti’s protection shows his growing trust in the Xia Emperor, and the emperor’s willingness to invoke two Daofathers as backup shows just how high Ji Ning’s stock has risen. The stage is set for a high-stakes tribulation ambush—and the involvement of multiple immortal-tier foes means this will not be a simple test of cultivation, but a battlefield of schemes and treasures.
