## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia misdirection and high-stakes psychological warfare. Hot off the heels of a stunning battlefield victory, the Northsea Legion celebrates — but the real action is in the shadows. The Godking summons both the Green Fox and Yu Wei for a tense interrogation, trying to gauge the true nature of Ji Ning’s sudden power spike. Meanwhile, Ji Ning maintains a masterful poker face, concealing his ascension to Pure Yang True Immortal behind a believable lie about a Heartforce breakthrough. The Godking, confident in his ability to read Yu Wei’s soul, falls for it completely. Enraged and threatened, he decides to escalate: a full-scale Realm War against the Grand Xia, with Ji Ning’s elimination as a primary objective. The board is set for an explosive confrontation.

## Key Plot Points

- The Green Fox kneels before the Godking in the dark world, reporting the defeat and blaming Ji Ning’s overwhelming Xingtian Formation.
- The Godking reveals his displeasure with Baiji, who failed despite receiving a Chaos Daofruit.
- In the barracks, the rank-and-file Celestial Immortals and Loose Immortals are ecstatic about the recent victory, praising Ji Ning’s leadership.
- Ji Ning returns to Yu Wei and shares a meal, disclosing that his “Heartforce Soul Method” has broken through — a deliberate lie to hide his true advancement to Pure Yang True Immortal.
- Yu Wei is forcibly summoned by the Godking, who probes her for the truth. Through careful analysis of Yu Wei’s reactions, the Godking concludes that Ji Ning indeed broke through in Heartforce, not in cultivation realm.
- The Godking explains why Heartforce Soul Method is even more alarming than a Pure Yang breakthrough: it is exceedingly rare (less than a handful of beings in the Three Realms have mastered it) and allows Ji Ning to control the Xingtian Formation with ten to twenty times more power.
- Realizing Ji Ning is a growing threat to the Seamless Gate’s chances in the Qi of fortune contest, the Godking resolves to trigger a Realm War on the Grand Xia, bringing the might of the Fifth World to bear and targeting Ji Ning for assassination.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Heartforce (心力) and Heartforce Soul Method** – Heartforce is the rarest and most esoteric of cultivation powers in Desolate Era, derived from a cultivator’s absolute sincerity and Dao-heart rather than energy or divine power. Mastering a Heartforce Soul Method is considered far more difficult than reaching Pure Yang True Immortal, and those who do are game-changers on the battlefield. Ji Ning’s false claim is strategically brilliant: the Godking knows how hard Heartforce breakthroughs are, and thus finds the lie more plausible than the truth.

**Xingtian Formation (刑天阵)** – A massive war formation named after the mythical god Xingtian, a headless warrior who fought against Heaven. In the novel, it requires 100,000 Loose Immortals as a foundation and 1,000 Celestial Immortals as a framework, manifesting a temporary divine spirit of god-tier durability. Ji Ning’s ability to lead this formation effectively magnifies his personal power manifold.

**Qi of Fortune (气运)** – A core Xianxia metaphysical concept. Every realm and faction accumulates Qi of fortune — a sort of cosmic luck that influences the outcome of battles, the emergence of geniuses, and even the weather of fate. Both the Nuwa camp and the Seamless Gate are fighting proxy wars through their Empyrean Gods and True Immortals to steal each other’s Qi of fortune, building advantages before the inevitable full-scale war.

**Realm War (界域战争)** – Not just a battle, but a campaign that spans an entire major world and draws in the majority of Empyrean Gods and True Immortals from an entire realm. Gods and Immortals die in droves. The Godking’s decision to escalate to this level signals a major turning point in the conflict.

## Reading Guide

Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up — the stakes just got nuclear. This chapter isn’t about flashy sword slashes or explosive formations; it’s a quiet, tense standoff where a single lie could tip the cosmic scales. Ji Ning’s decision to hide his Pure Yang True Immortal status is a brilliant piece of psychological warfare, and watching the Godking piece together a wrong conclusion from perfect evidence is both satisfying and terrifying.

Pay close attention to the Godking’s thought process: he’s not stupid. He’s ancient, cunning, and paranoid. Yet Ji Ning’s perfected Dao-heart and Yu Wei’s absolute trust create a perfect deception. The Godking’s decision to start a Realm War is a direct result of his miscalculation — he thinks he’s nipping a Heartforce monster in the bud, when in reality he’s about to face a Pure Yang Sword Immortal who’s been holding back.

Also, note the beautiful domestic moment before the storm: Ji Ning and Yu Wei sharing a meal, her pregnant belly, his rare levity. It’s a calm before the chaos that makes the coming war feel all the more personal. If you love Xianxia that mixes cold strategy with warm character moments, this chapter delivers.
