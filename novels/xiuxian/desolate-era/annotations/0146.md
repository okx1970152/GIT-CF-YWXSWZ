## Chapter Overview
The Treasure-Gathering Assembly is in full swing, and Ji Ning is getting his first taste of how the big spenders of Anchan City operate. What starts as a casual observation of bidding wars quickly turns personal when Beishan Baiwei puts Ji Ning's simplified Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation up for auction. The bidding escalates into a tense showdown, culminating in a surprise 360-tael bid from a legendary figure: the Ferocious Fire Child, a reincarnated Immortal and child prodigy of formations. Ji Ning walks away with a fat stack of Primordial Liquid tokens and a front-row seat to the furious vendetta between Beishan Baiwei and Beishan Hu.

## Key Plot Points
- The Wandering Cavern's Treasure-Gathering Assembly opens with a Flamefire Stone, showing the cynical profit-seeking of merchants like Hun Wuqi who bid purely for resale value.
- Several treasures come and go, giving the reader a tour of the hierarchy in Anchan City: Yinglong Guards, Meng clan prodigies, and the Ferocious Fire Child are all introduced or mentioned.
- Beishan Hu's Human-grade top-quality Qushan Seal sells for a modest 110 taels, which visibly disappoints him and fuels his jealousy.
- Beishan Baiwei then unveils Ji Ning's simplified Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation. Beishan Hu objects publicly, but the Wandering Cavern backs the item's authenticity by offering 200 taels themselves.
- The Ferocious Fire Child, a reincarnated Immortal and genius formation master, swoops in with an outrageous bid of 360 taels of Primordial Liquid, stealing the spotlight.
- After the auction, Beishan Baiwei hands Ji Ning the 360-tael payoff in standardized 'Heavenly Treasures Mountain' tokens and explains their use as the universal hard currency of the Grand Xia.
- Ji Ning's internal monologue shifts to strategizing about flying swords, as he plans to use his Primordial Liquid to upgrade his Lesser Thousand Sword Formation with high-quality, same-source swords.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Primordial Liquid (元液) as Hard Currency**  
In the infinite worlds of Xianxia, cultivation resources often bypass the law of supply and demand. Primordial Liquid is the ultimate energy concentrate: so pure and dense that a cultivator can absorb it instantly without the bottleneck of refining natural energy manually. In this novel's economy, it functions exactly as gold does on Earth, but with the added prestige of being usable by any serious practitioner. The "tokens" issued by the Grand Xia's Heavenly Treasures Mountain are the standard bearer notes of the cultivation world—backed by the dynasty's immortal power.

**The Ferocious Fire Child: A Reincarnated Immortal**  
The archetype of a "reincarnated Immortal" is a staple of Xianxia lore. It explains why a six-year-old boy can casually outbid a room full of Wanxiang Adepts and be hailed as a peerless formation master. The idea is that his soul has retained knowledge and insight from a previous lifetime—an elegant narrative device to justify a child prodigy without breaking internal logic. It also creates a deep sense of mystery: what kind of monstrous being was he before he died?

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia economics. If you've ever wondered how an interstellar-level cultivation society manages currency and trade, this is your answer. The Treasure-Gathering Assembly isn't just about loot—it's a social battlefield where wealth and face are measured in taels of Primordial Liquid. Pay attention to the contrast between Beishan Baiwei's humble request (owning the formation for a year) and the explosive payoff. Also, the Ferocious Fire Child's entrance is a reminder that in this world, age and appearance mean absolutely nothing. If a toddler outbids you, you don't laugh—you get nervous.
