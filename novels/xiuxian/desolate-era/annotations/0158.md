## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, the trials are over, and Ji Ning has officially punched his ticket into the Black-White College—one of the most exclusive clubs in the entire Grand Xia! But this chapter isn't just about the celebration. It's that sweet, sweet moment where hard work pays off, where the monster genius gets his due recognition from the Primal Daoist elders, and where we see the quiet, human side of a cultivator who just wants to make his parents proud. We also get some solid bro-time with Mu Northson and a very useful favor from Beishan Baiwei. Get ready for the calm before the storm of real cultivation begins.

## Key Plot Points
- The six Primal Daoists who oversaw the trials discuss Ji Ning's performance with open admiration, noting his Domain of the Dao and his control of over seven hundred flying swords, which forced Yinxue Wenjian to retreat in shame.
- Ji Ning and Mu Northson wait at the mountaintop. Mu Northson barely passes his trial and arrives full of joy. The two share a moment of mutual congratulations.
- The Primal Daoists officially announce that Ji Ning and Mu Northson are now Black-White College disciples and hand them their disciple tokens, which they immediately refine, gaining the college's protective aura.
- Ji Ning chooses a personal peak, naming it Darknorth Peak after his swords and as a memorial to his parents. He then flies back to the main entrance to meet Uncle White.
- Beishan Baiwei is waiting there. He congratulates Ji Ning and vents about the famous geniuses who failed, revealing his own pride in having backed the right horse.
- Beishan Baiwei asks to borrow three of Ji Ning's ten retainer slots. Ji Ning generously offers nine, keeping only one—which he intends for Meng Yan, the self-sacrificing warrior he met on the road.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Retainer Slots (随从名额)**: In sects like the Black-White College, formal disciples are granted a number of retainer slots—essentially a backdoor entry for friends, family, or talented followers to live and study within the sect's grounds. Even though retainers aren't official disciples, they can listen to lectures and access some training resources, which is a massive opportunity in a top-tier sect. This system is a classic Xianxia trope: the protagonist's rise pulls others up with him. Nine retainers is an enormous favor Beishan Baiwei is asking, and Ji Ning's willingness to part with them shows both his gratitude and his confidence that his own path doesn't depend on hoarding such perks.

**Darknorth Peak (北冥峰)**: Ji Ning names his personal mountain after his sword set (the Northsea Swords) and the place where his parents met and fell in love. The term "北冥" (Darknorth/Northsea) originates from the Zhuangzi, where "Northsea" (冥海) is the primordial ocean in the far north, home to the legendary Kun peng (a giant fish-bird). Naming his peak this is Ji Ning's way of declaring his ambition to cultivate something legendary—while also keeping a piece of his parents' story alive in his daily life.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a classic "settling in" breather after the intensity of the trials. Savour the elders' praise—it's a clear signal that the college recognizes Ji Ning as a future Immortal-tier asset. But pay attention to the names of the failed geniuses: Hua Chong (who also practiced the Crimsonbright Diagram), Liushui Lian, etc. Their presence reminds us that Ji Ning's competition at this level is no joke, even if they failed. The real gem is the retainer slots: nine out of ten given away! That's not just generosity; it's a strategic move to build political capital with Beishan Baiwei. And that one slot Ji Ning keeps? He's thinking of Meng Yan—a character we'll want to remember. The quiet moment at Darknorth Peak where Ji Ning thinks of his parents is a poignant reminder that even a rising monster genius has a heart, and his drive is deeply personal.

## Deep Dive: The Dao Domain Threshold
One detail the elders marvel at is Ji Ning's "Domain of the Dao" (道之域境). In the cultivation world of Desolate Era, anyone who can manifest a Domain of the Dao at the Zifu stage is considered a reincarnated exalted one or a monster genius. Why? Because normally, only at the Primal Daoist level do cultivators have enough soul strength and Dao comprehension to project their understanding as a persistent field that suppresses enemies and enhances their own techniques. Ji Ning doing this at early Zifu is like a high schooler acing a PhD-level exam. This feat alone justifies the elders' hype and explains why Yinxue Wenjian (a peak Zifu with his own sword domain) felt humiliated.
