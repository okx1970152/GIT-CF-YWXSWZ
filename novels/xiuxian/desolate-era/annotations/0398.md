## Chapter Overview
Welcome back to the Grand Xia, fellow Daoist! Our boy Ji Ning is finally home at the Black-White College, and he’s rolling in loot—specifically, a mountain of Daoist scriptures taken from the demon kings and evil patriarchs he obliterated during his time at Mount Innerheart. The chapter opens with a touching master-disciple wine session where Ji Ning gifts these priceless texts to the Diancai Immortal, hoping to strengthen his sect against the gathering storm. But the chapter’s real gut punch comes when the Diancai Immortal reveals: the Grand Xia is in chaos, assassination attempts are rampant within Anchan City itself, and worst of all—Mu Northson, Ji Ning’s ever-cheerful junior brother, vanished over two decades ago. The stage is set for a new, darker arc.

## Key Plot Points
1. **A Master’s Pride**: The Diancai Immortal openly admires Ji Ning’s growth, noting his divine sense has surpassed his own. Ji Ning responds with humble banter before turning serious about his master’s imminent Heavenly Tribulation.
2. **Providential Timing**: The Diancai Immortal reveals that several Earth Immortals have recently become Celestial Immortals, and he senses a monstrous calamity on the horizon. He must渡劫 now or risk perishing in the coming storm.
3. **The Gift of Scriptures**: Ji Ning unleashes a mountain of Daoist scriptures—including Pure Yang-level techniques, sword arts, and secret arts—obtained from slain demon kings. The collection is a hundred times richer than the Black-White College’s own library, sending the sect’s immortals into a frenzy of excitement.
4. **Wufeng Immortal’s Hope**: The oldest Loose Immortal of the College joyfully realizes the new scriptures might extend his lifespan by ten thousand years, a touching moment of shared fortune.
5. **The State of the Realm**: In a nighttime walk, the Diancai Immortal drops heavy exposition: over a hundred assassinations have occurred inside Anchan City in the last 20–30 years, and the Blood God Church has grown powerful enough to stalemate the ruling Anchan Northmont clan.
6. **The Disappearance**: The most chilling news: Mu Northson and his Dao-companion vanished over twenty years ago, with no trace. Ji Ning’s homecoming is now shadowed by urgency and grief.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **纯阳法门 (Pure Yang-level technique)**: In the novel’s cultivation hierarchy, techniques are ranked. Pure Yang is a step above ordinary Immortal-level techniques, providing a far superior foundation for a Ki Refiner. The Black-White College’s excitement over acquiring these is immense—it’s like a small town library suddenly receiving rare, ancient manuscripts.
- **冥冥感应 (A foreboding premonition from冥冥)**: This is a classic trope in Xianxia. High-level cultivators have a spiritual connection to the cosmic flow of fate. They can sense imminent calamity or fortune, though the details are always blurred. The Diancai Immortal’s feeling that he *must*渡劫 now or die is a serious narrative flag—it tells the reader the storm is real.
- **Black-White College’s Hierarchy**: The college has over ten Loose Immortals and Earth Immortals (返虚地仙/散仙). These are cultivators who have reached the peak of the mortal realm but failed or delayed their Heavenly Tribulation. They are immense powerhouses, but their lifespan is limited by the Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations.
- **Anchan City’s Laws**: The Grand Xia Dynasty strictly forbids killing within its cities. For over a hundred assassination attempts to happen *inside* Anchan City signals a complete breakdown of dynastic authority. This is the Xianxia equivalent of gang wars erupting in the heart of a superpower’s capital.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is classic “after the time-skip” setup. It serves as Ji Ning’s re-integration into his old world, reminding us of the stakes back home while his own power has soared. Pay close attention to the juxtaposition: Ji Ning effortlessly gifting scriptures that cause his masters to erupt in joy, while simultaneously learning that the friend he cares about most has disappeared. The Diancai Immortal’s warning—*don’t charge headfirst*—is clearly going to be the exact advice Ji Ning ignores. And the Blood God Church being able to stalemate a *marquis*? That’s a massive red flag. Some truly sinister power is backing them, and Mu Northson’s vanishing may well be tied to it.
