## Chapter Overview

Get your popcorn ready, fellow Daoists—the cage is open and the tigers are loose! Chapter 94 marks the explosive opening salvo of the Ji Clan's desperate gambit against the invading cultivators of Snowdragon Mountain. Patriarch Ji Jiuhuo reveals the masterstroke he's been hiding: a pre-laid grand formation that completely reverses the power dynamic. What was a panicked defense becomes a lethal hunting ground, with the mist serving as both a shroud and a guiding hand. The chapter splits our attention between the Ji Clan's assassins—our young protagonist Ji Ning among them, preparing his devastating Lesser Thousand Sword Formation—and the enemy camp, where Dong Zilao and Mu Si desperately try to counterplay with their own formations and vines. It's a tense, tactical dance where every breath could be the last, and the outcome is far from certain.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Patriarch’s Revelation**: Ji Jiuhuo admits he set up the grand formation long before the Snowdragon cultivators even arrived. He deliberately kept it secret from his own clan to maintain the authenticity of their outrage, a cunning move born from hard experience.
2. **The Kill Order**: The clan's strongest forces are split. Ji Jiuhuo, Granny Shadow, Old Servant Axing, and Ji Ning are tasked with eliminating the He Fang and He Xing siblings. Ji Liuzhen and Ji Yichuan are sent after Ju San. Dong Zilao and Mu Si are to be dealt with last.
3. **Dong Zilao’s Counter**: Realizing he's been outmaneuvered, Dong Zilao deploys his own Eight Trigram Blood Dragon Formation, summoning eight fierce blood-dragon phantoms. He orders Mu Si to use his Woodvine Epoch Art to probe the limits of the confounding formation.
4. **The Ambush Begins**: The Ji Clan’s four assassins approach the He Fang siblings. Ji Ning compresses the power of over seven hundred flying swords into a single, breathtakingly sharp sword-light—the ninth layer of the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation. The chapter ends just as this attack pierces He Xing’s shattered golden light.
5. **The Siege’s Logic**: Ji Jiuhuo makes the grim calculus clear: every enemy they kill reduces the pressure on their clan. Their only hope is to survive long enough to sign a contract with the Grand Xia Dynasty.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Art of Formation Warfare**: This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia tactical combat. Formations (大阵) are not just defensive shells; they are dynamic, multi-layered tools of misdirection and division. Ji Jiuhuo uses a *confounding formation* (迷阵) to isolate enemy units, creating localized numerical advantages—the classic “divide and conquer.” Dong Zilao's *Eight Trigram Blood Dragon Formation* (八卦血龙阵) is a perfect counterpoint: a self-contained domain that can envelop allies and provide defense. The clash is less about brute force and more about who can control the battlefield's geometry.

**The Desperate Art of "Probing"**: Mu Si’s Woodvine Epoch Art is a classic example of a "probing technique." In high-level Xianxia combat, when you can't see your enemy, the first move is always to gather information. Sending out tendrils of ki, growing vines, or releasing scouts to map the terrain is a fundamental skill. It's a delicate balance: you must expend energy to find a way out, but every bit of ki you burn is fuel you can't use for the final fight.

**Delegated Awe in Action**: Though Grand Xia has yet to physically appear, Ji Jiuhuo's mention of it serves as a powerful example of "delegated awe." The mere existence of this dynasty—and its ability to enforce a millennium of peace through a contract—is a testament to its unimaginable power. The Ji Clan isn't fighting to conquer; they are fighting for the right to buy time under the protection of a greater power.

## Reading Guide

This is one of those chapters where the strategic tension is more gripping than any single clash. Watch how the author uses the **omniscient view** to build suspense. We know Ji Jiuhuo's plan, we see the assassins approach, and we also see Dong Zilao's counter. The question isn't "will they attack?" but "who will crack first under pressure?" Pay close attention to how the factions are using their unique resources: the Ji Clan uses the discipline of their formation and the raw power of their strongest members (Ji Ning's sword formation is a terrifying flex). Snowdragon Mountain relies on the versatility of their techniques (poison, vines, blood dragons). This chapter sets the tempo for a multi-layered boss fight where roles can reverse in an instant. Get ready for the next chapter; the first blood is about to be drawn.
