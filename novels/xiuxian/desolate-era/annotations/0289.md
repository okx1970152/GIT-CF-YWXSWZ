## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists, because the aquatic palace’s grumpy yellow bear artifact spirit just dropped the biggest lore bomb of the arc: he single-handedly reforged three Immortal treasures over *three hundred and sixty million years*. We’re talking a complete meltdown of the Nineshield Seal, a radical deconstruction of the Su River Scroll, and an epic-grade fusion into the Thousandbull Sword that turns it into a sword-diagram-wielding monster. If that wasn’t enough, Ji Ning cracks the code on Spacetime Shifting—the spatial teleportation skill that marks a true Dao master—and then, like a phantom, returns to a ruined Brightheart Island before vanishing on a new journey. It’s a chapter of power-ups, emotional farewells, and the quiet weight of time itself.

## Key Plot Points

- The yellow-furred bear reveals he spent eons reforging Celestial Immortal Juhua’s three immortal treasures into one supreme weapon: the Thousandbull Sword, now a top-grade Immortal treasure with an integrated Su River Sword Diagram.  
- The Thousandbull Sword can move autonomously, a rare trait that nearly bridges the gap to Pure Yang treasures; Ji Ning’s Primaltwin can just barely suppress it.  
- The black ox inside the sword grieves for his first master Juhua; Ji Ning offers the platitude of reincarnation but understands the emptiness of it after billions of years.  
- Ji Ning trains for over a year and finally masters Spacetime Shifting—the foundational spatial technique of the Grand Dao of Qiankun (Space Dao).  
- The yellow bear warns Ji Ning that walled-in cultivation won’t make him a Celestial Immortal; he needs life-or-death tempering to break through his stagnant avenues of Fire and Wind.  
- Ji Ning returns to Brightheart Island to find it completely destroyed by the Shaoyan clan; he murmurs a quiet vow to rebuild and vanishes into the snowy sky, mirroring his departure from Yan Mountains years ago.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Insane Scale of Immortal Refining** – Three hundred and sixty million years isn’t just a big number; it’s a deliberate flex. In Xianxia cosmology, time is the ultimate currency, and a Celestial Immortal’s lifespan is limited. The fact that an artifact spirit could spend *thirty-six million centuries* on a single fusion project without any master to charge it is a testament to just how ancient and bizarre the aquatic palace’s treasures are. This isn’t forging—it’s geological ages of patience.  

**Sword-Diagrams (剑图)** – A sword-diagram is a metaphysical field that amplifies a sword’s power once drawn. Think of it less like an equipment enchantment and more like downloading a domain-expansion patch for your blade. In the Three Realms, any self-respecting Pure Yang Immortal Sword comes with one; having a sword without a diagram is like carrying a lightsaber with no plasma. The fact that Thousandbull Sword now has one elevates it from “nice find” to “legendary inheritance.”  

**Spacetime Shifting vs. Void Shifting** – The text distinguishes between *虚空挪移* (Void Shifting, a spatial teleport) and *虚空穿梭* (Void Shuttle, the godbeast natural talent). For godbeasts like the Skygreen Serpent, it’s instinct; for human cultivators, it’s the fruit of mastering a full Dao and achieving control over a region of Heaven and Earth. Ji Ning, still a mere Zifu-level Fiendgod Refiner, pulling this off is a sign that his Dao comprehension has genuinely reached the immortal level—even if his cultivation base hasn’t yet caught up.  

**The Daos of Rainwater, Fire, and Wind** – This arc parallels the Daoist concept of “Wu Xing” (Five Phases). But IET’s system is tri-elemental here. Rainwater (Water) is fully comprehended; Raging Fire and Gale Wind are stuck. The yellow bear’s advice—that only outdoor tempering (生死磨砺) can break the bottleneck—is pure Xianxia logic: insight doesn’t come from a quiet meditation hall; it comes from nearly dying in a storm of blades.

### Life-and-death tempering (生死磨砺)

A cultivation method where one deliberately places themselves in mortal danger to force rapid insight and mental breakthrough. A classic Xianxia training philosophy that contrasts with quiet seclusion.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a brilliant *calm before the inevitable storm*. If you’re here purely for action, you’re probably twitching right now. But don’t be fooled: the reforging of the Thousandbull Sword isn’t just a power-up handout—it’s IET setting up Ji Ning’s next-tier arsenal before an arc that will almost certainly test every new trick he’s learned. The Spacetime Shifting mastery is a subtle but massive tactical upgrade; Ji Ning can now fight like a Loose Immortal in mobility terms. And that final, quiet return to snow-blanketed Brightheart Island? It’s a literary echo of his first journey to Anchan City, closing one loop and opening another. The next time winter comes—the Immortal Fortune Conference will be here—and Ji Ning will be the one crashing the party.
