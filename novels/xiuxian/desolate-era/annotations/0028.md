## Chapter Overview

Ji Ning leaves the familiar walls of Western Prefecture City behind, heading south toward Eastmarsh Swamp—the first real frontier beyond his sheltered upbringing. But before he even reaches its legendary, danger-choked waters, fate throws a smaller trial in his path. A hunting party from a tiny tribe called Ironstone gets ambushed by a Huan Beast, a terrifying predator at the peak of Houtian perfection. Ji Ning, with one flawless arrow from afar, saves their lives and gains a local guide: Dala Shu, a weathered fisherman who knows every bog and beast-lair of the Swamp. This chapter serves as a quiet but crucial transition—a final threshold before the real adventure begins.

## Key Plot Points
- **Leaving home**: Ji Ning, now eleven years old and growing restless, departs Western Prefecture with only his maid Qiuye and the retainer Mo Wu, riding modest blackjaguars. The scene evokes a sense of finality as he looks back at the city.
- **The Ironstone Tribe's return trip**: A band of tribesmen, made up of both experienced hunters and a young novice named Ayi, makes its way home from a successful fishing expedition. They are jubilant but wary.
- **Huan Beast ambush**: A Huan Beast—a sleek, black-furred predator known for its stiff spiked mane—pounces on the exposed perimeter. Despite the tribesmen's coordinated spear defense, the beast overpowers them easily, revealing its Houtian-perfect cultivation.
- **Ji Ning's intervention**: From the forest, Ji Ning looses a single arrow that pierces the Huan Beast's skull, passes through a thick tree, and kills it instantly. The tribesmen recognize the shot as the work of a divine archer.
- **Recruiting a guide**: Ji Ning offers a generous piece of Beast-Head Gold to Dala Shu for guiding him into Eastmarsh Swamp. Dala, a burly veteran of decades, accepts after handing the gold to his one-armed companion for his family.
- **The one-armed man's analysis**: After Ji Ning departs, the one-armed man explains to the boy Ayi that Ji Ning is likely a chieftain's son on a coming-of-age trial, requiring a local guide because the Swamp's dangers cannot be navigated by maps alone.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Houtian perfection & beast hierarchy**: The Huan Beast is described as having reached "Houtian perfection"—the peak of the mortal cultivation stage. In the xianxia power structure, Houtian is still far below Xiantian (the threshold of true power), but for a small tribe without cultivators, a beast at this level is a death sentence. Note how the tribesmen's raw skill and internal energy cannot compensate for lacking a proper immortal-demon heritage.
- **Beast-Head Gold**: The gold ingot Ji Ning tosses is a standard unit of currency in tribal society (each piece exactly 10 jin). The "beast-head" stamp signifies its official weight and value. In a small tribe, this single piece can secure a family for generations—showing both Ning's wealth and his genuine gratitude.
- **Blackjaguar vs. tamed demon beast**: Although blackjaguars are fierce, they are ordinary fierce beasts, not demon beasts (which possess cultivation). The text subtly underlines Ji Ning's choice to remain low-key: as the heir of the Western Prefecture, he could easily ride a flying demon mount, but he chooses a ground-level, more humble approach to his first journey.
- **Eastmarsh Swamp's significance**: This is Ji Ning's rite-of-passage hunting ground. In many xianxia stories, such places serve as a crucible where the protagonist proves his mettle by hunting demonic beasts, collecting materials, and sometimes encountering ancient relics or hidden masters. The locals' fear and reverence for the swamp foreshadow the dangers ahead.

## Reading Guide
**Fellow Daoists and new readers alike**, this is one of those "calm before the storm" chapters that every cultivation epic needs. It's short, efficient, and does three things perfectly: it shows Ji Ning stepping out of his father's shadow (the Western Prefecture feels too small now), introduces the "ordinary people's" perspective on the supernatural dangers of this world, and sets up the meat of the next arc—Eastmarsh Swamp itself. The hunt is about to begin, and the stakes are about to go from perfect accuracy with a bow to real, down-in-the-mud combat. **Get ready for beast fights, ancient grudges, and a lesson in why Fiendgod Body Refining is called "unfair."** In the meantime, take a moment to appreciate that single arrow shot. It's a reminder that Ji Ning is no ordinary eleven-year-old—and that he's about to grow up *fast*.
