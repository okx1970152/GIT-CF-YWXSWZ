## Chapter Overview
Ji Ning has just broken through to the Xiantian realm via the legendary Crimsonbright Diagram, and the results are nothing short of spectacular. In this action-packed chapter, he puts his newfound water-and-fire mastery to the test—and absolutely demolishes a mid-stage Xiantian Huan Beast with a single, beautifully executed technique: the Water-Fire Lotus. The battle showcases the terrifying gap between a Crimsonbright Fiendgod and an ordinary cultivator. But when Tie Muzhan, the cold-blooded Xiantian hunter, springs his Yin-Yang Dual Qi Formation, Ji Ning reveals he’s not just an offensive powerhouse—his defense is equally unbreakable. Tie Muzhan is forced to burn two precious Dao talismans just to escape, swearing vengeance. The chapter closes with a promise of escalating conflict: the Tiemu Clan and Snowdragon Mountain will soon learn of Ji Ning’s monstrous talent.

## Key Plot Points
- **Instant Kill**: Ji Ning’s Water-Fire Lotus grinds the Huan Beast into oblivion in less than a breath. The beast, a Xiantian (mid) great demon, has zero chance to fight back.
- **Formation vs. Lotus**: Tie Muzhan activates his Yin-Yang Dual Qi Formation, but Ji Ning simply closes his lotus bud around himself. The formation’s black and white streams can’t breach the rotating water-fire defense—a clear sign Ji Ning’s comprehension already surpasses mortal formations.
- **Dual Control**: While defending, Ji Ning also manifests a second Water-Fire Lotus directly on Tie Muzhan. The hunter barely survives by using his formation’s energy and personal true essence.
- **Escape via Talismans**: Terrified, Tie Muzhan burns an Agility Talisman and a Lightness Talisman—both valuable Dao talismans—to outrun Ji Ning. He leaves his formation behind to save his life.
- **The Threat Looms**: Tie Muzhan vows to report Ji Ning’s existence to his clan’s Zifu ancestor and to Snowdragon Mountain, setting the stage for a larger war.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Crimsonbright Dominance**: This chapter is a textbook demonstration of why the Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens is considered the #1 Fiendgod Body Refining method in the Three Realms. A Xiantian (early) cultivator trained in it can annihilate a Xiantian (mid) great demon effortlessly and match a Xiantian (perfect) opponent in defense. The gap between ordinary cultivation methods and top-tier divine techniques is vast.
- **Water-Fire Lotus**: This technique embodies a “True Meaning of the Dao”—a partial, instinctive grasp of a universal law. It’s not a spell that requires chanting; it’s a manifestation of the cultivator’s enlightenment. The lotus grinds with the natural power of complementary opposites (water and fire) generating wind, a classic Daoist principle.
- **Dao Talismans**: Talismans like the Agility and Lightness varieties are pre-inscribed mystical items that can be activated with true essence to grant temporary buffs. They’re far less rare than the legendary Ten-Thousand-Mile Trace-Away Talisman (which teleports instantly over 10,000 li), but still costly. In a Xianxia setting, talismans are consumable trump cards.
- **Yan Mountains Power Structure**: The six overlords of the Yan Mountains all have Zifu Disciple ancestors—this is the minimum requirement to be recognized as a vassal of the Grand Xia Dynasty. The Tiemu Clan and Ji Clan are rivals, and Snowdragon Mountain is an even bigger power backing the Tiemu.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, fellow Daoists, because Ji Ning has officially graduated from “talented kid” to “existential threat.” This chapter is pure catharsis: watch a mid-tier Xiantian demon get turned into grilled snacks, then watch a cunning hunter turn into a fleeing rabbit. The Water-Fire Lotus is gorgeous—a technique that feels less like a spell and more like a law of nature bending to Ji Ning’s will. But don’t relax too much. Tie Muzhan escaped, and he’s running straight to the old monsters. The Zifu Ancestors of the Tiemu Clan and Snowdragon Mountain are about to hear that a boy barely twelve years old wields power that rivals their own Xiantian elites. The next storm is brewing, and Ji Ning’s name is about to become very, very infamous.
