## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in the quieter side of Xianxia—the emotional weight that hangs between battles. We get a heartfelt confession from Nine Lotos to Luo Qing about her feelings for Ji Ning, the quiet departure of Luo Qing, and a massive power spike for our protagonist as he breaks through to the tenth stage of the Crimsonbright Diagram. The chapter closes with a classic Xianxia cliffhanger: a jilted admirer, Chen Jin, is about to learn that the object of his long-standing affection has chosen a Dao-companion who is decidedly *not* him. The storm is coming.

## Key Plot Points
- Luo Qing is astounded to learn that Nine Lotos has developed genuine romantic interest in Ji Ning, a departure from her usual composure and selective nature.
- Nine Lotos calmly defends her choice, stating that a person's individual potential can surpass their clan's power, and that choosing a Dao-companion follows the heart.
- Luo Qing, respecting Nine Lotos's decision and feeling useless as a third wheel, decides to leave Serpentwing Lake to explore on her own.
- Ji Ning, completely unaware of the romantic storm brewing around him, achieves a major breakthrough in his Fiendgod Body Refining, advancing to the tenth stage of the Crimsonbright Diagram. This elevates his physical strength to rival a late Wanxiang-stage Fiendgod Body Refiner.
- With his new power, Ji Ning visits the Treasure Hall to select another powerful artifact, greatly enhancing his overall combat capability without anyone's knowledge.
- Back at the Black-White College, Luo Qing delivers the news to the lovestruck Chen Jin that Nine Lotos has chosen a Dao-companion, shattering his world.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Dao-companion (道侣):** This term goes far beyond a simple "lover" or "spouse." It implies a profound, lifelong partnership on the path of cultivation. The goal isn't just romance but mutual support in comprehending the Dao, surviving tribulations, and reaching the peak of power. Choosing one is a monumental decision that affects one's entire eternal life, which is why Nine Lotos stresses she needs more observation time.

**The Power Level of Silence:** This chapter brilliantly showcases a core Xianxia trope: the staggering disconnect between romantic and political intrigue and the protagonist's personal power progression. While an emotional dilemma unfolds in one sphere, Ji Ning is silently skyrocketing in power in another. This sets up a delicious conflict where the "weak tribal boy" others dismiss is already a force to be reckoned with, capable of contending with a Primal Daoist.

**The Starseizing Hand:** Mentioned in Ji Ning's internal thoughts, this is a top-ten divine ability from the primordial era. It treats the cultivator's hands as ultimate weapons to be refined, making them far tougher than even their Fiendgod body. It's a key part of Ji Ning's overpowered arsenal.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for some fireworks, folks. This chapter plants the seeds for an inevitable confrontation. Chen Jin isn't just some random background character; he's a determined pursuer from a powerful background who's just had his entire worldview flipped upside down. The fact that Nine Lotos chose Ji Ning, a "nobody" from a small tribe in the eyes of high society, is a massive insult to someone like Chen Jin. While Ji Ning is merrily breaking through realms and collecting treasures, an enemy is being forged in the fires of romantic jealousy. The question isn't *if* these two will clash, but just how badly Ji Ning will humiliate him when they do. Keep your eyes peeled for the fallout.
