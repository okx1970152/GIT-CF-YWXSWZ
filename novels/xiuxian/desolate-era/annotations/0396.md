This chapter is a narrative pause, used by the author to set expectations and build hype. The author confesses he needs time to carefully plan out all the plot threads—their foreshadowing, buildup, climaxes, and interweaving—to ensure a satisfying and coherent epic.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This kind of author's note is a classic tradition in Chinese web novel culture, especially for series published on platforms like Qidian. It's a direct line of communication between the author (often called by their pen name, like "Tomato") and the "Daoist"-addressed readers. Unlike Western authors who might use a blog or a social media post, Chinese web novelists frequently insert these meta-chapters into their release schedule. It’s a sign of respect, showing the author values quality over simply pumping out daily word counts. When a powerhouse writer asks for an "extra day off to prepare," it's usually a signal that the next story arc is going to be a legendary one—a "Daomerge" of plotlines, if you will.
