## Chapter Overview

The chapter shifts perspective from the Ji Clan's tense preparations to the enemy's camp. We see the Snowdragon Mountain Zifu Disciples gathering in Snowdragon City, a collection of cultivators united by greed and arrogance. The spotlight falls on Tongyu, a brutish Fiendgod Body Refiner whose strength rivals a Wanxiang Adept, and Nong Zidao, a renowned formation expert. Their arrival creates a formidable front. The chapter masterfully builds anticipation by contrasting the casual, overconfident revelry of the Snowdragon cultivators with the Ji Clan’s grim, well-drilled readiness. The calm before the storm is thick enough to cut with a sword. The enemy is coming, and they are not messing around.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Gathering at Snowdragon City:** Dongzi Qi successfully assembles a large force of Zifu Disciples, all eager for a share of the merit from crushing the Ji Clan. The atmosphere is one of casual arrogance.
*   **Tongyu's Dominance:** Tongyu, a powerful Fiendgod Body Refiner, establishes himself as the de facto leader. His mere presence (his face glowing with golden light, his Earth Dragon mount) inspires awe and a desire for flattery among the other cultivators.
*   **The Messenger and the Declaration of War:** The Ji Clan sends a mortal messenger to Snowdragon City, publicly declaring their formation on Oxhorn Mountain and challenging Snowdragon Mountain to come and break it. Tongyu forbids killing the messenger, opting to maintain the sect's "dignity" by crushing the Ji Clan with overwhelming force.
*   **Arrival of Nong Zidao:** The formation master Nong Zidao arrives on a white crane. He is treated with respect second only to Tongyu, recognized for his talent and the power of his Nong clan background. His arrival solidifies the attack plan.
*   **The March for Merit:** With nineteen Zifu Disciples (and two more combat-ready beasts), Tongyu and Nong Zidao decide to march on Oxhorn Mountain immediately. Their motivation is to secure the primary share of the merit for themselves, leaving latecomers with nothing.
*   **The Ji Clan's Grim Resolve:** Back at Oxhorn Mountain, the Ji Clan has finished their drills. They are confident. Ji Jiuhuo boasts that Snowdragon Mountain will lose dozens of cultivators before they break the formation. Ji Ning offers the remaining Primordial Liquid to the clan as a strategic resource.
*   **The Enemy is Sighted:** The chapter ends with the enemy force appearing on the northern horizon, a party of cultivators drinking and feasting as they fly towards the Ji Clan’s prepared battlefield.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Politics of Merit (功劳, Gōngláo):** In the Xianxia world, power isn't just about strength; it's also about resources. The "merit" from missions like this is a major currency for progression. The Snowdragon Mountain cultivators are not just good friends out for a fight; they are competing for a slice of a pie. Their decision to march early is a cold, calculated move to steal the biggest share from their own allies. This internal greed and lack of true unity is a classic narrative weakness, often exploited by a more desperate and unified opponent. The protagonist's clan, by contrast, is pooling its only treasure out of necessity, not selfishness.

**The "Delegated Awe" (代位敬畏):** This chapter is a masterclass in a classic Xianxia narrative device. Instead of showing us how powerful Tongyu is, the author shows us the *reaction* of other cultivators to him. The long-bearded elder, the silver-haired cultivator, and Dongzi Qi all behave with a mixture of fear, flattery, and reverence. Their awed whispers and eagerness to please are far more effective than a dry description of his battle prowess. You don't need to see the dragon burn a village to know its fire is hot; just watching the villagers quake in fear is enough. It builds immense, palpable dread for the coming confrontation.

**Primordial Liquid (元液) as a "Last Bullet":** Ji Ning offering the last of his Primordial Liquid to the clan is a powerful moment. Primordial Liquid is the ultimate essence of natural energy, bypassing the need for slow refinement. It's a cheat code for a quick burst of power or restoration. In a Xianxia world where battles are decided by who runs out of Qi first, having a vial of this is like having a hidden ace. Ji Ning giving it up shows his maturity, acknowledging that his personal breakthrough is secondary to the clan's survival in this immediate, life-or-death struggle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is the calm before the storm. Enjoy the delicious irony of the Snowdragon Mountain cultivators, who treat this invasion like a boozy picnic, completely unaware that they are flying into a meticulously prepared deathtrap. The tension is built perfectly—we know the Ji Clan has found a powerful formation manual, and the enemy’s arrogance is a perfect setup for a brutal comeuppance. The introduction of Nong Zidao adds a real twist; the Ji Clan’s formation is meant to be a headache for ordinary cultivators, but now a specialist is coming. This sets up a "breaker vs. maker" dynamic that raises the strategic stakes. Pay attention to the subtle power dynamics within the Snowdragon group—the way everyone falls in line behind Tongyu and Nong Zidao. This forced hierarchy is a brittle thing, and it will be interesting to see how it holds up under the pressure of a battle that is sure to be far more brutal than their little picnic suggests.
