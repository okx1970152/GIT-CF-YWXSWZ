## Chapter Overview
Ji Ning gets the full Black-White College treatment — a mountain of divine abilities and secret arts delivered by the Wufeng Immortal himself. But this generous parting gift comes with a heavy emotional toll: his master has gone wandering to temper himself, feeling helpless in the face of Ji Ning’s war with the Shaoyan clan. Meanwhile, an assassin has run out of patience. Floating Cloud Immortal is sweating bullets under a three-day Heavenly Oath, watching Ji Ning stroll around with fifty Golden Armored Imperial Guards. The chapter ends with a major political signal — the Zhuanshui clan, sworn enemies of the Shaoyan, extending an invitation.

## Key Plot Points
- Wufeng Immortal delivers nearly every top-tier divine ability and secret art from the Black-White College that suits Ji Ning: *Aqua Source Manual*, *Crimsonbright Diagram* (vol.3), *Heavenly Transformation*, *Thunder God Eye*, *Ten Thousand Venoms*, *Three Heads, Six Arms*, *Blazing Heart Eye*, *Soul-Shattering Art*, *Soul-Confusing Art*, and *Soul-Annihilating Art*. Ji Ning memorizes them all by dawn, burning each copy after reading.
- Emotional beat: Ji Ning feels a profound sense of belonging to the Black-White College, vowing to carry their legacy into the Immortal Fortunes Assembly.
- The yellow-furred bear gives practical advice on which divine abilities to prioritize, warning against the *Ten Thousand Venoms* as a pointless impurity for a Fiendgod Body Refiner. He also reveals that *Thunder God Eye* is a foundation for a top-100 divine ability of the Three Realms — the *God of Thunder Eye*.
- Yuchi Xiyue summons Ji Ning to hand him an invitation from the Zhuanshui clan, the second most powerful clan in the Grand Xia Dynasty and the mortal enemy of the Shaoyan. She shares hidden history: the Zhuanshui clan once claimed the title of Emperor during the Primordial Fiendgod Era.
- Ji Ning heads to the banquet with Whitewater Hound and Little Qing, escorted by fifty Golden Armored Guards — a sight that sends Floating Cloud Immortal into an absolute panic as his three-day deadline ticks down.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**“Read one, burn one” — Memory as a Cultivation Asset.** The Wufeng Immortal handing Ji Ning a stack of manuals and having him burn each one after memorization is classic Xianxia resource transmission. For cultivators, once a technique is in your soul, the paper is just a leak risk. This also mirrors real-world Chinese martial arts and secret society lore, where oral transmission and immediate destruction of physical copies prevented rival schools from stealing techniques.

**Divine Abilities vs Secret Arts.** The chapter draws a clear line between these two categories. Divine abilities (*Three Heads, Six Arms*, *Heavenly Transformation*) are universal combat transformations — almost every Fiendgod in the Three Realms knows them. Secret arts (*Soul-Shattering Art*, *Soul-Confusing Art*) are rarer techniques that bypass conventional defenses (soul attacks), often requiring specialized cultivation. Ji Ning is getting a dual toolkit: brute-force upgrades for the body and subtle weapons for the mind.

**The Zhuanshui Clan and the “Emperor” Title.** Yuchi Xiyue drops a fascinating piece of secret history: during the Primordial Fiendgod Era, the Zhuanshui clan once claimed the title of “Emperor” and fought the Xiamang (the current imperial clan) for supremacy. This is more than ancient lore — it explains why they are the only clan in the Grand Xia that can openly defy the Shaoyan and survive. In a Xianxia dynasty, the past never truly dies; it becomes political capital.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a transitional chapter that’s heavier on setup than action, but don’t sleep on the details. The *Thunder God Eye* / *God of Thunder Eye* pipeline is a major hint — Ji Ning is being set up to eventually wield a top-100 divine ability of the Three Realms. The *Ten Thousand Venoms* warning from the bear is a classic “more isn’t always better” moment in progression fantasy, reminding readers that cultivation involves trade-offs. And the Zhuanshui invitation? That’s the game actually starting. Ji Ning is no longer a lone swordsman from the wildlands; he has become a named piece on the Grand Xia political board. Also, poor Floating Cloud Immortal — he might be the most stressed Loose Immortal in the entire capital right now.
