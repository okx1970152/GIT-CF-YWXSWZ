## Chapter Overview
Holy moly, fellow Daoists—we’ve got a double-whammy of Cosmic Milestones in this chapter! Ji Ning *accidentally* unlocks a brand-new ability called **Sword Force** before he’s even fully mastered the Grand Dao of the Sword—yes, you read that right. Patriarch Subhuti was right to keep this a secret; it’s the kind of broken talent that makes other Sword Immortals cry into their flasks. Meanwhile, the three Mother Queens of the Broken Horn World hit their limit: eighteen years of watching Ji Ning fight, thousands of battles analyzed, and they *still* can’t crack the code of Heartforce. So they decide to stop being patient observers and start being active hunters. The Golden Cape General—the one Ji Ning has always feared—finally steps into the ring. And guess who’s watching from the shadows of the Starseizing Manor? The seven Fiendgods, ready to burn down the entire First Army. It’s a powder keg, and the match has just been lit.

## Key Plot Points
- **Sword Force Awakening**: While training in the featureless, Dao-devoid Realm of Annihilation, Ji Ning discovers a silver radiance at his fingertip. The yellow-furred bear explains it’s ‘Sword Force’—a power normally only accessible after mastering the complete Grand Dao of the Sword. Ji Ning, being Ji Ning, unlocks it anyway.
- **Cultivation Terminology Lesson**: The bear fills in the lore gap: Sword Force is akin to Taiji Force or other ‘essence-derived’ powers gained from full Dao mastery. Patriarch Subhuti intentionally withheld this to keep Ji Ning from reaching for the stars—but the kid reached anyway.
- **Heartforce Mastery Frustrates Mother Queens**: After eighteen years and tens of thousands of battles, Ji Ning’s Heartforce control becomes perfect—zero leakage. The three Mother Queens have *still* failed to fully deduce the secret of Heartforce. Only the Third Mother Queen has condensed it, but can’t use it yet. They decide capture is the only option.
- **The Golden Cape General Steps Up**: The ultimate trump card of the First Army finally acts. The General, an Empyrean God-equivalent, dispatches Ji Ning effortlessly with four black whips, binding him completely. Ji Ning’s resistance is useless.
- **The Seven Fiendgods Prepare to Engage**: Inside the Starseizing Manor, the yellow-furred bear reports Ji Ning’s capture. The red-haired Empyrean God gives the order: kill every single member of the First Army. The gloves are off.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Sword Force (剑之力)**: This is a premium Xianxia concept—a power that emerges *after* fully mastering a Grand Dao and then digging deeper into its essential nature. Think of it as the ‘executive bonus’ of the cultivation system: you’ve already reached the peak, and then you earn a passive ability that makes your sword sharper, your Taiji more balanced, or your flame more intense. Normally, you only get this *after* completing the Dao. Ji Ning getting a sliver *before* is like a high schooler acing a PhD qualifying exam.

**Heartforce Leakage & Deduction**: In Xianxia, observing a cultivator’s energy fluctuations is a legitimate way to reverse-engineer their techniques. The Mother Queens have been using Ji Ning as a living, fighting lecture hall for eighteen years. Once his Heartforce control became ‘perfect’ (no leakage), the lecture ended—they can’t learn from silence. This is why truly powerful cultivators guard their energy signatures like state secrets.

**The Starseizing Manor’s Dilemma**: Daoist Threelives’ instructions were ruthlessly pragmatic: if a successor dies, let him die and pick another. But the seven trapped Fiendgods can’t pick another successor while stuck in the Realm of Annihilation. Ji Ning is their *only* horse in the race. This moral asterisk is the only reason they’re willing to fight. Cold, hard logic behind loyalty.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for the most satisfying 'the cavalry has arrived' moment in this whole arc. The seven Fiendgods aren't just backup—they're *ancient*. Each one is a Demigod-level powerhouse trapped for eons, itching for a fight. When they say ‘kill every last member of the First Army,’ it’s not a threat; it’s a delivery notice. The chapter crackles with the tension of a stalemate finally breaking. Key things to savor: Ji Ning’s accidental Sword Force is a *huge* cheat code—this will pay dividends later when he’s back in a world with real Daos. Also, note the Mother Queens’ desperation: they can’t crack Heartforce, and it’s driving them to burn their best card, the General. This is the beginning of the endgame for the Broken Horn World arc. Buckle up.
