## Chapter Overview
Our boy Ji Ning is back, and he’s just made a *killer* equipment upgrade. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia resource management: the agony of choice in a treasure hall, the payoff of long-term thinking, and the sudden arrival of social complications in the form of two very different senior apprentice-sisters. From the cold, calculated joy of forging a new sword formation to the warm, slightly awkward reunion above a snow-covered lake, this chapter balances internal cultivation decisions with external plot hooks. Get ready for a deep dive into what makes a top-tier Earth-ranked treasure set tick—and a hint of the romantic tensions simmering beneath the surface of the Black-White College’s elite circle.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Final Choice:** Ji Ning agonizes over three top-grade Earth-ranked treasures before settling on the Coldblade Sword Formation, a nine-sword set that will immediately supercharge his Lesser Thousand Sword Formation, even though it means passing up the longer-term utility of the Earthfire Heartlamp and Cold-Sha Mirror.
- **The Bear’s Wisdom:** The yellow-furred bear artifact spirit confirms that even better treasures await in later War God Hall floors, reinforcing the classic Xianxia dynamic of delayed gratification and the understanding that a single master cannot hoard all the wealth of an ancient immortal.
- **Reluctant Acquisition:** Ji Ning mentally calculates the set's worth at over 100,000 jin of Primordial Liquid—a sum that would make even Primal Daoists sweat—and realizes his good fortune in obtaining them. He recognizes that the formation’s power scales directly with the quality of its swords, a fact he intends to exploit ruthlessly.
- **Out of Seclusion:** Ji Ning emerges to a snowy landscape and a slightly aggrieved spirit-beast, Qingqing, who complains she couldn't sense him while he was in the aquatic palace. The Whitewater Hound reveals he had to stop her from breaking in.
- **A Younger Generation Catching Up:** Ji Mo, the Ji clan’s second-best young talent, arrives seeking guidance. Ji Ning, now more settled, offers him an open invitation to train at Serpentwing Lake, signaling a maturation in his role as a clan protector.
- **Unexpected Guests:** A familiar voice rings across the lake as a flying sailboat approaches. It’s Nine Lotos and a silver-robed Qingqing from the Black-White College, arriving for a visit. Nine Lotos immediately has fun with the shared name ‘Qingqing’—a pointed joke aimed at Ji Ning’s serpentine spirit-beast.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Mathematician’s Choice: Earth-Ranked vs. Heaven-Ranked Treasures**
In the Xianxia economy, ‘rank’ (阶 jie) is not just a label; it’s a statement about who can use something. A Heaven-ranked (天阶 tianjie) treasure is *designed* for a Primal Daoist to wield, their limitless Yuan power acting as the ignition key. For a Wanxiang Adept, it’s a lump of dead metal. An Earth-ranked treasure, on the other hand, is the sweet spot for the Wanxiang stage—powerful enough to threaten everything short of a Primal, but usable *now*. Ji Ning’s top-grade Earth-ranked set being *stronger* than an ordinary Heaven-ranked flying sword is the Xianxia equivalent of finding a supercar engine that runs on regular gas.

**The Golden Formula of the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation (小千剑阵)**
The ‘Lesser Thousand’ in this formation’s name implies a scaled-down version of an unimaginably vast Thousand Sword Formation. Its entire philosophy is one of raw material supremacy. The formation’s complexity doesn’t change; its power is a direct, linear function of the flying swords’ quality. Giving it nine top-grade Earth-ranked swords is like plugging a budget PC into a supercomputer’s power supply. The bottleneck is no longer the formation’s design, but the limitlessness of Ji Ning’s soul force.

**The ‘Two Qingqings’ Joke**
In Chinese culture, duplicate names between people and their servants/pets are often a source of gentle humor. Nine Lotos, knowing that Ji Ning’s spirit-beast (the Skygreen Serpent) is called *Qingqing* / ‘Azure Green’, deliberately points out the coincidence with her friend, *also* named Qingqing / ‘Azure Green’. This is a classic friend’s tease—pointing out a trivial but amusing parallel to create a shared, memorable moment. It’s also a subtle social flex, showing Nine Lotos’s comfortable, bantering dynamic with her peer.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a breather episode that serves two essential narrative functions. First, it power-levels Ji Ning’s *primary* offensive tool—the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation—from a dangerous swarm of bees into a flight of hypersonic missiles. Keep an eye on the Coldblade swords in any upcoming fight; they’re now the engine of his devastating Karmic Sword Formation. Second, it performs a soft reset on his social environment. His clan—represented by the burgeoning talent Ji Mo—is stable. His comrades—represented by the surprise visit from Nine Lotos and Qingqing—are nearby and engaged. This calm before the storm sets the stage for whatever major quest or sect conflict is brewing around the corner. Pay attention to Nine Lotos’s demeanor; her playful teasing with the ‘Qingqing’ name hints at a causal closeness that goes beyond mere sect formality. The romantic subplot is simmering, and it’s about to get a lot more interesting.
