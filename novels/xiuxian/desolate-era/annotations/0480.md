## Chapter Overview
What do you do when you’ve spent eighteen years training to be practically invincible, only to be hogtied in a single move? If you’re Ji Ning, you get a front-row seat to a celestial beatdown of epic proportions. This chapter explodes from a moment of crushing personal defeat into a full-blown, jaw-dropping rescue mission. Just when the First Army of the Broken Horn World thinks they’ve bagged a prize, seven colossal Empyrean Gods of the Three Realms drop in like a meteor shower. The battle is less a fight and more a divine extermination—a swift, breathtaking display of raw power that leaves armies of horned warriors melting under divine light and a Sacred-grade general impaled in the blink of an eye. This is the moment the scope of the story changes forever.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning, despite his phenomenal growth and the combined power of his divine abilities, is effortlessly captured by the golden-caped general of the Broken Horn World’s First Army, who states Ning's power is "almost Sacred-grade."
- The general and his soldiers reveal their power hierarchy: ‘Heaven-grade’ is below ‘Sacred-grade’, the equivalent of an Empyrean God in the Three Realms.
- Without warning, seven powerful figures materialize around Ji Ning. Among them is the red-haired Empyrean God Redsnow, whom Ning recognizes.
- An enormous child (one of the seven) unleashes "Golden Blaze Divine Light," a sweeping gaze that instantly melts tens of thousands of horned warriors across a hundred-thousand-kilometer radius.
- An enchanting woman (also one of the seven) reveals her true form as a ten-thousand-kilometer-long white scorpion. Using her snowy eyebrows, she binds and shatters over six hundred of the elite violet-caped soldiers in an instant.
- The general, realizing his danger, abandons Ji Ning to attack the child, but his whip is blocked. It is the cold, devastating spear-thrust of Redsnow that pierces through spacetime itself to impale and kill the general instantly.
- Ji Ning realizes the crushing gap between himself and true Empyrean God-tier combatants.
- In the Broken Horn World’s Hall of Elders, the nine Elders panic at the power of these invaders, recognizing that even their strongest general was killed in one blow.
- The three Mother Queens of the Broken Horn World sense these alien Fiendgods and agree to personally intervene.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Sacred-grade (圣级) and the Power Hierarchy**: This chapter explicitly lays out the Broken Horn World’s power scaling. ‘Heaven-grade’ is the peak of their local Celestial Immortal equivalent, while ‘Sacred-grade’ maps directly onto the Empyrean God level of the Three Realms. The golden cape is a privilege of this tier. It’s a classic Xianxia trope to have a parallel power system in a foreign world, and the translator’s consistent use of these terms helps the reader instantly understand the comparative threat level. Ji Ning’s power is *almost* at the baseline of this tier, but as the general demonstrates, being at the baseline is still a world away from being a true veteran of that level.

**The Fiendgod Pantheon of a Primordial Attendant**: The sudden appearance of seven Empyrean Gods is a massive lore drop. These are not random powerful beings; they are described as top-tier followers of the legendary Daoist Threelives (the creator of the Starseizing Hand). The child with the neck ring and the white scorpion woman demonstrate exotic, potent divine abilities unique to ancient Fiendgods, contrasting with the more refined sword and spear arts of human cultivators. Their power isn't just higher; it's of a different, more ancient and primal quality.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a serious power spike—and a serious reality check. This chapter is the payoff for a long setup. For the last eighteen years, Ji Ning has been crushing everything in his path, and the readers have been riding that high. The author masterfully pulls the rug out from under you by having Ning captured instantly. It’s a humbling moment that sets the stage for the arrival of the absolute heavyweights. Watch how effortlessly these seven beings change the entire calculus of the battlefield. They are not just strong; they are walking calamities who treat an army of millions of elite soldiers as a minor inconvenience. The fight scene is paced perfectly: first the overwhelming, casual slaughter of the mooks, then the quick, brutal execution of the boss. It’s a classic "the cavalry has arrived" scene, but with the grim efficiency of ancient gods. Pay close attention to Redsnow—he’s the calm center of this storm, and his single thrust proves that some cultivators are on a completely different plane of existence.
