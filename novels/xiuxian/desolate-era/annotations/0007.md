## Chapter Overview

In this pivotal chapter, the four-year-old Ji Ning makes a life-altering choice among nineteen Fiendgod Body Refining techniques. After reading through several painful and dangerous methods, he fixes his gaze on the legendary Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens — recognized universally as the most powerful yet the most difficult method. Despite the Ji Clan's five prefectures never producing a single Xiantian lifeform through this technique, Ji Ning's unshakable Dao-heart, fortified by his Nuwa Diagram practice, leads him to choose it. The chapter closes with his first attempt at the Worship of the Sun and Moon, the entry posture.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning reviews the nineteen abridged manuals of Fiendgod Body Refining techniques. The first three he picks — Demon-Refining Tribulation Manuscript, Blood Demon Immortal Body, and Burning Sun Song — involve extreme physical torture.
- Yuchi Snow consoles Ji Ning, pointing out that only five of the nineteen are painful; the others are gentler.
- Ji Yichuan gives a cold but insightful lecture: Fiendgod Body Refining is inherently harder than Ki Refining, and methods that rely on agony for speed trade a less solid foundation for quick gains.
- Ji Ning discovers the Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens and learns that it is the indisputable number one, capable of reaching the Zifu stage (while most others stop at Xiantian).
- He also learns that this technique alone among the nineteen allows cultivation of great divine abilities like the Heavenly Transformation and Three Heads and Six Arms upon reaching Zifu.
- Ji Yichuan reveals that no one in the entire Ji Clan (five prefectures) has ever reached even Xiantian through this method, yet Ji Ning chooses it resolutely.
- Impressed by his son's firm Dao-heart, Ji Yichuan shows a rare smile and fetches the manual.
- Late that night, Ji Ning begins his first cultivation session under his parents' watchful eyes. He flawlessly performs the first diagram, Worship of the Sun and Moon, demonstrating extraordinary bodily control cultivated through years of the Nuwa Diagram. His parents hold their breath, hoping he can sense the two supreme stars.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In xianxia cosmology, cultivation paths are binary: Ki Refining (internal energy storage) and Fiendgod Body Refining (physical transcendence). The Crimsonbright Diagram is a classic "supreme but risky" technique — a staple trope where the strongest method demands the highest price. The concept of "Worship of the Sun and Moon" draws from ancient Chinese shamanistic and Daoist practices of paying homage to celestial bodies, believed to channel cosmic essence. The Taiyin (Moon) and Sunstar (Sun) are supreme stars in this universe, representing the balance of yin and yang. The Huang-Lao tradition holds that the sun and moon are the eyes of heaven and earth, and a cultivator who can connect with them gains access to primordial power. The blood-drop rebirth of later stages echoes the Daoist ideal of the indestructible golden body.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is not about action but about the philosophical and strategic foundation of Ji Ning's cultivation path. Readers should note the contrast between the painful quick methods and the slow, profound path of the Crimsonbright Diagram. Ji Yichuan's warning about difficulty versus his son's unwavering decision highlights Ji Ning's pragmatism and long-term vision. The physical perfection of the "Worship of the Sun and Moon" movement underscores the importance of solid roots: Ji Ning's early training with the Nuwa Diagram, a soul-forging visualization technique, gave him an edge. Pay attention to the suspenseful ending — whether Ji Ning succeeds in sensing the celestial stars will determine whether this bold choice bears fruit.

## The Crimsonbright Diagram {#crimsonbright-diagram}

The Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens is the apex of Fiendgod Body Refining in the Three Realms. Created by Daofather Crimsonbright, it operates by absorbing the primordial yin and yang forces from the Taiyin and Sunstar directly into the cultivator's body, causing divine runes to form naturally. No other method boasts such pure energy or the ability to reach the Zifu stage purely through body refinement. Later layers unlock legendary divine abilities such as the Heavenly Transformation (magnifying the body to cosmic scale) and Three Heads and Six Arms (doubling attack frequency). Its downside is extreme entry difficulty: only one in ten thousand can sense the two stars at all, and even fewer can maintain the connection long enough to form both runes. The technique has nine layers in total, but the Ji Clan's record shows that not a single member across its five prefectures has ever surpassed the third layer.

## Worship of the Sun and Moon {#worship-sun-moon}

The first diagram of the Crimsonbright Diagram is a ritualized movement called Worship of the Sun and Moon. The cultivator raises both hands high, then bows forward with a slow, deliberate motion, as if making an offering to the sun and moon. The posture mimics ancient sacrificial dances performed by shamans of the primordial era to commune with celestial forces. The goal is to align one's own internal life-soul resonance (fire produced by the heart, water gathered by the kidneys) with the external Taiyin and Sunstar. When performed correctly, the cultivator's body enters a state of extreme receptivity, allowing the fine essence of the two supreme stars to trickle in. Ji Ning's flawless execution on his first attempt is a testament to his powerful soul and the bodily control gained through years of the Nuwa Diagram — a foundation that boys raised without such training would lack.
