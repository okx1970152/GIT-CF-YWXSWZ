## Chapter Overview
The Immortal Fortunes Assembly rolls on with mechanical precision, and the first round concludes in a cascade of Immortal-ranked treasure awards—a sweet taste of victory for the top forty-eight. Ji Ning claims his prize and steps into the second round feeling confident, but he’s not the only one sharpening his edge. Among the lesser-knowns, a barefoot man with a crooked staff and a silent blade-wielding figure shatter everyone’s expectations. By the time the smoke clears, the assembly’s final pairing is set by none other than the ever-unpredictable Lu Dongbin: the Sword Immortal vs. the Blade Servant. Fellow Daoists, *get ready*: the stage is about to get violent.

## Key Plot Points
- **First round closes**: Ji Ning dispatches Zhuxiang of the Red Lotus Sect with overwhelming superiority, earning a low-grade Immortal-ranked flying sword from the Grand Xia Emperor. The other forty-seven winners also receive their treasures.
- **Emperor’s generosity**: The Grand Xia Emperor not only awards the treasures but personally restores everyone’s energy and heals their wounds with a falling rainbow shower.
- **Second round begins**: Xiangtian Xiao defeats Zhuanshui Gan with clear advantage, showcasing his formidable Saber Dao.
- **Blackstone Recluse’s eerie win**: The dark-skinned, barefoot Blackstone Recluse uses nothing but his Fuxi Staff Formation to completely lockdown Danzhu Daoist without ever entering close combat—a display of supreme formation mastery that sends a chill through the other geniuses.
- **Daonu Daoist’s shocking debut**: In the last match of the round, the nearly invisible Daonu Daoist chops Blood Demon Daoist in half with just two strikes, instantly marking himself as the top Saber Dao cultivator among the final twenty-four.
- **Lu Dongbin’s suggestion**: The mischievous True Immortal proposes pitting the strongest Sword Immortal (Ji Ning) against the strongest Saber Daoist (Daonu) for the next round, and the Grand Xia Emperor accepts.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Immortal-ranked treasures (仙阶法宝)**: These are graded artifacts that surpass mortal and earth ranks, bearing the “Immortal” prefix to indicate they were forged by or for True Immortals. At this Assembly, low-grade Immortal-ranked items are awarded to top-forty-eight finishers—a generous prize by sect standards, but “only” low-grade in the eyes of a carefree wanderer like Lu Dongbin. The hierarchy: Low-grade < Mid-grade < High-grade < Top-grade. A low-grade Immortal-ranked flying sword can easily fetch several million jin of Primordial Liquid.
- **Fuxi Staff Formation (伏羲棍阵)**: Named after the legendary sage Fuxi (伏羲), a primordial deity credited with inventing the Eight Trigrams and civilization. In Desolate Era, “Fuxi Staffs” are a set of portable formation stakes that allow a formation master to instantly deploy a complex array without pre-arranged terrain. Blackstone Recluse’s mastery of this technique makes him a feared grandmaster even without personal combat.
- **Dao of the Saber vs. Dao of the Sword**: Both are exalted Grand Daos, but the saber emphasizes overwhelming, unbending force, while the sword prizes flexibility, speed, and precision. The novel’s philosophy treats them as parallel pinnacle paths—hence Lu Dongbin’s excitement at pitting the best of each against each other.
- **The edge of body refining**: The text explicitly notes that Fiendgod Body Refining grants a massive late-game advantage, making Empyrean Gods equals of Pure Yang True Immortals after tribulation. This thematic undercurrent reinforces why many top contenders (including Ji Ning) are dual-cultivators or focus on body refinement.

## Reading Guide
- Watch for the shifting dynamics: after Blackstone Recluse and Daonu Daoist reveal their real power, the hierarchy of “obvious favorites” gets completely scrambled. Ji Ning, who thought he knew the playing field, suddenly faces an unknown darkness.
- This chapter quietly sets up **formation mastery** as a wildcard: Blackstone Recluse’s victory without throwing a single punch proves that tactical genius can trump raw energy.
- Lu Dongbin plays his usual trickster role—hiding his friend’s identity under a joke while funneling the plot toward the most dramatic match he can imagine. Ji Ning vs. Daonu is not just a random pairing; it’s a deliberate Dao Duel orchestrated by an amused immortal.
- Also note the Grand Xia Emperor’s careful stewardship: he handpicks the first-round pairings to protect his favorites, but the second round forces unavoidable collisions. The tournament logic here resembles a seeded bracket, but with a human (or immortal) touch.
