## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks the official assembly of one hundred aspirants before the Yinglong Guard’s trial. Ji Ning stands among them, about to take his first step toward the elite immortal corps of the Grand Xia Dynasty. A silver-haired, black-robed Immortal—exuding the oppressive aura of a black dragon surveying ants—lays down the law: only those who meet the minimum requirements will proceed to the deadly Mount Yujue Wasteland. As each cultivator announces their origin and cultivation level, the gathered elites size one another up. Ji Ning’s declaration—Black-White College, Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens, ninth layer—draws stunned whispers and immediate recognition. Then a name cuts through the air like a blade: Dong Yi of Snowdragon Mountain. The two gazes lock. Neither needs words. The chapter drips with tension, setting the stage for a trial that will test not just power, but the weight of unfinished vengeance.

## Key Plot Points
- **Assembly at the Crimson Dragon Mountains:** Ji Ning and ninety-nine other cultivators gather before a silver-haired, black-robed Immortal who oversees the Yinglong Guard recruitment.
- **Revealing the Guard's Might:** The Immortal announces that over twenty Immortals are currently cultivating in the Crimson Dragon Mountains, and that Celestial Immortals occasionally arrive from the Imperial Capital to lecture—underscoring the Guard’s overwhelming power and heritage.
- **Individual Roll Call:** Each candidate states their origin, cultivation method, and realm. The Immortal uses a bronze mirror to verify the claims, warning that lying means instant death.
- **Ji Ning's Declaration:** Ji Ning announces himself as a dual cultivator (body and Ki) of the Black-White College, at the ninth layer of the Crimsonbright Diagram—the legendary body-refining technique. The other candidates remark on his recklessness, judging his Ki Refining weak.
- **Snowdragon Mountain Appears:** The final candidate, Dong Yi of Snowdragon Mountain, announces himself as a peak Wanxiang Adept. His eyes meet Ji Ning’s, carrying clear recognition. Ji Ning remains cold, recalling his parents’ and uncle’s deaths at Snowdragon Mountain’s hands.
- **A Mentor’s Whisper:** The silver-haired Immortal sends a private mental transmission to Ji Ning, hinting that the Crimsonbright Diagram has special qualities that can only be unleashed after joining the Yinglong Guard, and advises him to return alive.
- **Departure:** After refining entrance talismans, the group is carried by clouds into the heart of the Crimson Dragon Mountains toward the teleportation array.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Yinglong Guard (应龙卫):** The Grand Xia Dynasty’s own elite immortal enforcement corps, named after the mythical winged dragon Yinglong. In Xianxia lore, such organizations serve as both the empire’s sharpest sword and its most exclusive training ground—rivaling even local marquises. Only Wanxiang Adepts (or exceptional Zifu cultivators practicing the Crimsonbright Diagram) may apply.
- **Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens (赤明九天图):** The number-one Fiendgod Body Refining technique since the primordial Fiendgod Era. It is paradoxically both legendary and widely disseminated—hinted to be deliberately promoted by the Grand Xia for reasons tied to its special properties. Cultivators who master it are treated as anomalies even by Immortals.
- **Dual Cultivation (炼体炼气兼修):** Many Xianxia protagonists pursue both Ki Refining and Fiendgod Body Refining simultaneously. The former grants long-range power and magical versatility; the latter grants monstrous physical resilience and regeneration. Ji Ning’s choice to reveal only his body-refining level here is a subtle flex—his Ki side is still catching up, but his body alone already matches an early Wanxiang Adept.
- **Snowdragon Mountain (雪龙山):** A major sect that clashed fatally with Ji Ning’s family. In the Xianxia genre, sect vendettas often follow a protagonist across provinces, making this encounter feel both inevitable and ominous. Dong Yi’s polite nod is a classic “smile before the storm” trope.

With over twenty Immortals on-site and occasional Celestial Immortal lectures, the Guard dwarfs even top sects like the Black-White College. For a cultivator like Ji Ning, joining means access to divine abilities, secret arts, and a direct pipeline to the Grand Xia's Imperial Capital. In the ruthless Xianxia world, such institutions are the fast track from "talented kid" to "force to be reckoned with." Every candidate here is a killer in their own right, and the trial will thin the herd ruthlessly.
