## Chapter Overview

This tranquil, character-driven chapter follows four-year-old Ji Ning through a typical day: his morning routine with personal maids Chuncao and Qiuye, a hearty breakfast of demon beast meat, his trip to the training grounds accompanied by eighteen Crimson Armored Guards, and his immersion in books. The chapter reveals his methodical approach—he has spent two years reading extensively about the world and cultivation before taking any action. It culminates in an evening conversation where Ji Ning solemnly declares to his parents, "I want to cultivate! I want to become an immortal!" setting the stage for his formal training.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning’s morning routine highlights the contrast between his privileged life and tribal poverty; he brushes with pure salt and eats demon beast meat daily.
- He reflects on his past life on Earth and his current acceptance of being served.
- Through conversation with Qiuye, the luxuriousness of his lifestyle is underlined: salt and earthenware jars are precious in ordinary tribes.
- Ji Ning reads voraciously; he has learned to read pictographs by naming characters, and now pursues knowledge of cultivation and world geography.
- The chapter describes the power structure: Western Prefecture, the five Ji Clan prefectures, and their place within the ancient, vast Grand Xia Dynasty.
- Ji Ning has achieved 'split-mindedness' (一心二用) through two years of visualizing the Nuwa Diagram, allowing him to perform two tasks simultaneously.
- He decides he has learned enough and now seeks to begin training.
- At dinner, he formally announces his wish to cultivate, surprising his mother Yuchi Snow and prompting his father Ji Yichuan's cold, skeptical question: "Do you know what it means to cultivate immortality?"

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- In xianxia culture, 'visualization' (观想) is a foundational soul-tempering technique. By mentally focusing on sacred images—here, the goddess Nuwa—cultivators absorb cosmic energy that directly strengthens the soul. The stronger the soul, the easier it is to develop divine sense (神念) and to multi-task in combat.
- The use of salt for oral hygiene reflects ancient Chinese customs; the extremely refined salt of the Ji clan marks their elite status. In many tribes, salt is a luxury reserved for cooking.
- Books being traded for "a thousand goat hides" underscores the rarity of written knowledge in a dangerous, demon-infested world, making Ji Ning’s access to a library a huge advantage.
- The Grand Xia Dynasty's mention as existing for "countless millions of years" emphasizes the time scale of xianxia worlds—far beyond mortal dynasties—and hints at the immortal cultivators who sustain such regimes.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a quiet, preparatory interlude before Ji Ning's formal training begins. Readers should note his patience: he doesn't rush into cultivation but spends two years gathering information. This reflects his pragmatic Dao heart—a key trait that distinguishes him from hot-headed protagonists. The split-mindedness ability foreshadows his later prowess in controlling multiple flying swords in battle. Ji Yichuan's final cold question sets up the coming challenges: cultivation is not just about desire, but about understanding its dangers and costs.

## The Nuwa Diagram {#nuwa-diagram}

The Nuwa Diagram (女娲图) is a supreme visualization technique gifted to Ji Ning by Judge Cui before his reincarnation. By mentally envisioning the goddess Nuwa—a revered creator deity in Chinese mythology who repaired the heavens and molded humanity—the practitioner absorbs natural cosmic energy that directly strengthens the soul. In the Desolate Era universe, soul strength is critical for cultivators because it enables powerful divine sense (telepathic perception) and multi-tasking during combat (controlling multiple magic treasures). Ji Ning’s ability to achieve split-mindedness at such a young age is a direct result of regular Nuwa Diagram visualization. The technique also improves his memory and logical reasoning, helping him absorb knowledge rapidly.
