## Chapter Overview
Hold onto your Dao-hearts, fellow cultivators! The chessboard is set, and Ji Ning is about to become the Reaper. After mastering the basics of the Five Treasures Sword Art under his master’s tutelage, Ning gets the green light to go on the offensive. But he’s not going to be a blunt instrument—he’s going for the jugular. With Patriarch Subhuti acting as his divine intelligence agency, Ji Ning gets the full strategic lay of the land: the power distribution of the Seamless Gate across the Zhuanshui Territories, the location of his personal nemesis Shaoyan Chou’s sixteen hidden clones, and the exact profile of his high-value targets—Empyrean Gods and True Immortals. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia war prep, shifting from personal training to proactive, surgical strikes. The quiet before the storm is over; the blade is about to fall.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Master's Briefing:** Patriarch Subhuti lays out the grand strategy, advising Ji Ning to avoid the heavily fortified Xiamang Imperial worlds and instead target the slightly weaker domains of the ancient Human Five Emperors.
- **Target Selection:** The Dongxuan Major World, under Emperor Zhuanxu’s territory, is chosen. It hosts a staggering nine Empyrean Gods and True Immortals from the Seamless Gate, making it a high-value hunting ground.
- **The Intel Package:** Subhuti provides a detailed scroll of the entire Seamless Gate power structure in the Zhuanshui Territories, specifically highlighting the sixteen clones of Ning’s hated enemy, Shaoyan Chou.
- **Deploying Assets:** Ji Ning returns the Ninefang Empyrean God to the Starseizing Major World for a pre-arranged plan, confirming his mastery over the Aquatic Palace and its spatial capabilities.
- **The Hunter’s Arrival:** Ning arrives in the Dongxuan Major World, using his advanced Heartforce to scan the entire realm undetected, and immediately transforms via the Arcane Art to begin his hunt.
- **The Prey’s Hubris:** The chapter closes with a glimpse into Shaoyan Chou’s mindset. He is smug, arrogant, and utterly convinced of his own safety, hiding in a small, insignificant outpost, completely unaware that the wolf is already at the door.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Strategic Patience (策略耐心):** Chapter 595 perfectly illustrates a key distinction in Xianxia: the difference between a *brute* and a *strategist*. A lesser protagonist would have charged into the enemy’s stronghold. Ji Ning, guided by his master, treats the war like a galactic-scale assassination mission. He calculates the cost-benefit ratio of killing Celestial Immortals (low return) versus Empyrean Gods and True Immortals (high return), and chooses his battlefield based on enemy density. This analytical, long-game thinking is a hallmark of a strong Dao-heart—it’s not about the thrill of the fight, but the efficient elimination of threats.

**The Power of Intel (情报的力量):** This chapter showcases how supreme powers use intelligence in Xianxia. Patriarch Subhuti’s ‘Dream of the Three Realms’ ability isn’t just a training tool; it’s a strategic surveillance system that can track the clones of a minor True Immortal across different dimensions. In this universe, information is a weapon as powerful as any sword. The detailed scroll is the final proof—Ning isn’t hunting blind; he’s walking into a room with a detailed map of every guard, every alarm, and every escape route.

## Reading Guide
Get ready to savor the dramatic irony, folks! We know exactly where every single one of Shaoyan Chou’s clones is, and we know he thinks he’s the smartest man in the room. The narrative then flips to his perspective, showing us his smug, self-congratulatory thoughts about his cleverness. This is the literary equivalent of watching a mouse preen itself while a hawk is already circling in a clear blue sky. The satisfaction here isn’t just in the coming action; it’s in the absolute, crushing certainty of the trap closing. Ji Ning is no longer the reactive hero; he’s the proactive angel of death, and he has his list.
