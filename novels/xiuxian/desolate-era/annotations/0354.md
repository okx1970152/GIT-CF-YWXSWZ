## Chapter Overview
The Immortal Fortunes Assembly isn’t just about combat rankings—it’s a giant matchmaking event between the top geniuses of the Grand Xia and the Immortal powers of the Three Realms. This chapter delivers the emotional payoff of the selection aftermath: Lu Dongbin, one of the most legendary Sword Immortals in existence, publicly claims Yu Wei as his disciple, while Ji Ning must prepare to say goodbye. The chapter is a bittersweet blend of triumph and impending separation, punctuated by a fascinating backroom scene where the Xia Emperor himself negotiates with a reclusive Pure Yang True Immortal named Evergreen over Ji Ning’s future mentorship.

## Key Plot Points
1. Lu Dongbin announces his acceptance of Yu Wei as his disciple, shocking the Celestial Immortals and igniting widespread jealousy. A True Immortal as famous and dedicated as Lu Dongbin is a prize second only to a Daofather.
2. Yu Wei hesitates, looking to Ji Ning for reassurance. He encourages her to accept, knowing that both of them will soon be separated from the Grand Xia world anyway for their own immortal tutelage.
3. Lu Dongbin tells Yu Wei that he would have accepted Ji Ning too, but the Grand Xia Emperor blocked it—confirming Ji Ning is being reserved for someone else within the Crimsonbright lineage.
4. Yu Wei and Ji Ning share a tender, bittersweet moment, vowing to both become Celestial Immortals and live forever together. However, a dark shadow looms as Yu Wei’s past life trauma whispers doubts.
5. The scene shifts to a peaceful minor world where the white-robed Xia Emperor visits the Evergreen Temple, a humble retreat of the Evergreen Sword Immortal—a Pure Yang True Immortal with a fiercely independent Dao-heart.
6. The Xia Emperor explains the situation: the Three Realms are in turmoil, the Six Paths of Reincarnation have collapsed, and geniuses like Ji Ning must be nurtured. He attempts to lobby Evergreen by invoking their master’s orders.
7. Evergreen bluntly rejects the notion that birth or luck determines a cultivator’s fate, emphasizing that only one’s own power is reliable. He accepts Ji Ning as a disciple solely out of respect for their master’s wishes, handing over a small green sword token.
8. The Xia Emperor leaves, satisfied. The token is now in play—Ji Ning must decide within ten days whether to accept Evergreen’s mentorship.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Lu Dongbin and the Eight Immortals**  
Lu Dongbin (吕洞宾) is the leader of the legendary Eight Immortals of the Upper Cave (上洞八仙), one of the most beloved figures in Chinese folklore. In Daoist mythology, he is a swordsman, a poet, and a trickster who roams the mortal world testing people’s virtue. In *Desolate Era*, he is reimagined as a top-tier Pure Yang True Immortal—a fearsome Sword Immortal whose passion for teaching makes him a wildly coveted master. His appearance here is both a populist flex (he’d teach a mortal if he found them worthy) and a major flex of status: when he scolds the Celestial Immortals for being jealous, they can’t even talk back because he’s that far above them in the cosmic hierarchy.

**The “Master Leads the Disciple to the Door, Cultivation is Personal” Philosophy**  
Evergreen Sword Immortal’s line, “师傅领进门，修行在个人,” is a classic Chinese saying about education. The master opens the door and gives you the foundation—but whether you walk the path or stumble into a ditch is entirely on you. This is a core Xianxia philosophy that explains why Immortals often disappear for centuries after accepting a disciple. They teach the principles, deposit a technique manual, and then vanish into the void. It’s not negligence; it’s a deliberate cultivation philosophy that tests the disciple’s own Dao-heart. Evergreen embodies this to an extreme—he taught some disciples for ten years and considered his job done.

**Crimsonbright Lineage & The Daofather’s Control**  
The Xia Emperor mentions “our master”—this confirms he is a disciple of Daofather Crimsonbright, one of the most supreme beings in the Three Realms. The entire Immortal Fortunes Assembly was, at the highest level, a recruitment event for the Crimsonbright lineage. The Daofather personally selected Mu Northson, while Ji Ning and Blackstone Realperson were “reserved” for powerful True Immortals within the same faction. This is Xianxia dynasty politics at the cosmic level: even the Celestial Thearch is just a caretaker; the real rulers are Daofathers and Chaos True Gods.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a classic Xianxia *harvest chapter*—the period after a tournament arc where all the seeds that were sown (talent, relationships, grudges) get their payoff. For first-time readers, the key tension here is Yu Wei’s hidden trauma. Her internal monologue about “rather letting my soul scatter than harm Ji Ning” is a massive red flag. This isn’t a random fear; it’s foreshadowing her backstory as a former Asura who was brainwashed by the Seamless Gate. Enjoy the sweetness of the Lu Dongbin acceptance and the Dao-companion vows, but keep your Xianxia-savvy eyes peeled—this story is not done testing Yu Wei yet.

The Evergreen Temple scene is a masterclass in contrast. You go from the grand, glittering Skylight Palace with a thousand Celestial Immortals to a humble temple with a guy watering vegetables. And that “guy” is a Pure Yang True Immortal who talks to the Grand Xia Emperor like he’s an annoying neighbor. This is the flavor of Xianxia’s highest level—the true experts couldn’t care less about worldly power. Evergreen’s philosophy is brutally cold: trust only yourself. Disciples, friends, subordinates—all can betray you. This chapter plants a seed for Ji Ning’s eventual worldview; he’s about to be taught by a master who doesn’t believe in emotional attachments.
