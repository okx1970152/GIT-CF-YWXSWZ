## Chapter Overview

Six years of sweat, blisters, and ten thousand swings of the sword—and Ji Ning finally breaks through to the realm of subtlety, achieving Unity of Man and Sword at the tender age of ten. The chapter showcases his painstaking path to mastery, where the Dripping Sutra and the Crimsonbright Diagram have forged his body and soul. But his joy is a double-edged sword: news of his breakthrough reaches the viper-like Ji Lie, who reacts with an earth-shattering rage that leaves mountains cratered and demonbeasts dead. The stage is set for a brewing storm within the Ji Clan.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning, now a ten-year-old Fiendgod Body Refiner and Houtian-perfect Ki Refiner, dominates a sparring match against nine elite Nine-Fang Warriors despite using only one sword and one-tenth of his strength.
- Under the pressure of the coordinated assault, he achieves the second realm of swordplay: Unity of Man and Sword, where body, mind, and blade move as one with subtlery and precision.
- The breakthrough is the result of six years of relentless training—two years of foundation, one year to reach the first realm, and three more years of live combat practice.
- Chuncao and Qiuye, now in their early twenties, celebrate his achievement with genuine devotion, having grown from maids to loyal attendants who see him as their 'heaven'.
- In the mountain wilderness, Ji Lie, second-strongest of the Western Prefecture and a scheming elder, hears the news and flies into a towering rage, killing his prized Earth-Armor Dragon in a blind fury.
- Ji Lie's reaction underscores the growing threat to Ji Ning and his family, as the underhanded power struggle within the Ji Clan intensifies.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In Eastern cultivation fiction, the progression of sword techniques is often described in three realms: Basic (foundational moves), Subtlety (入微, where movements become precise and efficient), and Unity of Man and Heaven (天人合一, harmonizing with natural forces). Here, Ji Ning achieves the second step, also called Unity of Man and Sword (人剑合一), but this is still just the gateway—true mastery lies in fusing with the Dao, a path that can take millennia.

The Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens is introduced here as the foundation of Ji Ning's Fiendgod Body Refining. This number-one Fiendgod scripture draws power from the Sunstar and Taiyin Star, allowing a cultivator to construct a divine body that surpasses ordinary humans. Its first stage manifests divine patterns of the Golden Crow and Moon Rabbit on the practitioner's back, a legendary omen that marks a peerless genius.

Feud politics in the Grand Xia Dynasty are brutal and nuanced. Ji Lie's rage is not just petty jealousy—in a clan where succession is based on strength, a young prodigy like Ji Ning threatens to tip the balance of power for generations. Killing inside cities is forbidden, but outside the walls, the law of the jungle rules among cultivators.

### Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens

The number one Fiendgod Body Refining method, divided into nine layers; requires absorbing power from the Sunstar and Taiyin Star.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for the calm before the storm. Ji Ning's record-breaking speed in achieving Unity of Man and Sword is a massive flex, but it also paints a giant target on his back. Ji Lie is no barking dog—he's a viper who has already lost a position once and is willing to crush anyone who stands in his way. If you enjoy seeing the protagonist steadily stack power-ups while the villains start sweating, this is the chapter that sets the *real* conflict in motion. Also, keep an eye on the divide between Ki Refining and Fiendgod cultivation: Ji Ning is already a walking freak of nature, and he hasn't even hit his teens.
