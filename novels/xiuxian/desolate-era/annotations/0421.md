## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up. Chapter 0421 is a masterclass in world-building and political maneuvering wrapped in a seemingly peaceful tea ceremony. Ji Ning and his master, the Diancai Immortal, are summoned for a chat with the legendary Celestial Immortal Tonghe—a top-tier power player of the Anchan Northmont clan who’s been living undercover as a mortal for eons. This isn’t just a casual meet-and-greet; it’s an alliance negotiation wrapped in the philosophy of Karma and survival. The calm, hearth-side vibe is a brilliant setup for the heavy geopolitical bombshells that drop, especially the reveal about the mysterious Basilisk Combined Formation and its connection to the Cloud of Blood Tower, the Grand Xia’s #1 assassin guild. It’s a chapter where the storm clouds of the Three Realms’ great calamity are painted in vivid detail, all while sipping tea from a tree planted by a Celestial Immortal’s mortal teachers. Peak Xianxia atmosphere.

## Key Plot Points
*   The chapter opens with Ji Ning and the Diancai Immortal meeting the enigmatic Celestial Immortal Tonghe, who has been presumed dead for ages and living a simple, mortal life to avoid Karmic entanglement. The vibe is immediately established as intimate, philosophical, and loaded with hidden gravitas.
*   Tonghe’s lecture on Karma is a key world-building segment: every connection breeds danger. His ‘solution’—feigned death and hiding in a mortal world—is presented as a viable but unattractively lonely path to immortality, contrasting the cultivator’s usual path of seeking connections and power.
*   The main plot hook is revealed: a great calamity is sweeping the Three Realms. Tonghe explicitly states that even True Immortals and Empyrean Gods won’t be safe, though Daofathers might be fine. The local chaos, including the Blood God Church, is just the surface symptom of this hidden, global conflict.
*   Tonghe proposes a defensive alliance: the Black-White College, the Ji Clan, and the Anchan Northmont clan must stand together. This is a pragmatic, survival-oriented move, not a heroic charge. He explains that acting alone would make them a target, but a united front can weather the storm.
*   The investigation into the Basilisk Combined Formation leads Ji Ning to Heavenly Treasures Mountain. The encounter escalates drastically when the Ancestral Celestial Fox himself appears, highlighting how serious Ji Ning’s query is and how highly the Xia Emperor values him.
*   The chapter’s big reveal: The Basilisk Combined Formation is the signature technique of the Cloud of Blood Tower, the Grand Xia’s most notorious assassin organization. The Ancestral Celestial Fox shares a top-secret dossier linking them to the shadowy forces behind the chaos, implying the assassin guild is a major pawn of the ultimate enemy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **Karma (因果) and the ‘Feigned Death’ Strategy:** Celestial Immortal Tonghe’s entire philosophy is a brilliant application of Karmic theory. In Buddhist-influenced Xianxia, Karma isn’t just about good and bad deeds; it’s the fundamental web of cause and effect that binds you to the world. Every promise, every friendship, every enmity generates Karma. By ‘severing Karma’ and letting the world believe he is dead, Tonghe has effectively cut all threads to his past life, minimizing the risk of attracting trouble. This is a classic trope—the ‘hidden master’ who avoids tribulations by rejecting the world—and it highlights the supreme value of living long over living large.
*   **The Hierarchy of Immortals:** Tonghe casually drops that “even True Immortals and Empyrean Gods” might die in the coming calamity, while “Daofathers might be safe.” This reinforces the ironclad power structure of the Three Realms. True Immortals (Pure Yang True Immortals) and Empyrean Gods (True Gods) are the rulers of the world, but above them are Daofathers (Ancestral Immortals) who have mastered a Grand Dao. The gap between these tiers is so vast that a catastrophe that annihilates the top tiers can be merely a “difficult week” for the very peak. It’s a sobering reminder of the exponential power curve in this universe.
*   **Heavenly Treasures Mountain and the Ancestral Celestial Fox:** This segment is a classic Xianxia “information gathering” scene. Heavenly Treasures Mountain is the go-to intelligence and auction house, a neutral ground backed by immense power. The personal appearance of the Ancestral Celestial Fox—the founder himself—is a massive signal. It shows that Ji Ning’s reputation has reached the highest echelons of power. The Xia Emperor’s favor has granted him access to state-level secrets that even other Celestial Immortals cannot buy. This “delegated awe” underscores the political weight Ji Ning now carries.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a shift in gears, because Chapter 0421 is mostly setup—but it’s *excellent* setup. If you’re here for non-stop, flashy sword fights, this chapter might feel slow. But if you’re a lore-hound who loves seeing the political chessboard being laid out, this is pure gold. Pay close attention to Tonghe’s philosophy; it gives you a crucial insight into how ancient, powerful beings think when they’re not acting. The reveal about the Cloud of Blood Tower and the Basilisk Combined Formation is the chapter’s real explosive hook. This is the first clear link between the weird formations at Serpentwing Lake and a major, organized hostile force. The alliance being formed here will be the bedrock for all the coming conflicts. Soak in the atmosphere, respect the tea, and brace yourself for the storm to break.
