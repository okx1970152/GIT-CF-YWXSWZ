## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Author’s Note as a Xianxia Ritual**  
In the fast-paced world of Chinese web novels, an author’s note like this one is a cherished tradition. It’s the equivalent of a daoist master pausing mid-lecture to take a sip of tea and rearrange his jade slips. For a serialization that can run for years and millions of words, these brief, human moments are where the author acknowledges the audience as fellow travelers. “Let Tomato sort out the plot” is a phrase of humble authority—it admits the complexity of the story while reassuring readers that the creator is in control. The promise of “two chapters tonight” is a sacred pact in the web novel community, especially after a pause.

**The Grand Xia–Seamless Gate War**  
This war is the culmination of a cold war that has been simmering for hundreds of chapters. The Seamless Gate, an ancient and shadowy organization from a rival chaosworld, has been infiltrating and manipulating the Three Realms from the shadows. The Grand Xia Dynasty, the most powerful mortal empire under Heaven, is now openly engaging them. This is not a small skirmish; it is a conflict that will involve Celestial Immortals, Empyrean Gods, and the very fabric of the Three Realms. For seasoned Xianxia readers, this announcement signals the beginning of a war arc that will reshape the entire power structure of the story.
