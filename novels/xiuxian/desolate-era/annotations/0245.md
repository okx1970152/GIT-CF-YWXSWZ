## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists—Ji Ning is done playing nice! After nine long years of silent cultivation at Winged Serpent Lake, the Sword Immortal has finally dropped the hammer on Snowdragon Mountain. This chapter is a masterclass in psychological warfare: a simple letter containing nothing but his name and a seven-day deadline sends four Zifu Disciples into a panic, forcing them to escalate the crisis all the way up the sect hierarchy. The tension is thick enough to cut with a sword as we see the gears of a Xianxia power structure grind into motion. Meanwhile, on the other side of the world, a Primal Daoist—an ancient monster who has lived for over a thousand years—decides he's had enough of this young upstart's insolence and sets off on a collision course with our hero. The stage is set for an epic confrontation, and trust me, you won't want to miss what happens next.

## Key Plot Points
*   The four Zifu Disciples of the Yan Mountain branch are paralyzed by Ji Ning’s letter. They recognize the terrifying Sword Intent within the writing, confirming he can kill them with a single blow.
*   Unable to send a full report to the distant Snowdragon Mountain within the ten-day deadline, they are forced to crush a high-level message talisman, signaling "Ji Ning himself is about to attack" to their superior, Realperson Xu Ke.
*   Realperson Xu Ke, who fled the Yan Mountain years ago out of fear, is panicked by the news. He immediately seeks an audience with his master, the Snowfeather Daoist, one of the three Primal overlords of the sect.
*   The Snowfeather Daoist is angered by Ji Ning's arrogance. Despite knowing Ji Ning is a Black-White College disciple and a monstrous genius, his own pride as a Primal-perfection expert will not allow him to back down.
*   The chapter ends with the Snowfeather Daoist personally leading a formidable war party from Snowdragon Mountain, heading straight for the Yan Mountains to confront Ji Ning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **Message Talismans (信符):** These are one-way communication devices used in sects when long-distance travel is too slow. They come in pairs; when one is crushed, its twin shatters to send a pre-arranged signal. Different colors or sizes represent different meanings, making them a critical tool for quick crisis escalation in a Xianxia world. This is a classic trope for building suspense, as it creates a time-delayed information chain.
*   **The Primal Daoist Hierarchy:** The chapter explicitly states that Primal Daoists have sub-stages (Early, Mid, Late, Perfection). The Snowfeather Daoist is at "Primal Perfection," which means he has been cultivating for over a thousand years and is at the very peak of his realm, just one step away from becoming a Wanxiang Adept himself (quite a feat). This is a deliberate power calibration. We know Ji Ning can kill Wanxiang Adepts, but this guy is playing in a completely different league.
*   **Sect Politics and Face (面子):** The Snowfeather Daoist's decision to personally intervene is driven entirely by "Face." The death of his sect members was already a huge loss of prestige, but to be *driven out* of a territory by a single junior? That would be an intolerable disgrace. For a top-tier power like Snowdragon Mountain, maintaining its reputation of invincibility is more important than the lives of a few disciples.

## Reading Guide
*   **Enjoy the Worldbuilding:** Pay close attention to the power structure. The hierarchy from Xiantian to Zifu to Wanxiang to Primal is made very tangible here. The Snowfeather Daoist’s insane, over-the-top entrance (the ninety-nine male and female Zifu attendants alone!) is a perfect example of Xianxia's love for dramatic, hierarchical displays of power. It’s there to make you feel just how insignificant a normal cultivator is compared to a true immortal.
*   **Ji Ning's Growth:** This chapter is a huge confidence boost for the reader. Our boy Ji Ning, who started as a mortal child in a tribal village, has now grown so strong that a mere **letter** can terrify multiple Zifu Disciples and force a Primal Daoist to mobilize his entire legion. The chapter title may be "A Final Ultimatum," but the subtext is "Ji Ning is the new boss on the block."
