## Chapter Overview

Fellow Daoists, buckle up. In this chapter, Ji Ning transitions from a surgical strike against a single traitor to a full-blown, multi-location decapitation operation against the Seamless Gate. After effortlessly erasing Shaoyan Chou’s sixteen avatars like an after-dinner snack, the Northern Sea Sword Immortal unleashes his true power. Mounting a perfected Karmic Punishment Deity, he partners with the Seven Radiance Empyrean God to descend upon three hidden Seamless Gate lairs in the Zhuanxu Realmverse. The result? Nine True Gods and Celestial Immortals—gone in the span of a few breaths. The chapter is a masterclass in surgical aggression, showcasing what happens when a near-Daofather-level combatant decides to stop playing defense. It’s a declaration of war, a power flex, and a terrifying reminder that in the game of cosmic chess, Ji Ning has just moved a rook straight into the enemy’s backline.

## Key Plot Points

*    **The Appetizer:** Ji Ning, having maintained his disguised form to avoid alarming the higher-ups, corners Shaoyan Chou’s sixteenth avatar. In a moment of cold, clinical judgment, he reveals his true face, listens to the traitor’s pathetic pleas for mercy, and kills him with a single spear-thrust. This was a necessary, quiet preliminary step before the real chaos.
*    **Unleashing the Army:** Back in his Starseizing Major World, Ji Ning addresses his long-pent-up army of Immortals. Forced into hiding for endless ages, they are eager to taste war. Under his command, they form into a perfect Karmic Punishment Deity, a colossal headless battle avatar of pure destruction, marking their first official deployment into the wider conflict.
*    **The First Strike (Iron Soldier Tower):** The scene shifts to a Seamless Gate lair where three True Gods and Immortals are relaxing with wine and dancers. The party is shattered when a thirty-thousand-meter Karmic Punishment Deity appears above them. Ji Ning smashes through their formation restrictions in nine sword-strikes and, with a casual sweep of his sword-light, annihilates all three enemies before they can even formulate a coherent escape plan.
*    **The Rout:** The massacre is absolute. Immortal Iron Soldier and Golden Spear True God are cut down as they try to flee. Ji Ning’s sword-light, spanning tens of thousands of meters, turns the battlefield into a zone of death, wiping out the lower-ranked Loose Immortals and Celestial Immortals as collateral damage. He then tears through space to reach the next target before the news can spread.
*    **The Third Strike (Perfect Coordination):** Arriving at the third lair, Ji Ning repeats the process. This time, the Seamless Gate's Daofather manages to send a desperate telepathic warning, but it's too late. The formation is shattered, and the three defenders are slaughtered. The chapter highlights the weakness of a True God: even if one avatar manages to flee, the destruction of the soul in the other seven causes the remnant soul to collapse.
*    **A Mission, Not a Revenge:** The Seven Radiance Empyrean God reports a successful strike on the second lair. Ji Ning, unsatisfied with a mere nine kills, boards his Void Boat and departs for another major world, signaling that this is only the beginning of his offensive.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

*   **The Karmic Punishment Deity & Seven Radiance Empyrean God:** These aren't just big monsters; they are high-tier war formations. The Karmic Punishment Deity (刑天神) is formed from a massive army of coordinated Immortals, channeling their power into a singular, colossal avatar. The Seven Radiance Empyrean God (七曜天神) is a similar, often smaller-scale, but still devastating formation. Their appearance signals that an army has arrived, not just a single cultivator. The fact that Ji Ning has perfected his control to the point of being able to teleport the Deity through space is a massive power-up, as it removes his main weakness—slow movement.
*   **The “Daofather Puppet” Benchmark:** The text explicitly states Ji Ning’s combat power in the Karmic Punishment Deity has exceeded that of a Daofather Puppet. In Xianxia, these benchmarks are crucial for the reader to understand the power ladder. A Daofather Puppet is a supreme war construct capable of fighting a real Daofather for a time. Beating one means Ji Ning’s formation is now a legitimate threat to the highest echelons of the Three Realms.
*   **The Weakness of True Gods:** This chapter beautifully illustrates a subtle rule of the setting. True Gods have incredible regenerative powers and can split into multiple avatars. However, their soul is still the core of their existence. If the primary soul is destroyed across multiple bodies, a surviving remnant cannot sustain itself. It’s a high-stakes gamble: you can try to split your soul to escape, but if the majority of your “self” is killed, the surviving piece will simply dissolve.
*   **The Cost of Loose Immortals:** The chapter references the "three Calamities and nine Tribulations" and the fate of Loose Immortals. These are Immortals who failed their Heavenly Tribulation and are forced to live in a half-life, constantly facing increasingly powerful tribulations every few thousand years until they die. Their desperation and accumulated experience make them powerful, but ultimately, they are tragic figures on borrowed time.

## Reading Guide

Breathe in. Read this chapter for the sheer *satisfaction* of a perfectly executed plan. This isn't a desperate struggle; it’s a controlled demolition. Watch how Ji Ning, fresh from his isolated cultivation, is not just stronger—he’s *faster* and *more precise*. The chapter is a highlight reel of what peak "Progression Fantasy" looks like: the reward for hundreds of chapters of training is not just a new spell, but the ability to render entire enemy strongholds obsolete in a single scene. The description of the Seamless Gate True Gods going from drinking wine to being grinded to dust in the span of a few seconds is the pure, uncut essence of what makes this genre so addictive.
