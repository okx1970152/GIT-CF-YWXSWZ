## Chapter Overview
Settle in, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is an emotional gut-punch wrapped in a Xianxia mystery. The six remaining powerhouses of the Ji Clan gather in a mist-shrouded forest to decide their fate against the vengeful Snowdragon Mountain sect. But before the chapter dives into the war council, it detonates a long-buried bombshell about Ji Ning's father, Ji Yichuan. We learn the secret of his 'weakened' strength: his Zifu withered years ago in a desperate battle, and he can only use his full power one last time before becoming a cripple. The chapter masterfully shifts from a heart-wrenching farewell between father and son to a high-stakes exploration beneath the earth, where the clan stumbles upon a hidden stone chamber at the heart of a massive elemental ore vein. This could be the legacy of an Immortal—a final, desperate hope that might tip the scales of the coming war.

## Key Plot Points
- The Clan Patriarch, Ji Jiuhuo, makes the call: six of them will stand against Snowdragon Mountain, using layered formations to buy time and delay the inevitable.
- Ji Ning tries to object, but his father, Ji Yichuan, reveals a devastating truth: his Zifu was permanently damaged in a battle to protect Ning's mother years ago. His path of immortality is severed, and his 'weakened' state has been a permanent reality.
- Ji Yichuan admits he has only one desperate attack left within him. He was saving it to protect Ji Ning one last time, but now he chooses to spend it on the battlefield for the clan, a final, glorious blaze before he becomes a cripple.
- The group decides to investigate the bizarre energy fluctuations they sensed earlier, which point towards a rich, undiscovered elemental ore vein beneath their feet.
- Ji Ning uses his Water-Fire Lotus to dig a tunnel, and after a long descent, they hit an impenetrable stone wall—the surface of a man-made stone chamber.
- The stone door is pushed open, revealing an entrance that bursts with incredibly dense natural energy, confirming the group's suspicion: they have found a potential Immortal's treasury.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Severed Immortal Path (仙路已断)**: This is a tragic concept unique to Xianxia. It doesn't mean a cultivator is simply sick or injured—it means they have hit a hard, irreversible ceiling in their cultivation base. For Ji Yichuan, his internal "sea" (the Zifu) has shriveled and can no longer expand or even hold enough elemental ki to power a magic treasure. This is a spiritual death sentence for a cultivator, a fading echo of lost potential.

**The Forbidden Art Trade-Off (禁术)**: In Xianxia, forbidden arts aren't just powerful—they come with a specific, Faustian cost. Here, Ji Yichuan's art trades a lifetime of damage in mere minutes. The incremental rule is key: a cultivator in desperation isn't just "exhausted"; they are burning a specific resource. Ji Yichuan is literally burning the structural integrity of his Zifu itself. When the half-hour is up, his spiritual foundation collapses, and he drops from a Zifu master to a mundane cripple.

**The Immortal's Legacy (仙人遗迹)**: Finding a hidden stone chamber deep underground, protected by stone that resists divine abilities, is a classic Xianxia trope. It represents the "Delegated Awe" of an off-screen powerhouse. The Immortal who built it is long gone, but the sheer density of the energy and the impenetrable rock speak volumes about a level of power far beyond anyone in the room. This is the Xianxia equivalent of finding a crashed alien spaceship—a potential game-changer from a forgotten age.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in raising the stakes for a showdown. The emotional core here is Ji Yichuan's farewell. Watch how his character, who is usually the ice-cold warrior, shifts here. He smiles. He admits his weakness. His 'love' is expressed in the raw, physical sacrifice he's about to make. This is his moment of true, tragic vulnerability. On the other hand, the discovery of the stone chamber is a massive narrative carrot. It creates a powerful suspense: will they find a weapon or resource inside that can save them? Or will it be a trap that delays them further? The contrast between the clan's desperate sacrifice and this new, mysterious hope creates a brilliant tension. Note how the author uses the clan's collective awe of the Immortal's power (the Delegated Awe rule) to sell the magnitude of the discovery—it’s a moment of pure, reckless Xianxia optimism before the storm.
