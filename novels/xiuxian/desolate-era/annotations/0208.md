## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a slow-burn setup that feels like the calm before a storm—and my fellow Daoists, the storm is coming *fast*. Ji Ning finishes nurturing his cold-sha to the eighth grade and sets out to plant a Golden Flame Earthfire vein, a process that demands intense caution due to the clashing energies already inside him. Meanwhile, the net closes in: the Dragon Whale King, now a Primal-level demon after his lucky breakthrough, learns of Ji Ning's location and sets off for vengeance. And to top it off, an old rival from the Black-White College, Dongyi, is leading two other Wanxiang Adepts directly toward the same hidden earthfire mountain, having sworn Heavenly Dao oaths to trade the location for treasures. Three threads converge on a single desolate peak. Get ready for the clash.

## Key Plot Points

- **Cold-sha progress and Qingqing’s stingy generosity**: Ji Ning’s cold-sha reaches the eighth grade. Qingqing, still a miser at heart, gifts Mu Northson a tiny piece of ten-thousand-year ice as a “congratulations” present—causing Ji Ning to cover his forehead in amused exasperation.
- **The memory of home and unfinished business**: Ji Ning’s thoughts drift to Sister Qiuye, Qingshi, and the Winged Serpent Lake. He reaffirms his resolve to become a Yinglong Guard, investigate his parents’ past, and protect those he loves forever. This is classic Dao-heart tempering, showing his drive is still rooted in loyalty, not mere ambition.
- **Abandoning small trade, embracing the big picture**: After two massive group battles in the Mount Yujue Wasteland, Ji Ning realizes that spending time haggling with individual Wanxiang Adepts over cold-sha or earthfire veins is beneath him. His mindset is shifting from survival-mode to king-of-the-hill pragmatism.
- **Dragon Whale King’s hunt begins**: Having advanced to the Primal stage, the Dragon Whale King receives a tip about Ji Ning’s location and sets off with cold fury. His tenfold-augmented aura signals that this is no longer a Wanxiang-level threat—Ji Ning is facing a Primal demon for the first time.
- **Dongyi’s secret deal**: Dongyi, the same Wanxiang Adept who smiled and hid hostility toward Ji Ning back at the Black-White College, has traded the location of a hidden earthfire vein for treasures. He leads two other cultivators—a bald woman with red markings and an unnamed male—to the exact same mountain where Ji Ning is currently meditating. The convergence is inevitable.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Earthfire and cold-sha compatibility**: In Xianxia cultivation, extreme Yin and Yang energies cannot coexist peacefully in a single body. Ji Ning is taking a calculated risk by planting both a cold-sha and an earthfire—one slip and his Violet Palace detonates. This is a classic “balancing opposites” trope rooted in Daoist alchemy, where the True Sun and True Moon must be harmonized.
- **Divine sense warning against spatial tears**: Ji Ning’s soul instinctively recoils when he looks at the magma below. This is a common Xianxia mechanism—when a cultivator’s soul attains a certain purity, it can sense existential danger (like spatial distortions) that normal sight cannot. It’s a built-in “spider-sense” that becomes sharper as one’s Dao-heart strengthens.
- **Heavenly Dao Oath (天道誓言)**: Dongyi and his companions have sworn oaths on the Heavenly Dao itself. In Xianxia, breaking such an oath invites karmic backlash so severe that even Immortals fear it. That’s why Dongyi is confident no one will betray him—the oath makes double-crossing a death sentence.
- **The Dragon Whale King’s upgrade**: He broke through from Wanxiang to Primal after the last battle. In Xianxia, a single major realm breakthrough can multiply power tenfold or more. Ji Ning, at the Zifu stage, is about to face a Primal demon—a terrifying gap that will test his newly acquired techniques.

### Black-White College (黑白学宫)

A premier immortal sect in the Anchan Commandery, known for its elite teachings and powerful overseers; Ji Ning is a formal disciple here.

## Reading Guide

Buckle up, fellow Daoists—this is the classic “three-way convergence” setup that Xianxia does so well. Ji Ning is inside a mountain belly, completely absorbed in a delicate cultivation process that he can’t interrupt. Outside, a Primal demon is flying straight toward him with murder in his heart. And to make things even messier, three Wanxiang Adepts (including a snake in the grass) are heading to the same spot for a treasure deal. The tension is about to snap. Pay close attention to Ji Ning’s mental state: his memories of Qiuye and the Winged Serpent Lake show that despite his cold, pragmatic exterior, his emotional roots are deep. That emotional steel is what fuels his Dao-heart—and what makes him dangerous. Also, note how casually the Dragon Whale King dismisses his own subordinates: this is a demon king who has tasted real power after his breakthrough and feels invincible. Arrogance before the fall? We’ll see.
