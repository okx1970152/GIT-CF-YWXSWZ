## Chapter Overview
If you thought you’d seen Ji Ning get pushed to the absolute brink before, you haven’t seen anything yet. This chapter brings the *Hidden Tribulation of the Violent Rain* volume to a shattering, gut-punch of a conclusion. The burning crimson of karmic hellfire takes center stage as Ji Ning, his Dao-heart hanging by a thread, faces the ultimate test of spiritual endurance. But while he’s locked in a desperate internal war against the flames, his enemies aren’t idle. The Shaoyin Ancestor, with cold, clinical precision, executes a plan that doesn’t just try to kill Ji Ning—it *erases* him from the Three Realms entirely. Get ready for a chapter that burns bright with agony, awe, and the cruel hand of fate.

## Key Plot Points
- The crimson hellfire descends upon Ji Ning and the Scourgewind Fiend-Demon, an inescapable punishment for the karmic sin they just accumulated. The pain is immediate and absolute.
- Ji Ning is sent crashing to the ground, screaming in agony. His Primaltwin, back in the aquatic manor, suffers the same fiery torment, showing that the soul-burning is linked across all avatars.
- The Scourgewind Fiend-Demon, a veteran of a million years of tribulations, is able to withstand the first wave with some rationality intact. Ji Ning, though in far greater visible distress, shockingly does not die instantly, astonishing the watching Celestial Immortals.
- The Whitewater Hound, little Qing, and Mu Northson watch in utter despair as the hellfire rages, knowing they can only pray for Ji Ning’s Dao-heart to hold.
- Realizing Ji Ning’s freakish resilience, the Shaoyin Ancestor and the six Celestial Immortals attempt to suppress him with Pure Yang treasures. Even in his reduced, pain-maddened state, Ji Ning’s Starseizing Hand instinctively bats them all away.
- Shaoyin Ancestor finally reveals his ace in the hole: the Realm-Refining Tower (a true Innate Spirit treasure). Instead of trying to beat Ji Ning down, he activates the tower to banish him, creating a spatial vortex that swallows Ji Ning and casts him completely out of the Three Realms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Karmic Hellfire (业火)** is one of the most terrifying metaphysical punishments in the Eastern Xianxia cosmology. It’s not a physical flame; it is a *sin-burning* flame that targets the soul and the Dao-heart directly, making any physical advantages (like the Eight-Nine Arcane Art) completely useless. This is a direct extension of the universal law of Karma—actions of mass slaughter or other massive sins carry a karmic debt, and this hellfire is the Heavenly Dao’s method of demanding payment. If the Dao-heart is not strong enough, the soul is instantly reduced to nothingness. Ji Ning’s survival, even for a few moments, is a testament to the immense Dao-heart he has forged in his resolve for the Dao.

**The Realm-Refining Tower** entering the story here is a classic example of a supreme treasure used not for direct destruction, but as a plot device for spatial relocation—a *banishment* attack. In Xianxia, high-tier treasures often have world-shaping or dimension-traveling properties, making them capable of feats that defy normal physics. Being expelled from the Three Realms is not merely being thrown to a faraway continent; it means being cast into the chaotic, primordial Void between universes, a fate usually worse than death for anyone below a certain power level.

## Reading Guide
This is the end of a major story volume, and the final page delivers a devastating cliffhanger that resets Ji Ning’s entire world. The hellfire trial itself is a brilliant showcase of Ji Ning’s core strength: not his swords, treasures, or divine abilities, but his unyielding spirit. However, the lesson of this chapter is also a brutal reality check—even a perfect Dao-heart can be overpowered by sheer force of circumstance and a well-laid trap. The Shaoyin Ancestor’s decision to banish rather than kill Ji Ning is a cold, strategic masterstroke that eliminates a monstrous threat without engaging in a fight they might even lose. As one volume closes, a terrifyingly unknown one opens. Where has Ji Ning been sent, and how will he survive in the space between worlds?

## Deeper Dive: The Anatomy of a Banishment
The Shaoyin Ancestor’s strategy here is a textbook example of Xianxia tactical escalation. First, he tried a full-frontal assault. That failed. He tried direct suppression with treasures. That failed. Finally, he resorted to a change in tactics: *banishment*. The Realm-Refining Tower, an Innate Spirit treasure, doesn’t just trap; it *repositions* the target. This is a crucial narrative tool. Killing Ji Ning might have triggered some hidden master protection or an unforeseen back-up. But throwing him into the void? That is a timeless, unreachable prison. It is a fate that allows the villain to claim victory while simultaneously setting the stage for the hero’s next, greatest adventure in a completely alien landscape.
