## Chapter Overview

Holy jade lotus hellfire, our boy Diancai *actually made it*! The old Earth Immortal weathered the Six-Nine Tribulation and ascended to the glittering realm of Celestial Immortality. But while Heaven celebrated with seven-colored clouds and blooming golden lotuses, the Bloodfiend Church’s Blooddrop was lurking in the wings, waiting to see if the gamble paid off before blowing a precious Greater Teleportation Dao-seal to escape. Ji Ning’s Primaltwin gets the full story—how his Master’s Heartfiend Tribulation became the perfect cover for a two-pronged assassination attempt, and how Ji Ning’s true body annihilated seven Dharma Kings to keep that window of vulnerability shut. Back in the mountains, Ji Ning pieces together the location of the mysterious faction that tried to kill him: the Eastwood School. And he’s *not* the type to let a secret ambush slide without investigating.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Ascension**: The Diancai Immortal completes his breakthrough, and the scene is *loaded* with celestial glory—auspicious clouds, golden lotuses blooming from the earth, and a cosmic resonance that sounds like ethereal music. The narrative takes a moment to explain the transformation: his Primordial Spirit, Yuan power, and soul all merge into a single Golden Core. He is now a true Celestial Immortal, free from the Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations.
- **Blooddrop’s Exit**: Blooddrop, who had been lurking inside the black skiff formation, officially acknowledges the birth of a Celestial Immortal. He admits he was only waiting to see the result, then burns a Greater Teleportation Dao-seal to escape. The Wufeng Immortal grins and considers it a fair trade.
- **The Wufeng Immortal’s Report**: The old madman excitedly recounts how Ji Ning killed seven Dharma Kings and fended off a second wave of assassins *while* the Diancai Immortal was helpless in his Heartfiend Tribulation. Diancai is visibly guilty, but Ji Ning brushes it off.
- **Celebration and Political Realignment**: The Diancai Immortal’s new status is a *big* deal for the Black-White College. The Wufeng Immortal immediately starts planning the next steps: reporting to the Tortoise Mountain Celestial Immortal of the Anchan Northmont clan. He explains the symbiotic relationship between the college and the marquis clan—and that Diancai needs five top-tier Immortal swords, which the Northmont clan can supply.
- **Ji Ning’s Investigation**: Ji Ning’s true body, having been the decoy, begins tracking down the location of the mysterious faction that ambushed him. Using a Greater Teleportation Dao-seal, he jumps a million kilometers away, lands in a random city, and puppeteers a local Zifu elder into giving him the map coordinates. By cross-referencing the Wildgoose Mountains with his mental map of the Grand Xia, he deduces that the base is the Eastwood Mountains—home of the Eastwood School.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Golden Core & Celestial Immortality**: This chapter provides a beautiful, almost alchemical description of the breakthrough from Earth Immortal to Celestial Immortal. In the novel’s system, the Primal (soul) and the Yuan power (energy) are fused into a single entity called the **Golden Core** (金丹, jīn dān). This isn’t a purely magical process—it’s a classical Daoist alchemical concept. The phrase “一粒金丹吞入腹，方知我命由我不由天” (After swallowing a single Golden Core, I realize my fate is mine to command, not Heaven’s) is a famous line from the *Wuzhen Pian* (Awakening to Reality), a foundational Daoist internal alchemy text by the Song dynasty master Zhang Boduan. It symbolizes the ultimate transcendence of mortality. After this, the cultivator is no longer subject to the periodic Calamities and Tribulations that plague lesser immortals.

**Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations**: Once a cultivator reaches the Primal Daoist stage, the Heavenly Dao begins to systematically purge them. The “Three Calamities” occur every 300 years, and the “Nine Tribulations” every 900 years. This is the cosmic equivalent of a stress test, designed to cull those unworthy of true immortality. Escaping the Five Elements and leaping out of the Three Realms means you’ve passed the final exam—the Six-Nine Tribulation in this case—and are no longer a target for these periodic disasters.

**The Six-Nine Tribulation**: The tribulations are named by the pattern of thunderbolts. A Two-Nine Tribulation is 18 bolts; a Three-Nine is 27; the Six-Nine that Diancai overcame is 54 bolts. The number is a direct measure of the cultivator’s potential and power. Overcoming a Six-Nine is a massive statement—it’s not the absolute pinnacle (Nine-Nine is the highest for Fiendgod Body Refining), but it places Diancai firmly above the average Celestial Immortal, meaning he was a sleeping giant all along.

**Sect-Marquise Symbiosis (安澶北山氏)**: The Wufeng Immortal explains a critical piece of Grand Xia politics: the Black-White College is headquartered within Anchan City because it exists *under the umbrella* of the Anchan Northmont clan (the ruling marquis). In exchange for the right to recruit talent from the entire commandery, the college serves the marquis clan’s interests. Now that the college has a Celestial Immortal, the balance of power shifts. The marquis clan will be forced to offer generous gifts (like top-tier Immortal swords) to keep the relationship strong. This is the Xianxia equivalent of a small nation suddenly developing a nuclear deterrent.

### Eastern Lin School / Eastwood School (东林山脉 / 东林宗)

A mysterious hidden faction discovered by Ji Ning; its base is concealed in the Eastwood Mountains and features a deadly Divine-Poison Flame Furnace formation and a black-mist cave.

## Reading Guide

Fellow Daoists, prepare for one of those rare chapters where the *system* gets a beautiful moment in the sun. If you’ve ever wondered what the fuss about “Golden Core” is, this is your answer. The chapter explicitly links it to classical Daoist alchemy, giving you a lore infusion that makes the power fantasy feel rooted in something ancient. Diancai’s ascension is a cathartic payoff for anyone who’s been rooting for the old man—he’s not just strong, he’s *legitimate* in the eyes of Heaven.

Ji Ning’s investigation scene is a classic “knowledge is power” moment in Xianxia. He doesn’t just run away and lick his wounds; he immediately performs a forensic spatial analysis, cross-referencing local landmarks with a perfect mental map to pinpoint the enemy’s base. This is the sign of a mature cultivator who treats his own survival as a strategic problem rather than a emotional crisis. The Eastwood School is now on his radar, and with the Seventy-Two Transformations, he’s got the perfect infiltration tool.

Keep an eye on the “five Immortal swords” thread. The Diancai Immortal’s equipment is about to get a major upgrade, and the Anchan Northmont clan’s reaction to his new status will ripple through the political landscape for volumes to come. The celebration is real, but the storm clouds are gathering.
