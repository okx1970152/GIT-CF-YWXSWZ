## Chapter Overview

In this pulse-pounding chapter of *Desolate Era*, Ji Ning turns the Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation into his own hunting ground. Using his newly comprehended Rainwater Sword Domain—a physical projection of his Dao—he ambushes the eight Zifu Disciples of Snowdragon Mountain with surgical precision. Two swift strikes, two heads fly. The survivors, now six, are utterly terrorized. They throw down every defensive trick they have: formation flags, blood banners, venomous insects, and living vines. Their fear is palpable. Meanwhile, the Ji Clan patriarch, Ji Jiuhuo, reaches out to Ning with a crucial transmission, hinting that there’s more to this fight than blind slaughter. This chapter is a masterclass in xianxia tactical warfare—blending stealth, domain control, and overwhelming force.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning unleashes the Rainwater Sword Domain, a natural manifestation of his Dao comprehension, causing a soft rain to fall within the formation. The Snowdragon cultivators dismiss it as ordinary rain—a fatal underestimation.
- Maintaining their Eight Trigrams Formation, the eight move cautiously. Ji Ning, riding the dragon tail of the prison formation, condenses the ninth-layer Lesser Thousand Sword Formation into a single thread of sword-light.
- The first victim is the triangular-eyed cultivator, who reacts too late. His head is severed cleanly.
- The second victim, the silver-haired cultivator, dies an instant later as the same thread of light cuts through his armor. The formation collapses.
- The remaining six quickly form a new defensive array: Dongzi Qi's Eight Trigrams Blood Dragon Formation, a triangular pagoda shield, vines, poison insects, and nine summoned beasts from a blood banner.
- Ji Ning is struck by a homing fire phoenix talisman and a lightning attack, which blast a hole in his abdomen. However, his Fiendgod body instantly regenerates.
- Ji Jiuhuo contacts Ji Ning via telepathy, warning him not to act rashly and detailing the enemy’s layered defenses. The chapter ends on this tactical pause.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Dao Domain (道之域境)**: This is a legendary stage of enlightenment where a cultivator's personal Dao becomes so refined that it actively influences the environment around them. Unlike a simple "domain" in Western fantasy, a xianxia Dao Domain is the literal projection of a cultivator's Dao-heart into reality. Ji Ning's domain takes the form of gentle rain—a reflection of his sword-art's fluidity and connection to water. It's so natural that ordinary cultivators mistake it for mundane weather, which is precisely the point. If you see rain in a formation, and you didn't summon it yourself—run.

**Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation (冥龙锁天阵)**: A five-node defensive formation (Head, Body, Claws, Pearl, Tail) that coordinates the power of multiple Zifu Disciples. Each node controls a part of the dragon, and together they form an inescapable cage. Ji Ning, riding the Tail node, turns the formation's own power against its users. The name "Blackflame" evokes the primordial darkness of Pangu's era—an appropriately ominous title for such a lethal array.

**Eight Trigrams Blood Dragon Formation (八卦血龙阵)**: A classic Daoist battle array based on the eight trigrams (Bagua). Each trigram corresponds to a natural element. The blood dragon variant adds a sacrificial edge—common among demonic or ruthless sects like Snowdragon Mountain. It's a formidable defensive formation, but it can't save them from a hunter who's already inside their cage.

### Fiendgod Body Refining

A cultivation path that strengthens the flesh to godlike levels, granting abilities like rapid regeneration; Ji Ning heals from a talisman blast instantly.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point for Ji Ning’s combat career. Watch how he transitions from a reactive fighter to a proactive hunter. The Rainwater Sword Domain is not just a power-up—it’s a philosophical statement. He’s no longer relying purely on raw strength; his own Dao is beginning to shape the battlefield. The dramatic moment where the eight cultivators realize the rain is Ji Ning’s domain is classic xianxia irony: they dismissed a god as a drizzle. Also, keep an eye on Ji Jiuhuo’s transmission. The patriarch’s calm, strategic mind suggests that there’s more to this fight than a simple kill-count. Why warn Ning to hold back? What other layers does the Snowdragon team have? Buckle up—this hunt is far from over.
