## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because the Immortal Fortunes Assembly just got a whole lot more interesting! After weeks of brutal, bloody competition, the Brightmoon Landscape finally reveals its first true dark horse, and it’s not some handsome, brooding sword prodigy—it’s a slovenly, fat youth named Sanpang, aka the Slovenly Realperson. The entire pantheon of Pure Yang True Immortals and Celestial Immortals watching from the Heavenly Edifice is utterly floored when he unleashes the legendary Grand Tortoise Divine Ability, a move that screams “I am the personal disciple of a primordial overlord.” While the high-and-mighty immortals rush to figure out who this nobody from the Black-White College is, our main man Ji Ning is quietly leveling up with Yu Wei, proving that even in a tournament full of monsters, the real monsters are just getting started.

## Key Plot Points
The first half of this chapter is a masterclass in “delegated awe” as the nine Pure Yang True Immortals and nearly a thousand Celestial Immortals freak out over the Slovenly Realperson’s Grand Tortoise Divine Ability. We learn the brutal hierarchy of disciplehood: personal disciples get the real juice (full resources, personal training), while nominal disciples are basically glorified audience members allowed to listen to lectures. The fact that Sanpang got the special inheritance at Wanxiang means the Grand Tortoise Emperor is dead serious about him. Meanwhile, down in the mortal realm, the Wufeng Immortal is just happily huffing copium, thinking his little slovenly might get adopted by a random Celestial Immortal. The scene then cuts to two alpha-dog contenders—Xiamang Zishan and Cangwu Jiu—who are also having a breakthrough moment, with Zishan suddenly comprehending his fourth Dao and claiming he’s the strongest in the arena. Finally, we check in on Ji Ning and Yu Wei. After six months of non-stop slaughter, Ji Ning has an epiphany and goes into seclusion, with Yu Wei standing guard and watching him with a gaze so tender it could melt a Zifu Disciple’s Dao-heart.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Grand Tortoise Divine Ability (玄武大神通):** This is a direct callback to Xuanwu (玄武), the Black Tortoise, one of the Four Symbols (Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, Black Tortoise) in Chinese constellations. Xuanwu is a composite beast—a tortoise entwined with a serpent—and is worshipped as a powerful deity of the north, water, and longevity. In the Desolate Era cosmology, the Grand Tortoise Emperor is a bona fide primordial overlord, so having his technique is like showing up to a street fight with a nuclear launch code. The visual of Sanpang’s skin turning into a tortoise shell pattern is a classic xianxia way of showing that the body itself becomes a divine artifact.

**Nominal vs. Personal Disciple (记名弟子 vs. 亲传弟子):** This is one of the most important social hierarchies in xianxia. A nominal disciple is basically a fan with a backstage pass—they can listen to lectures but rarely get personal attention. A personal disciple is the chosen one who gets the family jewels (secret techniques, resources, protection). The gap is so massive that even Celestial Immortals are jealous of a Wanxiang kid who gets personal-disciple treatment. This explains why the entire Heavenly Edifice is in an uproar: Sanpang isn’t just strong; he’s *backed*.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a brilliant example of how xianxia uses **reputation shock** to build stakes. The Slovenly Realperson hasn’t even done anything on-screen in this chapter, yet his off-screen impact is enough to make the highest powers in the land scramble for intel. It’s a classic trick: show the immortals panicking, and you instantly know the kid is a big deal. Meanwhile, the parallel progression of Xiamang Zishan and Ji Ning reminds us that this tournament has multiple legitimate powerhouses, not just one lucky slob. Keep an eye on Sanpang—his “sloppy on the outside, terrifying on the inside” archetype is a fan favorite for a reason. Also, that quiet moment between Ji Ning and Yu Wei is a rare oasis of calm before the brutal battles ahead; savor it while it lasts.
