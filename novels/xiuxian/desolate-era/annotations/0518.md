## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists—the war meeting is finally here, and the Xia Emperor is laying out the survival plan for the Grand Xia. This chapter is all about scale and stakes: eleven thousand Celestial Immortals, over a million Loose Immortals, and a battle formation so massive it turns an army into a living divine spirit. The mood shifts from awe at the sheer numbers to a quiet, chilling recognition that even the most powerful world might fall. It’s strategy porn, Xianxia-style, with a side of mythological backstory that makes you realize just how deep the power tiers go.

## Key Plot Points
- The Xia Emperor reveals the full extent of the Seamless Gate’s conquests: several hundred major worlds already taken, over a thousand in active conflict. The Grand Xia is now the frontline.
- A grand strategy is unveiled: out of 11,000 Celestial Immortals, 9,000 will be divided into nine armies, each with 1,000 Celestial Immortals and 100,000 Loose Immortals, all training in the legendary Xingtian Formation.
- The backstory of Xingtian the True God is recounted: a decapitated Fiendgod who turned his nipples into eyes and navel into a mouth, broke through to True God mid-battle, and killed the Celestial Thearch. This is the trump card the Xia Emperor is deploying.
- Commanders for the nine armies will be chosen from among the Celestial Immortals, not the Empyrean Gods/True Immortals, through a practical test of formation control. Only those who master the formation in half a month can compete.
- Yu Wei quietly points out Dongyan老祖 among the five True Immortals present, highlighting how quickly power levels are escalating around Ji Ning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Xingtian Formation (刑天阵) and Its Logic**
In Chinese mythology, Xingtian is one of the most badass figures in the *Classic of Mountains and Seas*—a severed giant who never stopped fighting. The novel adapts this into a battle formation where 100,000 Loose Immortals act as the *foundation* (drawing ambient energy from a million kilometers), 1,000 Celestial Immortals act as the *skeleton* (directing that energy), and a single commander acts as the *brain*. The result is a temporary divine body so durable that even a True Immortal can’t easily break it. This is peak Xianxia "scale magic": turning quantity into quality through formation theory.

**The Status of Loose Immortals in War**
Remember that Loose Immortals (Ki Refiners who failed their tribulation and live under periodic doom) are usually treated as second-class in peace—but in war, they become the *indispensable engine*. Why 100,000 per army? A single Loose Immortal can draw energy from a 10,000km radius; multiply that by 100,000, and you get a million-km range. Their raw, untamed power is harnessed by the more disciplined Celestial Immortals. It’s a beautiful example of how cultivation tiers work together in Xianxia worldbuilding.

**The Ancient Human Imperial Clan**
The Xia Emperor’s trump card isn’t just the formation—it’s that he got it from Xingtian himself, who is a True God of the ancient human imperial clan. This clan includes figures like Suirenshi (who discovered fire) and Shennongshi (the Divine Farmer). In the novel’s cosmology, these legendary mythic figures are the absolute peak of power, so ancient that even the Daoist and Buddhist leaders tread carefully around them. The Xia Emperor’s connection to them is what gives his words weight.

## Reading Guide
This is a setup chapter, but don't sleep on it. The real treat here is watching Ji Ning process the *scale* of what’s coming—and realizing that even his monstrous talent is just one cog in a machine of thousands. Pay attention to the quiet moment where Yu Wei points out Dongyan老祖: it’s a gentle reminder that the people they know are caught in the same gears. The chapter also sets up a competitive selection process for the nine commanders, which means we’re about to see which Celestial Immortals have the *leadership* chops, not just individual combat power. That’s a rare angle in progression fantasy. Also, Xingtian’s backstory is just metal as hell. Keep your eyes peeled for how this formation plays out in actual battle—because when a 100,000-immortal army manifests a headless god of war, you *know* it’s going to be spectacular.
