## Chapter Overview

It's celebration time at the Black-White College! The Diancai Immortal has officially shed his mortal coil and become a Celestial Immortal, and the entire sect is partying like it's the Primordial Fiendgod Era. But this chapter isn't just about popping cosmic champagne. It’s about the ripple effects of a single breakthrough—how one ascension reshapes the political landscape of an entire commandery. Get ready for a masterclass in Xianxia diplomacy, complete with a legendary figure stepping out of the shadows after eons. The core takeaway? In this world, power attracts power, and favors of unimaginable weight are the currency of survival.

## Key Plot Points

- The Black-White College celebrates as the Diancai Immortal publicly announces his successful tribulation, solidifying his status as the sect's second Celestial Immortal in history. The atmosphere is electric with hope and renewed confidence.
- The Wufeng Immortal dispatches the Bihai Immortal to inform the Anchan Northmont clan. The Tuoshan Celestial Immortal, initially shocked, sees the strategic value of a new Celestial Immortal ally in these turbulent times.
- In a private council, Tuoshan reports to his elder brother, the legendary "Tonghe Ancestor," a surviving Celestial Immortal from the Primordial Fiendgod Era. Tonghe decides to bet big on Diancai, offering five top-grade Immortal-ranked flying swords from his personal collection.
- The Diancai Immortal is presented with the stunning gift at the celebratory feast. The sheer value of the swords (top-grade, not high-grade) stuns everyone, including Ji Ning, revealing the clan's immense wealth and strategic desperation.
- After the feast, Ji Ning, Diancai, and Tuoshan travel to the Anchan Marquis estate. The Diancai Immortal reveals his knowledge of the Tonghe Ancestor’s true identity to Ji Ning, setting the stage for a meeting with a being who has not been seen since the mythical battle on the Celestial River.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Value of a Top-Grade Immortal Treasure**: The gift of five top-grade Immortal-ranked flying swords is a truly monumental event in this setting. To put it in perspective, a single high-grade Immortal-ranked treasure is already a clan heirloom. Five top-grade swords are a complete arsenal that would massively boost any Celestial Immortal's combat power. The Tonghe Ancestor's decision to part with them shows he is playing on a completely different strategic level, treating a cataclysm like an incoming flood that requires building the strongest possible dam.

**The Celestial Immortal's Burden**: Becoming a Celestial Immortal isn't just a personal victory; it's a public declaration of a sect's long-term viability. Without a Celestial Immortal, a sect like the Black-White College is just a collection of Primal and Loose Immortals slowly dying off. A Celestial Immortal represents a "true immortal" who can protect the sect's legacy for millennia, acting as a stabilizing core and a powerful deterrent against enemies. This is why the celebration is so ecstatic—it is, quite literally, the sect's survival insurance.

**The Hidden Ancestor Trope**: The revelation of the Tonghe Ancestor is a classic Xianxia trope. Many powerful ancient clans are not ruled by their most public figures but by a reclusive "ancestor" who has lived for eons. These beings are often assumed dead by the outside world, a tactical feint that allows the clan to maintain a secret trump card. Their re-emergence is always a sign that the situation is incredibly grave. The Tonghe Ancestor, a veteran of the Primordial Fiendgod Era, sensing the "storm to come," is a powerful narrative signal that the world's peaceful days are numbered.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a critical moment of recalibration. We see Ji Ning transitioning from a rising star to a recognized pillar of power. It's not just his own strength that's noted, but his potential as a "vector" for even greater forces (the Tonghe Ancestor wants to meet him, sensing his extraordinary background). Keep an eye on the subtle shift in how the Diancai Immortal speaks to Ji Ning—the master is now relying on his disciple's presence to feel secure. This inversion of the traditional student-teacher dynamic is a beautiful payoff for Ji Ning's growth. Also, get hyped—the mention of the Primordial Fiendgod Era is always a promise that the power levels are about to get seriously silly.
