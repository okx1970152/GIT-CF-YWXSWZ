## Chapter Overview
Welcome back to Anchan City, fellow Daoists! Ji Ning returns home to the Swallow Mountain area after over thirty years of divine training, only to find the world has changed. The Ji Clan is alive and kicking, Beishan Baiwei is safe (surprise!), but the entire Anchan Commandery is in turmoil. Forces comparable to the old Snowdragon Mountain are being wiped out left and right. Our beast-skin-clad hero navigates his homecoming with his signature blend of cool analysis and deep emotional gravity—visiting the Black-White College, reuniting with Autumn Leaf, and stirring up a hornet's nest of interest from the Beishan clan's top brass. And just when he thinks the big news is over, he learns his master, the Diancai Immortal, is about to face his Heavenly Tribulation. The stakes are quietly, beautifully raised.

## Key Plot Points
- **Ji Ning learns of the Beishan power shift**: Beishan Yin has become the new Marquis of Anchan, but surprisingly, the defeated Beishan Heihu and his son Beishan Baiwei still live safely in Anchan City—a rare leniency in brutal Xianxia succession politics.
- **Anchan Commandery is in chaos**: Over the past twenty to thirty years, multiple top-tier tribes and sects (comparable to the old Snowdragon Mountain) have been wiped out. The region is on edge, and the Black-White College warns Ji Ning not to be used as cannon fodder by the Beishan clan.
- **Ji Ning's mysterious master remains secret**: The Mountain-Carrying Celestial Immortal pays a personal visit to probe Ji Ning's whereabouts over the past three decades. Ji Ning politely refuses to name his teacher, adding to the legend of his mysterious apprenticeship.
- **The Diancai Immortal is about to undergo tribulation**: The Wufeng Immortal reveals that Ji Ning's master has returned and is in secluded meditation, preparing for his Heavenly Tribulation. This is a major, emotionally charged revelation for Ji Ning.
- **The social landscape has shifted**: Multiple new Earth Immortals have emerged in the Black-White College (including Bihai Daoist), and the old Immortal Duohe and Wusui are now genuinely respectful to Ji Ning after his breakthrough.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **The Politics of Mercy in Ancient Clans**: In standard Xianxia clan succession, the loser is usually exiled or killed. The fact that Beishan Heihu and his son are allowed to stay in their old mansion in Anchan City after being defeated by Beishan Yin is a *massive* sign of unusual magnanimity—or perhaps political calculation. It subtly suggests Beishan Yin is a more sophisticated ruler than a simple vengeful warlord.
- **Eight Great Powers of Anchan Commandery**: The chapter lists the Black-White College, Heaven-Rending Sword Sect, Hundred Flowers Fairyland, Heavenly Saint Sect, Blood God Sect, Lieyan Clan, Mulan Clan, and Donghe Clan as the eight supreme forces beneath the Marquis. In Xianxia, these namedrop lists serve not only as worldbuilding but as a power-tier compass for readers—if these ancient powers start falling, you know the story is shifting from local to universal stakes.
- **The Weight of a Master's Secret**: The Mountain-Carrying Celestial Immortal's visit is a classic Xianxia 'information probe' scene. That Ji Ning refuses to name his master is both a point of pride (his master is powerful enough to defy Crimsonbright Daofather's implied claim) and a point of caution (the Three Realms' undercurrents are so dangerous that even a Daofather worries about exposure). It's the quiet, continental-plate shifting of faction politics.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in **post-timeskip worldbuilding done right**. Instead of info-dumping, I Eat Tomatoes (IET) lets us rediscover Anchan Commandery through Ji Ning's eyes—the tension is in the *details*. The tribal politics are surprisingly humane (Beishan Heihu's survival), the chaos is ominous ("forces comparable to Snowdragon Mountain are being wiped out"), and the emotional anchor is rock-solid (Ji Ning's reunion with Autumn Leaf, his silent awe at his master's impending tribulation). Keep your eyes peeled on that Diancai Immortal tribulation foreshadowing—it's one of those quiet, devastating setups that Xianxia masters love to spring on us. Also, note how Ji Ning's status has *rocketed*: Immortals who once condescended to him now speak with respect and caution. The kid's gone from "promising disciple" to "unsolvable puzzle that even Daofathers are curious about."

## Deep Dive: The Art of the Homecoming in Xianxia
In Xianxia, a character's return to their origin point after a period of intense training is more than just a plot checkpoint—it's a **spiritual consolidation**. Ji Ning doesn't just check on the Ji Clan; he breathes in the scent of his father's old estate in his mind, walks familiar paths in silence, and lets the emotional gravity of 'home' reforges his Dao-heart. This is a deliberate counterbalance to the cosmic-scale power he's accumulating. The genre teaches us that a cultivator who forgets their roots becomes brittle, like a sword forged without tempering. Ji Ning's controlled vulnerability—a brief whisper of longing, a quiet hug—is not weakness; it's the proof that his heart is still beating, still human, and thus still capable of the absolute sincerity that powers his heartforce.
