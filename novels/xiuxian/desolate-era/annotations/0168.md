## Chapter Overview
The newly minted Black-White College disciples step into the Dao Debate Palace, only to find the social battlefield even more treacherous than the puppet arena. Ji Ning meets the enigmatic and captivating Senior Apprentice-Sister Jiulian, whose lotus-like aura immediately draws attention—and the burning jealousy of Yinxue Wenjian. What starts as friendly banter swiftly escalates into a high-stakes challenge: a hundred catties of Primordial Liquid on the line, a puppet duel under the observant eyes of eight senior disciples, and a clash of egos that has been simmering since the mountain cave. The stage is set for a test of both martial skill and nerve.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning, Mu Northson, and Bu Ying enter the Dao Debate Palace and find a group of senior disciples, including the striking Jiulian, who has just lost a debate to Yinxue Wenjian.
- Jiulian shows immediate, unusual interest in Ji Ning, actively drawing him into conversation, which sparks visible jealousy in Yinxue Wenjian.
- Yinxue Wenjian escalates a simple sparring invitation into a high-stakes bet of a hundred catties of Primordial Liquid, attempting to pressure the newcomer.
- Ji Ning calmly matches the bet, producing a Tianbao Mountain token from his Wuyou Cavern winnings—a detail that surprises the senior disciples.
- Both disciples select their puppets: Yinxue Wenjian chooses his signature Big Dipper type, while Ji Ning picks a Thousand Sword puppet.
- The chapter closes with the barrier descending on the arena; the duel is about to begin, leaving the outcome hanging in suspense.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
One of the hidden gems in this chapter is the introduction of the **Dao Debate Palace (论道殿)**, a staple of many Xianxia sects. Unlike a mere sparring ground, it serves as a formal venue for disciples to exchange insights on the Dao. The use of identical-tier puppets ensures the outcome hinges on *comprehension of the Dao*—not raw power, artifact quality, or leftover cultivation from past lives. This reinforces a core Xianxia tenet: true mastery is about understanding the laws of heaven and earth, not just stacking power levels. The token-based currency system (Primordial Liquid tokens from Tianbao Mountain) also highlights how cultivation resources double as a universal high-tier currency, a clever world-building detail that weaves economics into the immortal realms.

## Reading Guide
**Get ready for a bout of psychological warfare before a single sword-strike lands.** This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia social dynamics: the way a simple conversation can trigger a jealous rival into escalating a harmless duel into a grudge match. Yinxue Wenjian’s calculated provocation and Ji Ning’s cool, unflinching response perfectly mirror their earlier cave encounter. If you love scenes where a protagonist’s casual confidence infuriates a prideful antagonist, this one delivers. The puppet-reveal at the end—Thousand Sword vs. Big Dipper—also hints at starkly different combat philosophies. Will raw offense or flexible tactics win the day? Tune in to the next chapter for a clash that’s as much about heart as it is about technique.
