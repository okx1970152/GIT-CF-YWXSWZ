## Chapter Overview

The chessboard shifts. With Ji Ning successfully banished to the Realm of Annihilation, the Seamless Gate turns its attention to the loose ends left behind—namely, the Shaoyin clan. In this chapter, we witness the cold pragmatism of cosmic politics: the Seamless Gate dangles the terrifying evidence of Ji Ning’s power before the Shaoyin patriarch, forcing his hand. Poison Claw Immortal, a survivor of the ancient Pangu era, makes a lightning-fast calculation: uproot his entire civilization, abandon his territory, and beg the Xia Emperor for a truce. When that door slams shut, he does what he does best—he runs—straight into the enemy’s arms. The result is a geopolitical realignment that sets the stage for the coming storm, all while the azure-robed Gate Lord smiles in the shadows, having gained a formidable new weapon.

## Key Plot Points

- The azure-robed woman (Qinghu) from the Fifth World notes that Yu Wei hasn’t slept yet, but knows she must within three days; she muses that Ji Ning is as good as dead, but his corpse can still be useful to lure the Shaoyin clan.
- A golden-robed messenger delivers a Watermark Technique scroll to Immortal Xuanji of the Shaoyin clan, showing the full footage of Ji Ning slaughtering eighteen Celestial Immortals.
- Xuanji, horrified, rushes back to the clan’s World-Sealing Tower and convenes all seven Celestial Immortals.
- Poison Claw Immortal watches the recording, his expression darkening. He recognizes incomparable monstrous talent when he sees it.
- He immediately orders the entire Shaoyin population—tens of billions—to be relocated into the World-Sealing Tower’s inner world. The clan abandons all three of its ancestral lairs.
- That night, Poison Claw Immortal appears at the Grand Xia capital and requests an audience with the Xia Emperor, seeking a mediated peace with Ji Ning.
- The Xia Emperor refuses, stating he will take neither side in a direct conflict between them. He warns Poison Claw Immortal against joining the Seamless Gate.
- Poison Claw Immortal, with no other option, defects to the Seamless Gate. The azure-robed Gate Lord welcomes him warmly as a fierce general.
- The Shaoyin clan’s entire civilization vanishes from the Grand Xia without a trace, a ghost of its former self.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Watermark Technique (水印之术)** is a classic immortal recording spell—think of it as a magical “CCTV footage” that can be replayed by injecting Immortal power. In a world without smartphones, this is how intelligence and propaganda are shared among top-tier cultivators. Its use here is a masterstroke: the Seamless Gate lets the evidence speak for itself, forcing the Shaoyin clan to face the reality of Ji Ning’s power without the Gate having to say a word.

**The World-Sealing Tower (镇界塔)** is an Innate Spirit Treasure (先天灵宝)—a treasure born from primordial natural forces, possessing its own spiritual nature and an internal world. In the Desolate Era universe, such treasures are the ultimate refuge for nomadic civilizations. An entire tribe can pack up and disappear into one, making them the Xianxia equivalent of a mobile home fortress. The Shaoyin clan’s ability to evacuate tens of billions within the span of a single incense stick shows the terrifying efficiency of Loose Immortal logistics.

**Poison Claw Immortal’s survival philosophy** is a classic trope in Chinese cultivation novels: the cunning, slippery villain who always has an escape plan. His line, “A tree moved may die, but a man moved may live,” is an adaptation of the Chinese proverb 树挪死，人挪活 (shù nuó sǐ, rén nuó huó), which emphasizes adaptability over stubborn attachment to land. In the cutthroat world of immortal politics, where a single mistake can mean extinction, this mindset is what has kept him alive for countless millennia.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in *show, don’t tell* political maneuvering. Watch how the Xia Emperor handles the situation: he doesn’t lie, doesn’t bluff—he simply states the truth coldly. His refusal to take sides is itself a declaration of alignment. And Poison Claw Immortal’s reaction? Pure survival instinct. He doesn’t waste time on fury or denial. He *calculates*, he *acts*, he *disappears*. That’s why he’s lasted so long.

Pay attention to the azure-robed woman’s quiet satisfaction. She didn’t have to lift a finger; the evidence did all the work for her. This is the Seamless Gate’s true power: not brute force, but the art of turning enemies into assets. Ji Ning may think he won a battle, but the war has just entered a new front.

Also note the absence of Ji Ning himself. For the first time in a while, the narrative momentum comes entirely from off-screen ripples. The protagonist is stranded in the Realm of Annihilation, and the pieces on the board are already moving against him. That’s the sign of a well-crafted Xianxia—you feel the tension even when the hero is not on stage.

## Character Focus: Poison Claw Immortal and the Shaoyin Survivalist Ethos

The Shaoyin clan’s patriarch, Poison Claw Immortal (毒丑仙人), is one of the most fascinating antagonists in the early Desolate Era saga. He is not a power-hungry tyrant bent on world domination; he is a *survivor* first and foremost. His philosophy is cold and pragmatic: preserve the clan at any cost, even if it means sacrificing territory, pride, or moral high ground. His decision to uproot his entire civilization in a matter of minutes is a testament to his ruthless clarity. In a genre where villains often monologue or cling to their castles until the end, Poison Claw Immortal’s willingness to cut and run makes him terrifyingly effective. He will be a thorn in the protagonist’s side not because he is stronger, but because he is *slippery*.
