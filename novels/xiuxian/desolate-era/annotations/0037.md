## Chapter Overview
Fellow Daoists, strap in—this chapter is a masterclass in the sheer *catharsis* of Xianxia storytelling. A divine beast, cornered by a scheming human and his overpowering formation, is pushed to its absolute limit. We watch as the Skygreen Serpent King endures a brutal grinding assault, its scales ripped away, refusing to submit even in the face of death. Just when it seems like all hope is lost and Tie Muzhan’s gloating reaches its peak, the serpent undergoes a miraculous combat breakthrough, unlocking the legendary Void Shuttle ability and escaping the formation! The turn of tables is delicious, and the fallout is even better. Tie Muzhan’s rage is biblical, and all his hatred gets laser-focused on one person: our boy, Ji Ning. The chapter ends with the hunter becoming the hunted, as Tie Muzhan unleashes his tracking Ice Wasp to pursue the fleeing Ji Ning. It’s a heart-pounding, high-stakes setup that leaves you desperate for the next chapter.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Huan Beast failing to catch Ji Ning on the water, forced to return to the main event: the Skygreen Serpent King’s desperate struggle against the Yin-Yang Dual Qi Formation. Tie Muzhan attempts psychological warfare, trying to convince the serpent that resistance is futile, but the proud divine beast refuses to yield. Eschewing talk, Tie Muzhan amps up the formation’s power, subjecting the serpent to agonizing grinding that tears off its scales and flesh, but the serpent still refuses to submit.

Seeing that the formation alone might kill the serpent before it breaks, Tie Muzhan makes a fatal miscalculation born of arrogance. He offers a “fair” duel: if the serpent can defeat him without the formation’s help, he’ll let it go. The serpent accepts, and a one-sided battle ensues. Tie Muzhan is stronger, his Blackwood Thorn Whip perfectly counters the serpent’s agility, and he relentlessly beats it down. However, this extreme combat pressure becomes the catalyst the serpent needed. In a moment of desperate evasion, the Skygreen Serpent suddenly vanishes and reappears thirty meters away—it has comprehended Void Shuttle.

Panic and rage consume Tie Muzhan as he realizes the serpent, which can now teleport, has completely escaped his grasp. He correctly deduces that Ji Ning’s repeated training battles with the serpent inadvertently pushed it to this breakthrough. Twisting his fury into a grudge, he commands the Huan Beast to release a tracking Ice Wasp, which was coated with Ice Flower Dew when it fired its spike at Ji Ning earlier. The chapter ends with Tie Muzhan setting off in hot pursuit, his murderous intent now fixed on the young protagonist.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a textbook case of a **Combat Breakthrough** (临阵突破), a classic and beloved trope in Xianxia. The principle is that intense, life-or-death pressure can force a cultivator or demon to shatter their previous bottlenecks and suddenly comprehend a higher realm. The Skygreen Serpent had been sparring with Ji Ning, building up its ‘experience bar,’ and the fight with Tie Muzhan was the final push over the edge.

The **Void Shuttle** (虚空穿梭) ability is the payoff for the Skygreen Serpent reaching Unity of Man and Heaven in movement technique. Not all beasts have good spatial talents; this is a unique, overpowered trait specific to the Skygreen Serpent as a divine beast. Tied to the concept of ‘Void’ (虚空), the ability to instantaneously teleport within a short range represents a massive power spike. As Tie Muzhan notes, this ability allows the serpent to access dangerous, treasure-laden locations that are normally sealed off, effectively putting it on a fast track to becoming an even stronger monster.

Finally, the chapter involves **Tracking Method** (追踪术). The Huan Beast’s spike was coated with **Ice Flower Dew** (冰花液), whose scent lingers for days and can be detected by the specialized **Ice Wasp** (冰蜂). This is a common Xianxia logic: leaving a minuscule, imperceptible mark on a fleeing enemy that a specialized beast or technique can follow, forcing the protagonist into a desperate situation rather than a clean escape.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a chase sequence, fellow Daoists! This chapter masterfully sets up a classic Xianxia cat-and-mouse game. Tie Muzhan, having lost his prized serpent, is now a cornered and furious beast himself. His rage is pure fuel, and he will be relentless. Pay close attention to how Ji Ning deals with the pressure of being hunted by a late-stage Xiantian expert. This is the first major test of his guile and resourcefulness.

Notice, too, the beautiful touch of the Skygreen Serpent watching the water after escaping. In its own bestial way, it has formed a bond of mutual respect with Ji Ning. This ‘fighting partner’ relationship is a staple in cultivation stories, where two rivals inadvertently help each other grow stronger. Will this bond pay off later? It’s a thread to keep in mind.

Also, take a moment to appreciate Tie Muzhan as a villain here. His anger isn't just pantomime evil; it’s the frustration of a meticulous planner whose perfectly-laid trap was undone by a wildcard. His reaction—blaming Ji Ning rather than his own miscalculation—is a perfect example of the flawed, arrogant mindset of a Xianxia antagonist who is doomed to fail against a protagonist with a good Dao Heart.
