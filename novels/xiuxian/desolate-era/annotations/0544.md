## Chapter Overview

Calm before the storm. Ji Ning and Yu Wei savor a rare moment of domestic peace as the Seamless Gate refuses to come out and fight, but both know this is just the pause before the hammer drops. Meanwhile, the clouds of a full-scale realm war are gathering across the Three Realms. Veteran Empyrean Gods and True Immortals are being shuffled like chess pieces into new formations, and the arrival of the infamous Jueming Twin Immortals at the Seamless City spells trouble for the Grand Xia camp. And just when Daofather Crimsonbright thinks he’s made a smart move by sending Evergreen and Snowdance to reinforce, his old brother from the chaos era—Patriarch Subhuti—drops a bombshell: a realm war is breaking out, and every disciple is walking into a meat grinder. Get ready, fellow Daoists. The gloves are off.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning and Yu Wei discuss the Seamless Gate’s inaction; Ji Ning analyzes their two strategic options (abandon or reinforce the Grand Xia) while gently touching Yu Wei’s pregnant belly, underlining his personal stake in the coming war.
- Scene cuts to a broken star in the void where 29+ Empyrean Gods and True Immortals have gathered, led by the three overpowered Fiendgod Lords of Tunlong Mountain. The Jueming Twin Immortals use a gourd to transport the entire strike team, then open a spatial passage straight to the Seamless City in Baiji Commandery.
- At the Seamless City, the Sovereign of Greenfox, True Immortal Baiji, and the rest welcome the twins with careful courtesy; no telepathy is used—paranoia runs high.
- On the Eight Dragons Cloud City wall, the Xia Emperor spots the twins and immediately recognizes the danger: their spellcasting can boost the entire enemy army, neutralizing the advantage of Ji Ning’s Xingtian Manifestation.
- Back at Daofather Crimsonbright’s Daoist palace, the Daofather and his disciples are watching recorded battle footage from the Jiuyang Realmverse realm war, which has already consumed 19 Empyrean Gods and True Immortals. The Xia Emperor reports the Jueming Twins’ arrival and requests reinforcements.
- Crimsonbright dispatches Evergreen Sword Immortal and Snowdance Fairy to the Grand Xia.
- Suddenly, Patriarch Subhuti appears, warping the space around Crimsonbright. He reveals that sending those two is a death sentence—the realm war for the Crimsonbright Realmverse is about to erupt, and the Grand Xia is only one front. The Xia Emperor’s forces will be decimated.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Realm War (界域之战)**: A large-scale conflict fought across an entire realmverse—a macro-region of the Three Realms containing multiple worlds. Unlike a local “world war,” a realm war pits Daofather-level forces against each other and can consume thousands of Celestial Immortals and tens of thousands of Loose Immortals. The one Crimsonbright’s camp is watching (Jiuyang Realmverse) has already killed 12 on one side and 7 on the other, a brutality that terrifies even veteran True Immortals.
- **Qi of Fortune (气运)**: A metaphysical resource in Xianxia fiction—a realm’s accumulated “luck” or “destiny.” If a world’s qi of fortune is stolen by an invading force, the original ruler loses his right to rule, making political boundaries a matter of actual supernatural weight. Ji Ning’s brief observation that “as long as the Seamless Gate doesn’t take the field, the fortune stays with us” is a perfect encapsulation of this concept: sometimes not fighting *is* winning.
- **Jueming Twin Immortals (觉明二仙)**: Not named after “awareness” and “brightness” by accident; these two plump, affable-looking cultivators are renowned for their spellcasting and formation-boosting abilities. In realm wars, a support-focused duo like this can shift the balance more than ten frontline warriors. The fact that they arrived via a gourd (a classic Daoist treasure motif) underscores their connection to the more esoteric, “journey-to-the-west” side of the cultivation world.
- **The Seven Gods of Heaven’s Punishment (刑天神)**: The Xingtian Formation that Ji Ning currently leads is one of the most terrifying war assets in the Three Realms. But Subhuti’s quiet assurance that Ji Ning can “escape” even as everyone else dies tells us that even a Xingtian Manifestation is not an automatic win against a properly prepared realm-scale enemy.

### Patriarch Subhuti (菩提老祖)

Ji Ning's secretive mentor, a Primordial True God born from the Chaos who masters the Grand Dao of Spacetime; an old brother of Daofather Crimsonbright.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a textbook example of how Xianxia epic fantasy builds tension before a big battle. We get *two* stages of escalation in rapid succession: first, the local reinforcement (Jueming Twins), which makes the Grand Xia side nervous but not hopeless. Then Subhuti drops the real nuke—the entire Crimsonbright Realmverse is about to become a war zone. Every named character who is sent to the Grand Xia now has a death flag flying over their head. Pay close attention to the way the Xia Emperor reacts when he spots the twins: that’s a man who knows his odds just got worse, but he hasn’t yet realized the war hasn’t even truly started. And the image of Ji Ning stroking his wife’s belly, trying to hold onto a moment of peace, is the emotional anchor for all the violence to come. The phrase “calm before the storm” has never been more literal in a cultivation novel.

## The Three Lords of Tunlong Mountain — Why They Matter

Among the reinforcements gathered in the void, the Three Fiendgod Lords of Tunlong Mountain (吞龙山三妖神) stand out. They are the kind of peak combatants who are *individually* stronger than the Xia Emperor or True Immortal Baiji. In the Xianxia power scale, that makes them almost invaluable pieces. The fact that all three joined the Seamless Gate means that the Nuwa camp lost a strategic ace. In a realm war, having one such lord can decide a battle; having three is a statement of intent. Keep an eye on this trio—their presence alone tells us the Seamless Gate is not messing around in the Grand Xia theater.
