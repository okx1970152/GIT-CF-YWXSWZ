## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to a chapter that is less about bloody sword fights and more about a *mind-blowing lore drop* that redefines everything you thought you knew about power levels! After the intense duels of the Dao Debate Palace, Ji Ning is basking in his winnings—both in precious Primordial Liquid and a newly forged reputation as a freakish monster genius. But the real prize isn't the currency; it's the knowledge dropped on him by College Lord Bihai. In a moment of quiet mentorship, Ji Ning learns the universe’s true hierarchy of *Daos*—from the common ordinary paths up to the absolute, supreme Heavenly Daos. Get ready for a lecture that hits harder than any flying sword, as Ji Ning discovers he’s already walked through the gates of the legendary **Dao of the Sword**, a path that marks him as a true **Sword Immortal**! The stage is set, the lore is laid, and the journey to becoming a legend has officially begun.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Aftermath of Victory**: Still buzzing from his string of victories, Ji Ning collects a massive haul of Black-White Pearls from his gambling battles. His friendship with Mu Northson is solidified as the young white-robed disciple hypes him up.
*   **The Primal Daoist Judgment**: The high-ranking Primal Daoist overseers, who watched the entire tournament, call Ji Ning out for a private conversation. Their casual, gossipy assessments from before transform into profound respect.
*   **The Revelation of the Daos**: College Lord Bihai drops a cosmology bomb. He explains the rigid hierarchy of the Dao: the supreme **Heavenly Dao**, the exalted **Grand Daos**, and the common ordinary **Daos**. He uses this to explain why Ji Ning, who has only grasped a single path (the Rainwater Dao), could defeat someone like Beishan Xuantang who had grasped five.
*   **The Birth of a Sword Immortal**: The bombshell: Yu Wei was able to beat Ji Ning because she is a reincarnated Immortal, *and* because her comprehension is deeper. More importantly, Ji Ning learns that the path he is on—the Dao of the Sword—is one of the highest **Grand Daos**. By achieving ‘Sword Heart Illumination’, he has automatically qualified as a true **Sword Immortal**.
*   **A Grand Reward**: For this monumental achievement, the Black-White College awards Ji Ning a staggering two thousand Black-White Pearls, ten times more than a mere Domain of the Dao.
*   **A Quiet Command**: The mysterious Diancai Immortal, his mentor, appears only through a mental message, telling Ji Ning to consolidate his gains and visit him in three days. The chapter ends with a clear announcement: the **Eighth Scroll: Yinglong Guard** has officially begun.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Dao Hierarchy (天道、大道、道)**: This chapter gives us a beautiful glimpse into the Xianxia concept of the Dao. In Daoist philosophy, the ‘Dao’ is the ineffable, ultimate principle that underlies all of reality. This novel literalizes that into a combat-power tier system. Think of it like this: **Ordinary Daos** (like Rainwater, Ice) are the foundational laws of physics you learn in school. **Grand Daos** (like the Dao of the Sword, or Space) are like mastering an entire branch of science—chemistry or quantum physics—which gives you exponentially more leverage over the world. **Heavenly Daos** are the absolute laws of the universe itself—the speed of light, the law of gravity—things that cannot be broken and which even the gods must obey. Ji Ning skipping straight to a Grand Dao is like a high school student publishing a paper that rewrites the laws of thermodynamics.

**Sword Immortal (剑仙, Jian Xian)**: In classic Chinese mythology, the term ‘Sword Immortal’ isn't just a title for anyone good with a sword. It specifically refers to a cultivator who has chosen the ‘Way of the Sword’ as their path to immortality. These figures (like the famous Lu Dongbin) are often portrayed as free-spirited, highly skilled, and incredibly deadly. In *Desolate Era*, this has been codified: you aren't a Sword Immortal until you've formally entered the Grand Dao of the Sword by achieving **Sword Heart Illumination**. It’s a formal rank, not just a compliment.

**The Yinglong Guard (应龙卫)**: The chapter title itself is a hint at the next major arc. The Yinglong Guard is the Grand Xia Dynasty's elite corps of Immortal cultivators. It's where the most powerful and talent-rich young geniuses of the realm gather. Ji Ning’s reputation here marks him as a prime candidate, and we know his goals will now point toward joining their ranks. This is a classic Xianxia trope: the sect arc is ending, and the national-/realm-spanning tournament/army arc is beginning.
