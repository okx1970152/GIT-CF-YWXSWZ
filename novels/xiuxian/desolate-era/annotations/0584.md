## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up—this is a pure **cultivation and lore information dump** of the highest order, and if you’ve ever wondered what makes the *absolute top-tier* treasures in Desolate Era tick, this is your chapter. Ji Ning just picked up a set of **three thousand six hundred Star-Gold Beads**, and they’re not just any shiny objects—they are an **Innate Supreme-grade Spirit-Treasure** bearing the legendary **Ninefold Chaos Seal**, a seal so profound that *even Lady Nuwa and Daoist Three Purities* couldn’t fully crack it. What follows is a beautiful cascade of cosmic history, artifact pride, and a jaw-dropping display of Ji Ning’s absurd potential that makes an ancient artifact spirit eat his words. Get ready for some mind-expanding cosmology and a power-up that’s less about fighting and more about **understanding the universe itself**.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning refines the three thousand six hundred Star-Gold Beads with his Pure Yang divine power in seconds, leaving his soul imprint. The artifact spirit—a proud, handsome child—immediately starts bragging.
- The child reveals the bead’s origin story: they were formed from star crystal nuclei in the chaos, nurtured in Pangu’s World, then acquired by Daoist Three Purities, who erased all prior seals and fused the **Ninefold Chaos Seal** onto them.
- The Ninefold Chaos Seal is a natural manifestation of the chaos itself, infinitely profound and ever-changing—like pi—and impossible to fully refine by brute force alone. Even Lady Nuwa (pre-Pangu level) only got to the eighth level in three years; Daoist Three Purities reached only the seventh.
- The child claims the beads are the basis for the legendary **Slaughter God Diagram** (a.k.a. the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation), making them a “supreme killing treasure,” though the other artifact spirits tease him for being self-proclaimed.
- Ji Ning sits down to try. Using his **Level 4 Heartforce**, he forces his way through the first level of the Chaos Seal in one go—a feat that impresses even the spirits.
- Upon refining it, Ji Ning gains an epiphany: compared to this Chaos Seal, the **Grand Dao of Dripping Water** is simplicity itself. His comprehension of it skyrockets immediately.
- He then demonstrates his crazy talent by merging the thirty-six hundred beads into **Thirty-Six Heavens** (the final fusion stage), a feat that even the strongest disciples of Daoist Three Purities could never achieve. The artifact spirit is completely floored.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Innate Spirit-Treasures & the Chaos Seal** – In Xianxia cosmology, treasures born from primordial natural forces (先天灵宝) often have a “spirit” and require soul-imprint bonding rather than simple blood-bonding. The **Ninefold Chaos Seal** (九重混沌禁制) is a fascinating concept: it’s a naturally occurring, infinitely complex pattern that manifests within the chaos itself—think of it as the **DNA of the universe**. Just as the mathematical constant pi has no end and no pattern, the Chaos Seal cannot be brute-forced; you must *grasp its essence*. That Daoist Three Purities, the supreme leader of the Daoist path, could only refine the seventh level after endless ages tells you everything about its depth.

**Three Purities’ Legacy** – The trio of artifact spirits debating here (the proud child, the old man on the boat, the bald monk) are classic Xianxia tropes: ancient spirits who have spent eons bickering and waiting for a worthy master. Their dynamic adds levity to what could otherwise be a dry lore dump. The mention that the **Slaughter God Diagram** (诛仙剑图, often translated as the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation) was *based* on this Chaos Seal adds an incredible layer of retroactive significance—this bead set is essentially the prototype of the deadliest formation in Daoist myth.

**The Thirty-Six Heavens** – Condensing 3,600 beads into 36 is explicitly described as a **Daofather-level** feat. The Chinese term “三十六诸天” references a classical Daoist/Buddhist division of layers of heaven. For a cultivator at Ji Ning’s stage (Wanxiang Adept) to achieve this, even with Heartforce, is an impossible miracle—it shows that his soul and willpower have already surpassed the bottleneck of his cultivation realm, and that Heartforce is truly a cheat code when it comes to controlling complex treasures.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a **reader’s delight** if you love the *“power system exposition + protagonist flex”* combo. It’s almost entirely dialogue and internal reflection, but don’t skip a single line—every sentence is loaded with worldbuilding juice. Pay attention to the artifact child’s pride and the other spirits’ teasing; it gives the beads a personality that makes them feel like a true companion rather than just a stat boost. If you’re a fan of the deeper metaphysics in *Desolate Era*, the comparison between the Chaos Seal and the Dao hierarchy is a **gold mine**—it justifies why Ji Ning suddenly starts comprehending the Grand Dao of Dripping Water at turbo speed. Also, enjoy the moment when the smug artifact spirit finally gets humbled: his expression when Ji Ning pulls off the Thirty-Six Heavens fusion is *chef’s kiss*.
