## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, Daoists! Chapter 21 lights the fuse on a powder keg. The initial wave of Snowdragon Mountain Zifu attackers has been annihilated, leaving only the fleeing Nong Zidao to warn the next batch. The Ji Clan breathes a sigh of relief, thinking they've weathered the storm and just need to wait for the Grand Xia envoy. But then, a **Wanxiang Adept**—a True Immortal—arrives on the scene, a being from an entirely different power tier. This isn't a minor branch struggle anymore; the main sect has taken notice. The chapter brilliantly sets up two parallel tracks of tension: the looming existential threat from outside (the Wanxiang Adept attacking the formation), and the quiet, world-shaking internal transformation happening within it (Ji Ning's breakthrough). Get ready for a clash of strategy, raw power, and the birth of a new legend.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Messenger and the Trap:** Nong Zidao frantically warns a junior disciple not to enter the Oxhorn Mountain formation, revealing that Tongyu and his entire team perished inside.
*   **A False Sense of Security:** The Ji Clan elders analyze the situation, concluding that their desperate gamble has worked. With the local branch's Zifu elites decimated and only two days until the Grand Xia envoy arrives, they believe they are safe.
*   **The Wanxiang Adept Arrives:** True Immortal Xu Li, a Wanxiang Adept of Snowdragon Mountain, arrives with a new squad of eight Zifu Disciples. He does not charge in blindly.
*   **Analyzing the Silence:** Xu Li deduces the fate of the previous force through a simple but chilling clue: the ground tremors from battle have completely stopped, meaning the fighting is over, and not in their favor.
*   **The First Test of the Formation:** Xu Li, respecting the unknown formation's power, chooses to attack from a distance using his **Wanxiang Projection**. He smashes a massive magical seal down onto the mountain.
*   **Ji Ning's Breakthrough:** While the clan fights for their lives, Ji Ning is deep within the formation, on the verge of a monumental breakthrough in the Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens. He comprehends the principle of Yin-Yang mutual transformation.
*   **The Birth of the Lotus:** The breakthrough triggers a cosmic phenomenon: Sunstar True Fire and Taiyin True Water descend across the void, forming a massive Water-Fire Lotus that envelops Ji Ning as its core, a spectacular sight visible even from outside the formation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The Power Tier Gap: Wanxiang Adept vs. Zifu Disciple
This chapter showcases a classic Xianxia principle: the sheer, qualitative gap between major cultivation realms. In the last chapter, a group of Zifu Disciples was a terrifying threat to the Ji Clan. But the arrival of a single **Wanxiang Adept** immediately changes the equation. The text explicitly states that a Wanxiang Adept attacking from a distance with their **Wanxiang Projection** can only use a *tenth* of their full power. Despite this handicap, his attack strains the entire Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation, which was previously capable of crushing multiple Zifu-level enemies. This emphasizes that a higher realm isn't just more *ki*; it's a fundamentally different quality of existence and power. For a young clan like the Ji, a Wanxiang Adept is an existential calamity.

### The Blackflame Dragon Prison Formation in Action
This chapter gives us our first real look at this defensive formation in combat. It's not just a static shield; it's a coordinated fighting system. The formation manifests a massive black dragon, and the controllers can dynamically swap roles (like making the azure-armored Flood Dragon the "Tail" node). We see the "Tail" of the dragon, which is the most flexible node, used to parry the Wanxiang Adept's seal. This confirms that the formation's strength is in its ability to link multiple weaker cultivators into a single, formidable entity, allowing them to punch far above their individual weight classes—at least for a little while.

### The Meaning of the Water-Fire Lotus
Ji Ning's breakthrough to the seventh level of the Crimsonbright Diagram is marked by the appearance of the **Water-Fire Lotus**. This is a powerful symbolic image. Water (Taiyin) and Fire (Sunstar) are fundamentally opposed forces. By comprehending their mutual transformation—how one gives rise to the other in an endless cycle—Ji Ning achieves harmony. The lotus itself represents purity, spiritual enlightenment, and rebirth in both Eastern and Western traditions. The image of him as the "stamen" of this divine lotus highlights his central, catalytic role in this event; he is not just absorbing power, but becoming the very heart of a new cosmic understanding, a perfect fusion of the two supreme celestial forces.

## Reading Guide

*   **Contrast is Key:** Pay attention to the difference in tone between the Ji Clan elders in the first half of the chapter and their reaction when Xu Li arrives. The shift from proud relief to sheer panic is a masterful setup for the threat to come.
*   **Watch the Real Battlefield:** The physical battle outside is a diversion. The *real* climax of this chapter is Ji Ning's internal battle for enlightenment. His quiet, deep meditation is the polar opposite of the violent clashes, but it holds the key to the clan's survival.
*   **A Setup for a Clash of Titans:** This chapter sets the stage perfectly for the next one. We have a Wanxiang Adept attacking from the outside, and Ji Ning, a peak Xiantian Fiendgod Body Refiner on the cusp of a Zifu-level breakthrough, inside. The question isn't *if* they will clash, but *when* and *how* the newly empowered Ji Ning will match this monstrous opponent.
*   **Don't Forget the Time Limit:** The Grand Xia envoy's two-day deadline is still ticking. Every moment of delay is a victory for the Ji Clan. The Wanxiang Adept being cautious and analyzing the formation is buying Ji Ning the precious time he needs.
