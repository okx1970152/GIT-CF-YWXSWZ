## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow cultivators, buckle up. Chapter 426 delivers the emotional gut-punch we’ve been waiting for, followed by a masterclass in strategic planning. Ji Ning finally finds his junior apprentice-brother, Mu Northson, but the reunion is not one of joy—it’s a brutal confrontation with years of hidden trauma. Northson is a shell of his former self, hollowed out by grief and forced labor. But our favorite Sword Immortal doesn't flinch. The chapter lays out a daring, high-stakes prison break that involves using a divine ability to turn into a single strand of hair—yes, you read that right—to infiltrate an impenetrable fortress, snatch a legendary soul, and steal a super-weapon puppet. It’s a classic Xianxia heist plot, woven with deep emotional stakes that will test the unbreakable bond of the two brothers.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Reunion:** Ji Ning confirms the bad news. The mysterious force has Northson trapped in a forbidden zone, bound by a brutal Heavenly Dao Oath that will annihilate his soul if he tries to escape.
*   **The Prize:** Northson reveals the forbidden zone’s primary project: a terrifying divine-fiend puppet. A Celestial Immortal piloting this thing is practically invincible below the True Immortal level. It's a game-changing war machine, forged over decades by all the captured puppet grandmasters.
*   **The Infiltration Plan:** Ji Ning reveals he can use the Seventy-Two Transformations to turn into a strand of Northson’s hair. He’ll hitch a ride into the heavily guarded inner sanctum where the puppet is stored, completely undetectable.
*   **The Crucial Weakness:** Northson points out a fatal flaw in the plan. To force the enemy to release Yu Xia’s soul, Ji Ning would have to use his signature Immortal swords, giving away his identity. This could turn a simple prison break into a personal feud with a terrifyingly powerful force.
*   **The Final Choice:** Ji Ning acknowledges the risk but refuses to leave his brother behind. He presents Northson with a simple ultimatum: agree to the plan, or Ji Ning will go in alone. The chapter ends on this gut-wrenching decision, placing the emotional weight squarely on Northson’s shoulders.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **The Heavenly Dao Oath (天道誓言):** In Xianxia, this isn't a pinky promise. It's a metaphysical contract with the universe itself. Breaking it—like trying to leave the Eastwoods Mountains—results in instantaneous soul death. It’s a perfect narrative device to show the absolute control the enemy force has over its prisoners.
*   **Heist Mechanics:** The plan here is a pure Xianxia heist, which is always fun. The roles are classic: the Infiltrator (Ji Ning as a hair), the Inside Man (Northson), the Goal (a super-weapon), and the MacGuffin (Yu Xia’s soul). The trade-off between time-sensitive objectives (seizing the soul vs. seizing the puppet) creates a tight, clever strategic layer that elevates the chapter beyond a brute-force escape.
*   **True Immortal & True God (真仙 / 真神):** The chapter clarifies a critical power hierarchy. The divine-fiend puppet makes a Celestial Immortal “nearly invincible beneath the True Immortal and True God level.” This reinforces the incredible ceiling of power in this universe. True Immortals and True Gods are the ultimate benchmarks; anything below that, even with the best toys, is playing catch-up.
*   **Seventy-Two Transformations (七十二变):** This is a legendary divine ability, famously used by Sun Wukong (the Monkey King) in *Journey to the West*. It allows the user to take on the form of any object, creature, or person perfectly. In this context, Ji Ning using it to become a hair on his brother's head is a brilliant, practical application of a classic trope.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, because this is where the emotional stakes and the tactical plotting converge into a perfect storm. Pay very close attention to the way Northson’s trauma is described—the “ritualized grief” of carving statues, his hollow silence. This is a masterful piece of character writing that completely changes the tone of their reunion. On the flip side, watch Ji Ning’s response. His initial desire to kill is instantly tempered by a cold, calculating resolve to save his brother and his brother’s love. That switch is the mark of a true cultivator. The plan itself is a joy to read; feel the tension as they discuss the hair infiltration, the soul snatch, and the potential for exposure. The final decision Northson must make is what makes this chapter sing. It’s not about if they can do it, but if Northson can bear to let his brother take that risk. This is Xianxia friendship at its finest.
