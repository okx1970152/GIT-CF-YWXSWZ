## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because Ji Ning just announced his arrival on the big stage—and he did it by kicking a Primal perfection Daoist right in the teeth. The battle at Winged Serpent Lake is over before it even had a chance to become a slaughter, and the result is seismic. Snowfeather Daoist, one of Snowdragon Mountain’s three ancestral patriarchs, came ready to crush a rising star. He left with his tail between his legs, retreating without even being truly defeated. This chapter is a perfect encapsulation of the Desolate Era experience: a clash of supreme sword-arts, a showcase of Fiendgod physical dominance, and a cold, clinical assessment of where Ji Ning stands after nine years of silent cultivation. The kid is no longer a prodigy with potential—he’s a full-blown monster, and the whole Anchan Commandery is about to find out.

## Key Plot Points

- The chapter opens in the heat of battle, with Snowfeather Daoist unleashing the true power of his Heaven-ranked Mandarin Duck Split-Light Swords, transforming them into two hundred-zhang-long flood dragons of pure sword-light.
- Ji Ning, in his Heavenly Transformation, matches the dragons blow for blow, but his Northsea Swords—only peak Earth-ranked—are outclassed, forcing him to switch tactics from technique to raw power.
- Realizing his sword-art is superior but his weapons are inferior, Ji Ning abandons the ranged duel and activates the Windwing Evasion, closing the distance with phantom-like speed—a move that terrifies Snowfeather Daoist.
- Snowfeather Daoist, unwilling to risk close combat against a Fiendgod Body Refiner, immediately uses an escape technique to retreat to his war vessel, then publicly concedes the engagement and withdraws.
- The chapter then shifts into a reflection period: Ji Ning analyzes his growth over nine years, comparing his Crimsonbright Diagram (11th level), Starseizing Hand (1st cycle), and Three-Foot Sword (5th stance) against his opponent’s advantages in treasure quality.
- The Ji Clan elders, especially Ji Jiuhuo and Ji Liuzhen, erupt in ecstatic celebration, recognizing that this battle will make Ji Ning’s name legendary across the entire Anchan Commandery.
- Snowfeather Daoist, aboard the fleeing war vessel, orders Adept Xuke to relocate the Xu clan out of the Yan Mountains, acknowledging that Ji Ning is now on a level where the sect cannot easily coerce him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Power of a “Battle of Fame” (成名之战)**
In Xianxia culture, an “成名之战” (Battle of Fame) isn’t just a fight—it’s a pivot point where the protagonist, by clashing with an established powerhouse, instantly elevates their social standing from “promising junior” to “force to be reckoned with.” The outcome matters less than the fact that the fight was *close* and the opponent *retreated*. Ji Ning didn’t kill Snowfeather Daoist, nor did he need to; the mere act of forcing a Primal perfection Daoist to flee is a declaration that the old rules no longer apply to him.

**The Fiendgod vs. Ki Refiner Dynamic**
This battle is a textbook example of the asymmetric warfare between Fiendgod Body Refiners and Ki Refiners. The Ki Refiner (Snowfeather) has a huge advantage in range, magic treasure quality, and sustained Dao-art output. The Fiendgod (Ji Ning) has monstrous regeneration, speed, and the ability to turn the engagement into a close-quarters death sentence the moment he closes the gap. Snowfeather’s retreat is not cowardice but tactical survival—a Ki Refiner who lets a Fiendgod into striking range has already lost.

**The Starseizing Hand Bottleneck**
Ji Ning’s Starseizing Hand is still only at the first cycle, and the text makes it clear why. This top-ten divine ability requires immense resources of Five Elements quintessence—rare materials that Ji Ning cannot easily obtain in the Yan Mountains. This is a classic cultivation “resource wall”: the technique is there, the talent is there, but the necessary treasures are not. It sets up a future motivation for Ji Ning to explore the wider world and accumulate wealth.

## Reading Guide

- **Savor the “battle of equals” pacing.** This is the first time Ji Ning has faced a top-tier Primal without needing a desperate gambit or a trump card. Notice how he experiments: he matches sword-arts first, then switches to raw speed when he identifies the disparity in weapon quality. It’s a clinical, strategic performance.
- **Pay attention to the reaction of the Ji Clan elders.** Ji Jiuhuo’s emotional outburst—“even if I were to die of old age right now, I would have no regrets”—carries the weight of a man who spent decades fearing his clan would be crushed. This battle is emotional catharsis for them.
- **Watch for the ripple effects.** Snowfeather Daoist’s order to the Xu clan to relocate is a huge signal. The sect is recalibrating: they won’t bow, but they won’t casually provoke Ji Ning anymore. This sets a new baseline for all future interactions with Snowdragon Mountain.
- **Notice Ji Ning’s own assessment.** He knows he didn’t “win” in the absolute sense. This humility is not false modesty; it’s the analytical Dao-heart of a Sword Immortal who understands that victory is a matter of margins. His internal calculation of weapon quality, divine ability levels, and resource bottlenecks is a roadmap for his next phase of growth.

## Deeper Dive: The Artifact Spirit and the Hierarchy of Magic Treasures

For readers new to Xianxia, a passing line in Ji Ning’s reflection deserves unpacking: the Mandarin Duck Split-Light Swords have “nascent intelligence.” In Desolate Era’s treasure hierarchy, Mortal, Earth, and Heaven-tier artifacts are lifeless tools—powerful, but unthinking. Only at the *Immortal-tier* does a treasure begin to develop a true artifact spirit (法宝之灵), a sentient entity that can communicate, offer advice, and operate the treasure independently to some degree. Snowfeather’s swords are Heaven-tier, top-grade, meaning they are *approaching* that threshold—their dragon forms have living eyes and a spark of instinct, but not yet a full soul. This is why the black ox in the aquatic palace is such a huge deal; he is the fully-formed spirit of Celestial Immortal Juhua’s weapon, a being with memories and will. As Ji Ning grows, his own Fiendgod Blood-Refined weapons will also evolve toward this state, absorbing killing intent until they awaken. The moment a weapon gains a voice is one of the most iconic milestones in Xianxia progression.
