## Chapter Overview
Well, folks, the good times are officially over. After the explosive chaos of Ji Ning’s breakout, we zoom over to the losing side’s perspective, and it’s a brutal reality check. The silver-armored general, Buchasi, returns to his base in the Donglin Sect only to deliver a terrifyingly cold verdict: the entire sect is to be purged. This chapter is a masterclass in showing the sheer, systematic horror of the enemy organization. It’s not just about revenge; it’s about clean-up, protocol, and the cold calculus of power. We see the cold-blooded slaughter of thousands in mere moments, the general’s return to the ominous ‘Fifth World’, and his subsequent dressing-down by the terrifying Cave Master. The cushy assignment in the Grand Xia World is revoked, and Buchasi is sent to a far more dangerous war front. Buckle up, because the stakes just got a whole lot higher.

## Key Plot Points
1.  **The Fall of the Donglin Sect:** Buchasi, having failed his mission, returns to the Donglin Sect. Without warning, he crushes the sycophantic Sect Leader to a pulp with a phantom mountain. He then gives the entire sect of 39,000 disciples ten breaths to kneel and become his slaves. Only those who kneel before the count of five are spared; the rest, over 20,000 people, are systematically annihilated by the Nine Heavens Divine-Poison Flame Furnace. The sect is utterly erased.
2.  **Return to the Fifth World:** The general and his demon guards retreat through a dark mist tunnel, emerging in a vast, fortress-filled world known as the ‘Fifth World’. This is the heart of the enemy’s domain. The scale of the fortress is described as so immense that even a Celestial Immortal couldn’t see its far end.
3.  **The Punishment of Buchasi:** Buchasi reports to his superior, the Cave Master, a being whose aura rivals the Grand Xia Emperor. He confesses his failure and mentions Ji Ning’s knowledge of the **Arcane Art. The Cave Master, though acknowledging the difficulty, punishes Buchasi for his failure, transferring him and his squad to the war-torn ‘Beishan Major World’ with the heavy task of killing five Celestial Immortals within a century.
4.  **The Grand Scheme is Revealed:** After Buchasi leaves, the Cave Master discusses the occupation of the Grand Xia World with his seductive disciple. He reveals the true reason for their caution: the Xia Emperor is a descendant of the ancient imperial clan of the Primordial Pangu World, possessing hidden depths. Furthermore, the Xia Emperor is a sworn brother of Daofather Yinglong, a major power in the Three Realms, making a direct assault extremely risky.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Dao Oath (天道誓言 / Heavenly Dao Oath):** A core Xianxia concept. This is a magically binding promise sworn to the universe itself. Breaking it results in catastrophic karmic backlash, often instant death or the collapse of one's cultivation. Ji Ning swore one not to reveal the secrets of the puppet castle, which is the only reason the general wasn't executed on the spot. It's a powerful tool to ensure secrets are kept, but also a major plot device that allows the villain time to regroup.
- **True Immortal & Empyrean God (真仙 / 天神):** These are the top-tier powerhouses of the Three Realms, existing above Celestial Immortals. The Cave Master is likely a True Immortal (a peak Ki Refiner) or Empyrean God (a peak Fiendgod Body Refiner), putting him on the same level as the Grand Xia Emperor. This chapter confirms that the invasion force is led by such beings, but they haven't acted yet because they fear the hidden trump cards of the Xia Emperor and his connection to Daofather Yinglong. This also shows the extreme caution required when planning a world-wide invasion in this setting.
- **Primordial Pangu World (上古盘古世界):** The original, primordial world from which the current Three Realms were formed. The Xia Emperor is a descendant of its ancient imperial clan. This is a huge deal in Xianxia lore, as it implies a deep heritage, forgotten techniques, and powerful bloodline abilities far beyond ordinary cultivators. It's a classic 'hidden background' trope that makes the Grand Xia World a much tougher nut to crack than a normal world.

## Reading Guide
- **Empathy for the Enemy?** This chapter brilliantly humanizes the 'bad guys' without making them sympathetic. Buchasi is a competent, ruthless commander who made a mistake. His despair at losing his cushy assignment, his fear of his own master, and his grim acceptance of a near-suicidal mission make him a tragically realistic villain. It makes the organization feel real and scary, as opposed to being full of cackling idiots.
- **Systematic Horror:** Pay attention to how casually the Donglin Sect is destroyed. The countdown, the cold calculation of who lives and dies, the complete annihilation of all life and evidence—it’s industrial-scale violence. This isn't just a revenge killing; it's a corporate restructuring with magical fire. It firmly establishes the enemy as a highly organized, terrifyingly efficient machine of war.
- **The Real Scope of the War:** This chapter is our first major look at the *other side* of this conflict. The 'Fifth World' and the 'Beishan Major World' hint at a multi-world war effort. The Grand Xia World was a plush assignment, a rear-echelon job. Now that the operation is compromised, the front-line has opened up for Buchasi. This expands the narrative from a simple revenge story into a massive, multi-dimensional conflict.
