## Chapter Overview
The cavalry has arrived! And by "cavalry," we mean a seven-colored colossus with a grudge and a spear. After the gut-wrenching betrayal of the Evergreen Sword Immortal, the Crimsonbright Realmverse forces are on the ropes against the Three-Eyed Fiendgod. But just when the despair is deepest, Ji Ning unleashes his ultimate hidden card: the Seven Radiance Empyrean God of the ancient Starseizing Manor, commanded by the legendary Bloodfire Empyrean God himself. This isn't just a power-up; it's a statement. The chapter delivers a masterclass in Xianxia battlefield dynamics—the terror of a Daofather-tier fighting machine, the awe of a long-lost legend returning to war, and the sheer, bone-rattling joy of an upset. Get ready, because the old guard is back, and they’ve come for blood.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning summons the Seven Radiance Empyrean God from the aquatic mansion, a fusion of the seven Starseizing Manor Empyrean Gods. They've been waiting for this moment for an age.
- The Seamless Gate forces attempt to intercept the new arrival, assuming he's just another minor reinforcement. They are catastrophically wrong.
- The Seven Radiance Empyrean God demonstrates his Daofather-threshold power, casually obliterating multiple White-Faced Flood Dragons, Zhu'yan Beasts, and Heavenly God Puppets with a single, elegant spear sweep. A fleeing Empyrean God is also killed, revealing the formation's sheer terror.
- Daofather Ink Bamboo and the Godking immediately identify the formation as the Starseizing Manor's legendary Seven Radiance Empyrean God, commanded by the ancient Bloodfire Empyrean God.
- The Three-Eyed Fiendgod (commanded by the three Devouring Mountain demon gods) recognizes the old legend and feels a mix of fear and arrogance, believing their modern power can surpass the ancient War God.
- The chapter ends on a spectacular collision, with the Seven Radiance Empyrean God's massive spear strike sending the Three-Eyed Fiendgod flying backward. The tide of battle has turned.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Legend of the Starseizing Manor & Bloodfire Empyrean God**: In the Desolate Era's primordial history, the Starseizing Manor was the seat of Daoist Threelives, a Primordial True God of immense power. His most famous retainer was the Bloodfire Empyrean God, a War God so famous his name was spoken in the same breath as Yang Jian, the ancient Heavenly Court's mightiest warrior. The Starseizing Manor fell from grace long ago, but its hidden legacy—the Seven Radiance Empyrean God Formation—remains a devastating trump card. Bloodfire's signature is "snowflakes adrift, hair as red as blood," a visual motif tied to his mastery of a frost-tinged, time-distorting spear Dao.

**Empyrean God Formations vs. Karmic Punishment Deities**: This chapter brilliantly contrasts two types of colossal war-formations. Karmic Punishment Deities (刑天神) are formed by a large number of Immortals, absorbing the power of Heaven and Earth. They are immensely powerful but sluggish to command. Empyrean God Formations (天神阵), like the Seven Radiance, are formed by a small group of top-tier Empyrean Gods. They fuse their own divine power into a single, dense body, making them far more agile and precise—perfect for "extreme martial arts" gods like Bloodfire. The "Three-Eyed Fiendgod" is another variant of this Empyrean God Formation.

**"Legends Never Die" Trope**: A classic Xianxia moment is the return of an ancient, presumed-dead expert. It's not just about raw power; it's psychological warfare. The name "Bloodfire Empyrean God" carries millennia of weight. Just hearing it strikes fear into enemies who grew up on tales of his exploits. This chapter perfectly weaponizes that reputation, showing how a legend's return can demoralize the enemy and galvanize the allies. The fact that a "broken" manor like Starseizing still has this ace up its sleeve is a dramatic, satisfying reversal.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is pure, concentrated catharsis. If you've been feeling the pressure of the Seamless Gate's advance, this is the payoff. Watch how the Seven Radiance Empyrean God fights—it's not brute force like the Three-Eyed Fiendgod. It's elegant, precise, and coldly efficient. The description of his spear creating snowflakes and distorting time is a beautiful example of how high-level Dao comprehension manifests in combat. The throwback to the Starseizing Manor's glory days also adds a layer of "forgotten history" that anchors the current conflict in a wider, mythic timeline. Finally, savor the moment Daofather Ink Bamboo's confident smile falters. That's the taste of a game-changer arriving on the battlefield.
