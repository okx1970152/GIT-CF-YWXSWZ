## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in the classic Xianxia "calm before the storm." We begin with a beautifully serene, domestic morning on Brightheart Island. Ji Ning practices his increasingly pure swordplay while a pregnant Yu Wei watches, and even an old gardener dozes off in the sun. It’s a slice of hard-won peace. But this tranquility is shattered by a massive Grand Teleportation right onto the island. The Seamless Gate has assembled a terrifying strike force—including four clone-level Empyrean Gods/True Immortals and a host of Hellish Enforcers—under the guidance of the traitorous Duchou. Just as all seems lost, the sleepy old gardener stands up with a yawn and a curse, freezing the entire group of enemies with an unseen power. *Someone’s cover just got blown.*

## Key Plot Points

- **Domestic Bliss & Dao Discussion:** The chapter opens with a tender scene between Ji Ning and the pregnant Yu Wei. He explains that his Primaltwin is ready for his tribulation, only waiting to master the Dao of the Sword. Yu Wei, now at the Celestial Immortal level, spends her time leisurely and is content, though she is constantly aware of the dangers that lurk.
- **The Assault Force Arrives:** Immortal Duchou, with his knowledge of the area, executes a perfect Grand Teleportation, landing the Seamless Gate's assassination squad directly on Brightheart Island. The squad includes powerful clone avatars of Immortal Fuhua, Fairy Witheredgrass, Empyrean Qishou and Empyrean Sanyang, alongside Wuxiao and the terrifying Hellish Enforcers from the Bloodcloud Hall.
- **Desperate Call for Help:** Sensing the overwhelming killing intent, Ji Ning knows he has no chance of winning. His only hope is that the Seven Empyrean Gods can be summoned in time, but he is prepared to fight to the death to protect Yu Wei and his unborn child.
- **The Sleepy Old Gardener Revealed:** Just as the enemies prepare to strike, the unassuming old gardener who was pruning bushes and sleeping through the morning abruptly stands up, complains about the noise, and with a casual display of power, *freezes every single one of the 55 intruders in space*. The identity of this mysterious powerhouse is a total bombshell.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **The "Hidden Expert" Trope:** The reveal of the old gardener is a beloved and classic Xianxia trope—the seemingly insignificant, powerless background character who turns out to be an unfathomably powerful being. It highlights the core philosophy of Xianxia: never judge a cultivator by their appearance. This is a staple of the genre, often used to provide a shocking twist and a sudden reversal of fortune.
- **Grand Teleportation (大挪移):** This is a high-level spatial transportation technique that allows a cultivator to instantly travel between two points, even across different worlds or territories within the same world. It’s a mark of a powerful cultivator and can only be performed if one has a precise "lock" on the destination. That Duchou could perform one directly onto Brightheart Island shows his meticulous planning.
- **Hellish Enforcers (地狱死士):** These are not ordinary fierce soldiers. The term "Hellish" implies a cultivation path forged through extreme, hell-like tempering, creating fighters who are utterly fearless and berserk. They are deployed as disposable, high-powered weapons, meant to overwhelm the target with sheer numbers and ferocity before the main powerhouses even need to act.

### Imprisoning Space (封锁空间)

A high-level technique, often used by powerful cultivators, to freeze or seal a volume of space, preventing movement or teleportation within it.

## Reading Guide

Hold on to your robes, fellow Daoists, because this is where the roller coaster gets *wild*. We just had a moment of peace and tranquility, and BAM—enemy at the gates. The tension is masterfully built. You feel Ji Ning’s helplessness; he’s a Celestial Immortal, but he’s staring down four high-tier clones and an army. The dread is palpable.

But then, the curveball. That old gardener we’ve seen sleeping in a previous chapter? He’s the key. The author, I Eat Tomatoes, is a master of this setup-and-payoff structure. The shock isn't just that someone is powerful; it's that he was hiding in *plain sight* on the most targeted island in the world. Now the question is: *Who is he?* Is he the Xia Emperor’s plant? A hidden guardian from the Black-White College? Or something far older and stranger? The identity of this character is about to flip the entire power dynamic of this arc on its head.
