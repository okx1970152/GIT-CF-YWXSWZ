## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to the chaos of the Brightmoon Landscape Painting! This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia "hide-and-seek" with the highest stakes possible. The Grand Xia Emperor is sweating bullets over political schemes among attending Pure Yang True Immortals—especially that Buddha-backed Mahasthamaprapta. Meanwhile, Ji Ning has figured out that fame is a double-edged sword: after his glorious kill of the Floating Cloud Immortal, he's now a prime target on the Heavenly Treasures Mountain "will definitely be top 100" list, which is basically a death warrant in a free-for-all. Watch our boy outsmart a killer white-robed maiden, crush her with an upgraded Water-Fire Lotus and a 700-sword swarm, and brood in a cave about how the hunting is only going to get harder.

## Key Plot Points
*   **Politics at the Peak:** The Xia Emperor frets that Mahasthamaprapta Bodhisattva might use the Immortal Fortunes Assembly to kidnap a promising talent, revealing deep rivalries between Daoist and Buddhist factions even at the level of Pure Yang True Immortals.
*   **Fame is a Curse:** Ji Ning realizes his heroic victory has put a target on his back. He's now one of 32 contestants publicly "confirmed" by the Heavenly Treasures Mountain to be in the top 100—meaning everyone hunts him.
*   **The Disguise Upgrade:** To avoid being swarmed by over a hundred enemies again, Ji Ning swaps his trademark beast-hide robe for a generic black robe. A simple but clever survival tactic in a world of super-senses.
*   **Crushing an Elite:** Ji Ning easily defeats a powerhouse white-robed maiden using his perfected first-grade Earthfire/Coldsnare Water-Fire Lotus combined with the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation. The maiden is forced to concede rather than die.
*   **The Hunting Logic:** The chapter lays out the brutal math of the final stage. Early on you find easy targets, but late-game, only the strongest survive, each hoarding a fortune in token talismans. The real harvest comes from the big fish.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Politics of Immortal Sects:** This chapter gives a great look at the nuanced "faction politics" in the Xianxia world of the Three Realms. The Xia Emperor isn't just worried about brute force; he's deeply concerned about the "Pure Yang True Immortals" who've come to spectate. Each Immortal represents a major power bloc (Daoist, Buddhist, etc.), and their relationship with his master, Daofather Crimsonbright, defines the political landscape. Even at the level of Celestial Immortal ascension, these ancient friendships and rivalries shape the fate of the younger generation.

**The "Top 100" Death List:** In a massive Xianxia tournament like the Immortal Fortunes Assembly, information is power. The Heavenly Treasures Mountain, a neutral intelligence-gathering and trading organization, publishes a list of the 32 most likely top-100 contestants. For the listed warriors, this isn't a badge of honor—it's a strategic nightmare. Every other contestant sees you as the biggest obstacle to their advancement, making you the primary target for ganking squads. This dynamic of "fame as a liability" is a classic and brutal trope of the genre.

**Upgraded Spells & Resources:** Ji Ning's Water-Fire Lotus spell has been dramatically improved. The key was upgrading his "Earthfire" and "Coldsnare" to "first grade." These are not mere ingredients; they are rare, fundamental natural energies that a cultivator must search out and carefully refine. Upgrading his foundational resources from second-grade to first-grade was a year-long investment that paid off, making his spell harder and more durable.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a high-stakes game of cat and mouse! Ji Ning has officially graduated from "rising star" to "the guy everyone wants to murder." This isn't about proving himself anymore; it's about survival through overwhelming displays of power, forcing his enemies to make the right choice—concede or die. Pay close attention to the brutal logic of the late-stage tournament: the real battles are saved for the end, when the survivors are the most powerful and carry the most loot. Also, keep an eye on the sideline drama with Lu Dongbin and the Xia Emperor. Their casual argument about "luck" and who gets to pick first from the promising youths is a huge hint that the real prize of this assembly isn't the top rank, but the attention of these ancient, god-like mentors.
