## Chapter Overview
In this rare and refreshing breather chapter, author I Eat Tomatoes (IET) directly addresses his readers with a classic web novel tradition: the single-chapter vote request. After a hiatus caused by his son's illness, Tomato candidly admits the story's popularity is still recovering and asks fans to dust off their forgotten recommendation tickets. There is no combat, no plot progression, no twists—just a warm, transparent moment between a creator and his community, reminding us that *Desolate Era* is not just a fictional universe, but a shared journey.

## Key Plot Points
- IET acknowledges a long absence from writing vote-request chapters.
- Readers in the comment section prompt him by saying their tickets have been going to waste.
- He explains the reason for the current dip in popularity: a hiatus due to his son's sickness.
- He makes a direct, earnest appeal for recommendation votes to support the story's recovery.
- The chapter closes with a "(To be continued…)" indicating that normal story progression will resume.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Single-Chapter Vote Request (单章求票)** is a beloved, time-honored ritual in the Chinese web novel ecosystem. Authors, especially those on platforms like Qidian, periodically pause the main narrative—sometimes mid-arc—to write a brief, personal note begging for recommendation votes, monthly passes, or other reader metrics. These requests are not seen as annoying interruptions; rather, they are a form of community bonding. A skilled author like IET makes the request feel like a friendly chat, cementing reader loyalty by appearing vulnerable and sincere. For Western readers, think of it as a creator's "state of the union" address, complete with behind-the-scenes honesty about real-life disruptions.

**The Hiatus Effect (停更影响)** is a major reality in serialized web fiction. A long break—whether due to illness, creative block, or personal emergencies—can cause a sharp drop in readership and ranking metrics. Recovery is a slow, uphill climb that requires both consistent updates and direct audience engagement. This chapter is a prime example of that recovery strategy: being transparent, humble, and rallying the troops.

It's a testament to the unique intimacy of the serialized Xianxia community. If you're a new reader, consider this your official invitation to cast your recommendation ticket.
