## Chapter Overview

The battlefield shifts from blood-soaked swords to the shadowy realm of divine strategy. Daofather Crimsonbright’s war council receives a stunning surprise—an envoy from the Divine Farmer, Shennong, one of the legendary Three Sovereigns of Humanity, has arrived. The Sovereign’s intervention injects a wave of cautious hope through the Crimsonbright camp. Simultaneously, across the cosmic battlefield, the Godking of the Seamless Gate finds himself cornered. In his desperation, he turns his gaze toward a secret weapon he has long held in reserve: the bound soul of Yu Wei, Ji Ning’s wife. The chapter masterfully contrasts the public, hopeful arrival of a mythic hero with the private, chilling scheming of a cosmic villain, setting the stage for an emotional and tactical storm.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Divine Farmer’s Envoy:** A young Daoist appears before Daofather Crimsonbright, bearing a message from the Divine Farmer. He confirms the Sovereign is aware of the Daofather Puppet and claims they have a “chance” to win. He requests a private audience, clearing the war room of all immortals and demons.

2. **The Hype of the Three Sovereigns:** Ji Ning reflects on the status of Suiren, Fuxi, and Shennong. The narrative re-establishes them as beings on par with the leaders of Daoism and Buddhism, the reason humanity rules the Three Realms. Their very presence in the discourse shifts morale.

3. **Radiance’s Cautious Analysis:** The Seven Radiance Empyrean God, an ancient veteran of the Dawn War, gives Ji Ning a sober reality check. He admits Shennong’s move brings hope, but warns that the Seamless Gate has similarly terrifying countermeasures. This is a “contest of wits,” and nothing is certain.

4. **Ji Ning’s Personal Motivation:** Amidst the grand strategy, Ji Ning’s thoughts turn to his newborn daughter, Brightmoon. The desire to protect her and Yu Wei intensifies his hunger for power, grounding the cosmic war in a deeply personal, emotional stake.

5. **The Godking’s Desperation:** The scene shifts to the Godking’s dark throne. He is visibly anxious, unable to uncover Shennong’s plan. He calls upon his master—the Godking of the Seamless Gate who merged with the Heavenly Dao—for guidance.

6. **The Master’s Answer:** A mysterious fluctuation grants the Godking the insight he needs. He realizes with cold clarity that the “key” to victory lies not in a new formation or weapon, but in Yu Wei, Ji Ning’s Dao-companion. The chapter ends with her submissive arrival before the Godking, a chilling setup for the next act.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Three Sovereigns of Humanity (三皇)** are the bedrock of Chinese civilizational mythology. In the real-world mythos, they are semi-divine culture heroes: **Suiren (燧人氏)** invented fire, **Fuxi (伏羲氏)** introduced hunting, fishing, and the Eight Trigrams, and **Shennong (神农氏)** taught agriculture and herbal medicine. The novel genius of IET (I Eat Tomatoes) is elevating them from these “ancient inventors” to the absolute apex of cosmic power within the Three Realms. In “Desolate Era,” they are the direct peers of the Daoist and Buddhist founders, the ultimate reason humanity isn’t just another race but the **master of the Three Realms**. Their authority is so absolute that even beings like Daofather Crimsonbright bow in immediate respect. This is a classic Xianxia power-up: taking a revered cultural figure and scaling them to the level of a multiverse-defining “Final Boss” or “Secret Weapon.”

**The interplay between Fate and Free Will** is a core theme here. The Godking is desperate because he can’t see everything. Even his deep connection to the Heavenly Dao has limits, especially when a being as powerful as a Sovereign acts. This creates a tense strategic game where both sides are trying to out-maneuver each other, adding a layer of intellectual conflict to the physical war.

**The “Puppet Master” Trope** is in full effect. The Godking treats his followers, even a powerful one like Yu Wei, as tools to be deployed. His master’s voice provides the “Win Condition,” and he summons her not as a commander to a valued officer, but as a craftsman reaching for a specialized tool. This cold, transactional relationship is the dark heart of the Seamless Gate’s methodology.

## Reading Guide

Gods and giant monsters are cool, but **strategy and desperation** are what make a war *feel* real. This chapter is a perfect example of the *other* battlefield in Xianxia: the information war. The fight isn't happening with swords right now, but with political moves, secret plans, and revealed trump cards.

Get ready for some serious emotional warfare. The chapter sets Ji Ning’s personal happiness (his newborn daughter) directly against the Godking’s cold calculation (using his wife as a weapon). The second half is pure villain POV, giving you a front-row seat to the Seamless Gate’s gears turning. It’s tense, ominous, and proves that sometimes, the quiet moments before the storm are the most terrifying.

Also, pay attention to the status of the “Three Sovereigns.” This chapter establishes them as a massive, untapped force. A single message from Shennong is enough to shift the entire battlefield’s morale. Knowing this will make their future actions—or lack thereof—all the more significant.
