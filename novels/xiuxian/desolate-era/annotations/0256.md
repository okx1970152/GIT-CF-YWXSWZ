## Chapter Overview

Welcome to the Wujiang Immortal Estate, fellow Daoists! But don’t expect a cozy treasure trove. Ji Ning and company crash through the dimensional barrier only to land smack in the middle of a military drill for a thousand-strong fish-monster Dao-Soldier army. And guess who the commander is? A Primal-level, bare-chested mermaid. Yes, *that* kind of welcome. Before anyone can even breathe, the entire valley turns into a tsunami of angry scales and battle armor. The chapter ends on a delicious cliffhanger—but the real meat here isn’t the fight; it’s the philosophical battle between Ji Ning and Nine Lotos that happens right before the storm. Grab your popcorn (and your flying swords).

## Key Plot Points

- Nine Lotos gives Ji Ning a blunt, tactical lecture on the value of networking. She argues that a single act of heroism only creates a debt of gratitude, which is disposable, while friendship is a renewable resource. In her view, Xue Hongyi is winning the “relationship game” simply by chatting more with Shaoyan Nong.
- Ji Ning counters with his Dao-heart principle: forcing closeness with a superior damages his spiritual integrity. He’d rather let relationships grow naturally, even if that means missing out on a powerful patron. This is the *second* major clash of worldviews between the two, and Nine Lotos ends up red-eyed and frustrated.
- The ship finds the estate via Shaoyan Nong’s golden token. The transfer is seamless—lush green mountains one second; a barren, sandy wasteland the next.
- In the distance, a massive assembly of mermaid monsters finishes a synchronized drill. They spot the humans, and the leader—a Primal-level female mermaid with a killer instinct—orders an all-out charge to seize the estate key. The thousand-strong army transforms into a single, towering tidal wave in their Dao-Soldier formation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The debate between Ji Ning and Nine Lotos is a classic Xianxia tension: **personal Dao-heart vs. worldly pragmatism**. In Chinese cultivation culture, “friendship” (交情, *jiaoqing*) and “a debt of gratitude” (人情, *renqing*) are fundamentally different social currencies. A debt of gratitude is transactional—you help someone, they owe you one. Friendship, on the other hand, requires repeated, genuine interaction. Nine Lotos is essentially trying to teach Ji Ning *guanxi* (关系)—the art of strategic relationship-building—which is a huge deal in both Xianxia and real-world Chinese business culture. Ji Ning’s refusal isn’t stubbornness; it’s a philosophical stance that purity of action matters more than social climbing. This tension foreshadows deeper character conflicts down the road.

Also, note the visual: a *bare-chested female mermaid* commanding a Dao-Soldier army. In Chinese folklore, merpeople (鲛人, *jiaoren*) are known for weaving fabrics of unparalleled lightness and weeping pearls. The author reimagines them here as militarized, tribal shock troops, giving the ancient myth a brutal, Xianxia-era upgrade.

### Consort / Dao-companion (道侣 daolu)

A cultivation partner bound by a shared Dao-heart and lifelong commitment, often romantic but always deeply spiritual.

### Tonkin / Tianyou Mountain Range

The vast mountain range where the Wujiang Immortal Estate is hidden, spanning over a hundred thousand kilometers.

## Reading Guide

Don’t skim the dialogue in the first half—this conversation between Ji Ning and Nine Lotos is character development gold. It shows that their relationship isn’t all moonlight and swordplay; they have fundamentally different ideas about how to navigate power structures. Nine Lotos plays the long game of social calculus; Ji Ning plays the game of inner truth. Which approach is “right”? The novel doesn’t hand you an answer—it lets both sides argue convincingly, and that moral ambiguity is part of the fun. Meanwhile, the mermaid army’s formation attack—turning a thousand individual monsters into a single tidal wave—is a textbook example of **Dao-Soldier synchronization** (道兵合一), a staple of high-tier Xianxia warfare. Pay attention to how the group reacts to being outnumbered and outgunned from the very first second inside the estate. This is going to test everyone’s nerves, not just their treasures.
