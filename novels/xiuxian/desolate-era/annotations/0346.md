## Chapter Overview
The Immortal Fortunes Assembly reaches its boiling point as the final three slots are decided. We witness a soul-stirring clash between two titans of overwhelming head-on power—the Slovenly Daoist and Craftsman Wood—where a flaw in a secret art decides the outcome in a split second. Then, Blackstone Recluse demonstrates the devastating suppression of a formation master against a speed-type opponent, proving that even the fastest kite needs airspace to fly. And through it all, the Xia Emperor’s cold, calculated scheming becomes undeniable: he is not just an observer but a king meticulously pruning the branches of power among the great clans. The stage is now set for the final battle, and Ji Ning is about to step into the sealed formation.

## Key Plot Points
- The Slovenly Daoist vs Craftsman Wood fight reaches a fever pitch of brutal head-on clashes. The Slovenly Daoist’s Suturing Waves art stacks power like a tidal surge, pressing Craftsman Wood to the brink of defeat.
- Just as the Slovenly Daoist’s power looks unstoppable, the secret art reaches its limit and crashes. Craftsman Wood seizes the opening and delivers a series of devastating hammer blows, forcing the Slovenly Daoist to concede.
- The Slovenly Daoist accepts his crushing defeat with shocking grace, praising Craftsman Wood as a kindred spirit. The Pure Yang True Immortals remark on his dao-heart—the real jewel is not winning, but how fast you bounce back.
- Blackstone Recluse vs Cangwu Crane begins. Blackstone deploys a Fuxi Staff Sealing Array that nullifies Cangwu Crane’s greatest weapon: speed. Cangwu Crane is trapped within the formation, forced into a slow, grinding close-quarters fight he cannot win.
- Blackstone methodically wears down his opponent with the formation’s advantage and equal close-combat skill. Cangwu Crane is viciously injured and forced to admit defeat, making Blackstone the second member of the top three.
- Lu Dongbin openly teases the Xia Emperor about the matchups, revealing that the Emperor deliberately arranged these pairings to suppress the rising power of the great clans (like the Cangwu clan).
- The chapter ends with Ji Ning rising, Yu Wei’s soft words of caution ringing in the air, as he walks toward the sealed formation for his own decisive battle.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Daofather vs Chaos True God**: The chapter mentions Daofather Crimsonbright and the Black Tortoise Grand Emperor. In the power hierarchy of the Three Realms, Daofathers represent the apex of Ki Refining, while Chaos True Gods are the equivalent pinnacle for Fiendgod Body Refining. These beings can dictate the position of even the Celestial Thearch—they are not advisors but absolute landlords of reality.
- **Surging Waves (叠浪) Secret Art**: This is a classic Xianxia trope where a technique’s power multiplies with successive executions, like a wave building height. The catch, as shown here, is that it has an upper limit. If you cannot land the killing blow before the art crashes, you are wide open. It’s the eternal gamble: stack big, or stack smart.
- **The Dao-heart (道心) after defeat**: The Slovenly Daoist’s ability to laugh off a loss that costs him a Daofather’s direct tutelage is a major Xianxia character moment. Losing isn’t failure; letting the loss break your cultivation is. This is why the veteran Immortals praise him—a shattered dao-heart is worse than death.
- **Imperial Matchmaking as Political Pruning**: The Xia Emperor is not running a fair tournament for the entertainment of gods; he is actively managing the power balance among the great clans (like the Cangwu and Shaoyan houses). A genius from a minor background is a controllable asset; a genius from a great clan is a potential coup. This cold, strategic approach to Immortal politics is a recurring theme.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, warriors of the Dao! This chapter is a masterclass in how Xianxia novels balance hype with consequence. The Slovenly Daoist vs Craftsman Wood fight is a brutal lesson in risk management: no secret art is infinite, and every technique has a price. The true winner here isn’t even the one who advanced—it’s the guy who lost and still made the Hardened Immortals nod in admiration. Watch how a broken technique and a quick recovery can be more impressive than a victory. Then, Blackstone teaches a lecture in tactical suppression: formation masters aren’t just summoners, they are battlefield architects who can nullify your greatest strength by simply changing the geometry of the arena. And the best part? The entire tournament is rigged. The Xia Emperor is playing 4D chess, using matchups to snuff out the political ambitions of ancient clans. This is the grand Xianxia tradition of ‘might makes right, but scheming makes the tournament brackets.’

Also, keep an eye on Yu Wei’s one line. In a storm of explosions and roaring cultivators, her quiet ‘Be careful’ is carrying the weight of the entire Three Realms. Ji Ning is walking into the sealed formation now. The final three await.
