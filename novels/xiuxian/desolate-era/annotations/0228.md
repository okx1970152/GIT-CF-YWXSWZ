## Chapter Overview
Our boy is back! After four years away, Ji Ning returns to Brightheart Island, and it's a homecoming filled with warmth, family banter, and a quiet but immense power flex. He reunites with Qiuye and the grown-up Qingshi, introduces his two Wanxiang-level spirit-beasts (Little Qing and Uncle White), and drops the kind of news that makes clan elders weep with joy: he's joined the Yinglong Guard and claimed a permanent, protected territory around Ten Thousand Swords City. But beneath the celebration, Ji Ning is planning something far more calculated. He's not just here to sip tea and reminisce—he's planting his flag in Yan Mountain for the long haul, and that means a storm is brewing for a certain mountain sect that wronged his family.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning returns to Brightheart Island and reunites with Qiuye (now a Xiantian cultivator) and Qingshi (still Houtian, but a towering young man). The household dynamic is warm and familiar, with Qiuye scolding Qingshi like a true older sister.
- Ji Ning reveals that his pet snake, Little Qing (the Skygreen Serpent), and Uncle White (the Whitewater Hound) are both at the Wanxiang level. Qingshi is awestruck, realizing that his big brother now commands power that dwarfs the entire Yan Mountain region.
- Ji Ning officially announces that he has joined the Yinglong Guard, the Grand Xia Dynasty's elite immortal corps. As a new member, he was granted a protected territory (ten thousand li radius) centered on Ten Thousand Swords City.
- He produces the Yinglong Guard token, which, when hung at the city lord's manor, will project a spectral Yinglong dragon over the city walls—a clear warning to any would-be aggressors that the territory is under the Guard's protection for a thousand years, even if Ji Ning dies.
- The clan leadership arrives en masse. Patriarch Ji Jiuhuo, Granny Shadow, and the elders are ecstatic, immediately discussing plans to expand the clan's territory with the newfound backing. Ji Ning approves, delegating his spirit-beasts to assist in the expansion.
- After three days of clan festivities and mentoring the younger generation (who feel disappointingly average compared to his Black-White College peers), Ji Ning retreats to his meditation chamber and enters the aquatic palace, setting the stage for his next phase of cultivation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Yinglong Guard & Territory Mechanics**: The Yinglong Guard (应龙卫) is the Grand Xia Dynasty's elite immortal corps, named after the winged Yinglong dragon of Chinese mythology—a rain-deity and servant of the Yellow Emperor. In the novel, joining this corps is a massive milestone: it's not just a badge of personal power, but a tool for clan-building. The token Ji Ning gives to the Ji Clan functions as a sovereign 'non-aggression pact' writ large. Hanging it over the gate causes a spectral dragon to appear, signaling to any enemy or rogue cultivator that attacking this city means offending the Grand Xia's state apparatus. Even after Ji Ning's death, the protection lasts a thousand years—a classic Xianxia "face" mechanism where one genius's climb can elevate an entire bloodline for centuries.

**The Unequal Glare of Talent**: The chapter subtly highlights the ruthlessness of the cultivation world's talent hierarchy. Ji Ning returns to his clan, where the young geniuses are celebrated as the best in a generation. To him, they are merely "average." This isn't arrogance—it's a reflection of his environment. At the Black-White College, his peers are the top 0.01% of the entire Grand Xia Dynasty. Seeing his clan's prodigies through that lens is like an Olympic gold medalist returning to a small-town high school track meet. It underscores that in Xianxia, "genius" is a relative term, and the real monsters live in the capitals.

## Reading Guide
This is a "breather" chapter—a warm, satisfying payoff chapter where the protagonist reaps the rewards of his early struggles. The emotional core is Ji Ning's reunion with his "family" (Qiuye, Qingshi, Uncle White), but the strategic core is the Yinglong Guard territory reveal. Make note of this token; it's not just a plot device for safety, but a declaration of intent. Ji Ning isn't hiding. He's building a fortress, openly, in the heart of his enemy's hunting ground. The tranquility here is the quiet before a very deliberate, very cold revenge arc. Also, keep an eye on Qingqing—a Wanxiang perfected Skygreen Serpent with a big mouth and a bigger ego—she's going to be a fun chaotic presence in the claustrophobic politics of Yan Mountain.

The final line, where Ji Ning enters the aquatic palace, is a classic Xianxia "cut to the power-up montage" signal. The party is over; the grind resumes. Next up: what's he going to do with all that loot and insight?
