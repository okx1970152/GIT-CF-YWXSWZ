## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up—because Ji Ning just completed his first major power play in the big city. After shocking the entire Wuyou Caverns with his Water-Fire Lotus, he’s now safely tucked inside the Beishan Blacktiger Estate. But make no mistake: our boy isn’t just hiding. He orchestrated this “accidental” public display like a chess grandmaster, knowing full well that news of a monstrous Zifu genius spreads faster than wildfire in Anchan City. And the payoff? It comes faster than even he expected. The very next morning, the terrifying Beishan Blacktiger himself—father of his new bro Baiwei and a candidate for the next Marquis of Anchan—personally shows up at his courtyard. The chapter’s strength lies in the quiet tension: a calm bath conversation reveals Ji Ning’s cold, calculated strategy, while the final encounter with Beishan Blacktiger delivers the visceral, “you are a speck before me” pressure that Xianxia does so well.

## Key Plot Points
- **Settling In**: Ji Ning is given an opulent guest courtyard in the Beishan Blacktiger Estate, staffed by twelve beautiful maids, including a Xiantian-level head maid. Beishan Baiwei promises to bring records of all major Commandery powers for Ji Ning to study before choosing a sect.
- **The Calculated Splash**: In a private bath conversation with Uncle White, Ji Ning confirms his strategy was deliberate. He wanted to shock the world at the Wuyou Caverns so that all neutral powers would see him as a genius to be recruited or befriended, while his only enemy (Snowdragon Mountain) can’t touch him under the Beishan Clan’s protection.
- **Beishan Blacktiger’s Domain**: In a dark, secluded space, Beishan Blacktiger is seen training—swallowing a massive, hundred-zhang-long black flame serpent composed of pure fire. Beishan Baiwei reports the day’s events. Blacktiger muses on whether Ji Ning is a reincarnated mighty figure, but concludes he must simply be a rare genius with an incredible fortune. He decides to meet Ji Ning personally the next day.
- **The Power of an Aura**: The next morning, Ji Ning is meditating on his Water-Fire Lotus when Beishan Blacktiger arrives with a triangular-eyed retainer. The description is pure Xianxia gold: Blacktiger is like an “ancient, ferocious beast” whose mere presence makes it hard to breathe, while the old man is a “cold, venomous serpent.” Both give Ji Ning a sense of lethal threat, instantly putting Xue Hongyi’s power into perspective.
- **A Brief Audience**: Blacktiger exchanges only a few sentences with Ji Ning. He praises his composure (saying fewer than five Zifu cultivators can remain calm before him), tells him to visit often after joining a sect, and leaves. The entire meeting is a power display and a seal of approval. Baiwei visibly relaxes only after his father departs.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The “Making a Splash” Strategy (一鸣惊人)**: This is a classic Xianxia and historical Chinese political tactic. A rising talent, lacking a patron, deliberately demonstrates overwhelming potential in a high-profile setting. The goal is to become an “asset” that multiple major powers want to claim—so that one’s enemies are deterred by the complex web of interest, and one’s path to a top-tier sect is smoothed. Ji Ning is essentially doing a public beta test of his own market value. The Wuyou Caverns was the perfect venue: a neutral ground where nobles and immortals gather to gamble, where rumors spread at the speed of a divine sense transmission.

**The Beast vs. Serpent Duality (凶兽与毒蛇)**: Ji Ning perceives Beishan Blacktiger as a “primordial ferocious beast” and his retainer as a “cold venomous serpent.” This is a classic Xianxia framing for two types of terrifying power. The beast represents overwhelming, direct, crushing force—the kind that kills you before you can blink. The serpent represents hidden, subtle, deadly precision—the kind that strikes from the shadows when you least expect it. In the hierarchy of a great clan, the beast is the visible fist (the general), and the serpent is the invisible knife (the spymaster or assassin). Ji Ning having a visceral reaction to *both* types in one room is a strong signal of the power level he’s now brushing against.

**Fire Devouring & Fiendgod Cultivation**: Beishan Blacktiger’s practice of swallowing a hundred-zhang black flame serpent is a hallmark of high-level Fiendgod Body Refining. In Desolate Era, truly powerful cultivators don’t just *use* fire—they *consume* its essence, internalizing the Dao of Fire into their very flesh and blood. The flame serpent is not a spell he cast; it is an extension of his own divine will and cultivation base. This is light-years beyond what young Zifu geniuses can do, and it visually establishes the gap between “monster genius” and “monster of the generation that rules a commandery.”

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in **atmospheric world-building through brief encounters**. The actual plot advancement is minimal—Ji Ning talks in a bath, and a powerful guy comes to look at him for thirty seconds. But the *weight* of those thirty seconds is immense. Pay close attention to how IET uses physical sensation to convey power levels. When Beishan Blacktiger walks in, it’s described in terms of Ji Ning’s breathing and his internal “danger sense” flaring up. This isn’t just flavor text; it’s a quantifiable system in Xianxia where a cultivator’s soul and Dao Heart can sense lethal threats long before a fight starts.

Also, notice the beautiful symmetry in Ji Ning’s strategy. He doesn’t want to be a hidden card; he wants to be a public treasure. The moment he showed off his Water-Fire Lotus, he became an invite-only asset for every major sect in the Commandery, and a protected guest of the Beishan clan. This is the Xianxia equivalent of a young CEO giving a TED Talk that goes viral to get a meeting with a venture capital firm. Smart play.

Get ready, though—because Blacktiger’s approval is just the first domino. The sect recruitment war is about to begin.
