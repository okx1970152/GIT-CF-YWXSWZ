## Chapter Overview

What a wild, whiplash-inducing ride this chapter is! We go from the gut-punch of a clan doomed by a single reckless killing to a sudden ray of hope, only to have that hope smashed by the cold reality of sect retaliation. Then, just as the Ji Clan is debating the merits of scattering into the wilderness, the ultimate trump card arrives: a warship flying the banner of the **Marquis of Anchan himself**. The chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia tension—a rapid cycle of triumph, terror, calculation, and awe that leaves you breathless.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Aftermath of the Slaughter**: Ji Yichuan and Ji Jiuhuo arrive to find Xu Li and Nong Zidao dead. The Patriarch is shocked to learn that Ji Ning killed a Wanxiang Adept with a single move, instantly transforming the clan's outlook.
2. **Uncle White's Breakthrough**: The Whitewater Hound, having absorbed Primordial Liquid, has finally reached the Xiantian level and can speak. His reunion with Ji Ning is a rare warm moment amid the carnage.
3. **The Fallen's Last Threat**: The captured Zifu Disciples reveal a horrifying truth: Xu Li's death will be instantly detected by Snowdragon Mountain, and a stronger expert will arrive within a day. The earlier victory feels hollow.
4. **Cold Execution**: Ji Ning, using his Divine Sense to wield the Annihilation Demon-Slaying Sword without any visible fluctuation, effortlessly slaughters all seven prisoners—demonstrating his terrifying new level of control.
5. **The Snowdragon Response**: We cut to Snowdragon Mountain's headquarters. The peak master **Xue Hongyi**, a top-three Wanxiang Adept and a member of the Yinglong Guard, is dispatched to Yan Mountain. His speed hint at a true monster descending.
6. **The Marquis's Arrival**: Just as the Ji Clan debates fleeing, a massive warship appears bearing the banner of the **Marquis of Anchan (Northmont clan)** . General Dong calls for the Ji Clan to present themselves. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger of overwhelming power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Weight of a Wanxiang Adept's Death**: In a sect like Snowdragon Mountain, Wanxiang Adepts are the middle management of immortality—powerful enough to be dangerous, but not so high that their death triggers total war without investigation. The fact that the sect can *instantly know* the time and place of a Wanxiang's death via soul tablets or tracking talismans is a classic trope. It removes the possibility of hidden escapes and forces the protagonists to face immediate, overwhelming pursuit. This is the Xianxia version of a GPS tracker on a corporate executive.

**The Yinglong Guard**: The elite immortal corps of the Grand Xia Dynasty. Think of them as the celestial special forces—membership requires being at least a Wanxiang Adept, and they learn far superior techniques than ordinary cultivators. Xue Hongyi's membership drastically raises the threat level. He is not just a random Wanxiang; he is a *battle-hardened state operative* who can face multiple ordinary Wanxiang Adepts on his own. This is the dynasty's way of policing its territory, but also its way of giving ambitious talents a career path.

**The Marquis of Anchan**: The ruler of the entire Anchan Commandery, a feudal lord with near-absolute power over his territory. When a Marquis sends an envoy, it's like a governor showing up in a small town. His authority overrides even local sect disputes, but only if the envoy intervenes. The arrival of the Northmont banner is the ultimate wildcard—it could be salvation or a new doom.
