## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists—this chapter is a one-two punch of raw power demonstration and cold, gathering storm clouds. Ji Ning takes command of the Xingtian Formation for the very first time and absolutely **shatters expectations**, proving that his Heartforce mastery makes him a freak even among freakish Celestial Immortals. But while the allied forces celebrate their new champion, the Seamless Gate is quietly finishing its own preparations in a remote commandery. The countdown to war just got a whole lot shorter. Buckle up—this is the calm before the epic clash.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Xingtian Formation Trial Continues:** Ji Ning takes his turn commanding the hundred-thousand-Loose-Immortal-plus-one-thousand-Celestial-Immortal formation, struggling at first with its immense power but quickly adapting.
*   **A Crushing Victory:** Ji Ning manages to shatter eight pieces of Chaotic Goldstone with a single palm strike, leaving a ninth nearly destroyed. This places him **solidly first** out of all 359 Celestial Immortals who have attempted the trial so far. The Xia Emperor is visibly impressed.
*   **The Nine Great Generals Announced:** Based on the trial results, the nine commanders of the new Celestial Immortal Legions are named. Ji Ning is officially appointed as the leader of the **Northern Bright Army**.
*   **The Xingtian God's Reward:** Each of the nine generals receives a unique command token, personally forged by the ancient god Xingtian, containing the true technique of the Xingtian Formation. The token also serves as a voucher to claim one treasure or technique from Xingtian's vault—*if they survive the war*.
*   **A Parallel Mobilization:** The chapter cuts to the remote Baiji Commandery, where the Seamless Gate has assembled a massive floating fortress. Three thousand Celestial Immortals who defected from the Grand Xia are training under the 'Muddy Water True Art', waiting for the order to strike.
*   **The Calm Before the Storm:** The Seamless Gate leader, Greenfox, confirms that war will be declared the moment the defectors' training is complete. The Baiji True Immortal, a cold and ancient figure, declares he has been waiting a billion years for this very moment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Xingtian Formation (刑天阵): A God's Forgotten Weapon**

This chapter introduces one of the most iconic war formations in the *Desolate Era* universe. The Xingtian Formation is not just a big, flashy combat trick. It is a **divine technique bestowed by the ancient True God, Xingtian**, a figure from Chinese mythology famous for continuing to fight after being decapitated by the Yellow Emperor. In the novel, this formation requires an absolutely staggering foundation: **100,000 Loose Immortals** to act as the living circuits and **1,000 Celestial Immortals** to form the framework. The result is a temporary, towering divine spirit—a fragment of Xingtian's own power condensed into a single, overwhelming avatar. As Ji Ning notes, controlling this formation is akin to controlling the Grand Thousand Sword Formation—raw power is useless without the mental fortitude to guide it.

**The Chaotic Goldstone Standard (混沌金石): Forged from Chaos**

The material used to test the Xingtian Formation's power is not just any random rock. Chaotic Goldstone is mined from the endless, primordial **Chaos** that exists outside of structured worlds like the Three Realms. Only beings of the True God / Daofather level or above can even *access* these mines. Its incredible hardness makes it the perfect standard for measuring peak-level destructive force. The fact that Ji Ning shattered eight pieces—two more than the presumed limit for a powerful Celestial Immortal—is a testament to his freakish Heartforce amplification.

**The Qi of Fortune (气运): A Realm's Invisible Shield**

While not explicitly named in this chapter, the underlying strategy of the Seamless Gate's betrayal is tied to the Xianxia concept of *Qi of Fortune* or 'Destiny'. A realm's accumulated luck and fate is a tangible resource in this universe. By causing internal defections, the Seamless Gate isn't just gaining soldiers; they are actively **damaging the Grand Xia's Qi of Fortune**, making it vulnerable to external conquest. Every Celestial Immortal who switches sides is a blow to the realm's destiny, creating an opening for invasion.

## Reading Guide

This chapter beautifully demonstrates the classic Xianxia tension between personal glory and the big picture. Ji Ning's triumph is phenomenal—he is the star of the show, the unparalleled genius who outshines everyone. But the real focus of the chapter is the **systematic mobilization of war**. The formation of the Nine Legions, the rewards from the Xingtian God, these are all the machinery of a great conflict. The author cuts away from the victorious celebration to the Seamless Gate's cold, efficient preparation in Baiji Commandery, and the contrast is deliberately chilling. We see the tragic scale of the coming war: even legendary figures like the Baiji True Immortal are reduced to just one of many commanders in a vast army. Keep an eye on that 'Muddy Water True Art'—a technique specifically designed for defectors often suggests a hidden cost or a mechanism of control that could be the key to a future counter-stroke.
