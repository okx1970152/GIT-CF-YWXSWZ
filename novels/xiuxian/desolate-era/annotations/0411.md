## Chapter Overview
Diancai Immortal is deep in his Heart Demon Tribulation—completely helpless. But the Blood God Sect has sniffed him out, and they’ve brought the big guns: the Blood God Sovereign himself and his Seven Dharma Kings. Ji Ning, Wufeng Immortal, and Uncle White are the only line of defense. With the Sovereign matching a Celestial Immortal and each Dharma King now a bonafide 500,000-year-Loose-Immortal-level threat, this is a perfect storm. Ji Ning has a trump card hidden inside the formation—his Primaltwin—and he’s about to unleash a Nine-Sword formation that nobody saw coming.

## Key Plot Points
1. Ji Ning’s divine sense detects the Blood God Sect’s black ship from 60,000 li away. He identifies the Blood God Sovereign and the Seven Dharma Kings—a force that recently ambushed other Earth Immortals mid-tribulation.
2. The Wufeng Immortal panics: even he can’t hold off a single Dharma King alone, let alone seven. Ji Ning calmly instructs him to retreat inside the sealing formation and guard the Diancai Immortal personally.
3. The Blood God Sovereign taunts Ji Ning and orders the Seven Dharma Kings to bypass him and kill the Diancai Immortal while he engages Ning directly.
4. The Seven Dharma Kings fuse into a thousand-zhang blood dragon formation and strike the sealing formation, cracking it.
5. Ji Ning’s Primaltwin, hidden inside with nine top-grade immortal-ranked flying swords, emerges. He executes a nine-sword technique that shatters the blood dragon and counter-attacks, killing intent blazing.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Heart Demon Tribulation (心魔劫):** The last and most internal of the three major tribulations (wind, fire, thunder) before an Earth Immortal becomes a Celestial Immortal. The cultivator’s soul is pulled into a dream world where their deepest emotional wounds and desires manifest. They have zero awareness of or defense against the outside world during this process, making them extremely vulnerable to assassination. This is why trusted guardians are always stationed nearby.
**Six-Nine Tribulation (六九雷劫):** The Thunder Tribulation, one of the three. Surviving it means the body has been purified enough to carry the Celestial Immortal seed. But passing the thunder alone doesn’t guarantee success—the heart demon must be conquered.
**Dharma King Formation (合阵之术):** In Xianxia, group combat formations allow multiple weaker cultivators to merge their power and Dao comprehension into a single, larger entity—often an elemental beast or god. The Blood God Sect’s Seven Dharma Kings use such a formation to boost from “ordinary” Loose Immortals to a million-year level. When Ji Ning’s nine-sword strike shatters their beast-form, it’s a brutal demonstration that raw coordination has limits against absolute qualitative power.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a fight that flips the scare factor on its head. Ji Ning just showed he can one-shot a combined-force blood dragon with his Primaltwin's hidden nine swords. But the tension isn’t over—the Blood God Sovereign hasn’t moved yet, and the Diabolic Premonition (原始煞气?) that warned Ji Ning earlier still hasn’t been explained. There’s a third party watching. Who leaked the tribulation site? What kind of dark organization can pinpoint Earth Immortals mid-tribulation across the entire Grand Xia Dynasty? This chapter sets up a conspiracy layer bigger than a single sect rivalry. Buckle up—the game is about to get multi-dimensional.
