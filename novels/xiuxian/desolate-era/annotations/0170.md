## Chapter Overview
The training ground of the Black-White College is buzzing, and Ji Ning is the reason. After dismantling Yinxue Wenjian in a clean duel, the new kid on the block has just sent a shockwave through the old guard. But the real test isn't over. Bu Ying, the ringleader who organized this hazing effort, steps into the arena to salvage his crew's pride. What follows is a masterclass in puppet combat and a brutal lesson in why you should never judge a cultivator by his grade—Ji Ning is not just winning; he's dominating. The quiet genius from the wildlands is making a statement: the new generation has arrived, and they are *not* here to be humble.

## Key Plot Points
- **Yinxue Wenjian’s Crushing Defeat**: The first old disciple to challenge Ji Ning walks away with his tail between his legs. He loses not just the match, but a hundred Black-White Pearls and a hundred catties of Primordial Liquid. His shock is genuine because all conditions were equal (same puppet cores, same elemental power); the difference was purely in their *Dao attainment*—and Ji Ning is simply on another level.
- **The Secret to Ji Ning’s Edge**: The narrative pulls back the curtain on why Ji Ning is so freakishly strong at such a low cultivation stage. He has already achieved the **Domain of the Dao** (a realm normally for Primal Daoists), his swordsmanship has been honed by the Aquatics Palace and the Black-White Diagram, and his soul has reached the **Divine Sense** level—putting him on par with reincarnated Immortals. Against a normal genius, he cheats by being an outright monster.
- **Bu Ying Steps Up**: The old disciple who organized the persecution cannot back down. He is a fellow practitioner of the *Lesser Five Elements Sword Manual* and decides to handle Ji Ning personally. The stakes are set: a hundred Black-White Pearls and ten catties of Primordial Liquid.
- **The Two Forms Azurefire Sword**: Bu Ying unleashes a powerful wind-wheel formation (thirty-six flying swords), a technique that effortlessly blocks Ji Ning's initial rain of swords. Ji Ning adapts on the fly, splitting his twelve swords into two interlocking groups that merge into a single, devastating **Azurefire Sword**, piercing Bu Ying's formation in one clean move.
- **Conclusion & Social Shock**: Bu Ying loses. The Dao Debate Hall is in an uproar. The defeated Wenjian swears a rematch, and the observing genius Nine Lotus mentally labels Ji Ning a 'freak'—a talent comparable to a reincarnated Immortal, a monster that the ordinary geniuses simply have no shame in losing to.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Hierarchy of Talent in a Cultivation Sect**: This chapter beautifully illustrates the pecking order within an elite Xianxia school. The Black-White College is not a friendly academy; it is a survival-of-the-fittest proving ground. The 'old disciples' (Lao Dizi) are naturally expected to bully and pressure the new blood (*Xin Dizi*) to maintain their status and resources. When a new disciple like Ji Ning comes in and shatters that hierarchy, it creates a 'social detonation'—enemies instantly recalibrate, potential challengers hesitate, and his stock as a 'monster genius' skyrockets. Notice how the narrative explicitly contrasts him with 'reincarnated Immortals', the ultimate wild cards in the Xianxia universe who retain their past-life cultivation insights.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for some serious payoff, fellow Daoists! This chapter is a classic 'revenge is a dish served cold' beat, but with a Xianxia twist. Ji Ning isn't just beating bullies; he is *systematically proving* that his foundation is flawless. The puppet battle is a brilliant narrative device—it removes the variable of equipment and raw power, forcing the fight to be decided purely on *Dao comprehension* and *soul strength*. This is I Eat Tomatoes flexing his protagonist's cheat sheet in the most satisfying way. The nod to the 'Two Forms Azurefire Sword' as a direct counter to Yinxue Wenjian's earlier technique is also a nice touch, showing Ji Ning’s ability to analyze and adapt. Consider this chapter the official declaration that Ji Ning is no longer a promising talent—he is a faction to be reckoned with. The fight is clean, the lore drop about the Domain of the Dao is crucial for world-building, and the character dynamics are shifting fast. Enjoy the aura of a rising star.

## Deeper Dive: The Philosophy of the Three-Foot Sword & Immortal Beixing
While this chapter zeroes in on the clash of *Lesser Five Elements Sword Manual* masters, the ghost of another legendary sword-art looms over the entire scene. The previous chapter established that Ji Ning’s master, the Diancai Immortal, is obsessed with the legacy of **Immortal Beixing**, the creator of the **Three-Foot Sword**. Beixing’s philosophy is key: his sword was called 'Three-Foot' because a sword is merely three feet long—all that matters is whether it can cut down injustice. It is a path of ruthless, no-compromise action. This stands in stark contrast to the refined, almost academic nature of the *Lesser Five Elements Sword Manual* practiced by Bu Ying. Ji Ning is learning both, but his Dao-heart leans far more toward Beixing's primal simplicity. This tension between structured sect arts and the raw, free spirit of an ancient master will define Ji Ning’s growth moving forward.
