## Chapter Overview

Get ready for one of the most iconic turning points in early *Desolate Era*: Ji Ning’s breakthrough to the Xiantian realm via the legendary Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens. And of course, Tie Muzhan picks the worst possible moment to show up. In this chapter, our young swordsman is yanked from a profound state of Dao comprehension, pitted against a late-stage Xiantian foe with a formation ready, and forced to push through his metamorphosis under the nose of a hunter who cannot wait to claim the kill. The tension is delicious, the payoff is gigantic, and the sheer cosmic spectacle of Sunstar True Fire and Taiyin True Water descending? Chef’s kiss.

## Key Plot Points

- Tie Muzhan and the Huan Beast track Ji Ning to a pond, finding him cross-legged in a meditative state.
- Ji Ning’s natural protective manifestation—the two-layer lotus leaves formed from water and fire—instantly grinds a thrown stone to powder and withstands an entire tree being hurled at him.
- The tree’s impact shatters the lotus leaves and yanks Ji Ning out of his enlightenment state. His fury is colder than the Taiyin Star itself.
- Realizing that his breakthrough to become a Xiantian Fiendgod is imminent, Ji Ning immediately begins the process, drawing power from the Sunstar and Taiyin Star.
- The breakthrough manifests as a celestial spectacle: a massive water realm with a Moon Rabbit shadow and a sea of flame with a Three-Legged Golden Crow spirit, all centered on Ji Ning.
- Tie Muzhan, recognizing the Crimsonbright Diagram’s legendary phenomenon, forbids the Huan Beast from attacking, correctly recognizing that the descending Sunstar True Fire and Taiyin True Water would instantly obliterate anyone foolish enough to interfere.
- He sets up the Yin-Yang Dual Qi Formation as a fallback, planning to spring it the moment Ji Ning finishes and loses the stars’ protection.
- Ji Ning’s body is completely remade at the cellular level—his mortal shell shed, a true Primordial Fiendgod body forged by the dual essence of the supreme stars.
- As the manifested water and fire dissolve, Ji Ning opens his eyes, activates his divine patterns, and unleashes twin dragons of water and fire toward his enemies with the command: “Time to die.”

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a masterclass in xianxia breakthrough mechanics. The **Crimsonbright Diagram of the Nine Heavens** is the undisputed number one Fiendgod Body Refining technique in the Three Realms—every major tribe and clan has a copy precisely *because* it is legendary. Yet almost no one can actually use it successfully. Why? Because it requires drawing power from the two supreme stars—the **Sunstar** and the **Taiyin Star**—and synchronizing their opposing forces within the mortal body. The slivers of **Sunstar True Fire** and **Taiyin True Water** that descend are not just violent energies; they are the purest fragments of celestial creation. To touch them is to invite annihilation. That is why Tie Muzhan, a seasoned late-stage Xiantian hunter, does not dare to physically attack Ji Ning during the breakthrough—he knows the thermal recoil alone would erase him from existence.

The two animal manifestations—the **Moon Rabbit** (symbol of the Taiyin Star, associated with the lunar jade rabbit in Chinese mythology) and the **Three-Legged Golden Crow** (the solar crow that dwells in the sun from ancient folklore)—are the unmistakable hallmark of a successful Crimsonbright breakthrough. Their simultaneous appearance confirms that the cultivator is not merely crossing the Xiantian threshold but forging a body on par with the primordial Fiendgods of legend. This is not just an energy upgrade; it is a complete ontological transformation—every cell bursts and is re-forged from the ground up.

Also worth noting: **Fiendgod Body Refining** vs. **Ki Refining.** Ki Refiners at Xiantian are still fragile—a well-aimed sword through the heart ends them. Fiendgod Body Refiners, even at the earliest stage of Xiantian, can survive dismemberment and regenerate. The Crimsonbright Diagram, being the apex method, grants a body that laughs at mortal weapons and can command water and fire as innate abilities upon completion. That means Ji Ning just awakened with combat abilities that most Xiantian cultivators would envy even at the peak of their power.

## Reading Guide

If you’ve been waiting for Ji Ning to fully unleash the monster potential he’s been hinting at since the very first chapters—*this is that chapter.* The breakthrough scene is the kind of payoff that makes progression fantasy so addictive: the grinding, the patience, the stolen insight, and then the explosive, universe-bending moment when all those pieces click into place. Tie Muzhan, for his part, shows exactly the right kind of villainous competence: he recognizes the threat, plans around it, and doesn't gamble on a foolish charge. The fact that he *still* underestimates what a newborn Fiendgod is capable of says everything about the gap between someone who has studied power and someone who has become it.

Pay close attention to Ji Ning’s cold fury upon being pulled from his enlightenment. He does not scream, rant, or threaten. He simply says “Time to die” and manifests dragons made of water and fire. That quiet, absolute certainty is the hallmark of a real cultivator’s Dao-heart—and it should leave you grinning as you turn the page.
