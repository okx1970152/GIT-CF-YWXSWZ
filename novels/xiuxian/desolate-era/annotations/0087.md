## Chapter Overview

Hold onto your Dao hearts, fellow cultivators, because chapter 87 delivers a double whammy of worldbuilding and pure, unadulterated opportunity. We open with a celestial spectacle: a titanic, seven-colored vortex churning over the Yan Mountains. An Earth Immortal has just been born. But this isn't just a power-up fireworks show—it's a masterclass in political positioning. We meet the new Immortal, Daoist Fire Dragon, and watch him navigate a delicate dance of power with the ancient and mighty Beishan clan. What does a newly ascended immortal do when his breakthrough is witnessed by the number one power in the commandery? He plays it cool and accepts the invitation to the feast, of course. But the real meat of this chapter lies in its aftermath, where the Ji Clan elders—Ji Jiuhuo and Granny Shadow—rush to the breakthrough site only to find something far more valuable than a new Immortal's acquaintance: a spirit stone vein. The clan's fortunes may have just turned the corner.

## Key Plot Points

- A massive, colorful vortex of elemental energy appears in the wilderness near the Yan Mountains, signaling a breakthrough to the Void stage and Earth Immortality.
- The first to arrive is Beishan Luoxiao, a powerful Loose Immortal of the Beishan clan, the rulers of the Anshan Commandery under the Grand Xia Dynasty.
- The breakthrough cultivator reveals himself as Daoist Fire Dragon, a native of Anshan who spent centuries roaming the Eastern Seas and has returned to rebuild his destroyed tribe and seek vengeance.
- Beishan Luoxiao warmly invites the new Earth Immortal to Anshan City for a feast hosted by the Anshan Marquis, a clear bid to secure the allegiance of a powerful new asset.
- Hours later, Ji Jiuhuo, Granny Shadow, and her loyal old servant, A Xing, arrive at the site and discover, hidden deep underground, a massive spirit stone vein containing high-grade spirit stones—a resource that could transform the Ji Clan's fortunes.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Let's break down the political landscape we've just glimpsed. The **Grand Xia Dynasty** is no vague empire; it's a feudal state with a rigid hierarchy. The **Beishan clan**, holding the title of **Anshan Marquis**, is the hereditary sovereign of the entire Anshan Commandery. Their power is ancient, deep, and almost unassailable. When a new Earth Immortal appears *in their territory*, it's not just a spectacle—it's a political event. Beishan Luoxiao's immediate arrival and invitation is classic Xianxia Realpolitik: an immortal is a strategic asset, and you bring them into the fold before a rival can. Note how Daoist Fire Dragon doesn't immediately swear fealty; he accepts the feast. That's the beginning of a negotiation, a dance of mutual benefit that defines how power works at this level.

Now, the **Earth Immortal** stage. This is a major milestone. An Earth Immortal has successfully passed the **Heavenly Tribulation** (or at least a portion of it) and "escaped the cycle of the Five Elements," gaining a vastly extended lifespan. But they haven't achieved full Celestial Immortality. Our new friend Daoist Fire Dragon is a **Loose Immortal**, meaning he's likely a failed ascension who clung to life via a Dharma treasure or special technique, or he's simply an Earth Immortal who hasn't completed the final steps. The text notes Luoxiao has lived "tens of thousands of years," which is the classic mark of a powerful Loose Immortal—old, experienced, and terrifyingly wise. The breakthrough itself created that epic vortex because Heaven and Earth were forced to acknowledge the cultivator's new, elevated state. It's not just a light show; it's the universe stamping a "Level Up" form on the fabric of reality.

Finally, the **spirit stone vein**. In Xianxia lore, spirit stones are refined elemental energy crystals. They're used for cultivation, formations, puppetry, and currency. A *vein* is a motherlode. The fact that it contains *high-grade* spirit stones is game-changing for the Ji Clan, a middle-tier power. A resource like this could fund a century of growth, attract experts, and elevate them from a regional player to a commandery-level threat. The irony is delicious—the Immortal who caused the breakthrough inadvertently created a fortune for the very clan whose land he used.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a brilliant change of pace after the high-octane combat of previous sections. It's **pure setup**. But don't let the lack of sword-swinging fool you. Every word here is a seed planted for future story branches. Pay close attention to the political maneuvering between the Beishan clan and this new immortal. Will Daoist Fire Dragon become a loyal retainer of the Marquis, an independent agent, or a future antagonist? The Ji Clan's discovery of the spirit stone vein is the real payoff here—a tangible, gigantic resource that could be the key to Ji Ning's ascension. It's the spark that could ignite a clan-wide conflict, especially if other powers learn of it. The calm before the storm. Get comfortable, because the resources are in place. The real race is about to begin.

## The "Delegated Awe" of an Immortal

There's a fantastic literary device on full display here: **Delegated Awe**. We never see Beishan Luoxiao fight. We never see his power directly. We only see the effect of his presence and his reputation. Daoist Fire Dragon, a newly ascended Earth Immortal, *reacts with respect and caution* the moment he hears the name "Beishan Luoxiao." The fact that a living, breathing Immortal is deferential tells us everything we need to know about Luoxiao's power. This is how Xianxia establishes scale and threat levels without constant explosions. The reader infers the power from the character's awe. It's efficient, elegant, and deeply satisfying.
