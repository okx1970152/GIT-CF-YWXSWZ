## Chapter Overview

Realm wars are never won by a single blade… until a blade shows up that bends spacetime itself. This chapter delivers the thunderous payoff of Empyrean God Red Snow’s entrance: the Seven Radiance Empyrean God, a formation-entity so gracefully lethal it turns the tide of an entire Realm War in minutes. Where the Three-Eyed Fiendgod fights with brute, crushing force, the Seven Radiance God dances with time-distorting precision, and the gap is absolute. Daofather Mo Zhu watches his eighteen Empyrean Gods fold like wet paper and orders a full rout. But victory is never neat in the Desolate Era: the feast is barely underway before Daofather Crimsonbright learns this power came with a one-time price tag—Red Snow promised Ji Ning a single battle. The diplomatic scramble to lock the red-haired terror into permanent service is tense, sly, and deeply satisfying. And across the void, in the hidden Demonworld of Ten Thousand Demons, the Seamless Gate’s chief puppet master, Wuxiao Celestial Immortal, crushes his wine flask in fury and orders a Daofather Puppet rushed to completion. The war isn’t over. It’s only upgrading.

## Key Plot Points

- **Bodhi’s Reflection**: The chapter opens with Patriarch Bodhi and Jidian discussing the rarity of spacetime-path successors. Empyrean God Red Snow has touched the threshold of spacetime, making him the first true inheritor of Bodhi’s ultimate art. Bodhi admits the Innerheart Great World is no longer hidden from the Seamless Gate Lord, now fused with the Heavenly Dao, but takes hope in this new successor.

- **The Seven Radiance God Unleashed**: On the Baiji Commandery battlefield, Red Snow commanding the Seven Radiance Empyrean God proves utterly untouchable. His longspear distorts spacetime and scatters snow across the battlefield while the Three-Eyed Fiendgod (commanded by the Nine-Headed Lion) fails to land a single decisive blow. The Devouring Mountain Demon Gods admit defeat: Red Snow’s combat experience from the primordial era makes him a perfect battlefield predator.

- **Seamless Gate Rout**: Daofather Mo Zhu orders a full withdrawal. The pursuit turns into a massacre as morale collapses. Daofather Crimsonbright laughs openly; Mo Zhu descends the wall in silence.

- **The Celebration & The Bargain**: Ji Ning is acknowledged as the hidden architect of the victory, having summoned the Seven Radiance God. During the feast, Daofather Crimsonbright learns the truth: Red Snow’s team only promised one battle. After tense negotiation, Crimsonbright secures their continued help until the Realm War ends, leveraging his old friendship with Daoist Threelives and Ji Ning’s presence.

- **The Puppet Master’s Fury**: In the sealed Demonworld of Ten Thousand Demons, Wuxiao Celestial Immortal—the Seamless Gate’s true puppetry genius—learns of the defeat. He dismisses the failed demon gods as expected assets and orders 1.8 million Celestial Immortals to complete a Daofather Puppet within one month, threatening them with the terrifying Seven Flames Punishment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Seven Radiance Empyrean God vs. The Three-Eyed Fiendgod (七曜天神 vs. 三眼魔神)**

This chapter’s core combat dynamic is a classic Xianxia trope: the refined, artful warrior versus the raw, monstrous brute. The Three-Eyed Fiendgod is a colossal war-form made of raw divine power and fury—a battering ram of flesh and Dao. But the Seven Radiance God is something different: it moves with the elegance of a trained dancer and the precision of a cosmic force. Every spear thrust bends spacetime because its commander, Red Snow, has touched the threshold of the ‘path of spacetime.’

What makes this contrast so devastating is not just raw power—it’s *skill ceiling*. Red Snow has fought in countless primordial wars; his battle instincts are baked into his soul across billions of years. The Nine-Headed Lion (Three-Eyed Fiendgod’s commander) has comparable Dao-insight, but lacks the battle-hardened reflexes to counter a formation that treats war itself as an art form. This is the difference between a genius who *studied* warfare and a legend who *lived* it.

**The Starseizing Manor’s Seven Empyrean Gods (摘星府七大天神)**

The seven Empyrean Gods (Red Snow, Yuan Guang, Jushe, Snow Scorpion, and their three unnamed companions) are remnants of Daoist Threelives’ Starseizing Manor faction. After Threelives’ death, most of his followers dispersed. These seven stayed out of loyalty. They have been lying low, waiting for a new leader, and now support Ji Ning—who inherited Threelives’ Starseizing Hand. This explains why they agreed to Red Snow’s promise to help Ji Ning: they serve the legacy of their fallen master. Their continued deployment under Daofather Crimsonbright’s request is not a betrayal but a strategic extension of that loyalty.

**The Wuxiao Celestial Immortal (乌霄天仙) & Demonworld of Ten Thousand Demons (万魔界)**

Wuxiao is a terrifying Xianxia archetype: the genius recluse whose real power is not combat but creation. On paper, he’s a mere Celestial Immortal. In reality, he’s the Seamless Gate’s greatest puppetmaster, controlling an industrial nightmare of 1.8 million Celestial Immortals to forge war puppets. His true body is a puppet shell; even killing him only destroys one body. The Demonworld of Ten Thousand Demons is a sealed production zone where even the Lord God of the Seamless Gate cannot interfere—because Wuxiao operates under an even higher authority. His threat is deferred, but it’s looming: a Daofather Puppet is a game-changer that could undo Red Snow’s advantage.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for a *very* satisfying victory lap, fellow Daoists—but don’t get too comfortable. This chapter is a masterclass in “the battle is won, the war is far from over.” Red Snow’s Seven Radiance God is the undisputed star of the show; watching the Three-Eyed Fiendgod and three demon gods scramble for their lives is the kind of catharsis we’ve been waiting chapters for. But pay close attention to the subtext: Daofather Crimsonbright’s panic when he learns the seven Empyrean Gods were only here for one fight tells you how *fragile* this victory really is. Without Red Snow, the next battle might not go their way.

Also, savor the Wuxiao segment. It’s a classic Xianxia “meanwhile, in the enemy’s lab…” beat that sets up the next threat. The Daofather Puppet is described with the kind of industrial dread that makes you realize the Seamless Gate isn’t just an army—it’s an assembly line of nightmares. The Seven Flames Punishment is a chilling detail that reminds us of the ruthlessness lurking behind the enemy’s ranks.

Finally, keep an eye on Ji Ning’s position. He’s not only the future leader of the Starseizing Manor legacy, but now the *source* of the Realm War’s turning point. That places a target squarely on his back—and makes him invaluable to the Crimsonbright camp.
