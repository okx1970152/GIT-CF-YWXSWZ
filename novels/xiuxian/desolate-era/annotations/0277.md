## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, the mask has come off. Shaoyan Nong shows his hand in the most ruthless way possible, using Xue Hongyi as bait and Xiangliu Fang as his executioner. Ji Ning is cornered, his friends threatened, and the only way out is through a storm of blood and sword-light. But when the pressure is highest, a Sword Immortal is born. This chapter is all about the raw, beautiful moment when Ji Ning breaks through to the Grand Dao Domain, wielding the legendary Three-Foot Sword with a ferocity that even his enemies have to respect. Get ready for a demonstration of pure, unadulterated Xianxia martial spirit.

## Key Plot Points
Shaoyan Nong escalates the confrontation by revealing he knows Ji Ning’s mother’s true identity (Yuchi Snow, a defector from the Yuchi clan), destroying the hope that Ji Ning’s friends had of using his mother’s background to escape persecution.

Nine Lotos unleashes a powerful black lotus treasure, wounding Xiangliu Fang’s arm, but the primordial Fiendgod’s regeneration makes the damage trivial. Shaoyan Nong coldly mocks her efforts, making it clear that the Dongyan clan’s name means nothing in this situation.

Xue Hongyi, the traitorous informant, is ordered to kill Ji Ning. He accepts reluctantly, fully expecting to lose but trusting in his own life-preservation abilities.

Ji Ning, pushed to his absolute limit, achieves a profound breakthrough: he enters the ‘Grand Dao Domain’ of the Dao of the Sword. His soul transforms into a ‘Sword Soul’.

He unleashes the Sixth Stance of the Three-Foot Sword: ‘Grand Dao Domain’. The explosive sword-qi annihilates Xue Hongyi’s flying needles and then cuts the man down in a single, devastating blow.

Despite his newfound power, Ji Ning is still overwhelmed by Xiangliu Fang’s strength. Desperate, he unleashes his final trump card: a black bangle—a treasure from the ancient Daoist Threelives.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Grand Dao Domain (大道域境).** This is a major milestone, not just for Ji Ning, but in Xianxia cultivation theory. ‘Dao Domain’ usually refers to the ability to project one's comprehension of an ordinary Dao as a field of influence. But reaching the ‘Grand Dao Domain’ means you’ve done the same for one of the supreme, fundamental Grand Daos (like the Dao of the Sword). The novel explicitly states this is harder than fully comprehending an ordinary Dao. It’s a leap in qualitative power, not just a level-up.

**The Sword Soul (剑魂).** When a Sword Immortal reaches the Grand Dao Domain, their very soul transforms into a sword. This is not a metaphor; it’s a fundamental change in their spiritual essence. In Daoist internal alchemy, the soul is the seat of consciousness. Making it ‘sharp’ like a sword means their will, their perception, and their attacks become intrinsically more lethal. It’s the soul-level equivalent of a blacksmith forging a legendary blade.

**The Three-Foot Sword & Immortal Beixing.** The 'Three-Foot Sword' is not a spell; it’s a philosophical sword-art rooted in the concept of ‘killing all that is unjust.’ The final stances, including this one, cannot be taught directly. They must be *inherited* through the resonance of a Sword Immortal's will. Ji Ning didn’t just learn a new move; he absorbed the unyielding, principled spirit of its creator, Immortal Beixing. The text also reveals that only the first six stances are passed down through scriptures; the final three require a soul-to-soul transmission.

## Reading Guide
This is one of those ‘goosebump’ chapters that defines the Xianxia genre. Pay close attention to the flow of the breakthrough: the pressure, the internal shift, the laughter, and then the overwhelming force. The line “One day lived with unbridled satisfaction is better than a hundred years lived in frustration” isn’t just a cool quote—it’s the philosophical core of the Three-Foot Sword. It’s a doctrine of action over passive suffering.

Also, note the cold pragmatism of Shaoyan Nong. He isn't just a spoiled young master; he’s a canny operator who plans to profit from everyone's death, including his own ally Xue Hongyi. But that final, desperate throw of the black bangle from Daoist Threelives? That’s the real cliffhanger. We know this treasure was the original guardian of the aquatic palace. Just what kind of destruction is it about to unleash?
