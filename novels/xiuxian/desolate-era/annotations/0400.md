## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow cultivators, to the cold, calculating underbelly of Xianxia politics! This isn't a chapter about sword-fights or dramatic breakthroughs. It’s a chapter about logistics, paranoia, and the grim business of assassination. Our old friends at the Shaoyan clan—specifically, five Celestial Immortal Ancestors—are holding a board meeting, and Ji Ning is the sole item on the agenda. They’ve noticed he’s back, noticed his spirit-beasts have grown terrifyingly strong, and they’ve decided the most sensible course of action is to hire the mysterious, brand-new, and incredibly effective *Bloodcloud Hall* to put a contract out on his life. This chapter is a masterclass in how the truly ancient powers of the Three Realms operate: with zero emotion and maximum pragmatism.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Intelligence Report:** Ancestor Xuanji, the narrow-eyed Shaoyan Celestial Immortal, receives a report that Ji Ning has returned. Crucially, the report notes that both the Whitewater Hound and the Skygreen Serpent have reached the Earth Immortal (Void) stage, a feat of cultivation speed that screams “hidden opportunity.”
- **The Five Ancestors Convene:** Ancestor Xuanji summons the other four Shaoyan Celestial Immortals from their meditation. They debate the problem of Ji Ning, acknowledging his past grievances against them (the destruction of the Yuchi clan, the assassination attempt) and fearing his future potential.
- **The ‘Faction’ Risk Assessment:** A key point of debate is Ji Ning’s unknown master. If Ji Ning had joined Daofather Crimsonbright’s lineage, killing him would be difficult without offending a massive faction. Since he hasn’t, they feel free to act, though the more cautious Ancestors warn about potential “madmen” who might retaliate.
- **The Solution: Hiring the Bloodcloud Hall:** To mitigate the risk of angering an unknown master, the withered Ancestor ‘Kumu’ suggests hiring the Bloodcloud Hall. This mysterious, new assassination organization operates with absolute secrecy, sworn by a Heavenly Dao Oath.
- **The Price of a Genius:** The contract is confirmed. The silver-robed representative of the Bloodcloud Hall calmly accepts, noting Ji Ning’s high threat level (comparable to a million-year Loose Immortal) but confirming that anything is possible for a price.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a deep dive into **Faction-Based Realpolitik**, a core mechanic of the later-game Xianxia world. Gone are the simple tribal squabbles of the Yan Mountains. This is the adult world of the Three Realms.

1.  **Two Pillar Government of Sects and Clans:** A Celestial Immortal is *the* foundational power of a clan like the Shaoyan. They are the ultimate deterrent, the living nuclear weapons. Five of them convening for a single problem shows how seriously they take Ji Ning. They don’t bicker; they strategize.
2.  **The Immortal World’s Rules of Engagement:** The Ancestors discuss a crucial unwritten rule: ‘Don’t bully the weak with your seniority.’ High-level powerhouses rarely slaughter the disciples of other powerhouses directly. They let their juniors fight it out. If a disciple dies, it’s a failure of training, not a cause for war. This provides a (thin) layer of order in the chaos.
3.  **The Utility of Dark Organizations:** The **Bloodcloud Hall** is a classic trope of the cultivation world—a neutral, amoral organization that solves problems for a price. Its sudden emergence, its mysterious location (a separate world inside a cave-treasure), its absolute secrecy guaranteed by a **Heavenly Dao Oath** (a magic contract that will annihilate your soul if broken), and its ruthless efficiency make it the perfect tool for the powerful to solve their problems without getting their hands dirty.

### Dao-heart trial

A type of test common in Xianxia that targets a cultivator's innermost emotional weaknesses and spiritual resolve, distinct from physical or elemental trials.

### Wufeng Immortal

The oldest Loose Immortal of the Black-White College, known for his slovenly appearance; his lifespan was extended by the new scriptures, showing the value of shared resources within a sect.

## Reading Guide

This is a setup chapter, pure and simple. If you came here looking for action, you’ll be disappointed. But if you want to understand the *stakes*, this is essential reading. The Shaoyan clan isn’t just a bunch of loudmouthed young masters anymore. They are an ancient, calculating machine that sees Ji Ning as a bug that needs to be squashed. The introduction of the Bloodcloud Hall is a major narrative gear shift. Ji Ning isn’t just fighting enemies he can see anymore; he’s fighting an enemy he can’t, a professional killer corps that has a perfect record. The fun part is going to be seeing how the great hero handles a threat he can’t just sword his way through. Get ready for the hunt.
