## Chapter Overview

The Golden Sword Ceremony reaches its electrifying climax. After effortlessly weaving through a seven-on-one siege using his godlike footwork, Ji Ning systematically dismantles each opponent without even drawing his sword at first. The curved-blade youth forces a respectful one-on-one, testing his blazing-fast Leaving-Hand Saber technique, before conceding. Then comes the real challenge: Pi—the golden-furred, Fiendgod-trained secret weapon raised by the viper elder Ji Lie. But Ji Ning has been keeping secrets of his own. With a single, breathtaking display of the Dripping Sutra's ultimate form—Dripping Water Pierces Stone—he sends Pi crashing off the stage and seals his victory as the undisputed champion. The political ramifications are immediate and seismic.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning demonstrates Unity of Man and Heaven footwork, making him untouchable against all seven opponents simultaneously without using his weapon.
- The curved-blade youth (unnamed) challenges Ji Ning to three solo strikes using his signature Leaving-Hand Saber; Ji Ning blocks the third and the youth concedes gracefully, earning recruitment into the Ji Clan.
- Pi (Ji Pi) reveals himself as Ji Lie's hidden ace—a Fiendgod Body Refining practitioner at Unity of Man and Sword realm with a Bloodhell Saber technique.
- Ji Ning deliberately engages Pi in an extended exchange to gain combat experience, then finishes him with a single Realm-Entering sword strike.
- Prefectural Lord Ji Youyang's joy is matched only by Ji Lie's silent fury; Ji Lie publicly admits defeat but privately crushes his stone armrest in rage.
- The chapter ends with Ji Lie acknowledging Ji Ning's monstrous talent, smooths over the political fracture with a bitter concession.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Golden Sword Ceremony (金剑大典)** — A quadrennial life-or-death competition for all youths under sixteen within Ji Clan territories. Winners earn a Golden Sword, immense prestige, and access to advanced techniques. The blunt weapons are designed to minimize fatalities, but as we've seen, even a blunt sword can shatter bones in the hands of a master.  
**Divided Mind (一心二用)** — Ji Ning's ability to think two independent streams simultaneously—a sign of an exceptionally strong soul. In combat, this allows him to dual-wield with devastating efficiency, executing independent attack patterns with each hand.  
**Realm-Entering Sword (入境之剑)** — A momentary breakthrough into Unity of Man and Heaven triggered by executing a supreme technique. It's a sign of profound comprehension—at just ten years old, Ji Ning has achieved what most warriors take decades to glimpse.  
**Dripping Sutra (滴水经)** — One of the Ji Clan's Five Supreme Sword Techniques, its ultimate move "Dripping Water Pierces Stone" embodies the relentless, concentrated power of a single raindrop eroding a rock over time—devastating when unleashed in an instant.

### Tigerfiend (虎魔)

Nickname of Ji Lie, denoting his ferocity and power; second strongest of the Western Prefecture after Ji Yichuan.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a *chef's kiss* moment for progression fantasy lovers. Ji Ning doesn't just win—he dominates with style, economy, and terrifying composure. The contrast between the wild, desperate aggression of Pi and Ji Ning's serene, rain-like swordplay perfectly illustrates the core xianxia principle: technique trumps brute force. Notice how Ji Ning's months of training since the Winged Serpent attack have elevated his swordsmanship to a new level—this is the classic "training arc payoff" done right. Also, keep an eye on the curved-blade youth's recruitment; small character threads like this often pay off down the line. For new readers, this chapter is an excellent sampler of what makes Desolate Era great: insanely detailed cultivation combat, political scheming, and a protagonist whose quiet confidence is backed by real, earned power. Get ready for the aftermath—the Golden Sword is won, but the real wars are just beginning.
