## Chapter Overview

Ji Ning finally arrives in the Grand Xia imperial capital—but his welcome isn't exactly warm. The Shaoyan clan immediately picks up his trail, and Yu Shengong finds himself caught between the thrill of discovery and the headache of legality: the imperial capital is a kill-free zone under the absolute rule of the Xiamang royal clan. Meanwhile, Ji Ning nonchalantly tours the city, brushes off a divine sense probe with his Soul-Shattering Art, and heads straight for the Heavenly Treasures Mountain headquarters—the largest trade hub in the realm. He’s ready to cash in his spoils from Shaoyan Nong, and a crafty Wanxiang Adept named Yu Qi is already angling for his business. But little does Ji Ning know that his appearance has already been flagged by the Mountain’s intelligence network and dispatched to five long-term subscribers. The net is closing, but the capital’s rules buy him time.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Shaoyan clan’s dilemma**: Yu Shengong learns Ji Ning is in the capital. Under Patriarch Xuanji’s order to kill on sight, the capital’s no-killing rule forces a strategic pause. He heads to consult the Patriarch.
2. **Ji Ning’s confident arrival**: After glimpsing the Imperial City and the Sky Apex Hall, Ji Ning notices three tailers, including a Primal Daoist. He crushes their divine sense with Soul-Shattering Art, asserting dominance.
3. **Destination: Heavenly Treasures Mountain**: Ji Ning plans to exchange his massive haul of treasures for Primordial Liquid and Five Element essence to advance *Starseizing Hand*. The carriage enters the colossal qilin-shaped headquarters.
4. **The intelligence web**: The Heavenly Treasures Mountain’s water-mirror surveillance instantly identifies Ji Ning via the Blackwater Carriage, his beast companions, and his fur robe. Five parties with long-term subscriptions for Ji Ning’s whereabouts are notified.
5. **Yu Qi’s approach**: A purple-robed Wanxiang Adept named Yu Qi waits outside Ji Ning’s dining courtyard, then introduces himself as a treasure transaction specialist. He confirms Ji Ning’s identity and offers his services, hinting at the lucrative opportunities.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Grand Xia Imperial Capital**: The capital is the seat of the Xiamang royal clan, the absolute rulers of the dynasty. Even ancient tribes like Shaoyan, Zhuanshui, and Tiannong must tread carefully here. Killing is strictly forbidden—the Imperial Guard can arrive in a single breath.
- **Heavenly Treasures Mountain HQ**: Imagine a qilin-shaped city the size of a commandery capital. This is the central hub for high-tier treasures. Cultivators from all three realms come here to trade. It also operates an intelligence department that sells information on high-value targets—Ji Ning is now one of them.
- **Blackwater Carriage**: The flashy carriage of the late Shaoyan Nong is now a liability—it’s instantly recognizable. The carriage itself is a magic treasure, but its notoriety makes Ji Ning’s incognito plan a joke.
- **Four Young Masters**: Shaoyan Nong was one of the *Four Young Masters* of the capital—a title for wealthy, hedonistic scions, not necessarily the strongest. Think of it as the cultivation world’s celebrity socialite badge.
- **Soul-Shattering Art (撼神术)**: A divine sense secret art that weaponizes spiritual perception. Ji Ning uses it to suppress the Primal Daoist tailer, demonstrating that his soul strength is far beyond normal Zifu Disciples.

## Reading Guide

Ji Ning’s arrival in the capital marks a shift in the power game. He’s no longer a hunted fugitive in the wilderness—he’s walking right into the lion’s den, but the den’s own rules protect him for now. The Shaoyan clan’s hands are tied unless they can pull off an impossible assassination under the Imperial Guard’s nose. Meanwhile, Ji Ning is reveling in his newfound wealth, ready to liquidate and upgrade. But the scene with Yu Qi is a reminder: everyone wants a piece of him. This chapter sets up the high-stakes marketplace dynamic that will drive the next arc. Get ready for some serious treasure-trading and power-spending—Xianxia’s favorite shopping spree is about to begin! And don't forget, the intelligence network has sold his location to five unknown parties. Who else might be coming for him?
