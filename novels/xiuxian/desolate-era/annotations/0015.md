## Chapter Overview

Gather 'round, fellow Daoists, because the cage door is about to slam shut, and a young Ji Ning is about to have his first real taste of mortal combat. This chapter takes us inside the legendary Dragon Fort, where the clan’s elite heirs are tested not with theory, but with fang and claw. In this premiere trial, Ji Ning faces a Howling Moon Wolf—a Godbeast born with the blood of ancient Fiendgods. But the real drama isn't just the beast's speed or its steel-whip tail; it's watching Ji Ning slowly realize that his Houtian-level Ki Refining is useless against this monster's hide. This forces him to finally unleash the full, brutish power of the Crimsonbright Diagram. The chapter masterfully contrasts the cold, analytical parenting of Ji Yichuan, the warm maternal concern of Yuchi Snow, and the primal savagery of a young cultivator learning how his newly-forged divine body actually works in a fight. Get ready for a brawl that proves raw power sometimes beats a pretty sword swing.

## Key Plot Points

- **Introduction to Dragon Fort's Function:** Ji Ning and his parents arrive at Dragon Fort. The chapter explains its two-part layout: the Cage (the life-or-death arena) and the Beast Lair. The Cage itself is described as a formidable structure made of Blackwater Iron, a metal so tough that even a beginner Xiantian cultivator would struggle to damage it. The chain-woven ceiling is the only potential escape route, but it would take ten breaths of pure hacking to clear, an eternity in a fight.
- **The Defining Character Moment for Uncle White:** The narrative takes a moment to explain the nature of Godbeasts through the example of the Whitewater Hound, Ji Ning's guardian. This builds lore and reinforces how special Uncle White is, as he is a Xiantian-level Godbeast who has already transformed into human form. The bond between Ning and his ‘Uncle White’ is implicitly shown as a source of strength and protection.
- **The Wolf is Released:** Ji Ning’s father reveals his choice of opponent: a Howling Moon Wolf, a Godbeast. This is a massive, elegant, yet lethally intelligent creature. Ji Ning’s realization that his father has thrown him into the deep end is a moment of shock, setting the stage for a real struggle.
- **The First Exchange Fails:** Ji Ning attempts a textbook-perfect thrust, expecting to use the wolf's momentum against it. However, the Howling Moon Wolf's hide is so tough that the sword's tip fails to penetrate. The counter-attack sends Ji Ning flying into the wall, demonstrating that his normal Ki Refining skills are simply not enough to do damage.
- **Unleashing the Primordial Power:** Realizing the futility of his Houtian-level attacks, Ji Ning triggers the power of the Crimsonbright Diagram. His skin flushes red, and air trembles around him as the latent Taiyin and Sunstar power within his Fiendgod-refined body awakens. The change is palpable, and even the Howling Moon Wolf hesitates, realizing the prey has suddenly become a predator.
- **Decisive Victory with a High-End Technique:** Using the increased speed and strength from his Fiendgod body, Ji Ning lashes out with the ‘Lightning Flash’ technique from the Thunderfire Sword Fragments. The attack manifests as a blade-shadow that shifts nine times in mid-air, accelerating to a lethal degree, finally slashing a deep wound across the wolf's chest. The chapter ends with Ji Ning's cold assessment of his own technique.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a fantastic primer on the **hierarchy of being** in Xianxia. First, we have the natural progression from beasts to **demon beasts (yao shou)**. When animals gain intelligence and learn to absorb natural energy, they become demon beasts. This is a one-way ticket to power, but it's a slow, hard road. Just reaching the Xiantian stage (where they become **great demons** and can usually take human form) is a massive achievement.

Then we get the true powerhouses: **Godbeasts (shen shou)**. These aren't ordinary animals that got lucky; they are born with the 'blood of ancient Fiendgods'—think of it as a divine lineage. This means they are smarter, stronger, and tougher than regular demon beasts. A Houtian-level Godbeast can often dominate a Xiantian-level normal demon beast. However, this power comes with a price. Their very blood is so potent that it resists human form; they must reach the Zifu, Wanxiang, or even higher stages to shed their animal shape and speak. The Whitewater Hound, for instance, needs to be a Zifu-level cultivator to talk—hence his silence in this chapter.

The concept of **Divided Mind (一心二用)** is briefly alluded to in the term glossary. It's not just multitasking; in the logic of Xianxia, it is a sign of a supremely strong soul. For a cultivator to fight with a sword in each hand, each weapon executing a completely different attack pattern, requires not just training, but a fundamental mental splitting that multiplies combat ability many times over. It’s a rare, natural-born talent that separates geniuses from the merely talented.

Finally, we see the strategic genius of the **smart patriarch**. Ji Yichuan doesn't want to just teach Ji Ning how to swing a sword. He engineered a scenario where Ji Ning had his normal tactics completely fail, forcing him to rely on the raw, untamed power of his Fiendgod Body Refining. This is the classic Xianxia father-figure: cold, calculated, and ruthless in his methods of training, yet utterly confident in his son’s potential.

## Reading Guide

- **Don’t Underestimate the Setting:** The chapter spends a surprising amount of time describing the Blackwater Iron walls and the overhead chain net. This is not a wasted paragraph! It's the author building the atmosphere of an inescapable, primal arena. It makes the fight feel real and visceral. You can almost hear the clang of metal and the beast's roar bouncing off the dark walls. The description of the Howling Moon Wolf's graceful entrance also serves to build dread before the actual fight begins.
- **Watch the Parental Dynamics from the Viewing Platform:** The true narrative tension this chapter isn't just the bile and fur; it's the silent drama playing out on the viewing platform. Ji Yichuan's cold, analytical commentary contrasts sharply with Yuchi Snow's trembling worry. They are both experts, but they express their concern in very different ways. Seeing Ji Yichuan call his son's early performance 'average' is a window into his scale of expectations, and Yuchi Snow's immediate follow-up question shows how much she cares about the outcome despite the danger.
- **The Real Fight Begins with a Failed Thrust:** Pay attention to the moment Ji Ning's first attack fails. It's a huge clue to the metaphysics of this world. A perfectly executed, Houtian-level thrust is useless against the natural armor of a Godbeast. This is a classic reminder that in Xianxia, talent and technique must be matched by the right kind of cultivation (Ki Refining vs. Fiendgod Body Refining) to deal with specific threats. It's a brutal lesson in power scaling, and Ji Ning learns it the hard way.
- **The True Meaning of 'Lightning Flash':** Ji Ning’s finishing move is not just a light show. The description highlights 'nine rapid shifts' in mid-air. This isn't just for show; it's a technique that destabilizes the target's defenses, making the final blow far more devastating than a simple, direct slash. It's a reminder that even raw power in this world is often wielded with incredible sophistication.
