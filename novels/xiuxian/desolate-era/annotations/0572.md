## Chapter Overview
Alright, buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is the *moment*. The Evergreen Sword Immortal, smug inside his Daofather Puppet, thinks he's unassailable. He sneers at the top-tier Karmic Punishment Deity as a flashy, uncontrollable toy. But Ji Ning, barefoot and wielding colossal swords, has other plans. We get a massive cosmic-wide audience: Buddha Tathagata, the Dao Lord of the Three Purities, the Divine Farmer—the big guns are all watching. Then, the clash we've been waiting for happens. And it's not even close. Ji Ning's 'Creation' stance doesn't just block the Daofather Puppet—it *drives it to its knees*. The entire battlefield falls into a stunned, dead silence. This is a power display that rewrites the rules of the war.

## Key Plot Points
- The Evergreen Sword Immortal boasts about his absolute control over the Daofather Puppet, dismissing Ji Ning's top-tier Karmic Punishment Deity as impossible to fully command.
- A celestial audience of Buddhist and Daoist great powers (Tathagata, Amitabha, the Three Purities, the Divine Farmer) watches the battle, analyzing the stakes of the Realm War.

- On the battlefield, the Seven Radiance Empyrean God tests the Daofather Puppet and is easily repelled, confirming its terrifying raw power.
- Ji Ning steps up, concealing his true strength until the exact moment of impact. He unleashes the twelfth and final stance of his perfected Three-Foot Sword—'Creation.'
- The sheer, overwhelming force of Ji Ning's attack collapses the Daofather Puppet's defense, forcing it to its knees in a cataclysmic clash that shocks everyone, including his own allies.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Pinnacles of the Dao: Tathagata vs. the Three Purities**
This chapter gives us a peek at the top of the food chain. **Buddha Tathagata (如来佛祖)** mastered the complete **Five Elements Heavenly Daos (五行天道)**, which is the fundamental requirement for his supreme position. In contrast, the **Dao Lord of the Three Purities (三清道人)** took a far more radical path. He mastered the *pair* of Heavenly Daos: 'Yang' and 'Annihilation' as his foundation, then risked *everything* by committing suicide and entering reincarnation with a mental seal, forcing himself to master the complementary 'Yin' Heavenly Dao in a new life. This insane gamble, which could have resulted in his permanent death, is why he stands at the very pinnacle of power.

**The Meaning of 'Creation' (开天辟地)**
This is strictly a Xianxia trope at its finest. The name of Ji Ning's new stance, **'Creation'** or *Kaitian Pidi*, literally means to "split apart heaven and earth." It directly invokes the legendary act of **Pangu**, the primordial giant who separated the chaos to create the world. For a cultivator to use this name for their ultimate technique is a declaration of supreme ambition, claiming a power that rivals the foundational myth of the universe. Here, Ji Ning backs up the boast with action.

Also, don't skip the lore dump on the Three Purities. It's not just filler; it establishes the insane, life-wagering determination it takes to reach the absolute top of this universe. It sets a benchmark for what "greatness" truly requires, a benchmark Ji Ning is clearly racing towards. The entire cosmos is watching now.

## The Physics of Face-Smashing
In *Desolate Era*, as in much of Xianxia, the audience matters as much as the action. Having every major power in the Three Realms—from Kṣitigarbha to the Divine Farmer—witness the Daofather Puppet being put on its knees is the narrative equivalent of a universe-wide broadcast. Ji Ning isn't just winning a battle; he's publicly shredding the Seamless Gate's reputation for invincibility. The "Shockwave of Silence" is a classic literary device used here to perfection—the sudden, absolute quiet after the impact tells you more than any shouted dialogue could. This single blow has fundamentally shifted the psychological landscape of the war.
