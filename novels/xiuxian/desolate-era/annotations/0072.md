## Chapter Overview
The final battle between Ji Ning and the black-robed senior apprentice-brother reaches its bloody zenith, delivering one of the most satisfying takedowns yet. Ji Ning lands a brutal kill against a peak Xiantian adversary—but only by playing his trump card: divine sense. And as the echoes of his berserk howl fade, the mountain belly’s mysterious master stirs in rage, pulling a young disciple into the abyss. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia's signature rhythm: a desperate gambit, a shocking reversal, and the dark promise of an even deadlier threat on the horizon.

## Key Plot Points
- The black-robed senior apprentice-brother unleashes Corpsefiend essence mist, a poison so potent it would even threaten a Xiantian grandmaster. Ji Ning, only a recent Xiantian breakthrough, finds his body going numb—but his Fiendgod vitality fights back.
- Unable to afford a prolonged battle while poisoned, Ji Ning deploys his hidden ace: divine sense. He telekinetically hurls a stone at the black-robed man’s head from behind, breaking his stance and leaving him defenseless for a fatal sword thrust through the skull.
- The senior apprentice-brother dies in disbelief, and Ji Ning drags the poisoned mist from his body with his Crimsonbright divine power, finding the great method superior to his opponent's cultivation.
- Searching the corpse yields no breakthrough on the maze formation, but he gathers talismans and jars for later analysis.
- In the mountain belly, the six underlings are stunned by the death of their strongest. Their torment of the captives is interrupted by the opening of the forbidden underground chamber. The master—clearly the Zifu Disciple—calls for “Little Seven” with trembling rage, signaling the escalation of the conflict.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Divine Sense (神念)** is a game-changer in the Xianxia power hierarchy. It is a soul-based perceptual and telekinetic ability that normally only awakens at the Primal Daoist stage—several cultivation tiers above Xiantian. Ji Ning's possession of it at this level is an extreme anomaly, marking him as a true freak genius. Because its unwitting use could expose his ultimate secret, he only deploys it here as a desperate, no-escape gamble. The moment the stone flies, it's all or nothing.

**Corpsefiend Essence (尸煞精华)** is a classic poison type derived from refining the miasma of fallen corpses. In Xianxia, poison experts often specialize in such hidden attacks that bypass conventional defenses. The scene highlights the difference between a top-tier foundation and a middling one: the black-robed man, despite his raw power and poison mastery, lacks the essence-purity of the Crimsonbright Diagram, so his body succumbs while Ji Ning’s divine physique endures.

The **berserk howl** Ji Ning unleashes after victory—"Come on! Kill one, I’ll slay one!"—is a staple Xianxia trope: the hero, pushed to the brink and surrounded by enemies, sheds his composure for a moment of wild, untethered defiance. This kind of emotional outburst amid life-or-death pressure gives the character depth and shows the raw beast beneath the calm exterior.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a pure adrenaline shot. The battle is tight, the stakes are personal, and the victory is earned but ugly. Note how Ji Ning’s use of divine sense is not a flashy spell but a raw, physical trick: a telekinetically thrown rock. That’s the beauty of Xianxia combat—powers are rooted in the world's physics, not abstract magic. We also get a fantastic contrast between the arrogant senior and the trapped, desperate youth, making the kill all the sweeter. And that cliffhanger—the master calling Little Seven into the forbidden hole—sets up the next phase. Expect a confrontation with the Zifu Disciple himself, and possibly a deeper horror lurking below.
