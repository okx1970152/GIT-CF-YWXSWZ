## Chapter Overview
Get ready for a white-knuckle chapter that flips the entire battlefield equation on its head! Ji Ning has just broken through to Pure Yang True Immortal while hidden inside the Xingtian Formation—and nobody knows it yet. The Grand Xia is on the verge of collapse, the Xia Emperor has already ordered a retreat, and True Immortal Baiji is laughing in triumph. But then, in a single, silent, devastating strike, Ji Ning shatters Baiji’s golden core and obliterates him. The first major casualty of the war has fallen, and the game has just changed.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning’s Celestial Immortal golden core completes its transformation into a Pure Yang golden core, with his chest condensing five streams of elemental insight that link him to the Five Elements Source.
- The quality of his magic power undergoes a qualitative leap; he feels powerful enough to fight a White-Faced Flood Dragon alone, without any legion support.
- Using his newfound Pure Yang magic power and advanced Heartforce soul-manipulation techniques, he mobilizes an astonishing eighty percent of the Xingtian Formation’s maximum theoretical power—far exceeding any other Empyrean God or True Immortal on the field.
- The Xia Emperor, seeing no reinforcements and facing inevitable defeat, orders a full retreat and deploys the Eight-Dragon Cloud City.
- True Immortal Baiji gloats and urges his forces to prevent the Grand Xia from escaping, confident in victory.
- Ji Ning, still hidden inside the Xingtian Formation, executes a secret assassination attack (the ‘Assassination Sword’) that shatters Baiji’s Pure Yang golden core instantly.
- Baiji disintegrates into ash—the first Empyrean God/True Immortal death of the entire war.
- The chapter ends with the battlefield plunged into shock and the author’s note that this is the “final chapter of the day.”

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Pure Yang True Immortal (纯阳真仙):** In Daoist cultivation cosmology, the Pure Yang realm represents a critical purification of one’s spiritual essence. The golden core transforms from a Celestial Immortal’s “yin-yang mixed” state into pure yang—a more concentrated, profound, and durable form of magic power. This is the stepping stone toward the Daofather level (Grand Golden Core). The “Five Energies Rising from the Chest” (胸中五气) is a classic Daoist concept: by mastering the five elemental Daos (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth) a cultivator harmonizes with the Source of the Five Elements, which provides an external power source and accelerates the core’s evolution.

**Heartforce and the Soul-Manipulation Technique:** The novel’s Heartforce system is rooted in the idea that willpower and sincerity can be weaponized beyond brute magical force. Here, Ji Ning’s third-level Heartforce plus his own derived techniques allow him to control the Xingtian Formation to a degree normally impossible for even veteran True Immortals. The [Heart Sutra] from Old Man Yuan was the key that opened the door to combining Heartforce with soul control of vast military formations.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a textbook example of the “hidden breakthrough” trope—the protagonist upgrades mid-battle while everyone else thinks he’s stuck, then annihilates a major enemy with surgical precision. The emotional arc is deliberately constructed: first, the Xia Emperor’s defeatist monologue, then Baiji’s gloating, then the sudden, silent reversal. Tomatoes masterfully controls the pacing by contrasting the overwhelming noise of war with the death of the red-eyed old man, which is almost quiet in its description. Pay attention to Ji Ning’s internal calculation—he picks Baiji not out of rage but because Baiji is the weakest anchor of the enemy formation, and a single assassination could destabilize the entire Seamless Gate campaign. This is peak Ji Ning: cold, efficient, and utterly lethal when the mask of illusion finally drops.

**Worldbuilding note:** The Xingtian Formation is revealed to have a theoretical maximum power ceiling, and Ji Ning’s eighty percent control is framed as exceptional even by Empyrean God standards. This implicitly raises the question: what happens when a cultivator reaches one hundred percent control? A potential future power spike or a new type of perfected formation.
