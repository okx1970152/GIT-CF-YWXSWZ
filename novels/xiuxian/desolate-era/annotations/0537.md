## Chapter Overview
Get ready, because the battlefield just got a major power injection! Chapter 537 is a sprawling, pulse-pounding war chapter where the stalemate between the Grand Xia and the Seamless Gate finally breaks, and not in a good way for our heroes. The Xia Emperor plays his ultimate trump card—the legendary World-Sealing Grand Formation—only to have it brilliantly countered by the Seamless Gate's mysterious Chaos Daofruit. And just when you think the scales are balancing, True Immortal Baiji reveals he's been holding back a devastating reserve force of four White-Faced Flood Dragons, flipping the entire strategic situation on its head. This is a classic Xianxia war chapter: a tense game of chess where every move has a countermove, and the cost of losing is the utter annihilation of a realm.

## Key Plot Points
- The chapter opens with a detailed stalemate summary: Ji Ning is pinned by True Immortal Chibing, the other Xingtian Gods and armies are each locked in brutal, indecisive battles against Seamless Gate counterparts (golem Xingtian, White-Faced Flood Dragons, Bloodcloud Golems).
- The Xia Emperor finally activates the Grand Xia World-Sealing Grand Formation, a realm-wide super-weapon with the power to kill Pure Yang True Immortals. The tribulation clouds appear, terrifying the Seamless Gate forces.
- True Immortal Baiji calmly counters by crushing a 'Chaos Daofruit', releasing Chaos Force mist that envelops and weakens the formation's attack by 90%.
- The weakened attack still manages to destroy one White-Faced Flood Dragon and kill hundreds of Celestial Immortals, but it fails to achieve a decisive blow.
- True Immortal Baiji then reveals his hidden reserve: four new White-Faced Flood Dragons, one of which is powerful enough to single-handedly match the Grand Xia's elite Yinglong Legion.
- The chapter ends on a bleak note. The Xia Emperor has deployed all his forces, yet he is still at a disadvantage, with Baiji openly declaring his intent to conquer and rename the world.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a masterclass in the strategic depth of high-level Xianxia warfare, moving beyond simple brawls into the realm of counter-deployments and resource management. The key concept here is the 'Grand Xia World-Sealing Grand Formation' (大夏镇界大阵). Note the name: 'Sealing the World'. In a cultivation world where top experts can teleport or flee to other dimensions, the formation's first effect is to 'seal space', preventing escape and turning the battlefield into a one-way arena. Its foundation, the Tianmang Hall Imperial Palace, with the 3,600 commandery cities as nodes, is a classic trope of the Emperor using the entire realm's geographical power grid as a weapon. It's a work of supreme artifice, credited to the legendary Daofather Yinglong, a primordial divine beast.

The counter to this is the 'Chaos Daofruit' (混沌道果). This is not a cheap item one can buy at a store. It represents a True God or Daofather literally cutting away a sliver of their own insight into the Heavenly Dao of Chaos—their own comprehension of the ultimate law of reality. This is a permanent loss of cultivation, one that takes immense time and effort to restore. That the Seamless Gate would sacrifice such a precious resource to counter a single formation shows just how high the stakes are and how well-prepared they are. The 'Chaos Force' itself is introduced here as the highest tier of power, the raw stuff of the primordial chaos that only peak beings can manipulate. The hierarchy is clear: Ki/Divine Power < Immortal Power < Chaos Force.

Finally, we get the 'White-Faced Flood Dragon' (白脸蛟) as a military unit. It's a combined formation of 1,000 Celestial Immortals and 100,000 Loose Immortals, taking the form of a massive, terrifying beast. This is a common Xianxia trope—armies fusing their power into a giant, monster-like avatar to focus their strength. The fact that some 'White-Faced Flood Dragons' have an Empyrean God or True Immortal as a core commander, making them disproportionately stronger, shows the layered command structure of such formations.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is almost entirely a strategic war update, so don't expect personal duels or breakthroughs. Instead, enjoy the tactical tension. The highlight is undeniably the clash between the World-Sealing Grand Formation and the Chaos Daofruit—it's a philosophical battle between a civilization's greatest weapon (the formation) and a universe's ultimate power (Chaos Force). Pay close attention to the hierarchy of powers and the immense cost of the items being used. True Immortal Baiji is at his most smug and arrogant here, and his declaration of victory feels credible, which makes the undercurrent of desperation on the Grand Xia side all the more palpable. The author's note at the end is a charming, real-world reminder of the life of a serial web novelist, asking for monthly votes. This chapter sets up the need for a completely new variable to break this deadlock, and all eyes will be on who or what that will be.
