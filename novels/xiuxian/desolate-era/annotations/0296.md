## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists! Chapter 0296 serves up a piping-hot platter of high-stakes auction drama, simmering vendetta, and a surprise player that just might tip the scales. Ji Ning has planted himself right in the lion's den—the Heavenly Treasures Mountain headquarters in the Grand Xia imperial capital—and he's not hiding. With the Shaoyan clan glaring daggers from across the arena and a mysterious red-robed elder making moves that scream "I know something you don't," this chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia political theater. Oh, and did we mention a formation diagram from the legendary Turtle-Crab Immortals just hit the bidding block? Buckle up, because the game of thrones just got a whole lot spicier.

## Key Plot Points
- Ji Ning reviews an intelligence dossier on the most formidable Wanxiang Adepts expected to compete in the Immortal Fortunes Assembly. His own name appears—thanks largely to his killing of Shaoyan Nong and a Primal-level Fiendgod—placing him on the radar of every major power in the capital.
- The monthly Treasure Competition Assembly begins at Heavenly Treasures Mountain's headquarters, requiring a steep 10,000-catty Primordial Liquid deposit just to participate. Ji Ning pays up, explicitly aiming to make a name for himself so that major clans (like the Zhuanshui clan, enemies of Shaoyan) will learn of his presence and potentially offer alliances.
- The Turtle-Crab Immortals' formation diagram goes up for auction. Ji Ning bids against Shaoyan Xiu Shui, escalating the price deliberately in public view to cement his reputation as a thorn in the Shaoyan clan's side.
- A bald, red-robed elder enters the fray, topping Shaoyan Xiu Shui's bid with a decisive 110,000 catties. The elder's identity is a mystery, but his intervention sends a clear signal: he is either an ally of Ji Ning or someone with his own score to settle against the Shaoyan clan.
- The Shaoyan clan's fury is palpable, but they cannot act within the safe confines of Heavenly Treasures Mountain. The chapter ends with Ji Ning calmly noting that his plan to broadcast his arrival is working exactly as intended.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Immortal Fortunes Assembly (仙缘大会):** This is the Grand Xia Dynasty's equivalent of a nationwide talent-scouting tournament, open to Wanxiang Adepts from every corner of the realm. Winning here is a fast track to becoming a disciple of some of the most exalted figures in the Three Realms. Think of it as a cosmic-grade dragon boat race, except the boats are flying swords and the paddles are divine abilities. The assembly is so prestigious that merely being named in the pre-event intelligence dossier is a badge of honor—and a death sentence for those who prefer anonymity.

**Heavenly Treasures Mountain (天宝山):** This isn't your run-of-the-mill pawn shop. The Grand Xia capital's Heavenly Treasures Mountain headquarters is a fortress-city of commerce, run by a neutral mercantile behemoth that dwarfs even the imperial clan's direct economic influence. Their monthly Treasure Competition Assembly is where universe-class rarities change hands, and bidding wars here can set the stage for dynastic feuds. The deposit requirement—ten thousand catties of Primordial Liquid per guest—is a brutal filter: anyone who can afford that seat is a player worth watching.

**Turtle-Crab Immortals (龟蟹二仙):** In the vast lore of Desolate Era, these two demon Celestial Immortals are legendary grandmasters of formations, said to have studied under a true power of the Three Realms. Their "game piece" formation diagram, though a casual work, is a treasure trove of array knowledge that could transform a dedicated student into a formation grandmaster. That it sold for a mere 110,000 catties of Primordial Liquid is a reflection of how undervalued the art of formations is among the combat-focused cultivator elite.

## Reading Guide
Ji Ning's decision to "go public" in the imperial capital is a pivotal strategic pivot. Up to this point, he's been operating in the shadows—ambushing, retreating, and surviving. Now he's baiting the trap in broad daylight, daring the Shaoyan clan to make a move while stacking the deck with potential allies. The red-robed elder's intervention is a classic Xianxia trope: the mysterious benefactor who appears during an auction to throw a wrench in the antagonist's plans. But is he an ally? A rival? A pawn in a larger game? Keep your eyes peeled, because in this world, every gift comes with a price tag—and often a hidden blade. Also, note how the chapter's emotional beats are orchestrated: Ji Ning's cold calm versus Shaoyan Jun's barely contained rage. The contrast is not just character writing—it's a recruitment advertisement for any clan looking to adopt a young, ferocious, and recklessly bold sword immortal as a hired gun.
