## Chapter Overview
Who says being a genius is easy? The day after Ji Ning's explosive debut at the Dao Debate Palace, his Northmount Peak estate transforms into a mini social arena. Fellow formal disciples stream in one after another, eager to network with the new monster talent. Between polite conversations and humble gratitude, Ji Ning finds himself fielding admiration from his seductive servant Yun Lu (who is *deeply* frustrated by his indifference), bonding with Brother Beishan Baiwei, and receiving a jaw-dropping congratulations package from Baiwei's father—the terrifying Marquis candidate Beishan Heihu. Oh, and he also gets an update on his friend Meng Yan. Get ready, fellow Daoists, for a calm-before-the-storm chapter where political connections and reputation are the real treasures.

## Key Plot Points
1. **The Morning after Glory**: Ji Ning emerges from meditation, having solidified his insights from the Dao Debate Palace. His aura is sharper, and his excitement about the Dao of the Sword and becoming a Sword Immortal is palpable.
2. **The Beleaguered Beauty**: Yun Lu, the ultra-confident Zifu servant sent by Beishan Baiwei, fumes privately over Ji Ning's lack of attention. She compares herself to Yu Wei and vows to win him over, but Ji Ning's Dao-heart remains utterly impervious to her charms.
3. **The Social Influx**: Formal disciples arrive in waves—Brother Yongchun, the gossipy Primal Daoist overseers, and others. The gathering is a subtle political dance: talents are ranked, connections are made, and Ji Ning’s status as the group's apex prodigy is confirmed.
4. **The Nine Lotus Visit**: Senior Apprentice-Sister Nine Lotus arrives with two fellow beauties (Qingqing and Baishui), adding a layer of elegance and elevation to the social scene. Yun Lu’s inferiority complex deepens as she compares herself to these 'true celestial maidens.'
5. **The Heihu Gift Package**: When dusk falls, Beishan Baiwei arrives bearing a massive congratulatory gift from his father: two sets of high-grade Human-ranked flying swords, wings, a flying boat, a life-saving treasure, and a whopping 1,000 jin of Primordial Liquid. Ji Ning accepts, solidifying his alliance with the Beishan clan.
6. **News of Meng Yan**: Baiwei confirms that Meng Yan failed to enter any sect and offers to bring him to the Black-White College.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Politics of Prodigy Status**: In Xianxia, a martial prodigy's breakthrough isn’t just a personal milestone—it's a *social nuclear detonation*. The instant Jiang Li’s talent was recognized at the Dao Debate Palace, the power dynamics around him recalibrated. Enemies became flatterers; neutrals became eager friends. This chapter perfectly captures the "social detonation" trope: Ji Ning’s Northmount Peak becomes a place where reputations shift in seconds, and ‘monster genius’ is the only label that matters. It’s a dog-eat-dog world, and talent is the ultimate currency.

**The Art of the Political Gift**: Beishan Heihu's delivery of 1,000 jin of Primordial Liquid and a set of top-grade Human-ranked treasures isn't just generosity—it's a masterful political investment. In the Grand Xia Dynasty, backing a rising star early is a classic way for powerful noble families to secure future alliances. For Heihu, who is a candidate for the Marquis of Anchan, having a legendary Sword Immortal like Ji Ning in his debt is worth far more than these material goods.

**The 'Dao-heart' vs. Temptation**: Yun Lu’s failure to attract Ji Ning is a classic Xianxia subversion. In many stories, a beautiful woman is an easy distraction. But a cultivator with a true Dao-heart, like Ji Ning, sees such charms as a potential trap. His pursuit of the ultimate Dao of the Sword and the path to invincibility renders such earthly allure insignificant. This reinforces the text's central theme: true power comes from indomitable will, not worldly attachments.

## Reading Guide
This is a classic 'politics and networking' breather episode, but don't let the lack of combat fool you. The real battles are fought with words, gifts, and social maneuvering. Pay close attention to the subtle hierarchy forming. Notice how Ji Ning, despite his youth, handles the attention with a calm, controlled grace—never boastful, always polite, but with an unshakeable core. The visit from Nine Lotus isn't just a social call; it's another layer of his growing network. And the massive gift from the Beishan clan? That's a serious power play. Ji Ning is no longer just a talent from a backwater tribe; he's a political asset. The chapter sets the stage perfectly for the next big event, and it’s a masterclass in how 'the strong are respected' in the Xianxia world.
