## Chapter Overview
Holy crap, are you feeling that tension? Because I sure am. Chapter 495 delivers the brutal, bloody, and utterly satisfying payoff to the Anchan City siege, and it’s a masterclass in high-stakes Xianxia combat. Our boy Ji Ning has just demolished a formation of eighteen elite Celestial Immortals from the Seamless Gate, and he did it without breaking a sweat. We’re talking a complete and utter reversal of fortune. The chapter opens with the enemy in full panic mode, their perfectly laid trap shattered by Ji Ning’s monstrous strength. Their intelligence was wrong, their calculations were off, and they are paying for that mistake with their lives. It’s a thrilling sequence of pure, unadulterated, systematic slaughter. But amidst the carnage, we see the cold, calculating mind of a true Sword Immortal at work. Ji Ning isn't just overpowered; he’s efficient, conserving his Heartforce for critical kills. This chapter is a stark reminder that in the Three Realms, information is the deadliest weapon, and Ji Ning now wields it with absolute authority. Get ready for a chapter where the hunter becomes the hunted, and the only currency is cosmic annihilation.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Tables Are Turned:** The Seamless Gate’s eighteen-Celestial Immortal formation is broken. Ji Ning’s sudden appearance and terrifying power cause the survivors to scatter in utter panic, their perfect ambush in ruins.
*   **The Mechanism of Massacre:** Ji Ning uses his Three Heads, Six Arms technique in conjunction with the Starseizing Hand and the newly understood Sword Force. Each of his six sword fingers is now a killing blow on par with a Tonghe-level Celestial Immortal, allowing him to engage multiple peak experts simultaneously.
*   **Seamless Gate Reacts:** In the Fifth World, the terrifying trinity of the Seamless Gate—the Lord, the Lord of the Ten Thousand Demons Cave, and the Lord of the Bloodcloud Tower—watch the battle through watery mirrors. Their shock is palpable as they realize Ji Ning’s power has grown exponentially. They are completely baffled by his ability to improve in the Dao-less Realm of Annihilation.
*   **The Violetgrass Celestial Immortal's Last Stand:** A key enemy, protected by an azure light barrier and backed by the Floatcloud True Immortal, survives multiple direct hits. She crushes her summons talismans, desperately calling for aid from her powerful backers within the Seamless Gate.
*   **A Pure Yang Trap:** Two fleeing Celestial Immortals try to hide inside their Immortal estates to buy time for a Greater Teleportation Dao-talisman. However, Ji Ning has cleverly lent the Shaoyin clan’s Pure Yang Divine Bell to Yu Wei, who effortlessly traps them.
*   **Justice is Absolute:** The Violetgrass Celestial Immortal is left alone. As a furious Pure Yang True Immortal, Floatcloud, shatters the outer sealing formation and screams for Ji Ning to stop, it’s too late. Ji Ning unleashes all six sword fingers, shattering her protective barrier and annihilating her.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **The Seamless Gate's Intelligence Network:** The panic among the Seamless Gate experts highlights a core tenet of high-level Xianxia warfare: intelligence is paramount. At the Celestial Immortal level and above, battles are rarely won by brute force alone. Knowing your enemy’s techniques, treasures, and combat habits is essential. The Seamless Gate’s failure to account for Ji Ning’s new Heartforce techniques or the loan of the Pure Yang Bell is a fatal error that costs them their entire elite squad.
*   **The Power of Direct Lineage (再论师承):** The chapter explicitly calls out why Ji Ning is such an outlier. It’s not just his talent; it’s the legacy he carries. The “Starseizing Hand” (a top-ten divine ability) and Houyi’s archery techniques (the pinnacle of Heartforce in the Three Realms) are legacies from the most powerful beings in history. This reinforces the Xianxia trope that a peerless master is just as important as a peerless disciple. Without Daofather Subhuti’s guidance, Daoist Threelives’s technique, and Houyi’s conceptual framework, Ji Ning would be a strong Sword Immortal, but not the monster he has become.
*   **Conservation of Heartforce:** Ji Ning’s tactical choice to conserve his Heartforce is a critical detail. While his sword fingers (at Tonghe-level power) are devastating, his trump card—Heartforce—is a finite resource. In Xianxia, ‘ultimate attacks’ often come at a high price. Heartforce is depicted not as mana, but as a deep, spiritual well that must be carefully rationed in a prolonged battle.

## Reading Guide
*   **Visualize the Carnage:** When reading Ji Ning’s six-armed assault, imagine the sheer scale. Each sword finger is thousands of feet long, sweeping across the sky. The Celestial Immortals aren’t just being stabbed; they are being crushed and erased by pillars of pure Sword Force and divine power. Think less of a duel and more of a cosmic-scale cleaning operation.
*   **Watch for the Tension Cues:** Pay attention to the reactions of the Seamless Gate's leadership. Their quiet, calculating dread is a fantastic narrative device. It tells you, the reader, that Ji Ning isn’t just winning this battle; he is rewriting the entire power balance of the war. Their fear is your confirmation of his monstrous progress.
*   **The Irony of the Rescue:** The final scene is dripping with delicious irony. The Floatcloud True Immortal, a being of immense power, shatters the formation to save the Violetgrass Celestial Immortal. He does everything right—a powerful entrance, a booming warning—but he arrives a fraction of a second too late. This is a classic Xianxia trope: the last-minute rescue that fails, and it’s executed perfectly here to show that Ji Ning’s decisiveness is faster than even a True Immortal’s intervention.
