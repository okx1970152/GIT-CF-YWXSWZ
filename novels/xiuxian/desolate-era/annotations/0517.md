## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in *calm before the storm* and *gear acquisition* all rolled into one glorious sequence. Ji Ning, fresh from his near-death experience in the Realm of Annihilation, finally gets to raid the War God Hall's treasure vault for a second time. But the real meat? It's the visceral, overwhelming scale of the war he's about to step into. Picture this: our boy walks into the War God Hall hoping for a decent sword, walks out with a legendary Buddhist death-blossom set, and then teleports straight into a meeting with *ten thousand of the most powerful cultivators in the realm*. If you've ever wondered what a world-ending draft notice looks like in the Desolate Era universe, this is it. Get ready for some serious Xianxia politicking, a divine power-up that redefines "sword formation," and the chilling realization that even a top-tier monster like Ji Ning is just one fish in a very big, very violent ocean.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The War God Hall Treasure Selection:** Ji Ning returns to the War God Hall to claim his second selection of Pure Yang treasures from Daoist Threelives' collection. Faced with twenty-two sets, he initially hopes for flying swords. Two sets stand out: the *Three Pure Ones Swords* (three swords made by Daoist Three Purities) and the *Universal Salvation Sword of Ananda* (nine swords forged by the Buddha Ananda).
*   **Choosing the Nine Swords:** Despite the allure of the Three Pure Ones Swords, Ji Ning opts for the *Universal Salvation Sword of Ananda*, a nine-sword set. The logic is pure Xianxia math: nine Pure Yang supreme-grade swords synergizing in the Grand Thousand Sword Formation will output far more damage than just three, even if the three are slightly more potent individually. He also gets a taste of the artifact spirits, as nine golden monk children pop out to quiz him on his murderous intentions.
*   **The Tribulation on the Horizon:** We get a crucial update on Ji Ning's power scaling. The Nine-Petal Snow Lotus was a defensive/restraint tool. This new sword formation is pure, overwhelming attack power. The tone shifts dramatically as Ji Ning notes that his Primaltwin still hasn't weathered its tribulation, meaning his ultimate firepower is still locked away.
*   **Mobilization of the Armies:** The scene cuts to the Grand Xia Imperial Capital. The Xia Emperor has summoned every single Loose Immortal and Celestial Immortal under his domain. We see the sheer, jaw-dropping scale of it: over two hundred from just Anchan Commandery alone, with thousands more converging from across the three thousand six hundred commanderies.
*   **The Meeting of Ten Thousand Immortals:** Ji Ning and his group enter the Skymist Hall to find over nine thousand Celestial Immortals assembled, with the number swelling past ten thousand. The sheer density of power is intimidating, showing that even a monstrous genius like Ji Ning is just one elite among many. The Xia Emperor makes a grand entrance, flanked by five Empyrean Gods and True Immortals, finally calling the entire realm to arms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **The Xianxia "Arms Race"**: The core of this chapter is a classic progression fantasy gear-up. Ji Ning is not just getting a sword; he's getting a *systematic upgrade* to his fighting style. The choice between quality (Three Pure Ones Swords) and quantity (Universal Salvation Sword of Ananda) is a tactical puzzle. It perfectly illustrates the Xianxia philosophy that a cultivator's combat power is a mathematical function of their cultivation base, the quality of their techniques, and the synergy of their treasures. Nine swords in a formation > three, even if the three are individually better. This is the logic of the sword formation archetype.
*   **The Daoist and Buddhist Trinity:** The chapter name-drops two of the most significant figures in Chinese mythology and Xianxia: Daoist Three Purities (Sanqing) and the Buddha Ananda.
    *   **Daoist Three Purities:** This is the highest trinity in the Daoist pantheon, consisting of the Jade Pure One (Yuanshi Tianzun), the Supreme Pure One (Laozi), and the Numinous Treasure One (Lingbao Tianzun). In *Desolate Era*, IET cleverly conflates them into a single character, "Daoist Three Purities," implying that the three are merely avatars of a single supreme being. This is a great example of how the novel borrows classical lore and remixes it for its own power hierarchy.
    *   **Ananda:** In Buddhist tradition, Ananda was Gautama Buddha's first cousin and one of his ten principal disciples, famed for his incredible memory. Here, he is elevated to a Buddha-level (Daofather-level) creator. The "Universal Salvation Sword" and its little monk spirits asking Ji Ning if he will "sever the world's sufferings" is a darkly humorous bit of Xianxia—he's going to be doing that by *killing his enemies en masse*.
*   **The Concept of the "Great Tribulation"**: The chapter reinforces the gravity of the *Great Tribulation of the Three Realms*. This isn't a political squabble or a minor war. It's an existential crisis that overrides all other conflicts. The summoning of every single Celestial Immortal and Loose Immortal is the Xianxia equivalent of a total planetary draft. The fact that even reclusive, ancient powers are forced to send their forces shows that no one is safe. The authority behind this is absolute, coming from Daofather Crimsonbright, a top-tier power in the Three Realms.

## Reading Guide
Okay, fellow Daoists, let's talk about what *really* matters in this chapter: the sheer, visceral *scale* of the Xianxia endgame. This isn't just a fight; it's a system-wide mobilization. The image of ten thousand Celestial Immortals gathering in one hall is meant to feel overwhelming. Ji Ning, who can single-handedly fight nine Celestial Immortals, is suddenly just a face in a crowd of nine *thousand*. This is a masterful power-scaling trick by IET. Just as you think Ji Ning is the biggest fish in the pond, he gets dropped into the ocean. The war is going to be a meat grinder, and even a top-tier talent is going to have to work hard to survive.

Pay close attention to the character beats here too. Ji Ning's primary concern during the world's most serious military briefing is his pregnant wife, Yu Wei. He's thinking about how to get her excused from front-line combat. This perfectly frames his character arc for the upcoming war. He's not just fighting for the Three Realms; he's fighting for *them*. His Dao-heart is anchored in personal love, not abstract duty.

Also, don't sleep on the Nine Sub-Child Invisible Hooks. They were the number one treasure on the list! Ji Ning didn't pick them, but the fact that they were an option tells you a lot about the kind of crazy, terrifying weapons that are about to be unleashed. A set of hooks that locks space and prevents teleportation? That's a hard counter to any cultivator's escape plan. Just a reminder that Ji Ning's power-up is huge, but the enemy's arsenal is probably just as terrifying.
