## Chapter Overview
Alright, buckle up, because Ji Ning just got himself swallowed by a gargantuan Basilisk! This chapter is a claustrophobic, high-stakes horror show. Our boy is literally trapped inside a living, breathing primordial beast, and the twelve white-robed assassins of Bloodcloud Hall are using its body as a shooting gallery. Forget a fair fight—this is a 'digest the enemy from the inside out' special. The pressure is immense as Ji Ning defends against point-blank magic attacks, forcing him to reveal his trump card early: the Ancient River formation diagram hidden within the Thousandbull Sword. This battlefield-altering move creates a tense stalemate, pushing the assassins to a desperate, cost-heavy decision they wanted to avoid.

## Key Plot Points
- **Swallowed Whole:** Ji Ning’s true body is sucked into the Basilisk’s maw by a force he can’t resist. The Whitewater Hound, Wufeng Immortal, and the spying Bloodfiend all believe he’s doomed.
- **Close-Quarters Hell:** Inside the Basilisk, Ji Ning is assaulted from all sides by treasures and spells that emerge directly from the flesh and bone around him. Even his ‘Water Heavens’ defensive art can’t hold indefinitely.
- **The Ancient River Emerges:** Forced onto the back foot, Ji Ning activates the Thousandbull Sword’s hidden formation diagram, manifesting the ten-thousand-li-long ‘Ancient River’ to bind and pressure the Basilisk from both inside and out.
- **Cracks in the Formation:** The Ancient River’s suppression is *just* enough to strain the Basilisk Formation, stunning the assassins. Their leader recognizes it as a Pure Yang-level treasure and becomes greedily determined to kill Ji Ning for it.
- **The Forbidden Art:** To break the deadlock and bury Ji Ning, the leader forces his team to execute the ‘Basilisk Prison, Soul Annihilation’—the first-tier forbidden art of the formation. Ji Ning is plunged into the Basilisk’s corrosive blood lake.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**What’s a Formation Diagram (阵图)?** In the Xianxia gear ladder, normal magic treasures are just fancy clubs. But a Pure Yang-ranked treasure has an entire battlefield pre-installed as a ‘formation diagram’ inside it. You don’t just swing the sword—you *unleash the terrain*. The ‘Ancient River’ isn’t a spell; it’s a stable, reusable pocket dimension of a legendary river that acts like a battlefield control zone, crushing and binding all enemies in its area. This is the kind of firepower that marks a cultivator as being in the ‘I-have-a-legitimate-Pure-Yang-weapon’ tier.

**The Basilisk Formation and ‘Ten-Thousand Immortal’ Tactics:** The Basilisk isn’t a real animal—it’s a twelve-man tactical fusion. This is the classic ‘Ten Thousand Immortal Formation’ trope: multiple cultivators sync their energy patterns to project a single, massive, immortal entity. Their strength is pooled, their Ki is combined, and the giant construct acts as their shared body. The weakness is that if the construct is suppressed (like by the Ancient River), the energy drain on each individual member spikes dangerously, forcing them to either break the formation or burn their lifeblood in a forbidden art.

**What’s a Forbidden Art (禁术)?** The name isn’t for drama. These are techniques that trade an irreversible cost for a massive power spike. The ‘Basilisk Prison’ uses the users’ own blood essence and vitality as fuel. They are *literally* burning their magic potential and lifespan to crush Ji Ning. For a cultivator, this is like burning your retirement fund for a single, desperate bet. The greed for a Pure Yang treasure has to be overwhelming to make them agree to this.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the ‘pressure cooker’ moment. It’s not about Ji Ning being weak—it’s about Bloodcloud Hall deploying their A-team counter-strategy the moment they knew who he was. The Basilisk was the *plan*, not a random monster. Notice how the Bloodfiend, a Wanxiang-level demon himself, is reduced to a terrified spectator in his own arc. The real battle is between the Primal-level combatants. The ‘Basilisk Prison’ is the ultimate gear check: can Ji Ning’s Fiendgod body, which has survived Hellfire and divine tribulations, survive a bath of pure, corrosive demonic blood? This is life-or-death alchemy, and we’re about to find out if Ning’s technique is pure enough to burn through the impurity.

## Power Scale Assessment
- **Ji Ning (True Body):** A late-stage Primal Fiendgod Body Refiner wielding a Pure Yang-tier sword (the Thousandbull Sword). Can fully unleash the Ancient River formation diagram.
- **Earth Nine Team (Bloodcloud Hall):** Twelve Immortal-level cultivators piloting a combined Basilisk formation. The Basilisk's power is equivalent to a Celestial Immortal but with Ki-sharing vulnerabilities.
- **Bloodfiend (Blood God Cult):** A Wanxiang-level cultivator completely outclassed. Trapped by a Fuxi Staff Formation he can't break.
- **Wufeng Immortal & Whitewater Hound:** Alive and mobile, but the Whitewater Hound's formation is keeping the Bloodfiend at bay, not engaging the Basilisk directly.
