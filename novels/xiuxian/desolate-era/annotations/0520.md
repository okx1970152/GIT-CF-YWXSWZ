## Chapter Overview

This chapter is pure Xianxia geopolitical flexing, and it’s absolutely glorious. We move from the intimate personal trial of the Heartforge Tribulation prep straight into the Grand Xia’s war machine flexing its muscles. The focus is a massive, live-fire test of the Xingtian Formation—a deity-summoning super-weapon that turns a thousand Celestial Immortals and a hundred thousand Loose Immortals into a single, god-tier war avatar. The chapter is basically a high-stakes power-leveling montage where the most talented Celestial Immortals in the realm compete to see who can command the Xingtian God most effectively by smashing blocks of literal stardust. The tension is less “will they die” and more “who gets to be the pilot of the universe’s most expensive mecha.” Get ready for some number-crunching, some jaw-dropping feats of strength, and Ji Ning stepping up to the plate for his turn.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Xingtian Formation is Deployed:** A formation of 1,001 Celestial Immortals and 100,000 Loose Immortals merges their power and battle-will, condensing world’s vital energy from a million-li radius to form the massive, ten-thousand-zhang tall Xingtian God. The Grand Xia Emperor personally supervises the trial.
- **The Chaotic Goldstone Benchmark:** The Grand Xia Emperor introduces a test: each Celestial Immortal who commands the Xingtian Formation must strike a stack of ten blocks of Chaotic Goldstone. The more blocks shattered, the greater their command over the formation. It’s a raw power benchmark.
- **The Line-up:** Top-tier Celestial Immortals like Northpolar, Flying Rain of the Tiannong clan (a surprise powerhouse alchemist), and Celestial Immortal Tonghe all take their turns, with the top performers shattering six blocks. Tonghe ties for second place among the first half.
- **Ji Ning Takes the Stage:** After over three hundred attempts, Ji Ning is finally called up as a late-entry powerhouse. He forms the Xingtian God in his own image and begins to familiarize himself with its overwhelming power, leaving the audience speculating on his performance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Xingtian:** The Xingtian Formation is named after a real figure from Chinese mythology. Xingtian was a headless god who was decapitated by the Yellow Emperor in a primordial battle. Undeterred, Xingtian used his nipples as eyes and his navel as a mouth, and continued to fight, wielding a shield and axe. This myth perfectly captures the savage, unkillable spirit of the war avatar the formation creates. The Xingtian God features a torso-like “head” with eyes as nipples and a navel mouth, directly referencing this legend.

**Chaotic Goldstone (混沌金石):** This is one of the premier “chad” materials of the Xianxia universe. It’s so hard that only Empyrean Gods and True Immortals can normally damage it. The specks of gold within are “Chaotic Gold Essence,” a material so dense and indestructible that even Daofathers struggle to process it. A tiny amount is enough to make a Pure Yang treasure virtually unbreakable. This test isn’t just a flex; it’s a raw physics lesson in the power disparities of the Three Realms.

**The “Pilot” Dynamic:** The Xingtian Formation is a classic Xianxia “super-weapon” trope. The formation is a chassis of incredible power, but its effectiveness depends entirely on the “pilot” (the lead Celestial Immortal). No matter how powerful the engine, an imperfect driver will leave power on the table. This sets up a prestige contest where martial strength isn't everything—Dao comprehension and soul talent are the deciding factors in who can best steer this god of war.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a beautiful piece of “show, don’t tell” in terms of geopolitical power. The Grand Xia Emperor isn’t just mobilizing an army; he’s running a very public, very expensive aerial display to remind every observer (including potential spies from the Seamless Gate) that he commands a weapon so devastating it requires a million Immortals to witness its calibration. Pay attention to the subtle details: the way Celestial Immortal Tonghe, an independent expert, is perfectly fine with being slotted into the formation; the way the Grand Xia Emperor himself pilots the entire event with the calm authority of a CEO reviewing quarterly reports. This is statecraft through overwhelming force projection. Also, enjoy the brutal simplicity of the test. No complex trial or secret technique—just “hit this rock really, really hard.” Xianxia logic at its finest.
