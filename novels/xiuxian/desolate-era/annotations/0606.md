## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Qidian Economy: Alliance Masters & Tipping Culture** — If you're a Western reader seeing "Alliance Master" for the first time, it's a huge deal in Chinese web novel culture. This is not a simple "donation." It represents a substantial monetary tip within the Qidian ecosystem (the platform hosting the novel). Each "Alliance Master" tip is a significant amount (roughly 1000 RMB), and a book hitting 100 Alliance Masters on its launch day is a legendary benchmark of popularity and fan dedication. It's proof that the community has anointed the book as a hit on day zero. The "Red Alliance" is a self-organized fan group for IET, akin to a rowdy, passionate guild of supporters who coordinate tipping and voting campaigns. The author’s shock and gratitude are completely genuine; these fans are not just readers but vocal investors in the novel's success.
