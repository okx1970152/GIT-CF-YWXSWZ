## Chapter Overview

Chapter 31 delivers a monumental turning point in Ji Ning's cultivation journey. Trapped inside the Divine Abilities Hall, Ji Ning is pushed to his absolute limit by the endless waves of Lishi soldiers. But it's not brute force that saves him—it's a soul-shattering breakthrough. Driven by his fierce determination to protect his family at Oxhorn Mountain, Ji Ning's will crystallizes, his Dao-heart purifies, and he successfully manifests his 'Divine Sense', a soul-power normally reserved for Primal Daoists. This is the 'opening of the Heavenly Eye', a game-changing moment that instantly amplifies his combat power tenfold, allowing him to crush the trial and claim the coveted **first divine ability**. The chapter is a masterful blend of emotional stakes and cultivation mechanics, showing that the path to power is paved with love, loss, and an unbreakable will.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Desperate Struggle**: Ji Ning is overwhelmed inside the Divine Abilities Hall, his strength waning against the ever-stronger Lishi soldiers. Fueled by the memories of his family—Patriarch Ji Jiuhuo, Granny Shadow, his father Ji Yichuan, and Uncle White—he refuses to yield.
2. **The Divine Sense Breakthrough**: In a moment of extreme pressure, Ji Ning’s soul undergoes a qualitative leap. He opens his ‘Heavenly Eye’ and manifests Divine Sense, a soul-based perception and telekinetic power that is the hallmark of Primal Daoists.
3. **The Massacre**: With his new Divine Sense, Ji Ning can now control nearly a hundred flying swords simultaneously, each with the power of a peak Zifu Fiendgod. He effortlessly annihilates the remaining Lishi soldiers, clearing the trial.
4. **The Artifact Spirit’s Awe**: The Yellow Bear artifact spirit is visibly shaken, suspecting Ji Ning might be the reincarnation of a Celestial Immortal or an ancient Fiendgod, as such a breakthrough at his level is virtually unheard of.
5. **The First Divine Ability**: By killing all the Lishi soldiers, Ji Ning earns the top reward—the first divine ability left behind by the aquatic palace's master. The trial space begins to shift, with time being frozen relative to the outside world.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Heavenly Eye & Divine Sense: The Soul's Evolution**: This chapter is a goldmine for understanding Xianxia soul-cultivation mechanics. The hierarchy is crucial: Zifu Disciples have a 'Divided Mind' (一心二用), Wanxiang Adepts can manifest 'Divine Thought' (神念) for telekinesis, and **Primal Daoists** (元神道人) typically unlock the 'Heavenly Eye' (天眼) and 'Divine Sense' (神识). Divine Sense is a game-changer—it is a 360-degree, hyper-detailed sensory field that bypasses normal sight and renders most illusionary formations useless. Ji Ning achieving this at the Zifu stage is an anomaly of epic proportions, usually a sign of a **reincarnated Immortal** or a soul that received miraculous fortification. This ties back to his Earthly memories and his early training in the **Nuwa Diagram**.

**The Dao-heart: The Soul's True Commander**: The chapter's philosophical heart is the analogy of the Dao-heart (道心) being the 'military discipline' of the soul. In Xianxia, raw power is useless without a firm will. A strong Dao-heart allows a cultivator to control unruly energy, resist mental attacks, and push through seemingly impossible bottlenecks. Ji Ning's breakthrough wasn't just about accumulation; it was about **tempering** his will through the grief, desperation, and love he felt for his family. This is a classic Xianxia trope: true power comes from clarity of purpose, not just puffing up one's energy reserves.
