## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a gut punch. Ji Ning, armed with King Yan’s time-reversal abilities, is forced to bear witness to the final, brutal chapter of his past life parents’ story: Liu Hechuan and Dongliu Xue’s peaceful existence at the Nine Curves Sword Sect shattered by a Seamless Gate massacre. We see their love, their hope, and their utterly helpless end, reduced to ash and their souls harvested into a demonic gourd. Ji Ning watches it all with a cold, analytical surface that barely contains a tidal wave of fury. But he doesn’t explode—he *processes*. This chapter is less about action and more about the crystallization of a new, all-consuming hatred for the Seamless Gate, a hatred that eclipses even his grudge against the Shaoyin Clan. It also marks a critical turning point where Ji Ning’s investigation shifts from gathering data to executing judgment, as he coolly sets his sights on the corrupt Riftfreeze Daoist, the man whose actions indirectly led to this tragedy.

## Key Plot Points
- **Witnessing the End**: King Yan reverses time to show Ji Ning the final years of Liu Hechuan and Dongliu Xue. Their idyllic life at the Nine Curves Sword Sect is crushed when twenty-seven experts from the Seamless Gate transform into three black hydras and obliterate the entire sect.
- **The Powerlessness of Being Weak**: The chapter brutally highlights the gap between cultivators. The sect’s Loose Immortals and Primal Daoists are swatted aside in moments. Ordinary disciples, including Liu Hechuan and Dongliu Xue, are killed by the shockwaves of a battle far beyond their level. Their final moments are poignant, embracing each other without panic, accepting their fate on the road to the Yellow Springs.
- **The Soul Gourd**: The souls of the hundreds of thousands of slaughtered disciples are harvested into a black gourd, making it clear that the Seamless Gate’s massacre was a targeted operation to fuel evil techniques.
- **The Forging of an Oath**: Ji Ning, maintaining his composure only through his mastery of third-stage Heartforce (Sovereign), declares the Seamless Gate his greatest enemy. He also reveals his long-term plan: even if his parents’ souls are scattered, he will one day become a True God or Daofather and beg Mother Nuwa to recover their truesouls from the river of fate.
- **Confronting Corruption**: With King Yan’s guidance, Ji Ning avoids a war with the entire Huashan Immortal Sect. They go straight to the top. The Huashan Ancestor, a Celestial Immortal terrified of Ji Ning’s reputation, drags the sleazy Riftfreeze Daoist before him and offers Ji Ning complete freedom to decide the man’s fate.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **The River of Fate (命运长河)**: In Xianxia cosmology, the River of Fate is the metaphysical repository of all events and existences within that universe. Even after complete soul obliteration (魂飞魄散), the most fundamental "truesoul" (真灵) of a being is said to return to this river, leaving a faint imprint. Retrieving a truesoul from the river is considered a feat attainable only by the highest order of beings, like Pangu or Nuwa. This concept provides a powerful narrative framework for a "non-final" death, offering hope against even the most irreversible-seeming tragedy.
- **Yellow Springs (黄泉)**: This is the Chinese mythological equivalent of the River Styx or the underworld. Telling someone "we can accompany each other on the road to the Yellow Springs" is a profound declaration of a bond that transcends even death. It is not a curse; it is a romantic and tragic vow to enter the afterlife together, as a final act of companionship.
- **Karmic Fire (业火)**: This is a recurring consequence from Ji Ning’s past battles. It is a flame of pure negative karma that burns a cultivator from the inside out, a punishment for excessive slaughter. The fact that Ji Ning was "exiled to a deathland and trapped by karmic fire" and still survived is the primary reason the Huashan Ancestor treats him with such fear and respect. In the Xianxia world, surviving a direct punishment from the Heavens is the ultimate proof of a monster’s strength.
- **The Sect Politics of Apology**: The Huashan Ancestor’s behavior is a classic example of Xianxia realpolitik. He doesn’t defend his own disciple. He immediately offers Ji Ning complete authority over the Riftfreeze Daoist’s fate, including options like "tormenting his soul for a thousand years." This is a calculated move to appease a clearly superior force and protect the rest of the sect from collateral damage.

## Reading Guide
- **A Breather Arc’s Emotional Climax**: You’ve been waiting for this. The slow burn of the previous arcs, the investigation, the dawning horror—it all pays off here. If you’ve been tracking Ji Ning’s past-life story, this chapter is the tragic fireworks finale.
- **Observe Ji Ning’s Maturity**: Watch how Ji Ning processes the trauma. There’s no screaming, no reckless charge against an impossible enemy. He absorbs the information, reframes his entire strategic landscape (elevating the Seamless Gate to his primary enemy), and then proceeds with cold, methodical efficiency. This is a different Ji Ning from the one who raged against the Shaoyin Clan.
- **The Setup for Revenge**: The chapter ends with the Riftfreeze Daoist kneeling before Ji Ning, trembling. The chapter title practically screams "Judgment." Get ready for Ji Ning to dispense some icy, surgical justice in the next chapter to cleanse the palate after all this emotional devastation.
