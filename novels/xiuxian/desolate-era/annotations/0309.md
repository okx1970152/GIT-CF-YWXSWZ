## Chapter Overview

Forget everything you thought you knew about Ji Ning’s power ceiling—this chapter shatters it. The Shaoyin clan’s assassination attempt finally hits its apex with the terrifying Bone-Slaying Immortal-Killing Needle, a treasure that would normally obliterate any Wanxiang Adept. Instead of a clean kill, Cloudfloat Immortal gets a horrifying surprise: Ji Ning, through a perfect storm of preparation (Starseizing Hand second cycle, Three Heads Six Arms mastery) and raw grit, actually *survives*. But the real fireworks come when the Death-Sealing Formation is deployed: Ji Ning’s Primaltwin emerges with over two hundred Heaven-ranked flying swords, turning a one-sided execution into a true showdown of Immortal-level sword-qi. The fellow Daoists watching outside go from pity to jaw-dropping disbelief, and Xiao Lang’s smug grin gets wiped clean off his face. Get ready—this is the moment Ji Ning proves that calling him a ‘mere Wanxiang’ is a fatal mistake.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Bone-Slaying Needle Strikes** – Cloudfloat Immortal lands a lightning-fast strike with a one-use assassination treasure that pierces through the Water-Fire Lotus and forces Ji Ning to a desperate, six-sword defense. Each impact is described like a mountain crashing into him.

2. **Ji Ning’s Miraculous Survival** – Despite his Fiendgod body being covered in cracks, Ji Ning survives the needle due to three factors: second-cycle Starseizing Hand (pushing his strength to Earth Immortal level), his Three Heads Six Arms martial ability (allowing six simultaneous blocking attempts), and a key upgrade—his Heaven-ranked flying swords are tough enough not to shatter against the needle.

3. **Blood Disc Heaven-Sealing Formation Deployed** – Cloudfloat Immortal drops his second trump card: a one-use formation that completely seals off the area, costing a million jin of Primordial Liquid. Even the Imperial Guard and thirty-six Loose Immortals of the Zhuanshui clan can’t break through immediately.

4. **External Spectators React** – Tiannong Quge, Zhuanshui Xiaolou, Boyan Fairy, and Xiao Lang watch from outside, stunned by Ji Ning’s power. Xiao Lang shifts from gloating to dread; the others acknowledge that Ji Ning’s combat level has already surpassed ordinary Wanxiang geniuses into true Earth Immortal territory.

5. **The Primaltwin Reveal** – Just as Cloudfloat Immortal prepares for a one-on-one kill, Ji Ning’s true body vanishes into the aquatic manor, and his Primaltwin emerges, surrounded by over two hundred Heaven-ranked flying swords, with nine powerful Watersplitting Pisharkswords as the formation core. The sword-qi now surpasses anything Ji Ning has shown before.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The ‘Primaltwin’ as Ultra Trump Card**
In Xianxia, a Primaltwin (第二元神) is a separate soul-body that can cultivate and fight independently, often hidden until the most critical moment. Ji Ning’s Primaltwin is a Ki Refining specialist with over two hundred Heaven-ranked flying swords, meaning his sword-formation power is multiplied dramatically compared to his Fiendgod true body. This is a classic Xianxia structure: you think you’re facing one powerhouse, but he’s been hiding a second, arguably more dangerous, self in reserve. The shock value is as much psychological as it is tactical.

**The Hierarchy of Combat Power**
This chapter gives a crystal-clear demonstration of how Xianxia power scaling works: Ji Ning, as a peak Wanxiang Adept, can already burst into Earth Immortal level strength through his divine abilities (Starseizing Hand + Three Heads Six Arms). Being a ‘monster genius’ in Xianxia means your base cultivation level is a lie—your effective strength is always measured by your Dao comprehension, divine abilities, and treasures, not just your realm. Cloudfloat Immortal, a real Loose Immortal, is flummoxed because he planned for a Wanxiang, but Ji Ning fights like an Earth Immortal.

## Reading Guide

**Brace for Impact** – The opening sequence of the Bone-Slaying Needle is pure tension. The translation uses quick, staccato verbs and repetitive collision sound effects (“Clang!” x6) to mirror the frantic rhythm of a life-or-death parry. Don’t skim—this is Ji Ning at his most desperate and most brilliant.

**Watch the ‘Fellow Daoist Commentary’** – The reactions of Tiannong, Zhuanshui, Boyan, and Xiao Lang aren’t just filler. They serve as a live audience rating Ji Ning’s power level, and their shift from pity to awe mirrors the reader’s own emotional arc. Xiao Lang’s final “impossible!” is a mini climax in itself.

**Know the Threat** – Cloudfloat Immortal isn’t a random brute. His two treasures (the needle and the formation) cost a fortune and were supplied by the Shaoyin clan as an assassination package. His strategy is sound: kill fast, trap the area, then finish the weakened target. He fails only because Ji Ning’s ‘preparation trumps his preparation’—a classic Xianxia reversed ambush.
