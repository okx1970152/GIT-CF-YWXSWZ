## Chapter Overview
Alright, folks, strap in. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia 'face-smacking'—the moment where our hero Ji Ning finally stops holding back and reveals the true depth of his arsenal. After spending the earlier chapters as the calmer observer, Ji Ning springs his ultimate surprise: his Primaltwin, a second clone of himself, is not only real but has been quietly trained to the peak of the Primal stage. The entire battlefield—from the arrogant Xiao Lang to the calculating Keshui Xiaolou—is stunned into silence. This isn't just a power flex; it's a strategic declaration that Ji Ning's combat potential has just doubled overnight. The action then dives into a brutal, head-to-head clash between Ji Ning's Primaltwin and the desperate Loose Immortal called Floating Cloud, who throws everything he has—including a soul-obscuring mist—at our hero. The chapter pauses at the perfect cliffhanger, with Ji Ning about to unveil a brand-new, self-created defensive technique: the Lotus Sword World!

## Key Plot Points
1. **The Primaltwin is Revealed!** Everyone catches on that the black-robed Ji Ning standing in the Blooddisk Heaven-Sealing Formation is actually his second Primaltwin clone. The shock is immediate: Xiao Lang cries disbelief, Keshui Xiaolou recalculates Ji Ning's net worth, and even Shaoyan Nong's eyes go wide.
2. **Debate on Portable Grottoes:** The narrative clarifies the critical trade-off in the cultivation world: a strong, stationary fortress cannot be portable, and a portable grotto is weak. Ji Ning's aquatic manor is an exception—a treasure so valuable it rivals Pure Yang artifacts.
3. **Floating Cloud's Desperate Gambit:** The Floating Cloud Immortal, cornered by Ji Ning's Primaltwin and fearing the Minshui clan's Loose Immortals outside, unleashes his ultimate forbidden art—the Heaven-Earth-Man three-sword array with a devastating diagram.
4. **The Clash of Sword Formations:** Two monstrous formations collide. Floating Cloud's Heaven-Earth-Man diagram versus Ji Ning's Lesser Thousand Sword Formation, led by the Bishui Sharkswords. The result is a shocking stalemate, proving Ji Ning's Primaltwin has top-tier Loose Immortal combat power.
5. **Desperate Tactics:** Floating Cloud, realizing he can't win through brute force, deploys 'Mist Concealing the Heavens,' a secret art that blocks divine sense and sight, turning the battle into a blind hunt.
6. **Ji Ning Counters with a New Art:** As the chapter closes, Ji Ning, unfazed by the blinding mist, reveals he has been working on a new defensive technique. Combining the Grand Dao Domain, the Water-Fire Lotus, and his Self-Created World, he prepares to unleash the 'Lotus Sword World'.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Primaltwin: Splitting the Soul to Cheat Death**
For a Xianxia protagonist, a Primaltwin (第二元神) is the ultimate insurance policy and a sign of monstrous talent. Normally, cultivating a Primaltwin requires a cultivator to split their soul—a process so dangerous that most would choose death over risking permanent damage. That Ji Ning pulled this off at only thirty years old is a testament to his freakishly strong soul (thanks to his Earthly memories and the Nuwa Diagram). Functionally, a Primaltwin is a perfect clone with its own independent cultivation, allowing the user to be in two places at once, to train two different arts (Ki Refining vs. Fiendgod Body Refining), or, as seen here, to fight a two-front war. The fact that Floating Cloud didn't know about it means Ji Ning's intelligence network is good, or his timing was perfect.

**Loose Immortals: The Cursed Immortals**
Floating Cloud isn't a true Immortal; he's a 'Loose Immortal' (散仙). In the Desolate Era world, when a cultivator fails their Heavenly Tribulation but their Primal soul manages to survive, they are forced into this state. They are incredibly powerful, sometimes rivaling True Immortals, but they are cursed. They don't live forever; instead, they face a cumulative series of calamities—the Three Calamities and Nine Tribulations—that grow stronger every time. Most Loose Immortals are only a few hundred thousand to a million years old before they inevitably die, unlike the truly immortal Celestial Immortals. This is why the Shaoyan clan can afford to send him as a 'dead soldier'—he's a high-value, but ultimately expendable, asset.

**The Dao of the Sword: Grand Dao Domain**
When the narrator says Ji Ning has comprehended the 'Grand Dao Domain' of the Sword, it means he has reached a profound level of mastery. A 'Domain' is an area of influence projected by one's Dao comprehension. For a Sword Immortal, this means the space around him is saturated with his will and sword-intent, making it harder for enemies to move freely and easier for Ji Ning to detect attacks. Floating Cloud's mist counters this by blocking divine sense, relying on a different sensory plane. Ji Ning's counter, the Lotus Sword World, is a high-level fusion of his domain, his self-created world reality, and the defensive properties of the Water-Fire Lotus—essentially creating a miniature pocket dimension where he is the absolute law.

## Reading Guide
This is a fantastic moment for any progression fantasy fan. The joy isn't just that Ji Ning is strong, but that he has been *planning*. The reveal of the 200+ Heaven-rank flying swords and the Primaltwin feels earned because we, the readers, have been following his journey. We saw him buy those swords. We saw his careful cultivation. Now, we get to see the payoff. If you loved the strategic, methodical fights where victory comes from preparation and cultivating the right tools, this chapter is a pure dopamine hit. Keep an eye on the 'Lotus Sword World'—it’s not just a defensive trick, it's a sign that Ji Ning is starting to synthesize his knowledge into a unique, personalized style, which is the hallmark of a truly exceptional cultivator. Get ready for the mist to be cleared!
