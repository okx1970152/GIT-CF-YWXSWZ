## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because the Realm War just went from "intense stalemate" to "full-blown catastrophe" in the span of a single sword-stroke! The Evergreen Sword Immortal—the cold, self-centered prick we've all been side-eyeing—finally shows his true colors, and they're not pretty. In one devastating betrayal, he murders the gentle Snowdance Fairy (a loyal companion of endless ages!), carves through five Karmic Punishment Deities, and hands the Seamless Gate the momentum they've been craving. But the Crimsonbright Realm isn't going down without a fight. Enter Dongzhe the Archer—a disheveled beggar-turned-mythic-sniper—who pops a Chaos Daofruit like a candy and starts one-shotting White-Faced Flood Dragons. This chapter is a masterclass in high-stakes Xianxia warfare: treachery, sacrifice, and the cold calculus of life and death on a cosmic scale.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Betrayal**: The Evergreen Sword Immortal reveals himself as a Seamless Gate sleeper agent, coldly killing the Snowdance Fairy and turning on the Xia Emperor's forces. His hidden power erupts to a level rivaling the Baiji True Immortal himself, and he single-handedly dismantles five Karmic Punishment Deities.
- **The Snowdance Fairy's Fall**: The gentle, peacemaking Snowdance Fairy—the Evergreen's eternal companion—falls without fear or rage, her tragic death serving as the emotional gut-punch of the chapter.
- **Seamless Gate's Full Assault**: Daofather Ink Bamboo seizes the chaos instantly, ordering a total offensive. The three Devouring Mountain demon gods merge into a three-eyed Fiendgod, and White-Faced Flood Dragons begin breaking apart into swarms of Heavenly God Puppets.
- **Dongzhe the Archer**: The Crimsonbright Realm's hidden trump card arrives, a filthy beggar-turned-divine-archer who consumes a Chaos Daofruit to unleash arrows that one-shot multiple White-Faced Flood Dragons at Daofather-threshold power.
- **The Cost of War**: The chapter highlights the brutal arithmetic of realm-wide conflict—Dongzhe's arrows kill thousands of Celestial Immortals per shot, but each shot burns a Chaos Daofruit, while the Seamless Gate's Heavenly God Puppets offer sustained combat efficiency.
- **Ji Ning's Rage**: Ji Ning watches the betrayal unfold, his fury burning cold and quiet. The Evergreen's cruelty toward his own companion plants a seed of implacable hatred that will surely bear fruit later.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Karmic Punishment Deities (刑天神)**: These aren't just big fighters—they're battle formations composed of Celestial Immortals and Loose Immortals, manifesting as giant, god-like avatars with immense power. Think of them as mecha-suits forged from living cultivators, each deity representing the coordinated might of hundreds of immortals.
- **White-Faced Flood Dragon (白脸蛟)**: Another formation-type unit, typically made from 1,000 Celestial Immortals and 100,000 Loose Immortals. Their collapse and transformation into multiple Heavenly God Puppets is a classic Xianxia tactical gambit—trading raw mass for precision and durability.
- **Chaos Daofruit (混沌道果)**: This is not some common pill you pick up at the market. A Chaos Daofruit is a condensed fragment of a True God Daofather's personal comprehension of Chaos itself, sacrificed permanently to create this burst of power. Using one is the equivalent of asking a billionaire to burn a non-renewable chunk of their soul—expensive, painful, and brutally effective.
- **Heartforce (心力) & Dongzhe's Archery**: Dongzhe's arrows are terrifying not because of raw energy, but because they combine Heartforce (a will-based metaphysical power) with Chaos Force and supreme archery technique. This layered attack is what pushes his arrows to the threshold of Daofather-level power and makes them nearly impossible to dodge—the void itself is suppressed.
- **The Evergreen's Betrayal as a Classical Trope**: The "loyal companion who was actually a spy all along" is a classic Xianxia wartime shocker. What makes it cut deeper here is the personal dimension—the Snowdance Fairy's devotion across "endless ages" contrasts brutally with the Evergreen's cold, transactional view of her as a "loyal pet" whose death was simply an acceptable exchange.

## Reading Guide
If this chapter had a subtitle, it'd be "Trust No One." The Evergreen Sword Immortal's betrayal is perfectly paced—the reveal hits like a freight train because we've seen him as that cold, arrogant, but ultimately reliable ally. The author IET knows exactly when to pull the rug, and he pulls it hard. Keep your eyes on the Evergreen's internal logic: he doesn't even pause over the Snowdance Fairy's death. That coldness isn't just villainous—it's a mirror of the Seamless Gate's entire philosophy of cosmic utility. Meanwhile, Dongzhe's entrance is pure cinematic gold: a filthy beggar bossing around street urchins one minute, a one-man army of divine archery the next. The "humble origin, divine power" trope never gets old. Pay attention to the math of the battlefield—the cost-benefit analysis of Chaos Daofruits versus Heavenly God Puppets, the strategic choices of who to kill and who to ignore. This isn't just a brawl; it's a war of resources, patience, and nerve.
