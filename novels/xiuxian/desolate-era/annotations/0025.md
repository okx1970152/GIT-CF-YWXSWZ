## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to the final day of the Golden Sword Ceremony! And what a finale it promises to be. This chapter is a masterclass in escalating tension, swapping the brutal trial-by-fire of the lower rounds for a single, shocking proclamation that turns the entire tournament on its head. Ji Ning stands before the gathered might of the Western Prefecture—the Xiantian lifeforms, the tens of thousands of citizens, and his seven bitter rivals—not to fight them one by one, but to face them all at once. It's a declaration of war from a father who believes in his son's strength, a gambit that could either cement Ji Ning's path to the Golden Sword or shatter his reputation before it's truly begun. Get ready for a clash that is as much about politics and pride as it is about sword and steel.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Conspiracy Takes Shape:** We open with a private meeting between Ji Lie and his adopted son, Pi. Ji Lie reveals his plan to have Pi, the 'Tigerfiend', enter the Golden Sword Ceremony explicitly to stop Ji Ning. This isn't just about a tournament win; it's a political move to block the succession of the Prefectural Lord's heir.
*   **A Father's Gambit:** The chapter's defining moment arrives when Ji Yichuan, the legendary Dripping Sword, stands before the assembly and makes a shocking demand. He proposes that his son Ji Ning fight all seven other finalists at once. The crowd buzzes with disbelief, and even the Prefectural Lord is stunned, highlighting the extreme pressure and faith Ji Yichuan has in his son.
*   **The Weight of Expectation:** The narrative masterfully shifts perspective to show the public's reaction. The gathered thousands are a mix of excited, skeptical, and even sympathetic. They see Ji Ning, the smallest and youngest on the stage, as a pawn in his father's cold-eyed game, a ten-year-old boy about to be sacrificed for a display of arrogance. This builds a powerful underdog narrative around Ji Ning.
*   **The Stage is Set:** The seven opponents, each a formidable youth who fought through the month-long melee, are now united by a common enemy. The chapter ends on a perfect cliffhanger, with the seven of them charging as one. The unsharpened weapons underscore that this is a test of skill and internal force, not just a bloody slaughter, but the intent behind the charge is deadly serious.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a fantastic lens for understanding the brutal, hierarchical politics of a Xianxia clan. The Golden Sword Ceremony is far more than a mere competition; it is a public ritual that determines the successor to the Prefectural Lord—the ruler of the Western Prefecture. Winning the Golden Sword isn't just about prestige; it grants the victor the legal and social right to be the next in line, directly threatening Ji Lie's faction. This is why the presence of the Ten Stewards and all the Xianxia lifeforms is a huge deal; they are the power brokers witnessing a potential transfer of power.

The concept of a 'righteous son' (义子) is also crucial here. Pi, despite being adopted, is bound by a loyalty oath to 'Father' Ji Lie. In a stratified society where bloodline is paramount, an adopted son from outside the clan cannot inherit the top secrets or ruling position, but they can be trained as a powerful weapon to enforce the will of their patron. Pi is the perfect tool for Ji Lie—a legal and deadly obstacle placed in Ji Ning's path.

Finally, note the distinction between sharpened and unsharpened weapons. In young martial contests, blunted weapons are used to allow for a real contest of skill where a killing blow is less likely but severe internal injuries are still possible. The 'Dripping Sutra' technique, which will soon be used by Ji Ning, focuses on penetrating force, a style that is highly effective even without a sharpened edge.

### Fiendgod Body Refining

A cultivation path focused on hardening the body into a divine weapon. Practitioners gain immense strength, durability, and regenerative abilities, far surpassing Ki Refiners in physical combat.

### Divided Mind (一心二用)

The ability to sustain two independent streams of thought. In combat, this allows a warrior to fight with two weapons simultaneously, executing two different attack patterns at once.

### Tigerfiend (虎魔)

The nickname of Ji Lie, denoting his ferocity and wild power. He is a major political rival to the current Prefectural Lord and the second strongest Xiantian expert of the Western Prefecture after Ji Yichuan.

## Reading Guide

*   **Cheer for the Underdog:** You are meant to feel the weight of the world on Ji Ning's small shoulders. The author rams home how tiny, young, and isolated he looks compared to his seven larger, battle-hardened opponents. Let that feeling of hopelessness build, because it makes the inevitable eruption of his true power all the more satisfying.
*   **Mind the Power Dynamics:** Pay close attention to the non-verbal cues between the Xiantian experts. Notice Ji Lie's smug satisfaction, Ji Youyang's cautious neutrality, and Ji Yichuan's chilling certainty. The real battle in this chapter isn't just on the stage; it's a war of words and wills between the seated elders.
*   **Appreciate the Setup:** This chapter is pure, concentrated setup. It takes a simple tournament structure and injects a massive, personal, and political conflict into it. The focus is on building the pressure until it’s almost unbearable. The pacing is deliberate, allowing you to soak in the atmosphere of the roaring crowd and the icy glare of the legends before the violence begins.
*   **Whose Side Are You On?** The chapter cleverly turns the crowd against Ji Ning and his father, painting them as arrogant and foolish. But as the reader who knows Ji Ning's true ability (his Fiendgod body and his advanced swordsmanship), you are in on the secret. This creates a delicious dramatic irony. You are waiting for the moment when the crowd's pity turns to shock.

## Xianxia Action & Terminology Guide

*   **Fiendgod Body Refining vs. Ki Refining:** Ji Ning has chosen the path of Fiendgod Body Refining. This is not just a different vocabulary word; it's a fundamentally different way of being strong. A Ki Refiner is like a glass cannon, channeling external energy into powerful attacks. A Fiendgod Body Refiner's body *is* the energy. They have monstrous strength, immense durability, and the terrifying ability to regrow limbs. When Ji Ning's true power unleashes, he will not be casting spells; he will be a force of raw, physical destruction.
*   **Divided Mind (一心二用):** This is a concept you will see very soon. It is the ability to maintain two separate streams of thought simultaneously. In combat, this means a cultivator can wield a sword in each hand, executing two completely different attack patterns at the same time. It is the hallmark of a powerful soul and the key to turning a sword-wielder into a whirlwind of death. It is what separates a mere dual-wielder from a true master of simultaneous offense.
