## Chapter Overview

Consider this a brief intermission from the main saga. The author—known affectionately as Tomato (I Eat Tomatoes)—steps out from behind the curtain to share a personal note. He openly admits to a rough patch, a disrupted sleep cycle, and the need for a short reset. But he doesn't leave us empty-handed: he dangles a tantalizing preview that Volume 13 is nearly complete and that Ji Ning is on the verge of graduating from his master's tutelage and returning to the Grand Xia realm. The promise of a "more vast and thrilling story" is the true hook here, and it's enough to make any Xianxia fan's heart race.

## Key Plot Points

Since this is a meta-chapter written by the author, there are no plot events to recount. Instead, the key "points" are:

- The author admits to being unwell and needing rest.
- He owes two chapters and will make them up later.
- He announces the impending end of Volume 13.
- He teases that Ji Ning will soon leave his teacher and return to the Grand Xia world, implying a major shift in the narrative scale.

While this chapter contains zero action or dialogue, it carries significant weight for readers who follow the serial. It's a moment of transparency between creator and audience—a rare glimpse behind the writing curtain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In the Chinese web novel ecosystem, it's common for authors to post short "break notifications" or "author's notes" directly in the chapter feed. This is not considered unprofessional; rather, it fosters a sense of community and honesty. I Eat Tomatoes (Fanqie) is one of the most famous authors in the genre, and his candidness here—admitting mental fatigue and disrupted sleep—reflects the immense pressure of maintaining a daily update schedule. Many top-tier Xianxia authors write thousands of words per day for years. A brief hiatus is understood and often welcomed by fans, as it signals that the author is prioritizing long-term quality.

The term "hurry up and return to the Grand Xia" is also classic Xianxia foreshadowing. The "master → return to home realm" arc is a staple: after training under a hidden power, the protagonist re-enters his original world at a vastly higher level, ready to challenge enemies who once dwarfed him. This chapter effectively signals the end of the "disciple" phase and the beginning of the "dominator" phase for Ji Ning.
