## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a masterclass in Xianxia tension as the Seamless Gate’s strike force arrives at Anchan City, only to find their prey waiting. Eighteen veteran Celestial Immortals, led by the enigmatic Crimsonleaf Immortal, attempt a stealth approach but are immediately detected by Yu Wei’s heartforce. Confident in their numerical superiority, they halt three thousand li outside the city, trapping themselves within the Beishan clan’s defensive formation. But instead of panic, the Tonghe Immortal exudes a chilling, battle-hungry calm, refusing all offers of assistance. The stage is set for a monumental confrontation between a solitary, legendary swordsman and a coordinated immortal legion. The chapter builds to a breathtaking climax as the Seamless Gate deploys the impenetrable Bixia Formation, a defensive phalanx formed from the power of eighteen immortals, only to have the Tonghe Immortal unveil a never-before-seen sword technique that shakes the very heavens. It’s a classic setup for a one-versus-many duel where pride, ancient grudges, and hidden trump cards are about to collide.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Seamless Gate’s Advance is Detected**: Eighteen Celestial Immortals attempt to approach Anchan City but are instantly spotted by Yu Wei’s six-month-long, unceasing heartforce surveillance, three thousand li from the gate.
- **The Trap is Sprung**: The Tonghe Immortal activates the city’s formation, trapping the eighteen invaders within a massive, eighteen-thousand-li-wide barrier. The Seamless Gate, expecting this, remains unperturbed and chooses to fight within the sealed zone.
- **Ji Ning’s Offer is Rejected**: Ji Ning volunteers to fight alongside the Tonghe Immortal, offering his Fiendgod Body Refining prowess for close combat. The Tonghe Immortal bluntly refuses, stating Ji Ning would only “hold him back,” and insists the Black-White College merely spectate.
- **The Bixia Formation**: The Seamless Gate responds by forming the legendary ‘Bixia Formation,’ a defensive battle array that manifests a massive, dragon-headed turtle (a son of the dragon), renowned for its supreme defensive power.
- **Tonghe’s Trump Card**: In a shocking reveal, the Tonghe Immortal unleashes a sword technique never before seen in battle—the ‘Extermination Immortal Sword’. Six flying swords merge into one colossal blade, and five elemental pillars of light and a Yin-Yang Taiji cloud formation appear, causing even the confident Seamless Gate immortals to pale in fear.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Bixia (霸下) and the Sons of the Dragon**: The formation used by the Seamless Gate, the ‘Bixia Formation,’ draws its name from a mythological creature. In Chinese folklore, the dragon king had nine sons, each with a different appearance and temperament. **Bixia** (also known as Baxia or 赑屃) is one of these, depicted as a giant turtle-like creature with a dragon’s head. It is most famous for carrying stone steles (memorial tablets) on its back in temples and imperial palaces, symbolizing endurance, weight, and unbreakable strength. In the novel, this is adapted into a defensive formation that turns its users into an immovable, shelled fortress—perfect for absorbing the initial assault of a power like the Tonghe Immortal and testing his mettle before their own attack.

**The Extermination Immortal Sword**: This sword-art is a clear, deliberate reference to one of the four legendary swords of the **‘Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation’ (诛仙剑阵)** from the classic Ming dynasty novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (The Investiture of the Gods). In that epic, these four swords—Xian Slaying (诛仙), *Jue Xian* (Extermination Immortal), *Lu Xian* (Perishing Immortal), and *Xian Xian* (Demi-Immortal)—are among the most powerful weapons in the universe, capable of creating a devastating chaos formation. By naming his move the **Extermination Immortal Sword**, the Tonghe Immortal is signaling that he has delved into the deepest traditions of Sword Immortal lore, hinting at a power level that far exceeds his reputation as a simple Celestial Immortal. It is a declaration of intent before the battle even begins.

**The Burden of a Dao Heart in Protracted Sieges**: The core conflict of this chapter is not just a battle of swords, but a battle of wills and patience. The Tonghe Immortal’s decision to let the Seamless Gate come to him, rather than sallying forth, reflects a common Xianxia tactical philosophy: the **long-game calculation**. A cultivator with a truly firm Dao-heart does not panic under siege. Instead, they analyze the enemy’s strengths (Eighteen veterans, defensive formation) and their own (hidden techniques, home-field advantage). The Tonghe Immortal’s calm patience is a direct contrast to the Seamless Gate’s aggressive, albeit cautious, advance. He forces the enemy to commit, to be locked in his own chosen battlefield, transforming their numerical advantage into a psychological burden as they await an unknown attack.

## Reading Guide
- **Watch The Layers of the Trap**: Notice the perfect synergy at play. Yu Wei’s heartforce acts as an early warning system. The Beishan defensive formation is a soft cage. But the ultimate trap is the Tonghe Immortal’s silence. He *wants* them inside that formation, confident that he can destroy them all. This is the hallmark of a true veteran—using the opponent’s caution as a weapon against them.
- **Appreciate the Rejection of Ji Ning**: The Tonghe Immortal’s harsh refusal of Ji Ning’s help is a profound character moment, not rudeness. He sees that the Seamless Gate has brought overwhelming force specifically calibrated to kill *him*. Dragging Ji Ning or Yu Wei into that would be an unforgivable burden on his conscience. This pride is both a weakness and a source of immense power.
- **Prepare for a Class-Based Confrontation**: The Bixia formation is the ultimate ‘tank’ or ‘turret’ defense. It is meant to be unmovable. The Extermination Immortal Sword is the ultimate ‘anti-armor’ penetration technique. The clash will be a study in extremes—absolute defense vs. absolute offense. Pay close attention to the sound and visual descriptions; they are the key to understanding the scale of this confrontation.
- **A Note on Nuance**: The 18 Seamless Gate immortals are not mere grunts. They are described as ‘veterans who had campaigned in other major worlds.’ They are calm, disciplined, and coordinated. This makes them far more threatening than a mindless horde and raises the stakes. The Tonghe Immortal is not fighting thugs; he is fighting an elite, hardened legion.

## Fan Theory Corner: The Weight of Eons
A recurring mystery is the true nature of the Tonghe Immortal’s power. He vanished for an “eternity,” allowing the world to think him dead. Now, he emerges with a sword technique that visibly terrifies his foes. This is a classic ‘hidden master’ trope. The Tonghe Immortal is not just a Celestial Immortal; he is likely a **cultivator who has been refining a single Dao or technique for an unfathomable length of time**. His power may be closer to that of a *Chaos Immortal*—not in realm, but in sheer, accumulated understanding. The final, unleashed form of his sword art is likely not a spell, but the compressed, physical manifestation of that eternal devotion. It will be explosive.
