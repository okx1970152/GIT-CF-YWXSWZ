## Chapter Overview
The new kid steps onto the block! Ji Ning is officially installed as Patriarch Subhuti's nominal disciple, but even the title of 'nominal' comes with perks that would make most Wanxiang Adepts weep with envy. After receiving the lowdown from his senior, the white-robed Lord Jun, Ning finds out the two Dao-attendants that serve the Patriarch are actually primordial Congenital spirit-treasures in human form—a flex that sets the tone for just how ludicrously stacked Mount Innerheart is. Then comes the grand tour: the Divine Immortal Palace, a nine-layered library packed with techniques from every path imaginable, open to all on the mountain, and the legendary Three Realms Palace, a humble-looking pavilion where only the master's personal disciples can uncover the kind of secrets that let you walk the Three Realms sideways. Ning's spirit-beasts, Qingqing and Uncle White, are about to have the cultivation spree of their lives, while Ning himself sets his sights on the ninth floor.

## Key Plot Points
- **Congenital Spirit-Treasures Revealed**: The two attendant boys, Qingshui and Baihe, were originally Congenital spirit-treasures that took human form. Even senior disciples like Lord Jun must request an audience, yet these two serve the Patriarch constantly, emphasizing the master's transcendent status.
- **Touring the Living Quarters**: Lord Jun leads Ji Ning to a scenic, secluded spot on Mount Innerheart perfect for placing his personal estate. Ning is also warned not to kill the many powerful monsters on the mountain, though ordinary beasts are fair game for roasting.
- **The Two Daoist Palaces Explained**: The Divine Immortal Palace is a nine-tiered open repository for all living creatures on the mountain, while the Three Realms Palace holds the ultimate techniques restricted to personal disciples. Ning learns that to enter the latter, he must first master a technique from the ninth level of the former.
- **A Mountain Full of High-Level Beings**: Ji Ning, Qingqing, and the Whitewater Hound arrive at the Divine Immortal Palace to find the open space crowded with Primal and Return to Void-level experts, powerful Fiendgods, and great monsters. Silvermoon, the palace administrator, announces Ning's status, instantly flipping the entire crowd from casual observers to respectful juniors addressing him as Martial Uncle or Grandmaster.
- **The Price of Knowledge**: A bookshelf at the entrance reveals the 'currency' of learning: sweeping the mountain paths of Mount Innerheart once earns the right to choose any one technique from the first level.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Free vs. Restricted Knowledge**: The two-palace system is a microcosm of Xianxia's obsession with the Dao hierarchy. The Divine Immortal Palace is the 'open university'—anyone on the mountain can trade labor (sweeping paths) for access to countless techniques. But the truly game-breaking abilities, the kind that let you 'dominate the Three Realms,' are locked behind the Three Realms Palace, only for personal disciples. This reflects the real-world Daoist concept that the most profound truths cannot be transmitted lightly; they are earned through direct master-to-disciple succession. Ning, despite being a nominal disciple, gets a backstage pass—a direct signal of hidden divine favor.

**Congenital Spirit-Treasures (先天灵宝)**: In standard Xianxia, cultivators craft treasures graded from Mortal to Pure Yang. Here, the top-tier artifacts are born from Heaven and Earth themselves, like primordial lifeforms. The two attendants are living examples of this cosmic rarity. Their existence proves that on Mount Innerheart, even the furniture has a backstory worthy of a legend. It's a subtle way to show the Patriarch's realm is stacked with beings so ancient and powerful, they make the artifacts of the Grand Xia look like children's toys.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a classic 'settling in' interlude that does double duty as a major power-up teaser for Ji Ning's entire party. While Ning's new status is officially 'nominal', the perks (access to the Three Realms Palace, mountain-wide respect from Return to Void powerhouses) are deeply real. Enjoy the relaxed pace—the tension will ramp up soon enough. Keep an eye on the nine-layer ladder of the Divine Immortal Palace; it's a direct progression gate for Ning, and the rules imply he'll need to show off in front of the mountain's elites to get to the good stuff. Also, don't sleep on Qingqing and Uncle White's excitement: this is their chance to catch up, and given Ning's need for a strong squad, their growth will likely matter in the arcs to come.
