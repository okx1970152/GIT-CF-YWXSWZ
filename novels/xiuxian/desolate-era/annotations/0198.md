## Chapter Overview

It’s loot time, fellow Daoists! Chapter 0198 is a satisfying payoff chapter where Ji Ning and Mu Northson finally count their spoils from the four Wanxiang Adepts they slaughtered. And oh boy, are they fat! Thirty-two Earth-ranked treasures, eighteen hundred jin of Primordial Liquid, plus talismans and pills. But before Ji Ning can ramp up, the duo first makes a pit stop at the Yinglong Guard outpost to drop off the enslaved humans they rescued. In a rare moment of institutional mercy, a Primal Daoist overseer takes the poor souls in, acknowledging that only the children have a future. Then it’s off to a lonely cave where Ji Ning—after a jaw-dropping two-thousand-plus-jin foundation expansion—finally triggers his breakthrough to Wanxiang. The economic reality check on cultivation resource scaling is brutal, but for a monster like Ji Ning, the numbers just keep climbing.

## Key Plot Points

- **Loot division**: The two brothers tally the haul from Beihe Zhou, Xu Manquan, Nong San, and Ji He. Thirty-two Earth-ranked treasures (worth ~2000–3000 jin of Primordial Liquid) plus 1800 jin of liquid energy. Ji Ning takes all the Primordial Liquid; Mu Northson gets the treasures. The tacit understanding reflects their trust and Ji Ning’s pragmatic gratitude.
- **Resource economics explained**: A sobering Xianxia truth bomb—1,000 jin can push a Zifu Disciple to Wanxiang, but from Wanxiang to Primal? Tens of thousands. From Primal to Earth Immortal? Millions. Even Immortals can’t casually give that away. This sets up why mighty geniuses still have to grind.
- **Rescue delivery**: The duo lands at the Yinglong Guard outpost. A stern Primal Daoist initially rebuffs them (candidates can’t enter the base), but his attitude warms when he sees the rescued humans. He takes them in, acknowledging the children still have hope.
- **Parting with Iron Boy**: The little boy who bonded with Ji Ning and Mu Northson stares back as he’s led away. Mu Northson’s wistful “I wonder what will become of them” and Ji Ning’s philosophical “Everyone has their own lot” close the emotional side quest.
- **Breakthrough setup**: In a quiet cave, Ji Ning refines all 1,800 jin of Primordial Liquid plus his existing reserves and previous years of training. His Zifu Sea expands to a staggering 2,500 jin equivalent—well past the 1,600–2,000 jin his master guessed. The void finally resists, and Ji Ning knows he’s truly reached the peak of Zifu perfection.
- **The moment of transformation**: With a thought, the Zifu Sea begins its drastic evolution into a Wanxiang Adept’s foundation. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger as the breakthrough initiates.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Primordial Liquid (元液) – The Currency of Geniuses**  
Primordial Liquid is the ultimate fast-track resource in Xianxia: purified energy that can be absorbed instantly with zero side effects. But its exponential cost scaling is a classic trope. At the Zifu stage, a few hundred jin is a fortune; by the Wanxiang stage, even a sect’s elite disciple burns through thousands just for a minor boost. By the time you’re a Primal Daoist, you’re either a million-jin heir or you’re cultivating the slow way—breathing in natural energy for centuries. This economic cliff is what separates spoiled young masters from true geniuses who must earn their own breakthroughs.

**Zifu Sea Expansion – From Lake to Ocean**  
The Zifu (Violet Palace) is an internal dimensional space where a cultivator’s Yuan power pools. A “perfect” foundation means expanding this lake to its absolute physical limit before attempting the qualitative shift to Wanxiang. Normal geniuses top out around 1,600 jin. Ji Ning’s 2,500 jin is off the charts—a benchmark that hints at a Celestial Immortal-level foundation. In Xianxia, the bigger the Zifu Sea before transformation, the vaster the Wanxiang “world” you can create, and the stronger your Dao foundation for the later stages.

**Yinglong Guard – Brutal but with a Heart?**  
The Imperial Guard of the Grand Xia Dynasty is a cold, merciless machine that executes disobedient candidates on the spot. Yet here, a Primal Daoist officer treats rescued human cattle with genuine pity. It’s a rare glimpse of the institution’s human side—and a reminder that even in a cruel world, a cultivator’s choice to show mercy can resonate.

## Reading Guide

Get ready, because the next chapter is where Ji Ning finally transforms into a Wanxiang Adept. That 2,500-jin foundation is no joke—expect a spectacular expansion of his abilities. Meanwhile, Mu Northson’s cheerful support and the duo’s perfectly balanced partnership (one breaks through, the other guards) is a joy to watch. If you’re a new reader to Xianxia, pay close attention to the resource numbers: they’re not just filler; they’re the hard constraints that shape the entire power ladder. And if you’re a veteran, you know what a 2,500-jin Zifu Sea means—Ji Ning is about to become an absolute monster.
