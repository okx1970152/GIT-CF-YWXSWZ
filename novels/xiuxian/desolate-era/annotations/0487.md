## Chapter Overview

The chessboard shifts. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia political tension, where information is a weapon and every faction is watching. The Xia Emperor lays bare the Seamless Gate’s insidious infiltration of the Grand Xia—over sixty percent of his feudal lords are crying for help, but many of them are *already* Seamless Gate puppets. Meanwhile, the Shaoyin clan receives a chilling visit from a Seamless Gate envoy, who drops the bombshell that Ji Ning has returned from the inescapable Realm of Annihilation. The Shaoyin clan’s terror is palpable. Back in Anchan City, Yuchi Xiyue’s long-held grief finally shatters with joy at seeing her cousin-brother alive. And at the very end, Yan King delivers the news Ji Ning has been waiting for: the location of his parents’ reincarnation. The stage is set for a personal quest, but the political storm is only growing darker.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Xia Emperor and the Celestial Fox Immortal** discuss the dynasty’s fragility. Over 60% of commanderies have sent distress signals. The Emperor suspects many are feigning loyalty to the Seamless Gate. He adopts a strategy of strategic patience: let the enemy move, expose themselves, and then strike the vital point. Ji Ning is singled out as a crucial “chess piece” because of his monstrous talent and the Daofather behind him.
- **Yuchi Xiyue’s emotional reunion**: Told by Yan King that Ji Ning is alive and in Anchan City, she transforms from a melancholic, grieving woman into a whirlwind of desperate joy. She insists on leaving immediately, and Yan King accompanies her.
- **The Seamless Gate’s golden-robed envoy** visits the Shaoyin clan’s Xuanji Celestial Immortal. He reveals that the Seamless Gate knew about the Shaoyin clan’s use of the Realm-Suppression Tower to banish Ji Ning—proving their terrifying intelligence network. The envoy delivers the shocking news: Ji Ning is back. Xuanji is horrified, realizing this means Ji Ning has a Daofather backing him and will soon become a Celestial Immortal. The envoy offers the Shaoyin clan a lifeline: join the Seamless Gate, and they’ll protect them from Ji Ning.
- **Inside the Black-White College**, Ji Ning and Yu Wei are enjoying a quiet reunion when Yan King and Yuchi Xiyue arrive. Xiyue’s joy is overwhelming. The chapter closes with Yan King hinting at long-awaited information: the reincarnation of Ji Ning’s parents.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Realm of Annihilation (寂灭之域)**: This is not just a random wasteland. In the cosmology of *Desolate Era*, the Realm of Annihilation is the dark, storm-torn void between the Three Realms and the broader Chaosverse. It is completely empty—devoid of any Dao resonance. For a cultivator, this is a living nightmare. Because cultivation relies on resonance with the Heavenly Dao and the universe’s living laws, being cut off from that is like a fish out of water. Only the most extreme physical arts or Heartforce can function there. The fact that Ji Ning survived and *returned* from there in a mere eighteen years is a testament to either unimaginable luck or—as the Shaoyin clan now fears—the direct intervention of a Daofather.

**Two Realms of Reincarnation**: The original Six Paths of Reincarnation was shattered in the ancient war. Daofather Crimsonbright established a “Small Cycle of Reincarnation” (小型轮回) to keep souls cycling, but this localized system puts more political power in the hands of the Ten Kings of Hell. This is why Yan King’s intel about Ji Ning’s parents is so valuable—accessing reincarnation records is a politically delicate process, even for someone like Yan King.

## Reading Guide

Fellow Daoists, buckle up! This chapter is the calm before a *very* big storm. We’re shifting gears from the brutal survival of the Realm of Annihilation back to the grand political game of the Grand Xia. The Xia Emperor’s “let them move” strategy is classic Xianxia high-tier wisdom—patience and information are more powerful than brute force. Meanwhile, the Seamless Gate is playing 5D chess, and they’ve just shown the Shaoyin clan their hidden cards. The fear in Xuanji Celestial Immortal’s heart is delicious to read. But the real emotional gut-punch comes from Yuchi Xiyue’s outburst. After eighteen years of believing her last surviving relative was dead, the sheer joy of seeing Ji Ning alive is beautifully raw. And that cliffhanger about Ji Ning’s parents? Get ready. A personal mission is about to begin, one that might just shake the foundations of the underworld itself. Don’t blink!
