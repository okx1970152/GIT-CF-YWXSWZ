## Chapter Overview
We’re barely through the gates of Immortal Wu Jiang’s estate, and the gloves are already off! This chapter is a brutal, high-stakes slugfest where the party’s entry is met with an overwhelming force of demonic Dao-soldiers—thousands of them, working in perfect formation to deliver near-Immortal-level beatdowns. Youngmaster Shaoyin’s team takes heavy losses among the Zifu cannon fodder, but a single, explosive throw from his enigmatic bodyguard turns the tide, killing the Primal-level mermaid commander and collapsing the formation. The celebration is short-lived, however, as a second, far more terrifying wave appears: tens of thousands of demonic Dao-soldiers, commanded by an entity whose divine sense eclipses even Ji Ning’s. Get ready, fellow Daoists—this is where a simple treasure hunt turns into a fight for survival.

## Key Plot Points
- The group is immediately attacked by a thousand-strong formation of demonic Dao-soldiers, their combined power approaching that of an Earth Immortal. Shaoyin Nong orders the others to fight while he and his guardian hang back. Ji Ning, Yu Wei, Cangjiang Zhenren, and the others unleash their strongest attacks but are repelled.
- The demonic wave breaks through the ship’s barrier, smashing it into a mountain. The Zifu Disciples begin to die in droves, but Shaoyin Nong refuses to use any life-saving treasures, conserving them for later in the estate.
- The swarthy middle-aged bodyguard, ‘Uncle Fang,’ finally acts. He analyzes the Dao-soldier formation’s central node and throws a black shuttle that shatters the wave, killing over a dozen soldiers. A second throw instantly pierces and kills the Primal-level mermaid demoness commander, causing the entire demon army to rout in panic.
- The party quickly loots the corpses, collecting over two hundred suits of Dao-soldier armor, ranging from Zifu to Wanxiang grade—a massive haul, but bought with the lives of over a hundred Zifu Disciples.
- As they prepare to move deeper, a vastly more powerful divine sense sweeps over them. A second wave of demonic Dao-soldiers appears: tens of thousands strong, led by an entity far stronger than the first. Even the bodyguard admits he cannot hold them off.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Dao-Soldier Armor (道兵甲铠)** is a classic force-multiplier in Chinese cultivation novels. Think of it as the ultimate military hardware of the Xianxia world. It isn’t just a single powerful suit; it’s a **formation amplifier** that synchronizes the strength of multiple wearers. A thousand soldiers wearing the same pattern of Dao-soldier armor can link their Ki and Divine Power, unleashing attacks that dwarf their individual levels. This is why sects and dynasties covet them so much—an army of mid-level cultivators with the right armor can threaten even an Immortal. Here, we see its terrifying potential, and also the rarely-seen countermeasure: a transcendent expert who can perceive the formation’s “central node” and break it with a single, perfectly-aimed strike.

The **Two-Level “Surprise Attack & Punishment”** is also a classic Xianxia narrative pattern. The party survives the first wave (the “surprise”) and feels a moment of relief after the bodyguard’s incredible feat. But that relief is immediately crushed by an exponentially larger threat (the “punishment”), amplifying the tension and foreshadowing that the true dangers are only beginning. The transition from thousands to tens of thousands of Dao-soldiers is a textbook example of escalating the stakes right when the reader thinks the crisis is averted.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in efficient, high-stakes world-building. Pay close attention to how the **power dynamics** are established: the bodyguard’s two throws are a brutal demonstration of what a peak-level combatant can achieve against a formation, but the final, ominous warning that he “can’t hold them off” instantly resets the power ladder. The treasure loot is substantial, but the cost in manpower is deliberately highlighted to show Shaoyin Nong’s cold-blooded calculus—he is perfectly willing to burn through his own followers to preserve his ace cards. This sets the stage for a classic “treasure hunt political tension” scene where survival may depend on betrayals and alliances, not just raw power.
