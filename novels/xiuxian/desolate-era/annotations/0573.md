## Chapter Overview

Welcome back to the grand stage of the Realm War, fellow Daoists! Chapter 573 delivers the brutal, exhilarating payoff that this epic arc has been building towards. Ji Ning, in command of the Karmic Punishment Deity, doesn't just *hold the line* against the terrifying Daofather Puppet—he *dominates* it. The Evergreen Sword Immortal's carefully laid plans and arrogant boasts crumble into dust as Ji Ning's overwhelming, earth-shattering sword-strikes force his entire side to recalibrate. This isn't a desperate struggle; it's a masterclass in leveraging power, strategy, and a frostbitten Dao-heart. Watch as the war turns into a one-sided slaughter, the Evergreen Sword Immortal descends into panicked madness, and a final, desperate threat from the Godking himself calls upon the most painful sacrifice imaginable. Get ready for some high-octane war-god action and soul-crushing tension.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Tables Turn**: Ji Ning’s Karmic Punishment Deity, with his swordplay described as "opening heaven and earth," completely overwhelms and suppresses the Daofather Puppet. The Evergreen Sword Immortal is utterly shocked and humiliated.
- **Tactical Reassessment**: Daofather Ink Bamboo frantically instructs the Evergreen Sword Immortal to switch from a strength-based clash to a speed-based harassment strategy, highlighting the puppet's agility disadvantage against Ji Ning's brute force.
- **The Battlefield Splits**: Red Snow Heavenly God (Seven Radiance Heavenly God) leaves Ji Ning to face the Daofather Puppet alone, choosing to plunge into the main Seamless Gate army. The original plan of the Seamless Gate backfires catastrophically.
- **Total Domination**: Ji Ning proves he is a match for the Daofather Puppet in both raw power and pure speed, completely entangling it and preventing it from interfering in the main battle. The Evergreen Sword Immortal's nightmare begins.
- **The Slaughter Begins**: Bereft of their heavy-hitter, the Seamless Gate army is ruthlessly massacred. The Seven Radiance Heavenly God leads a devastating assault, and the Crimsonbright realm's forces, including the Xia Emperor, go on a vengeful killing spree.
- **The Godking's Final Gambit**: In a moment of sheer desperation, the Godking of the Seamless Gate directly threatens Ji Ning, vowing to kill Yu Wei if he does not stand down.
- **The Chilling Response**: Ji Ning responds with a silence colder than the void itself, stating that if the Godking kills Yu Wei, it will be a release from her torment. This perfectly distilled ruthlessness leaves the Godking stunned and terrified.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a powerful showcase of the rock-paper-scissors dynamic within Xianxia combat formations. The **Karmic Punishment Deity (刑天神)** is a colossus of brute force, formed by countless Celestial Immortals and Loose Immortals. It excels at overwhelming single targets but lacks agility. The **Daofather Puppet (道祖傀儡)** is a supreme single-entity war machine, incredibly tough and agile, designed to be a 'hero unit' that can cut through armies. The Seamless Gate’s original plan was a classic pincer move: have the ultra-mobile Daofather Puppet kill the less-mobile Karmic Punishment Deity, then go and kill the agile Seven Radiance Heavenly God. What they didn't account for is Ji Ning being a statistical anomaly—a Karmic Punishment Deity with more power AND equal speed. It's like expecting a tank to be slow and stupid, only for it to do a backflip and snipe you.

Also, note the chilling effect of a **"Dao-heart of absolute sincerity"**. Ji Ning's cold response to the Godking isn't cruelty; it’s the pure, terrifying logic of a man whose Dao-heart is so focused on the end goal that it can accept even the most painful sacrifices as a variable in an equation. He isn't bluffing; he has already processed the potential loss of Yu Wei as a strategic reality. This level of conviction is a core trope of the Progression Fantasy genre, showing a protagonist who has transcended mere emotional reaction to become a force of nature.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in **Strategic Reversal**. Pay close attention to how the author uses the contrast between the **Elegant Precision** of the Seven Radiance Heavenly God (Red Snow) and the **Overpowering Brute Force** of Ji Ning’s Karmic Punishment Deity to create tactical depth. When Red Snow leaves, it’s not an act of abandonment but a calculated bet on Ji Ning’s power, a bet that pays off in a terrifying display of strength.

The absolute highlight is Ji Ning's response to the Godking's threat. Read it slowly. The sheer weight of his silence and the quiet, devastating logic of his words—"Killing her would be a release"—is more shocking than any scream of rage. This is the mark of a Dao-heart forged in the Infinity Hells of past pain. The Godking, for all his schemes, is outmatched not just in power, but in conviction. This chapter proves that the most powerful weapon in the Three Realms isn't a supreme treasure but an unshatterable will.
