## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because Chapter 551 delivers a brutal, backstabbing gut punch that shatters the grim but predictable rhythm of the Realm War. All the strategic tension from the previous chapters—the cautious command, the paranoid preparations, the feeling of an invisible noose tightening—finally snaps, but not in the way anyone expected. Forget the massive Xingtian clashes or the looming threat of the three great Yaos of Mount Tunlong. The deadliest blow is struck not by the enemy, but from within our own ranks, in a single, heartless sword stroke. This chapter is a masterclass in building emotional stakes and then coldly dismantling them to remind us that in a Xianxia war, treachery knows no bounds. If you thought the battlefield was dangerous before, you haven’t seen anything yet.

## Key Plot Points
- **The High Command’s Dilemma**: Daofather Crimsonbright reveals his strategic bind. The Seamless Gate has better intelligence, meaning they will likely strike first. However, he senses a hidden enemy force and must hold back his own cards, leading to a tense waiting game where Ji Ning is identified as the enemy’s top priority target.
- **The Evergreen Sword Immortal’s Toxicity**: During a post-briefing chat, the famously cold and sharp-tongued Evergreen Sword Immortal publicly and repeatedly states his expectation of Ji Ning’s death in the war. The Snowdance Fairy apologizes for his rudeness, hinting at her long-standing, unrequited feelings for him.
- **Domestic Tension**: Yu Wei, heavily pregnant, expresses her deep unease and fear to Ji Ning before he departs for the next battle. Her premonition of danger hangs heavy in the air, setting the stage for the coming tragedy.
- **The Battle Begins**: The armies clash again on the scarred wasteland. The Evergreen Sword Immortal and the Snowdance Fairy, as usual, fight alongside each other in Ji Ning’s high-intensity theater.
- **The Supreme Betrayal**: Responding to the Xia Emperor’s order, Evergreen and Snowdance fly to aid a group of embattled Xingtian. In that moment of vulnerability, the Evergreen Sword Immortal, without a word of warning, drives his sword through Snowdance’s chest, shattering her core. He collects her treasures and immediately turns his attack on the ally Xingtian formations.
- **The Aftermath**: The Xia Emperor, witnessing Snowdance’s murder, is consumed by a fury so great it feels like his eyes will split. His earth-shaking roar is the final, raw punctuation to this devastating chapter of treachery.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter revolves around the ultimate act of Xianxia treachery: the betrayal of a fellow sect member. While outsiders and demons are expected foes, the real horror comes from the “Daoist brother” who stabs you in the back. The relationship between **Evergreen** and **Snowdance** is a classic, heartbreaking Xianxia trope. Snowdance embodies the “persistent, devoted fairy” whose sincerity is met with cold indifference, while Evergreen is the “heart of a sword” cultivator—so devoted to his own Dao that he can’t (or won’t) see the value of human connection. Her final “smile of release” is incredibly poignant in the genre, suggesting she was already dead inside from the emotional rejection; only this ultimate, physical betrayal could finally set her soul free. The Xia Emperor’s “睚眦欲裂” (yá zì yù liè) reaction—his eyes splitting with rage—is a powerful Chinese idiom used to describe the absolute pinnacle of fury, especially when triggered by seeing a dear one wronged.

## Reading Guide
This is the chapter where the story’s tension goes from a slow burn to an inferno. The mundane setup at the beginning might feel like a breather, but don’t be fooled. Pay close attention to the small character moments: Yu Wei’s gut feeling, the Evergreen Sword Immortal’s deliberate cruelty, and the Snowdance Fairy’s gentle attempts to smooth things over. These are all tiny dominoes perfectly arranged for a single, devastating push. The betrayal itself is a clinic in efficient Xianxia violence—cold, quick, and without a shred of guilt. The tragedy is doubled: Snowdance is a victim of both war and a broken heart. The one sentence you absolutely cannot miss is the Evergreen Sword Immortal’s internal justification as he kills her. It tells you everything you need to know about his character. Prepare for the fallout in the next chapter; the Xia Emperor’s rage will be a force of nature.
