## Chapter Overview

Fellow Daoists, buckle up. Chapter 117, "Endless Vast Skies," is the chapter where Ji Ning stops being the underdog and becomes the absolute nightmare of Snowdragon Mountain. The chapter opens with a grim omen: the Tongshui Branch's Zifu Disciples are dead, and their kin are screaming for blood. The Loose Immortal Xuli realizes this is no simple "demolish a minor clan" mission anymore—something is rotten in the Yan Mountains. But while the adults are arguing and plotting, our boy Ji Ning is busy doing what he does best: turning enemy loot into personal power-ups. Fresh off the slaughter of eight Zifu Disciples, he refines a pristine set of icy flying swords and pushes his Lesser Thousand Sword Formation to terrifying new heights. Then, with the cold precision of a seasoned killer, he walks back into the black mist to face the remaining Snowdragon elites. No speeches. No hesitation. Just rain, sword-light, and the coming storm. If you came for cultivation breakthroughs, tactical genius, and that "oh crap, we're the ones being hunted now" feeling, this is your chapter.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Tongshui Branch's Loss**: Lu Huang of the Tongshui Branch intercepts the Loose Immortal Xuli, revealing that both the Lu and An Clan's Zifu Disciples (including his own sister) sent to the Yan Mountains are dead. Dong Fanyu swears an oath that he had not lied, and a now-serious Xuli speeds toward the Yan Mountains.
- **Ji Ning's Loot Haul**: After the battle, Ji Ning gains fifteen new graded flying swords from the corpses, including a prized set of nine icy flying swords of the same origin which are perfect as a formation core.
- **Formation Breakthrough**: Attempting the ninth layer of the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation with too many graded swords strains his soul to the breaking point. Through an insight into "Yin and Yang transform into each other; from genesis, endless life is born," he reconfigures the eighth layer to be more powerful than his earlier ninth layer, allowing him to wield the full set of twenty-seven graded swords effectively.
- **Tools to the Clan Leader**: Demonstrating strategic maturity, Ji Ning passes the bulk of the non-essential spoils (treasures, pills) to Patriarch Ji Jiuhuo, keeping only what he needs for his personal combat role.
- **The Final Approach**: Ji Ning heads to engage Tongyu, the Fiendgod Body Refiner. Simultaneously, the rain begins—a signature sign of his arrival.
- **Tongyu's Heavenly Transformation**: The towering opponent, a head taller than a building, activates his Heavenly Transformation, roaring for Ji Ning to come out and fight.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a masterclass in two core Xianxia mechanics: **Loot Processing** and **The Breakthrough Loop**.

First, the **Loot Processing** scene is quintessential I Eat Tomatoes. After a major slaughter, the protagonist doesn't just walk away; he ransacks the corpses for all they're worth. The "full set of nine flying swords from the same source" (同出一源) is a classic lucky break. In formation-based combat, items that share a resonance or come from a single origin are more powerful than mismatched fragments. Like tuning an orchestra, a set of matched instruments plays a perfect symphony of death.

Second, this chapter features a beautiful **tangential breakthrough** (触类旁通). Ji Ning is wrestling with a formation problem when a line from the Crimsonbright Diagram manual—"Yin and Yang transform into each other; from genesis, endless life is born" (阴阳转化、相生无尽)—clicks in his mind. This is core Daoist philosophy in action. The principle of Yin-Yang (阴阳) is that seemingly opposite forces are actually interconnected and interdependent in the natural world, giving rise to each other as they interrelate. Ji Ning doesn't have to retreat into isolation for a week. He applies the abstract concept *immediately* to solve a concrete combat problem. This perfectly illustrates that in Xianxia, true insight ("comprehending the Dao") isn't just about sitting in a lotus position; it's about seeing the universal principles of the universe in the humblest of tasks, like arranging a sword array. He even notes that this insight *could* have broken his Fiendgod cultivation bottleneck, but rejects that path because it wouldn't help him *right now*. That’s the pragmatic Dao Heart at work.

### Yin-Yang transformation

A core Daoist principle of interconnected opposites; Ji Ning applies this insight to his formation to increase its power while reducing the strain on his soul.

### Loot Processing

A common Xianxia trope where the protagonist thoroughly searches enemy corpses for new weapons, pills, and techniques after a victory.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for the first true test of Ji Ning's new power. We've seen him wipe out a crowd of ordinary Zifu Disciples, but Tongyu is a different beast entirely. He's a **Fiendgod Body Refiner** (神魔炼体) with a **Divine Ability** (神通). That means he has ridiculous regenerative powers, raw physical strength, and the iconic Heavenly Transformation (法天象地), which turns him into a skyscraper of muscle and fury. Watch how Ji Ning approaches this fight. He won't try to overpower Tongyu with a head-on clash. He will use his newly upgraded sword formation like a scalpel, probing for weaknesses. Pay attention to the rain—it's Ji Ning's signature move from the Dripping Sutra (滴水经), and its arrival signals the beginning of the end. Also, keep one eye on the horizon: the Loose Immortal Xuli is rapidly approaching. The question isn't just whether Ji Ning can beat Tongyu, but how fast he can do it before the real monsters arrive.
