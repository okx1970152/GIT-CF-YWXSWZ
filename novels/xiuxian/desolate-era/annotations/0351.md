## Chapter Overview

Holy twists and turns, fellow Daoists! Just when we thought the Immortal Fortunes Assembly was wrapping up with the usual rankings and rewards, our beloved slovenly Zhang Qi gets yanked into an entirely different league of destiny. This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia's favorite trope: the hidden-background reveal. That seemingly random "Dark North Grand Divine Ability" he picked up? Turns out it was the personal handshake of none other than the Dark North Emperor, a genuine Daofather of the Three Realms! Meanwhile, Ji Ning and the other top three finalists get their own epic moment: a trip to the Grand Xia imperial vault to pick a divine ability, where all three independently gravitate toward the same legendary technique. It’s a feast of power progression, hidden lore, and political recalibration that will leave you grinning.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Fuhai God Arrives**: A terrifying, infamous demon-cultivator-turned-Empyrean God named Fuhai crashes the Heavenly Tribute Palace. His mere presence silences the Celestial Immortals and terrifies the remaining Wanxiang contestants.
- **Zhang Qi's Shocking Revelation**: Fuhai drags the bewildered slovenly youth to his side and reveals he is his “little junior apprentice-brother,” sent to fetch him by their master — the Dark North Emperor, a Daofather-level existence! It turns out Zhang Qi has been a Daofather's disciple all along, before even entering the Immortal Fortunes Assembly.
- **The Social Shockwave**: The instant the connection is known, the Celestial Immortals' looks of pity turn to envy. Lu Dongbin and the Xia Emperor confirm the story, explaining that the Dark North Grand Divine Ability could only be learned with the Dark North Emperor's explicit approval.
- **The Top Three's Reward**: While awaiting Daofather Crimsonbright's decision, Ji Ning, Blackstone, and Muchuan are taken to the Grand Xia imperial treasury’s sixth level to choose between divine abilities and secret arts.
- **The Choice: Zhulong's Eye**: After perusing hundreds of manuals, all three independently decide to take the same legendary divine ability: *Zhulong's Eye* — a technique ranked in the top 100 of the Three Realms, even in its incomplete upper-half form.
- **The Summons**: King Qi interrupts their study to announce that Daofather Crimsonbright has made his decision, and the three must return to the main hall immediately.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **The Dark North Emperor (玄武大帝)**: In Daoist mythology, the Dark North Emperor (Xuanwu Dadi) is a supreme deity associated with the north, winter, and the element of water, often depicted as a tortoise-serpent fusion. In *Desolate Era*, he is elevated to a Daofather — one of the highest Ki Refining cultivators in the Three Realms, whose authority supersedes even that of the Celestial Thearch. This chapter reveals that his "Dark North Grand Divine Ability" is a signature move that cannot be learned without his direct permission, making it a perfect token of hidden master-disciple lineage.
- **The "Grand Divine Ability" Status**: A technique called a **“Grand Divine Ability” (大神通)** is not just any powerful move; it is a top-tier, quasi-unique method created by a Daofather or True God. These techniques often contain the creator's unique Dao insight and can only be passed down through direct inheritance. Zhang Qi having one marks him as a major potential player even before his background is revealed.
- **Fuhai God (覆海天神)**: The name "Fuhai" literally means "Overturning the Seas," a fitting title for a fierce, demon-cultivator-turned-God. His mention of “smashing up the Heavenly Court” is a classic Xianxia flex — showing that even the Celestial Thearch’s palace is vulnerable to these top-tier beings.
- **Zhulong's Eye (烛龙之眼)**: Zhulong, or the "Torch Dragon," is a figure from ancient Chinese mythology described as a serpentine god whose breath brings winter and summer, and whose closed eyes bring night. This divine ability allows cultivators to collect the Nine Heavens Aurora into their eyes to produce a "Lifebound Candle Light," likely granting reality-bending or perception-based combat powers.

### Zhulong's Eye (烛龙之眼)

A legendary divine ability ranked in the top 100 of the Three Realms, created by the ancient god Zhulong. It involves collecting the Nine Heavens Aurora into the eyes to cultivate a 'Lifebound Candle Light'.

## Reading Guide

- **The Slap of Hidden Backgrounds**: If you’ve been feeling a bit disappointed that Ji Ning is the only one getting massive advantages, fret not! This chapter beautifully gives Zhang Qi (the Slovenly Daoist) a massive power-up in terms of *potential* and *connections*. It reinforces a core Xianxia truth: you never know who’s secretly a young master of a Supreme Elder.
- **The Three-Way Synchronization**: It's a brilliant narrative beat that Ji Ning, Blackstone, and Muchuan all independently pick the same divine ability. It shows that even among fierce rivals, their instincts as top-tier geniuses are aligned. It also sets up a potential shared cultivation experience or future contest of mastery.
- **The Emperor’s Calculated Gaze**: Pay attention to the Xia Emperor’s easy smile throughout this chapter. He was perfectly aware of Zhang Qi’s background and orchestrated the reveal in a way that maximized the political benefit for his dynasty, cozying up to the Dark North faction. There are no coincidences in Xianxia politics.
- **Whet Your Appetite**: The chapter ends on a delicious cliffhanger — Daofather Crimsonbright has chosen! The anticipation for who gets selected (and how the other top three will react) is at an all-time high. Get ready for the next thunderclap.

## Deep Dive: The Politics of a Daofather's Disciple

The shockwave of Zhang Qi’s revelation is not just personal; it’s political. The Immortal Fortunes Assembly was originally a recruitment drive for *one* Daofather (Crimsonbright). Now, it has accidentally produced a *second* one from the Dark North faction. This drastically shifts the balance of perceived power within the Grand Xia. Clans and sects will now look at the Black-White College and the Grand Xia capital differently — a single event has revealed the existence of a shadow powerhouse. The fact that Fuhai himself (a known troublemaker) was personally dispatched to fetch his junior is a silent, powerful declaration that the Dark North Emperor is watching, and he values his new disciple.
