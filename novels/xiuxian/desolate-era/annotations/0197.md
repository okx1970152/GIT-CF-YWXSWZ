## Chapter Overview

Holy *carp*, fellow Daoists. If you thought Ji Ning was just a sword-swinging madman, think again. This chapter is the ultimate “oh no, he’s got mind-control powers” twist, served with a side of cold-blooded tactical cruelty. Our boy Ning and his junior brother Northson are pinned down by six veteran Wanxiang Adepts from Snowdragon Mountain and other hostile factions. What follows is a masterclass in Xianxia combat—elemental defenses, sword-arts that bend space, and then the dirty bomb that nobody saw coming: a maxed-out Soul-Shattering Art powered by Ji Ning’s freakish Divine Sense–level soul. The result? Four out of six dead in seconds, one bloody escape, and a pile of loot that includes a shocking five hundred jin of Primordial Liquid. Get ready for the most satisfying “surprise, I’m a Primal-level psychic” moment in the series so far.

## Key Plot Points

- **Opening salvo**: The six Wanxiang Adepts unleash a coordinated strike: lightning, Cold Fiend, a whirlwind formation of nine cyclone-wheels, a horsetail whisk of three thousand white threads, and a stealthy gray mind-affecting vapor. Ji Ning’s Water-Fire Lotus holds (six layers peeled to three), revealing that his Dao insight has advanced dramatically.
- **Thorns Everywhere in action**: Ji Ning counters with the second stance of the Three-Foot Sword, a single jade sword-blade that feels “slow” but cuts through all obstacles. It smashes the Cold Fiend and the cyclone formation, but is dissipated by the whisk. The enemy is shocked that his ranged swordplay is this powerful.
- **The stalemate breaks**: Beihe Zhou’s double-black-serpent formation clashes with Ji Ning’s nine-blade Lesser Thousand Sword Formation in a deadlock. Mu Northson’s serpent puppet begins to weaken from the mental interference.
- **Soul-Shattering Art unleashed**: Ji Ning triggers his trump card—a Divine Sense–level Soul-Shattering Art (撼神术). The mental shockwave instantly stuns all six Wanxiang Adepts, leaving them dazed and falling.
- **Slaughter in the chaos**: While the enemies are incapacitated, Ji Ning executes Beihe Zhou (whose protective sword-qi fails after three strikes) and Xu Manquan via sword-blade. Mu Northson kills Jihe and Nong San. Beihe Zhou and Jihe die first; the remaining four wake up and try to flee.
- **Escape and carnage**: Ji Ning repeats the Soul-Shattering Art, suppressing them again. Dong Yi (Snowdragon Mountain) barely survives by burning a blood escape technique, but Xu Manquan is caught. Meng Liuqing escapes, but Mu Northson kills Nong San.
- **Loot phase**: They collect storage treasures and discover one bottle of 500 jin of Primordial Liquid. Ji Ning realizes this is a decisive resource that may let him break through to Wanxiang.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Soul-Shattering Art (撼神术)**: One of the three famous divine sense secret arts of the Black-White College. In Chinese Xianxia, divine sense (神念) is a perception ability that normally only appears at the Primal Daoist (元神) stage. Ji Ning, thanks to his freakish soul strength from his past life and Nuwa Diagram training, has achieved this early. The art weaponizes raw divine sense into a mental shockwave that stuns enemies—but its power scales directly with soul strength. Here, it’s so potent that even Wanxiang Adepts (whose souls are far tougher than mortals) are rendered helpless for critical seconds. This is a classic “hidden trump card” moment in cultivation fiction: the protagonist reveals a rare, overpowered soul-technique that turns a losing battle into a massacre.

**Protective Sword-Qi (护体剑气)**: A life-saving consumable left by a senior master inside a disciple’s body. It automatically activates when the disciple is in lethal danger, providing a temporary barrier. In this case, Beihe Zhou’s protective sword-qi withstood three full-powered strikes from Ji Ning’s Lesser Thousand Sword Formation blade—a testament to the senior’s cultivation level. These items are often one-time use and very precious; losing one is a huge blow.

**Primordial Liquid (元液)**: The densest, most refined form of natural energy. It can be absorbed instantly without needing to be refined, making it the ultimate currency for high-level purchases and the fuel for major breakthroughs. Here, 500 jin is described as just “a fraction” of what a Wanxiang Adept needs to break through to the Primal Daoist stage—underscoring the terrifying resource demands of higher cultivation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is pure Xianxia catharsis. The setup is a classic “surrounded by superior numbers” scenario, and the payoff is the unexpected reveal of Ji Ning’s soul-based trump card. If you’ve been wondering why the story has been hyping up his Divine Sense for so long—*this* is why. The Soul-Shattering Art doesn’t just even the odds; it utterly humiliates the enemy. Note the beautiful contrast: the enemy spends all their effort analyzing his Water-Fire Lotus and sword-blade, only to be felled by a mental attack they never saw coming. Also keep an eye on the 500 jin of Primordial Liquid—this is a massive resource injection. In Xianxia economics, a windfall like this almost always signals an upcoming breakthrough chapter. Also, Ji Ning’s casual use of “Thorns Everywhere” (the second stance of the Three-Foot Sword) shows how far he’s come in mastering Immortal Beixing’s legacy—his Dao comprehension is now strong enough to overwhelm Wanxiang-level defenses.
