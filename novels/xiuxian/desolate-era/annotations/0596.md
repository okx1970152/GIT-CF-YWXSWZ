## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks the beginning of a calculated, devastating strike by a vengeful Ji Ning against the Seamless Gate’s infrastructure in the Dongxuan Major World. It’s not a battle, but a massacre. Anyone who thinks Ji Ning is just a hotheaded avenger is in for a rude awakening. After locking down every last clone of the Celestial Immortal Shaoyan Chou through his master's intelligence, he systematically wipes them out using the overwhelming power of his Innate treasure. The Seamless Gate’s regional command, the Iron Weapon Tower, observes the destruction but completely misreads the situation, dismissing him as just another lucky madman. The stage is set for a terrifying game of cat and mouse, where the cat knows exactly where all the mice are hiding.

## Key Plot Points

*   Ji Ning, possessing the strength of a Pure Yang True Immortal, infiltrates a small, lightly guarded Seamless Gate outpost.
*   After effortlessly dismantling a Loose Immortal formation, he engages the outpost's Celestial Immortal commander, Shaoyan Chou.
*   Ji Ning kills Shaoyan Chou’s primary body with a single, devastating strike using the Banner of the Heavens, shocking the commander and his forces.
*   Using the power of the Heavenly Stargold Beads to manifest a Void Millstone, he captures and grinds the entire outpost into dust, including all of its minions and buildings.
*   Crucially, Ji Ning had already been given the locations of Shaoyan Chou's other 15 hidden clones by his master, Patriarch Bodhi. He systematically finds and kills every single one, leaving no trace of the Celestial Immortal in this world.
*   The primary Seamless Gate command in the region, the Iron Weapon Tower, discusses the incident. They consist of the Iron Weapon True Immortal and the infamous ‘Golden Red Couple’ (Golden Spear Heavenly God and Red Flower True Immortal).
*   The Golden Red Couple completely underestimates Ji Ning, viewing him as a lucky lunatic. The Golden Spear Heavenly God boasts he could kill Ji Ning with a single blow and decides to wait for him to attack another outpost so he can teleport in and handle it personally.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The ‘Clone’ Game:** Shaoyan Chou’s reliance on multiple hidden clones is a classic Xianxia survival strategy. For powerful cultivators with long lifespans, having a few clones scattered in secret locations is the ultimate insurance policy against assassination. If their main body is destroyed, they can simply take over a clone and continue their existence. Naturally, the counter to this is the power of a supreme Intelligence asset like Patriarch Bodhi, who can track every single clone across a Major World. This immediately transforms a slow, methodical search into a swift, surgical eradication.

**The Flawed Intelligence of Arrogance:** The Iron Weapon Tower’s reaction is a masterclass in the Xianxia trope of underestimating an unknown variable. Iron Weapon True Immortal is cautious, urging patience. The Golden Red Couple, however, are arrogant and dismissive, viewing Ji Ning as a mere pest. This hubris is a fatal flaw that sets them up for a fall. They see a single Celestial Immortal with a good treasure; they do not see the hand of Patriarch Bodhi pulling the strings. Their decision to wait for him to strike again is a high-risk, low-information gamble that speaks volumes about their decadent and complacent nature.

### Body-Heart Extraction

A forbidden Xinxia technique where a cultivator's knowledge and techniques are violently ripped from their mind and soul after death, often used for torture or intelligence gathering.

## Reading Guide

Get ready, because Ji Ning’s “rampage” is anything but random. This chapter is a brilliant piece of strategic misdirection. He puts on a perfect act as an emotionally unhinged avenger, a “madman just out for revenge.” This cliché story is so believable that the seasoned experts of the Seamless Gate fall for it completely. While they’re busy preparing to ambush a “lucky Celestial Immortal” with a fancy flag, they have no idea they are facing a Pure Yang True Immortal-level threat armed with supreme Innate treasures and the intelligence of a cosmic chessmaster. The hunter has been fooled into thinking he’s hunting a rabbit. Enjoy the delicious irony as the arrogant Golden Red Couple plan their next move, completely unaware of the predator they are truly hunting.
