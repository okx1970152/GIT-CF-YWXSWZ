## Chapter Overview
Get ready, folks, because Ji Ning is about to flip the entire power scale of the Yan Mountains upside down. This chapter is a pure, unadulterated **face-smashing** sequence. After a long buildup of being hunted and trapped, Ji Ning finally reveals his hand—and what a hand it is. We see the Ji Clan’s heir, once seen as a promising but fragile child, transform into an absolute monster. The chapter opens with the Greenflame Bird and Black Snake panicking from above, but their panic soon turns to awe as Ji Ning unleashes the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation against thousands of Dao-soldiers. It’s a turning point that redefines the threat level in the region. Brace yourselves for a massacre.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Ambush Turns:** Ji Ning, having been drawn into a trap by the Zifu Disciple’s underlings, is confronted by thousands of Dao-soldiers and eight Xiantian lifeforms. The Zifu Disciple himself is busy in the deep mountain, refining an evil treasure.
- **The Big Reveal:** Ji Ning uses his Divine Sense to control over three hundred flying sword treasures in a display of the Lesser Thousand Sword Formation. The enemy Dao-soldiers realize in horror that they are facing a Zifu Disciple-level combatant.
- **Absolute Decimation:** The Xiantian lifeform Dao-soldiers, who formed only eight fragmented phantom flood dragons rather than a single unified beast, are slaughtered like wheat by Ji Ning’s sword-light. Their cries of “A Zifu Disciple!” ring out as they fall.
- **New Enemy Emerges:** A black-furred zombie bursts from the mountain, displaying immense strength and toughness, but Ji Ning’s superior movement technique allows him to evade easily. It’s a dangerous but mindless brute.
- **The Master Arrives:** Just as Ji Ning finishes dealing with the zombie, a pale man in a black robe with unsettling green eyes emerges from the pit. The Zifu Disciple has finally been forced to step out.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a textbook example of the **“flexing the cultivation base”** trope. For newcomers, the concept of the Zifu Disciple is crucial. In the Xianxia power ladder, a Zifu Disciple is the first true step into immortality. The body becomes a vessel for an internal dimensional sea of liquid Ki, and more importantly, one can now control magic treasures with the soul. The enemy Dao-soldiers’ terrified cry of “He’s a Zifu Disciple!” is a panic response—in their world, this is like a company of medieval archers realizing they’re fighting a jet fighter. The Dao-soldier formation is also a classic Xianxia military tactic. Ordinary soldiers are linked by runic armor to pool their power, but they require a critical mass to form a cohesive, beast-shaped phantom. Here, the eight Xiantian leaders formed eight separate phantoms, making the formation crippled and vulnerable.

## Reading Guide
- **Pay attention to Ji Ning’s tactical mind:** Even while poisoned and enraged, he doesn’t just attack blindly. He observes the zombie’s weakness and correctly deduces the Zifu Disciple’s predicament (the guy needs his hands free for his project). This is the mark of a true cultivator—combat intellect.
- **Why the shock works:** The narrative makes sure you feel the shift in power. We went from desperate survival to absolute, one-sided slaughter in a single page. Savor the moment; it’s a classic “hidden power unleashed” scene that defines the genre.
- **The tension isn't gone:** The Zifu Disciple has finally appeared. He’s pale, creepy, and looks far more dangerous than his underlings. The fight isn’t over yet—it’s just entering its final, most dangerous phase.
