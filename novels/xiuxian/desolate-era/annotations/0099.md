## Chapter Overview
Family, sacrifice, and the raw thrill of ascension collide in this incredibly satisfying chapter. After the brutal, bloody skirmish in the mountain belly, our heroes have stumbled upon a treasure trove that could rewrite the Ji Clan's fate. This is a classic Xianxia resource discovery chapter, but it’s elevated by the palpable, heartwarming tension of a family determined to pour every last drop of its newfound fortune into its brightest star. We're not just getting a power-up; we're getting a masterclass in clan dynamics and emotional payoff. Get ready, because Ji Ning is about to take a quantum leap forward.

## Key Plot Points
- The party investigates the sealed stone chamber, using Ji Jiuhuo's scouting insect to confirm it's safe.
- They discover a pool containing a thin layer of shimmering, emerald-green **Primordial Liquid**—the ultimate, purified essence of natural energy. This liquid is the entire reason the massive elemental ore vein was formed.
- Ji Jiuhuo explains that this treasure, left behind by a long-gone Immortal, is more valuable than all the elemental ore in the mine. It can instantly be converted into cultivation power without any side effects.
- Despite the treasure's immense value for their own cultivation, Ji Jiuho, Granny Shadow, Ji Yichuan, and the entire elder council immediately and unanimously decide that Ji Ning must be the one to use it. The family's hope is placed squarely on his shoulders.
- After exploring the side room (a simple monk's cell with no other treasures), the elders leave Ji Ning alone in the sealed chamber.
- Ji Ning calmly begins his cultivation, using the Primordial Liquid as fuel. He effortlessly breaks through the bottleneck to form his **Zifu** (the Violet Palace), the essential foundation for an immortal's Ki Refining path, and begins filling his new spiritual space with a vast lake of Yuan power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Primordial Liquid & The Nine Revolutions Golden Pill**
This chapter introduces a cornerstone of Xianxia economics: the concept of "pure energy." For a cultivator, the bottleneck is rarely just *energy*. It's about insight (境界), a strong soul (魂魄), and the right technique (功法). Primordial Liquid removes the gridlock of *time*. Normally, a cultivator spends years slowly refining raw energy from the world or from elemental ores, purging the impurities that strain their body. Primordial Liquid is that time, instantly banked. This is why it’s described as comparable to a legendary **Nine Revolutions Golden Pill (九转金丹)**—a mythical pill from Daoist alchemy that allows a reincarnated Immortal to regain their full power in a single day. For Ji Ning, a mortal genius with top-tier insight, this liquid is the perfect key to bypass years of grinding.

**The Zifu Breakthrough (紫府)**
The creation of the Zifu is the Ki Refiner's first true leap from the mortal realm. It's not just a stronger dantian; it's the creation of a private dimensional pocket within the cultivator's body—a "Violet Palace" filled with purple mist. This is the fundamental container for their spiritual energy (Yuan power). Before this, a cultivator has a dantian; after, they have a universe in miniature. The text's poetic description—*"It was as if Pangu had split open Heaven and Earth!"* —is a deliberate callback to the primordial myth, linking a mortal's small breakthrough to the grand cosmic act of creation itself. This is where a normal expert becomes a *Disciple* of the Immortal path.

## Reading Guide
This is a "breather" chapter in the best possible way. After the breakneck action, we get a moment of sublime discovery and emotional closure. Pay close attention to how the elders interact here. This isn't just about a power-up; it's a concrete demonstration of why the Ji Clan is worth fighting for. Every single one of them, from the pragmatic Ji Jiuhuo to the stern Ji Yichuan, looks at Ji Ning not with envy, but with a fierce, united hope. The chapter's real treasure isn't the liquid—it's the unspoken pact of a family investing its entire future in its most capable member. This moment of trust and unity is the emotional bedrock that will propel Ji Ning forward. Enjoy the calm before the storm; he's about to become a much bigger target.
