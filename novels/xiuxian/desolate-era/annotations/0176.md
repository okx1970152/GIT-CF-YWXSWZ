## Chapter Overview

Fellow Daoists, strap in—this chapter is a turning point. Ji Ning has already stunned the Dao Debate Palace by defeating Senior Brother Bloodshadow, and now the Black-White College’s finest are circling him like hungry wolves. But instead of buckling under pressure, Ji Ning’s Dao-heart undergoes a radical purification. When Senior Sister Baixue deploys her dreaded Green Silk Nether Formation, Ji Ning is pushed to the brink—and something clicks. Mid-battle, his sword heart ignites, and he begins to comprehend the legendary first stance of the Three-Foot Sword: Sword Heart Illumination. This is the moment a genius becomes a monster.

## Key Plot Points

- The Dao Debate Palace is deadlocked after Ji Ning’s victory; no one wants to face him until Senior Sister Baixue steps up.
- Baixue uses the Heaven Net Puppet to weave a sealed ribbon sphere that traps Ji Ning’s five New Three Talents Lotus Swords.
- Ji Ning pushes himself to the limit, sustaining six simultaneous sword formations, but Baixue maintains the upper hand.
- The strain triggers a flashback: Diancai Immortal’s demonstration of the Three-Foot Sword’s first stance, “Sword Heart Illumination.”
- Ji Ning experiences a breakthrough *during* the fight—his sword heart clarifies, and his sword-light grows sharper, eventually shattering Baixue’s formation.
- Baixue loses cleanly; Ji Ning stands and challenges the rest of the hall.
- Diancai Immortal and the short old man confirm that Ji Ning has touched the threshold of the Sword Immortal path.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Sword Heart Illumination (剑心通明)** is a classic Daoist cultivation concept: the cultivator’s heart becomes a clear mirror that reflects the sword without distortion. In Xianxia, this is the foundational insight for any true Sword Immortal—it doesn’t grant raw power, but it *unlocks* the ability to intuitively wield sword techniques at a higher level. Diancai Immortal’s explanation is pure Xianxia pedagogy: understanding comes not from memorizing stances, but from *becoming* a sword. Also note the recurring motif of “fighting to temper the heart”—battles in this genre are often as much about spiritual refinement as about winning.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in “breakthrough under pressure”—one of the most satisfying tropes in progression fantasy. Watch how the author uses the collective attention of the entire college to amplify the tension. Also pay attention to the short old man (likely a Loose Immortal or elder) who candidly discusses Ji Ning’s progress with Diancai Immortal—he’s foreshadowing that Ji Ning is on the verge of something big. Mu Northson’s shout of encouragement is a nice slice-of-Xianxia moment showing genuine friendship in a competitive environment. Finally, note how the winning blow isn’t described in gory detail; the focus stays on Ji Ning’s mental clarity. That’s the mark of a sword-dao novel: the real battle is within.
