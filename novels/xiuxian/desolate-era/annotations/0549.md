## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists—the ceasefire is over, and the first real clash of the Realm War kicks off with a vengeance! This chapter is pure, concentrated battle, dropping us right into the chaos as the Flamedragon Realmverse‘s Xingtian battle formations slam headlong into the Seamless Gate’s army of war beasts. Ji Ning, piloting a thousand-zhang-tall headless Xingtian, is no longer holding back at full power. He single-handedly demolishes a Zhu’yan formation on contact, sending the enemy into a frenzy. But the real threat arrives: the three legendary demon-saints of Mount Tunlong, led by the speed-supreme Golden Eagle, have set their sights on him. The stage is set for a collision between the Flamedragon’s rising sword-god and some of the most terrifying warriors the Three Realms have ever known. This is the kind of chapter where the power scaling gets real.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the two armies making contact. Ji Ning, commanding a Xingtian formation of 3,000 Celestial Immortals and 300,000 Loose Immortals, deliberately holds his speed to match the rest of the group until the very moment of impact, then explosively unleashes his full combat power alongside the other commanding Empyrean Gods and True Immortals.

The first major kill comes fast. When a Zhu’yan beast commanded by a Pure Yang True Immortal tries to block his charge, Ji Ning outsmarts the enemy’s technique advantage by switching to a shield, forcing a raw contest of strength. The Zhu’yan is sent flying backward, visibly outclassed. Ji Ning then chases down and annihilates a second Zhu’yan formation nearly wholesale, mopping up the fleeing Celestial Immortals with a Pure Yang fan and the Yin-Yang Primal Qi Bottle.

The Flamedragon Realmverse forces rally around Ji Ning’s breakthrough, forming a dense sub-battlefield. However, the Seamless Gate also deploys its heavy hitters: the six demons of Mount Tonglian, who form a complementary defensive system, and—most critically—the three demon-saints of Mount Tunlong.

The Great Sage Tunshan (the Golden Eagle) dives at Ji Ning at blinding speed, bragging about his intent to claim the bounty. He is backed up by his two brothers, the broken-horned dragon and the nine-headed lion, both of whom begin carving their way toward Ji Ning, triggering a desperate game of interception and pursuit across the battlefield. The chapter ends with the Golden Eagle, having been smashed out of the sky by Ji Ning’s shield, roaring in frustrated rage for his brothers to join him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is an excellent showcase of **mass-scale Xianxia warfare**. The individual cultivator may be an immortal demigod, but strategy and formation matter immensely. Here’s a breakdown of the key tactical elements on display:

**The Xingtian Formation (刑天阵):** Named after the legendary headless war god Xingtian from Chinese mythology, this formation transforms massive numbers of assembled cultivators into a single, towering divine warrior. The power of a Xingtian is directly tied to its scale—Ji Ning’s command of 3,000 Celestial Immortals and 300,000 Loose Immortals gives him an overwhelming baseline of strength that he can channel through his own combat instincts.

**The Zhu’yan and White-Faced Flood Dragon:** These are not real beasts, but formation-manifested war constructs. The Zhu’yan (朱厌) is a mythical white-furred ape with red feet, said to appear wherever war is about to break out. The White-Faced Flood Dragon (白脸蛟) is a formation of 1,000 Celestial Immortals and 100,000 Loose Immortals that takes the form of a serpentine dragon. Both serve as the Seamless Gate’s heavy shock troops.

**Qi of Fortune (气运):** That abstract concept of a kingdom’s or realm’s destiny, which can be won or lost in battle. In early scenes of the war arc, protecting your realm’s ‘fortune’ is a strategic priority—it’s a magical resource as real as treasure.

**Realm War:** This is the highest tier of conflict in the Three Realms, pitting entire realmverses against each other. The death toll is measured in *hundreds of Celestial Immortals* per engagement. Each formation destroyed here represents the permanent loss of thousands of cultivators’ lives and centuries of cultivation.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is pure, adrenaline-pumping payoff for all the setup of the previous year-long ceasefire. If you love big-scale, tactical Xianxia combat, this is the good stuff. The tension builds not just from the raw violence, but from the *containment game*: both sides are carefully watching their super-heavy hitters, trying to keep them from breaking the line. The moment the three Tunlong demon-saints start moving, the entire battlefield shifts around them.

Watch how Ji Ning wins: he doesn’t out-technique the enemy; he *out-masses* them by leveraging the brute strength of his upgraded formation. It’s a reminder that even the most skilled swordsman has to adapt to the scale of cosmic war. The Golden Eagle’s first attack failing is a huge statement—it tells the enemy that their strongest speedster can’t just blitz Ji Ning. But the fight is far from over. The question now: can Ji Ning hold the line when all three demon-saints attack together? And what is *his* decisive opportunity waiting for?

Get ready for some serious ‘on the edge of your seat’ reading. The Realm War has truly begun.
