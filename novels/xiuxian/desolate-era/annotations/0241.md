## Chapter Overview
Well, fellow Daoists, the honeymoon phase is officially over. In this chapter, the serene romance on Serpentwing Lake is violently interrupted by the arrival of the Dongyan clan’s war fleet. Ji Ning and Nine Lotos are sharing a quiet, intimate moment—sipping the legendary Dongxian Wine and exchanging heartfelt vows—when an ancient, overpowering force descends from the heavens. The Beiqiu Immortal, a Loose Immortal leading nine Primal Daoists and an army of Wanxiang Adepts, comes bearing a direct order from none other than the Dongyan Ancestor: Nine Lotos is to return home immediately. The confrontation is brief but devastating, underlined by a brutal divine sense assault meant to humble Ji Ning. Despite his resolve, Ji Ning is forced to watch helplessly as Nine Lotos is taken away, leaving him with a chilling final message: “You are not worthy of her.” Cue the emotional devastation and the reforging of a Dao-heart.

## Key Plot Points
- Nine Lotos reveals the origin of the Dongxian Wine, sharing the legendary tale of Dong You (Immortal Dongyou), a mortal who defied heaven itself to reclaim his Celestial Fairy lover.
- A massive Dongyan clan war vessel arrives, immediately signaling its hostile intent with a suffocating divine sense assault aimed directly at Ji Ning.
- Ji Ning withstands the combined divine sense pressure of the Beiqiu Immortal and his nine Primal Daoist disciples, shocking the Immortal and hinting at Ji Ning’s formidable soul strength.
- The Beiqiu Immortal reveals the order comes from the Dongyan Ancestor himself, a being so ancient and powerful that even the clan leader is beneath him.
- Nine Lotos, despite her status as heir, has no choice but to submit to the Ancestor’s summons.
- Before departing, the Beiqiu Immortal delivers a crushing mental message to Ji Ning: he is unworthy of Nine Lotos, who is destined to be the leader of the Dongyan clan.
- Ji Ning, left alone on the lake, mutters “Not worthy?” with renewed, steely determination, signaling the birth of a far more formidable will.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The **Dongxian Wine (董仙酒)** is more than just a fancy drink—it's a loaded narrative device tied to a famous legend within the Desolate Era universe. Immortal Dongyou (董游) is a classic archetype in Xianxia: the "mortal who storms the heavens for love." His story of ascending from a mere cultivator to a leader of a major world and launching a full-scale war against the Heavenly Realm to reclaim his lover is a power fantasy within a power fantasy. The wine symbolizes **unshakable faith and defiance against cosmic hierarchy**, which is precisely what Nine Lotos is trying to instill in Ji Ning before the storm hits. She didn't just bring entertainment—she brought a manifesto. Also note the **divine sense assault** tactic here. The Beiqiu Immortal wasn't just flexing—he was testing Ji Ning's soul foundation. An ordinary Wanxiang Adept would have been knocked unconscious or worse. Ji Ning's ability to withstand it suggests an anomalously strong soul, which is often a telltale sign of a reincarnated Immortal or a cultivator with massive latent potential.

## Reading Guide
Okay, brace yourselves. This chapter is a gut punch for anyone who was hoping for smooth sailing for Ji Ning and Nine Lotos. The romantic idyll is shattered, and the sheer scale of the power imbalance is laid bare. The Dongyan clan doesn't just have a grumpy dad; they have a clan leader with a Celestial Immortal-level Ancestor who can order the entire force to move at a moment's notice. Ji Ning's reaction is the key here. He doesn't break. He doesn't beg. He doesn't rage impotently. His response is a quiet, ice-cold reaffirmation of will. That single muttered "Not worthy?" is the turning point. This is where his Dao-heart is truly tempered, moving from a talented boy to a man with a core, obsessive drive. The Beiqiu Immortal and his "not worthy" remark just lit a fuse under Ji Ning's ambition. Keep watching that fuse burn.
