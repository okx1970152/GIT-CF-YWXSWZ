## Chapter Overview
Fellow Daoists, the stakes just got *stupid*. The Immortal Fortunes Assembly has officially left the kiddy pool. After the brutal Brightmoon Landscape World trial, the survivors are summoned before the Grand Xia Emperor, nearly a thousand Celestial Immortals, and seven Pure Yang True Immortals. The air is thick enough to choke a Xiantian beast. Our boy Ji Ning gets a front-row view of the cosmic power hierarchy. But the real hammer drops when the Emperor announces the rewards: this isn't just a tournament for a pat on the back. This is a war for a ticket to the big leagues, with Immortal-ranked treasures, enough Primordial Liquid to buy a small world, and the ultimate prize—becoming a disciple of Daofather Crimsonbright himself. Get ready for glory, blood, and politics.

## Key Plot Points
- The ninety-six survivors of the Brightmoon Landscape World are assembled and healed by the tall-hatted attendant. The Slovenly Trulord gets a knowing smile from the attendant, hinting at his special status.
- Ji Ning observes the sheer scale of the Great Xia Dynasty's power: nearly a thousand Celestial Immortals line the hall, a sight unimaginable in the backwater Yan Mountains. The Emperor himself sits with seven Pure Yang True Immortals.
- The Grand Xia Emperor announces a brutal single-elimination bracket. The rewards are obscene: forty-eight people get Immortal-ranked treasures, twelve get ten million catties of Primordial Liquid each, and the top three get to pick any divine ability from the Imperial treasury.
- The real bombshell: Daofather Crimsonbright himself—the Emperor’s master—will select one of the final three to become his nominal disciple, with a guaranteed promotion to true disciple upon reaching the Celestial Immortal stage.
- The reaction is volcanic. The Celestial Immortals in the hall, none of whom are Daofather Crimsonbright’s true disciples, are consumed by envy and disbelief.
- Ancestor Xuanji of the Shaoyin clan is already scheming, ordering Shaoyin Xuan to fight like a madman for the chance to become a Daofather’s disciple.
- Ji Ning, Yu Wei, Yuchi Xiyue, and the Slovenly Trulord gather, with Yan Wang acting as their host and patron for this deadly event.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Daofather’s Disciple—A Ticket Beyond the Heavens**: This chapter is a masterclass in Xianxia incentive design. The Grand Xia Emperor isn't just handing out toys; he's offering a place in the lineage of a *Daofather*. In the cosmic hierarchy of the Three Realms, Daofathers and Chaos True Gods are the twin pinnacles of power—Ki Refining and Fiendgod Body Refining respectively. They dictate even the Celestial Thearch’s position. A Daofather’s true disciple is an instant VIP with a guaranteed path to immortality. The nearly one thousand Celestial Immortals in the hall are all mere nominal disciples of Crimsonbright; they've never even spoken to him. The sheer social leap from "mortal genius" to "Daofather's heir" is so massive that it warps the logic of the entire world. This isn't a reward; it's a *transmutation* of one's soul-level status.

**The Power of an "Ancestor"**: Ancestor Xuanji’s threat to Shaoyin Xuan reveals another key Xianxia law: a single Ancestor-level power can dictate the fate of an entire clan. He promises that if Xuan succeeds, the Celestial Immortals of the clan will bow to *him*. In this world, power isn't just personal; it's a political force that can instantly elevate a young genius to a position above millennia-old elders. This creates intense, multi-generational pressure on every cultivator in the tournament.

## Reading Guide
- **Watch Ji Ning's Gaze**: Pay close attention to how Ji Ning observes the environment—the sheer number of Celestial Immortals, the auras of the True Immortals. He's not just sightseeing; he's mentally mapping the power structure of the world he's about to conquer. This clinical analysis is pure Ji Ning.
- **The Emperor’s Master**: The reveal that Daofather Crimsonbright is behind this assembly recontextualizes everything. The entire tournament is a recruitment drive for a being who controls the laws of reality. This puts every single fight into a desperate, once-in-a-reincarnation perspective for the other contestants.
- **The Slovenly Trulord’s Shadow**: The tall-hatted attendant’s smile, the True Immortals’ focused gazes—the Slovenly Trulord is already a marked man. He's Lord Xuanwu’s disciple, which means he's a rival for the ultimate prize. His presence adds a layer of heavy politics to the competition. He’s a walking symbol of a power bloc that even the Emperor has to respect.
