## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because Ji Ning has just walked into the ultimate cultivation library, and he's about to suffer a severe case of *I-want-all-of-these-itis*. This chapter is a glorious tour through the Three Realms Palace's divine abilities, where Ji Ning finally gets his hands on the legendary Eight-Nine Arcane Art. But before he can start transforming into a bird or tanking Pure Yang treasure strikes, he needs to pass a trial. And spoiler alert: this trial makes sweeping mountain paths look like a walk in the park. The chapter balances dizzying power fantasy — "My body can become as tough as a Primordial treasure?!" — with the classic ground rule: pick one, master it, don't be a greedy fool.

## Key Plot Points

- Ji Ning reads the abridged manual for the Eight-Nine Arcane Art and learns its origin: a co-creation by the leaders of the Buddhist and Daoist sects, meant to push the body to Pangu's level, but ultimately stuck at nine cycles.
- He compares it to his Starseizing Hand, noting the key difference: Starseizing Hand only trains the hands, while the Eight-Nine Arcane Art trains the entire body.
- He flips through other divine abilities: Xingtian's Annihilating Axe, the Lushen Body, the Lightning God Eye, Buddha's Palm Paradise, the Heaven-Slaying Sword Formation, and more.
- Jidian (the mad monk) warns him against greed: pick two or three at most, master one, and you'll be a lord of the Three Realms. If you try to learn everything, you'll be mediocre at everything.
- Ji Ning decisively chooses the Eight-Nine Arcane Art as his first pick, acknowledging it as the king beneath the True God level.
- He flips to the trial section and immediately realizes that obtaining this technique will be exponentially harder than learning the Candle Dragon Eye.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight-Nine Arcane Art (八九玄功) is one of the most iconic divine abilities in Chinese mythology, closely associated with Sun Wukong (the Monkey King) in *Journey to the West*. In that classic novel, the Seventy-Two Transformations was taught to the Monkey King by his master, Patriarch Subhuti — the very same mysterious figure who appears in *Desolate Era* as Ji Ning's master. The novel weaves this real mythological inheritance into its own Xianxia framework: here, the technique is a collaborative creation of the Buddhist and Daoist leaders, not a random monkey-brained power-up. The number "Eight-Nine" (八九) is a symbolic Daoist number representing infinite variety, not literal seventy-two forms. The term "Diamond-Indestructible" (金刚不坏) is borrowed from Buddhist doctrine, where it describes the indestructible body of a Buddha. In Xianxia, it's the ultimate flex: "Your strongest treasure just gave me a papercut. Barely."

## Reading Guide

This is a classic Xianxia "shopping for superpowers" chapter, and the key is to enjoy the worldbuilding without getting overwhelmed. The author is flexing the sheer scope of the Three Realms' power system — every divine ability here has been foreshadowed or will be relevant later. For new readers, treat this as a delicious menu of future badassery. Pay special attention to the rule Jidian lays down: choose two or three, master them, or waste your life. This isn't just good advice — it's a direct statement about Ji Ning's character. He is the ultimate pragmatist; he won't try to be a jack of all trades. The Eight-Nine Arcane Art is his first pick, and the difficulty of its trial sets the stakes for the next arc. Get ready for a brutal test of pure body-refining supremacy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes: The Starseizing Hand vs. the Eight-Nine Arcane Art

Ji Ning's internal comparison between his inherited Starseizing Hand and the freshly discovered Eight-Nine Arcane Art is a subtle but delicious piece of Xianxia math. The Starseizing Hand is a top-ten divine ability of the Three Realms because it has True God-level cycles, meaning it can scale all the way to the absolute peak. But it only trains the hands. The Eight-Nine Arcane Art stops at Empyrean God (True God is not achievable), meaning it's "merely" number one *beneath* the True God level. However, it trains the whole body. This means Ji Ning's current foundation is already superior in ultimate potential, but the Eight-Nine Arcane Art gives him an immediate, practical boost to his entire physique. It's a perfect complement: his hands are already terrifying; now his entire body will catch up.
