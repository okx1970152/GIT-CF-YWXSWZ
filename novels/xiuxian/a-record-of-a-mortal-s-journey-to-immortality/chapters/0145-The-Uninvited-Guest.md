Chapter 145: The Uninvited Guest

“That’s right, Senior Martial Brother Wu, an excellent idea!” The old man surnamed Ye’s eyes lit up with excitement.

He turned his head and looked expectantly at Zhong Lingdao. “Sect Leader, could I meet the owner of that Ascension Token? I’d like to make a trade with him—to have him voluntarily give up the Foundation Establishment Pill!”

Zhong Lingdao hesitated for a moment after hearing this, then nodded in agreement. But he again instructed the old man not to resort to coercive or excessive means to force a deal. Only then did he allow the Wang Junior Martial Brother to take him to meet the uninvited guest.

The old man and Junior Wang left the great hall. They immediately stepped onto a leaf-shaped flying tool and rose into the air, heading straight for Yellow Maple Valley’s Guest Welcome Pavilion.

Inside a room of the Yellow Maple Valley Guest Welcome Pavilion, a young man lay on his bed staring blankly at the ceiling. It was Han Li, who had arrived at Yellow Maple Valley alongside the victors of the Ascension Assembly.

Back then, after killing the two cultivators who had ambushed him, Han Li had continued his journey without further incident, arriving at a hidden mountain range in Lan Province where the Ascension Assembly was held shortly after. He had witnessed in person arena battles even more brutal than what Hu Pinggu had described.

After a life-and-death struggle, the final victors of each arena were decided. At that point, the guides from the seven great immortal sects finally appeared. Yellow Maple Valley’s guide was that same Junior Wang.

When Han Li saw the Yellow Maple Valley guide, he weighed the risks carefully and decided to take a gamble. He presented the Ascension Token to the guide, who was greatly surprised.

The guide immediately said he could bring Han Li back to the sect, but that the specifics of how to handle him and the token would be up to the sect leader. After all, the last time such an Ascension Token had been turned in was four or five hundred years ago.

Han Li naturally had no objection. Even finding the mountain gate of Yellow Maple Valley on his own would have been trouble enough.

And so, under the astonished gazes of the other cultivators, Han Li boarded a huge boat-shaped flying tool with the ten victors. Together, they were brought back to Yellow Maple Valley by the guide, Junior Wang. He was arranged to stay here and await the relevant reply. As for the other ten, they were separated from him, taken to some unknown place.

Han Li had been here for three or four days now. He had not even stepped out of his room. Apart from a boy of eleven or twelve who delivered meals on time every day, he had seen no one else.

This was not because Han Li was well-behaved. It was just that the guide surnamed Wang, after settling him in and testing his spiritual root attributes, had clearly informed him that because he was not yet a disciple of Yellow Maple Valley, the room had been sealed with some restrictive spells. He was temporarily prohibited from leaving, as doing so would trigger the spells and trap him.

Hearing that from Junior Wang, Han Li naturally would not ask for trouble. Moreover, after learning that he possessed the four-attribute False Spiritual Roots, Han Li’s mood had been somewhat depressed for quite a while.

Although he had long been aware of his poor aptitude for cultivation, hearing it confirmed still left him disheartened for an entire day. It seemed that if he wanted to make any real progress on his cultivation path, he would have to rely on external aids like medicinal pills.

However, after reaching the ninth layer of the Eternal Spring Art, he felt that the effects of the Yellow Dragon Pill and the Golden Marrow Pill had noticeably diminished. They no longer helped him as much as before. It seemed he would have to find some new medicinal formulas—to concoct pills truly suited for cultivators—if he did not want to fall behind in his training.

Han Li was lost in thought when he suddenly heard footsteps outside the door—more than one person. His spirits lifted. It seemed that after waiting for several days, the long-awaited news had finally arrived.

“Young friend, are you settling in well here?”

The door was pushed open gently. The voice of Junior Wang, the guide, came through. He entered from outside, followed by a red-faced old man.

“Greetings, Immortal Wang!” Han Li immediately jumped off the bed and bowed respectfully. He knew well that courtesy cost nothing and that a humble demeanor would only bring him benefits.

“And this is…?” Han Li glanced at the old man and asked with some uncertainty.

“This is my senior martial brother, surnamed Ye.” Junior Wang smiled and gave a brief explanation.

Surnamed Ye? Han Li was startled. Could it be that his murder of Gold Light Elder and theft of the token had been exposed? Had someone from the Ye Clan come looking for him? But although the old man’s expression was not particularly pleasant, he did not seem to be gnashing his teeth in hatred. There must be some other trick at play. Han Li thought to himself, but on the surface he showed no sign of abnormality. He immediately spoke in the same respectful tone:

“Greetings, Immortal Ye!”

The old man had already given Han Li a thorough once-over. The more he looked, the more ordinary Han Li seemed—nothing remarkable about him at all. This gave him a bit more confidence in the purpose of his visit.

So when he heard Han Li’s greeting, his own expression turned genial. “Hehe! Young friend Han, no need to be so formal! Since you have come to our Yellow Maple Valley bearing an Ascension Token, then you are already a disciple of our sect. You may call me Uncle Ye—no need to stand on ceremony!”

Han Li’s heart relaxed a little at these words, but he also grew more puzzled.

Since the other party was speaking so politely, it seemed they were not here to settle a grudge. But wasn’t this courtesy a bit excessive? What did he mean by “not an outsider”? Han Li couldn’t make sense of it.

“Young friend Han, what Senior Brother Ye says is correct! Our sect leader has agreed to let you join our valley as a disciple. Moreover, he has set aside one Foundation Establishment Pill for you—it is ready and waiting!” Junior Wang said lightly with a smile.

“Really?” Even though Han Li was usually calm and composed, hearing this news made him unable to suppress his excitement. He felt like jumping for joy to vent the thrill in his heart.

Seeing Han Li like this, Junior Wang smiled, not at all surprised. It seemed Han Li’s reaction was exactly what he had expected.

“Junior Wang, I’d like to have a private word with Disciple Han. Would you mind stepping out for a moment?” The old man was growing impatient and finally spoke the words he had been wanting to say ever since he entered the room.

“Of course, then I’ll head back to the sect leader first. Senior Brother, after you’re done talking with Disciple Han, please come over as well.” Junior Wang sighed softly to himself, glanced at Han Li, and said flatly before leaving the room.

Now only the old man surnamed Ye and Han Li remained.

Han Li stared blankly at what was happening. Why had that man surnamed Wang left so quickly? And he had left behind someone who called himself Uncle to discuss something with him! Although he didn’t know what this Uncle Ye wanted, Han Li had a faint bad feeling.

The old man noticed Han Li’s unease, but he didn’t care at all. He was confident that what he could offer would surely open the eyes of this young man who had seen little of the world, making the transaction go smoothly.

“Disciple Han, your uncle here is a straightforward man, so I won’t beat around the bush. Let me speak plainly. This matter concerns the Foundation Establishment Pill that has been allocated to you. I would like to buy it from you. What do you think?” The old man got straight to the point.

Buy the Foundation Establishment Pill that was allocated to him? He hadn’t heard wrong, had he? Who would give up such a pill to someone else? Han Li was stunned for a moment, then his expression changed dramatically—it turned very unpleasant.

“Disciple Han, rest assured. I am not asking you to offer up the Foundation Establishment Pill for nothing. Seven or eight mid-grade spirit stones, some low-to-high-grade talismans, and a few excellent magical tools—your uncle here can still produce these. If that’s not enough, I also have some pills that can advance one’s cultivation. Although they can’t compare to the Foundation Establishment Pill, they are still rare medicines within the sect. As long as Disciple Han agrees, all of these can be exchanged for the Foundation Establishment Pill.” The old man naturally saw Han Li’s change in expression and quickly explained.

After hearing this, Han Li’s expression improved considerably. He could feel the sincerity in the other party’s words. It seemed this Uncle Ye had no intention of robbing him by force; he genuinely wanted to buy the Foundation Establishment Pill.
