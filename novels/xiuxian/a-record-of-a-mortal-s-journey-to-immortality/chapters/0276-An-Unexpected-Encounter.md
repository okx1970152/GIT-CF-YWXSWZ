Chapter 276: An Unexpected Encounter

After spending most of the spirit stones in his pouch, Han Li had managed to gather most of the ingredients needed for the new ancient pill formula.

For the remaining deficiencies, Han Li gritted his teeth. After taking the risk of selling two four-hundred-year-old spirit herbs, he resolved to participate in a few more missions hunting down demonic cultivators. This way, he could earn a considerable bounty of spirit stones, and the contents of the enemy's storage pouches could be exchanged for even more.

With the several Talisman Treasures in his possession and the power of the Great Derivative Art, Han Li could safely escape even if he encountered a late Foundation Establishment cultivator. Conversely, if an early or mid Foundation Establishment demonic cultivator ran into Han Li, they would naturally have no chance to fight back and would perish at his hands.

Of course, a freak like the Ghost Spirit Sect's Young Master, with his Foundation Establishment cultivation, would never participate in such operations due to his status.

As a result, over the past year, Han Li had gained a small reputation among the Foundation Establishment cultivators of the Seven Sects.

Everyone knew that Yellow Maple Valley had produced a new ruthless character. Though only at mid Foundation Establishment, he had ambushed and killed over a dozen demonic cultivators in a row and escaped unscathed, which was considered incredible.

Han Li had used the spirit stones he earned to gather the remaining materials for the ancient formula. He had heard that the camps of the other sects also had similar trading posts, so he had asked this Fatty Chen to keep an eye out for any pill formulas. However, it seemed there was no luck today.

Han Li was about to take his leave when a waft of fragrant wind suddenly blew past him.

Slightly startled, Han Li turned his head to see a cold and beautiful female cultivator standing behind him.

Recognizing that moving face and full figure, Han Li's expression remained unchanged on the surface, but his heart grew a little uneasy. It was Junior Chen, with whom he had shared an intimate moment. Thinking back to that fiery and passionate night, a strange feeling stirred within him.

He hadn't seen this woman since they parted ways at the forbidden land, and he didn't know when she had arrived here.

"Fifth Uncle, this is what you asked for!"

Junior Chen gave Han Li a brief, indifferent glance. A flicker of surprise passed through her eyes before she walked up to Fatty Chen and handed him a storage pouch.

"Hehe, Qiaoqian, my dear niece, you've come just in time! We're in a hurry for this batch of goods!" Fatty Chen was overjoyed to see her, his chubby face trembling as he spoke.

"Fifth Uncle?"

Han Li was stunned by this form of address.

He had never imagined that the fat man he had known for over half a year was actually Junior Chen's elder. With his mid Foundation Establishment cultivation, he must hold a high position in the Chen Clan.

Aside from that first glance, Junior Chen remained coldly indifferent and didn't even look in Han's direction. This left Han Li feeling a bit uncomfortable.

"Brother Chen, I'll take my leave now. I'll come again another day!" Not wanting to linger, Han Li spoke a few polite words and turned to walk out of the exchange hall.

Only then did Chen Qiaoqiao cast a cold glance at Han Li's retreating figure. With a furrowed brow, she asked Fatty Chen, who was inspecting the storage pouch:

"Fifth Uncle, did that coward come to you to buy something?"

"Coward?"

Fatty Chen was completely baffled by Chen Qiaoqiao's question.

"Mm, the guy who was standing here just now!"

"You mean… Han Li?" Fatty Chen blinked his little eyes, a look of disbelief spreading across his face.

"That's the one. What's wrong with that?" Chen Qiaoqiao asked, a little mystified.

From their trip to the forbidden land, Han Li had not left this Junior Chen with a good impression. She had always thought of him as a lucky speculator who avoided battles.

Fatty Chen was silent for a moment, then suddenly sighed. His tone became somewhat strange as he said:

"Qiaoqiao, you've just arrived, so there are some things you don't know yet. I don't know why you think this guy is a coward, but your fellow sect member has a considerable reputation here. He's a fierce character who has wiped out over a dozen Foundation Establishment cultivators. He is by no means an ordinary person. I was even thinking that after this war is over, I would recommend him to the Clan Leader. If we could win this man over, he would be a great help!"

"He wiped out over a dozen Foundation Establishment cultivators?" Junior Chen was stunned, a look of horror appearing on her previously cold face. She simply couldn't believe what she was hearing.

She couldn't help but stare blankly at the entrance of the exchange hall, but by now, Han Li had already left the building.

After leaving the exchange, Han Li pondered for a moment before heading towards his master Li Huayuan's residence. He planned to pay his respects as usual, fulfilling his duties as a disciple.

As a Core Formation cultivator, Li Huayuan's residence naturally couldn't be compared to that of an ordinary cultivator. He lived in a very comfortable house at the very center of the great formation, and his young wife lived with him.

Of the seven senior fellow apprentices Han Li had, aside from the eldest, who was still stationed at Green Wave Cave, the others were all away on missions. Only Han Li and his Fourth Senior Brother, Song Meng, remained here.

When Han Li had first arrived at this place, he had gone to pay his respects to Li Huayuan. His master had been taken aback and greatly surprised by Han Li's mid Foundation Establishment cultivation.

After all, only a few years had passed since Han Li had successfully established his foundation.

Under normal circumstances, even a cultivator with excellent aptitude would need over ten years to advance from the early stage to the mid stage. The speed of Han Li's progress naturally piqued Li Huayuan's curiosity.

After checking Han Li's body and finding no clues, he asked about the reason for his rapid increase in cultivation.

Naturally, Han Li didn't reveal the truth. Instead, he put on an act of ignorance, leaving Li Huayuan feeling frustrated for a while before finally letting the matter go. After all, with a major enemy at their doorstep, he had no time to spend too long studying this issue.

Regarding the Yan Clan's defection and Dong Xuan'er's capture, Li Huayuan and Hong Fu were both aware. Apart from being extremely furious, they didn't blame Han Li too harshly. After all, the enemy was too strong, and it was already difficult enough for Han Li to have escaped with his own life.

As Han Li was thinking this, he arrived outside Li Huayuan's residence.

To his surprise, his Fourth Senior Brother, Song Meng, was standing outside the house with his arms crossed, looking exceptionally alert, as if he were standing guard.

Seeing Han Li, Song Meng's face broke into a slight smile. He said in a deep voice, "Junior Han, here to pay your respects to our master again?"

Han Li gave a soft chuckle and replied with an "Mm."

Since Han Li had earned his fame, this Senior Brother Song had forced a sparring match with him in a secluded spot. Although Han Li had won by a narrow margin thanks to his sharp artifacts, their relationship had gradually warmed, and the fellow apprentices had begun to chat and laugh with each other.

"Junior Han, our master is discussing matters with a few guests inside. Wait outside for a moment! Speaking of which, I've recently made some progress with my technique. How about we have a sparring match later?" Song Meng said with a grin, explaining the situation to Han Li. But then he immediately made a suggestion that gave Han Li a headache.

Ever since this Senior Brother Song had lost to him, he would ask for a rematch almost every time they met. Han Li, however, was naturally unwilling to casually reveal his true capabilities and steadfastly refused.

If they were back at Yellow Maple Valley, this Senior Brother Song would certainly not let him off so easily! But fortunately, here there were many powerful cultivators to serve as opponents. After pestering Han Li a few times with no result, Song Meng couldn't stand it anymore and went looking for others. This, at least, allowed Han Li to breathe a sigh of relief.

So, upon hearing the last sentence, Han Li gave a wry smile and didn't dare to pick up that thread.

However, Han Li was somewhat curious about the guests meeting with Li Huayuan. The fact that Song Meng had used the word "discussing" meant the people inside were most likely Core Formation cultivators.

Just as he was about to try and pry some information from Song Meng, the tightly shut door suddenly swung open, and several male and female cultivators walked out.

Li Huayuan and his wife followed closely behind, seeing them off with cupped fists.

As expected, they were all Core Formation cultivators! Han Li saw them clearly. Four men and three women, all with unfathomably deep cultivation.

He and Song Meng didn't dare to block their path and quickly stepped aside, standing respectfully with their hands down.

Li Huayuan glanced at Han Li and smiled slightly, but didn't pay any more attention. The other cultivators also noticed Han Li, but most just swept a glance over him. After all, a mid Foundation Establishment cultivator wasn't worth their notice.

However, one female cultivator, tall and perfectly proportioned with a veil covering her face, had just finished scanning Han Li when her body suddenly trembled, and she stopped in her tracks. At the same time, a pair of bright, clear eyes flashed with a strange, unidentifiable emotion.

"Fairy Nan Gong, have you seen my worthless disciple before?"

The other cultivators noticed the female cultivator's strange behavior and looked at her with some surprise. Li Huayuan, feeling quite puzzled, couldn't help but ask.

The words "Fairy Nan Gong" struck Han Li's ears like a hammer, making his heart skip a beat. A complex melange of emotions surged within him.

Though he managed to control his expression and keep it unchanged, he couldn't help but look at this female cultivator.
