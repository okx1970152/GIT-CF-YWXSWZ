Having resided within Doctor Mo’s body for some time, Yu Zitong’s soul had begun to experience a phenomenon called "assimilation."

"Assimilation" was a passive form of possession. It occurred when an external primordial soul lingered too long inside another person's body, gradually being influenced by the host's soul on an unconscious level. The two souls would interact and merge, but in the end, only one consciousness could survive. It was a凶恶 phenomenon.

Seeing this, Yu Zitong was left with no choice but to consider an主动 possession. His reluctance was not born from any kindness, but from his fear of the legendary Three Iron Laws of Possession in the cultivation world:

First, a cultivator could not possess a mortal. If they attempted to, the mortal’s body would be unable to withstand the process and would collapse.

Second, only a cultivator with higher magic power could successfully possess one with lower power. The greater the gap in power, the safer the process.

Third, regardless of their cultivation level, a cultivator could only attempt possession once in their lifetime. Any second attempt would cause their primordial soul to vanish for no reason.

These three iron laws had been tested countless times and had never been broken. They restricted countless villains who sought to use possession to stir up trouble, and opportunists who tried to use the technique to escape calamity. Heaven had placed these warnings on such an逆天 act, preventing the world from falling into chaos through its misuse.

If Doctor Mo had been a cultivator, Yu Zitong would not have been afraid. He would have fought tooth and nail with him for the body. But Doctor Mo was merely a mortal, without a shred of magic power. His body could not withstand the possession process at all; it would probably collapse halfway through the attempt.

Furthermore, even if Yu Zitong found another person’s body to hide in, he could not avoid the fate of assimilation. He would simply face the same dilemma again, and it would be even worse. His magic power would decrease sharply with each entry and exit of his soul, and he would soon be trapped inside someone else’s body, eventually being assimilated.

He had to remember that, having become a primordial soul, he had no physical body to sit and replenish his energy. The magic power he carried was used once and lost forever. It was also diminishing with the passage of time, and he did not know how much longer he could last.

Faced with the twin pressures of his soul having nowhere to hide once Doctor Mo’s body collapsed from the blood curse, and the danger of being assimilated by the host’s soul, the survival-driven Yu Zitong was forced to set aside their enmity. After much deliberation, he reluctantly made contact with Doctor Mo and told him the entire situation, including the利害关系.

At first, Doctor Mo was furious, but he soon recognized the great opportunity before him. Without a moment's hesitation, he drew up three articles of agreement with Yu Zitong and reached a deal, revealing his true nature as a ruthless leader.

First, Doctor Mo was to follow Yu Zitong’s instructions to control his own consciousness and avoid assimilating the other’s soul as much as possible. In return, Yu Zitong would teach him secret techniques to slow down his aging and grant him temporary access to magic power.

Second, Doctor Mo was to find a child with spiritual roots who could cultivate the Eternal Spring Art. He would teach the child to cultivate this technique, and when the time was right, Doctor Mo would use his temporarily acquired magic power to perform the possession and gain a new life.

Doctor Mo had initially wanted to cultivate the technique himself, but his attempts came to nothing, leading to ridicule from Yu Zitong. It was then he learned that those without spiritual roots could not cultivate magic power, and he was one of those ordinary people the cultivators called "those without roots."

Third, after a successful possession, the reborn Doctor Mo was to use the buffer period to help Yu Zitong find a suitable physical body and assist him in his own possession.
