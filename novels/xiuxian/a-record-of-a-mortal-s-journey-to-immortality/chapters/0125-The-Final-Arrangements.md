"Time? A place name?" Xi Tieniu was stunned for a moment, seemingly having no recollection. But seeing Han Li's seriousness, he knew this was the key to whether he could perform a meritorious service. He lowered his head and racked his brains.

Half a quarter-hour later…

"I've got it!" Xi Tieniu suddenly looked up and shouted, his face full of joy.

"I remember that female immortal said that before attending the Immortal Ascension Assembly, she wanted the male immortal to accompany her to a place called 'Tainan Valley.' She said there were other immortals there too."

"Tainan Valley?" Han Li muttered the name twice, finding no trace of it in his memory. He had simply never heard of the place.

Han Li turned his gaze to Sun Ergou. If this place was local, this "local snake" should know something about it.

"There's no such place in Jiayuan City! If there really was a valley like that, I would remember it very clearly." Sun Ergou frowned and shook his head vigorously.

"You're not mistaken?" Han Li's gaze shifted back to Xi Tieniu, his tone turning cold and stern.

"Absolutely not! The woman also said that if they traveled for just half a day more, they would meet up with her friends at Tainan Valley." Xi Tieniu quickly swore to heaven.

"Half a day's travel! If a person were walking, it wouldn't be outside the vicinity of Jiayuan City. But these two were riding flying beasts, which greatly expands the range. Still, it shouldn't extend beyond the borders of Lan Province," Han Li calculated to himself.

"Do either of you know if there is a place called 'Tainan Valley' or simply 'Tainan' in all of Lan Province?" Han Li's expression softened slightly as he asked the two.

Sun Ergou and Xi Tieniu exchanged a glance and then shouted out almost simultaneously:

"Tainan Temple"

"Tainan Mountain"

"Two places called 'Tainan'?" Han Li was taken aback, feeling a bit of a headache.

"Young master! No, it's just the one!" Sun Ergou answered, taking the lead.

"Tainan Temple is built on Tainan Mountain," Xi Tieniu added, not wanting to be outdone.

"Oh! Excellent. Then Tainan Valley must be there," Han Li said with relief.

"But young master, we have never heard of a 'Tainan Valley' near Tainan Mountain. Could it be a mistake?" Sun Ergou warned, puzzled.

Han Li let out a "heh heh" laugh upon hearing this. "It's no mistake. That's the place!"

"You two are not cultivators, so naturally, you would not know of it. I suspect that's a place where some cultivators reside permanently," Han Li thought excitedly.

"Where exactly is this Tainan Mountain located?" After his initial excitement subsided, Han Li realized he still didn't know where the place was, so he asked casually.

"Young master, Tainan Mountain is at the southernmost part of Lan Province, forty li west of Guanggui City," Sun Ergou said respectfully.

"Southern Lan Province?" Han Li frowned. This was directly opposite to his target, the Poison Tyrant Fortress, which was to the north. It was a complete disconnect. It seemed he would have to make several trips.

"Sun Ergou, when we return, make Xi Tieniu the Vice Chief of the Siping Gang. I know you are not entirely happy about it, but since I made a promise to this man, I must keep my word," Han Li instructed Sun Ergou.

"I wouldn't dare. Whatever you say, I will do. I will not utter a single word of complaint!" Sun Ergou, startled, remembered the words Han Li had spoken to him at the beginning, and his face grew even paler.

"Relax. I know where your loyalty lies. This bottle contains the antidote pill. It can completely remove the poison from your body, putting your worries to rest. This is also something I promised you before. I treat everyone equally and have no intention of deceiving you." Han Li pulled out a bottle of pills and handed it to Sun Ergou.

Seeing this, Sun Ergou was overjoyed. The poison of the "Decayed Heart Pill" in his body had been causing him to be unable to eat or sleep well. Now that he could be completely rid of it, how could he not be excited?

"Thank you, young master! Thank you, young master! I will definitely serve you with unwavering loyalty!" Sun Ergou spoke with utmost sincerity as he accepted the bottle.

Han Li nodded indifferently.

The reason he had so readily given Sun Ergou the antidote was mainly that it was troublesome to have to give him a temporary antidote every month. Since he was about to leave Jiayuan City for a while, it was just easier to completely cure him. Of course, if the two of them showed any sign of betrayal in the future, Han Li would not hesitate to kill them both immediately and replace them with others.

Furthermore, since he had no immediate use for the Siping Gang, Han Li had decided to make it one of his escape routes among his "third burrow" contingency plans.

Han Li understood very well that there was no such thing as loyalty without reason, nor betrayal without cause. Using brute force to control someone was the most effective method but also the most inferior, as it could lead to backfire at any time. Therefore, to ensure long-term loyalty, it was best to use a combination of kindness and authority.

By giving Sun Ergou the antidote, he could significantly increase his loyalty for a long time. At the same time, it would establish an image of a man of his word who rewards merit and punishes mistakes in front of both Sun Ergou and Xi Tieniu. This was beneficial for Han Li's long-term control over the two.

These were Han Li's silent considerations.

After watching Sun Ergou swallow the antidote, Han Li suddenly said something that left both of them pleasantly surprised.

"I will leave Qu Hun here with you for the foreseeable future. But you are absolutely forbidden from using him to stir up trouble. Remember, while Qu Hun is very powerful, there are even more extraordinary people and beings in this world. Using him recklessly might actually bring a deadly calamity upon you. Do you understand?" Han Li said in a grave voice.

"Yes, young master, this one understands. I will definitely take good care of Lord Qu Hun. Please rest assured, young master." Sun Ergou nodded his head like a pecking chicken.

"Had I no further need of Qu Hun for close protection, and had he not been so conspicuous, making him inconvenient to accompany me on my journey, why would I have entrusted him to this man?" Han Li sighed, shaking his head imperceptibly, a touch of reluctance in his heart.

"You two should take care of yourselves. You may leave now. There is no need to come see me for the time being. I am going on a journey and don't know when I will return." Han Li gently waved his hand, dismissing the two.

Upon hearing this, Sun Ergou and Xi Tieniu bowed respectfully and backed out of the room, leaving only Han Li behind, lost in thought.

"I wonder what kind of cultivators I will find in this Tainan Valley? If I just show up, would it be unwise? Would I encounter any danger?" Han Li mused, his mind wandering to a distant, single-minded state, entering a realm of absolute absorption…

Time flew like a shuttle. In the blink of an eye, two months had passed. At this time, in Jiayuan City, Han Li was nowhere to be found. For a very, very long time to come, his figure would never appear there again…
