## Chapter Overview

New continent, new rules, and a depressingly familiar lesson: out in the deep sea, getting mauled by second-rank birds is a Tuesday. Our chapter opens with five bedraggled Green Spirit Sect cultivators—one Foundation Establishment, four Qi Condensation—trying to recover from a chase that has clearly been going on way too long. Just as they think they’ve caught a break, a pack of Eagle Kite Beasts shows up to prove them wrong. But this isn’t a simple rescue. It’s a carefully scripted test of information warfare, where the mysterious formation master uses a single question to expose a hidden weakness: the Beast Tide. And oh, just when you think the chapter is winding down, the silence hits the perfect dramatic note. The disciples are rescued, but they are also seen, assessed, and recorded.

## Key Plot Points

1. **Survival chase reaches its end**: The Green Spirit Sect party, drained and cornered, prepares to make a desperate, likely fatal stand against pursuing Eagle Kite Beasts. Their low mana and battle fatigue set a grim, realistic tone.
2. **Illusionary formation as a lifeline**: The party stumbles into an illusion formation, leading them to a hidden mountain range and a mysterious barrier. This reveals that someone (or something) has established a high-level base here.
3. **Puppet master’s surgical strike**: Before the beasts can strike, over a dozen pitch-black ape puppets emerge from the barrier and annihilate the Eagle Kite Beasts with precise energy beams. The display is efficient, silent, and utterly terrifying to the rescued cultivators.
4. **Polite wall of caution**: The formation master (Han Li, though he never names himself) invites the party inside. The Foundation Establishment leader, wary of unknown danger, politely refuses and goes on the back foot.
5. **The interrogation**: Han Li’s cold voice probes the leader, extracting key intel about the Green Spirit Sect’s weakness, its history of the Beast Tide, and the obvious lack of a senior escort. This query is the true core of the chapter.
6. **The bombshell question**: Han Li’s casual query, “Beast Tide? Which year?” hits the party like a physical blow. The chapter ends with their stunned silence, making it clear that this is a loaded term with deep, fresh wounds.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Beast Tide (兽潮)**: A classic xianxia plot device, the Beast Tide is not a gentle migration of fluffy critters. It’s a large-scale, catastrophic event where swarms of demon beasts erupt from their territories, overwhelming nearby human settlements, sects, and cultivation territories. The “tide” can be triggered by a shift in celestial patterns, a powerful new beast lord forcing weaker beasts out of their territory, or humanity’s own over-harvesting of spirit resources. In this chapter, it’s a known historical scar: the Green Spirit Sect lost its only Core Formation elder to one twenty years ago, leaving the sect permanently crippled. This also shows the ruthless ecology of the Mortal Stream: a single decade’s disaster can permanently demote a sect from “minor but stable” to “completely irrelevant.”

**Eagle Kite Beast (鹰鸢兽)**: Not just a random bird. “Yuan” (鸢) often refers to the black kite, a bird of prey known for its keen eyesight and aerial hunting. These beasts are second-rank, meaning they’re roughly equivalent to a late-stage Qi Condensation cultivator in raw power. But a coordinated flock of twelve? That’s a death sentence for a lone Foundation Establishment cultivator with a bunch of Qi Condensation juniors in tow. Their natural ability to see through illusions makes them especially threatening ambushers.

## Reading Guide

Let’s be real: this chapter is a masterclass in tension management. You’ve got a rescue that doesn’t feel like a rescue—it feels like an audit. The author uses the classic “strangers in a strange land” setup perfectly: the rescued cultivators are not handed a warm welcome; they’re given a cold, clinical assessment. The real fight isn’t the birds; it’s the conversation. And that ending? Chef’s kiss. The chapter cuts right on the question, leaving you to imagine the silent horror on the disciples’ faces. It’s a brilliant cliffhanger that doesn’t need a sword fight. The final line is a quiet, sharp sting: the Beast Tide is not just history; it is an open, unhealed wound.
