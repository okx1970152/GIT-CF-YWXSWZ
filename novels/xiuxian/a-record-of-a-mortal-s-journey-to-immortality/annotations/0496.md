## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up. Chapter 496 is a masterclass in high-stakes, high-speed reversal—the kind that makes you want to both cheer and groan for the poor soul on the losing end. The fight for the legendary Heaven-Mending Pill reaches its razor's edge, and we get a front-row seat to what happens when a Nascent Soul stage cultivator lets greed override surgical precision. Every elder present is a predator, but Brutal Beard? He's the apex apex-predator with a hidden trump card that turns a sure win into a total wipeout. Get ready for a lesson in why you never, ever count your spirit herbs before they hatch.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Move:** Wan Tianming, tired of being tangled, commits to an all-or-nothing play. He releases his Nascent Soul and uses a legendary **Celestial Net True Thunder** to trap Brutal Beard, while teleporting his own Nascent Soul to snatch the Heaven-Mending Pill.
- **The Counter:** Brutal Beard reveals he once killed a Thunder Whale and obtained its **Lightning-Absorbing Stone**. The thundercloud, which should have been an insurmountable cage, is completely neutralized. In a blink, Brilliant Beard has Wan Tianming’s defenseless physical body in his hands.
- **The Reversal:** Caught in a lose-lose, Wan Tianming is forced to toss the Heaven-Mending Pill back to his enemy to save his own body from destruction. The entire power dynamic flips in less than a breath.
- **The Chase Begins:** With the pill in hand, Brutal Beard turns and flees the Void Heaven Palace. Every other Nascent Soul elder—Heavenly Truth, Extreme Yin, the Carefree Hermit, and even the now-re-embodied Wan Tianming—immediately gives chase.
- **Parting Orders:** Before leaving, Extreme Yin issues a cold command to his subordinate Wu Chou, ordering him to guard Han Li and lending him the two Celestial Capital Demon Corpses. This leaves Han Li in a complex, controlled environment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Nascent Soul Liberation and Its Deadly Costs.** In xianxia, the Nascent Soul is a cultivator’s second life—a condensed spiritual core that can flee a destroyed body and survive, often seeking reincarnation or a new vessel. But releasing it from the body is not a casual power-up. It’s terrifying: you gain abilities like **Nascent Soul Sword Flight** (instant teleportation) and greater magical output. But you also become *incredibly* vulnerable. If your physical body is destroyed while the soul is out, or if a stronger cultivator devours your soul directly, you are gone for good. That’s why Wan Tianming’s gamble is so desperate—and why his defeat is so absolute.

**True Thunder and Lightning-Absorbing Stones.** “True Thunder” (真雷) is a peak-level lightning technique, often held as a clan’s ultimate, inherited trump card. It is treated as *the* answer to most magical defenses. But the novel introduces a clever counter: rare demon beasts like the Thunder Whale naturally produce **Lightning-Absorbing Stones** in their bodies. It’s a beautiful example of the Mortal Stream’s *uncaring system design*—no technique is invincible if your opponent has the specific, rare part from the correct monster. Victory is about who has the better intel and more obscure loot.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a tectonic shift in the arc’s power dynamics. The Heaven-Mending Pill is no longer a prize to be won; it is now a McGuffin in motion, carried by a fleeing tyrant. Pay close attention to how the elders break off their fights instantly when the calculus changes—there is no honor, no grudge-holding. The moment Brutal Beard has the pill, the entire board resets, and everyone’s only goal is to catch the runner. This also leaves our favorite dark-skinned, stone-faced protagonist, Han Li, in a precarious spot: alone with Wu Chou, under the theoretical ‘protection’ of two demon corpses, and watched by the ghost of an old acquaintance. The game is far from over.
