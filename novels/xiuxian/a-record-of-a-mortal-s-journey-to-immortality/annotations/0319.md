## Chapter Overview
Han Li pulls a sneaky all-nighter—not to party, but to plant a secret formation right under the Black Fiend Cult’s nose inside the imperial palace. He waltzes in, sets up his ace in the hole (the Reversed Five Elements Formation), and slips out without a soul noticing. Back at the Qin residence, he plays it cool, keeping his trump card to himself while the rest of the Yellow Maple Valley crew buzzes with pre-battle excitement. Then Chen Qiaoqian, the ice queen herself, suddenly asks him for a walk and starts digging into old secrets. The chapter is a masterclass in Han Li’s calculating caution and his awkward dance around a past that refuses to stay buried.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li flies to the imperial palace at night, ignoring the Seven Great Sects’ ban, and locates a forgotten bamboo grove as the site for his formation.
- He activates a sound-isolation barrier and spends two hours partially setting up the Reversed Five Elements Formation, creating a life-saving fallback for the upcoming battle.
- He returns undetected and keeps the formation a secret from his allies, reasoning that fewer people knowing equals greater security.
- The next morning, the mood among the Yellow Maple Valley disciples is eager but naïve; Han Li notes internally that most have never fought a Foundation Establishment cultivator and are dangerously overconfident.
- Chen Qiaoqian unexpectedly invites Han Li for a walk and confronts him about his performance in the Blood Forbidden Trial and about an incident seven or eight years ago—the time he rescued her.
- Han Li deflects with practiced lies, but his inner turmoil reveals he is troubled by her persistence and by his own unresolved feelings tied to her past relationship with Senior Brother Lu.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Reversed Five Elements Formation (颠倒五行阵)** — This is a classic xianxia defensive array that reverses the normal flow of the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) to create illusions, concealment, and spatial confusion. In orthodox Daoist alchemy, the Five Elements represent dynamic cycles of creation and destruction; reversing them disrupts the natural order, making the formation both powerful and unpredictable. Qi Yunxiao’s improved version is portable—a rare luxury that transforms Han Li from a mere participant into a strategist with a fortified position.

**Sound-Isolation Barrier (隔音结界)** — A basic but essential spell that seals off all sound within a designated area. In xianxia, it is the cultivator’s version of “Do Not Disturb,” used for secret rituals, sensitive conversations, or—as here—stealthy array placement. The fact that Han Li deploys one in an empty bamboo grove highlights his obsessive caution: even with no one around, he leaves nothing to chance.

**Playing the Pig to Eat the Tiger (扮猪吃老虎)** — A classic Chinese idiom and a beloved character archetype in xianxia. It describes someone who deliberately appears weak or foolish to lull opponents into underestimating them, then strikes when they least expect it. Chen Qiaoqian accuses Han Li of exactly this, and she’s not wrong—the whole novel so far has been Han Li hiding his true capabilities behind a mask of mediocrity.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a quiet but critical setup. On the surface, it’s a logistical scene: Han Li plants a formation and survives a social interrogation. But beneath that, it reveals two key character layers. First, Han Li’s worldview is shifting from pure survival mode to something like *operational planning*—he is building fallbacks, managing information (who gets to know what), and observing his allies with cold, evaluative eyes. He’s not just hiding; he’s preparing a stage where he controls the variables.

Second, the Chen Qiaoqian subplot finally resurfaces after hundreds of chapters. Her persistence proves that some debts of gratitude don’t fade with time, and Han Li’s awkwardness shows a rare crack in his otherwise ironclad pragmatism. He can lie to her face without blinking, but he can’t stop the old memory from stirring. For long-time readers, this is a welcome payoff—and a reminder that even Han Li’s heart has a locked drawer he refuses to open.

Get ready for the battle night. The formation is set. The secrets are half-unearthed. Now we wait for the storm.
