## Chapter Overview

Folks, buckle up—this chapter is a master class in “the Mortal Stream victory you didn’t see coming.” Profound Bone, the thousand-year-old ghost who has been plotting, scheming, and monologuing his undead heart out, finally gets what’s coming to him. But here’s the twist: Han Li doesn’t actually *land the killing blow*. The old ghost’s own greed and overconfidence in the Asura Holy Flame—a power he never truly mastered—becomes his undoing. The moment Profound Bone is turned to ash, the Dry Blue Ice Flame, now ownerless, suddenly reveals its *true* terrifying nature. Han Li goes from victorious to nearly flash-frozen in seconds, forced to make a desperate gamble with his Gold Devourer Beetles. But just when all seems lost, the ice flame spontaneously condenses into a single, fist-sized blue pearl—the *Dry Blue Pearl*—and the cold vanishes. Our boy grabs the prize, pockets a mysterious unburned rib from the ashes, and discovers that a certain emerald arrow has a mind of its own. This is peak *RMJI*: a victory that feels earned not because the hero overpowered the villain, but because the villain’s own fatal miscalculation handed the hero the win on a silver platter.

## Key Plot Points

1. **Profound Bone’s Overreach and Destruction:** The old ghost fuses his Dry Blue Ice Flame with the borrowed Asura Holy Flame. But the power backlashes (Magical Backlash) and consumes *him* instead of Han Li. A classic case of a killer trying to wield a weapon he can’t control.

2. **The Dry Blue Ice Flame’s True Power Unleashed:** With Profound Bone gone, the flame is no longer suppressed. It instantly freezes the entire high platform into a mountain of ice. Han Li’s Spirit Rhinoceros Pendant is pushed to its limit as his mana drains at a terrifying rate.

3. **Han Li’s Last-Ditch Calculation:** Faced with certain freezing, Han Li decides to sacrifice his entire Gold Devourer Beetle swarm—sending them to devour the ice—even if it means losing them all. It’s a classic Mortal Stream cost-benefit analysis: the beetles are irreplaceable, but a life is worth more.

4. **The Anomalous Condensation:** Just before Han Li releases the beetles, the Dry Blue Ice Flame spontaneously condenses into a single blue pearl (the Dry Blue Pearl), ending the crisis. The ice vanishes instantly.

5. **The Brave Acquisition:** Han Li, seeing a once-in-a-lifetime treasure, risks recovering the pearl. He uses the last of his Bane Evil Divine Lightning to bind and restrain it, then stores it in a jade box. He knows he has no idea how to use it yet, but abandoning a power of this caliber is simply not an option.

6. **Salvage and Discovery:** Han Li recovers the injured Blood Jade Spiders. He finds a single, unburned white rib from Profound Bone’s ashes—likely another sealed storage item. He also discovers the emerald green arrow, which has lost all spirit.

7. **The Arrow’s Sudden Flight:** When Han Li tests the arrow with the Crying Soul Beast (nostrils humming yellow light), the arrow, terrified of the beast’s ability, suddenly revives and flees toward the stone steps, revealing it had been playing dead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Magical Backlash (反噬, Fangshi):** This is a major mechanic in *RMJI* and xianxia in general. Forbidden or unstable techniques—especially those involving borrowed or unrefined power—have a tendency to turn on the caster. Profound Bone didn’t die because Han Li overpowered him; he died because the Asura Holy Flame, which he had not fully mastered, “backfired” and devoured him. This is a core Mortal Stream principle: power without mastery is a death sentence.

- **Dry Blue Ice Flame vs. Asura Holy Flame:** These are not just random magical glows. In the *RMJI* universe, high-tier flames like these are quasi-sentient forces of nature. The Dry Blue Ice Flame is a primordial Yin-cold flame; the Asura Holy Flame is a destructive, purifying flame. Fusing them is an act of extreme arrogance—one that requires absolute, perfect control. Profound Bone, despite his millennia of experience, overestimated himself in his greed for a quick kill.

- **The Crying Soul Beast and the Emerald Arrow:** The Crying Soul Beast (啼魂兽)—a rare “soul-crying beast” obtained from Yuan Yao—has a signature ability: it can inhale and consume souls and ghostly entities. The emerald arrow’s *terror* of the beast’s yellow mist strongly suggests that the arrow houses a bound soul or tool spirit (器灵). The arrow going limp and then suddenly fleeing when threatened is a telltale sign of a sentient or semi-sentient artifact trying to avoid destruction.

- **The Unburned Rib:** Profound Bone was a thousand-year-old ghost who had long sacrificed his physical body. Yet a single rib survived the Asura Holy Flame. This follows a classic xianxia trope: high-level beings often store their most valuable secrets (maps, manuals, treasures) *inside* their own skeleton, using their own body as a natural vault. This rib is almost certainly a “safe” containing something important.

### Magical Backlash (反噬)

A common xianxia concept where a forbidden or unstable technique, or a borrowed power that the user cannot control, turns on the caster with lethal consequences.

### Tool Spirit (器灵)

A one-time bound spirit sealed into a magical treasure to boost its power. The seal can only be attempted once in the treasure's lifespan, and the spirit can show signs of independent will or fear.

## Reading Guide

- **Theme of the Chapter: “Let the Villain Defeat Himself”:** Pay very close attention to how Han Li’s victory is structured. He doesn’t unleash a powerful new technique; he simply survives long enough for Profound Bone’s own greed and poor judgment to erase him. This is the essence of the Mortal Stream’s *frugal victory* philosophy.

- **Han Li’s Psychological Compass:** Notice his immediate shift from near-panic (about to sacrifice the beetles) to cold analysis (binding the pearl). Even when he’s excited about the treasure, he doesn’t drool or make reckless decisions—he carefully tests its safety with the last of his divine lightning. This is Han Li: always calculating, always hedging his bets.

- **A New Layer of Adventure:** The spontaneous flight of the emerald arrow at the end is a classic serialization hook. It opens a new mini-mystery right when the main conflict is resolved, reminding the reader that the world is full of hidden threats that don’t care about the heroes’ victory celebrations.

- **Power Escalation:** This chapter sets a new ceiling for “dangerous flames” in the world. The Dry Blue Ice Flame, even when ownerless, was almost powerful enough to kill Han Li in seconds. The Asura Holy Flame, which the old ghost failed to control, is implied to be even *more* terrifying. This recalibrates the reader’s expectations for the kind of power that exists in the Spirit Realm and beyond.
