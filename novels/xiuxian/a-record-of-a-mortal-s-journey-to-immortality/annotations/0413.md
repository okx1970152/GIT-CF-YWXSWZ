## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a perfect entry point into the unique culture of Chinese web novel serialization. Unlike Western novels, which are typically released as complete books, xianxia and other genres are published chapter by chapter online, often daily. This creates a direct, almost conversational relationship between author and reader. When life gets in the way—a family event, illness, or travel—authors post "leave requests" (请假条 / qingjia tiao) like this one, a time-honored tradition in the community. It's a sign of respect: the author doesn't want loyal readers waiting in vain, so they communicate transparently. It's also a reminder that behind every epic tale is a real person. The tone here is casual and penitent, using the familiar particle "哦" (o) to soften the announcement and convey genuine regret. Western readers might find it strange that a schedule change merits its own chapter, but in the serialization culture of Chinese web novels, this small act of courtesy is part of the unwritten contract between storyteller and audience. It's not a filler; it's a social ritual.
