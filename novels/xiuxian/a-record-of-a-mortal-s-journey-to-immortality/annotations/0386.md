## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with a pre-dawn gathering. Feng Sanniang leads the assembled cultivators toward a designated sea zone, signaling that the hunt is about to begin. A parallel scene unfolds a thousand li away: two streaks of light—one gold, one yellow—are fleeing across the ocean, pursued by a massive patch of white mist that emits the eerie sound of a crying infant. The pursuer is revealed to be the Koi Infant Beast, and its terrifying speed and ability to generate Water Element Thunder force the two elders to accelerate their escape. Meanwhile, on the island, the six formation-controlling cultivators take their positions, banners in hand, creating a semicircular pocket formation. Han Li observes from behind Qu Hun, and the tense atmosphere reaches its peak as the fleeing lights appear on the horizon. The chapter ends with the Koi Infant Beast's arrival imminent—a perfect cliffhanger before the storm breaks.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Hunt Begins**: Feng Sanniang leads the team toward the ambush site at dawn, marking the start of the operation's execution phase.
2. **Parallel Pursuit**: A golden and a yellow light are pursued across the sea by white mist, setting up the Koi Infant Beast's arrival. The pursuer's infant-like cries create a deeply unsettling atmosphere, reinforcing the beast's unnatural nature.
3. **The Assault's Momentum**: The beast is already in pursuit, meaning the formation must be prepared to receive a charging enemy, not a stationary target—raising the stakes for timing and precision.
4. **Formation Deployment**: Six late-Foundation cultivators, each with a blue banner, form a semicircular "pocket" over the sea, ready to trap the beast the moment it enters the kill zone.
5. **Final Warning**: Feng Sanniang receives a flying sword message and orders the team to seal the formation gate immediately upon the beast's entry. The countdown has begun.
6. **Vision of the Beast**: Han Li and the others see the golden and yellow lights racing toward them, with only white mist behind—the true form of the Koi Infant Beast remains hidden, preserving its mystery for the battle ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

***Hidden Threats, Structured Ambushes, and the War of Information in the Mortal Stream***

This chapter demonstrates a classic Mortal Stream dynamic: the hunt for a grade-6 Koi Infant Beast is not a straightforward creature encounter but a layered tactical operation involving fleeing elders, a baited trap, and an approaching enemy that arrives already mid-pursuit. The beast is not stationary; it is charging directly at the formation, meaning the ambush must be executed with perfect timing or risk being overrun. The infant-like cries and thunderous environment serve as psychological warfare even before combat begins, amplifying stress and creating an oppressive pre-battle atmosphere.

The Korean tale also leans heavily on the "unseen threat" trope: all readers and characters see is white mist and hear eerie cries. The actual form of the beast is withheld, preserving horror and uncertainty until the climax. This fits the Mortal Stream's principle that visible danger is often less frightening than the unknown—and that a cautious protagonist like Han Li, who feels a nagging unease he cannot shake, is usually right to listen to that instinct.

**The Inescapable Logic of the "Pocket Formation"**  
The semicircular formation deployed by the six cultivators is a textbook battlefield maneuver in the cultivation world: create a "pocket" with an open end, lure the enemy into it, then seal the opening. The strategy itself sounds straightforward, but the execution hinges on split-second timing and the ability to maintain formation integrity under pressure from a charging beast of superior speed. This is not a duel of honor but a hunting expedition designed for maximum asymmetry in favor of the hunters.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is the calm before the storm—and it does an excellent job of building tension without descending into overwrought drama. The Koi Infant Beast is already in pursuit, the formation is set, and the elders are racing toward the trap. Han Li's uneasy feeling, which he tries to dismiss as paranoia, adds a layer of psychological weight that rewards readers who know how often his instincts have saved him before. The scene is deliberately paced, giving readers time to absorb the stakes without rushing. Pay close attention to the way the beast's approach is described: the sound of thunder, the infant's cry, the white mist—these are sensory cues designed to unsettle without revealing. The real battle is about to begin, and everything hinges on whether the formation holds.

## Deep Dive: The Logic of Unease in Mortal Stream Combat

Han Li's unease in this chapter is not generic paranoia; it is a survival instinct refined by years of life-or-death situations. The Mortal Stream does not reward protagonists who ignore their gut feelings—and Han Li's decision to set up an additional defense array (the "碧水青甲阵") around his own position is a direct result of that unease. This is not about bravery or premonition; it is about layered risk management. Even when the tactical calculus suggests victory is certain (two Core Formation cultivators plus a high-grade formation against a single grade-6 beast), Han Li prepares for the scenario where the plan fails. This is the core of his survival doctrine: trust the plan, but always have a backup plan for when the plan breaks.
