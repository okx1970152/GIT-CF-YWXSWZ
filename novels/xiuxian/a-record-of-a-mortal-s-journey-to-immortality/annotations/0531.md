## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists—Han Li has officially entered his offshore solo-farming arc, and trust me, it is every bit as methodical, cold, and satisfying as you'd expect. After years of cultivation within the safety of his Mist Sea cave, the man has finally set sail into the deep wilds to begin his systematic hunt for high-grade demon beast inner cores. This chapter is pure "quiet phase" Mortal Stream gameplay: setting up traps, waiting with inhuman patience, and executing flawless single-target takedowns. But what makes it shine is the delicate, high-stakes economy of the Rainbow Garment Grass lure system—one wrong calculation, and our favorite paranoid cultivator could be staring down a beast that outranks him. If you love watching Han Li treat the open ocean like a carefully managed personal hunting ground, this one is for you.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li finishes cultivating the Soul-Nourishing Wood into a necklace of carved beads for passive spirit protection; he departs his cave alone three days later, avoiding the Deep Abyss entirely due to its unpredictable demon beast concentration and crowded cultivator presence.
- He spends a month traveling westward, deliberately ignoring coral islands closer to his cave to avoid complications, finally stopping at a stretch of fiery-red coral reef suitable for transplanting the Rainbow Garment Grass.
- Han Li sets up four layered spell formations around the reef and then carefully plants the lure grass in the center, calculating that it will take nearly a month of Green Liquid stimulation to reach the four-hundred-year maturity needed to attract grade-six beasts.
- During the waiting period, three or four grade-five demon beasts are drawn by the early-stage grass; Han Li dispatches them effortlessly with multiple Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords, noting that while their inner cores are useless for his alchemy, they still fetch a high price in spirit stones.
- On the third day, a grade-six giant cyan shrimp emerges from a whirlpool in a cloud of black Qi; Han Li holds his position until the beast commits to charging the lure, then activates his yellow barrier formation to briefly trap it.
- In a rapid sequence, Han Li binds the shrimp with the Five Elements Ring, then finishes it with two crossed azure sword strikes; afterward, he extracts the beast's soul with his Soul-Gathering Bowl and stores the translucent inner core, also severing the pincers as valuable materials.
- The chapter ends with Han Li cooling his heels by the lure grass, confident that the same waters must hold more grade-six beasts, fully settled into his patient predator routine.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The translucent quality of the grade-six inner core marks a subtle but important milestone in the cultivation world's resource tier system. Inner cores (neidan 内丹) borrow from real Daoist internal alchemy—where the term originally describes the condensed spiritual essence formed inside a practitioner's own body through meditative refinement. Xianxia literalizes this into an actual, harvestable physical object from demon beasts. The shift from a solid, opaque grade-five core to a semi-translucent, glowing grade-six core signals that the beast's life-force has concentrated to a higher density, making it far more valuable for alchemy and crafting. This visual gradation—translucency equals higher quality—is a recurring trope in cultivation fiction, allowing readers to instantly gauge a treasure's worth at a glance.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a textbook demonstration of why Mortal Stream fans love the "quiet victory" phase just as much as the big boss fights. There is no desperate last stand here, no cornered gambit—just Han Li running his cold, efficient algorithm of lure, trap, bind, kill, harvest, repeat. The real tension isn't in the fight itself (which is over in seconds) but in the setup: calculating the exact growth stage of the Rainbow Garment Grass, betting that no higher-grade beast shows up early, and trusting the formation's confinement time. For readers new to the offshore arcs, take note: this is how Han Li builds his wealth between the major dungeons and sect wars. The inner cores, materials, and silent accumulation of power here are what fund his leaps to higher cultivation later. So sit back and enjoy the methodical massacre—it's therapeutic in its own cold way.
