## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in applied psychological warfare and the cold, bureaucratic horror of the cultivation world. Han Li, having swiftly neutralized the Five Friends of Mengshan’s physical threat, now faces a more delicate target: the institutional curse binding them. The chapter wastes no time on dramatic displays of power; instead, it pivots into a tense interrogation where the real conflict is not between the cultivators and their captor, but among the captives themselves. A devastating schism erupts within the Five Friends, revealing the raw, selfish calculus that lurks beneath their sworn brotherhood. Han Li observes this implosion with chilling detachment, only stepping in to reassert control and offer a terrifyingly clinical analysis of their predicament. The “Blood Curse” is not a mystical binding contract but a simple, vicious piece of magical engineering—a Verbal Curse—and the cure is a high-stakes gamble that turns their survival into a brutal test of nerve.

## Key Plot Points
*   The second member of the Five Friends of Mengshan openly taunts Han Li, shocking his companions and appearing to seek his own death.
*   The fourth member realizes the second is trying to sacrifice the group to protect their third sister (his Dao partner), who is still in the enemy’s clutches.
*   A bitter argument erupts over the morality of sacrificing three lives for one, revealing the second’s desperate motivation: his partner is pregnant with his child.
*   Han Li coldly interrupts their internal drama, asserting his absolute control and demanding information about their employer. He makes it clear they have no right to choose their own deaths.
*   The black-faced elder, resigned, reveals they are under a “Blood Curse” restriction that prevents them from divulging key secrets.
*   Han Li examines the elder and identifies the curse not as blood magic, but a simpler, more elegant “Verbal Curse” based on a triggering incantation.
*   He presents two solutions: the safe method of obtaining the dispelling incantation, or the high-risk method of forcibly erasing the curse with his own divine sense, a gamble that could kill them instantly if he fails.
*   The second brother volunteers his life for the dangerous procedure, offering himself as a test subject for the sake of his Dao partner and unborn child.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter offers a fascinating deconstruction of mystical curses in the xianxia genre. The “Blood Curse” (血咒) is initially presented as a dark, visceral ritual involving blood, strange symbols, and an immediate, horrifying death for those who transgress. This plays on common folk beliefs in sympathetic magic, where a part of the person (blood, hair, name) can be used to affect the whole. However, Han Li quickly peels back this layer of mystique, revealing it to be a “Verbal Curse” (言咒). This is a far more rational and pragmatic explanation for the genre. The power isn’t in the blood or the symbol, which are just props to inspire terror and obedience. The real mechanism is a simple trigger-and-response spell imprinted on the victim’s consciousness. This demystification is a core trait of the “Mortal Stream” (凡人流) philosophy: high-level practitioners don’t rely on vague magic; they use precise, often mundane, mechanisms to achieve their ends. The horror isn’t in the supernatural, but in the cold, systematic nature of the control.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for some serious high-tension negotiation tactics, fellow cultivators! This chapter is a perfect example of how Han Li doesn’t just win by being stronger—he wins by being more ruthless in the social arena. Watch how he lets the Five Friends tear each other apart before he even says a word. He’s not trying to be cruel; he’s trying to maximize his information gain. The real treat here is the lore drop on the Blood Curse. The narrative sets it up as this terrifying, unbreakable shackle, only for Han Li to casually dismantle its mystique with a book he read once. This is the power of knowledge over brute force. The choice he gives them—certain exposure from him *or* a gamble on the curse killing them—is a classic Mortal Stream dilemma. It’s not about finding the perfect escape; it’s about choosing the least terrible option. The second brother’s desperate gamble, born from love and a desire for legacy, adds a rare, fragile touch of humanity to this otherwise cold transaction, making the stakes feel profoundly personal.

## In-Depth Analysis: The Mechanics of a Verbal Curse
The chapter’s technical explanation of the Blood Curse perfectly illustrates a key worldbuilding principle in *RMJI*: magic has rules, and those rules can be understood and exploited. A Verbal Curse (言咒) is essentially a targeted psychological and spiritual trigger. It’s a specific command that, when spoken or even thought, activates a killing matrix embedded within the victim’s own soul. This is far more sophisticated than a simple poison or a knife to the heart, and far more difficult to create and maintain. Han Li’s two solutions highlight the dual nature of such a problem. The first method—a counter-incantation—is the “key and lock” approach, a pure expression of institutional knowledge. The second method—forcible erasure by a stronger divine sense—is a brute-force hack, a testament to individual power and spiritual strength. This dichotomy is a recurring theme in the novel: problems can be solved either through superior intelligence and knowledge, or through overwhelming force. The fact that Han Li is offering both options shows his confidence, but the risk inherent in the second underscores the brutal, high-stakes nature of this world where even a solution can be a fatal gamble.
