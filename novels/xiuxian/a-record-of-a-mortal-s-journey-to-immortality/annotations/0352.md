## Chapter Overview

This isn’t a story chapter—it’s the author stepping out from behind the curtain, bleary-eyed and feverish, to issue a correction. After several nights of crunch, Wang Yu’s body gave out, and his fever-addled brain accidentally dropped a thread regarding Qu Hun. By the time he realized the oversight, the chapter was already live. This brief note serves as both an apology and a patch note: go back, re-read the previous chapter, and the missing piece should now be there. It’s a raw, human moment that reminds us just how brutal the serialization grind can be.

## Key Plot Points

This is a meta-note, not a narrative chapter, so there are no traditional plot points. Instead, the key takeaway is:
1. **Author Transparency:** The author openly admits to a physical and mental lapse (fever, exhaustion) that caused an error in the previous chapter.
2. **Retroactive Fix:** The error—a missing clarification about Qu Hun—has been corrected in the already-published previous chapter.
3. **Reader Trust:** The note functions as a signal of care: the author values continuity enough to interrupt the current release to ensure readers have the correct information.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Author’s Leave Note” (请假条) Tradition:** In the world of Chinese web serialization, this is known as a *qingjia tiao*—a formal leave request or correction notice. These notes are a beloved (and sometimes dreaded) part of serialization culture. They acknowledge that behind every chapter is a human being who sometimes gets sick, miscalculates, or forgets a detail. Far from damaging trust, a well-timed, honest note like this actually *strengthens* the bond between author and reader. It says, “I know you’re paying attention, and I care enough to fix it.”

**Why “Qu Hun” Matters:** Qu Hun is the soulless giant corpse-servant created from Zhang Tie’s body. At this stage of the story, Han Li is navigating a complex, high-stakes conflict in the Blood Forbidden Trial zone where every asset—especially an invulnerable, mindless enforcer—must be carefully accounted for. Forgetting to address Qu Hun’s status in a battle-heavy chapter would create a glaring hole in the reader’s tactical picture, which is exactly why the author rushed to correct it.

### Author’s Leave Note (请假条)

A formal notice issued by a web novel author to explain a delay, correction, or scheduling issue; it is a standard part of the serialization culture in China.
