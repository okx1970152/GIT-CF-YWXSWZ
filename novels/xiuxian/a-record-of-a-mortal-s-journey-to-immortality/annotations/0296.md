## Chapter Overview

After the nerve-fraying social tribulation of the Tianque Assembly, Han Li is back in full control, and this chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s second most important combat skill: psychological domination. The old man and his granddaughter—who just pulled a fast one on a Qi Condensation cultivator—find themselves completely outmatched against Han Li’s Foundation Establishment presence. Han Li doesn’t just overpower them; he systematically dismantles their confidence, pins them down with a tracking technique, and calmly forces them into a deal they can’t refuse. This is the quiet violence of a predator who has already won before the negotiation begins.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li intimidates the grandfather-granddaughter pair by releasing a controlled burst of Foundation Establishment-level spiritual pressure, forcing the girl back several steps and shattering the old man’s confidence.
- The old man immediately realizes that Han Li is not a naive cultivator but a sharp-minded counter-player, and he mentally revises his survival estimate downward.
- Han Li reveals the purpose of their meeting: he wants the concealment technique that allowed them to pass as mortals in the main hall.
- The old man confesses the technique comes from a nameless, incomplete Daoist scripture that his granddaughter found in a mountain cave—and he offers to retrieve it.
- Han Li plants a tracking mark on both of them, ensuring he can locate them regardless of any attempt to flee, and tells them to return home to wait.
- As the pair leave, the young girl shows clear envy and fascination with Han Li’s power, a detail that the old man misses but Han Li observes from hiding.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Tracking Mark (追踪标记):** A low-grade spell that plants a strand of the caster's spiritual sense into a target, allowing them to be tracked over long distances. In the Mortal Stream, this is not a friendly GPS; it is an unbreakable leash that converts a "voluntary" negotiation into an involuntary transaction. The victim knows they are being tracked, which is the entire point—it creates psychological submission before the deal even begins.

The **Social Tribulation (社会试炼)** is in full effect here. Unlike a physical duel, this conflict is fought entirely through conversational speed, tonal control, and the psychology of commitment. Han Li doesn't draw a weapon; he draws a mental line that his opponent is already too afraid to cross. This is the Mortal Stream's highest form of combat for a Foundation Establishment cultivator who can't afford to waste mana on every trivial confrontation.

### Social Tribulation

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

If you came here for flashy sword beams and exploding talismans, this chapter might feel quiet. But do not mistake stillness for absence of action. This is Han Li at his most terrifying: he has already won the negotiation before the old man even understands what is being traded. The pressure release, the calm questioning, the naming of the technique, the instant deployment of a tracking mark—every single move is a calculated blow in a social battle that lasts less than a quarter of an hour. Also, watch that girl. Her fascination with Han Li’s exit technique is a seed that may or may not grow, but in the Mortal Stream, even a glance can be a contract.
