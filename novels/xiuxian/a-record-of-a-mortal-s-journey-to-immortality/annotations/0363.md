## Chapter Overview

Our boy Han Li has finally surfaced from the bottleneck grindstone and taken a good, hard look at the map. And the picture? It’s grim. The Yue Kingdom cultivation world isn’t just chaotic—it’s a collapsing crab-basket where demonic and righteous paths are feasting on the corpse. Han Li runs through his escape options with the cold precision of a survival algorithm: north leads to the utterly uncrossable Boundless Sea, east and west are enemy territory, and south? South means brushing up against the endless, blood-soaked war between the Nine Nations Alliance and the terrifying Moulan Tribe’s “Dharma Warriors.” Our man is trapped between a rock and a hard place, and neither choice is good. It’s the perfect setup for a desperate gambit: maybe, just maybe, it’s time to dust off that mysterious ancient teleportation array.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li’s information-gathering tour across the mountain peak reveals that Yue has descended into a chaotic, three-way battleground between the demonic path, the Righteous Alliance, and the collapsing local sects. Staying is not an option.
- He systematically eliminates escape routes: flying east or west leads straight into the demonic and righteous headquarters; going north runs into the Boundless Sea, a body of water so vast and lethal that even a Nascent Soul cultivator once failed to cross it after a year of flight.
- The only remaining direction is south, but that path leads to the Nine Nations Alliance, a zone locked in a grinding, generations-old war with the Moulan Tribe—ferocious nomadic cultivators called “Dharma Warriors” who wield a unique, devastating school of “Spiritual Arts.”
- The history of the Moulan conflict is revealed: resource scarcity drove them to invade the richer nine nations, leading to a brutal war that eventually forced the nine to band together. Even now, it’s a never-ending cycle of bloodshed.
- Han Li realizes the six fleeing sects will likely join the Nine Nations Alliance, making any southern relocation a choice between re-joining them as cannon fodder or facing the war zone alone.
- Cornered by geography and politics, his mind circles back to the same unlikely solution: repair the ancient portal beneath the earth. It’s a gamble, but one he can test and potentially reverse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Dharma Warriors and “Spiritual Art”: A Xianxia Schism**: The Moulan Tribe’s “Dharma Warriors” (法士 / Fa Shi) represent a fascinating schism in the standard xianxia paradigm. While normal cultivators obsess over rare magic tools, flying swords, and powerful talismans, the Moulans poured all their talent into mastering the raw elemental Dao techniques (道术 / Dao Shu). They then hybridized this with their own shamanistic prairie traditions to create “Spiritual Arts” (灵术 / Ling Shu). This isn’t just flavor text; it’s a structural choice. In resource-poor environments, hording external tools is less efficient than perfecting your internal engine. The Dharma Warriors are a walking reminder that the cultivation world isn’t a single unified system—isolated groups evolve completely different, yet equally terrifying, paths to power based on their environment’s brutal limitations.

**The Boundless Sea & The Ends of the Earth**: The Boundless Sea is a classic xianxia trope: a geographic hard wall. You see this all the time—a sea, desert, or mountain range that is simply *too much*. The legend of a Nascent Soul elder failing to find its end after years of flight isn’t just world-building; it’s a subtle power statement. It reminds us that even at the apex of the human realm, cultivators are still tiny fleas on the back of a truly enormous world. The vastness of the world creates a sense of genuine risk and scale that a teleportation-based plot can then subvert.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for some serious geopolitics, folks! This chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s signature move: the strategic map-phase. No flashy fights, no secret treasures—just pure, cold-blooded survival calculus. Han Li isn’t reacting to a monster; he’s reacting to the *map itself*. Watch as he systematically eliminates the entire accessible continent as a viable retreat route. The pressure is building not through an immediate threat, but through the slow, crushing weight of a chessboard with no safe squares left. This is where the “chicken rib” of the ancient teleportation array starts to look a whole lot more appealing. Han Li is cornered by logistics, and in this world, that’s the most dangerous corner of all. The stage is being set for one of his classic desperate gambles—and we all know how those tend to pay off.
