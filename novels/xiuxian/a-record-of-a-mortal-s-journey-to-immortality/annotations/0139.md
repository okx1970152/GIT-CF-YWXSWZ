## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in the sheer, grinding reality of the low-level cultivator's world. Forget epic duels and godlike powers. Han Li, now at the ninth layer of Qi Condensation, is just one face in a crowd of thousands, struggling to master spells that are, in his own words, "chicken ribs." The Great Southern Assembly is in its final, frantic days, a perfect storm of desperation and information. For Han Li, the goal isn't glory or treasure. It's education. He wanders from stall to stall, not buying, but *listening*—absorbing every scrap of knowledge about demon beasts, materials, and magical tools like a starving student. But beneath this calm surface of market observation, the chapter builds to a single, explosive point of drama: the public appearance of a **Magical Treasure Fragment**. The ensuing argument is a perfect, compressed lesson in cultivation-world economics, showing how desire meets cold, hard utility in a world where nothing is given and everything is a transaction.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Supporting Spell Arsenal**: Han Li successfully masters two new supplementary spells, the Hiding Technique and Voice Transmission Technique, which are standard-issue tools for any cultivator.
- **Learning the Localized Arts**: After his mana doubles, Han Li finally gets a handle on the Quicksand and Freezing Techniques, though their range is still limited. His frustration then excitement perfectly frames his progress.
- **The Final Trade Frenzy**: The Great Southern Assembly reaches its peak, with thousands of cultivators and high-level experts coming out of the woodwork to observe the competition.
- **Market as University**: Confirming his role as an information gatherer, Han Li forgoes buying to just walk the market, absorbing critical knowledge about materials like Silver-Winged Ant eggs and Iron-Tailed Snake scales.
- **The Argument at the Stall**: The chapter's core drama unfolds: a tenth-layer master, the 'Dark Han,' tries to trade a small, glowing, invisible-making cloth (claimed to be a **Magical Treasure Fragment**) for a special alms bowl.
- **The Power of the Demonstration**: The Dark Han proves the cloth's worth by enveloping a live demon beast and a glowing dagger, making them vanish completely, even from a Celestial Eye Technique scan.
- **The Brutal Rejection**: The stall owner, a member of the Ye Clan, is unimpressed. He argues the cloth is too small for practical use, comparing it unfavorably to a common Invisibility Talisman, and demands spirit stones instead. The chapter ends on this standoff.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter presents a perfect case study in the "Ascension Assembly Bottleneck Economy." Notice that the crowd is filled with cultivators stuck at bottlenecks—the tenth-layer master trying to trade, the Ye Clan disciple guarding his wares. Those with true growth potential avoid these public bazaars where ripples of information travel fast. The result is a market of frustration: high-quality items (like the Magical Treasure fragment) are dangled in front of people who lack the resources or connections to use them. The fragment’s power—perfect, undetectable invisibility—is wasted on a cultivator at this stage, just as the stall owner points out.

This also reinforces the philosophy of **鸡肋**: "chicken rib." The fragment is tempting to eat, but a waste to discard because you have no way to cook it. It's a core feeling of the Mortal Stream: the world is filled with useless treasures and priceless garbage. Every cultivator is an economist, not a hero.

The Hiding Technique's vulnerability to the Celestial Eye Technique is another classic worldbuilding detail. In Xianxia, the technology is always in an arms race: a spell is created, a counter-spell immediately exists. There is no ultimate trump card, only the next tier of power. It’s a system designed to keep you perpetually anxious and perpetually clawing for the next level.

The appearance of a first-tier demon beast (the Metal-Eating Mouse) as a mere pet for a tenth-layer master is also a subtle reminder of the power gap. For a ninth-layer Han Li, this little creature is a source of awe and an item of discussion. For the master, it’s just a way to prove a point about his magical cloth.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to Han Li’s mindset here. He's not hunting for treasures. He's *studying the syllabus*. He treats the market like a library, absorbing information about materials, tool properties, and prices. This is the core of his character—he prepares, he observes, he learns the rules before he even thinks about playing the game. His final position at the stall is a perfect example: he didn't run to the commotion; he simply followed the path of maximum information density.

The chapter sets up a crucial dynamic for the future. The Magical Treasure fragment is a **dead asset** for all the players in this scene. None of them have the ability to use it properly, but its very presence causes conflict and reveals information. This is a classic power struggle seed: no one can use the treasure, but everyone wants to keep it from others. The stall owner’s blunt refusal is also a refreshing dose of realism—in this world, an item's value is based on its *utility*, not its rarity.
