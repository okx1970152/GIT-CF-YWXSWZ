## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is a full-throttle, pedal-to-the-metal chase sequence that puts the 'mortal' back in mortal cultivation. Our boy Han Li is caught between a rock and a hard place—or more accurately, between a slick-talking, mind-controlling Nascent Soul old monster (妙鹤真人) and a pissed-off, ninth-grade bird-demon with a serious grudge (Feng Xi). The chapter opens with Han Li already suffering the effects of the Wind Spirit Restriction, a nasty little leash planted by Feng Xi, and he's racing against the clock to reach a small island where a teleportation formation awaits. But just as he thinks he's safe, the sky tears open with a terrifying screech, and Feng Xi shows up in his true beast form, moving at speeds that defy logic. It's a desperate three-way race: Han Li trying to teleport, the demon trying to kill him, and the old Daoist trying to steal the treasure. The tension is thick enough to cut with a flying sword.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li is in agony, struggling to suppress the Wind Spirit Restriction planted by Feng Xi. He realizes that when Feng Xi first cast it, the demon was weakened, which is why Han Li managed to escape earlier.
- He forces himself to fly at full speed, covering a two-day journey in half a day to reach the island with the teleportation formation.
- Behind him,妙鹤真人 is pursuing him relentlessly, using a powerful, seductive mind-control technique to try and stop him. Han Li barely resists it thanks to his Great Derivative Art.
- Just as Han Li is about to land on the island, a piercing screech from Feng Xi's true form—a horrifying bird-fish hybrid—cuts through the sky. The demon is here to collect his Wind Lightning Wings and his revenge.
- Facing imminent death, Han Li uses his own Wind Lightning Wings, but even they might not be enough to outrun Feng Xi in his natural form.
-妙鹤, seeing Feng Xi's true form and realizing he's a ninth-grade demon beast, is terrified. But his greed for the Void Heaven Cauldron overrides his fear; he even uses a secret technique involving his own essence blood to boost his speed.
- Han Li reaches the plaza just in time, shouting at the Sublime Sound Sect disciples, who confirm the teleportation formation is complete, though untested.
- In a final desperate act, he charges into the formation as Feng Xi literally crashes through the ceiling above him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a masterclass in the 'Mortal Stream' survival doctrine. Let's break down the cold, hard logic:
- **The Wind Spirit Restriction (风灵劲):** This is not just a poison or a curse. It's an 'energy lock' planted by a superior being, acting as both a leash and a kill switch. It perfectly illustrates the cultivation world's systemic cruelty: the strong can cripple the weak with a single technique, turning them into a passive resource. Han Li's response is pure Mortal Stream: he feels fear, but immediately transitions into cold, analytical calculation to find a way out.
- **The Greed vs. Fear Calculus:**妙鹤 is a textbook example of a Nascent Soul elder who is usually too cautious for his own good. Yet, the Void Heaven Cauldron is such a legendary prize that it overrides his survival instincts. This is a classic xianxia trope—a treasure so valuable it makes even the most rational old monsters act like desperate fools. The narrative highlights this by showing his terror at the ninth-grade beast, but his inability to turn away.
- **Demon Beast True Form (本体):** High-level demon beasts often prefer to fight in human form. However, when they are *really* serious—like when they want to kill you and take back their stolen treasure—they revert to their true form. This allows them to use their innate天赋 abilities at maximum capacity. For a speed-type beast like the Gale Wind Beast, this is a terrifying final form, shifting from 'very fast' to 'teleportation-level fast.'

## Reading Guide
This is one of those 'edge-of-your-seat' chapters that rewards attention to detail. Pay close attention to the power dynamics. Han Li is utterly outclassed by both pursuers, yet he survives through a combination of preparation (the teleportation formation) and ruthless decision-making (charging in, untested). Notice also how the storyteller maintains a clinical, almost documentary tone even during the most frantic moments. When Han Li's face turns pale, it's a rare, contained emotional crack that is immediately sealed as he reaches his goal. The joy of escape is never shown; only the cold acceptance of the next set of dangers.

Also, pay attention to妙鹤's desperation. His 'sincere' voice is a weapon, and his decision to burn his own essence blood—a permanent, life-shortening cost—shows just how high the stakes are. This chapter perfectly sets the stage for a showdown inside the underground chambers, where all three parties will collide. The teleportation formation is complete, but 'untested' is a terrifying word in the cultivation world. It could send Han Li to freedom, shred him into pieces, or dump him right into another trap.
