## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists—the pressure cooker is about to whistle. Two Nascent Soul old foxes play a game of chicken over a cosmic secret, a fake Bug Demon goes on a murder spree to muddy the waters, and our favorite paranoid farmer finally settles back into his cave for some long-overdue potion brewing. This chapter is a classic Mortal Stream breather—no high-octane duels, just the cold, quiet sound of the world closing in from all sides, and Han Li calmly ignoring it all to tinker with his alchemy furnace. Foreshadowing has never felt so cozy.

## Key Plot Points

Patriarch Extreme Yin is having a bad two years. He’s been camped out on Qiyuan Island, neglecting his own sect and the demonic alliance war, trying to find Han Li. The Qi-surnamed Taoist, leader of Biyun Gate, calls him out on his obvious lie: this isn’t about revenge for a dead grandson. After a tense verbal standoff, Extreme Yin realizes the secret is already leaking—Xuanji from Ten Thousand Laws Gate has shown up, plus other disguised Nascent Soul cultivators. He finally throws in the towel and voice-transmits the truth about the Void Heaven Cauldron to the Taoist. The latter’s greed is instantly ignited, and he scrambles off to deploy more searchers. Meanwhile, elsewhere at sea, a young man impersonating Han Li (the so-called “Bug Demon”) massacres a group of low-level cultivators for their storage pouches. A Foundation Establishment observer watches from the shadows and flees in the opposite direction, confirming that the false Bug Demon ruse is now in play. Han Li returns to his mist-shrouded island, rests, organizes his haul from the sea voyage, and enters his alchemy chamber to break through to a new level of pill refinement—using level-five demon cores as a warm-up.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Heart Demon Oath (心魔大誓) is one of the cultivation world’s most ironic institutions. In theory, swearing by your heart demon means you’ll suffer catastrophic inner-demonic backlash if you break your word—a cosmic kill switch that’s supposed to keep oathbreakers honest. In practice, as the Qi Taoist bluntly points out, “If heart demons were truly that effective, we side-path cultivators would have died out long ago.” This is the Mortal Stream’s signature cynicism: even the rules of cosmic justice are treated as negotiable when profit is on the line. The oath is a tool, not a religion. It only works when powerful people *choose* to believe it does—and they often conveniently stop believing when the prize is big enough.

This chapter also gives us a perfect example of the *secret spreading game* in xianxia geopolitical warfare. Extreme Yin tried to monopolize the Void Heaven Cauldron intelligence. But once he brought in allies for the original expedition (Wan Tianming, Old Devil Man, etc.), the secret had too many holders. Each one calculates differently: Wan Tianming leaked to his own elder Xuanji to gain an edge; Xuanji’s arrival on Qiyuan Island confirmed to the Taoist that something big was up. In the Mortal Stream, you can’t keep a secret forever once it needs to be used—you have to weigh containment against utility, and someone always blinks.

### Fake Bug Demon

A cultivator deliberately impersonating Han Li's insect-controlling identity; a tactic used to "muddy the waters" and make the real target harder to find.

## Reading Guide

So, Han Li is back in his happy place: a quiet cave with piles of monster cores and zero social obligations. Don’t let the calm fool you. The net is tightening. Extreme Yin just greenlit the full Biyun Gate search apparatus. The fake Bug Demon is actively racking up a body count, which will only make the real Han Li’s reputation more terrifying and more hunted. And that Foundation Establishment witness who saw the impersonator? He’s a loose end that will eventually lead pursuers closer to the truth. This is the Mortal Stream’s *quiet victory* phase—the calm between storms where the protagonist stockpiles resources, refines skills, and lets the enemy’s overreach and paranoia do the hard work. Han Li isn’t winning by fighting; he’s winning by *not being found*. And his new alchemy breakthrough is going to be crucial for the next leg of his survival run. Keep your eyes on the furnace, friends.
