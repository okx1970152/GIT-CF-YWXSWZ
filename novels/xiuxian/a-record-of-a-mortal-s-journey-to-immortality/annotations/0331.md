## Chapter Overview
Well, well, well. Look who just walked right into a trap! Emperor Yue, riding high on his puppet-destroying rampage, chases Han Li and the crew straight into a seemingly flimsy bamboo grove. He thinks he's got them cornered, ready to dissolve them into goo with his bloody demonic light. But oh, how the tables turn. That pathetic little formation they ducked into? It was just the appetizer. Han Li coolly pulls out not one, but four identical formation banners, and then an unassuming formation disk. And *bam*—the whole game changes. What was a desperate shelter becomes an inescapable five-colored prison. Emperor Yue’s smugness evaporates into sheer panic when he finds he can’t even fly away. Cue the hunter becoming the hunted. Get ready for a satisfying reversal, fellow Daoists: this is what happens when you let a man with a plan and a pocket full of arrays have a few minutes of prep time.

## Key Plot Points
*   Emperor Yue effortlessly destroys the remaining puppets and pursues Han Li's group into a bamboo grove.
*   Han Li leads his team directly into the "Reversed Five Elements Formation" he had previously set up as his contingency plan.
*   Emperor Yue tries to trap and dissolve the grove with his protective demonic blood light.
*   The formation initially appears overwhelmed, causing panic among Song Meng, Zhong Weiniang, and Chen Qiaoqian's senior brother.
*   Chen Qiaoqian, however, sees through Han Li's calm demeanor and calls him out, asking if he has a deeper plan.
*   Han Li confirms her suspicion and deploys four "Formation Banners" to reinforce the core array, followed by a "Formation Disk".
*   The combined power transforms the fragile barrier into a solid "Five-Colored Radiance" that is completely impervious to Emperor Yue's attacks.
*   Realizing he's trapped, Emperor Yue tries to flee through the sky but finds himself trapped in a spatial loop, unable to escape the formation's boundaries.
*   Han Li instructs his teammates to prepare their "Talisman Treasures" for a coordinated counterattack, declaring that the Emperor cannot escape quickly.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Reversed Five Elements Formation (颠倒五行阵)** – This isn't just any random barrier; it’s a legendary minor forbidden array rooted in Chinese cosmological theory. The "Five Elements" (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) are the fundamental building blocks of all matter and energy. A formation wielding this power can manipulate the balance of these forces to create illusion, concealment, or, as we see here, a self-contained spatial prison. Emperor Yue’s flight loop is a classic feature: the formation confuses the enemy’s directional sense, preventing escape. In *RMJI*, obtaining a portable set of formation tools is like getting a get-out-of-jail-free card, allowing a clever weakling like Han Li to completely reverse the predator-prey dynamic against a much stronger foe.

**Formation Banners vs. Formation Disk** – In the world of Xianxia grand formations, you need specialized tools to anchor and channel the massive energy required. Formation Banners are like the "spikes" driven into the ground to mark the formation's edges and absorb power from the environment. The Formation Disk acts as the "brain" or master controller. Han Li used a simple barrier first, then activated the full set of banners (the real framework) and finally the disk (the activation switch), flipping the entire spatial landscape from a defensive shell into an inescapable cage. It's like a circuit: the disk completes the circuit, turning the entire grove into a powerful magical engine.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the payoff for all of Han Li’s obsessive preparation. Notice how he didn't panic at all, even when his formation was about to break. He was just waiting for the enemy to commit, to fully invest himself in the attack, before springing the real trap. This is the *essence* of the "Mortal Stream" philosophy: brains and preparation beat raw power every time. For Western readers new to Xianxia, think of the "Reversed Five Elements Formation" not as a magic spell, but as a piece of incredibly advanced technology—a programmable, multi-phase reality anchor that Han Li pre-loaded into the terrain. The fact that Emperor Yue, a terrifying Core Formation-level powerhouse, is completely bamboozled by a Qi Condensation cultivator’s setup is the key takeaway. This isn’t luck; it’s a calculated checkmate. Also, pay attention to the subtle horror of the Emperor’s situation: he can see the sky, he can move, but the universe itself has been turned into a maze he cannot escape. That is a deeply unsettling, and utterly satisfying, fate for a villain.
