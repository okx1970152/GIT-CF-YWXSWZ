## Chapter Overview

Chapter 406 pulls back the curtain on the “polite negotiation” and shows what really goes on behind closed doors in the cultivation world. Han Li has just struck a massive deal with Fan Jingmei of the Sublime Sound Sect—or so it seems. While he steps out to gather materials, the true face of this transaction is revealed, and it’s not pretty. What started as a straightforward trade turns into a sharp lesson in the Dark Forest logic of the Chaotic Star Sea. Get ready to see a mask drop so hard it shatters.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Han Li meeting Elder Zhao and Fan Jingmei. He immediately clocks Elder Zhao as a Core Formation First Layer cultivator with abnormally red cheeks, which raises a red flag. He refuses the offered tea out of ingrained caution—a smart move. Fan Jingmei, using a polished and deferential tone, reveals that the Sublime Sound Sect has been tracking Han Li’s small, cautious sales across Heaven Star City. She puts him on the spot, forcing him to admit he has a large stockpile of beast materials, but she frames her request as a plea for help, placing herself in a weak position.

Han Li, weighing the risks of making an enemy of an unknown sect against the convenience of unloading his hoard, agrees to the trade. He leaves to fetch the materials.

But the moment he’s gone, the mask drops. Fan Jingmei coldly asks Elder Zhao if he can subdue Han Li instantly to avoid paying. Elder Zhao warns her not to underestimate Han Li—his pure magic power and hidden treasure aura mark him as no easy target. So, she pivots to a new plan: drag Han Li into their upcoming operation as either an asset or a disposable scapegoat. The scene ends with a disturbingly intimate exchange between Fan Jingmei and Elder Zhao, revealing their true relationship and a predatory interest in a young female disciple.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Polite Predator” Trope:** The most dangerous transaction in a xianxia novel is not the one that starts with a blade; it’s the one that starts with tea and a polite smile. Fan Jingmei’s performance—deferential request, confession of weakness, personal appeal—is a textbook social engineering tactic in the cultivation world. The moment the target agrees, the mask drops. This isn’t unique to this novel; it’s a core trope in “Mortal Stream” literature, where every handshake is a potential strangulation. The reader is constantly reminded that “face” and “courtesy” are often the sharpest weapons in a cultivator’s arsenal.

**The “Skinny on Sublime Sound Sect”:** This chapter gives us our first real look at the Sublime Sound Sect as a political actor. They are not a combat powerhouse, but they are politically agile, charm-oriented, and unafraid to use their networks for dirty work. Fan Jingmei’s shift from grateful ally to cold strategist is a masterclass in how a mid-tier faction operates: always calculating, always looking for leverage, and never hesitating to turn a friendly vendor into a liability.

**A Dark Joke on “Face”:** Fan Jingmei’s line about the sect’s “good reputation” is a cruel dramatic irony. She literally says “our reputation has always been good” while already planning to either exploit or discard Han Li. This is a recurring theme in the novel: the cultivation world’s public morality is a layer of paint over a rotting frame. The sign of an experienced cultivator is how quickly they see through the paint.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a classic “double reveal” setup. On the surface, it’s a trade negotiation. Beneath the surface, it’s a plot to entrap Han Li. And then, at the very end, it reveals the intimately corrupt relationship between Fan Jingmei and Elder Zhao. Pay close attention to Han Li’s reaction to the tea: that one, small refusal is all the reader needs to be reminded that he is not a fool. The real tension here is that we know Han Li is walking into a trap, but we don’t know if he knows it. This sets up a delicious narrative game: will he see through their scheme before they spring it? Or will he play along, pretending not to see, until the time is right to turn the tables?

Also, note the function of the young female disciple, Lian’er. She is a silent witness to the corruption, and she is terrified. Her reaction—relief at being ordered out—tells us more about the internal culture of the Sublime Sound Sect than any exposition could. In the Mortal Stream, a single tight-lipped expression is often more telling than a thousand words.

## The Duality of the Cultivation World: Beneath the Surface

This chapter is a stunning deconstruction of the “benevolent superior” archetype. Fan Jingmei, with her elegant dress and polished speech, embodies a certain ideal of a female sect leader. But her voice is a weapon, not a sign of civility. The moment Han Li’s back is turned, her voice drops to ice, and she talks about him as a resource to be used and discarded.

This dual-layered interaction—courteous surface, predatory interior—is a hallmark of the novel. It means that for the reader, every polite conversation is now automatically suspicious. Han Li’s method of dealing with this is his consistent policy of smiling but never drinking the tea, listening but never fully trusting. He doesn’t need to see the knife to know it’s there; the very fact that someone is being polite is enough to activate his survival instincts. The chapter is a stark reminder: in this world, a friendly face is always the beginning of a trap.
