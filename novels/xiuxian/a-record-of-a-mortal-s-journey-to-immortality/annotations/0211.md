## Chapter Overview

Reverse! Reverse! Reverse! This chapter is an absolute rollercoaster of fortunes. Just when Li Shizu thinks he’s won the grand prize with Han Li’s surprise haul of spirit herbs, the Moon-Masking Sect pulls off a last-minute comeback that completely flips the script. It’s a brutal lesson in the Mortal Stream: no victory is safe until the final tally is announced. And in the middle of all this chaos, Han Li finds himself the center of attention—not just for his loot, but for an unexpected offer that could fundamentally change his path in Yellow Maple Valley. Get ready, because the dust is settling, but the real game might just be beginning.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Reversal Arc:** Han Li drops his massive pile of spirit herbs, and Li Shizu is ecstatic. He genuinely believes he’s secured victory, and he even taunts Fu Yunzi over the lost Bloodline Wyrm inner core.
- **The Turn of the Tide:** But the Moon-Masking Sect’s later disciples submit more than ten stalks each. Their total eclipses both Clear Void Sect and Yellow Maple Valley combined. The bet is lost.
- **The Cost of Defeat:** Old man Qiong collects the Bloodline Wyrm inner core from a devastated Fu Yunzi. Li Shizu, in a rare moment of straightforwardness, promises to send two pieces of Iron Essence within twenty years.
- **Reactions and Department:** Fu Yunzi nearly spits blood from frustration and shame, while the other sects watch with barely concealed Schadenfreude. The forbidden land expedition officially concludes.
- **Han Li Watches Nangong Wan:** As the Moon-Masking Sect departs, Han Li looks for Nangong Wan but receives no acknowledgement, leaving him feeling a bitter pang that he eventually suppresses.
- **Li Shizu’s Inquiry:** After the others leave, Li Shizu finally turns his attention to Han Li, asking how he gathered all those herbs. Han Li spins a well-rehearsed tale of a quarrel and a lucky strike.
- **An Unexpected Offer:** Satisfied with the explanation, Li Shizu makes a shocking declaration: he intends to take Han Li on as a Disciple on Probation. The entire Yellow Maple Valley contingent is stunned with disbelief.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**"Stealing a chicken only to lose the rice" (偷鸡不成蚀把米):** This classic Chinese idiom perfectly encapsulates Li Shizu and Fu Yunzi’s fate this chapter. They tried to outmaneuver old man Qiong, thinking they had the win secured, only to end up losing their bets and their dignity. It’s a favorite narrative tool in xianxia—a sharp reminder that overconfidence and greed often lead to spectacular backfires.

**The Scent-Sniffing Beast Audit:** Before the sects depart, each team leader has the harvested herbs checked by Scent-Sniffing Beasts. This is the cultivation world’s equivalent of a customs inspection—a cold, bureaucratic mechanism designed to ensure no one smuggles extra resources. In a system built on scarcity and zero-sum competition, even the “winners” are not trusted without oversight.

**The Weight of a Betting Loss:** In the Mortal Stream, bets are not friendly wagers; they are binding contracts enforced by reputation and raw power. Fu Yunzi did not hand over the Bloodline Wyrm inner core because he was a good sport. He handed it over because welching in front of seven sects would destroy his credibility and invite devastating retaliation. Reputation is a currency here, and losing it can be fatal.

## Reading Guide

**Han Li’s “Fisherman” Narrative:** The story Han Li tells—of hiding and profiting while two stronger cultivators kill each other—is a near-perfect Mortal Stream survival tactic. It checks all the boxes: no direct confrontation, exploitation of enemy conflict, and maximum gain for minimum risk. It’s also incredibly convenient and suspicious, but Li Shizu is too pleased with the outcome to press further. For now, Han Li’s gamble on the story has paid off.

**The “Reward” That Is a Trap:** Li Shizu’s offer to take Han Li as a Disciple on Probation sounds like a golden ticket. But any reader familiar with the Mortal Stream knows the truth: this could just as easily be a leash. A high-level cultivator taking an interest in a low-level nobody is rarely pure generosity. Han Li’s internal calculation should already be running—weighing the benefits of protection and resources against the costs of increased scrutiny and potential exploitation.

**The Bitter Glance:** Han Li’s disappointment at Nangong Wan’s cold departure is a rare moment of emotional vulnerability. It’s quickly suppressed, but it reveals that even in this calculating world, he is not immune to the sting of unreciprocated connection. For a character defined by ruthless pragmatism, this is a subtle crack in the armor.

**What Comes Next?** With the forbidden land expedition over, the real test begins. Han Li now has high-level attention, a possible new mentorship, and the envy of his peers. He is standing at a crossroads where every step must be measured, every word weighed. The game has changed, and the stakes have never been higher.
