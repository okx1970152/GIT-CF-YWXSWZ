## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists! Chapter 135 is a pure information-delivery chapter, but don’t let the lack of action fool you—this is a *world-shattering* lore dump that redefines the entire game board. Our party of rogue cultivators (led by Hu Pinggu) finally gets down to brass tacks about the **Ascension Assembly**, the brutal tournament that serves as the only shortcut for independent cultivators to join the great immortal sects of Yue. The rules sound simple—under forty, cultivation at seventh layer or above—but the reality is a meat grinder. Hu Pinggu and Blue-Robed Taoist drop one grim revelation after another: the Assembly is stacked *heavily* in favor of cultivation clan prodigies who’ve been hoarding pills for decades, and rogue cultivators like our protagonists are cannon fodder unless they have trump cards. Han Li, as always, listens and calculates. His trademark nose-rub signals he’s already filing this intel into a thousand contingency plans. Get ready for a cold, hard dose of the cultivation world’s institutionalized class divide.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Rules of the Arena**: Hu Pinggu spells out the basic conditions—seventh layer minimum, under forty, verified by Bone Observation Art. No other restrictions, which means the competition is a free-for-all of desperate talent.
- **The True Purpose of the Tainan Gathering**: Over a thousand cultivators have flooded Tainan Valley, most using the trading meet as a scouting ground to assess future opponents in the Ascension Assembly.
- **The Myth of Layer Superiority**: Hei Jin and Blue-Robed Taoist immediately dismantle Wu Jiuzhi’s panic about high-layer opponents. A weaker cultivator with a powerful talisman or magical tool can still win. This reinforces the Mortal Stream principle that strategy and equipment can outweigh raw power.
- **The Seven Arenas**: Seven arenas, one per great sect. Ten survivors per arena become sect disciples, bypassing internal competition for Foundation Establishment Pills.
- **The Eleventh Layer Trap**: Blue-Robed Taoist explains why two eleventh-layer cultivators died fighting each other: they were cultivation clan plants, secretly hoisted to high layers by pills, lying low for decades to steal a Foundation Establishment Pill seat. When two such clan plants collided, mutual destruction occurred.
- **Rogue Cultivator Despair**: Hu Pinggu and Black Wood confirm that rogue cultivators have almost no chance of winning against clan-backed fighters, and even if they become sect disciples, successful Foundation Establishment is rare. The system is rigged.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Foundation Establishment Pill (筑基丹)**: This is the single most critical resource in the lower cultivation realm. It allows a Qi Condensation cultivator to attempt the leap to Foundation Establishment, which multiplies lifespan and power. In the *Mortal Stream* universe, these pills are tightly controlled by sects and clans. The Ascension Assembly is one of the few ways a rogue cultivator can earn the right to take one—but the odds are abysmal.

**Bone Observation Art (观骨术)**: A technique that estimates a person’s true age by examining their skeletal structure. It’s a common anti-fraud measure in cultivation world competitions, ensuring no old monsters sneak into youth tournaments.

**The Eleventh Layer Cultivation Clan Plant**: This is a core example of the *Mortal Stream’s* class warfare. Clans raise their mediocre talents in secret, feeding them pills to brute-force their cultivation to high layers, then send them to the Ascension Assembly to “steal” Foundation Establishment Pill qualifications from genuine prodigies. It’s a calculated investment that sometimes backfires when two clan plants meet and kill each other.
