## Chapter Overview

Ah, the bureaucracy of immortality! Welcome to Chapter 147, where Han Li officially becomes a cog in the Yellow Maple Valley machine. After the heart-pounding gamble with the Ascension Token, our favorite profit-calculating cultivator must now navigate the cold, hierarchical reality of a major immortal sect. This chapter is less about flashy fights and more about the quiet, grinding tension of political maneuvering. Han Li gives up his precious Foundation Establishment Pill (ouch, the pain is palpable even through the text), and in exchange, buys himself a foothold and a future ally. While the tension of the Assembly fades, a new, more insidious struggle begins: the fight for resources, status, and the right to simply have enough time to cultivate in peace. For a mortal-stream hero, this kind of social infrastructure is its own deadly battlefield.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Summons**: Han Li is finally brought before Sect Leader Zhong Lingdao and a hall full of administrators. His plain appearance draws no more than a cursory glance from the gathered elders. This reinforces his status as an insignificant ninth-layer cultivateur in a world of giants.
- **The Sacrifice**: The main event! Han Li formally confirms he will give up his right to a Foundation Establishment Pill. The narration makes it brutally clear how much this pains him—a rare moment of genuine emotional conflict, as he mentally compares it to all his other "snack-grade" pills, knowing this one is on a completely different level.
- **The Trade**: The sect leader leverages the relinquished pill to placate Elder Ye’s faction, solving a political problem neatly. In return, he promises Han Li will be "satisfied" with compensation, though the exact terms remain vague. The old man Ye is overjoyed, confirming this was a win for his family.
- **The Orientation**: Senior Wang escorts Han Li out of the hall and delivers a dry, factual briefing on the sect’s structure. This is the true purpose of the chapter: a massive lore dump disguised as orientation.
- **The Three Tiers**: The internal hierarchy of Yellow Maple Valley is laid bare: the vast, exploited masses of *Manager Disciples* (Qi Condensation), the slightly better-off *Supervisor Disciples* (failed Foundation Establishment), and the privileged elite of *Foundation Establishment* cultivators who can have their own caves, endless spirit stones, and no chores.
- **The True Power**: The chapter ends by making the real power dynamic clear: the sect leader is just a high-level manager. The true decision-makers are the Core Formation and Nascent Soul cultivators who can destroy the sect on a whim.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Sect as a Corporation (宗门即企业)**: One of the most brilliant and brutal aspects of *A Record of a Mortal's Journey* is how it re-frames a classical cultivation sect not as a family of like-minded dao-seekers, but as a hyper-capitalist corporation. This chapter makes that explicit. Yellow Maple Valley runs on a rigid internal labor market with three distinct castes: *Manager Disciples* (minimum-wage grunts), *Supervisor Disciples* (middle management, still no equity), and *Foundation Establishment Cultivators* (the senior partners with ownership stakes). Note how there is no morality in this structure—only resource allocation and performance metrics. A disciple’s value is determined by their proximity to a "breakthrough," and the sect’s sole purpose is to generate those breakthroughs to maintain its political power. Han Li is not joining a school; he is accepting an entry-level position at a ruthless, life-extending multinational.

**The Foundation Establishment Pill Lottery (筑基丹经济)**: The chapter gives a chillingly low success rate: out of the *hundreds of best disciples* who earn a Foundation Establishment Pill, only 20-30 succeed. This is the "foundation lottery" of the cultivation world. It reinforces the novel’s core theme that hard work and talent are necessary but insufficient. The Pill itself is a consumable, finite resource that forms the ultimate barrier to entry. By giving his up, Han Li is essentially skipping a turn in the lottery to earn political capital, betting that his hidden asset (the mysterious vial) will give him a better chance when he eventually crafts his own.

### Bottleneck Economy (瓶颈经济)

A systemic description of the cultivation world where the scarcity of resources and the high failure rate of breakthroughs create a desperate market for enhancement pills and political favor.

## Reading Guide

This chapter feels like a deep breath after a sprint. For readers used to non-stop action, the "orientation section" might feel slow, but don't be fooled. This is the most important kind of information for a Mortal Stream protagonist. Han Li is not memorizing rules; he is **building a map of the power structure**. He identifies who the real predators are (the Foundation Establishment and above), who the administrators are (the middle-men you can bribe or play), and who the anonymous masses are (everyone else). This understanding is his primary weapon in the next arc. Pay special attention to the fact that the Founding Establishment cultivators don’t have to do chores—this is Han Li’s new, concrete goal. The "reading" is about understanding that sect life is not a safe haven but a different kind of Dark Forest, where the currency is not just spirit stones, but connections, face, and the strategic management of your own potential.

## Deeper Dive: The Economy of Immortality

This chapter perfectly illustrates the **Bottleneck Economy** mentioned in earlier arcs. The entire incentive structure of Yellow Maple Valley is warped by the Foundation Establishment bottleneck. Most disciples are trapped, working for scraps, unable to advance. The sect leaders control the "Pills" as a monopoly, doling them out as rewards to ensure political loyalty. This is not a meritocracy of virtue, but a **bloodline-and-favor meritocracy**. The Ascension Token is the perfect symbol: it bypasses the system entirely, rewarding institutional service over personal skill. Han Li played this game perfectly by giving up his token’s reward, buying goodwill from the sect leader and a powerful elder, even as he inwardly curses the loss. This chapter is a textbook on how to survive capitalism in the immortal world.
