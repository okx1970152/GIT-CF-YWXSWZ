## Chapter Overview
It's *showtime*. And by "show," I mean Han Li finally drops the act and unleashes the full, terrifying spectrum of his combat capabilities on a group of unlucky Black Wind Flag cultivators. The kid gloves are off. This chapter is a brutal, efficient, and almost clinical demonstration of the absolute power gap between a Foundation Establishment cultivator with top-tier techniques and a squad of Qi Condensation mooks. Han Li doesn't just win; he makes a statement. He controls over a dozen magic tools simultaneously, uses talismans as if they're going out of fashion, and springs tactical surprises one after another. This isn't a fight; it's an execution that leaves everyone—allies and enemies alike—staring in utter disbelief. The young prince's carefully laid plans crumble to dust as he realizes he's brought a knife to a nuclear war.

## Key Plot Points
1.  **Multi-Tool Supremacy (大衍决 in Action):** Han Li demonstrates the terrifying potential of his Great Derivative Art, flawlessly controlling over a dozen top-grade magic tools of different colors (gold, black, red) in a coordinated assault. He utterly shreds the "Black Wind Formation" before it can even be activated, killing several Black Wind Flag cultivators in rapid succession.
2.  **The Blood Attendant Contained:** Just as a monstrous roar signals the mysterious transformation of the blood-robed Blood Attendant, Han Li nonchalantly produces the "Sky-Covering Bell," a trophy from the Moon-Masking Sect, and traps the entire bloody light mass inside it. The young prince's trump card is cancelled before it can be played.
3.  **Talisman Barrage:** Turning his attention to the young prince and his manager, Han Li unleashes a storm of fireball talismans. The sheer volume overwhelms their defenses, leaving the young prince battered and disoriented.
4.  **Manager Wang Eliminated as a Threat:** In the chaos, Han Li uses his superior speed to capture the masked Manager Wang, severing one of his arms and rendering him helpless. The young prince is now truly alone.
5.  **The Fugitive Prince's Last Resort:** In a desperate bid to flee, the young prince activates his protective baleful qi, a technique said to corrupt magic tools. He tries to fly over Han Li, believing his defense makes him untouchable. However, the chapter ends with Han Li summoning a massive cyan greatsword, preparing to cut him down, signaling that the prince's trump card may be useless against his overwhelming power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Great Derivative Art (大衍决):** Remember when this technique was just about strengthening divine sense? Well, this chapter shows its true, battle-breaking application. In standard Mortal Stream logic, a Qi Condensation cultivator is lucky to control one or two magic tools effectively. Foundation Establishment might let you control three or four. But the Great Derivative Art, a masterpiece from the Thousand Bamboo School, is a cheat code for multitasking. It splits the cultivator's consciousness, letting them control a literal swarm of magical artifacts simultaneously. This isn't just a power-up; it's a fundamental shift in the *type* of combat. Fighting Han Li now means fighting a one-man army where every element moves with perfect, independent precision. It turns a "duel" into a "force concentration" problem for the enemy.

**Protective Baleful Qi (护体煞气):** This is the dark side of the coin in the cultivation world. We've seen the righteous path's golden shields and blue barriers. This is its demonic counterpart—a shroud of corruptive energy that specializes in *ruining* things. The young prince's confidence comes from a classic xianxia rule: magic tools are fragile and can be "stained" or "corrupted" by malevolent qi, rendering them useless. However, this tactic works against enemies who rely on throwing delicate weapons. Against Han Li, who can gather a massive sword of pure, condensed spiritual energy, the "corruption" strategy is likely to fail against raw, overwhelming force.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in Han Li's evolution from a solo survivalist to a *manager of force*. Watch how he transitions from using the Sky-Covering Bell (a direct, powerful control tool) to a tactical, multi-wave attack, and then to a psychological weapon (that smile!). He's not just defeating enemies; he's taking control of the entire battlefield's narrative. The "Four Meng Mountain Friends" serve as our audience surrogate. Their shock and awe are ours. Notice the cold, mechanical inevitability of Han Li's victory. There's no excitement, no gloating, just a series of efficient, calculated moves. This is what a Foundation Establishment cultivator with a killer instinct and high-tier assets looks like in full flow. Get ready for the next step: can the young prince's desperate gambit survive Han Li's finishing blow?
