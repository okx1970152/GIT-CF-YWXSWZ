## Chapter Overview

Han Li finally gets his mana back—just enough to pop open his storage pouch—and immediately starts treating it like a borrowed sword. The chapter opens with an unexpected method of qi transfer that, let’s be honest, nobody here walked in thinking would be a *real* option. And yet it happens, and Han Li, being Han Li, doesn’t let the moment linger. Once his storage pouch is open, he’s already moving: grabbing the things he actually needs, ignoring the girlfriend-bait treasures, and slipping out the door while the mana lasts. Mei Ning’s big reveal comes when she learns—dun dun dun—that Han Li is a Core Formation cultivator. That changes everything about the power dynamic in the room. Oh, and the Crying Soul Beast is suddenly *very* excited about something in this ghost-infested domain. That can’t be a coincidence.

This chapter is a classic “quiet victory” breather. It is a prelude to action, not the action itself. Han Li uses every second of his temporary mana to execute a cold, efficient supply run, and the Crying Soul Beast’s sudden excitement foreshadows that the real trouble—or opportunity—is just around the corner.

## Key Plot Points

- Mei Ning volunteers the mouth-to-mouth qi transfer method, surprising even Han Li with her directness.
- The actual transfer scene is brief and practical; Han Li suppresses his desire after a moment and calls it a tactical transaction.
- Han Li opens his storage pouch and immediately sorts through his assets, discarding unused ancient treasures (the golden mirror, blue basket, silver bell).
- Han Li reveals his true cultivation level (Core Formation) to Mei Ning, shocking her and deepening her awe.
- The Crying Soul Beast becomes suddenly excited upon entering the Netherworld Domain, hinting at nearby ghost-type activity or valuable spiritual sources.
- Han Li uses his temporary mana to scout the settlement, finding a supply of “Sinking Water” which he stockpiles.
- He stops at the residence of middle-aged Feng, hinting at a future interaction or reconnaissance target.
- The strand of recovered mana is already fading by the chapter’s end, imposing a strict time limit on Han Li’s next moves.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Spirit Essence Qi (通灵之气):** This is not a standard resource in cultivation novels. It’s a specialized energy stream that can only be generated by a specific type of female cultivator—in this case, one with a special physique. The fact that it can reactivate a sealed mana pool makes it a battlefield currency of the highest order. In the Mortal Stream logic, anyone who knows about it will treat its bearer as a mobile mana battery, which is exactly why Mei Ning chose Han Li so carefully.

**Crying Soul Beast (啼魂兽):** This little monkey is one of the coolest spirit-beast designs in *RMJI*. Its sniffer is a specialized ghost detector, and whenever it gets excited in a place full of yin energy, you know something is about to go sideways. It’s basically Han Li’s supernatural “bad vibes sensor,” and its sudden activity here is a clear narrative signal that the calm won’t last.

**The Netherworld Domain / Yin-Ming Land (阴冥之地):** This is a whole different ecosystem from the standard cultivation world. Regular mana is suppressed, “yin force” becomes the primary usable energy, and the regular rules of flight, perception, and combat are all tossed out the window. It’s a lawless frontier even by the Chaotic Star Sea’s standards. The fact that Han Li is entering it with a fresh Core Formation level is both an advantage and a huge red target on his back.

## Reading Guide

This is one of those “breather and prep” chapters where *RMJI* shows its strength in pacing. But don’t let the calm fool you—every detail here is tactical. Han Li’s choice to reveal his Core Formation level to Mei Ning isn’t just honesty; it’s a power play. He’s telling her, loud and clear, “I am not a junior you can discard once your mana returns.” It’s a leash.

Also, the fact that Han Li starts scouting and looting while his mana is still active tells you everything about his priorities. He doesn’t waste a single second on celebration. The Sinking Water supply run might seem like a minor logistical detail, but in a mana-sealed environment, having a unique local resource stockpiled can be a game-changer for future formations or trades.

The Crying Soul Beast’s excitement is the chapter’s single biggest foreshadowing flag. Get ready for the Netherworld Domain to throw something truly nasty—or valuable—Han Li’s way very soon.
