## Chapter Overview
Well, fellow cultivators, the big moment is finally here. After sixty years of sitting on his stone stool and staring at walls, Han Li steps out of his cultivation chamber a brand-new man. The Golden Core is done, the Nascent Flame is his to command, and the first treasure he’s been eyeing from that golden page for the better part of a century is finally within reach. This chapter is essentially a victory lap—a tour of the farm, a pat on the back for those Bloodjade Spiders, and a jaw-dropping reveal of tens of thousands of Gold Devourer Beetles that have quietly evolved into gold-speckled killing machines while he was away. But don’t let the laid-back vibe fool you. Between the lines, Han Li is already running the numbers on his next bottleneck. The quiet victory phase is never truly quiet—it’s just the calm before the next storm of resource scrambling and sect-level predation.

## Key Plot Points
- **Han Li emerges from seclusion** after sixty years, having successfully formed a four-colored Golden Core. The breakthrough is so sudden it nearly destabilizes from sheer disbelief.
- **A tour of the insect chambers** reveals that the Gold Devourer Beetles have undergone a generation-spanning mutation. Through repeated cycles of Nightshade Grass feeding, mutual consumption, and egg-laying, they have multiplied to tens of thousands and developed golden-flecked carapaces.
- **The two Bloodjade Spiders** have been nurtured to peak fourth-rank with Spirit Nourishing Pills. They now resemble the legendary White Jade Spider—translucent and crystalline—and respond to Han Li with eager affection.
- **Han Li reflects on his Core Formation process**: he only completed the Second Revolution of the Triple Revolving Heavy Origin Art, supplemented by the Great Derivative Art’s third layer. The breakthrough was unexpected, and the author’s note admits to skipping two plot points to speed up the progression.
- **The chapter ends with Han Li setting his next goal**: gathering materials to forge the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Sword, a matched set of treasures, so he can nurture it with Nascent Flame and deploy the Sword Image Splitting Technique at full power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Core Formation (结丹)** is the third major realm in the xianxia ladder. It compresses the liquid Foundation Establishment energy into a solid, internalized Golden Core—a permanent structural upgrade that multiplies lifespan (500–600 years), enables the use of Dan Fire (Nascent Flame), and allows the cultivation of one’s designated Magical Treasure. In *RMJI*, the process is brutally resource-dependent: Han Li’s success relies on accidental cultivation speed from the Triple Revolving Heavy Origin Art, a desperate gamble with the Great Derivative Art, and the lifesaving backup of the Heaven Vial’s accelerated herbs. Note the **Demonic Core (煞丹)** alternative that Qu Hun formed—a dark-aligned core variant produced by forbidden cultivation methods, which locks the user into a different path of advancement.

The **Gold Devourer Beetles’ evolution** follows a gritty biological logic rather than magical convenience: cannibalism under optimized feeding conditions triggers a rapid generational arms race. This mirrors real-world insect farming techniques (selective pressure, culling weak lines) and keeps the Mortal Stream’s obsession with preparation over luck intact.

The **Sword Image Splitting Technique (剑影分光术)** is a high-level ability within the Azure Origin Sword Art that manifests phantom sword images identical to the user’s flying sword. It scales with cultivation and is a classic *jianxiu* (sword cultivator) signature—turning one blade into a swarm. For Han Li, mastering this is a direct power multiplier that shifts his combat doctrine from “survive with hidden tricks” to “overwhelm with coordinated blade-rain.”

## Reading Guide
Get ready, fellow Daoists—this is the *exhale* chapter. After the nail-biting tension of the battle with Master Mo and the long, silent slog of the cultivation chamber, Han Li finally gets to breathe. The tone shifts from “will I survive the night?” to “look at my shiny new beetles.” Enjoy the moment of quiet satisfaction—it’s a luxury the Mortal Stream rarely grants. Watch how Han Li’s inner voice immediately pivots from celebration to logistics: he’s already planning the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Sword material hunt. Even his victory is a launchpad, not a finishing line. Also, pay close attention to the author’s note at the end—it’s a rare, transparent peek behind the curtain, where the writer admits to cutting two subplots to keep the pace reader-friendly. That kind of honesty is part of the web novel culture that makes *RMJI* feel lived-in.
