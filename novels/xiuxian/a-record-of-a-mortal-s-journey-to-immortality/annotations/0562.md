## Chapter Overview
Welcome back to the Mortal Stream deep dive! This chapter is a quiet victory interlude—but don’t let the low combat count fool you. Han Li finally shakes off the Ripping Wind Beast’s Wind Spirit Restriction, masters the legendary Wind Lightning Wings, and unlocks the Lightning Escape Technique. It’s a huge power spike for our favorite cautious farming simulator, wrapped in the usual tense logistics: escaping a ninth-grade demon’s pursuit, finishing an artifact the original blacksmith couldn’t, and plotting his return to the Inner Star Sea. Plus, there’s a nice little loot surprise that makes you feel the grind was worth it.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li’s Wind Spirit Restriction subsides after two days of silence, indicating the Ripping Wind Beast has stopped actively triggering it—likely because the beast is busy dealing with the backlash from the Heaven Thunder Seed (or similar damage).
- Han Li instantly seizes the window, drinks a drop of Spirit Liquid, and flees the area at top speed.
- The Ripping Wind Beast, exhausted and unable to suppress the foreign energy in its body, is forced to stop on an island to recuperate. It vows to retrieve the Wind Lightning Wings.
- Over a month later, Han Li finds a deserted island, sets up a hidden cave, and begins working on the Wind Lightning Wings.
- He discovers the wings were retrieved too early and are not fully solidified. Using his Bixie Divine Lightning, he completes the final refinement, even infusing golden threads into the silver feathers—an unexpected upgrade.
- After soul-binding the treasure and absorbing it, he tests it in the air. Pure magic power yields no speed gain, but when he channels divine lightning into the wings, he activates the legendary Lightning Escape Technique—teleportation-like bursts of speed.
- Han Li marvles at the technique, then immediately plans his next move: head to the Sublime Sound Sect’s market, use their teleportation array to return to the Inner Star Sea, and lie low until he forms his Nascent Soul.
- He checks the loot from the Turtle Demon and Poison Flood Dragon, finding a black turtle shell and blood-red scales that could make top-grade armor—and also demon cores and rare materials.
- Off-page, the Turtle Demon has reported the Poison Flood Dragon’s death to the local Flood Dragon clan chief, a ninth-grade Li Fire Flood Dragon—setting up future trouble.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Lightning Escape Technique (雷遁术)**: In Chinese xianxia, escape techniques are ranked. Lightning Escape is considered one of the fastest, rivaled only by Light Escape. It’s not just a speed boost; it functions like short-range teleportation, making the user nearly impossible to track with ordinary divine sense. The fact that Han Li now wields this through a treasure rather than a personal cultivation method is a major tactical upgrade.
- **Essence Blood (精血)**: A cultivator’s life force concentrated. Using even a single drop permanently reduces lifespan and cultivation foundation. Han Li used one here simply to soul-bind the wings—showing how seriously he values this treasure. In classic Daoist inner alchemy, Essence Blood is linked to the Three Treasures (Jing, Qi, Shen). Losing it is never trivial.
- **Wind Lightning Wings (风雷翅)**: The name follows a common xianxia naming convention where ‘wind’ and ‘thunder’ are paired as complementary forces (wind fuels fire, thunder awakens life). The treasure is not yet fully evolved—it has golden threads from absorbing Bixie Divine Lightning, hinting at potential upgrades.
- **Spirit Liquid (万年灵液)**: This is not just a mana potion; in xianxia logic, ten-thousand-year spirit liquids are rare treasures that instantly replenish cultivation energy. The Ripping Wind Beast cannot afford one, underlining Han Li’s resource advantage even against a vastly stronger foe.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a classic Mortal Stream power spike—Han Li doesn’t luck into a freebie; he earns his new toy through patient refining and a near-death chase. The Lightning Escape Technique is a game-changer for his survival toolkit, and the loot from the demon hunt gives him crafting options. But don’t get too comfortable: the Turtle Demon has snitched to a ninth-grade Flood Dragon. That debt isn’t going to pay itself. Also, pay attention to how Han Li’s mind works during the testing phase: he methodically tests each mode, identifies the inefficient magic-only mode, then pivots to the lightning mode and discovers the real prize. That’s pure Mortal Stream problem-solving in action.
