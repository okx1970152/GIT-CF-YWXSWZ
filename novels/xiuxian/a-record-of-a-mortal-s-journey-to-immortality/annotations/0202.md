## Chapter Overview
Oh, snap a girl walked into a kill zone without realizing it. In a single well-timed sentence, the green-clad bystander reminds both Han Li and the barefoot Sword Sect hulk that *silence* is the only logical answer when a murder weapon ties you to a Core Formation elder’s granddaughter. Now the standoff becomes a life-or-death chess match over a ten-zhang line, and our favorite mortal is about to teach everyone why closing distance with him is a terminal mistake.

## Key Plot Points
- The burly man’s Silver Radiance Sword proves overwhelmingly powerful: it entangles all seven Golden Cicada sub-blades, absorbs two top-grade artifacts Han Li throws at it, and crushes them instantly.
- Han Li is forced to use the Green Solidifying Mirror (from the Many Treasures Woman) to freeze the silver sword and his remaining blades in midair.
- The burly man recognizes the mirror and accuses Han Li of killing one of the “Moon-Masking Twin Jewels,” revealing that the artifact belonged to a granddaughter of a Core Formation elder from the Moon-Masking Sect.
- Han Li realizes the implication: he has no evidence to prove he didn’t kill her, and a Core Formation elder will hunt him as the “murderer” holding her artifact.
- The green-clad girl innocently suggests “keeping it a secret,” forcing both fighters to acknowledge that the only reliable solution is to silence everyone present.
- The burly man and Han Li tacitly accept the necessity of a final, no-holds-barred duel.
- Han Li uses the Smoke Step technique to close to within ten zhang, then kills the burly man with a single swing of a near-invisible silk thread.
- The chapter closes with the green-clad girl directly asking Han Li if he will kill her too.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Green Solidifying Mirror & The Moon-Masking Twin Jewels**  
The mirror is a known top-grade defensive artifact linked to the famous twin female prodigies of the Moon-Masking Sect—descendants of a Core Formation elder. In the xianxia social code, merely holding a dead elder’s family heirloom is enough to mark you as the killer, because “nothing else makes sense.” Han Li’s predicament is a textbook example of *circumstantial guilt* in a world where no one bothers to investigate the truth for a nobody like him.

**The “Ten Zhang” Killing Rule**  
Han Li’s claim that anyone within ten zhang without protection is simply dead is a *threat escalation* trope rooted in the Mortal Stream’s emphasis on mobility and surprise over raw artifact power. The smoke step + invisible thread combo is the brutal payoff of all those stealth and agility techniques he cultivated in the wilderness. In the western reader’s mind: think of it as a rogue who has been building his Blink + Execute skill tree from the tutorial zone.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for the aftermath fall-out: Han Li just killed a second formidable Qi Condensation fighter, but the real monster on his tail is a Core Formation elder who won’t care about his side of the story. The green-clad girl’s fate is now the biggest question mark—will Han Li break his own “no loose ends” rule for a timid stranger, or will he commit yet another cold kill to protect his escape? Buckle up, fellow Daoists—the mortal’s moral calculus is about to meet a real stress test.
