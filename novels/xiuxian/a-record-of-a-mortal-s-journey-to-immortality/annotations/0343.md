## Chapter Overview

Get ready for a chapter that’s all about talking—but not the boring kind. Han Li has just captured “Qu Hun,” a cultivator who claims to be from the Yu Ling Sect of the Demonic Path. What follows is a masterclass in interrogation from both sides: Han Li’s cold, probing logic versus the prisoner’s desperate, layered lies. Every sentence is a trap, every pause a calculation. And just when you think the prisoner is about to crack, he drops a truth bomb that rewrites everything we thought we knew about the Seven Sects’ political landscape. Spoiler: It involves a secret alliance, a false defection, and a betrayal so deep it makes Game of Thrones look like a tea party.

## Key Plot Points

• Han Li cuts off the prisoner’s attempt to claim he’s a rogue cultivator, showing he’s not buying any easy lies.
• The prisoner confesses he’s from the Yu Ling Sect of the Demonic Path (Tiandu Country) and that his body was destroyed 5–6 years ago.
• He explains why he hasn’t returned: his cultivation was crippled by the Possession, and returning would mean death at the hands of ambitious sect brothers.
• Han Li calls his bluff, saying the Six Demonic Sects have already invaded Yue, and threatens to extract and destroy his Primordial Soul.
• The prisoner accidentally reveals he knows the invasion timeline, exposing his lie about being ignorant of current events.
• Cornered, the prisoner demands that Han Li swear a soul-artifact oath before he’ll talk. Han Li agrees without hesitation—but we know he’s planning to exploit a loophole.
• The prisoner says the Seven Sects’ combined forces have likely already been crushed, and warns Han Li not to go to the camp.
• He drops the biggest bombshell: Spirit Beast Mountain has always been a secret branch of the Yu Ling Sect, and its “defection” was a fake smokescreen to lure in the other two countries’ cultivators for a decisive battle.
• Even more shocking: Moon-Masking Sect itself was originally connected to the Harmony Sect (Demonic Path), though it truly broke away centuries ago.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Six Demonic Sects vs. Seven Great Sects:** The Demonic Path in Tianluo Nation is organized into six major sects, each with specialized dark arts. The Seven Great Sects of Yue are their orthodox counterparts. The enmity runs deep, but as this chapter reveals, the boundary is far blurrier than anyone admits.

**Soul Refinement (炼魂术) & Soul Artifacts:** Soul Refinement is a forbidden technique that extracts and tortures a target’s soul. Cultivators who practice it often bind their soul artifacts to their own soul, making self-cursing oaths exceptionally dangerous—breaking such an oath can cause the artifact to betray the user. Han Li’s quick agreement to swear on his Soul-Gathering Bowl is therefore clever: he doesn’t actually practice the technique, so the oath is meaningless.

**Possession & Cultivation Loss:** The penalty for Possession is severe—a cultivator can lose multiple major realm levels. This is why the Yu Ling Sect prisoner is stuck at Qi Condensation despite once being a higher-level cultivator. It takes decades of re-cultivation to recover.

**Spirit Beast Mountain & Yu Ling Sect:** This chapter introduces a core Dark Forest theme: ostensibly orthodox sects may secretly be controlled by the Demonic Path. Spirit Beast Mountain’s “independence” is a long-term infiltration scheme. The implication is that the entire Yue war may be a trap designed by the Demonic Path.

## Reading Guide

This is a “social battle” chapter, dense with information warfare. Pay close attention to Han Li’s interrogation technique: he uses false information (the invasion claim) to force the prisoner into a slip, then uses that slip to crack open deeper secrets. The prisoner’s “truth” is itself a layered mix of confession and misdirection—even his surrender speech contains hooks meant to manipulate Han Li’s greed or curiosity. The biggest takeaway: nothing in the cultivation world is as it seems. Sects have hidden pedigrees, defections can be bait, and every “alliance” is a potential knife in the back.

Also note how Han Li handles the soul-artifact oath: he doesn’t actually practice Soul Refinement, so the oath carries no real karmic weight. This shows his ability to exploit technicalities—a hallmark of his survivability. The chapter ends with a dizzying revelation that recontextualizes the entire war arc. If you thought the Seven Sects were fighting a straightforward defensive war, think again.

## Chapter Vocabulary

**Yu Ling Sect (御灵宗):** The demonic sect specializing in controlling spirit beasts and puppets, with a long history of covertly influencing the orthodox sects of Yue.
**Spirit Beast Mountain (灵兽山):** One of the Seven Great Sects of Yue, revealed to have been a branch of the Yu Ling Sect all along; its “defection” was a ruse.
**Moon-Masking Sect (掩月宗):** The most powerful among the Seven Great Sects, originally connected to the Harmony Sect of the Demonic Path, but truly independent for centuries.
**Harmony Sect (合欢宗):** A demonic sect known for dual cultivation arts; the Moon-Masking Sect’s ancestor sect.
**Soul Refinement (炼魂术):** A forbidden technique that extracts and tortures a soul; practitioners risk backlash when swearing by soul artifacts.
**Soul Artifact Oath (魂器誓言):** A self-binding oath sworn on a soul-bound magical tool; breaking it can cause the artifact to turn against the user.
