## Chapter Overview
Holy smokes, fellow Daoists, buckle up — this chapter is a *double-whammy* of pure, concentrated Mortal Stream tension. On one side, we get a front-row seat to the cold-blooded political marriage and the chillingly casual discussion of mass murder as a wedding gift. The Ghost Spirit Sect isn’t just powerful; it’s *efficient*, and the Yan Family is learning that selling your soul to a demonic sect comes with a very specific price tag. On the other side, Han Li stumbles into the most awkward love quadrangle you’ve ever seen, starring a completely enchanted Dong Xuan’er and a man whose beauty could probably start a war. It’s a perfect vignette: the high-stakes, world-shaking politics of the cultivation world contrasted with the messy, petty, and incredibly human drama of jealousy and infatuation. Get ready for a chapter that proves the Mortal Stream’s true horror isn’t always a blood array — sometimes it’s just being forced to witness a public scandal.

## Key Plot Points
- The Ghost Spirit Sect Young Lord, Wang Chan, presses the Yan Family on their evacuation plan for the upcoming invasion of the Yue Nation. The scholar, Xuan Ye, reveals the family will abandon distant and mortal members, showing their pragmatic ruthlessness.
- Wang Chan casually proposes a *horrifying* solution for the two hundred Foundation Establishment cultivators currently in Yanling Fort: use the Ghost Spirit Sect’s Netherfire Grand Array to melt their bodies and harvest their souls for Yan Ruyan’s Blood Spirit Great Art blood sacrifice.
- Xuan Ye vehemently opposes, fearing the backlash from other nations. Yan Family Ancestor, loath to act directly, cleverly deflects the responsibility onto the Ghost Spirit Sect, framing it as their ‘betrothal gift’ to Yan Ruyan.
- Wang Chan, after some consideration, accepts the role of the villain, coldly stating the souls will be his gift. The transaction is complete; the fate of two hundred cultivators is sealed with polite smiles and a bow.
- The scene cuts abruptly to Han Li, who is experiencing a very different kind of crisis. He’s walked into a standoff in a back alley: an infatuated Dong Xuan’er is clinging to an extraordinarily beautiful male cultivator, while Yan Yu and Senior Brother Feng fume helplessly nearby.
- Han Li realizes with a sinking feeling that he can’t just walk away. Red Robe’s parting instructions hang over him like a sword of Damocles. The two jealous suitors immediately latch onto him as their last hope to rescue Dong Xuan’er from the stranger’s ‘clutches.’

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- ***Blood Spirit Great Art & Blood Sacrifice (血祭/血灵大法):*** This isn’t just ‘evil magic.’ In the Mortal Stream, blood sacrifice is a brutally efficient *alchemical process*. Using countless lives as fuel for a single technique isn’t an act of madness; it’s a logistical calculation. The Ghost Spirit Sect is proposing to ‘refine’ (炼化) the cultivators’ physical bodies for energy and keep their souls for Yan Ruyan. This reinforces the grim truth that in this world, a cultivator’s soul is just another high-grade resource to be harvested, not a sacred essence.
- ***The Politics of Offerings (聘礼/嫁妆):*** In traditional Chinese culture, a betrothal gift (聘礼) from the groom’s family is a significant social and economic transaction. Wang Chan cynically weaponizes this tradition. He offers the massacre of two hundred people as a ‘gift,’ but by accepting it, the Yan Family is publicly acknowledging its debt and subservience. Yan Family Ancestor’s counter-move is to frame it as the Ghost Spirit Sect *doing its duty* for its future daughter-in-law, a clever political dodge to save face.
- ***Bewitching Charm (狐媚之功):*** This chapter heavily hints that Dong Xuan’er is under the influence of some kind of supernatural ‘charm’ or illusion technique, not just simple infatuation. In Chinese folklore, foxes (狐) are famous for their shape-shifting and seductive powers. This man’s effect on her is so immediate and overwhelming that it strongly suggests a specific cultivation art designed to cloud the mind and enthrall the senses. Han Li’s analytical observation confirms he is considering this angle.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in tonal contrast. Watch how the cold, corporate horror of the first half — where mass murder is discussed with the same politeness as a real estate deal — is immediately followed by the comically mundane jealousy drama of the second half. It’s a classic Mortal Stream technique: after a dose of soul-crushing darkness, give the reader a breather with a petty personal problem. Pay close attention to Yan Family Ancestor’s negotiation tactics. He’s a textbook example of a Mortal Stream politician: he doesn’t refuse the demonic deal outright, he just shifts the blame and the work to the other party to protect his own reputation. And finally, enjoy Han Li’s silent, mounting horror at being dragged into this love quarrel. He’d rather face a cave full of demonic beasts than have to manage a love-struck junior disciple in front of her jealous suitors. It’s a wonderfully human moment for our usually calculating protagonist.
