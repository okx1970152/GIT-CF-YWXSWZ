## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists. Buckle up, because the moment we have all been waiting for has finally arrived. The tension in the Void Heaven Palace is thick enough to cut with a flying sword. The old devils are all in position, the Blood Jade Spider is out, and the Void Heaven Cauldron is sitting at the bottom of that terrifying ice-cold flame pit, just waiting to be yanked out. But nothing in *RMJI* ever goes smoothly, does it? Han Li is caught between a rock and several very hard, very old places. Extreme Yin is barking orders, Man Brutal Beard is ready to flex, and the scholar is watching like a hawk. Our boy is walking a razor’s edge, and the only card he has left is to play the waiting game and see if the chaos he is counting on actually arrives on schedule. Get ready for some heavy lifting.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li finally gets a clear look at the legendary **Void Heaven Cauldron**, which is submerged in the **Azure Blue Ice Flame** at the bottom of a deep, cold pit.
- Extreme Yin Patriarch explains the nature of the **Azure Blue Ice Flame**—an otherworldly cold fire that can refine even a Nascent Soul—establishing why the cauldron has been impossible to retrieve until now.
- The **Blood Jade Spider** is summoned and scrutinized by the old devils, who confirm it is the best tool for the job due to its grade and its unique webbing, which resists the ice flame.
- Extreme Yin Patriarch commands Han Li to begin the retrieval process, ordering the spider to wrap the cauldron in its web and pull.
- Extreme Yin Patriarch summons his two **Fire Pythons**, feeds them special pills, and uses a secret technique to turn them into living cables that descend into the pit to bite down on the cauldron.
- A tense, physical tug-of-war begins: the combined power of the spider and the twin pythons manages to shake the cauldron, but it remains too heavy to be lifted immediately.
- To tip the scales, Extreme Yin Patriarch calls on **Man Brutal Beard** to cast the **Berserker Technique** on the spider, a buff Han Li himself lacks the power to provide.
- The chapter ends on a cliffhanger as the incantation begins, with everything riding on this single, desperate pull. The author also signs off with a note about being tired, indicating this is the only chapter for the night.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The **Azure Blue Ice Flame (乾蓝冰焰)** is a prime example of a xianxia concept known as "Yin Fire" (阴火). In traditional Chinese cosmology, Yin and Yang are not just polar opposites but can manifest in unexpected ways. Fire is typically Yang—hot, bright, and expansive. Yin Fire subverts this expectation. It looks like a flame but possesses the extreme, soul-freezing properties of ultimate Yin. This is a common trope in cultivation novels to create a paradoxical hazard that defies common sense and requires specialized, often rare, counters to overcome. The fact that it is said to be "not of this realm" hints at an even higher order of power, making it a perfect, untouchable guardian for a supreme treasure.

The **Berserker Technique (狂暴之术)** is a classic auxiliary spell in the xianxia world designed for spirit beasts. It forcibly overclocks the creature's physical abilities, pushing it far beyond its normal limits for a short burst of power. However, it inevitably comes with a severe backlash—the beast is often exhausted, weakened, or even damaged afterward. This reflects the brutal, resource-expenditure logic of the cultivation world: you don't strengthen the beast for free; you burn its vitality as fuel for a single, critical operation. Man Brutal Beard casting it on Han Li's behalf is just another form of the old devils squeezing the maximum utility out of an asset they consider disposable.

## Reading Guide
This is the heavy machinery chapter! After all the suspense, lore, and power plays, we finally get to the physical act of trying to pry the treasure loose. Pay close attention to the dynamics here. Han Li is not a hero leading the charge; he is a specialist technician whose tool is being remotely operated by a committee of terrifying shareholders. His complete powerlessness—his internal calculation that he cannot even fake incompetence—is the core emotional reality of this scene. The real conflict is not between the spider and the cauldron, but the ticking clock: can the cauldron be pulled out before the Fire Pythons are consumed by the ice flame, and before one of the old devils decides they don't need Han Li anymore? Enjoy the tense, physical spectacle while it lasts, because the real fireworks will start the second that cauldron touches open air.

## Deeper Lore: The Artifact Arms Race
The retrieval method in this chapter is a perfect microcosm of xianxia engineering. Facing an environment that is "immune to magic power" (the ice flame), cultivators don't give up; they pivot to *physical* and *biological* solutions. The **Golden Silk Silkworm (金丝蚕)** is mentioned as another creature whose thread is immune to the flame, but it is too weak to pull the cauldron. The **Blood Jade Spider (血玉蜘蛛)** is the superior choice because it is both immune and stronger. This illustrates a recurring principle: there is always a counter, but the counter requires specific, rare resources to work at the required scale. The entire scene is a xianxia-style engineering problem: find the right material (spider silk), the right power source (spider + pythons), and the right buffs (Berserker Technique) to bypass a hard system rule (the flame's invulnerability to magic).
