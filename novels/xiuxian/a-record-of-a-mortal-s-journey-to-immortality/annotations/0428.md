## Chapter Overview
Get ready, fellow loot divers! The treasure hunt hits a critical milestone, but not before Han Li turns into a one-man pest control service with a grudge. After the giant insect swarm showed up, the strange man—our latest walking corpse candidate—takes one look at the sheer scale of the Gold Devourer Beetle cloud and decides discretion is the better part of valor. But Han Li is not in the mood to let debts go uncollected. What follows is a textbook Mortal Stream pursuit: a cold, methodical chase where Han Li burns through trump cards (sword fusion, lightning-laced counterattacks) to ensure this foe doesn’t wander off to become *next season’s* problem. The payoff? A neat pile of ashes, a suspicious skull he can’t quite toss out, and a crimson scroll that makes him freeze mid-air. Back at the formation site, the final restriction crumbles, and the team uncovers a legendary artifact—the Sealing Spirit Pillar—worth a king’s ransom in spirit stones. But in Han Li’s world, anything that valuable is rarely just a payday.

## Key Plot Points
* **Strategic Pursuit:** The strange man attempts to flee after seeing the overwhelming beetle swarm. Han Li, operating on a strict “no loose ends” policy, chases him down across a vast distance.
* **Sword Fusion Upgrade:** To close the gap, Han Li unleashes a new tactic—fusing seven small flying swords into a single giant green sword that dramatically boosts his flight speed.
* **The Kill:** A classic misdirection play: two swords split into four beams, drawing the enemy’s attention while a merged, silent sword delivers the killing blow, laced with golden lightning that turns the man to ash.
* **Loot Inspection:** Han Li secures three items from the remains: an ancient blue treasure, a storage pouch, and a mysterious crimson scroll that leaves him stunned and contemplative.
* **Skull Dilemma:** He retrieves the now-masterless ghost skull. Despite finding it sinister, he stores it away—a decision that perfectly embodies the Mortal Stream’s “hoard everything, throw away nothing” logic.
* **Final Restriction Broken:** The team successfully dismantles the last barrier, wiping out the residual “Chi-E” creatures.
* **The Sealing Spirit Pillar:** Shi Die identifies the central stone pillar as a legendary *Sealing Spirit Pillar*, a composite artifact made of over a dozen rare materials and valued at 5,000–6,000 spirit stones. The revelation brings both excitement and a new weight of significance to their discovery.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Sealing Spirit Pillar (封灵柱):** This is a classic trope in xianxia archaeology—a purpose-built artifact from an ancient era, designed to contain or suppress something inside a ruined site. It’s not just treasure; it’s a *warning label in stone*. The fact that Shi Die recognizes it instantly from her texts highlights a key genre skill: a cultivator’s true wealth isn’t just in magic swords, but in the rare, esoteric knowledge they possess. Knowing what it is versus knowing *what it’s sealing* are two very different things.

**Primordial Ancient Treasure (洪荒古宝):** The scroll Han Li loots is flagged as potentially belonging to this category. In the cultivation world hierarchy, “Primordial Ancient Treasures” are weapons from a bygone era. Unlike modern magic tools, they can’t be absorbed into the body or refined like a lifebound treasure, but their raw power output is often far superior. They are a mark of an owner with real pedigree—or, in Han Li’s case, a murderous and ambitious cultivator.

**Sword Fusion:** The technique where multiple flying swords merge into one is a common high-level tactic. It’s not just about adding power; it’s about changing the *properties* of the weapon—sacrificing versatility (multiple independent swords) for raw thrust and penetration power. This is a classic Han Li trade: immediate tactical advantage over long-term flexibility.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a brilliant showcase of the “cleanup phase” in a Mortal Stream arc. The battle is won, but the real tension lies in what Han Li *chooses to keep* and what it all *means*. Note how he doesn’t obsess over the scroll’s contents for too long—he files it away for later analysis, proving his priority is always the immediate tactical situation (getting back to the team) over gawking at mysterious loot. The Sealing Spirit Pillar’s value is openly stated, but a veteran reader knows the true story is just beginning: whatever that pillar is holding down is the real prize, and it’s never going to be free. The kill itself is a masterclass in leverage—Han Li doesn’t overpower the man with raw magic; he outlasts him, outmaneuvers him, and then executes with surgical precision. This is the template for how a mortal-stream cultivator wins: not by being the strongest, but by being the one who never stops chasing.
