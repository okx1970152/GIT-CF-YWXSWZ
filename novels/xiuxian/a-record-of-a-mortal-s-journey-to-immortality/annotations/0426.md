## Chapter Overview
Get ready for a sudden storm in the middle of the ocean! This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream tension: the team is literally one step away from cracking the formation when a lunatic crashes the party. Forget the quiet, scholarly ruin exploration—this is a chaotic free-for-all where tempers, greed, and a terrifyingly weird new magic tool are thrown into the blender. We see our cast of Core Formation cultivators forced into an unwanted alliance, and more importantly, we watch Han Li execute his signature move: staying back, keeping his head down, and letting the bigger fools fight it out. A new type of power—the Primordial Ancient Treasure—makes its explosive debut, completely changing the calculus of the fight and setting a new standard for what "valuable" means in this brutal world.

## Key Plot Points
1. **The Mad Island Master Appears:** Just as the team is about to activate their formation-breaking array, a mid-Core Formation cultivator with a wild appearance (white hair, leather vest, strange basket at his waist) flies in. He immediately claims the island as his territory and accuses the group of trespassing.
2. **Instant Violence and a Trap:** The Island Master ignores the overwhelming numbers against him and attacks Hu Yue with his basket. The basket proves to be a bizarre magic tool that *traps* Hu Yue's flying daggers instead of destroying them, showcasing a unique "capture" mechanic.
3. **The Fight Escalates:** Seeing their companion in a bind, Jin Qing, the Jian cultivator, and Han Li (with Qu Hun) all join the fray. They overwhelm the stranger with their combined attacks.
4. **The Island Master's Trump Card:** Facing defeat, the Island Master unleashes his true weapon: a "Primordial Ancient Treasure" in the form of a bamboo basket. In one move, it absorbs and immobilizes all their magic tools at once, demonstrating its overwhelming superiority over modern gear.
5. **The Grim Revelation:** The Jian cultivator identifies the opponent's treasure as an ancient artifact, igniting both fear and desire in the group. The killing intent among the "allies" subtly shifts from self-defense to treasure hunting.
6. **A Flesh-Skull Ritual:** As the team prepares their ultimate attacks (thunder magic, ghost banners), the Island Master bares his chest to reveal two *living, wriggling skulls* embedded in his flesh. He feeds them his own fingers, and they fly off to counter the incoming spells, revealing the terrifying, self-sacrificial cost of his power.
## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**All About Primordial Ancient Treasures (洪荒古宝):** The Jian cultivator's shout identifies a whole new category of magical items. In xianxia logic, the world's cultivation systems have evolved over eons. Modern treasures are refined with "current" techniques, are flexible, and can be absorbed into the body. Primordial Ancient Treasures, however, are relics from a forgotten era. They are rigid, are impossible to store inside the body (must be carried like a regular basket), and generally have a single, hyper-specialized power. However, that one power is terrifyingly potent. Think of it as a modern multi-tool versus a single-use, ancient, artifact-grade sledgehammer. The island master's "capture" basket is a perfect example—it doesn't just block; it fully restrains several magic tools at once.
## Reading Guide
This chapter introduces a fantastic piece of world-building that will color conflicts for a long time. Pay close attention to how Han Li reacts to the crisis—he's the only one focused on the *situation* rather than the *loot*. While the others hear "Ancient Treasure" and their eyes go wide with greed, Han Li's internal narration is all about "preserving his trump cards" and "not standing out." This is the quintessential Mortal Stream survival instinct. The chapter also establishes a brutal new threat style: the "self-mutilating caster." This island master doesn't just use a spell; he feeds his own fingers to his living skulls. It's a visceral reminder that in this world, power always has a price, and the price is often carved from your own body.
