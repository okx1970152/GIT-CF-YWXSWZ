## Chapter Overview

War is coming to the Yue Kingdom, and Han Li has just been handed a draft notice he can't refuse. Our favorite paranoid mortal finds himself conscripted into a mine-guarding mission alongside fellow cultivators from the other sects. The good news? It's not a suicide charge against demonic elites. The bad news? The revelation that the Six Demonic Sects have invaded is the official confirmation that the Yue Kingdom's cultivation world is about to enter a meat grinder. But rather than despair, Han Li does what he does best: pivots his entire cultivation strategy on the spot, shelving the Azure Origin Sword Art in favor of the Great Derivative Art and those wonderful, wonderful puppets he's been stockpiling. This is a quiet, logistical chapter—the kind that sets the table for a feast of chaos to come.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li is intercepted by Xuan Le of the Moon-Masking Sect, who produces a legitimate mobilization order signed by all Seven Sect leaders. Despite his reluctance, Han Li has no choice but to comply.
- Xuan Le recognizes Han Li from the Blood Forbidden Trial years ago, revealing that Han Li's reputation has lingered longer than he might have expected.
- Han Li reports the Yan Clan's demonic collusion and the massacre at Yanling Fort, handing off that hot potato to higher authorities.
- The mission: reinforce the guard at a medium-to-large spirit stone mine. The original defense is laughably weak—just one Foundation Establishment elder and a handful of Qi Condensation disciples.
- Xuan Le sets up layered defenses with a new formation, organizes patrols, and allows off-duty cultivators to cultivate freely—a competent commander.
- Han Li, in his off-time, performs a cold strategic recalibration: the Azure Origin Sword Art won't cut it until Core Formation. He shifts to the Great Derivative Art and puppet crafting for immediate survivability.
- The chapter closes with a brief author's note apologizing for a delayed update, transparent about the slow writing speed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The **Mobilization Order (征调令)** is a powerful piece of institutional authority in the xianxia sect system. Signed by all Seven Sect leaders, it represents a rare moment of unity—where factional rivalries are temporarily shelved to face an existential external threat. For a disciple like Han Li to refuse such an order would be to defy the entire alliance, which explains his grudging acceptance even though every instinct screams "run and hide." This is a classic Mortal Stream tension: the survival-driven protagonist forced by system mechanics into danger he would otherwise avoid.

The **Four Demon-Slaying Array (四煞阵)** is a mid-grade formation that balances offense and defense. In resource-constrained environments like a mine guard post, such a formation is a cost-effective multiplier, allowing a small squad to punch above its weight. It's not a one-shot-kill trump card, but a persistent layer of deterrence—perfect for the kind of "we can't cover all angles" situation Xuan Le faces.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a textbook example of the "quiet victory" phase in the Mortal Stream—where the real action is not a fight scene but a logistics and strategy scene. Han Li doesn't throw a single punch, yet the chapter is packed with cold calculation: threat assessment, resource optimization, and a complete overhaul of his cultivation roadmap. Pay attention to how he processes the trauma of his near-death encounter with the Ghost Spirit Sect Young Lord. He doesn't dwell on fear or shock; he immediately translates that terror into a concrete, multi-point plan (Great Derivative Art, puppet mastery) to close the gap. That's the Mortal Stream mentality in a nutshell: fear is data, and data drives action.

Also, keep an eye on the author's note at the end. For Western readers unfamiliar with Chinese web novel culture, these "leave request notes" (请假条) are a staple of the serialization ecosystem—an honest, sometimes apologetic acknowledgment that the writer is human and the next chapter is coming. It's a small window into the production reality behind the epic.
