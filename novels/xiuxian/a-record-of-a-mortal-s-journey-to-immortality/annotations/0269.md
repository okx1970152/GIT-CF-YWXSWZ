## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter delivers a brutal lesson in why you *really* don’t want to be on the receiving end of a prepped ritual spell. The Battle of the Spirit Mine takes a sharp, one-sided turn as the Devil Flame Sect unveils its terrifying trump card: the Green Sun Demon Flame. Our defending Seven Sect coalition, fresh off skirmishes, is suddenly staring into the abyss. We get a front-row seat to strategic retreats, shocking casualty rates, and a last-minute escape plan that feels almost too good to be true. And of course, it wouldn’t be the Mortal Stream without a sudden, catastrophic cave-in to remind everyone that the world has no intention of making survival easy. Han Li is left clawing his way out of the rubble, alone, reminding us all over again that in this world, a “lucky break” is usually just the prelude to a new kind of disaster.

## Key Plot Points
- Lü Tianmeng, recognizing the lethal danger of the ritual, orders a full retreat into the protective Four Fiends Formation. Xuan Le follows suit, but the withdrawal is chaotic; many cultivators are held in combat and cannot disengage.
- The Heavenly Fiend Sect opponents try to pin Han Li in place, but he coolly sacrifices his offensive momentum, uses his White Scale Shield to tank the assault, and makes a clean exit. A classic Han Li pragmatism move—live to fight another day.
- The red-robed Blazing Flame cultivators complete their ritual, merging thirteen streams of azure flame into a titanic fireball. They then split it into a volley of smaller fireballs that annihilate any Seven Sect disciple they touch. Magic tools and defensive spells are utterly useless; the demon flame consumes everything on contact.
- The seven sects suffer devastating losses in the first wave: over a dozen Qi Condensation disciples and one Foundation Establishment cultivator are erased without a trace.
- The demon flame wave then turns on the Four Fiends Formation itself. The formation holds initially, but under constant assault, the four-colored barrier begins to dim, signaling its imminent collapse.
- The defenders are thrown into a panic. Facing the prospect of capture by enemy sects known for vicious soul-extraction, a glimmer of hope appears when Yu Xing, a long-time resident, reveals the existence of a secret underground escape tunnel.
- The entire force retreats into the tunnel, hoping to escape to a point dozens of *li* away. But as they make their way through, a massive cave-in triggered by the surface battle buries them all.
- Hours later, Han Li is shown alive, crawling out of a hole in a stone wall, his lizard puppet beast having helped him escape certain death.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Green Sun Demon Flame (青阳魔火):** This is a classic example of a “high-cost, high-damage” tactic in the xianxia world. It’s not just a fireball; it’s a specialized technique (a *demon flame skill*) that requires a team of cultivators to synchronize their power through ritual banners. The novel explicitly states that casting it permanently reduces the user’s cultivation base. This reinforces a core Dark Forest combat principle: any spell that completely negates ordinary defenses must come with a terrifying price tag. The Blazing Flame cultivators are not just powerful—they are burning their own futures for this single battlefield advantage, a perfect illustration of the “kill the goose” economy of war in the Mortal Stream.

**The Four Fiends Formation (四煞阵):** A typical “layer cake” formation common in siege defense. It generates a multi-layered, often elemental barrier. The fact that it can withstand several rounds against this demon flame, but begins to fail under sustained punishment, highlights the resource attrition nature of xianxia battles. It’s not a “game over” button; it’s a consumable asset that buys time. The narrator’s clinical observation of its fading color is a perfect example of the Mortal Stream’s detached, logistical view of magical power.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in how to build tension and demonstrate a power gap without the hero instantly pulling a miraculous win. The Seven Sect coalition is *outclassed* in this tactical zone, and the smart response is to run. The entire sequence is a brutal cost-benefit demonstration: personal survival is prioritized over honor, objects (tools, formations) are consumed at a terrifying rate, and even a “safe” escape route becomes a death trap. Pay close attention to the kinds of enemies and strategies Han Li won’t fight. The mere presence of this ritual forces him to retreat, reinforcing that his power curve is still climbing, and that survival often hinges on knowing when *not* to fight. The sudden cave-in at the end is a classic reminder: even when the immediate enemy is behind you, the environment itself is a hostile agent in the Mortal Stream.
