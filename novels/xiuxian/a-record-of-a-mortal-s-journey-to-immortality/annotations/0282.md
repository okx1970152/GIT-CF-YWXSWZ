## Chapter Overview

Xin Ruyin’s guarded shock turns to a nervous, conditional yes. Han Li finds the formation master he needs, and she finds the medicine she needs—but this is no simple fairytale transaction. The chapter opens with a tense negotiation, where Han Li must convince the sharp-eyed Xin Ruyin he isn’t here to trap her, and she must confess she *can* repair the ancient teleportation array without overpromising. A half-year timeline is set, which immediately locks the next leg of Han Li’s journey into a precise countdown. The chapter closes with a soft but important social warning from Han Li, foreshadowing trouble from the dead men’s allies, and Qi Yunxiao and Xin Ruyin’s strange refusal to leave their home—a decision that places them squarely in harm’s path.

## Key Plot Points

1. **The Negotiation Dance**: Xin Ruyin is visibly torn between the hope of a cure and the terror of an unknown debt. She demands the request be “within conscience,” a red flag in Han Li’s experience but a fair boundary for her.
2. **Han Li’s Confession and Offer**: He admits his own uncertainty about her skill, revealing the ancient teleportation array diagram. This humility is a deliberate social tool—it lowers her guard and frames the task as a challenge, not a demand.
3. **Xin Ruyin’s Diagnosis**: She confirms the array is an “ancient teleportation array” and, more importantly, that only a small corner is damaged, which falls within her studied expertise. Half a year is all she needs.
4. **The Uneasy Departure**: Han Li warns the couple to move, as the dead cultivators’ allies will trace them. But Qi Yunxiao and Xin Ruyin show a troubling reluctance, leaving Han Li with a cold knot of unease.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Social Tribulation (社会试炼)**: This chapter is a masterclass in a core Mortal Stream conflict type: the social tribulation. Unlike a sword battle where you can see the enemy, this is a conversational duel where a wrong word can waste half a year or convert an ally into an enemy. Han Li performs a flawless operation—he calibrates his offer (a priceless herb), frames his demand as a technical puzzle, and uses humility to disarm suspicion. Xin Ruyin, for her part, runs her own risk assessment, checking her conscience threshold before accepting. Both understand that a favor in this world is a debt that can compound dangerously. The half-year timeline is not a schedule but a trap door; if she fails, both sides lose face and leverage.

**Ancient Teleportation Arrays**: In xianxia cosmology, teleportation is rare and expensive. "Ancient" arrays are usually remnants of a fallen, more advanced civilization, and their repair knowledge is often a lost art. That Xin Ruyin has studied even a fragment of one is a testament to her obsessive specialization. This isn't just a plot device; it signals that Han Li has found a genuine master of niche, high-value knowledge.

### Social Tribulation

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for some *smooth* negotiation, fellow Daoists. Han Li has finally found the woman who can unlock his path out of Yue, but the chapter is a reminder that every transaction in the Mortal Stream comes with a surveillance camera. Watch how he builds trust: he doesn't brag, he doesn't threaten, he just shows her the blueprint and asks if she likes puzzles. This is the "liar's poker" of immortal cultivation—everyone knows the stakes, and nobody blinks first. The real tension isn't the half-year wait; it's the idiot couple who aren't smart enough to move. You already know that bird is going to come home to roost.

Also, take a moment to appreciate Xin Ruyin's sharp mind. She catches Han Li's transient distraction and notes it without drama. She's playing her own game, and Han Li knows it. This is the kind of ally you want, and the kind you fear—competent enough to fix your life, sharp enough to see through your every mask.
