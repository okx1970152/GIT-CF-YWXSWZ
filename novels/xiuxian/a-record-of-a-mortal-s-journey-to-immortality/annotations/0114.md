## Chapter Overview

Ah, fellow daoist, welcome to a chapter of pure, unadulterated *social tribulation*! After the brutal efficiency of Han Li’s cultivation and his bloody exit from the Seven Mysteries Sect, we now find him in a far more treacherous environment: the inner politics of a wealthy mortal household. Chapter 114, "Yan Ge's Foolish Heart," serves as a masterclass in Han Li's other core skill set—his ability to read a room, don a mask, and use information asymmetry as his primary weapon. He is no longer a martial prodigy or a lone wolf; he is a shy, humble nephew, a role he plays with chilling precision. But the chapter’s true soul is the tragic, almost archaeological discovery of a genuine romantic—Senior Brother Yan Ge. Yan Ge is a relic of a simpler, more emotional world, a man who wears his heart on his sleeve for a woman who has long since moved on. Get ready for a chapter where the sharpest blade is a feigned blush, and the greatest monster is a broken heart. We are now deep in the 'Social Tribulation' arc, and the stakes are entirely different.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li spends an uneasy first night in the Mo Estate, remaining vigilant due to the uncertain attitude of his hosts.
- The next morning, he is fetched by Yan Ge, Doctor Mo's first disciple, who is talkative and curious about Han Li’s background.
- During their walk, they encounter Mo Yuzhu and Wu Jianming in the garden, a couple whose public display of affection irritates Han Li.
- Yan Ge is immediately rendered speechless by his infatuation with Yuzhu, leaving Han Li to handle the social introduction alone.
- Han Li deploys a masterful social bluff, posing as a shy, low-status relative seeking a favor, instantly making himself forgettable to the arrogant Wu Jianming.
- Mo Yuzhu expresses clear displeasure at Yan Ge’s persistent, open admiration, reinforcing the hopelessness of his feelings.
- After they leave, Han Li snaps Yan Ge out of his daze and listens to the man’s frank confession of a lifetime of unrequited love.
- Han Li evaluates Yan Ge as a ‘rare antique’—a character type he has only ever read about in stories.
- The chapter ends with them arriving at the small building where the wives of Doctor Mo await Han Li with a ‘grand surprise.’

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Social Tribulation (社会试炼):** This is a critical concept within the Mortal Stream genre that is sometimes overlooked. Unlike pure cultivation novels that focus solely on power levels and treasures, *RMJI* recognizes that navigating complex human relationships and political hierarchies is a form of conflict as dangerous as any battle. Han Li’s tactics here are not those of a warrior, but a spy. He reads the room, identifies the power dynamics (Wu Jianming is an arrogant noble, Yan Ge a lovesick fool), and selects a persona (the unassuming relative) that makes him a non-threat, a piece of furniture to be ignored. This is his primary method of controlling information in a low-power environment.

**The 'Play-Act' Han Li:** Note how Han Li’s internal ‘cold comfort’ is juxtaposed with his external ‘shy embarrassment.’ This is the defining characteristic of the late-stage Han Li. He has learned that the most effective concealment is not hiding in a cave, but hiding in plain sight, wearing a mask that makes others underestimate him. His immediate slip into a polite, hesitant demeanor after his sleepless night shows how instinctively he weaponizes his own persona.

**Yan Ge as a 'Type':** Yan Ge represents a stock character in Chinese fiction: the ‘love fool’ (情种, qing zhong). This is a figure of intense, often self-destructive romantic devotion. In the context of the cold, transactional world of cultivation, Yan Ge is a fascinating anachronism. His emotional honesty is a stark contrast to Han Li’s calculated performance, and his tragedy is that his world (and the woman he loves) has moved on to harsher, more practical realities. He is the last of a dying breed in this world of power and scheming.

### Social Tribulation

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

### Social Bluffing

A core survival tactic in the Mortal Stream, where a protagonist uses ambiguous information and an opponent's own expectations to force a favorable outcome without physical violence.

### Ascension Assembly

A competitive gathering where low-level cultivators compete for the right to join an immortal sect; referenced here as a path for those unable to break through naturally.

### Radiance Pill

A fragrant pill associated with imperial concubines, known for its long-lasting aroma and mosquito-repelling effects.

## Reading Guide

- **Watch the Mask Slip:** Pay close attention to the transition from Han Li’s internal annoyance to his external performance. The gap between what he thinks and what he says is the primary source of tension and humor in this chapter.
- **Pity the Senior Brother:** Yan Ge is not a fool; he is a victim of his own virtue. In a world that rewards cunning, his sincerity is his fatal flaw. Try to see his confession not as pathetic, but as a quiet, desperate act of defiance against a world that has moved on without him.
- **The 'Cold Comfort' is Key:** When Han Li offers a quote from the *Book of Songs* (诗经), it is not genuine empathy; it is a calculated social lubricant. He is ‘handling’ an emotional situation with a polished, classical response. This is a glimpse into his growing arsenal of social tools, borrowed from the classics to smooth over awkward human interactions.
- **Foreshadowing the 'Surprise':** The final line about the wives waiting to give Han Li a “grand surprise” is a classic xianxia cliffhanger. In the Mortal Stream, a “surprise” from authority figures is rarely good news. It is a promise of a new test, a new negotiation, or a new trap for the protagonist to navigate.
