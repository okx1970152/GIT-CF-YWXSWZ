## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow void-crossers! After the chaos of the teleportation, Han Li finally lands on a tiny island in the vast, unknown Star Seas. This chapter is a classic “set up shop” breather—but if you think our favorite paranoid cultivator is going to kick back and enjoy the sea breeze, think again. He scopes out the local mortal town, picks out the best mountain real estate, and then proceeds to dig an entire secret underground base complex linking two peaks. Oh, and he buries the Heaven Vial in a hidden garden and starts thinking about raising a demonic core puppet army. Standard Tuesday.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li lands at a small eastern harbor town, introduces himself to the local elder as the assigned “Immortal Master” for the island’s protective formation.
- He learns the town’s spirit stone contribution is negligible; he will have to fund the entire formation himself—a cold calculation that he files away without complaint.
- He inspects the two local peaks: one tall (denser qi) and one short (sturdier, with a pre-existing cave). He chooses the shorter peak for his decoy cave and the taller one for his true secret dwelling.
- He spends two full days digging a hidden underground tunnel connecting the two peaks, using his magic tools and Qu Hun’s brute strength.
- He sets up a “Reversed Five Elements Formation” at the decoy entrance, then adds two more large-scale formations (Heavenly Wind Furious Array, Phantom Form Heaven Net Array) to secure the entire area against intruders.
- Inside the true cave, he plants spirit seeds, buries the Heaven Vial under a broken treasure fragment, and sets up insect chambers for his white spiders and other strange insects.
- He reviews the “External Incarnation” technique from the Yue Emperor’s jade slip, analyzes its risks, and decides to wait until he has trained the Great Derivative Art to the second layer.
- He plans to first restore his cultivation to early Foundation Establishment, then practice the External Incarnation, and finally pivot to the “Three Revolutions Heavy Origin Art” as his path toward Core Formation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**身外化身 (External Incarnation):** This is a legendary Daoist and xianxia concept where a cultivator splits their consciousness to create an independent second body. In classical mythology, this is the ultimate “one becomes many” god-tier move. But in the Mortal Stream, as Han Li bluntly notes, the version he has is just a fancy puppet that can cultivate on its own—a far cry from the myth. That said, even a “just a puppet” that reaches Core Formation is a game-changer in this resource-starved world.

**分神 (Soul Divide):** The ability to split one’s consciousness to control multiple magical tools or puppets simultaneously. It is a high-level art that only Foundation Establishment cultivators or above can even attempt. The Great Derivative Art is a specialized mental cultivation technique that strengthens the divine sense to enable this skill.

**煞丹 (Demonic / Malign Core):** A dark-aligned alternative to the true Golden Core (金丹). A Demonic Core is formed using extreme methods—often blood sacrifice, forbidden arts, or special pills like the Blood Condensation Five Elements Pill. It is inferior to a true Golden Core in purity but still confers massive power, making it a viable path for a puppet or a desperate cultivator.

## Reading Guide
This is a “breather and blueprint” chapter—don’t expect explosions or betrayals. Instead, enjoy watching Han Li methodically build his new fortress in an unknown sea. Every detail, from the hidden tunnel to the triple formation layers to the insect chambers, screams “I have learned my lesson from every single ambush I’ve survived.” The highlight is his cold, rational breakdown of the External Incarnation technique: he acknowledges the risk, calculates the odds, and chooses a patient, staged approach. This is peak Han Li—no flashy heroics, just a long-term plan to stay alive and get stronger. Also, note how he buries the Heaven Vial with a broken treasure fragment as camouflage—paranoia is a survival trait in this world.

If you’re new to the Mortal Stream, this chapter is a great example of the “quiet victory” phase: the protagonist spends resources and time building infrastructure that will pay off hundreds of chapters later. No instant gratification—just cold, patient preparation.
