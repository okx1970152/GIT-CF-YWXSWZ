## Chapter Overview
Breathe in. Breathe out. The mortal stream is alive and well. After the high-octane brutality of the Blood Forbidden Trial, Han Li does something almost unheard of in heroic fantasy: he sleeps, then plans. This chapter is a slow, satisfying inhalation, a deliberate pause that shows exactly what makes *A Record of a Mortal's Journey* tick. Our boy didn't just survive; he *won*, and now he's cashing in his chips with the cold, logistical precision of a master accountant. We get a full inventory of his spoils, a peek at his new, unusable trophies, and the satisfying sting of institutional betrayal when his promised reward is gutted right in front of him. But does Han Li rage? Of course not. He files it away, collects his single Foundation Establishment Pill, and quietly begins his multi-year march toward the alchemy lab. This is the quiet before the storm, and it’s absolutely riveting.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li wakes from a blissful, dreamless sleep after his return from the Forbidden Land, having achieved his primary goal of survival.
- He immediately begins a methodical inventory of his gains: a solid haul of spirit stones, a pile of assorted magical tools, and two items of intense curiosity—a mysterious silver page and an Archer Puppet.
- The Archer Puppet is revealed to be a high-grade tool that is currently useless to him; it requires a Soul Division technique that is only viable for Foundation Establishment cultivators, highlighting the structural power ceiling of Qi Condensation.
- The silver page, a trophy from the Giant Sword Sect cultivator, remains an unsolvable puzzle for now.
- Three years pass in a montage. News arrives that his reward has been slashed to a single Foundation Establishment Pill, with the standard “filial offering” excuse. Han Li accepts this with cold, internal derision.
- During this time, Junior Sister Chen successfully breaks through to Foundation Establishment, while her brother fails and returns to his clan.
- Han Li’s reputation as “that lucky guy who found all the herbs and got a Core Formation master” slowly fades.
- After three years of preparation, Han Li’s alchemical ingredients are fully ripe. He heads to Yuelu Hall to rent an Earth Fire room, cleverly leveraging his status as Patriarch Li’s disciple-on-probation to smooth over the transaction with the notoriously ugly steward.
- The chapter ends with Han Li proactively paying a mid-grade spirit stone as a deposit for a long-term Earth Fire rental, his plans finally set in motion.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Economics of a Bottleneck** — This chapter is a masterclass in the 'Bottleneck Economy' that defines the cultivation world. Han Li isn't just hoarding herbs; he's engaging in long-term capital planning. The fact that it took him *three years* just to prepare the ingredients highlights that true advancement (like Foundation Establishment) isn't just about finding a pill; it's about navigating a brutal supply chain that requires enormous time, resources, and institutional access. The single pill he received from the sect is a pittance compared to the empire of investment he's building himself.

**Soul Divide vs. Spiritual Sense** — The distinction Han Li makes here is crucial. What most Qi Condensation cultivators do is use their *outward projection* of spiritual sense to scan a room. True ‘Soul Divide’ is a permanent mutation of the soul, essentially creating a secondary consciousness that lets a cultivator control multiple magic tools simultaneously. It’s like the difference between glancing at a clock and growing a second pair of hands. This is why Han Li can’t use the Archer Puppet: his soul isn’t ‘big’ or ‘strong’ enough to be split without shattering.

**The 'Big Tree' Mentality** — The phrase *da shu di xia hao cheng liang* (大树底下好乘凉), or "enjoying the cool shade under a big tree," is a fundamental principle of Chinese social and political strategy. It's not just lazy nepotism; it's a calculated survival tactic. By barely even being acknowledged by Master Li, Han Li has passively acquired a priceless asset: a reputation that makes people like the ugly steward and Old Man Ye think twice before messing with him. In the Dark Forest, even the *perception* of a connection is a potent shield.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the ultimate example of why Han Li is such a compelling protagonist. He’s not the one charging in with a sword or a flashy technique. His victory here is one of *process*. He successfully planned, executed, and is now calmly entering the maintenance phase of a massive project. Notice how he handles the theft of his reward. There is no dramatic confrontation. He simply makes a cold note of the debt and moves on. The disciple-on-probation status that was meant to placate him is now his most active tool, used to grease the wheels of the bureaucracy. Every interaction, from the slumbering steward to the bribed official, is a piece on his board. This is the slow, grinding, deeply satisfying core of the Mortal Stream. Get ready, because the next big phase—the actual alchemy—is about to begin, and after three years of prep, Han Li is not about to let anything go wrong.
