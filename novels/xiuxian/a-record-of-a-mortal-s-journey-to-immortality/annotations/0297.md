## Chapter Overview

Han Li pulls off a textbook social infiltration—no explosions, no sword lights, just a quiet, cold surgical strike on the weakest link. After tailing the Prince’s household from a distance, he slips past mortal guards, puts a maid under a mind-control spell that demanding conditions make nearly unusable for most, and extracts the exact intel he needs in a single breath. The chapter’s real reward is not the answer itself but the calm, clinical way Han Li navigates a high-risk social environment, confirming that even without a single fight, the Mortal Stream’s tension is razor-sharp whenever the protagonist is surrounded by unknowns who might—or might not—be lethally connected.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li vanishes from his tree perch, uses both Invisibility Technique and the rare Qi Attraction Art (a spell that mortals can’t detect and lower-level cultivators can’t sense) to ghost through the manor.
- Instead of brute-force interrogation, he picks a specific maid—the one the Prince sent to fetch the young prince during the banquet—reasoning that a servant with direct access to the master is his best intel vector.
- Han Li uses the Mind Control Art, a Foundation Establishment spell listed as “top-ten chicken ribs” because it only works on mortals and requires abnormal divine sense; he casually masters it where most peers can’t even qualify.
- In a rapid Q&A, he learns that Intendant Wang (the powerful steward) has been the Prince’s lifelong companion, rarely leaves the manor, and most crucially, that the young prince did a total personality flip at age ten—from violent and abusive to polite and deferential.
- Han Li immediately codes that behavioral shift as suspicious, confirming his hunch that the pair is hiding something dangerous, but still judges them unlikely to be Demonic Path plants (since the change happened too long ago to be a fresh operation).
- After dismissing the maid, he tracks down Immortal Master Wu’s courtyard, and as the old Daoist returns to his room, Han Li is already sitting inside, smiling.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Mind Control Art (控神术) – The Chicken Rib Classic**
This spell is one of the most emblematic “chicken rib” (tasteless to eat, a pity to discard) techniques in xianxia. It *sounds* incredible—absolute obedience from a target as long as you’re not a cultivator—but its restrictions are so brutal that most Foundation Establishment cultivators can’t even practice it. It *only* works on mortals (any cultivator can dispel it by circulating qi through their mind), *and* it demands abnormally strong divine sense. So by the time you’re a cultivator powerful enough to use it, mortals are beneath your notice, and against another cultivator it’s useless. Han Li’s ability to use it effortlessly is a quiet flex: his divine sense is already far above his peer group, a hidden edge that most of his opponents never see coming.

**Qi Attraction Art (引气决) – The Foundation Establishment Masquerade**
This is the stealth technique of the Foundation Establishment tier. By withdrawing the cultivator’s spiritual aura into the body, they appear as an ordinary mortal to anyone at a lower cultivation level. In a world where spiritual sense is a primary surveillance tool, this is the ultimate social camouflage. Han Li layering *both* a mortal-grade Invisibility Spell and a cultivator-grade Qi Attraction Art is a sign that he treats information security as a war, not a convenience.

**The Personality-Flip Diagnostic**
Han Li’s instant suspicion of the young prince’s ten-year-old transformation is a fascinating window into the Mortal Stream’s paranoia logic. In a real-world setting, a violent child becoming polite might be seen as maturation or a good tutor. In Han Li’s world, any drastic behavioral shift in someone connected to power is read as a hostile takeover, a possession, or a cult indoctrination. The fact that he doesn’t dismiss it outright but files it as “not Demonic Path” is a testament to his cold, probabilistic reasoning.

### Social Tribulation (社会试炼)

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where a protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in “social tribulation”—the kind of high-stakes conflict that doesn’t involve a single sword swing but is every bit as tense as a pitched battle. Watch how Han Li’s entire toolkit is tailored for information warfare: he picks the right target (the maid with access, not a random servant), uses a spell most cultivators dismiss as worthless, and extracts intel in under a minute. The payoff is not a treasure map or a power-up—it’s a psychological profile of the enemy’s base. That is pure Mortal Stream efficiency: knowing your threat’s backstory, their timeline of odd behavior, and their degree of isolation is worth more than a shiny new Talisman Treasure. Also, note the quiet horror of the Mind Control Art: the maid has no memory of serving him and wakes up disoriented, utterly violated without even knowing it happened. That’s the Mortal Stream’s version of a clean job.
