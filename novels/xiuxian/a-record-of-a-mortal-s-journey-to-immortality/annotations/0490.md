## Chapter Overview

Let the battle royale commence! Chapter 490 kicks off the long-awaited showdown for the Void Heaven Cauldron, and it’s a masterclass in Mortal Stream escalation. Forget fair fights and alliance pacts—this is a four-way brawl where every Nascent Soul elder has a hidden card, every alliance is a temporary convenience, and the moment someone shows weakness, the vultures circle. Man Brutal Beard unveils a game-changing trump card that rewrites the power balance on the spot, forcing the righteous alliance to eat their own words. Meanwhile, Han Li—the *real* prize of this whole affair—is forced to hang back and watch the fireworks, fully aware that he’s just a bargaining chip in a game far above his pay grade. And lurking off-stage, a thousand-year-old ghost is waiting for his moment to strike. Buckle up, because this is where the Void Heaven Palace arc hits its boiling point.

## Key Plot Points

- Man Brutal Beard outright rejects Wan Tianming’s proposal to split the Void Heaven Cauldron evenly, choosing violence over diplomacy.
- Wan Tianming, expecting an easy numerical advantage, attacks first but is stunned when his magical tool is instantly turned to stone by the Three-Eyed Leopard’s new ability.
- Exposition: the chapter clearly distinguishes between “Ancient Wild Breeds” and “Mutated Spirit Beasts,” with the latter being a once-in-a-thousand-years rarity whose abilities can single-handedly overturn a power balance.
- The revelation that the Three-Eyed Leopard has undergone an Anomalous Transformation reshapes the battlefield: Extreme Yin and the Confucian-robed elder immediately abandon thoughts of a fair split and commit to an all-out attack alongside Man Brutal Beard.
- Tianwuzi engages the Confucian-robed elder’s Blue Thorn Birds with the Spirit-Turning Turtle’s freezing mist, while the gaunt elder uses a bizarre willow-branch technique to trap Extreme Yin’s Heavenly Corpses in inescapable green silk bindings.
- Han Li retreats to the edges, fully aware of his own vulnerability: if the righteous side decides to kill him—the owner of the Blood Jade Spider—he is dead before anyone can save him.
- The Profound Bone Saint remains a silent observer below the altar, his intentions completely opaque, leaving Han Li to quietly resent the old fox’s calculations.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Anomalous Transformation (异化术) vs. Ancient Wild Breeds (蛮荒异种) – The Two Rare-Beast Systems**

One of the most succinct bit of xianxia bestiary lore in recent chapters. The novel explicitly draws a line between two kinds of ultra-rare creatures:

- **Ancient Wild Breeds** are the last surviving lineages of ancient species that have changed over time. They are scarce, but they are a *breed*—multiple individuals exist somewhere. Finding them is the hard part.
- **Mutated Spirit Beasts** are unique individuals that randomly mutated during an advancement. No two are alike. Their abilities can be game-breaking (like turning treasure-grade objects into *stone*), but they are one-in-ten-thousand lottery wins that only happen above Grade 4.

The practical implication for the cultivation economy is brutal: you cannot *plan* for a mutant beast. You can nurture a spirit beast for centuries and never see a mutation. It’s a systemic lottery that, when won, instantly elevates a mid-tier cultivator to a line that even Nascent Soul elders must respect. Man Brutal Beard’s Three-Eyed Leopard just became that lottery ticket.

**The Stone-Transformation Ability – Where Daoist Alchemy Meets Xianxia Weaponry**

The leopard’s ability to turn magical tools to stone is a *direct counter* to the treasure-heavy combat doctrine of high-level cultivators. In Daoist alchemical thought, stone represents the primordial, unchanging state of matter—by “reverting” a refined magical artifact back to base rock, the beast is essentially imposing an *anti-alchemy* rule on the battlefield. This is not a matter of brute force; it’s a systemic override that bypasses the user’s cultivation level entirely. It’s a reminder that in the Mortal Stream, a single well-placed rule-break can neutralize mountains of raw power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a brilliant example of how *RMJI* escalates conflict not by raising numbers, but by revealing *new rules*. The battle doesn’t get bigger because the cultivators shout louder; it gets bigger because a single hidden card—a mutated beast with a bizarre ability—forces everyone to recalculate their entire strategy in real time. Watch how Wan Tianming’s confidence evaporates the moment his “advantage” is obsolete. Watch how Extreme Yin instantly switches from “maybe I should bail” to “all-in.” And above all, watch Han Li, the nominal owner of the key that everyone is fighting over, stand at the sidelines *doing his best impression of a potted plant*—because that’s exactly what he is in this scenario: a high-value asset that cannot afford to be noticed as a liability. The Profound Bone Saint’s silent presence is the cherry on top: the real wild card hasn’t even moved yet. Get ready, fellow Daoists—this arc is about to get *expensive*.
