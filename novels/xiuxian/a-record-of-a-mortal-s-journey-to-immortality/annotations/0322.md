## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream confrontation. The Yellow Maple Valley squad, having just discovered the Four Symbols Formation’s hidden weak point, blasts their way out and finds themselves face-to-face with the Black Fiend Sect’s reinforcements. Old acquaintances reappear—Green Pattern, the smooth-talking Daoist from the Tai Nan Meeting, and Tie Luo, the bald-headed brute who fought Han Li before—and the tensions of the past finally come into sharp, lethal focus. What starts as a tactical break-out quickly escalates into personal vendetta as Han Li pieces together the truth behind an old assassination attempt. The chapter delivers a satisfying collision of past and present, layered with clinical battle analysis, strategic bluffing, and the cold fury of a man who finally has his target in sight.

## Key Plot Points
- Liu Jing confirms the Four Symbols Formation’s weak point and orders a concentrated artifact strike, breaking the barrier and releasing the trapped squad.  
- The outside world reveals three powerful Foundation Establishment cultivators guarding the palace: Green Pattern, Tie Luo, and the Ice Fiend.  
- Tie Luo is shocked to recognize Han Li, mistakenly labelling him a Thousand Bamboo School member, revealing the old confusion about Han Li’s puppetry skills and drawing all eyes to him.  
- Han Li finally puts together the pieces: Green Pattern’s exaggerated friendliness at the Tai Nan Meeting was a cover for a plot to assassinate him via yellow-robed pursuers, and his intent to kill the Daoist crystallizes into cold determination.  
- Liu Jing calls out the enemy’s stalling tactic and orders an immediate attack, plunging everyone into a full-scale melee.  
- Han Li and another Yellow Maple Valley disciple flank Green Pattern, and Han Li launches a devastating multi-artifact strike to probe the Daoist’s defense.  
- Green Pattern reveals his ‘Greenwood True Shield,’ a pentagonal prism barrier that he claims is impervious to all Foundation Establishment attacks, taunting Han Li with the news that only Core Formation power can break it.  
- The scheming lives of the Black Fiend Sect’s Blood Attendants are exposed: Ye She has been training in the Blood Prison instead of guarding the gate, leaving the Ice Fiend isolated and forced to buy time alone.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Four Symbols Formation (四象阵)** draws its name from the Four Celestial Symbols of ancient Chinese cosmology—Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, and Black Tortoise—representing the four cardinal directions and the arrangement of stars in the night sky. In xianxia fiction, formations named after these symbols are almost always defensive or trapping arrays that embody cycles, balance, and illusion. Breaking one requires more than brute force; it demands spotting the flaw in its elemental flow, which is exactly what Han Li does here. The fact that the enemy’s formation was shoddily maintained because some of its operators had been killed is a classic Mortal Stream touch: no magic is perfect; systems fail when personnel fail.

**The “Greenwood True Shield”** is another fine example of how xianxia translates classical philosophical concepts into combat mechanics. The term “Greenwood” (青木) evokes the Wood element of the Five Phases, associated with growth, vitality, and resilience in the cycle of creation. A Wood-attribute defensive barrier would naturally be tenacious and self-repairing, making it the worst possible match for a direct frontal assault. Han Li’s willingness to probe it regardless, and his cold silence at Green Pattern’s taunt, signals that this isn’t the end of the fight—it’s the beginning of a layered attrition battle where resource management and tactical adaptation will matter more than raw power.

## Reading Guide
- This chapter is a “truth bomb” payoff for attentive readers who have been wondering about the yellow-robed assassins and the suspiciously generous treatment Han Li received during the Tai Nan arc. Green Pattern’s revelation of his role in the plot should hit like a hammer if you’ve been tracking the long-term mystery.  
- Han Li’s reaction to the truth is restrained but lethal: no dramatic speech, no rage—just a cold, immediate operational shift to killing intent. This is textbook Mortal Stream: the protagonist processes betrayal as data, then acts on the calculation without emotional waste.  
- The chapter also introduces a juicy piece of faction drama among the enemy ranks: the four Blood Attendants are far from a unified team. Ye She prioritizes personal cultivation over duty, forcing the Ice Fiend to scramble. In a Dark Forest environment, disunity among the predators can be a fatal vulnerability for the prey to exploit.  
- Finally, pay attention to how Green Pattern uses Han Li’s first name without any honorific—“Han Li”—a deliberate social signal designed to create confusion and imply a pre-existing intimacy that might make his own side unsettled and Han Li’s allies suspicious. It’s a small but sharp psychological weapon in a world where every syllable can be a trap.
