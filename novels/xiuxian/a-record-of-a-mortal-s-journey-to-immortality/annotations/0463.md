## Chapter Overview
The scramble for Han Li’s allegiance reaches its climax as three Nascent Soul figures—each with their own dark agenda—pour promises and threats into his ears. Trapped between a rogue hermit’s hollow inheritance, a demonic patriarch’s luxurious but predatory offer, and a vengeful ghost’s lethal blackmail, Han Li must choose his poison. The chapter showcases the brutal reality of being a lower-realm cultivator caught among ancient powers, where every “gift” comes with strings made of razor wire. Get ready for a masterclass in survival politics, fellow Daoist—sometimes the smartest move is the one that keeps you alive long enough to make a better one later.

## Key Plot Points
- **Qing Yi Hermit offers nominal mentorship**: his pitch is that he has no disciples and his inheritance will be Han Li’s, but Extreme Yin Patriarch immediately undercuts him, revealing the hermit’s poverty.
- **Extreme Yin Patriarch makes a lavish counter-offer**: he dangles authority, resources, beautiful female servants, and even promises to capture Fairy Zi Ling as a dual-cultivation furnace for Han Li.
- **Profound Bone Saint issues a veiled threat from the shadows**: he reminds Han Li that only he knows the formula for the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng pill, and that joining Extreme Yin is necessary for their mutual plan to destroy the patriarch.
- **Han Li weighs his options internally**: he finds the hermit’s offer cleanest, scorns the patriarch’s bribes, but fears the Profound Bone Saint’s leverage; he ultimately decides to accept Extreme Yin’s nominal discipleship as the least-bad path for the time being.
- **Extreme Yin gives Han Li the Profound Yin Ring**: an ancient treasure that serves as both a token and a defensive artifact, but the Profound Bone Saint immediately warns Han Li that it is countered by a matching Yang Ring on the patriarch’s person.
- **The group arrives at the Ice and Fire Path canyon**: over thirty Core Formation cultivators are waiting, including Yuan Yao, who shows visible unease at Han Li’s new association with demonic elders; Han Li sits beside Extreme Yin under his watchful gaze.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The “Nominal Disciple” (记名弟子) Trap**: In cultivation fiction, taking a *disciple on probation* or *nominal disciple* is a low-commitment relationship that can be dissolved at any time. Extreme Yin’s offer to make Han Li a nominal disciple first, then hold a formal ceremony later, is a deliberate power play: it gives the patriarch full control over the timeline while extracting loyalty from Han Li immediately, with no guarantee of true inheritance. This system is pervasive in xianxia—a lower cultivator may be strung along indefinitely as a glorified pawn.

**The Profound Yin Ring / Yang Ring Weakness (玄阴环/阴阳环)**: The concept of paired treasures (阴阳环) is rooted in classical Chinese cosmology, where Yin and Yang are complementary opposites. In a martial context, such paired artifacts are designed to be used together; but when one person holds the controlling Yang Ring while the other holds a subordinate Yin Ring, the weaker party is structurally vulnerable. The Profound Bone Saint’s warning reveals that Extreme Yin deliberately gave Han Li a leash, not a gift.

**Why the Formula for Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng Alone Matters (九曲灵参)**: In xianxia alchemy, rare spiritual herbs often require specialized processing steps that are not widely known. The Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng is a legendary, sentient plant that can even transform to avoid capture. Without the secret formula to refine it into a pill, the raw herb is worthless—or even dangerous. The Profound Bone Saint’s leverage over Han Li rests entirely on this information monopoly, a classic “knowledge-is-power” trope in the cultivation world.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a textbook example of the Mortal Stream’s core tension: Han Li is not a Chosen One who can defy the strong; he is a small fish forced to swim among sharks. Pay attention to how his internal calculation prioritizes long-term survival over short-term gain. He despises Extreme Yin’s promises but accepts them because the only alternative (the Profound Bone Saint’s threat) is immediate death. The irony is that the “gift” of the Profound Yin Ring is revealed as a lock before the chapter even ends. Also note Wu Chou’s hostility—Han Li has just gained a powerful patron, but also a dangerous rival. The setting of the Ice and Fire Path canyon, with its eerie red-and-blue split, promises a deadly trial ahead. This chapter sets the stage for one of the series’ great dungeon crawls, where political intrigue and elemental peril will collide.
