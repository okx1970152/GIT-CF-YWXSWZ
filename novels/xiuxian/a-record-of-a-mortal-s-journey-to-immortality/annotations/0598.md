## Chapter Overview

Welcome back to the Netherworld Domain, fellow survivors! Chapter 598 is a masterclass in Mortal Stream *aftermath management*. Han Li has just eliminated a threat (goodbye, Elder Feng), but instead of a victory lap, we get a tense, quiet scene of preparation and politics. The chapter’s true weight isn’t in the kill—it’s in the knock on the door that follows. Three mysterious cultivators arrive with a proposition that could redefine the entire power structure of this cursed, mana-sealed village. Get ready for a shift from pure survival to calculated alliance-building.

## Key Plot Points

*   **Silent Elimination:** Han Li uses a tricolored Gold Devourer Beetle to kill the middle-aged man, Feng, with a swift, silent strike. The kill is clean and leaves no direct evidence.
*   **A Moment of Rest:** After the assassination, Han Li returns to his dwelling to find Mei Ning asleep. He covers her with a beast hide, a rare, quiet gesture of care. The memory of their earlier kiss lingers, but he does not dwell on it.
*   **Preparation for the Cold:** Han Li tasks Mei Ning with sewing high-grade beast hides into protective clothing against the yin wind, revealing his methodical planning for the harsh environment.
*   **Unexpected Visitors:** A knock at the door brings three cultivators: the long-bearded old man (Five Dragons Sea), a talisman master from Heavenly Talisman Sect, and a cultivator from Four Seas True Academy.
*   **A Politicized Death:** The visitors reveal that a strange insect killed Elder Feng during the night. They imply they suspect Han Li’s involvement, but they are not there to accuse him—they are there to recruit him.
*   **The Offer of Power:** The three cultivators propose that the supernatural residents of the village unite to overthrow the Great Elder and seize control of the village and its precious yin beast crystals.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The Politics of a Mana-Sealed Village

This chapter provides a fascinating look at the social structure within a community stripped of its supernatural power. The “Great Elder” is not necessarily the strongest cultivator, but the one who arrived first and consolidated power over the material resources (the yin beast crystals). This creates a unique power dynamic where cultivators, who would normally dominate mortals, are reduced to a subordinate class. The “insult” the cultivators feel is a core theme: in the xianxia world, strength is destiny. To be forced to obey mere mortals is a violation of the natural order as they see it. This entire subplot highlights the Mortal Stream’s emphasis on resource control and social maneuvering over raw magical power.

### The Significance of the Golden Devourer Beetle

We see the Gold Devourer Beetle deployed as a perfect assassin. Its ability to squeeze through cracks, its silent attack, and its near-impenetrable hide make it an ideal tool for covert operations. This usage reinforces its status not just as a combat pet but as a multi-purpose strategic asset. Han Li’s calm control, even with a wisp of fading divine sense, shows his mastery over this tricky tool.

### Netherworld Domain

A realm of death and yin energy sealed by a mana-eruption, where cultivators cannot use their powers.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pivot point. The elimination of Elder Feng is a clean, satisfying resolution to a local threat, but it’s the political aftershock that matters. The arrival of the three cultivators introduces a new layer of complexity to the Netherworld Domain arc. Han Li is being offered a shortcut to influence and resources, but at the cost of a dangerous alliance.

Notice the contrast between Han Li’s cold, efficient murder and the domestic quiet of the scene with Mei Ning. This juxtaposition is classic *RMJI*—the protagonist can be a ruthless killer one moment and a pragmatic caretaker the next, so long as both actions serve his survival. His decision to cover her with a blanket is not born of romance but of practical asset management. Keep an eye on this offer—will Han Li join the conspiracy, or will he calculate that isolation is still the safer path?
