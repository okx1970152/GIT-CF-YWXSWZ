## Chapter Overview

The winds of the Chaotic Star Sea are stirring, and the Celestial Sound Sect is assembling its strike team. What began as a straightforward revenge mission has suddenly escalated. A new, terrifying name has been dropped into the mix: the Old Fiend Chi Huo of Yuan Gui Island. This chapter is all about the layers of power and the shadows they cast. Our man Han Li, ever the silent observer, finds himself in the middle of a camp that’s more volatile than he first thought. Get ready for a chapter that’s a masterclass in setup, dripping with tension and the subtle maneuvering of cultivator politics.

## Key Plot Points

*   Han Li feels a familiar resonance with a female cultivator named Zhuo Ruting. She reminds him of Nangong Wan.
*   The expedition is delayed. Madam Fan and Zhuo Ruting are anxiously waiting for a late arrival, which causes some frustration among the hired help.
*   A massive, ominous black cloud arrives, carrying a woman in purple and the dreaded Old Fiend Chi Huo.
*   The woman in purple, revealed to be Wang Ning—the Ziling Fairy—explains that she hired Chi Huo to counter an intelligence report of a late Core Formation leader at the enemy’s base.
*   Wang Ning promises the position of sect leader to whichever of the two sisters helps her avenge their mother’s death, revealing the high stakes of the internal politics within the Celestial Sound Sect.
*   The group finally departs, flying for half a month to a desolate island staging point, ready to strike the bandits’ nest at dawn.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Let’s talk about this chapter’s ultimate wildcard: the Old Fiend Chi Huo. In western high fantasy, a powerful ally is often a simple boon. In the world of *Mortal Stream* xianxia, inviting a ‘demon’ character like Chi Huo is a classic move that comes with enormous risk. The phrase “请神容易送神难” (inviting a deity is easy, seeing them off is difficult) is central here. These high-level heretical cultivators are not bound by sect loyalty or righteous codes. They are transaction-based predators. You hire them for their power, but you are also putting yourself on their radar. Their ‘reward’ is rarely a simple payment of spirit stones; it could be a future debt, a claim on spoils, or simply the privilege of being owed a favor by a nascent powerhouse. Madam Fan’s immediate anxiety is a sign of good survival instincts. The Ziling Fairy’s ability to secure Chi Huo’s aid shows immense political acumen and a willingness to deal with the devil for a shot at victory.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a perfect example of how the Mortal Stream builds its world through rumor and character introduction. We don’t see Chi Huo fight; we only hear his *name* and the reaction it provokes. That’s the point. His reputation is his weapon. Pay close attention to the social tension between the three sisters of the Celestial Sound Sect. The promise of the sect leader position is a major dangling carrot that will almost certainly create friction. Han Li is doing what he does best: keeping his mouth shut, his head down, and his mind cataloging every piece of data. He’s a passenger on a ship he doesn’t control, and he knows it. The real question is, when the Old Fiend shows his true colors, will Han Li be caught as collateral damage, or will he be ready to move before anyone else?
