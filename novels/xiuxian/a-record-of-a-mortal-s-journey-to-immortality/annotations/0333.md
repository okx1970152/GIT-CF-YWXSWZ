## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the final confrontation with the Yue Emperor, showcasing a masterclass in defensive formation warfare and the brutal efficiency of a well-timed Talisman Treasure volley. Han Li’s newly developed technique of “burst-discharging” a Talisman Treasure—sacrificing its longevity for a decisive spike in power—turns the tide against a foe wielding a suspiciously powerful blade. The aftermath is pure Mortal Stream logic: while his grateful allies rush off to loot the palace, Han Li quietly collects his own prize from the corpse, his mind already fixed on the “Blood Coagulation Five Elements Pill.” It’s a clean, cold victory, with no grand speeches or melodramatic finales.

## Key Plot Points

- The Yue Emperor, trapped in Han Li’s formation, is repeatedly teleported back to his starting position every time he attempts to attack Han Li directly. His blade-light and blood-light attacks are either absorbed or rebounded.
- Frustrated and panicking, the Yue Emperor realizes the formation is not a simple illusionary array but an “Absolute Sealing Array” with powerful translocation and reflection properties.
- Sensing Han Li’s next move, the Yue Emperor encases himself in a defensive blood-storm. Han Li opens the formation, unleashing his Ruler Talisman Treasure as a massive swarm, followed by Chen Qiaoqian and Zhong Weiniang’s Talisman Treasures.
- In the ensuing clash, the two junior sisters’ Talisman Treasures are completely destroyed by the Yue Emperor’s demon blade, forcing a mutual destruction.
- Han Li, using his newly mastered “burst-discharge” technique, pours the remaining power of his Ruler Talisman Treasure into a single, overwhelming attack that finally obliterates the Yue Emperor’s blood-light and reduces his body to a heap of rotting flesh.
- With the enemy dead, Han Li dismisses his allies to search the palace for loot under the guise of dismantling the formation, then sends his beast puppets to collect the spoils from the Yue Emperor’s corpse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Absolute Sealing Array” (禁断大阵):** The term the Yue Emperor uses in his moment of realization is a classic signifier in xianxia. It marks a formation that is not simply a barrier or a maze, but one that actively “forbids” and “severs” specific actions—like teleporting an attacker away or reflecting their own power back at them. It represents the highest tier of mortal-realm defensive formation work, a system designed to turn an enemy’s strength against them.

**The Economics of Talisman Treasures (符宝经济学):** This chapter provides a harsh lesson in the finite nature of Talisman Treasures. They are not rechargeable batteries; each use chips away at their core energy. Han Li’s “burst-discharge” tactic is the equivalent of burning a magical artifact for a single, massive explosion. It’s a calculated trade-off: losing a valuable resource to secure a guaranteed kill, a textbook example of the Mortal Stream principle of accepting short-term losses for long-term survival. The two junior sisters losing their Talisman Treasures entirely is a devastating blow to their combat power, highlighting the brutal cost of this battle.

**The ‘Thankless Victory’ Cleanup:** Han Li’s strategic withdrawal of his allies to let them loot the palace is a brilliant piece of social engineering in the Dark Forest. He correctly calculates that the Yue Emperor’s corpse will yield the most valuable prize (the Blood Coagulation Five Elements Pill), and by feigning disinterest and generously “gifting” them the palace’s spoils, he avoids jealousy and suspicion, reinforcing his role as the pragmatic, non-greedy junior brother.

### Burst-Discharge (齐放)

A high-risk technique where a cultivator releases a Talisman Treasure’s remaining power all at once for a massive spike in destructive force, at the cost of the item’s immediate destruction.

## Reading Guide

**Buckle up for a satisfying, tactical takedown.** This chapter is pure cat-and-mouse, with Han Li playing the patient, calculating mouse who has finally cornered a very dangerous cat. Watch how the Yue Emperor’s panic grows as his power is rendered useless—it’s a rare moment where a high-level bully realizes he’s the one trapped. The scene of his body shattering like porcelain is a wonderfully cold, visceral image that perfectly fits the Mortal Stream’s unflinching depiction of death. Pay attention to Han Li’s offhand comment about his new technique; it’s a quiet reminder that his greatest asset is not his raw power, but his ability to experiment and innovate under pressure.
