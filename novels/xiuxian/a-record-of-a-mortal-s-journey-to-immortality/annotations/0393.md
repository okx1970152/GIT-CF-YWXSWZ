## Chapter Overview

In one of the most electrifying "reap what you sow" moments in the early arc, Han Li returns to his cave dwelling to find his carefully nurtured strange insects gone—and something far more dangerous and valuable in their place. What begins as frustration quickly turns into cold calculation and barely contained excitement as he pieces together the clues: a shredded door, an abandoned silver shell, and a strange glittering sphere hanging beneath a tree. Within minutes, he goes from mourning lost assets to testing and bagging a top-12-ranked terror from the Strange Insect List. Buckle up, fellow Daoists: the Gold Devourer Beetles have arrived.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li discovers every single one of his raised strange insects has vanished; their escape and restrictive spells are obliterated.
- He finds a single bright silver insect shell, confirming the intruder is not one of his original insects.
- After scanning the cave dwelling, he spots a silver sphere hanging under a tree in the medicine garden.
- Through his jade slip (Imperial Spirit Sect notes), he identifies the sphere as a cluster of Gold Devourer Beetles—ranked #12 on the Strange Insect List.
- Han Li tests them with Qu Hun's demonic fire; the beetles devour the flames alive, proving their frightening resilience.
- He deploys his two Bloodjade Spiders to web-capture the entire swarm.
- Knowing the webs won't hold forever, he shoves the wrapped mass into a jade box and stores it.
- He searches the cave one last time, finds only burrowing holes, then retrieves his formation artifacts and flees the site.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Strange Insect List (奇虫榜)** — A staple of xianxia worldbuilding, this ranking system categorizes rare and often terrifying insect-type demon beasts by their combat utility, rarity, and raw destructive potential. Unlike regular beasts, many of these insects have specific behavioral weaknesses (e.g., fear of jade/wood, counter-predators). Han Li's newly acquired Gold Devourer Beetles (#12) outrank the Golden-Back Demon Mantis he previously coveted, making this a major upgrade in his tactical arsenal.

**Spirit Beast Pouches vs. Storage Pouches** — A standard storage pouch cannot contain living creatures (they would suffocate). But Strange-List insects are described as "vitality tenacious enough to survive without air," making them an exception. Dedicated spirit beast pouches, however, can also shrink large creatures for storage—a crucial distinction for field combat.

## Reading Guide

This is a pure **"quiet victory"** chapter: no duels, no big power plays, just Han Li performing a methodical forensic investigation of his own cave, then calmly deploying his assets to capture a massive potential trump card. The prose leans into that cold, analytical voice—note how his joy is immediately bounded by risk awareness (the webs won't hold forever, must flee afterward). This is the Mortal Stream at its most satisfying: a protagonist who treats a fortuitous encounter as a logistics problem to be solved, not a miracle to celebrate. The Gold Devourer Beetles are now his most terrifying hidden asset—and the narrative does not let us forget that they could just as easily consume him if he makes a mistake.
