## Chapter Overview
Fellow Daoists, get ready—because this chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s favorite pastime: **information warfare disguised as a polite trade meet**. Our hero Han Li, still playing the role of “Brother Li” to keep his true identity under wraps, attends an exclusive exchange of Core Formation treasures. But he’s not there for the shiny things. He’s hunting for one specific piece of intel: the location of an eighth-grade demon beast’s lair. The room buzzes with greed and surprise, and just when it looks like Han Li’s gamble will fail—who speaks up but his own “hostage,” the cunning and composed Wen Siyue. The cliffhanger is sharp, the intrigue is delicious, and the Mortal Stream economy of “pay with a secret, not with a sword” is on full display.

## Key Plot Points
- The exchange meet reaches its climax: each Core Formation cultivator presents rare materials and treasures, from seventh-grade demon beast sinews to “Coiling Earthworm” beast eggs.
- Han Li reveals his true objective: he’s not trading for treasure but for the location of an eighth-grade demon beast’s nest, offering his entire stockpile of materials as payment.
- The room reacts with shock and disbelief; no one volunteers the information, and the swarthy Sun cultivator denies knowing anything.
- Just as Han Li’s search seems to hit a dead end, Wen Siyue—the beautiful Foundation Establishment woman he has kept as his “guest” under duress—unexpectedly claims she knows the location.
- Madam Fan tries to silence her, but Wen Siyue defiantly insists on her knowledge and asks for a separate condition, raising the stakes for the next chapter.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Let’s talk about the **Mortal Stream’s information economics**: In a world where direct violence is costly and the powerful can crush you, knowing the right secret is often more valuable than owning the best magic tool. Han Li isn’t trying to buy a sword or a pill—he’s trying to buy coordinates. This reflects a deeper xianxia principle: *the map is worth more than the treasure if you know where the treasure is buried*. Also, note the “eighth-grade demon beast.” In the beast-ranking system common to xianxia, higher-grade demons are essentially roaming natural disasters; hunting one is not a casual adventure but a multi-Core-Formation-level military operation. Han Li’s interest in one signals a plan we haven’t yet seen—and Wen Siyue’s sudden involvement suggests she’s been holding cards of her own.

## Reading Guide
**Pay close attention to Wen Siyue’s shift in demeanor.** Until now, she has been a dutiful, forced companion—a “hostage” in all but name. Here, she breaks protocol by speaking out, even against her own sect elder’s command. This is a huge character moment: she is *choosing* to engage with Han Li on a level beyond mere physical closeness. Is she plotting an escape? Testing his trust? Or is this a calculated gamble to gain leverage of her own? The Mortal Stream never gifts information freely—every secret comes with a price tag, and Han Li is about to find out what hers is. Buckle up, the negotiation is about to get personal.
