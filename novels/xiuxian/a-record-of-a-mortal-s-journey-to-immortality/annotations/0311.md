## Chapter Overview

Han Li has just revealed himself as a mid-Foundation Establishment cultivator, and the Black Fiend Sect immediately marks him as premium sacrifice material for their leader’s blood cultivation. But the young prince and his Blood Servant bald man have miscalculated badly: Han Li is not some early-Foundation scrub they can bully. This chapter is a masterclass in crowd control and asset management as Han Li systematically dismantles the Black Fiend Sect’s ritual setup with brutal efficiency, forcing the bald man into an all-out defensive transformation that buys Han Li just enough time to pivot to the real threat—the Black Wind Formation being activated by the lesser cultists.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li’s identity is exposed: the young prince recognizes him as the “Qin Clan” member, confirming Han Li’s cover identity is blown.
- The bald Blood Servant (mid-Foundation) immediately identifies Han Li as mid-Foundation and calls for the Black Wind Formation to assist—showing he knows Han Li is out of his solo league.
- Han Li blitzes the chaff before the formation can stabilize, killing four black-robed cultivators in seconds using superior speed and deadly precision.
- The bald man counters with a full blood-light transformation, becoming a massive floating sphere that proves immune to Han Li’s top-grade artifacts—a classic trump card that forces Han Li to adapt.
- Han Li reads the room perfectly: recognizing the banner ritual as a threat similar to the Green Sun Demon Flame, he repositions the White Phosphorus Shield to the Meng Mountain Four and leaves the barrier to preemptively dismantle the formation.
- The young prince’s greed blinds him to tactical reality; he sees Han Li only as a better sacrifice, not as an immediate existential threat.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Black Wind Formation (黑风阵)** – This is a classic sect battle tactic in cultivation fiction: a multi-cultivator ritual array that amplifies power or generates a domain effect. Here it functions as a strategic equalizer, letting a group of Qi Condensation cultivators pin down a higher-tier Foundation Establishment enemy. The vulnerability is that it requires setup time—Han Li exploits that window mercilessly.

**Blood Servant (血侍)** – A title indicating a cultivator who has been “enhanced” through blood-path techniques, likely serving directly under a blood-cultivation Sect Leader. The blood-light body transformation we see here (invulnerability sphere) is reminiscent of top-tier demonic techniques like those of the Ghost Spirit Sect’s young master. In the Mortal Stream, such transformations are powerful but often come with drawbacks—mobility loss, mana drain, or self-damage.

**Green Sun Demon Flame callback** – Han Li’s flashback to the spirit stone mine incident is a key survival instinct: once you’ve seen a ritual that summons unstoppable fire, you don’t let another one finish. This chapter reinforces the Mortal Stream rule that pattern recognition and historical experience are combat multipliers.

## Reading Guide

Fellow daoists, prepare your popcorn—this is the kind of chapter that reminds you why Han Li survives when everyone else dies. He doesn’t stand around waiting for the formation to complete; he barely lets the bald man finish his boast before he’s already gutted four cultists. The tactical read is gorgeous: Han Li instantly identifies the banner ritual as a scaled threat and re-prioritizes mid-fight. Also, that moment when the Meng Mountain Four’s second brother tries to show off and gets his artifact crushed like a paper toy? Chef’s kiss. The Mortal Stream doesn’t reward pride—it punishes it. If you’re enjoying Han Li’s calculated aggression, buckle up, because the Black Wind Formation is still brewing, and the bald man isn’t out of tricks yet.
