## Chapter Overview

This chapter weaves together Han Li’s recollection of his deepening friendship with Zhang Tie, the quiet progress of his own cultivation, and the detailed introduction of a brutal martial art. Han Li successfully completes the first layer of the Nameless Oral Formula but suffers internal injuries in the process. Doctor Mo’s excessive concern unsettles him, planting seeds of suspicion. Meanwhile, Zhang Tie eagerly takes up the Elephant Hide Art, a technique famous for its agonizing middle layers and terrifying peak power. The stark contrast between Han Li’s cautious wariness and Zhang Tie’s blind enthusiasm lays the groundwork for the different paths they will walk.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li recalls his friendship with Zhang Tie, forged from shared backgrounds and temperaments.
- The stone cultivation chamber is described as a privilege obtained by Doctor Mo, highlighting his influence in the sect.
- Han Li breaks through the first layer of the Nameless Oral Formula but nearly ruptures several meridians, suffering moderate internal injuries.
- Doctor Mo’s reaction to Han Li’s injury is unusually anxious, far beyond normal master-disciple concern, making Han Li uneasy.
- Doctor Mo teaches Han Li medicine generously but refuses to teach him any martial arts beyond the Nameless Oral Formula.
- Zhang Tie is taught the Elephant Hide Art, a rare and painful technique with nine layers: first three easy, middle three excruciating, last three powerful but with monthly agony.
- The technique’s originator was allegedly born without pain, allowing him to master it fully.
- Zhang Tie, attracted by the power, ignores the risks and reaches the peak of the first layer in two months.
- To break through to the second layer, Zhang Tie trains daily under a high waterfall at Red Water Peak, following Doctor Mo’s advice.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Elephant Hide Art belongs to the “hard external” tradition common in Chinese wuxia and xianxia, where practitioners endure extreme physical suffering to gain near-invulnerability. This mirrors real-world martial arts like the Iron Shirt or Golden Bell Cover, which historically involved conditioning the body through repeated impacts. However, the novel exaggerates the pain-reward ratio into a near-supernatural level. The concept of a technique that becomes smooth after the sixth layer but retains monthly agony adds a cyclical curse mechanic, making it less like a simple power-up and more like a lifelong burden. This reflects the “mortal stream” theme: every shortcut carries a hidden cost, and even the greatest power is never free.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to Doctor Mo’s behavior: his emotional investment in Han Li’s cultivation and recovery is disproportionate, hinting at ulterior motives. Han Li’s unease is the reader’s cue to remain suspicious. Zhang Tie’s choice of the Elephant Hide Art, driven by short-sighted greed for power, foreshadows future suffering or exploitation. The detailed description of the technique’s pain thresholds is not just worldbuilding—it will likely become a critical plot element later. The waterfall training also establishes a classic xianxia training trope, but the novel may subvert it with darker consequences.

## Elephant Hide Art {#elephant-hide-art}

The Elephant Hide Art is a rare external cultivation technique that emphasizes physical toughness and immense strength at the cost of excruciating pain during the intermediate stages. Its nine-layer structure—easy start, brutal middle, then smooth again with periodic agony—makes it a high-risk, high-reward choice. In the context of the Seven Mysteries Sect, such a technique is considered nearly lost because few can endure the suffering required to advance past the fourth layer. The technique’s originator, said to have been born without the ability to feel pain, represents an extreme outlier. For a normal cultivator like Zhang Tie, the lack of firsthand experience with severe pain leads him to underestimate the long-term toll. This technique starkly embodies the “mortal stream” principle: every power gained comes from a precise trade-off, often paid in flesh and spirit.
