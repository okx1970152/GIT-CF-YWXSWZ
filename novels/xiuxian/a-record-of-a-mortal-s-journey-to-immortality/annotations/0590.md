## Chapter Overview

New continent, new rules! Han Li and Mei Ning wash ashore at a mysterious village in the Netherworld Domain—a place where mana goes to die and only “netherworld force” works. The villagers are suspicious but welcoming to cultivators (because hey, strong backs are useful even without spells). But before Han Li can settle in, the ground starts shaking: a giant four-eyed winged ape is charging the walls. Classic Mortal Stream: you don’t even get a cup of tea before the local raid boss shows up. This chapter sets the tone for the Chaotic Star Sea sub‑arc, where raw power isn’t the answer, and observation is the only weapon that still works.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li and Mei Ning arrive at a stone‑walled village surrounded by a purple mist formation. Han Li immediately notes the food shortage and the yin‑cold energy radiating from the central platform.
- The lean man from the beach reports to the village elders; the pale‑skinned, narrow‑eyed elder marks Han Li with a sharp glance—first sign of local trouble.
- In the meeting hall, the plump elder identifies them as cultivators by their storage pouch, calmly explains the Netherworld Domain’s rules: no mana, only netherworld force; cultivators can still be useful via beast‑crystal arrays.
- Han Li learns that the elder himself was a Foundation Establishment cultivator before being swept in by the ghost mist, and that five or six other cultivators live in the village.
- Just as Han Li is about to dig deeper, the entire hall trembles. A giant beast—the “Jiaozheng beast”—bursts from the black yin wind, a forty‑meter‑tall, four‑eyed, winged ape with a black club.
- The village reacts with practiced discipline: spears and shields are distributed. The purple mist from the high platform forms living tentacles that trip the beast before the wall, and a volley of arrows and spears rains down.
- Han Li watches from a rooftop, analyzing the netherworld‑force technique being performed by four cultivators on the platform.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Netherworld Domain is a quintessential “sealed–mana zone”—a staple trope in xianxia where the hero must reinvent himself when his greatest weapon (spells, magical treasures) is taken away. Here, the only usable energy is “netherworld force” (阴冥之力), a yin‑aligned power that cannot fuel spells but can drive crude arrays made from beast crystals. This echoes Chinese folk notions of the underworld (冥界), where the living cannot draw on Yang‑world qi. The ghost mist that drags ships here is reminiscent of the legendary “yin fog” in Chinese maritime folklore—a supernatural hazard that leads to a parallel realm. The village’s purple mist is a formation restriction (禁制), but it runs on this netherworld force rather than spiritual qi, showing how locals have adapted to the hostile environment. The Jiaozheng beast, though a fictional monster, borrows design cues from the winged ape in the *Shanhaijing* (Classic of Mountains and Seas) but is given a local twist: it can survive the yin wind and is drawn to the village as a source of flesh and energy.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a textbook Mortal Stream transition: Han Li is thrown into a completely new ecosystem and forced to gather intelligence before he can act. Pay attention to how he reads the village’s social hierarchy (the lean man, the pale‑eyed elder, the plump elder) and its defensive architecture (the stone platform, the purple mist). The giant beast attack isn’t just an action scene—it’s Han Li’s first demonstration that the locals can fight back, and a hint that brute force alone won’t work here. Note also the flicker of interest from the pale‑skinned elder toward Mei Ning—a classic predator signal in this world. For now, Han Li stays in observer mode, cataloging every detail. The real battle hasn’t even started yet.

## The Jiaozheng Beast – A New Threat Category

The *Jiaozheng* (狡狰) beast is described as a giant grey ape with four blood‑red eyes, fleshy wings, and a black club. Its name doesn’t come from orthodox Chinese mythology but is likely a compound coined by the author to evoke “cunning” (狡) and “fierce” (狰). This beast represents a new tier of opponent in the Chaotic Star Sea: no magic, just raw physical destruction, yet it’s still vulnerable to a well‑coordinated formation defense. The villagers treat it as a known raid boss, which tells us that such attacks are routine, and that the village’s netherworld‑force system is battle‑tested. For Han Li, the beast is a piece of actionable intelligence: he now knows the power ceiling of the local environment, and that cooperation is the only path to temporary safety.
