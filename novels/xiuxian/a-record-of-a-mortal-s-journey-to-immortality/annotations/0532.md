## Chapter Overview

It’s been seven years since Han Li vanished from the Blood Forbidden Land and resurfaced on Abyss Island. The end of the tumultuous Starfall War arc has arrived—not through a cataclysmic battle, but through Han Li’s quiet, decisive exit. This chapter is the Mortal Stream’s perfect exit: a single, surgical kill that signals to the reader that Han Li’s Foundation Establishment days are done, and the Inner Star Seas are a problem for other people. He doesn’t fight the system; he finds the exit and walks through it. The chapter also delivers a thick lore dump on the state of the Chaotic Star Sea: the Abyss has become a death zone thanks to a mystery beast, the Star Palace and the Rebel Alliance are still at war, and every cultivator is trapped between frying pan and fire. The tension is real, and Han Li’s solution is elegant—he leaves.

## Key Plot Points

- **Three Core Formation hunters stalk a Glass Beast.** The vulture-faced elder Qiu, pale-golden-faced Min, and brutish Xuan have been hunting a rank-six Glass Beast for days. Their chatter reveals the state of the Outer Star Seas: the Abyss has been effectively sealed since a massive beast riot two years ago, and teleportation back to the Inner Star Seas is blocked by the ongoing war.
- **The hunters spot a lone early Core Formation youth.** All three assume an easy ambush—until they watch him kill the powerful Glass Beast in a single, impossible strike. The youth’s sword splits mid-aira secret technique allowing a second, identical blade to bypass the beast’s defense and decapitate it instantly.
- **Suspicion freezes aggression.** The three Core Formation hunters are powerful enough to gang up on the beast, but the casual, effortless precision of the kill makes them halt. They re-scan with spiritual sense, find nothing amiss about the youth’s early Core Formation aura, and grow paranoid. Is he hiding his real cultivation? Is there a trap?
- **Han Li loots the beast and leaves without a word.** He descends, collects the Glass Beast corpse, and flies off—never acknowledging the hidden observers. The hunters remain frozen by uncertainty, letting him slip away.
- **The chapter ends on a note of brutal, clean closure.** Han Li has successfully exited the war-torn Outer Star Seas and returned to a state of solo mobility. The world he leaves behind is still burning, but his path is now clear.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Glass Beast (琉璃兽):** The name “Glass Beast” derives from *liuli* (琉璃), a translucent, brightly colored glass-like substance highly prized in ancient Chinese art and Buddhist iconography. In the novel, the name reflects the beast’s crystalline, translucent body—a visual cue that it is not a regular demon beast but a rare variant associated with high medicinal and alchemical value. The beast’s white-thread attack is a natural-born defense, while its white crystal ball core is a high-grade magical material.

**The Abyss/Abyss Island (奇渊岛):** The term *qiyuan* (奇渊) translates to “strange abyss”—and that’s exactly what it is. In earlier chapters, the Abyss was a dangerous but viable hunting ground for Core Formation cultivators. The two-year-old beast riot turned it into a deathtrap that even Nascent Soul cultivators flee from. This is a classic “escalation wake-up call” in xianxia: when even the local hunting ground turns into a no-go zone, it’s time to move on. The Four Laws Sage’s near-total loss (only his Nascent Soul survived) on last year’s expedition is a clear signal that the power balance in the Outer Star Seas has permanently shifted.

**Teleportation Talisman (传送符):** This rare artifact allows a one-way trip through long-distance arrays. Its presence in the black market on Abyss Island is a classic Mortal Stream detail: no system is perfectly locked down; there is always a cracked door for those with enough resources and the right connections.

**The Mortal Stream’s “True Victory” Pattern:** A true Mortal Stream victory is often achieved not by the protagonist overpowering his enemies, but by their own hesitation, overreach, or self-destruction. The three Core Formation hunters freeze because the kill *looks* impossible. Han Li didn’t beat them; they beat themselves by not committing. This is the essence of the survival-first philosophy: let the system and its predators eliminate each other while you take the single, clean shot and leave.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a *clean exit*—the kind every long-term reader of *RMJI* learns to appreciate. No dramatic confrontations, no speechifying, no final showdown. Just Han Li doing what he does best: killing efficiently, leaving no witnesses to his true capabilities, and walking out the door while everyone else is still debating the odds. The best part? The three Core Formation hunters never even realize they were seconds away from dying. They think they’re the apex predators. They’re wrong.

The chapter also serves as a world-state update for anyone who’s been wondering what happened to the Chaotic Star Sea after the war arc. The answer: it got worse. The Star Palace and Rebel Alliance are still bleeding each other dry, the Abyss is now an apocalypse zone, and every cultivator is scrambling for an exit. Han Li found his. The question is: where does his path lead next?

Kick back and enjoy the satisfaction of watching the protagonist out-think the system one last time before he vanishes over the horizon.
