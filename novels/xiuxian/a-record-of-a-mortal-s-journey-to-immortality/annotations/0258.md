## Chapter Overview

This chapter is *pure*, concentrated clan politics—no fights, no magic duels, just a masterclass in strategic calculation delivered through dialogue. The Yan Clan convenes a wartime council to debate the Ghost Spirit Sect’s marriage proposal, and scholar Xuan Ye steals the show with a brutally pragmatic analysis that lays out the full geopolitical map. Get ready for a deep dive into realpolitik, Dark Forest logic, and the cost of survival in a world about to be swallowed by war.

## Key Plot Points

- The Yan Clan Ancestor confirms the Great Blood Spirit Art is the top demonic technique, requiring dual cultivation with a Heavenly Spiritual Root (Yan Ru Yan) and a Dark Spiritual Root (the Ghost Spirit Sect Young Master). This explains the sect’s obsessive courtship.
- The Ghost Spirit Sect leaked the Six Sects’ invasion plan to the Yan Clan as a show of good faith and a lure—they want an alliance, not just a bride.
- Scholar Xuan Ye dismantles the hope of the Seven Sects: they have already been suppressing the Yan Clan, will offer no real reward, and are unlikely to win the war anyway.
- Xuan Ye’s core argument: ally with the likely victor (the demonic path), which is in a golden expansion phase, and use that momentum to grow the Yan Clan until they can stand as equals—or even surpass—the Ghost Spirit Sect.
- To verify sincerity, Xuan Ye proposes the Life-Death Curse exchange: the Ghost Spirit Sect’s Young Master and two Core Formation cultivators sign a mutual non-aggression pact with Yan Ru Yan and two Yan Clan uncles, guaranteeing safety for 200-300 years.
- The Ghost Spirit Sect accepts immediately, shocking the Yan Clan. Yan Ru Yan returns to lead them to the main hall for further talks, and the Ancestor dismisses all others, keeping only Xuan Ye.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Life-Death Curse (生死咒):** This is not a simple vow. In the cultivation world, oaths sworn on one's life force and soul are binding metaphysical contracts backed by the Heavenly Dao. Breaking one invites catastrophic karmic retribution, often resulting in immediate qi deviation or death. It's one of the few reliable institutional safeguards in a world of backstabbers.

**Dual Cultivation & Spiritual Root Synergy:** The Great Blood Spirit Art requires a male-female pair with complementary extreme roots (Heavenly vs. Dark Spiritual Root). This is not romance—it's a brutal efficiency equation. Their combined cultivation speed and power output are exponentially higher than any single practitioner, making them a walking strategic weapon capable of "sweeping the Six Sects."

**"Chicken Rib" (鸡肋):** A classical Chinese idiom—the rib of a chicken: tasteless to eat, yet a pity to throw away. In xianxia, it describes a technique or treasure that sounds amazing but is useless without the right conditions. The Great Blood Spirit Art is a prime chicken rib for anyone without Heavenly/Dark Roots.

### Two-Sided Fear (两头怕)

A strategic standoff where both parties fear each other's lethal potential, creating a cold war dynamic rather than open conflict.

## Reading Guide

This is the chapter where the Yan Clan's mask slips entirely. Xuan Ye and the Ancestor openly discuss the trade-offs: offer up their prized genius as a bride in exchange for a foothold in an ascending demonic empire, all while planning to eventually backstab or outgrow their new masters. There is zero sentimentality—Yan Ru Yan is a strategic asset, and her marriage is a calculated investment in the clan's future.

Pay special attention to the *two-sided fear* dynamic: the Yan Clan is scared of the Ghost Spirit Sect's power, but the Ghost Spirit Sect is scared of losing access to Yan Ru Yan's Heavenly Spiritual Root. The Life-Death Curse is the peace treaty between two rational predators. This is the Mortal Stream at its finest: alliances are made not from trust, but from mutually assured destruction.

Also note how the scholar dismisses the Seven Sects as already hostile and doomed—this is the first clear signal that Yue Nation's old order is about to collapse, and everyone is scrambling to pick a side.
