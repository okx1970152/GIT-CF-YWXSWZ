## Chapter Overview

Finally, the quest to find the array master pays off—but not in the way Han Li expected. He tracks down Qi Yunxiao, the co-creator of the Reversed Five Elements Formation tools, and quickly discovers the man is desperate for spirit herbs to cure a gravely injured friend. Han Li tries to set up the perfect *quid pro quo*, offering a trade for repairing the teleportation array. But before a deal can be struck, a panicked maid arrives: the “friend” in question is none other than the array master herself, trapped by a group of rogue cultivators. This chapter is a masterclass in Han Li’s calculated altruism—he recognizes a perfect opportunity to gain a massive debt of gratitude, but only after coldly assessing the risk. The payoff is swift and brutal: a textbook display of the absolute power gap between a Foundation Establishment cultivator and mere Qi Condensation thugs.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li finds Qi Yunxiao, who is eager to trade more array flags for another aged spirit herb, revealing that his close friend—the array master—is still unwell.
- Han Li internally calculates the value of the arrays vs. his own main goal (repairing the teleportation array) and decides to redirect the negotiation.
- Before he can make his counteroffer, a maid arrives in a panic: the array master is trapped by a group of seven to eight Qi Condensation cultivators.
- Qi Yunxiao immediately begs Han Li to intervene; Han Li coldly assesses the risk, confirms the enemies are only low-level cultivators, and agrees.
- Flying via the Divine Wind Boat, they arrive at the scene in minutes. The attackers’ formation is on the verge of breaking.
- Han Li stops Qi Yunxiao from charging in, proposing instead to eliminate the entire group to prevent future retaliation from the clan.
- Qi Yunxiao, furious, agrees. Han Li then unleashes two black and six golden light beams, instantly and effortlessly killing all seven or eight attackers before they can even react.
- The group is left in awe of the sheer power gap; Qi Yunxiao then rushes into the woods to call out to the trapped array master, Lady Xing.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Quid Pro Quo” Art of Negotiation**: Han Li’s mental calculus in this chapter is a perfect example of the Mortal Stream’s “never lift a finger without benefit” principle. He doesn’t reject Qi Yunxiao’s offer for more array flags because he’s noble; he rejects it because taking the flags would make it harder to ask for the teleportation array repair later. This is a high-level negotiation trap: by *not* taking the immediate reward, he builds a larger debt and a stronger position. The “reluctant expression” he puts on is a classic Chinese social tactic—to make the other party feel they owe you, you must first appear to be making a sacrifice.

**One-Shot Kill & The Power Gap (境界压制)**: The scene where Han Li effortlessly kills seven or eight Qi Condensation cultivators with a single wave of his hands is the novel’s explicit demonstration of realm suppression (境界压制). In the Mortal Stream, this is not an exaggeration. A Foundation Establishment cultivator is qualitatively superior in both mana density and reaction speed. Even the highest Qi Condensation cultivators (layers 11-12) are like toddlers against a Foundation Establishment cultivator with ranged magical tools. This scene is a brutal reminder of the rigid, unforgiving class system of the cultivation world.

**The “Cleanse Once and For All” Logic**: Han Li’s proposal to eliminate the entire group, not just scare them off, is a core tenant of Dark Forest survival—‘cut the grass and pull up the roots’ (斩草除根). In the Mortal Stream, mercy is a liability. If the attackers are from a clan, they will simply return with news and possibly stronger fighters, turning a manageable problem into a continuous, existential threat. Han Li’s suggestion is not cruelty; it is cold, efficient resource management.

### Realm Suppression (境界压制)

The absolute advantage a cultivator has over someone in a lower realm; it is not a simple stat increase but a qualitative difference in energy purity and physical capabilities.

### Cut the Grass and Pull the Roots (斩草除根)

A classic Chinese survival idiom cautioning against leaving future threats alive after a victory; failure to do so invites endless future retaliation.

## Reading Guide

Behold, the ultimate power fantasy of the Mortal Stream: not a flashy, drawn-out battle, but the cold, efficient application of overwhelming force. Han Li doesn’t even break a sweat. He doesn’t need a strategy, a trump card, or a life-saving technique. He simply outruns their ability to react. The Divine Wind Boat’s speed compared to the maid’s previous escape also serves as a subtle reminder of Han Li’s value as an asset.

Note the contrast between Qi Yunxiao’s panic and Han Li’s icy calm. Qi Yunxiao is about to throw himself into a losing fight out of loyalty and desperation. Han Li stops him with a single hand and then proposes the ‘cleanse’ plan with a faint smile. This is the defining separation between a talented craftsman and a true survivor. Qi Yunxiao is skilled, but Han Li is a predator.

Finally, the chapter ends on a cliffhanger with Qi Yunxiao calling out for “Yin’er.” We have yet to meet the legendary array master face-to-face. Given she is the co-creator of the Reversed Five Elements Formation, her personality and state of health will be crucial to the story’s next move. Is she grateful or resentful of this rescue? Does she have the knowledge to fix the ancient teleportation array? Get ready, because the real negotiation is about to begin.
