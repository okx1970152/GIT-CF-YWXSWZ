## Chapter Overview

Well, *that* was a masterclass in disguise. For this chapter, our man Han Li has swapped his lone-wolf mountain-cave act for a brand new performance in the capital. He’s now “Young Master Han” from the Qin estate, complete with a servant who’s more interested in his own aching feet than in any secret plans. But don’t let this mortal-stage comedy fool you. The real drama is playing out two tables over, where five desperate rogue cultivators, the “Mengshan Five Friends,” are having a very hushed (or so they think) conversation about being press-ganged by a masked enemy. Our guy is listening with his Foundation-Establishment-level Divine Sense, treating their soundproof barrier like it’s made of tissue paper. This is a classic social tribulation: a high-stakes intelligence-gathering mission disguised as a late lunch.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li continues his “social engineering” performance as a bumbling but wealthy new arrival in the capital, with the weary servant Qin Ping playing the part of his perfect foil.
- At the restaurant, Han Li spots a table of five Qi Condensation cultivators, which immediately raises his suspicions in the war-torn context of the demonic invasion.
- Using the Qi Attraction Art, Han Li conceals his cultivation level, presenting himself as a complete mortal to the low-level cultivators.
- Han Li’s Divine Sense effortlessly pierces the soundproof barrier the cultivators set up, allowing him to overhear their entire conversation.
- The group, calling themselves the “Mengshan Five Friends,” reveals they have been captured and placed under a life-threatening restriction by a masked, Foundation Establishment–level enemy force that has already taken hundreds of cultivators.
- The group’s leader, the dark-faced elder, explains why appealing to the Seven Great Sects for help is futile: the sects are already stretched thin fighting the demonic path, the enemy is utterly anonymous, and their unique restriction is unlikely to be broken by anyone else.
- The chapter ends on a note of grim resignation, with the five friends terrified and trapped, unaware that a far more powerful observer has just overheard their entire predicament.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The “Social Tribulation” and Loose Lips
This chapter entirely hinges on the classic *xianxia* trope of the “hidden elder.” Here, Han Li is acting the part of a naive mortal, but the revelation of his true power is a cold, calculated decision. The “Social Tribulation” is a test that has nothing to do with combat strength and everything to do with intelligence, deception, and controlling information flow. How Han Li chooses to leverage this overheard secret—if at all—will be the real mark of his survival instincts, not the strength of his spells.

### The Qi Attraction Art (引气术)
This is a basic utility technique for Foundation Establishment cultivators. It functions as the ultimate suppression cloak in the lower realms, making the user appear as an ordinary mortal to any cultivator of a lower tier. In the Dark Forest logic of *RMJI*, this is the ultimate defensive tool for a cultivator who wants to gather intelligence without being flagged as a threat. Han Li’s use of it here is a textbook example: treat your own aura as a resource to be hidden or revealed for maximum tactical advantage.

### Soundproof Barriers in the Mortal Stream
The fact that a Qi Condensation cultivator’s soundproof barrier is utterly worthless against a Foundation Establishment cultivator’s Divine Sense is a brutal structural reminder of the power hierarchy in this world. The technique is not flawed; its user simply has no comprehension of the chasm that separates their realm from the next. In the Mortal Stream, information security is a direct function of cultivation base, not clever technique.

### Social Tribulation

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex social relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

Welcome to the quietest kind of excitement! After the life-and-death scrambles of the Blood Forbidden Land, this chapter is a return to the core skill that keeps Han Li alive: *information warfare*. Pay close attention to the shift in tone here. The mortal comedy (Qin Ping’s aching feet, the fawning waiter) is a perfect contrast to the cold, calculating core of the scene. Han Li is gathering high-value strategic intelligence without lifting a single finger.

Think of the Mengshan Five Friends. They’re not evil, they’re not dumb. They’re just five rogue cultivators who ran into a threat that was leagues above their weight class. Their conversation is a perfect snapshot of the precarious existence most cultivators lead in this world—forced into impossible choices by a system that grants them no mercy. The real question this chapter whispers to us is: how will Han Li use this information? Will he help them? Exploit them? Or simply file the intelligence away as a potential future weapon? Get ready to watch a master social engineer at work.
