## Chapter Overview
The ambush gets ambushed. After tracking Steward Wang to the Xin Estate, Han Li and the Mountain Brothers discover the Young Prince inside, who then leads them on a midnight chase to a remote temple. But the trail is a trap—the Young Prince has turned the tables, and waiting for them in the sky is a blood-robed Foundation Establishment cultivator and a squad of Black Fiend enforcers. The chapter delivers a clean, cold reversal: the hunters have become the hunted, and the odds just shifted dramatically.

## Key Plot Points
- The Meng Mountain Brothers initially suspect a leak when the Red Moth expert is absent, but Han Li dismisses the theory, noting no ambush was triggered upon entry.
- They proceed to Steward Wang’s quarters, where Han Li detects the Young Prince’s presence and adjusts the plan.
- The Young Prince, after a contemplative walk under the moon, changes into a blood-red masked outfit and flies away on a blood-colored artifact, revealing his true affiliation and cultivation (11th layer Qi Condensation).
- Han Li and the Mountain Brothers follow him to a ruined temple outside Yue; the Young Prince vanishes inside, protected by a simple Boulder Fall Formation.
- Han Li breaks the formation with brute-force puppet bombardment and Black Dragon Harpoons, impressing the Mountain Brothers with his sheer power.
- As they prepare to descend, the Young Prince reveals himself in the sky behind them with a gaunt accomplice, a squad of masked Black Fiend disciples, and a Foundation Establishment blood-robed enforcer.
- The chapter ends with the Mountain Brothers realizing they have fallen into a counter-ambush and now face death.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Boulder Fall Formation (落石阵):** A basic earth-attribute defensive formation that creates a yellow barrier to protect a small area. In the cultivation world, formations are a specialized art—most cultivators can only break them by overwhelming their energy capacity, not by clever dismantling. This is why Han Li’s method of using multiple puppet beams and a high-grade magic tool is so effective: he simply pumps in far more power than the formation can absorb. The Mountain Brothers were baffled by it earlier, which highlights the divide between a Foundation Establishment cultivator with war resources and Qi Condensation stragglers.

**Foundation Establishment vs. Qi Condensation:** In this world, Foundation Establishment cultivators are a different species. Han Li breaks a formation that gave four Qi Condensation cultivators trouble by sheer force—along with the Mountain Brothers’ earlier frustration—because the gap in raw magical power is like comparing a pile driver to a hand hammer. This chapter also introduces a second Foundation Establishment foe, instantly turning the odds into a knife-edge standoff.

**Blood Fiend / Black Fiend Operators:** The Young Prince’s transformation from a troubled noble to a masked blood-cultivator echoes the Jianghu trope of hidden masters who maintain a public identity to mask their secret cultivation. His betrayal of the Mountain Brothers—who once belonged to the same organization—reinforces the Mortal Stream’s zero-sum logic of power and loyalty: yesterday’s external tools today’s decoys.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a textbook example of the Mortal Stream’s favorite reversal: the one who seems to be fleeing is actually laying a snare. Notice how Han Li’s caution—lingering to watch Steward Wang’s room—only gives him a slight window; he still gets caught in the trap. The tension isn’t in a big fight, but in that moment of discovery: the hunters realize they’re encircled. The bald, browsless Foundation Establishment enforcer’s cold amusement carries more threat than a thousand shouting goons. Seasoned Mortal Stream readers will immediately start counting resources and escape routes—Han Li’s trump cards, the bluffs he can deploy, and the fact that his best strategic option usually starts with ‘not dying today.’ The knife’s edge just got sharper.
