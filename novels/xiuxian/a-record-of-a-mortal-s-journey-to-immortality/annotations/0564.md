## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in psychological tension and quiet power games. Han Li visits the Sublime Sound Sect hoping to use their teleportation array back to the Inner Star Seas, only to learn it’s incomplete—missing those pesky Dream Stones. Madam Fan, the sect leader, is all smiles and courtesy, but sharp eyes will notice the cracks beneath the surface. She insists he stays the night, and Han Li, ever the calculator, accepts. That night, she delivers the real bombshell: she knows exactly who he is, not through magic or spiritual sense, but through his *scent*—an ability that makes her one of the most dangerous social players he’s encountered in the Chaotic Star Sea. The chapter is all setup, but damn if it isn’t tense.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li arrives at the Sublime Sound Sect and is formally received by Madam Fan.
- He directly asks about borrowing the sect’s teleportation array back to the Inner Star Seas.
- Madam Fan explains the array is unfinished, lacking the rare Dream Stones; a previous extraction attempt ended in casualties.
- A young female disciple (Yunqin) interrupts with a cough during the explanation, revealing internal sect friction.
- Han Li initially plans to leave, but Madam Fan suggests he stay the night—he accepts after a meaningful pause.
- After Han Li departs, a confrontation between Madam Fan and Yunqin shows Yunqin’s loyalty to Elder Yun Tianxiao and her distrust of Han Li.
- That night, Madam Fan visits Han Li’s room and directly addresses him as “Elder Han,” exposing his identity.
- Han Li’s initial shock is quickly controlled; he demands to know how she recognized him.
- Madam Fan reveals her unique olfactory memory: she can distinguish and permanently remember individual body scents. She had memorized Han Li’s scent from a prior encounter.
- The chapter ends with Han Li’s silent recalculation of his situation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces a detection method that breaks the usual rules. In most xianxia, disguises fool the eyes and even spiritual sense, but Madam Fan’s ability bypasses both—she smells him. The concept of “body scent” (体息) ties into traditional Chinese ideas of each person having a unique vital essence (精气) that can be perceived by those with heightened senses. The Sublime Sound Sect (妙音门) is a small, trade-oriented sect in the Chaotic Star Sea, and its internal politics are a classic Mortal Stream mini-power-struggle: a divided leadership, a suspicious elder (Yun Tianxiao), and a sect leader trying to carve out her own influence. Note how Han Li doesn’t threaten or panic when exposed; he simply files the data and re-calibrates.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for a high-tension social simulation. Han Li’s identity is blown wide open, but with zero combat, zero fireballs, and zero screaming—just two characters sitting on a bed, talking. Pay close attention to Han Li’s micro-reactions: the momentary stiffening, the controlled recovery, the deliberate calm. Madam Fan is *good*—she plays her card with perfect timing and frames her ability as both a service and a threat. The chapter also sets up a potential fork: will Han Li treat her as an asset, a liability, or a target? The internal sect struggle between her and Yun Tianxiao suggests she might be looking for an ally. Classic Mortal Stream: every friendly face is also a leash.
