## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a quiet victory interlude that shifts focus from direct conflict to logistics, strategy, and long-term asset building. Han Li acquires Divine Thunder Bamboo from Fairy Zi Ling in exchange for becoming an honorary elder of the Sublime Sound Sect—a political arrangement that leaves both sides feeling they got the better deal. While the Sublime Sound Sect leaders debate the wisdom of this trade, Han Li returns to his cave and begins cultivating the bamboo using the Green Liquid from the Heaven Vial. Two years of resource gathering follow as he assembles the materials for his Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords. The chapter culminates in a rare moment of personal triumph: the White Thunder Bamboo matures into legendary Gold Thunder Bamboo, confirming Han Li's biggest windfall yet.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li inspects the Divine Thunder Bamboo and confirms its authenticity through a simple test (an electric arc repels a blade).
- Fairy Zi Ling gifts the bamboo along with a Sublime Sound Sect elder token; Han Li accepts the position but remains guarded.
- After Han Li leaves, Madam Fan and Zhuo Ruting question Zi Ling's decision. Zi Ling explains that White Thunder Bamboo is actually a "chicken rib"—valuable in name but hard to use. She argues that forming a connection with a reliable Core Formation cultivator is more prudent than gambling on a stranger.
- The three women agree to a temporary leadership structure without a sect leader, acknowledging their vulnerability after recent defections.
- Han Li researches bamboo cultivation methods, transplants the bamboo, and begins a two-year grind: accelerating the bamboo with Green Liquid while spending heavily to gather materials for the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords.
- The chapter ends with a success: the bamboo matures to ten thousand years, emitting pale gold lightning that instantly vaporizes a flying sword tester. Han Li experiences a rare, open smile.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Divine Thunder Bamboo (天雷竹) is one of the "three divine bamboos" of the cultivation world, but its practical value is far from absolute. The bamboo's grade is determined by age: under 2,000 years, it releases ordinary white lightning; 2,000–5,000 years produces white with increasing power; 5,000–10,000 years yields blue lightning; and above 10,000 years, the lightning turns pale gold—this is the legendary "Gold Thunder Bamboo" (金雷竹), said to be especially effective against evil arts (辟邪之雷, Demon-Repelling Lightning). The chapter also references the historical chaos a half-section of Gold Thunder Bamboo caused in the Starfall Sea, reinforcing the high value and scarcity of ten-thousand-year spirit plants.

The term "chicken rib" (鸡肋) appears again—a classical Chinese idiom from the *Three Kingdoms* describing something tasteless to eat but a pity to discard. Zi Ling's argument that White Thunder Bamboo is a chicken rib perfectly captures the Mortal Stream's resource economics: something can be legendary in name yet almost useless in practice, making it a perfect bargaining chip for a clever player.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for a satisfying payoff chapter. After the high-pressure trials and battles of previous arcs, this is Han Li doing what he does best: turning a suspicious trade into a massive personal gain. Watch how Zi Ling rationalizes her decision—she thinks she's playing the long game, but Han Li's Green Liquid turns her "chicken rib" into a true treasure. The contrast between the sect's cautious optimism and Han Li's silent victory is what makes Mortal Stream storytelling so rewarding. Also, note the cost disclosure: Han Li literally bankrupts himself gathering materials for the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords. Nothing in this world comes free, not even for the protagonist.
