## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to the chilly heart of the Void Heaven Palace! Chapter 486 is a masterclass in **suspended tension**, where our eternally paranoid protagonist performs a perfect hide-and-seek routine with the scariest old monsters of the Chaotic Star Sea. Han Li gets a mysterious scroll, nearly freezes his meridians off, and then watches the Big Three (Man Brutal Beard, Extreme Yin, and the scholar-robed elder) decide they aren’t going to share the Void Heaven Cauldron—they’re just going to break someone’s toy silkworm. The stage is set for a showdown where everyone is armed with ancient spirit beasts, and no one is playing nice.

## Key Plot Points

- **Han Li’s secret retrieval:** After confirming he is not being watched, Han Li cuts a hole in a cyan stone and retrieves an old, yellowed scroll hidden inside. The location is suspicious—just the kind of place you’d stash something the ancient owners didn’t want anyone to find.
- **Caught in the act (almost):** Extreme Yin Patriarch’s voice transmission calls him back just in time. Han Li seamlessly blocks the hole and pretends to be resting, demonstrating his trained ability to snap from covert operation to social performance in under a second.
- **The icy high platform:** The group ascends the steps, where a bone-chilling blue light has thickened the ice by inches. Han Li is nearly frozen stiff until Extreme Yin reminds him to use the White Rhinoceros Pendant—a rare moment where the old demon’s “generosity” actually proves useful.
- **The goal revealed:** At the center of the platform sits a small altar with a large, blindingly blue hole. Seven or eight Golden Silk Silkworms are yanking golden threads from the depths, trying to pull out the Void Heaven Cauldron.
- **The standoff:** Wan Tianming and his righteous-path allies are already there with two ancient spirit beasts—a white Cold Jiao and a translucent Li Gui tortoise—on loan from two legendary old freaks. Man Brutal Beard attacks but is blocked. The chapter ends with the three demonic-path leaders reluctantly agreeing to a coordinated beast assault.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Golden Silk Silkworm (金丝蚕) – Not your average bug:** In the world of *RMJI*, spirit insects are often graded on specialized lists (like the famous **Strange Insect List / 奇虫榜**), and the Golden Silk Silkworm sits comfortably in the upper tier. It produces gold threads that can bind magical treasures and even shake ancient artifacts. Wan Tianming’s ability to borrow one—let alone seven or eight—signals a level of sect resource mobilization that even the demonic-path overlords didn’t anticipate. Think of it as bringing a high-end industrial crane to a fistfight.

**Cold Jiao (寒蛟) & Li Gui (离龟) – Status symbols on loan:** These two creatures are loaned from even higher-realm cultivators (the “madwoman” and Tian Yuanzi), which instantly changes the battlefield calculus. In the Mortal Stream, borrowing a powerful spirit beast is a social act: it signals political debt, mutual favor, and a willingness to escalate. Man Brutal Beard’s frustration is not just about the beasts’ power—it’s that Wan Tianming *out-politicked* him before the fight even started.

**Daoist Names (道号) – Identity and legacy:** The scholar-robed elder is referred to by multiple names (Qing Yi Jushi, and his spirit beast is the “Qing Ji Niao / Green Thorn Bird”). In cultivation culture, a Daoist name (号 / Hao) often reflects a cultivator’s philosophy, specialty, or reclusive identity. Switching between these names mid-chapter reinforces the layered identity game that high-level cultivators play.

## Reading Guide

- **Watch the scroll.** Han Li has a habit of stumbling into plot-critical items at the worst possible moment. The fact that it was hidden in an otherwise unremarkable stone, inside a frozen platform that even Nascent Soul cultivators haven’t fully explored, suggests this is not a random loot drop. Keep an eye on that yellowed scroll—it might just be the ace up his sleeve or a ticking time bomb.
- **The “borrowed beast” economy.** This chapter is a textbook example of how the *RMJI* power ecosystem operates: you don’t just fight with your own strength—you borrow, loan, and trade spirit beasts, treasures, and favors. Han Li, stuck at the Foundation Establishment level, is watching a war fought with weapons he can’t even afford to rent.
- **Han Li’s silence speaks volumes.** He does not scream, does not panic, does not even complain about the cold out loud. His only reaction to Extreme Yin’s call is internal cursing and a seamless performance of compliance. This is the Mortal Stream in action: your only freedom is the privacy of your own thoughts.
