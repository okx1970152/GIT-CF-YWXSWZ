## Chapter Overview
Alright, fellow Daoists, buckle up. The Void Heaven Palace arc is not over, but our favorite mortal is already doing what he does best: getting the heck out of dodge. This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream problem-solving. Han Li has his prize, he has a target—the Outer Star Seas—and there's a shiny inter-island teleportation array sitting in front of him. The only catch? A pair of local Core Formation gatekeepers, a squad of suspicious rogue cultivators, and one too few chairs at the table. What follows is a beautiful, layered negotiation that has absolutely nothing to do with martial prowess and everything to do with information, leverage, and the willingness to part with treasures that are 'tasteless to eat, yet a pity to discard.'

## Key Plot Points
- Seven rogue cultivators, including Han Li in disguise, prepare to use a Star Palace teleportation array, but the number of spots is limited to six.
- A blue-clad man (Han Li) calmly targets the thin man in the group, offering two tier-five demon cores in exchange for his spot.
- When the thin man refuses, Han Li uses secret voice transmission to deliver a threat based on information he covertly extracted, revealing the man’s collusion with Star Palace officials to inflate fees.
- The thin man, terrified his corruption will be exposed, immediately caves and cedes the spot.
- The bald Core Formation elder objects to the substitution, so Han Li bribes both gatekeepers with a pair of flying-knife treasures, which they eagerly accept.
- Han Li reflects on his tactics: he drugged the thin man and used hypnosis to extract his secret, then leveraged it. He also notes the two treasures were acquired from Hu Yue, killed by the Profound Bone Saint.
- The gatekeepers lift the array’s restrictions, accept payment, and hand out teleportation talismans. Han Li steps onto the formation without hesitation, heading for the ‘Qi Yuan’ island.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Magical Treasures (法宝, Fa Bao)** in the *RMJI* world exist in a strange economy. They are not as common as a xianxia beginner might expect; many are destroyed in battle, hoarded by the strong, or hidden away. Gifting one to a Core Formation cultivator is a massive favor—they can use it immediately (albeit at 70% power), skipping the decades-long refinement period. This explains why the bald elder broke protocol so cheerfully.
- **Voice Transmission (传音, Chuan Yin)** is a classic tool in xianxia drama. By speaking directly into the target’s mind, a cultivator can deliver a private threat or piece of information without anyone else knowing. It is the ultimate tool for sowing discord, revealing a hidden co-conspirator, or, in this case, blackmailing someone in a crowded room.
- **The “Chicken Rib” Philosophy (鸡肋, Ji Lei)** returns! Han Li internally calls the two treasures “tasteless to eat, yet a pity to discard.” This ancient idiom describes something that is valuable but not useful to its owner, making it a perfect bargaining chip. Han Li’s hoarder mentality means he has a drawer full of such ‘chicken ribs’ ready for precisely this kind of transaction.

## Reading Guide
- Pay attention to how Han Li’s victory is engineered. He does not fight anyone. He wins because he identifies a *structural* weakness: the thin man has a secret he cannot afford to reveal, and the gatekeepers are corruptible with a resource they covet. The fight happens entirely within the social and information domain.
- This chapter also reinforces Han Li’s core rule of ‘never reveal a treasure.’ He presents himself as a wandering cultivator (Fellow Daoist Qu), using a disguise and keeping his true strength hidden. Even when offering bribes, he frames them as items he “cannot use yet,” maintaining his cover as someone of lower status.
- Finally, note the cold simplicity of the conflict resolution. There is no dramatic shouting match. Once the threat is delivered, the thin man yields instantly. Once the bribe is accepted, the gatekeepers comply. In the Mortal Stream, the most effective negotiations are often the quietest ones.
