## Chapter Overview

The hunting party thought they had the upper hand—until the fourth Blood Servant showed up. Chapter 323 delivers the kind of tactical pivot that defines *A Record of a Mortal’s Journey to Immortality*: just when Han Li’s Giant Sword Art is about to clinch a personal vendetta, an old face from the past steps in to flip the board. Wu Jiuzhi, the light-fingered youth from years ago, reappears as a Foundation Establishment cultivator fighting for the other side. And then the Four Great Blood Servants do something that changes the calculus entirely: they initiate a synchronized Demonic Transformation. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger that promises a fight far beyond the scale of what came before—and a reunion that stings more than any blade.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li’s Giant Sword Art—drawn from the Azure Origin Sword Light page—proves devastating. It shatters Green Pattern’s bronze mirror shield in one blow and locks the Azure Wood True Shield in a grinding stalemate.
- Senior Brother Wang shatters the two golden rings that had been harassing him and prepares to tip the scales against Green Pattern, hoping to claim a share of the spoils.
- A streak of yellow light rescues Green Pattern at the last second. The newcomer is Wu Jiuzhi (Wu the Nine-Fingered), the crafty young thief Han Li encountered during his early travels, now a Foundation Establishment cultivator.
- Green Pattern signals with a whistle, recalling Tie Luo and Bing Yao from their separate battles. The Four Great Blood Servants are now assembled in full force.
- Liu Jing immediately recognizes the danger of facing four Blood Servants at once and calls the Yellow Maple Valley group into a defensive formation alongside Han Li.
- Han Li’s internal reaction to seeing Wu Jiuzhi is layered—there is no hatred, only a quiet, bitter recognition that fate has forced an old acquaintance into enmity.
- Green Pattern declares a synchronized Demonic Transformation. The four Blood Servants cocoon themselves in blood-red light, signaling an all-or-nothing escalation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Giant Sword Art (巨剑术):** This is not a generic “big sword spell.” In xianxia, the *Giant Sword Art* is a high-level sword-manipulation technique that compresses a cultivator’s mana into a massive, single-strike construct. The fact that Han Li can use it at early Foundation Establishment is a testament to the quality of the Azure Origin Sword Light page and his own unusually robust divine sense. It’s a technique with no subtlety—just overwhelming, concentrated force.

**Matched Tool Sets (成套法器):** Han Li’s ability to control over a dozen tools simultaneously is not raw power—it is clever engineering. The text explicitly notes that these tools are a matched set. In xianxia terms, a matched set consumes mana as if it were a single tool while allowing the user to activate multiple attacks. This is why Han Li, who trained the Great Derivative Art to strengthen his divine sense, favors them. It is a logistical advantage, not a magical shortcut.

**Demonic Transformation (妖化):** When a cultivator or a half-demon combatant undergoes Demonic Transformation, they surrender their human form for raw demonic power. The process is not a gentle empowerment—it is a forced mutation. The blood-colored cocoon phase suggests a dramatic, violent restructuring of the body. This is a last-resort tactic, and its initiation here signals that the Blood Servants believe they cannot win a conventional fight.

**Wu Jiuzhi’s Reappearance:** Wu Jiuzhi was introduced during Han Li’s early Qi Condensation years as a clever, light-fingered youth. In the cultivation world, such chance encounters rarely lead to happy reunions. The novel deliberately uses this callback to underscore a brutal theme: the cultivation path is narrow, and old friends often end up on opposite sides of a killing field.

## Reading Guide

**The emotional weight of this chapter hangs on one reunion:** Han Li vs. Wu Jiuzhi. There is no dramatic declaration of betrayal—just a quiet sigh from Wu Jiuzhi and a cold acknowledgment from Han Li. If you remember the young thief’s skill and charm from earlier volumes, this moment hits differently. It’s a reminder that in this world, kindness and shared history are debts that expire the moment survival demands a choice.

**The logistics nerds will love the Giant Sword Art breakdown.** The chapter explicitly states that Han Li’s mana limit is five independent tools, and that his ability to throw more than a dozen at once comes from them being a matched set. This is classic Mortal Stream behavior: victory comes from system knowledge and preparation, not spontaneous power-ups.

**The cliffhanger is clean but heavy.** Four Demonic Transformations, simultaneous. Any veteran reader knows that synchronized transformations in xianxia are never a bluff. The next chapter is going to be a chaos of raw, monstrous violence—and Han Li, for all his clever tricks, is about to face something that no matched tool set can easily solve.

**Watch for the tone shift.** Up to this point, the fight was cold and tactical. Now, with Wu Jiuzhi’s appearance and the Demonic Transformation initiation, the chapter pivots to something more desperate. The Four Great Blood Servants are no longer fighting to win—they are fighting to destroy.
