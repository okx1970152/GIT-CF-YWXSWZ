## Chapter Overview
Han Li makes it out of the frying pan and into… a very confusing fire. After a desperate escape from the Wind Tear Beast via a hidden teleportation array, he lands in a cave filled with strange cultivators who are all waiting for a mysterious group mission—and they clearly think he’s part of it. The man they’re looking for is surnamed Li. Han Li is not surnamed Li. The mortals in the last arc were all named various permutations of Mo, which is a different surname. This is what we call a classic case of mistaken identity in the Dark Forest, and Han Li, being Han Li, does not correct them. He plays along. Get ready for a chapter where Han Li learns that the best disguise is sometimes just letting other people’s assumptions do the work.

## Key Plot Points
*   Han Li successfully triggers the teleportation array, sacrificing a mouthful of Essence Blood to speed it up. The Wind Tear Beast arrives too late to stop him.
*   Feng Xi, enraged by Han Li’s escape, channels his fury into Miao He, who arrives right after the teleportation ends. Miao He is defeated, his physical body destroyed, and only his nascent soul escapes.
*   In the aftermath, the Flood Dragon clan launches a massive, years-long hunt for high-level human cultivators. They specifically ask for someone surnamed Li before attacking.
*   With no real Li-surnamed cultivator found among the victims, the blame falls on a phantom figure. The entire sea region is thrown into chaos.
*   Han Li arrives safely at his destination—a teleportation nexus on Huangming Island, inside the Inner Star Seas—and secretly sabotages his arrival array to prevent pursuit.
*   He is immediately mistaken for a late Core Formation member of a five-person expedition team by a skull-faced, blue-eyed Core Formation cultivator.
*   Han Li plays along, feigning ignorance of the mission’s rules to gather information without revealing his true identity.
*   The remaining team members arrive, including an elder bearing a mysterious green flame emblem, completing the group for an unknown assignment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
### The Surname Li Crisis: Why the Flood Dragons Are So Angry
This chapter introduces a fascinating cultural plot device: the mass manhunt for anyone surnamed Li (厉). The Flood Dragon clan is not looking for Han Li (韩立) by his personal name; they’re hunting by clan name. In Chinese culture, one’s surname is paramount—it represents lineage, ancestry, and a shared identity that spans generations. By asking for “a Li,” the flood dragons aren’t asking for a specific person’s description; they are asking for a target belonging to a specific clan. This makes the hunt simultaneously more indiscriminate (anyone with that surname is a valid target) and more specific (most people either are or aren’t a Li). Han Li’s survival here is a stroke of sheer luck: the Wind Tear Beast only knew him by his face and his actions, not his family registry.

## Reading Guide
Sit back and enjoy the irony. The entire demon clan and human world are tearing itself apart looking for a “Li” cultivator, and the actual culprit—Han Li—lands right in the middle of a new adventure, instantly mistaken for a different late-stage Core Formation powerhouse. The chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s favorite technique: *letting other people’s mistakes be your disguise*. Han Li doesn’t lie once; the skull-faced man just assumes he’s part of the team because of his cultivation level and arrival time. The tension is wonderfully inverted: usually Han Li fears being discovered; here, he fears the other shoe dropping and the real Li showing up. Plus, we get a delicious taste of the wider consequences: the flood dragons are furious, the human cultivators are terrified, and everyone is blaming some poor, non-existent Li. A perfect palate cleanser before the real mission begins.
