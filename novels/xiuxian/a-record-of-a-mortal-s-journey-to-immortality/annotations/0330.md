## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a textbook example of what makes *RMJI* so brutal and so addictive: a winner-takes-all duel where every single move is a resource-management decision wrapped in blood and demonic light. Emperor Yue, having absorbed the blue-robed man’s cultivation, pops a self-damaging, self-only healing pill that resets the entire fight, then reveals a hidden killing technique—the Blood Drill—that punches through Han Li’s defenses like wet paper. Han Li, for the first time in a long while, stares death in the face and makes the coldest possible call: retreat. No heroic last stand, no dramatic vow to avenge the fallen. Just a single, unambiguous “Go.” That’s the Mortal Stream in a nutshell.

## Key Plot Points
- Emperor Yue downs a Bone Marrow Replenishing Pill, restoring his blood-light and injuries to near-pre-Heaven Thunder Seed levels. The pill is a self-harming, one-time-only trump card only usable by practitioners of specific demonic arts.
- In the brief reprieve, Zhong Weiniang regains consciousness and declares her intent to kill Emperor Yue for murdering Senior Brother Liu. Han Li immediately warns that the sentiment is mutual.
- Emperor Yue’s blood-light thickens to several *zhang*, and the stolen magical tools within begin to dissolve—a visual signal that his power has spiked dramatically compared to earlier.
- He retrieves the fire-red orb dropped by the blue-robed man, completing the set of “Three Blood-Condensing Five-Element Pills,” a treasure that benefits Core Formation advancement.
- Emperor Yue taunts Han Li about the Heaven Thunder Seed before launching a sudden “Blood Drill” attack—a compressed true essence projectile that punches through two defensive tools and the Azure Origin Sword Shield in one hit.
- Han Li barely dodges a fatal blow, taking a shoulder wound instead, and immediately orders the group to retreat. Ten puppets are left behind to block the pursuit.
- Emperor Yue hesitates over using his last remaining Blood Drill, showing that even his trump card is finite. The chapter closes with the puppets encircling him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Blood Drill — the “Budget Sword Intent” of Demonic Arts**: The Blood Drill is essentially a hyper-compressed, one-shot spike of true essence that the caster has been cultivating in secret for a very long time. In real-world xianxia terminology, many high-level demonic or orthodox arts have “secret killing moves” like this—techniques that demand a massive upfront investment in pain and time for a single, devastating output. Emperor Yue’s shock at Han Li’s survival is a reminder that even the strongest secret move can fail against an opponent who simply does not cooperate by dying on schedule.

**The Bone Marrow Replenishing Pill**: This is not a “magical health potion.” It is a specialized, self-harming pill that only works for cultivators of certain demonic arts, and it permanently damages the user’s cultivation base. In the Mortal Stream, there is no free lunch: every miracle recovery comes with a long-term price tag, and Emperor Yue is essentially burning his future to survive the present. That is the *RMJI* way.

**The “Three Blood-Condensing Five-Element Pills”**: These orbs—one from the dead blue-robed man and one now in Emperor Yue’s hands—are not consumables you swallow. They are alchemical treasures that assist Core Formation. The fact that Emperor Yue traded his ally’s death for them (and nearly got himself killed in the process) shows how resource-acquisition and betrayal in this world are two sides of the same coin.

## Reading Guide
- **Watch Han Li’s decision-making**: He does not hesitate, does not bargain, does not try one more spell. The moment he sees the Blood Drill’s power, he recalculates the odds and gives the order to flee. This is not cowardice; it is an asset-preservation strategy. Ten puppets are a better sacrifice than his own life.
- **Emperor Yue’s hesitation is key**: He has one Blood Drill left, and he knows Han Li dodged the first one. The tactical symmetry here—both sides running low on trump cards—is the Mortal Stream’s favorite kind of tension.
- **Choral structure**: Han Li’s voice transmission and his final public warning create a moment of internal discord within the group, but his authority holds. The dynamic of “cold leader + shaken followers” is a recurring pattern in survival arcs.
- **Foreshadowing**: Emperor Yue’s reference to the Heaven Thunder Seed confirms that Han Li’s reputation as the one who killed the blue-robed man is already established. Enemies are starting to treat him with targeted caution—a double-edged sword in the Dark Forest.
