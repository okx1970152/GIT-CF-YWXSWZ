## Chapter Overview
Sixty years of solitary cultivation. That’s the headline here, folks. After the relentless, high-octane beast-hunting arc in the Outer Star Seas, Chapter 403 is Han Li’s ultimate “quiet victory” phase—a massive, decade-spanning time-skip where he locks himself away to transform his mountain of demon cores into raw cultivation power. But don’t mistake this for a boring montage. This chapter isn’t about the fight; it’s about the *strategy*. From overcoming cultivation bottlenecks to experimenting with an ancient spirit herb’s bizarre effects on his dormant Gold Devourer Beetles, every action here is a calculated part of his long game. The payoff? A sky-splitting, city-shaking Core Formation heaven phenomenon that signals Han Li’s official entry into the ranks of the cultivation world’s major players. Get ready for a masterclass in resource management and patient ambition.

## Key Plot Points
- **Safe Return to Heaven Star City**: After months of tense travel from the Outer Star Seas, Han Li makes it back to his cave residence on the thirty-ninth layer of Heaven Star City, immediately collapsing into a deep sleep from total exhaustion.
- **The Post-Trip Stockpile**: Han Li uses his newly acquired alchemical formulas and accelerated spirit herbs to refine a massive stockpile of pills from his collected demon cores, finally solving his long-standing medicinal resistance problem.
- **The Rainbow Skirt Grass Experiment**: While sorting his inventory, Han Li becomes fixated on the uncanny attraction his century-old Rainbow Skirt Grass has for demon beasts. On a whim, he tosses a stalk to his hibernating Gold Devourer Beetles, but they show no response.
- **Dissipating Cultivation for the Three Revolutions Heavy Origin Art**: Han Li begins the slow, dangerous process of lowering his cultivation to early Foundation Establishment to properly practice his core technique.
- **The Beetles Awaken**: After a failed first attempt, Han Li uses the Green Liquid to accelerate another stalk of Rainbow Skirt Grass. This time, the dormant Gold Devourer Beetles swarm and devour it instantly, becoming agitated. Han Li sees a new experimental path opening up.
- **The Sixty-Year Seclusion**: With full preparations complete, Han Li enters life-and-death seclusion, dedicating himself entirely to cultivation for sixty long years.
- **The Sky Splits, A Core Forms**: The chapter climaxes with a massive heaven phenomenon—black clouds, silver lightning, and a visible vortex of spiritual qi—as Han Li successfully forms his Golden Core, shocking every cultivator in Heaven Star City.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Gold Devourer Beetle (噬金虫 / Shi Jin Chong)**: These are not just any bugs. In the xianxia hierarchy of rare spirit insects, the Gold Devourer Beetle is a legendary “strange insect” (#73 on the Imperial Spirit Sect’s ranking). They are notoriously difficult to control and are prized for their near-indestructible shells and ability to devour virtually any material. The fact that Han Li is now experimenting with their feeding patterns shows just how far his cultivation expertise has come—he’s not just using them as a blunt weapon, but actively trying to understand their biology. That’s the mark of a mature cultivator.

**The Rainbow Skirt Grass’s Aura**: The novel refers to a “strange aura” emitted by the grass that attracts demon beasts. The Chinese term “霓裳草” literally evokes a rainbow-colored garment, which is a reference to the celestial dance attire of Daoist immortals. This cultural hint suggests the grass’s scent is not merely an animal attractant, but something that mimics a trace of celestial, immortal-level spiritual energy—a kind of “primordial deliciousness” that even the most selective beasts find irresistible. This may explain why Han Li is so keen to study it.

**Core Formation Heaven Phenomenon**: In xianxia, breaking through to Core Formation is one of the most pivotal milestones. The “dark clouds pressing down, silver snakes dancing, and a vortex of spiritual energy” are the universe’s *announcement* that a qualitative change has occurred. This isn’t just noise; it’s a cosmic signal that a cultivator has successfully crystallized their compressed qi into a permanent, self-sustaining core. The reaction of other cultivators—envy, jealousy, curiosity—is a core part of the Mortal Stream’s social fabric because a new Core Formation master is a major shift in the local power balance.

## Reading Guide
This is a “patience reward” chapter, folks. The first half might feel like a quiet inventory check, but that’s exactly the point. *A Record of a Mortal’s Journey to Immortality* is famous for making you feel the *weight* of cultivation. Han Li didn’t just magically jump to Core Formation; he spent sixty years in a room, fighting boredom and mental fatigue with nothing but pills and his own will. The beauty here is in the details: the casual decision to experiment with the Rainbow Skirt Grass, the cold logic of selling materials through small-time merchants to avoid attention, the careful dissipation of his own cultivation base. If you’re reading this for the action, you might be bored. But if you’re here for the slow, methodical grind of a survivor climbing the ladder of immortality, this chapter is *peak* Mortal Stream. The final scene—the city shaking with his success—is the payoff for all that silence. It’s earned, and it tastes *good*.
