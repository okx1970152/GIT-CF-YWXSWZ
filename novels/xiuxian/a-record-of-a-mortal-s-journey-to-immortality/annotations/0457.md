## Chapter Overview

The hunt for the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng begins in earnest in this chapter, but the real battle is fought long before any traps are sprung. Xuan Gu and his ghostly partner launch a search using the Soul Search Grand Art, while Han Li, with his usual paranoia, prepares layered countermeasures against the very formation flags Xuan Gu gave him. It’s a chapter of silent, tense maneuvering—both sides are setting their boards, adjusting their pieces, and waiting for the other to make the first move. And just when the trap seems ready, who steps into the crossfire but an unassuming white rabbit? The game is afoot, fellow daoists—and Han Li has already flipped the board before the opponent even realized they were playing.

## Key Plot Points

- Xuan Gu uses the Soul Search Grand Art with his ghost partner to locate the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng, which has hidden its main body inside a hollow boulder.
- Han Li, suspicious of the formation flags Xuan Gu gave him, supplements them with a dozen more yellow flags of his own, revealing he fully expects the original flags to be rigged.
- Xuan Gu tests the formation from a distance using his master control flag and, satisfied that his own trap is intact, departs to the rocky mountain to wait.
- Han Li uses a concealment technique to vanish from the grove after Xuan Gu leaves, confirming his position in the ambush is a carefully chosen spot.
- The chapter ends with a white rabbit—a potential incarnation of the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng—arriving at the edge of the formation, lured by the overwhelming stench of the Musk Orchid Beast feces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Soul Search Grand Art (搜灵大法):** This is not a gentle mind-reading technique. In xianxia, a “soul search” implies violently rifling through the ambient spiritual signatures of the environment (or a living being’s soul) to extract specific information. Xuan Gu and his partner use it here to track the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng by its unique spiritual aura—a sign that this technique can function as a supernatural tracking system, not just an interrogation tool.

**Musk Orchid Beast Feces:** This is a classic xianxia inversion of “what is foul to humans is exquisite to a spiritual entity.” The Musk Orchid Beast is normally prized for the calming incense from its horn; the fact that its waste—the vile byproduct—is what the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng craves is a delightfully subversive detail. It reinforces a core Mortal Stream theme: the universe’s logic is rarely aligned with human aesthetics or morality.

**Golden Lightning Bamboo (金雷竹) and Evil-Repelling Divine Lightning:** Xuan Gu’s entire tactical assessment of Han Li hinges on whether the Evil-Repelling Divine Lightning has been “spent.” This is a recurring resource-management mechanic in xianxia combat: a cultivator’s most powerful trump card is often a finite resource that must be strategically conserved or risked. The fact that Xuan Gu believes Han Li’s lightning is exhausted is a major miscalculation waiting to be exploited.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s philosophy of layered preparation. Han Li accepts the presumably rigged formation flags from Xuan Gu, but then—without a word—deploys over a dozen additional flags of his own, altering the entire battlefield beneath the opponent’s nose. Meanwhile, Xuan Gu’s own paranoia leads him to “verify” the formation from a distance, confirming his trap is still in place. Both men are playing 3D chess, but Han Li has already placed a piece on the board that Xuan Gu hasn’t even seen yet.

Also, pay attention to the ghost partner’s explicit fear of the Evil-Repelling Divine Lightning—he says it can “erase” a formless spirit like him in a way that no ordinary attack can. This is a textbook example of element-versus-entity counterplay in the xianxia system: not all power scales are linear. A low-level cultivator with the right element (Yang-aligned lightning, in this case) can kill a far “higher” existence if the creature’s fundamental nature is weak against it. That knowledge alone makes Han Li’s hidden Golden Lightning Bamboo swords a ticking time bomb—and Xuan Gu has just publicly assumed the bomb is defused.

Finally, the white rabbit’s arrival at the very end is a perfect suspense hook. The Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng is a master of transformation and escape; its incarnation is already on the scene, but has it truly fallen for the trap, or is it probing with just one of its countless forms? The chapter leaves us hanging on the answer.
