## Chapter Overview

If you thought Han Li’s battle with the Poison Flood Dragon was already a brutal war of attrition, you haven’t seen anything yet. This chapter is a textbook case of quantity-over-quality warfare shoved down the throat of a classical demon beast. Han Li unleashes a monstrous swarm of Gold Devourer Beetles—over a hundred thousand strong—and turns the refining chamber into a buzzing hell. But raw numbers come with a cost: the flood dragon’s venom blood turns expensive beetles into toxic puddles with every spray. This is not a fair fight—it’s a resource trade-off where Han Li calculates his losses like a merchant auditing a failing warehouse. And just when you think he’s got it under control, a new nightmare surfaces: the turtle demon has a Self-Healing Body. Uh-oh.

## Key Plot Points

- The Poison Flood Dragon’s scales collapse under the Gold Devourer Beetles’ assault, but its venom blood sprays out and kills massive numbers of insects.
- Feng Xi and the flood dragon are visibly shaken by the sheer scale of Han Li’s swarm—over a hundred thousand beetles, unheard of in the Chaotic Star Sea’s insect-driving circles.
- The flood dragon’s venom light gradually weakens and fails, and it is devoured to death.
- Han Li extracts the flood dragon’s demon core and captures its fleeing soul using the Crying Soul Beast’s paralysis, then stores the soul in a jade bottle.
- Feng Xi breaks the light barrier with his giant swords, but Han Li retrieves the Wind Lightning Wings before she can claim them.
- The turtle demon is revealed: battered beyond belief, but its Self-Healing Body rapidly regenerates all damage, making it nearly immune to attrition tactics.
- Han Li realizes his Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords—even combined into a giant sword—lack the raw power to decapitate an eighth-grade demon beast.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Self-Healing Body (自治之体)** — In Chinese beast mythology and xianxia, certain creatures are born with a “self-governing body” that grants near-instantaneous wound recovery and even limb regeneration. This is not a cultivated skill but an innate constitution—a genetic lottery win in the demon world. While common among low-grade beasts (lizards, starfish-like monsters), it is extremely rare among high-grade monsters, making this turtle a uniquely annoying opponent who cannot be rushed with swarm attrition.

**Insect Driving (驱虫术)** — The art of commanding spirit insects is a niche specialization within the cultivation world. The chapter explicitly explains why: insects breed slowly (hundreds of years per generation), require decades of training, and can be wiped out in a single bad fight. Unlike swords or talismans, a driven insect swarm represents a generational investment—often passed from master to disciple across centuries to amass even a thousand-strong force. Han Li’s hundred-thousand swarm is thus a world-breaking anomaly that shocks even veteran demon beasts.

**Giant Sword Technique (巨剑术)** — A classic xianxia trick where multiple flying swords of the same set are merged into a single colossal sword. The power of a combined sword is exponentially greater than the sum of its parts, but even this is not enough against a demon body that has been tempered for centuries. The chapter subtly reinforces Han Li’s weakness: his swords are young (only decades old) and have not been fully tempered, making them insufficient for top-tier targets.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a perfect demonstration of the Mortal Stream’s core lesson: **numbers and attrition are tools, not solutions.** Han Li wins the first round by out-resourcing the flood dragon, but the turtle immediately throws a wrench into that plan. Pay attention to Han Li’s reaction—he doesn’t panic, doesn’t groan, doesn’t curse his luck. He simply admits the problem, runs the math, and begins recalibrating. This is the cold, pragmatic mindset that keeps him alive: no matter how powerful your trump card, the universe will always throw a counter-card. The tension now shifts from “can he kill all three” to “can he get out alive with what he’s already earned.

### The Cost of a Cheap Trophy

Han Li scoops the Wind Lightning Wings the moment Feng Xi’s barrier breaks, stuffs it into his bag, and doesn’t even look at it twice. This is peak Mortal Stream behavior: when the battlefield gives you a window, *take the asset and sort out the logistics later.* The Wind Spirit Beast’s fury at this casual theft is almost comic—she’s pinned in a losing battle, watching her hard-won treasure pocketed as if it were pocket change.
