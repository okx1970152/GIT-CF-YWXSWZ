## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow daoists, to the final act of a fight that has turned into a slow, grinding chess match. Last time, Han Li and his scheming opponent were locked in magical combat, unable to break free. This chapter forces both combatants to confront the same ugly truth: this isn’t about who has the flashiest technique anymore. It’s about who runs out of fuel first. Watch as Han Li abandons his defenses, starts snacking on random herbs, and drags a perfectly rational villain into a state of screaming disbelief. The tension here is quiet, cold, and utterly ruthless—pure Mortal Stream attrition warfare at its finest.

## Key Plot Points
- The battle between Han Li and Senior Brother Lu transitions into a pure war of attrition, with both sides channeling all their magic power into their respective treasures (the Sword Talisman and the Azure Flood Dragon Banner), unable to spare effort for other attacks.
- Both cultivators begin using spirit stones to replenish their magic power; Senior Brother Lu is shocked to see Han Li pull out a mid-grade stone, which recharges faster than his own low-grade wind stone.
- Senior Brother Lu briefly considers creating a Wind Blade to assassinate Han Li, but is forced to abandon the attempt when Han Li exploits the distraction to send the giant sword straight at him, threatening mutual destruction.
- Han Li voluntarily drops his blue protective barrier, exposing his unguarded body, which confuses Senior Brother Lu and forces him into a psychological trap where any attempt to attack is met with a counter-threat of immediate death.
- As the attrition drags on, Han Li begins eating various herbs and plant roots from his storage pouch, further unsettling his opponent, who cannot guess the purpose of the strange behavior.
- The Azure Flood Dragon begins to dim while the giant sword remains bright; Senior Brother Lu realizes in panic that Han Li’s magic power should have run out first, but is screaming “Impossible!” as his own reserves fail.
- In desperation, Senior Brother Lu recalls the banner, forms a final massive Wind Blade aimed at Han Li, and attempts mutual destruction, but Han Li evades using Smoke Step, leaving his opponent to be bisected by the giant sword.
- Han Li collapses to the ground, the fight won, the giant sword floating dimly above the corpse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**War of Attrition as a Combat Doctrine** – This chapter exemplifies a key feature of Mortal Stream combat: when both sides are evenly matched in technique, the deciding factor is resource management. The fight is reduced to a logistical calculation—who has more spirit stones, better grade stones, and fewer ways to waste power. Han Li’s use of a mid-grade stone, which recharges faster, is a subtle but massive advantage that flips the expected outcome.

**Mid-Grade Spirit Stone** – These are extremely rare at the Qi Condensation level. Most disciples operate on low-grade stones, which recharge magic power slowly enough that they are negligible during intense combat. A mid-grade stone, however, provides a meaningful stream of energy, enough to turn a war of attrition. That a junior disciple like Han Li possesses one underscores that he is far more prepared than he appears, and it’s a moment of genuine shock for Senior Brother Lu—a crack in the confidence of a more senior opponent.

**Voluntary Shield Drop as Psychological Warfare** – Why would any sane fighter drop their defenses in the middle of a duel? Because a shield that costs nothing to maintain is one thing; a shield that siphons your limited magic power is a luxury you can’t afford. Han Li’s decision to expose his body is a cold, utilitarian calculation: reduce energy drain and bait the opponent into overstepping. It also disguises his real limit—he may have been near empty anyway, and the shield was doing less good than the pretense of fearlessness.

## Reading Guide
This is a textbook Mortal Stream fight scene: no sudden power-ups, no dramatic final forms, no emotional speeches. Just two men squeezing the last drop of energy out of their depleted reserves while one of them plays mind games with his own life as bait. The tension lies in watching a rational, calculating villain slowly unravel as he realizes the numbers don’t add up the way he expected. Pay attention to Han Li’s decision to drop the shield—it’s a sign that his internal calculation has reached a winning conclusion, and everything after is just the execution.

One moment to savor: Senior Brother Lu’s final scream of “Impossible!” This is not an anime villain’s monologue—it is the sound of a mind that trusted its own arithmetic breaking under the weight of a variable it never accounted for (the mid-grade stone and Han Li’s unknown herb stash). In the Mortal Stream, arrogance is not punished by fate, but by faulty algebra.
