## Chapter Overview

Well, the mask is officially off. After a tense game of cat-and-mouse with the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng (that white rabbit was way smarter than it looked!), Han Li finally outsmarts the little escape artist and bags it. But his victory is short-lived, because the real battle is about to begin. Our boy is no fool—he knows the Profound Bone Saint is a coiled viper, so he sets up a hardcore defensive formation and summons his trump cards, making it crystal clear: “Try me, grandpa.” And try him the Profound Bone Saint does! The alliance shatters in spectacular fashion as the old ghost reveals he’s had a hidden ace up his sleeve all along—a mysterious partner and a cursed net! This chapter is a masterclass in escalating tension and the bitter, paranoid logic of the Mortal Stream, where trust is just a weapon waiting to be used.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li successfully sets a trap for the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng’s incarnation, a clever white rabbit, using the Great Derivative Art and a specialized formation. The rabbit’s feigned retreat and ambush backfire, leading to its capture by the golden silk net.
- Sensing the Profound Bone Saint’s intentions, Han Li establishes a strong defensive position, laying out an ancient treasure and releasing his Gold Devourer Beetles as a visible threat. He makes a point of demonstrating his reformed formation, countering the Saint’s secret control.
- The verbal confrontation escalates. Han Li reveals he saw the Saint’s partner transform to search for the ginseng, shattering the pretense of a one-on-one alliance. He directly asks if the Saint wants a fight that would leave them both weak for Extreme Yin.
- The Profound Bone Saint scoffs at the “both sides weakened” claim, asserting that killing Han Li is merely an “inconvenience” he is willing to pay. He attacks the formation, testing its integrity.
- Han Li effortlessly neutralizes the Saint’s tampering of the formation flags, showcasing his deeper control over the array, which surprises the Saint.
- The Profound Bone Saint unleashes his true trap: the golden silk net, a gift, transforms into the “Yin Soul Thread,” a dark curse that instantly binds Han Li and seals his mana. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger with Han Li completely trapped and powerless.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng (九曲灵参)**  
This isn’t just a rare herb; it’s a classic trope in Chinese mythology and xianxia: a spirit plant that has cultivated intelligence and can transform into an animal or even human form. Ginseng, in particular, is a staple of Chinese medicine, believed to be a powerful life-giving root. The “nine bends” in its name imply a profound, deeply rooted vitality that has taken on a sentient, even mischievous, nature. Capturing one is a major prize, as they are said to be incredible aids in overcoming bottlenecks, especially for Nascent Soul formation. They are the ultimate escape artists, masters of “earth遁” (earth escape), making them nearly impossible to catch—hence Han Li’s elaborate setup.

**Yin Soul Thread (阴魂丝)**  
This is a classic example of a “treacherous treasure” in the Mortal Stream. It appears as a benevolent gift, a useful tool, only to be activated remotely as a deadly trap by its true master. In Daoist alchemy, the concept of “Yin” and “Soul” combined creates a spiritual pollutant that corrupts and binds. The “threads” (丝) evoke the image of a spider’s web—invisible, inescapable, and designed to drain the life from its victim. It highlights the deep paranoia of this world: never trust a gift from a potential enemy, no matter how genuine it seems.

**Formation Flag Tampering (阵旗手脚)**  
The Profound Bone Saint’s attempt to secretly control the formation’s flags is a clever piece of technical sabotage. In Chinese magical systems, arrays are composed of “flag” or “plate” components that channel spiritual energy. If a knowledgeable enemy can “write” their own spiritual imprint onto a flag, they can hijack the entire formation. Han Li’s counter—a simple spell seal that resets the control—shows that he is not just powerful, but deeply knowledgeable about the nuts and bolts of magical combat. This is a key trait of a survival-focused protagonist.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for the next chapter, because this is a classic Mortal Stream reversal. Han Li is completely sealed, his mana blocked. In a heroic story, this would be the moment for a dramatic rescue or a hidden power to break free. But this is *RMJI*. Right now, his odds are 0%. His only hope is that his preparations were so deep and layered that the Profound Bone Saint can’t afford to just kill him without a long, drawn-out fight. Pay close attention to the Gold Devourer Beetles and the ancient treasure he set out—those are his only cards now. The question isn’t *if* he escapes, but *what* he has to sacrifice to even the odds. The Profound Bone Saint has just chosen violence, and Han Li is going to show him just how expensive that choice really is. This is a high-stakes lesson in Mortal Stream economics: never corner a survivor without counting his hidden mines.
