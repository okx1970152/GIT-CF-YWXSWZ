## Chapter Overview

Our favorite calculating cultivator finds himself in a rather awkward position: stranded in the Netherworld Domain with zero mana, and now he has to turn down a perfectly nice lady’s request for a ride out of hell. But here’s the thing about Han Li—when he says no, he’s already running the numbers on whether the deal can be renegotiated. And sure enough, Mei Ning drops a bombshell that could change everything: she can give him a temporary boost of the legendary Spirit Essence Qi, a rare essence that can punch through the mana-sealing effect of this desolate realm. The catch? He has to take her *and* search for her missing brother within three months. This chapter is a masterclass in transactional negotiation, wrapped in ancient cultivation lore and a dash of mortal tension.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li firmly but politely refuses to take Mei Ning with him up Stormwind Mountain, citing his inability to protect her in a danger zone.
- After a long silence, Mei Ning reveals she possesses the Jade Phoenix Marrow Body, capable of transferring a thread of Spirit Essence Qi to a willing male cultivator.
- Han Li verifies the claim by examining the vermilion mark on her arm, which transforms into a silver phoenix pattern when pressed.
- The negotiation formalizes: Mei Ning will give him the qi in exchange for being taken out of the Netherworld Domain and for Han Li spending three months searching for her elder brother.
- Han Li accepts the three-month deadline condition, and Mei Ning asks when he needs the qi, showing a surprising level of trust.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Jade Phoenix Marrow Body (通玉风髓之体):** This is one of those specialized female physiques that pop up in cultivation novels like a rare collectible card. The woman who has it doesn't get any special combat boost, but her body naturally generates a single thread of Spirit Essence Qi (通灵之气) upon reaching Foundation Establishment. This qi is one of the "Seven Great Pure Essences" of the cultivation world, and it's a coveted resource for male cultivators—it can cleanse their meridians and boost their cultivation level almost like an ancient miracle pill. The twist: it must be given willingly. No forcing, no plundering. And she only gets one shot in her entire life to transfer it. Lose your virginity before that? The qi vanishes. Talk about a high-stakes purity mechanic.

**The Vermilion Mark / Red Pill (守宫砂):** The bright red spot on Mei Ning's arm is a cultural artifact from ancient China. It was traditionally made from a substance fed to geckos (守宫), and when applied to a woman's skin, it was said to remain bright red if she was a virgin and fade if she wasn't. In xianxia, it's often used as a quick visual check for purity, especially in the context of special spiritual constitutions like Mei Ning's that are tied to virginity. It's not just a cosmetic relic—in this world, it's functionally part of the cultivation economy.

## Reading Guide

Alright, fellow Daoists, if you were hoping for an explosive fight in the Netherworld Domain, you'll have to wait. This chapter is all about the *art of the deal*. Han Li is backed into a corner: mana zero, options limited, and a ticking clock on his escape. Mei Ning holds a rare key, but she's no pushover—she knows she has leverage and uses it to extract a condition he can't fully guarantee (finding her brother). The beauty of this scene is Han Li's response: he doesn't promise the moon; he offers a hard deadline of three months. It's a classic Mortal Stream negotiation: give enough to get what you want, but always leave yourself an exit clause. Watch how Han Li transitions from a tired recluse to a sharp, calculating negotiator the moment a viable path opens. There's history here, trust being built on a razor's edge, and a very unique transaction about to go down.
