## Chapter Overview
Han Li finally meets the woman behind the arrays: Xin Ruyin. And let’s be real—this is not your typical "damsel in distress" meeting. Sure, he just saved her life, but the narrative immediately pivots into a high-stakes psychological chess match. Han Li, the master of reading rooms and calculating outcomes, instantly recognizes a different kind of opponent. This chapter is a masterclass in how the Mortal Stream handles social conflict: not with a blade, but with layered, graceful verbal jousting where every sentence is a probe and every polite smile conceals a thousand calculations.

## Key Plot Points
*   Han Li observes Qi Yunxiao’s lovesick behavior, confirming that the man is deeply, unrequitedly in love with Xin Ruyin. This emotional lever will be key in future negotiations.
*   The group returns to Xin Ruyin’s secluded mountain home, which is likely protected by powerful, invisible formations. This reinforces her value as an Array Master.
*   Upon meeting Xin Ruyin, Han Li immediately assesses her as being exceptionally sharp and perceptive. He knows dealing with her will be far more difficult than dealing with Qi Yunxiao.
*   During their conversation over tea, Han Li expertly evades all of Xin Ruyin’s attempts to probe his background and identity, maintaining his strategic opacity.
*   Qi Yunxiao, eager to fulfill his promise, offers Han Li his array flags and plates. Han Li seizes the moment to pivot the conversation to his real objective.
*   Instead of taking the offered arrays, Han Li shocks them both by offering a cure for Xin Ruyin’s illness—an eight-hundred-year spiritual herb—in exchange for solving "a certain problem" he has (fixing the teleportation array).

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a pure gem of *social tribulation*, a core concept in Mortal Stream fiction. The true battle here is not magical but conversational. Han Li’s initial reaction—immediately clocking Xin Ruyin as "trouble"—is a classic Han Li move. He values predictable allies (like Qi Yunxiao) over brilliant ones (like Xin Ruyin). The "probe and parry" dialogue where Xin Ruyin asks, "What sect are you from?" and Han Li responds with a flawless deflection is a textbook example of *worldly wisdom* (人情世故, renqing shigu). It’s the art of social combat, where saying nothing at all is the most powerful shield. Han Li’s decision to offer a high-value, specific cure rather than accept the compensation of arrays is a masterclass in *strategic pivoting*: he skips the secondary reward to aim directly for his primary goal—access to an Array Master’s talent.

## Reading Guide
Watch how Han Li shifts his social mask in this chapter. With Qi Yunxiao, he is a patient, slightly amused elder. With Xin Ruyin, his tone becomes polite, formal, and impenetrable. This constant internal calibration is the lifeblood of the Mortal Stream reading experience. The chapter highlights a crucial rule: a rescued person is not necessarily a grateful ally, and a cultivator’s most dangerous weapon is not their magical treasure, but their accumulated *information dis*advantage. Han Li’s frustration with Xin Ruyin’s intelligence is a good sign for the reader—it means the story is about to move to a new level of complexity. The mention of a hidden illness is a plot hook we must file away, as it is the leverage Han Li will use to unlock the next, critical stage of his journey.
