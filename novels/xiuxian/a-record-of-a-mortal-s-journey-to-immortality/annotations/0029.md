## Chapter Overview

The carefully maintained pretense between Han Li and Doctor Mo shatters in this chapter, and let me tell you—*it hits hard*. After the pulse-check cold open, the mask drops, and we get the first real look at Doctor Mo not as a frail old physician, but as the man who once earned the title 'Ghost Hand.' Han Li’s survival instincts kick into overdrive, but raw calculation meets raw power, and for the first time, he finds himself completely outmatched. This is the chapter where the hunter shows his fangs, and the prey realizes just how deep the trap goes.

## Key Plot Points

Doctor Mo examines Han Li’s pulse in silence, and his dissatisfaction is palpable. Han Li follows him inside, trying to stay invisible, but the tension is suffocating. The mysterious giant stands behind Doctor Mo like a silent statue, never moving. Han Li tries to sneak a glance and gets caught immediately—a small defeat that signals much bigger ones to come.

Then comes the visual gut-punch: Doctor Mo’s face is covered in living black qi, writhing like countless tentacles. His expression shifts from the usual wooden mask to something openly sinister and mocking. The pretense is over. Han Li instinctively reaches for the iron cylinder hidden in his sleeve—a minor comfort—but Doctor Mo moves like a ghost, seals his acupoint in a flash, and strips him of the weapon before he can even process what happened.

What follows is a masterclass in psychological pressure. Han Li tries flattery, submission, and calm negotiation, but Doctor Mo is impervious. When Han Li finally goes silent and meets his gaze with cold, unreadable eyes, Doctor Mo responds with three ‘Good!’s—praise that feels more like a death sentence. He admits what both of them have known all along: Han Li was never a disciple, and Doctor Mo was never a master. The chapter ends with the revelation that this entire relationship has been a transaction, and the bill is now due.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the **acupoint-sealing (点穴)** technique as a combat method, which is a staple of wuxia and xianxia fiction. By striking specific points on the body, a practitioner can paralyze an opponent instantly without causing lasting harm. It emphasizes the difference in skill level between Han Li and Doctor Mo: Han Li didn’t even see the move coming.

The **black qi (黑气)** on Doctor Mo’s face is a classic xianxia/wuxia visual cue. In Chinese folk and cultivation lore, black energy often signals demonic arts, possession, or severe internal corruption. It’s the external sign of an internal rot—both physiological and moral. In Doctor Mo’s case, it likely points to the forbidden techniques and parasitic soul-sharing we first glimpsed in earlier chapters.

Doctor Mo’s dialogue—his cold admission that he “never truly saw you as a disciple”—cuts to the core of the cultivation world’s social structure. In orthodox Daoist tradition, the master-disciple bond carries deep obligations: the master imparts knowledge, and the disciple carries the lineage. In xianxia fiction, that ideal is often weaponized. A master may invest resources in a disciple not out of care, but because the disciple is a tool to be harvested later. The term ‘炉鼎’ (human furnace) comes to mind here: a practitioner treated as a consumable resource rather than a person.

### Ghost Hand (鬼手)

The Jianghu alias of Doctor Mo Juren, earned during his aggressive prime in Lan Province.

## Reading Guide

If you came here expecting a warm mentor-student relationship, welcome to the real dark forest of *A Record of a Mortal’s Journey to Immortality*. Doctor Mo’s shift from frail physician to sinister schemer is the novel’s first major gut-punch, and it sets the tone for everything that follows: *trust is a liability*. Pay close attention to Han Li’s response here. He doesn’t panic, doesn’t beg—he calculates. The moment his flattery fails, he switches to a cold, emotionless gaze, trying to force a psychological opening. That’s the core of his survival strategy, and it’s on full display for the first time.

Also, keep an eye on the mysterious iron giant standing behind Doctor Mo. He’s barely acknowledged here, but the stage is being set for something much bigger. And that iron cylinder Han Li reached for? In a world where everyone is hiding their ace in the hole, even the smallest detail matters.
