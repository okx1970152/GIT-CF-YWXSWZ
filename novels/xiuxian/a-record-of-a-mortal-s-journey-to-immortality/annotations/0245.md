## Chapter Overview

In Chapter 245, the "bridal candidate" problem takes an unexpectedly scandalous turn. After Han Li made his refusal vehemently clear in the previous chapter, his master Li Huayuan is now the one who wants to back out—but he can't say why. The reason? Dong Xuan'er, the beautiful disciple of Senior Sister Hongfu, has a reputation that makes a Jianghu tavern wench look like a paragon of virtue. Through his wife, Li Huayuan learns of the girl's long string of rumored lovers, her multiple confinements, and the shadow of the mysterious "Senior Brother Lu." Yet Hongfu, instead of forcing the issue, proposes a surprising compromise: send the two "rejects" on a joint mission to the Yan Clan's Treasure-Grabbing Assembly. This sets the stage for a journey that promises more political maneuvering than treasure hunting.

## Key Plot Points

- The young matron reveals the scandalous rumors surrounding Senior Sister Hongfu's disciple, Dong Xuan'er, including her entanglement with multiple male disciples and even a past connection with the infamous "Senior Brother Lu."
- Han Li, privately confirming the Lu connection based on his own knowledge, is keenly aware that at least *some* of the rumors are true, but wisely keeps his mouth shut.
- Li Huayuan, upon hearing the rumors, shifts from pushing for the marriage to desperately wanting to back out, worried about his own reputation by association.
- After a tense three-day wait, Han Li learns the compromise: Hongfu agreed to cancel the marriage on the condition that Han Li accompany Dong Xuan'er to the Yan Clan's Treasure-Grabbing Assembly.
- The assembly is revealed as a political networking event hosted by the Yan Clan, the top cultivation family of Yue, offering the legendary Talisman Treasure "Heaven-Encompassing Pagoda" as the grand prize.
- Li Huayuan's wife learns a piece of shocking information from Hongfu during a private conversation, the details of which are left deliberately vague, setting up future plot points.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The Art of "Saving Face" in Cultivation Politics

This chapter is a masterclass in *mianzi* (face/mianzi) dynamics within the cultivation world. Li Huayuan cannot simply say, "Your disciple is a scandalous mess; I don't want her marrying mine." Such a direct insult would create a blood feud. Instead, he must orchestrate a retreat that preserves Hongfu's "face" (and her dignity as a Core Formation elder). The entire negotiation—the rumors, the hesitation, the "compromise" mission—is a carefully choreographed dance to avoid shaming a powerful peer. In xianxia, *mianzi* is not vanity; it is a currency of social stability that can prevent or trigger all-out war among cultivators.

### The Yan Clan and the Treasure-Grabbing Economy

The Yan Clan's Treasure-Grabbing Assembly is a classic example of a cultivation world power play. By offering a coveted Talisman Treasure (a replica of a true magical treasure, carrying a fraction of its original power) as a prize for the juniors of other Core Formation experts, they are purchasing goodwill and creating a network of obligations. This is not about charity; it is a strategic investment in alliances. In the Mortal Stream, such generosity always has a hidden cost, and the "friendship" of a great clan is a debt you may be called upon to repay.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for a road trip, fellow Daoists, but not the fun kind. This isn't a training arc; it's a political exile for two people who really don't want to be stuck with each other. On one side, you have Han Li—our favorite paranoid pragmatist who's probably already calculating how far away he can stand from his travel companion. On the other, you have Dong Xuan'er, a girl whose reputation is so bad that her own master treats her like a ticking scandal bomb. This journey to the Yan Clan isn't about winning a treasure; it's about Hongfu forcing a lesson in humility on her disciple and buying face for everyone involved. But with "Senior Brother Lu's" shadow still looming and the Yan Clan's "generosity" hiding unknown strings, this seemingly boring errand has all the ingredients for a spectacular disaster. Just what Han Li needs.
