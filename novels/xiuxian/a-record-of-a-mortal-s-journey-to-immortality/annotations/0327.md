## Chapter Overview
Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because Chapter 327 is a masterclass in tactical efficiency and the cold, hard calculus of power. We open with a tense preparation for a True Treasure activation, dive straight into Han Li’s clinical takedown of the Ice Fiend using his signature layers of deception, and then pivot to a jaw-dropping display of what happens when a Foundation Establishment cultivator brings out the big guns. The chapter is a perfect Mortal Stream sandwich: a gritty, resource-managed struggle on the ground, followed by a terrifyingly casual demonstration of overwhelming force from the air. Han Li’s mental math is running at full power, and the payoff is a visceral reminder of why preparation and patience always beat raw desperation in this world.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The True Treasure Prep:** Liu Jing needs dedicated protection to safely activate his firebird True Treasure, revealing how risky and resource-intensive these high-tier assets are.
*   **Han Li’s String Trap:** Han Li uses his transparent thread while pressing the Ice Fiend with his sword, ultimately reeling it in for a kill. The narration emphasizes that the Ice Fiend’s crystalline body, while tough, has a hidden cost: a loss of tactile sensitivity, making it vulnerable to his low-key assassination tactic.
*   **The Ice Fiend’s End:** Han Li’s rapid-cutting barrage shatters the Ice Fiend, leaving only its indestructible claw as a trophy—which he disgustedly discards due to its human origin.
*   **The Black Fiend Lord Watches:** A middle-aged man in a secret chamber notes the Ice Fiend’s death with cold pragmatism, dismissing it as a lost asset rather than a comrade. This reinforces the utter callousness of the cultivation world.
*   **The True Treasure Unleashed:** Liu Jing blows his activated firebird toward the transforming Blood Servants. The little bird effortlessly transforms into a massive flame giant and teleports to Qingwen’s fiendish form, landing a single peck as the chapter ends.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter gives us a crisp look at the *True Treasure (真宝)*, a refined upgrade from the earlier *Talisman Treasure (符宝)*. While a Talisman Treasure is a portion of a real treasure’s power sealed into a paper talisman, a True Treasure is the real deal—or close to it. Activating one is harder, but the result is incomparably more devastating. In the Mortal Stream universe, this is the ultimate sign of a Foundation Establishment cultivator’s willingness to burn capital; bringing out a True Treasure means the situation is serious enough to risk your most precious, life-saving card. It’s a calculative decision, not a hero’s gesture.

Han Li’s fight also showcases a classic xianxia combat philosophy: the unorthodox technique. His transparent thread is a mundane item turned lethal through tactical ingenuity and resourcefulness. The Ice Fiend’s downfall isn’t a lack of power but a hidden flaw in its transformation—a loss of sensation. This reinforces the core tenet that in a world of overwhelming force, intelligence and exploiting weakness can level the playing field.

## Reading Guide
Alright, web novel veterans, this is the kind of payoff we live for. Han Li’s fight is a textbook example of why he’s a fan-favorite protagonist. He doesn’t just outpower the enemy; he *outprepares* them. The transparent thread? That was set up chapters ago. He’s the kind of guy who keeps a USB bomb in his drawer, and we love him for it.

Also, please take a moment to appreciate the casual horror of the Ice Fiend’s fate: a once-human cultivator’s limb survives as a perfect, indestructible weapon, and Han Li just tosses it aside because it gives him the creeps. It’s a small reminder of the gory, pragmatic reality of this path. Get ready for Liu Jing’s True Treasure to absolutely wreck house in the next chapter—that’s how you show a Foundation Establishment cultivator’s true, terrifying ceiling.
