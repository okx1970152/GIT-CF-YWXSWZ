## Chapter Overview

Betrayal, desperation, and a cold dash for survival define this chapter. Han Li, having just formed his Core in the previous volume, finds himself once again on the run—not because he is weak, but because the terrain has shifted under his feet. A carefully orchestrated double-cross unfolds as the Crimsonflame Elder reveals his true allegiance, turning the tide against the rebels of the Sublime Sound Sect. For Han Li, the battle is less a heroic clash and more a tactical reset: he withdraws without a second thought, cataloging clues about Demonic Cores, ancient teleportation arrays, and a ghost-head token that seems to link everything together. The chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s core ethic—when the mask drops and the killing begins, the only smart move is to run.

## Key Plot Points

- The middle-aged man commands a ghost-head treasure and attempts to coordinate with Crimsonflame Elder, using a Cold Dawn Pearl to pin down Extreme Yin Ancestor.
- Crimsonflame Elder suddenly turns on the middle-aged man, trapping him in a massive dark cloud; Extreme Yin Ancestor seizes the Cold Dawn Pearl and neutralizes it with ease.
- The allied cultivators scatter in panic; Han Li and Qu Hun have already fled before the others even react.
- Four Hidden Kill Sect Core Formation cultivators (revealed as Demonic Core projections) launch a suicidal assault on the two old fiends, buying the rest a chance to escape.
- Han Li overhears the terms “Demonic Core” and “projection,” confirming his suspicion that Wuchou’s Profound Yin Art is linked to the Yue Emperor’s *Profound Yin Scripture* and the ancient teleportation array.
- Han Li recalls the five-colored skeleton near the teleportation array and the ghost-head token, drawing a connection between multiple past events.
- The chapter ends with Han Li fleeing, hoping Extreme Yin Ancestor’s overconfidence has left no backup trap.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Demonic Cores and Projections (煞丹与分身)**  
In standard cultivation, a Golden Core is the pinnacle of a Qi Condensation cultivator’s achievement. But here, “Demonic Core” refers to a twisted, dark-aligned version—usually associated with forbidden arts that sacrifice lifespan or humanity for raw power. A “projection” (分身) means a cultivator splits their consciousness into multiple bodies, each carrying a portion of their Demonic Core. The four Hidden Kill Sect cultivators who fought to the death were such projections—expendable, yet capable of stalling even Extreme Yin Ancestor for a few precious breaths.

**Profound Yin Art vs. Profound Yin Scripture**  
The *Profound Yin Scripture* is a legacy jade slip Han Li found earlier, which described a sinister path to Core Formation using yin energy. Wuchou’s specialized “Profound Yin Art” is almost certainly derived from it. The narrative hints that the ancient teleportation array found in Tiannan may have been used by the same reclusive sect to send their arts across the sea, linking two seemingly separate worlds under one dark lineage.

**The Ghost-Head Token**  
In earlier chapters, Han Li acquired a token carved with a vicious ghost head—he never understood its full purpose. Now, witnessing the middle-aged man’s ghost-head treasure and the sudden betrayal, he suspects the token is connected to a larger power play. In the Mortal Stream, such tokens often act as key identifiers for hidden organizations or as triggers for binding oaths.

## The Cruel Logic of Betrayal

Why did Crimsonflame Elder turn? The classic Mortal Stream answer: because he was never really on the middle-aged man’s side. Power in this world flows upward. Extreme Yin Ancestor is a Nascent Soul cultivator; Crimsonflame Elder had more to gain by aligning with the stronger party than by fighting a losing battle. The “master-apprentice bond” the middle-aged man invoked is nothing but sentiment—sentiment that gets you swallowed by a cloud and left for dead. This chapter is a harsh reminder that in the cultivation world, loyalty is a renewable resource, and it’s almost always auctioned to the highest bidder.
