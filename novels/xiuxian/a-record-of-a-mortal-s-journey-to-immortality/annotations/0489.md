## Chapter Overview

The power play tightens. Han Li’s Blood Jade Spider is forced into a berserk state by Man Brutal Beard to pull the legendary Void Heaven Cauldron, but just as victory seems within reach, the righteous-path cultivators return—led by Wan Tianming, who reveals he planted a fossil beast to spy on them all along. The chapter ends with a brutal ultimatum: share the treasure or watch the spider die. Han Li, once again, is a bargaining chip between gods.

## Key Plot Points

- Man Brutal Beard uses a “Berserk Technique” on the Blood Jade Spider without Han Li’s consent, pushing the creature into a rage state with potential aftereffects. Han Li’s resentment is silent but sharp—he files this insult as data.
- The Void Heaven Cauldron is gradually dragged upward, releasing an intensified cold surge; Green Bamboo Hermit shields Han Li and reveals that many past treasure-seekers have failed against this same trap.
- Han Li notices his spider’s silk beginning to glow with a faint blue light—a subtle clue that the cauldron’s power is already interacting with the silk.
- A barrier is torn from behind: Wan Tianming and his righteous-path party have returned, having slaughtered the demonic side’s sentries (Green Thorn Birds and corpse puppets).
- Wan Tianming reveals a Fossil Beast that had been hiding in the stone to spy, turning the tables on Man Brutal Beard and Extreme Yin.
- The ultimatum: either kill the spider and let the cauldron sink again, or split the treasure equally. Han Li is not consulted—his asset is now the centerpiece of a high-stakes hostage negotiation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Berserk Technique (狂暴之术):** A common trope in xianxia where a temporary power boost comes with a hidden price—loss of control, damage to the target, or a rebound effect. In the Mortal Stream, such “gifts” are always a leash. Here, it is applied to Han Li’s spirit beast without consent, reinforcing his status as a disposable tool.

**Fossil Beast (化石兽):** A creature with the rare ability to merge seamlessly into stone, rendering it effectively invisible to spiritual sense once burrowed. Its sole niche is surveillance—a perfect low-cost, high-value intelligence asset in a world where every secret is a weapon.

**Void Heaven Cauldron (虚天鼎):** The supreme treasure of the Void Heaven Palace, sealed in an innermost chamber. Lifting it triggers a massive yin-cold surge that has killed unprepared cultivators in the past. The cauldron’s weight is not merely physical; it is bound to the space itself, making extraction a test of brute force and endurance.

**Hostage Negotiation in the Mortal Stream:** Wan Tianming’s offer is not a negotiation; it is an ultimatum dressed in polite words. The demonic side has no good option—either lose the cauldron or share the prize. This mirrors the classic Mortal Stream principle: when you are the weaker party, every “choice” is a cage; you pick the least-bad one.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for a classic stalemate in the Dark Forest. Han Li is once again the invisible pawn everyone talks around but never to. His spider is the key asset, yet his opinion is irrelevant. Watch how he stores every slight—the silent resentment behind his calm mask is the only weapon he has right now. The faint blue glow on the spider silk may be a crucial detail; in the Mortal Stream, nothing is coincidence. Also, keep an eye on Xuan Gu’s hesitation—his calculated sideline posture suggests he is waiting for a specific moment to strike.
