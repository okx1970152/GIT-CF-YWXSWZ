## Chapter Overview

Finally, some peace and quiet! After the tense auction and the chaotic vibes of Qiyuan Island, our favorite paranoid cultivator Han Li gets to do what he does best—run away from crowds and find a remote, utterly hidden spot to set up shop. This chapter is a beautiful, methodical deep breath: a logistical pivot where Han Li shops for maps, scouts the ocean for a private island, and leverages a swarm of grade-one demon fish to create the perfect secret cave. It’s the Mortal Stream equivalent of “let me just build my base before the next disaster”—and the paranoia-rich details (covering the island with extra fog arrays, testing the bottom of the sea) remind you that this man *learns* from every close call. Plus, a tantalizing cliffhanger with a mysterious jade slip and a box of five-colored beads—what’s he planning?

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li purchases a detailed sea chart and demon beast report from Blackstone City, noticing far too many dangerous zones marked around Qiyuan Island.
- He learns the famous deep-sea abyss is half a month’s flight south, but deliberately flies north to avoid crowded cultivator zones near the abyss.
- After over two weeks of travel, Han Li discovers a small, uninhabited island with a decent mid-to-high-grade spirit vein—but no cultivators have settled there because it’s perpetually shrouded in an unusual white sea fog.
- Curious, Han Li dives beneath the island and discovers the fog is produced by a massive swarm of low-grade demon beasts called “Swallowing Sea Fish,” which spit seawater mist as a play behavior.
- Recognizing the natural concealment value, Han Li decides to establish his new cave dwelling here, carving it out of a small mountain range in half a day.
- To enhance security, he first sets up an illusion formation, then spends a week refining over a dozen additional fog-generating arrays that merge with the natural sea mist—making the island nearly invisible from the air.
- Two days later, Han Li studies the pale yellow jade slip from the Inch of Gold Pavilion’s old merchant and finds something that leaves him pacing, thoughtful, and reaching for a mysterious white jade box containing five-colored beads.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Swallowing Sea Fish & Natural Camouflage**: These little guys are a perfect example of how xianxia uses minor demon beasts to create geography-logic rather than just monster-fodder. They’re only grade-one low-grade—barely a threat—but in *massive swarms*, their idle mist-spitting behavior can obscure an entire island. Han Li exploits this not by fighting but by *working with the system*: he adds artificial fog arrays that blend with the natural cover. This is classic Mortal Stream engineering: don’t destroy the environment; *use* it.

**The “Flawless Illusion” Trope**: Han Li first sets up a visual illusion to make the mountain range look like a forest, but then he catches himself: “What if someone flies into the fog by accident and sees through it?” So he layers *more* concealment—the fog arrays—that compound the effect. In Chinese web novel logic, illusion formations alone are never enough for a truly paranoid cultivator; you *always* stack layers of sensory deception, because the cultivation world is full of rogue experts with sharp spiritual sense. This layered-defense mindset is a hallmark of the “survival first” school of cultivation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a textbook example of *RMJI’s* quieter strengths—the strategic pivot rather than the sword fight. If you’ve been craving a breather after the tension of the Blood Forbidden Trial arc, here it is: Han Li shops for maps like a tourist who’s *very* worried about pickup artists, flees to the literal middle of nowhere, and then spends a week rigging his island to be invisible. The Mortal Stream rule at work here is simple: **avoiding attention is a combat skill**. Every pond he carves, every array he buries, every Swallowing Sea Fish he counts—it’s all paranoia payback for every time he’s been caught with his pants down before. Also, pay close attention to that ending. The five-colored beads and the pale yellow jade slip are clearly setting up something big. Storage pouch hinting, fellow Daoists—always a good sign that the peaceful days are numbered.

## Chapter Lore

- **Swallowing Sea Fish (吞海鱼)** – A grade-one low-grade social demon beast with silver bone spines instead of scales; known for spitting seawater mist as a playful behavior, large swarms can obscure entire islands.
- **Silver-Winged Bird (银翼鸟)** – A grade-four demon beast that Han Li encountered and chased into the sea fog on a whim, leading to his discovery of the hidden island.
- **Fog Arrays (幻雾阵)** – A low-grade formation that generates thick artificial mist; Han Li refined over a dozen sets and buried them across the island to merge with the natural sea fog and create an additional concealment layer.
- **Five-Colored Beads (五色小圆珠)** – A mysterious object in a white jade box retrieved by Han Li after studying the Inch of Gold Pavilion jade slip—its purpose is not yet revealed.
