## Chapter Overview

For once, the world isn't trying to kill Han Li. After the claustrophobic gore of the Blood Forbidden Trial and the cold dismissal from Nan Gong Wan, this chapter offers a rare moment of civilised stillness. Han Li visits Qi Yunxiao to collect the improved Reversed Five Elements Formation array tools, and the scene plays out like a breath of fresh air. There's tea, mutual respect, and a quiet exchange of favours. But beneath the calm surface, the chapter reinforces a core Mortal Stream lesson: even when nobody is fighting, the protagonist is still building capital—social, strategic, and artefactual. Qi Yunxiao’s growth as a craftsman is on full display, and Han Li’s careful maintenance of a beneficial connection shows that in this world, a well-tended relationship can be as valuable as a high-grade talisman.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li arrives at Golden Horse City's famous teahouse, “Clear Spring Teahouse,” which enforces a bizarre “one pot per person per day” rule. The crowd is quiet, orderly, and mostly cultivators.
- He presents a jade pendant token to the teahouse owner, who immediately recognises him as Senior Han and leads him outside the city.
- The owner guides Han Li through a hidden, formation-protected compound in a valley west of the city, following a seemingly random path to avoid triggering defensive arrays.
- Qi Yunxiao emerges from his workshop, visibly frustrated by another failed attempt at an unknown project. His surprise and delight at Han Li’s arrival immediately lift his spirits.
- The transaction: Han Li returns the sealed *Cloud Sky Notes*, and Qi Yunxiao delivers the improved Reversed Five Elements Formation tool set, which now operates at one-third of the original formation’s power (up from one-tenth).
- Han Li is genuinely impressed—an array that can trouble Core Formation cultivators is now significantly stronger, a major strategic asset.
- They share tea made by Qi Yunxiao’s friend, a special blend infused with spiritual energy through a decade-long research process. Han Li praises it sincerely; Qi Yunxiao is embarrassed by the praise.
- The chapter closes with a quiet, domestic afternote—no battle, no betrayal, just a craftsman’s pride and a cultivator’s strategic satisfaction.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Reversed Five Elements Formation (颠倒五行阵)**  
This is a *Minor Forbidden Array*, a legendary formation type in xianxia lore. Unlike standard battle formations that amplify attack or defence, a Forbidden Array specializes in illusion and concealment. “Reversed Five Elements” means it disrupts the natural order of wood, fire, earth, metal, and water, creating a space where standard perception and qi tracking become useless. Qi Yunxiao’s improvement raises it from 10% to 33% of the original’s power—a massive leap in a world where even a tenth could stop a Core Formation cultivator.

**The “One Pot Per Person Per Day” Rule**  
This is not just a quirky business tactic. For cultivators, tea infused with spiritual energy is a minor cultivation resource. If someone with a high cultivation base drained the entire stock in one binge, ordinary low-level patrons would get nothing. This limit ensures equal access—or at least a token attempt at fairness—in a world where the strong usually take everything.

**The Sealed Book Transaction**  
Qi Yunxiao’s *Cloud Sky Notes* was protected by a restrictive seal. Returning it sealed shows Han Li’s honesty and earns real goodwill. In the Mortal Stream, such gestures are rare and valuable—they buy trust that no amount of spirit stones can purchase.

### Restrictive Seal

A spell or technique that binds an object (like a book) to prevent unauthorized access; the seal must be confirmed intact before a transaction is considered honest.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in the “quiet victory” phase of a Mortal Stream arc. Nobody draws a blade. No one screams betrayal. But Han Li just walked away with a significantly upgraded defensive array that could one day save his life against a Core Formation cultivator. Qi Yunxiao, meanwhile, recovered his family heirloom without a fight. Both sides won. That’s the quiet, efficient, mutually beneficial transaction that the cultivation world occasionally allows—when the parties are smart enough to keep it clean. Also, pay attention to the tea: the fact that a Foundation Establishment cultivator like Han Li praises it so highly tells you that Qi Yunxiao’s friend is no ordinary alchemist. That name might resurface later.
