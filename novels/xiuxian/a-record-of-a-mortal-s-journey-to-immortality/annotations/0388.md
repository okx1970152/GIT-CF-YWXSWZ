## Chapter Overview

Whose side are you on? The answer, in the Mortal Stream, is always: your own. This chapter rains chaos down on the Six Serenity Hall expedition, and Han Li’s survival instincts—honed over decades—kick in faster than anyone else’s. When the demonic heir Wu Chou appears to claim the Koi Infant Beast, the elders’ secret negotiation spirals into a mutual betrayal pact, and the moment someone drops a token that reeks of a deeper conspiracy, Han Li doesn’t wait for the knife to fall. He runs. And so begins one of the most tense, multi-directional chases in the Chaotic Star Sea arc—where knowing when to flee is the truest mark of a survivor.

## Key Plot Points

- Wu Chou of Extreme Yin Island appears with his two female attendants, claiming the Koi Infant Beast’s demon core for his grandfather, the legendary Extreme Yin Ancestor, who is about to emerge from seclusion.
- Elders Miao and Gu, already weakened from using the ancient Heaven-Parching Halberd, are caught between losing face and provoking a demonic overlord.
- A secret exchange between Elder Gu and Wu Chou takes place; Elder Gu throws Wu Chou a ghost-head token that clearly proves something shocking.
- Diviner Qing immediately recognizes the danger and flees without warning; Han Li, reading the same cues, launches the Divine Wind Boat and escapes toward the unnamed island a second later.
- Elder Gu and Elder Miao split up to chase down Han Li and Diviner Qing, while Wu Chou, begrudgingly, is left to silence the remaining witnesses—Feng Sanniang, the scholar, and the rest.
- Han Li’s choice to run is not panic—it is a cold, data-driven assessment that the situation has become a killing field, and he refuses to be caught in the crossfire.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Extreme Yin Ancestor (极阴祖师) and the Demonic Yin Art (玄阴功)**  
In the Chaotic Star Sea, the Extreme Yin Island is a name that strikes terror even into Core Formation cultivators. The “Extreme Yin” heritage is built on the Demonic Yin Art—a supreme-grade demonic cultivation path known for its aggressive, corrosive qi and fearsome speed. The fact that Wu Chou, an early Core Formation cultivator, can openly defy two elders at once is a testament to the power gap between ordinary techniques and top-tier demonic lineages. In xianxia, lineage is more than blood—it is a technique bloodline that multiplies combat power.

**The “Silencing Pact” Trope**  
When a high-level cultivator’s identity or secret is accidentally revealed to low-level witnesses, the standard protocol is total elimination. The elders’ decision to trade the witnesses’ lives for silence is a classic Dark Forest move: information asymmetry cannot be fixed by negotiation—only by removing the audience. Han Li’s instantaneous recognition of this logic is what separates him from ordinary cultivators.

**Koi Infant Beast (婴鲤兽)**  
A grade-6 aquatic demon beast whose carcass contains valuable materials beyond its demon core. Wu Chou’s greed for the crab pincers and blue coral shows that in the Mortal Stream, even a dead beast is a battlefield of competing claims.

### Ghost-Head Token (鬼头令牌)

A black identification token that reveals the bearer’s true allegiance; its appearance triggers the silencing order.

## Reading Guide

This is a masterclass in reading the room—or rather, reading the other cultivators’ bodies. Diviner Qing’s sudden flight is the critical signal; Han Li doesn’t hesitate because he trusts his own judgment. He trusts it enough to burn the Divine Wind Boat’s energy without a second thought.  

Notice the small details: Han Li’s recognition that the black qi feels similar to the Blood Refining Divine Light—a callback that ties the Demonic Yin Art to the grayscale jade slip he acquired earlier. Nothing in his past storage pouches is forgotten.  

The chapter ends on a deliciously cruel cliffhanger: Elder Gu himself is chasing Han Li. An early Core Formation hunter versus a mid-Qi Condensation prey with a soulless puppet and a speeding boat. The odds are clear, but Han Li’s record of turning impossible situations into narrow escapes keeps the reader gripping the page.  

Get ready for the chase.
