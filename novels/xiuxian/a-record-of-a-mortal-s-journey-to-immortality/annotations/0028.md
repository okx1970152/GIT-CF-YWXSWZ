## Chapter Overview
Get ready, cultivators—this is where Han Li’s internal cheat code finally activates in a serious way. After all that careful pill-preparation and cautious planning, he breaks through to the fourth layer of the nameless formula, and the result is nothing short of a sensory rebirth. For the first time, we see the formula deliver a *qualitative* upgrade, not just incremental gains. But here’s the twist: even as Han Li revels in his newfound power, his nominal master Doctor Mo returns—looking like death warmed over—with a mysterious giant in tow. This chapter is a masterclass in the *Mortal Stream* tension between genuine breakthrough and rising paranoia. The high of achieving minor success is immediately undercut by the ominous return of the man who wants to use him.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li decides to prepare healing and antidote medicines as contingency, prioritizing survival over pure cultivation speed. This shows he’s already thinking like a veteran mortal-stream cultivator: always expect betrayal or disaster.
- He uses the Yellow Dragon Pill and Golden Marrow Pill, and the combined medicinal power instantly breaks the bottleneck, pushing him into the fourth layer of the nameless oral formula.
- The fourth-layer breakthrough triggers a massive, qualitative upgrade: his five senses (sight, hearing, smell) become superhuman. He can hear an earthworm burrowing from dozens of meters away and see tiny spider silk with perfect clarity.
- He also gains enhanced physical lightness and mental stamina, capable of going days without sleep.
- Han Li realizes this is only “minor success,” but it gives him a taste of what higher layers might offer, deepening his greed for advancement.
- Doctor Mo returns to Spirit Hand Valley after nearly a year, looking dramatically worse—his face gray and lifeless, as if his time is nearly up.
- Behind Doctor Mo trails a mysterious figure: a towering giant fully concealed in a dark hood and green robe, so large Han Li feels genuinely intimidated.
- Han Li decides in advance to hide his real progress, controlling his true qi at third-layer intensity to deceive Doctor Mo’s pulse examination. The chapter ends with the test looming.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Layer-Based Cultivation Physics:** This chapter explicitly introduces that breakthrough thresholds in xianxia are tied to medicinal potency and qualitative leaps. The fourth layer isn’t just stronger—it fundamentally rewires perception. This reflects a common xianxia trope where crossing a cultivation boundary grants a “new sense” (spiritual sense or divine sense), often manifesting as hyper-acute awareness of one’s surroundings without relying on the five ordinary senses. Han Li’s range of “several dozen *zhang*” here is the rough seed of his later spiritual sense ability.

**Sensory Overload as Milestone Marker:** In many cultivation stories, a major breakthrough often causes a temporary sensory flood—a sign that the cultivator’s body is adjusting to a higher energy density. Han Li’s experience of hearing everything at once, smelling new scents, and seeing micro-details is a textbook version of this trope, but delivered with the novel’s trademark pragmatic restraint: no mystic visions, no enlightenment—just sharper tools for survival.

**Doctor Mo’s Decaying Appearance:** Doctor Mo’s gray, lifeless complexion signals that his cultivation base is collapsing or his lifespan is critically low. This is a classic *Mortal Stream* setup: the senior master is running out of time, making his desperation for a vessel (Han Li) more urgent. The mysterious giant hints that his plans are expanding beyond mere possession—he’s preparing resources for something larger.

## Reading Guide
This is one of those rare chapters where Han Li gets a genuine *win*, and you can almost feel the reader’s relief—but don’t relax. The *Mortal Stream* formula is always “two steps forward, one step into an ambush.” The breakthrough is real, but it’s immediately weaponized: Han Li has to lie, suppress his true power, and face a master who’s clearly on the verge of doing something drastic. Watch how Han Li’s decision to hide the truth mirrors his earlier rule about never revealing treasures. His paranoia is a survival instinct, and this chapter proves it’s justified. The giant in green is the first real “unknown threat” that isn’t just Doctor Mo—so keep your eyes peeled. Also, note the phrase “minor success.” In xianxia, once a cultivator tastes a higher layer, they become addicted to the climb. Han Li’s greed for the fifth and sixth layers is now a major motivator.
