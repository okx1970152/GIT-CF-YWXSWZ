## Chapter Overview

Our favorite paranoid bean-counter Han Li is back at the Star Dust Pavilion, and this chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream social dynamics. No explosions, no bloodshed—just a quiet, razor-sharp negotiation where every smile, every glance, and every tossed storage pouch is a tactical move. Lady Lan, the pavilion’s shrewd manager, tries to size up Han Li while he plays his cards so close to his chest they might as well be invisible. The result? A silent duel of wits that’s every bit as tense as a sword fight, culminating in a promotion to the mysterious sixth floor. If you’ve ever wondered how a cultivator buys his way up the social ladder without throwing a single punch, this is your blueprint.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li shares a table with a fellow mid-Foundation Establishment cultivator (surname Fan) in a scene of perfect, distrustful silence.
- Qu Hun’s identity as a mana-bearing refined corpse piques Fan’s curiosity, but neither man breaks the ice.
- Lady Lan returns with two puppet golems carrying trays of top-grade magic tools; the red silk covering them resists divine sense probing, surprising both Han Li and Fan.
- Fan buys the Cold Crystal Blade for 800 spirit stones, showing self-aware humor about his limited budget.
- Lady Lan’s “No need to check” line is a calculated performance; Han Li internally dissects her hidden use of divine sense to instantly count the payment, exposing the transparent social game.
- Han Li bluntly states his actual needs: Foundation Establishment pill formulas or a large set of magic tools, not random single items.
- Lady Lan reveals that such items require a trip to the sixth floor, but first she must verify that Han Li has over 1,000 spirit stones in his possession.
- Han Li tosses her his pouch without hesitation; she verifies it with a quick sweep and escorts him upstairs.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces a subtle but vital rule of the cultivation economy: **access is tiered by wealth, not just cultivation realm**. The sixth floor of the Star Dust Pavilion is not open to anyone who can climb stairs—it is gated by a liquid-asset test of 1,000 spirit stones. In the Mortal Stream, this is a recurring pattern: the world’s most valuable resources (high-grade pill formulas, complete artifact sets) are always hidden behind a paywall that filters out the desperate and the poor. Lady Lan’s little divine-sense trick also reinforces a classic xianxia truth: a merchant’s smile is often the sharpest blade in the room.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to Han Li’s internal monologue when Lady Lan performs her “trusting” gesture with Fan’s payment. This is not a petty judgment—it is a fundamental part of Han Li’s survival toolkit. He reads people by watching what they do when they think no one is watching. His cold, analytical silence is not rudeness; it is intelligence-gathering. The chapter’s real action is in the gap between what people say and what Han Li observes.
