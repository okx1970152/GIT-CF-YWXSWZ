## Chapter Overview
Your boy Han Li has officially entered the Ring Mountain, and what he finds is an absolute nightmare of demon-beast density. Within hours, he's already tangling with low-grade beasts and getting chased by a mid-grade Flying Plume Peacock. But here's the kicker—while every other disciple is hunting for ripe, ready-to-harvest spiritual herbs, Han Li is playing the long game. His target? Useless little seedlings that nobody else would bother with. With his secret vial's green liquid, he can accelerate their growth, turning what others consider worthless into a golden ticket. Meanwhile, outside the forbidden land, the Core Formation big-shots are sweating bullets over their bets, each one trying to out-scheme the others before the dust settles.

## Key Plot Points
- The seven-sect disciples enter the forbidden land in waves, each choosing their own direction with a clear sense of purpose; Zhong Wu the ugly man follows suit, deploying a dozen yellow dots before entering.
- Outside the barrier, the Core Formation cultivators are locked in their own silent war of nerves: the Clear Void Taoist is visibly sweating his Bloodline Wyrm bet, while Han Li's Martial Grandfather Li secretly reveals he's sent the sect's top three Qi Condensation fighters, proving he's a wily old fox.
- Han Li encounters a Boulder Beast, a low-grade demon beast, and bisects it with his thread-like magic tool, then quickly leaves to avoid attracting other predators.
- After hours of travel, Han Li realizes the Ring Mountain is swarming with demon beasts, and the deeper he goes, the worse it will get—making the search for hidden herbs a logistical nightmare.
- Han Li reveals his secret strategy: he's targeting young, unripe spiritual herb seedlings that other disciples consider worthless; using his mysterious vial's green liquid, he can accelerate their growth into usable pill ingredients.
- The chapter ends on a note of tension: Han Li has only three days left, and with scattered herb locations, ambush-ready demon beasts, and rival disciples, his success is far from certain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Han Li's strategy in this chapter is a textbook example of what longtime xianxia readers call "thinking sideways." Every other disciple in the Blood Forbidden Trial is chasing after ripe, mature herbs—because that's the obvious, direct path to a Foundation Establishment Pill. But Han Li, being the cautious pragmatist he is, realizes that the real gold rush isn't in competing with the strongest fighters; it's in exploiting what they leave behind. Seedlings are worthless to anyone without a way to accelerate their growth, so no one will fight over them. This is the core of the "Mortal Stream" philosophy: don't fight for scraps at the top of the pile; leverage your unique assets to create value from nothing.

The green liquid from the mysterious vial is essentially a "time compression" cheat code, but it comes with caveats. The seedlings can only survive a year or two outside their native environment, meaning Han Li has to work fast once he's back. And even with the vial, he still needs to physically harvest the plants and bring them out alive. This isn't a win-button; it's a logistical puzzle that rewards preparation over raw power.

The Core Formation cultivators' poker game outside the forbidden land is a wonderful side dish. The Clear Void Taoist is practically weeping over his Bloodline Wyrm inner core—a rare, expensive alchemical component that forms inside a snake-type demon beast. Losing it would be a catastrophic financial blow, even for a Core Formation elder. Martial Grandfather Li, meanwhile, is playing the fool, pretending to worry while secretly having deployed the sect's elite. This reinforces the mortal-stream principle that in the cultivation world, even your allies are always holding cards close to their chest, and mutual trust is a luxury no one can afford.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is pure setup, but it's the kind of setup that pays off beautifully. Pay attention to the way Han Li thinks: he doesn't panic about the demon-beast density; he immediately reframes it as a limitation that makes his seedling strategy even more valuable. This is the same cold calculation we've seen from him since day one—he's always looking for the angle that others are too busy or too arrogant to see.

Also, keep an eye on Zhong Wu's yellow dots. In the Mortal Stream, anything a seasoned cultivator "releases" before entering a dangerous area is either a tracking tool, a surveillance net, or an early-warning system. Our boy Han Li might think he's alone in the mountain, but someone is already following his trail, and that's rarely good news for the protagonist.

Finally, the seed-planting detective work: Han Li mentions a cave that produces "Purple Ape" fruits, which is one of the three main ingredients for Foundation Establishment Pills. This isn't just world-building; it's a direct hint that the vial's acceleration ability is going to be a major factor in how he overcomes the bottleneck that's been haunting him. Bookmark this strategy—it's going to define his mid-game entirely.
