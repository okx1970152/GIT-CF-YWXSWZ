## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists—this chapter is a masterclass in psychological tension and self-inflicted horror. Doctor Mo, having failed to subdue Han Li through poison and martial prowess, unveils his final, terrifying secret: a forbidden technique that requires him to impale himself with seven ritual blades. The chapter opens with Han Li facing what he believes is his own execution, only to watch the blade plunge into Doctor Mo’s own shoulder instead. What follows is a grotesque transformation scene that redefines the stakes of their conflict. Han Li, pinned down by the inhuman Iron Servant, can only watch as Doctor Mo undergoes a ritual that turns him into something far more sinister than a mere Jianghu master. This is the moment where the cultivation world’s darkest horrors are fully unveiled, and Han Li’s survival instinct is put to its most extreme test yet.

## Key Plot Points

- The chapter begins with a tense, silent standoff: Han Li is pinned down, a strange blade held over him. He braces for death, but the blade never strikes his flesh—instead, Doctor Mo drives it into his own shoulder, shocking Han Li completely.
- Doctor Mo praises Han Li’s nerve, revealing that he has seen countless braggarts crumble under pressure. Han Li, internally relieved yet embarrassed, does not correct his misimpression, but the moment marks a subtle shift in their dynamic.
- Doctor Mo proceeds to insert seven identical blades into key points across his own body: shoulders, thighs, abdomen, chest. The grotesque sight makes Han Li suspect a devastating technique is being prepared—and he is the likely target.
- Han Li attempts to exploit Doctor Mo’s trance to escape, but the Iron Servant’s hand instantly pins him down again. Han Li accepts, bitterly, that he is being held by a creature immune to all conventional attacks.
- Doctor Mo begins to tremble and convulse, his face contorting in pain. A ghostly black mist emerges from his face—more concentrated and sinister than ever before, with living tendrils that seem to fight against his control.
- The chapter ends with Doctor Mo roaring the name of his forbidden art: the “Seven Ghost Soul Devouring Art.” The technique is now in motion, and Han Li can only watch.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The *Seven Ghost Soul Devouring Art* (七鬼噬魂大法) is a prime example of the dark, self-sacrificial techniques that populate the Mortal Stream universe. Unlike Western magic where power often comes from external pacts or mana expenditure, many forbidden arts in xianxia demand physical self-harm as a price. Here, Doctor Mo must impale himself with seven ritual blades to activate the technique. The number seven (七) is significant in Chinese folklore and Daoist ritual, often associated with the seven apertures of the head, the seven stars of the Big Dipper, or the seven po souls. The “ghost” (鬼) element signals that this technique invokes malevolent spiritual entities—likely one for each blade. The fact that the mist’s tendrils appear to resist his chanting suggests that controlling these spirits is a battle of will, not a clean summoning. This reinforces a core xianxia principle: forbidden power always extracts a toll, and the user must dominate the very forces they unleash.

The “Iron Servant” (铁奴) remains a mystery, but his complete invulnerability—even to attacks aimed at the groin—hints at a body that has been radically altered through martial or alchemical means. In the Jianghu setting, such “iron body” techniques are extreme variants of *hard qigong*, where practitioners condition their bodies to withstand blows that would shatter normal bones. The Iron Servant may be one such product, but his lack of speech and robotic obedience suggest something more sinister: perhaps he is a person whose mind has been broken or replaced.

### Seven-blade self-impalement

A ritualized self-harm technique where blades are inserted into specific points on the body (shoulders, thighs, abdomen, chest) to serve as anchors or conduits for a forbidden art.

## Reading Guide

This is a threshold chapter. Up until now, Doctor Mo has been a scheming, manipulative adversary whose threats were psychological and pharmacological. The *Seven Ghost Soul Devouring Art* marks his transition into a being that has fully embraced the dark side of cultivation. Pay close attention to the description of the black mist and its living tendrils—it’s not a passive aura but an entity that Doctor Mo must actively suppress. Also, note Han Li’s reaction: he does not scream, beg, or weep. He screws his eyes shut and braces for death, and when death doesn’t come, he feels… embarrassment. This is classic Han Li—pragmatic, thin-skinned, and always calculating, even when faced with a ritual of body horror that would drive most protagonists into full panic.

The chapter also subtly reinforces the “never lift a finger without benefit” rule from earlier chapters. Han Li’s survival in this moment is not due to courage but to a combination of stubbornness, embarrassment, and the faint hope that he might be too valuable to kill. It’s a very human, very mortal response—and that’s what makes it so effective. If you’ve been wondering how far Doctor Mo is willing to go, this chapter gives you the answer: he is willing to carve himself open to get what he wants. That level of commitment should terrify everyone, Han Li included.
