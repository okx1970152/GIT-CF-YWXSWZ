## Chapter Overview
Get ready, dear reader. You’ve been wondering why the *Blinking Sword Art* is such a legendary pain in the neck? Well, this chapter reveals the punchline in a way that will satisfy all your suspicions and then some. Han Li expects a single rare manual from his crazy, loyal friend Li Feiyu, and is preparing for a lecture on its esoteric mysteries. Instead, he gets hit by a plot twist that leaves him momentarily speechless. The chapter is a masterclass in delayed payoff: it sets up a comedic scene of a hot-tempered Han Li and a shamelessly cheeky Li Feiyu, before pivoting to a mystery that deepens the novel’s central cultivation puzzle.

## Key Plot Points
- **Han Li’s Explosion:** The chapter opens with Han Li unleashing his pent-up frustration. He doesn’t hold back, yelling at Li Feiyu for the insane risk of stealing from the Seven Peaks Hall. This is a rare outburst that comic relief serves to highlight the severity of the crime they could be caught for.
- **Li Feiyu’s Composure:** Li Feiyu, the ‘Fierce Tiger,’ is unfazed. He responds by picking his ear, looking innocent, and hamming up a show of being wronged. His theatrical whining is a brilliant foil to Han Li’s serious pragmatism.
- **The Big Reveal:** To explain himself, Li Feiyu casually hands Han Li a manual. When opened, it reads *Blinking Sword Art*. Surprise? No. The real shock comes when he reveals that the entire pile of books—seventy-four in total—are all *Blinking Sword Art*. The look of stunned disbelief on Han Li’s face is priceless.
- **The Mystery Deepens:** Instead of resolving the problem, the sheer volume of identical copies poses a new, more troubling enigma. Is this a hoax? A test? A trap left by the sect’s founder? Han Li’s shift from anger to analytical focus signals the beginning of his investigation into the secret.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter plays with the concept of a **secret technique cache** that defies the typical narrative of a single, rare manual locked away. In many cultivation stories, finding *the* one special tome is the goal. Here, finding a mountain of them is a paranoid obstacle. This subverts the trope to emphasize wasted effort and tactical redundancy. What appears to be a clear path to power immediately becomes a risk-reward puzzle. The “*Chicken Rib*” (*鸡肋*) principle comes to mind—the techniques are valuable in theory but dangerously impractical to exploit without understanding their true nature. The manual’s source from the sect’s founder, the Seven Ultimates Sage, also hints at a deep, intentional obscurity left by a master at the end of their life, a common xianxia hook where an ultimate secret is hidden in plain sight.

## Reading Guide
- **Appreciate the Tone:** Notice the stark contrast between Han Li’s immediate, heat-of-the-moment reaction and Li Feiyu’s cool, theatrical calm. It is a classic dynamic of the cautious calculator vs. the risk-loving friend, and the translation captures this clash beautifully.
- **The Puzzle Setup:** Don’t skim over the revelation of seventy-four identical copies. This is not a plot hole; it is a plot mystery. It is the first major clue that the *Blinking Sword Art* is not what it seems. Pay attention to how Han Li responds to this confusion—by immediately beginning to examine the book in his hands—because this investigative mindset is the foundation of his successful cultivation path.
- **Li Feiyu’s Motivation:** Beyond the humor, note Li Feiyu’s absolute commitment. He didn’t just steal one risky manual; he cleared out the entire collection. This reckless generosity reveals the depth of his bond with Han Li, turning a scene that could be pure comedy into a statement of true friendship built on shared secrets and risk. This is a rare moment of loyalty in a world defined by backstabbing.
