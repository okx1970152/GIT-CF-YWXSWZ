## Chapter Overview

Fellow Daoists, buckle up—this is the chapter where Han Li finally unleashes his ace in the hole, and *hoo boy* does it deliver. After months of discreet preparation, our favorite cautious mountain boy decides the moment has come to see if those legendary Gold Devouring Beetles actually live up to the hype. Spoiler: they absolutely do. The eccentric demonic cultivator, who until now was styling on a whole squad of Core Formation cultivators with his ghost skulls and forbidden treasures, finds himself staring at a swarm that eats his magic wheel for breakfast. If you've been craving that first big "oh no, *he's* the monster now" moment, this is the chapter.

## Key Plot Points

- The eccentric demonic cultivator's true combat power is laid bare: his black banners and ghost skulls are Primordial Ancient Treasures, not ordinary magic tools. He's been holding back until now.
- Jian the loose cultivator loses his three banner treasures in a single exchange—the ghost skull chews them to pieces. Both Jian and Hu Yue visibly waver, considering retreat.
- Shi Die stands uselessly at Qu Hun's side, a Foundation Establishment observer who cannot participate in a battle of this level.
- Han Li, having assessed the situation, decides the risk of the expedition failing outweighs the risk of exposing his ace. He deploys the Gold Devouring Beetles.
- The eccentric man's first counter, a black dharma wheel, is utterly consumed by the beetle swarm. His second counter—feeding more fingers to the ghost skulls to transform them into stronger ghost heads—initially creates a stalemate, but even碧渊鬼火 (Abyss Ghost-Fire) cannot burn the beetles.
- The chapter ends on the ultimate cliffhanger: an even louder buzzing rises in the distance, and the eccentric man's face turns bone-white.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Primordial Ancient Treasures (洪荒古宝)** are a distinct category of magical weapon in the *Mortal Stream* world-building. Unlike modern treasures, which can be absorbed into the body, nourished with spiritual sense, and refined layer by layer, Primordial Ancient Treasures are physically wielded artifacts from a bygone era—bulkier, cruder, but packing raw power that often exceeds modern equivalents. Jian's black banners being *gu bao* emphasizes just how strong the eccentric's skull actually is: it eats ancient treasures for breakfast.

**Ghost Spirit Sect (鬼灵门)** is one of the Six Demonic Sects of the Tianluo Nation. Their signature style involves controlling ghostly entities and cursed artifacts, often using severed body parts, skulls, and sacrificial rituals to empower their tools. The eccentric man's technique of severing his own fingers to feed his ghost skulls is a textbook example of the demonic path's willingness to pay permanent bodily costs for temporary combat boosts. In xianxia lore, this is called "blood sacrifice as tactical expenditure"—the user treats their own body as a consumable resource.

### Cross-Feeding Ritual (献祭)

A hallmark practice of demonic-path cultivators: sacrificing one's own blood and flesh (here, severed fingers) to empower summoned entities like ghost skulls, transforming them into a stronger combat form at a permanent cost.

## Reading Guide

Now, here's the *real* payoff moment for all those chapters where Han Li was quietly raising his bugs. The Gold Devouring Beetles have been hyped on the Strange Insect Ranking since their introduction, but this is the first time we see them in full combat against a serious threat. Notice the narrative rhythm: the eccentric man escalates his power three times (banners → skulls → skulls fed fingers), and the beetles laugh at every escalation. That's not just a fight scene; that's a *power benchmark*. Han Li is publicly signaling that he now possesses a trump card capable of matching a peak Core Formation demonic cultivator. From this point on, the other cultivators on this expedition will look at him *very* differently—and so should you.

## Why Insect Swarms are a Big Deal in Xianxia

You might be thinking, "Cool bugs, but I've seen swarms in other stories." In the *Mortal Stream* context, insect-based cultivation is a niche, high-risk, high-reward path. Normal cultivators don't bother with it because raising a swarm to combat-readiness takes years of resources, careful feeding, and constant protection against the swarm eating each other. The fact that Han Li has a full, combat-ready colony of Gold Devouring Beetles is a quiet testament to the Heaven Vial's cheat-level acceleration. Every other cultivator in this chapter is still relying on personal treasures or inheritance items. Han Li is fielding a *self-replicating, devour-and-grow biological weapon*. That's the terrifying edge of the Mortal Stream: the protagonist wins not by being the strongest, but by owning a system the world hasn't seen before.
