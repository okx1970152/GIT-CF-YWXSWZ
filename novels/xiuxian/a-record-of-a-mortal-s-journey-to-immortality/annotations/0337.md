## Chapter Overview
This chapter gives us a rare glimpse into the “quiet victory” phase of Han Li’s mortal stream existence. No explosions, no high-speed chases, no trump cards drawn at the last second—just a cold, methodical man gathering intelligence and checking on his old assets. He locates his Five Colors Sect target, then pays a surprise visit to Sun Ergou, the former nobody he elevated to gang boss over a decade ago. The reunion is less a happy homecoming and more a geopolitical audit: has this investment paid off, and is it still secure? But just as Han Li is about to leave, he drops a bombshell that changes the entire tone of the visit—Sun Ergou is living on borrowed time, poisoned into a quiet countdown he never noticed.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li eavesdrops on a banquet at a tavern where a group of wealthy scions are thanking the young master of the Five Colors Sect for rescuing them from kidnappers.
- From their conversation, Han Li learns that the Five Colors Sect Leader (his assassination target) is back at the Li Residence preparing for his 60th birthday celebration.
- Han Li calculates his next move: a night visit to the Li Residence. If no other Foundation Establishment cultivators are nearby, he will fulfill his contract and eliminate the sect leader.
- With daylight still available, Han Li shifts to his second agenda: a check-in on the Siping Gang, the organization he quietly installed Sun Ergou to run over a decade ago.
- Sun Ergou, now a wealthy and powerful gang lord with nine concubines, is shocked to see Han Li again after so many years. He immediately falls back into his old, deferential posture.
- Han Li’s examination reveals a cruel twist: Sun Ergou has been slowly poisoned and has only one month left to live.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The “Puppet Check-In” in Mortal Stream Logic:** Han Li didn’t install Sun Ergou out of charity or friendship—he planted a strategic asset in a mortal power network and let it grow unattended for over a decade. His visit is purely an audit: is the asset still viable? Has it attracted unwanted attention? This is classic Mortal Stream governance: build your power base, then let it run autonomously while you focus on your own cultivation. A protagonist who micromanages every mortal connection is doing it wrong.

**The Silent Poison as a Power Statement:** Sun Ergou has been living high, unaware that someone is slowly killing him over weeks or months. The narrative doesn’t tell us who planted the poison or why—only that it exists. This reinforces a core Dark Forest principle: just because you feel safe doesn’t mean you aren’t already a target. The most dangerous attacks are the ones that don’t announce themselves until they’re over.

## Reading Guide
**A Quiet Chapter, But Loaded.** This chapter is almost entirely setup—Han Li locates his target, checks his assets, and discovers a complication. But that final reveal about the poison transforms the check-in from a routine maintenance visit into a survival puzzle. Is the poison meant for Sun alone, or is it a trap aimed at Han Li through his proxy? An old hand at Mortal Stream reading will know: a complication that appears right when you think the mission is simple is never a coincidence.

**Sun Ergou: A Cautionary Tale.** We last saw Sun Ergou as a desperate dockworker. Now he’s a fat, cat-sitting gang boss with nine wives. He’s enjoyed the ride, but the moment his mysterious benefactor reappears, his entire world tilts. His character arc is a compressed lesson in the Mortal Stream: *getting power is one thing—keeping it, and keeping those who gave it to you happy, is another.*

**Han Li’s Social Performance.** Notice how Han Li speaks to Sun: no warmth, no updates on his own life, no interest in Sun’s accomplishments beyond a clinical acknowledgment. He is not checking in on an old friend. He is inspecting a tool that has been left in storage. The moment the inspection reveals a defect (the poison), he reframes the entire visit. This is a textbook example of how Han Li de-prioritizes emotional connection in favor of tactical assessment.
