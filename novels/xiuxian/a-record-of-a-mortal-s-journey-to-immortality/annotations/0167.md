## Chapter Overview
Fellow Daoists, brace yourselves. The mask is off, and the knives are out. In a brutal, no-dialogue-allowed opening, Han Li and the previously smug Senior Brother Lu finally collide head-on. This is not a testing of waters; it is an immediate, all-in exchange of their highest cards. We see the full spectrum of Han Li’s pre-Talisman-Treasure arsenal—drills, explosives, homing fireballs—only to watch them get dismantled by a single, high-grade Wind Wall talisman. The chapter instantly establishes Senior Brother Lu as a terrifyingly capable predator, not a mere thug, forcing Han Li to take the ultimate gamble and deploy his trump card. The chess match begins.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li’s cover is blown; recognizing the futility of hiding, he steps out to face Senior Brother Lu directly.
- Senior Brother Lu instantly identifies Han Li from a single, chaotic encounter months prior, revealing his frightening memory or meticulous nature—a sign of a truly dangerous opponent.
- Han Li immediately deduces that negotiation is impossible against such a cunning foe and commits to a first-strike strategy without a single wasted word.
- Han Li unleashes a three-stage layered assault: a steel ring, explosive spheres, and a volley of homing fireballs.
- Senior Brother Lu, caught off guard by Han Li's preemptive aggression, is forced to switch from offense to defense, deploying a cherished high-grade Wind Wall talisman.
- The Wind Wall effortlessly neutralizes all of Han Li's attacks, establishing a clear gap in their immediate magical firepower.
- Han Li, recognizing he is outgunned, makes the cold calculation to activate his ultimate defense to buy time for his Talisman Treasure.
- The chapter ends with Han Li erecting a three-layered defensive shell—steel ring, Darksteel shield, and a blue light barrier—preparing for the coming storm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a masterclass in the structural logic of a Qi Condensation duel. The key dynamic isn't just power, but **resource consumption**. Han Li's initial volley—the ring, the spheres, the fireball salvo—are all "standard issue" attacks. Senior Brother Lu's response, the high-grade Wind Wall talisman, represents a significant expense. In the Xianxia economy, talismans are disposable, pre-charged magical batteries. Using one means burning capital that could have been saved for a life-or-death moment. That Senior Brother Lu is willing to burn it immediately shows he considers Han Li a genuine threat worth the cost. This contrasts perfectly with Han Li's own calculated response: pulling out his ultimate trump card (the Talisman Treasure) which requires a lengthy preparation time and a secure casting environment. The "burn rate" of resources is the true battlefield of a Xianxia fight. You don't just win by having a bigger stick; you win by forcing your opponent to empty their pockets on your terms.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, because this is where the gloves come off. This chapter is all about establishing the threat level. We've seen Han Li fight scrappy and smart, but here he fights a mirror of himself—a calculating, resource-rich predator. Pay close attention to how quickly both parties assess the situation and commit to lethal force. There is no "let's talk this out," no posturing. It's pure, cold-blooded tactical warfare. The action is a fantastic display of the "magic-as-munitions" principle in the Mortal Stream. Note how Han Li's first volley fails. This is not a sign of weakness, but a deliberate information-gathering probe. He now knows the limits of his standard kit and exactly what he's up against. The stage is set for a clash of trump cards. The question isn't if Han Li will use the Talisman Treasure, but whether he'll survive long enough to finish the cast.
