## Chapter Overview

Han Li witnesses a live-action magical duel on the mountain peak, where an arrogant Senior Brother Lu terrorizes the two Fu Murong brothers with his powerful Azure Arc Slash. When the brothers try to use other disciples as human shields, Han Li nimbly sidesteps the role, drawing a sharp, disapproving look from the newly arrived fairy-like beauty, Senior Sister Nie. After the dust settles, Han Li returns to his herb garden to analyze the Foundation Establishment Pill recipe he obtained—and immediately runs headfirst into a terrifying resource bottleneck. Even with a heaven-defying cheat, the path to Foundation Establishment is paved with ingredients he has never even heard of.

## Key Plot Points

- Senior Brother Lu humiliates the Fu Murong brothers in a flashy, dominating display of spell power, splitting his Azure Arc Slash mid-flight to chase both targets.
- One brother attempts to use Han Li as a human shield, but Han Li subtly shifts away, leaving the youth cursing and scrambling.
- The stocky young man with the wooden crutch creates an earthen wall to block the attack, inadvertently becoming a target for Lu’s future revenge.
- Senior Sister Nie, a breathtaking blue-robed beauty, descends from above, effortlessly extinguishes the Azure Arc with a firebird, and rebukes Lu while thanking the stocky young man for his intervention.
- Nie delivers a private, cold judgment to Han Li via sound transmission, criticizing his “looking out for yourself” attitude—a judgment Han Li silently dismisses as foolish idealism.
- Back at the Hundred Herb Garden, Han Li pores over the Foundation Establishment Pill recipe, realizing that while he can grow the thirty-one auxiliary ingredients, the three main materials (Jade Marrow Mushroom, Purple Ape Fruit, and Celestial Spirit Fruit) are completely absent and unknown in his current world.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Standing Aloof (独善其身, dú shàn qí shēn):** This is a crucial character moment for Han Li. In the Mortal Stream worldview, “not being anyone’s shield” is not cowardice; it is survival 101. Han Li’s internal justification—that Lu would never dare kill a sect brother publicly—is a cold tactical calculation, not a lack of courage. The blue-robed beauty’s rebuke represents the standard “righteous path” expectation, which Han Li quietly and pragmatically rejects. The stocky young man who did stand up is now marked for Lu’s future retaliation, proving Han Li’s point. This scene is a perfect microcosm of the mortal-stream principle: the world rewards those who pick their battles, not those who act on impulse or social pressure.

**The Three Guards of Foundation Establishment:** The three main herbs mentioned—Jade Marrow Mushroom, Purple Ape Fruit, and Celestial Spirit Fruit—are classic late-stage qi-condensation bottleneck treasures. They are rare, slow-growing, and fiercely contested. In cultivation fiction, these ingredients are basically the “entry exam” for the Foundation Establishment path, and their existence immediately signals that Han Li’s next major story arc will involve hunting for them in the wild or trading at high-stakes cultivator gatherings.

## Reading Guide

**Prepare for the grind.** Han Li has just discovered that having the Heaven Vial’s accelerated growth isn’t enough—he still needs seeds or specimens of these ultra-rare herbs. This is a classic Mortal Stream setup: the cheat gives you the *potential* to succeed, not the guarantee. The path forward will involve research, networking, risk assessment, and possibly a great deal of bloodshed. Also, keep an eye on Senior Brother Lu and the stocky young man—their quiet conflict isn’t over, and Han Li’s cold observation of these dynamics is a preview of his future social navigation. The blue-robed Nie is also a figure to watch; her elegant power and swift intervention mark her as a serious player in the sect hierarchy.
