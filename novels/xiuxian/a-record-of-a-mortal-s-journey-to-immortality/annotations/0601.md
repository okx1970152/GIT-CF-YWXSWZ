## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, *A Record of a Mortal’s Journey to Immortality* delivers a quiet hammer blow of grief and a cold pivot toward calculated opportunity. Han Li and Mei Ning finally confirm the fate of her missing brother—not through a dramatic encounter or a villain’s confession, but by stumbling into a nameless village warehouse. The corpse is already bones. The belongings are stacked like inventory. Mei Ning’s reaction is a raw, contained sob that dissolves into silence. And Han Li? He does what Han Li always does: files the tragedy, turns his attention to the next practical step—gathering Yin Beast Crystals. But he doesn’t fight alone. His secret weapon, the Crying Soul Beast, gets its first real showcase, and it steals the scene. The chapter ends with a dead giant beast, a smiling cultivator, and a pointed glance toward the west. This is the Mortal Stream at its most efficient: grief is a footnote, and profit is the bottom line.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li and Mei Ning arrive at Red Earth Village after visiting three other settlements. The village leader confirms that no newcomers have survived—several skeletons were found after the last rift opening.
- Mei Ning identifies her brother’s bloodstained robe and personal storage pouch in the village warehouse. She breaks down in private grief but emerges composed, declaring they can now head directly to Stormwind Mountain.
- Before leaving, Han Li decides to collect Yin Beast Crystals, recognizing that the Netherworld Domain is a rare opportunity for such resources.
- Han Li reveals his strategy: deploy the Crying Soul Beast. The beast effortlessly kills a giant clawed beast by inhaling its energy through a yellow light, leaving a dried-out corpse and a green crystal.
- Mei Ning connects the monkey to a legendary fierce beast from old tales, her expression darkening as she recognizes its true nature.
- Han Li pockets the crystal and announces there are more powerful Yin beasts to the west, ending the chapter on a note of cold, single-minded efficiency.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Netherworld Domain**: This pocket dimension operates on its own rules, where Yin energy replaces spiritual qi. Normal cultivators struggle here, but Yin-attributed techniques and beasts from the ghostly spectrum thrive. The domain’s ecosystem is entirely self-contained—beasts reproduce, energies cycle, and survival depends on adaptation.

**Yin Beast Crystals**: These are condensed energy cores formed inside Yin-type spirit beasts. In the wider cultivation world, such crystals are rare because Yin-rich environments are scarce. They serve as raw materials for demonic or ghostly techniques, artifact refinement, and alchemical recipes. Han Li’s decision to stockpile them is classic long-term planning.

**Crying Soul Beast**: A rare beast-type spirit creature with a natural affinity for devouring Yin energy, ghostly matter, and even souls. In xianxia lore, such beasts are often legendary or extinct, making Han Li’s possession of one a significant hidden asset. Its advanced form is associated with the Profound Bone Saint’s techniques, giving it a dark and powerful lineage.

**Subduing a Beast vs. Raising One**: In the Mortal Stream, Han Li treats the Crying Soul Beast not as a pet but as a tool. Its care is pure logistics, and its deployment is tactical. This aligns with the broader xianxia convention: beasts of power are bound through contracts, pills, and mutual benefit, not emotional attachment. The moment it becomes inefficient, a true Mortal Stream protagonist would replace it without hesitation.

## Reading Guide
In this chapter, grief is not a detour. It is a stop sign you roll through. The narrative gives Mei Ning exactly fifteen minutes to cry, and then she’s back on her feet, her voice composed, her eyes still swollen but her head clear. That is the Mortal Stream’s way: no dramatic mourning arcs, no revenge speeches. Just acceptance and forward motion. Han Li does the same. He doesn’t offer hollow comfort. He pats her shoulder once—one pat—and steps outside to give her privacy. That single gesture is more powerful than a thousand words of sympathy. It says: *I respect your grief enough to not interrupt it.*

Then, the chapter pivots to cold, mercenary logic. Han Li wants Yin Beast Crystals. Not because they’re shiny, but because once he leaves this place, he may never see them again. That is the heart of his character: every environment is an extraction opportunity. He doesn’t just survive the Netherworld Domain; he audits it for profit. And his method of extraction? Letting a tiny, adorable-looking monkey eat the life force out of a giant beast until its body shrivels into a husk. The contrast between cute appearance and brutal function is deliberate, and it lands perfectly.

New readers should pay attention to Mei Ning’s shift. She starts as a companion on a mission. By the end of the chapter, she is a witness. She sees what Han Li’s beast can do, and she connects it to old legends. Her expression turns dark. She is not afraid of Han Li—but she is beginning to understand the depth of his resources. That moment of realization is worth more than any fight scene. In the Mortal Stream, reputation is built not by words, but by the things your allies quietly discover about you.
