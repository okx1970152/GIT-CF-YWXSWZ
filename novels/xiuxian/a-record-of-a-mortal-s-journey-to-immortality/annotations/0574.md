## Chapter Overview

Alright, buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is a total genre shift! We’ve been deep in the politics and slaughter of the Blood Forbidden Trial, the tense negotiations of the Ghost Spirit Sect—and now we’re suddenly setting sail on the open sea! It’s the “New World” expansion pack for A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality. The scale just blew up. Han Li, having secured a foothold and a stash of loot, is now staring out at the endless ocean, ready to leave the petty squabbles of the Yue Nation’s seven sects behind. It’s a quiet victory chapter, a moment of strategic reorganization before diving into the next, far more dangerous layer of the cultivation world. Get ready for sea monsters, pirate cultivators, and a whole new set of terrifying old monsters.

## Key Plot Points

Han Li, after his harrowing experiences and political victories, finally finds a moment of relative peace to take stock of his situation. The chapter opens with him alone in his secret chamber within the Qixuan Sect’s valley, his mind far from the internal politics of the sect. He’s not thinking about rewards or seniority; he’s thinking about the ocean. He retrieves a jade slip he obtained during his trials, specifically the fragment of the Kun Peng Art.

He spends the chapter engaged in a deep, methodical audit of his resources and future paths. He reviews the incomplete Kun Peng technique, a body-tempering and flight art of immense power, but laments its fragmentary nature and the difficulty of filling in the gaps. He then systematically goes through his other gains—spirit herbs, talismans, and other technique jade slips—weighing their value not for immediate profit, but for long-term strategic survival in the Chaotic Star Sea. He makes a calculated choice, selecting a taboo demonic technique for enhancing his weapon, marking a clear shift toward preparing for a more brutal, lawless environment.

The chapter ends with Han Li’s gaze turning outward, his mind set on the horizon. He has made his choice: the Chaotic Star Sea is his next destination. This isn’t a spur-of-the-moment adventure; it is a cold, logical next step in his millennia-long pursuit of immortality.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces a major shift in “scale.” The “Chaotic Star Sea” is a classic trope in Chinese web novels, representing a “new map” that is exponentially larger and more dangerous than the protagonist’s starting region. Think of it like leaving the tutorial zone to enter the open world. The moral and political rules of the Yellow Maple Valley and the Seven Sects of Yue no longer apply. The Chaotic Star Sea is a frontier ruled by raw power, where rogue cultivators, demonic overlords, and ancient sea clans fight for territory and resources. The Kun Peng itself is a mythical creature from the *Zhuangzi*, a giant fish that transforms into a massive bird capable of soaring across the entire cosmos. Finding a technique named after it signals that Han Li is beginning to look at the *big picture* of cultivation, aiming for heights that can’t be reached by staying in a small pond.

### Chaotic Star Sea

A vast, lawless ocean region in the *RMJI* world, serving as a "new world" frontier where rogue cultivators, demonic sects, and ancient sea clans vie for power, far more dangerous than the mortal continent.

## Reading Guide

If you’ve been feeling like the “mortal world” arc was getting a bit crowded, get ready for some breathing room—of the terrifying kind. This chapter is the calm before the storm, the moment Han Li looks at a map of a vast, unexplored ocean and decides to jump in. It’s a classic “leveling up the setting” moment. Don’t expect big fights here; instead, enjoy Han Li doing what he does best: processing information, calculating his odds, and making a ruthless, long-term strategic move. Pay attention to the technique he ultimately chooses. It tells you a lot about how he expects to survive in the Chaotic Star Sea. It won't be through diplomacy.
