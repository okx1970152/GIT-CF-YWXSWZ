## Chapter Overview

The trap is sprung. In this action-packed chapter, the long-hunted Koi Infant Beast is finally cornered by Feng Sanniang’s formation team and the two Core Formation elders of the Six Allies Hall. What follows is a spectacular clash of spells and treasures, showcasing the terrifying firepower of a grade-6 demon beast and the extreme lengths cultivators will go to in order to claim a prize. But just as victory seems certain, a chilling and unexpected twist turns the hunters into the hunted, proving that in the Mortal Stream, the sea’s true depths are measured in treachery, not just fathoms.

## Key Plot Points

- The ambush is triggered: Feng Sanniang and the formation team successfully trap the Koi Infant Beast inside the Six Avoidance Water Wave Formation.
- The Koi Infant Beast unleashes its powerful Water-Gang Thunder in a desperate counterattack, forcing the formation holders to strain their magic power and several to spit blood.
- Elder Miao and Elder Gu activate their legendary bronze halberds, a pair of peculiar treasures specially designed to counter the beast, growing them to massive size.
- The Koi Infant Beast, sensing the deadly threat from above, performs a final, desperate strike, creating a massive whirlwind of water and thunder that shatters the formation.
- Just as the formation collapses, the two Core Formation elders successfully unleash their bronze halberds, piercing and subduing the Koi Infant Beast.
- The elders, exhausted but triumphant, fly down to claim their prize, while the other cultivators watch with suppressed greed.
- In a shocking twist, a jet-black, sinister wind erupts from the sea beneath the beast, flash-freezing the entire area, including the Kiño Infant Beast, into a block of ice, and then lunging at the two Core Formation cultivators.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The spellcraft in this chapter vividly illustrates a key principle of *RMJI*: the battle is a resource war, not a test of honor. The `Water-Gang Thunder` is a classic example of a “great killing move,” a trump card that consumes immense mana. The `Essence Blood` (精血) spat onto the formation banner by Feng Sanniang is a more extreme example—a cultivator’s most refined life force, permanently lost when used, representing the ultimate price for a short-term power surge. The fact that she pays this cost without hesitation confirms the high stakes: survival and profit come before personal longevity. This is the cold economy of the cultivation world at play.

## Reading Guide

This is a textbook example of a “hunt gone wrong” sequence in the Mortal Stream. Pay close attention to the tactical layers: the formation is not a simple cage but a complex tool requiring constant, depleting mana input; the Koi Infant Beast is not a mindless monster but a calculating predator that saves its powerful moves for the perfect moment; and the supposed victors are not safe until the treasure is literally in their hands. The final twist is a brutal reminder of a core rule: in the chaotic, lawless overseas regions, the biggest predator is always waiting just out of sight. Watch how Han Li, even in a supporting role, is already calculating the risks and conserving his strength, a lesson for any reader.

## The Economics of Immortality: Essence Blood

The term `Essence Blood` (精血) appears here as a literal, physical price tag for victory. In Daoist-influenced cultivation lore, a cultivator's body is a finite resource. Essence Blood is the most concentrated form of their vitality, often described as their "life's essence." Using it provides an immediate, powerful boost to techniques, but the cost is permanent and irrecoverable. It damages the cultivator's foundation, shortens their lifespan, and can be a fatal wound to their long-term potential. Feng Sanniang's willingness to pay this cost underscores the desperate reality of the Mortal Stream: the difference between a temporary loss and a fatal setback is often measured in how much of yourself you're willing to burn.
