## Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces Han Li and Zhang Tie as Doctor Mo's disciples on probation. Doctor Mo teaches them a set of nameless oral formulas but explicitly refuses to teach martial arts, warning them that failure to master the formulas within half a year will result in being sent to the outer halls. Han Li, still a naive boy, secretly hopes to fail so he can return home. Meanwhile, Han Li learns about the Seven Mysteries Sect's rigid hierarchy and Doctor Mo's unusual background as an outsider who saved the sect leader's life and was granted a position as an honorary elder.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li is awakened by Zhang Tie, who shares food and displays a helpful nature.
- They visit Doctor Mo, who ignores them until they have stood for a long time.
- Doctor Mo formally accepts them as disciples on probation, emphasizing that he will teach only health-preservation oral formulas, not martial arts.
- He sets a six-month deadline: if they fail to meet standards, they become outer disciples.
- Han Li's inner thoughts reveal his excitement at being a sect member and his secret wish to fail so he can see his family.
- In the following days, they study medicine, reading, writing, and basic martial theory (twelve meridians, eight extraordinary meridians, acupoints).
- After one month, they are separated from other boys to focus full-time on the nameless oral formulas, which they must not reveal.
- Through overheard conversations, Han Li learns the sect's structure: a main sect leader (direct descendant of the Seven Ultimates Sage), three deputy leaders, Outer Halls (Flying Bird, Treasure Gathering, Four Seas, Outer Blade), Inner Halls (Hundred Forging, Seven Peaks, Worship, Blood Blade), and an Elder Council.
- He also learns Doctor Mo's backstory: once an outsider physician, he saved Sect Leader Wang Lu from a fatal ambush, earning a position as an honorary elder in the Worship Hall.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Seven Mysteries Sect Organization**: The sect is portrayed as a Jianghu (martial arts underworld) organization with militaristic hierarchy. Outer halls handle business and low-level tasks, while inner halls hold elite combatants and core techniques. This structure reflects typical xianxia sect dynamics where power and resources are concentrated at the top.
- **Nameless Oral Formulas (无名口诀)**: These are basic cultivation techniques that focus on health and longevity rather than combat. In xianxia, such formulas are often the first step toward inner energy cultivation. Doctor Mo's emphasis on secrecy and punishment hints at their true value.
- **Twelve Main Meridians and Eight Extraordinary Meridians**: Traditional Chinese medicine concepts integrated into martial arts. Meridians are energy channels; the twelve regular ones correspond to organs, while the eight extraordinary ones govern deeper energy reserves. The ability to sense and control qi through these channels is foundational for cultivation.
- **Doctor Mo's Role**: As a Worship Hall honorary elder (供奉), he is a respected but non-combat figure. His medical skills earned him privilege, but his martial ability remains unknown. This reflects the pragmatic nature of xianxia sects: they reward useful skills even from outsiders.

## Reading Guide

- **Han Li's Naivete**: At this stage, Han Li is still a village boy who dreams of returning home. He does not yet display the calculating caution that defines his later character. Notice how his desire to fail the test contrasts with his eventual survival-driven mindset.
- **Doctor Mo's Cold Demeanor**: His indifference, delayed acknowledgment, and strict warning create an atmosphere of tension. He is not a warm teacher; he is a taskmaster. This aligns with the style guide's emphasis on transactional relationships.
- **Worldbuilding Foundations**: The chapter establishes the sect's hierarchical structure and the existence of a founder (Seven Ultimates Sage). These details will shape Han Li's understanding of the power dynamics he must navigate.
- **The Nameless Formula's Significance**: Its secrecy and Doctor Mo's focus indicate it is more than a health exercise. This is a subtle introduction to the cultivation system that will drive the plot.

## The Seven Ultimates Sage {#seven-ultimates-sage}

The Seven Ultimates Sage (七绝上人) is the legendary founder of the Seven Mysteries Sect. According to sect lore, he possessed extraordinary martial arts and established the sect's original techniques and structures. The current sect leader Wang Lu claims direct descent from him, which grants legitimacy and authority. In xianxia tradition, a sect's founder often represents its peak martial lineage, and his techniques become treasured inheritances. The fact that Wang Lu is a "direct descendant" suggests that bloodline or close apprenticeship, not just skill, determines leadership.
