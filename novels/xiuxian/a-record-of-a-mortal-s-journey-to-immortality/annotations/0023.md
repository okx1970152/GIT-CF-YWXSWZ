## Chapter Overview

This chapter is where Han Li makes the jump from passive holder of a mysterious artifact to active investigator. After a near-fatal encounter with a Heart Demon—the cultivation world’s version of a psychic ambush—he rediscovers the long-forgotten bottle and decides, with chilling pragmatism, to test its contents on living animals. The result is grotesque, unsettling, and perfectly in step with the Mortal Stream’s core aesthetic: no wonder, no reverence, just raw observation and cold method. Han Li doesn’t worship the bottle. He experiments on it.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li suffers a Heart Demon Invasion triggered by emotional instability, barely escaping with the help of his family’s peace talisman.
- The ordeal pushes his cultivation to the peak of the third layer of the Nameless Oral Formula.
- He rediscovers the mysterious bottle, forgotten for years, and resolves to investigate its secret.
- He observes the transactional nature of the kitchen steward: no Doctor Mo, no delivered meals.
- He purchases two rabbits and feeds them diluted green liquid, documenting the horrifying effect—rapid, balloon-like swelling.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The distinction between Qi Deviation and Heart Demon Invasion is important. Qi Deviation is an energy imbalance—the body’s internal circuits going haywire. Heart Demon Invasion is a spiritual attack: an internal entity born from unresolved attachment, guilt, or trauma that tries to overwrite the cultivator’s will. The peace talisman works because it is a material symbol of filial bond—a stable anchor that the chaos cannot erode.

Han Li’s decision to “dilute” the green liquid shows early scientific instinct. He does not down the drop raw. He tests a fraction at a time. This caution will become his trademark.

The rabbit swelling is not random body horror. In the logic of the cultivation world, the liquid appears to be accelerating tissue growth at a violent rate. The mechanism mimics what the bottle later does to plants: forced growth. But animal tissue cannot sustain the same speed. The bodies rupture from within.

## Reading Guide

This chapter introduces the novel’s central dynamic: Han Li treating his treasure as an object of study, not worship. The bottle is not a sentient benefactor—it is a tool he must crack through trial and error. Readers are primed for a long learning curve.

The interaction with the kitchen steward also reinforces the world’s transactional logic: no benefit, no service. Han Li does not complain. He accepts, learns, and moves on. He is beginning to understand that this world runs on exchange, not kindness.

Watch for the detail that the liquid “sends an unbidden chill into the heart” when he looks at it. The text is giving you a warning: this is not a friendly potion. Han Li ignores the chill because he has a hypothesis to test. That willingness to push through unease in pursuit of useful knowledge is one of his defining traits.

Note also that the rabbit experiment is described without flinching. The narrative does not pause for moral reflection. That absence is itself the message.
