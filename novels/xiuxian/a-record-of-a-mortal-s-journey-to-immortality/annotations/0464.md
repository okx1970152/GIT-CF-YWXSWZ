## Chapter Overview
The game board fills. Man Brutal Beard—demonic path’s heavyweight brawler—finally crashes the party in the Void Heaven Palace, and his entrance is anything but subtle. This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal-Stream power projection: one glance and a casual aura burst are enough to pin Han Li to the ground, making it crystal clear that Core Formation is still small fry in this room. The real tension, of course, isn’t the fight that doesn’t happen—it’s the blink-and-you’ll-miss-it chess match between three Nascent Soul monsters over Han Li’s fate as a bargaining chip. Brace yourselves, fellow Daoists: the “calm before the storm” phase just ended.

## Key Plot Points
1. The final Nascent Soul players gather: Wan Tianming’s trio arrives first, followed by the Star Palace elders and a few stragglers. Only Man Brutal Beard is missing, dragging the demonic faction into visible anxiety.  
2. Extreme Yin and the Azure-clad Elder cannot maintain their facade of calm as the deadline nears—a subtle but important crack revealing their strategic vulnerability without Man Brutal Beard.  
3. Man Brutal Beard’s entrance: a world-shaking howl, a golden meteor plummet, and a landing that shakes the ground. His body bears golden scales that gleam with cold, penetrating light—the signature of his Heavenly Devil Arts (*Tuomo Gong*).  
4. A single glance from Man Brutal Beard pins Han Li in place, nearly seeing through his mind; only the Great Derivative Art’s auto-defense rescues him. Extreme Yin steps in, claiming Han Li as a disciple.  
5. Man Brutal Beard probes: he openly asks for Han Li to be transferred to him, and when refused, his suspicion is instantly kindled—*why would Extreme Yin and the Azure-clad Elder both defend a mere probationary disciple so fiercely?*

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
### The Four Nascent Soul Powers & the “Discipleship” Gambit
This chapter brilliantly showcases how xianxia politics operate in a tense locked-room scenario (the sealed space of the Void Heaven Palace). The three-way balance between Extreme Yin’s demonic duo, Wan Tianming’s righteous trio, and Man Brutal Beard as a wildcard is constantly shifting. Extreme Yin’s claim that Han Li is his “disciple” is not a real master-disciple bond—it’s an emergency custody tactic. In the Mortal Stream, an affiliation claim (even a fake one) can be a shield, because attacking a “disciple” is a direct challenge to the master’s face. Man Brutal Beard’s suspicious reaction—*why would two Nascent Soul monsters defend a mere Qi Condensation boy?*—is the classic Dark Forest recognition of a hidden asset. This is not fatherly protection; it’s asset protection in a room full of predators.

### Heavenly Devil Arts (*Tuomo Gong*) & Physical Cultivation
Man Brutal Beard’s golden scale armor is a hallmark of body-cultivation techniques in xianxia, where a practitioner reforges their physical form rather than relying on external magical treasures. The “scale-armor” imagery draws on ancient Chinese folklore of demons and divine beasts (like dragons) whose scales are naturally impervious to weapons. The golden light and metallic sheen serve as visual shorthand for “this guy is not a wizard who dodges—he’s a tank who absorbs.”

## Reading Guide
Pay close attention to Man Brutal Beard’s tone in this chapter. He speaks with casual, dismissive arrogance, but his threats are not delivered with rage—they are delivered as statements of fact, which is far more terrifying. When he casually says “I think this boy might be better suited to my Heavenly Devil Arts,” it sounds like a jest, but his follow-up cold laugh and sudden suspicion prove he was probing for a reaction. This is a classic Mortal-Stream negotiation tactic: the predator reveals interest and then watches who flinches.

Also note Han Li’s position: he is a silent non-actor for the entire chapter. He cannot fight back, he cannot speak up—his only agency lies in his suppressed internal calculation and the automatic defense of his techniques. The chapter is a brutal reminder that until a cultivator reaches the Nascent Soul stage, he is a pawn on someone else’s board, and pawns who survive do so by being too valuable to be sacrificed but too weak to resist being moved.
