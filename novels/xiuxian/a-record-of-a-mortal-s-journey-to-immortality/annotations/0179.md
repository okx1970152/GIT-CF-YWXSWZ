## Chapter Overview

Friends, we have a *situation*! The Blood Forbidden Trial has officially begun, and our boy Han Li is doing what he does best: scanning the room, identifying threats, and calculating survival odds. But before the carnage starts, we get a delightful call-back to the Tai Nan market—the shy girl who sold him the brush is back, now a disciple of the Spirit Beast Mountain Sect and clearly being bullied by a bearded brute. Han Li, ever the pragmatist, smirks and waves, then makes the brute’s face turn purple with a single funny face. The kid never misses a chance to sow chaos without spending a drop of mana.

The real meat of the chapter, however, is the grand opening of the forbidden land. Seven Core Formation patriarchs unleash their signature treasures against an ancient, endless wind-wall formation. The visual is pure spectacle: transforming mud into giant stone swords, dimension-ripping cyan light, and a desperate, hours-long struggle to create a single passage. Han Li watches it all with cold, analytical eyes, comparing the sect’s array to a child’s toy. In the end, he and hundreds of other ragged, silent cultivators plunge through the tunnel, knowing that the moment they land, the truce is over. Game on.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li surveys the competition, mentally marking the thirteenth-layer experts he must avoid unless he can stab them in the back.
- He recognizes the shy, blush-prone girl from the Tai Nan meeting, now a member of the Spirit Beast Mountain Sect under the thumb of a brutish senior.
- Han Li antagonizes the brute with a funny face but backs off to spare the girl further abuse.
- The Core Formation leaders perform a “mud to stone” spell to test the barrier, which responds with a terrifying, all-encompassing wind-blade storm.
- After hours of effort, the seven patriarchs use their personal treasures to hammer open a dark, circular passage into the forbidden land.
- The disciples enter in waves; as Han Li passes through the exit, he is immediately teleported to a random location within the zone.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Wind Blade Array” Trope**: This chapter showcases a classic xianxia set-piece: an ancient, high-tier defensive formation that generates an endless storm of cutting force. The visual of a “wall” made of wind blades stretching to the horizon is a direct descendant of Jianghu-meets-Daoist alchemy imagery, where a barrier is less a “force field” and more a **hostile elemental ecosystem**. The fact that even seven veteran Core Formation cultivators (far above the Qi Condensation disciples) must grind their way through with a coordinated volley of personal treasures establishes the terrifying gap between an ancient restriction and mortal-grade magic.

**The “Blood Forbidden Trial” Logic**: This entry mechanism—a single, temporary passage opened by senior experts—is a staple of the “survival arena” sub-genre. It emphasizes that the space inside is *self-contained* and *inhospitable*. There are no respites, no safe zones. Once inside, the cultivators are thrown into random locations (teleportation upon entry is a classic trick to prevent early massacres) and must survive a gauntlet of natural hazards, rival disciples, and a finite number of exit opportunities. The term “Blood Forbidden” (血禁) combines “blood” with “prohibit,” codifying the zone’s nature: a prohibited place that demands a bloody price for entry.

**Core Formation Treasures**: The author takes care to describe each senior’s weapon—a ruler, a flying sword, a pink ribbon, a dragon staff, a great seal—as extensions of their personal Dao. In xianxia, a Core Formation cultivator’s magic treasure is not just a tool; it is a **part of their body and soul**, refined and grown over decades. The spectacle of seven treasures working in concert is meant to show raw, venerable power, which makes it all the more humbling that they can barely crack a weakened barrier.

### Qi Condensation (炼气期)

The foundational cultivation realm; all the competing disciples are at this stage, with the peak being the thirteenth layer.

## Reading Guide

Get ready for a classic “mob battle” opening: a sweeping wide-angle shot of all players, a tense pre-game silence, and a dramatic entry sequence that separates the wheat from the chaff. The chapter is a masterclass in setting stakes without a single fight. Han Li’s assessment of his surroundings is clinical—we don’t hear about how scared he is; we see him pick out who to run from and who to stab. This is peak Mortal Stream mentality.

Also, the reappearance of the Tai Nan brush girl is a small but welcome moment of continuity in a sea of new faces. It hints at a wider world where past transactions matter, and that Han Li’s social web, however thin, is beginning to expand. Finally, pay close attention to the teleportation mechanic at the very end: the fact that everyone is randomly scattered inside tells you the coming war is not a team sport. It’s every man, woman, and beast for themselves.
