## Chapter Overview

Well, well, well. Han Li hasn't even properly unpacked his bags in his new home province before the local vultures start circling. This chapter delivers a brutally efficient masterclass in why the Mortal Stream cultivation world is so terrifying: it’s not just the demonic cultivators and ancient beasts you have to watch out for. The lowliest mortal thugs are just as willing to murder you for a few measly silver coins. Our boy Han Li, fresh off the boat, is immediately targeted by a local gang for a simple heist. What follows isn’t a fight; it’s a textbook demonstration of the absolute power gap between a trained asset like Qu Hun and the mundane predators of the mortal world. Get ready for a cold, short, and very one-sided lesson in the value of a soulless enforcer.

## Key Plot Points

*   **An Obvious Trap:** Han Li is led into an isolated alley by two porters he hired at the dock. A dozen armed men emerge from the shadows, led by local thugs Black Bear and Sun Ergou, who plan to murder him for his silver.
*   **The Order is Given:** Reading the bloodthirsty nature of the attackers, Han Li coldly concludes they have done this many times before. Without a hint of hesitation or drama, he issues a single command to Qu Hun: kill them all, no mercy.
*   **The Execution Begins:** Qu Hun doesn't waste a second. He charges into the crowd, shattering skulls with his bare fists and kicking men through the air. His attacks are instantaneous, simple, and absolutely lethal. The thugs' iron clubs and knives shatter against his skin with no effect.
*   **The Predator's Realization:** Black Bear, a veteran fighter with good eyesight, immediately recognizes that Qu Hun is on a completely different tier of power. He abandons all thoughts of fighting or fleeing and coldly calculates that his only chance for survival is to take Han Li hostage, revealing a pragmatic, survival-first instinct that mirrors the cultivation world’s logic.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a fascinating look at the "mortal layer" of the xianxia world. The struggle for resources and survival isn't confined to cultivators; it permeates every level of society. The gang's mentality is purely zero-sum: a stranger with a heavy purse is seen as an invitation to wealth, not a customer. This mirrors the "Dark Forest" logic of the cultivation world, where every encounter is a potential threat or an opportunity for resource acquisition. The key difference is the *scale* of power. To a mortal gangster, a bag of silver is a life-changing treasure. To Han Li, it's a trivial nuisance. The sheer, casual brutality with which Qu Hun dismantles the gang underscores a core theme of *A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality*: the laws and dangers of the jungle apply everywhere, but the apex predators are not the ones holding knives in dark alleys. They are the ones who can turn those alleys into pools of blood without breaking a sweat.

### Tiger's Mouth (虎口 / Hu Kou)

The fleshy area of the palm between the thumb and index finger. In martial arts, a weapon strike powerful enough to split the "tiger's mouth" indicates extreme force that surpasses the user's grip strength.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a "show, don't tell" exercise in establishing narrative rules. Pay close attention to *how* the violence is presented. There’s no dramatic music, no internal monologue about the sanctity of life, and no witty one-liner from Han Li. The order is given, and the violence is executed with the cold, mechanical efficiency of a factory process. This isn't about Han Li being a hero or a villain; it's about him being a pragmatist. He assessed the threat, identified the most efficient tool (Qu Hun), and used it. The fact that the gang leader, Black Bear, has the tactical awareness to try and take Han Li hostage shows that even within the mortal world, there exists a hierarchy of competence. He is a smarter predator than his underlings, but his intelligence only makes his terror at the power gap more palpable. It perfectly sets the tone for how Han Li views his own place in the world: as a creature constantly calculating risk and reward, always ready to deploy overwhelming force to neutralize a threat before it becomes a problem.
