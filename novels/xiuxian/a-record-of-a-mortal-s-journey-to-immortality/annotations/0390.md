## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow travelers! Chapter 390 drops us straight into the deep end of a desperate, tactical melee. Han Li, having survived the initial betrayal at sea, turns predator. But this isn't a blazing clash of equals; it's a cold, efficient dismantling. Han Li, with his formation and Qu Hun's relentless support, systematically overwhelms a Core Formation elder of the Six Serenity Hall. The chapter delivers a brutal, satisfying kill, but it's the aftermath that truly defines the Mortal Stream: a frantic escape, a half-month of silent paranoia on the ocean floor, and ultimately, a single, quiet moment of revelation that changes the game entirely.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Assassination Combination:** Han Li, Qu Hun, and the pre-set water formation strike in a coordinated sequence. The formation slows Elder Gu, Qu Hun's attacks harass him, and Han Li uses a tactical feint with his silver sword to clear a path for his hidden Flying Needles, the chapter's true murder weapon.
- **A Core Formation Cultivator's Downfall:** Elder Gu, already weakened from his previous clash in the Teleportation Hall, is caught off guard by the layered assault. His protective "Hunyuan Bowl" is exhausted, leaving him vulnerable. Han Li exploits this, piercing the defense with blood-light and Flying Needles before decapitating the elder in a clean, silent stroke.
- **The Paranoia Protocol:** Immediately after the kill, Han Li's first actions are a textbook example of Mortal Stream survival: looting the corpse, retrieving his assets, fleeing a short distance, then plunging into the deep sea to hide beneath the red gauze that severs his aura. This sets up the tense cat-and-mouse game that follows.
- **The Half-Month Vigil:** A furious divine sense—likely Wu Chou's master—searches the area relentlessly for days. Han Li remains hidden, suppressing his breath with the precision of a hunted animal, waiting out the storm. The elder's eventual failure to find them highlights Han Li's mastery of concealment.
- **Revelation of the Spoils:** After emerging, Han Li finally inspects Elder Gu's storage pouch. The haul is immense: dozens of mid-grade spirit stones, a Talisman Treasure, an unknown high-grade talisman, a grade-5 demonic beast core, and—most importantly—five Drop Dust Pills. The chapter ends with Han Li in stunned silence, realizing the monumental value of his prize.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Flying Needles (飞针):** These are a class of magical tools, particularly infamous in the cultivation world as "yin weapons." They are small, fast, and often poisonous, designed for stealth and assassination. In the Mortal Stream, using such tools is a mark of pragmatism over honor—a cultivator who employs them is a survivor, not a duelist. The "yin" in their name often associates them with demonic or heterodox paths, but as Han Li demonstrates, utility transcends sectarian branding.
- **Talisman Treasures (符宝) and High-Grade Talismans:** A Talisman Treasure is a portion of a real magical treasure’s power sealed into a talisman. It is a one-time or limited-use asset, often created by dying Core Formation elders as a final act of legacy. The chapter mentions a golden sword Talisman Treasure and an unknown blue flood dragon talisman. In the deadly economics of xianxia, these are not just powerful weapons; they are irreplaceable strategic assets. The blue flood dragon talisman, being an unknown high-grade commodity, could be a trump card of immense value, a parallel to Han Li's own golden short sword.
- **Drop Dust Pills (降尘丹):** This is the pivotal item. As detailed in *The Alchemy Manual*, this is no ordinary elixir. It is a legendary Core Formation pill, a substance that can shatter the bottleneck for a Foundation Establishment cultivator on the cusp of the next great realm. A single pill would be a treasure worth killing for. Han Li has just acquired *five*. This is not an accomplishment; it is a seismic shift in his entire trajectory, a sudden injection of hope into a path that was previously blocked by impossible scarcity. The "Dust" in its name likely alludes to the settling of worldly desires or the stilling of turbulent inner qi, a poetic metaphor for the calm before the storm of transformation.

## Reading Guide
- **A Triumph without Emotion:** Notice how Han Li’s victory is utterly devoid of heroism. The narration doesn't revel in the kill. It presents the decapitation as a *process*—the final, logical step in a tactical sequence. His mind is already on the loot and the escape before the blood even settles. This is the purest expression of the Mortal Stream's "cold, utilitarian gaze."
- **The Weight of the Prize:** The final paragraph is the true climax. Han Li's stunned silence at the sight of the Drop Dust Pill is a rare, genuine emotional breakthrough for him. Do not mistake this for simple greed. This is the moment a possible future, previously locked behind an impossible gate, suddenly swings open. It is the reward for the paranoia, the planning, and the risk. The chapter’s real power lies in this quiet, powerful revelation.
- **The Faint Echo of Danger:** The appearance of the angry divine sense serves as a constant, invisible threat. It reinforces that this is not a clean victory. Han Li has burned his bridges, lost his secure base on Kui Xing Island, and made powerful enemies. This windfall does not solve his problems; it simply gives him the resources to face a new, more dangerous chapter of his life on the run.
