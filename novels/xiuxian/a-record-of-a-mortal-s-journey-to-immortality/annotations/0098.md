## Chapter Overview
The dust settles on Han Li’s Seven Mysteries Sect days as he embarks on the journey home. This chapter is a quiet, deeply human breather before the next arc of the cultivation world. We check in on the fates of those he left behind—Sect Leader Wang, whose thrifty use of a jade bottle of miraculous pills keeps him alive through countless gang wars, and Li Feiyu, who receives a silent parting gift and a hand-drawn smile. Meanwhile, Han Li travels by carriage with Qu Hun and the Cloud-Wing Bird, his mind fixed on one thing only: seeing his family one last time before he disappears into the wider world. There are no explosions, no spells, and no blade duels—just the quiet weight of a goodbye.

## Key Plot Points
- **The Legacy of Nourishing Elixir**: Sect Leader Wang Juechu survives multiple life-threatening injuries thanks to the three remaining pills. The jade bottle’s last contents later spark conflict among his descendants, a grim reminder that any treasure—even a healing pill—carries a bloody price.
- **Li Feiyu’s Farewell**: Returning to his room, Li Feiyu finds Han Li’s parting gift: a stash of carefully prepared medicine and a note. The note—brief, warm, and pragmatic—contains a drawing of Han Li’s smiling face and his wish for Li Feiyu’s marriage. Li Feiyu’s frantic climb and long vigil on the peak confirm the depth of their bond, but also his acceptance of Han Li’s departure.
- **The Road Home**: Han Li charters a carriage for a five-day journey. He has arranged everything with clinical efficiency: paying three taels, choosing a taciturn driver, and keeping Qu Hun and the Cloud-Wing Bird as his quiet companions. His only desire is to lay eyes on his parents and siblings before he leaves—perhaps forever.
- **A Rare Glimpse of Emotion**: As Han Li drifts toward sleep, he recalls his younger sister’s voice calling “Fourth Brother.” The memory is soft, fleeting, and deeply human—a rare crack in the pragmatic shell. The chapter ends with him standing at the edge of his childhood village, heart pounding.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **Nourishing Elixir (养精丹)**: This is one of the first examples of a healing pill whose value far exceeds its apparent function. In *RMJI*, even a simple “life-saving pill” can become a treasure worth killing over. The fact that only three are left, and they still cause a blood feud later, illustrates the core xianxia principle: **any resource that can extend survival is an invitation to conflict**. It also mirrors the “chicken rib” (鸡肋) logic—too valuable to discard, too scarce to share.
- **The Silent Farewell**: Han Li’s method of leaving—a note, medicine, a drawing—is a signature of his character. He does not engage in tearful goodbyes or emotional speeches. His affection is expressed through **practical care** (medicine to extend life) and **understated warmth** (a stick-figure smile). This is the Mortal Stream’s version of brotherhood: you don’t say “I love you,” you leave a bottle of pills and a joke about having children.
- **Sleeping Like a Child**: The narration explicitly compares Han Li’s restful sleep to the security of childhood. This is a deliberate contrast to the hyper-vigilance of his cultivation life. It signals that Han Li’s “home” is not merely a place, but a mental state of safety that he has not felt since leaving his village. The image of parents “driving away mosquitoes” is a tender, grounded detail that anchors his humanity before the story pivots back to the cold calculus of the cultivation world.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a **transition** and a **breather**, not a cliffhanger. Don’t expect action or philosophy. Instead, pay attention to what Han Li *chooses* not to do: he doesn’t burn bridges, he doesn’t take revenge on former enemies, and he doesn’t linger. His departure from the Seven Mysteries Sect is clean, quiet, and final—the mark of a man who has already filed the past under “settled accounts.” The real emotional weight is in what he says to himself as he drifts off. The village at the end is not just a destination; it’s a symbol of the risk he is taking by letting his guard down. Keep your eyes open for how this rare moment of vulnerability shapes his decisions once he steps onto the true immortal path.
