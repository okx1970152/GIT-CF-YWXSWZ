## Chapter Overview
Whoa, what a gut punch of a chapter! After the intense action of Li Changlao's detoxification, the story takes a sharp, intimate turn. We see the quiet aftermath in the Li household—a relieved wife arranging a future reward—but the real drama is happening far away, under a big tree. This chapter delivers a masterclass in the contrast between Han Li's cold pragmatism and Li Feiyu's burning passion. It's a heart-to-heart that peels back the layers of our two favorite survivors, exposing the raw, painful truth about Li Feiyu's timeline and the ticking clock of his forbidden pill addiction. Get ready, because things are about to get real between these brothers-in-arms.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Aftermath:** Li Shi finds the poison completely gone from her husband, confirming the successful cure. Her gratitude is immediate, but Han Li has already slipped away like smoke, leaving only a recovery prescription and no chance for a reward.
*   **The Summons:** Li Feiyu, summoned by Han Li’s blatant wink, ambushes him at a secluded spot. The playful roughness between them feels like a fragile normality.
*   **The Cruel Question:** Han Li cuts through the banter and asks the core question that's been haunting the chapter: “Does Zhang Xiuer know you only have a few years left?”
*   **The Shattered Mask:** Li Feiyu’s face goes bloodless. His silence confirms everything. This is the first time someone has forced him to face the existential reality of his relationship in the harsh light of his own impending end.
*   **The Explosion:** Li Feiyu reacts not with denial, but with a chilling, possessive declaration. He knows he can’t have Zhang Xiuer forever, but for as long as he draws breath, no other man will touch her. The raw, desperate ownership is both terrifying and tragic.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
*   **"Carp Leap" (鲤鱼打挺):** This is a classic kung fu movie move. It's not just a way to stand up; it signifies agility, martial training, and a readiness to fight. Han Li using it to spring up from the ground subtly reinforces that he’s always in a state of martial preparedness, even when relaxing.
*   **Marrow-Extracting Pill (抽髓丸):** The pill is a foundational curse in this world. A forbidden alchemical product that grants immense temporary power by drawing vital life essence. It is the ultimate symbol of the Mortal Stream's Faustian bargain—you can gain strength *now*, but you are literally paying with your remaining years. Li Feiyu’s fate is a grim bill that has just come due.

## Reading Guide
This is a crucial character episode. Forget the monsters and the magic for a minute—the real battle here is emotional. Pay close attention to Han Li’s role. He isn't being a hero; he’s being a brutally pragmatic friend. He knows that Li Feiyu’s romance is a bright flame that will consume him from within, and he’s trying to snuff it out to grant him a less painful end. Li Feiyu's response is pure Mortal Stream survival instinct: he can’t change his fate, but he can fiercely claim what’s his for the time he has left. This episode sets up a painful, inevitable reckoning. The chapter doesn't resolve the problem; it just screams from the rooftops that it *is* a problem, and it hurts. It's this kind of character-driven tragedy in a world of backstabbing cultivators that makes *A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality* so compelling.
