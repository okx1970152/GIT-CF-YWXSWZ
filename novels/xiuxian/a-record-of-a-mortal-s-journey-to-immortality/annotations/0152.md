## Chapter Overview

Two years have slipped by in Yellow Maple Valley, and Han Li has been doing what Han Li does best: keeping his head down, consuming pills like candy, and quietly breaking through to the eleventh layer of the Eternal Spring Art. But this isn't just a peaceful time-skip—it's a masterclass in the Mortal Stream philosophy of strategic invisibility. While the sect is captivated by a dazzling batch of new geniuses (including twins with the rare Lightning Spiritual Root and a kid with the legendary Profound Yin Eyes), our protagonist disappears into the background like a ghost tending his herb garden. The chapter also delivers a satisfying dose of schadenfreude: that reneging Hall Master Ye? His favorite grand-nephew failed Foundation Establishment. Sweet, sweet karma. But beneath the calm surface, Han Li is already planning his next move—the pills that carried him this far are failing, and he needs new formulas. The stage is quietly set for the next phase of his climb.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li examines his haul from the Foundation Establishment cultivator's inheritance: two mid-grade spirit stones (fire and earth) and three high-quality magic tools—a binding ring, a mist banner, and a brass bottle that preserves spiritual energy. He tests the brass bottle on the green liquid but finds it only extends preservation from 15 minutes to one day, confirming the liquid is more than mere condensed spiritual energy.
- He shelves the mystery and focuses on mass-producing Yellow Dragon Pills and Golden Marrow Pills, consuming them in huge quantities to compensate for their diminishing returns. After two years, his Eternal Spring Art reaches the eleventh layer—mid-tier among low-grade disciples—but the pills finally stop working entirely.
- Han Li decides to lie low for a few more years before seeking new pill formulas, aware that openly inquiring would invite deadly suspicion. He becomes a model steward for the herb garden, earning a raise to five low-grade spirit stones per month from Senior Martial Uncle Ma.
- The sect conducts a decennial recruitment, bringing in over a thousand new disciples. Among them are standout geniuses: Lightning Spiritual Root twins, an eleven-year-old at the ninth-layer peak without pills, and a child with Profound Yin Eyes. The strongest sect, Moon-Masking, even finds a Heavenly Spiritual Root girl, deflating Yellow Maple Valley's pride.
- Han Li is completely forgotten in the excitement—exactly as he wants. He learns that Hall Master Ye's grand-nephew failed Foundation Establishment, and he allows himself a rare, quiet laugh.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Mid-Grade Spirit Stones & Magic Tools.** In cultivation fiction, spirit stones are the universal currency and energy source, graded by purity. Mid-grade stones emit a strong, attribute-visible spiritual wave, while low-grade stones are nearly colorless. The three tools Han Li receives—a binding ring, a mist banner, and a preservation bottle—illustrate the classic utility spectrum of xianxia items: offensive, defensive, and support. The brass bottle's inability to hold the green liquid hints that the Heaven Vial's product operates on a plane beyond ordinary spiritual energy, possibly involving time or law-level principles.

**Genius Prodigies & the Talent Hierarchy.** This chapter introduces a cascade of special aptitudes that define the xianxia class system. The Lightning Spiritual Root is a mutated variation that grants exceptional destructive power but is still a "mutant" rather than a pure Heavenly Root. The Profound Yin Eyes are a rare physique allowing the user to cultivate a lost glare technique that suppresses ghosts—a throwback to Chinese folklore where certain "yin bodies" have innate spiritual sight. The Heavenly Spiritual Root (single-element, perfect for cultivation) is the crown jewel, and Moon-Masking Sect snatching one is a political blow to Yellow Maple Valley. All this talent serves as a narrative foil: Han Li has none of it, yet he outlasts them by patience and resource management.

### Marrow-Extracting Pill

A forbidden pill overdrafts life force for temporary power, leading to death within ten years; no longer active in this timeline.

## Reading Guide

This is a "breather" chapter that rewards patient readers with worldbuilding, setup, and a satisfying payoff of earlier threads. If you've been rooting for Han Li's quiet revenge on Hall Master Ye, this is your snack. But pay attention to the brass bottle test—it's a rare moment where the novel openly acknowledges that the Heaven Vial is not fully understood, even by the protagonist. The chapter also reinforces a core theme: the cultivation world's obsession with "talent" is often a distraction. While everyone gawks at the new prodigies, Han Li is methodically building his real advantage: a perfect cover for accelerated herb cultivation, a stable income, and zero notoriety. The next crisis will come not from being noticed, but from the pills running out. Get ready for the hunt for new recipes.
