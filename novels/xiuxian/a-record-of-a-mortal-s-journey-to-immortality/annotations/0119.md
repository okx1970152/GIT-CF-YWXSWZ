## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists! In this chapter, our pragmatic protagonist makes a predictably calculated move, offering a single, limited strike to the Mo family women—a surgical intervention that won’t stir up too much trouble. But the real action is happening off-screen. Han Li returns to his inn to find his eager gangland informant, Sun Ergou, bursting with a wild rumor: “immortals” are gathering near Jiayuan City for a so-called “Immortal Assembly.” For Han Li, this is the first genuine lead he’s ever had to the wider cultivation world. The chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s love for narrative tension through intelligence gathering: a single overheard conversation changes everything.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li lays out his terms to the Mo family women: he will make one of their two opposing gang leaders “disappear,” but only one. He argues that eliminating both dominant powers would draw too much dangerous attention and is not worth the risk.
- The women are surprised but remain cautious, asking for a day to deliberate. Han Li grants them until the next morning before leaving, showing he is willing to be patient but not indefinite.
- Upon returning to his inn, Han Li is met by Sun Ergou, who has been anxiously waiting with urgent news.
- Sun Ergou reports that a large group of “immortals” (cultivators) is about to gather near Jiayuan City. He claims this information is reliable, sourced from a drunken high-ranking member of the Siping Gang.
- The story behind the news: a gang member fleeing pursuers hid in a tree hollow and, using a “Tortoise Breath Art,” pretended to be dead. While doing so, he overheard a young couple riding a two-headed eagle discussing the “Immortal Assembly.”
- Han Li is internally ecstatic at the prospect of finally connecting with the wider cultivation world but maintains an impassive facade, grinding Sun Ergou for location and time details.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The **Immortal Assembly** (神仙会) is a classic trope in xianxia fiction. It functions as a narrative device to gather many cultivation factions in one place, often for competition, trade, or the announcement of a major event. For a solitary cultivator like Han Li, it is the holy grail of information—a chance to network, learn about resources, and grasp the lay of the land. The fact that he hears about it by chance, through a chain of mortal gossip, perfectly illustrates the Mortal Stream’s emphasis on luck and opportunism. A single overheard conversation can be a bigger boon than a year of dedicated training.

The **Tortoise Breath Art** (龟息功) is another Jianghu staple. It mimics a state of hibernation, slowing the user’s heart and breath to appear dead. It is often used for stealth or recovery, but here it serves as a clever plot device for eavesdropping. The twist—that the user only realizes the source of the conversation as he loses consciousness—is a perfect dose of the novel’s signature “grinding your teeth in regret” realism. You don’t get the full scoop for free; the universe always exacts a price for knowledge.

### Social Tribulation

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a textbook example of **Social Tribulation** (社会试炼), a core Mortal Stream concept. Han Li is not fighting a monster or a powerful cultivator here; his battlefield is a negotiation table where he must manage the expectations of desperate mortal women while limiting his own liabilities. His offer is cold, precise, and full of risk calculation: he gives a concession (help) but wraps it in a boundary (only one gang) that protects him from secondary consequences. This is the same kind of strategic thinking he applies to cultivation, and it is a delight to watch.

The second half of the chapter shifts to the classic Mortal Stream model of **Information as Power**. The entire narrative hinges on a rumor passed from a drunken official to a lowly gang member to an informant to Han Li. The story’s credibility is established through a layered narrative (eyewitness account, the witness’s clever escape, the subsequent gag order), which gives the reader a satisfying sense of puzzle-solving. Pay attention to how Han Li reacts: he is excited, but he does not leap at the chance blindly. His first concern is the time and place, which will form the basis of his next risk assessment. The cold, calculating journey is still on.
