## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to another chapter where our favorite paranoid pill-gatherer, Han Li, does what he does best: clean house and get the heck out of dodge. This chapter is a tight, efficient sequence of tactical murder, logistical confirmation, and a heavy dose of that classic *Mortal Stream* anxiety. The long-awaited teleportation formation is nearly complete! But as Han Li knows all too well, in this world, a sip of good luck is always chased by a gallon of bad. The chapter masterfully juxtaposes Han Li’s cold, professional violence with his growing internal dread over the ticking bomb of the Wind Spirit Restriction planted by the terrifying Feng Xi. It’s a short, sharp chapter that moves the pieces into place for the next major arc, proving once again that in the cultivation world, bureaucracy is just another battlefield, and patience is a weapon.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Island Hit:** Han Li and Madam Fan arrive at the island sheltering the teleportation formation. Han Li, impatient with Madam Fan’s speed, has been carrying her along. Action first, questions later.
*   **The Purge Begins:** Madam Fan calls out her two loyalists from the hidden cave. Han Li, without a moment’s hesitation, executes the third person—a blue-robed Foundation Establishment elder of the demonic path—with a single, casual sword flick. The power gap is absolute.
*   **Formation Status Check:** The duo and the two surviving women enter the underground hall. Han Li quickly dispatches the remaining five demonic path cultivators. The formation is 80–90% complete, needing only the ‘Dream Stone’ component. This is a direct greenlight for Han Li’s next move.
*   **The Physics of Teleportation:** Madam Fan confirms the other end of the formation is on Imperial Bright Island, a medium-sized island with an ancient one-way teleportation array. This reassures Han Li that there won’t be a nest of enemies waiting for him, a crucial risk calculation.
*   **The Unspoken Clock:** With the immediate obstacle cleared, Han Li’s mind turns back to his own impending doom. The Wind Spirit Restriction from Feng Xi hasn’t dissipated. He tried using the Gold Devourer Beetles to eat it, but failed. The chapter ends with that familiar *Mortal Stream* tightness in the gut—the feeling of running out of time.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Waiting Game of the Wind Spirit Restriction:** This chapter provides a masterclass in a core xianxia horror: the inability to act. The “Wind Spirit Restriction” is an energy lock planted by a vastly superior cultivator. It’s not a poison; it’s a leash. The fact that it can be triggered at any moment is a psychological weapon in itself. Han Li’s failed attempt to use his own trump cards (the Gold Devourer Beetles and the Evil-Repelling Divine Lightning) to remove it is a classic *Mortal Stream* moment. It reinforces the brutal system: even your most powerful assets have specific, counterable uses, and a curse from a higher realm cannot be solved with brute-force cleverness. You must simply wait, calculate, and hope the enemy makes a mistake.

**The One-Way Teleportation Trap:** The mention of Imperial Bright Island’s one-way-only teleportation formation is a beautiful piece of world-building. In many fantasy settings, teleportation is a simple "point A to point B" button. Here, it’s a physics constraint with strategic implications. It explains why the demonic path doesn’t use it for mass invasion. For Han Li, it’s a calculated risk: a one-way ticket means no easy retreat, but also guarantees the other side isn’t using it as a chokepoint for a counter-attack. Every layer of the world is designed around risk-reward logic, not convenience.

*【Deep Dive: The Psychology of the “Killing Chicken with a Dragon-Slaying Knife”】*
> Madam Fan’s flattery—“killing a chicken with a dragon-slaying knife”—is a common idiom in Chinese fiction. On the surface, she’s saying Han Li’s power is wasted on such weaklings. But on a deeper level, it’s a test. She is measuring his patience and his ego. A proud cultivator might scoff, “You dare use such a rude idiom for my actions?” Han Li’s lack of reaction—immediately turning his focus to the formation—is the correct *Mortal Stream* response. He does not take bait meant to puff him up or knock him down. He just takes the data and moves on.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the "calm before the storm" execution phase. The storm, of course, being Yun Tianxiao’s arrival. The key to enjoying this section is to see it not as filler, but as a critical logistical operation. Pay attention to:
1.  **Efficiency as a Character Trait:** Han Li doesn’t waste a single word. He doesn’t gloat. He kills the first guy before he can even speak. This isn’t just speed; it’s a strategic doctrine. Information is currency, and you don’t let the enemy spend theirs.
2.  **Madam Fan’s Role:** She is the perfect *Mortal Stream* ally. She knows her limits, she provides valuable assets (the formation), and she doesn’t flinch at Han Li’s methods. She is using him as much as he is using her, and both parties know it. That’s the foundation of a trustworthy relationship in this world.
3.  **The Unseen Threat:** The best part of the chapter is Han Li’s returning anxiety about the Wind Spirit Restriction. It reminds us that his victory here is just a small battle in a much larger, more terrifying war. The chapter ends with a rising sense of dread. That feeling is the real protagonist of the story. Get ready, because the countdown is on.
