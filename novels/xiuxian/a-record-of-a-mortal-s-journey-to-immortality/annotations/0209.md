## Chapter Overview
Han Li is out of the underground swamp, and he’s *packing*. The chapter delivers the denouement of the trial zone’s escape sequence, showing the aftermath of his desperate union with Nangong Wan, the cost of his freedom, and his return to the cutthroat competitive arena of the outer world. The magic of the temporary power boost is fading, the Taboo Treasure is spent, and all Han Li has left is his cold calculation and a lifetime’s worth of debt to a woman he’ll likely never see again. Meanwhile, back at the assembly point, the sect elders are doing their own frantic calculations as the Moon-Masking Sect fails to appear, and our boy slips back into the herd, armed with nothing but secrets and new scars.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li’s escape from the underground swamp is complete, achieved through a desperate, symbiotic exchange of power with Nangong Wan, who used a secret method to temporarily transfer her sealed power to him, pushing him to the peak of the thirteenth layer. In the process, her Golden Light Brick Talisman Treasure was completely spent, and she lost twenty to thirty years of her cultivation base.
- The temporary power boost, a gift of the Maiden Rebirth Art’s peculiar mechanics, will dissipate within hours, leaving Han Li with only the memory of that strength—and the harsh reminder of the gap between him and her.
- As Han Li journeys to the exit, he maintains his ruthless efficiency, silently killing a Spirit Beast Mountain disciple and looting his storage pouch in a blink-and-you-miss-it moment.
- The sect elders outside the forbidden land reopen the passage on the fifth day, and survivors begin to trickle out, including the Chen siblings, Zhong Wu, and the blue-clad youth. The tally of survivors shows Yellow Maple Valley in a strong position with five disciples.
- The Masked Moon Sect’s total absence as the deadline approaches creates a wave of anxiety among its elders, Grandmaster Qiong and Fairy Ni Chang, while the other sects watch with barely concealed satisfaction.
- Han Li emerges, looking pristine, and sits down among his fellow Yellow Maple Valley survivors. His intact state and eleventh-layer cultivation invite only contempt from those who assume he spent the entire trial hiding.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Price of Power Transfer (法力渡送)**: This chapter introduces a unique mechanic tied to the “Maiden Rebirth Art.” The lore states that during the “reincarnation cycle” of the technique, the practitioner can transfer their sealed power to a male partner, but cannot unseal it for themselves or transfer it to a woman. This is a fascinating variation on the familiar xianxia trope of dual cultivation or power sharing—it’s not a free boost, but a parasitic act of giving, with the giver taking a massive hit to their long-term foundation. The “twenty to thirty years of cultivation” lost by Nangong Wan is a staggering price, reminding us that any shortcut in this world has a brutal cost.

**The Talisman Treasure’s End (符宝威能耗尽)**: The Golden Light Brick, which served as Han Li’s ultimate trump card, is now a “useless scrap of paper.” This reinforces a key rule of the cultivation world: high-power items are consumable, a final legacy from a dying elder, not an infinite resource. A single use of breaking through a wall cost the entire treasure. This is why Taboo Treasures are hoarded and treated with such reverence—once they’re gone, they’re *gone*.

**The Arithmetic of Survivors**: The scene at the exit is a stark illustration of the trial’s systemic brutality. Yellow Maple Valley emerged with five survivors, far eclipsing the Giant Sword Sect’s paltry two. The casual glances of contempt at Han Li demonstrate the social hierarchy of the trial: those who fought are respected, while the “survivors” are suspected of cowardice. The real battle is over who gets the Foundation Establishment Pill, and the counting has already begun.

## Reading Guide
*Hello again, fellow survivors!* This chapter is the breather after the storm, but don’t let the calm fool you—Han Li’s head is spinning with the implications of his little detour. First, the good news: he’s alive, he’s out, and he has a name: Nangong Wan. The bad news? The power boost is fading, his ultimate trump card is spent, and he owes a debt he can never repay to a woman from a completely different league. This is a classic Mortal Stream tension: Han Li has gained *knowledge* and a *connection*, but both are assets he can’t leverage right now. What he can do is play the return game perfectly—slip back into the ranks, let everyone assume he was a coward, and keep his mouth shut. The real prize of this trial wasn’t the herbs or the pills; it was the secrets he now carries. Pay attention to the subtle anxiety of the sect elders—the formal competition is over, but the power struggle between the Seven Sects is just getting started.
