## Chapter Overview
Four years have passed, and our boy Han Li has officially—and quite publicly—failed his first attempt at Core Formation. But if you thought he’d be moping around, you’ve forgotten which story you’re reading. While *he* couldn’t quite make the leap, his soulless puppet-brother Qu Hun did the unthinkable: he formed a Demonic Core in the most spiritually barren corner of the Chaotic Star Sea. The chapter opens with a full-blown celestial spectacle—black clouds, thunder, a giant spiritual Qi vortex—that announces to every cultivator on Sea Ape Island that a new senior has been born. Han Li, ever the pragmatist, thwarts the inevitable social approach with a single icy threat, then immediately ghosts the island. The real prize isn't the failed memory of his own cultivation, but the successful tactical asset he now commands. And just when you think he's home free, he returns to Little Huan Island... only to find his secret bug basement completely untouched, yet somehow deeply, deeply unsettling. Get ready for a quiet victory lap with a killer punchline.

## Key Plot Points
- **Han Li's failed breakthrough**: After three years of trying, he admits defeat. No true yuan congeals, no Core forms. He stops cultivating and pivots entirely to supporting Qu Hun.
- **Qu Hun's Demonic Core success**: Using the Blood Coagulation Five Elements Pill and a rapid-demon technique (Blood Refining Divine Light), Qu Hun forms a "Demonic Core" in days—a faster, dirtier path to Core Formation than the orthodox Golden Core route.
- **Global spiritual anomaly**: The Qi vortex and thunder show on Sea Ape Island alarms the locals and draws a crowd. Han Li's single-line warning ("Whoever approaches… shall be killed!") asserts immediate dominance.
- **Strategic retreat**: Han Li immediately abandons the island and heads for Little Huan Island to retrieve his belongings, planning a full escape from the southwestern sea region.
- **Qu Hun’s loadout**: He's now equipped with the Chaotic Yuan Bowl (a real magic treasure) plus two talisman treasures—golden sword and golden skull—giving him enough punch to threaten other Core Formation cultivators.
- **The unsettling return**: Little Huan Island's formations are intact. The cave is undisturbed. But Han Li freezes at the sight of the bug chamber, hinting at new danger.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Core Formation (结丹)** is the paradigmatic bottleneck in xianxia. It marks the moment when a cultivator's liquid qi is compressed into a solid, spherical core—the first major qualitative leap in life-form advancement. In orthodox cultivation, this is achieved through perfecting techniques (like the Azure Origin Sword Art's seventh layer) and requires immense internal pressure. The "bottleneck" here is not a wall you break with a shout; it's a structural barrier. Han Li's failure is a feature, not a bug: it reinforces the Mortal Stream's rule that raw talent and persistent effort aren't always enough.

**Demonic Core (煞丹)** vs **Golden Core (金丹)**: This distinction is core to the novel's dual-path logic. A Golden Core is refined through purification and harmony; a Demonic Core is forged by force-feeding demonic medicines and using rapid techniques. It's faster, cheaper—but comes with hidden costs (alignment, future bottlenecks). Qu Hun's success with the Demonic Core is a tactical victory for Han Li, but the narrative whispers that shortcuts always carry interest.

**The Qi Vortex phenomenon**: In xianxia cosmology, forming a Core (whether golden or demonic) creates a visible disruption because the cultivator is essentially sucking the world's spiritual energy dry to power the transformation. The 5-*li* vortex screams to every nearby cultivator: *a senior is being born*. This is why secluded cultivation sites are so valuable—privacy is a premium that money can't always buy.

## Reading Guide

That's peak Mortal Stream resourcefulness—turning your own setback into a strength multiplier. Don't brush past his four years of quiet acceptance either; he took the L gracefully, silently pivoted, and made it work. Also, *that final scene*. The bug chamber is *completely* intact, and his face freezes. Something is *very* wrong. Are we watching the calm before the storm, or an ambush that's already been laid? Read on—but lock your formations first.
