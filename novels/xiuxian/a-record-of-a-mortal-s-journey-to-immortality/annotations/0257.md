## Chapter Overview

Alright, fellow cultivators, grab some tea—because this chapter is a *double-header*. On one side, we’re back in the trading trenches with Han Li, where he accidentally snags a formula that would make any spirit-beast tamer weep with joy (and any smart alchemist weep at the ingredient list). On the flip side, the Yan Clan is thrown into a full-blown existential crisis. A secret family council reveals that the Six Demonic Sects are breathing down their necks, and a marriage alliance with the Ghost Spirit Sect might be their only ticket to survival. It’s a classic Mortal Stream move: while Han Li’s playing the long game of resource accumulation, the big geopolitical gears are grinding, and the outcome will affect everyone.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li participates in a small-scale trading meet at Yanling Fort, where he successfully trades several talismans for a rare herb needed for his Focused Qi Pill, albeit one that needs accelerated growth.
- He attempts to trade for two other rare ingredients and any elixir formulas, but gets no takers—until the eccentric Daoist, Wuyouzi, unexpectedly offers an ancient formula.
- The formula is for the “Spirit Nourishing Pill,” a pill that can significantly boost a spirit beast’s rank over time. Han Li quickly realizes why others passed it up: the ingredients are astronomically rare, making the formula a “chicken rib” for ordinary cultivators.
- Undeterred, Han Li snaps it up with ten low-grade superior talismans, recognizing its immense potential value for his own long-term plans.
- Meanwhile, a secret Yan Clan council reveals the Ghost Spirit Sect’s generous yet binding proposal: marriage of their prodigy, Yan’er, to the Sect’s Young Leader, with the child inheriting leadership of both families.
- The Yan Family Ancestor drops a *second* bombshell: the Six Demonic Sects will invade Yue in five days, and the Righteous Alliance of Fengdu Nation is either unable or unwilling to help.
- The clan’s strategist, Xuanye, coolly dissects the situation with three sharp questions, exposing the hidden calculations behind the Ghost Spirit Sect’s offer.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Chicken Rib” (鸡肋) Economy:** This chapter is a perfect demonstration of the “chicken rib” principle. The Spirit Nourishing Pill formula is technically priceless—it can make a beast stronger over time. But the ingredients are so rare and valuable that any ordinary cultivator would rather use them for their own advancement. The formula is “tasteless to eat, yet a pity to throw away.” Only someone like Han Li, with a secret cultivation accelerator (the Heaven Vial) and a long-term vision for a trump card beast, can turn this rib into a feast. This is a core *Mortal Stream* dynamic: the value of a resource is entirely dependent on the cultivator’s unique circumstances and strategic assets.

**The Geopolitics of the Six Demonic Sects:** The sudden fall of Jiang and Juche nations mirrors the brutal efficiency of a dark forest system. When the balance of power shifts, there is no time for hesitation. The Righteous Alliance of Fengdu Nation is conspicuously absent, suggesting a deeper political game. This reinforces the *Mortal Stream* theme that individual survival is always at the mercy of macro-level power struggles. The Yan Clan’s dilemma is not just about a marriage alliance; it’s about choosing survival under a new predator's shadow.

### Chicken Rib (鸡肋)

A classical Chinese idiom describing something ‘tasteless to eat, yet a pity to discard’; in cultivation fiction, it refers to knowledge or resources that are valuable in theory but useless without the means to apply them.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in how *Mortal Stream* pacing works. Han Li’s trading scene feels like a slow, methodical victory—he *loses* the immediate prize (the rare ingredients he wanted) but *wins* a long-term strategic asset (the formula). It reinforces his core philosophy: don’t panic when the obvious path is blocked; look for the hidden value others have discarded.

Then, the brutal switch to the Yan Clan council is a classic *mortal stream* storytelling tool. We get a massive lore dump not through dry exposition, but through a tense political negotiation. The news of the invasion is a hammer blow, immediately elevating the stakes. Notice how the Yan Clan’s internal debate isn’t about honor or loyalty; it’s about **cold survival calculus**. The Ancestor frames the choice as an interest-based decision, and the strategist Xuanye immediately hones in on the gaps in the Ghost Spirit Sect’s story. It’s a perfect parallel to how Han Li would think, just at a different scale.

Pay close attention to the “Blood Spirit Art” and the inheritance clause. This is not a simple marriage; it’s a *merger* of two power structures. The Ghost Spirit Sect isn’t just looking for an alliance—they’re building a dynastic bridgehead in the Yue nation’s cultivation world.
