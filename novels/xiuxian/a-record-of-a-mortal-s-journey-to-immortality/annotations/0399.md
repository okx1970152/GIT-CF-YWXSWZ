## Chapter Overview
Han Li finally emerges from his island-sized shopping spree and takes the plunge—he’s heading out to sea to hunt demonic beasts. But first, he needs a bigger bag, better intel, and a ride to the right island. Little does he know, the *real* hunt is just beginning. The world is about to get a whole lot bigger, and a whole lot more dangerous.

## Key Plot Points
- **Stocking Up:** Han Li spends a month gathering data on outer-sea demonic beasts and buys two massive storage pouches (total capacity ten times his old ones) for over 1,000 spirit stones. The methodical preparation phase is in full swing.
- **The Temptation of the Auction:** He passes the Fengle Auction House and peeks at the catalog—grade 5-6 demonic cores, rare forging materials, treasures he’s never even heard of. But the prices are beyond his reach, and he wisely decides not to sell his thousand-year herbs to avoid drawing attention.
- **The Big Step:** Half a year later, Han Li and Qu Hun are at the Star Void Hall, ready to use the public teleportation arrays. The hall is a hub connecting to countless islands—a miniature map of the Starfall Sea’s logistics network.
- **The Coincidental Party:** A group of five cultivators (including a Core Formation duo) invites them to share the teleportation fee. Han Li, always practical, accepts. Their destination: Jade Emerald Island.
- **The Warning from a Stranger:** Before leaving, the old Core Formation cultivator offers a cryptic warning: on the Beast Islands, beware not just of the beasts, but of the *other cultivators*. A classic Mortal Stream insight—the most dangerous predator is often human.
- **First Impressions:** They arrive on a crude island outpost. The “street” is a ramshackle collection of stone and wooden huts, a far cry from Heaven Star City’s splendor. The local Star Palace official doesn’t even acknowledge them.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- **The Great Filter:** This chapter highlights the immense economic stratification of the cultivation world. Han Li, with his thousands of spirit stones, is *rich* by Qi Condensation standards, yet completely locked out of the top-tier auction. The gap between a mid-tier cultivator and a Core Formation powerhouse isn’t just about realm—it’s about access to resources. This is the system working as designed.
- **Logistics as Lore:** World-building in *RMJI* is often done through shopping. The fact that Han Li spent a month studying bestiary records, bought bigger pouches, and carefully planned his route isn’t filler—it’s the core narrative of a survivalist. Power isn’t just about cultivation; it’s about preparation.
- **The Beast Islands Economy:** Jade Emerald Island is a frontier outpost. The shabby huts, the lack of formality, and the cold shoulder from the Star Palace official all point to a place where the rule of law is thin. This is a resource-extraction colony, and the only law is the one you bring with you—or enforce yourself.

## Reading Guide
Welcome to the *real* Starfall Sea. The next arc isn’t about studying in a safe sect or scrounging for pills; it’s about sailing into the unknown to hunt monsters that could swallow you whole. Everything Han Li has prepared so far—the puppets, the talismans, the hidden tricks—is about to be field-tested. And as the old cultivator warned, the monsters are only half the danger. Pay close attention to Han Li’s interactions with new characters; in this world, a smiling face can hide a blade.

## Geopolitical Foreword: A Quick Horizon Scan
For readers new to this stage of the story: the Starfall Sea is an archipelago realm governed loosely by the Star Palace, but the real power on each island belongs to whichever Core Formation cultivator can claim it. There are three main kinds of territory—**Sect Islands** (controlled by the seven great sects), **Clan Islands** (held by immortal families), and **Free Islands** (open season for rogue cultivators). Jade Emerald Island is the latter kind. Which means anyone Han Li meets here is not bound by sect rules—only by their own strength and caution. This chapter, for all its shopping and teleporting, is Han Li’s formal entry into the lawless frontier.
