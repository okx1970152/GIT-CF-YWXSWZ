## Chapter Overview

Alright, fellow cultivators, fasten your seatbelts! The Forbidden Land arc is finally wrapping up, and Chapter 210 delivers the ultimate payoff—not in flashy sword fights, but in the cold, silent language of spirit herbs. We've got high-stakes politicking, a nail-biting wager between Core Formation elders, and the most satisfying humble-brag in xianxia history. If you've been reading for the spectacle, you might miss what's really going on here: this is where Han Li, the ultimate underdog, turns the entire system on its head by doing exactly what the system didn't expect him to do—succeed anyway. The chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream storytelling, where the real battle isn't fought with magic, but with calculation, patience, and a very well-timed storage pouch.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Waiting Game**: As the channel's closing time nears, a palpable tension grips the gathered sects. The Moon-Masking Sect, led by the stunning Nan Gong Wan, makes a dramatic, orderly exit, much to Old Man Qiong's relief and Fairy Ni Chang's emotional delight. The other sects' disappointment is barely concealed.
- **The Slickster's Survival**: Just as the channel is about to seal, Xiang Zhili—Yellow Maple Valley's resident old slickster—staggers out, having abandoned his Greatsword Gate and Daoist partners. His survival at the 10th layer shocks everyone, including his own elder, and sparks a bitter exchange between the Greatsword Gate master and Li Shizu.
- **The Wager's Trial**: Old Man Qiong takes charge, forcing the three betting sects to parade their harvests. Void Spirit Gate's Daoists deliver a stunning opening, with one producing over a dozen high-grade herbs, and another seven. Li Shizu's hopes crumble as the total tips against his favor.
- **The Ultimate Flip**: When all seems lost, and everyone has dismissed Han Li as a non-factor, he steps forward. With a single, unceremonious tip of his storage pouch, he unleashes a pile of over twenty spirit herbs onto the ground, single-handedly snatching victory for Yellow Maple Valley. The chapter ends on this cliffhanger, leaving the reader to savor the shock on the faces of everyone who underestimated him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

For the uninitiated, this chapter's entire climax revolves around the *wager economy* of the cultivation world. These aren't friendly bets; they are resource reallocation exercises between Core Formation elders. The wager's rules—mandated display, public counting, and Scent-Sniffing Beasts to prevent hoarding—mirror real-world bureaucratic checks designed to prevent corruption. But in the Mortal Stream, they serve a darker purpose: they ensure that the established power structure (sects with the most high-tier disciples) is reinforced, and that outliers (like a lowly Qi Condensation disciple) cannot game the system through hidden wealth.

The Scent-Sniffing Beast (嗅灵兽) is a brilliant narrative tool. It's not a pet; it's an institutional audit mechanism. Its presence explains *why* the system works the way it does—success isn't just about getting the herbs; it's about getting them through the check. This reinforces the Mortal Stream principle that the world's systems are designed to control resource flow, not to reward merit. Han Li's triumph isn't just in gathering the herbs, but in successfully passing them *through* the audit without being stripped of them by his own sect.

## Reading Guide

This is a “celebrate the little guy” chapter, but done the Mortal Stream way. There's no dramatic monologue, no heroic declaration. Han Li's victory is a quiet, cold, methodical act. Pay attention to the structure: the entire chapter builds suspense through a series of calculated disappointments (Void Spirit Gate's haul, Xiang Zhili's mediocre contribution) before delivering the payoff. This is textbook Mortal Stream pacing—the tension is in the *numbers*, not the noise. Also, note how Han Li's timing is perfect: he doesn't step forward early, but lets everyone else expose their hands first, then crushes them with his own. That's the mark of a true strategist.

Also, keep an eye on the social dynamics. Li Shizu's contempt for Han Li is palpable; he assumes a low-level disciple is worthless. This isn't just bad leadership; it's a fatal flaw in a Dark Forest world where threat assessment is everything. Han Li survives precisely *because* his own elder underestimates him. The chapter is a quiet, delicious revenge fantasy—not against the enemy, but against the system's own prejudice.

## The Unsung Hero: The Scent-Sniffing Beast

We need to give a shout-out to the unsung MVP of this chapter: the Scent-Sniffing Beast. This adorable, squirrel-like creature is the embodiment of the cultivation world's bureaucratic tyranny. It's not a weapon; it's a *checkpoint*. It ensures that no treasure is smuggled past the gatekeepers who control the resource pipeline. In a world of flying swords and elemental magic, the most terrifying audit is a fluffy nose that can smell your storage pouch from three zhang away. This is peak Mortal Stream worldbuilding: the system is so paranoid, it's built a biological lie detector to catch cheaters. Han Li's triumph is not just about gathering herbs; it's about surviving the *audit*.
