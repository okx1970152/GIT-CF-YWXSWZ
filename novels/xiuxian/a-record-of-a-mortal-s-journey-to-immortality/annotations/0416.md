## Chapter Overview

This chapter picks up in the wake of the Vast Sky Island disaster, offering us a critical behind-the-scenes look at the man who nearly ended our protagonist. We get to see Grand Immortal Profound Yin in his true, scheming form, casually dismissing everyone else as small fish while his eyes lock onto a prize far greater than any bamboo stalk. Meanwhile, Han Li, having escaped by the skin of his teeth, returns to Heaven Star City nursing a bruised ego and a healthy dose of anger. But the Mortal Stream never lets a protagonist sulk in peace. Just as he’s ready to write off the whole expedition, a new lead emerges from an unexpected, and frankly irritating, source. The chapter masterfully switches between high-level villainy and personal setback, before setting up a crucial reunion that promises to change Han Li’s immediate trajectory.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Villain’s Council:** Crimson Flame reports to Grand Immortal Profound Yin, detailing the fates of the trial participants. Profound Yin analyzes the situation, revealing he cares little for the small fry (including Han Li) as long as his main target, the ‘unfilial disciple,’ is secured. This scene establishes Profound Yin’s priorities and his cold, tactical view of the world.
- **The Spider Silk:** After Crimson Flame leaves, Profound Yin reveals a piece of white spider silk. His reaction—a mixture of shock, recognition, and maniacal joy—is a massive red flag for returning readers. The appearance of the Blood-Jade Spider silk is a far more significant find than any treasure, revealing a hidden, long-term ambition.
- **Han Li’s Return:** After a month of hiding, Han Li surfaces and returns to Heaven Star City, frustrated at having risked everything for nothing. The scene perfectly captures the Mortal Stream spirit of a ‘quiet defeat’—no dramatic tantrums, just cold logistical disappointment and a recalculation of his options.
- **The Unexpected Invitation:** Upon arriving home, Han Li receives a message talisman from Fan Jingmei (Madam Fan), the very woman who led him into the initial disaster. This sparks a rare moment of anger, but it’s immediately tempered by rational calculation. The promise of Divine Thunder Bamboo pulls him back into the game, proving that in this world, profit can always overcome recent trauma.
- **The True Face:** Han Li goes to the meeting and is met by a young woman in yellow. She introduces herself as Fairy Ziling (Wang Ning) and claims this is her ‘true face.’ This chapter ends with this revelation, questioning the nature of her beauty and her intentions.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Primordial Magnet Divine Light (元磁神光 / Yuan Ci Shen Guang):** A legendary, near-mythical technique in the Starfall Sea’s lore. Its claim to fame is that it can counter all Five Elements, making it the ultimate ‘anti-magic’ or energy-suppression art. Profound Yin’s skepticism is warranted; such techniques are often more theoretical than practical, or require conditions so specific they amount to a cosmic joke. It’s a classic xianxia trope of a pursuit for ultimate power that may be a dead end.
- **The Blood-Jade Spider (血玉蜘蛛 / Xue Yu Zhi Zhu):** The appearance of this spider silk is a huge worldbuilding moment. The Blood-Jade Spider is a legendary ancient insect with abilities that are incredibly rare and sought after. In the xianxia context, certain ancient beasts have inherent talents that transcend normal cultivation. For Profound Yin, finding a trace of this spider is not a curiosity—it’s the unlocking of an entirely new path to a long-desired goal, hinting at a plan greater than simple power accumulation.
- **‘True Face’ (真容):** The concept of a ‘true face’ in xianxia is multidimensional. It can mean the literal, physical face without any illusions, makeup, or disguises. But it can also carry a deeper, metaphorical weight, representing a character’s true nature or their choice to be vulnerable and honest. When a powerful cultivator like Ziling asks, “Is this face disappointing?” she is both stating a fact and testing the person before her, using the revelation of her ‘weakness’ as a social weapon.

## Reading Guide

- **Pay attention to the spider silk.** This chapter isn’t about the Divine Thunder Bamboo anymore. The focus has shifted to Profound Yin’s new obsession. This is a classic narrative pivot in RMJI—a threat isn’t just a boss to fight; it’s a system with its own evolving plans.
- **Watch Han Li’s emotional management.** His anger at Madam Fan is genuine, but he squashes it instantly for profit. This is a perfect example of the Mortal Stream protagonist’s core trait: emotions are real, but they are always subordinate to survival and resource acquisition.
- **The ‘True Face’ is a test.** Fairy Ziling is showing Han Li something she has likely hidden from everyone else. Whether it’s truly her face or another mask is secondary. The act of *showing* it is a deliberate choice to create a sense of intimacy and obligation. Han Li’s skepticism is the correct, survival-oriented response.
- **This is the calm before the storm.** This chapter serves as a crucial reset. The Vast Sky Island storm is over, and a new phase of politicking, secrets, and possible cooperation is about to begin. Buckle up for some intense and potentially misleading negotiations.

### On the Nature of Masks in Xianxia

The concept of wearing a ‘mask’—both literal and figurative—is a core survival tactic in the Mortal Stream. Han Li’s constant internal monologue is a mask; Profound Yin’s grandfatherly advice to Crimson Flame is a mask; Fan Jingmei’s sweet courtesy is a mask. When a character in this genre claims to be showing their ‘true face,’ a savvy reader should immediately become suspicious. In the cultivation world, vulnerability is often the most potent weapon of all. Fairy Ziling’s move here is a classic gambit: by seemingly disarming herself, she hopes to lower your defenses. Mastery of the social mask is the difference between being a pawn and a player in this game.
