## Chapter Overview
Han Li wakes up to find himself at the mercy of Nan Gong Ping, a Core Formation cultivator from the Moon-Masking Sect and Nan Gong Wan’s cousin. After a stinging pair of slaps (for the crime of having touched her while rescuing her), she offers him two wildly different repayment options: a fortune in spirit stones, or a guided path to recovery within her sect—complete with a dual cultivation partner and secret techniques. Han Li’s choice surprises even her, and his razor-sharp instincts for self-preservation shine through once again.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li is slapped twice for having “dared” to touch Nan Gong Ping during the rescue and for lying unconscious on top of her all night.
- Nan Gong Ping presents two choices: (1) immediate spirit stones and materials as compensation, or (2) following her sect, receiving pills, a dual cultivation partner, and possible secret techniques to regain his cultivation in one or two decades.
- Han Li, suspicious of the second option’s generosity, chooses the first—spirit stones only.
- He asks for and receives several pieces of Harmony Prime Jade, completing the materials needed to repair the teleportation array.
- Before leaving, Han Li asks her to pass his regards to Nan Gong Wan, causing a momentary pause.
- The chapter concludes with Han Li’s internal analysis of the technique that drained him—the True Samsara Art—and its harsh limitations.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Slap as Honor Code.** In the cultivation world—and in Chinese martial tradition generally—a slap to the face is a deep personal insult, not mere physical punishment. Han Li’s silent acceptance of the humiliation is a key Mortal Stream survival tactic: he absorbs the loss of face without protest because arguing with a Core Formation cultivator would only escalate the cost. The slap here also carries a layer of performative chastisement; Nan Gong Ping is arguably venting anger on behalf of her cousin as much as punishing Han Li for his audacity.

**The “Dual Cultivation” Offer.** Dual cultivation (双修) often carries a strong sexual connotation in xianxia, but in formal sect contexts it can refer to a structured partnership aimed at mutual cultivation progress—sometimes including, sometimes limited to, shared meditation and energy exchange. Nan Gong Ping’s offer to pair Han Li with a “young female disciple” is simultaneously generous and humiliating: it implies he cannot recover on his own, and she is handing him a solution like a physician prescribing medicine. Han Li’s rejection of this path is consistent with his lifelong refusal to be indebted to any faction.

**True Samsara Art and the “Semi-Suicidal” Technique.** This technique is a prime example of the Mortal Stream’s “cost-for-power” logic. It drains the caster’s own true essence to near death, then automatically absorbs anyone who probes her with spiritual energy—but only to the level needed to replenish her original loss, not to increase her power. The “semi-suicidal” designation is a brutal admissions: the technique only works when the caster is already on the verge of collapse, making it a last-resort gambit with a high risk of self-destruction.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream negotiation psychology and the careful management of power asymmetry. Observe how Han Li:
- Suppresses his anger after being slapped, recognizing that a verbal defense would only worsen his position.
- Recognizes the suspicious generosity of Option 2 and interprets the subtext (expectation, anxiety) in Nan Gong Ping’s eyes as a red flag.
- Chooses the financially generous but socially independent option, preserving his freedom and avoiding any long-term entanglement with the Moon-Masking Sect.
- Seizes the opportunity to ask for Harmony Prime Jade at the last moment, turning a compensation scene into a logistical win for his ongoing escape plan.

Also note the rare moment of personal sentiment—asking Nan Gong Ping to convey his regards to Nan Gong Wan—which stands out as a small, deliberate crack in his usual cold calculation, yet one he immediately walks away from without follow-up.
