## Chapter Overview
Alright, buckle up, because the treasure hunt just went from "high-stakes heist" to "full-blown comic tragedy." This chapter opens with the biggest faceplant of the expedition: Wan Tianming’s carefully orchestrated plan to lift the Void Heaven Cauldron using drugged-up Golden Silk Silkworms spectacularly backfires. The worms crash, the threads snap, and the righteous side’s mood goes from tense to apoplectic. Man Brutal Beard, naturally, cackles like a man possessed, while the demonic trio spends a few precious moments savoring the sweet taste of schadenfreude. Wan Tianming almost throws a Nascent Soul-level tantrum, but restrains himself, retreating with the dignity of a man who just lost a very expensive, very public gamble. In the aftermath, the power dynamics shift, the Profound Bone Saint continues to be an unsettling enigma, and Han Li quietly picks up the discarded silkworms as salvage—because in the Mortal Stream, one man’s trash is another man’s potential scientific breakthrough. The chapter ends with Extreme Yin, drunk on ambition, ordering Han Li to prepare the Blood Jade Spider for the next, make-or-break attempt, painting a target squarely on the protagonist’s back.

## Key Plot Points
*   **The Cauldron Heist Goes Bust:** The Golden Silk Silkworms, artificially strengthened by the Azure Herb, undergo a mass failure. Their golden light dims, their silk threads snap, and the half-ton cauldron crashes back into its icy hole, extinguishing most of the blue light. This is a total, systemic failure of the righteous factions’ plan.
*   **A Study in Reactions:** The fallout is a masterclass in different forms of power-play. Man Brutal Beard is openly scornful and mocking. Extreme Yin and Qing Yi Jushi are gleefully malicious. Wan Tianming’s face cycles through shock, fury, and finally, cold restraint—he chooses to retreat rather than escalate into a direct fight with the demonic side.
*   **Han Li’s Salvage Operation:** While the Nascent Soul overlords posture and sneer, Han Li spots value in the discarded, crippled Golden Silk Silkworms. He uses his “poor rogue cultivator” persona to secure them, adding another unpredictable asset to his ever-growing collection of weird bugs and cursed artifacts.
*   **The Profound Bone’s Continual Mask:** The Profound Bone Saint diagnoses the silkworms’ condition with clinical precision, revealing deep expertise that marks him as more than a simple junior. When Extreme Yin probes his identity, he deflects with practiced humility, maintaining his cover under the nose of the man he intends to destroy.
*   **The Trap is Set:** After the mess, the demonic alliance sets defensive countermeasures at the entrance (birds and Yin corpses), preparing for any retaliatory strikes. Extreme Yin then turns his full attention to Han Li, ordering him to prepare the Blood Jade Spider for the “one-shot” attempt to retrieve the Void Heaven Cauldron, raising the tension for the arc’s climax.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The term **天青 (Azure Herb)** is a wonderfully specific piece of xianxia lore. It’s not just any powerful herb; it’s a temporary amplifier. In the logic of many cultivation classics, certain rare spirit plants can “burn out” a beast’s potential for a short, massive gain, effectively force-levelling it. The vivid imagery of the silkworms’ golden light failing and their threads snapping like over-tuned rubber bands is a classic example of "药性过后反噬" (backlash after medicinal effect fades)—a core concept in xianxia where powerful, borrowed power always comes with a brutal, built-in payback period. It underscores the world’s unforgiving systemic logic: there are no free lunches, especially not on the path to immortality.

Meanwhile, the **金丝蚕 (Golden Silk Silkworm)** is one of the most famous spirit insects from Chinese literature. In the original folklore and later novels like this one, it’s not just a production unit for fabric; it’s a rare, powerful insect capable of immense physical strength and producing nigh-indestructible silk. Wan Tianming’s willingness to cripple a whole batch just for one more attempt at the cauldron shows the terrifyingly high cost of doing business at the Nascent Soul level, where even “priceless” assets can be treated as consumable resources.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a brilliant showcase of “the Mortal Stream’s quiet victory.” While the Nascent Soul big shots are having a dramatic meltdown and posturing for dominance, Han Li does something far more cunning: he stays calm, observes the scraps left behind by the titans, and scoops them up for himself. That’s the core gameplay of a survival-minded protagonist. You don’t win by shouting the loudest; you win by recognizing that your enemies’ failures create your opportunities. Pay close attention to how the Profound Bone Saint handles the interrogation from Extreme Yin. His is a dangerous game of psychological chess, wearing a mask of deference while his every act reveals his superior knowledge. Finally, feel that growing tension as Extreme Yin’s interest shifts exclusively to Han Li. The “fatherly kindness” from a Nascent Soul old demon is never a good sign—it’s usually the prelude to a very binding transaction. The scene is now perfectly prepared for the final, climactic gamble with the Blood Jade Spider.
