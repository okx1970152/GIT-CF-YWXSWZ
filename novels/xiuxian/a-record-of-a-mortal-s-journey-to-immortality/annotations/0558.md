## Chapter Overview
Alright, buckle up, fellow readers of the mortal path. We’ve been in a tense, agonizing stalemate for weeks, watching Han Li get drained like a living spirit stone battery. But you know our boy—he doesn’t just suffer in silence; he *calculates*. In this chapter, the slow burn finally ignites. The Wind-Thunder Wings are almost done, the three demons are running on fumes, and Han Li, looking like a trapped lab rat, pulls off the oldest trick in the book: the poisoned gift. This is a textbook Mortal Stream pivot—from passive endurance to active, devastating counter-strike. The tension here isn’t in a flashy fight; it’s in the quiet, terrifying moment when a turtle demon’s belly starts to swell.

## Key Plot Points
- The Wind-Thunder Wings are structurally complete, but the wind-thunder energy within them hasn’t fully stabilized, forcing the three demons (Feng Xi, Turtle Demon, Venomous Flood Dragon) to continue pouring in their own energy at a severe cost to their vital essence.
- Han Li deliberately releases a trace of pure spiritual energy to draw attention to a small bottle that has fallen from his sleeve.
- Feng Xi detects the energy, seizes the bottle, and uses the Wind Spirit Restriction to punish Han Li, confirming the bottle contains a potent spirit liquid.
- Despite initial suspicion, the demon trio, desperate to replenish their drained mana and avoid long-term cultivation damage, reluctantly decide to share and consume the diluted spirit liquid.
- The Venomous Flood Dragon and Feng Xi briefly discuss killing Han Li now that his wood-element energy is no longer needed for the refinement, but decide to wait until the treasure is completed.
- Observing the demons’ murderous intent, Han Li feigns resignation, sits down cross-legged, and begins a covert internal operation to isolate and neutralize the Wind Spirit Restriction using his Evil-Repelling Divine Lightning, preparing for his next move.
- The Turtle Demon, having recovered some energy, suddenly collapses without warning. His body grotesquely swells from the inside, the first clear indication that the spirit liquid was lethally poisoned.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Art of the Poisoned Gift (敬酒与罚酒):** Feng Xi’s line, “You refuse a toast only to be forced to drink a forfeit,” is a classic Chinese idiom encapsulating a brutal social coercion tactic. The “toast” represents the polite, beneficial offer; the “forfeit” is the painful consequence of refusal. In *RMJI*, this isn’t just a saying—it’s the operating system of the cultivation world’s power dynamics. Han Li, however, has just weaponized the offer itself, turning the “toast” into the “forfeit.” He knows the demons’ desperation, feeds their greed, and makes them participants in their own poisoning. It’s a masterclass in turning your enemy’s own logic against them.

**Wind Spirit Restriction (风灵劲):** This isn’t a mere paralysis technique. It’s an insidious energy lock planted deep in the victim’s meridians by a far more powerful cultivator. It can be triggered at the caster’s will to cause excruciating pain, making it a perfect leash for a captive. In typical stories, this would be an insurmountable problem. But Han Li’s approach to the Freezing Azure Flame Pearl earlier has given him a specific skill set: the ability to isolate and suppress foreign energies that his own cultivation level cannot refine or expel. He is turning his past experience with being frozen into a tactical playbook.

**Ten-Thousand-Year Spirit Liquid (万年灵液):** This is the ultra-rare, hyper-efficient mana potion of the cultivation world. A single drop can instantly restore a massive amount of spiritual power. Its value is immeasurable, making it the perfect bait for desperate, high-level cultivators. Han Li’s genius trick is diluting it to make the resource last longer in the narrative, while mixing in a potent, colorless poison that an expert poison-detector (the Venomous Flood Dragon) wouldn't recognize because it’s not a standard toxin but something uniquely deadly.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for the payoff! This chapter is the quiet before the storm, and the storm is going to be messy. Notice that Han Li doesn't celebrate his successful trick. He immediately pivots to his next problem: the Wind Spirit Restriction. Our boy doesn't take a single victory lap. The sequence here is crucial: 1) poison the demons, 2) neutralize the restriction, 3) then figure out how to escape three enraged, dying (or dead) high-tier demon beasts. The real action isn’t going to be a spell-slinging duel. It’s going to be Han Li exploiting a moment of absolute chaos. Pay close attention to his quiet, methodical preparation—that’s where the true battle is being won.
