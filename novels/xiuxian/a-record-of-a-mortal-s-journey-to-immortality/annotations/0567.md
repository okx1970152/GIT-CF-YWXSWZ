## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to another masterclass in cold-blooded Xianxia power plays! Chapter 567 is a textbook example of the Mortal Stream’s favorite game: the ambush. Han Li and Madam Fan are setting a trap for their treacherous Elder Yun, but the arrival of an unexpected guest—True Person Miaohe, an entirely unwelcome Nascent Soul cultivator—has just raised the stakes from risky to *hair-raising*. This isn’t a grand battle of equals; it’s a nerve-wracking prelude of deception, strategic calculation, and the quiet clinking of a teacup. Get ready for a scheme that could shatter in a single wrong word.

## Key Plot Points

*   **An Unexpected Problem:** Han Li and Madam Fan have successfully purged the island of Yun Tianxiao’s loyalists, ready for his trap. But a sound transmission reveals a major complication: True Person Miaohe, a Nascent Soul cultivator from the Biyun Sect who was allied with Yun Tianxiao, has also arrived.
*   **Han Li’s Cold Calculation:** While Madam Fan panics, Han Li’s mind instantly runs the new numbers. He isn’t afraid of Miaohe in a straight fight (he’s confident he can escape with his new Wind Lightning Wings), but the plan forbids a straight fight. He needs to kill Yun Tianxiao *before* Miaohe can react.
*   **The New Strategy:** Han Li proposes a classic Mortal Stream solution: understand the value of the relationship. Miaohe only helped Yun Tianxiao for “furnaces” (pretty female cultivators). If Han Li can kill Yun Tianxiao instantly, and Madam Fan then offers Miaohe a better deal, the old Daoist will have no reason to avenge a dead, inconvenient ally.
*   **The Warning Shot:** Before executing the plan, Han Li turns back to Madam Fan and delivers a calm, clinical threat about what will happen if she tries to betray him after the kill. It’s not a scream of rage; it’s a cold, logical insurance policy.
*   **The Bait and Ambush:** The plan unfolds with exquisite social theater. Madam Fan fakes a grievance about a disciple negotiating a deal for rare “Illusory Dream Stones.” The bait is irresistible. Lured by greed, Yun Tianxiao follows her into the back room, completely blind to the master assassin waiting inside.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

*   **Mortal Stream Negotiation:** The core of this chapter perfectly illustrates the Mortal Stream’s “Dark Forest” logic. Han Li doesn’t view Miaohe as an unbeatable obstacle or an enemy. He views him as a system variable with specific motivations (greed for cultivation resources/furnaces) that can be calculated and worked around. He doesn’t try to defeat Miaohe; he tries to cancel his reason for fighting.
*   **The "Illusory Dream Stone" Lure:** The name in Chinese, 幻梦石 (Huàn Mèng Shí), literally means “Illusory Dream Stone.” For resource-scarce cultivators in the Mortal Stream, a rare material is the most intoxicating lure. Yun Tianxiao’s fatal mistake isn’t stupidity, but simple greed—a universally exploitable weakness in this world.
*   **Swearing a Poisonous Oath (毒誓):** Madam Fan swears a “poisonous oath” to prove her loyalty to Han Li’s plan. In Xianxia and Chinese folk tradition, such oaths are considered cosmically binding. The “poison” (毒) refers to the terrible, self-inflicted consequences (death, failed cultivation, soul destruction) that the swearer invites upon themselves if they break their word. It’s a cultural guarantee far more serious than a simple promise in the West.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a taut, dialogue-driven thriller that builds tension not through action, but through planning. Pay close attention to Han Li’s rapid mental recalculation when the new variable (Miaohe) is introduced. He treats the problem as a systems engineer would: identify the vital node (Yun Tianxiao), calculate the stakeholders (Miaohe's profit/loss statement), and create a closed system where the node can be removed before the stakeholders can react. The final scene is a powerhouse of dramatic irony—we know the ambush is set, we see the trap being laid with the “Illusory Dream Stone” bait, while the victim walks into the stone room completely unaware. It’s like watching a horror movie from the monster’s point of view.
