## Chapter Overview

This chapter captures Han Li's mundane frustrations as a low-ranking disciple forbidden from learning martial arts. Through his solitary walk, we see his resentment, his concern for Zhang Tie, and his forced acceptance of Doctor Mo's restrictions. The chapter ends with a chance discovery: a heavy, sealed metal bottle hidden in leaf litter. This seemingly trivial find will become the most important object in the entire story—the first appearance of the Heaven Vial.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li walks out of Divine Hand Valley, heading toward Red Water Peak out of habit.
- He recalls visiting Zhang Tie daily to see his painful training under the waterfall, noting that the Elephant Hide Art's first layer already causes extreme suffering.
- He feels encouraged at the thought of pleading with Doctor Mo to let Zhang Tie switch techniques.
- Hearing weapons training from Hundred Forging Hall reminds him of his own forbidden status; Doctor Mo has strictly prohibited him from learning any martial arts, claiming it would interfere with his oral formula practice.
- He reflects that other disciples have improved greatly while he remains stagnant; even Zhang Tie has become tougher and stronger after just two months.
- He comforts himself by remembering that without Doctor Mo, he wouldn't have passed the probation test or been able to send money home.
- While walking absentmindedly, he kicks a hidden object and suffers intense pain in his big toe.
- After recovering, he uses a branch to uncover a fist-sized bottle that is surprisingly heavy, made of metal, with a green surface and dark green leaf patterns, sealed with a tight lid.
- He shakes it, hears nothing, tries to open it but fails.
- Still in pain, he decides to return to his quarters to apply medicine and examine the bottle privately, hiding it in his bosom.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The "Elephant Hide Art" exemplifies a recurring theme in xianxia: techniques that demand extreme physical suffering for incremental gains. This mirrors the idea that cultivation is not a gentle path but a ruthless transaction of pain for power. The fact that Han Li cannot learn martial arts despite wanting to underscores his lack of agency—a common scenario for protagonists in mortal-stream narratives, where the world does not accommodate individual desires. The mysterious bottle, meanwhile, introduces one of the genre's most potent tropes: the unassuming object that holds hidden cosmic significance.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a transition from Han Li's ordinary life to a new phase of opportunity. Pay attention to his emotional state: he is discontented, curious, and cautious. His decision to hide the bottle reveals his instinct for self-preservation. The bottle's weight, material, and sealed lid hint at its otherworldly nature. Readers familiar with the series will recognize this as the origin of the Heaven Vial, but for new readers, the mystery is just beginning.

## The Mysterious Vial {#mysterious-vial}

In xianxia, mysterious artifacts often serve as the protagonist's first major cheat. This bottle, made of heavy metal with leaf patterns, will later be revealed as the Heaven Vial, a treasure with time-manipulating properties. Its sealed lid and weight hint at its extraordinary nature. This chapter marks the beginning of Han Li's journey from a frustrated disciple to a resourceful cultivator. The bottle represents the first tangible piece of luck in a world that otherwise offers little.
