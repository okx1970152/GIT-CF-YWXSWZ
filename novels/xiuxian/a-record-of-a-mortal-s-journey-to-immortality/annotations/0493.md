## Chapter Overview
Sometimes, in a world of scheming old monsters and grudges that span centuries, the smartest move is to wait for the chaos to give you a perfect opening. That’s exactly what Han Li does this chapter—he doesn’t fight for the silver wolf treasure; he just happens to be standing in the right place when it flies right into his basket. While Nascent Soul overlords like Extreme Yin and Wan Tianming are locked in their high-stakes standoff over the legendary Void Heaven Cauldron, a lowly Foundation Establishment cultivator casually snatches a shape-shifting ancient treasure from under their noses. But the real show is just beginning: a terrifying blue flame emerges from the depths, and its mere presence unleashes an ice that freezes even Profound Yin demonic Qi solid. Get ready—the ultimate prize of the Chaotic Star Sea is about to break the surface, and no one is walking away unchanged.

## Key Plot Points
The Fire Wolf, double-teamed by Wu Chou and the Profound Bone Saint, transforms into a single-horned silver giant wolf and unleashes a mysterious silver light orb that shreds the Profound Yin python and completely dissolves the green ghost net. This unexpected trump card startles both attackers. The Profound Bone Saint, recognizing the wolf’s value, draws what appears to be an emerald dagger—but Han Li’s trained senses identify it as the stolen Gold Devourer Beetle Bamboo Arrow in disguise, a reckless move given Extreme Yin is directly above them. The silver wolf exploits an opening in the python’s regeneration, shrinking and darting through the half-mended hole to escape. The Profound Bone Saint attacks, but the wolf’s body proves unnaturally hard, deflecting the blow and sending it flying directly toward Han Li’s position. Han Li seizes the moment, first using a copper-ring treasure that manifests on the wolf’s limbs and head as a natural restraint, then immediately trapping it with his basket ancient treasure. Wu Chou is humiliated and enraged; the Profound Bone Saint is chagrined but hides it. The sage-robed elder and Extreme Yin witness the scene just as they lose the treasure contest with Wan Tianming, but Extreme Yin dismisses it—he assumes he can take it from Han Li later. The platform begins a violent, earth-shaking tremor. A dazzling blue flame emerges from the cave, instantly covering the entire platform with a weird blue frost that spreads with terrifying speed. Wu Chou hesitates for a split second and is nearly completely frozen solid, his Profound Yin demonic Qi powerless. Extreme Yin saves him at the last moment with the Heavenly Capital Corpse Flame. All the old monsters—Extreme Yin, Wan Tianming, and the others—fix their gazes on the blue flame as the legendary Void Heaven Cauldron, the Chaotic Star Sea’s first secret treasure, is finally about to be born before their eyes, their greed palpable.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Shape-Shifting Ancient Treasures (幻形通灵的古宝):** In *RMJI*, certain high-grade ancient treasures possess the ability to change their physical form, often transforming into sentient beasts. This is not mere decoration—it’s a sign of extreme refinement and spiritual power that makes the treasure capable of acting autonomously, fighting its own battles, and even fleeing when threatened. Capturing one is far harder than picking up a normal magic tool, which is why Wu Chou and the Profound Bone Saint, despite their vastly higher cultivation, failed to secure it quickly. **Heavenly Capital Corpse Flame (天都尸火):** A signature demonic-flame technique of Extreme Yin Island. Corpse-type flames in xianxia are typically parasitic and Yin-rot in nature, capable of devouring life force and even degrading other forms of energy. Here, Extreme Yin uses it as a counter to the mysterious blue frost, reinforcing the element-on-element logic of the Mortal Stream. **Void Heaven Cauldron (虚天鼎):** This is not just any magic treasure. It is the apex legendary object of the entire Chaotic Star Sea cultivation world—a secret artifact that all the Nascent Soul old monsters have schemed and risked their lives for. Its appearance signals the climax of a multi-century narrative arc. Even Extreme Yin, a man who never shows his emotions, is trembling with naked greed.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s central philosophy: the weak can win, but only if they let the strong exhaust themselves first. Han Li didn’t steal the silver wolf; he just happened to be there when it fell into his lap. His quick deployment of exactly the right counter-treasure—the copper rings that perfectly restrain the beast—shows that his preparation, not his power, is what makes him dangerous. Meanwhile, Wu Chou’s humiliation is a textbook lesson in the dangers of overconfidence and hesitation. He assumed a junior like Han Li was beneath his notice, and by the time he realized the treasure was slipping away, it was gone. And the emergence of the blue flame and the freezing platform serves as a reminder to both the characters and the reader: the cultivation world doesn’t care about your schemes. When the ultimate treasure shows itself, the environment itself becomes a weapon. Keep your eyes open—the real chaos is just beginning.

## Behind the Scenes: The Illusion of Control
Notice how Extreme Yin, despite being the most powerful figure present, fails to secure the silver wolf and can’t even spare a moment to punish Han Li for taking it. He rationalizes it as a temporary loss he can recover later, but the truth is simpler: in this world, nobody has real control. The treasure you plan for can fall to a bystander. The disciple you’re supposed to protect can freeze solid in an instant. Even a Nascent Soul cultivator is just a slightly bigger fish in a pond that’s about to be drained. The Mortal Stream’s uncaring system never lets anyone—genius or old monster—relax their vigilance for a single second.
