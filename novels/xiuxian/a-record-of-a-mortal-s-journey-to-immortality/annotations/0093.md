## Chapter Overview
Ah, what a way to cook a golden goose! This chapter is a masterclass in **Mortal Stream psychology**, where brains beat brawn and a few well-placed lies are deadlier than any flying sword. Our favorite pragmatist, Han Li, finds himself facing off against a blustering, arrogant dwarf who thinks the name of his clan is an invincible shield. What follows isn't a flashy, world-shaking magical duel—it’s a cold, clinical con. Get ready, fellow Daoists, to see Han Li play puppet master while his opponent dances right into a fireball. The result? A clean, satisfying, and completely overwhelming victory that leaves a mortal warlord utterly terrified and our protagonist quietly rifling through the ashes for loot.

## Key Plot Points
*   Han Li instantly deduces that the dwarf is either a liar or a nobody from the Ye Clan, deciding that killing him carries minimal risk.
*   Han Li adopts the persona of a naive, sheltered young cultivator, flattering the dwarf with feigned ignorance and helplessness to lower his guard.
*   The dwarf, completely fooled, drops his golden protective barrier to accept what he believes is the return of a talisman.
*   Using a classic 'look behind you!' stratagem, Han Li creates a perfect opening and instantly incinerates the dwarf with a Fireball Technique.
*   The entire exchange is witnessed by Jia Tianlong and Wang Juechu, who can't hear a word but see a mighty Immortal Master reduced to ash in an instant.
*   Han Li collects his spoils: a magical talisman, a mysterious black triangular token, and a Qin family genealogy book.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter is a fantastic example of how mental games are often more important than pure power in the *Mortal Stream* genre. Han Li doesn’t win by overwhelming the dwarf with a stronger fireball; he wins by controlling the flow of information. The ‘look behind you’ trick is as old as time, but in the context of a cultivator who dropped his magical barrier (a technique often called 金罩术 or ‘Golden Shield Technique’) out of sheer greed, it shows a devastating weakness of character. The power gap here was actually narrow, but the intelligence gap was a chasm.

The dwarf’s reliance on the name of the Ye Clan speaks volumes about the clan-based structure of the cultivation world. A name like that can be a shield, but for those who aren't core members, it’s a hollow threat. Han Li’s cold calculation that the man was a “nobody” is a brutal lesson in the world’s social hierarchy. The triangular token with the characters for “Ascend to Immortality” (升仙) is a classic piece of world-building—an enigmatic artifact that hints at a larger system (likely a trial for disciples) without giving away any concrete answers, perfectly preserving the mystery.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a highlight of Han Li’s early cunning. Watch how he uses his opponent's own arrogance as a weapon. The dwarf thought he was manipulating a naive youth, but he was the one being led to the slaughter. Pay close attention to the phrase “They saw, but understood nothing” regarding Jia Tianlong and Wang Juechu. This perfectly illustrates the “Absolute Domain” of a cultivator. To a mortal, the difference between a weak cultivator and a strong one is invisible. The gap is so vast it’s incomprehensible, which makes the fear Han Li inspires even more potent. And that final bit about the Qin genealogy? Classic Han Li. He finds a potential mystery and immediately jumps to the most cynical and practical explanation. It's a great character beat that underlines his pragmatic worldview.
