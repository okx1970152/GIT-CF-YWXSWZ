## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow cultivators, to another round of *Mortal Stream* scheming! Chapter 545—“Wind Stirs Beyond the Sea”—takes us deep into a closed-door exchange meet for Core Formation cultivators. But this isn't your average trading post. The atmosphere is thick with perfume, suspicion, and unspoken power games. Our favorite green-skinned hero, disguised as “Senior Li,” steps into a room where every male cultivator has a beautiful female cultivator glued to his side—and guess who gets assigned to our guy? Wen Siyue, the daughter of Wen Qiang from the Sublime Sound Sect. Yes, THAT Wen Siyue. The meet is hosted by the smarmy Yun Tianxiao, with his close companion Madam Fan (of the Sublime Sound Sect) at his side. The trading itself kicks off with mixed quality offerings, but the real drama is in the silent negotiations, familiar-yet-unrecognized faces, and the terrifying price these women are paying for a chance at cultivation resources. Get ready for a chapter that peels back the glittering surface of high-level society and shows us the transactional nature of every relationship.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li, using his Change-Shifting Art disguise, is guided into the exchange meet by Wen Siyue, a Foundation Establishment cultivator. She is clearly terrified of the host, Yun Tianxiao.
- The hall is arranged with a large round table and wide armchairs. Almost every male Core Formation cultivator has a beautiful, young female cultivator pressed against them, ranging from Qi Condensation to early Foundation Establishment.
- Two female Core Formation cultivators sit alone, suggesting they have no "companion" (or refuse the arrangement).
- Wen Siyue, recognizing a senior cultivator, actively leans into Han Li and is guided by his arm to an empty seat. The narration emphasizes her trembling and stiffness.
- Han Li realizes his hand has drifted to her breast, his fingers catching a hardened nub. He immediately and awkwardly pulls away and loosens his embrace, which startles Wen Siyue, as she expected different treatment from a typical senior.
- Han Li's internal reaction is one of discomfort, as he recognizes her as an old acquaintance's daughter. He does not wish to humiliate her further.
- Yun Tianxiao enters with Madam Fan (Madam Fan of the Sublime Sound Sect), clearly in an intimate relationship, confirming his deep ties to the sect.
- The host, Yun Tianxiao, formally introduces the exchange meet with thirteen Core Formation cultivators (two have already left). The first participant, a fat old man in a blue robe, produces several items including Strange Pill Pillars, a Crouching Tiger Shark Demon Core, and a Sea-Transforming Beast pelt. Han Li finds them underwhelming.
- The second participant, a strange, gaunt figure in a red headwrap, produces a single Coiling Earthworm demon beast egg (grade-five), which immediately draws intense interest from the other cultivators. The chapter ends on this note of high anticipation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Let's talk about the elephant—or rather, the beautiful human ornament—in the room. The scene where every male cultivator has a female cultivator pressed against them is a brutal, normalized part of the cultivation world's social structure. This is not romance. It is a **transaction**, often rooted in the **Furnace / Cauldron (炉鼎, Luding)** concept. In this system, female cultivators with special constitutions or high-grade cultivation are treated as consumable assets—living batteries whose vitality can be drained to boost a male partner's cultivation, often leading to permanent damage or death. Even if the relationship is "just" for social positioning, the woman's agency is nil; she is property, a prize, or a stepping stone. Wen Siyue's terror of Yun Tianxiao, and her surprise when Han Li doesn't take advantage of her, is a stark reminder that for these low-level female cultivators, every meeting with a senior is a game of Russian roulette.

Also, note the **Change-Shifting Art** (换形决, Huan Xing Jue) at work. This is a secret technique from the Profound Yin that allows Han Li to alter his body shape, skin color, and aura, making him completely unrecognizable even to someone he's met before. It's a cornerstone of his survival strategy: never show your true face when you don't have to.

### Furnace / Cauldron (炉鼎)

A dark cultivation practice where a weaker cultivator (usually female) is used as a living vessel to boost a stronger partner's cultivation, often at the cost of their own life or cultivation base.

## Reading Guide

Get ready, because this chapter is a masterclass in "show, don't tell." The power dynamics here are communicated entirely through body language and subtle social cues: the trembling of Wen Siyue, the immediate shift in atmosphere, the calculated smiles of the male cultivators. Han Li's reaction to being turned into a "furnace-user" is particularly telling—his discomfort is real, but it's not heroic outrage. It's the awkwardness of being forced to play a role he doesn't want, with a woman he feels a residual sense of responsibility toward. The end of the chapter, with the Coiling Earthworm egg sparking a feeding frenzy of interest, is a classic *Mortal Stream* setup: the appearance of a single valuable asset instantly reshuffles the entire social landscape. Keep your eyes on the eggs (and the eyes of everyone around the table)—the real conflict hasn't even started yet.

## Deep Dive: The Sublime Sound Sect's Shadow

This chapter offers a rare glimpse into the internal mechanics of the Sublime Sound Sect, the politically agile, charm-oriented faction we've seen before. Madam Fan's presence as Yun Tianxiao's intimate partner is not a casual romance—it is an institutional alliance. The Sects use their female members as intelligence assets and social lubricants, forging connections to powerful rogue cultivators like Yun Tianxiao. Wen Siyue is not just a furnaces; she is a hostage in a strategic network. The fact that Han Li recognizes both women and understands the game they are playing adds another layer of cold calculation to his own position. He is not just a trader at an exchange meet; he is a node in a web, and every move he makes ripples through the ambitions of the Sublime Sound Sect and its allies.
