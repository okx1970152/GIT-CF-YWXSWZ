## Chapter Overview
The night raid on the Yue royal palace kicks off with surgical precision and overwhelming force—until the Black Fiend Sect springs a nasty surprise. Our Yellow Maple Valley strike team splits into two groups: one to secure the emperor, the other to hit the Cold Palace core. What starts as a textbook decapitation operation runs straight into the Four Symbols Formation, a defensive array designed to stall high-level cultivators by wasting their mana on brute-force escape. Just when everyone’s starting to sweat, Han Li quietly pulls out a crystal ball and drops a bomb: he’s already found the crack. Classic Mortal Stream—victory through preparation, not raw power.

## Key Plot Points
- Liu Jing leads the charge over the palace walls with decisive authority, but Han Li hangs back, silently checking his Reversed Five Elements Formation from the previous night—it’s still intact.
- The team splits: Chen Qiaoqian and Zhong Weiniang go for the emperor; Liu Jing, Song Meng, Han Li, and three others head for the Cold Palace.
- The outer perimeter guards are crushed instantly: Liu Jing’s silk handkerchief reflects spells, and Song Meng’s Blue Silk Sword unleashes “Ten Thousand Threads Descending the World,” massacring most defenders.
- The Ice Fiend appears at the gate and triggers the Four Symbols Formation before the group can land a killing blow, trapping all seven in an icy illusion realm.
- The formation is obscure—no one knows how to break it properly, and brute force will drain mana needed for the main fight, creating a tactical bind.
- Han Li, who has been quietly observing with a crystal ball, announces he has found the formation’s weakness, shifting the group’s trajectory from attrition to precision.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Four Symbols Formation (四象阵)** in xianxia is derived from the Four Celestial Symbols—Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, Black Tortoise—which originate from ancient Chinese astronomy and cosmology. These symbols represent the four cardinal directions and seasonal cycles. In magical formations, “four symbols” usually means an array that traps enemies by cycling through elemental or illusory phases, forcing disorientation and resource drain. It is deliberately less common than Five Elements arrays, making it a niche choice for sects that want opponents to waste time on research instead of combat.

**Cold Palace (冷宫)** is a historical term from imperial China—a secluded, dilapidated section of the Forbidden City where disfavored empresses, concubines, or princes were confined. Its use here as the Black Fiend Sect’s base adds a layer of symbolic decay and hidden menace: a space once used for state punishment now repurposed for dark cultivation.

**Brute-force Formation Breaking**is a recurring tension point in xianxia strategy. Because formations are designed to be stronger from the inside, high-level cultivators often *can* smash their way out, but at a terrible energy cost. Smart enemies know this and use arrays not to *kill*, but to *soften up* the attackers before the real battle begins. This chapter is a textbook example of that logic.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a perfect Mortal Stream setup: the protagonists are winning *too* easily. The massacre of the outer guard—Song Meng’s flashy blue-thread attack—is deliberately over-the-top, designed to make the reader feel the power gap. Then the Four Symbols Formation lands like a cold slap, reminding us that the Black Fiend Sect has been planning for this invasion. The real tension isn’t the fight itself, but the resource math: how much mana should they burn now vs. save for the Four Blood Servants and the sect leader? Han Li’s quiet announcement of a known weakness is the classic pay-off to his earlier off-screen preparation (setting up the Reversed Five Elements Formation in chapter 320). He doesn’t fight harder; he arrives *prepared*. That’s the core of his character.
