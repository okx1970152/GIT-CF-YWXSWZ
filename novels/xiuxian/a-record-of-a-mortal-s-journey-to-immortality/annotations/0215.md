## Chapter Overview

Guess who just walked out of a furnace room looking like a whole new man? After nearly a full year locked inside a sweltering earthly fire chamber, Han Li emerges not just with a stockpile of Foundation Establishment Pills that would make most Qi Condensation disciples weep with envy, but as a fully-fledged Foundation Establishment cultivator himself. This chapter is pure, concentrated *Mortal Stream* satisfaction—no battles, no narrow escapes, no life-or-death gambles. Just logistics, patience, and the quiet triumph of raw perseverance. For a story that has trained us to expect betrayal at every turn and danger around every corner, watching Han Li calmly succeed through methodical repetition feels like a deep, earned exhalation. But the real gem here isn’t the breakthrough itself—it’s watching the system’s gatekeepers (in this case, the terrified ugly man at the earth fire counter) recalibrate their entire attitude the moment they sense a shift in cultivation rank. The Dark Forest doesn’t pause its rules even for a victory lap.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li’s first twenty-plus attempts at congealing Foundation Establishment Pills fail completely, leaving him with nothing but cracked blue solids and a heavy heart. He nearly gives up and considers seeking formal alchemy training.
- On a whim, he tries one last time—and it works. The first pill is slightly undersized, but it’s real. From there, his success rate jumps to roughly one successful congealing every three attempts, and his retrieval rate exceeds fifty percent.
- Over six months of uninterrupted, single-formula practice—sustained by Fasting Pills he cleverly acquired earlier—Han Li produces over twenty Foundation Establishment Pills. This quantity shocks even him, leading him to briefly wonder if alchemy is actually *easier* than everyone claims.
- After refining all his ingredients, Han Li decides to attempt his Foundation Establishment breakthrough on the spot. Eleven months after entering the earthly fire room, he emerges successfully—much to the horror and awe of the attendant, who had long assumed he was dead.
- The ugly man’s attitude shifts instantly from complaint to fawning deference the moment he detects Han Li’s new cultivation level. The greeting changes from “Junior Brother” to “Martial Uncle,” and every trace of familiarity evaporates.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Foundation Establishment Pills (筑基丹)** – These are the golden ticket in the low-level cultivation world. A Qi Condensation cultivator’s chances of breaking through to Foundation Establishment without one are abysmally low. In a system designed to hoard resources at the top, controlling the production and distribution of these pills is how sects maintain their grip on power. Han Li producing over twenty of them through sheer trial-and-error is absolutely *insane* by normal standards.

**The Ignored Truth of Pill Refining** – Han Li briefly wonders if alchemy is overhyped, but the chapter makes clear he’s benefiting from a privilege no ordinary alchemist ever gets: uninterrupted access to rare ingredients for *half a year* of concentrated practice on the *same* pill formula. Most sect alchemists have to jump between different formulas based on whatever materials trickle in. This is like learning to play a single song on the piano by practicing it eight hours a day for six months—of course you’ll get good at it. Han Li’s “talent” is really a structural advantage disguised as personal ability.

**Fasting Pills (辟谷丹)** – A staple convenience item in xianxia. A single pill replaces all nutritional needs for roughly a month. They’re the cultivation world’s answer to MREs—boring, functional, and absolutely indispensable for anyone planning a long seclusion. Han Li acquiring a bottle of these earlier by trading a few century-old herbs was a small but critically foresightful move.

## Reading Guide

This is one of those rare, deeply satisfying chapters where the protagonist simply *works* and *succeeds*. There’s no villain to outwit, no escape to scheme, no life-or-death gamble to sweat through. Just a locked room, a stockpile of ingredients, and a mountain of patience. If you’ve been feeling the tension of the Dark Forest grind—where every upward step seems to require someone else’s blood—this chapter is the system quietly acknowledging that sometimes, pure logistical stubbornness pays off. The real highlight, though, is the ugly man’s panic and instant recalibration when Han Li steps out. That moment is the cultivation world in miniature: rank is law, and even the friendliest familiarity disappears the moment the power gap widens. Enjoy the victory, because you know Han Li won’t get to rest on this laurel for long.
