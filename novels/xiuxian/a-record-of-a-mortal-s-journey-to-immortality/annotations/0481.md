## Chapter Overview

Remember how the Nascent Soul big shots agreed to a temporary truce to avoid a disastrous free-for-all before the Void Heaven Cauldron is even in sight? Well, surprise, surprise—that “truce” lasts about as long as it takes for a Core Formation cultivator to annoy Man Brutal Beard. This chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s toxic ecosystem: the absolute power gap, the casual slaughter of the weak, the arcane political chess game being played overhead, and our guy Han Li stuck in the middle, running silent calculations while everyone else treats him like furniture.

We open with a tense negotiation that ends in a fragile non-aggression pact. But no sooner is the ink dry than Man Brutal Beard decides to blow off steam by murdering a couple of Core Formation bystanders. One dies messily. The other—Yuan Yao—escapes using a mysterious trump card (Green Fire Thunder), hinting at a powerful, terrifying backer. This sparks a revealing conversation about the “Three Yang Old Devil” and exposes the Profound Bone Saint’s surprising connection to Man Brutal Beard. Finally, as the two main teams disappear into the inner hall, a pair of Star Palace elders reveal themselves, having secretly cracked the teleportation array a thousand years ago. Get ready, fellow readers: the predator hierarchy just got a new top tier, and nobody knows it yet.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Shinobi Accord Collapses**: Wan Tianming’s proposed “truce” is accepted, but it’s clearly a temporary standoff, not a peace treaty. Everyone knows the real fight starts when the treasure does.
- **Man Brutal Beard’s Purge**: Immediately after the Righteous Path departs, Man Brutal Beard casually murders a Core Formation male cultivator who was lurking nearby. He slices the man into seven or eight bloody pieces as casually as swatting a fly.
- **Yuan Yao’s Escape**: Yuan Yao uses Green Fire Thunder—a secret technique linked to the “Three Yang Old Devil”—to deflect Man Brutal Beard’s killing blow and flee into the pagoda. This marks her as a disciple of a terrifyingly powerful figure.
- **The Profound Bone Saint’s Shield**: When Extreme Yin suggests killing the other outsider (the Profound Bone Saint), Man Brutal Beard claims the man is a descendant of a benefactor and forbids anyone from harming him. This explains the Saint’s unwavering calm.
- **The Three Yang Old Devil Revealed**: The Nascent Soul monsters display open wariness of this enigmatic figure, confirming he is a wild card powerful enough to make even Man Brutal Beard think twice. They decide to let Yuan Yao go to avoid unnecessary bad blood.
- **Han Li’s Anxiety**: Han Li is forced to walk alongside his sworn enemy, Wu Chou, who now acts extremely friendly after a private conversation with Extreme Yin Patriarch. Han Li calculates this means his own death sentence has been signed for after the treasure is collected.
- **The Star Palace Strikes Back**: After everyone has departed, two Star Palace elders appear via the same teleportation array. They reveal that Star Palace cracked its restrictions a thousand years ago, making them the ultimate “orioles” waiting behind the mantises.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The Mantis and the Oriole (螳螂捕蝉，黄雀在后)**: This chapter’s title alludes to this classic Chinese idiom, meaning “The mantis stalks the cicada, unaware of the oriole behind.” It describes a situation where someone pursues a goal without noticing a greater predator waiting to exploit their effort. This chapter layers this structure beautifully: the Righteous and Demonic Paths are the mantises fighting over the treasure, while the Star Palace is the oriole, having secretly planned to ambush them all.

**Green Fire Thunder (青火雷)**: A secret technique of the Qing Yang Sect, an organization within the Demonic Path known for its fire-element arts. The fact that Yuan Yao possesses this indicates she is no ordinary rogue cultivator but a close disciple (or even more) of the Three Yang Old Devil, the sect’s terrifying leader.

**The Three Yang Old Devil (三阳老魔)**: This is a classic xianxia archetype: the irregular powerhouse. He is a Demonic Path cultivator, but his actions are said to be “erratic,” sometimes righteous and sometimes evil. This makes him unpredictable and dangerous to all established factions. His reputation alone is enough to stay Man Brutal Beard’s hand—a massive statement about his power.

**Nascent Soul Diplomacy**: Notice how the Nascent Soul cultivators speak. There is no shouting. Threats are delivered as matter-of-fact statements. The real negotiations happen via sound transmission (传声), a private communication method impervious to lower-realm eavesdropping. Han Li is constantly kept in the dark, a stark reminder of his place in the food chain.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is essentially Han Li being shown multiple high-level cards from different hands, none of which he can play. Man Brutal Beard, the Three Yang Old Devil, the Profound Bone Saint’s unknown patron, the hidden Star Palace elders—every one of these is a massive, terrifying piece on the board that Han Li can only observe and file away for future reference.

Pay close attention to how he processes this information. He doesn’t panic. He doesn’t wallow. His mind is a cold, swift database query: “Yuan Yao has a big backer. Profound Bone has an alliance. Wu Chou’s fake friendship means my death is scheduled.” He treats each data point as a variable in his survival equation.

The chapter also brilliantly sets up a three-tiered predator hierarchy for the upcoming dungeon crawl:
1. **The Visible Predators**: Extreme Yin, Man Brutal Beard, the Righteous Path elders.
2. **The Hidden Predator**: The Star Palace elders, waiting to spring their trap.
3. **The Scavenger-in-Chief**: Han Li, who knows he’s a piece on their board but is desperately calculating how to escape the game board entirely.

The question is not “Will Han Li fight?” but “Which cage will he choose to survive in, and can he pick the lock before the owner comes back?
