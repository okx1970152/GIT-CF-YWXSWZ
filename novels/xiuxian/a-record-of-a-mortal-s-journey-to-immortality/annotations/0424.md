## Chapter Overview

Alright, buckle up, fellow Daoists. After all that build-up, we finally get to the part where the group stares at a giant, creepy yellow mist dome and tries to look useful. This chapter is all about the pre-heist technical assessment, a classic Mortal Stream "let's see if we can even do this" moment. But don't let the lack of flying swords fool you—this is where the real battle begins: the battle of reputations, face, and pure, cold calculation. We see a proud genius humbled, a cautious protagonist leverage his secret assets, and a group of veteran cultivators realize their muscle is useless against the brain. It’s not a fight yet, but the lines are being drawn, and the hierarchy is shifting.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Expert Assessment Begins:** Hu Yue proposes that Han Li and Shi Die each pick a treasure first if they break the formation. This is a classic negotiation tactic to buy time and sweeten the pot, smoothing over earlier awkwardness.
- **Shi Die's Humiliation Arc Starts:** The arrogant female formation expert is the first to test. She throws a veritable armory of detection gadgets at the yellow mist—discs, crystal balls, copper mirrors—and fails spectacularly. The formation eats her probes for breakfast. Her face is completely lost.
- **Han Li Steps In (Calmly):** After watching Shi Die flounder, Han Li politely asks for a turn. Shi Die, now flustered and angry, snaps at him. This is a crucial social moment; he doesn’t fight her pride, he just lets his ability do the talking.
- **The Big Dipper Two-Circuit Formation:** Han Li whips out a set of high-grade formation flags. Instead of brute-forcing the ancient barrier, he uses *another* formation to attack it from a systemic level. This is a major flex that redefines him in the group's eyes.
- **Cracking the Illusion:** The coordinated attack from Han Li's formation flags causes the yellow mist to boil, churn, and finally *pop* like a soap bubble. The outer mirage is broken.
- **Subduing the Arrogant Expert:** Shi Die, completely humbled, immediately performs a 180. She drops her arrogant tone, addresses Han Li as “Senior,” and asks for guidance. Her respect is genuine because she recognizes true mastery when she sees it.
- **The Real Formation is Revealed:** With the yellow mist gone, the true nature of the barrier is exposed: a faint yellow light shield. The chapter ends on this note of a partial, not total, victory—a classic Mortal Stream reality check.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a masterclass in one of xianxia’s most underrated concepts: **Formations (阵法 – Zhèn Fǎ)**. In Western fantasy, magic is often personal; a wizard casts their own spell. In xianxia, a formation is a pre-set, autonomous magical structure that can sit untouched for millennia, guarding a treasure or a tomb. It’s the difference between a sword and a landmine. Breaking them requires skill, not just power.

Our heroine, Shi Die, learns a hard lesson: the **"Frog at the Bottom of a Well" (井底之蛙)** is a classic Chinese idiom for a small-minded person who thinks they know the world from their tiny corner of experience. She was confident in her skills, only to be utterly outclassed by a "rougher" cultivator (from her perspective) who had a more elegant solution.

And that solution—the **"Big Dipper Two-Circuit Formation" (北斗两仪阵)** —is a beautiful nod to astronomical Daoism. The Big Dipper (北斗 Běidǒu) is a celestial chariot in Chinese mythology, a symbol of universal order and power. Using it as the basis for an anti-formation attack is like solving a complex hacking problem by writing a virus that mimics the operating system's own code. Han Li isn’t just fighting fire with fire; he’s fighting the architect’s blueprints with a different set of blueprints.

## Reading Guide

Keep your eyes on the social dynamics, because they’re more important than the magical mechanics. The group’s initial offer was made in desperate hope. After Han Li’s display, that hope is tempered with *greed* and *respect*. Watch how Jin Qing and Hu Yue’s tone shifts from placating an unknown to genuinely consulting an expert. Also, note how the tall, silent Jian cultivator remains completely opaque. He’s not an egghead; he’s a client, and he’s letting the pawns spend their mana before he shows his hand. That’s textbook Mortal Stream strategy.
