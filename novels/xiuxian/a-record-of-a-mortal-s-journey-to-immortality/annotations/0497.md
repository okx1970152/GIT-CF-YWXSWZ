## Chapter Overview
The showdown in the Void Heaven Palace reaches a brutal crescendo. Wu Chou, the sole descendant of Extreme Yin, tries to use two Tian Du Corpses to corner the Profound Bone Saint—only to be utterly dismantled in seconds. The old ghost’s mastery over the Profound Yin Art is absolute, and he turns Wu Chou to ash with contemptuous ease. But the kill is not an end—it’s a lever. With no one else on the dais, the Profound Bone Saint turns his gaze to Han Li and drops a bombshell: he knows Han Li has a second Blood Jade Spider, and he knows exactly why it matters. A tense negotiation follows, forcing Han Li to weigh the impossible odds of escaping a cage of Nascent Soul politics.

## Key Plot Points
- Wu Chou attempts to use the two Tian Du Corpses to overpower the Profound Bone Saint, but his trump card is rendered useless in moments.
- The Profound Bone Saint demonstrates a perfect counter to the Profound Yin Art—his ghostly claw ignores demonic qi and pierces Wu Chou’s defense without effort.
- Wu Chou is incinerated by ghost-fire. The second Tian Du Corpse goes inert after losing its master.
- Han Li witnesses the entire execution and files away the tactical data, his hand already on his own ancient treasure in case the old ghost turns on him.
- The Profound Bone Saint reveals that he knows Han Li’s secret: the second Blood Jade Spider. He explains the pair-bonding nature of the species, a fact he discovered long ago while observing a wild pair.
- Han Li denies the existence of a second spider at first, but the Profound Bone Saint’s specific knowledge forces him to reconsider.
- Han Li internally calculates his options: helping the old ghost risks drawing every Nascent Soul monster back to the scene; fleeing seems wiser. He starts to leave, but the Profound Bone Saint says something that makes him stop.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Tian Du Corpses (天都妖尸)** are a type of high-grade refined corpse, roughly equivalent to mid-Core Formation cultivators in combat power. In xianxia, corpse-refining is a common demonic-path practice; these are not mindless zombies but semi-autonomous weapons controlled by the master’s spiritual sense. The fact that the Profound Bone Saint can sever that control with a technique shows his deep understanding of the corpse-refining art—or its underlying vulnerabilities.

**Blood Jade Spider (血玉蜘蛛)** pair-bonding is a clever worldbuilding detail that mirrors real-world biology: some spider species require a male-female pair to reach higher ranks. In cultivation fiction, this kind of niche rule turns a seemingly simple asset (a spider pet) into a strategic asset that only works when both halves are alive. It also explains why the Profound Bone Saint never used his own pair before his downfall—they were likely lost or died after Extreme Xuan’s betrayal.

**The Void Heaven Cauldron (虚天鼎)** is the supreme treasure of the Void Heaven Palace, sealed deep within. Its retrieval requires not raw power, but a specific key: a Blood Jade Spider pair strong enough to pull the cauldron’s chains. This reinforces the Mortal Stream principle that victory often depends on exploiting a systemic loophole rather than brute force.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a textbook example of what makes *A Record of a Mortal’s Journey* so addictive in its later arcs. The Profound Bone Saint is not just a powerful undead monster; he is an information differential engine. He knows things no one else does—about the Blood Jade Spiders, about the Profound Yin Art’s weaknesses, about what Extreme Yin’s descendant means as a bargaining chip. And he uses that knowledge with surgical precision.

Han Li’s reaction is equally satisfying: he doesn’t panic, doesn’t beg, doesn’t make heroic threats. He just runs the numbers, decides the risk is too high, and starts to walk away. That’s classic Mortal Stream pragmatism. But the Profound Bone Saint has a better card to play—and whatever he says next will likely force Han Li into a high-stakes gamble he cannot refuse.

Also, note the author’s note at the end: it’s a transparent apology for a chapter numbering error and a notice of reduced updates during Spring Festival. This kind of direct engagement with readers is a hallmark of web novel culture, and translating it faithfully preserves the serialization experience for Western readers.

## Deeper Dive: The Philosophy of the Ghost Claw
The Profound Bone Saint’s attack technique—transforming his hand into a green, then black ghostly claw that simply ignores Profound Yin demonic qi—is not just a cool move. It embodies a core xianxia trope: **perfect counterplay through understanding**. Because he once practiced the same art, he knows exactly where its energy flow is weakest. In this world, knowledge is not just power; it’s an immunity against entire systems of magic. This is why Han Li’s obsessive information gathering is never a waste—it’s his version of the ghost claw.
