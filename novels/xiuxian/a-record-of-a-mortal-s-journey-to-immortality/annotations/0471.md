## Chapter Overview

Alright, folks, gather 'round. This isn't just a rescue scene—it's a masterclass in *Mortal Stream negotiation tactics*. Han Li has just saved the stunning Core Formation cultivator Yuan Yao from a fiery death in the lava zone. But instead of accepting her tearful thanks, what does our favorite cautious bean do? He *turns his back and walks away*. Why? Because he's not running a charity; he's running a business. This chapter is a beautiful, cynical dance of social manipulation where Han Li deliberately strands his own rescuee just to force her to offer him the exact prize he's been eyeing from the start: her Crying Soul Beast. It’s cold, it’s calculated, and it’s *peak* Han Li. The chapter also delivers a crucial lore drop on the Soul-Crying Bead and leaves us with a delicious hint of suspicion—did Han Li just win a prize, or did a clever woman just unload a problem on him?

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Cold Shoulder:** After saving Yuan Yao, Han Li immediately tries to leave. This is a classic "desperate seller" tactic; by devaluing the rescue, he makes her desperate to *pay* for continued protection.
*   **The Bargaining Escalation:** Yuan Yao offers Green Fire Thunders, powerful explosive pellets comparable to a Nascent Soul cultivator's fire. Han Li dismisses them as insufficient, forcing her to ask what he *really* wants.
*   **The Charm Offensive (and Defeat):** Yuan Yao tries a seduction tactic, using her beauty and charm techniques. Han Li deflects this with practiced ease, noting that charming only works when it's safe, and nowhere in the Void Heaven Palace is safe.
*   **The Hard Demand:** After circling the issue, Han Li finally reveals his true goal: the Crying Soul Beast (Ti Hun Shou), a rare spirit beast with soul-devouring and ghost-dissolving abilities. He sees it as a strategic asset to counter the terrifying Profound Bone Saint.
*   **The Suspicious Transfer:** To Han Li’s surprise, Yuan Yao agrees almost *too* quickly. She hands over the beast and the Soul-Crying Bead with a hint of glee, making Han Li pause in suspicion. Is this beast more trouble than it's worth?
*   **The Lingering Question:** The chapter ends on a note of profound ambiguity. Why was Yuan Yao so eager to give it up? Is there a hidden flaw, or did Han Li just get played?

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Desperate Seller” Negotiation (消极谈判战术)**
This entire scene is a textbook example of *Mortal Stream* social warfare. Han Li doesn't just ask for what he wants; he creates a scarcity crisis first. By immediately leaving, he establishes that *his presence* is a valuable, finite resource. This forces Yuan Yao, who has none, to make an offer. It’s a negotiation tactic used by everyone from gangster leaders in wuxia to cultivators in xianxia: making the other party feel they are *asking* for a favor, not *offering* one, gives you all the leverage.

**The Crying Soul Beast and Ghost-Cultivation Counterplay (啼魂兽与鬼修克制之道)**
The Crying Soul Beast (Tí Hún Shòu) isn't just a random pet. In the chaotic world of cultivation, beast tamers and sects often nurture spirit beasts with specific countering abilities. The "Soul-Crying" (啼魂) ability is explicitly a counter to Yin and ghost-type (鬼修) cultivators. Knowing he will have to work with (and inevitably betray) the Profound Bone Saint—a thousand-year-old ghost in a Profound Soul Body—Han Li's desire for this beast is a perfect example of his systematic paranoia: he is already acquiring the counter-weapon before the alliance even begins.

**Charm Techniques and The Untouchable (媚术与钢铁意志)**
Yuan Yao uses her innate beauty and cultivation-enhanced charms to try and sway Han Li. In xianxia, charm techniques (媚术) are a legitimate combat and social tool, often taught by sects specializing in these arts. However, the text makes it clear that these techniques are a calculated move, not genuine emotion. Han Li's immunity to them isn't a sign of being a stoic hero; it's a result of his baseline assumption that *any* pleasing interaction is a trap. In the Mortal Stream, even a beautiful woman's smile is just another resource transaction waiting to be logged.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pure treat for anyone who loves watching a master tactician work. You might be tempted to see Han Li as a jerk for abandoning the woman he just saved, but don't. See it as a game of *Go*. He makes a move (the rescue) and immediately follows it with a calculated retreat to maximize his gains. It’s not about heroism; it’s about *leverage*. Keep a close eye on the very end. Han Li's hesitation is the most important part of the scene. Yuan Yao is too happy to give up the beast. This is a major red flag in the Mortal Stream. If a transaction feels too easy for the *protagonist*, it means the other party probably just swindled him. Is the beast cursed? Does it have a fatal flaw? Or is Yuan Yao just a master of misdirection, making a necessary sacrifice look like a favor? The suspense here is deliciously frustrating. Get ready for a deep dive into beast-taming lore and a potential showdown that our hero is now, slightly suspiciously, better equipped for.
