## Chapter Overview

Alright team, strap in. After several chapters of frantic escape, strategic maneuvering, and a very close brush with death, Chapter 607 sees our favorite paranoid cultivator, Han Li, slamming on the brakes. He’s not running *from* danger here, but finally sitting still long enough to face a new, existential threat: **his pills have stopped working**. This is a classic Mortal Stream reality check. The cheat codes are running out, and Han Li is forced to confront the grind. This chapter is a masterclass in strategic recalibration. We’re not watching a fight; we're watching a CEO audit his company’s assets and decide on a hostile takeover—of a sect’s spirit vein. Get ready for bureaucratic world-building, cold-hearted analysis, and the first seeds of a plan that will define the next major arc of the story.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Pill Problem:** Han Li discovers that the high-grade demonic core pills, which he relied upon to skyrocket his cultivation, have suddenly lost their effectiveness. This is a major crisis for his timeline. He theorizes it could be drug resistance, a side effect of the Green Flame Wine, or the Spiritual Communion Energy he got from Mei Ning.
- **The Resource Horizon:** With pills off the table, Han Li faces the grim reality of slow, traditional cultivation. He calculates it will take a decade of steady work to reach the peak of the Core Formation stage. This forces a change in strategy from a "resource extractor" to a "resource occupant."
- **The Heavenly South Real Estate Market:** Han Li analyzes the fundamental difference between the Heavenly South and the Chaotic Star Sea. In the star sea, spirit veins are abundant. In the Heavenly South, prime spiritual real estate is completely monopolized by sects and families, leaving nothing for a lone rogue cultivator.
- **The "Corporate Infiltrator" Strategy:** Han Li decides his best option is to join a major sect to enjoy the safety and resources of a high-quality spirit vein, hiding under their "big tree" until he reaches the Nascent Soul stage.
- **The Target is Set:** His eyes land on the Yunmeng Mountain, the most famous spiritual vein in the Heavenly South, controlled by three major sects. He begins analyzing the current geopolitical landscape to decide which one to infiltrate.
- **The Century of Peace:** Through a narrated history dump, we learn of the current balance of power. The four great powers (Righteous Dao, Devil Dao, Heavenly Alliance, and Nine Nations Alliance) are locked in a tense peace. The Nine Nations Alliance, after a massive war against the Moulan Dharma Warriors, successfully pressured the other three powers into a mutual defense treaty. This stasis is why there have been no major wars in over a century.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Alright, class, let's get into the nitty-gritty of how this universe's economy actually works, beyond just "step one: find treasure; step two: stab people."

**The "Pill Ceiling" and the Bottleneck Economy:** This chapter perfectly exemplifies the concept of a **Bottleneck Economy**. In the Mortal Stream, a cultivator's progression isn't a smooth curve. It's a series of brutal vertical walls. Pills and external aids are the climbing gear that let you scale those walls quickly. But the gear wears out. Han Li's pills have "leveled off." This isn't a bug; it's a feature of the system. The world is designed so that pure resource extraction (hunting demon beasts for pills) has a hard limit. To break through to the next major realm (Nascent Soul), you need a qualitative change in strategy, not just more of the same input.

**The "Big Tree Under Which to Cool Off" (大树底下好乘凉):** This is a classic Chinese proverb. For a cultivator who has been hunted for his treasures, the value of institutional protection is immense. Han Li isn't looking for a sect for "friendship" or "shared ideals." He is looking for a **spirit vein lease** and a **security shield**. This perfectly maps to his character: he treats a sect as a resource provider, not a family. He plans to use them for a decade, get his Nascent Soul, and then re-evaluate his relationship with them from a position of supreme power.

**The Balance of Powers:** This section gives us the realpolitik of the Heavenly South. The three major powers (Righteous, Devil, Heavenly Alliance) are locked in a cold war. The fourth power, the Nine Nations Alliance, functions as a **communal shield wall** against an external enemy (the Moulan). This dynamic is a direct echo of the Cold War-style "mutually assured destruction." The external threat keeps the internal factions from going to war. Han Li is trying to decide which "nation" to apply for a visa to, fully aware that the peace is fragile and could collapse at any moment.

### Bottleneck Economy

In the Mortal Stream, cultivation is not a smooth journey but a series of brutal walls. Progress is often gated by specific resources or conditions, forcing a constant strategic recalibration.

### Guardian Dharma Warriors

A school of spellcraft and body cultivation practiced by the Moulan tribes, a major external threat to the Heavenly South's Nine Nations Alliance.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a palate cleanser, a strategic planning session, and a massive lore dump all in one. If you came here for flashy action, you might be a little bored. But if you're here for the *real* action—the cold, calculated maneuvering that defines a true Mortal Stream protagonist—this is pure gold.

Pay close attention to how Han Li processes the bad news. He feels a moment of regret ("wry smile") and then immediately moves to problem-solving. There's no rage, no despair, no dramatic "woe is me" moment. He simply audits his assets, diagnoses the problem, and devises a new plan. This is the core of his character.

Also, enjoy the world-building. This is the clearest picture we've gotten of the *entire* Heavenly South theater of operations. We learn about the Nine Nations Alliance's origin, the Moulan threat, and the fragile peace that has held for a century. This isn't just background noise; it's the political map Han Li will be navigating for the foreseeable future.

Get ready. The "Infiltrate a Major Sect" arc is about to begin. And if you know Han Li, he's not going to just join one; he's going to game the system from day one.
