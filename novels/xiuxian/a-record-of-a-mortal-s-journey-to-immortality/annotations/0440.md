## Chapter Overview
Welcome back, fellow Daoists, to the high-stakes poker game that is the Void Heaven Palace! This chapter is all about information asymmetry, fragile alliances, and the cold calculus of survival. Our cautious herb-peddler Han Li is caught between two ancient monsters—one who wants him dead for unknown reasons (Extreme Yin), and one who wants him as a partner for a spectacular act of patricide (the Profound Bone Saint). It's a masterclass in the Mortal Stream art of saying "yes" while keeping one hand on your escape route. Get ready for a chapter where the real battle is fought through whispers, deals, and the silent reading of micro-expressions.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li assesses the floating palace as a high-risk, high-reward environment, explicitly comparing it to the Blood Forbidden Trial. His instinct screams "retreat," but the three-hundred-year opportunity window keeps him rooted.
- The Profound Bone Saint breaks his silence with a mocking observation: Extreme Yin's expression means Han Li is marked for trouble—and the old ghost enjoys the prospect.
- Han Li fires back with a veiled threat to expose the Profound Bone Saint's presence, establishing a cold-war dynamic between them. The Saint is furious but immediately recalculates.
- The Profound Bone Saint drops the bombshell: he knows of a way to greatly increase the odds of forming a Nascent Soul, and the key is inside the palace. He proposes an alliance to eliminate Extreme Yin.
- Han Li does not accept immediately. He stalls, thinks, and only agrees after extracting a concrete promise (the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng at the second barrier) and a partial explanation.
- Extreme Yin Patriarch's behavior is closely observed: even when coldly snubbed by the female cultivator Wen, he maintains a smiling, unruffled facade. This deepens Han Li's wariness toward him.
- The chapter ends with a quiet victory of logistics: Han Li extracts basic intelligence about the Void Heaven Palace from his new "ally," then settles into cultivation, having confirmed that high-level magic is suppressed inside the hall.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The **Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng (九曲灵参)** is a classic xianxia trope: a legendary spiritual herb so infused with qi that it gains a primitive intelligence, taking the form of animals and burrowing through earth to escape harvesters. In Chinese folk medicine cosmology, "spirit ginseng" is the ultimate extension of ginseng's real-world reputation as a panacea—here escalated to a sentient, self-moving treasure. The "nine bends" in its name often allude to the tortuous, layered curvature of high-quality ginseng roots, but also imply the difficulty of capturing it. The Profound Bone Saint's claim that it can boost Nascent Soul formation is a classic lure in the Mortal Stream: a specific, plausible, but unverifiable promise designed to turn a cautious observer into a committed ally.

The **Void Heaven Palace (虚天殿)** itself is a quintessential "ancient ruin" dungeon. In xianxia, such sites are always built by "primordial cultivators" (古修士) whose power and knowledge exceed the modern era's. The internal suppression of magic—allowing only minor spells—is a common design: it levels the playing field and forces confrontation to happen on other axes (social maneuvering, timing, trap knowledge) rather than pure combat power. Han Li's instinct to "retreat" vs. his greed for "opportunity" is the exact tension every such dungeon is built to exploit.

The **Profound Bone Saint's (玄骨上人)** status as a "thousand-year-old ghost" who sacrificed his physical form and reincarnation for revenge is a dark xianxia subversion of the Daoist pursuit of immortality. In classical Daoism, the soul's integrity is paramount; scattering one's Nascent Soul to extend existence is a desperate, heterodox act that permanently closes the door to any cosmic reset. This makes his hatred for Extreme Yin not just emotional but existential—his very state of being is a monument to that betrayal.

## Reading Guide
Pay close attention to the rhythm of negotiation between Han Li and the Profound Bone Saint. It's not a simple "yes" or "no." Han Li first **resists** (veiled threat), then **listens carefully without commitment**, then **demands evidence** (the second-barrier capture), and only then **accepts with escape clauses** in mind. This is the textbook Mortal Stream alliance formation: never trust, only verify—and always assume the other party is feeding you a version of the truth optimized for their goals. The fact that Han Li immediately starts probing for palace information after the deal is a subtle but important character beat: he is turning a potential leash into a data pipeline. Also worth noting: the Profound Bone Saint's offer includes an **unspoken loss**—the ginseng is the carrot, but the Saint's own "must-have" request (future assistance) is the hidden price tag. This asymmetry is where the next betrayal will likely come from.
