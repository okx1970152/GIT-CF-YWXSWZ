## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream tension-building: no explosions, no fights, just a room full of powerful cultivators whose histories, grudges, and agendas are already writing the script for the next bloodbath. Han Li enters the floating Void Heaven Palace’s main hall and immediately becomes a spider in a web of hostile and calculating gazes. The Profound Bone Saint wants to partner up. The Extreme Yin Ancestor shows up with a barely concealed look of delight upon seeing Han Li. An old enemy from Six Serenity Hall glares murder at him. Meanwhile, two true Nascent Soul seniors casually drop lore bombs: this palace opens only once every three hundred years, Barbarian Beard is on his way, and there’s a fruit inside that can extend lifespan. Han Li, who came in blind, now understands he has stumbled into a high-stakes treasure hunt where every smiling face hides a knife.

## Key Plot Points

1. Han Li scans the hall and recognizes the Profound Bone Saint, Fairy Ziling (who is accompanied by a mysterious green-robed man), and Elder Miao of Six Serenity Hall, who burns with murderous intent.
2. The Profound Bone Saint sends Han Li a transmission proposing a partnership for the treasure hunt—an offer Han Li neither accepts nor rejects outright, but keeps open.
3. The Extreme Yin Ancestor enters with Wu Chou, and Han Li’s internal alarm bells ring: he identifies the ancestor as the same being who once possessed Wu Chou.
4. The Extreme Yin Ancestor scans the room, lets out a cold sneer at a sallow-faced cultivator, then turns to Han Li with a look of barely concealed delight—a reaction that deeply unsettles Han Li and piques the Profound Bone Saint’s curiosity.
5. The Extreme Yin Ancestor exchanges pleasantries with two Nascent Soul seniors: a bookish old scholar (Senior Qing) and an icy beauty (Lady Wen). From their conversation, key lore drops: the palace opens once every 300 years, and Barbarian Beard (a feared Nascent Soul) is coming for Longevity Fruit.
6. Han Li realizes he is out of his depth but resolves to adapt on the fly—classic Mortal Stream survival logic: information is scarce, threats are everywhere, and every ally is a potential blade.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Void Heaven Palace & the 300-Year Cycle (虚天殿与三百年之约):** This is a classic xianxia dungeon setup: an ancient, sealed location that opens only at fixed intervals. The “once every three hundred years” timer creates a natural pressure cooker—multiple generations of cultivators cannot wait for the next opening, so the current batch must make their move now or lose the opportunity forever. It also explains why Nascent Soul seniors, who normally avoid each other, are willing to converge in the same space. The timer overrides their caution.

**Barbarian Beard & Longevity Fruit (蛮胡子与寿元果):** The name “Barbarian Beard” (蛮胡子) is a classic xianxia handle that combines “barbarian/fierce” (蛮) with “beard” (胡子), suggesting a rough, battle-hardened elder. His goal—Longevity Fruit to refine Longevity Pills—reinforces the Mortal Stream’s core theme: even the most powerful cultivators are driven by scarcity (lifespan). Immortality is not guaranteed; it must be fought for at every stage.

**Extreme Yin Ancestor & the “Look of Delight” (极阴祖师与“惊喜”):** The Extreme Yin Ancestor’s reaction to Han Li is a standout moment. In a normal story, a high-level villain spotting a low-level protagonist would be hostility or contempt. Here, it’s delight—an expression that signals interest, not threat. This subverts expectation and plants the seed that Han Li is not a random target but someone the Ancestor has been actively looking for. This is classic Mortal Stream setup: the protagonist is never “lucky” to catch a villain’s interest; it’s always a sign that a trap is being laid.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a positional poker hand, not a brawl. Pay close attention to who is looking at whom, and for how long. The Profound Bone Saint’s instant shift from cold stillness to active recruitment tells you he is cornered and desperate. The Extreme Yin Ancestor’s hidden delight tells you he has plans for Han Li that go beyond simple murder. Elder Miao’s undisguised hatred tells you he is emotionally compromised and will make a mistake. And the two Nascent Soul seniors, chatting casually about Longevity Fruit and Barbarian Beard, are providing the meta-lore that will dictate the stage for the entire palace arc. For Han Li, this chapter is about information triage: he has no power here, but if he can file away every glance and every dropped hint, he might survive long enough to find an angle. The key takeaway: in the Void Heaven Palace, the real battle hasn’t started yet—but the seating arrangements have already decided who will be sacrificed first.
