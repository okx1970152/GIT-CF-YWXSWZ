## Chapter Overview
After Chen Qiaoqian’s direct interrogation, Han Li maintains a flawless facade of innocence, forcing her to retreat with cold, quiet dignity. The chapter quickly pivots to the eve of battle: Han Li sends away the Meng Mountain brothers with practical gifts and a veiled warning, and the squad assembles for the night raid—only to discover that Sixth Brother Wu Xuan has mysteriously vanished. A minor internal mystery deepens before the main storm.

## Key Plot Points
- Chen Qiaoqian presses Han Li on his whereabouts during the Blood Forbidden Trial; he denies everything with calm precision, and she retreats without breaking his story.
- Han Li dismisses the four Meng Mountain rogue cultivators from the upcoming battle, gifting them superior-grade tools, and the dark-faced elder privately hints they know their “Fifth Sister” (Lü Huarong) will not reappear.
- The gathered Yellow Maple Valley squad finds one member missing: Wu Xuan has not returned to his inn since the previous morning, with his belongings still there.
- The chapter ends with Liu Jing deciding to proceed with the raid, setting aside the disappearance for later.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Silent Purge:** In the Mortal Stream, characters who become liabilities or loose ends rarely get dramatic farewells. Wu Xuan’s sudden disappearance is not yet a confirmed death, but the narrative treats it with the same cold functionalism as a broken tool—the team simply adjusts its headcount and pushes forward. This “disappear without closure” trope is a hallmark of the genre’s refusal to grant every character a sentimental exit.

**Gifts as Social Leashes:** Han Li’s decision to give superior-grade tools rather than top-grade ones is a deliberate psychological calculation. In cultivation culture, an oversized gift creates suspicion and obligation; a “generous but not exceptional” gift binds the receiver in gratitude without awakening greed. The old man’s parting confession (“We all knew.”) shows that even overt manipulation, when wrapped in plausible face-giving, is accepted as the natural order of a senior’s management.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a classic **breather before the storm**—the last minutely of calm before a high-risk operation. Its real weight lies in how the story chooses to spend this screen: not on rousing speeches or flashy equipment checks, but on quiet interpersonal resolution (Chen Qiaoqian’s retreat), asset management (the Meng Mountain farewell), and a tiny omen of trouble (Wu Xuan’s disappearance). The Mortal Stream novel trusts the reader to register these as the true currency of survival—loose ties cut, loose ends noted, and the objective kept coldly in sight. Watch how Han Li’s most important move in this chapter is *not* preparing a technique or a treasure, but calculating the right level of generosity for men he may one day call upon again.
