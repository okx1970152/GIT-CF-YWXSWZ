## Chapter Overview
*The masks come off, the puppets reveal their strings, and the real predator steps into the light.* This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream escalation: what starts as a tense three-way standoff among cultivators rapidly collapses into a single, horrifying revelation. Wu Chou’s casual confession of the entire conspiracy is just the appetizer. The main course? A high-stakes family drama between a demonic sect heir and his “uncle-master” that ends with a classic xianxia twist—**Possession Art**. The seemingly brash young heir is suddenly replaced by an ancient, narrowed-eyed nightmare: Extreme Yin Ancestor himself, using his grandson’s body as a living puppet. And here’s the kicker—that confident uncle-master? He’s starting to look a lot less confident. Get ready for a cold, calculating chess match between a cornered master and a rebellious disciple.

## Key Plot Points
- Zi Ling coldly exposes the traitors, pointing a finger at Elder Zhao and the other Sublime Sound Sect elder who had been pretending to be victims.
- Madam Fan’s world collapses: she realizes the elders she bribed and seduced to secure her position have betrayed her for a stronger master—Exreme Yin Island.
- Chi Huo, the old fire-monster lurking in the clouds, detects a hidden observer and unleashes a lightning attack, forcing **Wu Chou** to reveal himself.
- Without hesitation, Wu Chou admits to everything: hijacking the cargo, framing the Hidden Kill Sect, and bribing the elders. His lecherous focus then turns to Zi Ling.
- Zi Ling’s chilling counteroffer (“Kill everyone who murdered my mother, and I’ll marry you”) shuts down his advance with surgical precision—she knows she’s negotiating from a position of anger, not power.
- The middle-aged leader of the Hidden Kill Sect is suddenly addressed as “Uncle-Master Sun” by Wu Chou, revealing a hidden master-disciple relationship from Extreme Yin Island’s past.
- The real bomb drops: Wu Chou’s appearance twists and morphs into **Extreme Yin Ancestor** himself, using the *Possession Art* to control his grandson’s body as a vessel.
- The Ancestor reveals he is not at full power (only one-third), but the fact that he *chose* to attend in person signals this is no ordinary operation—the stakes involve the Heavenly Void Hall and a fragment of its legendary map.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter provides an excellent entry point to discuss one of the most terrifying and deeply-rooted concepts in Chinese xianxia: **Possession (夺舍/Duòshè)**. Unlike Western notions of “spirit possession” which often involve exorcism or a demonic influence, xianxia possession is a cold, calculated act of **identity theft and body hijacking** by a superior cultivator. The soul of a high-level practitioner—often an Ancient Elder at the Nascent Soul stage or beyond whose physical body is failing—forcefully overrides and erases the soul of a lower-level cultivator, stealing their flesh as a new vessel. This is not a battle of faith; it is a brutal, one-sided war of spiritual might.

The *Possession Art* shown here is a tactical variant: Extreme Yin Ancestor hasn’t permanently claimed his grandson’s body. He has temporarily “ridden” it, suppressing Wu Chou’s consciousness to project a fraction of his own terrifying power across a great distance. This is a show of overwhelming force and control—a way to personally oversee a critical operation (recovering the Heavenly Void Map fragment) without leaving his main body vulnerable. It reveals a fundamental truth of the Dark Forest: in the highest echelons of power, even family ties are just another vector for control.

## Reading Guide
- **Pay attention to the power dynamic shift.** When the Ancestor speaks, his voice comes from his abdomen, not his lips. This is a deliberate, uncanny detail the author uses to signal that the being inside that body is *not human anymore*—it is a higher existence deigning to use mortal vocal cords.
- **Madam Fan’s despair is your emotional barometer.** She represents the “managerial class” of the cultivation world—people who play politics and think alliances are built on favors and bodies. This chapter shows her system cracking under the weight of someone who operates on absolute force.
- **The middle-aged “Uncle-Master” Sun is a walking tragedy.** He fled Extreme Yin Island decades ago to escape his master’s tyranny. He thought he had freedom. He thought he had power. This chapter reveals he has merely been living on borrowed time, and his past has finally caught up with him. His tremor of fear when the Ancestor appears is the reaction of a prey animal that knows a predator has locked onto its scent.
- **Han Li is doing exactly what he should be doing: staying quiet, staying at the back, and gathering information.** He hasn’t even confirmed if the Divine Thunder Bamboo rumor is real yet. His hesitation is survival, not cowardice. In a chapter where everyone’s mask is being ripped off, the one who keeps his mask on the longest will win.
