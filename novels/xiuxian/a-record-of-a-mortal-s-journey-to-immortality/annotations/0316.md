## Chapter Overview
After a half-month of eerie calm following the brutal fight, both Han Li and the Black Fiend Sect seem to have agreed on a silent ceasefire. But during his meditation, Han Li’s finely honed senses detect several Foundation Establishment cultivators lurking outside his door. Before he can trigger a desperate escape, a familiar voice cuts through the tension: his reinforcements have finally arrived. This chapter is a quiet but vital pivot—a strategic breather where the demonic side reveals its internal calculations, and Han Li gets the first real backup from his own sect since arriving in the capital.

## Key Plot Points
* The Black Fiend Sect Master analyzes the failed mission with cold pragmatism: he does not punish Tie Luo, recognizing that fighting a Talisman Treasure-wielding enemy was simply beyond his subordinate’s capacity.
* The Sect Master deduces Han Li’s puppet technique is likely the Thousand Bamboo School’s art, but decides not to escalate—he orders his disciples to pull back to the palace and wait for his technique to reach completion.
* Han Li sends out his intelligence reports and waits; the demonic path cultivators remain suspiciously absent, confirming his fears about a potential alliance did not materialize.
* While meditating, Han Li senses multiple Foundation Establishment cultivators with concealment techniques outside his residence, immediately triggering his escape protocols.
* The hidden visitors turn out to be Fourth Senior Brother Song Meng, along with Third Senior Brother and Seventh Senior Sister—the first direct sect reinforcements to reach Han Li since the crisis began.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
A frequently overlooked worldbuilding detail in this chapter is the strategic value of **blood sacrifices (血祭)** for demonic cultivation. The Black Fiend Sect Master is nearing a breakthrough but still needs a Foundation Establishment cultivator’s essence to speed things up. Once he completes his technique, he claims he will fear no one below Core Formation—a dramatic power gap that reinforces why demonic sects relentlessly hunt high-quality prey. The blood sacrifice isn’t just cruelty; it is a *mechanism* that allows a cultivator to skip decades of normal cultivation by burning living beings as fuel. This is a core xianxia trope: the most efficient path to power is often the most monstrous one.

Also worth noting is **Tie Luo’s Demonic Transformation (化妖术)**, a skill that turns the user into a demonic form at the cost of vitality and self-control. In the lore of many cultivation novels, such transformations trade permanent damage for temporary combat superiority—and are considered a last resort for the desperate.

## Reading Guide
This chapter lives in the tension between two parallel calculations. On the demonic side, we see a coldly intelligent Sect Master who values logistical efficiency over revenge—he doesn’t punish failure, he debriefs it. On the protagonist’s side, we get a rare moment of legitimate relief when the unfamiliar auras outside the door turn out to be *friends*, not enemies. For any long-time reader of *RMJI*, this is suspiciously nice. Are we due for a sudden reversal? Maybe. But for now, savor the quiet moment—Han Li finally has back-up, and the sect’s senior disciples are about to learn exactly how troublesome their introverted eighth junior brother has been.
