## Chapter Overview

The calm before the storm finally breaks. Chapter 261 masterfully orchestrates a multi-layered thriller, cutting between Han Li’s quiet, suspicious observation within Yanling Fort, the meticulous preparation of a Ghost Spirit Sect ambush on the western peak, and a sudden, seismic shift in the entire cultivation world back in the Yue Kingdom. While Han Li remains focused on the personal dangers of the Treasure-Seizing Assembly, the reader is granted chilling omniscience: a trap is being set, and the entire nation is sliding toward war. The tension is no longer a matter of winning or losing a competition, but of who will survive the slaughter.

## Key Plot Points

- **Han Li’s analysis of the possessed woman:** Han Li is baffled by what happened to Dong Xuan’er. He knows that using a basic Celestial Eye Technique to control someone requires a massive realm gap, not a mere sub-stage difference. He’s deeply unsettled, suspecting either a Core Formation cultivator is hiding his cultivation or something far stranger is at play. His relief is temporary and fragile.
- **Dong Xuan’er’s return to form:** After her ordeal, Dong Xuan’er immediately falls back into her flirtatious, manipulative routine with the male Yellow Maple Valley disciples. Han Li sighs and withdraws, his wariness fully reignited.
- **The Assembly Announcement:** An anonymous voice announces the rules for the Treasure-Seizing Assembly: cultivators of the Yue Kingdom will gather on the west peak, foreigners on the east peak. A seemingly sensible separation that Han Li accepts without fuss, but which is clearly a setup for a targeted massacre.
- **The Ghost Spirit Sect’s trap:** The Ghost Spirit Sect’s Young Master, along with the Li brothers (two Core Formation cultivators), is overseeing the burial of a weakened Yin Flame Array on the western peak. They plan to activate the formation once everyone is inside, killing all the Yue Kingdom cultivators while preserving their souls and primordial spirits for the sect’s evil arts.
- **The missing attendees and pursuit:** Two cultivators fail to show up. The Young Master, without hesitation, orders the Ghost Spirit Twelve Guards to hunt them down and kill them to prevent any information leak.
- **The Yue Kingdom erupts:** A massive shift happens thousands of miles away. The Seven Great Sects are mobilizing. Core Formation elders, including Li Huayuan and Fairy Hongfu, are seen receiving urgent orders. The entire cultivation world is preparing for a full-scale war, and conscription orders are sent to every clan, threatening extermination for disobedience. The Yan family, having already fled, is the only one spared this threat.
- **Han Li’s unsettling intuition:** On the morning of the Assembly, Han Li ignores the forced camaraderie of the other cultivators. He chooses a remote corner, farthest from the magic formation, and watches with cold suspicion. He feels a deep, instinctive unease from the formation that logic cannot explain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- **Soul Confusion Arts and the Celestial Eye:** The text mentions that the “Celestial Eye Technique” is not just a basic perception spell but also a primitive form of mind-control. In xianxia, many foundational techniques have dual or hidden combat applications. Here, Han Li’s wariness stems from a disciple of the Moon-Masking Sect seemingly using it to instantly control another cultivator. The insight is that the power of such arts scales with the *difference* in cultivation level. A small gap (Early vs. Mid Foundation Establishment) should result in resistance, not instant submission, which is exactly why Han Li smells a rat. This reinforces the rule that in this world, an unexpected outcome is rarely a coincidence and is almost always a sign of a deeper, more dangerous power at play.
- **The Ghost Spirit Sect’s Means:** The Ghost Spirit Sect is preparing a “Yin Flame Array” to trap and kill their enemies. While the exact nature of this array is new, the principle is a standard xianxia trope: a demonic sect will go to great lengths to collect souls and essence. The Li brothers, the grotesque pair of one ancient and one childlike Core Formation cultivators, are a deliberate visual of how demonic cultivation can warp the physical form. Their presence underscores that this is not a low-stakes skirmish but a surgical strike by a major power.
- **The “Chicken Rib” and “Two-Sided Fear”:** While not directly named in this chapter, the underlying dynamics are built on these previously established concepts. The formation is a “chicken rib” to the Li brothers—a tool too weak for their power, but more than enough for their prey. The standoff between Han Li and the Ghost Spirit Sect is a textbook “Two-Sided Fear”: the sect doesn’t know Han Li is a variable, and Han Li doesn’t yet know the full scale of the trap. This mutual ignorance is the only thing keeping the field from immediate chaos.
- **Conscription Orders as Sect Power:** The scene of the Seven Great Sects issuing a joint conscription order to every clan in the nation, with death as the penalty for refusal, is a stark illustration of feudal authoritarianism in a xianxia world. The sects are not just schools; they are governing bodies with the power of life and death over every mortal and minor cultivation family. The Yan family’s flight is not just an escape; it is a declaration of rebellion against the entire established order, whether they intended it or not.

## Reading Guide

Alright, buckle up. This chapter is where the thriller-level pacing truly kicks in. Forget a simple treasure hunt—we’re now watching a *massacre* being set up in real-time while the entire cultivation world lights up with war drums.

Pay close attention to Han Li’s internal conflict. He can *feel* something is wrong. That nagging intuition that makes him drift to the farthest corner of the assembly area is what separates a survivor from a corpse in this world. He doesn’t have the facts—he has *vibes*. And he trusts them. This is a masterclass in Han Li’s “Mortal Stream” survival instinct.

The real treat here is the narrative parallax. We, the readers, get to see the Ghost Spirit Sect setting the trap, the Yue Kingdom mobilizing for war, and the foolhardy cultivators at Yanling Fort walking right into the net. It creates a deliciously agonizing tension. You want to scream at the characters to run!

This is the classic “the world is bigger than the local event” moment that RMJI does so well. The battle at Yanling Fort is about to become a small, messy footnote in a much larger war that’s breaking out all across the country. Get ready for a climactic explosion in the next few chapters.
