## Chapter Overview
The fragile calm of the Soul Reunion Art is shattered. Han Li’s forbidden ritual has attracted not just the local demon beasts, but something far more dangerous: a patronizing, godlike youth named Wen Tianren, who arrives in a flying beast chariot with a beautiful, frosty companion. While Han Li is busy dispatching a sneak attacker with clinical precision, he makes a terrifying discovery: this newcomer possesses divine sense on par with a Nascent Soul cultivator. The chapter ends with the heavy silence of two apex predators sensing each other across the island, each aware that the other is no ordinary opponent. The hunt has just become a two-way street.

## Key Plot Points
- A flying beast chariot and a group of Foundation Establishment female cultivators arrive at the island; the carriage’s master, Wen Tianren, identifies the celestial phenomenon as a Heaven-defying art (the Soul Reunion Art) rather than a treasure.
- Wen Tianren reveals his motive for the journey: he is tracking the Ghost Mist by correlating historical data with recent volcanic eruptions, proving himself a patient, methodical schemer, not just a powerful brute.
- He casually discusses recruiting the island’s caster as an Enforcement Envoy for his Divine Pigeon Hall, revealing a transactional, power-broker mindset.
- His companion, a cold, gorgeous girl, is clearly under his thumb; she dares not refuse him, but maintains a frosty distance, hinting at a complex hostage-like dynamic.
- On the island, Han Li’s Gold Devourer Beetles, still in the shape of the three-colored spear, ambush and kill a sneak attacker who was hiding in the rubble. The kill is instant and surgical.
- Han Li scans the incoming cultivators and detects Wen Tianren’s monstrous divine sense, realizing the newcomer is a late Core Formation cultivator of exceptional power—a true rival.
- Wen Tianren immediately senses Han Li’s probe and erects an anti-divine sense barrier, confirming he is acutely aware of the threat.
- The chapter ends with Han Li growing grim, recognizing that the isolated island’s quiet victory phase is over; a major player has entered the board.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**Ghost Mist (鬼雾)** is one of the Chaotic Star Sea’s most infamous natural disasters—a roaming, malevolent fog that devours cultivators and beasts whole. In xianxia, such phenomena are often less random than they appear; a powerful cultivator like Wen Tianren treating them as trackable data is a hallmark of the Mortal Stream’s “world as a system” logic. **Soul Reunion Art (还魂术)** is a major taboo technique: it allows a practitioner to call back a soul and restore a life, but at a severe cost—dropping an entire cultivation realm is common. Performing it marks the caster as either desperate or wealthy enough to absorb the setback, which immediately makes them a target for recruitment or predation. The flying beast chariot and a private guard of Foundation Establishment maidens are extreme displays of wealth; in the Chaotic Star Sea’s frontier economy, such a show signals that Wen Tianren is not just powerful but backed by an enormous institution or clan.

## Reading Guide
Get ready, fellow Daoists—the quiet refining arc just got a whole lot louder. Wen Tianren is the kind of villain you love to hate: the smug, overpowered prince who thinks he owns the world. The chapter’s real tension isn’t in the one-sided beetle kill (a nice flex from Han Li), but in the cold war of divine sense that follows. Wen Tianren notices Han Li, and Han Li knows he’s been noticed. Neither panics, but both understand that the rules of engagement have changed. This is textbook Mortal Stream escalation: the protagonist doesn’t roll a new boss by accident; the boss arrives because the protagonist’s actions (casting a Heaven-defying art) broke the local silence. Keep an eye on the mysterious, cold girl in the carriage—she’s the hostage, the tension gauge, and possibly the wildcard.
