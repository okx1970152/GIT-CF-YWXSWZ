## Chapter Overview

Welcome to the *second* half of the Falling Cloud Sect’s casting call, fellow daoists! Last time, we watched the elites get whisked away. Now, it’s the turn of a different breed of cultivator. This chapter is all about the brutal, quiet sorting machine of the sect itself. It’s not flashy, but it’s a perfect microcosm of the Mortal Stream’s core logic: you are evaluated, classified, and slotted into the hierarchy based on cold, hard numbers. Han Li steps into the “high-layer” batch, along with a handful of others, and immediately his paranoia reflexes kick in as he scans his new peers. The chapter ends without a fight, but the tension is all there in the quiet, tactical observation. Get ready for a masterclass in reading a room and evaluating the value of a new asset—yourself.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Drawbridge Goes Up:** Senior Qiu formalizes the filtering process. Those with techniques at the tenth layer or above—regardless of age or root quality—get a fast track to sect entry. The rest face a nearly insurmountable test. The sect’s efficiency in discarding the “unworthy” is laid bare.
*   **Han Li’s Promotion:** Han Li steps into the tenth-layer-plus group, alongside five other cultivators and a quiet woman in yellow. He is no longer a desperate, talentless boy; he is a *qualified applicant* in a system that values results.
*   **The Scouting Report:** As they fly over the sect, Han Li’s gaze sweeps over his fellow candidates. He notes their ages, their excitement, and their flaws. This is not idle curiosity; it’s a rapid threat assessment and resource audit on a moving platform.
*   **A Glimpse of the Undercurrent:** Han Li’s spiritual sense detects a faint, sinister coldness on the burly, bearded man. This is a key moment of tactical insight: Han Li knows the man is hiding a special, likely dangerous technique beneath the disguise of a standard cultivation base. The mask has a crack.
*   **The Grand Entrance:** The group passes through the sect’s massive, dangerous-looking mist barrier. It is a clear statement of power and isolation. They finally emerge into the “paradise” of the Six Marvellous Peaks: the sect’s core territory.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The “Bottleneck Economy” is on full display here. The Falling Cloud Sect isn’t looking for *geniuses* in this batch; they’re looking for *mature, reliable assets*. A cultivator stuck at the tenth layer for decades but with a stable, proven technique is a far safer investment than a flashy new genius who might peak and burnout in five years. The sect’s calculus is about long-term resource allocation, not just raw potential. Also, Han Li’s instant profiling of the bearded man is a classic example of the Dark Forest rule: trust nothing at face value. A cultivator’s public cultivation level is just their opening bid. The real value is in the hidden cards they’re holding.

### Bottleneck Economy

A systemic reality in cultivation worlds where progress is gated by scarce resources, forcing constant strategic shifts and preventing linear growth. The sect's selection process is a direct expression of this economy.

## Reading Guide

*   **Watch the Watchman:** Pay close attention to how Han Li’s internal monologue works. He doesn’t “think” about the other candidates. He *audits* them. He doesn’t “wonder” about the bearded man. He *files a tactical note*. This is the mindset of a survivor.
*   **The System is the Enemy:** The sect’s bureaucracy isn’t a neutral force. It’s a testing ground for your ability to game the rules. Han Li’s entire life has been a series of bypasses and optimizations to exploit the Bottleneck Economy. This selection process is just the next level of that game.
*   **The Quiet Test:** The most important event here is the arrival at the mist barrier. This is the sect showing off its power and setting a psychological trap. Han Li’s response—testing it with his spiritual sense and *immediately* withdrawing when blocked—is a perfect example of the “probe, don’t commit” survival rule. He learns its limits without drawing its wrath.
