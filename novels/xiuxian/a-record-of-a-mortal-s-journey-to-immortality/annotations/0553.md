## Chapter Overview

Buckle up, fellow Daoists, because this chapter is a masterclass in the Mortal Stream's unique brand of "the breakthrough that happens *despite* you." Han Li isn't charging a heavenly tribulation or having a dramatic epiphany. Instead, he's stuck in a demon lord's basement, frantically trying to digest a suspicious magic wine before his deadline runs out, only to stumble into the late Core Formation stage because *a different life-risking treasure nearly exploded inside him*. It’s tense, it’s accidental, it’s absolutely pragmatic, and it perfectly encapsulates why Han Li is not your typical protagonist.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li tests the coral wall's restrictions with his Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords and Evil-Crushing Divine Lightning, only to find it layered with a powerful, five-colored barrier immune to his attacks.
- He considers using his Gold Devourer Beetles to eat through the wall, but calculates the time required would be too long, giving the demon cultivator Feng Xi ample opportunity to catch him.
- Obedient but anxious, Han Li begins the arduous months-long process of refining the Azure Flame Wine.
- To speed things up and meet Feng Xi's half-year deadline, Han Li risks burning his own mana at double speed, supplementing his energy with precious Ten-Thousand-Year Spirit Milk.
- The true crisis arrives when the Dry Blue Ice Pearl, still stored in his stomach, becomes unstable, threatening to explode with his mana completely drained.
- In a desperate gamble, Han Li uses the remaining Azure Flame Wine to wrap the unstable pearl, which miraculously stabilizes it, likely saving his life.
- In the chaotic aftermath of this near-death experience, Han Li discovers he has unexpectedly broken into the late Core Formation stage.
- After stabilizing his cultivation, the half-year deadline arrives, and Feng Xi introduces Han Li to two other 'close friends'—the Poison Flood Dragon from the Mist Sea battle and a newly transformed giant turtle elder.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**乾蓝冰珠 (Dry Blue Ice Pearl)**: This isn't just a powerful ice-type treasure; it's a classic example of a 'life-risking treasure'—a one-shot item of immense power that can turn a desperate fight but is as dangerous to the user as it is to the enemy. Han Li's decision to keep it inside his body for months, suppressing it with Evil-Crushing Divine Lightning, is a high-risk, high-paranoia move. The fact that it nearly detonates due to internal energy fluctuations is a grim reminder that in this cultivation world, *your own body is a volatile reactor where everything is interacting in ways you can't fully predict.*

**碧焰酒 (Azure Flame Wine)**: This isn't a standard cultivation pill but a 'wine' (酒) made by a demon cultivator. In xianxia, higher-level ingredients and treasures are often processed through alchemy or crafting, but consuming a raw, volatile substance like this is a gamble. It was forced on Han Li, so its properties are untested by him. The slow, energy-intensive process of refining it reflects the Mortal Stream's rule that shortcuts to power are never free; they require grueling effort, constant resource expenditure (Ten-Thousand-Year Spirit Milk), and a high tolerance for risk.

### Mortal Stream

A subgenre of xianxia where the protagonist is an ordinary person who relies on pragmatism, resourcefulness, and caution rather than talent or destiny to survive a hostile cultivation world.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a perfect example of the "quiet victory" phase in a Mortal Stream arc. The climax isn't an explosion of power but a cold sweat and a 'what just happened?' realization. Pay close attention to Han Li's decision-making under pressure: when the Dry Blue Ice Pearl destabilizes, he doesn't pray or scream—he uses the only tool available (the Azure Flame Wine) in a last-ditch, rational Hail Mary. This is the core of his character: not the strongest, but the most resourceful and panic-proof. Also, note Feng Xi's introduction of the Poison Flood Dragon and the turtle elder. The stage is set for a forced, high-risk expedition. The relief of the breakthrough is immediately overshadowed by the arrival of two new, openly hostile predators. The dark forest just got a lot denser.
