## Chapter Overview

The quiet victory phase is in full swing. After the life-or-death chaos of the Blood Forbidden Trial and the tense confrontation with Senior Brother Lin, Han Li enters a rare stretch of calm logistics: picking through loot, weighing cultivation paths, and visiting his nominal master’s home turf. But this is no vacation. Every interaction—from the warm welcome of Senior Brother Yu Kun to the unexpected presence of a kind mistress—forces Han Li to recalibrate his paranoid instincts. The chapter is a masterclass in “the calm after the storm,” where the real battle shifts from swords and spells to social calculus and resource management. Fellow daoists who need a quick strategy guide: pay close attention to the *Great Derivative Art* and *Puppet Daoist Canon* pairing, because this little “side quest” is going to define Han Li’s entire mid-game toolkit.

## Key Plot Points

- **The Double Revelation**: Han Li finds the *Great Derivative Art* and *Puppet Daoist Canon* together in Senior Brother Lin’s jade slip. This is not a coincidence—the two techniques are designed to be paired, enabling a cultivator with a strong divine sense to control multiple puppets simultaneously.
- **The Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Han Li immediately identifies the core trade-off: cultivating these arts could cripple his already slim chance at Core Formation. He shelves the decision, but the seed is planted.
- **The Scavenger Hunt**: He recovers about two dozen intact puppets from outside the formation. The rest were destroyed by Lei Wanhe’s sword-light. The scene reinforces the Mortal Stream’s “no resource left behind” ethos.
- **The Master’s Lair**: Han Li visits Li Huayuan’s cave—a lush, scenic valley with a massive waterfall. He meets Senior Brother Yu Kun, whose warmth and familiarity immediately put Han Li on guard.
- **The Unexpected Mistress**: Li Huayuan’s wife is introduced—a mid-Foundation Establishment cultivator with a gentle, kind demeanor. Her presence humanizes Li Huayuan slightly, but Han Li remains vigilant.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### 《大衍决》The Great Derivative Art

The name “Great Derivative” (大衍) is borrowed from the *Book of Changes* (I Ching), where “the Great Expansion” (大衍之数) refers to the method of divination using fifty yarrow stalks, with one set aside. In xianxia, the term is often repurposed to describe techniques that multiply or divide one’s consciousness—a nod to the original idea of “expanding from the One.” Here, it serves as the crucial bridge between a weak divine sense and the ability to control multiple puppets, making it a corner-stone of the entire puppet-cultivation sub-system.

### 《傀儡真解》The Puppet Daoist Canon

The word *zhenjie* (真解) literally means “true explanation” or “authentic interpretation.” It implies that this text is the definitive, orthodox source of puppet-refining knowledge—as opposed to fragmented or corrupted copies. In the Mortal Stream’s information-starved economy, possessing a true canon is like owning a master blueprint; everyone wants it, but only the cunning survive long enough to use it.

### Puppet Cultivation vs. Self-Cultivation

A recurring tension in Mortal Stream novels: powerful external tools (puppets, arrays, talismans) demand time and resources that could be spent on personal cultivation breakthroughs. Han Li’s hesitation here perfectly embodies the genre’s central trade-off: do you become a hammer (core strength) or a Swiss army knife (versatility)? The answer is rarely clear, and the wrong choice can lock you out of the next realm forever.

### Soul Divide (分神)

An advanced secret art that permanently splits a cultivator's consciousness, enabling simultaneous control of multiple tools or puppets. Only viable for Foundation Establishment cultivators and above.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a classic “setup” moment in the Mortal Stream—no flashy fights, but immense narrative weight. Han Li’s decision about the *Great Derivative Art* will echo for hundreds of chapters, shaping his combat style, resource management, and even his future relationships. Notice how the author builds suspense through *inaction*: Han Li doesn’t commit to anything. He studies, he observes, he pockets the prize. This is the hallmark of a survivor. Also keep an eye on Li Huayuan’s “warmth”—in a world where every smile hides a calculation, even a master’s kindness deserves suspicion. The chapter closes with Han Li heading to his master’s cave, and that’s where the real test of trust begins.
