## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow readers—the tension just cranked up to eleven. In this chapter, the elusive Profound Bone Saint finally delivers on his promise and hands over the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng to Han Li. But nothing in this cultivation world comes without a catch. Just as Han Li secures the prize, the infamous Extreme Yin Patriarch descends upon him with terrifying precision. The chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream suspense: a quiet, cautious negotiation followed by a sudden, crushing power display that leaves Han Li scrambling for survival, stripped of even his defensive formation in a single blow.

## Key Plot Points

- The Profound Bone Saint presents the golden box containing the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng, but Han Li, ever cautious, first tests it with his Bane-Subduing Divine Lightning to ensure it isn't a trap.
- Han Li witnesses the white rabbit incarnation of the ginseng desperately try to merge with its main body, confirming the item's authenticity.
- After securing the ginseng, Han Li probes the Profound Bone Saint about the possibility of simply escaping, but the old ghost drops a bombshell: Extreme Yin has already placed a tracking mark on Han Li.
- Before any proof can be offered, the Profound Bone Saint senses Extreme Yin's approach and immediately flees, leaving Han Li completely exposed and furious.
- Extreme Yin Patriarch arrives in a spectacular display of Profound Yin Grand Art, obliterating Han Li's formation with a single casual strike, forcing Han Li into a desperate last stand.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The "Marked" Trope (做了手脚):** The Profound Bone Saint’s claim that Han Li has been secretly marked by Extreme Yin taps into a classic xianxia tension device. Spiritual tracking marks are often imperceptible to the victim, creating a crisis of paranoia. Han Li’s disbelief, rooted in his powerful spiritual sense, is a reasonable reaction, making the eventual confirmation of the mark a devastating betrayal of his own self-trust.

**Pocket Dimensions (小空间/小世界):** Although not explicitly named in this chapter, the location is part of the Void Heaven Palace — essentially a pocket dimension, an ancient creation of a long-dead cultivator of god-like power. These spaces are a hallmark of high-level xianxia, serving as self-contained worlds with unique laws, treasures, and dangers separate from the main realm.

**The Yin-Yang Counter (阴阳相克):** The Profound Bone Saint’s eagerness for Han Li’s Gold Lightning Bamboo artifacts perfectly illustrates the Mortal Stream’s emphasis on elemental counterplay. Gold Lightning Bamboo is a pure Yang/divine lightning material. Against Extreme Yin’s ultra-Yin Profound Yin Grand Art, it is the perfect inverse, promising a spectacular clash of opposing forces.

### Pocket Dimension (小空间/小世界)

An ancient creation of a god-like cultivator; a self-contained space with its own rules and dangers, separate from the main realm.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a classic “calm before the storm” structure that then suddenly throws the reader into the storm itself. Pay close attention to Han Li’s cautious process of verifying the ginseng — every single flick of lightning, every suppressed glance is part of his survival algorithm. The switch from this careful, deliberate pacing to the chaotic, overwhelming arrival of Extreme Yin is the narrative payoff. Also, note the Profound Bone Saint’s abrupt desertion; it’s a perfect reminder that in the Mortal Stream, every alliance is temporary and every partner is ready to abandon you to the wolves. The final image of Han Li, stripped of his defenses but pulling out his ultimate trump card (the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords), is pure, uncut Mortal Stream spirit.
