## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists, because Chapter 230 pulls the rug out from under everyone—including our ever-cautious protagonist. After the explosive puppet battle of the previous chapter, Han Li thinks he's the only hidden observer left. But the burly man, Yellow Dragon, isn't fooled. He calls out a hidden enemy, and out pops a familiar face: the eccentric, carving-obsessed old man from Han Li's entry exam. But this isn't a happy reunion. It's a meticulously laid trap by the Thousand Bamboo Sect, turning an old friendship into a venom-laced betrayal. This is a masterclass in *Mortal Stream* plotting: the quiet scene after the storm is often where the deadliest snare lies.

## Key Plot Points

- The burly man, now identified as **Yellow Dragon** of the Thousand Bamboo Sect, exposes a third party hiding in a mound of earth: the sacked man from the previous chapter's confrontation.
- The sacked man reveals his true identity as "Senior Brother Lin," Yellow Dragon's old sect mate, and the same carving-obsessed elder Han Li met at Yellow Maple Valley. This confirms he faked his death years prior.
- Yellow Dragon's initial joy at seeing his "Senior Brother" is a feint. He immediately inflicts a Gu poison (Black Silkworm Gu) via a handshake, revealing the entire affair as a trap orchestrated by the new Thousand Bamboo Sect Sect Leader, Jin.
- The trap was to lure Lin out by claiming the second half of the Great衍 Decision was hidden in the puppet beasts, using both a corrupted subordinate and a bribed secret shop. This reinforces the cold, transactional nature of the cultivation world.
- Two new Dharma Protectors arrive from the woods, and Lin, realizing escape is impossible, reveals he has cultivated the Great衍 Decision to the **third layer** and deploys over two hundred of his own puppets.
- A massive, spectacular puppet war erupts. Han Li, after quickly analyzing the power levels on display, correctly judges this is a fight far above his weight class and makes a decisive retreat, using the Divine Wind Boat at full speed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### The Thousand Bamboo Sect & The Great衍 Decision
This chapter offers a crucial lore dump on the **Thousand Bamboo Sect (千竹教)**, a powerful organization specializing in puppet and mechanical arts. The name evokes a sense of a vast, organized church-like structure (教, jiao), rather than a small, isolated sect. At the heart of this sect lies the **Great衍 Decision (大衍决)**, a legendary divine ability that governs the manipulation and control of their mechanical puppets. The fact that Lin has reached the *third layer* and can command 200 puppets underscores his status as a prodigal genius, making him a prime target for the new sect leader’s purge.

### The "Feigned Death" Strategy
Lin's situation highlights a classic *xianxia* survival tactic: **feigning death (诈死)**. It’s a high-risk, high-reward maneuver used by a politically defeated figure to remove themselves from the board. While it can buy time, it is notoriously difficult to maintain. The moment a "dead" man tries to re-engage with his old power base—as Lin did by contacting his old subordinates—they become a vulnerability. The *Mortal Stream* logic here is brutal: betrayal almost always comes from a trusted subordinate or old ally, not from an open enemy, and a rope of gold can buy any loyalty.

### Gu Poison
Yellow Dragon uses **Gu poison (蛊毒)**, a common yet fearsome trope in xianxia lore. Unlike simple chemical toxins, Gu are parasitic creatures, often insects or worms, cultivated through secret arts. The **Black Silkworm Gu (黑丝蛊)** is a particularly nasty variant that enters the bloodstream and can control or fatally poison the host. The fact that Lin immediately focuses on forcing out poisoned blood and consuming an antidote shows his deep experience with such insidious attacks. In this world, a handshake can be a death sentence, and any physical contact is a potential battlefield.

### Feigned Death

A high-risk survival tactic where a powerful figure pretends to die to escape political persecution, but risks exposure by contacting old allies.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a classic example of how *A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality* constructs its tension. The "quiet victory" phase after the previous chapter's action is where the real danger lies. Han Li learns a crucial lesson here: surviving a direct attack is only half the battle; the truly terrifying threats are the silent calculations and long-game traps being laid by unseen players. His decision to flee is not cowardice, but the highest form of wisdom in the Dark Forest. The contrast between the bombastic, all-in puppet battle below and Han Li's silent, calculated retreat is the very essence of the *Mortal Stream* protagonist's mindset.
