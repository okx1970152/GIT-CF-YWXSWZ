## Chapter Overview
This chapter perfectly executes the classic Mortal Stream reversal: the would-be predator becomes the prey. Elder Huang’s carefully laid ambush with the Wind and Cloud Banner is brutally neutralized by a high-level spell called the Heavenly Fire Technique, a scorching magma rain that forces Yellow Maple Valley into a desperate defensive crouch even before a single enemy blade is drawn. The chapter builds tension masterfully, shifting from the cold precision of the ambush to the slow, grinding horror of being caught in a magical kill box, then finally delivers the genre’s signature gut-punch: the enemy appears in overwhelming numbers, *and* they brought two Core Formation cultivators, including a legendary duo known as the ‘Crimson Beauty and the White Skeleton.’ Any hope of a clean fight evaporates instantly, and Elder Huang’s final order—to scatter and die alone—is the only logical, cruel decision left. This is peak *RMJI* tactical warfare.

## Key Plot Points
- Elder Huang uses his magic treasure, the Wind and Cloud Banner, to generate a concealing cloud that hides the entire Yellow Maple Valley expedition, positioning them for an aerial ambush.
- Han Li, ever the pragmatist, mentally compares the banner to his own Green Fire Miasma, noting its inferiority, and hands his silver greatsword to Qu Hun for no other reason than to maximize his asset’s utility before battle.
- The ambush is turned upside down as a colossal mid- to high-grade spell, the Heavenly Fire Technique, is cast upon them from above, turning the sky into a churning ocean of bloody magma.
- Elder Huang identifies the threat, forbids any attempt to flee, and forces everyone to combine their spiritual power into a single, massive shield to weather the mana-draining magical bombardment.
- The moment the spell ends and relief begins to surface, they realize they are completely surrounded by nearly a hundred Foundation Establishment disciples of the Six Demonic Sects.
- The true hammer drops as two Core Formation cultivators—the seductive, deadly ‘Crimson Beauty’ and her skeletal, horrifying partner, the ‘White Skeleton’—descend from the sky.
- Elder Huang recognizes them instantly as the infamous ‘Red Powder and White Skeleton’ duo, and with no hope left, he issues a cold, fatalistic command: break out individually and save whoever can.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
In many xianxia systems, mid-grade spells represent a terrifying qualitative leap from their lower-grade counterparts. A low-grade spell might shoot a single fireball; a mid-grade spell like the Heavenly Fire Technique (天火之术) turns the entire sky into a weapon platform, sustaining a strategic-level bombardment that drains the combined mana of a Foundation Establishment battalion. It’s not just a bigger fireball; it’s a change in the nature of warfare. The cost, as hinted by the churning magma-cloud with its hanging bubbles, is a lengthy casting time and immense spiritual power, which is why it’s usually the province of Core Formation masters or formation teams. The name itself, “Sky Fire,” isn't unique to *RMJI*; it’s a classic Chinese wuxia/xianxia trope for a fire spell that descends from the heavens, often linked to divine punishment or the power of the sun—though here its function is purely tactical: area denial and mana attrition.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a masterclass in comedic and lethal structure: the high of setting the perfect trap, the slow dread of realizing the trap has failed, and the final despair when the hunters reveal they were the true predators all along. Pay close attention to Han Li’s inner thoughts—they never dwell on the danger but on optimizing his available assets (handing his sword to Qu Hun, choosing the Green Fire Miasma’s ‘cloud within a cloud’ defense). This is the Mortal Stream’s core reading protocol: never let your eyes linger on the spectacle of the fireworks; instead, watch how the protagonist carefully selects the knife he’ll use to escape the burning building. The introduction of the ‘Red Powder and White Skeleton’ duo is also a classic *RMJI* power brushstroke: they are given a legend and an attitude, a deadly reputation that requires no demonstration.
