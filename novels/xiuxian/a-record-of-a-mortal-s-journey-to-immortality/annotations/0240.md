## Chapter Overview

Welcome back, fellow Daoists! Chapter 240 is what we in the trade call a “quiet victory” chapter—and in the Mortal Stream, those are just as crucial as any bloody showdown. The dust has settled on the Forbidden Land arc, and our boy Han Li has just done something monumental: he’s formally become the disciple of a Core Formation powerhouse. But instead of fireworks, what do we get? A tense gift-exchange, a poker-faced selection test, and a **massive lore bombshell** hidden in plain sight. Han Li finally gets his hands on a golden page to match his silver one, and the method he uses to peel back its secret is pure, cold, *mortal-stream* genius. By the end, he’s not celebrating—he’s fuming at a useless Sword Aura manual. But as any veteran reader knows, when the protagonist is annoyed by a “useless” treasure in xianxia, there’s always more to the story… Get ready for a chapter that’s all about logistics, subtle character dynamics, and a mystery that just got very, very interesting.

## Key Plot Points

*   **The Selection Gauntlet:** Li Huayuan presents Han Li with a handful of foundational cultivation techniques. Each has a unique survival-oriented “minor divine ability,” from defensive spirit armor to phantom projections. This is a master class in how even “low-tier” sects cater to a “live-to-fight-another-day” philosophy.
*   **Han Li’s Choice:** Despite the tempting options, Han Li’s eye is caught by a single unassuming golden page within a jade box. He recognizes its format instantly. It matches the mysterious silver page he’s had since his days under Doctor Mo. He chooses it, knowing it unlocks a riddle, even if it means settling for a mediocre cultivation art.
*   **The Ritual of Discipleship:** The formal adoption is cold and businesslike. Li Huayuan asks for Han Li’s sincere loyalty, and Han Li gives a perfectly diplomatic answer while his internal monologue runs a ruthless cost-benefit analysis of his new master. The scene underscores that in the *Mortal Stream*, even a master-disciple bond is a contract built on mutual utility, not sentiment.
*   **The Great Revelation:** Alone in his cave, Han Li uses controlled true fire to melt the surface of the silver page, revealing a hidden golden layer. The new page contains the *Sword Aura* technique—the very art that barefoot Giant Sword Sect cultivator was using. Han Li is initially furious, deeming it useless for a Foundation Establishment cultivator.
*   **The Game Changer:** In a fit of anger, Han Li tries to destroy the new golden page with a blast of his Azure Origin Sword Aura. But the attack is not blocked or deflected—it is ***absorbed*** and vanishes without a trace. Han Li’s fury instantly transforms into sharp, calculating interest. The page is clearly not what it seems.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**The “Thank-You-For-Teaching” Gift (谢师礼/拜师礼):** In both ancient Chinese tradition and xianxia norms, becoming a formal disciple often involves gifting the master. Here, the dynamic is inverted: Han Li’s “gift” is the life-debt he has already paid by saving the master’s wife. But the selection of a “gift” art from the master’s collection serves a similar social function. It’s a ritualized exchange of value that solidifies the bond, ensuring the master feels the transaction is complete and the disciple is in his debt only for future teachings, not for the initiation itself.

**The Nine Provinces of Yue (越国九州):** The geopolitical setting is expanding! The text mentions that the *Blinking Sword Art*’s creator came from Lan Province (岚州), while the Ghost Hand’s infamy was also there. The Yue Kingdom is divided into at least nine provinces. This constant naming of provinces is characteristic of the *Mortal Stream*, where the world feels vast and dangerous. A cultivator’s origin is a key part of their identity, hinting at regional power struggles and lost inheritances that can span entire provinces.

**Sword Cultivation vs. Spell Cultivation (剑修 vs. 法修):** This is a major distinction in xianxia. The *Ning Yuan Art* Han Li chose is a typical *Spell Cultivation* method—it focuses on building a deep mana pool and using versatile spells. The *Golden Page* technique, however, is a *Sword Cultivation* art. Sword Cultivators (*Jianxiu*) are notorious for being hyper-specialized: they pour everything into a single flying sword, making them monstrously powerful in offense but often vulnerable in other areas. Han Li’s initial disdain comes from the fact that he is not a Sword Cultivator, and picking up a new sword aura would be a major, time-consuming detour.

### Condensing Yuan Art (凝元功)

A perfectly ordinary, middle-of-the-road cultivation technique. Its only practical benefit is the "Spirit Gathering Art," which slightly increases the speed at which a cultivator draws in Qi.

### Nine Provinces of Yue (越国九州)

The Yue Kingdom is a major geopolitical region, divided into nine provinces (Lan Province, etc.) that contain both mortal cities and immortal clan territories, shaping the political landscape of the low-realm cultivation world.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterclass in the *Mortal Stream* aesthetic of **information asymmetry**. Han Li wins this round, but the “prize” is a puzzle, not a power-up. Focus on his process: he doesn’t ask Li Huayuan for help with the silver page. He doesn’t blurt out his discovery. He patiently waits, selects the golden page through sheer observation, and then experiments in the privacy of his own cave. The man learns *everything* the hard way—through his own silent, calculated efforts.

Pay close attention to Han Li’s internal monologue when he agrees to be Li Huayuan’s disciple. That “if you treat me sincerely” calculation is the bedrock of his entire philosophy. He is granting his loyalty as a conditional, revocable loan, not as a gift. This is the difference between a *Mortal Stream* protagonist and a wuxia hero.

Finally, note the symbolism. Han Li used fire to strip away a false layer and reveal the truth. But the “truth” was not what he expected, and when he tried to destroy it, his own power was consumed by the mystery. This is the cultivation world teaching Han Li a vital lesson: **nothing is ever as simple as it seems, and sometimes your greatest frustration is the seed of your greatest opportunity.**
