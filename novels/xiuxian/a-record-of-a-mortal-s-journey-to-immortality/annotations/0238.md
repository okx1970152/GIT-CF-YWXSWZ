## Chapter Overview
The pie from heaven has landed, but it’s wrapped in a much more complicated truth than Han Li ever expected. Li Huayuan finally reveals the real reason he accepted Han Li as a disciple—it was a straightforward transaction, not an act of generosity. The spiritual herbs were the prize, and Han Li’s discounted Foundation Establishment Pill was the price. But when a cultivation accident nearly killed Li Huayuan’s wife, those very herbs became her lifeline, turning Han Li into an accidental lifesaver. Now, the debt of gratitude hangs in the air, and Han Li is offered a blank check: ask for anything within the old monster’s power. But the chapter doesn’t just settle on the drama of negotiation. It takes a sharp, comedic turn when Han Li is abandoned to the tender mercies of Senior Apprentice-Brother Yu Kun—a man whose rambling tongue could wear down a mountain. After surviving a full-blown verbal siege, Han Li learns a new truth of the cultivation world: some battles are not won by swords or spells, but by sheer endurance of chatter.

## Key Plot Points
- Li Huayuan explicitly confesses that he took Han Li as a disciple specifically to claim half of the spiritual herb share. This is presented as a transparent, practical transaction, not an act of mentorship. The master’s honesty carries a dark edge: he admits it was a calculated move that was perfectly “fair” in his eyes.
- The narrative reveals that Li Huayuan’s wife suffered a life-threatening Core Formation deviation. She was saved solely by the medicines derived from Han Li’s herbs, which makes Han Li her indirect lifesaver.
- The wife insists on compensation not only out of gratitude but also because failing to repay a life-saving debt would flaw the Iceheart Art, blocking her path to Core Formation. Han Li’s windfall is thus tied to the practical mechanics of cultivation, not pure altruism.
- Han Li is given a day to think over his request. The wife shrewdly points out that Li Huayuan is “not a generous man usually,” framing this as a rare, calculated opportunity.
- Senior Apprentice-Brother Yu Kun takes Han Li on a tour of the estate. During the walk, Han Li encounters Fourth Senior Brother Song Meng—a combat maniac who instantly dismisses Han Li upon hearing he’s a newly established Foundation cultivator.
- Yu Kun’s compulsive rambling is weaponized. Han Li is trapped in the senior’s room for hours, subjected to a relentless torrent of words. The chapter ends with Han Li fleeing in exhaustion, realizing the true horror of the “talkative master” trope.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the rarely seen “compensation culture” within sect hierarchy. In the Mortal Stream, gratitude is not an abstract moral debt—it is a structural transaction with tangible cultivation consequences. The wife cannot advance to Core Formation without repaying Han Li’s life-saving act, as the Iceheart Art ties its progress to a clear state of mind (心境). A flaw in this “state of mind” is not an emotional hiccup; it is a hard block on her cultivation path. This logic grounds moral obligation in the very mechanics of spiritual progression, making every act of kindness or betrayal a strategic variable. The talkative senior disciple trope, while played here for humor, also reinforces the grinding, inescapable social texture of sect life. In the crowded, hierarchical world of low-level cultivators, even an ally’s virtue can become a weapon.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a palate cleanser after the high-stakes tension of the Blood Forbidden Trial, but don’t let the slower pace fool you—the negotiation that is being set up will define Han Li’s resource path going forward. Pay special attention to the wife’s intervention. She is not just a kind face; she is the one who opens the door to Han Li’s compensation, and her cultivation needs are the engine behind the master’s sudden willingness to open his coffers. The comedic relief of Yu Kun’s rambling also serves as a quiet world-building moment: in the Mortal Stream, no relationship is entirely free. Even a simple tour comes with a price—hours of endurance. And that fleeting encounter with Song Meng? It’s a cold reminder that in this world, your worth is measured in combat brackets. Han Li is now on the second step of that ladder, and the eyes of fellow disciples are already sizing him up.
