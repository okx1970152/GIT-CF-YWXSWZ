## Chapter Overview
Fellow Daoists, brace yourselves! Chapter 184 delivers the moment we’ve been waiting for: the official debut of Han Li’s first major trump card, the Gold Brick Talisman Treasure. And folks, it does *not* disappoint. After a surgical strike with the near-invisible thread takes out one unlucky foe, our favorite miser gets to test-drive his most expensive purchase yet against a thirteen-layer cultivator. The result is a scene of such brutal, overwhelming finality that even Han Li is left stunned. It’s a masterclass in the Mortal Stream combat philosophy: why fight fair when you can drop a *mountain* on someone? But the victory comes at a cost—a terrifying mana drain that leaves Han Li coldly calculating his next move. The chapter closes with him standing at the edge of a foreboding forest, his resources depleted, a perfect setup for the next round of deadly trials.

## Key Plot Points
- Han Li dispatches the Heaven’s Fortress disciple with a flickering, nearly invisible thread, demonstrating that preparation and surprise trump raw power.
- The Spirit Beast Mountain man, trapped by an Earth Imprisonment Technique talisman, frantically tries to break free with his two spirit beasts and iron rod tools.
- Han Li activates the Gold Brick Talisman Treasure for the first time, enduring a terrifying and involuntary mana drain of a third of his total magical power.
- The Gold Brick transforms into a mountain-sized block of golden energy and smashes the bearded man and his beasts into a bloody pulp, leaving a massive crater.
- Han Li is briefly dazed by the weapon’s sheer, overwhelming power before snapping back to his usual pragmatic self.
- He loots the bodies, destroys the evidence, and moves on, now low on mana and wary of the next ambush zone—a dark forest.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
**The Talisman Treasure Economy:** This chapter provides a visceral illustration of the concept of Talisman Treasures (符宝). In the cultivation world, these are not standard magic items. They are essentially "fragments of power" created by Core Formation cultivators as a dying act, sealing a portion of their artifact’s might into a single-use talisman. For a low-level Qi Condensation cultivator, possessing one is like a peasant holding a high-caliber rifle. The devastating power is intoxicating, but the cost of activation—nearly a third of a cultivator's mana—reinforces the Mortal Stream's core lesson: power is a resource to be spent, and every advantage comes with a heavy price tag. Han Li's immediate concern after the kill isn't relief, but the chilling awareness that he's now vulnerable.

## Reading Guide
Get ready for the payoff of a long-term investment! This chapter is a turning point for Han Li's offensive capabilities. For many chapters, he has relied on cunning, preparation, and the secret of his near-invisible thread. But the Gold Brick represents a new tier of power: the ability to simply *erase* an opponent who is far above his own cultivation level. The visceral description of the kill—the crater, the "bloody pulp"—is *meant* to be shocking, even for our stoic protagonist. It underscores the terrifying gap between a mortal cultivator and the power of a Core Formation artifact. Watch how Han Li processes this: his first reaction is not triumph, but a cold, analytical shock. He immediately recognizes the cost (mana drain) and the risk (loud noise, alerting others). This is the Mortal Stream in a nutshell—every victory is a temporary advantage in a war of attrition. Enjoy the spectacle, but feel the weight of the resource spent.
