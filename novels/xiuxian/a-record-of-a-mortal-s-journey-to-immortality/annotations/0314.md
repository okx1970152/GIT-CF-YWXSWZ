## Chapter Overview

Hold onto your spiritual roots, fellow mortals! Chapter 314 goes full-on info-dump, but the kind that re-wires everything you think you know. Han Li has bagged the Junior Prince and Steward Wang, and now it’s time to pry open their skulls (metaphorically, via an ethically questionable potion and a hypnosis spell). The payoff is staggering: the Black Fiend Sect Master isn’t some shadowy hermit in a cave—he’s the Chief Eunuch of the Yue Imperial Palace, with the mortal emperor himself as his puppet. But before you think “great, Han Li just has to storm the castle,” the story slams down a centuries-old rule from the Seven Great Sects: no disciple may set foot in the Imperial Capital. Suddenly, the biggest threat isn’t a demonic cultivator—it’s bureaucratic red tape with teeth.

## Key Plot Points

*   **Talisman Treasure Supremacy:** Han Li overwhelms the demonic giant with a dazzling display of the jade ruler Talisman Treasure, splitting it into hundreds of attacking projectiles. The enemy flees, proving that even a beast-like opponent knows when it’s outclassed.
*   **A Pragmatic Retreat:** Han Li doesn’t chase. He wisely conserves the Talisman Treasure’s dwindling power, prioritizing the safe extraction of information over a risky clean-up. The captured prisoner is worth more than a secondary kill.
*   **The Mind Extraction:** Using a potent truth serum followed by a hypnosis spell, Han Li effortlessly cracks the Junior Prince’s mind. The man reveals everything, showcasing the terrifying power gap between a Foundation Establishment cultivator and a Qi Condensation one.
*   **The Big Reveal:** The Black Fiend Sect Master is hiding in plain sight—as Li Poyun, the Chief Eunuch of the Imperial Palace. The mortal emperor is a puppet, and the entire capital is the sect’s headquarters.
*   **The Insurmountable Wall:** The chapter’s true climax is not a battle, but a rule. The Seven Great Sects have an ancient, unwritten law forbidding any disciple from entering the Imperial Capital. Han Li realizes that exposing this truth might get him punished, not rewarded. Good deeds are worthless against institutional dogma.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a masterclass in the “Social Tribulation” (社会试炼) at play. Han Li isn’t facing a magic barrier; he’s facing an institutional one. The “unwritten rule” (不成文的规定) preventing cultivators from entering the imperial city is a classic xianxia device that highlights the difference between raw power and political power. The mortal emperor is a symbolic figurehead, propped up by the sects to manage the mundane world. To interfere with him is to upset a delicate, centuries-old balance of power that no one sect is allowed to tip. It’s a perfect example of the Dark Forest logic applied to geopolitics: the sects are so terrified of one of their own gaining control of the throne that they’ve collectively agreed to leave it alone entirely. This creates a blind spot that a cunning third party can exploit, as the Black Fiend Sect has done. Han Li’s dilemma is the purest form of Mortal Stream conflict: the correct action is obvious, but the safe action is to do nothing.

## Reading Guide

Welcome to the bureaucratic nightmare that follows every victory. The central tension here is brilliant: Han Li has solved the mystery, but the solution is locked inside a restricted zone. Pay close attention to his internal calculus. He isn’t thinking about heroism or saving the emperor. He’s thinking about risk-reward ratios. Will the Seven Great Sects thank him for breaking their most sacred rule, or will they punish him just to maintain the appearance of their authority? This is the cynical heart of the Mortal Stream. The system is not built to reward justice; it’s built to perpetuate itself. Han Li’s final, weary realization—that sometimes “the authority of certain rules far outweighs the merits of a case”—is a cold, hard truth that separates this novel from any tale of righteous crusaders. He is not a knight; he is a man calculating whether the prize is worth the potential backlash.
