## Chapter Overview

If you thought the Mo Estate was just going to be a quick poison-drop-and-dash, think again. Han Li’s second visit turns into an unexpected Social Tribulation, where the real battlefield isn’t the poison he’s carrying, but the complex web of human relationships he has to navigate. Our boy executes a masterful Social Bluff, leveraging the fear of the Corpse Worm Pill to secure cooperation while showing just enough flexibility to seem humane. But the real meat of the chapter is a fascinating exchange with the Mo sisters, where we finally meet the gentle and learned Mo Fengwu. Here, Han Li deploys a new social tactic: posing as a naive, sheltered student, he weaponizes his own perceived innocence to lower defenses and gain trust. It’s a different kind of duel, fought with politeness, flattery, and strategic gifts, and it sets up a crucial information-gathering mission. The chapter ends with a return to the mortal underworld, where a new lead on local immortals emerges, proving that in this world, power comes from knowledge, not just talismans.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li allows Yan Shi alone to take the Corpse Worm Pill, sparing the other wives. This calculated act of “kindness” establishes his authority while creating a social debt.
- Mo Caihuan confronts Han Li, accusing him of being a “fake” who tricked her with “little tricks.” Han Li rolls his eyes, establishing a playful but wary dynamic with the sharp-eyed girl.
- Mo Fengwu is properly introduced. She is gentle, polite, and deeply interested in her father’s medical arts. Her soft voice and timid demeanor immediately invoke Han Li’s pity.
- Han Li agrees on the spot to return Doctor Mo’s manuscripts and prescriptions to Mo Fengwu. His immediate compliance is a deliberate performance, building a social bridge and consolidating trust.
- Mo Yuzhu remains silent throughout the exchange but casts a deep, ambiguous glance at Han Li as she leaves, suggesting she may be a more complex player than she appears.
- Han Li returns to the inn, where Sun Ergou and the witness Xi Tieniu are waiting. He formally appoints Xi Tieniu as the gang’s vice chief as a reward for information.
- Xi Tieniu recounts the story of the “male and female immortals” in detail. Han Li presses for key information: specific times or locations related to the immortal sighting.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

**Social Bluffing (炸胡/信息战)** – Han Li’s negotiation with Yan Shi is a textbook example of Social Bluffing, a core survival tactic in the Mortal Stream. He doesn’t need to actually harm the other wives; the mere *threat* of the Corpse Worm Pill, and the ambiguity of its effects, forces submission. By allowing Yan Shi to “sacrifice” herself alone, Han Li creates a scenario where the target feels grateful for a lesser evil. It’s a high-stakes mind game where information is the primary weapon and emotional manipulation is the fine print.

**The “Naive Student” Tactic** – Han Li’s conversation with Mo Fengwu is a masterpiece of calculated social misdirection. He deliberately poses as a shy, helpful young man, eager to please a gentle beauty. This isn’t kindness; it’s a strategic performance to secure an ally within the Mo Estate. In a world where trust is a rare and valuable commodity, building a reputation for being reasonable and accommodating is often more effective than raw displays of power. He creates a social debt, making her an unwitting asset, while keeping his true ruthless nature hidden.

**Immortal Masters and Mortal Gangs** – The chapter reinforces the hierarchical gap between the cultivation world and the mortal underworld. Han Li deals with the Si Ping Gang as a logistical asset, treating them as disposable tools for information gathering. The appearance of a steward from the Immortal Gathering Pavilion and the mention of the "Ascension Assembly" hint at a larger, more structured network of cultivators that exists just beyond Han Li’s current reach, a system where even low-level cultivators can barter, sell, and compete for a chance at ascension.

### Social Bluffing (炸胡/信息战)

A core survival tactic in the Mortal Stream, where a protagonist uses ambiguous information or threats to force a favorable outcome without physical violence. It relies on the opponent’s fear and guilt.

### Social Tribulation (社会试炼)

A type of conflict in the Mortal Stream where the protagonist must navigate complex human relationships and political hierarchies with the same cold calculation as a physical battle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a fascinating breather episode that’s anything but restful. It’s a masterclass in the Mortal Stream’s core credo: “Social Bluffing is a real combat skill.” Pay close attention to how Han Li’s tone shifts from cold pragmatism with Yan Shi to soft politeness with Mo Fengwu. He’s like a chess player, using different opening moves for different pieces. The contrast between the high-stakes psychological warfare in the Mo Estate and the crude information gathering with the gang leaders is deliberate. It shows that Han Li is equally skilled in navigating a noble’s drawing room and a back-alley tavern. This chapter is all about building the foundation for his next big move. The seeds planted here—a grateful ally in the Mo family, a piece of information about local cultivators, and the acquisition of a portrait of Jia Tianlong—will bear fruit in the trials to come. Enjoy the calm before the storm. Fellow Daoists, witness the power of a well-placed favor and a well-told lie.
