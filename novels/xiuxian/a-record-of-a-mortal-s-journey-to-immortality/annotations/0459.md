## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in Mortal Stream combat reversal. Han Li gets caught off-guard by a hidden trap in his own treasure, loses his magic power, and nearly gets possessed by a ghost king who somehow bypasses a complete formation barrier. But instead of panicking, we see Han Li systematically neutralize each layer of the crisis. The Gold Devourer Beetles buy him time, his eerie calm buys him space, and when the ghost head finally lunges for the kill—BAM. Golden lightning. Instant obliteration. This chapter reads like a textbook on how a cool-headed calculator turns an ambush into an execution.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li discovers the Golden Net was rigged with Yin Soul-Threads that freeze his magic power the moment he falls out with the stranger. This is a classic Mortal Stream betrayal: trust no treasure given by a temporary ally.
- The ghost stranger penetrates the formation barrier without triggering it—a feat that shocks Han Li and forces him to realize he’s up against something far more cunning than a simple opponent.
- The Gold Devourer Beetles clash with the green shadow’s python arms. Despite the beetles’ devouring ability, the ghost king can hold them off for a time.
- The Profound Bone Saint joins the assault on the barrier from outside, creating a two-front crisis.
- Han Li uses sound to command the beetles to gnaw through his Soul-Threads while keeping the ghost king occupied.
- The ghost king self-destructs its body to shake off the beetles, then launches its primordial ghost head for a direct possession attempt.
- Han Li instantly counters with a golden lightning arc from his mouth—the Purgatory Thunder—and in one move erases the ghost king’s soul entirely.
- Having crushed the green crystal pearl to prevent any revival, Han Li calmly demands the Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng from the Profound Bone Saint, issuing an ultimatum. The Saint, after a stunned silence, yields and tosses him the box.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

### Purgatory Thunder (辟邪神雷) — The Perfect Counterplay
The golden arc Han Li uses is derived from the Divine Thunder Bamboo (天雷竹), a legendary material whose lightning is naturally aligned to pure Yang. In Chinese cosmology and Daoist practice, Yang-aligned lightning is the absolute bane of Yin-type entities—ghosts, vengeful spirits, and undead cultivators. A ghost king’s formless dharma body, no matter how powerful, is fundamentally Yin in nature. The moment it gets hit by that golden arc, it doesn’t just “die”—it gets *unmade*. The Mortal Stream logic here is perfect escalation: Han Li doesn’t overpower the ghost king; he exploits the elemental counterplay that was already established in the novel’s world-building. The Purgatory Thunder was originally a trump card against demonic cultivators, but it turns out to be even deadlier against disembodied ghosts.

### Self-Destruct Possession (自爆夺舍) — A Desperate Gambit
The ghost king’s final tactic—self-detonating its manifested body to launch its primordial soul as a fast-moving ghost head—is a classic high-stakes possession attempt in xianxia. It’s a “I’ll sacrifice my current form to steal yours” move. The key weakness? The primordial soul is extremely vulnerable in flight. If the target can counter it before it enters the body, the attacker is left defenseless. In this chapter, Han Li’s golden thunder arc exploits this vulnerability perfectly: the ghost head is fast, but at point-blank range, there is no way to dodge a lightning strike.

### Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng (九曲灵参) — A Sentient Treasure
The ginseng at the center of this conflict is no ordinary herb. The “Nine-Bend” (九曲) in its name describes a legendary type of spirit ginseng that can transform and even develop a limited form of intelligence. In Chinese folklore, ginseng roots that have lived for centuries can become “ginseng spirits” capable of fleeing from harvesters. The Nine-Bend Spirit Ginseng in *RMJI* takes this to the next level—it is a sentient prize that both the ghost stranger and the Profound Bone Saint covet. Its surrender marks the victory’s final seal.

## Reading Guide

Get ready, folks—this is the turning point of the island arc. Han Li just destroyed a ghost king with a single breath attack. The Profound Bone Saint just folded without a real fight. And Han Li? He didn’t even break a sweat. The Mortal Stream formula is in full effect: yes, he took some damage and lost his magic power for a moment, but he came through with precise counters at every step—beetles for the soul-threads, thunder for the ghost head, cold words for the Saint. And the best part? He didn’t even need to use his most trumped-up cards to do it. If you’ve been wondering when Han Li would start feeling genuinely *dangerous*, this chapter is your answer.
