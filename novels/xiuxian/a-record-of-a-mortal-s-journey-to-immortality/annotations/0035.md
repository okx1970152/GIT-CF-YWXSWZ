## Chapter Overview

Get ready, fellow Daoists—this chapter is a wild, reckless ride that shows just how far Li Feiyu’s loyalty will go. Han Li sneaks out of Divine Hand Valley for another secret meeting, only to find his “Fierce Tiger” friend has pulled off the most insane stunt yet. The calm, cautious Han Li is forced to confront a pile of stolen manuals big enough to earn them both an execution if caught. It’s a moment of high tension, dark humor, and a rare crack in Han Li’s composure. The chapter also deepens the bond between the two friends, showing that in a world built on betrayal and self-interest, true loyalty can still exist—even if it comes wrapped in lunacy.

## Key Plot Points

- Han Li’s freedom of movement has grown: he now walks past Doctor Mo openly, having deduced that the doctor tolerates his comings and goings to avoid poisoning his willingness to cultivate the Eternal Spring Art. This is a quiet strategic victory.
- At the secret pond meeting spot, Li Feiyu is waiting barefoot, splashing water. He complains about Han Li’s lateness, then tosses him a gigantic bundle.
- Han Li initially thinks it’s food or a joke, but the bundle is heavy and hard. When he opens the knot with his signature Coiling Silk Technique (a flex that earns applause from Li Feiyu), he reveals a shocking sight.
- Inside the bundle is a huge pile of martial arts manuals—nearly a third of the entire Seven Peaks Hall collection. Each cover bears the stamped gold characters “Seven Peaks Hall Collection.”
- Han Li’s reaction is visceral: cold sweat, bulging eyes, and an explosion of anger. He threatens to cut ties, knowing that stealing these manuals is a capital offense.
- Li Feiyu, true to his reckless nature, treats the whole thing as a grand joke, though his actions prove just how far he will go for his friend.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The **Seven Peaks Hall** is the elite inner hall of the Seven Mysteries Sect, housing the sect’s ultimate techniques. Theft from its library is an automatic capital offense—no trial, no mercy. In xianxia logic, sects guard their martial heritage with absolute severity; the knowledge is both political capital and the foundation of the sect’s power. Li Feiyu’s willingness to risk this for Han Li is not just friendship—it’s a literal life-or-death gamble.

The **Coiling Silk Technique** is a high-level martial skill that emphasizes supple, inescapable binding movements. Li Feiyu’s admiration for Han Li’s mastery reinforces the theme that raw power isn’t everything; technique and adaptability can be just as lethal. In the mortal stream, a clever technique often beats brute force—a lesson Han Li has taken to heart.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point in the Han Li–Li Feiyu dynamic. We already knew Li Feiyu was fierce and loyal, but stealing the sect’s entire sword manual stash? That’s next-level. It also reveals Han Li’s darker side: his first instinct is not gratitude but panic, and he immediately considers cutting ties to save his own skin. But he doesn’t actually walk away—which tells you something. The bond between them, though built on a poisonous oath and a shared addiction secret, is becoming genuine. Pay attention to the contrast between Han Li’s surface calm and his internal alarm bells. That gap is the hallmark of his character.
