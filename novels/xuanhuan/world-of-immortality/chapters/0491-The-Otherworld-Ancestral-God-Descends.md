After years of silence, an Otherworld Ancestral God descended once more. The entire Soul World trembled.

The name Li Huiyou was not unfamiliar to certain ancient beings—he was an immensely powerful Otherworld Ancestral God.

His divine might surged across a hundred thousand li!

The entire Soul World shook, the vast earth splitting with enormous crevices, as if the world itself was about to collapse and sink.

Such was the pressure of an Otherworld Ancestral God!

Destroying suns, moons, stars, mountains, and rivers was but a single thought away.

Li Huiyou's true body emerged, stirring such a commotion—it was clear he came with hostile intent!

The entire Soul World was likely to face great calamity.

The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge stretched across heaven and earth, ancient and mottled, its stone body covered in sword cuts and spear holes, spanning tens of thousands of li.

A hazy figure, wrapped in sacred radiance, stepped off the stone bridge and in an instant appeared tens of thousands of li away.

With a thunderous roar, the blinding divine light dimmed the sun itself. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge blazed with holy light, its brilliance shining for myriad miles, illuminating heaven and earth.

Without a word, this mysterious Ancestral God–level expert attacked the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou.

One could clearly see an enormous earthen-yellow hand smash through the sky, striking at Li Huiyou, who had just emerged from the dark mysterious passage.

Li Huiyou was fiercer than a primeval demon king. His thousand-zhang-tall body was like a mountain of demons. His long gray hair danced like enormous serpents in the sky, exuding a terrifying, savage presence!

He also reached out a giant hand to meet the earthen-yellow hand in the sky.

With a thunderous rumble, in the blazing light, the two giant hands shattered the sky for tens of thousands of li. The firmament of the Soul World seemed about to split apart, as if the end of the world was descending.

The two hands clashed, shaking the eight desolates with their violent force!

Had the sacred radiance of the hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge not stabilized the heavens and earth by illuminating all directions, the Soul World itself might have been shattered.

At that moment, the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, spanning tens of thousands of li, suddenly trembled. Its ancient, majestic end rose high and slammed down violently!

With a pounding sound, the end of the stone bridge, filled with divine power and unfathomable might, struck the body of the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou.

As if a thousand thunderbolts roared at once, the sky and earth erupted with terrifying energy fluctuations, as if the power of destruction from before the creation of the world had torn through time and space, all crashing down on Li Huiyou's demonic body.

The thousand-zhang-tall Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou's towering form contained boundless divine power, but even he could hardly withstand such a blow.

Amidst the world-shaking thunderclaps, he flew sideways, crashing into the pitch-black, enormous, and unfathomably deep mysterious passage behind him, nearly collapsing it.

The hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge blazed with divine light. His right hand extended, forming a dragon shape, and a sharp beam of light shot forth, like a ten-thousand-li divine river falling from beyond the heavens, dazzling and brilliant.

With a crash, it plunged into the dark passage tens of thousands of li away.

Xiao Chen and the others, tens of thousands of li away, were moved. That seemed to be the Ancestral Dragon–shaped divine fist that the Martial Ancestor had once used! How could this person wield it as well? And with equal overwhelming power?

Heaven and earth shattered, ghosts wailed, and gods howled!

The surging ten-thousand-li divine river descended from beyond the heavens, gathering the power of countless stars, causing the mysterious dark passage to split apart.

The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou let out a long roar. His gray hair, a thousand zhangs long, danced like giant snakes across the sky.

“You want to shatter the Divine Gate and force me back? Not so easy!”

Every inch of his flesh and blood vibrated, unleashing a vast, unmatched divine power that stabilized the Divine Gate passage. Then, with a deafening roar, he tore himself free from the black mysterious passage once more.

His thousand-zhang-tall body stood like a demon mountain between heaven and earth, radiating boundless destructive force that shook the land in all directions.

Li Huiyou's eyes were ice-cold, filled with killing intent and a wild light. The battle-suit he wore gleamed with cold radiance, and a terrible murderous aura surged in all directions.

This was his true body—a powerful figure among the Ancestral Gods, far beyond the incarnation that had appeared in the Most Evil Land!

In his hand he held a giant war spear wreathed in black clouds, filled with destructive power—the true Great Annihilation War Spear!

It was a fine piece among Ancestral God weapons, originally belonging to the Otherworld Ancestral God Babra, and after his fall, it had been reallocated to Li Huiyou.

The terrible Great Annihilation War Spear, grasped by a true Otherworld Ancestral God, radiated a might that shook the distant starry sky.

Li Huiyou seemed to merge with the spear, like a sovereign who commanded the world, his power overwhelming!

“I sense the Heavenly Halberd, but the divine awareness I left on it has been wiped clean. It seems that incarnation has met with misfortune. I will wash a hundred thousand li in blood and make those who profaned my body pay a terrible price!”

At the distant horizon, Xiao Chen and the others could not help but look at Jin Sanyi.

The stuttering man, hugging the dark and cold Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, grinned wryly and said, “I-I-I finally get my hands on this Ancestral God weapon, and the real owner shows up. Br-brothers, I'm out of here!”

He directly shattered space, fleeing with the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, afraid that the powerful Otherworld Ancestral God would sense him and crush him.

“Shatter Heaven and Earth!”

The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou roared. His thousand-zhang demonic body seemed cast from iron and steel, filled with explosive power. He raised the Great Annihilation War Spear toward the south, pointing it at the hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge.

The Ancestral God’s overwhelming strike was no mere game. A terrifying hole tore open in the sky, and immense chaotic fissures collapsed.

If it fully erupted, it would shatter the world's essence and annihilate countless living beings.

The firmament trembled, like delicate porcelain about to shatter.

But the hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge clearly would not allow this. His left hand lifted lightly, and a seven-colored flower bud appeared between his fingers, crystal clear, gleaming with endless life force, just about to bloom.

A mighty wave of life energy surged forth, the opposite of the Great Annihilation War Spear’s destructive power. Divine light illuminated heaven and earth, blocking all the force of destruction. The two collided in a cataclysmic blast, and space itself annihilated!

Only after a long time did calm slowly return.

A single crystal-clear, budding flower had blocked the Great Annihilation War Spear!

“No one in this world can stop me!”

Li Huiyou’s voice was cold and emotionless. “I have come to end this world.”

In the next instant, the Great Annihilation War Spear pierced through the sky, stabbing straight toward the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, aimed at the hazy figure’s throat.

In that moment, the starry sky beyond trembled, and the Soul World's earth shook violently. Had the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge not severed the high heavens, mountains would have collapsed and seas would have raged.

Li Huiyou was not joking—he truly intended to sweep across a hundred thousand li and massacre without mercy.

At this moment, the killing intent of the Great Annihilation War Spear was unleashed to its fullest, its destructive aura sweeping across the sky! Four of the Soul World’s five moons shattered directly.

The Great Annihilation War Spear, in Li Huiyou’s hands, fully erupted in its savage and destructive divine power!

Facing this Ancestral God spear that oppressed sun and moon and shook the stars, the hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge raised his left hand high. The crystal-clear, delicate flower bud suddenly bloomed!

The divine flower opened for the first time, supreme among all blossoms!

It released its fragrance, overwhelming all beauty in the world. Seven-colored petals radiated divine light, and crystal-clear dewdrops rolled on their surface.

The flower trembled gently, its pistils vibrating, radiating light in every direction. Life itself reached its ultimate beauty, instantly neutralizing the boundless destructive aura.

And then, with a clear ring, the seven-colored divine flower collided with the indestructible Great Annihilation War Spear, withstanding it!

Suddenly, the seven-colored flower blazed with intense light, erupting with a vast, immense force that sent the Great Annihilation War Spear flying a thousand zhang into the air.

Li Huiyou was thrown backward, managing to stop before the dark mysterious space passage. His gray hair fell loose over his shoulders, his eyes even colder.

“This is not my divine weapon. Since you are somewhat capable, I will use my own divine law to destroy you!” With these words, he raised the Great Annihilation War Spear as if it were the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, and spoke slowly and deliberately: “Heavenly—Halberd—Annihilates—the—World!”

The terrifying voice shook the entire world. The power of annihilation seemed summoned from the primordial age. Vast black gates appeared in the sky, surging with even more terrible divine power, truly intent on ending the world.

The consequences would be severe. This attack was too powerful. If no one stopped it, heaven and earth would truly be torn apart.

“Six—Paths—of—Reincarnation!” At that moment, the hazy figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge also spoke slowly, pronouncing these four words.

The Six Paths of Reincarnation was a supreme divine art; few methods could counter it. It was truly the path of invincibility!

In an instant, all color drained from heaven and earth. Everyone’s six senses and eight perceptions were sealed, as if isolated in a dark prison, unable to perceive anything on the battlefield.

Terrible power sealed off the world!

The nine heavens shook with thunderbolt after thunderbolt, and the Soul World trembled violently.

After an unknown time, everyone’s senses slowly returned. In their ears came Li Huiyou’s icy voice.

“You actually managed to unleash the Six Paths of Reincarnation…”

By now, he had been driven back into the dark mysterious passage. A divine flower bloomed within, trying to shatter the deep passage and use clever force to destroy Li Huiyou’s body.

But at that moment, another powerful aura, like an ocean, surged from the depths of the mysterious dark passage, stopping Li Huiyou’s retreat.

A second Otherworld Ancestral God was about to descend!

If he successfully passed through and arrived in the Soul World, the consequences would be dire. Two Ancestral Gods working together would have unstoppable destructive power.

With a tremendous crash, the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge slammed forward, smashing Li Huiyou, who was struggling to break free, deep into the passage and then charging into its depths, shattering the pitch-black “Divine Gate” continuously.

“Damn it! I will return!” Li Huiyou roared in fury.

At the same time, a bone-chilling howl came from the depths of the mysterious passage—the roar of another terrifying Otherworld Ancestral God.

After the Divine Gate collapsed, it seemed to inflict a terrible shock on both powerful Otherworld Ancestral Gods.

Such passages were extremely difficult to open; they would not appear again for a short time.

When the sky cleared, the black mysterious passage was about to disappear completely. At that moment, the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge suddenly shot out, shrinking to the size of a palm and appearing in the hazy figure’s hand.

In a flash of light, the figure vanished completely.

Who was that person? Everyone wondered. When had an Ancestral God of such power appeared in this world, seeming to master the divine arts of several Ancestral Gods?

“Was it the Yellow Emperor or the Ancestral Dragon?” No one could be certain.

Ten days later, Xiao Chen and the others left the Soul World and returned to the Nine Provinces.

The same day, Xiao Chen learned important news: the Ancestral Dragon Village was appearing faintly, as if about to emerge from concealment.

He could hardly contain his excitement.

How many years had it been? The Ancestral Dragon Village had vanished beneath the earth, along with all its inhabitants.

Also known as the Primordial Ancient Village, it had been sealed for nearly two hundred years. Xiao Chen’s parents and family had all slept there.

All these years, through endless battles, whenever he felt utterly exhausted, the first thing he thought of was his family. But every time he appeared before Imperial City, he could only watch silently, unable to find any trace of the ancient village.

Hearing this news, Xiao Chen flew straight toward Yong Province.

“Ah-ya!” Keke followed closely.

Bai Qi, Wu Ming, Fatty Zhuge, and the others were also filled with curiosity about the important site within Imperial City, and they all flew toward Yong Province.

At the same time, Jin Sanyi, clutching the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, appeared like a ghost and followed.

“So similar!” Totti, Fatty Zhuge, and the others couldn’t help exclaiming when they saw the towering Imperial City once more.

Imperial City and the several ancient demon cities seen outside the Most Evil Land seemed to share the same origin. The pitch-black walls and enormous gates were all over a hundred meters high, inspiring awe.

Not far from Imperial City, at the site of the original ancient village, a hazy outline appeared. Vaguely, one could see the original form of the Ancestral Dragon Village.

They waited silently there for eight days before the ancient village gradually grew clear.

And on the tenth day, two small, hazy figures staggered up from the edge of the ancient village, stumbling out.

Xiao Chen and the others rushed over.

Two seven- or eight-year-old little girls, their skin pink and white, almost translucent, seemed to drip with moisture. They groggily rubbed their big eyes, yawned, stretched, and stumbled out.

“Wow! A bunch of handsome guys! Drool… They’re all mine, don’t fight over them!” The two little girls’ big, watery eyes lit up together. They chased each other, running over.

The group stared dumbfounded. These two delicate little girls were certainly something else.

Xiao Chen recognized them at once as Linglong and Tutu, who had an astonishing background—they were the two young daughters of the Yellow Emperor.

Beside him, Jin Sanyi beat his chest and sighed to heaven: “The lolis I’ve been waiting for are getting smaller and smaller! They used to be over ten years old, now they’re back to being little sprouts! Third Brother doesn’t like this kind of dish!” Totti nodded in deep agreement, while the others rolled their eyes at him.

“Creepy goldfish uncle!” The two little girls clearly remembered Jin Sanyi.

“Drool…” Together, the two pink girls fixed their eyes on Bai Qi.

At that moment, Jin Sanyi cried out again.

“There’s another person coming out!” Another figure rose from the edge of the Primordial Ancient Village, but this one was not a little girl. He was tall and imposing, with an extremely fierce aura, commanding respect without anger.

“Isn’t… isn’t that Yizhen’s brother, Baldy Number Two?” Jin Sanyi yelped.

Bo Shi glared at Jin Sanyi, his eyes fierce as a hawk’s, making the stutterer shiver.

“Wow, this handsome uncle is special!” Linglong and Tutu abandoned Bai Qi and ran to Bo Shi.

In Bo Shi’s hands, he held a sarira the size of a watermelon, its light flowing and enormous.

Meanwhile, many people were secretly observing the vicinity of the Primordial Ancient Village.

Because as the ancient village was about to emerge, Imperial City had been stirring strangely, and at this moment, it shot out a pillar of divine light. The giant city shook.

Xiao Chen and the others recognized it at once—it was the Killing Light!

Like a brilliant ribbon, it coiled around Imperial City.
