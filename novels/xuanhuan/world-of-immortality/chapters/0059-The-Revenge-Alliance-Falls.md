Xiao Chen strode forward, and the entire forest trembled with his steps. He moved with the weight of a giant, an oppressive aura that made Guluo and his two companions’ hearts seize and their breath catch in their throats.

Fear gnawed at Guluo’s soul. He already sensed that his life might be at its end, but he still struggled with all his might, like a cornered beast.

Xiao Chen’s body radiated a brilliant divine light. He left afterimages among the trees, evading the attacks of the Spiritist Li Lingfeng and the Spellmaster Carter while constantly circling Guluo. His hands swept out with dazzling radiance, each blow hammering Guluo until blood poured from his mouth and he could barely stand.

“Ahhh!” Guluo roared, hurling himself at Xiao Chen. The surrounding trees shattered as he pushed his power to the absolute limit, desperate to inflict a grievous wound.

But with a single burst of dazzling divine light, all of Guluo’s efforts came to nothing. Xiao Chen swung his right arm like a heavenly blade, and a blade-light several meters long slashed horizontally through the air, cutting clean through Guluo’s body at an angle. The upper half of his torso flew more than ten meters, scattering a rain of blood, before both halves of the corpse crashed down among the broken branches and fallen leaves.

Guluo’s life was ended. The founder of the Revenge Alliance had fallen. This meant that Xiao Chen had triumphed over an entire alliance in single combat!

A chorus of exclamations erupted from above the valley. It had been a brutal and fierce battle, and everyone now understood Xiao Chen’s terrifying strength. Shouts rang out.

But the fight was not over. Xiao Chen’s battle with the Spiritist Li Lingfeng and the Spellmaster Carter continued.

Brilliant light rained down over the forest—lightning, blades of light, dark netherfire. Carter unleashed every incantation he knew, sending wave after wave of violent energy crashing down.

The Spiritist Li Lingfeng displayed his innate spiritual powers. After his immobilization technique failed, he used his formidable psychic energy to weave illusions throughout the valley, trying to lure Xiao Chen into deadly traps. He also condensed his spiritual power into a massive sword of light, cleaving and slashing horizontally through the forest.

The battle dragged on for a long time. Xiao Chen formed a series of profound and mysterious hand seals. Radiant starlight shimmered around his body, forming an impenetrable light barrier shaped like a starry sky, which completely blocked the attacks from above.

As for the psychic illusions, they had little effect on Xiao Chen’s iron will.

When Xiao Chen elevated his power from the Fourth Heavenly Tier of Mortal-Shedding to the Fifth Heavenly Tier, a magnificent sword-light blazed into the sky. Divine radiance erupted from his hands, and an unstoppable beam of light pierced straight through the Spellmaster Carter’s body as he flew overhead. Another dazzling sword-light cleaved the Spiritist Li Lingfeng’s body in two.

Two corpses tumbled down. The fierce battle had ended.

The power of the Fifth Heavenly Tier of Mortal-Shedding was overwhelming. Delivered as a sudden, devastating strike, it had instantly eliminated two formidable enemies. The many practitioners watching from above the valley fell into stunned silence before bursting into an uproar.

Xiao Chen had triumphed in a one-man challenge against an entire alliance!

The various alliances watching from the valley slopes were all deeply shaken. Many onlookers felt as though they had vicariously experienced the bloody battle themselves, their blood running hot—a single warrior defeating an alliance!

Cheers, roars, and shouts filled the air above the valley.

Xiao Chen did not leave the valley. Instead, he walked deeper into it. All the alliances on the slopes watched with growing curiosity. Soon, however, they remembered that one member of the Revenge Alliance had escaped deeper into the valley.

Just a small fry—did he really need to hunt it down?

But the crowd quickly realized how wrong they were. That was no minor figure!

A fierce battle erupted!

From the very beginning, when Xiao Chen had first engaged Guluo and his group in this valley, he had sensed that one person seemed unusual. He had killed the weaker opponents first, but this particular individual had escaped.

And indeed, after finishing off the others and reaching the depths of the valley, Xiao Chen found the man had been waiting for him for some time.

Without a word, the battle erupted violently. That man turned out to be a terrifying Spellmaster! Then Xiao Chen realized with a shock—the person with matted hair, his face mostly hidden, was actually Rand, the one who had been with Yan Qingcheng.

Rand had dyed his golden hair a grayish-black with charcoal ash, and his jade-like face had been stained a dark red with grass juice. Combined with his disheveled hair, he was nearly unrecognizable. Xiao Chen had not noticed him before, and he had never imagined that Rand would make a move.

A golden-haired beauty as radiant as the sun, dressed up like this…

“Rand, why are you attacking me?”

“Because I want to. Because I like it!” Rand remained as arrogant as ever, utterly proud and disdainful of further explanation.

“Humph. I hope you can still be that arrogant when this battle is over.”

Xiao Chen had no reason to fear. Since Rand had come to him with that same old arrogance and contempt, there was nothing more to say. All that remained was to crush him with overwhelming force and shatter that proud, haughty demeanor.

Brilliant divine radiance erupted through the forest. The dazzling light surged like a raging sea, and a large swath of trees shattered and splintered. Endless radiance flooded the area.

This was not the energy fluctuation of a Mortal-Shedding Third Heavenly Tier practitioner!

Experts above the Fourth Heavenly Tier of Mortal-Shedding were locked in combat!

The many alliances and the watching experts were all astonished.

The battle was exceptionally fierce!

In the valley, rocks flew through the air, massive trees toppled one after another, and blinding light flared in continuous explosions. Sometimes whole trees were uprooted and sent spinning into the sky, where they shattered into pieces.

That final member of the Revenge Alliance was the true soul of the group, the most terrifying expert. He fought Xiao Chen blow for blow, causing all the onlookers to grow grim.

But Rand was far too arrogant. As the battle stalled, he repeatedly dove from the sky to bombard Xiao Chen with violent spell energy at close range. The sky was filled with blazing radiance, and fearsome energy cascaded down, shrouding the entire forest in divine light. Nearly all the trees were destroyed.

It was at that moment that Xiao Chen unleashed his full power, elevating his strength directly to the Fifth Heavenly Tier. A savage, annihilating light shot upward, and its brilliant radiance dragged Rand down from the sky!
