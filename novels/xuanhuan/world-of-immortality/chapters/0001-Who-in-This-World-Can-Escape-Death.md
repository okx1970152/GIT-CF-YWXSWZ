Who in this world can ever escape death?

Though you be peerless in grace and beauty, unmatched under heaven, in the end you are but rosy flesh and white bone. Though you be a favored child of heaven, ruling over ten thousand miles of rivers and mountains, in the end you will be reduced to a handful of yellow earth.

Deathless longevity—it is what all desire. But there is no ageless beauty, no immortal emperor. The fairest of maidens and the lords of a thousand years are no different from the common throng; all are trapped in the cycle of birth, age, sickness, and death. None may live forever in this world.

Yet the legend of deathless immortality has always lingered among men.

Laozi, Zhuangzi, Bodhidharma, Chen Tuan, Zhang Sanfeng—

Name after famous name echoing through the ages, like an ancient spell that forever drives later generations, making them believe that to live forever is not an absolute absurdity—that some have indeed attained that realm.

But time is the most merciless. As the years pass, even the once-undying legends are slowly ground away in the river of time.

Until, after endless silence, miracles burst forth again from the mundane.

On the night of the full moon of the fifteenth, Lan Nuo, a celestial maiden favored by heaven, would sever her mortal ties on Red Dust Peak in the Kunlun Mountains and shatter the void to ascend. Practitioners across the land were shaken to the core; the talk of longevity blazed once more into heated debate.

In recent days, over a hundred thousand people had poured into the Kunlun Mountains—from princes and nobles to peddlers and laborers, from every rank and class. They all shared the same purpose: to witness a miracle rarely seen in a thousand ages.

At last, the night of the full moon arrived. The majestic Kunlun Mountains, vast and beautiful, seemed draped in a veil of hazy moonlight, making this sacred mountain range as ethereal as a realm of immortals.

Through the moonlit night, Xiao Chen ran like the wind, his body drenched in blood, even his long black hair stained crimson. But his chiseled, handsome face held only indomitable defiance, and his eyes, bright as stars, blazed with steadfast determination.

He was fleeing for his very life!

The Royal Celestial Maiden, Zhao Lin'er, had sworn to destroy him. She led dozens of cultivators in a hunt that pressed him from all sides. Her face hidden behind a light veil, her figure willowy and graceful, her eyes like autumn waters, she moved like a startled swan—as light as a floating shadow, as ethereal as a banished immortal.

With no escape left, Xiao Chen charged toward Red Dust Peak!

Beneath the full moon, the area around Red Dust Peak was packed with people, a sea of humanity covering the hillsides. Yet a hundred thousand people gathered together were utterly silent—all of them gazing up at the white-clad woman on the summit.

On the peak, Lan Nuo stood in robes as white as snow. Bathed in the moonlight, her immortal form seemed to radiate a faint, holy light. Her white dress fluttered in the wind, and she was like the goddess of the moon, untouched by the dust of the mortal world.

Over the past half-month, she had attempted to break the void twice. Both times, at the moment of success, she had drawn back the foot she had taken.

One step away—she would live forever between heaven and earth!

But if she took that step, the endless red dust of the mortal world would forever leave her, and all earthly ties would be severed.

To draw the sword of wisdom and cut the bonds of the world—this took immense courage! For once she crossed that threshold, in the endless years of deathless longevity that awaited her, perhaps only infinite solitude would be her companion.

The heart of heaven is unfathomable; the feelings of immortals are as cold as frost.

Today, she had stood from dawn until now. Every memory of the mortal world surfaced in her heart, one after another. At last, the moment came to bid farewell to the earthly realm. A fierce radiance of sacred light suddenly erupted from the summit, covering the entire peak in a halo of supreme holiness.

Lan Nuo's skin was like ice and her bones like jade. In the sacred glow, she was so untainted, so pure. As the multitude watched in reverence, the void shattered, and she stepped forward with calm and unwavering resolve.

In that final instant, she turned her head and cast one last glance back at the mortal world. Her dreamlike, ethereal visage was forever etched into the hearts of all who saw it. A hundred thousand voices cried out Lan Nuo's name in unison.

But the orderly shouts soon broke into confusion. People saw two figures racing across the summit, hurled into the shattered void after Lan Nuo!

The chronicles of the Nine Provinces record that in the year 7316, the celestial maiden Lan Nuo broke the void through martial means and ascended to the World of Immortality. The Royal Celestial Maiden Zhao Lin'er was fortunate enough to share in this immortal fate and followed her into that realm.

As for Xiao Chen, he was not fated to be recorded in those pages.

In the instant the void shattered, Xiao Chen was shocked beyond words!

He had never imagined that such a day would come, that he would enter the World of Immortality in this manner. In that moment, his thoughts raced to many things—his family, his friends... he would never see them again. He would leave this mortal world forever.

A life-or-death escape had ended in such a result. To many, shattering the void to enter the World of Immortality was a glory that would echo through the ages. But Xiao Chen would have gladly given up this chance. He was so reluctant to leave this world—his parents, his loved ones... Farewell forever! He bid the red dust of the mortal world a silent goodbye.

Xiao Chen did not know that the Royal Celestial Maiden, Zhao Lin'er, had also shattered the void and followed into that realm.
