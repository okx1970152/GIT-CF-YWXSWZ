Pangu King descended in sudden majesty, invincible wherever he turned. With a single blow, he shattered both arms of the old Stone Man King, his divine might covering the world.

“No!” The old Stone Man King’s eyes shot out two terrifying beams of light.

But this was no moment for distraction. Pangu King dove from the sky, the great stone axe flashing like lightning, too swift to follow, and chopped down again—a strike with the power to sunder heaven and earth!

The old Stone Man King’s arms were already broken; his chest and skull groaned under tremendous pressure. Cracks spread, and he fled like a falling star.

Pangu King followed like a meteor chasing the moon, a beam of light clinging to his quarry, the giant axe about to land on the old Stone Man King’s crown.

A duel between kings spanned the ages; time and space were nearly irrelevant, no longer continuous. An instant became eternity!

The old Stone Man King flew backward without turning, clearly seeing the axe fall less than three inches from his skull.

“Ommmm—” At that moment, a terrible tremor split the air, like the most vicious insects of heaven and earth buzzing their wings. The old Stone Man King’s eyes were as deep as an abyss, unfathomable, drawing in the soul, but then his pupils turned green, nearly demonic. Two dreadful beams blazed forth, shooting out in an instant.

“Dang!” Two eerie green lights that could shatter all the ages and all the worlds crashed into the giant stone axe. The sound of metal striking metal pierced the barriers of the great worlds, spreading to every realm, shaking the souls of many mighty beings.

The great axe bucked upward under the impact of those unfathomable green lights, losing its edge.

The old Stone Man King finally reversed his desperate situation. His arms crackled, and the shattered stones appeared like phantom flowers, instantly repairing themselves. The wolf-tooth stone club returned to his hand.

But clearly, he had weakened by a measure. Those two terrible scorching green lights had taken part of his essence—a very dangerous consequence for a Stone Man King so grievously wounded.

“You are not the true Pangu King!” the old Stone Man King said, swinging his wolf-tooth club. Having barely reversed the situation, he had to seize the initiative, or the consequences would be dire.

“Boom!” The wolf-tooth stone club contained ten thousand divine principles—not merely powerful battle qi but also arcane laws and divine curses sweeping through heaven and earth, shattering this newly opened world in a flash. Chaotic light surged, covering the sky and coming down.

To personally open a world and then shatter it—such power was horrifying. But for the old Stone Man King, it was but a thought.

Yet Pangu King was stronger still—like a tiger descending the mountain, roaring across the land; like a flood dragon riding the clouds, leaping ten thousand li with divine might. A single axe swept away the heavens, shattering all divine principles, unstoppable.

“Clang!” The wolf-tooth club was knocked flying, tumbling into chaos.

“Crack!” The sharp stone axe slashed down diagonally, and a terrible sound rang out. The gods in the distance could not bear to watch—they closed their eyes.

Pangu King was too strong, unmatched. The old Stone Man King could not block him.

“Boom!” The great axe blade bit into the old Stone Man King’s shoulder. A thunderous crash rang out, and the axe did not pause. It cut diagonally through—first one stone arm fell to the ground, then the upper body and waist separated.

The gods were shocked. This scene was too terrifying! Stone Man blood sprayed; the old Stone Man King was cleaved diagonally at the shoulder, his upper body severed!

Even a Stone Man body, though made of stone, had blood—very little, but more precious, for it was the condensation of their essence.

Of course, the old Stone Man King could not be destroyed so easily. This was merely the separation of his trunk; it did not represent true death.

But Pangu King’s combat power reached the heavens. He would not let such an advantage slip. The stone axe trembled as he struck toward the stone skull.

At the same time, golden divine light blazed, chaos split apart, rising and falling in the distance. Heaven and earth were created, and divine principles shone everywhere.

This was a supplementary killing principle—true momentum to annihilate the old Stone Man King. It enveloped the fallen remains with a constant sound of cracking.

All the old Stone Man King’s hidden wounds in his body (except his head) burst forth at once. Both halves of his remains shattered, turning into two piles of gravel.

“Crack!” The great axe blade flashed a cold spark, and with a trembling sound, it chopped down.

Though the old Stone Man King resisted desperately, in the end he could not defend.

“Bam!” The stone axe split directly into his skull. That hard stone body parted, splitting in two along the hidden wound. Stone Man blood sprayed.

“Ancestor!” “No—Primarch!” The gods behind him cried out, and ten thousand divine principles swept out together, attacking Pangu King.

“Hiss!” The stone axe spanned the sky, the huge axe head like a heavenly door, blocking all the divine curses and laws outside.

“Boom, boom!” At the same time, Pangu King’s towering form strode forward, his two feet planting themselves on the two piles of gravel formed from the old Stone Man King’s remains.

Boundless divine power poured out like an ocean, ten thousand feet of divine radiance exploded forth, condensing into an eternal divine flame. Pangu King stood still like an eternal war god, unmoving.

He was using his unfathomable supreme power to refine the old Stone Man King, seeking to utterly destroy a Stone Man King.

Never could the gods have imagined such an outcome! Their Primarch King was an invincible existence in their eyes, yet today he was being cleaved and would be forever erased from the river of history. They could not accept this.

But Pangu King’s stone axe was like the gate of all worlds, blocking them; they could not break through it in a short time.

Also, the old stone turtle and that terrible nine-tenths stone man struck out, their combat power rivaling kings, preventing the gods from crossing.

And two peerless talents—Keke’s father and the Human Demon Ge Qian—displayed unmatched divine abilities, sealing heaven and earth, blocking countless laws and curses like two heavenly shields.

As for Xiao Chen, though he lacked supreme combat power or dual divine curses, his divine diagram unfolded, and the ancient scroll from heaven spread across the heavens, also like two impassable moats, blocking the gods’ advance.

Pangu King stood tall, his divine might covering the world without any worries behind him. He began refining the old Stone Man King, drawing essence from the two piles of gravel, slowly grinding him down with earth-shaking force.

The gods went mad. This was a terrible scene.

From the two piles of gravel, essence constantly poured out. The stones gradually dimmed. The old Stone Man King, imperishable for ages, now faced the nightmare of his soul’s destruction.

The old Stone Man King’s essence, spirit, and soul were distributed throughout the gravel. He let out a chilling howl, making every being present feel their hair stand on end, and then roared: “A billion years of sinking, a thousand generations of calling—my body imperishable, crossing the ancient barrier, from the fading battlefield, come back! Lend me the eternal instant of your battle soul!”

“O my enemy, O my past soul, descend against heaven!”

A demonic, mysterious force shook the ages and all worlds, breaking the walls of time, communicating with the long river of the primeval age, spanning layer after layer of great world barriers, connecting to lost years and spaces.

Piercing the ancient to connect the present, turning the impossible into possibility, borrowing the former great power, transmitting the imperishable battle soul!

Sky-shrouding black fog rolled, demon clouds surged, chaos retreated. This newly opened world expanded even more. All the black clouds surged toward the two piles of gravel on the ground.

This terrifying scene was extremely sinister, filled with unknown variables. An eerie, mysterious force surged.

The two piles of gravel, nourished by the black mist, seemed to revive. Their essence ceased flowing, and gradually they swelled.

The old Stone Man King was undergoing a terrible transformation. The gravel trembled, slowly knitting together, violently resisting the force of Pangu King who stood above him, trying to break free from beneath those two feet.

“Holy crap! The old undying firewood is coming back to life! Overlord Divine Fist!” the old stone turtle shouted. His two giant fists, like hammers, smashed down, shaking the two piles of gravel.

But the black mist surged. The two piles of gravel could not be erased. Gradually, light shone through. Many parts of the broken body had already healed together.

As time went on, Pangu King’s mighty form slowly dimmed.

“You are but a virtual body, not the true form, only a fleeting moment of life—and you want to kill me? At the instant I return to my peak, no one under heaven or earth can suppress me!” The old Stone Man King’s words were especially cold. Clearly, being shattered here had aroused boundless fury. With a great shake, the two piles of gravel on the ground recombined into a withered stone body.

The gods stirred, no longer attacking, retreating.

And Pangu King’s figure grew more insubstantial. Finally, the great axe in his hand vanished, and then his whole body blurred.

“Ooooooh—” A terrifying sound rang out. Pangu King’s phantom vanished. The old Stone Man King’s body recombined completely, standing up again, his stone hair seeming to flutter.

His two stone eyes were as deep as black holes, unsettling to behold. He had reversed the deadly situation and risen once more with such means—chilling indeed.

As he stood, he said nothing, merely coldly scanning the beings before him, sending a chill through the several great experts.

“Boom!” He took a step, rushing toward the Primordial Demon City. He had no time to delay.

“Bam!” The old stone turtle and the others struck. At this moment, they could not allow all their efforts to be wasted.

“Break!” But now the old Stone Man King was terrifyingly powerful. Truly, as if restored to his peak state, one punch repelled everyone.

“Shuffle, shuffle—” Suddenly, from the Eternal Unknown came a series of terrible sounds. A mysterious force tore through the barriers of the great worlds and shook this realm.

“You are to be executed!” The old Stone Man King’s voice was so cold it made the scalp tingle. The great world barriers were broken, and the scene of the Eternal Unknown appeared clearly—the Martial Ancestor wielding the stone knife, Yan Huang deep within the Eternal Source, and that one-armed body had shattered three levels of the stone steps.

This placed the old Stone Man King in a dilemma, caught between two fires.

Although some Ancestral Gods had already returned to the Eternal Unknown, they could not change anything. Half a giant stone skull floated there, swallowing heaven and earth, confronting the returning gods.

Also, the nine-colored Ancestral Dragon shone with holy light, coiled like a mountain range, casting out endless divine principles to block the gods—Keke’s mother herself, her power not inferior to Keke’s father.

“Kill!” The old Stone Man King’s form flickered, breaking through the blockade, charging toward the Divine City. The several great experts could not stop him.

“I pierce the ancient and connect the present; the past battle soul attaches to me. Your resistance is only throwing away your lives!”

“Pierce your head! Connect your ass! It’s just self-hypnosis and suggestion, forcibly burning your remnant potential. You really think this sorry wreck of yours can act against heaven? You think you’re me? You’re not even close, old man!” The old stone turtle swung three punches. Though he briefly stopped the old Stone Man King again, his own strength seemed completely drained, unable to strike again.

He made strange sounds to the nine-tenths broken stone man, then transmitted to Xiao Chen. Both rushed over quickly.

The old stone turtle waved his hand violently, forming an eternal imprint, forcibly fusing Xiao Chen with the broken stone man into one.

“Grandpa Turtle declares: if I don’t take down this old firewood today, let my name be Toad!” Xiao Chen merged with the stone man, his combat power skyrocketing. His whole body seemed to have endless divine power, draped in the ancient scroll, with five great Dao imprints revolving around him.

“Pierce the ancient and connect the present!” At that moment, the old Stone Man King shouted.

With a thunderous crash, Xiao Chen was instantly separated from the stone man. The divine power faded; he was forcibly torn apart.

“Before me, you have no chance!” The old Stone Man King was terrifyingly strong, enough to make everyone tremble. Perhaps he truly had turned that instant into an eternity, calling back the battle soul of the past, nearly restored to his peak state!

“Retreat!” the old stone turtle shouted, flying back with Xiao Chen and the nine-tenths conscious stone man, saying, “Grandpa Turtle is the Overlord; I will not become a tailless toad!”

The old Stone Man King’s gaze froze for an instant, then he looked toward the Eternal Unknown with a sinister expression, saying, “Breaking the stone steps will not stop your calamity—you are all courting destruction!”

As he spoke, he swung his club at the Divine City.

“Whoosh—” The wolf-tooth stone club made a terrible, piercing sound.

“Crack, crack—” Before it even touched the walls of the Primordial Demon City, they began to crack.

Now he was far more powerful than before. Perhaps driven by the threat of Pangu King, he truly had called back his ‘true self’ from the past, his combat power unmatched.

“My present self differs little from a billion years ago. There is no one under heaven or earth to oppose me. You shall all die!”

“Bam!” Ge Qian and Keke’s father before the Divine City were blasted flying, their bodies nearly destroyed, suffering unimaginable damage.

The old stone turtle was frantic. At this moment, he could not recombine with Xiao Chen and the stone man. He seemed unable to stop it, and regret would last forever.

But just then, a heart-shaking aura suddenly erupted ahead. The Human Demon Ge Qian stood up from the ground, his hair disheveled, walking forward step by step. Though he was decadent and melancholy, a formless aura was enough to move the spirits, shaking all worlds.

“What exists is forgotten;
the mundane world of red dust vanishes before the eyes.”

As his voice fell, the old Stone Man King’s shadow instantly dimmed and was sent flying. This was the peerless Human Demon, comprehending a divine principle at this critical moment—a talent so rare he could blast away a Stone Man King nearly restored to his peak combat power!

“Roar!” The old Stone Man King howled low, rising instantly and charging back, but his body began to shatter, and he was sent flying again. He was truly enraged.

“What has passed is remembered;
the eternal instant resides in the heart.”

At this moment, another terrifying aura shook the ages and all worlds. Keke’s father also rose from the pool of blood, proclaiming such a divine principle.

“Boom!” The charging old Stone Man King, under this divine principle, saw his dimmed body grow clear again—but his essence was stripped away!

The terrible divine principle shocked the gods. Keke’s father was truly a peerless talent. Along with the Human Demon Ge Qian, at this critical juncture, they comprehended even greater laws and power.

“Roar!” The old Stone Man King raged, his stone hair actually lifting as if it had become supple, wildly flailing. The wolf-tooth stone club and ten thousand divine principles swept in all directions, attacking Keke’s father, Ge Qian, and the Divine City before him!

“What exists is forgotten;
the mundane world of red dust vanishes before the eyes.
What has passed is remembered;
the eternal instant resides in the heart.”

At this moment, the Human Demon Ge Qian and Keke’s father both spoke softly, their divine principles perfectly connected, as if they were originally one being.

The positive combined with the negative, yin and yang complemented each other, perfectly merging!

“Boom!” The old Stone Man King was blasted flying, his hidden wounds bursting, many stones falling off.

“These two are excellent!” the old stone turtle said from behind, his eyes gleaming with strange light. He also shouted, “I say, well-prepared fellow, you should have succeeded long ago!”

“Thank you all—I have already succeeded!” At this moment, the Primordial Demon City shook violently, and a male figure shot up from within!

Meanwhile, at the Eternal Unknown, Yan Huang and the Martial Ancestor retreated rapidly. But in their retreat, they gathered up the three shattered stone steps—this was their fundamental goal.

“I never imagined that you would succeed before my eyes. But it is of no concern!” The old Stone Man King looked at the radiant stone man above the Divine City, then at the Human Demon Ge Qian and Keke’s father, and said, “You are remarkable indeed, truly peerless talents. But today your lives will end; you have no future!” Then he fixed his gaze on the Eternal Unknown, saying coldly, “Do you think that taking part of the stone steps will allow you to learn something? Impossible! Today everyone will die. Even if someone takes the final step, they cannot change anything. The ninety-nine stone steps…” He uttered ancient syllables that few could understand.

“Rumble—” At the Eternal Unknown, the three shattered levels of the ninety-nine stone steps trembled violently. Black mist surged, and a mysterious, sinister aura poured forth.

At this moment, even the Celestial Realm was shaken. Several great powers rushed toward the sealed world passages, seeking to investigate.

Block him! That was the uniform response of all parties.

At the same time, the old stone turtle attempted once more to merge Xiao Chen with the stone man. He shouted, “The situation is very bad—we must fuse together!”
