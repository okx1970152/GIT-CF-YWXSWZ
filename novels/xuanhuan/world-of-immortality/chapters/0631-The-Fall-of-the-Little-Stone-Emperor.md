Though motionless, he bore the aura of one who could swallow mountains and rivers, the bearing of one who overlooked all creation. The seven Stone-Man Kings were forced to bow their proud heads beneath his oppressive pressure!

He shook the ages—who could rival him? Such was his true portrayal.

A shocking transformation had occurred!

The seemingly flawless Little Stone Emperor, like a delicate porcelain vessel struck by a violent blow, was now crisscrossed with cracks. His body, which had dominated two worlds, was splitting apart!

No one knew what had happened. The Little Stone Emperor, who had surged to the absolute peak, was bleeding, his form collapsing...

The seven kings all lowered their heads, not daring to move. Even though they sensed the Little Stone Emperor was heading toward destruction, they still did not dare to act recklessly.

At this moment, he was undoubtedly at his most dangerous—he could easily destroy anyone!

"So this is how it is..." The Little Stone Emperor's disheveled hair flew wildly as he gazed up at the heavens, his eyes deep and profound, as if conversing with some unseen entity.

"Ultimate sublimation—I depart!" The Little Stone Emperor suddenly let out a great roar that spread across the myriad heavens. All the Stone-Man Kings were shaken.

"All things are incomplete; when flourishing, decline follows. When the sun reaches its zenith, it begins to move; when the moon is full, it begins to wane. The Great Dao has flaws—what is flawed must be destroyed."
