Ten years of bitter cultivation of the mysterious stone carving had deeply benefited Xiao Chen. His physique was constantly changing, and he vaguely felt as if he had been reborn, his bones and flesh remade. Yet what ultimately gave him faith in this Qi-Refining Diagram was a fierce battle deep in the Kunlun Mountains.

He had gone to witness Lan Nuo's divine act of breaking the void, but on the way, he was insulted by the guards of the Royal Celestial Maiden. That was the most desperate fight of his life. He cut down thirteen royal iron guards to break free, and in the chaos, he slew Zhao Lin'er's personal maidservant.

This proved that the mysterious stone carving was no ordinary scripture. Ten years of bitter cultivation had allowed his skills to reach a respectable level.

Day after day of practice deepened Xiao Chen's understanding of the Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram, and the more he understood, the more he felt it must have a continuation. He sensed that this was not a complete cultivation method!

Legend said that the stele bearing the inscription 'Ever-Pacify the Yellow River' had fallen from the Nine Heavens. Perhaps there were other divine steles like it, but the chance of obtaining the subsequent inscriptions was slim indeed!

Sunlight, moonlight, the essence of plants and trees... The natural world held endless primordial energy, countless forms of power. In the coconut grove, Xiao Chen circulated his refinement technique, drawing in the essence of heaven and earth to nourish his wounded body. A faint, misty glow surrounded him.

Two hours later, he emerged from his meditative stillness. His injuries were less severe than he had imagined. He estimated he could fully recover within ten days.

The red sun was setting in the west, its dying rays spilling across the sea, painting it a vivid crimson. The ocean met the sky; seawater, misty clouds, a stretch of fading red.

Then—"Boom!" "Boom!" "Boom!"—the beach began to tremble. Heavy, dull sounds reached Xiao Chen's ears. He turned to look, and his face instantly changed.

A silver behemoth crawled up from the sea onto the shore. Its body was a full fifty meters long. At first glance, it looked like a silver crocodile the size of a mountain, but upon closer inspection, the differences were clear.

Along its spine, like a silver mountain range, stood over a hundred enormous bone spikes, each two meters long, gleaming coldly. They rose like a forest of sharp spears, guarding its rear.

It was simply colossal. Its entire body was covered in gleaming silver scales, each over a meter long and half a meter wide. From afar, this silver giant, shimmering with cold light, looked utterly ferocious.

The silver beast had eight thick, powerful clawed arms. At the ends of those arms, sharp giant claws glinted with a somber light, chilling to behold. Moreover, it bore on its head a single, sharp horn seven or eight meters long, flickering with cold silver radiance. This was truly a terrifying giant monster.

Its jade-green eyes, huge as millstones, gave off torch-like ghostly light. It swept its gaze across the shoreline, then lumbered onto the sand.

Facing the setting sun, it opened its bloody maw, revealing a row of meter-long fangs like broadswords. It let out a terrifying roar that shook the nearby sea. The waves churned violently. Seabirds in the sky fled in terror, and some closer ones plunged straight into the ocean.

Such power! It truly shook heaven and earth!

Though Xiao Chen had covered his ears at once, they still rang with a deafening buzz. The colossal roar was like thunder exploding right beside him.

After roaring at the setting sun, the fifty-meter-long giant beast crouched on the sand, closed its huge eyes, and seemed to intend to rest here.

Xiao Chen was deeply shaken. The World of Immortality was indeed extraordinary!

Gazing at the silver behemoth like a mountain ridge, he remembered the ancient ferocious beast of seafaring legend—the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon!

This hideous, terrifying giant was exactly the same as the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon of legend!

Legend said that the ancient beast could overturn rivers and seas, summon wind and rain. Any ship that encountered it was doomed to destruction. It was the most savage monster in the ocean; even the Sea God would flee at the sight of it!

"A primordial savage beast that can rival gods... actually exists in this world!" Remembering those ancient tales, Xiao Chen's face was filled with shock. He had never thought that in the World of Immortality, he would witness such a legendary monster with his own eyes.

Though he was far away, Xiao Chen could already feel the fierce, baleful aura. This colossal ancient beast had torn apart countless sea creatures in its time!

Xiao Chen was very rational. He stood up, moved lightly, and slowly walked deeper into the coconut grove. Only when he was certain the ancient beast could not detect him did he stop and watch from a distance.

The Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon was like a silver mountain range, lying still and silent, as if turned to stone. Xiao Chen guessed that this beach might be its resting place, and it would probably sleep here through the night.

Ever since the ancient beast climbed onto the shore and roared at the setting sun, the nearby primeval forest had fallen silent. Only deep within the island came the howls of savage beasts.

A gentle sea breeze blew, and the evening glow was fading. In the dense forest by the sea, Xiao Chen startled countless birds into flight. The ground was covered with bird eggs—dinner was settled.

Moonlight flowed like water under a canopy of stars. Waves lapped gently against the sand. A faintly salty sea breeze stirred softly in the night. Xiao Chen slowly drifted into sleep in the coconut grove.

At dawn, a red sun rose. Golden light spread across the sea's horizon.

A soul-shaking, ear-splitting roar pierced the clouds!

The legendary ancient beast, the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon, was opening its bloody maw at the sun just rising above the ocean, bellowing a sky-shaking roar. The calm blue morning sea surged up from the sheer force of the sound waves. The seabirds that had been flying across the sky vanished in terror.

The massive silver dragon moved all eight arms and claws. The beach rumbled as it slowly stepped into the sea. The calm ocean instantly boiled like a tsunami, sending up towering waves as the silver form thrashed in the water.

Only when it gradually faded into the distance did the sea slowly calm down.

The tide rose and fell. The seawater washed away all traces of the dragon from the shore.
