Heaven and earth shattered, rivers flowed in reverse!

Stars flickered and died, the cosmos collapsed.

This was the true scene within the first sky curtain.

Time flowed backward. Li Huiyou, the Otherworld Ancestral God, displayed a supreme divine ability, attempting to reverse the river of time, charge into the primeval age, and kill Father.

The once deathly still land gradually came to life. Tender sprouts broke through the soil, fresh flowers bloomed, and green grass covered the earth. They were approaching the primeval era. The vast, desolate land of the Death World was showing signs of life.

"Awoo—!"

With a tremendous roar, the desolate land shook violently. A herd of mountain-sized primeval savage beasts thundered past. On the lush earth, wild beasts howled, and enormous demonic birds flapped their wings, spanning hundreds of zhang, blotting out the sky and covering the sun. Time and space were reversing, approaching the primeval age!

In the distance, the six Otherworld Half-Ancestors and the eight Domain Kings were utterly shocked, unable to speak. They could not even grasp the threshold of such a world-shaking divine ability.

Could one truly penetrate the ages and return to the past to kill an opponent? If so, this divine ability could be said to be invincible under heaven!

They were not Ancestral Gods, so they naturally could not know the secrets of this level of divine ability. This ability required first enveloping the present-day target before reversing into the primeval age. But even so, being able to drag the target back to the primeval age was still terrifying to the extreme.

The crowd clearly saw Father standing with his hands behind his back, walking leisurely into the primeval passage. He was not forced in but entered willingly.

"If I did not go with you this once, you would probably not be convinced. But displaying your divine ability before me is truly a waste of effort."

The cool white beast's voice carried magnetism, filled with powerful confidence.

"Aah!" Li Huiyou roared at the sky, raising the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd with one hand, pointing it diagonally at the southern heavens. Divine flames blazed around his body, and he merged with the starry sky above. All the stars in the heavens flickered.

Tens of millions of star rivers fell like great waterfalls, ten thousand rays of dazzling light linking the starry universe with the primeval land.

That region had completely separated from the space where Xiao Chen and the others stood, reversing toward the primeval age!

"Perish in the primeval age!"

Li Huiyou's Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, a full zhang in length, was pitch-black, flickering with an eerie cold light, and pointed at Father.

Terrifying light instantly engulfed the white beast, turning that place into a chaotic spacetime of pure divine power.

He wanted to strike Father back to the moment of his birth, reducing him to his weakest state.

But unexpectedly, within the eye of the chaotic spacetime storm, the white beast held a blade of grass in its mouth, unmoving. All the power could not touch his body in the slightest.

"I am eternal, imperishable for ten thousand ages. Though your divine ability defies heaven, what can it do to me?!"

The cool little beast was like a boulder in the chaotic eye of the storm, its aura heavy and unshakable. There was even less chance of being struck back to its infancy.

Father, hands clasped behind his back, walked step by step through the chaotic spacetime toward Li Huiyou, who was still unleashing his supreme divine ability. Father moved against the force of time and through the turbulence of space, walking straight up to Li Huiyou's front. He said, "Divine ability and supreme power have no effect on me."

Father was astonishingly ferocious, like a divine beast!

Li Huiyou completely changed color. This heaven-defying race was even more terrifying than legend suggested!

His world-shaking divine ability could not harm Father in the slightest, filling him with great unease. The Doomsday Heavenly Halberd in his hand drew upon the power of all the stars in the heavens, thrusting toward Father.

"Ding."

Father extended a snow-white little beast claw, fast and precise, and flicked the sharp edge of the halberd, holding it three inches from his body.

Li Huiyou let out a long cry. Ten thousand zhang of the sky were shattered by the sound. All the stars in the heavens shook. Such was the power of an Ancestral God—every movement was enough to shake the cosmos!

The Doomsday Heavenly Halberd's divine power reached the heavens, borrowing the might of the universe, sending down ten million layers of annihilation light, tearing down toward Father.

"Three Realms Reincarnation!"

At that moment, the white beast holding a blade of grass spoke these four words. It no longer used martial techniques, displaying a divine ability for the first time.

Instantly, the primeval heavens and earth split into three. Three great worlds separated, and the wheel of spacetime began to turn!

The power of reversed time and space was completely redirected. The primeval heavens and earth slowly receded, and the wheel of the three realms began to revolve!

Li Huiyou cried out, trying to pierce through heaven and earth with the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd to escape the terrifying vortex of the three realms reincarnation.

"Bang."

Li Huiyou had just rushed out but was instantly forcibly dragged back.

"Escape beyond the three realms! Impossible within the five elements!"

The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou roared in panic, desperately trying to flee this terrifying three realms reincarnation.

He tried to escape beyond the three realms, to enter the "Eternal Unknown Place." His figure rapidly grew faint, on the verge of disappearing.

"Sever."

To this, the cool white beast simply held the blade of grass and casually spat out this single word.

Just as Li Huiyou was about to vanish, his connection to the Eternal Unknown Place was suddenly severed. The white beast, not far away, had "severed" him out!

"Bang!"

The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou fell from ten thousand zhang in the sky. The stars in the deep firmament dimmed. The three great realms turned, dividing the three worlds, alternating in appearance, rotating toward Li Huiyou.

"Aah!"

Li Huiyou cried out. His Ancestral God power was rapidly draining away. The great rotation of the three realms devoured everything!

"Time, reverse! Primeval age, recede! Return!" Li Huiyou was truly frightened. Enveloped by the three realms reincarnation, his body was about to split apart. He once again activated the power of time, stabilizing the collapsing chaotic spacetime and returning from the primeval age.

"Boom!"

With a great sound, he finally returned. But the divine power of the three realms reincarnation remained unchanged, enveloping his body, not receding.

The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou was fixed in place. He roared, "Break through the three realms, escape heaven and earth—break for me!"

A series of massive lightning bolts, thick as mountain ranges, suddenly struck down from the heavens. Each bolt connected to a star, converging on Li Huiyou's location.

He wanted to forcibly flee, escaping the central region of the three realms reincarnation.

"Still!"

Father held the blade of grass in his mouth, speaking just one word again. Sacred light shot up to the heavens, instantly shattering the ten-thousand-zhang lightning and fixing that region of heaven and earth. The three realms reincarnation was unaffected, beginning its cyclic rotation.

This was the Ancestral God-level duel. Although there was no intense collision, any single divine light within could easily pierce a Half-Ancestor.

"You..." Li Huiyou was blocked again. Even as powerful as he was, his body began to emit creaking sounds, about to shatter into pieces.

He was extraordinarily shocked and enraged, roaring, "Eternal, imperishable! My body is immortal!"

Tens of millions of rays of light shot up to the heavens, merging into one with the celestial firmament. He connected with the vast starry sky, becoming an eternal unity.

"You don't decide that. I say you shatter, so you shall shatter." The cool little beast's voice was calm but extremely arrogant, and it solemnly cried: "Three Realms Reincarnation!"

"Boom!"

The power of three great worlds surged forth. The reincarnation of the worlds instantly drowned Li Huiyou.

The billions of starlight beams in the heavens were severed in an instant, losing connection with Li Huiyou, isolating him within the storm of the three realms.

"No!" The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou cried out in fury, "Open for me!" The Doomsday Heavenly Halberd in his hand shot up like a demonic dragon, swelling to the size of a mountain, slashing across heaven and earth.

"Rumble."

The power of the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd surged, but it could not block the three realms reincarnation.

When the demonic dragon was completely suppressed, the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou cried out in despair, shaking heaven and earth. The first light curtain of the Most Evil Place nearly shattered.

"Crack!"

The hedgehog-like divine armor on Li Huiyou's body split open, and then a harsh shattering sound rang out incessantly. The Ancestral God battle suit completely collapsed.

"Clatter!"

Shards of armor fell to the ground. The battle suit had completely left his body. Then, with a great boom, even the body of an Ancestral God, strong as he was, split apart and shattered under the divine ability of the three realms reincarnation!

The scene was extremely shocking. This was an Ancestral God!

In an instant, a body that should have been imperishable for ten thousand ages was destroyed.

In the distance, all the Otherworld Half-Ancestors and the Death World Domain Kings were terrified to the extreme, unwilling to believe what they were seeing.

Within the Lost Paradise, the crowd was also greatly shaken. Especially the little beast Keke, whose big eyes were shining, and whose little fists were clenched very tightly, filled with longing.

Li Huiyou's divine consciousness burst out, howling in fury. He wanted to reform his body. The shattered pieces quickly moved to gather together.

But the divine power of the three realms reincarnation mercilessly crushed over. The newly reformed Ancestral God body split apart again, and with a thunderous roar, it was smashed to powder, annihilated without a trace, not even a speck of residue remaining!

"You destroyed my physical body?!" Li Huiyou screamed. He could not accept this fact.

Although he was an incarnation of the true Li Huiyou, this incarnation was definitely forged from a true Ancestral God's body, immensely powerful.

To be destroyed like this... Father looked very delicate at that moment, but his small snow-white body standing alone between heaven and earth intimidated everyone.

He murmured to himself, "Since your divine consciousness is not extinguished, then let me perform the Six Paths of Reincarnation once more."

At these words, everyone was shaken. The great divine ability of the Six Paths of Reincarnation was unprecedented and ancient, a truly invincible divine ability. There were few laws and powers that could contend with it.

Li Huiyou immediately felt a sense of terror, trembling as he said, "You... you even know this divine ability?"

"There are many things I know..." Father became serious at this point, about to unleash some world-shaking divine ability.

"Swoosh!"

At that moment, Li Huiyou's divine consciousness shot like a beam of light toward the second sky curtain. He was frightened into retreating without fighting.

Just as Father was about to unleash the Six Paths of Reincarnation, he suddenly stopped and looked toward the outside of the first sky curtain.

This allowed Li Huiyou's divine consciousness to successfully escape into the second hazy sky curtain.

"I said I would use the Six Paths of Reincarnation to take you. Even if another person came, they could not stop it. Let you go in for a while first. I will come to take you later."

As Father spoke these words, he was not watching the fleeing Li Huiyou at all. Instead, he was constantly gazing outside the first sky curtain. There, a tall figure stood like a heavenly stele. It was he who had just sent out a powerful divine thought to interfere with Father, temporarily allowing Li Huiyou to escape disaster.

That giant shadow, like a heavenly stele, vanished outside the first sky curtain. Then the second sky curtain trembled slightly like water ripples, and the giant shadow charged inside.

Father did not stop him, quietly watching all of this.

The Ancestral God-level battle came to a temporary end. Keke immediately rushed over.

"Ya ya!" The little beast's big eyes were red, brimming with tears. Even as confused as she was, she now sensed something. She mumbled timidly, "You are..."

"Haha..." Father laughed heartily, tossing away the crystal sunglasses, and rubbed Keke's head.

He said nothing, but his expression was full of affection, completely different from the cool demeanor just moments ago.

"Not good! Damn it, time is too long. My power has faded severely. I cannot waste it. I must keep some to destroy one or two Otherworld Ancestral Gods." As Father said this, he transformed into a beam of light, reassembling a shattered great bluestone, merging into it. The crooked writing reappeared.

At that moment, a divine light emerged from the great bluestone, shooting toward the Slaughter Light that hung motionless before the second sky curtain.

The Slaughter Light, over ten li long but only as thick as a thumb, like a golden thread, quickly flew over, coiling around the Lost Paradise like a loyal guard, guarding it there.

In the distance, the six Otherworld Half-Ancestors and the eight Domain Kings were stunned, then immediately rushed into the second sky curtain. This place was too dangerous for them. Even the Slaughter Light had been tamed...

"The Most Evil Place has long been opened up by me. It can distinguish the divine power fluctuations of the Lost Paradise and will not block you," Father transmitted this divine thought, then fell completely silent, seemingly sinking into slumber.

This was not the true Keke's father, but a very powerful spiritual imprint he had left behind, always guarding the Most Evil Place.

"Too... too... too heaven-defying!"

When everything calmed down, Jin Sanyi was the first to jump up. The guy was now insanely excited, wishing he could hug the great bluestone and talk for three days and three nights as he kept circling it.

Wu Ming was even more agitated. Getting involved with such a dominant figure, entering the Most Evil Place would not be the deadly trial they had imagined. There was a good chance they would obtain the Eternal Light.

The Slaughter Light cleared the way, and Keke drove the Lost Paradise, charging into the second sky curtain.

Here, bees flew and butterflies danced, flowers and grasses exuded fragrance, divine springs flowed freely—a scene of extraordinary beauty.

The magnificent landscape stretched endlessly, seeming to have no end, connecting with the azure sky in the far distance.

And there, on the distant horizon, a mass of light like the sun bloomed with brilliant divine radiance, surrounded by rings of rainbow light.

It was only the size of a house, but it was filled with an eternal aura, as if it had existed there since before the creation of heaven and earth.

The Most Evil Place... Eternal Light!

"Absolutely right, that is the Eternal Light!" Wu Ming could not contain his excitement, muttering to himself.

"Bang!"

The Martial Imprint was sent flying. His weapon soul had grown much fainter, and the divine wood in his hand was covered in terrible scars.

In the distance, the powerful Ancestral Lord and the giant shadow like a heavenly stele were confronting each other. Clearly, the Martial Imprint was out of the game.

An Ancestral Lord-level battle—this was a true peak duel!

It was not like the fight between the Martial Imprint and Li Huiyou. One was a weapon soul separated from its main body for endless ages, deeply depleted—an Ancestral God weapon. The other was an incarnation of an Ancestral God—neither was a true Ancestral God.

"Ant, begone!"

The two confronting Ancestral God-level beings coldly and mercilessly shouted at the Martial Imprint.

It was not that they did not want to take the Martial Imprint's life, but they would need him to scout ahead later, sensing that the path ahead was unusual.

The Martial Imprint forced out a green breath and clenched his fist.

The second sky curtain trembled slightly. Before anyone could see clearly, a human figure had already appeared in front of the Martial Imprint. No one had seen how he had rushed over.

"Get up," he said to the Martial Imprint in a low voice.

At that moment, the Martial Imprint's expression froze, and he turned to stone on the spot.

After saying this, the withered human figure disappeared again. At the same time, an explosion of living light occurred at the position of the two confronting Ancestral God-level experts.

Powerful battle qi shook the entire Most Evil Place. Both powerful experts were sent staggering backward.

"...Just because of you, you dare to call him an ant?"

The calm and indifferent voice carried no emotion.
