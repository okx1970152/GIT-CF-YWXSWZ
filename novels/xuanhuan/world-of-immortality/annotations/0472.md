## Chapter Overview

Chapter 472 plunges Xiao Chen into the heart of danger: the Burial Weapon Valley, a place where even divine weapons gain sentience and half-ancestors tread carefully. The valley's oppressive stillness is broken by the appearance of a mysterious female projection, whose power rivals a half-ancestor of the seventh heaven. She warns Xiao Chen that entering the ninth path means death, but retreat is impossible — the world gate has closed. Joined by the Rakshasa Half-Ancestor, Skeleton King, Angel Holy God, and Asura Half-Ancestor, Xiao Chen launches a desperate joint attack against her. Yet she wields a "living" War Sword, far more potent than Xiao Chen's collected blades, and scatters them all with a single stroke. The chapter sets an ominous tone: the valley's depths conceal horrors beyond imagination, and the Martial Imprint itself is locked in battle with two other supreme beings.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen on the dark mountain path, facing an ethereal woman who embodies both beauty and lethal intent. She attacks with a sword light that shatters space, forcing Xiao Chen to use his divine armor and War Swords for defense. When she demands he surrender all his War Swords in exchange for safe passage, he refuses — a decision that sets the stage for confrontation. The arrival of four half-ancestor enemies (Rakshasa, Skeleton King, Angel Holy God, Asura) initially seems like a stroke of luck, as they join forces with Xiao Chen to attack the woman. But their combined assault is effortlessly neutralized by her living War Sword, which blazes with unrivaled brilliance and sends Xiao Chen tumbling into the depths of the valley.

After a long fall, Xiao Chen regains his footing and continues downward, encountering weapon spirits animated by the valley's cursed energy. A skeletal soldier wielding a decayed blade strikes with half-ancestor-level force, forcing Xiao Chen to expend great effort to destroy it. He then faces a rain of flying daggers that can pierce even his divine armor, barely surviving the assault. The Martial Imprint is seen battling two terrifying opponents — the woman with the War Sword and a man wielding a Chaos War Halberd — both at the ninth heaven of half-ancestor. Xiao Chen and Bai Qi, another survivor, flee through the valley, pursued by countless sentient weapons ranging from small rodents to gigantic T-Rex-like creatures. They realize the valley is a prison for a living Ancestral God weapon, chained at the bottom, whose breath alone sends sword beams for miles.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Burial Weapon Valley (葬兵谷) is a unique location where divine weapons and artifacts become sentient after absorbing the valley's residual energies. This concept draws from the xianxia trope of weapons developing wills over time, but here it is pushed to an extreme: even broken fragments take on animal forms and attack intruders. The "living War Sword" wielded by the female projection represents a weapon that has fully awakened, surpassing the inert War Swords Xiao Chen has collected. The Martial Imprint (武之印记) is a manifestation of pure martial will, capable of independent action but still limited by the valley's laws — it cannot fly here, and it struggles against opponents of comparable attainment. The half-ancestor (半祖) realm is divided into nine heavens, with those at the seventh heaven or above being nearly invincible in normal combat. The valley appears to be a sealed domain for a chained Ancestral God weapon, a being of near-divine power that has been restrained for unknown ages.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major confrontation between Xiao Chen and the forces of the valley. The mysterious woman and the man with the Chaos Halberd are likely guardians or prisoners of the chained Ancestral God weapon. Their power is far beyond what Xiao Chen can currently face alone, hinting that he will need to rely on his allies — Bai Qi, the Martial Imprint, and even the half-ancestor enemies may be forced into uneasy cooperation. The living War Sword she wields may be connected to the very weapon chained at the valley's heart, and Xiao Chen's own War Swords may resonate with it. The valley's depths clearly hold secrets that could advance his cultivation or unlock the next stage of his martial path. Readers should watch for the Martial Imprint's fate, the origin of the chained weapon, and whether Xiao Chen can escape before the valley claims him.
