## Chapter Overview
In this brutal chapter, Xiao Chen faces a coalition of cultivators on a snowy peak, orchestrated by alliance leaders who seek to test his strength and seize his dragon eggs. The scene is one of cold, calculated violence as Xiao Chen unleashes the full power of his Big Dipper Sealing Divinity at the Sixth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding. What begins as a probing attack quickly becomes a massacre, with Xiao Chen systematically dismantling his opponents. The chapter captures the tragic desolation of the World of Immortality: pristine snow stained with blood, and a lone fighter standing against a greedy mob. The emotional tone is one of grim necessity and mythic resolve, as Xiao Chen makes an indelible mark on the island's power dynamics.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the instigators' calls to attack being exposed as orchestrated by alliance leaders, revealing the political machinations behind the assault. Xiao Chen immediately demonstrates his superior speed and power by leaping into the air and killing a spellmaster at the Mortal-Shedding Third Heaven with a single kick, shocking the crowd. He then turns his attention to the most vocal provocateurs, cutting down members of the Nature Alliance, Treefolk Valley, and other factions with surgical precision. The Big Dipper Sealing Divinity forms a protective light screen that absorbs incoming spell and spiritual attacks, while the accumulated energy is released in a devastating burst that annihilates four foes at once. Xiao Chen fights with cold efficiency, using a stolen divine sword to bisect, decapitate, and impale his enemies. The narrative emphasizes his complete control over the technique, contrasting with his earlier reliance on luck against the space spiritist Liu Mu. By the end, Xiao Chen stands blood-soaked but unbowed, his image etched into the memory of all witnesses as a demonic, unstoppable force. The chapter underscores his shift from survival mode to active domination, as he deliberately intimidates and destroys to establish his reputation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Big Dipper Sealing Divinity (北斗封神) is a defensive technique that manifests seven star-like energy points, forming a protective light screen. It is derived from Xiao Chen's cultivation method and reflects the celestial symbolism common in Chinese mythology, where the Big Dipper (北斗) is associated with fate and control. The technique's ability to absorb and redirect attacks marks it as a sophisticated form of energy manipulation, akin to the "Beidou" practice in Daoist alchemy. The Mortal-Shedding realm (蜕凡) represents the first true step beyond mortality, with nine heavenly tiers indicating progressive refinement. At the Sixth Heaven, Xiao Chen's power is sufficient to overwhelm most opponents on the island. The alliances (e.g., Nature Alliance, Treefolk Valley) are pragmatic formations born of the island's sealed environment; they operate through political manipulation rather than direct confrontation, as seen in the orchestrated assault. The chapter also illustrates the importance of reputation in the World of Immortality, where perceived weakness invites attack.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a clear turning point for Xiao Chen: he no longer flees or hides but actively asserts dominance. Readers should pay attention to the psychological shift—Xiao Chen's internal coldness and his willingness to become a feared figure. The battle sets the stage for future confrontations with stronger enemies, particularly the illusionist Kailuo and the flying dagger master Wang Tong, who are mentioned in earlier chapters. The alliances will now treat Xiao Chen as a major threat, potentially forcing them to cooperate more closely. The chapter also raises questions about the limits of the Big Dipper Sealing Divinity: can it withstand attacks from higher-realm cultivators? How many more heavenly tiers can Xiao Chen ascend? The lasting image of Xiao Chen as a blood-drenched demon god foreshadows his growing legend on Dragon Island. The story is now poised for an escalation in both power and scheming.
