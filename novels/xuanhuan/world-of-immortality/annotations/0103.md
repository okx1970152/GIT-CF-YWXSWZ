## Chapter Overview

This chapter deepens the mythic horror of Dragon Island by unveiling an ancient malevolent ghost that has plagued a hidden village for generations. The tone is one of tragic desolation as the village's history of loss and helplessness is laid bare. Xiao Chen learns that the same ghost he sensed in the snowy mountains has followed him, compressing the already narrow window of opportunity to fight it. The chapter masterfully blends lore exposition with a sudden violent attack, raising the stakes and forging a grim alliance of necessity. Keke's defiant reaction to the mention of the Ancestral Dragon adds a touch of fierce loyalty amidst the dread, while the revelation that dragon king blood can harm the ghost offers a fragile hope.

## Key Plot Points

The old woman recounts the village's nightmare: a malevolent ghost, the only entity to escape the Death City, has preyed on them for uncounted ages. Each century, the ghost undergoes a molting, emerging in a weakened state to steal life force before entering a centuries-long sleep. The village's repeated attempts to fight back only brought worse retaliation, and now only a few dozen survivors remain. Xiao Chen reveals that he encountered the ghost in the snowy mountains and it likely followed him here, meaning the ghost's three-month weak period may have already passed one to two months, leaving less than a month. Suddenly, an attack occurs at the village entrance: a mist-shrouded woman (Yan Qingcheng) and the Purple Dragon King are injured, and the ghost is identified as the assailant. Crucially, the Purple Dragon King's blood wounds the ghost, revealing a potent weapon against it. The old woman realizes dragon king blood can both lift their curse and harm the ghost, setting up a desperate strategy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In Chinese mythology, a 魔鬼 (malevolent ghost) is a powerful undead being that actively seeks to harm the living, often by absorbing their life essence to strengthen its own ghostly body. The ghost's ability to undergo repeated molting (蜕变) echoes the cultivation concept of rebirth through shedding one's form, advancing toward a perfected state. On Dragon Island, the divine stele suppresses all power above the demigod level, capping the ghost at the Sky-Mastery realm but still making it overwhelmingly dangerous compared to mortal cultivators. The fact that dragon king blood harms the ghost taps into the traditional symbolism of dragon blood as a pure, yang-aligned substance that can dispel yin and evil. This chapter also reinforces the island's isolation and the cyclical tragedy of those trapped within its seal.

## Reading Guide

The chapter builds toward an inevitable confrontation with the malevolent ghost, now with a severe time limit. Xiao Chen's prior encounter with the ghost in the snowy mountains raises questions about its intelligence and motives—did it follow him intentionally? The alliance of necessity with Yan Qingcheng and the Purple Dragon King promises tense dynamics, as she remains an ambiguous figure. Readers should watch for how dragon king blood becomes a central resource in the coming battles, and whether the ghost's weakness period will be exploited in time. The deeper mystery of the Death City and the divine stele continues to loom, hinting that the ghost is only one symptom of a larger, more ancient secret on Dragon Island.
