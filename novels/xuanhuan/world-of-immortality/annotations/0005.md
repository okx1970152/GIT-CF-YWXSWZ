## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal moment in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey. Still recovering from his recent injuries, he stumbles upon a treasure that would make any practitioner in the World of Immortality envious: the eggs of the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon. The chapter opens with a rare moment of domestic tranquility—Xiao Chen cooking a rabbit over a fire, enjoying the simple pleasures of survival. But the peace is shattered by the dragon's unnatural agitation, drawing him to the beach. There, he witnesses the ancient beast laying two jade-like eggs, a scene that blends the sacred and the primal. What follows is a bold decision: Xiao Chen consumes one of the legendary dragon eggs, experiencing a painful transmutation that plants a silver light point in his Shangqiu acupoint. This single event redefines his cultivation path, hinting at a radical new method of acupoint divinization.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen continues his recovery from battle, spending his days hunting, cooking, and cultivating. His evening meal of roasted rabbit and wild fruit provides a moment of calm before the storm. The Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon, which has been relatively quiet, suddenly begins roaring and thrashing on the beach, disturbing the night. Driven by curiosity, Xiao Chen investigates and discovers the dragon has laid two enormous, luminous eggs. The dragon buries them in the sand and departs. The next morning, Xiao Chen digs up the eggs and, after testing their resilience, cracks one open. He consumes the core essence—a miniature dragon formed from primordial life energy. The essence burns through him, nearly destroying his body, but the Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram activates, refining the wild energy. After hours of agonizing cultivation, the dragon essence condenses into a single silver light point, settling into his left Shangqiu acupoint. This transforms the acupoint, making it shine like a beacon among the ordinary ones. Xiao Chen realizes that this light point represents a new approach: if he can repeat this process for all 365 major acupoints, the result would be unimaginable. As evening falls, the dragon returns from the sea, parting the waters, and the chapter ends on a note of looming confrontation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Dragon eggs are legendary treasures in Chinese mythology, often containing immense life essence that can bolster one's cultivation. In this chapter, Xiao Chen's consumption of the raw egg essence is a high-risk gamble—the wild yin energy of the dragon could have overwhelmed his body. His survival and refinement are due to the Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram, a cultivation method derived from the Yellow River Divine Stele. The acupoint system (穴道) is central to traditional Chinese medicine and Daoist body cultivation. There are 365 major acupoints corresponding to the days of the year. Xiao Chen's discovery that he can "divinize" these acupoints by absorbing beast essence sets him on a unique path, distinct from normal realm-based cultivation. This concept of acupoint divinization (穴道神化) blurs the line between physical tempering and spiritual enlightenment, a hallmark of Chen Dong's mythological dark fantasy style. The "silver light point" is a condensed manifestation of pure vitality, and the acupoint becomes a storage node for power, rather than a mere passage for qi.

## Reading Guide
This chapter establishes a foundational cultivation mechanic that will define Xiao Chen's growth for arcs to come. The silver light point in the Shangqiu acupoint is just the first step—readers should watch for how Xiao Chen acquires more beast essences to fill other acupoints. The dragon mother's return at the end foreshadows immediate danger; Xiao Chen has stolen from one of the island's apex predators, and retribution is likely. Meanwhile, the mystery of Zhao Lin'er and other survivors lingers. This chapter also reinforces the contrast between domestic survival and mythic cultivation—Xiao Chen is both a practical survivor and a destined explorer of ancient mysteries. The question of whether he can complete the divinization of all 365 acupoints before his enemies catch up drives the tension forward.
