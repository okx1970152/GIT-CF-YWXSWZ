## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a decisive naval confrontation as Xiao Chen ambushes three Asura Heavenly Kings—the Asura Saintess and two ancient elders—in the South Sea. The battle showcases Xiao Chen’s matured combat prowess after his death-world cultivation. Using the 108 reverse waterfalls to isolate one elder, he engages the Saintess in a palm-to-palm duel that tests his Supreme Martial Body against her Asura Sacred Hand. The Weng and Duo sounds of the Eight Origin Sounds prove devastating. Xiao Chen not only defeats the trio but also shatters the Asura Sacred Sword, a half-ancestor artifact, forcing the Asura Half-Ancestor to personally intervene from afar. The chapter ends with the Half-Ancestor dispatching an Earth-Penetrating realm elder, Cold Han Bing, to annihilate Xiao Chen. The tone is intense and climactic, setting the stage for Xiao Chen’s boldest challenge yet.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen opens the battle by summoning 108 towering reverse waterfalls that rise from the sea and bend upward toward the clouds, creating a spectacular prison that traps one of the old Asura elders. The Asura Saintess attacks first with her Asura Sacred Hand, whose thirteen black holes threaten to annihilate space itself. Xiao Chen counters with palm techniques that contain world-force, meeting her destructive power with pure vitality and martial strength. The five exchanges crack the Saintess’s arm bones and force her to retreat. The two elders then assault together: one uses the Asura Heavenly Way domain to compress space, and the other engulfs Xiao Chen in blood-red flames. The Saintess draws the Asura Sacred Sword—a half-ancestor refined artifact—and swings it in a massive blood-light slash. Xiao Chen counters with a War Sword and repeatedly strikes the Sacred Sword, eventually shattering it with a combined effort of the Demons-Suppressing style, Reverse Chaos style, and the Origin Sounds. After wounding all three, he seals them in his acupoint spaces rather than killing them, showing a rare restraint. The Half-Ancestor, enraged, sends a beam of destructive energy through the broken sword, but Xiao Chen escapes via Eight Aspect Extreme speed. Later, Wu Ming arrives and warns Xiao Chen that Cold Han Bing, an Earth-Penetrating realm Asura, has entered the Nine Provinces to hunt him. Xiao Chen coolerly declares he will kill this ‘relic’ to cripple the Asura clan’s high-end power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens the cultivation hierarchy. The Asura Saintess and her elders are Perfected Man realm Heavenly Kings, a tier just below the Half-Ancestor level. The Eight Origin Sounds first appeared on the Yellow-Clay Altar; the ‘Weng’ sound governs life and death resonance, while the ‘Duo’ sound manipulates Yin-Yang interplay, creating attacks that are both solid and void-like. Xiao Chen’s use of both sounds together amplifies their soul-shaking power. The Earth-Penetrating realm (彻地) lies above Perfected Man and below Heaven-Reaching (Half-Ancestor). An Earth-Penetrating expert is called an ‘Emperor’ (皇) and is a clan treasure—only five or six exist in the entire Asura race. Cold Han Bing’s dispatch signals the Half-Ancestor’s desperation. The breaking of the Asura Sacred Sword is significant: each clan’s half-ancestor artifact is tied to the ancestor’s own power, and damaging it is a direct insult that cannot be forgiven. The principle of ‘killing Heavenly Kings without provoking Half-Ancestors’ is a political threshold that Xiao Chen has now crossed.

## Reading Guide

The arrival of Cold Han Bing raises the stakes dramatically. Xiao Chen’s confidence in claiming he can kill an Earth-Penetrating emperor suggests he may have hidden trump cards from his death-world training—perhaps the third Origin Sound or a new War Sword technique. The trial battle with the half-ancestor remains scheduled, and now Xiao Chen faces an intermediate threat that will test his True Martial Body against a higher-realm opponent. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen plans to use the terrain of the Nine Provinces or the Bone Well to his advantage. The gathering of other foreign kings—Tiger Clan, Sun Knights, Five Elements Heavenly Kings, the Mysterious War King, and the Yaksha Queen—suggests a multipolar conflict is brewing. Xiao Chen’s success or failure against Cold Han Bing will determine whether the Half-Ancestors escalate further or retreat.
