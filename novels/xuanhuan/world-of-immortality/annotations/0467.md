## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers into the heart of the Corpse Sea, where Xiao Chen is cornered by a coalition of Half-Ancestors. The atmosphere is thick with tension and betrayal as Babra, an Otherworld Ancestral God, attempts to manipulate the group against Xiao Chen. But Xiao Chen turns the tables, exposing Babra's true origin and uniting the Half-Ancestors against him. A brutal battle ensues, only to awaken a terrifying entity slumbering within the sea of corpses—a being whose power dwarfs even the assembled Half-Ancestors. The revelation of the Ancestral God Corpse Toxin and Babra's personal tragedy adds a layer of tragic depth to the conflict, blending political maneuvering, cosmic horror, and raw grief into a single, unforgettable chapter.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter begins with Xiao Chen isolated and surrounded by thirteen Half-Ancestors, all coveting the Great Annihilation War Spear sealed within a jade box. Babra, the first to attack, proposes a joint strike to seal Xiao Chen, but Xiao Chen cleverly throws the box aside and reveals that Babra is not a native being but an Otherworld Ancestral God named Babra—a figure from ancient wars against the primal ancestors. This revelation shifts the alliance: the Half-Ancestors immediately surround Babra instead. The ensuing battle is fierce; Babra wields eight bone spears and a scorpion tail, and his indestructible Ancestral God bone body fends off multiple assaults. Desperate, Babra uses his Ancestral God essence blood to empower the War Spear, shattering the prison and deliberately awakening a sleeping sovereign in the Corpse Sea. The awakened being, mighty enough to threaten all Half-Ancestors, demands they remove the formation flags binding him. Forced to comply, the group discovers the jade box contains not only the War Spear but also liquid Ancestral God Corpse Toxin, which instantly afflicts Babra. In his agony, Babra realizes the toxin was refined from the corpse of a woman he once loved, revealing a grim scheme by the Otherworld to weaponize their own dead. The chapter ends with the mysterious sovereign's voice echoing, its immense power compelling obedience.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of 'Ancestral God Corpse Toxin' is a striking example of how ancient cultivation worlds weaponize even the dead. In Chinese mythology, powerful beings' bodies are often sources of both blessing and curse; here, the Otherworld sect deliberately refined the corpses of fallen Ancestral Gods into a toxin capable of killing demi-gods. This parallels historical Chinese alchemical practices where toxic substances were derived from rare materials, but on a cosmic scale. The 'Corpse Sea' itself is a macabre landscape formed from the decomposition of a powerful female Ancestral God, a reminder of the immense suffering caused by the wars between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld. The term '祖神' (Ancestral God) denotes beings who have touched the source laws of worlds, and their blood (精血) is a concentrated essence sought after by all. The '大破灭战矛' (Great Annihilation War Spear) is a legendary weapon that once wounded the Ancestral God Youchao, underscoring the epic scale of the conflict. Babra's tragic backstory—finding his beloved's body turned into poison—adds a deeply human element to these mythic battles, reminiscent of classical Chinese tragic tales where love and duty clash amidst war.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a masterful blend of political intrigue, high-stakes combat, and shocking revelation. Readers should pay close attention to how Xiao Chen uses his knowledge of the Otherworld to turn enemies into temporary allies—a key skill in the Hundred Races era. The emergence of the sleeping sovereign raises the stakes immensely: will it prove friend or foe? The discovery that the Great Annihilation War Spear is guarded by deadly Toxin suggests that the Otherworld has left many traps in the Death World. Note the parallels to the earlier snake-elephant dragon valley and the God Burial Valley; the pattern of ancient sealed dangers recurs. Xiao Chen's ability to endure and adapt remains central. The mention of 'liquid Ancestral God Corpse Toxin' may foreshadow future uses or antidotes. Finally, Babra's emotional breakdown hints at a deeper, more tragic history behind the Otherworld's cruelty—a thread that may humanize even the most monstrous foes. Keep an eye on the mysterious sovereign's identity; it may be connected to the Female Sovereign or other Death World powers.
