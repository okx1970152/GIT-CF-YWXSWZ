## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a spectacular display of pure martial power as the Dugu clan, legendary swordsmen from the Mortal World, launches a full-scale assault on the Divine Palace Sect's mountaintop stronghold. Their coordinated iron swords tear through defenses designed to withstand demigods, culminating in the brief, awe-inspiring appearance of a figure whose single sword strike shatters the sect's ancestor’s divine thought and pierces the half-ancestor treasure. Amidst the chaos, Xiao Chen and Keke seize the opportunity to plunder the sect’s herb garden and alchemy hall, adding immense resources to their arsenal. The chapter balances epic martial spectacle with tactical opportunism, deepening Xiao Chen's resource base while highlighting the terrifying legacy of the Dugu lineage.

## Key Plot Points

The Dugu clan surrounds Mount Tai, their combined sword intent causing flowers to wither and trees to shed leaves. The Divine Palace Sect, trusting in their half-ancestor treasure—the Crystal Palace—retreats inside. The clan’s first concerted sword strike blasts the palace into the sky; subsequent strikes shatter multiple illusory palaces and force the palace to retreat. When the sect ancestor’s divine thought manifests, a distant figure—implied to be Dugu Qiubai—unsheathes a great iron sword, and with a single unseeable stroke, cleaves the divine thought and pierces the palace gate. The Dugu clan then storms the palace, mercilessly slaying the cult’s elders and disciples. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen leads Keke into the sect’s depths. They bypass defenders, reach the herb garden, and Keke uses Paradise Lost to harvest the entire garden. They locate the alchemy hall, confront a powerful old herbalist, but are saved by ten Dugu swordsmen who force the herbalist to flee. Keke then absorbs the alchemy hall into Paradise Lost, securing a massive haul of medicines and spirit herbs.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Dugu clan is a legendary lineage in xianxia, famously associated with Dugu Qiubai, a peerless swordsman who sought a worthy opponent. Their martial philosophy treats the body and sword as one, generating pure sword intent without relying on spells or divine arts. The collective sword formation of dozens of Dugu clansmen, while not a formal formation, amplifies their individual power to confront a half-ancestor treasure. The Crystal Palace is a half-ancestor artifact, meaning it was forged by a being at the Half-Ancestor cultivation level—the threshold below full Ancestral Gods. Such treasures possess limited sentience and can manifest their creator’s residual will. The Paradise Lost realm inherited by Keke is a pocket dimension that can store living beings and treasures, useful for large-scale looting. The chapter also reinforces the distinction between martial cultivators (who train the body and sword) and spell-based cultivators (who rely on artifacts and magic).

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a showcase of the Dugu clan’s power, setting them as a major wildcard in the Mortal World’s cultivation landscape. Their appearance and the implied presence of Dugu Qiubai himself hint at upcoming conflicts at the highest level. For Xiao Chen, the looting of the Divine Palace Sect’s resources is a critical upgrade: he can now divinize multiple acupoints, accelerating his cultivation. However, his open participation in the attack may draw the attention of other factions that covet the treasure or seek revenge against the Dugu clan. Readers should watch for the consequences of Xiao Chen’s alliance with such an aggressive force, and whether the spirit medicines will trigger a breakthrough in his path.
