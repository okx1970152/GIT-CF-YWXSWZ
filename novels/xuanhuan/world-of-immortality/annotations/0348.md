## Chapter Overview

In this poignant chapter, Xiao Chen discovers a mirror forged by the Ancestral God Nuwa in a village of the dead, which can project scenes of the Nine Provinces – his long-lost mortal home. His emotional confirmation that he truly comes from that world shocks the villagers, who revere it as a lost holy land. The chapter then follows Xiao Chen and the villagers on a journey to a nearby city of fire-seed beings, where he observes the market, exchange of bones and artifacts, and the rigid laws that prevent open conflict. Most significantly, a curious child leads him to a forbidden well, where a mysterious force pulls him in, revealing a direct vision of the Nine Provinces. This chapter balances worldbuilding, emotional resonance, and mystery, advancing Xiao Chen's personal quest while deepening the lore of the world of the dead.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen arrives at a small village populated by powerful fire-seed beings known as the God Tribe (神族). Their elder reveals a sacred mirror—a failed attempt by Nuwa to breach the worlds—that can show images of the Nine Provinces. Xiao Chen confirms he originated from that world, causing astonishment. The elder explains that most who reverse death in this realm lose their past memories. Xiao Chen then joins a trade expedition to a nearby city, accompanied by the elder Li Mu and the curious child Tianya. The journey highlights the harsh desert terrain and ancient ruins. In the city, Xiao Chen observes the market where fire-seeds, bones, and artifacts are traded. He notices that colored-diamond skeletons like himself are rare and coveted. Later, Tianya leads him to the abandoned western district, once part of a fallen king's domain and now cursed. There, an ancient well exerts a mysterious pull on Xiao Chen, and he falls in, experiencing a vivid vision of the Nine Provinces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter provides deep insight into the death world's hierarchy and cultivation. Fire-seed (火种) beings evolve by absorbing other fire-seeds, with colored-diamond skeletons (blue, red, purple) indicating higher potential. The God Tribe are beings who have fully reversed death and can reproduce, but they still lack full memories of past lives. The concept of 'king-level' fire-seed beings (君王级别) who once ruled vast domains introduces the political landscape of the death world. Nuwa's mirror is a relic from a primordial age when she attempted to create a passage between worlds, highlighting the theme of separation between the Nine Provinces and the afterlife. The forbidden well, located on the ruins of a dead king's city, may be a remnant of such ancient power, capable of bridging the two realms. These elements enrich the novel's mythology and Xiao Chen's personal journey.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up several major threads: Xiao Chen's emotional reconnection with his homeland, the mystery of the well as a potential passage, and the political structure of the death world. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's origin affects his interactions with the God Tribe and his reputation among fire-seed beings. The well scenes hint at a deeper connection between the Nine Provinces and the death world, possibly linked to the Heavenly Steles or Ancestral Gods. Additionally, the mention of a fallen king-level being suggests that the true powerhouses of the death world are formidable and may become significant players. Xiao Chen's calm resolve despite his skeleton form reinforces his indomitable will.
