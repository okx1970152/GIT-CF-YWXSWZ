## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a full-scale confrontation where Xiao Chen deploys his entire collection of saint artifacts against the combined might of Chengzi, Sanxiao Niangniang, and hundreds of elite cultivators. The battlefield is the Devil's Plain, but the clash of legendary weapons—Fanyin Seal, Golden Dragon Scissors, Mixed Yuan Golden Dipper, Zhuxian Swords, and more—turns the sky and earth into a canvas of destruction. Outnumbered and outmatched in cultivation, Xiao Chen's arsenal of saint artifacts, each capable of demigod-level power, allows him to hold his ground and even slay opponents. The siege is relentless, but he discovers a new artifact: the "Sky as Close as an Inch" ruler, which teleports him across the continent to the Southern Wastes, offering a temporary escape from the web of enemies.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's saint artifacts are displayed in full glory: the Four Immortal-Slaying Swords and their array diagram, the Twelve-Petal Lotus Seat, the Ancient Holy Scripture, Taiji Diagram, Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, Satan's Staff, Sun Saint Stone, and more. Each artifact responds to his will, creating a dazzling armor of divine power. Chengzi, a direct disciple of the Original (Yuan Shi), attacks first with the Fanyin Seal, which transforms into a mountain and crashes down. Xiao Chen counters with a coordinated defense using the Zhuxian swords, the Ancient Holy Scripture, and Taiji Diagram. Then the Three Cloud Sisters (Sanxiao Niangniang) join with the Golden Dragon Scissors—two golden flood dragons that attempt to cut Xiao Chen—and the Mixed Yuan Golden Dipper, a cauldron that tries to devour him. Xiao Chen repels both using the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, Satan's Staff, Sun Saint Stone, and more. The siege intensifies as Five Hundred Arhats, Fallen Angels, Sun Cult experts, and White Tiger descendants arrive. Xiao Chen breaks out by using the saint artifacts offensively, slaughtering many opponents, including a group of Fallen Angels who demand his artifacts. Using the newly recognized "Sky as Close as an Inch" ruler, he instantly teleports to the Southern Wastes, a continent away. The chapter concludes with omens across the world: newborn prodigies in Great Xia, Great Shang, Rome (an illegitimate son of a fallen royal), and a baby who points to heaven and earth proclaiming supremacy, hinting at the rise of future major players.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is steeped in Chinese mythological references. Chengzi is a well-known disciple of Yuan Shi Tian Zun from the classic novel "Investiture of the Gods" (Fengshen Yanyi). The Three Cloud Sisters (Yunxiao, Qiongxiao, Bixiao) are also from that novel, wielding the Golden Dragon Scissors (Jinjiao Jian) and the Mixed Yuan Golden Dipper (Hunyuan Jindou). The Fanyin Seal (Seal Overturning Heaven) is another famous artifact from the same tradition. The reappearance of these beings confirms that the "gods" of Chinese mythology are actual powerful cultivators in the World of Immortality. The Four Immortal-Slaying Swords of Tongtian Jiaozhu form the legendary Zhuxian Array. The inclusion of Yazi, Suanni, and Pixiu—the dragon sons—adds to the bestiary of mythical beasts. The newborn prodigies echo the trope of fated heroes: the child in Great Xia who looks to the sky, the one in Great Shang with blade-like eyes, the Roman prince of a fallen house, and the Buddhist child who declares himself supreme all draw from diverse cultural and religious archetypes, blending them into the novel's syncretic fantasy.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point for Xiao Chen: he has survived an assault from multiple top-tier disciples of legendary sects, but now he is a hunted man across the entire Eternal Continent. His escape to the Southern Wastes offers temporary respite, but the saint artifacts make him a target. The newborn prodigies hint at new major players entering the stage—watch for these children to grow into formidable figures. Xiao Chen now faces a dilemma: how to cultivate without drawing attention while bearing these visible artifacts. The appearance of the "Sky as Close as an Inch" artifact opens possibilities for rapid travel but may have limitations or costs. The Southern Wastes, a wild and dangerous region, may provide a hiding place and opportunities for growth, but old enemies lurk. The next arc likely involves Xiao Chen's seclusion and adaptation, while the world prepares for the rise of these newborn heroes. Will the old gods and saints truly allow Xiao Chen to keep their treasures? And what forces are behind the birth of these prodigies?
