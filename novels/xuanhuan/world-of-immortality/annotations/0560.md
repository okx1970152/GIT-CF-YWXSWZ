## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen's long-awaited arrival in the Primordial Celestial Realm is immediately met with the most elegant of traps—an illusion woven from his own memories and desires. A talking parrot, a familiar cave, and a phantom of Qingqing all serve to lower his guard before a blood lotus, a War Ancestor-level predator, strikes. This chapter is a masterclass in psychological tension, as Xiao Chen must untangle the real from the fabricated while facing a foe that has fed on Ancestral Gods. The serene lake and brilliant light conceal a deadly carnivore, and the ensuing battle showcases Xiao Chen's growth in both perception and raw power.

## Key Plot Points

A colorful parrot greets Xiao Chen, claiming he is the reincarnated Stone Emperor (shizhong di) from this very realm. It leads him to a water-curtain cave, where a carving of Qingqing confirms she visited a century ago. The illusion feels deeply personal, but Xiao Chen notices a critical flaw—the parrot questions him about the Mortal World without prompting, revealing the scripted nature of the deception. When the blood lotus strikes, Xiao Chen is already prepared. He wounds her with thirty-three War Swords, forcing her to reveal her true form: a colossal blood-red flower. She retaliates with a shattered Divine City, which Xiao Chen counters using the demonic city from the Death World. The battle ends when Xiao Chen traps the blood lotus and her body within the ancient scroll, which absorbs their life force, visibly strengthening the images of the Five Kings. As Xiao Chen escapes, he witnesses four vermilion birds and a phoenix circling the sun before an immense dark cloud—carrying the same aura as the vanished ancestral god corpses—rolls in from the horizon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界) is the apex of the cosmos, home to all ultimate powerhouses. The blood lotus is a War Ancestor (战祖), a being whose combat might rivals true Ancestral Gods but who has not fully attained divine status. Her illusion art is a sophisticated mental trap that reads the target's consciousness to construct personalized scenarios—a far cry from crude magical mirages. The Divine City (神城) is a weapon cultivated from a supreme being's own body; a broken one still possesses devastating force. The ancient scroll (古卷) that sealed the passage to the Nine Provinces is revealed to be a self-strengthening treasure—it consumes trapped enemies to restore its own power. The mention of the Lotus King (莲王) hints at an even more terrifying entity that may seek revenge for the blood lotus's destruction.

## Reading Guide

Qingqing's carving and the parrot's claim about the Stone Emperor plant seeds of doubt—is Xiao Chen truly a reincarnation of a legendary figure, or just the victim of another trick? The ancient scroll's growing power suggests it may become a key weapon in the coming war. The approaching dark cloud, carrying the scent of the six otherworldly gods, promises an immediate confrontation. This chapter marks a shift from survival in the lower realms to a direct confrontation with the Primordial Celestial Realm's apex predators. Pay close attention to the scroll and the demonic city—both may play pivotal roles in the battles ahead.
