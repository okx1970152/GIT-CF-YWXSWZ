## Chapter Overview
The Dead City becomes a stage for both mortal combat and cosmic revelation. Internal duels—Dugu Jianmo's challenge to Yizhen, Liu Mu's decisive victory over Wang Tong—unfold against the thunderous assault of the dragon clan outside. Yet the true spectacle lies in the sudden appearance of multiple divine steles, each bearing cataclysmic carvings that seem to breathe with their own life. At their center, a black well pulses with malevolent energy, connecting the city to unknown depths. This chapter masterfully blends personal vendetta, philosophical transcendence, and world-shattering lore, advancing both character arcs and the overarching mystery of Dragon Island.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Dugu Jianmo confronting Yizhen, declaring that even Buddha is empty before absolute power. Yizhen, having reached a new stage of understanding, refuses direct combat and retreats with cryptic words, drawing the swordsman away. Meanwhile, Wang Tong attempts a backstab on Liu Mu using the legendary Little Li Flying Dagger, but Liu Mu's spatial abilities freeze the flying dagger inches from his back. Liu Mu, enraged by the assassination attempt, engages Wang Tong in a duel that proves the supremacy of space spiritists over even the most vaunted techniques. He defeats Wang Tong using simulated flying daggers formed from spatial energy. Before dying, Wang Tong reveals he was already fatally wounded by strange claw marks on his chest—wounds that suggest a non-human attacker. He collapses, leaving a chilling warning about the city's horrors. Externally, the dragon clan's assault intensifies, breaking through the dark clouds and allowing sunlight to pour onto the Dead City. The sunlight mingles with the city's death aura, turning it blood-red and triggering the manifestation of several divine stele shadows. These steles, including one inscribed "Eternal Pacify the Yellow River," surround a black well at the square's center. Their carvings depict apocalyptic scenes: flooding, volcanic eruptions, dragons in flight above a sea of blood, and a peaceful netherworld beneath the city. The steles seem to pulse with energy, as though they are living windows into other realms. Xiao Chen is stunned to recognize the Yellow River stele from his childhood, realizing its connection to this distant island. The black well emits discordant sounds—sometimes heavenly music, sometimes hellish screams—causing all cultivators to recoil. The city shakes violently under the dragon assault, and the steles vibrate as if nearing collapse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Little Li Flying Dagger is one of the most famous techniques in Chinese martial arts literature, originating from Gu Long's novels. Its appearance in World of Immortality as a legitimate cultivation art that slayed a god upon entering the higher realm reinforces the novel's theme of mortal legends transcending worlds. Liu Mu's victory over it demonstrates the principle that no technique is absolute—space spiritists are called kings among their peers because their command of spatial forces can nullify even the most feared assassination arts. The divine steles recall the ancient Chinese tradition of stele inscriptions that commemorate events or seal powers; here they serve as both historical records and magical anchors. The black well (魔井) is a classic motif in Xianxia, often representing a suppressed evil, a passage to another realm, or an ancient prison. The simultaneous appearance of multiple stele shadows suggests that the Dead City is a nexus point connecting different ages and dimensions. The contrast between the peaceful agricultural scenes on one stele and the apocalyptic warfare on another underscores the entire novel's central tension: the cycle of civilization and destruction.

## Reading Guide
The revelation of the Yellow River stele's shadow in the Dead City deepens the mystery of the divine steles and suggests Xiao Chen's destiny is intertwined with a network of such monuments across worlds. The black well is clearly a focal point for upcoming conflict—whether it is a prison, a passage, or a weapon remains to be seen. The dragon clan's assault provides an urgent clock: the city may fall soon, forcing all survivors to act. Liu Mu's triumph and Wang Tong's cryptic death raise questions about what killed the flying dagger heir and what other threats lurk in the Dead City. Yizhen's retreat with Dugu Jianmo sets up a potential future duel or alliance. Readers should pay close attention to the imagery on each stele, as they likely foreshadow greater events in the novel's cosmology. The next chapter will likely explore the well's nature and the steles' true purpose, possibly triggering an escape or a massive confrontation.
