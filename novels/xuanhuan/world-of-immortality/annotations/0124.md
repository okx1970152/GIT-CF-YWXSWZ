## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks the culmination of Xiao Chen's harrowing voyage across the Forbidden Sea aboard the mysterious King Ship. The journey is fraught with ghostly terrors and deathly presences, yet it also yields profound revelations—most notably, the Sacred Tree's growth of a golden fourth leaf and the eerie encounter with the headless monk Shenxiu, a legendary figure from the Mortal World. The chapter balances the tension of supernatural threats with moments of quiet wonder, as the King Ship gradually nears the edge of the cursed sea. Xiao Chen's bond with Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon deepens amid the chaos, while Yan Qingcheng remains a captive observer, her fate hanging in the balance. The closing confrontation with the Divine Ship, which once carried his allies, introduces a new layer of menace and uncertainty.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Keke's playful curiosity overriding caution, as she leaps onto a ghostly lantern near the ship's cabin—a decision that triggers a violent spectral response. A terrifying deathly roar reverberates, and black mist erupts, revealing dozens of malevolent shadows. Keke retaliates with her restraining divine art, shattering the balance and plunging the ship into chaos. The atmosphere becomes suffocatingly oppressive, with ghostly wails and a mysterious breath that seems to awaken a sleeping evil. However, the terror subsides after half an hour, and the ship continues under the starlight. The next critical development is the maturation of the Sacred Tree: its fourth leaf, formed from golden light, emerges after ten days of absorbing power from the Forbidden Sea. This tangible growth brings hope and joy, especially to Keke. As the ship accelerates toward the sea's edge, the water shifts from gold to pale gold, signaling escape. Then, a miraculous event unfolds—a headless monk, radiant and ethereal, walks across the forbidden waves, chanting the famous verse of Shenxiu. This figure, identified as the legendary Shenxiu, a tragic Buddhist genius eclipsed by Huineng, appears as a headless corpse yet exudes transcendent purity. He follows the ship for dozens of li before disappearing into the mist, leaving Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng shaken. Finally, after two more days, the King Ship breaks free of the Forbidden Sea, but is immediately confronted by the Divine Ship, which had sailed earlier with Yizhen and Liu Mu. The Divine Ship charges aggressively, and the King Ship responds with a ghostly roar, setting the stage for a clash.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The headless monk is explicitly identified as Shenxiu (神秀), a historical figure from the Tang dynasty and a prominent Chan Buddhist patriarch. His famous gatha—'The body is a bodhi tree, the mind a clear mirror. Always wipe it diligently, lest dust settle'—is contrasted with Huineng's more famous verse, which elevated Huineng to the Sixth Patriarch. Shenxiu is often seen as a tragic figure, overshadowed by his rival despite immense talent. His appearance as a headless corpse wandering the Forbidden Sea adds a layer of dark irony: a monk who sought purity now appears as a mutilated ghost, yet still retains a sacred aura. This reflects the novel's recurring theme of the tragic fate of legendary beings in the World of Immortality. The King Ship's ghostly phenomena and the Sacred Tree's absorption of sea energy further illustrate the setting's fusion of death and life. The golden Forbidden Sea and its gradual dimming symbolize the transition from a sealed, cursed realm to a wider world, a classic xianxia journey from confinement to liberty.

## Reading Guide

This chapter resolves the Forbidden Sea arc and sets up the next phase of Dragon Island's aftermath. Readers should note the Sacred Tree's growth as a potential key to future power—its four leaves now include a golden one, hinting at elemental variety. The headless monk's brief but haunting appearance deepens the lore of tragic historical figures reincarnated or trapped in this world. Pay attention to the reaction of Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon: Keke's boldness and the dragon's combativeness are becoming central traits. The confrontation with the Divine Ship raises immediate questions: Who is aboard? Why are they attacking? The Divine Ship was supposed to carry friendly allies like Yizhen and Liu Mu, so hostility suggests either betrayal or a new threat. The King Ship's ghostly response indicates it may have a will of its own. The tension between these two mythic vessels sets the stage for a high-stakes encounter in the coming chapters.
