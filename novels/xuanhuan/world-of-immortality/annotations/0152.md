## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen returns to Heavenly Emperor City after a period of intense cultivation, only to find his residence ravaged and the Little Defiant Dragon barely alive. The chapter masterfully juxtaposes the ancient, almost sentient grandeur of the city with the raw, personal tragedy of a companion wounded. It is a chapter of recovery and resolve, where Xiao Chen’s refined perception meets the desperate need to save his faithful ally. The emotional core lies in the use of the Sacred Tree — a divine artifact of immense power — to perform a miraculous healing that binds the tree to the dragon’s very flesh. This is not a chapter of combat, but of quiet, sacred action that reshapes the bonds between man, beast, and the world’s primordial forces.

## Key Plot Points
- Xiao Chen, having reached the peak of Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven yet unable to break through to the Ninth, has undergone a profound body refinement. His flesh and bones have been repeatedly shattered and rebuilt with condensed spiritual energy, approaching a ‘treasure body’ state that is a vessel of power in itself.
- His divine sense has sharpened dramatically, allowing him to perceive Heavenly Emperor City as a living, breathing ancient entity — a feeling that underscores the city’s mythical nature and Xiao Chen’s growing sensitivity to the world’s deeper currents.
- Upon returning to his home, he finds the Little Defiant Dragon with horrific wounds: deep cuts, shattered internal organs, and blood staining the ground. The dragon is listless but alive, a testament to its indomitable will.
- Hai Yuntian arrives and explains the cause: false rumors of Xiao Chen’s death led the Zhuge family’s Zhuge Kun and the Ligen family’s Hoffman to attempt stealing the dragon. They encountered other parties, resulting in a chaotic battle that left the dragon critically injured. The city’s Undying Sect and city guards eventually intervened.
- Xiao Chen wastes no time in employing the Sacred Tree — which previously belonged to Keke — to save the dragon. Through a process of synchronized resonance, he implants the tree into the dragon’s body. The tree merges with the shattered organs, and a cocoon of seven-colored light forms around the dragon, initiating a transformative healing.
- The chapter ends with the dragon encased in a dragon cocoon, the Sacred Tree now bonded to it, severing its previous connection with Xiao Chen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of a ‘treasure body’ (宝体) is central to this chapter. Unlike conventional cultivation that emphasizes realm breakthroughs, some paths focus on refining the physical form into a transcendent vessel. Xiao Chen’s method of using spiritual energy as blades to carve through his own flesh and bones is a brutal but effective form of body tempering, reminiscent of Buddhist ‘diamond body’ or Daoist ‘golden body’ practices. This reflects the novel’s theme that the path to power is a form of suffering and rebirth.
The Sacred Tree’s ability to heal by merging with a creature’s body is a profound event in xianxia logic. Spiritual plants are often sentient and can form symbiotic bonds, but the tree’s full integration into the dragon’s internal organs suggests it is more than a mere plant — it may be a living fragment of a primordial entity. The resulting ‘dragon cocoon’ is a classic motif in Chinese myth, where beings undergo a rebirth or evolution by being enveloped in a protective shell, emerging with enhanced abilities.
Heavenly Emperor City itself is described as an ancient life form, hinting at the concept of geomancy (feng shui) where locations possess their own spiritual consciousness or ‘dragon veins’. Such cities are often sites of prophecy and hidden power.

## Reading Guide
- This chapter sets up a major metamorphosis for the Little Defiant Dragon. The cocooning suggests its power and form may drastically change when it emerges. Readers should pay attention to what kind of being will break out from the multicolored shell.
- The false death rumor and the subsequent attack reveal the cutthroat politics of Heavenly Emperor City’s noble families. Zhuge Kun and Hoffman have made themselves mortal enemies of Xiao Chen. Expect a swift and brutal retaliation in the coming chapters.
- The Sacred Tree’s transfer to the dragon may leave Keke without her treasured possession. How she reacts to this loss — and whether she can reclaim it or if it permanently changes her — is a thread to watch.
- Xiao Chen’s inability to break through to the Ninth Heaven despite extreme body refinement suggests his unique cultivation path demands something more: perhaps the complete understanding of the Heavenly Stele diagrams, or a revelation about the connection between his acupoint divinization and the dragon transformation.
