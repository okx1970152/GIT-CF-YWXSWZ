## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, Xiao Chen makes a stunning debut in the Celestial Realm by personally slaying the Otherworld Stone King Lang Ya. Displaying a flesh-and-blood body that rivals the power of a true Stone King, Xiao Chen not only defeats a legendary ancient powerhouse but also forces three allied Stone Kings to back down through cold calculation and threat. The chapter culminates in a poignant reunion with long-lost allies from the Nine Provinces, including Ke Ke's father, Zhuge Pangzi, and other surviving heroes of the ancient era. The tone is one of hard-won triumph and the solemn weight of returning to a world that has forgotten him.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, controlling the 'White Chubby Kid' as a puppet, engages the Stone King Lang Ya in a brutal battle. Despite Lang Ya's fearsome techniques, Xiao Chen's overwhelming martial power and the Five Emperors Tower allow him to tear Lang Ya apart and begin refining his stone body. However, before the refinement is complete, three more Stone Kings—Zixiao Wang, Stone Corpse, and Xuankong Old Ancestor—arrive to rescue their ally and surround Xiao Chen. Using the Five Emperors Tower, a Heavenly Stele projection, and the cursed stone jar as leverage, Xiao Chen threatens to annihilate their lineages and disciples if they harm his friends. This gambit buys enough time for Ke Ke's father and a host of old comrades (Chu Xingkuang, Jue Dao, Yao Yao, Xuewu, Zhao Zhongyang, etc.) to appear, shifting the balance of power. The three Stone Kings are forced to retreat, and Xiao Chen reunites with the surviving heroes of the Nine Provinces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter highlights the rare phenomenon of a flesh-and-blood cultivator reaching the Stone King level—normally, Stone Kings are beings of petrified stone bodies. Xiao Chen's achievement of the 'Perfected Upper Ancestral God' path while retaining living flesh marks him as an unprecedented anomaly. The Five Emperors Tower is a legendary artifact comparable to the Three Sovereigns Mirror, capable of suppressing even Stone Kings. The cursed stone jar (Source of All Evil) is another peak treasure. Lang Ya, one of the four founding Otherworld Stone Kings, has a history stretching back to the wars of the Nine Provinces; his death here is a significant blow to the Otherworld faction. The reunion with old allies also reflects the long-lost bonds of the Nine Provinces civilization.

## Reading Guide
With Lang Ya slain and the other three Stone Kings temporarily cowed, Xiao Chen's position in the Celestial Realm is now unmistakably that of a major power. However, the threat from the Otherworld remains, and the ancient enemy behind the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps still seeks the Heavenly Steles. The reappearance of old friends suggests a rebuilding of the Nine Provinces' force, and Xiao Chen's next moves will likely involve consolidating his allies and pursuing the remaining Heavenly Marks. The chapter also sets up a potential alliance with the resurrected heroes and the cryptic White Chubby Kid, whose true nature remains ambiguous. Readers should watch for how the three surviving Stone Kings retaliate and what role the ancient lamp and the great ancient enemy will play.
