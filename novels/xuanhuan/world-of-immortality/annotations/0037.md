## Chapter Overview
This chapter pivots from careful observation to bold action as Xiao Chen orchestrates a perilous theft of dragon eggs from a Serpent-Elephant Dragon, one of the island's apex predators. The tone shifts from tense planning to explosive chaos when the dragon's rage spills beyond its valley, dragging Yan Qingcheng, Rand, and other cultivators into a stampede of primal fury. The chapter underscores the high-risk, high-reward nature of Dragon Island, where even a single misstep can trigger a cascade of destruction. Xiao Chen's strategic audacity is on full display, as he leverages the skeletons' loyalty and his own cold calculation to secure treasures that could reshape his cultivation path.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen and the three skeletons observe a Serpent-Elephant Dragon from a cliffside, noting its immense size and the five jade-green dragon eggs in its nest. After the dragon leaves to hunt, Xiao Chen convinces the reluctant skeletons to steal the eggs. They swiftly descend, each grabbing a millstone-sized egg, and retreat just as the dragon returns. The dragon discovers the theft and erupts into a frenzy, rampaging through the forest, destroying everything in its path. This chaos catches Yan Qingcheng, Rand, and several other cultivators who were scouting the area; they are forced to flee alongside panicked beasts. The theft is a calculated risk that pays off, but it sets in motion a series of consequences: the dragon's fury will attract attention, and the stolen eggs may contain latent dragon kings that could alter the island's balance of power. Xiao Chen's decision to take three eggs rather than all five shows a pragmatic restraint, but the dragon's wrath will not discriminate.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Serpent-Elephant Dragon (蛇象龙) is a hybrid beast combining features of an elephant and a serpent, a common motif in Chinese mythology representing the fusion of opposing forces. In the cultivation world, dragon eggs are considered supreme treasures, containing primordial life essence that can accelerate a practitioner's growth or even awaken latent bloodlines. The legend of the Ancestral Dragon and Companion Dragons is central: any dragon egg may hatch a future king, making their theft a gamble with cosmic stakes. The island's ancient seal suppresses divine abilities, forcing even demigod-level dragons into physical combat, which explains why the Serpent-Elephant Dragon relies on brute force rather than mystical arts. This seal is a key world-building element, emphasizing that Dragon Island is a prison as much as a treasure ground.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major resource acquisition for Xiao Chen, but the stolen eggs will undoubtedly attract predators and rivals. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen utilizes the eggs—whether for immediate cultivation breakthroughs or as bargaining chips—and how the dragon's rampage reshapes the island's political map. Yan Qingcheng and Rand are now aware of Xiao Chen's presence and his reckless daring; their next move may involve retaliation or opportunism. The hint that the eggs could contain future dragon kings adds a layer of long-term consequence. The chapter also reinforces the theme of survival through cunning over brute strength, a hallmark of Xiao Chen's character. Future chapters may explore the fallout as other factions learn of the theft and seek to claim the eggs for themselves.
