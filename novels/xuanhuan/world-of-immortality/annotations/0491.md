## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with a cataclysmic event: the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou descends upon the Soul World, his mere presence threatening to shatter the realm. A mysterious figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge intervenes, wielding a dragon-shaped fist and the legendary Six Paths of Reincarnation to drive him back. The battle is a clash of titans, shaking heaven and earth. After this awe-inspiring confrontation, the narrative shifts to a more intimate, hopeful tone: the long-lost Ancestral Dragon Village, Xiao Chen's home, begins to reappear after nearly two centuries. The chapter balances epic cosmic warfare with the poignant promise of reunion.

## Key Plot Points

Li Huiyou, an Otherworld Ancestral God in his true form, emerges through a dark passage, his divine pressure cracking the Soul World. He is met by a veiled half-ancestor standing atop the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, who engages him in devastating combat. The mysterious figure uses a dragon-shaped fist reminiscent of the Martial Ancestor and, crucially, the Six Paths of Reincarnation—a technique that seals all senses and leaves the enemy trapped in darkness. Li Huiyou is forced back through the passage, and even a second Otherworld Ancestral God is repelled before they can fully arrive. The bridge then shrinks and vanishes with the mysterious defender. Ten days later, the party returns to the Nine Provinces, where they learn that Ancestral Dragon Village—hidden for nearly two centuries—is reappearing near the Dead City. After days of waiting, the village materializes, and two mischievous little girls, Linglong and Tutu (daughters of the Yellow Emperor), emerge, followed by Bo Shi (Yizhen's brother) clutching a massive Buddha relic. The Dead City meanwhile emits a Slaughter Light, a dangerous omen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the culmination of xianxia power: the true form of an Otherworld Ancestral God, a being beyond half-ancestors, whose presence alone can shatter worlds. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge is a legendary artifact that can traverse dimensions and is used here as a weapon and shield. The Six Paths of Reincarnation is a primordial technique that manipulates the cycle of birth and death, sealing all perception—a direct counter to the Ancestral God's overwhelming power. Ancestral Dragon Village, or the Primordial Ancient Village, is the hidden birthplace of great figures and a seal for ancient secrets. Its reappearance signals a major shift in the world's balance.

## Reading Guide

The identity of the mysterious half-ancestor on the bridge remains unknown—could it be the Yellow Emperor, the old Ancestral Dragon, or someone else? The reappearance of Ancestral Dragon Village promises answers about Xiao Chen's family and the deeper mysteries of the Nine Provinces. The little girls Linglong and Tutu and Bo Shi will likely provide crucial context. Meanwhile, the Dead City's Slaughter Light suggests lingering danger. With Li Huiyou and another Ancestral God repelled but not destroyed, a future confrontation is inevitable. Readers should keep an eye on the evolving power dynamics among the half-ancestors and the fate of the hidden village.
