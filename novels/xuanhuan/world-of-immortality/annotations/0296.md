## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks the long-awaited departure from Dragon Island aboard the Ancestral Dragon Ship, a vessel of mythic proportions that crosses the sealed Forbidden Sea. The journey is not without strangeness: a doppelganger of Xiao Chen appears on the shore, and the fearsome King Ship confronts the divine vessel. After reaching the Eternal Continent, Xiao Chen visits Heavenly Emperor City, where he encounters Dugu Jianmo—once a cold, solitary swordsman, now a devoted husband and father. This reunion forces Xiao Chen to confront his own emotional emptiness and the fragmented memories of his lost love, Ruoshui. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen setting out to recruit powerful allies from the Pure Land and the Demon Sect Founder to save Qingqing, setting the stage for a new quest.

## Key Plot Points
The Ancestral Dragon Ship is summoned by the eleven Companion Dragon Kings. Xiao Chen and Keke board the ship, and as it sails away, Xiao Chen spots a figure on the shore that is his exact double—a mystery that will later demand resolution. The ship crosses the Forbidden Sea, encountering the sinister King Ship in a tense standoff that lasts three hours. Upon arriving at the Eternal Continent, Xiao Chen flies directly to Heavenly Emperor City, where he unexpectedly meets Dugu Jianmo, who is now married to A Bing and has a young daughter. The once-icy swordsman has softened, finding strength in family. Xiao Chen, in contrast, feels a chilling internal void; he recalls the memory of Ruoshui, who sacrificed her life essence to save him, and realizes he had deliberately severed those memories. This psychological crisis deepens when he learns that part of his soul is sealed in a stone statue in the Ancient God Wasteland. Determined to rescue Qingqing, Xiao Chen first visits the Pure Land to enlist the ancient elders, then flies to the divine island of the Demon Sect Founder, who agrees to help after Keke’s plea.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ancestral Dragon Ship and the King Ship are legendary vessels with distinct origins: the former is built from Ancestral Dragon bones and World Tree wood, summoned by the roar of nine or more Companion Dragon Kings, while the latter is a ghostly craft tied to deathly forces. Their confrontation on the Forbidden Sea highlights the duality of life and death in this world. The Pure Land is a secluded sanctuary of ancient cultivators, including the Turtle Patriarch and Wang Xianpo, representing the reclusive masters of the barbarian and forest tribes. The Demon Sect Founder (Chiyou) is a reborn ancient being with an eternal immortal body, now living on a floating divine island. His willingness to aid Xiao Chen stems from gratitude for past favors, showing the importance of karmic debts in xianxia culture. The concept of a split soul or sealed soul fragment is a common trope, explaining Xiao Chen’s emotional detachment and setting up a future quest.

## Reading Guide
This chapter serves as a transition from the Dragon Island arc to the Central Plains, closing one major storyline and opening another. Xiao Chen's psychological deepening—his confrontation with his own coldness and the mystery of his doppelganger and sealed soul—adds layers to his character beyond mere power progression. The reunion with Dugu Jianmo offers a poignant contrast: even the most rigid character can change through love and responsibility, while Xiao Chen finds himself growing more detached. Readers should pay close attention to the foreshadowing of the doppelganger and the sealed soul, as these will likely become key plot points in the upcoming rescue of Qingqing and the confrontation with greater threats. The recruitment of the Pure Land elders and the Demon Sect Founder signals that the mortal world incursion will involve top-tier powerhouses, raising the stakes substantially.
