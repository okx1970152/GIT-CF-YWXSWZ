## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, high-stakes confrontation between Xiao Chen and the surviving remnant of the Otherworld Ancestral God, Babra. After the destruction of his monstrous flesh-body, Babra’s residual consciousness escapes into a skeletal form, forcing Xiao Chen to renew his assault. The battle shifts from the sealed space of the Heavenly Steles to the open wasteland above, where both combatants unleash their full power. The stakes are cosmic: Babra once wounded Ancestral God Youchao and plotted against the Martial Ancestor, and Xiao Chen is determined to finally end this ancient evil. The chapter maintains a tone of grim, relentless violence, tempered by Xiao Chen’s analytical calm and the looming dread of the corpse river’s secrets.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen’s six Heavenly Steles crush and refine Babra’s flesh-body, reducing it to a cloud of blood mist. Despite long purification, a fist-sized mass of pure Ancestral God blood escapes into a nearby white-bone skeleton, granting Babra a new vessel. This skeleton wields eight bone spears and a scorpion-like tail, giving it formidable combat capability. Babra reveals he fears awakening a slumbering entity at the head of the corpse river, and the battle moves to the surface. There, Babra summons the Grand Annihilation War Spear, a half-ancestor-level weapon said to have pierced multiple Ancestral Gods in the past. Xiao Chen counters with the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, channeling a strand of Ancestral God essence to offset the spear’s power. The clash destroys dozens of mountains, sending shockwaves across the land. Xiao Chen suffers wounds despite his Ancestral God War Armor, but Babra is also shaken, no longer the invincible being he once was. The chapter ends with both combatants preparing for the next exchange, the outcome uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the culmination of Xiao Chen’s path as a wielder of the Heavenly Steles and War Swords. The six steles are artifacts of primordial power, capable of sealing and refining even remnants of an Ancestral God. The concept of “refining” (炼化) an enemy’s essence is a classic xianxia trope, where a cultivator purifies and absorbs an opponent’s energy to become stronger. Babra’s Grand Annihilation War Spear is a half-ancestor weapon, a class of artifact forged by beings just below true Ancestral Gods; such weapons can injure even god-like entities. The corpse river (尸河) is a common element in dark xianxia settings, a cursed waterway flowing through the underworld, often connected to sleeping horrors or sealed evils. The danger of awakening such a being adds a layer of suspense to the battle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point in Xiao Chen’s quest to eliminate the remnants of the Otherworld Ancestral Gods. While Babra’s defeat seems likely, the introduction of the corpse river’s hidden entity suggests larger threats await. Readers should note that Babra is no longer at full power; his fall from grace makes him a tragic but still dangerous antagonist. Xiao Chen’s reliance on the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree and the Ancestral God essence indicates he is tapping into deeper reserves of power. The next chapter may resolve this duel, but also raises questions about the identity of the sleeper in the corpse river and whether Xiao Chen can avoid attracting its attention. The battle also reinforces the theme of ancestral debt—Xiao Chen fights to avenge the Ancestral Gods of the Nine Provinces.
