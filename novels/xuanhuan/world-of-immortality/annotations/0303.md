## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a spectacular clash of half-ancestor powers as the battle for supremacy on the Eternal Continent escalates. Tongtian Jiaozhu, master of the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation, unleashes his full might against the formidable Qinglian Tiannu, only to be repelled. The relentless Qinglian then defeats Zhunti before facing Laozi in a legendary confrontation. The chapter balances breathtaking combat with simmering political and personal tensions among the supreme beings, showcasing Qinglian's terrifying combat prowess and setting the stage for deeper cosmic struggles.

## Key Plot Points

Tongtian activates the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation with his four legendary swords and the formation diagram, creating an incomplete but devastating Primordial Killing Field. Qinglian Tiannu, wielding four War Swords and the Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform, withstands the assault and identifies the formation's weakness—the four dragon vein frameworks. She shatters the field with her own War Swords, forcing Tongtian to retreat two steps in defeat. Tongtian leaves in disgrace. Next, Zhunti, armed with the seven-colored evil sword and the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, challenges Qinglian to a four-strike exchange. He is pushed back three steps, a clear loss. Finally, Laozi materializes and engages Qinglian. Using only the Taiji Diagram, he matches her full arsenal—War Swords, Lotus Platform, and Jade Lotus Leaves—before employing the legendary one-qi-transforms-three-purities technique, revealing three ancient Daoist incarnations. The battle remains unresolved at chapter's end. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen observes grimly, and Keke whispers something about the War Swords to him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation (诛仙剑阵) originates from the classic novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods), where Tongtian Jiaozhu deploys it to challenge multiple saints. The four swords—Slay Immortal, Kill Immortal, Extinguish Immortal, Trap Immortal—represent one of the most fearsome killing arrays in mythological lore. The Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform (十二品莲台) is a supreme Buddhist defensive treasure, often associated with the Buddha. Laozi's one-qi-transforms-three-purities (一气化三清) is a Daoist concept where Laozi manifests three separate entities representing different primal energies; it is a pinnacle technique in Chinese myth. The 'stepping back' metric (退后两步, 三步) is a face-driven measure of defeat among top cultivators, where even a slight regression signals inferiority in a direct confrontation.
