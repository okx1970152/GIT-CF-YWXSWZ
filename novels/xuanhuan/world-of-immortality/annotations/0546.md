## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a thunderclap of revelation and destruction. The old stone turtle, long dismissed as a senile, harmless relic crouching on the seventh Heavenly Stele, sheds his mask of decrepitude and erupts as a towering humanoid stone warrior. In three devastating punches, he shatters the Otherworld Stone Man—a peerless being on the verge of taking the final step to transcendence. The blow is so overwhelming and sudden that even the three Supreme Ancestral Gods in the Eternal Unknown cannot intervene in time. This is a moment of tragic triumph: a hidden ace finally played, turning the tide of a desperate war, but at the cost of the turtle's own near-annihilation. The chapter balances awe-inspiring martial might with the melancholy of a being that has waited eons for this single strike.

## Key Plot Points

The old stone turtle, previously a comical and decrepit figure, transforms into a humanoid stone body clad in his own shell as armor. With speed and power that reverse time and collapse space, he lands the first punch on the Otherworld Stone Man's back, pulverizing its chest. A second blow shatters the Stone Man's head, and a third grinds its entire form to powder. The three punches are delivered in an instant, completely controlling the flow of time and space to prevent any counterattack. The turtle then fades, his essence imprinting the Heavenly Stele as a carved image. Xiao Chen immediately follows up with the Eight Origin Sounds and the broken formation diagram, further damaging the scattered remnants. The Martial Ancestor appears, wielding the stone knife—a weapon from the Stone Man path—and cleaves through the blood mist and fragments. The Otherworld Stone Man is utterly destroyed, its essence scattered. The Otherworld Ancestral Gods, frantic and furious, manage only to gather a heap of stone powder that will require millennia to restore. The turtle's sacrifice and the coordinated assault prove that even the most powerful enemy can be defeated with cunning and timing.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The old stone turtle's transformation is a classic xianxia trope: the disguised master who feigns weakness to deliver a decisive blow. The three-punch kill sequence echoes the cultural ideal that the highest martial artists need only a few moves to settle a life-or-death contest—three strikes often symbolize finality and inevitability. The turtle's form fading into the Heavenly Stele as a carved image suggests a union between the warrior and the Dao of the stele itself, a concept of permanent memorial within the fabric of the world. The stone knife wielded by the Martial Ancestor is a relic of the Stone Man path, representing a purer, more primitive power that bypasses divine laws. The Eight Origin Sounds, derived from the Yellow Clay Altar, are primordial tones that resonate with the foundations of existence, used here to shatter an already broken enemy. The chapter also underscores the theme of sacrifice: even the oldest and seemingly weakest can become the pivot on which a cosmic war turns.

## Reading Guide

The old turtle's devastating intervention has crippled the Otherworld's greatest weapon, but the cost is immense—the turtle is now a mere imprint on the stele. The Otherworld's response, already hinted at in their plans to summon a supreme leader, will escalate the war to new heights. Readers should watch for the awakening of additional powers from the Eternal Unknown and the potential emergence of other hidden masters from the Nine Provinces' side. Xiao Chen's role, though supportive here, grows as he continues to gather Heavenly Marks and deepen his control over the steles. The stone knife's appearance and the Martial Ancestor's bold strike from the Eternal Unknown signal that the final confrontation is building. The turtle's final words echo with tragic pride: he may be 'just a shadow,' but for three moves, he was the supreme being under heaven. His legacy will ripple through the coming battles.
