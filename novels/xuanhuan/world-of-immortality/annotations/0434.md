## Chapter Overview

Chapter 434 throws Xiao Chen directly into a maelstrom of half-ancestor politics and brutal combat. Having returned to the Eternal Continent with the stone giant and multiple War Swords, he becomes an immediate target for a coalition of half-ancestors, including the Asura Half-Ancestor, the Yaksha Half-Ancestor, and the Skeleton King. The chapter is a relentless siege, testing Xiao Chen's newfound power from the Death World against beings who have lived for ages. The conflict is not merely personal; it is a clash of civilizations, with the half-ancestors seeking to seize the legendary swords that represent ultimate martial power. Amid the chaos, old allies and unexpected figures emerge, reminding readers of the vast, interconnected world of the Immortal Realm.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen unleashing the Four Killing Hands against the Asura Half-Ancestor, shattering his arrogance with a barrage of heavenly steles. The Asura Half-Ancestor counters with the Blood Asura Realm, a defensive space that incorporates the blood of past half-ancestors, and summons a Blood Spirit of comparable power. Xiao Chen's stone giant is corroded, but the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree empowers him, and the Ancestral Dragon Ship is drawn by its aura. The battle escalates as the Skeleton King and Yaksha Half-Ancestor join the fray, stealing War Swords from Xiao Chen. The Angel Sacred God also appears, claiming a sword. The half-ancestors openly coordinate to eliminate Xiao Chen and his allies. Meanwhile, Cold Hanbing, the once-famed First Emperor-level expert, leads a coalition of Asura and Yaksha emperors to massacre Xiao Chen's friends. However, the Mad Monk Ji Gong intervenes with a comedic but devastating transformation spell, turning the emperors into pigs, turning the tide. The chapter ends with a massive discharge of power from the stone giant as Xiao Chen recovers and prepares to counterattack.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter illustrates the ruthless politics of the half-ancestor level. The Blood Asura Realm is a unique defensive artifact that stores the blood of fallen Asura Half-Ancestors, allowing the wielder to temporarily wield the power of two half-ancestors. The War Swords, which number up to forty-nine according to the Great Derivation, are objects of ultimate desire, capable of reshaping the balance of power. The appearance of the Ancestral Dragon Ship, drawn by Keke's Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, reinforces the tree's connection to dragon-kind. The Mad Monk Ji Gong's spell, turning emperors into pigs, is a rare example of comic relief in the grim xianxia world, but also demonstrates that even supreme beings can be humbled by unconventional divine arts. The chapter also introduces the 'First Emperor' title for Cold Hanbing, a character who once dominated his generation but is now reduced to a pawn of half-ancestors, a reminder of the vast gaps between cultivation realms.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the shifting alliances among the half-ancestors. The Skeleton King appears to be playing a deeper game, manipulating events while appearing neutral. Xiao Chen's reliance on the stone giant and the War Swords is a double-edged sword, attracting both power and enemies. The reappearance of the three skeleton kings (now War Emperors) off-screen hints at larger forces converging. Ji Gong's intervention may prove pivotal, as his foolish appearance belies a deep understanding of the world's laws. The fate of Xiao Chen's friends remains precarious, and the threat of the poison plot against Keke lingers. Look forward to Xiao Chen's counter-offensive and the possible awakening of the female sovereign from the Death World.
