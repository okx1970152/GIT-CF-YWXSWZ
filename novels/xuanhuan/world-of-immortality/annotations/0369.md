## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen, now a Yellow Diamond skeleton of immense power in the death world, dominates the western domains with effortless kills, earning the silent respect of even the proud western lords. However, the expected battle with Giant King never materializes. Instead, the three regional sovereigns—Giant King, Beast King, and Hundred Battle King—greet him with surprising restraint, revealing that a mysterious female entity from the continent's interior has commanded peace among the outer kingdoms. This being, who achieved true resurrection from death, now influences the death world's politics from the depths. Xiao Chen, his curiosity piqued but his immediate goals unmet, leaves the death world and returns to the mortal world via the bone well. He arrives on the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival, only to witness a heartbreaking scene: Keke, the little white beast, has come to the Dead City to offer sacrifices, mourning him with all his favorite foods. This chapter blends political revelation in the death world with a poignant emotional reunion in the Nine Provinces.

## Key Plot Points

After reaching Yellow Diamond second-tier, Xiao Chen travels through the western death territories, casually annihilating a Bone Griffin city lord and an Angelic being who dared to challenge him, establishing his dominance. He then returns east to Pojun City, where he reunites with his followers Sha Polang and the Yellow Diamond Skeleton. He visits Giant City expecting a decisive battle with Giant King. Instead, Giant King, accompanied by Beast King and Hundred Battle King, welcomes him peacefully. They explain that they were ordered by a mysterious female powerhouse from the continent's interior—the same voice behind the soul-summoning song—to maintain order. She has achieved true resurrection, possessing a flesh-and-blood body. Shocked, Xiao Chen participates in secret talks but remains wary, feeling the three kings' compliance is fragile. He deliberately provokes Giant King afterward by killing three of his city lords, but Giant King does not retaliate, bound by the inner command. Dissatisfied, Xiao Chen leaves the death world management to his subordinates and returns to the Nine Provinces. Emerging from the bone well at the Dead City on Mid-Autumn Festival night, he is stunned to find Keke, the little white beast, laying out food and lighting candles in a mournful ceremony for him. The emotional impact is profound; Xiao Chen, invisible as a skeletal being, watches from the shadows, deeply moved.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter reinforces the hierarchy of the death world: fire seeds, diamond bone ranks (Red, Purple, Yellow, etc.), and the emergence of a supreme being who has achieved true resurrection—a state where a death world creature reverses its condition and regains flesh and blood. This is considered the ultimate achievement of the death world. The Mid-Autumn Festival (Zhōngqiū Jié) is a major traditional Chinese holiday celebrating family reunion, when people gather to appreciate the full moon and share offerings. Keke's act of bringing Xiao Chen's favorite foods and lighting candles to his supposed resting place is deeply symbolic, echoing the traditional custom of ancestor worship during such festivals. The festival's association with longing and separation sharpens the poignancy of Keke's grief and Xiao Chen's helplessness as a lone skeleton. The bone well serves as a critical portal between the death world and the mortal world, allowing Xiao Chen to travel between realms as he strengthens his soul.

## Reading Guide

The mystery of the inner continent's female sovereign deepens. Who is she, and what is her relationship to the death world's ultimate secrets? Xiao Chen's uneasy truce with the three kings suggests future conflict as his power grows. His failed attempt to provoke Giant King into breaking the ceasefire hints that he may eventually challenge the inner continent's authority. Meanwhile, the emotional reunion with Keke is a turning point—will Xiao Chen reveal his return to her? How will Keke react when she realizes Xiao Chen still exists? The Mid-Autumn Festival setting layered over this grave scene creates a bittersweet contrast that sets the stage for their potential reconnection in the next chapter.
