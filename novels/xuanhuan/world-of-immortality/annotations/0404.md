## Chapter Overview

This chapter strikes a masterful balance between warm reunion and cold vengeance, as Xiao Chen, now a Yellow Diamond skeleton in the Death World, returns to the mortal realm to settle a twenty-year-old grudge with Long Teng of the Southern Dragon Clan. The opening sets a festive tone—old friends gather at the Drunken Beauty Palace, music and wine flow, and even Keke gets tipsy on fruit wine. But this celebratory surface masks the underlying purpose: Xiao Chen has come to test the waters, to see if the ancient dragon king of the Southern Wastes still lurks in the depths, and to force Long Teng into a final confrontation. The chapter shifts seamlessly from camaraderie to brutal combat, showcasing Xiao Chen's growth since the pseudo-god calamity and reaffirming his place among the top powers of the world. The appearance of the Flower Emperor—revealed as the enchantress Yaoyao—adds another layer of intrigue, while the arrival of the Heaven-Overturning Seal at the climax hints at the enduring hatred of the Chan Sect.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, accompanied by Keke, Niu Ren, Liu Mu, and Fatty Zhuge, is enjoying a reunion at the Drunken Beauty Palace in Heavenly Emperor City when conflict erupts. A dragon youth of the Southern Dragon Clan harasses the establishment, leading Xiao Chen to kill him with a single, clean strike. This prompts a series of escalating attacks from Long Teng's subordinates, each of whom falls easily to Xiao Chen's overwhelming power. The hybrid dragon general 'Purple Lightning' is disintegrated by Liu Mu's Space Divine Law, demonstrating the might of a spatial spiritist. Long Teng himself finally appears, but after a brief, fierce exchange, Xiao Chen breaks off one of his dragon horns, humiliating him. The battle is cut short by the arrival of a colossal seal—the Heaven-Overturning Seal of the Chan Sect—suggesting that Xiao Chen's past enemies from the pseudo-god era have not forgotten him. The chapter ends with this new threat looming, but Xiao Chen's resolve remains unshaken. 

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Heaven-Overturning Seal' (Fanyin Seal) is a legendary artifact from Chinese mythology, associated with the sage Guang Chengzi of the Chan Sect. Its appearance here signals that the Chan Sect—a Daoist faction from the classic novel 'Investiture of the Gods'—seeks revenge for the pseudo-god calamity in which Xiao Chen slew many of their half-ancestor figures. The term 'Dragon-Gate Leap' (鱼跃) is the cultivation realm above Deathless Longevity, representing a metamorphosis akin to a carp leaping over the dragon gate in legend. The 'Space Divine Law' wielded by Liu Mu is considered a king among divine abilities, granting him supremacy over same-realm opponents. The Drunken Beauty Palace (闭月羞花殿) is a neutral venue for cultivators, but its mistress, the Flower Emperor, is a figure of immense beauty and cunning, now revealed as the demoness Yaoyao from the Heavenly Demon Palace. 

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation between Xiao Chen and the combined forces of the Southern Dragon Clan and his old enemies. Long Teng's defeat opens the door for further escalation, while the Heaven-Overturning Seal's arrival foreshadows a clash with the Chan Sect. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's friends—particularly Liu Mu and Niu Ren—will support him in the coming storm. The mention of Dugu Jianmo and Yi Zhen (the monk) hints at past allies who may reappear. Keke's drunken antics provide a touch of levity, but her presence also raises questions: can Xiao Chen protect her from the gathering storm? The chapter also deepens the lore of the Hundred Races, with the Dragon Clan's hybrid bloodlines and the territorial politics of the Southern Wastes. Expect the next chapter to deliver the long-awaited rematch between Xiao Chen and the Southern Wastes old dragon.
