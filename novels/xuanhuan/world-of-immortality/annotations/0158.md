## Chapter Overview

This chapter pivots from a farcical hot-spring confrontation into a sweeping introduction of three terrifying new cultivators emerging across the Southern Wastes, while Xiao Chen unwittingly gains a formidable new identity. The tone shifts from comedic tension—Xiao Chen chasing two noblewomen who stole his clothes—to the epic emergence of Dugu Jianmo, Yuwen Feng, and Wu Xingfeng, each demonstrating power that reshapes the landscape. Xiao Chen's casual treatment of Kanasi and Hai Yunxue masks his strategic cunning, and the rumors that he hails from the deepest, most dangerous part of the Southern Wastes grant him an unexpected aura of mystery and intimidation. The chapter ends with a herald announcing the three great academies' recruitment, setting up a major conflict arena.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen pursues Kanasi and Hai Yunxue after they take his clothes during a hot-spring bath, using his Undying Wings to capture them with dragon-shaped light claws. He calmly retrieves his garments and forces Kanasi—who faints from embarrassment—to sign multiple IOUs under the stunned gaze of Hai Yuntian, Zhuge Ming, and Hoffman. Later, Hai Yuntian reveals that rumors have spread that Xiao Chen originates from the unfathomable depths of the Southern Wastes, a region of primordial danger. This rumor, though false, grants Xiao Chen a fearsome reputation that can be leveraged. Meanwhile, far from Heavenly Emperor City, three young powerhouses demonstrate their might: Dugu Jianmo splits the sea with a single sword strike, accompanied by the savage Red Dragon King; Yuwen Feng tears through a herd of ancient beasts with murderous, demonic strength, roaring that he must return to the city; and a third figure named Wu Xingfeng awakens from a meditative state akin to petrification, his hair turning blue, and wonders who to challenge next. A distant voice announces the opening of the three great academies, hinting at a convergence of elite talent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The hot-spring scene, while comedic, adheres to the xianxia trope of 'misunderstanding leading to conflict'—a staple in cultivation stories where accidental nudity or theft of clothes creates interpersonal tension. However, the narrative treats it with dignity: the women's embarrassment is real, but the focus remains on Xiao Chen's calculated actions. The term 'Mortar-Shedding Eighth Heaven' appears, confirming Xiao Chen's cultivation level and the progression of the realm system. The three new cultivators each represent different cultivation archetypes: the Sword Cultivator (Dugu Jianmo, embodying the lone blade path), the Body Cultivator (Yuwen Feng, with terrifying physical and demonic power), and the Meditation Cultivator (Wu Xingfeng, who undergoes a death-like stillness before awakening). The Southern Wastes itself is established as a fabled, dangerous region—its 'deepest part' implies ancient mysteries and primordial beasts, a classic setting for hidden legacies. The little white turtle (Little Thief) continues to be a mystery, its black iron chain inscribed with dragon characters hinting at a forgotten dragon lineage.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up multiple threads for the arc ahead: Xiao Chen's fabricated identity as a Southern Wastes powerhouse will likely attract both allies and enemies who wish to test or recruit him. The three emerging cultivators—Dugu Jianmo, Yuwen Feng, and Wu Xingfeng—are clearly intended as major rivals or future opponents, each with unique combat styles. The mention of the three academies (Big Dipper, Battle God, White Deer) signals that Heavenly Emperor City will become a stage for competitive trials. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen navigates the politics of his new reputation, and whether the Little Defiant Dragon and Keke react to these new powerhouses. The little turtle's secret may also become relevant soon. This chapter marks a transition from survival on Dragon Island to maneuvering in the cultivation world's formal institutions.
