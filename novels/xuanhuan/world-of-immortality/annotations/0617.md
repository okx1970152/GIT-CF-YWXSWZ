## Chapter Overview
After an arduous journey from the Death World's deepest abyss, Xiao Chen and his companions finally ascend into the legendary Primordial Celestial Realm. The stark contrast between the deathly gloom below and the vibrant, life-filled paradise above is overwhelming. This chapter marks a pivotal transition, establishing a new base of operations in the Heavenly Realm while immediately revealing the complex political landscape of this higher plane. Through an interrogation of a local tiger demon, the group learns of the Otherworld's renewed influence over the past hundred thousand years. The chapter then takes a mysterious turn as Xiao Chen investigates a fire crow's cave, unearthing a sinister stone coffin that may hold ancient secrets—and danger.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the group's astonished arrival: the oppressive yin energy of the Death World gives way to celestial splendor. Sha Polang's exuberant antics provide comic relief as he declares his intent to enjoy Heavenly Realm life. A tiger demon's aggressive emergence is quickly subdued, and through interrogation, the group discovers that the Otherworld faction has grown powerful, with four new Ancestral God-level 'primordial ancestors' appearing over the past hundred thousand years, intimidating even the major powers. The tiger also reveals the local geography dominated by powerful demon lords and the distant location of the Otherworld's stronghold. Deciding to establish a sect nearby, Xiao Chen dispatches the Little Defiant Dragon and the three skeletons to subdue or capture the local demon lords. Xiao Chen himself heads to the most dangerous territory—the Fire Crow's cave—believed to hold a mere War Ancestor-level creature. However, upon arrival, Xiao Chen senses the area is an ancient holy land, far more significant than expected. Inside the cave, he witnesses an Otherworld cultivator begging the unseen master for the use of a legendary yin-yang plantain to track the escaped Nine Provinces cultivators. The plantain, a primordial spiritual root, guards itself with chaotic thunder. After the Otherworld cultivator departs, Xiao Chen and a disguised Keke investigate deeper, finding a short stone coffin containing a crow corpse. Ignoring warnings, they take the coffin back to the group, and the old skeleton reacts with extreme alarm, demanding it be sealed with heavy curses.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界), the highest realm in the World of Immortality's cosmology, often associated with primordial chaos and the origin of cultivation. The realm is described as infinitely vast, with a single region spanning tens of thousands of miles, yet it is but a corner of the Heavenly Realm. The concept of '守山兽尊' (guardian beast lord) reflects the tradition of powerful beings serving as protectors of sects or families, often with significant bloodlines. The yin-yang plantain (混沌芭蕉) is a type of spiritual root that grows at the intersection of extreme yin and yang, producing chaotic mist and thunder—a classic trope in xianxia for rare treasures that can only be harvested by the supremely powerful. The act of 'begging for a treasure' with a formal kneeling request underscores the hierarchy and respect for ancient power even among rival factions. The stone coffin containing a crow corpse hints at the theme of 'false death' or 'hidden rebirth' common in Chinese mythology, where a seemingly dead creature may be in a deep meditative state or sealed.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for the Heavenly Realm arc. Readers should note the political landscape: the Otherworld has grown in power over the past hundred thousand years, and their alliance with some Heavenly Realm giants may complicate Xiao Chen's goals. The fire crow's cave and the mysterious coffin will likely become a major plot point; the old skeleton's extreme reaction suggests the crow is far from dead and possibly an ancient being of terrible power. Xiao Chen's decision to bring the coffin back may have unforeseen consequences. Additionally, the Otherworld's quest to track the Ancestral Dragon Ship via the plantain threatens the remaining Nine Provinces survivors—a plot thread that will demand Xiao Chen's attention soon. Keep an eye on Sha Polang's continued humor as comic relief amid the rising tensions.
