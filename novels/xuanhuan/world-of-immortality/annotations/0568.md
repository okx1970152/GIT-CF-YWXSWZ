## Chapter Overview

The Sun Star burns with an intensity that reduces even divine metals to vapor, but for those who have reached the level of Ancestral God, it is a forge of opportunity. This chapter opens amidst a celestial assembly drawn by the imminent appearance of a heaven-defying war treasure. Two of the Celestial Realm's most fearsome monarchs—the Corpse Shadow and the Lotus King—manifest their imprints, commanding a fearful respect from all present. Yet a seemingly reckless old man with a goatee and a dry, rasping voice dares to speak out against their dominance, claiming that true treasures belong to the virtuous. This audacity shocks the crowd, yet the monarchs do not strike him down, hinting at hidden depths. The Phoenix Matriarch, ruler of this blazing domain, presides over the gathering, guiding the procession toward the ancient Sun Star Palace—the long-lost seat of a Stone Man King. The chapter is a masterful blend of epic scale, political tension, and the introduction of a mysterious figure who may be far more than he appears.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter establishes the Sun Star as a battleground for the highest powers. The Corpse Shadow and Lotus King arrive with overwhelming presence, yet they do not immediately claim the treasure, instead allowing a "virtuous contest." The witty, reckless old man provokes both, but his survival suggests he is either a fool or a concealed powerhouse—a classic xianxia trope. The Phoenix Matriarch reveals that the true Sun Star Palace was sealed for ages, and its opening requires more than brute force: only the main gate is accessible, and the palace tests entrants. This sets up a tension between those who would break in and those who respect the old king's rules. The Five Elements spiritual treasure guides the way, pointing to a cemetery of phoenix trees where the palace manifests. The chapter also introduces the enmity between the Six Experts of the Otherworld and the Four Experts of the Nine Provinces, with a veiled threat exchanged. Xiao Chen, still at Half-Ancestor level, is forced to accompany the shrunken old man, who drags him into the palace despite Xiao Chen's reluctance. The first victim appears: a Nine Provinces Ancestral God killed by a broken spear, signaling the deadly competition within.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Sun Star here is not the mortal sun but a Celestial Realm counterpart, made of deadly divine fire that even Ancestral Gods must respect—a reminder that higher worlds amplify natural phenomena. The Phoenix Matriarch, a peak existence, guards a grove of fire-essence parasol trees, which in Chinese myth are the phoenix's preferred perch. Her authority is absolute among her clan, yet she bows to the greater powers of the Corpse Shadow and Lotus King, illustrating the hierarchical nature of Celestial Realm politics. The old man's declaration that treasures go to the virtuous echoes a Confucian/Daoist ideal often invoked in xianxia, but here it is delivered with such brazen confidence that it borders on satire. The Stone Man King's palace, open only through the main gate, reflects the ancient belief that true power does not fear intrusion—the master is so confident that he welcomes all comers, trusting that only the worthy will survive. The broken spear killing a Nine Provinces Ancestral God early on underscores that this is a lethal tournament, not a treasure hunt.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a classic 'descent into the ancient palace' arc, but with a twist: the mysterious old man is clearly a wildcard. Pay close attention to his words and actions—xianxia veterans will suspect he is either an ancient power in disguise or has a direct connection to the stone man king. The tension between the Otherworld and Nine Provinces factions will almost certainly escalate into open conflict inside the palace. Xiao Chen's reluctant partnership with the old man may prove unexpectedly fruitful. Note the detail about the palace's main gate being the only safe entry—this will likely lead to traps, tests, or set pathways that reward courage and wit over raw power. The Phoenix Matriarch's neutrality may be tested as the treasure hunt progresses. Readers should also watch for any sign that the Corpse Shadow and Lotus King are not truly neutral but are manipulating events from the sidelines.
