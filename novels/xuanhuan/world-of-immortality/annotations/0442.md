## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges the reader into a tense, claustrophobic atmosphere as Xiao Chen, Tianwaitian, Renwairen, and the bear-like Yingxiong uncover a chilling relic—a prophecy book sealed by an Ancestral God three times ten thousand years ago. What begins as a curiosity swiftly becomes a psychological trap. The book begins to predict their actions with impossible accuracy, culminating in the revelation that only one of seven people will survive. The true horror sinks in when they realize that three additional beings have been hiding inside Xiao Chen's own body, bringing the count to exactly seven. As the weight of prophecy presses down, thirty plus skeleton kings emerge from the darkness, and an entity of even greater dread approaches.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the group examining the Doomsday Prophecy Book, a red-brown tome exuding an eerie, cold aura. As they flip through the pages, the book mirrors their reactions and predicts their future actions with uncanny precision—most notably depicting Tianwaitian calling the prophecy absurd, then later showing him and Yingxiong attempting to destroy the book. Despite their skepticism, the unfolding events align perfectly with the illustrations. When Ancestral God blood drips onto the book, it begins to corrode, destroying most of the latter pages. Only fragments remain: the words "hope of life" and "heaven's will". Then, a crucial detail emerges: the prophecy states there are seven people, but only four are present. Xiao Chen suddenly realizes with dread that three hidden beings have been residing in his acupoint realms—the same three Yin-Yang Tree spirits who gifted the Yin-Yang Fruits from earlier. They emerge, appearing as benevolent old men in black-white robes, and explain they meant no harm. But before trust can fully solidify, the dark outside the hall erupts with power: over thirty skeleton kings kneel, and a terrifyingly powerful presence approaches, hinting that the prophecy's count of seven may be incomplete or that a greater force now targets the group.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In Chinese xianxia mythology, prophecies often come with heavy connotations of "defying heaven" and "heaven's secrets must not be revealed." Ancestral Gods, though immensely powerful, are rarely depicted as having perfect foresight. This chapter questions the limits of even their knowledge. The Yin-Yang Tree spirits here are a unique touch: trees that have cultivated for over half a million years, achieving sentience and even forming their own miniature spiritual realm (the Yin-Yang Fruits realm). The number seven is significant—it suggests a complete but doomed group. The appearance of thirty-plus skeleton kings signals a coordinated force from the Death World, possibly under a sovereign or an ancestral god of death.

## Reading Guide

The prophecy's partial destruction leaves many questions: what did the lost pages reveal? Can Xiao Chen and his allies defy the prediction that only one will survive? The three Yin-Yang spirits are allies for now, but their long-term intentions remain untested. The approaching dark presence—likely a top-tier Death World sovereign—raises the stakes enormously. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen plans to use his new War Swords and the Eight Aspect Worlds in the coming battle. The tension between fate and free will is thematic; will the group accept or fight the prophecy? Also, note the reappearance of skeleton kings, which ties back to earlier arcs in the Death World. The next chapter promises a massive confrontation.
