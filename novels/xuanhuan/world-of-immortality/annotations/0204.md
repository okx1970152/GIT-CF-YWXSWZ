## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges Xiao Chen into a desperate night-time escape from Heavenly Emperor City, with the beautiful and cunning Hai Yunxue as his hostage. The tense negotiations unfold in the moonlight, dripping with false promises and hidden agendas. Xiao Chen secures a legendary Condensation Pearl to mask himself from the Hu family's soul imprint, only to discover it is a trap designed to drain his life force. The moment of betrayal is swift and brutal, turning allies into enemies and revealing the ruthless calculus behind noble families. The chapter balances intimate psychological warfare with explosive combat, culminating in Xiao Chen's cold execution of a young Hu family prodigy. The stakes are raised as Xiao Chen's newly attained Spirit-Treasury realm is tested against both younger generation challengers and the oppressive power of family elders.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen takes Hai Yunxue hostage, flying above the city to negotiate his escape. Hai Yunxue reveals that the Hu family has placed a soul imprint on him, allowing them to track him anywhere. She offers a Condensation Pearl to block the mark, but only in exchange for her freedom. Xiao Chen reluctantly agrees and returns to the Hai family mansion to retrieve the pearl. There, Hai Yuntian – once a friend – attempts a hidden assassination by stabbing himself to get close, but Xiao Chen's divine sense foils the attempt. Xiao Chen escapes with Hai Yunxue and the pearl, but the Hu family immediately gives chase. After using the pearl, Xiao Chen realizes it is a trap that devours his energy like a black hole. Hai Yunxue breaks free, her true nature unveiled as a cold schemer. Surrounded by the Hu and Hai elders, Xiao Chen is seemingly without hope. He provokes the assembly by declaring he would have crushed them all if they were the same age. A young Hu expert named Hu Feng steps forward to answer the challenge. Xiao Chen defeats him in a single, devastating blow – cutting off his head with a long knife, fusing sword light into his body with a rare divine technique. This display shocks the elders and underscores Xiao Chen's terrifying potential.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Soul imprints (精神烙印) are a common tracking method used by powerful cultivators, left on a target's spiritual sea so they can be sensed across vast distances. The Condensation Pearl (凝华珠) is a rare treasure that can neutralize such imprints and dodge spiritual detection, making it invaluable for fugitives. However, it can also be weaponized as a parasitic absorber of chi, showing that no artifact is without counter. Xiao Chen's ability to smelt weapons into his body (熔兵炼体) is a high-level divine art, typically requiring deep cultivation to master. It allows him to absorb and weaponize the essence of blades, strengthening his physical form beyond normal limits. His breakthrough into the Spirit-Treasury realm (识藏境界) marks a shift from basic mortal shedding to exploring the inner spiritual treasury, unlocking new perceptions and techniques. The challenge from Hu Feng of the younger generation reflects the xianxia tradition of dueling under equal terms to avoid the shame of elders attacking a junior.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's escape is far from over. Although he has humiliated the Hu family by killing their promising youth, the elders are now even more determined to destroy him. The Condensation Pearl has been rendered useless, and his true location may still be tracked by the Hu elders if the pearl's deactivation was incomplete. Hai Yunxue's betrayal runs deep – she is now a deadly enemy who knows Xiao Chen's personality and fighting style. A new wildcard is Xiao Chen's recent awakening to the Spirit-Treasury realm and his rare weapon-fusion technique; this will likely determine his survival in the coming battles. Watch for the involvement of the mysterious Hu Nu and Hu Shi, who have yet to fully exert their power. The political game in Heavenly Emperor City continues, with other families watching and waiting to see who falls next. The tension is building toward a definitive confrontation.
