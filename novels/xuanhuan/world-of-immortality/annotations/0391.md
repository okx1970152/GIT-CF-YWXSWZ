## Chapter Overview
This profoundly moving chapter marks the long-awaited reunion between Xiao Chen and Keke in the underground palace. Xiao Chen, now a violet-diamond skeleton from the Death World, reveals his true soul signature to Keke, triggering an emotional outburst of tears and joy. The chapter balances heartwarming reunion with lighthearted exploration of Keke's treasure-laden Paradise Lost, showcasing her collection of divine spiritual trees and her mischievous personality. The underground palace's ongoing struggle among kings provides a backdrop of tension, while the appearance of the Golden Lion King and the entry of Yin Ying and Yan Qingcheng with their sacred beasts set up future conflicts.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen finally reveals his identity to Keke by opening his soul signature, causing the little beast to burst into tears after years of grief and separation. The emotional reunion is handled with restraint; Keke's immediate concern is Xiao Chen's skeletal form, but he reassures her with lighthearted explanations about understanding life and death. Keke then proudly shows off her Paradise Lost garden, which contains numerous divine spiritual trees: the Immortal Tree from Mount Tai, a silver dragon tree from the southwestern sealed land protected by the Nine-Headed Serpent, and newly planted peach, pine, and lotus trees. She also reveals she has been collecting treasures and fighting off rivals like the Golden Lion King (whom she calls 'Yellow Fur'). Xiao Chen gifts her the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, which Keke's Sacred Tree absorbs, growing a new purple jade leaf—a demonstration of the Sacred Tree's near-heaven-defying power. They agree to separate and explore the underground palace's depths, where kings are already battling. The Golden Lion King arrives and exchanges tense words with Keke, while Yin Ying and Yan Qingcheng enter with the golden ape, the winged dragon, and the Peacock King.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The chapter deepens the lore of the Sacred Tree (also called the Dragon Clan Sacred Tree), which Keke possesses. It can absorb the essence of other divine artifacts, as shown when it drains the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree of its power and grows a new leaf. This mirrors the concept of 'devouring' or 'assimilating' treasures for cultivation, a common theme in xianxia where artifacts can cannibalize each other for evolution. The 'Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree' is a famous artifact from the Fengshen Yanyi (Investiture of the Gods), owned by the Daoist sage Zhunti; its appearance here as a consumable resource for Keke's tree indicates its downgraded status in this world. Keke's nickname 'Yellow Fur' for the Golden Lion King is a humorous callback to their rivalry and underscores the intimate naming conventions between powerful beings. The underground palace serves as a classic 'secret realm' where multiple factions compete for treasures, a staple of xianxia worldbuilding.

## Reading Guide
The reunion between Xiao Chen and Keke is a pivotal emotional milestone, but note that Xiao Chen remains in his skeleton form from the Death World. The chapter hints at his continued need to evolve: he is still a violet-diamond skeleton, not yet fully restored. Keke's Paradise Lost garden and the Sacred Tree's absorption power may become crucial for Xiao Chen's eventual resurrection. Pay attention to the growing presence of the Golden Lion King and the Peacock King—two powerful sacred beasts who will likely play significant roles in the upcoming conflicts over the underground palace's treasures. The appearance of Yin Ying (Third Princess of Great Shang) and Yan Qingcheng suggests political and romantic entanglements are far from over. The chapter sets up a three-way dynamic: Xiao Chen and Keke as covert allies, the Golden Lion King as a proud rival, and the various kings fighting for the ancient artifacts.
