## Chapter Overview

This chapter throws the reader into a brutal and desperate conflict. The Otherworld Ancestral Lord and the Death World Ancestral Lord clash with world-shattering power, while Xiao Chen and his allies are besieged by nine Half-Ancestors. Xiao Chen relies on the mysterious half skull to withstand attacks from an Eight-Heaven Half-Ancestor, but the odds are overwhelming. Just as annihilation seems certain, a star gate opens in the heavens, disgorging a motley crew of familiar faces—Wu Ming, the stuttering assassin Jin Sanyi, and the ever-playful Keke—who tumble directly into the battle, upending the tension with absurdist comedy.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huaiyou and the Martial Imprint engaging the Death World Ancestral Lord in a cosmic duel. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen, Bai Qi, the Golden Divine Halberd, and the Dark-Iron Seal are surrounded by nine Otherworld Half-Ancestors and eight Death World kings. Xiao Chen uses the half skull from the Death World to neutralize attacks from an Eight-Heaven Half-Ancestor, but the disparity in numbers is crushing. He tries to ally with the Death World kings by claiming kinship with Yi Tianzhong, but is exposed when Yi Tianzhong himself is present. The kings withdraw, leaving Xiao Chen alone against nine enemies. The Half-Ancestors taunt them as inferior beings. Just as they prepare a final annihilation strike, a star gate appears, momentarily staggering the Half-Ancestors. Out tumbles Wu Ming and his son, the portly Zhuge Pangzi, Totti, Jin Sanyi, and finally Keke. Jin Sanyi unwittingly lands on the Eight-Heaven Half-Ancestor's shoulders, and Keke drops onto his head, creating a scene of utter absurdity that contrasts sharply with the preceding horror. The chapter ends with the balance of power dramatically shifted by this inexplicable intervention.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the Death World rank of 'Ancestral Lord' (祖君), a being just below the true Ancestral Gods, possessing world-dominating power. The half skull that Xiao Chen wields is a fragment of a transcendent being, able to absorb divine attacks—a key asset in the Death World. The 'star gate' is a sudden spatial phenomenon that seems to respond to extreme crisis, possibly tied to the Eternal Light or other cosmic forces. Also notable is the Otherworld Half-Ancestors' explicit contempt for humans and dragons, reflecting the novel's theme of civilizational struggle. The comic relief from Jin Sanyi's stutter and Keke's careless antics provides deliberate tonal contrast, a hallmark of Chen Dong's style.

## Reading Guide

Readers should watch how Keke's arrival and the chaos of the star gate shift the battle. The gate's origin is left mysterious—could it be connected to the Eternal Light or a hidden ally? The comic characters are now stranded in the middle of a Half-Ancestor battlefield; their fate is uncertain. Pay attention to whether Xiao Chen can exploit this distraction to turn the tables or escape. Also, the Ancestral Lord battle beyond the second sky-curtain remains unresolved. The chapter sets up a major turning point where the overwhelming odds may be broken by the absurd intrusion of fate.
