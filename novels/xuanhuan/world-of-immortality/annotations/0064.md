## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers one of the most grueling and psychologically revealing battles yet. Xiao Chen, trapped in a sealed space domain, faces the Space Spiritist Liu Mu—a pale, sickly youth whose power belies his frail appearance. The fight is not merely physical; it is a contest of will against near-absolute spatial control. Every bone in Xiao Chen’s body creaks under crushing force, yet he refuses to yield. In a moment of supreme crisis, he unlocks a new defensive technique—the Big Dipper Seals the Gods—that not only saves his life but shatters the myth of the Space Spiritist’s invincibility. The chapter ends not in lingering enmity but in a wary, respectful alliance between two warriors who recognize each other’s worth.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen is immobilized within Liu Mu’s sealed space, a hundred-square-meter pocket dimension governed entirely by the spiritist’s will. The spatial pressure threatens to tear him apart, yet he forces his hand to launch a desperate sword-beam, which Liu Mu deflects with a shimmer of cyan light. Both combatants are pushed to the verge of collapse, their bodies soaked in sweat and trembling from exertion. Liu Mu then unleashes a rain of spatial blades—crescent-shaped energy that can cleave through almost any defense. Facing certain death, Xiao Chen channels his life force into a forbidden technique: the Big Dipper Seals the Gods. Seven brilliant stars materialize before him, spinning to form a luminous screen that absorbs the incoming blade-rain, merging it with his own power, and redirecting it as a single devastating beam that shatters the space domain. The explosion of energy tears the pocket dimension apart, and both fighters land on the bone-covered ground—Xiao Chen victorious but barely standing. In the aftermath, Liu Mu acknowledges defeat without bitterness, revealing that Space Spiritists are legendary among cultivators for being peerless among those of the same realm. Xiao Chen, surprised to learn this, realizes he has broken a long-held legend. They discuss the looming threat of the alliances on Dragon Island and agree to form a pact of mutual support, recognizing that survival may require trusted allies.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Space Spiritist (空间灵士) is a rare and exalted type of cultivator. Born with an innate affinity for spatial forces, they can create sealed pocket dimensions where they hold near-absolute authority. In the cultivation world, they are considered kings among their peers—rumored to be undefeatable by anyone of the same realm. Xiao Chen’s victory over Liu Mu is therefore a monumental achievement, proving that even such legendary advantages can be overcome with enough raw will and creative power. The technique Xiao Chen invents on the spot—Big Dipper Seals the Gods (北斗封神)—draws on the astral symbolism of the Big Dipper, a constellation revered in Chinese cosmology for its power over fate and the cycle of time. By channeling his energy into seven star-shaped nodes, Xiao Chen creates a defensive-absorbing-rebounding barrier that turns an enemy’s strength against them. This is not a pre-learned skill but a desperate improvisation born from his earlier training with the Big Dipper energy points, marking a pivotal moment in his cultivation journey. The battle takes place on the Bone Sea (骨海), a desolate plain of white bones near the divine stele that seals Dragon Island, adding a layer of grim, mythic atmosphere to the confrontation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen’s standing on Dragon Island. His victory over a top-tier Space Spiritist will undoubtedly ripple through the alliance networks, and readers should watch for how the news affects his reputation—especially among enemies like Yan Qingcheng and Zhao Lin'er. Liu Mu’s decision to ally rather than nurse a grudge adds a powerful new dynamic to the story; his spatial abilities could become crucial in future confrontations. The chapter also hints at the deeper mysteries of the Bone Sea and the divine stele—why did Liu Mu choose to cultivate there? What has he learned about the island’s seal? The tenuous pact between two former foes raises questions about trust and survival: in a world where betrayal is the norm, can this alliance hold? Looking ahead, the pressure from the various alliances is mounting, and Xiao Chen’s need for reliable companions grows ever more urgent.
