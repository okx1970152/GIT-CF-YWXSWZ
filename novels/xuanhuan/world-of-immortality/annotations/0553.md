## Chapter Overview

This chapter opens with the brutal execution of a Celestial Envoy at the hands of Ren Mo Ge Qian, a demon whose power eclipses even Ancestral Gods. The scene is one of raw, primal violence — a petrified arm shattering a divine being with contemptuous ease, followed by the consumption of her soul. This act serves not only to display Ge Qian's terrifying might but also to establish the high stakes of the conflict between worlds. Amid the carnage, Xiao Chen observes from the shadows, carrying the fragmented will of the fallen envoy in a token that promises a path to the Celestial Realm. The chapter then pivots to a tense confrontation between Xiao Chen and Ge Qian, where the demon speaks with melancholic menace, threatening to drown the Nine Provinces in blood if his demands are not met. The tone is one of tragic grandeur and imminent despair, painting Ge Qian as a figure of immense power and deep sorrow.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter's central event is the killing of a female Ancestral God-level Celestial Envoy by Ren Mo Ge Qian. Ge Qian uses his petrified left arm to punch through her skull, then unleashes golden rays from his eyes that pierce her soul and shatter her body. Despite her attempts to escape by dividing her divine consciousness into thousands of strands, Ge Qian's petrified arm expands to seal the heavens, trapping her. He then gathers her shattered soul into his stone hand and devours it whole, extinguishing all trace of her existence. Four Otherworld Ancestral Gods witness this but dare not intervene, instead fleeing in terror when Ge Qian shows signs of his melancholic state — a state that legend says precedes his most brutal massacres.

Xiao Chen, who had been hiding using the formation diagram, is exposed when Ge Qian destroys the space around him. The token given by the dying envoy attempts to flee but is seized by Ge Qian. Xiao Chen manages to break free using the formation diagram and grabs the token, but Ge Qian intercepts his escape by shattering the spatial passage. Their conversation reveals Ge Qian's demand that Xiao Chen repair the "divine city" (神城) quickly, under threat of unleashing genocide on the Nine Provinces and the four worlds. Xiao Chen denounces Ge Qian as a traitor to his own homeland, but Ge Qian responds with a phrase dripping with tragic self-justification: "Abandoned by heaven, forsaken by the world." He admits he had no choice. The chapter ends with the token activating, attempting to teleport Xiao Chen to the Celestial Realm, but Ge Qian steps across ten thousand li to cut off the path, leaving Xiao Chen's fate uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the immense power disparity between a peak Half-Ancestor (or even Ancestral God-level) being like Ren Mo Ge Qian and ordinary cultivators. Ge Qian's ability to kill an Ancestral God-level Celestial Envoy with such ease demonstrates the terrifying scale of power in the World of Immortality's upper realms. His petrified arm is a hallmark of the "Stone Man" path — a cultivation route that transforms the body into an indestructible substance, granting near-invincible physical prowess. The token given by the dying envoy is a common trope in xianxia: a last resort artifact that can transport the protagonist to a higher realm or serve as a key to hidden knowledge. The "Celestial Realm" (天界) is a higher plane of existence, distinct from the four worlds of Martial, Qi, Curse, and Soul, likely the source of the Supreme Ancestral Gods. Ge Qian's melancholic persona — a powerful being who laments his own fate even as he commits atrocities — is a classic tragic villain archetype in Chinese mythology, reminiscent of figures like the Overlord of Chu.

## Reading Guide

The chapter ends on a sharp cliffhanger: the token's teleportation is cut off by Ge Qian, but whether Xiao Chen escapes, is captured, or manages to activate the token at the last second remains unclear. The mystery of Ge Qian's past and his connection to the Nine Provinces deepens — why did such a powerful being betray his own world? What is the "divine city" he demands repaired? Readers should watch for Ge Qian's backstory possibly being revealed in the next chapters, and for the token's role as a potential game-changer. The token may lead Xiao Chen to the Celestial Realm, where he could find allies or ancient power to counter the Otherworld's invasion. Meanwhile, Ge Qian's threat to massacre the Nine Provinces raises the stakes, promising an imminent crisis if his demands are not met. The confrontation also hints at a deeper bond between Ge Qian and the stone man path, possibly linking to Xiao Chen's own cultivation.
