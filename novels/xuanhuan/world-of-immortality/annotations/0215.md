## Chapter Overview

Chapter 215 plunges Xiao Chen deep into the incomplete underworld forged by the ancestral god Suiren, where he must navigate a realm of lost souls and lingering powers to find Keke. The discovery of a rare Purple Diamond Yin Wood token, bearing Keke's claw marks, leads him to the court of Chu Jiang Wang, one of the legendary Ten Kings of the Underworld—a being who exudes both ancient authority and weary melancholy. This meeting reveals that the underworld is far from the orderly realm of myth; it is a fragmented, dangerous domain where few kings survive and chaos reigns. The chapter balances somber lore with the urgency of Xiao Chen's quest, as he learns he must journey to the mysterious Nine Serenity Platform to retrieve his companion. The atmosphere is thick with dread, yet pierced by the sacred radiance of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree and the lingering presence of Buddhist relics, hinting at deeper cosmic forces at play.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen obtains a Purple Diamond Yin Wood token—a precious artifact from the underworld used for transmitting spiritual messages. Recognizing Keke's claw marks, he invokes the wood's stored spiritual waves and hears a cacophony of cries, battles, and chaos, but not Keke's voice, intensifying his concern. He traces the token back to Chu Jiang Wang, who reveals that the underworld is an incomplete creation of Suiren, and only he remains of the Ten Kings. Chu Jiang Wang directs Xiao Chen to the Nine Serenity Platform, a journey of about half a month on the Black Dragon King's back, warning him to avoid powerful ghostly entities and dark palaces. Xiao Chen and Niu Ren travel through a bleak landscape of ancient tombs, shadowy palaces, and hordes of wandering souls, relying on the Sacred Tree's protective light to survive the oppressive yin energy. After ten days, they discover a crumbling Buddhist temple—a relic of Dizang Wang, the Bodhisattva who once vowed to empty the hells. There, the Burial Shroud and Suiren's Fire-Drill begin to stir, and they uncover a mysterious human leg beneath the rubble, a profound discovery that hints at the hidden presence of a supreme being.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The underworld depicted here draws heavily from Chinese folk religion, where the Ten Kings of the Underworld (十殿冥王) judge the dead and oversee reincarnation. Chu Jiang Wang (楚江王) is the second of these kings, traditionally presiding over a hell of boiling water. However, the novel subverts this by presenting the underworld as a failed divine project—Suiren's attempt to forge a complete nether realm, which collapsed. This reflects a common xianxia trope where primordial gods attempt to shape reality but leave broken realms behind. Yin Wood (阴木) is a legendary material said to grow in the underworld, notable for its spiritual properties, used for crafting alchemical ingredients and transmitting consciousness. The mention of Dizang Wang (地藏王) references the bodhisattva Kṣitigarbha, who vowed not to achieve Buddhahood until all hells are empty—a core figure in Mahayana Buddhist cosmology. His temple in the underworld signifies that even within a failed creation, traces of true enlightenment remain.

## Reading Guide

This chapter deepens the mystery of Keke's predicament and opens a new arc in the underworld. Pay close attention to Chu Jiang Wang's melancholy and his mention of the other kings' likely demise—this connects to the earlier appearances of the Three Skeleton Kings (Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, Lunhuiwang) on Dragon Island, whose soul-flames resembled his. The Nine Serenity Platform will likely be a pivotal location, possibly holding the key to Keke's rescue and a glimpse into Suiren's former power. The discovery of the Buddhist temple and the hidden leg foreshadows a major entity or relic, possibly Dizang Wang himself, who may have left a physical legacy. The reactions of the Sacred Tree and Suiren's artifacts suggest that the underworld holds deep ties to Xiao Chen's own cultivation path. Readers can anticipate a convergence of underworld politics, divine inheritance, and Xiao Chen's desperate drive to save his companion.
