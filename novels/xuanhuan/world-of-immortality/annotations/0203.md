## Chapter Overview

The forced wedding between Xiao Chen and Hai Yunxue is revealed as a political trap designed to eliminate the former Dragon Island survivor. What begins as a lavish ceremony quickly becomes a stage for Xiao Chen's desperate gamble: taking the bride hostage to secure his escape. The chapter maintains a sharp contrast between the surface warmth of the celebration and the cold calculations beneath—Hai Yunxue plans to have Xiao Chen killed after the wedding, while Xiao Chen, sensing the danger, uses the very ceremony as his escape route. The tension crackles with every polite exchange, each seemingly affectionate gesture carrying the weight of imminent violence.

## Key Plot Points

- Hai Yunxue's internal monologue reveals her ambition: she views Xiao Chen as a temporary stepping stone and has already decided he must die to erase the marriage's trace. She believes he cannot offer the status she craves.
- Xiao Chen, sensing the Hai family's murderous intent, decides to act before the hidden 'Tiger Slave' arrives. He plays along with the wedding festivities, then abruptly seizes control.
- In a stunning move, Xiao Chen takes Hai Yunxue hostage, cutting her neck with a borrowed blade to demonstrate he will kill her without hesitation. The Hai family is forced to let him leave the mansion.
- However, Xiao Chen's attempt to flee via the Undying Heavenly Wings is thwarted by hidden experts in the sky, and he is forced back to the ground, still holding Hai Yunxue.
- The chapter ends with Xiao Chen pressing the blade deeper into Hai Yunxue's neck, blood drawn, as he demands a clear path. The standoff leaves his fate uncertain, but his gamble has bought him a precarious chance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In the World of Immortality, political marriages between powerful families are often tools for alliance or elimination. Hai Yunxue's family intends to use the wedding to kill Xiao Chen without public blame—a common tactic among ancient houses. The 'spiritual sense' or 'divine consciousness' used by experts to lock onto opponents is a key ability of the Spirit-Treasury realm, which Xiao Chen has not yet reached. His 'Undying Heavenly Wings' are a forbidden technique from the Undying Sect that grants flight at the Mortal-Shedding realm, but here they are insufficient against the Hai family's Spirit-Treasury realm elders. The 'Tiger Slave' (虎奴) mentioned is likely a powerful assassin sent to finish the job.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks the beginning of a new conflict arc in Heavenly Emperor City. Watch for how the Hai family responds—they have publicly lost face and will likely escalate their pursuit. The mysterious 'Tiger Slave' remains an off-screen threat. Xiao Chen's hostage strategy is bold but temporary; his escape depends on outmaneuvering experts across multiple realms. Hai Yunxue's character is now established as ambitious and cold, potentially setting her up as a recurring antagonist or a complex figure whose loyalties may shift. The involvement of other noble families (like those who blocked the Hai elder) hints at deeper political games that could become allies or further traps.
