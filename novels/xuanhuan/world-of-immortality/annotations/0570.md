## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, Xiao Chen witnesses the tragic death of a Nine Provinces Ancestral God who clutched a legendary bow and nine arrows, lamenting his inability to return home. Taking up the weapon, Xiao Chen makes a solemn vow: to use these arrows to slay the enemy gods and bring the bow back to the Nine Provinces. The chapter then plunges into a tense, desperate game of cat and mouse within the labyrinthine corridors of the King's Star Palace, where divine sense and power are suppressed. Xiao Chen, aided by the enigmatic and duplicitous Old Goat, must evade the pursuing White Ancestral God, avoid a cannibalistic monster, and rescue a captured female ancestral god. The action culminates in Xiao Chen's first use of the arrows, claiming four lives, but the chapter ends on a chilling note as they stumble upon the stone figure of the Lotus King.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Nine Provinces Ancestral God dissolving into nothingness, leaving his longbow and nine arrows. Xiao Chen vows to avenge him and bring the bow home. Traversing the star palace's labyrinth, they witness a savage figure devouring a corpse, forcing a detour. The White Ancestral God and two allies ambush them, but the Old Goat uses a fake "Stone Order" to bluff their way past. A female Nine Provinces Ancestral God is captured and possessed; her captors demand the arrows. Xiao Chen seemingly surrenders, hiding the Primordial Demon City inside one arrow. He activates it to free the hostage and reclaim the arrows. Then he fires three arrows in a single volley, killing three Otherworld Ancestral Gods simultaneously. However, the White Ancestral God gives chase, and the Old Goat accidentally crashes into a stone woman with a stone lotus—the Lotus King—who turns around as the chapter closes. This sequence solidifies Xiao Chen's commitment to revenge and introduces a new, fearsome player.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The "Stone Order" used by the Old Goat references the legendary Stone Emperor (石中帝), a figure of ultimate power in the Stone Man path. This deception plays on the aura of authority that such tokens carry, a common trope in xianxia where even false artifacts can intimidate through sheer name recognition. The King's Star Palace itself is a classic cultivation setting—a remnant space of a past supreme being where normal laws are suppressed, forcing reliance on physical prowess and innate abilities rather than divine sense. The Lotus King (莲王) is a Stone Woman (石人), a being who has taken the Stone Man path beyond Ancestral God, achieving a state of near-perfection through petrifaction. The rotten-wood arrows are described as disastrous to Heaven—forbidden weapons that are expended upon use, underscoring the theme that ultimate power comes at irreversible cost.

## Reading Guide
The arrival of the Lotus King marks a turning point in the arc. Stone-Man level beings are the pinnacle threat, and her sudden appearance suggests the star palace holds deeper secrets. Xiao Chen now has only six arrows left; he must conserve them for the highest-priority targets. The mysterious figure who possessed the female ancestral god remains at large, and the White Ancestral God is still hunting him. The Old Goat's true identity is increasingly suspect—is he truly the Stone Emperor's brother, or a consummate con artist? Look for the star palace to yield more treasures or traps, and for Xiao Chen to refine his strategy against the Otherworld gods. The tragic tone of the fallen ancestor continues to fuel his determination.
