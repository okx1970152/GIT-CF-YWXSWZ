## Chapter Overview

Chapter 563 is a relentless, high-stakes action sequence that showcases Xiao Chen at his most desperate and resourceful. After calling forth the five Stone Man Kings' Dao imprints to trample an Otherworld Upper Ancestral God, he captures two powerful enemies—a young Ancestral God and a War Ancestor—and attempts to extract their soul imprints for his own advancement. But the Heavenly Realm is no place for quiet cultivation; a six-heaven Super Ancestral God immediately intercepts him, forcing Xiao Chen to destroy his captive and flee. The chapter escalates into a multi-day pursuit by an eight-heaven Super Ancestral God, culminating in a bloody confrontation where Xiao Chen, likely aided by the sudden appearance of Keke, manages to kill his pursuer. The tone is one of grim survival: the Heavenly Realm is vast but hostile, and every victory comes at a cost.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen uses the five Stone Man Kings' Dao imprints to overwhelm and trample a former Otherworld Upper Ancestral God, a symbolic and psychological blow to the Otherworld's faith. He then captures a young Ancestral God and a War Ancestor, sealing them in the Primordial Demon City and the Ancient Scroll respectively. His plan to extract their soul imprints via the 'steal heaven and earth' technique is interrupted by a six-heaven Super Ancestral God wielding a massive divine blade. Xiao Chen destroys the captive Ancestral God to remove a tracking mark, then engages the Super Ancestral God briefly before retreating due to approaching divine senses. He manages to shake off pursuit by killing the marked prisoner. However, an eight-heaven Super Ancestral God picks up his trail and hunts him across the Heavenly Realm for days. The chase spreads word of Xiao Chen's actions, stirring interest among other powers, including the enigmatic Horse Herder who mentions an 'ultimate treasure' (逆天战宝). After ten days of running and fighting, Xiao Chen finally kills the eight-heaven expert in a remote region—apparently with assistance from Keke, who is glimpsed at the scene. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen vanishing, his reputation now cemented as a dangerous player in the Heavenly Realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of 'Stone Man Kings' (石人王者) and their Dao imprints (大道烙印) is a central element of the cultivation system in this novel. Stone Men are beings who have petrified their bodies to achieve immortality, and the five kings represent the pinnacle of this path. Their imprints can be manifested as quasi-independent entities capable of suppressing even high-level Ancestral Gods. The 'steal heaven and earth' technique (偷天换日) Xiao Chen uses to extract soul imprints is a forbidden method that allows him to temporarily gain the Dao insights of his captives—a risky but rapid way to improve his cultivation. The 'Super Ancestral God' (级祖神) classification refers to groups within the Ancestral God realm based on 'heavenly tiers' (六重天, 八重天). The higher the heaven, the greater the power; an eight-heaven expert is far more formidable than a six-heaven one. The Horse Herder (牧马人) is a classic xianxia trope: a seemingly reclusive figure who tends divine beasts but is actually a hidden expert with access to rare treasures. His mention of an 'ultimate treasure' foreshadows a major plot development.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pivot from strategic victory to desperate survival. Pay attention to the thematic contrast between Xiao Chen's initial triumph (trampling the Upper Ancestor) and the immediate, overwhelming counterattack from the Otherworld. The multi-day chase emphasizes that the Heavenly Realm is not a place where one can rest on their laurels—every action draws predators. The Horse Herder's dialogue hints at a larger treasure hunt that will likely involve Xiao Chen in future chapters. The brief appearance of Keke at the end is significant; her increased involvement suggests she is becoming more active in Xiao Chen's battles. Note also the psychological impact on the Otherworld: the loss of an Upper Ancestor's image being trampled is a blow to their morale, which may lead to reckless actions. The chapter raises questions: How will the Otherworld respond to losing three experts? What is the ultimate treasure, and will Xiao Chen seek it? The pace is relentless, so this chapter is best read as a single breathless sequence.
