## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a significant shift in the power dynamics of Dragon Island. After being hunted by Zhao Lin'er and her Tree Men, Xiao Chen witnesses the formation of the first formal alliance against him. Zhao Lin'er, using her beauty and the formidable Tree Men as bait, hangs a blood banner that draws opportunistic cultivators to her cause. The tension is thick as Xiao Chen observes from the cliffs, a silent predator waiting for darkness. The chapter balances political maneuvering with a lighter subplot involving Keke's gluttonous adventure, which adds a touch of the absurd to an otherwise grim situation.

## Key Plot Points
Zhao Lin'er takes a decisive step by establishing the Blood Banner Alliance at Treefolk Valley. She posts a banner demanding the death of Xiao Chen as a prerequisite for alliance, leveraging her beauty and the combat power of the Tree Men to attract followers. Within half a day, thirteen cultivators join, including spiritists, spellmasters, and warriors. By evening, the renowned Forest Tribe strongman Yalode and Barbarian warrior Kael add their strength to the alliance, recognizing the opportunity to subdue the Tree Men for themselves. Xiao Chen, concealed on the valley wall, watches the coalition form over the course of a full day. He resists the urge to act rashly, instead planning a nighttime strike to demonstrate his defiance. Meanwhile, Keke wanders off into the valley and returns with a distended belly, having found and consumed a trove of life nectar or some similar treasure. She casually reveals that she left the sacred tree behind in the valley, a revelation that leaves Xiao Chen speechless at her carelessness with the priceless divine sapling.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the concept of tactical alliances in the World of Immortality. Zhao Lin'er's blood banner is a traditional method of rallying allies by offering a clear target—a kill order—as a bond of loyalty. It reflects the harsh, pragmatic nature of cultivation society, where strength and mutual benefit dictate allegiances. The Tree Men themselves are a rare form of sentient plant life, born from millennia-old trees nurtured by spiritual energy. Their immense physical power and connection to nature make them prized allies. The chapter also contrasts the brutal politics with the innocent, almost comic behavior of the divine beast Keke, whose gluttonous pursuit of heavenly treasures underscores the hidden richness of Dragon Island beyond its dangers.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation. Xiao Chen's patience will be tested as the alliance against him grows in power and numbers. Watch for his planned night strike—it may not achieve total victory, but it will send a clear message. The revelation that Keke left the sacred tree in the valley is a ticking time bomb; either it will be discovered by the alliance, or Keke's attachment to it may force Xiao Chen to act. Additionally, the presence of Yalode and Kael in the alliance suggests that other major players are taking an interest in eliminating Xiao Chen. The political landscape of Dragon Island is shifting, and Xiao Chen will need every ounce of cunning to survive.
