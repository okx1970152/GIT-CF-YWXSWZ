## Chapter Overview
This chapter presents a stark contrast between tragic past and absurd present. In the mysterious Burial Valley, Xiao Chen and Bai Qi witness the Martial Imprint’s devastating discovery: the body of the Martial Ancestor, stronger than any divine weapon, has inexplicably crumbled to dust after eons of nurturing. The emotional weight of this failure is palpable. Meanwhile, on Dragon Island, the perpetually lazy Keke, who has done nothing but sleep for 155 years, is about to break through to the half-ancestor realm—all while still fast asleep. The chapter juxtaposes the solemn grief of lost greatness with the almost comical ease of divine progress.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens in the second valley of the Burial Valley. The Martial Imprint, the sentient essence of the Martial Ancestor's techniques, is in anguish. He had placed the Martial Ancestor's superhumanly tough body in this valley to allow it to 'grow' like a divine weapon, hoping for resurrection. But after endless ages, the body has turned to bone dust, shattering his centuries of effort. He rages, but a crude carving on the giant tree hints that another being—possibly a powerful figure—has visited and left a mocking signature. This leads the Martial Imprint to suspect a hidden hand. He takes out his frustration on Xiao Chen, giving him a beating. Then, external threats emerge: the Fuxi Bagua artifact and a black qilin beast blockade the valley, accompanied by a woman's voice, indicating that many divine weapon spirits have awakened and are hostile. The Martial Imprint warns Xiao Chen and Bai Qi to stay put while he confronts them. The scene then shifts to Dragon Island, where the Dragon Clan War God King and many dragons gather as Keke, the little white beast, sleeps through her own breakthrough to the half-ancestor realm. Lightning strikes her but is effortlessly deflected by her seven-colored divine light. The gathered cultivators and dragons are dumbfounded by her laziness and incredible luck.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter brings in several classic xianxia concepts. The Burial Valley is a space-time anomaly where divine weapons can be nurtured or destroyed, echoing the trope of 'a place for forging or growing artifacts'. The Martial Imprint is a 'remnant soul' or 'mark of the Dao', a common idea where a supreme being's will or techniques can become autonomous. The Fuxi Bagua (Eight Trigrams of Fuxi) references the legendary Chinese sage Fuxi, often associated with the I Ching. The black qilin is a mythical beast. The breakthrough to half-ancestor (半祖) is a major cultivation milestone, equivalent to reaching a demigod level. Keke's effortless breakthrough while sleeping is a humorous twist on 'effortless mastery', a theme in Daoist philosophy where naturalness (ziran) leads to supreme achievement. The lightning tribulation (天雷) is a standard test for advancement, but Keke's nonchalant deflection underscores her extraordinary nature.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major mystery: who visited the Burial Valley and left that message? It also reveals that many divine artifact souls have awakened and are aligned against the Martial Imprint, indicating a larger conflict brewing. The Martial Imprint's frustration suggests that Xiao Chen may inherit some of his knowledge or power. Meanwhile, Keke's imminent half-ancestor breakthrough will make her a peer to Lan Nuo and other top cultivators, changing the power balance. Her lazy but genuine talent hints that she may have a unique path. The next chapter will likely follow the confrontation in the Burial Valley and the completion of Keke's breakthrough. Readers should watch for developments in the artifact war and Keke's new abilities.
