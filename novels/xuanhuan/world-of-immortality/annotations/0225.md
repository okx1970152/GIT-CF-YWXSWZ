## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a significant milestone for Xiao Chen as he breaks through to the **Sixth Heaven of the Spirit-Treasury realm**, restoring his vitality and refining his precious body. The narrative then shifts to the bustling capital of the Great Shang, where Xiao Chen, disguised and observing, witnesses the arrogance of the Central Lands' young elites. They dismiss the Southern Wastes as a backwater, unknowingly standing in the presence of the very man they dismiss as a dead legend. The chapter blends personal triumph with simmering social tension, setting the stage for future confrontations.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen achieves a dual breakthrough in both spiritual and physical cultivation, entering the Sixth Heaven of Spirit-Treasury. This restores his life force and transforms his body into an even more formidable treasure body. Having absorbed the essence of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, he is now a changed man. He travels to the Great Shang capital, Yin, where he spends time cultivating amidst the mortal world. At the famous Moon-Viewing Tower, he overhears a group of aristocratic youths from local powerful families openly deriding the Southern Wastes and its cultivators. They claim the only notable youth from that region, Xiao Chen, is likely dead. Yan Qingcheng, Qilao, and Yuwen Feng are also present, and the youth Yin Feng casually kills a challenger with his nether flame. The chapter ends with the youths arrogantly stating that Xiao Chen was the only possible equal from the South, but he is probably gone.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Cultivation realms in this world include Mortal-Shedding, Spirit-Treasury, Sky-Mastery, and beyond. The Spirit-Treasury realm focuses on opening the body's inner treasures and developing divine abilities. Xiao Chen's unique path involves divinizing acupoints, which now resonate with the sacred tree he absorbed. His breakthrough here recovers his vitality, a critical issue after his life force was drained. The Central Lands (中土) are the heartland of the Eternal Continent, home to ancient kingdoms like Great Shang. The Southern Wastes are considered a remote frontier. The casual killing of a challenger by the noble youth demonstrates the brutal hierarchy and lack of legal consequence for the powerful. The Moon-Viewing Tower is a famous location associated with the legendary poet-immortal Li Bai.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen remains hidden, choosing to observe rather than reveal himself. The obvious arrogance of the Central Lands youths is a setup for his eventual debut in this new arena. The mention of Zhao Zhongyang, the one Southern Wastes prodigy who never fought Xiao Chen, hints at a future clash. Keep an eye on Yan Qingcheng and Qilao, who are pretending not to know him. The tension is palpable: Xiao Chen has grown immensely but is still underestimated. The stage is set for his first major confrontation in the Central Lands, likely against these very youths or their elders.
