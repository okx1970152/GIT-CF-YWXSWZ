## Chapter Overview

This chapter masterfully juxtaposes the farcical humiliation of a defeated Ancestral God with the cataclysmic clash of supreme powers in the Death World's depths. Sha Polang's grotesque antics—a crude comedy of barefoot stomping and borrowed kung fu calls—contrast sharply with the emergence of the Emperor's City, a living fortress that shatters an incarnation of the legendary Eternal King Meng Degang. The chapter also advances the Sacred Sacrifice plot, recycling fallen enemy gods into cultivation fuel, while the three skeleton kings execute a cunning heist that may alter the balance of power. The tone remains unflinchingly mythic, blending raw violence with sardonic humor and cosmic dread.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Keke cornering an Otherworld Ancestral God who killed the Azure Dragon King. Overwhelmed by Xiao Chen's Ninth Heaven pressure and Keke's nine-colored divine light, the god is helpless. Sha Polang then subjects him to a humiliating beating, mixing fake martial arts shouts with physical abuse, and even stepping on his face with a bare foot. The Ancestral God, driven to despair, admits defeat and begs for death. Xiao Chen subsequently performs the Sacred Sacrifice on this god, extracting his divine essence.

Simultaneously, in the Death World's interior, the battle at the Seven Demon Cities intensifies. The Emperor's City from the distant horizon rises and approaches, its owner a shadowy figure of immense power. This figure strikes down the incarnation of Meng Degang, shattering his stone body into five parts. Before Meng Degang can reassemble, the three skeleton kings—Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, and Lunhuiwang—appear, each snatching a piece of his remains and vanishing without trace, cutting off his spiritual link to them. Enraged, Meng Degang searches vainly but cannot find them.

Back on the outer battlefield, Xiao Chen's group finishes off the remaining enemies: a stone man is destroyed by the stone man puppet, and two more Ancestral Gods self-destruct rather than face Sha Polang's mockery. Xiao Chen absorbs their essence as well. Finally, Xiao Chen returns to the Seven Demon Cities and attacks Meng Degang's remaining half-incarnation, driving him to fury.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Sacred Sacrifice (圣祭) is a forbidden technique from the Martial Ancestor's heterodox chapter, which allows a cultivator to extract the life essence of a fallen god and refine it into their own body. It is portrayed as a brutal but efficient method of gaining power, emphasizing the cutthroat nature of the cultivation world.

The Upper Ancestral God (上祖神) path refers to a cultivation route that focuses on mastering myriad world laws rather than the Stone Man path of physical deification. Keke has stepped onto this path, allowing her to wield nine-colored divine light and seal enemies.

The three skeleton kings (Qin Guang, Yan Luo, and Wheel) are ancient disciples of Suiren, now evolved to the Earth-Penetrating realm. Their ability to sever a Stone Man King's connection to his severed parts and teleport instantly indicates profound mastery over death energy and spatial laws.

The Emperor's City is a mobile fortress that can crush demon cities; its owner is a mysterious entity from a distant imperial city, likely a peerless War Ancestor or Stone Man King who has taken the final step.

## Reading Guide

This chapter lays groundwork for several key arcs. The three skeleton kings' theft of Meng Degang's body parts gives Xiao Chen a potential bargaining chip or weakness to exploit. The Emperor's City emerging as a third force may intervene in the Seven Demon Cities siege, possibly aiding or opposing Keke's rescue. Readers should note that Meng Degang is now furious and missing half his stone flesh, which may make him more dangerous but also more reckless.

The comic relief with Sha Polang serves to humanize the otherwise grim process of executing an Ancestral God—a reminder that even the mightiest can fall to ridicule. The Sacred Sacrifice also sets a precedent for how Xiao Chen can rapidly advance his strength.

Looking ahead, the next chapters likely focus on the confrontation at the Seven Demon Cities, with Xiao Chen, the Emperor's City, and the Otherworld powers all converging. The skeleton kings' whereabouts and their prize remain a mystery, hinting at a future surprise.
