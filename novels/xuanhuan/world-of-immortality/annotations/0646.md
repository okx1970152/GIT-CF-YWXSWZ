## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen enters the legendary Five Emperor Tower, a structure said to rival the Three Sovereigns Mirror. Inside, he discovers five stone carvings depicting the martial arts of the Five Emperors. Immersing himself in their essence, he experiences a profound enlightenment, grasping the primal movements of the Five Emperor Seal, Fist, and Sword. However, his meditation is interrupted by a terrifying female corpse who forces her way into the tower, driven by a sinister will to reach the 'Source of All Evil' sealed on the second floor. The chapter balances Xiao Chen's cultivation breakthrough with the escalating horror of the corpse's relentless assault, culminating in a desperate plan to escape the tower using the remnants of a fallen Stone Man King.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen studies the five stone carvings in the Five Emperor Tower, entering a state of unity with the Five Emperors' divine essence. He nearly masters the Five Emperor Seal, which shakes the tower, but cannot yet solidify the Five Emperor Fist and Sword. A thick iron chain from the second floor attacks him, but the carvings' light defends him. The female corpse invades the tower and repeatedly assaults the second floor, each time being grievously wounded but regenerating. The source of the 'Source of All Evil' behind the seal proves extremely dangerous. A residual soul of a Stone Man King appears, revealing that he once sought the Source of All Evil and was trapped. He explains that to escape, Xiao Chen must use the female corpse's body to break out as a brand imprint on the stone jar. Xiao Chen and the residual soul jointly enter the corpse, destroy the demonic shadow's will, and transform into a brand on the stone jar, succeeding in escaping the tower with the first layer intact.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Five Emperor Tower is a legendary artifact associated with the Five Emperors (五帝), primordial culture heroes in Chinese mythology often connected to the origins of civilization and martial arts. The stone carvings transmit their essence directly, a common xianxia trope where ancient beings leave their Dao imprints for worthy successors. The 'Source of All Evil' (万恶之源) is a concept reminiscent of primordial chaos or malevolent energy that must be sealed. The female corpse's invulnerable body and regenerative ability demonstrate a cultivation level beyond Stone Man Kings, hinting at the pinnacle of the Stone Man path. The idea of becoming a brand imprint on an artifact to escape a sealing space is a unique method of transcendence, blending the physical and metaphysical.

## Reading Guide

This chapter advances Xiao Chen's cultivation significantly, as he gains initial mastery of the Five Emperor Seal, a technique that will likely be crucial in future battles. The female corpse and the mysterious demonic shadow hint at a larger conspiracy involving the Source of All Evil. Readers should pay attention to the residual soul of the Stone Man King, as his sacrifice and knowledge may have lasting implications. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen's successful escape, but the stone jar now contains both the tower and the female corpse—setting up potential conflict when the demonic shadow's true body learns of this development. The suspense around the Source of All Evil remains unresolved, promising a major plotline ahead.
