## Chapter Overview
This chapter shifts from epic battle to tense observation. Xiao Chen infiltrates the hidden realm of the Otherworld Ancestral God Qiu Tian, who is undergoing a perilous self-cauldron transformation using the Shattering Demon, Planting God technique. Xiao Chen witnesses the struggle between the newborn divine seed and the original cauldron body—a battle of life and death. Rather than assassinate the weakened deity, Xiao Chen conceives a plan of breathtaking audacity: to usurp Qiu Tian's cultivation and use the Ancestral God's body as his own cauldron for a breakthrough. The chapter masterfully builds suspense as Xiao Chen waits for the perfect moment, balancing patience with the knowledge that even a single error could mean annihilation.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen uses the power of the broken formation diagram and the Ancestral Lord Warship to silently cross into the sealed second dark space, avoiding detection by the Ninth Heaven Half-Ancestor guard. He discovers a massive sacrificial altar where Qiu Tian lies. The Ancestral God has split into two: a newborn baby (the divine seed) struggling to emerge from an ancient bronze body (the cauldron). The self-cauldron technique requires the baby to fully merge and break the old form to achieve rebirth. Xiao Chen observes the week-long struggle, noting that both entities are critically exhausted. He resists the urge to strike early, understanding the opportune moment comes when both are at their weakest. Finally, as the baby begins to crack the cauldron, Xiao Chen activates the formation diagram, shattering the cauldron and invading the space to assert his own will as the seed. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen entering the cauldron, his fate uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Shattering Demon, Planting God technique (碎魔种神) is a legendary method created by the Demon Sect Founder. Normally, a cultivator uses another person as a 'cauldron' (鼎炉) to grow a new, more powerful self—as Yan Qingcheng once attempted with Xiao Chen. However, Qiu Tian is performing an extreme variant: using his own body as both seed and cauldron, a self-sacrificial approach that risks mutual destruction. Xiao Chen's plan to intercept this process is unprecedented; he acts as a parasite, aiming to absorb the Ancestral God's accumulated power. The hierarchical structure of the Otherworld is also highlighted: even a Ninth Heaven Half-Ancestor is treated as an ant by true Ancestral Gods, underscoring the vast power gap Xiao Chen is attempting to bridge.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's gamble will either grant him an immense leap in cultivation or destroy him utterly. The chapter ends as he enters the cauldron, leaving the outcome uncertain. Readers should watch for the results of this audacious act in the next chapter. Will Xiao Chen successfully usurp the Ancestral God's power and become a Half-Ancestor or even reach a higher realm? Also note the mention of the arena battle (角斗大赛) where Qiu Tian was scheduled to fight Ai'er—this foreshadows future conflicts and suggests that Xiao Chen's actions may disrupt the Otherworld's power balance, drawing even greater enemies.
