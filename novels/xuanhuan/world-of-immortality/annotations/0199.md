## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a masterclass in political irony. After Long Teng's dramatic public visit to Xiao Chen's residence, every major power in Heavenly Emperor City—from the beast-fighting clans to the Undying Sect—misreads the signal and descends upon Xiao Chen with invitations, gifts, and even marriage proposals. The protagonist, who was nearly besieged by enemies moments ago, suddenly becomes the most sought-after figure in the city. Yet beneath the flattery and false warmth, Xiao Chen sees through the transparent self-interest of these factions. The chapter skillfully juxtaposes the frantic courtship of the elite with Xiao Chen's reflective self-mockery, while also setting up a deeply personal conflict as Yan Qingcheng is pressured into a political union she does not want.

## Key Plot Points

Long Teng's departure leaves Xiao Chen pondering the unknown threats still lurking. Before he can act, a cascade of visitors arrives: Zhuge Ming, Hai Yuntian, Qilao, a Rigen family representative, the courtesans Ice Qin and Fire Dance, the city lord's third son, and various other factions. All clamor for his favor, offering banquets, estates, and even their daughters. The reason is twofold: first, the rumor that Long Teng—a primeval being from the Southern Wastes—has a special relationship with Xiao Chen; second, a leaked secret from the Undying Sect claims the Demon Sect Founder is still alive and linked to Xiao Chen. These two misinterpretations transform Xiao Chen from a potential target to a prized asset. Meanwhile, within the Undying Sect, Liu Qingfeng plots to secure Xiao Chen's loyalty by marrying off his disciple Yan Qingcheng to him. Yan Qingcheng vocally refuses. The chapter concludes with Hai Yunxue arriving in person to further the courtship, prompting Zhuge Ming to pull Xiao Chen aside and warn him against her charms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of "courting" a powerful ascetic is a staple of xianxia politics. Families and sects offer marriage alliances, resources, and protection in exchange for access to extraordinary talents or rare beasts. Here, Xiao Chen's value is multiplied by his association with three divine beasts: Keke, the Little Defiant Dragon, and the newly acquired Blue Dragon King. In this world, controlling even one sacred beast can elevate a family's status; three makes Xiao Chen a kingmaker. The "Demon Sect Founder" is a legendary figure from the mortal world's history, now known to be the Devil trapped on Dragon Island. The revelation that he is alive and connected to Xiao Chen sends shockwaves through the cultivation world, as the Demon Sect was once the supreme power. Additionally, the "ancient being in the Southern Wastes" (Long Teng) belongs to a lineage so old that it is treated with near-religious deference—a force not to be trifled with.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point in the political arc of the Eternal Continent. Readers should pay close attention to the contrast between public perception and reality: Xiao Chen's sudden popularity is built on layers of misunderstandings that could collapse at any moment. The introduction of Liu Qingfeng as a calculating elder within the Undying Sect adds a new layer of factional maneuvering. Yan Qingcheng's refusal to be a pawn may signal a shift in her relationship with Xiao Chen from hostility to something more nuanced. Meanwhile, Hai Yunxue's arrival and Zhuge Ming's warning foreshadow potential romantic and political complications. The coming chapters will likely test whether Xiao Chen can maintain this precarious advantage once the truth emerges.
