## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts back to the mortal world, where the grand "Hua Shan Sword Discussion" is abruptly interrupted by a kilometer‑long flying sword from a cultivator of the otherworldly realm. A thuggish but immensely powerful young man named Tianwaitian appears, casually deflecting the sword with a sneeze and a slap, revealing that the mortal world holds formidable power beneath its humble surface. The scene blends irreverent humor – Tianwaitian's lecherous mutterings about Chang'e – with escalating cosmic tension as an old cultivator from the cultivation world descends. The discussion quickly turns to the sealed Nine Provinces and the hidden dangers of awakening ancient forces. In a sudden turn, the crazed old man, a legendary figure from earlier chapters, teleports beside Xiao Chen and drags him into the fray, setting the stage for a clash between two worlds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with six cultivators fleeing in terror after being blown away by a mysterious sneeze, realizing the mortal world is far more dangerous than legends suggest. A thuggish young man, Tianwaitian, climbs Hua Shan and interacts with the crowd. The old madman and a child prodigy, Renwairen, are also present. An old cultivator from the cultivation world arrives on a kilometer‑long flying sword, threatening the gathering. Tianwaitian casually shrinks the sword with a slap, then engages in verbal sparring with the old cultivator. He reveals that the mortal world's sealed Nine Provinces contain dangers that would annihilate the cultivation world if unleashed – a warning that the seal protects both realms. The old cultivator proposes ten young‑generation duels to settle the matter. Tianwaitian and Renwairen call for their champion, but realize they lack suitable young fighters – the mortal world's best are either too old or too weak due to the seal. The crazed old man then teleports to Xiao Chen, recognizing him despite his changed appearance, and demands "repayment," forcibly dragging him into the conflict. This connects the mortal world plot directly to Xiao Chen's journey from the World of Immortality.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter references the concept of the "Nine Provinces Seal" (九州封印), which suppresses the spiritual energy and cultivation levels of the mortal world, explaining why native practitioners are weak. This is a common Xianxia trope where a sealed realm or depleted spiritual energy prevents cultivators from advancing. The "Hua Shan Sword Discussion" (华山论剑) is a direct homage to Jin Yong's wuxia novels, where martial artists gather to determine the strongest – here subverted by the arrival of true cultivators. The characters Tianwaitian (天外天) and Renwairen (人外人) play on the idiom "山外有山, 人外有人" (beyond mountains are mountains, beyond men are men), implying there is always someone stronger. Their informal, nearly thuggish demeanor contrasts with the lofty expectations of cultivators, adding a layer of subversive humor. The crazed old man's ability to see through Xiao Chen's disguise hints at a supernatural perception far beyond normal divine sense.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a pivotal shift, revealing that the mortal world is not as weak as it seemed and that the Nine Provinces seal is a central mystery. Xiao Chen's return to the mortal world and his encounter with the crazed old man set up a direct confrontation with the cultivation world's young elites. Readers should pay attention to the implications of the seal – it suggests that the mortal world was once far more powerful and may become a battlefield for ancient forces. The humorous tone of Tianwaitian and Renwairen contrasts with the gravity of the revelations, hinting that the world is larger and more interconnected than Xiao Chen imagined. The upcoming duels will likely showcase Xiao Chen's growth and force him to reveal his identity and abilities, potentially drawing the attention of the cultivation world's higher powers. Also watch for further appearances of ancestral God–related secrets.
