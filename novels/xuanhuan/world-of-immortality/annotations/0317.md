## Chapter Overview

This chapter masterfully contrasts the warmth of a familial New Year's celebration with the cold, deadly confrontation waiting beyond the village. Xiao Chen returns to his ancestral home, sharing a festive meal with his kin and the mischievous Keke, while two mysterious little girls—Linglong and Tutu—reveal that the Yellow Emperor himself foresaw danger in this ancient village. As night falls, an unnatural slumber claims all the villagers, and Xiao Chen steps out to face a coalition of enemies led by Hai Yunxue and the young prodigy Ye Tian. The chapter is a crucible of emotion and violence: a tender homecoming shattered by a brutal duel, where Xiao Chen must rely on the freshly acquired Yellow Clay Altar and the mysterious ancient lamp to survive half-ancestor intervention.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen shares a delayed New Year's dinner with his family and villagers. The two little girls, Linglong and Tutu, reveal their father, the Yellow Emperor, instructed them to find a mysterious ancient village—likely Xiao Chen's home—as a safe haven during calamities. As the meal concludes, an unnatural sleep overcomes all villagers, including the two girls, leaving only Xiao Chen and Keke awake. The ancient lamp floating beside Xiao Chen and the Yellow Clay Altar become his constant companions. Stepping outside, he faces Hai Yunxue's forces, Fang Tianqi, and Ye Tian, the latter seeking revenge for his sister Ye Jun, whom Xiao Chen killed earlier. Ye Tian unleashes the Golden Divine Bell, a legendary treasure that can trap and crush foes. Xiao Chen breaks the bell with his Supreme Hand and Reverse Dragon Seven Steps, then kills Ye Tian's physical body. However, half-ancestors intervene: the White Tiger Saint Emperor and the Three Infant Lord save Ye Tian's soul, resurrect him, and repair the bell. They declare Ye Tian immortal and destined to kill Xiao Chen in the future. Xiao Chen stands defiant on the Yellow Clay Altar, which protects him from half-ancestor attacks, and taunts them, claiming he will destroy their lineage when no half-ancestors remain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台) is a mysterious artifact that appears to protect Xiao Chen specifically against half-ancestors—supreme cultivators just below the level of Ancestral God. Its power activates only when a half-ancestor strikes, reflecting a thematic layer of fate and protection. The ancient lamp (古灯) floating beside Xiao Chen is similarly enigmatic, seemingly a part of the altar. The concept of a cultivator's soul escaping physical death is common in xianxia: Ye Tian's soul (Yuan Ying) was preserved by the Three Infant Lord, a half-ancestor, allowing instant resurrection. This reinforces the idea that killing a powerful cultivator requires destroying both body and soul. The chapter also references the Yellow Emperor (黄帝) as a historical figure who foresaw future events, tying the mortal world's mythology directly into the World of Immortality's lore. The village's inhabitants falling into a prolonged sleep without aging hints at a time-skip or protective stasis, a trope used to allow the hero to depart without worrying about family.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a recurring tension: Xiao Chen is now a known enemy of multiple half-ancestors and entire cultivation families. The Yellow Clay Altar is his only defense against such overwhelming power—but it is limited. Pay attention to how the ancient lamp and the altar function; they seem linked and may grow in importance. Ye Tian has been revived with his indestructible golden bell, promising a future rematch. Hai Yunxue continues to scheme as a persistent political and emotional antagonist. The two little girls, Linglong and Tutu, are clearly significant—children of the Yellow Emperor—and their role in the village mystery will likely unfold later. The chapter ends on a note of grim defiance: Xiao Chen vows to one day end the era of half-ancestors. With his cultivation now including the complete Heavenly Stele diagram, he is on the path to becoming a true martial artist who could challenge even the highest powers. Watch for his continued training and confrontation with the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden next.
