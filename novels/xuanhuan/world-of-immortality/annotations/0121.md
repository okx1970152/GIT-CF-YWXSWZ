## Chapter Overview

The King Ship, a ghastly vessel shaped like an upside-down skull, arrives at the shore as a bridge of death opens for boarding. The Demon facilitates the passage, and Keke cheerfully stocks the ship with fruits. Yet the chapter's emotional core shifts to a solemn farewell: ten royal dragon lineages—Storm Dragon, Lion King Dragon, Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon, Sword Dragon, and others—gather to bid goodbye to the little defiant dragon. Their affectionate gestures and the subsequent speculation about the little dragon's identity deepen the mythic atmosphere. Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng watch in awe as a new layer of Dragon Island's mysteries unfolds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the King Ship nearing the shore. Its design is macabre: an inverted skull with a crescent-shaped gap serving as the hull, exuding deathly light. A bridge of dark radiance extends to the beach, allowing boarding. The Demon urges Xiao Chen and his group to step on. Keke, now recovered from its earlier bloat, gleefully loads a mountain of fruits onto the ship. Suddenly, ten powerful dragon roars echo from behind. The ten most royal dragon lineages—including Storm Dragon, Lion King Dragon, Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon, and Sword Dragon—arrive to see off the little defiant dragon they have cherished. They nuzzle the small dragon, infusing it with radiant light, showing deep reluctance to part. The Demon, Xiao Chen, and Yan Qingcheng all speculate: could this be a young Ancestral Dragon? They quickly dismiss the idea, but the Demon notes an ancient inscription suggesting that a single dragon with unique potential would not leave with the Dragon Kings. The little dragon is clearly extraordinary. Then the dragons notice Keke's sacred tree hat. The Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon reaches for it, but Keke angrily protests. The Lion King Dragon tries to seize it by force, shattering Keke's binding art with raw strength. However, the dragons pause, recognizing that the binding technique is a Dragon Clan divine art. Keke fearlessly points at each dragon, chattering indignantly. The chapter ends on this tense, enigmatic standoff.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter highlights the hierarchy of dragon species in the novel. The ten royal lineages (暴龙, 狮王龙, 八臂恶龙, 剑龙, etc.) represent the purest bloodlines of dragon-kind on Dragon Island. Their appearance underscores the little defiant dragon's hidden value—it is not just a random orphan but a being of potential that even royal dragons honor. The speculation about a young Ancestral Dragon ties into the core mythology: any dragon can evolve into the Ancestral Dragon by overcoming ten thousand dragons, but the little dragon's singular status suggests a different path. The King Ship, resembling a skull, belongs to the deathly realm, contrasting with the vibrant golden sea and the life-filled dragons. Keke's binding art, identified as a Dragon Clan technique, reveals that her sacred tree hat—the Dragon Clan Sacred Tree—is of immense importance to dragons. Her fearless defiance hints at her own mysterious lineage, possibly linked to a primordial being that dragons respect.

## Reading Guide

This chapter plants several seeds for the arc ahead. The little defiant dragon's identity will undoubtedly become a major plot point—its potential may eclipse even the Companion Dragon Kings. Keke's sacred tree is now confirmed as a Dragon Clan treasure, setting up future conflicts with dragon-kind. The King Ship offers a deathly escape route, but its master (the Demon) is an uncertain ally. Xiao Chen now carries two extraordinary creatures: Keke and the little dragon, but also a captive Yan Qingcheng. Her jealousy and the Demon's cryptic knowledge add layers of tension. Watch for how these relationships evolve and what the Demon's true intentions are for the King Ship. The farewell scene also suggests that the dragons are making a calculated gamble by entrusting their young to outsiders.
