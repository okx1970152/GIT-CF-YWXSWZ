## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen, traveling incognito in the Great Shang capital, witnesses a confrontation between local aristocratic prodigies and visiting cultivators from the Southern Wastes and Western Frontier. After a series of humiliating defeats, Xiao Chen intervenes with a devastating display of the Complete Vessel Seal, exposing his formidable power and earning an invitation to a prestigious tea party hosted by the Third Princess. The chapter balances political tension with breathtaking martial arts, showcasing Xiao Chen's growth since leaving the island.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Yin Feng and four other nobles demanding the tower be cleared so they can host Yan Qingcheng and A Bing. A fire cultivator from the Western Frontier, Huo Niao, challenges Yin Feng but loses in a duel of Divine Fire versus Nether Fire. Then a five-on-five battle erupts: Yuwen Feng, the siblings A Shui and A Bing, Qilao, and Yan Qingcheng all engage the nobles but are soundly defeated, their pride wounded. Xiao Chen, who had been observing quietly, is targeted by Yin Feng. Using the Complete Vessel Seal, he first absorbs Yin Feng's Nether Fire into a crystal bottle, stunning the crowd. When Yin Feng unleashes a Scythe of the Dead formed from concentrated Nether Fire, Xiao Chen counters with a full-powered Vessel Seal blast that shatters the scythe, extinguishes the flames, and sends Yin Feng and a companion flying. The other nobles are shaken. Yin Feng recognizes the technique and concedes defeat, offering friendship and a position, but Xiao Chen refuses and leaves. Yin Feng then announces the Scholar-Swordsman Tea Party in three days, hoping to attract Xiao Chen. The chapter ends with the defeated Southern Wastes experts staring at Xiao Chen's retreating figure, their worldviews shaken.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the cultivation culture of the Great Shang capital, where young elites rely on skill rather than family backing (at least in public). Nether Fire and Divine Fire are opposing flame techniques; Yin Feng's Nether Fire is feared for its corrosive, dark properties, while Huo Niao's Divine Fire represents pure, burning light. The Complete Vessel Seal is a supreme art of the Forest Tribe, previously unseen by these nobles. Its ability to absorb and redirect energy makes it a formidable defensive and offensive tool—a classic xianxia technique that demonstrates Xiao Chen's mastery of diverse traditions. The invitation to the Scholar-Swordsman Tea Party echoes historical literati gatherings in Chinese culture, where martial prowess and scholarly refinement are both celebrated. Such tea parties serve as neutral grounds for networking and political maneuvering among young elites.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up Xiao Chen's growing reputation in the Central Plains. Note that he intentionally uses a technique from the Forest Tribe, not his own signature methods like the Four Killing Hands or Undying Wings, suggesting he is still hiding his true identity. The tea party promises further intrigue and potential alliances. Watch for the Third Princess's role and whether Xiao Chen's disguise will hold. The defeat of the Southern Wastes experts foreshadows the power hierarchy in the Great Shang capital—even Yan Qingcheng and Qilao, once dominant on Dragon Island, are now outclassed. Xiao Chen's demonstration positions him as a major player, and his refusal to engage with the nobles hints at a deeper caution. The coming tea party will likely introduce new rivals and allies.
