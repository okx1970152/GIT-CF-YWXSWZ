## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a monumental turning point as the Devil Plains become the stage for an apocalyptic convergence of ancient powers. The Twenty-Four War Swords are manifesting, and the long-sealed Paradise Lost world stirs, drawing legendary figures from across the Eternal Continent and beyond. Keke decides to pursue the Paradise Lost world, driven by its childlike conviction that it rightfully belongs to her. Meanwhile, a bloody four-nation war serves as a sacrificial backdrop, and young elites begin to form strategic alliances. The chapter blends epic battle scenes with intimate character moments, setting the stage for a world-shaking conflict where nothing is as it seems.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Keke learning about the Paradise Lost world from King Chu Jiang in the underworld. She becomes obsessed with claiming it, believing it is hers by right. On the Devil Plains, the Twenty-Four War Swords are nearing full materialization, their sword-qi now forming solid pillars. The demoness Yaoyao appears, proposing an alliance among younger-generation experts, including herself, Jue Dao, Xiao Xuanxuan, and the Roman Sama. She reveals that the Paradise Lost treasure may be tied to the underworld and Keke's abilities. Meanwhile, the four empires—Fan, Great Zhou, Rome, and Great Shang—engage in a devastating war, with the Grand Formation claiming millions of lives. On the third night after the war, all the souls vanish mysteriously. Legendary figures begin to arrive: Kong Xuan, the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, Tongtian's four swords, and finally Primordial and the Buddha himself. The chapter ends on a note of cosmic anticipation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter draws heavily on Chinese mythological and xianxia tropes. The Paradise Lost world is a concept of a fallen celestial realm, echoing the Buddhist idea of a lost pure land. The Twenty-Four War Swords are artifacts of prehistoric power, linked to the Otherworld civilization. The Grand Formation is a classic sacrificial array that uses blood and souls to unlock seals. The appearance of deities like Primordial (Yuan Shi), Tongtian, Kong Xuan, and the Buddha integrates characters from the Investiture of the Gods and Buddhist cosmology. The war itself mirrors historical conflicts but is reframed as a deliberate sacrifice to awaken divine treasures. The demoness Yaoyao's flirtatious yet manipulative dialogue is emblematic of the 'demonic seductress' archetype in xianxia, combining danger with allure.
