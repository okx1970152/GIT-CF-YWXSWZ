## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a masterclass in tactical brutality as Xiao Chen, still in his yellow-diamond skeleton form, turns the Yaksha King's overwhelming offensive into a one-sided slaughter. The Yaksha King arrives with god-like authority, instantly killing a foreign king to demonstrate his dominance, but his attempts to breach the Paradise Lost are futile. Xiao Chen, protected by Keke's invincible realm, systematically executes opponents one by one, taunting the Yaksha King with the memory of their previous encounter. The emotional core of the chapter lies in the seamless partnership between Xiao Chen and Keke, their bond transcending death and form. The tone is grim and epic, blending horror with a cold, martial beauty as blood rains across a shattered landscape.

## Key Plot Points
The Yaksha King appears alone, his presence shaking heaven and earth; he instantly kills a foreign king who dared to express discontent, tearing him apart with his bare hands to assert absolute authority. Xiao Chen, watching from within the Paradise Lost, casually remarks that he had previously torn out half the Yaksha King's heart, enraging him. The Yaksha King attacks the Paradise Lost but fails entirely, and he forces nine other kings to join him in a coalition assault. Xiao Chen, in response, waits for his power to fully recover and then strikes, using the Divine Seal of the Flower to kill one king, the Immovable Wisdom King Seal to crush another, and the Slay God technique to dismember a third. As more kings press the attack, Xiao Chen unleashes his Space Rend divine art, splitting a fourth king in twain. He then uses the Inverse Dragon Seven Steps, killing the Zombie King and another opportunistic king with the seventh step. Keke attempts to drag the Yaksha King into the Paradise Lost but he barely breaks free; however, Xiao Chen seizes the moment to cut off his arm with a war sword.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases the supreme defense of the Paradise Lost, a realm that nullifies all divine abilities and makes its owner nearly invincible against those of equal or lesser cultivation. The techniques Xiao Chen employs are drawn from his unique blend of martial and divine arts: the Divine Seal of the Flower manifests a phantom that scatters petals-like blades; the Immovable Wisdom King Seal summons a towering avatar that crushes enemies; the Inverse Dragon Seven Steps is a legendary dragon combat art that tramples foes with escalating power. The Yaksha King is at the Perfected Man realm (至人境界), a stage above Fish-Leap where one's body and soul are nearly indestructible. The chapter also reinforces the theme of using terrain and resources strategically: Xiao Chen conserves his strength within the Paradise Lost and strikes only when fully recovered, a tactic born from the harsh lessons of the Death World.

## Reading Guide
Readers should pay close attention to the political dynamics among the foreign kings: the Yaksha King's arrogance has alienated even powerful allies like the Asura King and Golden Lion King, who hold back during the assault. This sets the stage for future fractures among the Hundred Races. Xiao Chen's collection of corpses from his kills suggests he is stockpiling life essence for his own flesh-and-blood rebirth, a major milestone foreshadowed since his time in the Death World. The wound inflicted on the Yaksha King—the loss of an arm—will likely fuel a personal vendetta, ensuring further confrontations. Meanwhile, Keke's growing confidence and mastery of the Paradise Lost hint at even greater feats to come. The next chapter may see Xiao Chen exploiting the chaos or the Yaksha King regrouping with reinforcements.
