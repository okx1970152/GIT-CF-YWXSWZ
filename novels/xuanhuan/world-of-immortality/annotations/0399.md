## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's journey through the Death World. Having achieved full flesh-and-blood rebirth at the Perfected Man realm, he unleashes overwhelming power by hurling a two-kilometer mountain to annihilate Giant City, instantly killing the Hundred Battle King and Beast King. Yet his true test comes from a silent, white-bone skeleton wielding the legendary Immortal-Slaying Formation and four swords of Tongtian. The ensuing battle spans over a day and destroys multiple cities, forcing Xiao Chen to push his newly reborn body and martial arts to their limits. More than a combat spectacle, this confrontation yields a chilling revelation about the nature of half-ancestors—that the feared Tongtian may never have truly existed, only a projection shaped by mortal faith and the Death World's abyssal depths.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's first act of dominance is to pulverize Giant City with a two-kilometer rock, instantly burying it and creating a massive basin. The Three Kings—Giant King, Hundred Battle King, and Beast King—emerge from the rubble, joined by a calm white-bone skeleton carrying four swords and a formation diagram. Sha Polang identifies the Giant King holding the broken sword of their ally, the Yellow Diamond skeleton, confirming his death. Xiao Chen, with cold efficiency, slays Hundred Battle King and Beast King in a single blow each, forcing the Giant King to flee toward the skeleton. The skeleton, unmoved, reveals it is not Tongtian Jiaozhu but a projection born from the Death World's deepest abyss and collective human belief. Xiao Chen engages the skeleton in a prolonged battle, using the Dragon Clan Sacred Tree, a War Sword, the Eight Origin Sounds, and the Four Loose Techniques. The Immortal-Slaying Formation proves immensely powerful, but Xiao Chen eventually neutralizes it by binding the formation diagram with the Sacred Tree. In the end, the skeleton fades away, inviting Xiao Chen to the ancient demon city in the Death Continent's core. Xiao Chen cannot prevent its escape, leaving the mystery of Tongtian unresolved.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter directly invokes the mythos of the Immortal-Slaying Formation from the Ming dynasty novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods). Tongtian Jiaozhu, the head of the Jie Sect, was one of the supreme Daoist sages who wielded the four swords—Immortal-Slaying, Immortal-Destroying, Immortal-Trapping, and Immortal-Severing—to command the deadliest formation in existence. In *World of Immortality*, the formation appears as a legacy of the pseudo-god era, a remnant of the half-ancestor battles. The white-bone skeleton's confession that Tongtian never truly existed but was a projection of mortal faith combined with a Death World abyssal power is a core theme of the novel: the illusory nature of many supreme beings. The Perfected Man realm (至人), which Xiao Chen has now reached, is the stage above Fish-Leap (鱼跃) and signifies a being who has perfected their physical form and consciousness, approaching the level of the ancient Ancestral Gods. The Death World's depths are hinted to contain even greater secrets and more authentic powers.

## Reading Guide

This battle is a capstone for Xiao Chen's achievements in the Death World. His ability to face and nearly defeat a being wielding Tongtian's signature array signals that he is ready to transition back to the Nine Provinces. However, the skeleton's escape and invitation to the ancient demon city plant seeds for future exploration. Readers should watch for the deeper implications of the projection theory: if Tongtian can be an illusion, what about the other slain half-ancestors? Keke's presence throughout the fight, though still sidelined, suggests it may play a larger role in battles to come. The chapter also closes a major arc for the Eastern Death World factions, as the Three Kings are dead and the balance of power shifts. Xiao Chen's next moves will likely involve confronting the true sovereign of the Death Continent's abyss.
