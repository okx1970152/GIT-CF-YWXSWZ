## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, decisive victory for Xiao Chen as he single-handedly dismantles the Revenge Alliance, proving that his reputation as a relentless, cold-blooded fighter is well earned. The battle is not merely a physical confrontation but a statement of intent: Xiao Chen will not be hunted, and he will meet aggression with overwhelming force. The tone is unflinchingly violent and strategically cold, reflecting Xiao Chen's transformation from a desperate survivor into a dominant predator on Dragon Island. Beyond the carnage, the chapter unveils a hidden antagonist from an unexpected quarter, setting the stage for deeper, more personal conflicts that reach into the Undying Alliance.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen advances through the mountain forest, his overwhelming pressure causing the very ground to shake. He targets Guluo, the founder of the Revenge Alliance, and dispatches him with a single devastating blow, cleaving his body in half. This death marks the symbolic end of the alliance. However, the battle continues against the remaining two core members: Li Lingfeng, a Spiritist, and Carter, a Spellmaster. They unleash a barrage of lightning, light blades, and dark netherfire, while Li Lingfeng uses illusion techniques. Xiao Chen defends with the Big Dipper Sealing Divinity, a starry light screen that absorbs attacks and resists mental interference. When he raises his power from Mortal-Shedding Fourth Heaven to Fifth Heaven, the fight ends instantly—he impales Carter and bisects Li Lingfeng with radiant sword-light. The watching cultivators erupt in shock and awe.

But Xiao Chen is not finished. He walks deeper into the valley, pursuing the last surviving member of the alliance, whom he sensed was different. That person turns out to be Lande, the proud golden-haired Spellmaster previously seen with Yan Qingcheng. Lande had disguised himself with dyed hair and stained skin, hiding his identity. The two engage in a fierce battle, with Lande using high-speed aerial bombardment. Xiao Chen matches him blow for blow, and when Lande becomes overconfident, Xiao Chen unleashes his full Mortal-Shedding Fifth Heaven power, dragging him down from the sky with a devastating eruption of destruction light.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of a single practitioner challenging an entire alliance is a powerful trope in Xianxia, symbolizing the ultimate assertion of individual strength over collective power. Xiao Chen's use of the Big Dipper Sealing Divinity, a technique that manifests seven star-points into a protective light screen, draws on the mythological significance of the Big Dipper (北斗) in Chinese cosmology as a source of cosmic order and martial power. This technique is not merely defensive; it absorbs and redirects energy, reflecting the protagonist's strategic depth.

The disguise used by Lande—dyeing his distinctive golden hair with charcoal ash and staining his skin with plant juice—highlights the pragmatic, resourceful nature of cultivators in this world. Appearance can be a weapon or a vulnerability. Lande's willingness to hide his identity suggests he is operating independently or under orders that require subtlety, adding a layer of political intrigue to the island's conflicts.

The realm divisions, specifically the jump from Fourth to Fifth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding, demonstrate a significant power gap. In Xianxia cultivation systems, each sub-realm brings a qualitative increase in strength, making higher-tier cultivators almost untouchable to those below. Xiao Chen's decision to conceal his true realm until the critical moment is a classic tactical move, maximizing the element of surprise.

## Reading Guide

This chapter reshapes the power dynamics on Dragon Island. With the Revenge Alliance annihilated, Xiao Chen has sent a clear message to all factions: he is not to be trifled with. However, the revelation that Lande—a key member of Yan Qingcheng's Undying Alliance—was the hidden hand behind the ambush raises serious questions. Is Lande acting alone, or with Yan Qingcheng's approval? This sets up a potential direct confrontation between Xiao Chen and the Undying Alliance, one of the island's most formidable groups.

Readers should pay attention to the reactions of the onlooking alliances in the next chapters. Some may seek to ally with Xiao Chen; others may fear his rising power. Additionally, Lande's defeat is not a death—he is dragged down, but his fate is left open. Will he return, humiliated and vengeful? Finally, Xiao Chen's demonstration of the Big Dipper technique and his strategic acumen suggest he is growing into a true powerhouse. His next moves, whether to consolidate gains or pursue deeper mysteries, will be critical.
