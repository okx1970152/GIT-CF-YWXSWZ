## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, mythic confrontation between Xiao Chen and the Otherworld Ancestral God Babra, a remnant soul possessing his original bone body and wielding the legendary Great Destruction War Spear and Immortal Shield. Xiao Chen is pushed to his absolute limits, suffering severe injuries and near-defeat despite wielding the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, twenty-seven War Swords, and the Six Heavenly Steles. The battle showcases the terrifying power of a true Ancestral God even in a degraded state, and Xiao Chen's desperate struggle forces a profound internal awakening. The chapter's emotional arc moves from crushing disadvantage to a miraculous reversal, ending with the absurd yet triumphant flight of Babra, terrified by the manifestation of three hundred sixty-five shadow deities from Xiao Chen's divinized acupoints.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Babra locked in a dire struggle, the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree pressing against the Great Destruction War Spear while a strand of Ancestral God essence from the tree pins the blood-red spear. Babra mocks Xiao Chen, claiming his power is insufficient, and unleashes the spear's full might, sending Xiao Chen flying with broken ribs and severe internal injuries. Xiao Chen retaliates by summoning six Heavenly Steles, shattering Babra's spear and forcing him back, but cannot sustain the technique. Babra reforms his weapon and continues his assault, driving Xiao Chen to the ground repeatedly. In a last-ditch effort, Xiao Chen applies drops of Ancestral God blood to the Sacred Tree, awakening the Ancestral God essence within it, and uses it to blast through Babra's defenses. Babra then reveals the Immortal Shield, an artifact said to be indestructible even by Fuxi and the War Ancestor, and the battle turns again. Xiao Chen, on the verge of collapse, pushes his Profound Art to its limit, causing his three hundred sixty-five divinized acupoints to erupt with blinding light. A great deity appears behind him, controlling the War Swords, and together they shatter the Immortal Shield and wound Babra, ultimately tearing off his left calf bone. As Xiao Chen presses his advantage, Babra suddenly sees three hundred sixty-five shadowy figures looming behind Xiao Chen and flees in terror. After the battle, Xiao Chen realizes only the first deity had real combat power; the other three hundred sixty-four were empty phantoms that scared Babra away. He spends half a month escaping the ancient formation and emerges to find cultivators flocking toward the rumored 'most sinister place' in the Soul World, which appears to be the upstream of the underground corpse river.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter features the culmination of Xiao Chen's unique cultivation path: the full divinization of all three hundred sixty-five major acupoints, which corresponds to the number of days in a solar year and is considered a complete microcosm of the human body in traditional Chinese medicine and Daoist alchemy. The manifestation of three hundred sixty-five deities echoes the concept of 'inner gods' or 'body gods' found in Daoist internal alchemy, where each bodily organ and acupoint houses a divine spirit. The Ancestral God Babra's weapons—the Great Destruction War Spear and the Immortal Shield—are named in a style that emphasizes their absolute, world-ending attributes, common in xianxia for weapons of supreme beings. The 'most sinister place' of the Soul World alludes to the dangerous depths of the death realm, a recurring theme in the novel's cosmology. Babra's exclamation in an 'otherworld language' ('Qiugelema') is a humorous touch, illustrating that even transcendent beings have vulgar moments.

## Reading Guide

The chapter marks a major turning point in Xiao Chen's power progression: his acupoint system now seems capable of manifesting actual combat entities, though the full potential of this ability remains untapped. The appearance of three hundred sixty-four empty phantoms alongside one powerful deity suggests that Xiao Chen's cultivation still has a long way to go before he can command a true legion of god-like avatars. The reference to the 'most sinister place' upstream of the corpse river teases a new location fraught with danger and possibly containing treasures or beings that even Ancestral Gods fear. Readers should pay attention to how Xiao Chen's Profound Art evolves from this point, and whether he can learn to fully activate the three hundred sixty-five deities. The temporary alliance with Babra's severed leg bone may also have future significance, as the Ancestral God's bones are priceless materials. The next chapters will likely explore the Soul World's dark secrets and Xiao Chen's continued growth toward Half-Ancestor level.
