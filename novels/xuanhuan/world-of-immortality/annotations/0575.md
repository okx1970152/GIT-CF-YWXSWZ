## Chapter Overview

Chapter 575 is a whirlwind of celestial-scale deception and narrow escapes. Fresh from the chaos of the Sun Star Palace, Xiao Chen is immediately ambushed by a mysterious giant hand—one of the Celestial Realm's supreme powers. With the wily Old Goat at his side, he must outrun not one but six kings and war ancestors, each capable of crushing worlds. The chapter masterfully blends frantic action with shrewd trickery: the Old Goat diverts the pursuers by pinning the theft of the Heaven-Defying Battle Treasure on the Lotus King, then doubles back to snatch the prize from under their noses using a forbidden formation. Yet the climax is a masterful anticlimax—the legendary treasure turns out to be nothing more than a thumb-sized stone key, leaving both characters and readers stunned. The emotional tone oscillates between desperate flight, comedic banter, and genuine bewilderment, all while deepening the mystery of the Celestial Realm's ultimate secrets.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's escape from the Sun Star Palace is thwarted by a colossal hand that descends from the sky, nearly crushing him. The Old Goat identifies the attacker as one of the Celestial Realm's hidden giants. As more kings—the Stone Corpse, Lishi Beast, and a blood-colored war ancestor—converge, the Old Goat proclaims that the Heaven-Defying Battle Treasure has already been taken by the Lotus King. This lie scatters the pursuers, allowing Xiao Chen and the Old Goat to flee momentarily. But the Stone Corpse and Lishi Beast are not fooled; they continue the chase, forcing the duo to sprint toward the Lotus King's domain, where a real battle has erupted over the treasure. In the confusion, the Old Goat's earlier planted formation activates, teleporting the treasure into his hands. He and Xiao Chen dive underground, hide with Pangu's stone token, and escape detection. When they finally crack open the treasure's chaotic stone shell, they find a plain stone key—no power, no inscriptions, utterly ordinary. The Old Goat, crestfallen, recalls a memory from eons past: he once saw two stone man kings playing chess, and the keys they used were identical to this one. The chapter ends with the implication that the key is meant to unlock something of immense significance, but no clues remain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter illuminates the hierarchy and politics of the Celestial Realm—the highest plane in the World of Immortality. The 'kings' (王者) and 'war ancestors' (战者) are beings who have either taken the final step of the Stone Man path or returned from ancient death as 'heaven-defying warriors'. Their incarnations alone can pulverize worlds; their true bodies are rarely deployed. The 'Heaven-Defying Battle Treasure' is a legendary artifact said to grant a breakthrough to any cultivator who obtains it. Yet its revelation as a stone key is a classic xianxia subversion: ultimate power often comes in the most humble form. The stone key motif echoes Chinese mythological treasures like the 'key of the Yellow Emperor' or the 'stone lock of the Kunlun Mountains', which unlock hidden realms or supreme inheritances. The Old Goat's recollection of stone man kings playing chess with such keys ties into the Daoist symbolism of chess as a metaphor for cosmic strategy and fate.

## Reading Guide

The stone key is now a central mystery: what door does it open? Readers should note the Old Goat's fragmented memories—they hint at a deeper connection between the key and the primordial stone man kings. His claim that he will 'truly return' in three thousand years suggests his real body is dormant, and his current form is a projection. The Celestial Realm's political landscape is unstable: six kings have shown interest, and the Lotus King, Stone Corpse, and Lishi Beast remain active. The key may be the linchpin for an upcoming power shift. Keep an eye on the Old Goat's true identity—his boastful manner and sudden recollection imply he may be far more than a damaged war ancestor. Xiao Chen now possesses a means to potentially unlock a sealed realm or inheritance, which will likely draw even more dangerous enemies. The next chapters may involve deciphering the key's purpose or seeking the location of the corresponding lock.
