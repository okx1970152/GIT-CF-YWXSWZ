## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the narrative from wilderness survival to urban political intrigue, as Xiao Chen attends a lavish banquet hosted by the Mande family in Heavenly Emperor City. The social gathering serves as a stage for character introductions and subtle power plays, most notably the encounter between Xiao Chen and Qilao—Kanasi's infamous elder brother, known as "the Pervert" for his savage combat style. What begins as a tense negotiation over past grievances evolves into a profound discussion about Lan Nuo, the legendary prodigy from the Mortal World. The chapter culminates in a hidden spiritual duel that reshapes the garden, revealing Qilao's remarkable prowess and Xiao Chen's unyielding spirit. The aesthetic balances opulent scenery with the quiet violence of a battle fought entirely within the mind.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen attends the Mande family banquet with his group, where he is confronted by Kanasi, who complains to her elder brother Qilao about Xiao Chen's past treatment of her. Qilao, however, does not immediately retaliate; instead, he draws Xiao Chen aside for a private conversation. This strategic withdrawal dismays other young nobles like Hofman and Lidongbo, who had hoped for a public conflict. Qilao reveals that he once glimpsed Lan Nuo in Heavenly Emperor City a year ago and has been captivated ever since. He asks Xiao Chen to recount her history. Xiao Chen narrates Lan Nuo's story coldly: her early mastery, near-fatal cultivation of the Divine Guide, self-sealing in ice for thirteen years, and miraculous rebirth. The tale deepens Qilao's admiration. After the exchange, Qilao proposes a silent duel of divine consciousness. The two engage in a fierce spiritual battle that silently destroys much of the garden—pulverizing flowerbeds, cracking the stone bridge, and sinking the ground by ten meters. Neither gains an obvious advantage. Qilao, a Spiritist, is surprised that Xiao Chen's spiritual power rivals the Spirit-Treasury realm. The duel ends inconclusively, but Qilao acknowledges Xiao Chen's strength. Onlookers react with disappointment or awe. Dugu Jianmo appears briefly, his mere presence silencing a boastful young woman. The chapter reinforces Xiao Chen's growing reputation and sets up future interactions with Qilao and the Mande family.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter features a spiritual duel (神识之战), a form of combat where cultivators project their divine consciousness to attack the opponent's mind. Such battles are exceptionally dangerous: a lapse can result in mental destruction or a vegetative state. Qilao is identified as a Spiritist (灵士), a practitioner with innate supernatural abilities—here, exceptionally powerful divine sense. The distinction between Spiritists and standard cultivators is important: Spiritists often have advantages in mental arts but may lack physical toughness. Lan Nuo's story exemplifies the trope of self-sealing to overcome a crisis—a method where a cultivator freezes themselves in a state of suspended animation, often for decades, to wait for external conditions to change or for a breakthrough to occur naturally. Her thirteen-year ice burial and subsequent youth are classic elements of xianxia transcendence. The mention of the Divine Guide (神引) technique implies a supremely rare cultivation method that can grant immense power but carries extreme risks.

## Reading Guide

Qilao's interest in Lan Nuo introduces a potential romantic subplot, but more importantly, it establishes that Lan Nuo's legend has spread across the Eternal Continent. Readers should watch for how her reputation affects Xiao Chen's standing. The spiritual duel demonstrates that Xiao Chen's divine consciousness is unusually strong—possibly tied to his unique acupoint cultivation. This will likely become a key advantage in future confrontations. The chapter also plants seeds of enmity with Hofman and other young nobles, who are eager to see Xiao Chen fall. Dugu Jianmo's cameo reinforces the dangerous cast of characters in Heavenly Emperor City. The upcoming beast-fighting tournament and the political maneuvering among families like the Mande and the prince's court suggest that Xiao Chen's time in the city will be anything but peaceful. Pay attention to how alliances shift after this display of strength.
