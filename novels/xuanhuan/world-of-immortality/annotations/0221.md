## Chapter Overview
After three quiet years in the Pure Land, Xiao Chen sets out once again into the vastness of the Eternal Continent. This chapter marks a transitional moment, bridging his isolation with the return to the wider world. The tone shifts from reflective nostalgia to the bustling energy of the Central Plains, where news of the Heavenly Palace of the Ancestral God Youchao stirs the cultivator community. The appearance of a decayed Taoist named Shen Xibao—claiming descent from the legendary Shen Gongbao—adds a layer of ominous humor. Xiao Chen observes quietly, his analytical mind already weighing the dangers and opportunities of the coming events.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen leaves the Pure Land on foot, trekking through the Southern Wastes and into the Central Plains. He passes through ancient roads and legendary sites, reflecting on the history of this land. Upon entering the Great Shang Kingdom, he reaches a bustling border city and overhears discussions about the Heavenly Palace of Youchao, which has been open for three years. Legendary figures such as Kong Xuan and Lord Tongtian are said to be moving to breach its final barrier. A decayed Taoist named Shen Xibao appears, claiming to be a descendant of the infamous Shen Gongbao. With the eerie phrase 'Dao you qing liu bu', he compels a crowd of cultivators to follow him, demonstrating a power that seems to bend reality. Xiao Chen, intrigued and cautious, decides to join the procession, recognizing that where such forces converge, both peril and opportunity abound.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter draws heavily on the mythology of Fengshen Yanyi (Investiture of the Gods). Shen Gongbao is a notorious figure from that epic, known for his ability to bring misfortune and his phrase 'Dao you qing liu bu', which he used to lure countless immortals to their doom. His descendant Shen Xibao inherits both the name and the cursed persuasion skill. The mention of Kong Xuan—a peacock spirit with the Five-Colored Divine Light—and Lord Tongtian—a supreme Daoist sage—places the Heavenly Palace at the center of cosmic-scale events. The Ancestral God Youchao is a primordial culture hero who taught humanity to build shelters. Such a palace would contain treasures of incalculable value, explaining the gathering of the most powerful beings in the world.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major new arc centered on the Heavenly Palace of Youchao. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen navigates the chaotic convergence of legends, from the decayed Taoist's uncanny influence to the impending clash of demigods and sages. The reappearance of Kong Xuan and Lord Tongtian signals that the stakes are rising far beyond mortal conflicts. Xiao Chen's decision to follow the procession—despite his usual caution—hints at both his need for growth and the pull of destiny. The next chapters will likely introduce new allies and enemies, as well as deeper lore about the Ancestral Gods.
