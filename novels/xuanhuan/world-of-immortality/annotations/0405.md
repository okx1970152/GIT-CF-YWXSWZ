## Chapter Overview

This transitional chapter bridges Xiao Chen's adventures in the Eternal Continent with his long-awaited return to the Nine Provinces. It opens with an explosive clash between Keke and the legendary Fanyin Seal, showcasing the white beast's evolved combat prowess with the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree and Paradise Lost. The chapter then pivots to Xiao Chen's brutal and decisive duel with the dragon king Long Teng, a vendetta that has festered for years. After consolidating his position in Heavenly Emperor City through cold political maneuvering—accepting the submission of former enemies—Xiao Chen sets course for the mortal world of the Nine Provinces. A brief interlude in the Death World introduces seven young Divine Tribe warriors, whose irreverent energy and naive bloodlust add a surprising note of levity before the final journey home. The tone swings from awe-inspiring artifact combat to savage dragon-slaying, then to wry political negotiation and finally to the humorous enthusiasm of youthful explorers.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen is challenged almost immediately upon appearing in the skies above Heavenly Emperor City, as the Chan Sect's Fanyin Seal hurls itself at him like an entire mountain. Keke intercepts it with the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, blocking the sacred artifact and then absorbing the energy of thirteen divine swords that form a demon-subduing formation. The Fanyin Seal's master, evidently a Chan Sect expert, withdraws after Keke dissolves the swords within Paradise Lost. This display instantly reestablishes Xiao Chen's terrifying reputation. Shortly after, Long Teng of the Southern Wastes dragon clan arrives to confront Xiao Chen. Long Teng, who had once toyed with Xiao Chen as a pawn, is now hopelessly outmatched. Xiao Chen shatters both of Long Teng's dragon horns, then obliterates his body entirely using the Hand of the Supreme Being and a heavenly stele manifestation, leaving no trace. The power gap is absolute. The Hai and Ligen families, having participated in earlier attempts on Xiao Chen's life, send messengers bearing the heads of their own elders and half-gods as a gesture of unconditional surrender. Xiao Chen dismisses them without audience but tacitly accepts their submission. He then gathers his allies—Liu Mu, Niu Ren, and the seductive Yao Yao—and announces his departure to the Nine Provinces. Before leaving the Eternal Continent, he makes a brief trip to the Death World to collect seven young Divine Tribe warriors who will train in the mortal world. Their arrival outside the Dead City is marked not by solemnity but by their excited and mischievous plans to hunt exotic races for food, promising a chaotic future in the Nine Provinces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Fanyin Seal is a legendary artifact from the Chan Sect, classically wielded by the disciple Chengzi to crush enemies like a mountain. Its appearance here signals that sect-level powers of the Eternal Continent are taking interest in Xiao Chen's activities. The Seven-Colored Sacred Tree and Paradise Lost are revealed to be of an even higher artifact grade, capable of absorbing and destroying lesser divine treasures by stripping their spiritual essence. This detail underscores Keke's mysterious origin and its deepening connection to primal divine forces. The battle between Xiao Chen and Long Teng demonstrates the supremacy of the Eight Origin Sounds over dragon clan techniques; the 'Weng' syllable, derived from the Yellow-Clay Altar, directly counters the dragon's 'Dragon Chant.' The subsequent offering of enemy heads by the Hai and Ligen families is a classic xianxia trope of fatalistic surrender: when a cultivator's power surpasses a clan's ability to resist, self-mutilation of the offending members is the only path to survival. The Divine Tribe's unique biology—infants sleeping for over a century before awakening—mirrors the 'sleeping giant' concept found in Chinese myth, where delayed growth often signifies immense latent potential.

## Reading Guide

This chapter resets the stage for the Nine Provinces arc. Xiao Chen enters the mortal world not as the charismatic hero of old, but as a Yellow Diamond skeleton—visibly changed, colder, and more ruthless. The young Divine Tribe warriors are clearly set up as both comic relief and future powerhouses, their irreverent attitudes promising friction with the established powers of the Hundred Races. Their casual mentions of hunting Saint Dragons, Demon Apes, and Angels hint at specific future conflicts, and readers should keep track of these boasts—they may foreshadow key battles. The political debts incurred by the Hai and Ligen families may prove useful or dangerous later. Long Teng's death also solidifies the Southern Wastes dragon clan as a hostile faction; the three young dragon kings who chose not to intervene on Long Teng's behalf may reappear as either allies or neutral forces. The Chan Sect is now actively hostile, and Xiao Chen's cultivation path toward flesh-and-blood rebirth remains incomplete. Watch for how his skeleton form affects his interactions in the Nine Provinces, where the Hundred Races now openly vie for dominance.
