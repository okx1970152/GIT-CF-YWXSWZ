## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen, the Little Defiant Dragon, the Nine-Headed Serpent, and Sha Polang gazing upon a colossal black demon city suspended above a sea of mist. The city radiates an oppressive, ancient malevolence, and to their horror, they see the corpses of Old Farmer and Mo Xiaowang—two heroic half-ancestors who fought alongside them—hanging from the city gates like trophies. A stone statue of a small white beast, identical to Keke, floats nearby, but Xiao Chen's piercing golden eyes reveal the truth: it is not the little divine beast but a petrified ancestor of her cursed Heaven-Defying Clan, a being that failed in the Final Step and was drained of essence long ago. From within the city, a seductive yet terrifying female voice speaks—she is the True Wood Female Sage, who claims the title of "Ancestral Reborn Female Sage King," a being unmatched under heaven. This chapter sets the stage for a clash of titans, exploring the weight of sacrifice, the nature of ultimate power, and the strategic patience required to survive in a war against beings who have lived for multiple civilization cycles.

## Key Plot Points

- Xiao Chen and his companions discover a demon city that exudes overwhelming evil energy, with four war swords mounted on its gates, each radiating killing intent. The city is built atop a sea of black mist, suggesting it is a weapon refined through countless eons.
- The corpses of Old Farmer and Mo Xiaowang are displayed on the city walls, a deliberate provocation that ignites Xiao Chen's fury. However, he suppresses his rage and analyses the situation coldly.
- A stone statue of a white beast, indistinguishable from Keke in form, rises above the city. Xiao Chen's golden eyes—a divine art that peers into the essence of things—see that it is not Keke but an ancient member of the Heaven-Defying Clan who attempted the Final Step of the Stone Man path and failed, leaving her body petrified.
- The female sage identifies herself as "True Wood Female Sage," claiming she was known as the reborn ancestor unparalleled among her generation. She reveals that she is gathering corpses to extract Eternal Light, a substance formed from the death of ten or more ancestral gods, and she seeks Eternal Source to complete her own evolution.
- Xiao Chen, despite his own immense combat power and the strength of his allies, recognizes that the female sage's power is on par with the Otherworld's supreme ancestral gods, and a direct assault would be folly.
- Using his mastery of the Eight-Aspect Worlds, Xiao Chen evades her spatial locks and leads the group away, deciding to retreat to the Devil Plain in the World of Immortality to collect more Heavenly Marks before launching a decisive counter-strike.
- The chapter ends with Xiao Chen stating that he intends to dismantle one or two demon cities in the near future, signaling that the confrontation has only been postponed, not avoided.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The Stone Man Path is a legendary and often self-destructive cultivation route where the practitioner's body gradually petrifies, gaining near-indestructibility at the cost of losing flesh and blood. Taking the Final Step—ascending the ninety-nine stone steps—is the ultimate goal, requiring vast resources and often the sacrifice of countless lives.
- **Eternal Light** is a rare substance formed when at least ten ancestral gods die and their souls condense into a single, radiant beam. It can be used to forge supreme weapons or to nourish a cultivator's transformation. **Eternal Source** is an even purer form, produced when multiple Eternal Lights merge; it is said to be capable of elevating a being to the level of an supreme ancestral god.
- **Heavenly Marks** are divine imprints left by Heavenly Steles. Xiao Chen already possesses several, and each mark enhances his connection to the primordial stelae, granting him access to lost techniques and amplifying his combat power. He needs two more to reach a critical threshold.
- The **Devil Plain** is a location in the World of Immortality rich in history. A Heavenly Stele once stood there, and it was where the martial imprint, the skeletons of Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, and others first emerged. It is also where Keke's Paradise Lost once appeared.

## Reading Guide

- This chapter introduces a formidable antagonist in the True Wood Female Sage. Her power is such that Xiao Chen, even after millennia of cultivation and countless victories, cannot challenge her directly. This sets a clear power ceiling and raises the stakes for the coming arcs.
- The revelation that Keke's stone statue is an ancestor of her clan deepens the mystery around the Heaven-Defying Clan. Readers should note how this might connect to Keke's own potential and the nature of her curse.
- Xiao Chen's strategic retreat and decision to gather Heavenly Marks foreshadows a future confrontation where he will leverage every resource at his disposal. Pay attention to the number of marks he collects; each one brings him closer to a realm where he can directly contend with stone beings.
- The return to the Devil Plain rekindles memories of key events from earlier arcs (the three skeleton kings, the martial imprint, the Buddha's fall). This could signal a convergence of plotlines.
- The mention of "one or two demon cities" suggests that Xiao Chen's next actions may involve attacking other strongholds, possibly gaining new allies or treasures in the process.
