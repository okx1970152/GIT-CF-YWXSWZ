## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the setting from the wild Southern Wastes to the grand city of Heavenly Emperor City. Xiao Chen, having won a gambling match on Beast-Fighting Street, finds himself the unwilling master of a blue-haired girl from the powerful Mande family. He purchases a residence near the famed three academies, a strategic location that places him at the heart of the city's elite circles. The chapter combines domestic settlement with deepening mysteries: a white-shelled turtle bound by a black iron chain reveals ancient dragon script, hinting at a forgotten power. The atmosphere is a blend of urban comedy and ominous foreshadowing, as Zhuge Liang and the beautiful young master Hai Yuntian discuss an upcoming beast tournament that will draw the continent's strongest warriors. The chapter ends with a direct challenge from the blue-haired girl's brother, a young expert rivaling even the Sword Demon Dugu Jianmo, setting the stage for high-stakes conflict.

## Key Plot Points

1. Xiao Chen becomes a celebrity on Beast-Fighting Street after winning Kanasi (a Mande family heiress) as a servant in a gambling match, drawing the ire of local nobles.
2. He acquires a three-courtyard estate from Hai Yuntian, a beautiful young nobleman, on Academy Road opposite the Beidou Academy, using borrowed gold.
3. Examining the white-shelled turtle he bought earlier, Xiao Chen discovers a fine black iron chain engraved with ancient characters, dominated by the word 'dragon'. The process of reading them with his spiritual sense causes immense pain but also tempers his divine consciousness.
4. He names the turtle 'Xiao Zei' (Little Thief) and senses it is far more than an ordinary beast.
5. In the evening, Zhuge Liang and Hai Yuntian visit, and over wine they discuss the upcoming grand beast-fighting tournament in three months, which will attract young experts and holy beasts from across the continent.
6. The blue-haired girl Kanasi returns with her elder brother, who is said to be on par with Dugu Jianmo, demanding a duel in Beidou Academy's square.
7. Xiao Chen agrees to the challenge, eager to test his improved cultivation at the eighth heaven of Mortal-Shedding.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter introduces Heavenly Emperor City (天帝城), a legendary metropolis in the Southern Wastes believed to gather the dragon qi of the land. The three academies—Beidou (北斗), Doushen (斗神), and Bailu (白鹿)—are centers of cultivation and learning, attended by young elites. The beast-fighting culture is deeply embedded in the city's economy and social hierarchy; powerful families like the Mande and the Ligen control colosseums. The white turtle with a dragon-inscribed chain echoes ancient Chinese legends of turtles carrying cosmic secrets (e.g., the Luo River Chart). The black iron chain's resistance to spiritual probing and the burning pain recall common xianxia tropes of hidden inheritance guarded by trials. The character 'dragon' (龙) suggests a connection to the dragon lineage Xiao Chen has encountered throughout his journey. The upcoming tournament mirrors historical 'beast fights' but elevated to a mystical scale, with holy beasts and young demigods competing.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a breather after the intense Dragon Island arc, establishing Xiao Chen's new base and social network in the Eternal Continent. Pay attention to the white turtle—it is clearly no ordinary pet; its black chain and dragon script foreshadow a major subplot involving ancient dragon mysteries. The challenge from Kanasi's brother sets up a combat showpiece that will test Xiao Chen's new powers, especially the Undying Wings and the Slay God technique. The upcoming beast tournament will likely bring back familiar faces from Dragon Island (such as Dugu Jianmo and the Phoenix Saintess) and introduce new power players. Note the subtle tension between the Mande family's dominance and Xiao Chen's outsider status—his victory in the duel could either cement his reputation or draw greater enmity. The three skeletons remain at the inn, perhaps preparing for their own role in the city's underground.
