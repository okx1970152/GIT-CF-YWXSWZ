## Chapter Overview
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Keke ransacking an elixir chamber after a fierce battle. The spoils are meager—most pill bottles are empty or contain mundane medicines—but four cauldrons yield a rare prize: four green jade spirit pills with sentience. After dividing the spoils, Xiao Chen enters the Nirvana realm, yet finds that his seventy-two divinized acupoints and perfected divine meridians prevent the typical energy trough, making him a uniquely stable presence in that volatile realm. Keke, meanwhile, undergoes the first signs of its third transformation, its snowy fur taking on a golden tint. The second half of the chapter shifts to the mortal capital Luoyang during the Peony Gathering, where cultivators from the Eternal Continent and the Mortal World converge. Xiao Chen reunites with old acquaintances—Yao Yao, Jin Sanyi, Liu Mu, and others—and encounters new figures like the golden-haired Totti, the illegitimate son of the Sun Holy God. The atmosphere brims with political tension and personal grudges, setting the stage for a major confrontation.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen and Keke secure four supreme spirit pills from the alchemical cauldrons, but the elder cultivator who had been nurturing them escapes with the bulk of the sect's treasures. Xiao Chen refines one pill, divinizing seven major acupoints and pushing his total to seventy-two. His cultivation at the Nirvana realm stabilizes because the acupoints continuously replenish his energy, nullifying the feared low period. Keke's transformation into a golden-furred form begins; the white beast now boasts speed and power that match Xiao Chen's own. The scene moves to Luoyang, where the Peony Gathering has become a de facto summit for both mortal and immortal cultivators, all drawn by rumors that the city houses a power that can oppose the Yellow Clay Altar. Xiao Chen walks openly into this lion's den, testing the resolve of his enemies. He meets Yao Yao, the demoness of Heaven Demon Palace, who flirts with him but maintains a wary distance. Jin Sanyi the stuttering assassin reappears, still lecherous and comic. Liu Mu offers a friendly greeting. Most notably, Xiao Chen spots Totti, the Sun Holy God's bastard son, and contemplates using him to exact revenge for the Sun Holy God's past enmity. The gathering of so many hostile factions under one roof promises an explosive clash.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Nirvana realm (涅槃境界) is typically a treacherous cultivation stage where a practitioner's power fluctuates wildly and often bottoms out, leaving them vulnerable. Xiao Chen's unique Martial Body, strengthened by seventy-two divinized acupoints and a complete network of divine meridians, stabilizes his energy—a major advantage few cultivators possess. The Peony Gathering in Luoyang echoes real-world Chinese traditions where peony festivals were celebrated, but here it is fused with mythic significance. Luoyang is described as a birthplace of many Ancestral Gods, from Fuxi and Nuwa to the Yellow Emperor, making it a focal point of worldly power and ancient mysteries. The Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台), introduced earlier as a protective artifact tied to Xiao Chen's lineage, is said to contain a power that can even counter the primordial village's influence. The presence of cultivators from both the Eternal Continent and the Mortal World, including disciples of Chan and Jie sects, reflects a rare moment of cross-realm convergence, ripe for political and martial rivalry.

## Reading Guide
This chapter serves as a transitional breather after the intense battle at the Divine Palace, allowing Xiao Chen to consolidate his gains and scout the opposition. Readers should pay close attention to Keke's transformation, as it hints at a far-reaching evolution that will dramatically enhance her combat capabilities. The introduction of Totti, the Sun Holy God's son, sets up a potential leverage point Xiao Chen might exploit against the Sun Religion. The Peony Gathering is clearly a powder keg—with so many hostile sects and individuals in one place, a major confrontation seems inevitable. Xiao Chen's bold appearance suggests he is ready to challenge his enemies openly, but he also seems to be gathering intelligence and allies. Keep an eye on Yao Yao; her ambiguous loyalty makes her a wildcard. The chapter's closing lines, with Xiao Chen eyeing Totti, foreshadow a scheme that could trigger conflict between Xiao Chen and the Sun Holy God's faction.
