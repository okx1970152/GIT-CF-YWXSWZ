## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen plummets into an endless darkness, the Five Emperor Tower clutched in his hands, the furious shadow of a being that was once a supreme expert sealed within. In the depths of an abyss that seems to connect to countless worlds, he uses the terrifying seals inherited from the female corpse to wound the shadow, but the price is high — the female corpse's peerless, undying body begins to crack. This chapter is a descent into the most forbidden places of the Death World, a crucible of desperation and discovery. It balances the brutality of survival against a creature that has transcended the limits of the Stone Man path with the quiet, centuries-long cultivation that follows, using the Five Emperor Tower as a divine city. The emergence of a mysterious stone man studying the Eighth Heavenly Stele, long thought blank, brings a new layer of ancient mystery.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen falls into the dark abyss, which is lined with spatial cracks linking to many worlds. The shadowy remnant, sealed in the Five Emperor Tower, fights back viciously. Xiao Chen spontaneously executes divine seals from the female corpse's body, which prove devastatingly effective against the shadow. The shadow is nearly destroyed but escapes, losing a leg in the process. Afterward, Xiao Chen discovers that the female corpse's body is slowly fracturing, as if too powerful a vessel cannot endure in the real world — a phenomenon he connects to the fate of the Little Stone Emperor. He then enters a five-hundred-year cultivation trance, using the first layer of the Five Emperor Tower as his personal divine city, a method that grounds him in the path of the Stone Man while allowing him to hear ethereal Dao lectures. Emerging from cultivation, he senses a recurring disturbance near the Eighth Heavenly Stele and finds a stone man (a stone king) repeatedly touching it, causing ancient texts and diagrams to appear on the previously blank stele.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of 'Heaven's Jealousy' (天妒) is central to this chapter: beings of extraordinary power — such as the Little Stone Emperor, the female corpse, and the shadow — cannot exist indefinitely in the living world without self-destructing. This adds a tragic, fatalistic layer to cultivation: the higher one ascends, the closer one comes to annihilation. The Five Emperor Tower (五帝塔) is a legendary stone artifact from an ancient age, one of the most revered divine cities ever known. Using it as a foundation for a divine city is an unprecedented, audacious move, as divine cities are normally constructed from a practitioner's own power. The Eighth Heavenly Stele (第八面天碑) was believed to be blank, but this chapter reveals that it contains hidden writings that only appear when a specially attuned being — perhaps a Stone Man or a being of a certain cultivation level — touches it. The concept of a divine city (神城) is central to the Stone Man path: a cultivator builds an internal city as a crucible for nirvana and rebirth.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a pivot point. After the explosive, desperate flight from the Stone Man Kings and the shadow, Xiao Chen gains a period of peaceful cultivation that solidifies his own path. The cracking of the female corpse foreshadows that this powerful vessel may not be usable forever, raising the stakes for his own progress. The appearance of the mysterious stone man at the Eighth Heavenly Stele opens a new storyline — who is this entity, and what secrets does the stele hold? Readers should note the detail that the abyss connects to many worlds, suggesting it may be a nexus or a passage. Also, the Five Emperor Tower’s potential as a divine city is a game-changer for Xiao Chen’s cultivation. The shadow’s defeat and loss of a limb may have future consequences. The tone remains tragic and mythic, with moments of desperate action giving way to profound contemplation.
