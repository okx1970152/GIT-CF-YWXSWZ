## Chapter Overview
This climactic chapter marks the final confrontation with the Four Ancient Kings as they race to seize the Sole True Realm. Through the power of the Sacred Oracle Bone Diagram, the primordial war souls of Pangu, Nüwa, Suiren, Fuxi, Shennong, and the Five Emperors are summoned from the dawn of time. Each delivers a single, devastating strike—their strongest from life—annihilating the Ancient Kings utterly. The tone is tragic and epic, blending the grandeur of ancient myth with the sorrow of fleeting sacrifice. The summoned heroes fade after their final act, but the gates to the True Realm loom before the survivors, guarded by an even greater horde of imperial wills.

## Key Plot Points
The Four Ancient Kings (Xuhuang and three others) are forced to retreat from the oracle bone light and attempt to breach the gate to the Sole True Realm before the summoned heroes can stop them. Pangu emerges first from the temporal storm, his stone axe splitting the sky, and cleaves Xuhuang in two—a blow that shatters both body and Dao. The other nine heroes follow: Nüwa, Suiren, Fuxi, Shennong, Yellow Emperor, Zhuanxu, Di Ku, Yao, and Shun. Their united attack overwhelms the remaining three Ancient Kings, who die screaming about their failed gambit. The heroes then advance toward the gate, where the combined killing intent of the Twenty-Seven Emperors and Thirty-Six Sovereigns of the True Realm strikes them. They shatter and reform step by step, sacrificing themselves to open the way. Meanwhile, a colossal primordial beast blocks the path, but Pangu stays behind to fight it, letting the others pass into the gate.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Sacred Oracle Bone Diagram, assembled in this chapter, is a primordial artifact that can summon war souls from the distant past. The heroes called upon are the foundational figures of Chinese mythology: Pangu the creator, Nüwa who mended the heavens, Suiren who brought fire, Fuxi who devised the trigrams, Shennong the divine farmer, and the Five Emperors (Yellow Emperor, Zhuanxu, Di Ku, Yao, Shun). Their cultivation is at the Stone Man King or Ancestral God level. The phenomenon of 'Heaven's Jealousy' explains why supreme beings cannot remain long in the real world—their bodies crack and decay. The Twenty-Seven Emperors and Thirty-Six Sovereigns are the rulers of the Sole True Realm, a cosmic prison or paradise for peak cultivators, sending killing intent across space to destroy intruders.

## Reading Guide
This chapter resolves the Four Ancient Kings arc decisively, but the gate to the Sole True Realm remains a looming threat. The sacrifice of the primordial heroes reinforces the novel's theme of selfless struggle for future generations. Xiao Chen and his allies are now faced with the next challenge: the hostile inhabitants of the Sole True Realm. Pay attention to the nature of the Twenty-Seven Emperors and Thirty-Six Sovereigns, as they may become the next antagonists. Also, note the fate of the primordial beast and Pangu, left fighting at the chasm. The next chapters will likely explore the Sole True Realm and Xiao Chen's path to confronting its collective will.
