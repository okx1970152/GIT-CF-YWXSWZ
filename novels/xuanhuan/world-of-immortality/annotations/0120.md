## Chapter Overview

This chapter presents a sharp contrast between chaotic adventure and solemn ritual. The opening depicts the adorable yet troublesome Keke fleeing from the Black Iron Seal, a fearsome sacred artifact. The pursuit leaves destruction across the landscape, but the seal inexplicably vanishes under the little beast's mysterious influence. Later, the solemn summoning of the King Ship—a dark vessel from the Forbidden Sea—brings a grim, mythic weight. Xiao Chen secures the devil's shed skins to cloak his skeleton allies, and then performs a blood ritual to call the ancient ship. The tone shifts from frantic chase to dread-laden ceremony, emphasizing the world's dual nature of wonder and terror.

## Key Plot Points

Keke is chased across the island by the Black Iron Seal, a sacred artifact that had once attacked the divine stele in the Dead City. The seal is immensely powerful and nearly crushes Keke multiple times, but she uses her Sacred Tree (hat) for protection. The seal eventually disappears, surprising even the devil. Keke's role in causing two sacred artifacts to vanish (the Golden Divine Halberd earlier and now the iron seal) deepens the mystery of her origins and powers. Xiao Chen secures from the devil three shed human skins, which he intends for the three skeletons to wear as disguises when they reach the mainland. Then, at the shore, the devil prepares a summoning array. Xiao Chen's blood activates the spell, and after a barrage of ghostly phenomena, a massive black ship—the King Ship—rises from the Forbidden Sea, radiating deathly aura. The chapter ends with the ship's terrifying appearance, in stark contrast to the earlier radiant Ancestral Dragon Ship.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The King Ship (君王船) is a concept drawn from the idea that supreme cultivators or "kings" could forge vessels to cross sealed seas. Unlike the Ancestral Dragon Ship, which is a divine rainbow vessel, the King Ship is described as a sinister black craft linked to the underworld. This reflects a common xianxia duality: light and dark paths to transcendence. The devil's shed skins (蜕皮) are a grotesque but practical item; they come from a being undergoing reanimation, and retaining such relics shows how even discarded forms hold practical value in this world. The summoning ritual uses the caster's blood to power ghostly formations, connecting to the novel's frequent theme of blood sacrifice as a key to unlocking ancient mechanisms.

## Reading Guide

Readers should keep an eye on Keke's growing relationship with sacred artifacts. Her ability to cause their disappearance may be a pivotal ability later, possibly linked to her true origin as a divine beast of the World Tree. The King Ship is now a potential escape vessel for Xiao Chen, but its sinister nature suggests a dangerous journey. The three skeletons' disguises open the door for them to enter the mainland without persecution, setting up new dynamics. Meanwhile, Yan Qingcheng remains a captive, and her unpredictable reactions could affect the group's cohesion. The devil's true motives and identity become more elusive, as he willingly parts with his shed skins and aids in the summoning.
