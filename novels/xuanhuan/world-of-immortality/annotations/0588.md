## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the long-awaited confrontation between Pangu King and the three Otherworld Stone Man Kings, culminating in the sealing of the mysterious stone gate that connects worlds. The battle spans multiple realms—the Nine Provinces, the Heavenly Realm, and the Death World—and shakes the foundations of all existence. Pangu King wields the Divine Diagram as a living artifact, and his final act shatters the diagram into the Nine Provinces while sealing the open gate. The Stone Corpse reveals his origin as a being that fell from that very gate, driving the Heavenly Realm giants to seal it permanently. The chapter then jumps forward 2,800 years, showing Xiao Chen awakening from a long seclusion and returning to a transformed Nine Provinces. The epic scale of the battle and the subsequent mystery surrounding the gate shape the new era of the story.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with a terrifying vision: the Eternal Unknown has been utterly destroyed, revealing a corner of a strange world through an open stone gate atop the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. Pangu King, wielding his colossal stone axe, smashes the Giant Palace into the stone steps, shattering both. Three Otherworld Stone Man Kings—Lu Zhan and two others—rise in fury, but Pangu King declares this is the moment to settle ancient debts. He uses the Divine Diagram, now fully awakened with life, to attack the open stone gate. A massive collision blurs all perception, and the gate is nearly closed, but the diagram shatters and falls into the Nine Provinces. The gate seals shut, locking a shadowy figure inside. The Stone Corpse, an ancient undead giant, reveals that he himself once fell from that gate and only later gained intelligence. He rallies other Heavenly Realm giants to reinforce the seal. The three Stone Man Kings rage against this, but the seal holds. Pangu King sends Xiao Chen, He Zu, and others into the Heavenly Realm, warning them to flee the impending chaos. The battle ends with a Celestial Realm giant falling in the Nine Provinces and the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps crashing into the Otherworld. Over the next 2,800 years, the Heavenly Realm's giants secretly investigate ancient histories and hidden truths. Xiao Chen, who had been in seclusion since being sent to the Heavenly Realm, awakens and receives messages from He Zu and others. He decides to return to the Nine Provinces, where he finds the land vibrant with life—a dragon and a human city appear on the horizon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps are a sacred path to transcendence, often associated with the 'final step' of the Stone Man cultivation path. In this chapter, they serve as a battlefield, demonstrating that even the highest realms can be shattered by conflict. The Divine Diagram (神图) is a legendary artifact formed from the imprints of fallen Stone Man Kings; Pangu King uses it as a living weapon, highlighting its sentient nature. The stone gate itself is a cosmic portal, with one door open to a dead world and one closed, evoking deep mythological questions about the origins of the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld. The Stone Corpse's revelation—that he is a being who fell from that gate—adds a layer of tragic mystery to his character, linking him directly to the ancient catastrophe. The concept of 'Heavenly Realm giants' (天界巨头) refers to the supreme powers of the celestial realm, each with their own ancient agendas. The time skip of 2,800 years emphasizes the vast scale of cultivation timelines, where centuries pass like moments for half-ancestors and ancestral gods.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a major turning point in the cosmic conflict, closing the 'stone gate' arc that has been building for many chapters. Pay attention to the Stone Corpse's sudden proactive role—his origin is a crucial clue to the nature of the Otherworld and the ancient war. The shattering of the Divine Diagram into the Nine Provinces may have lasting consequences, as fragments of its power now lie scattered across the mortal world. Xiao Chen's decision to return to the Nine Provinces after 2,800 years sets up a new phase of the story, where he will re-engage with the mortal realm and likely gather allies. The mystery of what the Heavenly Realm giants are searching for remains open—their investigation of 'more ancient histories' suggests hidden truths about the primordial age. The appearance of a dragon and a city at the end hints that the Nine Provinces has recovered under new rulers, possibly including Keke's family or other powerful beings. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen interacts with this changed world and whether the sealed gate remains truly closed.
