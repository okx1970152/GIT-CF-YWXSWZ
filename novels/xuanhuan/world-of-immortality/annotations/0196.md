## Chapter Overview

The Xuanhuang Beast Arena becomes the stage for an awe-inspiring confrontation as Keke, the snow-white divine beast, faces the Golden Lion King. The battle escalates beyond mortal comprehension when the lion king's third eye opens, conjuring a grey, hopeless world that engulfs the sky. Yet Keke, pushed to her limits, unleashes a profound power from her own eyes—black and white divine light that sweeps across the illusory realm, causing mountains to crumble, seas to evaporate, and the grey world to collapse entirely. The chapter culminates in a shocking revelation: an old warrior in golden flames recognizes Keke's true race and retreats in trembling awe, leaving the crowd and Xiao Chen with more questions than answers about her origins.

## Key Plot Points

The Golden Lion King, having pushed Keke to the brink, activates its third vertical eye, conjuring a vast grey world of deathly stillness—a domain that seems to exist between reality and illusion. The oppressive atmosphere grips every spectator in the arena, creating a sense of impending doom. Keke, initially passive, is forced to respond. Its eyes blaze with contrasting light: the left eye emits dazzling white radiance, the right a deep, abyssal black. These beams sweep across the illusory world, unraveling its fabric. Every mountain the light touches shatters; every sea dries; the very land sinks into nothingness. The grey world disintegrates completely, and the Golden Lion King falls from the sky, its body nearly broken, its third eye bleeding. An old man shrouded in golden divine fire catches the lion king and, upon seeing Keke, is struck with profound recognition. He utters incomprehensible words, bows toward the western void, and flees without explanation. Xiao Chen hears a voice warn that such a being as Keke invites heavenly jealousy, stirring a deep unease within him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The third eye (天眼) is a classic motif in Eastern mythology, symbolizing supreme insight, spiritual perception, and destructive divine power. In xianxia, the opening of a third eye often marks a being's ascension to a higher level of consciousness. The grey world created by the Golden Lion King echoes the Buddhist concept of the 'saha world'—a realm of endurance and suffering, but here rendered as an illusion of hopelessness. Keke's eyes, one white and one black, evoke the primordial duality of yin and yang, light and dark, often associated with the ultimate creative and destructive forces. The old man's reverence and the mention of the War Clan (战族) hint at Keke's lineage—a race of legendary warriors who once contended with gods. The phrase 'heavenly jealousy' (天妒) is a recurring theme in Chinese literature: extraordinary beings are often fated to an early demise, a tragic notion that now shadows Keke.
