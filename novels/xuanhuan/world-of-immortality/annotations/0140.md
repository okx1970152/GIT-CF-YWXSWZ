## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a deceptively tranquil interlude within the opulent Bi Yue Xiu Hua Hall, where wine, music, and dance mask the coils of political maneuvering. Xiao Chen, accompanied by the scheming merchant Zhuge Liang, navigates the seductive attentions of the palace's courtesans, Fire Dance and Ice Qin, while sharpening his observations of the Beast-Fighting Palace's power games. The tension ratchets sharply when Yan Qingcheng arrives in male disguise, transforming the evening into a fiery drinking duel that doubles as a covert information hunt. Beneath the surface of revelry, old grudges and new schemes converge, setting the stage for a deeper conflict.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Zhuge Liang being entertained by the alluring courtesans of the Drunken Beauty Palace. Xiao Chen remains cautious, noticing that Fire Dance is a Mortal-Shedding cultivator - an unusual caliber for a hostess. Zhuge Liang revels in the company, but his sharp commercial eye soon picks up that the courtesans are unusually proactive, hinting at a hidden agenda. Yan Qingcheng, in male garb, confronts Xiao Chen in the rose garden, leading to a verbal spar that escalates into a drinking contest inside the hall. She forces everyone into a binge, using her extraordinary capacity to outlast them all. When the others collapse, Yan Qingcheng uses a spiritual technique to extract the location of a hidden dragon king from the courtesan Ice Qin. Meanwhile, Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon, unnoticed, slip away to explore the palace depths, hinting at future discoveries.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases the 'drinking contest' trope common in xianxia, where characters compete in alcohol consumption as a test of will, constitution, or even cultivation. Unlike ordinary mortals, cultivators can expel alcohol using their energy, but social etiquette often forbids cheating, making it a battle of endurance. Yan Qingcheng's use of a 'soul search' or mental interrogation technique after feigning drunkenness is a classic xianxia method of extracting secrets without violence. Additionally, the Bi Yue Xiu Hua Hall represents the type of elite entertainment venue common in cultivation worlds, where power, money, and beauty intersect. Such establishments often serve as neutral ground for negotiation and espionage, with courtesans trained in both arts and intelligence gathering.
