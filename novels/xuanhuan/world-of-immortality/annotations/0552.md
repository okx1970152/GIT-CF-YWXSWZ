## Chapter Overview
This chapter is pivotal for Xiao Chen's cultivation progression and the overarching conflict. A mysterious Taoist imparts the coveted Stone Man secret method and gifts Xiao Chen a Primordial Demon City, setting the stage for his future breakthroughs. However, a darker undercurrent emerges when the Taoist detects treacherous marks on Xiao Chen's war swords, left by the formidable Renmo Gegan. Meanwhile, Gegan himself makes a shocking move in the mortal world: he slays a celestial envoy, openly defying the authority of the Celestial Realm. The chapter thus balances profound cultivation insights with escalating political tension, hinting at an imminent war between worlds.

## Key Plot Points
The Taoist transmits the Stone Man path to Xiao Chen, a method so dangerous it is described as a 'self-destructive road.' Xiao Chen tests it with a stone spear, causing partial petrification. After tense negotiation, the Taoist yields the Primordial Demon City (a Nirvana-city for Stone Man cultivators), but refuses to part with the stone spear. Xiao Chen refines the city over three months. Crucially, the Taoist uncovers insidious marks on three of Xiao Chen's war swords, planted by Renmo Gegan—a powerful figure who attempted to simulate the swords' power and spy on Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen erases the marks. In the chapter's second half, Gegan, idling on a boat overseas, receives a celestial envoy delivering a 'decree' from the Celestial Realm. Gegan, already brooding, kills the envoy with his terrifying stone arm, despite the presence of four otherworld half-ancestors. This act signals open defiance against the Celestial Realm and solidifies Gegan as a volatile, unpredictable force.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Stone Man path is a legendary cultivation method in which the practitioner undergoes a process of gradual petrification, eventually achieving an indestructible stone body. As the Taoist explains, the extent of petrification alone does not determine combat power—vitality and life essence are equally crucial. The Primordial Demon City serves as a Nirvana vessel for Stone Man cultivators, allowing them to consolidate life force during the perilous transformation. The divine diagram (神图) and the war swords are deeply interconnected artifacts; Gegan's attempt to simulate their spiritual imprint is a form of parasitic cultivation, aiming to eventually control Xiao Chen's treasures. The Celestial Realm (天界) is a higher dimension that communicates with the mortal world through envoys; Gegan's murder of one is a direct challenge to its authority, suggesting a schism among supreme powers.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen now possesses both the Stone Man secret method and a fully refined demon city, tools that will be instrumental in his journey toward the highest realms. However, the discovery of Gegan's hidden marks serves as a stark warning: even trusted figures may harbor dangerous agendas. Readers should watch for Gegan's next move—his defiance of the Celestial Realm may have far-reaching consequences, potentially drawing Xiao Chen into a broader conflict between worlds. The chapter also deepens the lore of the Stone Man path, which may become critical for Xiao Chen's own cultivation choices. Finally, the Taoist's conditional cooperation (waiting until Xiao Chen reaches the eighth heaven of Half-Ancestor) sets up a future alliance or confrontation.
