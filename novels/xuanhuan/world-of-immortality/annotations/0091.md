## Chapter Overview

The Dead City, a place of desolate silence, erupts into a battlefield of mythic proportions. Multiple ancient sacred artifacts—an iron seal, a divine halberd, a Buddha's Dharma Wheel, a bronze eight-trigram, and even parts of an Ancestral Dragon—unleash their power against the divine stele that seals the demon well. This chapter is a breathtaking display of legendary forces clashing, as the stele struggles to hold the line and the bone sea outside answers with a tide of deathly light. Within the city, Xiao Chen and the surviving cultivators find themselves trapped in a labyrinth of ancient palaces, hunted by an unseen giant presence. The atmosphere is one of escalating dread and awe, as the true scale of Dragon Island's hidden history begins to unfold.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the fading of the phantom steles, leaving only the true 'Eternally Settle the Isolated Island' stele to suppress the demon well. The well shakes violently, suggesting a connection to another world, but the stele's light intensifies and forces the well back into submission. In a dramatic turn, the stele's engravings come alive: an image of a brutal dragon, a divine man, a sea of blood, a dead city, and a pure land merge into a curtain of light that seals the well once more. However, this stability is short-lived. An iron seal from a distant mountain, a divine halberd from a ruined mountain range, the Buddha's Dharma Wheel from the city gate, and a bronze eight-trigram from a collapsed valley all fly toward the stele, striking it with tremendous force. Each is repelled, but the combined assault causes the stele to tremble. The dragon race above joins the attack, firing spells and sending an Ancestral Dragon's horn and claw, but the stele, reinforced by the guardian stone railings around the plaza, holds firm. Outside the city, the boundless bone sea erupts with a ghastly green death-light, attacking the dragons. Xiao Chen and the others realize they must retreat from the center. They desperately try to find the city's exit, but the streets twist and mislead them. They come across two buildings: Fallen Demon Hall and Falling God Hall, whose names suggest a grim history. A twenty-meter-tall humanoid shadow flashes past. Soon, a cultivator is found crushed into a pulp by a huge claw print, and Xiao Chen and Liu Mu sense a massive hidden predator. The survivors begin to scatter; some die as they wander into danger. Xiao Chen and his group (including Liu Ruyan and Yan Qingcheng) remain together, but they cannot find a way out. The city seems to actively confound direction.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The divine stele is a recurring motif in 'World of Immortality,' representing celestial sealing power. Here, the stele bears the inscription 'Eternally Settle the Isolated Island,' echoing the earlier 'Eternally Settle the Yellow River' that Xiao Chen encountered in his youth. The demon well is a classic xianxia trope: a portal to a dark, otherworldly realm, often containing imprisoned evils or countless souls. The artifacts attacking the stele are legendary weapons from mythological times: the Buddha's Dharma Wheel is tied to Buddhist lore, the bronze eight-trigram to Daoist symbolism, and the Ancestral Dragon's horn and claw to the primordial dragon lineage. Their simultaneous assault suggests a coordinated effort to break the seal, hinting at a grand conspiracy. The bone sea outside the city is a sea of undead, animated by a deathly green light—a common xianxia setting for ancient battlefields. The buildings 'Fallen Demon Hall' and 'Falling God Hall' evoke themes of divine downfall, common in dark fantasy. The giant shadow may be a guardian or a remnant of a fallen god.

## Reading Guide

This chapter raises the stakes and mystery. Who controls the artifacts? What lies within the demon well? The survivors are trapped in a city that seems alive and hostile. Pay attention to the buildings and inscriptions—they may hold clues about the city's origin. Xiao Chen's ability to see the phantom steles may prove crucial. The giant predator hints that the Dead City is not just a ruin but a hunting ground. The next chapter will likely explore the city's deeper secrets and the fate of the trapped cultivators. The alliance between Liu Ruyan and Yan Qingcheng remains precarious—trust is thin. Xiao Chen's connection to the steles and his unique cultivation may be the key to survival.
