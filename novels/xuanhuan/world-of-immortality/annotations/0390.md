## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, the political landscape of the ancient relic shifts dramatically. After the brutal slaying of War King Jingpu, the Golden Lion King seizes the Baste Ancient Spear and departs, leaving the Zombie King and Undying King empty-handed. The God of War arrives, slaughtering those who accuse him of the murder, asserting his dominance. Meanwhile, the Fallen Angel Wang cunningly exposes Xiao Chen as the true killer, inciting the assembled kings against him. Xiao Chen, in his yellow-diamond skeleton form, uses the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree to defend himself, but the pressure mounts as the Asura King appears, coveting the treasure. The chapter culminates in the opening of an underground palace that houses the legendary White Bone King Banner, sending all kings into a frenzy. Xiao Chen escapes and reunites with the Zombie King and Undying King, guiding them to the palace, while Keke stealthily enters the scene.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the aftermath of the War King's death. The golden hand manifested earlier is revealed to be the Golden Lion King wielding the Baste Spear, who effortlessly drives off the Zombie King and Undying King. He takes the spear and leaves, fulfilling his promise to Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen heals his wounded yellow-diamond skeleton by absorbing life-source liquid. Then the scene shifts to a relic where many kings compete for a purple jade artifact. The Asura King appears and snatches it, then questions who killed the War King. The God of War arrives, smashes the accusing kings with terrifying ease, denies killing the War King, and slays the Wind Spirit King and Shadow King for inciting trouble. He departs but glances at Xiao Chen. The Fallen Angel Wang then publicly reveals that Xiao Chen is the true killer of the War King, noting his battle with Yecha Tianwang and his human identity. This enrages the other kings, and the Asura King attacks Xiao Chen for the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree. Before a full melee erupts, the blood of the slain kings triggers the opening of an underground palace, revealing the White Bone King Banner. All kings rush in. Xiao Chen flees, uses the ‘Weng’ sound to repel pursuers, then sheds his disguise and leads the Zombie King and Undying King back to the palace. Inside, he spots Keke sneaking in.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Asura King and God of War represent the pinnacle of combat among the hundred races. Asura, derived from Buddhist mythology, are powerful demigods, while the God of War is a title for an unbeatable warrior. The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, originally belonging to the sage Zhunti, is a half-ancestor artifact of immense defensive and offensive power. The White Bone King Banner is a legendary weapon forged from the bones of Ancestral Gods, capable of controlling the undead. The ‘Weng’ sound is one of the Eight Syllables of Origin, a primordial technique governing life and death. Xiao Chen’s yellow-diamond skeleton form indicates his cultivation in the world of the dead, where bones transcend mortal limits and the path to physical rebirth is slowly realized. The gathering of kings and the appearance of such a relic echo the ongoing upheaval among the hundred races, where power is the ultimate currency.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for the upcoming struggle for the White Bone King Banner, which is likely to draw even more powerful figures. Xiao Chen’s true identity is now known to the Fallen Angel Wang, making his position perilous. The God of War’s neutral stance may be tested, and the Asura King’s ambition for the banner could lead to a fierce confrontation. The reappearance of Keke hints at the little beast’s involvement in the treasure hunt. The political alliances among the kings are fragile, and Xiao Chen’s network with the Zombie King and Undying King may prove crucial. Keep an eye on the shifting loyalties and who will ultimately claim the banner. The chapter’s events also deepen the mystery of the ancient ruins and the fate of the Ancestral Gods’ artifacts.
