## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen enters the Otherworld (Celestial Realm) and immediately faces immense dangers. He witnesses a terrifying stone corpse probing a mysterious passage to the Waste Land, then encounters the reclusive Red Fire Qilin who warns him of the precarious state of the Nine Provinces' bloodline in the Otherworld. The chapter culminates in an ambush by two powerful cultivators who recognize Xiao Chen's origin and intend to kill him. Amidst the confrontation, Xiao Chen discovers that one of his enemies carries the legacy of a Stone Man King from the Otherworld—a heritage that must not be allowed to spread. This chapter establishes the Otherworld's political landscape, the hostility toward the Nine Provinces, and introduces a new deadly pursuit.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen observes the stone corpse entering the Waste Land through a chaotic space channel, suggesting that even supreme beings seek the secrets of that forbidden realm. Two mysterious figures discuss the Waste Land's history and decide not to follow. Xiao Chen travels through desolate lands to find Red Fire Qilin, who at first mistakes him for a treasure hunter. After clarifying, Red Fire Qilin reveals that the Otherworld is on the brink of conflict, driven by the search for a 'defying heaven war treasure'. He also points out that Xiao Chen's aura marks him as from the lower realm, a dangerous identity in the Otherworld. Xiao Chen learns that the Nine Provinces remnant is in a disadvantageous position. Upon leaving, he is ambushed by two cultivators in divine chariots who had been scouting for treasures. They instantly recognize him as a 'criminal of the Land of Chaos' and decide to kill him. Xiao Chen realizes that one of them carries the imprint of a Stone Man King from the Otherworld—a legacy that could empower his enemies. He resolves to eliminate them to prevent their heritage from spreading. This chapter advances Xiao Chen's understanding of the Otherworld's power structure and sets up a direct conflict with an ancient lineage.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Otherworld (天界) is the upper realm of the World of Immortality's cosmology, distinct from the Eternal Continent and the Death World. It is described as vast and dangerous, with powerful beings like 'stone corpses' (石尸) and 'supreme ancestral gods' (上祖神). The 'Waste Land' (废土) is an ancient battlefield or forbidden zone from the previous civilization era, known to have killed a Stone Man in the past. The concept of 'stone man' (石人) is a cultivation path leading to ultimate power, and their legacies can be passed down through bloodlines or imprints. The 'defying heaven war treasure' (逆天战宝) is a rumored artifact that could tip the balance of power, driving conflicts among major factions. The Nine Provinces (九州) bloodline is considered 'criminal' (罪人) in the Otherworld, reflecting the ancient enmity between the Otherworld and the mortal ancestral gods. Adapting to the celestial aura (天界精气) is necessary for cultivators from lower realms to avoid detection.

## Reading Guide
This chapter introduces the Otherworld as a setting rich with ancient secrets and complex politics. Pay attention to the mention of the 'Waste Land' and the stone corpse – these may become significant later. The warning from Red Fire Qilin about the precarious status of the Nine Provinces bloodline sets up Xiao Chen's need for allies and caution. The ambush by the two cultivators reveals that the Otherworld is already aware of Xiao Chen's arrival; this suggests that powerful beings may have divined his presence. The discovery of the Stone Man King imprint in one of the enemies is a critical development – this lineage could offer Xiao Chen a new path to power, but also makes him a target for those who seek that heritage. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen resolved to kill his pursuers, so expect a fierce battle in the next chapter. The tone remains tense and foreboding, emphasizing the immense scale of the Otherworld and Xiao Chen's vulnerability.
