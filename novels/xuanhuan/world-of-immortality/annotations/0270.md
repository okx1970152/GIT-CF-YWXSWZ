## Chapter Overview
This chapter brings a poignant reunion and a decisive turning point. Xiao Chen visits the Southern Wastes dragon clan, where the Little Defiant Dragon has taken human form as a resolute child. After receiving cryptic gifts from the old dragon and the phoenix maiden, he returns to Heavenly Emperor City only to fall into a trap set by Hai Yunxue. Forced to flee, he visits the Pure Land one last time before embarking on a three-year journey into the unknown northern wastelands. The chapter closes with legendary sages stirring, hinting at world-shaking events.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen meets the Southern Wastes old dragon, who reveals that powerful figures have taken an interest in his survival. The Little Defiant Dragon appears as a seven- or eight-year-old child, still taciturn but loyal, and gifts Xiao Chen a bundle of rare spirit herbs. The old dragon lends him a dragon horn as a protective artifact, and the phoenix maiden delivers a gift from her grandmother. In Heavenly Emperor City, Xiao Chen kills the villain Hoffman and is confronted by Hai Yunxue, who has assembled Duobao Daoist, Yunzhongzi, and the third son of the White Tiger Saint Emperor to kill him. Xiao Chen escapes using a sacred artifact that opens a spatial channel. He briefly visits the Pure Land, where Qingqing is absent, and then decides to penetrate the forbidden northern territories—the Ancient God Wasteland—using multiple sacred relics. The journey lasts three years, and at its end, sages like Sun Wu, Zhuangzi, and others sense an incoming presence.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Southern Wastes dragon clan is a surviving lineage from Dragon Island, where the divine stele sealed most dragons. The Little Defiant Dragon's human form signifies his cultivation progress; his gift of spirit herbs aids Xiao Chen's acupoint divinization. Duobao Daoist and Yunzhongzi are direct disciples of Tongtian and Primordial, respectively—figures from the Fengshen Yanyi mythos, indicating the involvement of paramount powers. Hai Yunxue's trap showcases political and strategic maneuvering among major clans. The Ancient God Wasteland is a legendary region where even gods cannot cross, suggesting extreme danger and mystery. Xiao Chen's three-year journey through such hostile territory parallels classic xianxia trials that forge a cultivator's spirit.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's solo journey into the north marks a shift from urban intrigue to wilderness exploration. The chapter sets up a long time skip, allowing his cultivation to mature off-screen. The awakening of legendary figures at the end suggests that Xiao Chen's travels have triggered a larger cosmic event—perhaps the arrival of otherworldly beings or the awakening of ancient powers. Readers should watch for how his relationships with the Little Defiant Dragon and the Pure Land elders evolve, and whether the protection promised by the old dragon and phoenix maiden will be tested. The trap by Hai Yunxue indicates that old enemies remain vigilant, and Xiao Chen's status as a global fugitive will continue to shape his path.
