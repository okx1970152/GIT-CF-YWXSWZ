## Chapter Overview

Chapter 544 delivers the long-awaited confrontation between the Martial Ancestor, the ultimate martial artist of the Nine Provinces, and an Upper Ancestral God of the Otherworld. The chapter is a masterclass in high-stakes, mythic combat, showcasing the forbidden Sacred Sacrifice technique—a taboo art that links the practitioner to a supreme being and burns their divine essence. The battle unfolds at the Eternal Unknown, a realm beyond time and space, where the Martial Ancestor, in his prime at the Ninth Heaven of Half-Ancestor, stands alone against a being that has half-stepped into an unknown realm. The chapter reaches a fever pitch when Xiao Chen arrives with the Yellow Clay Altar, unleashing the Eight Origin Sounds to disrupt the enemy's ultimate technique. A stone man, pursuing Xiao Chen, crashes into the scene, turning the confrontation into a chaotic three-way standoff. This chapter is a turning point, demonstrating that even the highest powers of the Otherworld can be challenged by the indomitable will of the martial path.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Martial Ancestor already deep in the Sacred Sacrifice ritual. He uses a hundred and eight drops of his own divine blood as a conduit, linking his life force to the Upper Ancestral God. The Upper Ancestral God counters with the ‘Purgatory Song’ (第七神曲 and later 第一神曲), a series of devastating sonic techniques, and summons a thirty-three-story divine pagoda to crush the Martial Ancestor. The Martial Ancestor, refuting the idea that martial arts exclude divine abilities, responds by manifesting the twelve War Ancestor forms—each a complete entity at the Ancestral God level—fused into his own combat style. This overwhelming display of martial power sends the pagoda flying and pushes the Sacred Sacrifice deeper into the enemy. The tide turns when Xiao Chen, riding the Yellow Clay Altar, blasts through the Eternal Unknown. He channels the Eight Origin Sounds, which inherently counter the Purgatory Song, breaking the Upper Ancestral God's rhythm. Exploiting the moment, the Martial Ancestor shatters the enemy's upper body with a direct strike. But before the kill can be completed, the stone man—which has been hunting Xiao Chen across worlds—enters the fray, creating a temporary deadlock. The chapter ends with the stone man roaring in fury, his presence now a threat to all parties.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Sacred Sacrifice is a forbidden technique from the Martial Ancestor's heretical chapter (邪忌篇). It is a form of ‘living sacrifice’ that inverts the natural order: instead of the practitioner being consumed, the technique forces a supreme being to become the offering. This aligns with the broader xianxia theme of defying heaven and breaking taboos. The Upper Ancestral God is a tier above ordinary Ancestral Gods, representing the peak of power in the Otherworld hierarchy. The twelve War Ancestor forms are a manifestation of the Martial Ancestor's fusion of the ‘Twelve Zodiac War Ancestors’ (十二生肖战祖)—ancient martial gods of the primordial age. This technique exemplifies the martial path's ultimate expression: unifying diverse martial intents into a single, overwhelming force. The Eight Origin Sounds, emitted by the Yellow Clay Altar, are the primordial tones of the universe, capable of neutralizing any sonic or ritual technique. They are a recurring motif connected to Xiao Chen's cultivation and the ancient heritage of the Nine Provinces.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for the final escalations of the war. The Martial Ancestor's near-victory shows that the Upper Ancestral Gods are not invincible, but the intervention of the stone man introduces a wild card that could tip the balance. Readers should watch for how the alliance between the Martial Ancestor, Xiao Chen, and the other Ancestral Gods will handle this new threat. Xiao Chen's ability to wield the Eight Origin Sounds at will is a critical asset, but the stone man is specifically targeting the Yellow Clay Altar, linking Xiao Chen directly to the conflict. The chapter also hints at the 'final step'—the stone man's unfinished evolution—which may become a key plot point. The Eternal Unknown is now a battlefield for supreme powers, and the fate of the Nine Provinces hinges on the outcome of this chaotic convergence.
