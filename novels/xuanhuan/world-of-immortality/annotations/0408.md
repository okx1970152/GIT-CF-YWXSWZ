## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal moment as Xiao Chen returns to the world in flesh and blood, confronting the dreaded Yasha King in an epic battle that shakes heaven and earth. The tone is one of triumphant vengeance and strategic mastery. Xiao Chen, now fully resurrected from his yellow-diamond skeleton state, faces a king who had slaughtered many of his allies. The fight showcases Xiao Chen's new abilities—the World Gates of the Eight Aspect Worlds—as he systematically breaks through the Yasha King's formidable defenses. It's a chapter of catharsis and a declaration that Xiao Chen is back stronger than ever.

## Key Plot Points

After years in the death world, Xiao Chen appears in human form, directly confronting the Yasha King. Their battle begins with a clash of glares that kills two nearby minions. The Yasha King employs his 'Void Transgression' technique, which renders him immune to most attacks, and wields the devastating 'Sword Flame Scorching Sky'. Xiao Chen counters with the Eight Aspect Worlds, opening each world gate in sequence—Heaven, Earth, Water, Wind, Thunder, Marsh, Mountain—to overwhelm the Yasha King. The Marsh gate traps the Yasha King, and the Thunder gate shatters his sword. Finally, the Mountain gate smashes him, breaking his Void Transgression. The Yasha King flees in humiliation, using the 'Boundless Distance' technique to escape, but Xiao Chen pursues, landing a blow that destroys his arm. The chase along the Yangtze River shocks cultivators who witness the once-invincible Yasha King being routed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Eight Aspect Worlds' technique originates from Xiao Chen's enlightenment on Fuxi's Bagua, manifesting eight elemental realms. The 'World Gates' are a deeper application, allowing Xiao Chen to release the destructive power of each realm in a controlled manner. This represents an advancement from simply using the Eight Aspect Worlds for movement and defense. The Yasha King's 'Void Transgression' is a rare divine ability that makes the user immune to laws and techniques, but Xiao Chen's realm-based attacks overwhelm it by striking from multiple dimensions simultaneously. The 'Boundless Distance' technique, first introduced as a teleportation treasure, is here used as an escape art. The chapter also highlights the hierarchy of kings in the Hundred Races era—Yasha King was considered undefeatable until now.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a major milestone in Xiao Chen's return arc. It demonstrates that his death world cultivation has paid off, granting him powers that surpass even top-tier kings. The defeat of the Yasha King signals to all races that Xiao Chen is a force to be reckoned with. Readers should watch for the reactions of other kings and how this event reshapes the power balance. The pursuit of the Yasha King is not yet complete, and his escape may lead to further conflict. Additionally, note the use of the Eight Aspect Worlds in a new, more offensive capacity—this may be a precursor to even greater techniques.
