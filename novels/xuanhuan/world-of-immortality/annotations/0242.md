## Chapter Overview

This chapter pivots on a tense verbal duel between Xiao Chen (disguised as Xiao Shishui) and Zhao Lin'er, the Sun Religion Saintess. Zhao Lin'er uses psychological pressure and false information about a legendary spatial treasure to force Xiao Chen to reveal his identity. The emotional stakes are high, rooted in past grudges from Dragon Island. The tone is sharp and confrontational, with undercurrents of attraction and hostility. The chapter also sets up potential future alliances and escalates Xiao Chen's willingness to act openly.

## Key Plot Points

Zhao Lin'er methodically probes Xiao Chen's identity, first by calling him by his true name, then by sharing details about the Tianya Chichi treasure that could allow a return to the mortal world. Xiao Chen maintains his disguise as Xiao Shishui, but his emotional reaction to the prospect of returning home betrays him. Zhao Lin'er's clever trap forces him to admit his identity, leading to an explosive confrontation. Xiao Chen retaliates with brazen physicality, grabbing and holding her while daring her to call the guards. Despite her fury, Zhao Lin'er dismisses the knights and proposes a cooperation to find the treasure. Xiao Chen reveals that he killed the Tiger family demigod, shocking her. He then declares that he will no longer hide his identity and intends to fight openly, challenging all enemies. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen's firm decision to stand and face the coming storm, marking a turning point in his approach to the power struggles of the Eternal Continent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Sun Religion is a major Western cult in the World of Immortality, wielding political and military power through institutions like the Solar Knights and the Pope. Zhao Lin'er's title 'Saintess' signifies a pure, divinely chosen leader responsible for representing the religion and often guarding sacred artifacts. The Guan Shen divine art is a unique ability of the Saintess that allows her to see through disguises and perceive a person's true essence, making her a formidable investigator. The Tianya Chichi treasure is a legendary spatial artifact capable of bridging the unbridgeable distance between the Eternal Continent and the Mortal World—a prize that drives many cultivators to desperation. The Tiger Clan represents one of the ancient beast-blood lineages of the Central Plains, and killing a demigod of that clan is a provocative act that invites immediate retaliation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a fragile and ambiguous alliance between Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er. While they share the goal of returning home, their deep-seated enmity means any cooperation will be fraught with betrayal and second-guessing. Xiao Chen's decision to openly reveal himself signals a shift from stealth to direct confrontation, which will likely trigger immediate attacks from the Tiger Clan and other enemies. Readers should watch for how Zhao Lin'er's motives evolve—is she genuinely seeking reconciliation or merely using Xiao Chen as a tool? The mention of the Tianya Chichi treasure and the spatial-link divine artifact opens a new narrative arc: the possibility of returning to the mortal world, which could fundamentally shift the characters' priorities.
