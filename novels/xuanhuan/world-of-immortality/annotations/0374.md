## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal crescendo of violence as Xiao Chen's doppelgänger—the flesh-and-blood Wu Zhanhun—locks into a fateful duel with the Sacred Dragon King, while the true Xiao Chen, still a purple-diamond skeleton, demonstrates terrifying power by slaying a flying corpse king with a single concentrated Origin Sound. The chapter crackles with the tension of multiple king-level confrontations unfolding simultaneously across the plains outside Chang'an. The stakes are laid bare: the doppelgänger is not merely a copy but a separate entity with its own ambitions, and Xiao Chen himself is now a force that can kill a king—a feat that sends shockwaves through both mortal and death worlds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's doppelgänger already locked in a fierce, equal battle with the Sacred Dragon King, as the protagonists (Jin Sanyi, Liu Mu, Niu Ren) observe from the four directions. The doppelgänger wields the devastating 'Return to Origin' technique, channeling spatial energy from his left hand and temporal energy from his right—a sign of his mastery over the Eight Aspect Worlds and his own unique path. Meanwhile, the true Xiao Chen, having just slain a sacred dragon and a pair of night rakshasas outside Chang'an, arrives at the battlefield. He is shadowed by the Lich King (Wu Yao Wang), who watches but does not intervene. A flying corpse king arrives with reinforcements, intending to aid the Sacred Dragon King before the night raksha king himself arrives. But Xiao Chen acts decisively: using the concentrated 'Weng' sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin, he annihilates the flying corpse king and his retinue of zombies in a single, devastating strike. The killing of a king is witnessed by all—including the Lich King, the Fallen Angel Princess, and the War King—and it instantly elevates Xiao Chen's threat level in the eyes of the Hundred Clans. The doppelgänger, however, refuses outside help, insisting he will behead the Sacred Dragon King alone. The chapter ends with the sense that a new era of 'king-killing' has begun.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Syllables of Origin (本源八音) are a central mystery of Xiao Chen's cultivation. Derived from the Yellow-Clay Altar, these primordial sounds govern life and death; the 'Weng' syllable, in particular, can be concentrated into a beam of pure annihilation. Xiao Chen's ability to kill a king-level expert with a single focused blast underscores how rare and heaven-defying such a technique is. In the power hierarchy of the World of Immortality and the Death World, a 'king' (王) is a being who rules a region and commands immense fire-seed power. Killing one is a legendary feat that breaks the unspoken balance among the Hundred Clans. The doppelgänger's dual-hand technique—spatial left, temporal right—is a hallmark of his independent cultivation, diverging from Xiao Chen's original path. It draws from the same Eight Aspect Worlds foundation but has evolved into something uniquely his.

## Reading Guide

The central tension now is: who is the real Xiao Chen? The doppelgänger has his own soul, his own ambitions, and a declaration that he will marry Zhao Lin'er. The true Xiao Chen must decide whether to confront, absorb, or ally with this fragment of himself. Meanwhile, the night raksha king is still approaching, and the Sacred Dragon King's fate will draw even more powerful players into the fray. Pay close attention to the reactions of the Lich King and the War King—their restraint and sudden interest signal that Xiao Chen's display has shifted the political calculus. The question of how the true Xiao Chen can regain a physical body and reclaim his legacy is now more urgent than ever.
