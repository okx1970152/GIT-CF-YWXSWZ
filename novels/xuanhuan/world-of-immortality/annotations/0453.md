## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a dual climax of mythic proportions. On one front, Xiao Chen engages in a desperate, star-shattering duel against two Half-Ancestors—the Asura King and the Skeleton Sovereign. On another, Lan Nuo ascends to the Half-Ancestor realm and forges her Wisdom Sword in the heart of the sun, a feat of supreme courage and skill. The tone shifts between the raw, brutal violence of cosmic combat and the serene, sacred radiance of spiritual attainment. Xiao Chen, wearing an Ancestral God battle suit and wielding blood-stained War Swords, proves that a cultivator below the Half-Ancestor level can still challenge the mighty. Lan Nuo's achievement mirrors the ancient legends of Xing Tian and other primordial heroes who tempered their weapons in celestial fire. The chapter ends with a quiet, almost wordless nod between the two, sealing a shared history of transcendence.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's confrontation with the Asura Half-Ancestor and the Skeleton Sovereign unfolds in the starry sky beyond the mortal world. He wears the formidable Ancestral God battle suit, which absorbs the brunt of their attacks, and wields twenty-seven War Swords that he controls like extensions of his will. He uses drops of Ancestral God blood on his primary sword to inflict grievous wounds that even Half-Ancestors cannot easily heal. The battle destroys numerous small planets, with Xiao Chen taking heavy blows but steadily wearing down his opponents. The Asura Half-Ancestor is split in two by a single sword stroke, and the Skeleton Sovereign's bone staff shatters under the relentless assault. Meanwhile, on Mount Chimiti Cask, Lan Nuo completes her breakthrough. She severs her connection to thousands of stars, channels their essence into her body, and then flies directly into the sun to forge her Wisdom Sword using solar fire and her own spirit. She emerges radiant, holding a sword that dims the light of sun and moon. Returning to the mortal world, she briefly meets Xiao Chen, who nods to her and vanishes, his task complete. The chapter highlights the cost of such power—Xiao Chen is bloodied and weary, Lan Nuo has risked annihilation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter illuminates several key cultivation concepts in the World of Immortality. The 'Half-Ancestor' (半祖) realm represents a threshold of near-ancestral divinity, where the cultivator can manipulate celestial forces and forge soul-bound weapons. Lan Nuo's method of forging her Wisdom Sword in the sun's core echoes ancient Chinese myths of weapons tempered in heavenly fire, such as the legendary swords of Gan Jiang and Mo Ye. Xiao Chen's use of Ancestral God blood on his blade is a potent narrative device: blood of the primordial sages carries lingering divine power that can corrupt or destroy lesser beings. The Ancestral God battle suit he wears is a relic of unimaginable antiquity, providing near-absolute defense but not complete invulnerability—the Half-Ancestors' attacks still shake him through the armor. The War Swords themselves are fragments of a larger set (likely the twenty-four swords from earlier arcs), and their coordination hints at a formation or ultimate technique yet to be revealed. The concept of 'Che Di' (Earth-Penetrating) realm as Xiao Chen's current level is a reminder that cultivation is endless—even after defeating Half-Ancestors, there are higher peaks to climb.

## Reading Guide

As we move forward, watch for how Lan Nuo's new power and Wisdom Sword will influence the balance of the Hundred Races. Her ascension may attract unwanted attention from other Half-Ancestors or even the hidden sovereigns of the Death World. Xiao Chen's disappearance after the battle is deliberate; he may be recovering or seeking deeper cultivation. The War Swords and the Ancestral God battle suit remain mysterious—their full potential and origins are yet to be explored. The nod between Xiao Chen and Lan Nuo is a quiet acknowledgment of mutual respect, but their paths diverge. Expect that Lan Nuo's sword will soon taste battle, and Xiao Chen's return will shake the world again. The tension between mortal and immortal, human and foreign race, is far from resolved.
