## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a pivotal sacrificial battle that weaves together tragic family history, cosmic war, and the enduring legacy of the Nine Provinces' ancestors. The chapter opens with a reflective discussion on the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, the true ancestral gods of the Nine Provinces, whose meritorious deeds have been nearly lost to the ages. It then transitions to the planning of an all-out assault on the Otherworld's blood altar, a construction intended to summon a Holy Ancestor from the Sole True Realm. The emotional core of the chapter is the confrontation between the Human Demon Ge Qian and his father, the broken-winged ancestral dragon, who was imprisoned in the eighteenth layer of the primordial prison. Forced to fight his own son, the dragon, in a moment of restored clarity, chooses to lead a suicide charge directly into the blood altar, sacrificing himself and thirty stone men to thwart the Otherworld's plan. The chapter ends with Pangu King, Chiyou, and Xing Tian joining the offensive, and Xiao Chen summoning the seven Heavenly Steles to suppress the ninety-nine stone steps, escalating the war to its highest stakes.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter begins with Xiao Chen and Shi Zhongdi discussing the nature of true Emperors and Sovereigns—cultivators who reached the absolute pinnacle but were forced to leave for the Sole True Realm. This establishes the cosmic scale of the conflict. The assembled forces of the Nine Provinces, protected by the nine ancient lamps, assault the ninety-nine stone steps, aiming to destroy the blood altar. The Otherworld stone king reveals that he personally orchestrated the death of the Ancestral Dragon and has held Ge Qian's father captive for eons. He uses the broken-winged dragon as a weapon, forcing a father-son battle. However, the dragon defies expectations—he rounds up the thirty stone men and, with a heart-rending cry, crashes into the altar, triggering the activation of the Heavenly Steles. Pangu King, Chiyou, and Xing Tian then lead a direct charge against the stone kings. Xiao Chen, in a meditative state, summons all seven Heavenly Steles from across the cosmos to fall upon the stone steps, beginning the final suppression.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors (三皇五帝) are foundational figures in Chinese mythology, often credited with civilizing the ancient world. In the novel, they are elevated to peerless cultivators who protected the Nine Provinces and now exist beyond the mortal plane in the Sole True Realm. The concept of "Emperor" (皇) and "Sovereign" (帝) as supreme cultivation realms is explained: true Emperors cannot remain in the mundane world without causing its collapse, forcing them to depart. The blood altar is a ritual structure that uses sacrifice to bridge dimensions, a common trope in xianxia. The broken-winged dragon's self-sacrifice echoes the Confucian virtue of filial piety turned on its head—where the father chooses to die for his son's future, a tragic inversion of expected roles. The seven Heavenly Steles are ancient artifacts tied to the fate of the Nine Provinces, each containing the imprint of a supreme being, and their simultaneous summoning represents a catastrophic escalation of power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a must-read for understanding Ge Qian's tragic backstory and the depth of the war against the Otherworld. The broken-winged dragon's final roar, "Without wings, in hell looking up to heaven," is one of the novel's most emotional moments, symbolizing undying hope in utter despair. Pay attention to the alliance forming: Pangu King, the Human Demon, and Xiao Chen are now direct combatants against the stone kings. The failure of the blood altar summoning is a major setback for the Otherworld, but it also means they will escalate their efforts. Xiao Chen's connection to the Heavenly Steles is now fully activated—he functions as their focal point. Future chapters will likely explore the consequences of this battle, the fate of Ge Qian, and whether the stone kings can prevent the steles from sealing the passage. The nine ancient lamps also play a pivotal role; they are revealed to be the tombs of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, suggesting they may awaken at a critical moment.
