## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen returns to the Death World to settle old scores and prepare for the coming war, only to be confronted by a devastating discovery upon his return to the Eternal Continent. After refining the devil angel into a loyal puppet and destroying a projection of the elusive Xutian, he witnesses the Imperial City merging with the Celestial Emperor City and vanishing. The chapter culminates in a gut-wrenching revelation: the Ancestral Dragon Ship, once the vessel of hope for the Nine Provinces, lies decayed and empty, its passengers all dead. Xiao Chen's grief is palpable as he realizes he has missed the three-thousand-year appointment and that countless heroes perished. This chapter is a tragic turning point, blending action, mystery, and profound loss, setting the stage for Xiao Chen's renewed determination to avenge the fallen.

## Key Plot Points

- Xiao Chen visits the devil angel (Mo Tianshi) in the Death World, learning that Xutian has placed a bounty on him. He refines the devil angel into a loyal puppet, tasking it to gather forces from the Death World for a future assault on the Otherworld. This act underscores Xiao Chen's long-term strategic thinking and his willingness to use any means to build an army.
- Confronting Xutian's projection, Xiao Chen uses the Five Emperor Tower to destroy his demon city and shatter the projection. However, Xutian's true body escapes and vows to kill Xiao Chen in five hundred years, revealing that he is extremely cautious and never exposes his real form. This sets up a future conflict with a nearly untouchable enemy.
- Xiao Chen witnesses the seven demon cities burning and collapsing to empower the Imperial City, which then merges with the Celestial Emperor City and disappears. The emergence of the Celestial Emperor City, wielding the Pangu Axe, and its claim that the Heavenly Emperor never died, adds a layer of mystery to the Death World's ancient politics.
- Fleeing the scene as multiple Stone Man King-level divine senses probe the area, Xiao Chen returns to the Eternal Continent through a demon cave. He learns that thousands of years have passed, and the world has entered a new age of mythology.
- The most devastating plot point is Xiao Chen's discovery of the Ancestral Dragon Ship, completely rotted and abandoned. All its passengers—the heroes who left the Nine Provinces—are dead. The ship shows signs of a vicious battle, with black stains of blood. Xiao Chen suspects a black shadow from the Little Stone Emperor's tomb pursued them, leading to their demise. He is filled with grief and rage, knowing he missed the ancient appointment and cannot bring them back.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ancestral Dragon Ship (祖龙船) is a legendary vessel built from the bones of an Ancestral Dragon and the wood of the World Tree, capable of crossing the forbidden seas and even traversing between worlds. It was used to transport the greatest heroes of the Nine Provinces, such as Yao, Shun, Yu, Xing Tian, and Chi You, to safety. Its decay and the death of its passengers symbolize the extinguishing of an age's hope. The Five Emperor Tower (五帝塔) is a primordial stone artifact said to rival the Three Sovereigns Mirror, containing the power and imprints of five ancient emperors. Using it as the foundation for his divine city, Xiao Chen gains a formidable weapon. The concept of the Celestial Emperor (天帝) being a single entity who never died, rather than a title, adds depth to the mythology of the Death World and its interactions with the Otherworld.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a crucial turning point in Xiao Chen's emotional journey. The grief he experiences upon discovering the Ancestral Dragon Ship's fate will likely fuel his actions in the coming arcs. Readers should pay close attention to the black shadow that Xiao Chen suspects pursued the ship—it may be a key antagonist yet to be revealed. The appearance of the Celestial Emperor City and the immortality of the Heavenly Emperor suggest that ancient powers are not as dead as they seem. Additionally, Xutian's true body remains a threat, hidden and plotting. The five-hundred-year deadline he gave Xiao Chen may be a ticking clock for future events. Xiao Chen's plan to unite the Death World's forces hints at a massive war ahead, and the alliances he forges there will be critical.
