## Chapter Overview

In the ancient capital of Chang'an, the battle between the young generation's top experts reaches its crescendo. Xiao Chen, having declared he would kill Ye Tian "like butchering a dog," engages the Six Excellences—the legendary prodigies of the cultivation world. Despite the intervention of Xia Ding, Ling Xuyan, Fang Tianqi, and Ming Shi, Xiao Chen's overwhelming power proves unstoppable. This chapter showcases the terrifying might of a true martial body, as Xiao Chen's physical strength and battlefield dominance dwarf even the most formidable treasures. The night becomes a blood-soaked testament to his inexorable rise, with two of the Six Excellences falling before his hands. The desolate atmosphere of the moonlit capital is shattered by explosive combat, leaving the remaining young masters shaken to their core.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen begins by tearing through the defenses of the four intervening experts, using the Indestructible Imperial Heavenly Bell as a weapon after wrestling it from Ye Tian. He smashes the nine bronze tripods of Xia Ding, then crushes Ye Tian's body and extracts his consciousness, killing him utterly. The bell itself shatters under the strain but is immediately absorbed into Xiao Chen's eighty-one divine acupoints, where it begins to reform. Next, Xia Ding challenges Xiao Chen to single combat, unleashing his nine ancient bronze tripods that exude chaotic ancestral power. Xiao Chen counters with the Eight Aspect Worlds and the Eight Primordial Sounds, breaking the tripods' sacrificial formation. In the climax, Xiao Chen unleashes the Reverse Dragon Seven Steps—stepping onto and shattering each tripod, and ultimately crushing Xia Ding's body and soul with the seventh step. The remaining three—Ling Xuyan, Fang Tianqi, and Ming Shi—are left staring at the carnage, now resolved to fight together against this unstoppable foe. The chapter ends with the ancient capital in uproar, the myth of the Six Excellences permanently shattered.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter highlights several core xianxia concepts. The Six Excellences represent the top young geniuses of the cultivation world (修真界), a realm separate from the mortal world. The cultivation level known as Nirvana (涅槃) or Calamity Fire (劫火) is the fourth major realm, where practitioners undergo a transformative rebirth—Xiao Chen's explosive growth is attributed to this. The Reverse Dragon Seven Steps is a legendary dragon clan combat art that draws on the power of the earth and heavens to crush opponents. The "Upper Heaven Hand" appears to be a manifestation of Xiao Chen's martial body, allowing him to channel raw physical power. The nine bronze tripods of Xia Ding are modeled after the legendary Nine Tripods of Yu, vessels of supreme authority that can seal and destroy. The Indestructible Imperial Heavenly Bell is revealed to be a sealed half-ancestor artifact, so powerful that even shattering it cannot destroy it; it reforms stronger each time. The concept of soul-sealing and the "living sacrifice" ritual (活祭) illustrates the dark, sacrificial nature of some cultivation arts.

## Reading Guide

With two of the Six Excellences dead, the power dynamics among the younger generation have shifted dramatically. The remaining three—Ling Xuyan, Fang Tianqi, and Ming Shi—are now united against Xiao Chen, setting up a three-on-one confrontation of unprecedented scale. Xiao Chen's absorption of the Imperial Heavenly Bell into his acupoint world hints at a new weapon that will grow with him. Meanwhile, the older powers watching from the shadows, including the half-ancestors and the elder generation of the cultivation world, may now be forced to intervene. The chapter also leaves Xiao Chen's status as a "martial body" ascendant; the implications for the wider conflict between the mortal world and the other world are profound. Readers can anticipate a savage, multi-sided battle as the resentment of the fallen Six Excellences' factions boils over.
