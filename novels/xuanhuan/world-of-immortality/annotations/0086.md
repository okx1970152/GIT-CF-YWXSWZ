## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a dramatic shift from the political and martial conflicts of the previous arcs into a realm of supernatural horror and cosmic mystery. After a night of slaughter among the island's factions, the bone sea becomes the stage for an eerie supernatural event: a ghostly ancient city emerges amidst yellow rain and black lightning, with blood soon pouring from the sky. The chapter blends the desolate, tragic atmosphere of the novel with mythic horror, raising the stakes beyond mortal cultivator squabbles. The death of Yizhen's senior brother Yichi adds a personal layer of grief and foreboding, while the revelation of a mysterious woman wielding the power of time hints at forces far beyond the Mortal-Shedding realm. The tone is one of awe and dread, as the characters must confront the unknown.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the aftermath of the night's chaos: Yan Qingcheng is taken captive by Xiao Chen, who delegates her training to Liu Ruyan. However, the narrative quickly pivots to a supernatural event—the weather turns unnatural, with yellow wind and black lightning, followed by a downpour of yellow rain that turns into blood. Amid this, a colossal ancient city materializes in the bone sea, its gates open to reveal ghostly soldiers drilling inside. A Buddha skull is embedded in the city gate, suggesting that even a figure of such power once fought against this city. Practitioners who flee into the nearby forest are killed by trees that become demonic. The various alliances cease their conflict and unite in fear. Then, a wounded Yizhen arrives, reporting that a mysterious figure shrouded in black mist killed his brother Yichi with three sword strikes that shattered his body into eighteen pieces. Yizhen was saved only by a woman in colored mist who used a divine law—specifically, the power of time—to slow the attacker. This revelation shocks everyone present, as time manipulation is legendary and seldom seen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter heavily draws on the Chinese tradition of the Ghost Festival (鬼节), believed to be a day when the gates of the underworld open and spirits roam the earth. The author uses this folk belief to create a sense of cosmic horror, as the island itself becomes a liminal space between life and death. The appearance of the ancient city with a Buddha skull embedded in its walls evokes the idea that even the most enlightened beings have contended with this malevolent force. In xianxia cosmology, divine laws (神则) are the highest order of power, governing the fabric of reality. The woman's use of time power places her at a level far above the Mortal-Shedding realm, hinting at the presence of beings like the vanished gods. The bone sea itself has been a site of mystery, and the disappearance of the divine stele suggests that the city's arrival may be connected to the island's ancient sealing.

## Reading Guide
This chapter opens a new layer of mystery in the Dragon Island arc. Readers should watch for further revelations about the ghost city and its purpose—does it house the suppressed dragons, or is it the prison of something darker? The identity of the black-mist killer and the woman with time power will be crucial to the plot; her intervention suggests she may have her own agenda regarding the city. Yizhen's grief and loss may drive him to seek revenge or deeper understanding, potentially leading to a new character arc. Xiao Chen's analytical nature will likely lead him to investigate the city's connection to the missing stele and the gods. The chapter also sets up a temporary truce among the factions, but this unity is fragile and will surely break. The scale of the mystery has escalated, leaving readers eager for answers.
