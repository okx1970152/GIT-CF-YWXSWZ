## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks Xiao Chen's first major cultivation breakthrough on Dragon Island, ascending to the Mortal-Shedding Sixth Heaven. The breakthrough is accompanied by an awe-inspiring manifestation of dragon shadows circling his body, a visible sign of his unique acupoint-divinization path. However, this display of power attracts the greed of the island's factions. The chapter masterfully contrasts the sublime beauty of the snow-covered peaks with the ugly, incited greed of the crowd. Xiao Chen's response is characteristically cold and provocative—he nonchalantly reveals he has eaten a Lion King Dragon egg as breakfast, driving the mob into a frenzy. The tone is one of gathering storm, balancing Xiao Chen's newfound strength against the weight of collective opposition.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen standing on a snow peak, his dragon shadow manifestation shocking the onlookers. This phenomenon stuns the watching cultivators, who recognize it as a sign of immense progress. The crowd, incited by agents of the Natural Alliance (Yalode) and Treefolk Valley (Zhao Lin'er), demands he hand over his dragon eggs. In a calculated move, Xiao Chen retrieves the broken Lion King Dragon egg and hurls it across the valley, revealing he has already consumed it. His blunt declaration that he ate the egg for breakfast infuriates the mob, as they realize such a treasure has been 'wasted.' Amid the escalating tension, Xiao Chen orders the three skeleton kings to retreat with the remaining eggs into the snowy depths, protecting their hard-won assets. He also sends Keke away with a promise of food. The chapter ends with the first brave souls charging up the peak, setting the stage for a bloody confrontation. Notably, Yan Qingcheng and the other top experts are absent, suggesting they are watching from the shadows.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter reinforces the concept of acupoint divinization (穴道神化), Xiao Chen's unique cultivation path. By consuming dragon eggs and crystals, he purifies his acupoints into nodes of concentrated life essence, which now manifest as dragon shadows when his emotions run high. This is a classic xianxia trope—the physical manifestation of one's internal power. Mortal-Shedding is the first true cultivation realm, and its six heavenly tiers represent significant leaps in combat ability. The Lion King Dragon egg is a legendary treasure; consuming it directly is considered extraordinarily wasteful by most cultivators, who would rather hatch or refine it. This highlights Xiao Chen's pragmatic ruthlessness: he prioritizes immediate power over long-term potential. The crowd's incitement by hidden agents reflects the political scheming common in xianxia—alliances manipulate public opinion to weaken rivals without direct conflict.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the absent figures: Yan Qingcheng, Rand, Kailuo, and Wang Tong are not present in the mob, indicating they are either biding their time or pursuing other objectives. The three skeletons' retreat with the remaining dragon eggs sets up a future plotline: will Xiao Chen's 'dragon raising plan' come to fruition? The mob's charge is inevitable, but Xiao Chen's calm preparation suggests he has a plan. The chapter's core conflict revolves around the clash between individual cultivation progress and collective greed—a recurring theme in cultivation stories. Next chapter likely delivers the long-awaited battle, where Xiao Chen's Sixth Heaven power will be tested against multiple foes. Watch for how his dragon shadow ability evolves in combat.
