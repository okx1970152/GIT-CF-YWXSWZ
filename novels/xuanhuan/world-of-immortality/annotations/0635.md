## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen, wielding the peerless Three Sovereign Mirror, makes a desperate break into the Otherworld, drawing the pursuing Stone Kings and Celestial Realm giants into the heart of enemy territory. His gambit: to force a confrontation between the invading kings and the Otherworld's four founding ancestors, using the mysterious and deadly Ninety-Nine Stone Steps as the battlefield. The chapter is a relentless, high-stakes chase that balances tactical cunning against the overwhelming weight of ancestral power. As Xiao Chen ascends the ominous stone staircase, the fate of the Mirror—and of the Nine Provinces—hangs on a knife's edge.

## Key Plot Points
Using the Three Sovereign Mirror's reflective ability, Xiao Chen tears open the world barrier and flees into the Otherworld, with Lu Zhan and several Stone Kings in hot pursuit. To buy time and sow chaos, Xiao Chen destroys a floating island containing a nascent demonic city that harbored a future Stone King—a grave blow to the Otherworld's future power. The Old Skeleton covertly assists by intercepting the fleeing city. Xiao Chen suffers near-fatal shattering from the combined force of multiple Stone Kings, but is saved by Life-Reviving Grass taken from the Little Stone Emperor's tomb, which quickly mends his body and soul. Qingqing awakens from the Dream Flower and adds her power. The four founding ancestors of the Otherworld finally manifest—Lu Zhan, Lang Ya (a spirit without body), and two silent, abyssal figures. Xiao Chen ascends the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, deliberately putting himself at the heart of the forbidden zone, and forces the pursuing kings to decide whether to follow him into that cursed place. The chapter closes with the ancestors marching upward, the kings hesitating, and the Mirror pulsing with unknown potential.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps are a recurring symbol of forbidden transcendence in the novel—a staircase that leads to a mysterious sealed door, embodying the Otherworld's greatest mystery and threat. The four founding ancestors are beings who have survived since the primordial age; their awakening signals an escalation beyond ordinary Ancestral Gods. The Three Sovereign Mirror, ranked as the third-greatest stone artifact in the world, can reflect any attack and even replicate techniques—a powerful tool that Xiao Chen uses to multiply the damage he deals. The Life-Reviving Grass, cultivated with a Stone King's blood, is an example of extreme healing treasure, highlighting the resources available to the most ancient cultivators.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a monumental standoff. Will the pursuing kings dare to follow Xiao Chen onto the stone steps, risking the unknown horrors at the summit? The behavior of the two silent ancestors—especially their coordination—hints at a deeper unity among the four. Xiao Chen's grasp on the Mirror remains tenuous; its power drains him even as it protects him. Readers should watch for cracks in the alliance between the Otherworld ancestors and the invading kings, as Xiao Chen's entire plan relies on pitting them against each other. The next chapters will likely reveal whether the steps hold a path to survival or a trap for all.
