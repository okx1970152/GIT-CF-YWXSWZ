## Chapter Overview

The Martial Imprint, a being of immense but deceptive power, has finally revealed its true purpose. After months of pretending to select disciples in the Soul World, it suddenly abandons the farce and pursues its real target: the legendary Burial Valley of Arms. This chapter pivots from political observation to high-stakes discovery, as Xiao Chen and the ancient general Bai Qi are drawn into a race against time. The atmosphere shifts from the crowded, tense streets of Mangzhe City to the desolate wasteland where ancient secrets lie. The appearance of a guardian giant skull and the opening of a world gate signal that the Martial Imprint's schemes are far grander than anyone anticipated.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen observes the Martial Imprint's 'selection' of disciples in Mangzhe City, noting among the chosen the unlikely figures of Totti, Zhuge Liang, and even the stuttering Jin Sanyi. He meets Wu Ming, the artifact-refining genius, and they share drinks while discussing the gathered kings – War King Mabaoo, Battle God King, Yaksha Queen, Earth King, Sea God King, and more. A key moment is the appearance of a golden-haired boy, the son of the Golden Lion King, whose very presence reminds Xiao Chen of that formidable opponent. Suddenly, the Martial Imprint vanishes, sensing something at Bai Qi's ancient castle. Xiao Chen follows immediately, arriving to find Bai Qi confronting the Martial Imprint. The true purpose is revealed: the Martial Imprint seeks the Burial Valley of Arms, a sealed realm where ancient weapons are interred. It performs a massive summoning ritual, attracting the attention of all powerful beings. A gigantic guardian skull smashes down, but the Martial Imprint withstands it and forcibly opens a world gate, crying out for the Martial Ancestor as it enters. Xiao Chen and Bai Qi follow, with dozens of experts closing in behind them.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Martial Imprint (武之印记) is a mysterious entity that is not a true ancestor but possesses powers that frustrate even half-ancestors. Its ability to summon divine souls and open sealed realms reflects esoteric cultivation arts. The Burial Valley of Arms (葬兵谷) is a legendary location where divine weapons are laid to rest – a trope in xianxia representing concentrated power and danger. The guardian giant skull is reminiscent of ancient protective spirits bound to sacred sites. The kings mentioned – Earth King, Sea God King, Fire Spirit King – represent elemental cultivation lineages common in Chinese mythology. The hierarchy of realms (Perfected Man, Earth-Penetrating, Half-Ancestor) frames the vast power gaps between characters.

## Reading Guide

The valley likely contains weapons that could tip the balance of power. Readers should watch for the Golden Lion King's son – he may reappear with his father. The Martial Imprint's past lies are now exposed, but its power is undeniable. The next chapters will likely involve fierce battles and discoveries within the Burial Valley, potentially revealing secrets about the death world and the war swords.
