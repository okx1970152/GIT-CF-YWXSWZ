## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a masterclass in controlled brutality. Xiao Chen, already wounded from a prior clash with Dugu Jianmo, turns his assassin into a living interrogation tool, forcing the truth from the Basde brothers. The confession reveals a web of enemies: the Rigen family’s Hofman, petty jealousy over Yan Qingcheng, and a paranoid prophecy tied to the Basde spear. But the chapter’s true center is the climactic duel with Yulanmu, who unleashes the Ancient War Triple Seal—a technique of legendary confinement. Xiao Chen’s response is both a testament to his growing mastery and a brutal declaration of intent. The clash leaves him broken but closer than ever to the Spirit-Treasury realm.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen holding Morica aloft on the Basde spear, forcing Yulanmu to answer for the ambush. Morica’s confession lays bare three motives: Hofman’s bounty, resentment over Yan Qingcheng’s betrothal, and the brothers’ own premonition from the spear’s power. Xiao Chen dismisses the prophecy as self-serving. When Morica dies from his wounds, Yulanmu attacks with the Ancient War Triple Seal—a rare technique from the War Clan lineage that seals heaven, earth, and the practitioner himself. For a moment, Xiao Chen is frozen, stabbed by Yulanmu’s sword. But Xiao Chen, having already touched the edge of Spirit-Treasury, breaks free and kills Yulanmu with the spear. The victory is costly: his body is riddled with wounds, his hand bones broken from an earlier clash with Dugu Jianmo. Yet the pressure has pushed him to the very threshold of the next realm. He flies home, plants the Phoenix feather as a ward, and awaits the dawn.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ancient War Triple Seal (封天封地封人) is a hallmark technique of the War Clan (战族), a legendary race of warriors whose battle arts could manipulate the energies of heaven and earth. Yulanmu, as a descendant of a small Western tribe, can only approximate its power, but it still nearly immobilizes Xiao Chen completely. The Basde spear is another relic of a bygone age—a weapon said to have slain gods. Its ability to grant prophetic insight to its wielder hints at a deeper connection between weapons and fate in this world. Xiao Chen’s acknowledgment that he has “touched the true Spirit-Treasury” signals the beginning of his formal entry into the second cultivation realm, a critical step where the body’s hidden treasures begin to open.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen is now at the critical juncture: severely wounded but on the verge of a major breakthrough. The Phoenix feather he planted as a talisman suggests he expects retaliation, likely from the Rigen family. Dugu Jianmo’s decision to let him leave rather than strike while he was down hints at a code of honor that may shift future alliances or rivalries. The next chapters will likely pivot into Xiao Chen’s intensive recovery and the formal breach into Spirit-Treasury. Readers should watch for how he uses this new power to settle old scores, particularly with Hofman and the Rigen clan. The mention of a “Phoenix feather” also promises that the Phoenix Saintess’s favor may soon be called upon.
