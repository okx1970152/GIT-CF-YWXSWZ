## Chapter Overview

Chapter 365 delivers a masterclass in strategic combat and ruthless counterattack. Xiao Chen, now a Yellow Diamond skeleton in the world of the dead, faces the duplicitous Giant King, who breaks his word and ambushes him with ten elite Yellow Diamond second-tier fire seed beings. The chapter crackles with tension as Xiao Chen fights desperately against overwhelming odds, using the 'Weng' sound and the Hand of the Supreme Being to carve a path through his enemies. But the true brilliance lies in the reversal: after a clever bluff forces the Giant King to chase shadows, Xiao Chen turns the tables completely, launching a lightning raid deep into the Giant King's territory. The tone is a blend of grim survival, tactical cunning, and the cold satisfaction of turning an enemy's scheme against them.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen discovering that the Giant King is a secret super-evolutionary being, tipped off by an owl messenger that self-destructs. This revelation forces Xiao Chen to reconsider his upcoming duel, but before he can plan, the Giant King strikes preemptively: ten Yellow Diamond second-rank fire seed beings encircle Nightmare City, joined by the white-jade Giant King himself, who bears a golden lotus mark on his brow—a hallmark of super-evolutionary status. Xiao Chen fights savagely, using the 'Weng' sound to disrupt his foes and the Hand of the Supreme Being to shatter their bones. He kills two of the ten, but the Giant King holds back, observing. Realizing he is outnumbered and outmaneuvered, Xiao Chen bluffs by shouting for the Beast King (another powerful ruler) to join the fight, creating a moment of distraction. In that instant, he breaks through the encirclement, killing a third enemy, and escapes at incredible speed. The Giant King, enraged, pursues but cannot catch Xiao Chen due to his Eight Aspect Worlds speed.

Rather than flee, Xiao Chen executes a stunning counterattack: he reverses course and storms five major cities under Giant King's domain, killing each Yellow Diamond second-tier city lord and absorbing their fire seeds. This bold strike weakens the Giant King's power base and leaves the eastern region in turmoil. The Giant King, after fruitlessly searching for Xiao Chen near Beast King City, receives news of the raids and must race back to protect his capital—but it may be too late.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens the lore of the Dead World's hierarchy. Fire seed beings are ranked by the quality of their bones: from white to blue, purple, and finally yellow diamond (黄钻). Xiao Chen has reached Yellow Diamond second-tier, making him a formidable 'king' among his peers. However, the Giant King is revealed as a 'super-evolutionary' (级进化者), a rare being whose bones are white jade and bear a golden lotus mark—identical to the Three Kings (Qin Guang Wang, Yan Luo Wang, Lun Hui Wang) from earlier arcs. This suggests that super-evolutionary status is tied to a special lineage or awakening, and that the three skeleton companions Xiao Chen lost may have been of this class. The 'Weng' sound (嗡字天音) is one of the Eight Origin Sounds, a primordial technique that governs life and death; in the Dead World, it can shatter weaker souls but is less effective against concentrated fire seeds. The Hand of the Supreme Being (上苍之手) remains Xiao Chen's signature martial art, allowing him to destroy divine artifacts and bones through sheer physical power.

The tactic of raiding an enemy's territory while they pursue you is a classic strategic gambit seen in Sun Tzu's Art of War, and here it demonstrates Xiao Chen's growth from a reactive survivor to a proactive hunter.

## Reading Guide

This chapter showcases Xiao Chen's strategic evolution. Pay attention to how he uses deception (the false call to Beast King) and speed to turn the tide. The Giant King's golden lotus mark is a crucial clue—it connects to the Three Kings' origins and may hint at a deeper mystery about the Dead World's ancient rulers. The reference to Beast King (兽王) introduces a third major power in the eastern region, setting up potential three-way conflicts. Xiao Chen's raid on the five cities yields five powerful fire seeds, which he can use to break through to the next realm—this will be a key power-up. Watch for the Giant King's reaction: his humiliation may drive him to more desperate measures. This chapter also reinforces the Dead World's law of the jungle: only the strongest and most cunning survive, and alliances are temporary tools.

Overall, Chapter 365 is a satisfying blend of brutal combat and clever strategy, proving that Xiao Chen's mind is as sharp as his skeleton.
