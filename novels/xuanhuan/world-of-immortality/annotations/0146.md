## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen steps into the bustling yet treacherous streets of Heavenly Emperor City, only to be drawn into a web of petty provocations orchestrated by unseen hands. The chapter opens with a brawl against the soldiers of Prince Li Sunmiao, a seemingly minor clash that reveals the complex political undercurrents of the city. Xiao Chen quickly deduces that the real enemy is not the prince but a hidden schemer using the prince's guards as pawns. Meanwhile, Prince Sunmiao hosts a banquet for the young elites of the city's most powerful families, where the name 'Xiao Chen' becomes a lightning rod. The gathering introduces several key players—Kanasi of the Mandel family, the insidious Zhuge Ming, the perceptive Hai Yunxue, and the arrival of Yan Qingcheng, whose presence signals the ongoing entanglement between Xiao Chen and the Undying Sect. The chapter balances action, deduction, and political intrigue, setting the stage for a deeper conflict in the city.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen is waylaid by a military captain (校尉) and several soldiers under the banner of Prince Li Sunmiao. The captain attempts to humiliate him but is brutally slapped down; Xiao Chen shatters their blades with his bare hands and hurls them into the river after extracting information. He learns the captain was bribed by a scar-faced thug, who in turn is a pawn for a larger power. Xiao Chen tracks down the scar-faced man and confirms the plot: someone wants to provoke a conflict between him and the prince. He chooses not to pursue the chain further, recognizing the mastermind has covered their tracks. The prince receives word of the incident and, after initial fury, orders an investigation. A squad of soldiers, including two Mortal-Shedding Fourth Heaven guards, attempts to arrest Xiao Chen but is effortlessly defeated. Xiao Chen leaves a message for the prince: 'I am a low-key person; don't provoke me.' The scene shifts to Prince Sunmiao's banquet, where local elites have gathered. He asks about Xiao Chen and receives varied reactions. Kanasi angrily labels Xiao Chen a 'super hooligan,' recalling her earlier humiliation. Zhuge Ming smirks with veiled contempt. Hai Yunxue, sister of Hai Yuntian, deflects to the newly arrived Yan Qingcheng. Yan Qingcheng's entrance is described with classical beauty and marks the chapter's close, hinting at her continued role in Xiao Chen's fate.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the political landscape of the Eternal Continent. Heavenly Emperor City is a semi-independent territory under the nominal control of the Tianluo Kingdom. Prince Li Sunmiao represents the royal court, but his authority is openly challenged by the city lord, Liu Daoming. This tension filters down to street-level provocations: the prince's soldiers are used as tools to ensnare Xiao Chen in a conflict with the crown. The concept of 'face' (面子) is central: the prince cannot ignore an attack on his men, yet Xiao Chen's direct defiance also makes him a target. The gathering of young elites (精英) from families like Mandel, Zhuge, and Hai showcases the intricate social hierarchy. Each family wields economic or martial power. The upcoming beast-fighting tournament—the 'Superbeast Arena'—drives much of the activity, as spirit beasts and dragon kings are prized assets. Yan Qingcheng's presence confirms that the Undying Sect (不死门) is monitoring Xiao Chen, and her cultivation, now at Mortal-Shedding Sixth Heaven after her trials, makes her a formidable force.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is a pivot from the mythic wilderness of Dragon Island to the political intrigue of a human city. Xiao Chen's restraint in not escalating the conflict with the prince shows growth—he avoids unnecessary bloodshed while making a statement. Watch for how Prince Sunmiao will respond; his pride is wounded, but he is also intelligent enough to see the manipulation. The banquet scene introduces several characters who will likely play roles in the coming arcs: Kanasi (whose earlier loss to Xiao Chen is a subplot), Zhuge Ming (from the beast-fighting merchant family), and Hai Yunxue (whose brother Hai Yuntian is an ally). Yan Qingcheng's arrival is especially significant: she is now a captive turned potential partner under the 'Shatter Demon, Plant God' technique, and her relationship with Xiao Chen is fraught with tension. The chapter also hints at a hidden enemy trying to embroil Xiao Chen in city politics. The upcoming beast-fighting tournament will likely be the stage for the next major conflict.
