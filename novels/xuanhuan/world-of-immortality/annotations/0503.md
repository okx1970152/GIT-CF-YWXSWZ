## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen's solitary ordeal in the sealed Heavenly Emperor City reaches its cataclysmic zenith. After five hundred years of relentless body destruction and rebirth—each cycle more agonizing than the last—he finally shatters the barrier between the mortal and the divine. This chapter is a monument to suffering transformed into power: Xiao Chen ascends to the Half-Ancestor realm, absorbing lightning tribulations from multiple worlds, fusing the souls of ancient kings into his own being, and mastering thirty War Swords that surge with the echo of a primordial array. The chapter closes with the sudden assault of three legendary weapons, a grim reminder that even transcendent power buys no peace.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen trapped inside a cage of stone ribs within the Heavenly Emperor City, his body exploding into gory fragments for the thirty-ninth time in a single half-hour. A resonant voice from the city's heart declares that every supreme cultivator must endure such torment to forge an unbreakable will. Over the next decade, Xiao Chen reaches the ninth heaven of the Earth-Penetrating realm through repeated shattering and reformation. In the thirteenth year, he thrusts one foot into the Half-Ancestor realm, becoming a lower-ranked Battle Saint. Resolving to be more, he throws himself into the full breakthrough. Lightning from every world descends upon the city; Xiao Chen absorbs it all, simultaneously dissolving the lingering souls of former Half-Ancestors and kings he had refined into his body. Their power merges with his, making him a Half-Ancestor whose combat strength equals several of his peers combined. The thirty War Swords—drenched in his blood—erupt from his being, and a broken celestial array diagram manifests around him, integrating with his flesh. A hundred-year meditation follows, in which he comprehends the array's secrets, refines his Eight Aspect Worlds and Origin Eight Sounds, and hones his body to a new peak. After five centuries, he steps out of the stone cage, clutching the half-skull of the Stone Man. The Heavenly Emperor City returns to the world of immortality. Immediately, three apocalyptic artifacts—the Immortal Shield, the Demon Blade, and a thirty-three-layered tower—shatter the void and attack the city, heralding an immediate trial of his ascended might.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Half-Ancestor realm (半祖) represents the threshold between mortal cultivation and the supreme Ancestral God level. In xianxia tradition, such breakthroughs often require 'body refining' through systematic destruction and rebirth, as epitomized by Xiao Chen's centuries of suffering. The lightning tribulation from multiple worlds is a common motif, testing the cultivator's preparedness; here, it mirrors Keke's effortless awakening, highlighting the contrast between innate genius and hard-won power. The 'Thirty War Swords' and their broken array diagram evoke the Great Derivation (大衍之数), a Daoist concept where completeness (forty-nine) leaves one variable to fate. Xiao Chen's cultivation path—merging the Martial Imprint, ancestral artifacts, and the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree—reflects the syncretic nature of this world's power system.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen has finally reached the Half-Ancestor realm, but his trial is far from over. The sudden assault of the Immortal Shield, Demon Blade, and thirty-three-layered tower suggests that rival powers—possibly the Otherworld Ancestral God Babra or the Soul World's sovereigns—have been watching and now strike while his power is still untested. Pay close attention to how the broken War Sword array and his Ancestral God War Armor will fare against these weapon-spirits. The half-skull of the Stone Man remains a wildcard; its properties may prove decisive. This chapter sets the stage for the next phase of Xiao Chen's journey: from ascendant victor to besieged defender, fighting not only enemies but the very laws of the heavens.
