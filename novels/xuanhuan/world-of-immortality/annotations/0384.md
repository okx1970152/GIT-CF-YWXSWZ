## Chapter Overview

This chapter presents a tense standoff at the threshold of an ancient ruin, where the white beast Keke, with a comically scrawled wooden sign, blocks the advance of a coalition of foreign kings. The atmosphere is one of precarious balance—the Hundred Races vie for entry while Keke, armed with the Lost Paradise, refuses to yield. Xiao Chen, observing from the shadows as a yellow diamond skeleton, grapples with his hidden reunion with Keke and the desperate need to refine life source liquid for his resurrection. The discovery of a War Sword sealed in a stone tower shatters expectations, hinting at a far greater number of these legendary weapons than previously known, and deepening the mystery of the ruins.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Keke’s defiant wooden sign, which amuses even the hostile kings. Snow Dance, a white-robed maiden, single-handedly slays several Sacred Dragons, demonstrating her terrifying power. The foreign kings split into two camps—one led by War King Jingpu and a yellow diamond skeleton, the other by the Golden Giant King and Zhao Zhongyang—and a chaotic battle erupts. Keke, from within the Lost Paradise, occasionally unleashes seven-colored light, preventing any faction from advancing. Xiao Chen seizes the chaos to collect dragon corpses, refining them into life source liquid over several days, which visibly strengthens his soul and increases his affinity with the Heavenly Stele methods. As the group pushes deeper into the ruins, they encounter a massive stone tower with formidable seals. Keke ignores it, leading the kings to break it open, revealing a single War Sword—one that is clearly distinct from the twenty-four swords Xiao Chen previously encountered. This discovery raises the terrifying possibility that there are far more War Swords, perhaps as many as forty-nine, as the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden once prophesied.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of life source liquid (生命源液) is central to Xiao Chen’s resurrection in the Dead World. Refined from the flesh and soul of living beings, it allows undead skeletons to regain flesh and blood, bridging the gap between death and life. The War Swords (战剑) are tied to the cosmological principle of the Great Derivation (大衍之数)—fifty paths, of which forty-nine are used, leaving one as the unchanging constant. This implies a set of forty-nine War Swords, far exceeding the twenty-four Xiao Chen once possessed. The Hundred Races (百族) are ancient lineages emerging in the era of chaos, each with unique origins and bloodlines. Keke's Lost Paradise (失乐园) is revealed as a mobile sanctuary filled with divine herbs, acting both as a treasure house and a defensive retreat.

## Reading Guide

The discovery of a War Sword unrelated to the previous set reshapes the entire power structure of the world. Readers should watch for how many more such swords exist and what ancient power they serve. The tension between the original Xiao Chen (in skeleton form) and his flesh-and-blood doppelgänger remains unresolved—the clone's separate ambitions may soon collide with the original’s plans. Keke’s resolute defense of the ruins suggests she knows more than she lets on; her instinct to avoid the stone tower hints at deeper knowledge of the dangers within. The Hundred Races are now fully active, and their conflicts will shape the Nine Provinces’ future. Xiao Chen’s cultivation through life source liquid will accelerate, potentially allowing him to reclaim a physical form and confront his enemies directly.
