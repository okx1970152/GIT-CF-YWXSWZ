## Chapter Overview

This chapter bridges the Death World and the Mortal World, following Xiao Chen's return as a Yellow-Diamond skeleton. After a brutal confrontation with the Yasha King and Skeleton King alongside the War Clan warrior Mabao, Xiao Chen absorbs the Skeleton King's fire seed and ascends to a new tier of power. Back in the Death World, he seeks out the three dominant kings of the eastern region—the Giant King, Hundred Battle King, and Beast King—to learn the forbidden art of Life Source Liquid refinement. The chapter closes with an ominous portent: a five-colored skeleton walking across an ancient battlefield, hinting at powers that may eclipse even the current rulers. The tone shifts from visceral combat to calculated negotiation, ending on a note of mystery and impending danger.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's time in the Mortal World expires, pulling him back to the Death World through the bone well. Before departing, he tears open the Yasha King's chest, gravely wounding him but not securing a kill. In the Death World, he visits the divine tribe village where the elder Li Mu has retrieved a wounded Sacred Dragon for food, symbolizing the changing times. Xiao Chen then travels to Giant City to confront the three kings—Giant King, Hundred Battle King, and Beast King—and demand the method for refining Life Source Liquid. Initially resistant, the kings finally reveal the secret after much pressure. The refinement process involves harnessing living souls and life energy, a dark art that Xiao Chen decides to use on his enemies in the Nine Provinces. The chapter ends with the appearance of a five-colored skeleton, radiating sacred light, on the silent ancient battlefield, suggesting a new player entering the stage.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Life Source Liquid (生命源液) is a rare substance in the Death World, refined from the essence of living beings. It accelerates cultivation and purifies fire seeds, making it highly sought after but morally questionable. The three kings—Giant King, Hundred Battle King, and Beast King—represent the major power blocs in the eastern Death World. Their willingness to part with the refinement method indicates the shifting balance of power. The five-colored skeleton is a new type of being, its bones glowing with divine light, reminiscent of the highest evolved undead. Such skeletons are rare and often signify a being that has achieved near-resurrection. The Death World's hierarchy (white, blue, purple, yellow, diamond) is further expanded with the introduction of the five-colored tier, suggesting realms beyond yellow diamond.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen now possesses the forbidden knowledge of Life Source Liquid refinement, which he plans to use against his mortal enemies like the Yasha King and the Nine-Headed Serpent. This sets up a darker, more ruthless phase for the protagonist. The five-colored skeleton's appearance is a major cliffhanger—it could be a new ally, a rival, or a harbinger of a larger threat from the depths of the Death World. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's moral line shifts as he embraces darker methods. The alliance with the War Clan's Mabao also hints at future cooperative battles. The coming chapters will likely explore the five-colored skeleton's origin and Xiao Chen's return to the Mortal World with the Life Source Liquid.
