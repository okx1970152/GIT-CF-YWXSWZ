## Chapter Overview
This chapter presents a fascinating dual narrative: in the Burial Valley, Xiao Chen and his allies uncover a half-skull belonging to a stone man—a relic of the primordial path—while on Dragon Island, Keke undergoes her Half-Ancestor tribulation with characteristic laziness and gluttony. The stone man half-skull proves mysteriously reactive to different holders, each experiencing unique sensory phenomena. Meanwhile, the mysterious woman with the War Sword and her yellow shadow pursue Xiao Chen deeper into the valley, her true nature revealed as a weapon forged from the flesh of a previous civilization's Ancestral God. The chapter masterfully balances mythic dread with lighthearted humor.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen discovers a half-skull of a stone man in the debris of the Burial Valley's first valley. Bai Qi, the God of Slaughter, attempts to use his Great Returning Art to glimpse the skull's original appearance, but his immense soul power flows into it without effect. The half-skull exhibits bizarre properties: when Xiao Chen holds it, he hears the weeping of gods and demons; Bai Qi hears the clash of divine weapons; and the Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal claim to see seductive celestial maidens. This suggests the skull resonates differently with each being's nature.

The Martial Imprint is violently thrown into the second valley by a mysterious woman wielding a War Sword, accompanied by a yellow shadow carrying a stone ball. The Martial Imprint reveals the woman's true identity: she is a sentient weapon formed from the flesh of an Ancestral God from a previous civilization, combined with discarded divine materials, which self-assembled in the Burial Valley. She is arguably the most terrifying entity present.

The group flees deeper into the second valley, where they encounter a half-skull of a stone man the size of a house. The chapter cuts to Dragon Island, where Keke is undergoing a Half-Ancestor tribulation but is too sleepy to notice, napping through lightning strikes. Only the mention of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree's fruit rouses her. She accidentally absorbs the heavenly tribulation lightning and emerges unharmed, demanding to know who attacked her.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The stone man (石人) is a unique cultivation path in *World of Immortality*, where the cultivator's body petrifies into stone, eventually becoming a supreme being. The half-skull discovered here likely belongs to a primordial stone man. Bai Qi (白起) is a historical Chinese general who became the "God of Slaughter" in mythology; here he is a powerful cultivator of soul arts. The Great Returning Art (大还原术) is a technique to reveal an object's original form, often used in investigations.

The mysterious woman is a weapon born of a previous civilization's Ancestral God flesh—a concept of cyclic worlds and reincarnation of divine materials. Keke's tribulation exemplifies the novel's humor: even a world-shaking ascension can be met with adorable laziness, undercutting typical xianxia tropes.

## Reading Guide
This chapter raises several critical threads. The half-skull's connection to Xiao Chen's own stone man fragments may unlock deeper truths about his cultivation path—will he find the other half? The mysterious woman's pursuit suggests she wants the War Swords, but her power may be beyond Xiao Chen's current ability to oppose. Meanwhile, Keke's successful (if lazy) ascension to Half-Ancestor will make her an immensely powerful ally. Her acquisition of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree fruit hints at further evolution. The contrast between the mythic horror of the Burial Valley and Keke's comic tribulation highlights the novel's tonal range; readers can enjoy both the deepening cosmic mysteries and the endearing personality of the little white beast.
