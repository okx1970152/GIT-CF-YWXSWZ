## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's death-world journey, as he undertakes a perilous refinement using a king's blood pool and a miraculous blood lotus. The atmosphere is thick with deathly essence and the promise of life—a contradiction that defines the World of Immortality. Keke, the white beast, reveals a ferocity and power that startles even the most hardened foreign kings, defending Xiao Chen with a blend of childish innocence and terrifying divine might. The Asura King, a Perfected Man realm powerhouse, descends upon the underground palace, and the Golden Lion King unleashes his dreaded third eye. The chapter is a crucible of transformation and conflict, where the boundaries between life and death blur and Xiao Chen edges closer to a true rebirth.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen sits at the center of a blood pool, suffusing his yellow-diamond skeleton with a king's essence. A blood lotus emerges—a pure crystallization of vital energy—which he plants within his own chest, drawing the pool's power into his bones. A blood infant, later revealed to be a blood corpse worm that has fed on the dead king for eons, attacks but is swiftly trapped by Keke in a bronze cauldron from the Wuyi sealed palace. The Asura King, enraged by Xiao Chen's theft of the White Bone King Banner, smashes through the earth to kill him. Keke intercepts with Paradise Lost, shattering the Asura King's divine ropes and fighting off multiple kings with the Sacred Tree. The Golden Lion King joins the fray, his third eye partly opening, but Keke's own eyes blaze with black and white beams that forcibly close his eye and kill two other kings outright. As the chaos peaks, Xiao Chen stands, fully charged, and unleashes the Lingxi Sword Waves, his Four Killing Moves, and the Eight Aspect Worlds, driving back the remaining assailants.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The blood pool and blood lotus are key cultivation resources in the death-world setting, representing the concentrated life essence of a fallen king. Such resources are both treasure and poison—untreated, the blood contains deadly toxins that can kill even half-ancestors. The Perfected Man realm (至人境界) is a advanced stage beyond Fish-Leap, granting cultivators immense power and longevity. The blood corpse worm is a fascinating twist on undead evolution: a lowly worm that bathed in king's blood for ages and attained a realm akin to a heavenly king. Keke's eye beams are a mysterious inheritance—she does not fully control them, but they echo the primordial powers seen in earlier arcs, hinting at a divine lineage that transcends the death world. Her ability to suppress the Golden Lion King's third eye, which is itself a sacred beast ability, underscores her exceptional origin.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's refinement is not yet complete—he has absorbed the king's essence but must fully assimilate it. His sudden eruption into battle signals that he has reached a new threshold, but the real test lies ahead. Keke's hidden power is now exposed to the foreign kings, making her a prime target. The Asura King and the Golden Lion King are both humiliated and will seek revenge. The White Bone King Banner remains at stake, and the alliance of kings is now fully aware that Xiao Chen and Keke are the ones who hold it. The death-world's inner continent and the mysterious figure behind it still loom, and the path to Xiao Chen's flesh-and-blood rebirth is fraught with greater dangers. Watch for how Xiao Chen balances his dual cultivation between death and life as he inches toward a true resurrection.
