## Chapter Overview

This chapter brings the climactic conflict surrounding the Dead City to a harrowing close, only to unfold into a sweeping, decade-spanning epilogue that reshapes the entire world of the Eternal Continent. The tragic sacrifice of Ancestral God Suiren, the apparent fall of Xiao Chen into the demon well alongside him, and Keke's heart-wrenching grief mark the end of an era. Yet, through the profound revelations of Zhou Wenwang—the seer who unveils the truth of the virtual world—the narrative commits to an astonishing transformation. The illusory half-ancestors perish, the nine ancient lamps seal the Nine Provinces, and the Four Worlds collapse into the real. Then, with a ten-year leap, we witness the rise of new powers, the re-emergence of ancient beasts, and the unresolved question of whether Xiao Chen truly perished or awaits a return.

## Key Plot Points

Suiren's self-sacrifice is the emotional and metaphysical heart of the chapter. In a final act of desperate nobility, he uses the Yellow Clay Altar and the demon well to purge the virtual beings, but at the cost of his own existence and Xiao Chen's apparent death. Keke's desperate crying and Zhou Wenwang's mournful burial of Suiren's ashes underscore the tragic gravity. Zhou Wenwang then reveals a stunning truth: the world had been an illusion, a 'virtual' layer created by the dreams and faith of humanity, and the half-ancestors worshipped as gods were mere projections. The real Ancestral Gods, like Suiren, had been fighting a hidden war. The collapse of the virtual realm releases the nine ancient lamps, which seal the Nine Provinces and restore the primordial reality. The following decade sees a complete power shift: the illusory half-ancestors are gone, and new mortal-born experts rise—the Three-Headed Golden Lion King, the Nine-Headed God Serpent, Hai Yuntian as the Sunflower King, Niu Ren as the Bull Demon King, and the Dragon Kings stirring. Yet, the fate of Xiao Chen remains a haunting mystery, and the world buzzes with the name of a hero turned demon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of the 'virtual world' is a profound xianxia twist on the nature of reality. In Chinese mythology, the line between the tangible and the imagined is often blurred; here, it is made literal. The 'half-ancestors' such as the Buddha, Laozi, and Tongtian are revealed to be projections of mortal consciousness, while the true Ancestral Gods like Suiren, Fuxi, and Shennong are the authentic, primordial beings. This inversion of the familiar mythological hierarchy is a daring narrative choice, emphasizing that the path of cultivation is not merely about power but about discerning truth from illusion. The 'nine ancient lamps' are deeply symbolic, representing the eternal vigilance of the Nine Provinces. Their sealing of the world echoes the cosmological function of divine artifacts in Chinese folklore—objects that bind fate and stabilize reality. The time skip and the rise of new heroes (including descendants of mythical figures like Houyi and Yu the Great) align with the cyclical nature of xianxia epics: eras end, and new champions emerge from the ashes.

## Reading Guide

The chapter leaves readers with a profound sense of loss and anticipation. Xiao Chen's apparent death is ambiguous—the narrative deliberately avoids confirming his end, and the final paragraphs recall his name as a taboo, hinting at a legacy that may yet return. Pay close attention to the scattered details: Keke's disappearance and the Dragon Kings' search, the emergence of the Golden Lion King and other beasts, and the political maneuvers of Zhao Lin'er, who now wields ancient human weapons. The collapse of the virtual world is not the end; it is a reset. The next arc will likely explore the restored Primordial Celestial Realm, the fate of the true half-ancestors who fought in the heavens, and the slow discovery of Xiao Chen's fate. The stage is set for a new generation of conflicts, but the shadow of the past—and of Xiao Chen's sacrifice—looms large. Watch for the return of familiar faces and the unveiling of the world's deepest secrets.
