## Chapter Overview
This chapter concludes the elimination rounds of the beast-fighting tournament in Heavenly Emperor City, setting the stage for the upcoming King War. The final day delivers a series of stunning upsets: a plain-looking white tiger dominates through pure martial skill, a golden three-headed lion with third eyes crushes all challengers, and most surprisingly, a black turtle—dismissed as a joke—smashes a jade rhino and a blood ape with terrifying speed. The true shock arrives when the first match of the King War is announced: the Little Defiant Dragon must face a golden lion dragon king from Dragon Island—a rematch born of old grudges. The atmosphere is electric, the stakes are elevated, and both beasts have grown immensely since their last encounter.

## Key Plot Points
The white tiger, without using any divine light, systematically destroys several quasi-king beasts using ancient combat techniques, earning the dread of all spectators. The golden three-headed lion king, each head possessing a third eye, tears through opponents with inhuman efficiency. The black turtle, initially mocked for its unimpressive appearance, reveals incredible speed and strength, defeating a jade rhino and a blood ape in quick succession, securing its place in the King War. A historical aside reveals that the Ligen family's arena was destroyed decades ago by a mysterious youth from the Southern Wastes, leaving a legacy of fear. The three main arenas announce the prize: up to sixty thousand gold coins for the champion, plus a legendary beast king relic—a sacred stone from a Buddhist beast. The King War's first match pits the Little Defiant Dragon against the golden lion dragon king, a mighty opponent that once nearly killed it on Dragon Island. Both dragons have evolved: the golden lion now has an Ancestral Dragon tail, while the Little Defiant Dragon exudes a battle intent so sharp it makes the protective energy shield tremble.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The beast-fighting tournament is a grand spectacle in Heavenly Emperor City, reflecting the importance of spirit beasts in this world. The "beast king relic" (兽王舍利) is a sacred object left behind by a beast that once served the Buddha, hinting at Buddhist influences on the Eternal Continent. The concept of "king lineage" dragons (王系龙族) denotes dragons of supreme bloodline, capable of evolving into Ancestral Dragon forms. The Ligen family's history—their arena destroyed by a lone youth from the Southern Wastes—illustrates the dangers of provoking hidden experts, a recurring theme in xianxia where the weak must respect latent power. The use of large-scale energy shields and arrays to contain dragon-level combat shows the sophistication of cultivation-based infrastructure in major cities.

## Reading Guide
The stage is set for the King War, and the first match is a direct confrontation between two rivals from Dragon Island. Readers should watch how the Little Defiant Dragon's stubborn will and newfound power measure against the golden lion dragon king's calm confidence. The white tiger and black turtle are dark horses that may later emerge as serious threats. The political subplot involving the Ligen family and the mysterious youth suggests deeper currents in Heavenly Emperor City that could affect Xiao Chen's plans. The beast king relic prize hints at future cultivation breakthroughs for the winning beast. This chapter balances tournament excitement with worldbuilding, foreshadowing intense battles to come.
