## Chapter Overview
This chapter delves into the profound mysteries of cultivation as Xiao Chen undergoes three sessions of sacrificial refinement using the flesh of fallen Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Meanwhile, Keke completes a startling transformation into a little Ancestral Dragon, only to suppress it with sheer will. The old stone turtle drops cryptic hints about the nature of the Heavenly Steles, suggesting they can be “grown” rather than discovered. The chapter culminates in Xiao Chen’s emergence from the abyss and a fierce confrontation with the Otherworld Ancestral God Leng Xingyi, wielding two small Heavenly Steles like primordial bricks.

## Key Plot Points
- Keke absorbs the life essence of the Ancestral Dragon corpse and undergoes a transformation, emerging as a little Ancestral Dragon with two jade horns. She forcefully suppresses her dragon form, preferring her original cute appearance, and hides her horns under her fur.
- The old stone turtle delivers a maddeningly cryptic explanation about the Heavenly Steles: they “grow” from the earth like plants, and understanding this requires deep insight. Xiao Chen is frustrated but begins to suspect a profound connection to the Stone Man relics.
- Xiao Chen performs three consecutive sacrificial refinements using the corpses of Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Each refinement strengthens his physical body, opens deeper acupoints, and solidifies his soul within his flesh. His 365 major acupoints shine with divine light, and mysterious dark acupoints also begin to awaken.
- The Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, nourished by the Ancestral God flesh, sprouts an eighth leaf shrouded in chaotic mist, its power vastly increased.
- After the third refinement, Xiao Chen’s body becomes a peerless treasure vessel, capable of locking his consciousness against even Ancestral God-level soul attacks.
- He bursts out of the abyss and immediately clashes with Leng Xingyi, an Otherworld Ancestral God. Using the two small Heavenly Steles as weapons, he shatters Leng Xingyi’s lower body and forces the ancestor to retreat in shock.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Sacrificial refinement (圣祭) is a taboo cultivation method where a practitioner absorbs the residual life essence of a supreme being—here, an Ancestral God—to forge their own body into a divine artifact. This echoes the ancient Daoist concept of “using heaven and earth as a furnace” to refine one’s spirit. The Ancestral Dragon is the pinnacle of dragonkind, and Keke’s ability to voluntarily suppress such a transformation highlights the strength of her inherited bloodline from her father. The old stone turtle’s riddle about the Heavenly Steles “growing” suggests these monuments are not merely carved stones but living entities that evolve, a notion that subverts typical xianxia tropes of static treasures.

## Reading Guide
The old stone turtle’s words will likely reverberate throughout future chapters—pay close attention to any mention of “growing” steles or the Stone Man, as they may unlock the ultimate secret of the cosmos. Xiao Chen’s body is now fortified against soul attacks, removing his greatest vulnerability, but his soul remains mortal; whether this protection can withstand a true Ancestral God’s full power remains uncertain. Keke’s new form and the eighth leaf of the Sacred Tree both hint at even greater potential. The battle with Leng Xingyi is interrupted; expect the Otherworld ancestors to escalate their response now that Xiao Chen has shown he can wound them.
