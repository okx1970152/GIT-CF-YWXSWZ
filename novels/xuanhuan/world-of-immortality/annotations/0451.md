## Chapter Overview

This chapter centers on Lan Nuo's perilous and majestic ascension to the half-ancestor (半祖) realm, a process that draws the gaze of the entire cultivation world. Xiao Chen, now emotionally detached from his past reverence, observes with calm clarity. As hidden half-ancestors launch treacherous attacks to derail her breakthrough, Xiao Chen intervenes with his twenty-seven War Swords, standing as a lone protector. The chapter weaves together philosophical introspection—Xiao Chen’s divine sense undergoes a subtle elevation—with escalating tension, culminating in the open mobilization of the Sun Sect and Tiger Clan against him. The stage is set for a direct clash between the champions of the mortal world and the foreign half-ancestors.

## Key Plot Points

- Lan Nuo channels the power of three hundred sixty-five divine beams from the stars, forging her half-ancestor body amid thunder and celestial radiance, a spectacle visible across continents.
- Xiao Chen arrives but feels no lingering attachment; his mind expands, and his divine sense achieves a minor breakthrough, briefly vanishing from the perception of those nearby.
- Multiple half-ancestors launch blood-colored sword beams (twenty to thirty li long) to disrupt Lan Nuo’s ascension. She absorbs their essence instead of being harmed, demonstrating her extraordinary resilience.
- Shanwaishan appears with the battle-axe and shield of the ancient war god Xing Tian, warning any attacker that four half-ancestors (including Xing Tian and Chiyou) will exact vengeance.
- Xiao Chen dons black iron armor and manifests twenty-seven War Swords, challenging any half-ancestor to face him. His reputation as the ‘Great Demon King’ who dares to slaughter half-ancestors terrifies and awes onlookers.
- The Sun Sect and Tiger Clan conspire to deploy ancient formations that can suppress or wound half-ancestors, planning to trap Xiao Chen during the chaos.
- The chapter ends with Lan Nuo’s breakthrough nearing completion and Xiao Chen ready to meet the combined assault of his enemies.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The half-ancestor ascension process draws upon the power of the cosmos—stars, thunder, and world essence—making the cultivator temporarily vulnerable but also capable of absorbing attacks. This mirrors the concept of ‘heavenly tribulation’ where adversity is transformed into strength.
- Xing Tian’s weapons (干戚) are legendary artifacts from Chinese mythology; the unyielding warrior who fought after decapitation symbolizes absolute defiance and is invoked here as a deterrent to half-ancestors.
- The Sun Sect’s ancient battle formation echoes the historical use of military arrays in xianxia to challenge higher-realm opponents, reflecting the power of collective will and ancient oaths.
- The War Swords (战剑) that Xiao Chen wields are tied to the Heavenly Steles; his possession of twenty-seven indicates his deep connection to the primordial seal and his growth since Dragon Island.

## Reading Guide

- This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation. Watch for how the hidden half-ancestors respond to Xiao Chen’s provocation—will they dare to face someone who has killed half-ancestors before?
- The alliance between the Sun Sect and Tiger Clan suggests organized opposition. Their ancient formation could be a key factor in future battles, potentially wounding Xiao Chen and inviting half-ancestor intervention.
- Lan Nuo’s successful ascension may shift the balance of power. Her relationship with Xiao Chen remains ambiguous, but she could become a vital ally in the coming war.
- The mention of four half-ancestors backing Lan Nuo (Mountains Beyond Mountains, Heaven Beyond Heaven, Man Beyond Man, and Xing Tian) indicates a growing coalition against the foreign races, raising the stakes for the entire Nine Provinces.
