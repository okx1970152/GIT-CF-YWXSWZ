## Chapter Overview

This chapter depicts a climactic battle in the Otherworld as the rebellious Stone Man makes a desperate stand against the combined might of the indigenous ancestral gods and the three Supreme Ancestral Gods. Xiao Chen arrives with a Death City from the dead world, turning the tide. Meanwhile, far from the battlefield, the Supreme Ancestral Gods guarding the ninety-nine stone steps are themselves attacked by ancient beings: a Tianren survivor and the Martial Ancestor himself. The chapter interweaves multiple scales of conflict, from the physical destruction of the divine city to the cosmic clash of ultimate powers.

## Key Plot Points

- The rebellious Stone Man, having waged war across the Otherworld, is now cornered by the native Stone Man rooted in the divine city and countless ancestral gods. His body is torn apart, with limbs severed.
- Xiao Chen arrives with a second Death City, smashing it into the divine city to aid the rebellious Stone Man. The Death City breaks apart but buys precious time.
- The divine city's Stone Man attempts to absorb the broken Death City to repair his own city and complete his final step toward ascension.
- However, a mysterious being (related to Xiao Chen's stone man?) lies dormant within the Death City fragments, threatening to disrupt the repair.
- Elsewhere, the three Supreme Ancestral Gods are attacked by an ancient Tianren being, then by the Martial Ancestor, who has apparently revived. This diverts their attention from the main battlefield.
- The chapter ends with the Martial Ancestor confronting the Supreme Ancestral Gods, who vow to kill him again.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The 'Stone Man' is a rare and powerful cultivation path, where a cultivator gradually petrifies their body to achieve immortality and supreme power. The 'final step' refers to the ultimate transformation, often requiring a divine city and immense resources.
- The 'Supreme Ancestral Gods' are the highest-tier beings in the Otherworld, guarding the ninety-nine stone steps—likely a path to transcendence.
- The Tianren (Celestial People) are an ancient race predating humans and dragons, known for their primordial power. Their involvement hints at deeper historical cycles.
- The Martial Ancestor is a legendary figure from the mortal world's martial tradition, now revealed to have survived or revived, capable of challenging the Otherworld's elite.

## Reading Guide

- This chapter sets up a multi-front war: the Stone Man's struggle, Xiao Chen's intervention, and the attacks on the Supreme Ancestral Gods. Readers should watch for how these threads converge.
- The fate of the dormant being within the Death City fragments is a ticking bomb; will it awaken and aid or hinder?
- The Martial Ancestor's reappearance is significant; his past defeat by the Supreme Ancestral Gods suggests a personal vendetta.
- The chapter ends on a cliffhanger with the Martial Ancestor facing the three Supreme Ancestral Gods. The outcome will likely shake the balance of power.
