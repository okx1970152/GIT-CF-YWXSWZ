## Chapter Overview
This chapter presents one of the most harrowing and transformative sequences in the arc: Xiao Chen, wielding the forbidden technique from the Martial Ancestor's legacy, performs a living sacrifice on the Otherworld Ancestral God Cold Star Wing. The tone is one of tragic, mythic horror—an ancestral god, a being of supreme power, is reduced to screaming agony as his blood and essence are consumed to fuel Xiao Chen's ascension. Yet this is not a simple victory; the chapter is layered with cosmic consequences. An upper ancestral god from the eternal unknown senses the death of his descendant and sends a thirty-three-story pagoda to intervene, but a mysterious mass grave beneath the Curse Realm absorbs the blood and shadows, adding a new layer of mystery. Xiao Chen's cultivation undergoes a rebirth, his flesh hardening into a near-indestructible form, but the chapter ends with a sudden shift: the ancestral gods Yan Huang and the old Ancestral Dragon arrive on the Ancestral Lord Warship to summon him back to the Death World, hinting at even greater upheavals.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen completes the living sacrifice of Cold Star Wing, using the forbidden scripture from the Martial Ancestor. The ancestral god's soul and blood are annihilated, his flesh turned to raw power that Xiao Chen absorbs, causing his body to shed its old skin and regenerate into a stronger, more refined form. The ancestral god attempts a final counterattack with a soul-shaped death bell, but it is shattered by the two Heavenly Marks and the divine diagram. Just as the sacrifice reaches its peak, an upper ancestral god from beyond the worlds sends a thirty-three-story pagoda to break through the Curse Realm, but a massive beam of black light erupts from a mass grave beneath the Curse World, absorbing the blood radiance and the remnants of Cold Star Wing's soul. Xiao Chen falls into the abyss beneath the Heavenly Stele, where the old stone turtle tells him that he must gather all Heavenly Marks for a future revelation. Later, Yan Huang, the old Ancestral Dragon, and other ancestral gods appear on the Ancestral Lord Warship, taking Xiao Chen back to the Death World, where he witnesses a shocking scene that sets up the next phase of the conflict.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The living sacrifice (活祭) is a forbidden technique that reverses the typical power hierarchy—a mortal-level cultivator devouring an ancestral god's essence. It is a direct affront to the cosmic order, and the upper ancestral god's reaction underscores the stakes: the eternal unknown houses beings far above the ancestral gods we have seen. The mass grave (千万人坑) is a mysterious formation from the Curse Realm, likely a relic of ancient battles or sealed powers. The Heavenly Marks (天痕) are remnants of the Heavenly Steles, and the stone turtle hints that collecting them all may lead to a major discovery. The Ancestral Lord Warship (祖君战船) is a vessel forged from the bones of Death World lords and Otherworld ancestral gods, infused with Eternal Light, capable of crossing worlds. This chapter also reinforces the theme of sacrifice: the ancients gave their lives for hope, and now Xiao Chen walks a similar path, albeit with a darker, more aggressive method.

## Reading Guide
The chapter raises several important threads for the coming volumes. First, what exactly is the mass grave, and why did it absorb Cold Star Wing's blood and soul? Its intervention suggests that the Curse Realm itself may be an active player in the war. Second, the old stone turtle's cryptic advice to gather all Heavenly Marks points to a convergence of the Heavenly Steles—possibly the key to unlocking the Primordial Celestial Realm or a weapon against the Otherworld. Third, the sudden arrival of the ancestral gods and the shocking scene in the Death World indicate that the war is escalating beyond the Curse Realm. Xiao Chen's power has grown, but he still faces an upper ancestral god who is now aware of him. The question is whether he can reach the ancestral god level before the final confrontation. The next chapters will likely delve into the secrets of the Death World and the true nature of the Heavenly Marks.
