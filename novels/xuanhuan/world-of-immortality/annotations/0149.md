## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers one of the most intense and transformative duels in Xiao Chen's journey so far. Pitted against Wu Xingfeng, a northern prodigy wielding the legendary Five Elements Divine Arts, Xiao Chen finds himself utterly outmatched at the peak of Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven. The battle is a brutal, one-sided affair that forces Xiao Chen to confront the limits of his power. Yet out of near-defeat and physical ruin, he achieves the breakthrough he had long sought—ascending to the Ninth Heaven in the crucible of combat. The chapter crackles with desperation, raw emotion, and the mythic thrill of a warrior remaking himself through sheer will.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen unleashing the second of his Four Killing Hands—Reverse Chaos—to counter Wu Xingfeng's Wood Fist, barely escaping the overwhelming life-force attack. Wu Xingfeng presses his advantage with the Water Fist, and Xiao Chen responds with Suppress Demon, the third killing hand, yet is still driven back. When Wu Xingfeng delivers the Fire Fist, Xiao Chen is forced to use Collapse, the fourth and most destructive technique, but the impact shatters his arms and sends him flying. In that moment of utter defeat, the pressure of battle breaks the bottleneck he had struggled against for so long, and his power surges into the Ninth Heaven. His broken bones heal instantly as his body undergoes a radiant transformation. Xiao Chen then rises with Undying Wings, countering Wu Xingfeng's Metal Fist with a raw, devastating strike that forces the northern genius to retreat. The reversal is absolute: Xiao Chen has not only survived but claimed a new level of power, announcing his arrival as a Ninth Heaven expert.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Four Killing Hands (Slay God, Reverse Chaos, Suppress Demon, Collapse) are derived from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art, Xiao Chen's foundational cultivation method. Each technique embodies a Dao-imprint—a condensed mark of cosmic law that makes them far more than ordinary martial arts. The Five Elements Divine Arts used by Wu Xingfeng are an ancient and revered system, far surpassing the crude mortal version of Five Elements Fist. In xianxia tradition, breakthroughs achieved under life-or-death duress are considered the most potent and authentic, as the body and spirit are pushed to their absolute limits. The journey from Eighth to Ninth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding is a major milestone; Ninth Heaven practitioners are rare among the younger generation and can contend for the top ranks in any regional competition.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's breakthrough to the Ninth Heaven fundamentally changes his standing in Heavenly Emperor City. With the upcoming beast-fighting tournament and his ongoing entanglement with the Undying Sect, northern powers like Wu Xingfeng's faction, and local nobles such as Prince Sun Miao, his newfound strength will draw both allies and enemies. Watch for how Wu Xingfeng processes this defeat and whether the Northern elites will seek revenge. The reactions of Yan Qingcheng, Hai Yunxue, and Kanasi also hint at shifting political tides. Readers should also note that Xiao Chen has not yet mastered the fourth killing hand, Collapse, to its full potential—this suggests future refinement and even greater power.
