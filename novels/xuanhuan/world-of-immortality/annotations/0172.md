## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a visceral, desperate duel that pushes Xiao Chen to the limits of his endurance. Impaled by the legendary Bastard God Spear, he faces the brown-haired Morika in a battle that tests not only his martial skill but his unyielding will. The scene is steeped in dark, mythic intensity as Xiao Chen employs a sound-based footwork technique to synchronize with his opponent's heartbeat, demonstrating a profound unity with the Dao. The silent observer Dugu Jianmo adds an ominous layer of tension, and the arrival of Morika's brother escalates the stakes to a life-or-death climax. This is a chapter of raw endurance and tactical brilliance, not heroic triumph.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, already grievously wounded with the Bastard God Spear piercing his left ribs, confronts the confident Morika. He uses a unique sound-killing technique by matching his footsteps to Morika's heartbeat, causing him to hemorrhage and fly backward. Morika attempts to seize the spear and overpower Xiao Chen, but the protagonist's fluid, transcendent movements neutralize every attack. Xiao Chen enters a state of unity with heaven and earth, his actions becoming effortless yet devastating. When Morika's elder brother Yulamu arrives, Xiao Chen seizes the moment: he drives the spear deeper through Morika's body and lifts him into the air, impaling him on the same weapon that wounded him. Dugu Jianmo watches the entire battle without interfering, his intentions unclear.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Bastard God Spear is a relic of the legendary War Clan, a tribe of god-slaying warriors whose weapons are permanently stained with divine blood. The sound-killing footwork Xiao Chen uses is not merely a martial trick but a manifestation of his imminent breakthrough to the Spirit-Treasury realm—a state where a cultivator begins to perceive and manipulate the Dao directly. By syncing his steps with Morika's heartbeat and the earth's pulse, Xiao Chen achieves a form of 'Heaven and Man as One,' temporarily elevating his power beyond his wounded body. This chapter also demonstrates the brutal reality of Mortal-Shedding combat: even with a pierced torso, a cultivator's life force and will can sustain extraordinary feats.

## Reading Guide
This chapter cements Xiao Chen's reputation as an indomitable fighter who can turn even his own wounds into weapons. His ability to resist the spear's deadly energy and use it against its wielder is a testament to his ruthless practicality. Morika's death ensures his brother Yulamu will seek revenge, setting up a future confrontation. Dugu Jianmo's passive role remains ambiguous—is he an ally, a bystander, or a waiting predator? Xiao Chen's brush with the Spirit-Treasury realm suggests his cultivation is accelerating, a development that will have major implications in the Heavenly Emperor City arc. The chapter also reinforces the novel's tone of tragic resilience: there is no glory, only survival at a terrible cost.
