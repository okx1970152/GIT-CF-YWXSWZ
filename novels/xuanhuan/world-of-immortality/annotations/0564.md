## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the stage to the Primordial Celestial Realm, a vast and desolate realm where ancient secrets slumber. Through the dialogue of an Otherworld Ancestral God, we learn of a 'defying heaven' family—a clan cursed to transform from human to beast form, nearly wiped out in an age-old war. This is the first explicit revelation of Keke's true lineage and the tragic history of her bloodline. Simultaneously, Xiao Chen, who has been pursuing an eight-heaven Half-Ancestor, stumbles into the very battlefield where a legendary member of that family once fought a desperate war. There, he encounters a young member of the cursed clan: a tiny white beast with a pacifier, eerily similar to Keke yet more innocent and silent. This encounter unveils a miniature divine city hidden beneath the ruins, and triggers an unexpected reaction from the five stone king imprints within Xiao Chen, hinting at profound connections between the defying heaven clan and the stone-man path.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with an Otherworld Ancestral God in a panic after a blood light incident in a forbidden ancient land. He reveals that the region is the site of a historical massacre where a member of the 'defying heaven' family—a family cursed to become beasts—single-handedly fought multiple enemies, wounding even a celestial giant before being killed. The curse transforming human cultivators into animal forms is explicitly tied to this family, and only a handful of its members survive. This lore directly explains Keke's origin and her beast-like appearance. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen barely escapes the initial blood light and the pursuing eight-heaven Half-Ancestor, who is killed by the mysterious forces of the land. When Xiao Chen emerges, he spots a tiny, silent white beast with a pacifier. This young beast, clearly a relative of Keke, recognizes familiar aura on Xiao Chen and leads him through an underground tunnel to a ruined divine city. The city is broken but contains a miniature replica at its center. The young beast transforms into a mist and merges into this miniature city, which then causes the five stone king imprints from Xiao Chen's earlier cultivation to manifest and revolve around the structure. This suggests the miniature city is a stone city in formation, and the defying heaven family may be directly connected to the stone men and the final step.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of a 'defying heaven' family (逆天家族) is a common trope in xianxia, referring to clans that rebel against fate or cosmic order, often resulting in curses or persecution. Here, the curse transforms humans into beasts, explaining Keke's adorable but powerful form. The Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界) is described as the highest and most ancient realm, vast and sparsely populated, a fitting stage for ancestral god conflicts. The broken divine city is likely the remains of a stone man who attempted the 'final step'—the ultimate metamorphosis on the stone-man path. The miniature city within is a nascent stone city, suggesting the young beast is cultivating towards that state. The five stone king imprints are marks of supreme stone men from a past civilization; their resonance with the miniature city implies the defying heaven family has a historical bond with these stone kings. This chapter deepens the mythology of the stone-man cultivation path and ties it intimately to Keke's clan.

The upcoming chapters will likely explore Xiao Chen's connection to this family and the potential for the miniature city to become a weapon or a gateway to higher cultivation.
