## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges into the heart of a dead world palace, where the remnants of a fallen king's power—a blood pool and a skull altar—become the focal point of a desperate scramble among foreign kings. The White Bone King Banner, a relic forged from the bones of Ancestral Gods, radiates a suffocating aura that draws every king present into a frenzy of greed and violence. The atmosphere is thick with menace: the blood river that dissolves flesh, the eerie cries of a blood infant born from a dead king's essence, and the clash of supreme artifacts create a tableau of mythic horror. Amid this chaos, Xiao Chen, still a yellow diamond skeleton, watches from the shadows, his patience and cunning marking him as a predator among wolves. The chapter balances visceral action with tactical manipulation, setting the stage for a decisive confrontation.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Asura King shattering the palace's ancient seal, unleashing a terrifying wave of energy that wounds even him. Inside, the kings find the palace filled with bones that rise to attack, but their power quickly turns the hall to bone dust. The true danger appears as a blood river—a stream of a dead king's blood that can burn through Fish-Leap realm experts. The kings trace it to a blood pool, where a skull altar stands, crowned by the White Bone King Banner. The Asura King immediately claims it, provoking the Zombie King and Death Spirit King. Xiao Chen, posing as the Yellow Diamond Skeleton King, refuses to bow to the Asura King and incites others to attack him. As chaos erupts, the Fallen Angel King recognizes Xiao Chen from a hidden mark and attempts to blackmail him. Xiao Chen tricks her into the path of Keke, who captures her in Paradise Lost. Meanwhile, the Golden Lion King enters the fray with the Basde Ancient Spear, battling the Asura King, while the War God King appears to seize the banner. In the struggle, the Asura King grabs the banner and tries to escape, only to be ambushed by Xiao Chen, who strikes him from behind with his bone palm. The chapter ends with the Asura King enraged but still alive, setting up a direct clash.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Dead World features a unique hierarchy of undead cultivation. Skeletons evolve through colored diamond tiers: white, blue, purple, yellow, and five-colored, each corresponding to increasing power. The Yellow Diamond tier is equivalent to the Fish-Leap realm, while five-colored diamond approaches the Perfected Man realm. The 'king's blood' in the pool is a key concept: the congealed essence of a supreme dead being can dissolve lesser beings but also serves as a medium for creating blood souls—intelligent fragments of the original king. The White Bone King Banner is a supreme artifact, said to be forged from the bones of Ancestral Gods, capable of commanding undead armies. Its allure is so great that even Perfected Man realm kings like the Asura King risk everything to claim it. The presence of the blood infant, a regenerated consciousness from the king's blood, adds a layer of horror, showing that even in death, old kings can still exert influence.
