## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a masterclass in xianxia combat philosophy as Dugu Jianmo, the Sword Demon of the Southern Wilderness, takes center stage at the Moon Lake book-sword gathering. After three years of unseen cultivation, he faces a succession of Yin Capital's elite warriors—Bo Shi and Chen Hangjin—and dismantles their most formidable techniques with a single iron sword. The chapter crackles with the tension of regional pride: the arrogant Yin Capital nobles have mocked the Southern Wilderness as a land of barbarians, and Dugu Jianmo's victories restore honor with cold, surgical precision. Yet his triumph is incomplete; the return of Chu Xingkuang, one of the Four Champions of the Great Shang Empire, raises the stakes to a new level. When Dugu Jianmo, wounded and spent, points his sword at Xiao Chen in the crowd and declares him the true first expert of the Southern Wilderness, the chapter pivots from a display of solitary might to a challenge that will echo through the coming arcs. The atmosphere is one of mythic confrontation—crystal lake, blossoming peach trees, and the clash of transcendent powers under a moonlit sky.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Bo Shi ordering the rescue of his fallen comrades and stepping into the arena himself, challenging the spectral sword-wielder. Dugu Jianmo descends from the sky, his iron sword cutting a rainbow arc, and engages Bo Shi in a devastating exchange of blows. Bo Shi unleashes his signature technique, the Purple Prison Space, sealing Dugu Jianmo in a domain of condensed violet energy. Within that suffocating space, Dugu Jianmo's body grows paper-thin and he cleaves through the prison, launching himself as a human sword to shatter Bo Shi's defenses and send him crashing to the ground. The crowd erupts in shock. The Third Princess of Great Shang offers Dugu Jianmo a cup of precious tea, but he rejects her gift with cold indifference, earning both awe and resentment. Chen Hangjin, a cunning noble of the Qi Prince lineage, seizes the opportunity to challenge the wounded swordsman with a wager: the loser must obey the winner's command. Chen Hangjin's Nine Nether King Wheel descends like a master of the underworld, but Dugu Jianmo once again transforms his body into a blade, piercing the wheel and defeating him. Two successive victories against the pride of Yin Capital leave the crowd stunned. Chu Xinghuang arrives—one of the Four Champions—and immediately dismisses the Southern Wilderness as barbarian scum. He offers Dugu Jianmo a Seven-Turn Golden Elixir to heal before fighting him, but Dugu Jianmo refuses and instead points his sword at Xiao Chen, naming him the true first expert of the Southern Wilderness.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The cultivation hierarchy in this chapter is vividly illustrated through the contrast between realms and techniques. Bo Shi is at the Spirit-Treasury realm (识藏境界), capable of using a domain-like technique—the Purple Prison Space—which traps the opponent in a confined space of concentrated energy. This reflects a common xianxia trope where high-level cultivators manipulate space or create self-contained worlds. Dugu Jianmo's counter is a physical metamorphosis: he compresses his body into a thin, blade-like form, embodying his philosophy that 'every grass and tree can be his sword, including himself.' This is a classic example of sword intent (剑意) reaching the pinnacle, where the practitioner transcends the weapon and becomes the weapon. The Nine Nether King Wheel is a technique of the Chen family, channeling the power of the underworld into a grinding wheel of annihilation—a direct reference to Buddhist and Daoist concepts of reincarnation and judgment. The arrival of Chu Xingkuang and the mention of the Four Champions (帝都四杰) establishes the Great Shang Empire's elite structure, where the strongest young warriors are recognized and feared. The Seven-Turn Golden Elixir (七转金丹) is a legendary healing medicine that can instantly restore vitality, a staple of xianxia pharmacology.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major confrontation between Xiao Chen and Chu Xingkuang, one of the Four Champions of the Great Shang Empire. Dugu Jianmo's declaration that Xiao Chen is the true first expert of the Southern Wilderness places immense pressure on the protagonist—a reputation he must now live up to or be exposed as a fraud. The chapter also deepens the political landscape of the Eternal Continent: the Yin Capital nobles, the Qi and Chen princes, and the Third Princess are all maneuvering for influence, and Xiao Chen's role becomes increasingly entangled with these powers. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen responds to this public call-out and whether he can match the expectations set by Dugu Jianmo's legendary feats. Additionally, the mention of the Southern Wilderness's mysterious elder (perhaps Long Teng) and the hidden power of the War Clan remains unresolved, adding layers to the ongoing arc. The chapter's emphasis on domain techniques and physical transformation hints at the evolving combat system, suggesting that higher realms unlock increasingly esoteric abilities. The narrative rhythm accelerates from solitary duel to gauntlet challenge, promising explosive action in the next installment.
