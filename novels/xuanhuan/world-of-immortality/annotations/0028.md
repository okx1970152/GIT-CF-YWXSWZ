## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a brutal, high-stakes confrontation that pivots on betrayal and desperation. The hunt for Xiao Chen reaches its climax as Zhao Lin'er and Guluo corner him, but the tides turn with the unexpected intervention of the giant apes and the mysterious young celestial horse. The atmosphere is tense, violent, and charged with the raw survival instinct that defines Dragon Island. Xiao Chen, cold and efficient, forces his enemies to reveal their final cards, ending the chapter on a note of savage uncertainty. The stakes are personal and existential, and no one leaves unchanged.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a direct assault: Guluo and Zhao Lin'er attack Xiao Chen simultaneously, but the corrosive corpse miasma weakens them. Xiao Chen fights with lethal precision, severing Zhao Lin'er's hair at the neck and cutting down a third attacker. The three skeletons assist, cornering the enemies. Just as Xiao Chen declares the end, a pack of giant apes—former allies from earlier chapters—arrives, drawn by the commotion. They surround Zhao Lin'er with vengeful intent. However, a sudden lightning net from the young celestial horse knocks down most apes, and the horse swoops in to carry Zhao Lin'er away. The crimson ape leader recovers and chases after them. Left alone, Guluo reveals his hidden flight scroll—a one-person escape tool he had saved for this exact moment. Xiao Chen throws a sword with immense force, severing Guluo's right arm, but Guluo escapes into the night. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen standing amidst the aftermath, having failed to kill either target.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The chapter showcases signature elements of Chinese xianxia combat: the use of corpse miasma (尸瘴) as a battlefield hazard that drains vitality, demonstrating how environment and preparation affect power even among high-level practitioners. The giant apes are not mere beasts but spirit-cultivators, capable of absorbing moonlight and forming bonds of loyalty and vengeance. The celestial horse (天马) is a mythical creature tied to purity and destiny; its ability to generate lightning reinforces its divine nature. Guluo's flight scroll represents the disposable artifact trope common in xianxia, where practitioners carry one-use treasures for emergencies. The focus on 'saving the best for last' reflects the strategic depth of resource management in cultivation culture.

## Reading Guide
This chapter closes the first major hunt arc on Dragon Island, but both Zhao Lin'er and Guluo survive, ensuring future conflict. Pay attention to the celestial horse's sudden intervention—its allegiance seems to lie with Zhao Lin'er, but its earlier interaction with Xiao Chen hints at complexity. Guluo's escape with a severed arm establishes a deep grudge and potential alliance fractures. Xiao Chen's response—cold, immediate, and without triumph—reinforces his hardened survivor mentality. Readers should watch for how these unresolved threads weave into the broader island power struggles and what new threats emerge from the rival alliances now targeting Xiao Chen.
