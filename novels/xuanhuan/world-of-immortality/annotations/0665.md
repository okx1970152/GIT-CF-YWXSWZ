## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a climactic moment in the Otherworld's desperate attempt to summon their ultimate power—the Saint Ancestor, a being from beyond the known worlds. The Otherworld gods erect a colossal blood altar and chant ancient hymns, tearing open a space gate. A monstrous black claw emerges, but an ancient mighty being from the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps (the Demon Emperor) intercepts, crushing the gate and severing the claw. However, the Saint Ancestor's incarnation later returns, overcoming the Demon Emperor and breaching the stone gate. The chapter ends with the resurrected Little Stone Emperor blocking the Saint Ancestor's path in the Primordial Celestial Realm, creating a standoff between two transcendent powers.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Otherworld constructing a thousand-zhang blood altar, their four ancestral gods and countless deities praying fervently. The space gate opens, and a black-scaled claw the size of a mountain reaches through. The Demon Emperor from the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps seizes the opportunity to attack, tearing off the claw and destroying the gate. The Saint Ancestor's incarnation later reappears, wielding immense borrowed power from myriad worlds. He seals the Demon Emperor within the First Ancient City, trapping him alongside the Seventh Heavenly Stele. He then opens the stone gate at the top of the steps, releasing a demon shadow that guards the realm. The incarnation leads the four ancestral gods—Lu Zhan, Lang Ya, and others—into the Celestial Realm to destroy the remaining heroes of the Nine Provinces. They arrive at the battlefield where the Little Stone Emperor once fell. There, a rough hand leads a small figure that grows into the majestic Little Stone Emperor himself. The Saint Ancestor recognizes that this being is not a simple resurrected corpse but a figure that has transcended time, spanning past, present, and future. He orders his forces to retreat, acknowledging that even his incarnation cannot match this power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The summoning of the Saint Ancestor via blood sacrifice is rooted in ancient Chinese rituals where deities or ancestors were invoked through offerings of blood and incense. The "Ninety-Nine Stone Steps" and "First Ancient City" are key locations in the Stone Man cultivation path—a perilous journey that leads to transcendence or death. The Little Stone Emperor is a legendary figure from earlier arcs, known for his invincible stone body and his seven demon stars. His reappearance here, seemingly resurrected, is tied to the Daoist concept of "past, present, and future" as one—a supreme being existing across all time simultaneously. The chapter also explores themes of power borrowed from worlds, resource exhaustion, and the limitations even of supreme beings.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is pivotal for understanding the scale of the Otherworld's threat and the emergence of a new power. The Saint Ancestor is a temporary incarnation, meaning his time is limited; however, the seal on the stone gate is now broken, releasing the demon shadow that may act independently. The Little Stone Emperor's return is a major revelation—his true state remains ambiguous but suggests a plan spanning eons. The Seventh Heavenly Stele is now trapped inside the First Ancient City with the Demon Emperor, which will affect Xiao Chen's quest to collect the steles. In the next chapter, the standoff between the Saint Ancestor and the Little Stone Emperor will likely escalate, and the other ancestral gods' reactions will be crucial. Readers should also watch for any sign of Xiao Chen or other heroes from the Nine Provinces.
