## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the setting to the Soul World (魂界), a desolate realm of death and lingering spirits, where Xiao Chen pursues the elusive 'most evil place' (最邪之地) that may hold the key to Eternal Light. The chapter opens with the aftermath of a grand Ancestral God's appearance on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, which has left the gathered half-ancestors shaken. But the true focus is Xiao Chen's unexpected reunion with the ancient war god Bai Qi, who has been cultivating in this forsaken land for nearly two centuries. Together, they confront the startling evolution of the Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal—two former artifacts of Xiao Chen that have mysteriously grown to half-ancestor level and may be on the verge of becoming true Ancestral God weapons. The chapter blends atmosphere, revelation, and strategic alliance-building, deepening the mystery of the Soul World and its connection to the Death World.

## Key Plot Points

After the half-ancestors scatter following the Ancestral God's intervention, Xiao Chen decides to explore the Soul World rather than immediately pursue the Eternal Light. He learns that the Soul World is a realm of the dead, similar to the Death World, and that many undead races (skeleton, wraith, zombie) originally fled here from the Death World. The most significant revelation comes when he encounters the Golden Divine Halberd—now a sentient weapon of terrifying power—fighting Bai Qi, the legendary general known for his blood-soaked history. Bai Qi reveals that the Halberd and the Dark-Iron Seal have been rapidly evolving over 170 years, rising from half-ancestor second heaven to sixth heaven, an unheard-of pace. He suspects they are drawing power from the legendary 'most evil place,' a location so sinister that even Ancestral God Suiren once warned about it. Through their conversation, Xiao Chen learns that ancient records tell of a half-ancestor shield that fell into the most evil place and emerged centuries later as an Ancestral God weapon—the Eternal Heaven Shield. This convinces him that the most evil place may actually connect to the Death World, and that Eternal Light likely resides there. Xiao Chen and Bai Qi form a temporary alliance to capture one of the two evolving weapons, hoping to use them to locate the most evil place's entrance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter deepens the cosmology of the 'World of Immortality' series by introducing the Soul World (魂界) as a distinct realm between the living world and the Death World. This is a classic concept in Chinese mythology—a liminal space where souls linger before entering the underworld. The Soul World's desolate landscape and its inhabitants (火种生物, 亡灵, etc.) parallel the Death World, suggesting a continuous cycle of death and rebirth. The phrase '最邪之地' (most evil place) is reminiscent of forbidden zones in xianxia lore, often connected to primordial chaos or sealed dangers. The idea that a divine weapon can spontaneously evolve by absorbing the essence of such a place is a compelling narrative device, echoing the concept of 'heavenly treasure' evolving through exposure to cosmic forces. Bai Qi (白起) himself is a historical figure from the Warring States period, infamous for slaughtering hundreds of thousands; his presence as a 'war soul' (战魂) who continued cultivation after death is a common trope in Chinese fantasy, where historical villains or heroes achieve immortality through martial prowess.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point that shifts the narrative from the grand half-ancestor conflict to a more focused, mystery-driven quest. The introduction of Bai Qi as an ally (and potential rival) adds depth to the world-building, and the evolving weapons foreshadow an escalation in power scales. Readers should pay attention to the hints about the most evil place—its connection to the Death World and its possible role in the ultimate fate of the Ancestral Gods. The alliance between Xiao Chen and Bai Qi is fragile; Bai Qi's motivations are unclear beyond capturing a weapon, and Xiao Chen must decide whether to trust a figure known for his ruthless past. The next chapters likely involve a stakeout or trap for the Golden Halberd, and possibly the first direct encounter with the most evil place. The chapter also reminds us that time has passed (nearly 180 years since the pseudo-god catastrophe), and Xiao Chen's cultivation is now at the Perfected Man realm, making him a credible partner for a half-ancestor like Bai Qi.
