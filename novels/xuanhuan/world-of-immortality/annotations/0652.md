## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a poignant bridge between eras, spanning thousands of years of Xiao Chen's journey through the surviving worlds. It opens with the miraculous growth of a golden lotus within a dormant Buddha, a sign that ancient divine powers are stirring toward rebirth. Xiao Chen then tours the desolate remains of Chiyou's dwelling and the Burial Valley, learning that all major ancestor weapons have been reclaimed by their primordial owners—Fuxi, Nuwa, and Suiren—in preparation for a greater war. Returning to a technologically transformed Nine Provinces, he walks the land in memory and grief, meeting Kai Luo, the last living witness of his age. Their silent toast marks the close of one era. The chapter culminates in a startling revelation: the old stone turtle, Shi Zhongdi, and the mysterious old skull are the same being, and Xiao Chen immediately demands the location of the eighth Heavenly Stele. The tone is melancholic and reflective, yet charged with the anticipation of final confrontations.

## Key Plot Points

The golden lotus blooming inside the Buddha body suggests that the lost Buddha is undergoing a profound rebirth; Xiao Chen withdraws, knowing that external interference would hinder the process. He visits Chiyou's empty dwelling, confirming that the blood essences of his fallen friends—Jin Sanyi and others—have dried, ending any hope of resurrection from that source. Journeying through the Curse Realm, Qi Realm, Soul Realm, and finally the Burial Valley, Xiao Chen learns that all the ancient ancestor weapons have been taken: the Copper Eight Trigram by Fuxi, the corpse-mud divine weapon by Nuwa, and the black knife reforged by Suiren. This signals that the ancient powers are mustering for a climactic battle. Back in the Nine Provinces after three thousand years, Xiao Chen walks from the Yellow River across the west and encounters the aged Kai Luo, the former illusionist spiritist from Dragon Island, in a familiar bar. Their wordless communion over strong drink speaks of shared loss and survival. Later, Xiao Chen spots the white-shelled turtle, which reveals itself as the old stone turtle and Shi Zhongdi in disguise, confirming that they are the same entity as the old skull that guided him before. Overjoyed and urgent, Xiao Chen demands the location of the eighth Heavenly Stele, setting the next stage of his quest.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'golden lotus in the Buddha body' is a classic Buddhist metaphor for enlightenment arising from the physical shell—a rebirth of divine consciousness. The 'ancestor weapons' tied to mythological figures like Fuxi, Nuwa, and Suiren are more than mere artifacts; they are extensions of those Ancestral Gods' will. Their reclaiming of these weapons signals that the ancient powers are no longer dormant. The 'Four Worlds'—Martial (Eternal Continent), Qi (Cultivation World), Curse (Incantation Realm), and Soul (Soul Realm)—are distinct cosmological layers that Xiao Chen traverses; the Burial Valley within the Soul Realm is a graveyard of peerless weapons. The revelation that the old stone turtle, Shi Zhongdi, and the old skull are the same being is a profound twist on character identity, suggesting that what appeared as separate entities were all manifestations of a single ancient consciousness—possibly a Stone-Man King who fractured his soul for survival. The 'source of all evil' sealed within the stone jar is now linked to a prison within the Five Emperor Pagoda, continuing the theme of ancient evils awaiting release.

## Reading Guide

This is a transitional chapter that ties off many lingering threads from the earlier arcs and sets up the final movement. Readers should note the reappearance of ancient weapons and their owners—this is a clear sign that the cosmic war is approaching its endgame. The meeting with Kai Luo serves as a final farewell to Xiao Chen's mortal connections; all his human friends from the past are now gone. The major lore bomb is the unification of the old stone turtle, Shi Zhongdi, and the old skull into a single entity. This will likely be key to understanding the stone jar, the source of all evil, and the true history of the Stone-Man path. Xiao Chen's demand for the eighth Heavenly Stele is his next objective; obtaining it may lead him to the final answers about the Heavenly Steles and his own cultivation. Keep an eye on the golden lotus Buddha, who may emerge as an ally or a wildcard in the coming battles.
