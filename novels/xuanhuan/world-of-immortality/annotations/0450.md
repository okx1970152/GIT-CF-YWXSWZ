## Chapter Overview
After crossing the Eastern lands, Xiao Chen arrives in the snow-covered Western continent with his skeletal companion Sha Polang. Their respite in a small town leads to an unexpected reunion with Kailo, the once-formidable illusionist spiritist from Dragon Island, now an aged keeper of a dim bar. Kailo reveals that the Tiger family and Sun God Church have mobilized against Xiao Chen, setting the stage for confrontation. More momentously, the chapter unveils that the legendary prodigy Lan Nuo is poised to break into the Half-Ancestor realm that very night, drawing countless witnesses to Mount Mitikas. The chapter balances foreboding political tension with the wonder of an imminent transcendent event.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Sha Polang traversing the wintry Western lands, stopping at a nearly deserted bar. The proprietor, an ancient figure, eventually reveals himself as Kailo, the brilliant illusionist who once opposed Xiao Chen on Dragon Island. Now aged and having inherited fragments of Satan's power, Kailo confirms that the Tiger family and the Sun God Church are actively mobilizing elite forces—including ancient Sun Knights—hinting at a coordinated assault. The narrative then pivots dramatically when the Purple Dragon King appears, seeking a final material for Lan Nuo's sacred sword. She identifies Xiao Chen and, after a terse negotiation, permits him to witness Lan Nuo's imminent ascension. Jin Sanyi, Tianya, and Qier arrive with absurd news of having captured Santa Claus, providing dark comic relief. Amid the gathering throng of witnesses, Xiao Chen spots Tiger family experts and Sun God Knights among the spectators, underscoring the tension. The chapter closes with the ethereal figure of Lan Nuo on the peak, bathed in moonlight, on the verge of history.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Half-Ancestor (半祖) realm represents a pinnacle of cultivation, a step below the Ancestral Gods themselves. Achieving it is a cataclysmic event that reshapes the practitioner's body and soul, often accompanied by forging a personal sacred artifact. Lan Nuo's need for materials like seven-colored divine iron highlights the immense investment required. Illusionist spiritists like Kailo are rare, using mental powers to deceive reality; Kailo's turn to a devil's legacy (Satan) reflects a common trope in xianxia where practitioners seek alternative paths when their innate arts plateau. The Western setting blends European folklore—Christmas, Santa—with the Eastern cultivation framework, showing the world's diversity. The 200-year timeskip underscores the ephemeral nature of mortal heroes compared to cultivators.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a pivotal moment: Lan Nuo's ascension to Half-Ancestor. Readers should watch for whether outside forces attempt to disrupt the ceremony, given the presence of the Tiger family and Sun God Church. Xiao Chen's visit to Lan Nuo may reignite old tensions or forge new alliances. Meanwhile, Kailo's ambiguous role—a former enemy turned informant with devilish ties—could become a wildcard. The comedic subplot with Jin Sanyi and the captured Santa hints at lighter interludes but may have unforeseen consequences. Additionally, the gathering of elites from multiple factions portends a powder keg. The next chapters will likely focus on the ascension itself and its fallout.
