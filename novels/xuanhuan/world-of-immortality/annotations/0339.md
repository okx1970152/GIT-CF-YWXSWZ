## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the long-awaited reckoning for the half-ancestors who have tormented Xiao Chen and his allies. In a brutal, unrelenting sequence, Xiao Chen systematically destroys three half-ancestors—the Three-Infant Grandmother, Zhunti, Jieyin, and Yuan Shi—casting their remains into the Demon Well. The tone is one of tragic inevitability, as the illusory nature of these beings is laid bare. The chapter balances visceral combat with mythic revelations about reality and illusion, culminating in the shocking appearance of Suiren, the fire-drilling ancestor, who deflects a devastating attack from beyond the sky.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen kills the Three-Infant Grandmother by shattering her three bodies with the Reverse Dragon Seven Steps and Hand of the Supreme Being, then tosses her into the Demon Well. He moves to assist Chiyou against Zhunti, but Chiyou chooses self-destruction to return to his true demonic form, leaving Xiao Chen to face Zhunti alone. Xiao Chen kills Zhunti when the Demon Well claims him. Jieyin then attacks, but Xiao Chen overpowers him with the combined force of the Eight Aspect Worlds, Eight Syllables of Origin, eight ancient lamps, and eight war swords, casting Jieyin into the abyss. The Three Pure Ones of Laozi kill two of the Three Holy Spirits, but their forms dissipate. Xiao Chen kills the last Holy Spirit and then confronts Yuan Shi, who is defended by ancient heavenly soldiers. A massive chaotic hand from beyond the sky attacks, but Suiren emerges from the Dead City's darkness, wearing the burial shroud, and deflects the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge. With Suiren's protection, Xiao Chen kills Yuan Shi with a sword strike and casts him into the Demon Well.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Syllables of Origin (嗡咯啊吼哞咄噪叨) are primordial sounds more ancient than the Buddhist six-syllable mantra, representing the fundamental vibrations of existence. The Demon Well serves as a nexus of annihilation, swallowing half-ancestors and signifying the collapse of their illusory realm. Suiren, one of the true human ancestors, appears as a shrouded figure, confirming his role as a protector of humanity. The chapter deepens the theme of illusion versus reality: many half-ancestors are said to be born from dreams, not true primordial beings. This philosophical underpinning raises stakes for the final conflict between the unreal and the real.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks the climax of Xiao Chen's revenge against the half-ancestors who killed his friends and destroyed his village. The appearance of Suiren suggests that the true Ancestral Gods are awakening to counter the false gods. The fate of the remaining half-ancestors—such as Buddha and Laozi—remains uncertain. Keep an eye on the "chaotic hand" from beyond; it likely belongs to Hongjun, the ultimate false god. Xiao Chen's actions here set the stage for the final battle between the mortal world and the illusions of the cultivation world.
