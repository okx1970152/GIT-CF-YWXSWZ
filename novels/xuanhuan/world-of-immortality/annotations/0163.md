## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a cathartic yet brutal reversal as Xiao Chen turns the tables on his four Spirit-Treasury tormentors from the Ligen family. After being broken and humiliated, he uses Keke's unexpected binding power to immobilize them, then exacts a cold, surgical vengeance. The second half shifts to a tense gathering in Heavenly Emperor City, where Xiao Chen's presence triggers an involuntary domain-field collision with several of the continent's most powerful young figures: Dugu Jianmo, Yuwen Feng, and others. The chapter balances visceral horror with epic foreshadowing, setting the stage for a new power struggle among the young elite.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen is cornered by four middle-aged Ligen family experts at Mortal-Shedding Ninth Heaven, but they are at the higher Spirit-Treasury realm. They torture him, breaking several bones, and discuss killing him to cover up their actions. Xiao Chen baits them by claiming he has ties to the deepest southern wastes, buying time. The four send a spiritist to verify; he confirms sightings of Xiao Chen with dragon kings from the deep south, making them hesitate. At that moment, Keke returns carrying a massive golden ginseng and unleashes a dazzling seven-colored radiance that immobilizes all four Spirit-Treasury experts. Xiao Chen then methodically takes his revenge: he cuts off their left hands, left ears, lower legs, then arms and legs, throws them to ant nests, and finally decapitates them. He recovers in a cliff for three days, his body cultivation allowing rapid healing. News of the four experts' severed heads hanging from trees spreads. Xiao Chen returns to Heavenly Emperor City and immediately encounters a standoff between Dugu Jianmo, Yuwen Feng, and a blue-haired youth. As Xiao Chen approaches, all three release powerful energy, and a domain field spontaneously forms, drawing in other top talents like Wu Xingfeng. The field intensifies, indicating an impending clash of the young generation's peak forces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Spirit-Treasury realm (识藏) is the second major cultivation realm after Mortal-Shedding (蜕凡). It focuses on opening the hidden treasury of divine consciousness within the body, granting vastly enhanced spiritual perception and power. Xiao Chen, at the Ninth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding, is inferior in realm but his exceptional foundation and mastery of techniques like the Slay God and the Imperishable Seal allow him to contend with higher-realm opponents, though not four at once. The "domain field" (域场) that appears among the young masters is an advanced phenomenon where the convergence of powerful wills and energies creates a tangible pressure field, often a precursor to battle. It reflects the cultivation concept that true experts exert influence on their surroundings simply through presence. The Ligen family's willingness to torture a younger cultivator breaks the unwritten rule that older generations should not directly harm juniors, highlighting the fierce survival dynamics of the Eternal Continent. Keke's ability to bind four Spirit-Treasury experts emphasizes her mysterious divine origins and hints at her latent power, which surpasses normal cultivation logic.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a significant turning point: Xiao Chen has publicly clashed with a major noble family and survived, but the confrontation with the young masters sets up the next major conflict arc. Watch for how the domain field resolves—will it erupt into open battle, or will some external force intervene? Xiao Chen's revenge against the Ligen family will likely draw retaliation from their elders, so his time in Heavenly Emperor City may be limited. The appearance of Yuwen Feng and the blue-haired youth suggests that the upcoming Beast Tournament will be a gathering of the continent's most talented young cultivators, and Xiao Chen's reputation is now firmly established among them. Keke's growing power is also a wildcard; her ability to restrain Spirit-Treasury experts will attract attention and desire from even greater powers.
