## Chapter Overview

Chapter 65 marks a decisive shift in Xiao Chen's strategy from passive survival to proactive offense. The brief calm after his battle with the space spiritist Liu Mu is shattered as the broader cultivator community migrates into Dragon Island's interior, bringing political scheming and open hostility to his doorstep. The Undying Alliance's declaration of a month-long life-and-death pursuit forces Xiao Chen to confront the reality that isolation is no longer viable. This chapter crackles with the tense atmosphere of a world where alliances are fragile, hidden hands pull strings, and a single determined warrior must decide whether to strike first or be cornered. The tone remains dark, analytical, and deeply strategic, with Xiao Chen's internal calculations driving the narrative forward.

## Key Plot Points

Eight days after Xiao Chen's deadly clash with Liu Mu, the outer regions of Dragon Island have emptied as hundreds of cultivators migrate inward, settling in the forests and mountains near the Bone Sea. This mass movement is driven by survival: the island's sealing traps everyone indefinitely, and safety lies in numbers. The various alliances establish scattered encampments, each eyeing the others with wary greed. Xiao Chen's appearance immediately draws attention; his recent victory over the Revenge Alliance has marked him as a formidable blade. He learns from a would-be recruiter that Yan Qingcheng's Undying Alliance has publicly announced a month-long hunt to kill him. This news confirms his worst fears: despite the ten-person cap on alliances, powerful factions like the Undying Alliance can still manipulate events through proxies and influence. Xiao Chen recognizes that the current restrictions are temporary and that a brutal reshuffling of power is inevitable once the island's secrets are mapped. Rather than form a conventional alliance, he decides to recruit a small circle of top-tier fighters like Liu Mu—a high-risk, high-reward strategy. He locates the unconventional monks Yizhen and Yichi at their Damo Alliance camp, where they share intelligence: Yan Qingcheng and her allies have been diverted by the discovery of a young Companion Dragon King in a mountain range, thinning the Undying Alliance's strength. Xiao Chen's resolve hardens; he will strike preemptively, targeting the Undying Alliance's exposed forces while their leader is absent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The migration of cultivators into Dragon Island's interior reflects a common trope in xianxia: the fortified survival strategy of centralized settlement in a hostile wilderness. The 'ten-person cap' on alliances is a fragile social contract created to prevent any single faction from dominating the island, but as Xiao Chen notes, power imbalances and hidden sponsorship will inevitably erode such rules. The Companion Dragon King (半生龙王) is a rare dragon born directly from a dragon mother, destined for kingship if it matures; capturing one grants a cultivator unparalleled authority on the island, as it can challenge even an Ancestral Dragon. The 'forbidden zone' around the Heavenly Stele, where destructive energy prevents deeper exploration, continues to serve as a training ground for pushing one's limits. The monks Yizhen and Yichi embody the 'worldly monk' archetype: they cultivate without strict dietary rules, treating meat as 'vegetarian' due to their transcendent mindset, highlighting the World of Immortality's emphasis on inner cultivation over external dogma.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for Xiao Chen's most aggressive gambit yet. The key tension to watch is whether he can execute a lightning raid on the Undying Alliance's weakened base before Yan Qingcheng returns with the Companion Dragon King. The introduction of the Companion Dragon King as a legendary prize raises the stakes for every faction; its capture could shift the entire power balance on Dragon Island. Additionally, Xiao Chen's plan to form a loose alliance of top-tier experts like Liu Mu foreshadows a new kind of power bloc—one built on mutual strength rather than political maneuvering. Readers should also note the increasing focus on the Heavenly Stele and the forbidden zone, which hints at deeper mysteries that will likely drive later arcs. The chapter's closing line—'Xiao Chen resolved to strike'—promises a violent confrontation that will test his newfound strategic confidence and reaffirm his reputation as a force to be feared.
