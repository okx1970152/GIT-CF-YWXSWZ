## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen, merged with the Old Goat into a pseudo–Counter-Heaven Warrior, unleashes devastating power that shakes the Pure Land and the Sun Star Palace. He crushes an Upper Ancestral God known as the Mud Man, then engages an ancient Divine City from an Otherworld Stone-Man King. Multiple celestial giants—Lotus King, Stone Corpse, Lishi Beast, and a blood‑shrouded warrior—enter the fray, each with their own agendas. The chapter features explosive confrontations, the destruction of a Stone-Man King’s city, and a cunning plot by the Old Goat to seize the Heaven‑Defying War Treasure using a hidden formation inscribed on Lotus King’s petals.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen emerges from his cultivation with overwhelming power, killing three otherworld cultivators with a sword beam. The Old Goat merges with him via the divine diagram, granting him the might of a Counter‑Heaven Warrior. Xiao Chen dominates the battlefield, effortlessly disintegrating the Mud Man’s arms and forcing the Upper Ancestral God to flee into an ancient divine city. Xiao Chen attacks the city, but it proves nearly indestructible. The battle escalates as Lotus King, Stone Corpse, Lishi Beast, and a blood‑red figure all join in, each hoping to claim a piece of the divine city or the treasure. They tear the city apart, each taking a fragment. The Old Goat’s true plan emerges: he has inscribed a “number one forbidden formation” on petals provided by Lotus King, which activates to capture the Heaven‑Defying War Treasure. The four giants turn on Xiao Chen, but Lotus King aids his escape. Xiao Chen smashes the Floating Island in retaliation and escapes the Sun Star, returning to the Pure Land with the treasure in the Old Goat’s grasp.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter highlights the two major cultivation paths: the Stone‑Man path (石人路), which focuses on physical divine power and flesh indestructibility, and the Upper Ancestral God path (上祖神), which emphasizes mastery of the myriad laws and divine principles of the universe. The Mud Man exemplifies the latter; his body is weak, but his control over laws makes him formidable. The Heaven‑Defying War Treasure (逆天战宝) is a legendary artifact that can overturn heaven and earth, attracting even ancient giants. The “missing one” (遁去的一) concept from the I Ching is referenced: the formation diagram’s nature as the one that fled the great derivation makes it elusive. The giants of the Celestial Realm (Lotus King, Stone Corpse, etc.) are ancient beings near the peak of power, each with their own agendas.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen’s alliance with the Old Goat remains uneasy—the Old Goat’s motives are uncertain, and his connection to the Lotus King hints at a deeper scheme. The Heaven‑Defying War Treasure has been captured, but by whom? The Old Goat’s formation may channel the treasure to an unknown destination. Xiao Chen now has enemies among the celestial giants, particularly the Stone Corpse and Lishi Beast, who attacked him. The Upper Ancestral God Mud Man and his faction have lost their divine city but remain dangerous. The Lotus King’s intervention suggests she may have her own agenda linked to the Old Goat. Readers should watch for the Old Goat’s true intentions, the fate of the treasure, and whether Xiao Chen can protect the Pure Land from retaliation.
