## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with the lingering mystery of the dried corpse and the giant skull head floating in the Forbidden Sea. The destructive aura emanating from the corpse is so potent that it can annihilate even powerful beings outside the Paradise Lost sanctuary. Xiao Chen and his companions decide to leave the corpse in the skull and return to the Eternal Continent. This journey marks a significant shift from the deathly underworld back to the vibrant, politically charged realm of the living. Xiao Chen, accompanied by Keke, lands in Heavenly Emperor City, where he reunites with the portly merchant Fatty Zhuge. Through him, Xiao Chen learns of the Southern Wastes Dragon Clan’s rise to dominance and their oppressive treatment of the city’s inhabitants. The chapter culminates in Xiao Chen's swift and brutal elimination of Dragon Clan underlings, including Long Teng’s own son, and his public demand for Long Teng to face him. The tone blends nostalgia, political tension, and the cold fury of a warrior returned from the dead.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter begins with a tense resolution to the skull-head mystery: the dried corpse is identified as the source of the destructive energy, and Xiao Chen decides to seal it away rather than risk further calamity. After leaving the Forbidden Sea, Xiao Chen and Keke fly to the Eternal Continent. Upon reaching Heavenly Emperor City, they encounter Fatty Zhuge, now the head of the Zhuge family, who complains bitterly about the Dragon Clan’s seizure of the Xuanhuang Beast Arena and their insults to the city’s beauties. Xiao Chen, enraged by the Dragon Clan’s arrogance, immediately steps in. He kills a Dragon Clan servant with a single palm strike, then wounds the Ringleader – a purple-clad servant who planned to take Ice Qin and Fire Dance. The servant’s escape attempt is foiled, and Xiao Chen kills him. He sends a surviving lackey to summon Long Teng. At this point, Liu Mu and Niu Ren appear, astonished to find Xiao Chen alive. Their joyful reunion is cut short when Long Teng’s son, a white-clad dragon youth, arrives with experts. Xiao Chen kills the youth without hesitation, ordering him to bring Long Teng. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen calmly waiting in the Closed Abyss of Pleasure, ready for the coming confrontation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the power dynamics in the post-illusion collapse world. Heavenly Emperor City, once a neutral hub, has fallen under the shadow of the Southern Wastes Dragon Clan. The Dragon Clan, led by Long Teng, is a formidable force with deep roots in the region. Their high-handed methods reflect a common xianxia trope: the strong oppressing the weak. Xiao Chen’s return signals a potential shift in balance. His ability to kill a Mortal-Shedding realm opponent with ease demonstrates his growth since his time in the death world as a Yellow Diamond skeleton. The Closed Abyss of Pleasure (闭月羞花殿) serves as a neutral ground for cultivators to gather, reminiscent of the ‘Drunken Beauty Palace’ from earlier arcs. The reappearance of characters like Fatty Zhuge, Liu Mu, and Niu Ren reinforces the interconnected nature of the Eternal Continent’s elite. The Dragon Clan’s reliance on high-level Dragon Kings and their taboo against outside interference sets up a classic conflict between individual prowess and institutional power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation with Long Teng, a long-standing antagonist who once manipulated events on Dragon Island. Readers should watch for the arrival of Long Teng and the potential involvement of other powerful figures—perhaps the War King or the Golden Lion King. The fate of the skeleton kings (Sha Polang and the five-colored skeleton) remains open, and their possible return could tip the scales. Keke’s reaction to the Dragon Clan will be crucial; she has a personal grudge against Long Teng for her imprisonment and Xiao Chen’s near-death. Xiao Chen’s new abilities, honed in the death world, will be tested against a true powerhouse. The political landscape of the Eternal Continent is shifting, and Xiao Chen’s return may ignite a larger war among the Hundred Races. The next chapter promises a long-awaited duel that could reshape the balance of power in the Southern Wastes.
