## Chapter Overview

Chapter 609 marks a pivotal turning point in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey and reunites him with long‑lost allies. Having absorbed the essence of a Stone Man from the Peerless King Meng Degang, Xiao Chen pushes through to the peak of the Half‑Ancestor realm – the Ninth Heaven – and stands on the precipice of a momentous choice between the Stone Man path and the Upper Ancestral God path. The chapter opens with a solemn cultivation scene as Xiao Chen, Keke, the Little Defiant Dragon, and others bathe in the Stone Man's vital energy. The atmosphere is one of sacred opportunity: divine light shimmers, bones are reforged, and acupoints blossom with star‑like radiance. Yet this quiet progress is shattered when the three skeleton kings – Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, and Lunhuiwang – reappear after tens of thousands of years, dragging half a shattered Stone Man city and engaging in their trademark roguish banter. Their reunion with Xiao Chen is both heartwarming and comic, injecting a much‑needed lightness into the grim Death World setting. The chapter then escalates into a tense confrontation with the wounded Peerless King, as Xiao Chen and his assembled allies test their newfound power against a crippled but still formidable foe. The emotional core lies in the return of old friends and the protagonist's steady ascent toward the ultimate thresholds of power, even as the threat of the Otherworld looms ever larger.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen completes a Sacred Sacrifice on the Stone Man's corpse, absorbing its essence and breaking through to the Ninth Heaven of Half‑Ancestor. His 365 divinized primary acupoints manifest a hundred incarnations, each seated in a chaotic void, and the collective roar of these deities shakes the Death World. Keke, guided by the Martial Ancestor's True Scripture, strengthens her physical body and steps onto the Upper Ancestral God path. The Little Defiant Dragon reaches the Seventh Heaven of War Ancestor by merging the Stone Man essence with his dragon combat arts. The three skeleton kings – now at the Upper Ancestral Lord realm – suddenly appear and casually steal half of Meng Degang's ruined city, prompting a chaotic confrontation. Xiao Chen and his allies surround the wounded Peerless King, using the Stone Man King puppet, the Divine Diagram, the Ancient Scroll, and three Heavenly Marks to pressure him. The skeletons employ mysterious curse techniques to hinder Meng Degang's movements. Xiao Chen ultimately unleashes a true Heavenly Stele (not a mere mark) as a weapon, draining his energy but smashing off one of the Peerless King's stone arms before Meng Degang escapes into the depths of the Death World. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen clutching the severed stone arm and asking the skeletons about the woman Qingqing.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Xiao Chen's acupoint manifestation – where each of the 365 primary acupoints births a god‑like shadow – draws on the Daoist concept of the body as a microcosm of the universe. In traditional Chinese cultivation lore, the body's apertures correspond to stars, and their full activation can produce divine avatars. The Sacred Sacrifice (圣祭) technique originates from the Martial Ancestor's forbidden scriptures; it consumes the life essence of a supreme being to empower the practitioner, a taboo method that skirts the natural order of cultivation. The skeleton kings' use of curse magic recalls the 'witchcraft' or 'curse path' (咒术) often associated with the underworld in Chinese myth. Their playful demeanor contrasts sharply with the deadly power they wield, echoing the 'mad immortal' archetype. The True Heavenly Stele invoked here is not a mere imprint but the physical stele itself – a relic from the primordial age that sealed gods and suppressed worlds. Its usage as a bludgeon reflects the raw, untamed nature of such artifacts, beyond refined techniques. The chapter also touches on the Three Skeleton Kings' unusual speech patterns: they employ exaggerated, theatrical language, including mock poetry and bombastic declarations, which is a hallmark of comic relief characters in xianxia and serves to lighten the otherwise grim atmosphere.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a milestone for Xiao Chen, bringing him to the very peak of the Half‑Ancestor realm and forcing him to confront the choice between the Stone Man path and the Upper Ancestral God path. Readers should pay close attention to the implications of this decision – it will define his cultivation direction and possibly his role in the coming war against the Otherworld Supreme Ancestral Gods. The reappearance of the three skeleton kings is not merely a happy reunion; their knowledge of Qingqing and their connection to the underworld may hold keys to deeper mysteries. The Heavenly Stele that Xiao Chen used so briefly is now proven as a devastating weapon, but its cost was extreme – he was left drained. This suggests that mastery of the steles is still incomplete. The arm of Meng Degang, although a trophy, may also contain residual Stone Man essence or secrets. Finally, the skeletons' parting mention of Qingqing opens the door to a subplot that has been dormant for millennia. Keep an eye on the Death World's inner reaches, where both Meng Degang and Qingqing may be found. The next phase of the story will likely involve either pursuing the wounded king or locating the lost saintess.
