## Chapter Overview

This chapter sets the stage for one of the most immense confrontations in the World of Immortality. Keke's father, empowered by the ancient scroll of the Martial Ancestor and the five Dao imprints of fallen Stone Man kings, meets the ancient Stone Man King of the Otherworld at the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps within the Eternal Unknown. The battle is a clash of titans, with Keke's father pushing beyond his limits to wound the seemingly invincible old king. Though his body shatters, he inflicts a lasting mark. In the aftermath, the Stone Man King's fury turns toward the Nine Provinces—toward the one attempting the Final Step. But he is intercepted by Renmo Ge Qian, a complex and world-weary figure who chooses this moment to stand against his own side, declaring that he will not allow the one who once showed him kindness to be destroyed. The chapter ends with Ge Qian's dark, world‑ending chant, promising devastation to any who would oppose him.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Keke's father confronting the ancient Stone Man King atop the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. Wielding the scroll of the Martial Ancestor, which contains five Dao imprints of legendary Stone Man kings, he merges with these imprints, elevating his combat power to rival a Stone Man king himself. A fierce battle ensues, with both combatants pushing their limits. The Stone Man King wields a wolf‑tooth stone club stained with the blood of kings, while Keke's father uses the scroll and his own heaven‑defying techniques. Despite being gravely wounded—his chest shattered into blood mist—Keke's father lands a devastating counter: he tears away part of the Stone Man King's scalp and shatters his left arm, proving that the ancient king is not invincible. However, his own body disintegrates, and the scroll carries his remnant form to Xiao Chen, who flees with the treasure. The Stone Man King, enraged, turns his attention to the Nine Provinces, intending to destroy the one attempting the Final Step. He enters an underground pure land in Luoyang, but Renmo Ge Qian blocks his path. Ge Qian, a melancholic and immensely powerful cultivator who has walked the Upper Ancestral God path, declares he will not allow the Stone Man King to harm the person who once showed him kindness. A standoff ensues, with Ge Qian unleashing his self‑created 'heaven‑forsaken' divine technique, a devastating power that threatens the very fabric of the cosmos. The Stone Man King, impressed but unmoved, prepares to fight him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps represent the ultimate trial for Stone Men, guarded by the three Supreme Ancestral Gods of the Otherworld. The 'Final Step' refers to a Stone Man's complete transformation into a supreme stone being, an event of immense civilizational significance. The ancient scroll is a legendary artifact of the Martial Ancestor, containing the Dao imprints of five once‑invincible Stone Man kings from a forgotten age. These imprints are not mere techniques but the condensed essence of supreme beings, capable of elevating a half‑step warrior to near‑kingly power. Ge Qian's 'heaven‑forsaken' technique reflects his tragic philosophy—a man abandoned by heaven and earth, who cultivates pure destructive power as his only truth. His path as an Upper Ancestral God (above ordinary Ancestral God) parallels the Stone Man path, demonstrating that there are multiple routes to supremacy. The Eternal Unknown is a domain outside normal space‑time, where the three Supreme Ancestral Gods dwell and guard the Ninety-Nine Steps.
