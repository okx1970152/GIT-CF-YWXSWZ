## Chapter Overview
This chapter throws Xiao Chen into a high-stakes duel with a Half-Ancestor inside the legendary King Ship. The atmosphere crackles with tension as the mortal world's powers gather—Half-Ancestors from multiple races, allied onlookers, and a raucous band of friends led by the mischievous Ji Gong. The tone shifts from dark humor (young heroes jeering at the Yaksha Half-Ancestor) to brutal, mythic combat when Xiao Chen, fused with his stone statue body, enters the dread vessel. The King Ship itself, a skull-like craft floating on the Forbidden Sea, becomes a character, exuding deathly energy and hiding a corpse that even Half-Ancestors fear.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, having reached the Perfected Man realm, accepts the Yaksha Half-Ancestor's challenge to duel inside the King Ship. Before entering, Keke gives him the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, whose seventh leaf has grown, and he merges with his stone statue—a ten-meter-tall replica of himself that once sacrificed itself to seal a spatial rift. Inside the King Ship, Xiao Chen surprises everyone by using the stone statue's overwhelming physical power to land a sword blow that injures the Half-Ancestor, forcing him to bleed for the first time in eons. The Half-Ancestor, enraged, unleashes his 'Myriad Laws Immunity' technique and pursues Xiao Chen deeper into the ship. There, Xiao Chen discovers a dried corpse radiating destructive energy—the very source of the ship's dread—and seizes it as a weapon. Meanwhile, outside, Ji Gong and his young followers continue to mock the Half-Ancestor, and the gathered gods and alien races watch in disbelief as a mere Perfected Man wounds a Half-Ancestor. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen raising the corpse to strike, hinting at a turning point in the battle.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The King Ship (君王船) is a recurring artifact in the World of Immortality, built from the bones of an Ancestral Dragon and the wood of the World Tree. It sails the Forbidden Sea and is said to be linked to the underworld. Half-Ancestors (半祖) are beings just below Ancestral Gods, possessing domain-level powers like the Yaksha's 'Myriad Laws Immunity' (万法不侵). The stone statue body is a manifestation of Xiao Chen's cultivation path—the Stone Man (石人) path, where the body undergoes petrification to become indestructible. The Seven-Colored Sacred Tree (七彩圣树) is Keke's inherited treasure, now mature with seven leaves, each representing a phase of the beast's power. Ji Gong (济公佛爷) is a legendary Buddhist immortal from Chinese folklore, here reimagined as a trickster half-ancestor.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up the climactic duel inside the King Ship. Watch for how the dried corpse Xiao Chen wields will affect the battle—it may be the key to defeating the Half-Ancestor. The involvement of Ji Gong suggests that other hidden powers are maneuvering behind the scenes. Also note that Xiao Chen's stone statue is now greatly reduced in size but denser in power, hinting at his continued evolution on the Stone Man path. The chapter balances tense combat with irreverent humor, a hallmark of the novel's tone. Readers should anticipate a possible interference from other Half-Ancestors and the potential awakening of the King Ship's true occupant.
