## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a masterclass in Xiao Chen’s tactical survival amid the awakened Hundred Clans. Forced to flee from the relentless Night King and Skeleton King, he deliberately trespasses into the territories of the Douluo Clan and then the Giant Clan, weaponizing ancient enmities to turn the tables. The atmosphere shifts from desperate flight to a calculated gamble, as Xiao Chen uses the Douluo King’s neutrality and the Giant Clan’s sacred altar to wound his pursuer. The chapter crackles with tension, showcasing clan politics and Xiao Chen’s ability to read the battlefield. The arrival of Mabao, a War Clan king of terrifying power, sets the stage for an even deadlier confrontation.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen first flees to the Douluo Clan’s island, where the imposing Douluo King blocks his path. Rather than attack, Xiao Chen reveals that a War Clan king is nearby, hoping to spark a conflict. The Douluo King sees through the ploy but allows Xiao Chen to pass, then forces the Night King to withdraw from the island. Undeterred, the Night King and Skeleton King wait outside. Xiao Chen heads for Giant Island, knowing the Giant Clan harbors a blood feud with the War Clan. The Giants indeed ambush the Night King, but the Night King proves devastatingly powerful, injuring many Giants. However, when the Giants ignite their sacred fire, a mighty projection appears and batters the Night King, forcing him to retreat wounded. Xiao Chen seizes the moment to chase his enemy, but is intercepted by Mabao, a War Clan king whose Heaven-Deficient Finger technique once nearly killed him. The Giant warriors rally behind Xiao Chen, creating a tense standoff. The Night King’s escape and Mabao’s sudden appearance shift the balance of power once more.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Hundred Clans are ancient races awakening across the Nine Provinces, each with deep-seated rivalries. The Douluo Clan and War Clan are sworn enemies, as revealed by the Douluo King’s reaction to the War King’s presence. The Giant Clan’s blood feud with the War Clan is personal: a Giant King was killed by a War King, which is why they eagerly attack the Night King (an ally of the War Clan). The Giant Clan’s sacred altar and projection are a potent defensive mechanism, burning a priest’s lifespan to summon a semi-divine guardian. This shows that even without individual peak cultivators, clans can rely on ritualistic power. Xiao Chen’s use of the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree (a sage artifact) to wound the Night King highlights his resourcefulness. The term “War King” (战王) denotes a ruler of the War Clan, while “Night King” (夜叉王) is a fierce Yaksha lord.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen’s strategy of manipulating clan hatreds has succeeded, but only partially: the Night King is wounded but far from dead, and the appearance of Mabao—a War Clan prodigy of immense power—introduces a new, formidable enemy. The Giant Clan has now committed to Xiao Chen’s side, but their numbers alone may not suffice against a War King. The unresolved chase leaves Xiao Chen in a precarious position: he must either fight Mabao or find another escape. The chapter reinforces that raw power alone is not enough; intelligence and alliances are crucial in the Hundred Clans era. Readers should watch for how the Night King recovers and whether the Douluo King will eventually take a side.
