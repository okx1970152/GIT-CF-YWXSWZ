## Chapter Overview

This chapter thrusts Xiao Chen into a crucible of suffering and revelation. Impaled by three legendary swords, he is driven toward the third Heavenly Stele, where a fading voice—the last pure martial artist—offers a brutal bargain: kill half-ancestors in the Paradise Lost to gain the true martial way. Meanwhile, Keke is wounded by the Primordial and surrounded by supreme beings who seek her death. Xiao Chen's fury ignites as he pulls the swords from his own body to defend her, declaring war on the half-ancestors. The clash of ideology—pure martial art versus divine spell—takes center stage in a realm where all supernatural powers are nullified.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, impaled by three swords from the Four Swords of Zhuxian formation, enters a deathlike state but is sustained by his sixteen divine acupoints. He finds himself before the third Heavenly Stele, a towering monument that completes the Heavenly Stele Profound Art's cultivation diagram. An ancient voice, the last pure martial artist, dismisses Xiao Chen as a mere spell-user and challenges him to kill half-ancestors using only martial arts—without spells or divine power—in exchange for teaching him the true essence of martial combat. Meanwhile, in the Paradise Lost—a sealed garden where all supernatural abilities are suppressed—the Primordial wounds Keke with a sword and intends to seal her soul forever, citing destiny. Tongtian, Laozi, Buddha, and other half-ancestors observe without intervention. Xiao Chen, witnessing Keke's suffering, pulls the three swords from his own body and strides into the Paradise Lost, declaring his intent to kill the Primordial and all half-ancestors present. The ancient voice egging him on, Xiao Chen now faces an impossible battle in a realm where he can rely only on his physical body.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The third Heavenly Stele is a pivotal artifact in Xiao Chen's cultivation, completing the foundation he began with the Yellow River Stele and the Dragon Island Stele. The voice of the last martial artist represents a rare xianxia concept: a cultivator who pursues only physical refinement, rejecting all divine techniques, magic, and even qi manipulation—a nearly extinct path. The Paradise Lost (失乐园) is a unique sealed space where even half-ancestors lose all supernatural abilities, forcing them to rely on their raw physical forms. This creates an equalizing battlefield where Xiao Chen's recent 'weapon-body fusion' and acupoint divine meridian network give him a potential edge. The Four Swords of Zhuxian are drawn from the Fengshen Yanyi legend, here serving as both execution tools and keys to Xiao Chen's transformation. The half-ancestors—Primordial, Tongtian, Laozi, Buddha, Allah, Kong Xuan, Sun God, White Tiger Saint Emperor—represent the apex of cultivation in this world, making Xiao Chen's challenge audacious to the point of lunacy.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major turning point: Xiao Chen must win a pure martial duel in the Paradise Lost against multiple half-ancestors, stripped of all divine techniques. His newly acquired third stele diagram and the sixteen acupoint divine meridian network may be his only advantages. Keke's fate hangs in the balance—wounded by Primordial's sword, her soul may be sealed unless Xiao Chen can rescue her. The ancient voice's challenge may unlock a new, purely martial dimension to Xiao Chen's combat style, which could foreshadow his growth in future arcs. Readers should pay close attention to how Xiao Chen adapts to a combat environment where his usual powers are nullified, and whether he can truly kill a half-ancestor by martial means alone. The confrontation also deepens the enmity with Primordial and aligns Tongtian as a potential ally (he refused to kill Keke). The chapter's emotional weight lies in Xiao Chen's desperate fury and Keke's helpless concern for him even as she bleeds.
