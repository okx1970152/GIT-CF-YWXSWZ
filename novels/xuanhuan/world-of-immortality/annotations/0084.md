## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a decisive turning point in Xiao Chen's strategy on Dragon Island. No longer content to react to threats, he forms a pact with the fearsome Space Spiritist Liu Mu and launches a preemptive strike against his enemies. The night raid on Treefolk Valley is merely the opening move, quickly swallowed by a far larger conspiracy. A shadowy hand—likely the legendary swordsman Dugu Jianmo—triggers a continent-wide bloodbath, plunging the chaotic island into a new Great Reshuffling. The atmosphere is one of cold calculation and sudden, brutal violence, as Xiao Chen and Liu Mu cut through the chaos toward their true targets.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and Liu Mu seal their alliance, recognizing mutual benefit. Liu Mu reveals a disturbing sense of being watched by an unseen presence, adding an layer of mystery to his character. Their first joint action is a feint: Xiao Chen dispatches the seductive Li Yun of the Red Powder Alliance to broadcast a false threat against the Natural Alliance, while the real target is Treefolk Valley and Zhao Lin'er. Under the cover of darkness, Liu Mu's Space Spiritist powers prove devastating, annihilating the Treefolk Valley defenders in moments. However, Zhao Lin'er has already fled, sensing danger. Before they can press further, the entire encampment erupts into a massive, coordinated purge—the very night other power players chose to settle scores. This is no random chaos but a signal for a new round of alliance-crushing. Xiao Chen and Liu Mu, undeterred, turn toward the Natural Alliance, wading through the carnage. They find the alliance already destroyed, with Yaluode and Kai'ao missing. The chapter closes with the pair racing through a battlefield of screaming cultivators and blinding energy, the larger game now fully engaged.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter highlights two rare and deadly cultivation paths: the Space Spiritist and the Little Li Flying Dagger. Space Spiritists are said to be 'kings among their peers' because their ability to seal and control a pocket of space renders most conventional attacks useless within their domain. Liu Mu's effortless massacre of Treefolk Valley demonstrates this terrifying mastery. The Little Li Flying Dagger, though not deployed in this chapter, looms as a constant threat. Originating with the legendary Li Xunhuan in the mortal world, the technique is said to be inescapable and has already nearly killed Xiao Chen once. The mention of Dugu Jianmo, descendant of the peerless swordsman Dugu Qiubai, further expands the roster of deadly lineages active on Dragon Island. These references tie the novel’s power system to Chinese martial literature, where names like Li Xunhuan and Dugu Qiubai carry mythic weight.

## Reading Guide

This chapter resets the board for the next major conflict. With the Great Reshuffling reignited, expect the fragile alliance system to collapse into all-out war. Xiao Chen and Liu Mu now face the combined hostility of Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, Wang Tong, and the mysterious Dugu Jianmo. Zhao Lin'er's escape means she remains a wild card, possibly seeking new protectors. Watch for the first direct clash between Xiao Chen and these top-tier opponents, and pay attention to the fate of the Natural Alliance's leaders—their survival or death will shift the balance. The alliance between Xiao Chen and Liu Mu is still untested in prolonged combat; its resilience may determine whether this night's gamble pays off.
