## Chapter Overview
This chapter undergoes a radical shift in pacing, moving from a tense confrontation with the treacherous Xu Tian to a seclusion spanning thirteen millennia. Xiao Chen returns to the Nine Provinces after capturing a clone of Xu Tian and acquiring a stone spear that had belonged to a Stone Man King. He then submerges into the earth, using the Nine Ancient Lamps to cultivate and search for missing war swords. Over thousands of years, he transforms from a War Ancestor into a Six-Heaven Super Ancestral God, mastering ever more profound divine laws. The chapter also depicts the brutal end of a short civilization cycle at the hands of the Otherworld gods, which Xiao Chen witnesses in helpless silence. The tone is one of epic patience and tragic observation, culminating in a jarring transition to a modern-day scene in Yanjing, where archaeologists detect ancient iron armor and mysterious underground life signs. This chapter redefines the scale of time in the story and deepens Xiao Chen's character through endured sorrow and solitude.

## Key Plot Points
Xu Tian, having lured Xiao Chen into a sealed dead city, reveals the difficulty of discussing secrets in the death world and then attacks Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen unleashes the ancient scroll containing the imprints of five Stone Man Kings, which absorbs Xu Tian's stone spear and overwhelms the clone, destroying it. Xiao Chen obtains the stone spear and uses the scroll to escape back to the Nine Provinces. Once there, he goes into a deep, centuries-long seclusion beneath the land, activating the Nine Ancient Lamps to guide his cultivation. Over the next several thousand years, he slowly collects nineteen war swords and deepens his understanding of the divine diagram. Eventually, he breaks through from War Ancestor to Ancestral God, reaching the Sixth Heaven of the Super Ancestral God realm. The Otherworld gods then prematurely end a short civilization cycle, slaughtering all advanced races—humans, elves, dragons—and leaving the continent desolate. Xiao Chen remains hidden, enduring the massacre without intervening, aware that his time has not yet come. After a period of deep depression and stillness, he cultivates for another six thousand years, emerging with a new state of equanimity. The chapter closes with a dramatic shift: in a modernized Yanjing, two men discuss an archaeological dig that has unearthed fragments of ancient iron armor and detected life signs deep underground, hinting that Xiao Chen may be stirring once more.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter exemplifies the xianxia trope of extended seclusion (闭关) taken to its grandest extreme—thirteen thousand years. The use of the Nine Lamps (九盏古灯) as cultivation aids ties back to earlier volumes where they were discovered as sacred artifacts linked to the Nine Provinces' seal. The Heavenly Steles (天碑) and war swords are recurring motifs representing the fragmented inheritance of the Martial Ancestor. The concept of a 'civilization cycle'—where the Otherworld gods periodically erase all sentient life and reseed the world—draws on Buddhist ideas of kalpas and cyclical creation and destruction. The 'Super Ancestral God' (级祖神) marks a distinct power tier above the ordinary Ancestral God, requiring the fusion of primal divine laws. The modern excavation scene introduces a technological layer, blending xianxia with near-contemporary urban imagery—a common subgenre known as 'urban xianxia' that highlights the passage of eons and the resilience of ancient relics. The iron armor fragments likely belong to ancestors from the Nine Provinces, and the underground life detection may signal the awakening of Xiao Chen or another sealed powerhouse.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is primarily a bridge across millennia, setting up the final phase of the story. Readers should note Xiao Chen's psychological hardening: he witnesses genocide but holds back, a decision that adds layers to his character. The appearance of modern civilization suggests that humanity has recovered, but the Otherworld gods' cycle continues. The detection of life underground and the ancient armor pieces are clear hooks for the next arc—Xiao Chen's return to action. Key questions: What will Xiao Chen do now that he has reached Super Ancestral God level? How will he confront the Otherworld gods after seeing their casual cruelty? The excavation team's find may accelerate his emergence. Also, the mention that Xu Tian's true body is near the final step hints at future confrontation. The chapter's lengthy time-skip also allows secondary characters to have aged or passed; pay attention to whether any old allies reappear. Finally, the shift to modern Yanjing signals that the story is entering a new era—an urban-cultivation hybrid—which may bring fresh challenges and alliances.
