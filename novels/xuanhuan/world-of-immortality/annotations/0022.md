## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers into the eerie Death Swamp, where Xiao Chen joins a desperate battle between sentient skeletons and mindless zombies. The clash of undead forces is rendered with visceral, archaic prose, emphasizing the brutal survival laws of the World of Immortality. Xiao Chen's tactical acumen shines as he aids the three skeleton kings, earning their trust and a glimpse of their mysterious evolution. Yet the swamp holds deeper dread: a king-level undead entity lurks at its heart, forcing a strategic retreat. The chapter balances chilling horror with tactical foresight, as Xiao Chen plots to turn this deathscape into a weapon against his pursuers.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen observes the battle between three skeletons and five zombies, noting the zombies' superior durability but the skeletons' speed. He joins the fray, attacking a zombie's weak point (eyes) and eventually shattering one after eighteen blows to the same spot. With his help, the skeletons gain the upper hand, tearing apart three zombies while Xiao Chen finishes two. After the battle, the skeletons extract five green crystals from the corpses and offer two to Xiao Chen, which he declines. The crystals are then evenly divided and absorbed by the skeletons, visibly strengthening their bones. As dawn breaks, Xiao Chen explores deeper into the swamp but is halted by the skeletons' urgent warnings of a deadly presence—an Undead King. He retreats and decides to use the swamp as a defensive position, believing his pursuers will be lured there. Cut to the beach: Gulou, the brown-haired enemy, watches two ships approach, fearing the Eight-Armed Evil Dragon will attack them. He has set up a signal flag to warn the newcomers. The chapter ends with tension on both fronts: Xiao Chen's trap and Gulou's rescue.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter delves into the undead ecology of Dragon Island. Zombies here are not the shambling corpses of Western lore; they are rubbery, iron-hard bodies with terrible resilience, their only vulnerability being the eyes. Skeletons, conversely, are swifter and more agile, able to coordinate and even absorb crystal energy to strengthen their frames—a process resembling cultivation. The 'corpse miasma' (尸瘴) that rises at dusk is a recurring hazard in such death-tainted zones, and its dissipation at dawn allows the living to move more freely. The mention of an 'Undead King' alludes to a hierarchy among the dead, suggesting that even in death, the strong dominate the weak. The green crystals extracted from the zombies are a form of 'deathly' spiritual stone, potent but infused with yin energy.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation. Xiao Chen's decision to use the Death Swamp as a battlefield is both clever and reckless—the Undead King looms as an unknown variable that could disrupt his plans. Meanwhile, Gulou's reinforcements from the ships will likely join the hunt for Xiao Chen, escalating the danger. The three skeletons are becoming more than mere tools; their offering of crystals and their fear of the Undead King suggest a growing bond and a deeper lore. How will Xiao Chen handle the arrival of fresh enemies? And what role will the Undead King play when the fighting begins? The next chapters promise a chaotic three-way struggle.
