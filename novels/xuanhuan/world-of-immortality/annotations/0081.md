## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a masterful blend of high-stakes assassination, brutal three-way combat, and a sudden cataclysmic intervention that reshapes the battlefield. The tension is relentless: Xiao Chen survives a near-mythical killing stroke from the Little Li Flying Dagger, only to face a coordinated assault from the genius illusionist Kailuo, the flying dagger heir Wang Tong, and the ethereal yet deadly Yan Qingcheng. Just as the conflict reaches its peak, a primordial Snow Jade Dragon erupts from its slumber, sending all combatants scrambling for survival. The chapter is a showcase of Xiao Chen’s growing combat mastery, his ruthlessness toward enemies, and the ever-present danger of Dragon Island’s ancient beasts. The tone is cold, brutal, and mythic — a classic example of the novel’s tragic, desolate aesthetic.

## Key Plot Points

Wang Tong finally unleashes his legendary flying dagger, timing it perfectly as Xiao Chen’s Sealing Divinity light screen flickers out. The tiny, translucent blade appears almost impossibly, aiming directly for Xiao Chen’s heart. In a moment of desperate fortune, the crystalline shards of Keke’s rainbow birth-eggshell, which Xiao Chen carried as keepsakes, intercept the dagger and shatter its deadly momentum. Shocked but unharmed, Xiao Chen turns his fury on the trio of attackers. He engages Kailuo’s illusions and Wang Tong’s second dagger with cold precision, deflecting the second flying dagger with his own Big Dipper Sealing Divinity technique. The coordinated assault fails to finish him. Then the Snow Jade Dragon — a fifty-meter-long beast of white ice, with a dragon’s head, a wolf’s body, and a crocodile’s tail — bursts from the mountain, enraged by the noise. It smashes the peak, sending all parties fleeing. In the chaos, Yan Qingcheng attempts to strike Xiao Chen from above, but he counters with devastating force, sending her tumbling into the snow. A subsequent avalanche buries her. Xiao Chen chooses to hide beneath the snow rather than flee with the crowd, demonstrating his tactical patience. Hours later, he emerges, surveying the destruction and reflecting on the power of dragons and his own path of cultivation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Little Li Flying Dagger is a legendary weapon technique originating from the mortal-world hero Li Xunhuan, a figure who could slay gods with a single throw. Its appearance here, wielded by his descendant Wang Tong, carries immense cultural weight for readers familiar with Chinese wuxia. The dagger is described as ‘a point of light like a comet,’ and its failure to pierce Xiao Chen is a shocking subversion of its reputation. The eggshell fragments of Keke — the rainbow divine jade eggshells — prove to be far more than mere mementos; they are remnants of a primordial divine beast’s birth, possessing hardness beyond refined steel. The Snow Jade Dragon introduced in this chapter is a formidable creature from the ice-bound regions of Dragon Island, known for its violent temper when disturbed. Even on an island where divine powers are suppressed, its physical might alone can shatter mountain peaks. The chapter also illustrates the brutal pragmatism of the World of Immortality: Xiao Chen shows no hesitation in nearly killing Yan Qingcheng, a beauty of peerless fame, because enmity is not softened by charm.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for several ongoing threads. Yan Qingcheng has been buried alive under an avalanche — is she truly dead, or will she survive as a bitter enemy? The Snow Jade Dragon’s rampage has scattered the alliances, but Wang Tong and Kailuo remain active and will likely seek revenge for their failed assassination. Xiao Chen’s survival and calm retreat demonstrate his increasing resilience, but the encounter reminds him (and the reader) that Dragon Island holds far greater threats than mortal cultivators. The appearance of a primeval dragon capable of leveling mountains raises the stakes for future exploration. Readers should watch for the consequences of Yan Qingcheng’s disappearance — her Undying Alliance may fracture or rally against Xiao Chen. Meanwhile, the mystery of Keke’s origins grows deeper with the revelation that her eggshell can block a legendary weapon. This chapter is a turning point where Xiao Chen transitions from hunted survivor to a hunter in his own right, ready to take the initiative.
