## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with a breathtaking display of power as a hazy figure from the legendary Imperial City faces down three of the most formidable Otherworld experts: the Female Stone Sage Zhenmu Nüsheng, the Epochal King Meng Degang, and an inscrutable Upper Ancestral God named Bolinqi. The Imperial City master moves with transcendent authority, repelling each opponent with seemingly effortless strikes. The battle escalates as seven incomplete Stone Men, long sealed beneath the Death World, are summoned to turn the tide. Amid this chaos, Xiao Chen seizes the moment to lead Keke and his allies out of the deadly formation, immediately launching a counter-pursuit against fleeing Otherworld cultivators. Keke's past torment is touched upon, and when a reckless Ancestral God insults her, Xiao Chen's cold fury spills over into decisive violence. The chapter balances cosmic warfare with personal stakes, deepening the mystery of the Imperial City while advancing Xiao Chen's own war.

## Key Plot Points

The Imperial City figure engages in three simultaneous clashes, each showcasing his supreme martial skill and law manipulation. He shatters Zhenmu Nüsheng’s finger, rips a gate tower off Meng Degang’s demonic city, and forces the Upper Ancestral God Bolinqi into a defensive posture. Recognizing the threat, the three Otherworld peak experts unite, joined by two additional flawed Stone Men and a host of Ancestral God-level supporters. The Imperial City master is nearly overwhelmed until he activates a hidden formation, releasing seven incomplete Stone Men that he had previously resurrected and controlled. These stone titans reverse the momentum, pinning the three leaders while the Imperial City figure cuts down lesser Ancestral Gods. A new wave of reinforcements from the Death World’s abyss—a scaled demon, a sacred undead knight, and other Stone Man-level beings—stabilizes the enemy lines. Xiao Chen, watching from cover, decides not to enter the melee. Instead, he uses the Eight Aspect Worlds to escape the sealed area and chases down scattered Otherworld fugitives. He unleashes the Stone Man King puppet to trap a wounded Stone Man and personally confronts an Ancestral God who insulted Keke, showing no mercy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Stone Men (石人) are beings who undergo a supremely dangerous path of cultivation, attempting to transform their bodies into indestructible stone. The “final step” represents the culmination of this process, granting quasi-Ancestral God power. The seven incomplete Stone Men here are those who failed that final step yet retain formidable strength. Upper Ancestral Gods (上祖神) follow a different path, focusing on commanding myriad world laws rather than physical perfection. The Death World is a realm of death and rebirth, home to fire-seed beings and ancient powers. Keke’s Heaven-Defying Clan (逆天家族) is a cursed lineage that was transformed from human to beast form, and its blood holds incredible properties sought by cultivators. The Imperial City (帝城) is a mysterious entity that appears in times of great conflict, possibly tied to the vanished Heavenly Emperor. The Stone Man King puppet Xiao Chen wields is a powerful artifact from earlier chapters, capable of challenging even true Stone Men.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the Imperial City figure’s identity—clues suggest he may be a reincarnation of the Heavenly Emperor or a previous Stone Man King who once dominated the Nine Provinces. The seven incomplete Stone Men could become pivotal allies or unpredictable variables. Xiao Chen’s decision to pursue rather than confront the peak powers reflects his tactical growth; he is conserving strength and targeting weak points. Keke’s blood is now a high-value target, meaning she will need protection in future arcs. The emergence of Death World’s deep powers (the scaled demon, sacred undead) heralds an escalation in the war. The chapter’s ending, with Xiao Chen’s cold vengeance, signals that he will not tolerate any threat to his companions. The formation breaking and the Imperial City master’s true intentions remain shrouded, setting up a major confrontation in the chapters to come.
