## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a decisive turning point in the Mortal World conflict. Xiao Chen unleashes his full fury on Mount Tai, slaughtering the top-tier cultivator Deng Yu and numerous others, cementing his reputation as an unstoppable force. The battlefield shifts from open combat to psychological warfare as allies and old foes alike witness his ruthless efficiency. Yet amid the carnage, the chapter is equally defined by quiet moments of reunion, loss, and introspection—Xiao Chen learns the fate of Ruoshui, confronts grief through the eyes of a friend, and resolves to enter seclusion to internalize the six Heavenly Stele diagrams. The narrative balances epic-scale violence with intimate emotional weight, setting the stage for a new phase of cultivation.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen kills Deng Yu, the strongest young cultivator of the cultivation world, and follows up with a swift massacre of five ambushers using his Eight Aspect Worlds technique, shocking both allies and enemies. The blue-robed Fang Tianqi, a Nirvana-realm expert, retreats rather than face Xiao Chen alongside the assassin Jue Dao and the scriptures-wielding Samo. After the battle, Xiao Chen reunites with several key figures: Jue Dao acknowledges him as a peer, Hai Yuntian pleads for his sister's life, and Yan Qingcheng openly admits she still harbors a desire to fight him. The white-shelled turtle appears in human form and offers to train the Little Defiant Dragon. Xiao Chen visits his old friends Niu Ren, Liu Mu, and Yizhen, learning that Qingqing has been taken by Taihao and the Xuanwu Patriarch. Most devastatingly, Chen Fang confirms that Ruoshui is dead—her body seized by the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden. Xiao Chen erases his memories of her from his heart, but the pain resurfaces. He seals his emotions by deciding to visit Chiyou and then enter long-term seclusion to master the complete stele inheritance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The cultivation realm known as 'Nirvana' or 'Jiehuo' (劫火) is a volatile stage where a cultivator's power fluctuates drastically, making combat unpredictable. Fang Tianqi wields this level, explaining his caution. The 'Six Paths of Reincarnation' and 'Thirty-Six War Swords' are legendary techniques from the ancient Dragon Island steles. Xiao Chen's 'Martial Imprint' is a unique path that forgoes divine abilities for pure physical cultivation, granting him terrifying combat efficiency. The concept of sealing part of one's soul is a recurring xianxia trope—Xiao Chen's emotional numbness is hinted to stem from a fragment of his soul still trapped in a stone statue in the Ancient God Wasteland. The 'Ancestral Dragon Ship' and 'King Ship' are the only means of crossing the sealed Forbidden Sea, connecting the Mortal World to the Eternal Continent. The Mortal World's spiritual essence is thin compared to the cultivation world, but its ancient battlefields and primordial sites still hold immense potential.

## Reading Guide
With Xiao Chen preparing to enter seclusion, readers can anticipate a major power-up that will allow him to challenge demigods and half-ancestors. The unresolved fates of Qingqing (captured by the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden) and the Little Defiant Dragon (being trained by an ancient dragon king) are likely to drive the next arc. The revelation that Ruoshui's body was stolen will fuel Xiao Chen's cold fury, while the half-ancestors he brought to the Mortal World (Primordial, Tongtian, etc.) are recovering their artifacts and may soon clash with the invading cultivation world. The chapter also underscores the theme of memory and identity—Xiao Chen's deliberate erasure of Ruoshui contrasts with Chen Fang's raw grief, suggesting that true emotional growth cannot be achieved through suppression. The coming chapters will likely focus on Xiao Chen's transformation as he reconciles his martial power with his humanity.
