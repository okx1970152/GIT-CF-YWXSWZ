## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a dramatic pivot as Xiao Chen, impersonating the slain Otherworld Ancestral God Choutian, ventures into the heart of enemy territory—the ruined homeland of the Otherworld cultivators. The once-desolate continent transforms into a blooming paradise as Ancestral Gods descend. The assembly reveals two earth-shattering developments: the Otherworld plans to invade the Death World, and an Upper Ancestral God is attempting to break through to an unprecedented realm. The chapter is steeped in tension, blending infiltration suspense with cosmic-scale revelations about the upcoming war.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, having trained among the ancestors, adopts the guise of Choutian using a half-stone skull to mask his identity. He learns Choutian's techniques from Yan Huang, the Old Farmer, and others, and carries the sealed Dead City within his acupoint spaces—a deadly gamble. He sets off with the 'envoy' to the Otherworld's sacred gathering place: a fragment of their original world preserved after a cataclysmic war. There, he observes the assembly of at least fifteen Ancestral Gods, including familiar enemies like Lan Yufeng and Lu Lupei. The head Ancestral God reveals that his father, an Upper Ancestral God who died exploring the Death World, has returned as an Ancestral Lord Warship, providing vital intelligence about that realm. The Otherworld now plans to invade the Death World as a new battlefield. Simultaneously, a mighty fluctuation indicates someone is making the final breakthrough step, but is being opposed by unknown forces—possibly from the Celestial Realm. Xiao Chen realizes the situation is dire; the Otherworld might gain a supreme being.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'final step' refers to breaking through from Upper Ancestral God to a realm beyond—perhaps the Creator level or the realm of the Primordial Celestial Realm. The chapter introduces the concept of 'worlds as opposites' (light/dark, real/void), with the Death World as the dark reflection of the living worlds. The Otherworld's origin story—their home continent shattered in an ancient war—adds depth to their civilization's motivation. The use of 'divine territories' where Ancestral Gods can create life and transform landscapes showcases their power. The infiltration technique using a half-stone skull to hide one's true essence is a clever xianxia trope. The 'Sigh of Grief' technique combines curse and martial art, reflecting the hybrid cultivation systems in the novel.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up the climax of the Death World arc. Pay attention to Xiao Chen's risky containment of the Dead City—it may become a key weapon. The news about the Celestial Realm's possible intervention introduces a wild card. The antagonistic figure attempting the final step may emerge as a new supreme threat. The gathering of so many Ancestral Gods suggests a major offensive is imminent. Readers should also note the tension between Xiao Chen's disguise and the danger of exposure, especially when interacting with familiar enemies like Lan Yufeng. The next chapters will likely see Xiao Chen's infiltration tested and the breakthrough attempt's outcome.
