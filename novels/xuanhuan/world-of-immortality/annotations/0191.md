## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a spectacular, high-stakes beast battle that shifts the power dynamics of the six sacred beasts. Keke, the adorable yet terrifyingly powerful creature, intervenes in the ongoing struggle and single-handedly challenges the established order. The narrative pulses with tension as Keke takes on the Golden Lion King—a beast capable of dominating Spirit-Treasury realm cultivators—and humiliates it through raw strength and relentless determination. The chapter crackles with sensory energy: golden light, multicolored radiance, earth-shattering impacts, and the ominous promise of the Lion King's third eye. It's a masterclass in escalation, blending cute character moments with brutal combat, and ends on a gut-wrenching cliffhanger that leaves Xiao Chen and the readers in breathless suspense.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Keke already engaged in battle against the six sacred beasts, having stunned all onlookers. Keke focuses on the Golden Lion King, using her seven-colored light to immobilize the beast and smash it repeatedly into the arena floor, drawing blood after sixty-three impacts. The Lion King's third vertical eye, surrounded by ancient runes and divine flame, proves exceptionally difficult to seal—a source of intrigue and dread. As Keke successfully neutralizes this threat, the other three sacred beasts (White Tiger, Golden Ape Jinzi, Peacock King) attack in concert. Keke dodges their combined assault, then recognizes the Peacock King as the greatest threat due to its mysterious Chaos Azure Feather. She charges through the feather's destructive mist directly at the Peacock King. At the moment her defenses are weakest, the Golden Lion King—cold and strategic—unleashes its Destruction Eye and Reincarnation Eye, flooding the arena with deadly light. The chapter freezes on this moment of crisis, with Xiao Chen's heart sinking.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The battle showcases several core xianxia tropes: the 'sacred beast' hierarchy, where bloodline and innate abilities determine power; the 'third eye' as a source of ultimate destructive or transcendental power (common in Chinese mythology, e.g., Erlang Shen); and the 'Chaos Azure Feather,' a reference to the primordial chaos (hundun) that is both a substance and a state of being, often used to represent the origin of all things. Keke's seven-colored light and the Lion King's eyes represent different cultivation paths: one of binding and control, the other of pure destruction and reincarnation. The arena setting, the Xuanhuang Arena, serves as a neutral ground for testing strength—a staple of the genre where political and personal conflicts are resolved through formal combat.
