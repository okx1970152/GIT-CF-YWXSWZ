## Chapter Overview

In this pivotal chapter, Xiao Chen awakens from months of silent cultivation on the Yellow Clay Altar, having broken into the perilous Nirvana realm. The mortal world's New Year's Eve contrasts starkly with the siege awaiting him: enemies from the Sun Church, the Tiger Clan, and the cultivation world have converged on the ancient village, patient as wolves. Chen's first death tribulation has reshaped his body and soul, granting him power that rivals Longevity-level experts. The chapter crackles with a tragic, desolate intensity as Chen steps from the altar into a blood-soaked reckoning, using the legendary 'Weng' sound technique to annihilate over a hundred foes. It is both a coming-out party for his new strength and a declaration of war against the powers that have hunted him.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's breakthrough to Nirvana marks a fundamental transformation: he has survived one of nine death tribulations, each of which can obliterate nine out of ten cultivators. His physical body has been reforged—his gray hair turned black, his skin gleaming like crystal—and his combat power now equals a Longevity realm expert. He emerges on New Year's Eve, a night of human joy, to find the ancient village ringed by enemies who have waited months for him to leave the altar's protection. Among them are a Sun Knight, a cultivator brother of the legendary Fang Tianqi, and Hai Fanyun, father of Hai Yuntian and Hai Yunxue. Chen dispatches the first two with contemptuous ease, his Supreme Hand shattering artifacts and bodies alike. Hai Fanyun attempts to flee but is run down by Chen's Eight-Aspect Worlds speed. Despite reinforcements from the Tiger Clan arriving, Chen kills Hai Fanyun with a single 'Weng' sound that shatters his soul. Returning to the village, he unleashes the same technique on the assembled pursuers, erasing over a hundred enemies in one blast. He then seats himself on the Yellow Clay Altar and issues an open challenge to all who wish to kill him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nirvana realm (涅槃) is the fourth major cultivation realm, characterized by extreme danger and volatility. Its name derives from Buddhist concept of nirvana, but in this context it means a cyclic death-and-rebirth process. Each of the nine 'death tribulations' (死劫) forces the cultivator to undergo a life-or-death crisis; surviving one strengthens the body and soul dramatically, but failure means total annihilation. This realm is a great filter that separates true geniuses from those who peaked early. The 'Weng' sound technique (嗡字音) is an ancient, soul-shaking chant that resonates with the universe's primordial vibrations, acting as both an offensive weapon and a defensive barrier. The Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台) is a mysterious object associated with Ancestral God Suiren; it exudes a power that even half-ancestors fear, and it has been Chen's refuge and cultivation platform. Hai Fanyun's 'Black Lotus' technique (黑莲) is derived from a demonic variant of Buddhist lotus symbolism, emphasizing corruption and destruction.

## Reading Guide

The chapter ends with Chen's bold declaration from the Yellow Clay Altar, inviting all enemies to come and challenge him. This sets up a major convergence of young experts and old rivals from across the Eternal Continent. Readers should watch for the arrival of top talents like Fang Tianqi, who will surely seek revenge for his brother's death, and for the Tiger Clan's next move after losing their half-ancestor ally's father. Chen's mastery of the 'Weng' sound technique hints that the 'Eight Syllables of Origin' may play a larger role in his growing arsenal. The Nirvana realm's volatility means Chen's power will fluctuate; future chapters may show him weakened or facing a second death tribulation at a critical moment. Keep an eye on Hai Yunxue, who witnessed her father's death and will not rest until she avenges him.
