## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a spectacular display of Xiao Chen's refined combat supremacy as he storms Beidou Academy, systematically dismantling its four strongest disciples. The atmosphere crackles with tension as Xiao Chen, likened to a humanoid tyrant dragon by onlookers, methodically crushes opponents from the sixth to the eighth heaven of Mortal-Shedding. His cold efficiency and devastating raw power are contrasted with the academy's shocked crowd, marking a turning point in his journey across the Eternal Continent. The chapter balances piercing action with subtle character observations, setting a new standard for the stakes Xiao Chen now commands.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's instant defeat of Sixth Heaven Wang Ming, shocking the crowd. He then faces the invisible Spiritist Xu Feng, using his premonition ability to nullify the advantage of stealth, and breaks both of Xu Feng's arms. Next, Seventh Heaven Xu Long, known as Iron Fist, confronts Xiao Chen. Expecting a power match, Xu Long is overwhelmed when Xiao Chen's crystalline fists shatter his defenses, causing multiple fractures. Xu Long reveals his flight ability as a Spiritist, but Xiao Chen counters with the Undying Wings, granting him aerial superiority and crushing Xu Long midair. Finally, Eighth Heaven Lu Muda enters—a top disciple who recently graduated. After a tense negotiation, they engage. Their battle is a fierce conflict of equals, but Xiao Chen unleashes the Slay God technique, an ultimate killing move from the Divine Stele, to overpower Lu Muda and claim victory. The crowd erupts, with students from neighboring academies cheering. The chapter establishes Xiao Chen's terrifying reputation and foreshadows future challenges from Beidou's inner academy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the realm hierarchy within the Mortal-Shedding stage: the six, seven, and eight heavenly tiers represent significant power gaps. Spiritists, like Xu Feng, are innate practitioners of mysterious arts such as invisibility, while warriors like Xu Long blend physical training with spiritual arts. The Undying Wings technique, derived from the Undying Sect's demonic scriptures, allows flight even below the Spirit-Treasury realm—a rare advantage that makes Xiao Chen a formidable airborne combatant. The Slay God technique is one of four ultimate killing moves Xiao Chen learned from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art; it concentrates devastating power into a single strike, draining the user significantly. The academy system, with inner and outer divisions, mirrors the competitive nature of cultivation where reputation and strength determine status.

## Reading Guide

With this victory, Xiao Chen has publicly declared his arrival on the Eternal Continent. The Beidou Academy's inner disciples, hidden champions rumored to be even stronger, may now take notice. His uses of the Undying Wings and Slay God techniques also signal his connection to the Undying Sect, which could draw both allies and enemies. Meanwhile, the mysterious ruined divine halberd and the King Ship remain as unresolved threads, hinting at greater conflicts beyond petty academy rivalries. Readers can anticipate political machinations, recruitment attempts, and perhaps a confrontation with Yan Qingcheng's faction in the coming chapters.
