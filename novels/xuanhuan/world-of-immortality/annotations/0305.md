## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks an emotional watershed for Xiao Chen as he confronts the impossible choice between destroying his beloved Ruoshui's physical body and allowing the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden to live. The half-ancestors press for a decisive kill, forcing Xiao Chen to accept that Ruoshui's consciousness is gone forever. What follows is a raw, introspective journey through grief, stagnation, and eventual renewal. The chapter balances mythic-scale conflict with deeply personal sorrow, then transitions into a meditative recovery arc that culminates in a cultivation breakthrough. It is both a farewell and a new beginning, setting the stage for Xiao Chen's reengagement with a world in upheaval.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Keke struggling to hold the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, preventing the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden from using it. Primordial, Tongtian, Zhunti, and Chiyou press forward, forcing the maiden to realize she faces annihilation. Xiao Chen, tormented, ultimately decides to kill her despite the destruction of Ruoshui's body. The half-ancestors attack, but discover a lotus carving on the bridge—the maiden's original form—and a carving of Ruoshui's figure. Xiao Chen delicately removes Ruoshui's carving and places it in Paradise Lost with a memorial stele, then leaves the bridge to the half-ancestors. He flies away with Keke, returning to his mortal home. For three months, he sinks into deep grief, isolating himself and losing direction. His parents and childhood friends gradually pull him back into ordinary life—temple fairs, fishing, hunting, travel. This reconnection with the mundane world and nature sparks a spiritual shift. Over the following months, he travels widely and experiences a profound change in perspective, culminating in a breakthrough to Sky-Mastery Sixth Heaven. He emerges with renewed purpose, only to learn of vast changes: Dragon Kings warring for Ancestral Dragon status, new half-ancestors from the Cultivation World descending, Chiyou attempting to free an ancient god, and a rising young generation tournament.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge is revealed to be an artifact of immense age, with carvings that have become sentient beings over the ages—the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden originated as one such lotus carving. This reflects the xianxia trope that even inanimate things can gain life and cultivation through exposure to spiritual energy over endless years. The half-ancestors' decision to kill the maiden despite her pleas highlights the ruthless pragmatism of high-level cultivators, who act without hesitation when they perceive a threat. Xiao Chen's grief and subsequent recovery touch on the theme of heart demons in cultivation, where emotional entanglements can cause stagnation or breakthroughs. His path from despair to clarity mirrors the Daoist concept of returning to simplicity—finding wisdom in ordinary life, not just in isolated training. The breakthrough at Sky-Mastery Sixth Heaven without deliberate cultivation illustrates how profound emotional and psychological shifts can directly elevate one's cultivation level.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's emotional recovery sets the stage for a more resolute protagonist ready to face the world's turbulence. Pay attention to the growing conflict between the native half-ancestors of the Eternal Continent and the newly arrived half-ancestors from the Cultivation World—this will shape power dynamics in upcoming arcs. The young generation tournament and Dragon Kings' war for Ancestral Dragon status are seeds for major events. Also note Chiyou's attempt to free an ancient god at Changyang Mountain, which may introduce a new force. Xiao Chen's renewed determination suggests he will not shy from these conflicts, and his connection to Keke and the Paradise Lost realm remains a wild card. The chapter closes with a sense of forward momentum, promising both personal growth and escalating stakes.
