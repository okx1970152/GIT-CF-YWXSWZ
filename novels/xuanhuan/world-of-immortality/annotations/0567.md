## Chapter Overview

After the desperate struggle to secure the dual city, Xiao Chen finds a moment of respite—but the Celestial Realm is no sanctuary. This chapter balances the quiet solemnity of Pangu's sacred land with the blazing intensity of the Sun Star, as Xiao Chen seeks a legendary treasure that might turn the tide for the Nine Provinces. The emotional tone shifts from the weight of history to the sharp edge of opportunity, with a lingering sense that even the gods are watching.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen successfully places the composite city within the Yellow-Clay Altar and follows He Zu's plan to transport it to Pangu's old land, where the immense stone axe of the progenitor stands as a silent guardian. There, he witnesses a strange sight: cultivators and even Ancestral Gods making pilgrimages to the Pangu shrine, stepping one by one in reverence. This reinforces the mythic scale of the Nine Provinces' history. Deciding to seize the chance before leaving the Celestial Realm, Xiao Chen heads to the Sun Star, where a rumored 'heaven-defying treasure' is said to appear. He arrives to find a gathering of elite cultivators, including the mysterious Stone Corpse and the Lotus King—two supreme beings whose mere presence signals that the treasure is of world-shaking importance. The chapter ends with the treasure's location still uncertain, but the involvement of such powers raises the stakes enormously.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Sun Star in the Celestial Realm is not a ball of deadly fire but a vibrant world of its own, inhabited by Phoenix clans and spiritual flora. This reflects the World of Immortality's cosmology where celestial bodies are layered realms. Pangu's stone axe, left in his old land, symbolizes the primordial origin of the Nine Provinces' cultivation path. The 'final step' mentioned by He Zu is the ultimate breakthrough a Stone Man can attempt—a transformation that even with perfect preparation carries only a one-in-ten chance of success. The Stone Corpse and Lotus King are figures of dread: the former is a corpse that has cultivated to an unfathomable level, and the latter is a legendary 'giant' of the Celestial Realm, demonstrating that even the 'dead' or 'dormant' can wield terrifying influence.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's search for the treasure is a gamble. The presence of the Stone Corpse and Lotus King suggests they have their own designs—are they guardians, rivals, or something else? The Phoenix clan, as neutral ground, may become a key player if the conflict escalates. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen navigates this gathering of powers, and whether the treasure will prove worth the attention it has already drawn. The chapter also hints at the wider chessboard: the Three Supreme Ancestral Gods are occupied, but their return could spell doom. Every move Xiao Chen makes now counts double.
