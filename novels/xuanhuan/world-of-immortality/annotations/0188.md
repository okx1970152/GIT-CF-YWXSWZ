## Chapter Overview
The beast-fighting tournament reaches its climax as legendary sacred beasts clash in the arena. The appearance of the Five-Colored Divine Light—a technique from the ancient myth of the Peacock King in Fengshen Yanyi—shocks both young and old spectators. The Golden Lion King, with its three terrifying eyes (Destruction and Reincarnation), faces off against the Peacock King, while the White Tiger and the golden ape Gold engage in brutal combat. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen watches anxiously as the Little Defiant Dragon enters a life-or-death battle against the Crimson Blood Dragon King, and Keke’s restless energy hints at an impending disruption.

## Key Plot Points
The Golden Lion King unleashes its Destruction Eye, a blood-red beam, only to be neutralized by the Peacock King’s Five-Colored Divine Light, which sweeps away the attack and even momentarily engulfs the Lion King. The Lion King then opens its second eye, the Reincarnation Eye, releasing a dark light that threatens to send its opponent to annihilation. On the ground, the White Tiger’s divine black characters absorb energy attacks and open spatial tears, making it nearly invulnerable, but Gold counters by splitting into nine solid bodies with the legendary Nine Lives Divine Art, overwhelming the Tiger with coordinated strikes. In the sky, the Little Defiant Dragon and the Crimson Blood Dragon King engage in pure martial combat, using dragon flying techniques and ancient warfare, exchanging bloody wounds as they ascend to the heavens. Xiao Chen restrains a frantic Keke who wishes to join the fray, but Keke suddenly vanishes using a mysterious teleportation ability Xiao Chen has never seen before, raising alarms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Five-Colored Divine Light originates from the classic Chinese novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods), where the Peacock King Kong Xuan uses it to brush away any magical artifact or being, rendering him nearly invincible. Its appearance here links the story to that mythic tradition. The three eyes of the Golden Lion King—Destruction and Reincarnation—reference common xianxia motifs where divine beasts possess innate supernatural abilities tied to cosmic forces. The Nine Lives Divine Art of the golden ape Gold is a rare cultivation technique that allows the practitioner to manifest multiple solid bodies, each a true life. The beast tournament itself reflects a recurring theme in xianxia: using life-and-death combat to force sacred beasts to evolve and unlock their potential.

## Reading Guide
Keke’s sudden teleportation is a shocking development; its true capabilities remain unknown, and its impulsive nature may lead to chaos in the arena. The Peacock King’s lineage suggests a deeper connection to ancient mythological figures, which could have far-reaching implications. Xiao Chen’s decision to let the Little Defiant Dragon fight is a gamble—this battle may either elevate the Dragon King or lead to its death. With Keke gone and the dragon kings at risk, the next chapters promise intense action. Keep an eye on how the Five-Colored Divine Light interacts with the Lion King’s third eye, and watch for Xiao Chen’s own potential breakthrough as he faces external pressures.
