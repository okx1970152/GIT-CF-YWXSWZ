## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts focus from the cosmic battles of the Celestial Realm back to the mortal world, where the long-hidden secrets of the Nine Provinces begin to surface. Two parallel excavations—one in Yanjing and one in Shandong—unleash phenomena that defy mortal understanding. In Yanjing, a blood-red beam of light erupts from underground ruins, accompanied by the shocking discovery of a nine-colored sacred tree. In Shandong, an oil well drilling operation is disrupted when a strange being emerges from two and a half kilometers beneath the earth, claiming to be an ancestor. The chapter masterfully contrasts the mundane concerns of modern civilization—oil prices, government agencies, and media stunts—with the awe-inspiring and terrifying return of powers that have slept for ages. It serves as a reminder that the mortal world is not as removed from the cultivation world as it seems, and that the ancient past is waking.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens in Yanjing at a lavish private club, where a middle-aged man confirms the presence of life fluctuations deep underground. Moments later, a massive blood-red beam, hundreds of meters long, erupts from the excavation site, instantly vaporizing several workers. The site manager, Wu, witnesses his team’s panicked reports and decides to withdraw and notify the authorities, but a young assistant secretly contacts a foreign organization to sell the information. Below, the team detects a bundle of rotting flesh among countless bones, and then discovers a stunning treasure: a small tree about a foot tall, radiating nine-colored light, with nine leaves of different hues. This tree is unmistakably the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree that once belonged to Keke, now grown to a higher stage. The scene then shifts to a small city in Shandong, where an oil well suffers a mysterious accident: the drill bit shatters, and fragments are retrieved with long strands of hair tangled in them. A supervisor, Zhang, angrily dismisses the story until the wellhead itself emits rising hair and a disheveled head. A young man in ancient ragged clothing emerges, moving as if floating, and demonstrates terrifying power by effortlessly crushing the drill bits and bending steel pipes. He claims to have been sleeping at a depth of 2,500 meters and awoken by the drilling. His calm, ancient demeanor and physical invulnerability leave the workers in awe and terror, with several fainting. The chapter ends as the being identifies himself as an ancestor, implying a direct lineage to humanity’s forgotten heritage.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nine-Colored Sacred Tree is a direct continuation of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree that was central to Keke’s identity and power. In earlier arcs, the tree had only seven leaves, each corresponding to a color and a stage of Keke’s growth. The appearance of two additional leaves (nine total) indicates that the tree has undergone further evolution, likely linked to Keke’s own breakthrough to the Half-Ancestor realm or higher. The tree’s ability to repel all objects with nine-colored divine light marks it as an artifact of supreme rank. The sleeping cultivator represents a recurring trope in xianxia: ancient powerhouses who seal themselves in deep earth or other dimensions to avoid the erosion of time or to await a critical moment. His claim to be an “ancestor” is not mere posturing; in the World of Immortality’s cosmology, the Nine Provinces have produced countless peerless figures who have outlived ordinary history. His emergence in the mortal world signals that the boundary between the mortal and immortal realms is thinning, and the great war is reaching even the most remote corners.

## Reading Guide

This chapter lays the groundwork for a major resurgence of ancient powers within the mortal realm. Readers should pay close attention to the fate of the Nine-Colored Sacred Tree, as it will likely reconnect with Keke or Xiao Chen in future chapters. The sleeping ancestor’s identity remains mysterious, but his casual display of strength and his familiarity with the modern world’s technology suggest he is not a simple savage. The mention of foreign organizations conspiring to seize the treasure hints that international cultivation factions are also stirring. The story is building toward a convergence of multiple plotlines: the war in the Celestial Realm, the awakening of Keke’s clan artifacts, and now the reawakening of dormant powers within the Nine Provinces’ own soil. Xiao Chen’s eventual return to the mortal world will likely intersect with these events, creating a collision of civilizations and eras. For now, the mortal world sleeps unaware, but the tremors are growing.
