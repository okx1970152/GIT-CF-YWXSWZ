## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, the protagonists venture deeper into the Death World, arriving at a mysterious heavenly curtain that veils the legendary Forbidden Domain of Eternal Light. Three powerful factions converge: Xiao Chen and his allies, the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou with nine Half-Ancestors, and a Death World Ancestral Lord commanding eight King-level fire seed beings. The Martial Imprint confronts Li Huiyou, but the situation escalates when news of a prior arrival sends all parties rushing toward the Eternal Light. This chapter sets the stage for an unprecedented clash between the living, the undead, and the otherworldly, raising the stakes for the fate of the Nine Provinces and beyond.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen, Wu Ming, Jin Sanyi, and Keke traversing a dark passage between two massive green stones, emerging into a starlit void where a Starry Door formed from countless light points appears. This door, reinforced with Eternal Light material, creates an indestructible temporal passage linking worlds. Meanwhile, at an ancient demonic city, the Martial Imprint battles a beam of pure Ancestral God killing intent that shoots from the sky. The Martial Imprint is pierced but survives. The group then discovers a dead city on the horizon and realizes they are deep in the Death World. The Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou, in his first incarnation, appears with nine Half-Ancestors, having sought the Eternal Light for eons. He reveals that four Otherworld Ancestral Gods originally attempted to reach this domain; two perished in chaotic time streams. A second faction appears: a Death World Ancestral Lord with eight King-level fire seed beings, exuding supreme power. The Ancestral Lord reveals that the area before them is merely a special domain, not the true depths of the Death Continent. Tension builds as all three sides eye the Eternal Light, but Li Huiyou suddenly departs upon learning someone has already entered the Forbidden Domain, forcing the others to give chase. The chapter ends with the group arriving at a vast, hazy sky curtain—the true barrier to the Forbidden Domain of Eternal Light.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of "Eternal Light" (永恒之光), a legendary substance believed to strengthen artifacts and create indestructible spatial passages. It is highly coveted by Ancestral Gods and Half-Ancestors as it may be key to ultimate transcendence. The "Death World Ancestral Lord" (祖君) represents the pinnacle of fire seed beings—similar in power to an Ancestral God but originating from the undead realm. Fire seed beings can achieve flesh-and-blood rebirth at higher cultivation levels, yet some, like the Ancestral Lord, retain their skeletal form out of choice or habit. The term "Half-Ancestor" (半祖) is used for cultivators on the verge of Ancestral Godhood, while "King-level" (君王) refers to powerful fire seed beings below Ancestral Lord. The chapter also showcases the "Martial Imprint" (武之印记), a sentient martial will that embodies the pure martial path. Its battle against the Ancestral God killing intent demonstrates the clash between martial fundamentals and divine law.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the dynamics between the three factions: the protagonists' group (Xiao Chen, Martial Imprint), the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou, and the Death World Ancestral Lord. The sudden departure of Li Huiyou hints at a hidden player who has already breached the Forbidden Domain, possibly a fourth party with unknown motives. The Eternal Light's true nature and its connection to the larger conflict between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld remain to be explored. The Martial Imprint's confrontation with the Ancestral God killing intent suggests that he may be able to match Ancestral God-level power, though at great cost. The chapter also raises questions about the geography of the Death World—this region is still an outer boundary, with even deeper and more dangerous realms ahead. Future chapters will likely reveal the contents of the Forbidden Domain and the identity of the early intruder.
