## Chapter Overview

In this tension-filled chapter, Xiao Chen's declaration of a "Kill Yaksha Edict" sends shockwaves through the assembled racial kings at Smoke and Rain Tower. The War Clan king Jing Pu seizes the opportunity to paint Xiao Chen as a threat to all non-human races, attempting to unite them against the human cultivator. However, Xiao Chen's measured response and his lethal demonstration against a Sacred Dragon remind all present that he is not to be trifled with. The chapter masterfully weaves political intrigue with raw displays of power, setting the stage for the looming confrontation with the Yaksha king himself.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with War Clan king Jing Pu's provocative accusation that Xiao Chen's "Kill Yaksha Edict" is merely a precursor to a broader campaign against all races. Xiao Chen calmly refutes this, pointing out the Yaksha's brutal massacre of human cultivators and subtly reminding each race of their own ancient enemies—the War Clan's feud with the Battle God Clan, the Fallen Angels' conflicts with the ancient Heaven, and the Ant Clan's own hidden threats. This strategic counterattack destabilizes the fragile unity Jing Pu sought to build. As the standoff intensifies, the Three-Eyed Golden Lion King unexpectedly sides with Xiao Chen, blocking Jing Pu's advance. Outside, the Inverse Dragon King and Black Dragon King intercept the Ant King and Fallen Angel princess respectively, preventing them from aiding their allies. Xiao Chen then takes to the sky and confronts a passing Sacred Dragon, slaughtering it with brutal efficiency—using the Origin Sound, the Hand of the Supreme Being, and raw martial power. This display of overwhelming force silences many critics and reinforces the credibility of his kill order. The chapter ends with the racial kings reassessing Xiao Chen's strength and the mysterious final room remaining closed, hinting at deeper plots.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Hundred Races era is a central concept in this arc: after the collapse of the illusory world, many ancient races have re-emerged in the Nine Provinces, each with their own territories, histories, and grudges. The War Clan, Fallen Angels, Ant Clan, and Sacred Dragons are among the most prominent. Xiao Chen's strategy of exploiting historical enmities—such as the War Clan's rivalry with the Battle God Clan—shows his political acumen. The "Kill Edict" is a declaration of war against a specific race, which carries immense weight in a world where reputation and power determine survival. The Sacred Dragon's death is significant because dragons are considered nearly invincible; Xiao Chen's victory reinforces his status as a top-tier combatant. The Dragon Clan's Sacred Sword wielded by the Black Dragon King is a legendary artifact, and the Inverse Dragon King's self-sealing cultivation method is a unique path to power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up several threads for the near future. First, the Yaksha king is expected to arrive in Chang'an soon, and the alliances formed here will likely determine the outcome of that confrontation. The mysterious figure in the last room who never emerges may be a powerful player watching events unfold. Xiao Chen's political maneuvering has bought him some breathing room, but Jing Pu's hostility remains a clear threat. The involvement of the Inverse and Black Dragon Kings indicates that the old Dragon Island allies are still loyal and active. Readers should watch for how the various racial kings react to Xiao Chen's display of power—some may be swayed, while others will become more determined to oppose him. Also, the Sacred Dragon's death may provoke the Sacred Dragon Island's wrath, adding another layer of conflict.
