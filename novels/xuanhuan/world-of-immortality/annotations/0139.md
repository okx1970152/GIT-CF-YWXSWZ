## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, the narrative shifts from the primal wilderness of Dragon Island to the bustling urban expanse of Heavenly Emperor City. Xiao Chen, accompanied by Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon, enters this grand metropolis and becomes immersed in its vibrant and dangerous currents. A fleeting glimpse of Yan Qingcheng reminds us of lingering enmities and Undying Sect networks. Overheard rumors in a busy tavern reveal that dragon kings from Dragon Island have arrived on the mainland, and a wave of powerful beast fighters — including potential saint beasts — is gathering for high-stakes arena battles. The chapter closes with a reunion with the merchant Zhuge Liang, who steers Xiao Chen toward opportunities and likely trouble.

## Key Plot Points

The arrival at Heavenly Emperor City marks a significant transition from survival to urban intrigue. Xiao Chen immediately demonstrates his situational awareness by spotting Yan Qingcheng near an Undying Sect contact point, indicating his past conflicts still shadow him. The city's vibrant beast fight culture is explored through overheard conversations: rumors circulate that several young dragon kings have been brought to the continent, and owners of rare beasts are converging to challenge them. This establishes the arena as a major stage for future power struggles. The narrative worldbuilds through natural dialogue, revealing how powerful beasts are traded, trained, and fought. Zhuge Liang's reappearance introduces a resourceful but unscrupulous ally who views the beast trade as a goldmine. He promises to introduce Xiao Chen to the high-stakes world of beast betting and palace pleasures. The chapter ends with a visit to the 'Closed Moon Shames Flower Hall,' a luxury venue that doubles as a hub for information and danger, setting the stage for Xiao Chen's next moves in this urban landscape.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Heavenly Emperor City embodies the grandeur of the World of Immortality, with its towering walls, moat filled with crocodiles, and diverse population of myriad races. The beast fighting arenas (斗兽场) are more than entertainment; they are institutions where spiritual beasts prove their worth and owners gain or lose fortunes. The appearance of dragon kings and saint beasts in the city signals a major event, likely drawing competitors from across the continent. The Undying Sect's secret contact points demonstrate how major sects operate shadow networks in cities. The antiques street and luxury palaces reflect the city's economic sophistication. Zhuge Liang's character embodies the merchant class that thrives on such commerce, and his schemes represent a new kind of challenge for Xiao Chen.

## Reading Guide

This chapter lays crucial groundwork for the coming urban arc. Readers should note the presence of Yan Qingcheng, which suggests the Undying Sect's ongoing interest in Xiao Chen. The rumors about dragon kings and beast fights hint at an upcoming competition that could serve as a major plot driver. Zhuge Liang's conniving nature promises both opportunities and betrayals. The lush palace setting may offer information or entangle Xiao Chen in new schemes. Pay attention to how Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon react to the city; their unique natures might attract unwanted attention. The three skeletons' absence from the main scenes suggests they may play a distinct role later. Overall, the tone shifts from survival to social maneuvering, and readers can expect intrigue, beast battles, and political games ahead.
