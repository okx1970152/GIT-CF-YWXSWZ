## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a poignant turning point in the narrative, blending profound grief with the spark of hope. For over a year, Keke has mourned atop the stone statue that imprisons Xiao Chen, her cries carried by the desolate winds of the Ancient God Desert. The half-ancestors—Zhuangzi, Sun Wu, the Buddha, Laozi, and others—visit in turn, each leaving a blessing of light upon the statue. Xiao Chen finally emerges, his body forged anew and his consciousness restored, only to find the world changed: the desert is being severed from the Eternal Continent, and a space vortex hurls him into the unknown. The chapter closes on a breathtaking revelation—he stands before the Yellow River, back in the Mortal World.

## Key Plot Points
Keke's vigil over the stone statue, which is pierced by eight War Swords, lasts more than a year. The Devil (Chiyou) urges her to abandon hope, but Keke refuses, begging him to save Xiao Chen. Half-ancestors arrive one by one: the Buddha, Sun Wu, Zhuangzi, Laozi, and the Yellow Emperor, each projecting a light into the statue. Xiao Chen awakens inside the stone, emerging from a shattered hole in the forehead. His two sacred instruments—the Golden Divine Halberd and the Dark-Iron Seal—are badly damaged and rage against the half-ancestors who sealed him. Xiao Chen flies across the Ancient God Desert using the Eight Aspect Worlds, only to discover that a great space rift is cutting the desert off from the mainland. Qingqing appears on the far side, her face pale with injury from trying to cross the barrier. As she reaches out to him, the space vortex erupts, and Xiao Chen is pulled into a swirling abyss. He awakens to the roar of the Yellow River—he has returned to the Mortal World.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ancient God Desert (古神荒漠) is one of the Four Extremity Forbidden Zones of the Eternal Continent, a land so hostile that even demigods perish without protection. The half-ancestors who visit Xiao Chen—the Buddha, Laozi, Zhuangzi, Sun Wu (Martial Saint), and the Yellow Emperor—are legendary figures from Chinese mythology and xianxia lore. Their act of leaving light imprints on the statue suggests a form of blessing or seal that aids Xiao Chen's rebirth. The 'Martial Imprint' (武之印记) from the Heavenly Stele is the supreme martial cultivation legacy Xiao Chen carries, which now conflicts with his newly awakened Spatial Divine Law. The space vortex that transports him to the Mortal World hints at the fragile boundaries between realms, a recurring theme in the novel.

## Reading Guide
The chapter ends with Xiao Chen standing before the Yellow River, signaling a dramatic shift in setting. The Mortal World arc is about to unfold, and readers should watch for how Xiao Chen adapts to a realm where his power is vastly greater than those he left behind. Keke's fate hangs in the balance—she has been taken back to Dragon Island by the Devil. The half-ancestors' mysterious blessings and the damaged state of the Golden Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal suggest that Xiao Chen's cultivation path will undergo further evolution. The space rift and the prophetic voice from earlier chapters also remain unresolved, hinting at larger cosmic forces at play. This chapter is a bridge between the epic battles of the Eternal Continent and the personal journey of rediscovery that awaits Xiao Chen.
