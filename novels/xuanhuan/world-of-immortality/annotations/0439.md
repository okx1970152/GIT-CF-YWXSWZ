## Chapter Overview
After decades of imprisonment in the grey void of the Death World, Xiao Chen and his companions—the Bear, Tianwaitian, and Renwairen—discover a drifting palace complex emerging from the fog. This eerie structure, built from red-brown stone, is lined with massive pillars that bear carvings of demons, spirits, and skeletons. The group soon realizes these are not mere carvings but actual seals, imprisoning powerful beings at the Earth-Penetrating level and even King-level existences. The chapter establishes a deeply unsettling atmosphere as the protagonists grapple with the implications of a power that could seal such formidable entities. Their cautious exploration leads to a horrifying discovery: one of the sealed corpses has vanished, and a shadowy presence now stalks them. The chapter builds dread through slow, deliberate pacing and a sense of inescapable mystery.

## Key Plot Points
The floating palace drifts silently through the grey mist, appearing ancient and desolate. The Bear, impatient after ten thousand years of captivity, eagerly investigates the stone pillars. Using borrowed power from the stone statue, he cracks open a carving, revealing a human-shaped depression—proof that a living being was once sealed within. When he opens a second pillar portraying a demon, a shock of grey hair appears, and the demon corpse briefly moves, startling the group. This event confirms that the pillars contain the remains of sealed beings, many of whom were Earth-Penetrating or King-level experts. The discovery raises a chilling question: who possessed the power to trap so many supreme beings in stone? 

The group hears a vague legend of a past overlord who unified the Death World and suppressed all dissenters—perhaps the same entity responsible for these seals. The Bear claims to have seen the corpse of his "kinsman" stand up and laugh, but the body later lies still on the steps, suggesting an intelligent, malevolent presence is at work. Over three months, they sense a cold, observing aura but cannot locate its source. Finally, they decide to open the great hall's doors. Inside, the hall is pitch black, with a decaying corpse emitting a foul stench. The Bear again claims a shadow is following them, heightening the terror. The chapter ends with them pressing deeper into the palace, uncertain of what awaits.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter delves into the hierarchy and history of the Death World's cultivation system. The "Earth-Penetrating" realm (Che Di) is a level above the Perfected Man realm, and beings at this level are styled "Emperor" (皇). Kings (君王) are even higher. The concept of sealing powerful enemies in stone pillars is a classic xianxia trope, often used to show the overwhelming might of an ancient overlord. The mention of a "Primeval Sovereign" (上祖君) who once unified the Death World echoes the novel's broader theme of illusory versus real power, as many legendary figures in this world were born from mortal faith. 

The Bear's ten-thousand-year imprisonment is a nod to the extreme timescales common in xianxia, where characters can endure near-eternity through tenacious will. The grey mist and oppressive atmosphere mirror the desolate, tragic tone of the Death World, a place where even half-ancestors fear to tread. The idea that the floating palace moves of its own accord, as if it were a ship of the dead, reinforces the sense of a world where even architecture is animated by ancient, malevolent forces.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major new arc in the Death World, introducing a mysterious ancient power that sealed countless kings. Readers should pay close attention to the legend of the overlord who tamed the Death World—this figure may be connected to the "female sovereign" mentioned earlier or to the three skeleton kings who have now evolved into War Emperors. The shadowy presence stalking the group is clearly a threat, and its identity remains unknown. The decaying corpse in the main hall suggests that something in this palace is still alive, or at least active. 

The next chapters will likely explore the interior of the floating palace and reveal its secrets. Xiao Chen's goal is to escape the grey prison, but this palace may hold the key—or a new, deadlier trap. The Bear's claims of seeing a moving corpse and shadow may be more than paranoia; the Death World has rules that defy logic. Also, the "Primeval Overlord" may be a half-ancestor or even an Ancestral God, setting up a confrontation that challenges Xiao Chen's growing power.
