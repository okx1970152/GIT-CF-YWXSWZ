## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal inversion of power as Xiao Chen, now operating at a level far beyond normal half-ancestors, single-handedly annihilates a two-heaven half-ancestor and his entire retinue with contemptuous ease. The rescue of Wu Ming and other familiar faces from the Curse World serves as a stark reminder that the Otherworld's occupation is both brutal and porous. Yet the true weight of the chapter falls on the terrifying escalation that follows: the Curse World itself is sealed by Supreme Ancestral Gods, three of their ultimate weapons appear in the sky, and nine otherworld ancestral gods manifest simultaneously. The desperate retreat and the sudden rise of the Heavenly Stele from the abyss—ancient, immense, and seemingly sentient—shift the battlefield from a rescue mission to a cosmic confrontation that could decide the fate of entire worlds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Keke moving through the Curse World to gather fleeing survivors. He stumbles upon a battle: Wu Ming, Bo Shi, Miaochi, Totti, and others are surrounded by otherworld cultivators led by a two-heaven half-ancestor who boasts of avenging a fallen ancestral god. Xiao Chen intervenes with devastating efficiency—a single palm strike obliterates the half-ancestor and his forces, showcasing his immense growth. Keke promptly opens Paradise Lost to shelter the rescued cultivators. They then encounter Zhang Sanfeng's Taiji Diagram sweeping through the sky, killing otherworld cultivators before vanishing, a sign that the legendary martial sage is also active in the war. However, the moment of hope is short-lived. Yan Huang, the Old Ancestral Dragon, the Old Farmer, and the Martial Imprint emerge from the abyss with grave faces: the entire Curse World has been sealed by Supreme Ancestral Gods, making escape to the Death World nearly impossible. Almost immediately, three supreme weapons—the Immortal Shield, the Demon Blade, and the Thirty-Three Story Pagoda—appear overhead, and nine ancestral god silhouettes manifest. The situation is dire. But then, from the abyss, a Heavenly Stele expands violently, pushing back the nine figures and threatening the weapons. The chapter ends with this titanic stalemate, leaving the outcome uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter reinforces several key cultivation concepts. The term 'two-heaven half-ancestor' refers to the second sub-realm within the Half-Ancestor tier, indicating that Xiao Chen's combat power now far exceeds even established half-ancestors. The 'Taiji Diagram' is a classic Daoist artifact associated with Laozi and supreme yin-yang mastery; Zhang Sanfeng, a historical figure deified in xianxia, wields it as a weapon of mass purification. The three supreme weapons—Immortal Shield, Demon Blade, and Thirty-Three Story Pagoda—are each legendary artifacts from previous civilization eras, now in the hands of the Otherworld's Supreme Ancestral Gods, the highest tier below the Ancestral God level. The 'Heavenly Stele' (天碑) is a recurring motif connected to the ancient stele that sealed Dragon Island and the Yellow River stele; its sudden emergence suggests it is a sentient or semi-sentient artifact responding to the crisis, possibly linked to the will of the Nine Provinces' ancestral gods. The chapter also hints at the Old Farmer's mysterious past as a survivor from a previous civilization.

## Reading Guide

The chapter delivers a classic rhythm: swift rescue, then overwhelming escalation. Readers should note that Xiao Chen's power demonstration is deliberately kept brief—the narrative focus quickly shifts to the larger, more terrifying power play of the Supreme Ancestral Gods. Pay attention to the Old Farmer's cryptic words about 'not enough time' and the fact that the Heavenly Stele's rise may be a temporary measure. The nine ancestral gods are not fully revealed, setting them up as a future threat. Zhang Sanfeng's fleeting appearance suggests he will be a key ally in the coming battles. The next chapter will likely explore the confrontation with the supreme weapons and the nine silhouettes, and may reveal more about the Heavenly Stele's role in the war. Xiao Chen's relationship with the half stone skull and his readiness to use it against supreme ancestral gods will be critical.
