## Chapter Overview
This chapter bridges two major arcs: Xiao Chen's perilous journey through chaotic space-time toward the legendary "Most Evil Place," and Keke's long-awaited awakening as a Half-Ancestor on Dragon Island. The dual narrative highlights the vast scale of the World of Immortality—while Xiao Chen confronts the remnants of lost civilizations and a skeletal Ancestral Dragon, Keke emerges from a century-long slumber with a comically overpowered new technique. The chapter sets up their eventual reunion and a major confrontation at the heart of the unknown.

## Key Plot Points
In the chaotic space-time, the Martial Imprint and Xiao Chen encounter a cool little white beast that writes crooked characters and taunts the Martial Imprint. Following its path, they spend months breaking through a spatial barrier and emerge into a barren wasteland. They discover ruins of a prehistoric civilization with anti-matter weapons; a metal man and a broken warship attack but are destroyed. Crossing into a lush paradise, a titanic white-bone Ancestral Dragon rises from a shattered mountain. The Martial Imprint fights it while Xiao Chen retreats. Afterward, they approach a colossal city resembling the Dead City, which the Martial Imprint identifies as the "most evil place". Meanwhile, on Dragon Island, Keke sleeps through 150 years and awakens as a Half-Ancestor, complaining of hunger and immediately using its "Eat Everything" technique to gather all spiritual herbs in the region. It then sets off for the Soul World to save Xiao Chen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of the "most evil place" (最邪之地) draws on xianxia tropes of forbidden domains that contain the greatest dangers and secrets. The chaotic space-time is a common element in Daoist cosmology, representing the primordial chaos from which order emerges. The white-bone Ancestral Dragon ties into the novel's dragon mythology; even as a skeleton, it retains terrifying power and a fire seed, reflecting the death-world cultivation path. Keke's breakthrough to Half-Ancestor during sleep is a classic xianxia trope of effortless genius, but the author subverts it with humor—Keke's "ultimate technique" is literally eating all nearby treasures, blending the sublime and the absurd. The juxtaposition of epic danger and comedic relief is a signature of Chen Dong's style.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up the final approach to the "most evil place", which likely contains major revelations about the Twenty-Four War Swords and the Ancestral Gods. Readers should pay attention to the white-bone Ancestral Dragon—its existence hints at the true scale of dragon history. Keke's awakening and decision to head to the Soul World suggests a convergence of storylines; Xiao Chen and Keke may reunite in the depths of the death world. The cool little beast in the chaotic space-time remains mysterious—its resemblance to Keke raises questions about Keke's origins and the true nature of the divine beasts. The chapter also emphasizes the Martial Imprint's exhaustion, indicating that the trials ahead will push even him to his limits.
