## Chapter Overview

The long-awaited reunion between Xiao Chen and Keke brings both joy and sorrow—Keke's childlike demeanor masks over one hundred thousand years of suffering, sealed in the Seven Demon Cities. But this is no time for sentiment. The Otherworld faction, led by the Undefeated King Meng Degang and the True Wood Saintess, has laid an elaborate trap to refine the seven cities and all within. Xiao Chen, however, turns the tables. He begins a Holy Sacrifice of a captured Upper Ancestral God to heal Keke's grievous wounds and push her further on her path. Meanwhile, the Seven Demon Cities reveal their true nature: they are arranged in a Big Dipper formation, and together they erupt with world-shaking power, shattering the sealing array. Yet the greatest shock comes as a mysterious Emperor City is sensed beyond the seven, suggesting a deeper layer of the ancient scheme.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen wastes no time. Directly before the gathered enemies, he begins a Holy Sacrifice (圣祭) of the Upper Ancestral God that Keke had sealed, channeling the victim's life essence into Keke. The process reveals the depth of the wounds Keke had suffered—a dark deathly energy that had persisted even after a hundred millennia, slowly eroding her vitality. With the sacrifice complete, Keke's injuries are fully healed, and she takes another solid step on the Upper Ancestral God path, her body now as tough as a dedicated physical cultivator's. Outside, the Undefeated King Meng Degang and the True Wood Saintess continue to press the refining array with three shattered worlds at Meng Degang's command. But the Seven Demon Cities activate a hidden bond; they align in a Big Dipper (Beidou) pattern, connecting through a black river of divine power. Seven surging plumes of dark fire lift the sealing canopy. The formation's true power is revealed—the cities break free, smashing the three worlds and threatening to tear apart the grand array. Meng Degang and the True Wood Saintess are forced to redouble their efforts. Meanwhile, Sha Polang's relentless mockery and Xiao Chen's cold calculation maintain the pressure. At the chapter's end, a new presence is felt: an Emperor City, the center of the Big Dipper pattern, hints at an even greater power behind the seven cities.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of "Holy Sacrifice" (圣祭) here originates from the Martial Ancestor's heretical chapter—a forbidden technique that extracts the essence of a living being to empower another. The target must be a powerful cultivator; here an Upper Ancestral God is refined. The "Upper Ancestral God" path is an alternative to the Stone Man path, focusing on mastery of myriad laws rather than physical petrification. The Seven Demon Cities forming a Big Dipper (Beidou) pattern is rich in Chinese cosmology. Beidou governs death and fate in Taoist lore, so this formation connotes inescapable destiny. The Emperor City (帝城) at its head represents the supreme authority—the seat of the "Heavenly Emperor," a figure hinted at in earlier chapters. Meng Degang's ability to wield three shattered Otherworlds as weapons demonstrates the terrifying power of a Stone Man-level being who has not yet taken the final step.

## Reading Guide

The revelation of an Emperor City beyond the seven demon cities changes the stakes entirely. What is this city, and who or what sleeps within it? Is it the source of the sealing power, or a prison for something even greater? Xiao Chen and his allies have won a reprieve but not the war—the Undefeated King and True Wood Saintess remain, and now an even deeper mystery surfaces. Readers should watch for whether the Emperor City will be antagonistic or serve as a hidden key. Additionally, Keke's recovery and improved power suggest she will play a central role in the battles to come. The alliance's taunting—especially Sha Polang's—may provoke enemies into rash action, a tactic that could backfire. Expect escalating confrontation and ancient secrets unraveling in the next chapters.
