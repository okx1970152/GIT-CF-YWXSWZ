## Chapter Overview

Chapter 549 delivers one of the most harrowing confrontations in the arc, as Xiao Chen returns to the Nine Provinces only to be intercepted by Ge Qian – an Upper Ancestral God who has survived three civilization cycles and bears a terrifying stone arm. This chapter combines philosophical debate with raw atrocity: Ge Qian argues that mortals are mere ants, and demonstrates his point by slaughtering millions with a single gesture. The emotional stakes are immense as Xiao Chen is forced into a cruel bargain to save the remaining lives of his homeland. The tone is desolate, oppressive, and filled with the weight of impossible choices.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng approach the Nine Provinces but sense an invisible threat. The source is Ge Qian, an Upper Ancestral God accompanied by four Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Ge Qian reveals his disturbing origin – he killed his own brothers to rise to power. He offers a deal: repair his broken Primordial Demon City, and he will spare the Nine Provinces. When Xiao Chen hesitates, Ge Qian orders the massacre of millions to demonstrate his power. Four Ancestral Gods descend upon the Nine Provinces, destroying entire regions and soaking the land in blood. Xiao Chen, unable to stop them, agrees to take the ruined city. Upon returning to the Death World, he examines it and ultimately destroys it. From its remains, three War Swords emerge, hinting at deeper secrets.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of an Upper Ancestral God (上祖神) – a being of supreme power who has transcended ordinary Ancestral Godhood. Ge Qian's stone arm represents a unique fusion of the Stone-Man path and conventional cultivation, suggesting a hybrid approach to reaching the highest realm. The Primordial Demon City (太古魔城) is a key artifact type in this world: ancient, sentient cities that can suppress and even kill Ancestral Gods. The War Swords within such a city are legendary weapons tied to the Heavenly Steles. The philosophical debate about the value of mortal life echoes the law-of-the-jungle ethics often seen in the Otherworld.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major new antagonist: Ge Qian, whose power and ruthlessness dwarf previous enemies. His stone arm and his desire to restore his demon city suggest he may become a recurring threat. Xiao Chen's decision to destroy the city rather than repair it shows his defiance, but it also means Ge Qian's promise to spare the Nine Provinces may now be void. The three War Swords he discovers are a critical power-up, but their true potential remains unclear. Watch for how Xiao Chen will integrate them and how Ge Qian will retaliate. The tension between the Otherworld gods and the remnants of the Nine Provinces is now at a breaking point.
