## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers one of the most awe-inspiring spectacles in the novel's cosmic history: the Martial Ancestor, restored to his prime at the Ninth Heaven of Half-Ancestor, engages three Supreme Ancestral Gods in a battle that shatters the very fabric of space-time. The engagement unfolds across the legendary Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, a trial that grinds even the strongest bodies to dust. Meanwhile, in the Otherworld, the stone man's rampage reaches a cataclysmic climax, Xiao Chen's Dead City is obliterated, and the Yellow Clay Altar—acting through a stone skull—unleashes the Eight Origin Sounds to annihilate three Otherworld Ancestral Gods. The chapter balances sky-rending martial combat with desperate sacrifice, presenting a tragic, mythic narrative of civilizations on the edge of annihilation.

## Key Plot Points

The Martial Ancestor, now radiating the vitality of a young god, directly confronts three Supreme Ancestral Gods in the void beyond worlds. His opening palm strike collapses six black holes; a second exchange turns the eternal unknown into primordial chaos. The Supreme Ancestral Gods attempt to trap him within a sealed universe, but the Martial Ancestor shatters it with a single gesture. The battle moves to the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, where each step is like a mountain. The Martial Ancestor climbs to the forty-ninth step, his body repeatedly splitting under the pressure, forced to use an Ancestral God soul as a substitute to survive one destruction. An ancient Celestial race elder sacrifices himself by traveling back through time to attack the past selves of the Supreme Ancestral Gods, causing their present bodies to crack and bleed. One Supreme Ancestral God sends his consciousness back to counter, leaving the elder severely wounded. The Martial Ancestor retreats after recognizing he cannot advance further. Parallel to this, the Otherworld stone man wreaks havoc but is nearly annihilated, leaving only his head. Xiao Chen's Dead City is shattered by six Otherworld Ancestral Gods. The Yellow Clay Altar, hidden within a stone skull, swallows Xiao Chen and emits the Eight Origin Sounds, killing three Otherworld Ancestral Gods instantly. The stone man in the divine city below stands on the verge of his final transformation, radiating a glory that surpasses legend.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps are a recurring trial in the novel, symbolizing the ascent toward supreme martial or divine power. Each step exerts a unique pressure that tests the body and soul; even the Martial Ancestor can only reach halfway. The concept of traveling backward in time to attack a foe's past self is a rare and forbidden ability. The Celestial elder's sacrifice demonstrates that such actions can alter the present, but the Supreme Ancestral Gods can counter by projecting their own minds into the past. This reflects the novel's layered view of causality—time is fluid but not easily changed. The Eight Origin Sounds, derived from the Yellow Clay Altar, are among the most devastating techniques in the world, rivaling the Six Paths of Reincarnation. Their appearance here confirms the altar's sentience and its connection to the primordial Ancestral Gods. The stone man's transformation is a critical plot device: a being on the verge of achieving a new stage of existence, one that could tip the balance of the war between worlds.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major pivot in the war. The Martial Ancestor's retreat signals that even the peak of Half-Ancestor power is insufficient against the full might of the Supreme Ancestral Gods and the sealed trials of the stone steps. Readers should watch for the consequences of the Divine City stone man's imminent final step—his awakening could bring either salvation or ruin. The Celestial elder's sacrificial time-attack may have opened a vulnerability that the defenders can exploit later. Xiao Chen, now protected within the stone skull alongside the Yellow Clay Altar, is positioned for a potential rebirth or empowerment. The Eight Origin Sounds' devastating effect on three Ancestral Gods shows that the altar is a weapon of last resort. The chapter closes with a sense of suspended dread: the Otherworld has suffered losses, but the mortal world has lost its greatest bastion, the Dead City. The coming chapters will likely explore the stone man's fate and Xiao Chen's next move.
