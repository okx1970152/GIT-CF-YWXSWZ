## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen storms the imperial palace of the Nine Provinces, cutting down Sun Knights and Tiger Clan scions with brutal efficiency. He confronts Zhao Lin'er, now Empress and Solar Saintess, who offers a trade—the location of his childhood friend Su Ying—in exchange for amnesty. But the information is a trap. Following the lead to Luoyang, Xiao Chen walks into the Nine Heavens Ten Earths God-Slaying Demon-Exterminating Formation, a legendary killing array reinforced by half-ancestor artifacts and the ancient divine power of Luoyang City. The Yellow Clay Altar strains under the assault; Xiao Chen is wounded by the Heaven-Overturning Seal and bombarded by countless enemies. At the brink of defeat, the demonic mark on his forehead—the brand of the Dead City—activates, tearing open a dark passage of unknown origin. This chapter crackles with tension, shifting from imperial politics to a desperate siege that tests Xiao Chen's martial body and the limits of his protection.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's assault on the capital is swift and merciless: he decapitates nine Sun Knights with a single sweep of his halberd, then kills two Tiger Clan experts who dare confront him. He searches their residual consciousness for information but finds it blocked. In the imperial garden, he meets Zhao Lin'er—though the exchange reveals she is merely a projection. She offers Su Ying's location in exchange for ending their enmity, claiming she was pressured by half-ancestor factions. Xiao Chen accepts the bargain but remains skeptical, later massacring dozens more of his enemies within the palace before departing. At Luoyang, he enters the valley where Su Ying is supposedly held, only to trigger the fully manifested Nine Heavens Ten Earths God-Slaying Demon-Exterminating Formation. Multiple half-ancestor artifacts—the Heaven-Overturning Seal, the Soul-Falling Bell, the Sun Holy Mirror, the White Tiger Sacred Patterns, the Three Infant Divine Lamp, and the Seven Colored Murderous Sword—bear down on him simultaneously. Worse, the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge channels the ancient sacred power of Luoyang itself, suppressing the Yellow Clay Altar. Xiao Chen takes a devastating blow from the seal, coughing blood, but as the combined assault intensifies, the demonic pattern on his forehead blazes with black flames, opening a dark tunnel of terrifying power. The chapter ends with the formation still active and Xiao Chen's fate uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nine Heavens Ten Earths God-Slaying Demon-Exterminating Formation is one of the five mightiest killing arrays in the World of Immortality, a spatial trap that manifests countless deadly illusions, from knife mountains to seas of fire. It is here reinforced by actual half-ancestor sacred artifacts—the Heaven-Overturning Seal (Fanyin Seal) of the Chan Sect, the Soul-Falling Bell (Luohun Bell) that attacks the spirit, the Sun Holy Mirror of the Solar Church, and others. Luoyang itself is an ancient divine capital, bearing the imprints of Ancestral Gods like Nuwa, Fuxi, and Shennong; its residual power, when channeled, can rival even the protective Yellow Clay Altar. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge (Tongtian Dead Bridge) is a legendary structure connecting realms, here used to direct Luoyang's divine light into the trap. Xiao Chen's forehead mark is a ‘demonic pattern’ that appeared when the Dead City descended, likely a seal or key tied to the Heavenly Stele. His martial body's ability to survive a blow from the Heaven-Overturning Seal—a divine artifact that has slain gods—underscores his extraordinary resilience at the Nirvana realm.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up one of the most dangerous sieges Xiao Chen has faced, combining half-ancestor artifacts with ancient territorial power. The dark passage opened by his forehead mark is a wild card—will it lead to escape, or something worse? Watch for the fate of Su Ying, whose location remains uncertain despite Zhao Lin'er's claim. The Empress herself is playing a deep game, physically distant but still manipulating events. The half-ancestor disciples (Hu Ben, Ye Xiao, and others) are now united against Xiao Chen, but their alliance depends on the formation holding. With the Yellow Clay Altar under pressure, Xiao Chen may need to rely on the Dead City's power—or the stone man within him—to break free. The chapter also deepens the tension between personal grudges and the larger war against the false gods of the cultivation world.
