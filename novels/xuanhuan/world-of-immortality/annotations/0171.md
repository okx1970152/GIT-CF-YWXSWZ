## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a masterclass in xianxia combat, pitting Xiao Chen against Dugu Jianmo, the heir of the legendary Sword Demon lineage, in a duel that pushes both to their limits. The battle is not merely a clash of techniques but a spiritual contest, with Xiao Chen entering an ethereal state that hints at the threshold of the Spirit-Treasury realm. Yet just as the duel reaches a stalemate, a cowardly ambush shatters the scene, introducing the Bastard Ancient God Spear—a weapon stained with the blood of gods. The emotional tone shifts from the purity of mortal combat to the cold fury of betrayal, raising the stakes for Xiao Chen as he faces a new, hidden enemy.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Dugu Jianmo locked in a fierce exchange, their blade light and sword aura tearing through the night sky. Dugu Jianmo unleashes the legendary 'Heaviest Sword' technique, a slow but unstoppable strike that compresses the weight of a mountain into a single blow. Xiao Chen, in response, enters a profound state of emptiness, moving instinctively and countering with a strike born of pure intuition. Their attacks collide, generating a cataclysmic explosion that levels nearby structures. Both are grievously wounded but refuse to yield. The duel continues for hundreds of rounds, with Xiao Chen discarding his practiced loose techniques for a flowing, instinctive blade style that matches Dugu Jianmo's sword intent. When both lose their weapons after a vicious exchange, Dugu Jianmo declares 'I am my sword' and turns his body into a living blade, while Xiao Chen counters with an ethereal palm strike. The decisive moment arrives when Dugu Jianmo summons countless debris as flying swords, only for Xiao Chen's transcendent calm to shatter each one. Before either can land a finishing blow, a brown-haired youth ambushes Xiao Chen from the darkness, impaling him with the Bastard Ancient God Spear—a weapon said to have slain gods. Dugu Jianmo expresses contempt for the sneak attack, but the youth insists Xiao Chen must die. Xiao Chen, despite being pierced, pulls the spear free and rises, vowing to kill both the youth and his brother.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases two key cultivation concepts: the threshold of the Spirit-Treasury realm and the notion of 'I am my sword'. Xiao Chen's 'empty state' is a precursor to Spirit-Treasury (识藏), the second great realm, where the practitioner awakens inner divine consciousness and perceives the world through intuition rather than the senses. Dugu Jianmo's 'I am my sword' philosophy reflects a pinnacle of martial arts cultivation—transcending the tool and merging with the weapon, such that the body itself becomes a divine blade. This echoes ancient Chinese swordsmanship ideals where the ultimate sword is no sword at all. The Bastard Ancient God Spear (巴斯德古神矛) is a relic from a foreign tradition, suggesting the World of Immortality contains artifacts from various mythological systems. Its blood that never dries is a marker of divine killing, a common trope in myths where weapons that slay gods become cursed or sanctified. The ambush also highlights the code of honor among top cultivators: Dugu Jianmo's disdain for the sneak attack signals that even enemies respect the purity of single combat.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a critical turning point: Xiao Chen's duel with Dugu Jianmo is left unresolved, and a new, treacherous enemy emerges from the shadows. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's near-death experience with the god-killing spear affects his cultivation—being pierced by such a weapon may either cripple him or temper him further. The brown-haired youth mentions a brother, hinting at a duo of assassins connected to the ongoing feud with the noble families or the Beidou Academy. The unresolved duel with Dugu Jianmo also suggests a future rematch once both have recovered. Xiao Chen's vow to kill both brothers sets the stage for a vicious hunt in the coming chapters, raising the question of whether he can survive his wounds and exact revenge before the next attack comes.
