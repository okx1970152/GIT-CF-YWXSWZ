## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a cataclysmic turning point, pushing Xiao Chen to the brink of annihilation. Cornered by four Otherworld Ancestral Gods and a coalition of Stone Man Kings, he deploys every lethal treasure in his arsenal. The chapter opens with him trapped at the stone gate, forced to leverage the mysterious stone jar—the dreaded 'Source of All Evil'—as a bargaining chip. He manages a temporary escape, but the kings, having nearly destroyed the terrifying shadow demon, return to renew the siege. In a heart-wrenching gambit, Xiao Chen transfers all his divine artifacts to Keke and thrusts her to safety, sacrificing himself. He is then hurled into the sealed stone gate alongside the stone jar and the lingering demon shadow. The chapter blends visceral combat with poignant farewell, illuminating Xiao Chen's cunning and his unbreakable bond with Keke.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen is cornered at the stone gate by Lu Zhan and three Otherworld Ancestral Gods. He wields the stone jar, its seal partially opened, emitting baleful aura that forces the four ancestral gods to retreat. However, before he can fully escape, the coalition of kings—having subdued the shadow demon using the Three Sovereign Mirror—returns and seals off all exits. Keke's parents open a dimensional tunnel to rescue them, but the kings repeatedly cut it off. With help from the Stone Crow and Keke's Xuanzu, Keke nearly breaks free. Xiao Chen makes a split-second decision: he strips himself of all his supreme treasures—the Divine Diagram, the ancient scroll, the king-tier divine city, the Heavenly Marks, and the Heavenly Stele—and transfers them to Keke, pushing her out through the tunnel. He retains only the stone jar. The kings, terrified of the jar's potential, use the Three Sovereign Mirror to seal Xiao Chen into the stone gate along with the jar. Inside, he comes face to face with the shadow demon, which, though severely weakened, still pulses with menace. The stone jar's seal continues to leak evil energy. Facing certain death, Xiao Chen greets the demon with sardonic humor, nicknaming it 'Little Demon Demon.'

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The stone jar, known as the 'Source of All Evil,' is a cursed artifact whose origins are veiled in primordial terror. Even Stone Man Kings fear it, as its unsealing might release a malevolent force capable of rivaling them. The chapter demonstrates the concept of 'sealing' as a supreme containment method: the stone gate is permanently locked by the combined divine power of the Three Sovereign Mirror and the kings, etched with prohibitive runes. Xiao Chen's selfless transfer of his treasures to Keke echoes the classic xianxia trope of a master sacrificing for a disciple or companion—a poignant moment amid cosmic warfare. The shadow demon (魔影) is likely a remnant of a fallen supreme being, possibly connected to the curse spirits mentioned earlier. Its ability to survive multiple rounds of suppression hints at an indomitable nature. The bond between Xiao Chen and Keke, deepened through countless trials, becomes the emotional fulcrum of this desperate gamble.

## Reading Guide

This chapter ends on a devastating cliffhanger: Xiao Chen is sealed inside the stone gate with both the stone jar and the shadow demon. Readers should watch for how he will navigate this seemingly hopeless situation. The stone jar's mystery deepens—what entity lies within, and why do even the kings dread it? The shadow demon, though weakened, is still a formidable presence; will it attack, observe, or perhaps bargain with Xiao Chen? Meanwhile, Keke now carries all of Xiao Chen's supreme treasures and is free. Her next moves will likely involve seeking allies or attempting to grow powerful enough to rescue him. The chapter also hints that the coalition of kings is not omnipotent—they feared the jar and the demon, suggesting weaknesses that can be exploited. Future chapters may reveal the origins of the stone gate and the seal, as well as the true nature of the 'Source of All Evil.'
