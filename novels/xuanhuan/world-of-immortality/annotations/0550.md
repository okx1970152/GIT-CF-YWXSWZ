## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a major turning point in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey. After obtaining three more War Swords from the Broken City, he synthesizes a fourth fragment of the divine formation diagram, achieving a new level of power. Then follows a grueling seventeen-year training arc in the Death World's depths, where he reforges himself from a mortal level to the Eighth Heaven of Half-Ancestor. The chapter closes with an encounter at a primordial demon city, where a mysterious Daoist—backed by four legendary immortal-slaying swords—awaits him. The tone is a blend of strategic ingenuity, meditative endurance, and mythic dread.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen shatters a stone mountain within the broken divine city and retrieves three War Swords. He immediately integrates them into his incomplete formation diagram, fusing them to produce a fourth fragment, which dramatically increases the diagram's completeness and power. He suspects that Ren Mo Gecui (the Otherworld Ancestor God) may have deliberately guided him to this outcome, possibly as part of a larger scheme. Resolving to become stronger, Xiao Chen embarks on a seventeen-year self-imposed trial in the Death World's outer regions. He begins by sealing his own cultivation and walking as a mortal, fighting ordinary fire-seed creatures, gradually unlocking his power. This process sharpens his mental fortitude and elevates his divine sense from the Seventh Heaven to the Eighth Heaven of Half-Ancestor. He eventually kills a sovereign-level creature and ventures into the deeper Death World, where he witnesses bizarre and terrifying phenomena: a colossal mile-high skeleton, an immortal garden surrounded by bones, ruins that weep, and a ghostly divine song he cannot approach. At last, he arrives at a primordial demon city, but before he can test its occupant, a figure appears—a tall Daoist in ancient robes, carrying four swords on his back, speaking with calm expectation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'War Swords' (战剑) are legendary weapons tied to the Heavenly Steles, each containing fragmentary laws of the universe. When assembled into the ‘formation diagram’ (阵图), they gain world-suppressing power. The ‘Eight Heaven Half-Ancestor’ realm (八重天半祖) is a high tier within the Half-Ancestor stage, representing a near-complete mastery of one's domain. The seventeen-year training arc echoes classic xianxia tropes of seclusion and mortal renewal. Xiao Chen's method of sealing his own power and walking as a mortal to refine his mind is reminiscent of ‘heart cultivation’ (修心), a common Daoist practice to break through bottlenecks. The mysterious Daoist with four swords is unmistakably connected to the ‘Four Immortal-Slaying Swords’ (诛仙四剑) of Tongtian Jiaozhu from the *Fengshen Yanyi* tradition, signaling the entry of a legendary figure into the narrative.

## Reading Guide
This chapter delivers a satisfying payoff for Xiao Chen's relentless pursuit of the War Swords, while setting up a crucial future confrontation. The seventeen-year time skip is a classic 'training arc' that allows the protagonist to level up without excessive chapter-by-chapter montage—note how Xiao Chen's divine sense breakthrough to the Eighth Heaven is earned through hardship, not gifted. The eerie phenomena in the deeper Death World hint at lore still to be uncovered: What is the source of the weeping ruins? The singing mist? The mile-high skeleton? The final appearance of the four-sword Daoist raises the stakes dramatically—is he an ally, a test, or a new antagonist? The chapter title allusion to 'Tongtian' or a remnant of his lineage suggests a major collision between Xiao Chen's chaotic formation diagram and the legendary immortal-slaying sword formation. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's newly integrated fourth diagram fragment interacts with the Daoist's four swords.
