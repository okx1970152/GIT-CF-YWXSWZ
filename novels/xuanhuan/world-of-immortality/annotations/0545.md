## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers one of the most intense and visually overwhelming confrontations in the World of Immortality arc: the Martial Ancestor, in his prime, locks combat with the terrifying Stone Man – a being one step away from the final transformation. The conflict unfolds in the Eternal Unknown, a dimension beyond normal space-time, where every blow shatters the fabric of reality. Xiao Chen, riding the Yellow Clay Altar, joins the fray with the Eight Origin Sounds and the incomplete formation diagram, but the power gap is immense. The chapter is a masterclass in escalating stakes, concluding with a desperate escape to the Curse Realm and the enigmatic appearance of the ancient stone turtle at the seventh Heavenly Stele.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Martial Ancestor meeting the Stone Man's charge head-on. Their clash is described as 'two immortal irons battling on a wooden bed,' with space itself as the fragile floor. The Stone Man's punches can kill Ancestral Gods, yet the Martial Ancestor holds his ground for several exchanges, his hands bleeding but spirit unbroken. The three Supreme Ancestral Gods seal the Eternal Unknown, trapping everyone inside. Xiao Chen, hidden within the Yellow Clay Altar, unleashes the full power of the Eight Origin Sounds, momentarily forcing the Stone Man back. The incomplete ninth sound – a mysterious tremor that wounds even the Stone Man – appears but is too unstable to sustain. Xiao Chen then sacrifices his puppet, the Ancestral God Chou Tian, to buy time. The ancient Heavenly Man (Tianren) strikes at the Stone Man's past self, a rare temporal attack, but the Stone Man's will is too strong. In the climax, Xiao Chen successfully merges the War Swords with the incomplete formation diagram, stabbing the Stone Man and causing his stone blood to flow. The Martial Ancestor activates a pre-prepared formation fueled by two burning ancient demon cities, smashing the foundation of the ninety-nine stone steps. The group escapes to the Curse Realm, where the seventh Heavenly Stele looms, and an old stone turtle waits atop it, facing the pursuing Stone Man.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'Stone Man' is a unique cultivation concept in this novel, representing a being who has refined their body into an indestructible stone-like state. The 'final step' is the ultimate transformation, turning the Stone Man into an eternal, near-omnipotent entity. The Eight Origin Sounds (嗡、咯、阿、吼、哞、咄、噪、叨) are primordial vibrations that can harm even supreme beings; the incomplete ninth sound suggests a supreme unity beyond the eight. The 'Tianren' or Heavenly Race is an ancient civilization from a prior cosmic era, possessing advanced spiritual techniques and temporal manipulation abilities. The 'Eternal Unknown' is a dimension outside normal time and space where the three Supreme Ancestral Gods reside, guarding the ninety-nine stone steps. The use of two ancient demon cities as fuel for the escape formation highlights the desperation and resourcefulness of the Martial Ancestor, sacrificing immense power sources to break free.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up several crucial threads for the coming arcs. The incomplete ninth sound and the formation diagram's potential are left as unresolved tools for Xiao Chen. The Stone Man, wounded but not defeated, is now chasing the group with a burning grudge. The old stone turtle's appearance suggests it holds knowledge about the seventh Heavenly Stele and may become an ally or guide. The Martial Ancestor's plan to distract the Supreme Ancestral Gods by attacking the stone steps has succeeded partially, but the war is far from over. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will integrate his new power sources – the Yellow Clay Altar, the formation diagram, and the Eight Origin Sounds – into his martial path. The stakes have been raised: the Otherworld now knows Xiao Chen is a major threat, and the Stone Man's personal vendetta makes every future encounter deadly.
