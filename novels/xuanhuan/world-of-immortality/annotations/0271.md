## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges Xiao Chen into the heart of the Ancient God Desert, one of the Eternal Continent's four extreme forbidden zones—a desolate wasteland where even gods cannot survive without sacred relics. Amidst golden sands and lethal gales, Xiao Chen achieves a long-awaited breakthrough to the Sky-Mastery realm, becoming a demigod. But the triumph is immediately shadowed by a violent conflict between his newly awakened Spatial Divine Principle and his cultivated martial body, leading to a shocking physical disintegration and rebirth. The chapter concludes with a haunting prophecy that questions the very reality of the World of Immortality, echoing through the endless desert.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen treks alone through the Ancient God Desert, enduring blistering temperatures, corpse-tearing winds, and a landscape of golden sand. Using twelve-grade lotus throne, Four Immortal-Slaying Swords, and other sacred relics, he cultivates daily, perfecting the Heavenly Stele Profound Art. His divinized acupoints multiply and divine meridians extend. After a prolonged effort, he breaks through to the Sky-Mastery realm, awakening a Spatial Divine Principle—a power considered king among same-level cultivators. However, the spatial ability immediately clashes with his martial body, cracking his flesh and shattering his bones. In a surreal moment, he experiences body separation: one form shatters while another, composed of divinized acupoints and divine meridians, remains intact. The two bodies later merge, resolving the conflict temporarily. Over the next three years, Xiao Chen continues to cultivate in the desert depths, divinizing forty-nine acupoints and extending divine meridians throughout his body. He presses deeper, using his relics to move slowly through the restricted region. Finally, he discovers an ancient temple, a Taoist temple, and a floating divine island. A resonant voice declares that this world is merely a projection—the real world lies elsewhere—shattering the metaphysical foundations of his journey.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ancient God Desert (古神荒漠) is one of the four extreme forbidden zones of the Eternal Continent, each marking an edge of the known world. These zones are impassable to even demigods without extraordinary protections, echoing the xianxia trope of dangerous borderlands that conceal ultimate secrets. The Sky-Mastery realm (御空境界) is also called the demigod stage—a pivotal threshold where a cultivator can fly and command natural forces, bridging mortal and divine. The acquisition of a Spatial Divine Principle (空间神则) is particularly significant: in xianxia lore, space and time laws are ranked supreme, allowing practitioners to challenge opponents several realms above their own. The body-splitting and regeneration scene hints at a unique dual-body constitution, perhaps the first manifestation of a 'treasure body' or 'divine body' that will become Xiao Chen's hallmark. Finally, the voice proclaiming the world an illusion introduces a Daoist/Buddhist ontological twist—suggesting that the familiar cultivation realms are merely shadows of a truer universe, a concept that resonates with the idea of 'world as projection' found in philosophical Daoism.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a major milestone in Xiao Chen's power progression (Sky-Mastery) but also introduces a profound crisis: the incompatibility between his martial foundation and new spatial power. Readers should watch how this conflict evolves—his martial body's ability to regenerate and split may be a hidden strength rather than a flaw. The voice's declaration that the World of Immortality is a projection serves as a narrative hinge: it sets up a larger cosmic mystery that will likely define the novel's later arcs. Expect new revelations about the true nature of the realm, and possibly the return of characters like Lan Nuo or the demon. The desolate desert setting also provides a contemplative backdrop for Xiao Chen's internal growth and grieving for Keke, whose loss drives his relentless cultivation.
