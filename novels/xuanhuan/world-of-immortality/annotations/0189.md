## Chapter Overview

The beast tournament in Heavenly Emperor City reaches its electrifying climax as six young sacred beasts clash in a battle that defies the limits of their age. The Golden Lion King, a terrifying prodigy with two vertical eyes, unleashes the power of the reincarnation eye, forcing the Five-Colored Peacock King to reveal a legendary Chaos Green Feather just to survive. Then, when the Lion King turns on the golden ape Gold and the White Tiger, a tiny golden monkey appears out of nowhere—and throws the Lion King across the arena. This chapter delivers both epic mythic combat and a breathtaking twist that will leave readers laughing and awestruck. The tone shifts seamlessly from dread to shock to comedy, all while advancing the deep mysteries of the World of Immortality's beast lineages and the growing legend of Keke.

## Key Plot Points

The battle begins with the Golden Lion King opening his second vertical eye, the reincarnation eye, which tears open a gate to the underworld. The Five-Colored Peacock King is swallowed by the ominous black mist and nearly lost to the cycle of reincarnation. But at the last moment, a brilliant cyan light pierces the darkness: the Peacock King possesses the Chaos Green Feather, a primordial artifact that once belonged to a legendary ancient being, and uses it to sever the reincarnation power and escape. The Golden Lion King, enraged, abandons the Peacock and instead charges at Gold the golden ape and the White Tiger, intending to send them into the abyss. Meanwhile, Keke, disguised in a stolen golden monkey skin and clothes, sneaks back into the arena after Xiao Chen forbade it from participating. The Lion King's dark energies nearly engulf the two beasts, but Keke, standing at the edge of the black mist, casually brushes it aside with her paw. She then dashes to the Lion King, climbs onto its back, and with two hands grabs its shimmering mane and flips it over her shoulder, throwing the mighty beast to the ground. The entire arena is stunned into silence, then erupted in disbelief. Xiao Chen watches from his private booth, rubbing his temples in exasperation. The chapter ends with Keke proudly clutching two handfuls of golden mane, chirping in her childish voice.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'reincarnation eye' (轮回之眼) draws on Chinese Buddhist and folk beliefs about cycles of rebirth and the gates of hell. In many xianxia novels, a cultivator or beast who can manipulate reincarnation is considered supremely dangerous, as they can bypass physical defenses and forcibly end or transfer an opponent's existence. The Chaos Green Feather (混沌青羽) belongs to the mythos of the Five-Colored Peacock, a legendary being from the Investiture of the Gods era, whose five-color divine light could sweep all phenomena. The green feather is said to be one of five primordial feathers formed from chaos, granting the user the power to cut through any law or dimension—even the gates of hell. The beast tournament itself is a staple of xianxia world-building, where spirit beasts fight for prestige and treasure, and hidden elites often reveal themselves. The golden monkey disguise is a humorous nod to the classic Journey to the West's Sun Wukong, though Keke's true form is far more mysterious.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a major turning point: Keke's innocent but reckless intervention has now exposed her power to every major faction in the city. The Golden Lion King's mysterious origins and the revived legend of the Chaos Green Feather will surely draw even greater scrutiny from the ancient families and academies. Xiao Chen's fragile peace in Heavenly Emperor City may soon shatter as enemies and allies alike realize the depth of his companion's abilities. Keep an eye on how Kanasi, Qilao, and the other young elites react in the coming chapters—some may seek to befriend Xiao Chen, while others will plot to steal Keke. The reincarnation eye also hints at a deeper connection to the Death World and the power of the Undying King, a thread that may tie back to Dragon Island's secrets. The stage is set for an explosive shift in the balance of power among the younger generation.
