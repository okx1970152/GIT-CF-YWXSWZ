## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, Xiao Chen—wielding the reforged stone giant and three War Swords—faces the Yaksha Half-Ancestor in a battle that pushes him to his limits. The Yaksha, enraged by earlier humiliations, drags Xiao Chen into his 'Myriad Worlds' space, a personal domain where he attempts to break Xiao Chen's will through illusion and brute force. The clash shakes the foundations of the world, and Xiao Chen's mastery of the Four Killing Hands combined with the stone giant's raw power proves devastating. But as the Yaksha falls, new threats emerge: other half-ancestors, coveting the War Swords, step forward to challenge Xiao Chen. The chapter delivers a visceral combat sequence and sets up a broader confrontation with the ancient powers of the Hundred Races.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Yaksha Half-Ancestor using the terrified corpse of a fallen ancestral god as a weapon, wounding himself in the process. Xiao Chen takes advantage, driving the stone giant against him. The Yaksha, humiliated and desperate, opens a spatial gate to his Myriad Worlds domain, where he unleashes a barrage of over a hundred sacred mountains at Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen shatters them with the stone giant's enormous stone sword, despite his right hand cracking from the strain. The Yaksha then reveals his 'Ten Thousand Laws, Do Not Touch' immunity and his 'Sword Fire Burns the Heavens' technique, but Xiao Chen counters with the three War Swords that had previously been absorbed into the stone giant. The swords pierce the Yaksha's body, draining his essence.

The Yaksha then uses his Myriad Worlds illusion to borrow power from Xiao Chen's friends and family across the realms, attempting to force Xiao Chen to kill them. Xiao Chen refuses to be swayed and destroys the illusions. A mysterious female voice from the heavens—the young sovereign—intervenes, shattering the illusion and wounding the Yaksha with a backlash. With the Yaksha weakened, Xiao Chen unleashes the full power of the Four Killing Hands and the stone giant, beating the half-ancestor into the golden sea. The battle ends with the Yaksha utterly defeated, his body broken.

Tianwaitian and Renwairen step in to stop further fighting, warning Xiao Chen that other half-ancestors—the Skeleton King and the Asura Half-Ancestor—now eye his War Swords with greedy intent. They demand a 'friendly spar,' but Xiao Chen's defiance and another drawn War Sword give them pause.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter highlights several core xianxia concepts. The 'Half-Ancestor' represents a being that has transcended the usual mortal and immortal realms, approaching the power of an Ancestral God. The 'Myriad Worlds' (万法世界) technique allows a cultivator to create a personal domain where they can manipulate reality, borrowing faith and divine power from across the cosmos—a rare and terrifying ability. The 'Four Killing Hands' (戮神、逆乱、镇魔、崩裂) are ultimate techniques derived from the Heavenly Stele, each embodying a principle of destruction. The War Swords are ancient weapons that can slay half-ancestors, and their appearance draws the interest of the highest powers. The voice from the heavens that stops the illusion may belong to a powerful female sovereign from the Death World, hinting at deeper layers of the cultivation hierarchy.

## Reading Guide
After this climatic battle, readers should watch for the shifting political landscape. The Yaksha Half-Ancestor, though defeated, is not killed, and his humiliation will likely fuel future revenge. More immediately, the Skeleton King and Asura Half-Ancestor openly covet Xiao Chen's War Swords, setting up a direct confrontation with two half-ancestors simultaneously. Tianwaitian and Renwairen's intervention prevents bloodshed for now, but it is only a temporary reprieve. Xiao Chen's return to the Nine Provinces with new power and artifacts will attract enemies and allies alike. The mysterious female sovereign remains an enigma—her connection to Qingqing and her role in the Death World will become crucial in later arcs. The chapter reinforces Xiao Chen's rise to the top tier of combatants but also shows that half-ancestors are not his only worry; the politics of the Hundred Races and the greed for ancient treasures will create new threats.
