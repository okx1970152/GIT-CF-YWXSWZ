## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal transformation for Xiao Chen in the Death World. After a rampage of slaughter across the Eastern and Western domains, harvesting sixteen Yellow Diamond second-tier fire seeds from ten experts and six city lords, he retreats to the desolate frontier to cultivate the sacred 'Weng' sound of the Eight Origin Syllables. The chapter contrasts the brutal, resource-driven hierarchy of the fire seed beings with the serene, almost alchemical process of soul purification using Life Source Liquid. Three arrogant angel skeletons attempt to frame him to the Divine Tribe, only to be destroyed by the ancient power of Li Mu. The climax is Xiao Chen's complete purification—his soul spotless of deathly aura, his golden bones saturated with life essence—achieving the pinnacle of the Yellow Diamond realm and preparing to return to the Nine Provinces.

The emotional tone oscillates between weary determination and sacred renewal. The opening tension of the hunt gives way to the quiet intensity of cultivation, then to the sharp conflict with the angels, and finally to the radiant, almost holy transformation. This chapter is a study in contrasts: the grim law of the strong in the Death World versus the luminous purity of a being reversing death itself.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's campaign across the Death World has been devastatingly effective. He has slain ten top-ranked experts and six city lords, accumulating sixteen of the thirty Yellow Diamond fire seeds needed to advance. The remaining seeds must come either from declaring war on the kings of the Eastern domain—a suicidal move at his current stage—or by hunting in the West, where the awe-inspiring Angel King rules a unified territory. Xiao Chen chooses a third path: using the Eight Origin Sounds to cultivate slowly, but his peace is shattered by the arrival of three angel skeletons, emissaries of the Angel King.

The angels, named Xiao De and others, attempt to recruit Xiao Chen into the Angel King's service. When he refuses, they feign retreat but then frame him to the Divine Tribe, claiming he murdered their kin and refined their flesh into Life Source Liquid. Li Mu and the child Tianya arrive, initially sensing the Life Source Liquid's aura on Xiao Chen. A tense moment arises, but Li Mu trusts his judgment and recognizes the frame-up. He kills the three angels with devastating speed, revealing the hidden might of the Divine Tribe elders.

With the threat neutralized, Xiao Chen turns to the Life Source Liquid he had collected. Guided by Li Mu's advice, he uses it not merely for soul purification but for a complete metamorphosis. The process is excruciating—the liquid burns away all deathly impurities from his fire seed and bones—but after fifteen days, his soul shines without a trace of death qi, and his Yellow Diamond skeleton radiates sacred life essence. He achieves the perfect Yellow Diamond state, a pinnacle that makes him a true overlord of the outer domains, second only to the Angel King. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen gazing toward the Nine Provinces, ready to return with newfound power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Death World operates on a ruthless hierarchy of bone quality: from white to blue, purple, yellow, and ultimately diamond. Xiao Chen has ascended from Purple Diamond to Yellow Diamond second tier, and now to the absolute peak of Yellow Diamond. The 'fire seed' (火种) is the soul essence of undead beings; absorbing thirty same-tier seeds allows evolution to the next level. This echoes the xianxia trope of 'cauldron refinement' where external resources fuel internal breakthroughs, but with a deathly twist—the seeds are harvested from slain foes, making each step a testament to combat dominance.

Life Source Liquid (生命源液) is a Green substance refined from the bodies of powerful living beings, often by evil fire seed cultivators. It holds immense purifying power: it can cleanse a fire seed of all deathly impurity, turning a skeleton's aura from necrotic to sacred. This process mirrors the Buddhist concept of 'śūnyatā'—emptying the self of defilements to achieve purity. The fact that Xiao Chen uses the liquid without having murdered innocents for it sets him apart from typical Death World power-seekers, aligning him with the righteous path of the Divine Tribe.

The Eight Origin Sounds (本源八音) are primordial vibrations that govern creation and destruction. Xiao Chen's mastery of the 'Weng' sound allows him to cultivate without devouring fire seeds, a rare ability that hints at his unique destiny. In xianxia, sound-based cultivation is often associated with ancient, transcendent techniques that bypass normal resource constraints.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a critical power milestone for Xiao Chen. His purification to Yellow Diamond pinnacle marks the end of his long struggle in the Death World 'outer domains' and the beginning of his readiness to re-enter the Nine Provinces. The reader should note that Xiao Chen now has a completely pure soul—no trace of death aura—which may allow him to interact with the living world more fully. His golden skeleton, saturated with life essence, is almost indestructible and radiates a sacred glow that commands respect even from the Angel King.

The reappearance of Li Mu and Tianya reaffirms the Divine Tribe as powerful but neutral allies. Their quick dispatch of the three angel skeletons reveals that the surface-level hierarchy of the Death World (Angel King, Giant King) does not encompass all powers—the Divine Tribe elders are hidden masters who have chosen isolation over territory.

Forward-looking threads: Xiao Chen now possesses the strength to challenge the Angel King, but chooses to return to the mortal world instead. This suggests that his priority remains the Nine Provinces and his unresolved conflicts there—particularly with the doppelgänger on the stone statue. The purification may also have implications for his ability to reclaim his physical body or merge with his remnant soul. The chapter ends on a note of quiet triumph and anticipation: Xiao Chen is ready to walk the lands of the living again.
