## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen emerges from the depths of hell encumbered with an impossible array of sacred relics. Wielding the Four Immortal-Slaying Swords, the Taiji Diagram, and the Twelve-Petal Lotus Throne among others, he has become a walking armory of mythic proportions. Yet this wealth of power comes with a heavy cost: Keke, the little white beast, enters a deep slumber within the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree to rebuild its physical form. The chapter sets a tragic, desolate tone as Xiao Chen realizes he has made enemies of almost the entire world and must now flee into hiding. The intervention of Zhuangzi and two mysterious girls helps cleanse the spiritual imprints from his relics, but he is soon confronted by the powerful Chengzi and a group of Jie Sect cultivators. The scene is both awe-inspiring and terrifying, as Xiao Chen brandishes his sacred armaments against foes who are at the peak of cultivation.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen leaves hell with a staggering collection of holy artifacts: the Taiji Diagram, the Three Sacred Spirit Crown, the Four Immortal-Slaying Swords, the Sun Holy Stone, the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, Satan's Magic Wand, and the Twelve-Petal Lotus Throne. He reflects that he looks like a nouveau riche in terms of power, but he knows that he is now at war with many powerful enemies. Keke communicates that the light of the Lost Paradise is forcing it to rebuild its body, and it will sleep. Xiao Chen reassures Keke, promising to wait and to keep its favorite yin wood fruits. Keke enters the Sacred Tree, which then sinks into Xiao Chen's body. Zhuangzi (in butterfly form) and two young girls (daughters of powerful figures) arrive to help cleanse the spiritual imprints from Xiao Chen's relics, allowing him to control them fully. After they leave, Xiao Chen is ambushed by Chengzi, a famous disciple of Yuanshi, leading a group of Jie Sect experts. A young man among them insults Xiao Chen and attacks, but is instantly killed by the Four Immortal-Slaying Swords. Xiao Chen then deploys the full Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation, shocking the Jie Sect cultivators. Chengzi produces the Heaven-Overturning Seal, but Xiao Chen counters by revealing multiple other relics: the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, Satan's Magic Wand, the Taiji Diagram, the Sun Holy Stone, the Ancient Bible, and the Three Sacred Spirit Crown. The display leaves even the mighty Chengzi stunned and speechless, highlighting the absurdity and unparalleled power of Xiao Chen's armament.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is a parade of legendary artifacts from Chinese mythology and beyond. The Four Immortal-Slaying Swords and the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation are famous from the novel *Fengshen Yanyi*, where they belonged to Tongtian Jiaozhu, the head of the Jie Sect. The Taiji Diagram is the signature treasure of Laozi, the founder of Daoism. The Twelve-Petal Lotus Throne is a Buddhist relic of the Buddha. The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree is the weapon of a great Buddhist sage. Satan's Magic Wand and the Ancient Bible bring in Western religious iconography, showing the novel's global scope. The Heaven-Overturning Seal is another *Fengshen* artifact, used by Yuanshi's disciple. The concept of cleansing spiritual imprints is crucial: without this, Xiao Chen's enemies could recall these artifacts at will. Zhuangzi, the Daoist philosopher, appears as a butterfly, a nod to his famous dream parable. The two little girls are likely connected to the ancestors mentioned earlier. This blending of Daoist, Buddhist, and Western mythologies enriches the world-building and emphasizes the destructive scale of the conflict.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a major power shift for Xiao Chen, but it also makes him a prime target. With Keke asleep, he loses a key emotional anchor and must rely on his own cunning. The sheer number of sacred relics he possesses will attract the attention of every major power on the Eternal Continent, especially the Jie Sect and the Tiger Clan. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen plans to evade his enemies or whether he will be forced into open confrontation. The intervention of Zhuangzi suggests that some powerful beings are watching over him, perhaps because of his connection to the Sixteen War Swords. The fate of the Jie Sect retaliation will be critical in the next chapters. Additionally, Keke's sleep may herald a transformation upon awakening. Will Xiao Chen find a refuge in the wilderness, or will he become a hunted phantom across the lands?
