## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a long-awaited clash between two young dragon kings in the heart of Heavenly Emperor City's beast fighting arena. The Little Defiant Dragon, once dismissed as the weakest of its kind, faces the mighty Golden Lion King Dragon in a life-or-death duel that transcends mere sport. The tone is epic and tragic, a collision of ancient bloodlines and indomitable wills. The stakes are not just survival but the very essence of dragon kingship—the victor will absorb the defeated's Ancestral Dragon qi, a symbolic marker of supremacy. Every strike, every feint, is laden with mythic weight, as the arena's human spectators witness a battle that echoes the primordial wars of dragons.

## Key Plot Points

The battle begins with tense stillness as both dragons recognize each other's presence. The Little Defiant Dragon strikes first, using its speed and a deceptive tail whip to wound the Golden Lion King Dragon's head, drawing golden-tinged blood. The Golden Lion King Dragon responds with overwhelming force, its greater size and strength driving the Little Defiant Dragon to the brink. The Little Defiant Dragon feigns collapse, luring the Golden Lion King Dragon into a fatal charge, then uses its horn to tear a gaping wound in its opponent's neck. Enraged, the Golden Lion King Dragon takes to the air, unleashing torrents of golden divine light. In a desperate gamble, the Little Defiant Dragon reveals the legendary Reverse Dragon Seven Steps, ascending step by step into the void. With the fifth step, it crushes the Golden Lion King Dragon's back; with the sixth, it shatters its entire body, leaving only the head intact. From the fallen king's head, a stream of pure Ancestral Dragon qi flows into the Little Defiant Dragon, marking its profound transformation. The crowd erupts in shock—a dragon king has been slain, and a new sovereign has risen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Dragon kings in Chinese mythology are apex beings, often associated with emperors and divine rule. The Reverse Dragon Seven Steps is a legendary ancient dragon combat art, said to be so potent that it can trample even gods underfoot. Its appearance here underscores the Little Defiant Dragon's hidden heritage and the depth of its training. The absorption of Ancestral Dragon qi is a symbolic act—this qi is not raw power or a soul fragment, but a pure emblem of dragon lineage and sovereignty. By claiming it, the Little Defiant Dragon asserts its right to ascend among the highest dragon bloodlines. The arena setting, meanwhile, reflects a recurring theme in the World of Immortality: the commodification of sacred beasts, where celestial beings are forced to battle for mortal spectacle.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a definitive turning point for the Little Defiant Dragon. Its victory will send shockwaves through Heavenly Emperor City and the broader world. Expect repercussions from the Golden Lion King Dragon's former allies and owners—powerful families will not forget the loss of such a valuable asset. Xiao Chen's emotional investment during the fight deepens his bond with the dragon, setting the stage for even greater challenges. The Reverse Dragon Seven Steps technique hints at a reservoir of ancient dragon wisdom that the Little Defiant Dragon may continue to unlock. Readers should watch for how the newly acquired Ancestral Dragon qi influences its growth, and whether this victory draws the attention of even more formidable foes, both human and draconic.
