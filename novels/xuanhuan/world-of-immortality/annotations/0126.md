## Chapter Overview

After months of peril and isolation on Dragon Island, Xiao Chen finally sets foot on the Eternal Continent—the heart of the World of Immortality. But the respite is fleeting. The moment the King Ship deposits him, Yan Qingcheng, Keke, and the little defiant dragon on the shore, they are met by a patrol of the Undying Sect. These disciples, waiting for Yan Qingcheng’s return, mistake the little defiant dragon for one of the legendary Companion Dragon Kings. The scene crackles with tension as Xiao Chen’s claim is challenged, leading to a swift and decisive show of force. The arrival of the Second Senior Brother—a powerful cultivator with an inscrutable demeanor—escalates the stakes, drawing Xiao Chen into the political web of the Undying Sect even as he learns of the bloody war that erupted over the dragon kings who sailed on the Divine Ship.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the King Ship’s eerie departure, transporting Xiao Chen and his party safely ashore. Almost immediately, they are surrounded by a dozen young cultivators from the Undying Sect, who recognize Yan Qingcheng with a mix of joy and suspicion. Through their excited chatter, Xiao Chen learns that the return of the Divine Ship months ago triggered a catastrophic battle along the coast—demigods, fallen angels, and dragon kings clashed, leaving countless dead. The disciples’ covetous gaze fixates on the little defiant dragon, whom they assume Yan Qingcheng tamed. When Xiao Chen bluntly asserts ownership, a young challenger steps forward. Without hesitation, Xiao Chen seizes him by the collar and flings him into the sea. Keke, offended by being called a “puppy,” unleashes a burst of seven-colored light, hurling every disciple who had pointed at her into the ocean. Yan Qingcheng, caught between her sect loyalties and her dependence on Xiao Chen, confirms that the dragon belongs to him. To further provoke her, Xiao Chen casually announces that they are husband and wife—a claim that shocks the disciples and infuriates Yan Qingcheng. The stalemate is broken when the Second Senior Brother arrives, a middle-aged man with a deceptively ordinary appearance and piercing eyes that mark him as a formidable practitioner. He invites Xiao Chen to the Undying Sect, and the group departs, careful to avoid patrols of fallen angels and a winged dragon still hunting in the region.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eternal Continent is the primary landmass of the World of Immortality, a realm where countless cultivation sects, ancient races, and primordial beasts vie for supremacy. The Undying Sect, to which Yan Qingcheng belongs, is a major power with deep roots and an altar that can remotely divine the life status of its disciples thousands of miles away—a testament to the sect’s esoteric mastery. The mention of demigods, fallen angels, and dragon kings underscores the brutal Darwinian hierarchy of this world: even demigods were slaughtered in the scramble for the Companion Dragon Kings. The little defiant dragon, though not a true dragon king, possesses a stubborn pride that commands respect, while Keke’s casual display of seven-colored divine power hints at her lineage’s connection to primordial deities. Xiao Chen’s claim of marriage is not just a taunt; in the xianxia tradition, public declarations can carry weight, binding reputations and forcing political consequences.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks Xiao Chen’s transition from a survivor of a sealed island to a player in the wider continent’s power games. The Undying Sect’s internal politics and Yan Qingcheng’s compromised position will likely force Xiao Chen into a delicate balancing act: he must navigate sect hierarchies without revealing his own secrets—including the stolen Sacred Tree and the demon’s bargain. The Second Senior Brother’s quiet observation suggests he is already weighing Xiao Chen’s potential as an asset or a threat. Readers should watch for how Yan Qingcheng’s public humiliation and Xiao Chen’s brazen claim affect their uneasy alliance. Meanwhile, the absence of Liu Mu, Yizhen, and the other dragon kings foreshadows a larger conflict brewing across the continent. The chapter closes with a restrained tension, hinting that Xiao Chen’s arrival has not gone unnoticed by the continent’s hidden powers.
