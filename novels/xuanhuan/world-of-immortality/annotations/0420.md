## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a dramatic pivot from the explosive carnage of the previous massacre to the brooding stillness of the Dead World. The Yaksha half-ancestor, humiliated and enraged by Xiao Chen's slaughter of over a thousand enemy cultivators—including more than a dozen Perfected Man realm Heavenly Kings—launches a furious but futile search. Xiao Chen wisely retreats to the Divine Village in the Dead World, knowing that returning to the Nine Provinces would mean facing the combined wrath of multiple half-ancestors. The chapter then settles into a somber meditation on cultivation plateaus: three years of motionless seclusion yield only a single sub-realm advancement, from the Second to the Third Heaven of the Perfected Man realm. Faced with the terrifying prospect of permanent stagnation at this level—the fate of countless historical geniuses—Xiao Chen makes a fateful decision to venture into the depths of the Dead World. This journey reopens threads connecting to the mysterious female sovereign who once sang the soul-summoning song, the three skeleton kings, and the elusive second primordial sound, the 'Duo' tone.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Yaksha half-ancestor's bitter failure: his carefully orchestrated ambush has been utterly overturned by Xiao Chen's mass killing. The ancestral god corpse poison left no residual souls, making resurrection impossible. His frantic search across the ten thousand immortal mountains yields nothing, and he is further humiliated when he and the ancient demon Chi You both fail to reclaim the 'Abyss Prison' from superior beings within the sealed domain. This cosmic defeat sends him storming back to the Nine Provinces, leaving the Celestial Burial Valley sealed once more. News of Xiao Chen's feat spreads across the realms, shocking all. In the Dead World, Xiao Chen settles into the Divine Village for long-term seclusion. He requests that elder Li Mu deliver a message to Keke, warning her to stay safe. Three years pass with glacial cultivation progress: only one sub-realm gained, and the rate is dwindling. Xiao Chen realizes he faces the same dead end that trapped countless ancients. Unable to raise his realm further, he commits to mastering the second of the Eight Origin Sounds, the 'Duo' tone. After a fourth year, he decides to move. Keke arrives, and with the fearless little beast at his side, he ventures deep into the Dead World. He deliberately skips the cities of the sovereign Yi Tianzhong, but a yellow-diamond skeleton's betrayal leads to a confrontation with three Perfected Man Heavenly Kings. One of them unleashes a spirit-crushing attack that is unmistakably similar to the 'Duo' tone, which sends a thrill of realization through Xiao Chen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Perfected Man realm (至人境界) is the apex of mortal cultivation before the threshold of Ancestral Godhood. In xianxia cosmology, this level is often described as the perfection of the human body, where further improvement is nearly impossible through conventional training. Many legendary figures spent centuries or millennia stuck here, making Xiao Chen's slow progress a deeply authentic and sobering turn. The 'Eight Origin Sounds' (本源八音) are primordial vibrations that predate even Buddhist mantras. The first tone, 'Weng' (嗡), governs life and death; the second, 'Duo' (咄), is a soul-shattering attack. Mastering them requires not just cultivation but resonance with the fundamental laws of reality—often aided by artifacts like the Yellow Clay Altar, which Xiao Chen no longer has. The Dead World's internal hierarchy is brutal: sovereigns like Yi Tianzhong rule vast territories dotted with 'Heavenly King' puppets at the Perfected Man realm. These are not independent cultivators but vassals bound by life-source cultivation and fear. Xiao Chen's earlier killing of Yi Tianzhong's five-colored skeleton king now marks him as a target. The female sovereign adjacent to Yi Tianzhong's domain, who once sang the soul-summoning song that drew fire-seed beings thousands of miles, remains one of the story's deepest mysteries. She may be related to Qingqing or the three skeleton kings, and her territory offers a powerful lure for Xiao Chen's investigation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a breathing space after the intense action of the previous volume, but it is far from filler. The cultivation plateau Xiao Chen faces is a genuine narrative turning point: he cannot rely on linear power growth forever. His decision to seek the second primordial sound in the depths of the Dead World—potentially by confronting a Heavenly King who uses that very technique—sets up a targeted quest. The reappearance of the female sovereign's territory and the skeleton kings (Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, Lunhuiwang) from the Celestial Burial Valley hints that many threads are converging. Watch for how Xiao Chen's journey deepens the mystery of the Dead World's connection to the illusory half-ancestors and the fate of Qingqing. The next chapters promise a clash with the three Heavenly Kings and potentially an encounter with the enigmatic female sovereign that could reshape Xiao Chen's destiny.
