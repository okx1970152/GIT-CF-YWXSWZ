## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a stark wake-up call to Xiao Chen and the reader alike. After a period of relative calm and cultivation progress, he is suddenly confronted by three never-before-seen dragon kings and then ambushed by four terrifyingly powerful cultivators from the so-called "Defeated Clan." The shift from discovery to torment is swift and brutal. Here, Xiao Chen experiences a level of power far beyond his own—a chilling lesson in the dangers of underestimating the reach and cunning of one's enemies. The chapter is not one of triumph but of harsh humiliation, and it sets the stage for a desperate struggle that will test Xiao Chen's resolve to its limits.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen returns to the God Temple ruins on the divine island, hoping to search further for treasures like the Suiren Fire-Drill. From Keke's animated gestures, he learns that she and the Little Defiant Dragon were captured by a mysterious "great demon king" who forced them to fight three unknown dragon kings. These three dragon kings are revealed to be descendants of ancient savage dragons that escaped the Dragon Island seal, making them immensely valuable—but they depart before Xiao Chen can interact. While examining the ruins, Xiao Chen is suddenly trapped by a powerful "domain" laid by four cultivators. They are all at the Spirit-Treasury realm, far above his Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven. They proceed to torture him, breaking his arms and shoulder blades, and humiliating him with contemptuous words. Xiao Chen recognizes from their taunts that they are avengers for younger family members he previously defeated. Despite the pain, he maintains his defiance, goading them by pointing out their age and lack of real talent, hoping to provoke them into revealing their identities or making a fatal mistake. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen still at their mercy, but his spirit unbroken.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the concept of a "domain" (域)—a spatial control technique used by high-level cultivators to trap enemies. It acts as a bounded field that suppresses movement and powers, making escape nearly impossible for a lower-realm opponent. The term "Spirit-Treasury" (识藏) is the second major cultivation realm, a significant leap from Mortal-Shedding. A single Spirit-Treasury expert can dominate multiple Mortal-Shedding cultivators, and four working together are virtually unstoppable for Xiao Chen. The "Defeated Clan" refers to families like the Mande family that Xiao Chen has publicly shamed. In the World of Immortality, family honor is paramount, and senior members will use underhanded methods to eliminate those who have humiliated their juniors, often without revealing their identities to avoid public backlash. This chapter also reveals that ancestral divine artifacts like the Suiren Fire-Drill may be found in scattered ruins across the Eternal Continent, emphasizing the deep history of this world.

## Reading Guide
This chapter builds tremendous tension as Xiao Chen faces his most dire situation yet. The sudden power imbalance teaches readers that the Eternal Continent holds dangers far beyond young geniuses. Pay close attention to Xiao Chen's taunts—they are not mere bravado but a calculated strategy to force his attackers into anger or carelessness. The mystery of the "great demon king" who captured Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon remains unresolved, and the three dragon kings could become either allies or future threats. The next chapter is likely to reveal the identities of the four attackers and whether Xiao Chen can turn the tables or be rescued. The reader should also consider the implications of these attacks for Xiao Chen's relationships with the Undying Sect and his other allies.
