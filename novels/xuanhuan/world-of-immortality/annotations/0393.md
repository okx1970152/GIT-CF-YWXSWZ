## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges into the heart of the kings' conflict beneath the ancient ruins. Keke, the snow-white divine beast, seizes the White Bone Sovereign Banner from the formidable Asura King, triggering a chain reaction of violence. Xiao Chen, still clad in his yellow-diamond skeleton, masterfully exploits the chaos, shouting misdirection and collecting the remains of fallen kings. The battlefield expands with the emergence of Golden Lion King, Five-Colored Peacock King, War God King, Blood Infant, and the giant Mao Nu. Amid the carnage, Xiao Chen retreats to the blood pool atop a skull altar, beginning a crucial cultivation session to refine the kings' life essence for his eventual rebirth. Keke stands guard, ensuring his solitude.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Asura King wielding the White Bone Sovereign Banner, only for Keke to snatch it away using Paradise Lost. Enraged, the Asura King unleashes his Asura Heavenly Realm, but Xiao Chen shouts that the king has swallowed the banner, turning the other kings against him. A melee erupts: the Golden Giant King is slain by the Asura King's divine chains, while the Golden Lion King and Five-Colored Peacock King renew their rivalry. Mao Nu, a dark-furred giant with power exceeding the golden bloodline, arrives and forces even the Perfected Man realm Asura King into the ground with a single blow. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen systematically collects the corpses of four fallen kings, invaluable materials for his flesh-and-blood rebirth. As the chaos deepens, he and Keke slip back into the underground palace, eliminate two pursuers, and settle at the blood-formed sacrificial altar. Xiao Chen begins to absorb the kings' blood essence, with Keke keeping watch—though the little beast soon distracts itself with spiritual fruits from Paradise Lost. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen immersed in cultivation, the storm above still raging.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The White Bone Sovereign Banner is a legendary artifact forged with bones of Ancestral Gods, enabling the wielder to command undead armies. Its theft by Keke highlights the beast's unique ability to bypass spatial protections using Paradise Lost. The Asura King operates at the Perfected Man (至人) realm, a tier above Yellow Diamond, demonstrating command over space and divine techniques like the Asura Heavenly Realm. King-level combatants in this world possess life essence that can be harvested—Xiao Chen's collection of corpses is a direct path to physical rebirth. The blood pool atop a skull altar is a potent resource for death-world beings; absorbing it strengthens the soul and bone body. The appearance of Mao Nu, a super-evolutionary giant of the dark-furred lineage, shows that hidden peaks of power exist beyond the known king hierarchies.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's cultivation at the blood pool promises a major step toward regaining a flesh-and-blood body, but he is interrupted—what forces will disturb him? The Asura King, though momentarily deceived, remains a deadly threat, and the White Bone Sovereign Banner's true owner may seek revenge. The chaos among the hundred races is far from over; the Golden Lion King and Peacock King's duel, the War God King's ongoing battle with the Blood Infant, and Mao Nu's rampage all set the stage for further escalation. Readers should watch for Xiao Chen's strategic deployment of the collected kings' remains and whether Keke can withstand the coming storm. The banner's power may also attract even greater beings.
