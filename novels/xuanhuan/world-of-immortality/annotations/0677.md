## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks the apocalyptic climax of the war between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld. The five Saint Ancestors of the Otherworld, ancient beings who were thought sealed beyond the True Realm, force their way through the collapsing blood altar and descend into the world. Their arrival triggers a cataclysmic conflict that engulfs all heavens and myriad worlds, leaving a trail of blood and sacrifice. The tone is one of tragic grandeur and desperate struggle, as heroes and villains alike fall in the grinding war machine. The fate of civilizations hangs in the balance, and every character is pushed to their breaking point, with many meeting their end in the chaos.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the five Saint Ancestors emerging from a crimson sacrifice platform, their bodies nearly shattered from the perilous journey. They immediately merge with their five Dao origin imprints, challenging the combined forces of Pangu, the Three Sovereigns, and the Five Emperors. The battle then erupts across all fronts, with Stone Kings clashing in the heavens and mortal armies colliding on earth. Xiao Chen, now a peerless Half‑Ancestor, fights his way through multiple Stone Kings, using the Five Emperor Tower and the Heavenly Stele to devastating effect. However, the war exacts a terrible toll: Keke’s mother is killed by the Otherworld ancestor Gud, the Stone Crow (an ancient calamity star) falls after slaying two kings, and the master of Tianwaitian and Renwairen is destroyed. The Human Demon Ge Qian, revealed as the Tenth Son of the Ancestral Dragon, unleashes his ultimate technique—the Heaven‑Abandoned, World‑Forsaken—annihilating two of the Otherworld’s twin Stone Kings. Meanwhile, Dugu Jianmo reappears after three civilization cycles, wielding his iron sword to save old friends, but even he cannot stem the tide. The chapter ends with Pangu summoning a nine‑fragmented Stone Man who reunites and dons the Heavenly Emperor City, signaling a new phase of the conflict.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ninety‑Nine Stone Steps, the sacred path leading to the Otherworld’s supreme powers, are central to this chapter. The five Saint Ancestors are the ultimate rulers of the Otherworld, comparable to the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors of the Nine Provinces—beings who have transcended ordinary cultivation and embody the highest Dao. The concept of the “original Stone Man” is introduced: a being whose body was split into nine parts, each a stone weapon or artifact, and who can be reassembled to gain near‑invincible power. The Heavenly Emperor City, a legendary artifact that rides atop the Stone Man, is a treasure that once belonged to the Little Stone Emperor. The Five Emperor Tower, which Xiao Chen wields, is one of the three greatest artifacts in the world, alongside the Three Sovereign Mirror and Five Emperor Tower. This chapter also highlights the tragic cost of the war, as even the most powerful beings, such as the Stone Crow (a being with the aura of a king-level Stone Man), can fall when overwhelmed by numbers.

## Reading Guide

Readers should brace for a relentless wave of casualties as the author spares no character. The descent of the five Saint Ancestors raises the stakes to the highest possible level, and the battle is far from over. The appearance of the reassembled Stone Man with the Heavenly Emperor City suggests that a trump card has been played by the Nine Provinces, but whether it can turn the tide remains uncertain. Xiao Chen’s own path is at a crossroads; he has not yet taken the final step of the Stone Man or the Upper Ancestral God path, but his combat prowess is now on par with Stone Kings. The next chapter will likely show the fate of this newly manifested Stone Man and whether the Saint Ancestors can be stopped. Readers should watch for hints of the ‘ancient enemy of the Five Emperors’ mentioned earlier, as this being may be the true orchestrator behind the Otherworld’s invasion.
