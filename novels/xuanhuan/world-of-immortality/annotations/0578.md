## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a profound elegy for the fallen heroes of the Nine Provinces. Xiao Chen, numb with grief, walks through a battlefield strewn with the remains of his friends and allies. After years of despair and wandering in the Death World, he achieves a spiritual breakthrough, reaching the War Ancestor realm—a state where his divine sense equals an Ancestral God. He returns to the Nine Provinces, gathers the broken corpses of his comrades, and scatters their ashes across the land in a solemn burial. Then he ventures to the Weapon Burial Valley to collect more War Swords, and witnesses a fierce battle where Keke's father, a member of the cursed Heaven-Defying Clan, fights alone against multiple Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Xiao Chen joins the fray, using his new powers to strike down enemies.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's utter despair after the devastating battle that annihilated the Nine Provinces' forces. He walks through the carnage, recognizing fragments of his friends: Yan Qingcheng, Niu Ren, Liu Mu, Jin Sanyi, Dugu Jianmo, Chen Fang, Wu Ming, and Keke, whose paw prints stain the broken warship. He spends seven years in despair, then eight years wandering the Death World. In the fifteenth year, his divine sense finally shatters and reforms, elevating him to the Ancestral God level, making him a full War Ancestor. He returns to the Nine Provinces, cuts away the battlefield, and scatters the blood-soaked earth over the land, burying the fallen. He then goes to the Weapon Burial Valley, where he collects one more War Sword, bringing his total to thirty-four. Upon returning, he witnesses Keke's father, a being of astonishing power, battling a host of Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Keke's father kills one Upper Ancestral God and declares his intention to massacre them all. Xiao Chen joins the fight, using his four killing weapons (Primordial Demon City, Ancient Scroll, Divine Diagram, and Heavenly Marks) to slay two enemy Ancestral Gods. The chapter ends with Keke's father still fighting in the Eternal Unknown, having killed a powerful foe.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The War Ancestor (战祖) realm is a unique cultivation path where a cultivator achieves Ancestral God-level divine sense and physical body but lacks full comprehension of the myriad worlds' source laws. This allows them to match Ancestral Gods in combat. Xiao Chen's breakthrough mirrors the cyclic nature of life and death in xianxia cosmology—his despair and grief catalyze his spiritual evolution. The Weapon Burial Valley (葬兵谷) is a legendary location where sentient weapons beyond the half-ancestor level reside. The Heaven-Defying Clan (逆天家族) is a cursed lineage that transformed from human to beast form, hunted by the Otherworld. Keke's father's use of the Six Paths of Reincarnation (六道轮回) is a supreme technique that grinds enemies across six worlds, a classic Daoist and Buddhist concept of cosmic cycles.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen has fully accepted his loss and transformed his grief into power, becoming a War Ancestor. His collection of thirty-four War Swords and the strengthening of his Divine Diagram set the stage for his return to the Celestial Realm. The appearance of Keke's father as a fearsome combatant introduces a powerful ally and reveals more about the cursed family's history. Readers should note that the Otherworld's Upper Ancestral Gods are now actively threatened; the balance is shifting. Xiao Chen's decision to scatter the remains across the Nine Provinces is a solemn act of closure, but his rage remains. Watch for how he will use his newfound strength to challenge the Otherworld's supreme powers and whether Keke's father will survive the fight.
