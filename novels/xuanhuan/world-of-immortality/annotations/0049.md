## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a decisive shift in Xiao Chen's survival strategy on Dragon Island. After witnessing the formation of a powerful alliance against him in Treefolk Valley, he chooses to strike back not with a full-scale battle, but with a symbolic blaze that signals both his defiance and his tactical cunning. The fire he sets is not an act of reckless rage but a calculated message: he is not prey to be hunted. The sudden, supernatural rain that quenches the flames, seemingly summoned by Dragon Island itself, adds an eerie layer to the island's mysteries. The tone is one of grim determination and strategic patience, contrasting Xiao Chen's cold efficiency with Zhao Lin'er's humiliated fury. Meanwhile, the closing revelation of the Companion Dragon King's discovery raises the stakes to a new level, promising chaos on the island.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, having observed the alliance forming in Treefolk Valley, resolves to deliver a warning before disappearing. He and his three skeleton servants set fire to the valley's periphery, using their Nether Darkflame to spread the blaze quickly through the oil-rich vegetation. The fire rages for an hour, destroying half the valley before a mysterious rainstorm extinguishes it. Zhao Lin'er immediately deduces Xiao Chen's involvement from the skeletons' claw marks, deepening her vendetta and turning the alliance's founding moment into a public humiliation. The incident becomes a scandal that erodes the alliance's credibility. Realizing the power plays are escalating toward a violent scramble for the Companion Dragon King, Xiao Chen makes a strategic decision: he will not compete for the legendary beast, but instead will hide and cultivate, waiting for others to find a way off the island. This decision marks a shift from immediate survival to long-term planning. The chapter also hints at greater forces at work: the rain's origin suggests Dragon Island itself may be sentient or governed by ancient formations that react to events, and the final dragon roars announce the discovery of the Companion Dragon King, a creature whose mere cry shakes the skies and terrifies all beasts.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces several layers of xianxia world-building. The Treefolk (or Tree-Men) are sentient ancient trees that have achieved humanoid form, born from valleys that produce Life Nectar, a substance of immense vitality. Their evolution is tied to the land's spiritual richness. One elder Treefolk is undergoing metamorphosis into a fully human-like form, hinting at a higher stage of cultivation for plant-based beings. The fire used by Xiao Chen, Nether Darkflame, is a special flame emitted by the skeletons' soul-flames—a type of yin fire that contrasts with normal flames. The sudden rain that extinguishes the fire is attributed to Dragon Island's own mysterious power, reinforcing the idea that this land has a will or ancient formation that reacts to events. The Companion Dragon King is introduced as a supreme dragon born from a dragon egg of one of the dragon king lineages, capable of rivaling ancient beasts like the Tyrant Dragon or Lion King Dragon even in infancy. The island's suppression of divine power makes such discoveries both dangerous and valuable.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's approach to Dragon Island's power games. He chooses withdrawal over confrontation, a decision that will define his arc for the coming chapters. Readers should watch for how his seclusion pays off—and what critical developments he may miss while hidden. The fire may have been a symbolic victory, but it solidifies Zhao Lin'er's hatred and motivates her to pursue him more aggressively. The mysterious rain hints that Dragon Island is more than a passive setting; it may act as a balancing force. The closing introduction of the Companion Dragon King sets the stage for the island's next major conflict. Will Xiao Chen be able to remain in hiding while such a prize is contested? The tension between his desire for safety and the pull of opportunity will be a key driver in the chapters ahead. Pay attention to how the alliances evolve and who will emerge as the true power players on Dragon Island.
