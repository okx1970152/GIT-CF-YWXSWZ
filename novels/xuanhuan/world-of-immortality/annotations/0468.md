## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers into a profound moral crisis as the half-ancestors confront the horrifying truth behind the entity they have been laboring to free. The corpse sea hides a being born from the forced merger of two legendary Twelve Zodiac War Ancestors—the Tiger and the Snake—now corrupted by Otherworld Ancestral Gods and renamed Mo Xiaowang. The half-ancestors must decide whether to continue freeing a potential cataclysm or face annihilation themselves. The tension is palpable, only to be shattered by the sudden, awe-inspiring appearance of the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, carrying a figure of such purity that the very essence of the corpse sea is purified. This chapter balances grim revelation with mythic wonder.

## Key Plot Points

After days of exhausting effort, Babra finally extracts the formation flag from the fifth island, revealing a jade box sealed with otherworld runes containing what is hinted to be the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd. More critically, the island holds a stone tablet inscribed with the true nature of the being in the corpse sea: the Tiger War Ancestor and the Snake War Ancestor were forcibly merged into a single consciousness, their memories partially erased and replaced with a killing intent, intended as a weapon against the Nine Provinces and the Four Worlds. The half-ancestors, horrified by this revelation, refuse to proceed, declaring they will not be instruments of doom. Mo Xiaowang, angered, threatens to slaughter them one by one, summoning the spectral forms of the tiger and snake. 

In the climactic moment, the sky shatters as the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge tears through space, connecting the corpse sea to the outside world. A figure shrouded in blinding sacred light travels the bridge from tens of thousands of miles away in an instant. His mere presence purifies the corpse sea, dispersing all deathly energy and filling the realm with holiness. He confronts Mo Xiaowang, speaking cryptic words about dreams and reality before the world seals shut, leaving the half-ancestors in stunned awe. The bridge withdraws, and the corpse sea vanishes, leaving behind a lingering sense of sacred power and unanswered questions.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Twelve Zodiac War Ancestors (十二生肖战祖) are a unique class of beings in the World of Immortality. Although not all are true Ancestral Gods, their combat power rivals that of the highest deities, making them formidable weapons in the ancient war against Otherworld gods. Their birth was limited to fewer than ten before a cataclysmic battle. The concept of a being formed by merging two such titans into a single entity—with deliberately implanted killing intent—illustrates the ruthless methodology of Otherworld Ancestral Gods. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge is a legendary artifact that appears in times of great need, often associated with transcendent beings. Its sudden appearance here signals intervention from a power beyond half-ancestors, perhaps an Ancestral God or even a figure from a higher realm. The sacred figure's ability to purify the corpse sea with sheer presence underscores the ultimate disparity between the mundane and the divine.

## Reading Guide

This chapter raises several crucial questions: Who is the mysterious figure on the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge? His words—“A smile fades, everything turns to emptiness”—hint at a transcendent understanding of reality, perhaps Zhuangzi or another Daoist sage. Will Mo Xiaowang remain sealed, or has the bridge's intervention only delayed his release? The half-ancestors now know the truth and cannot unlearn it; their loyalty is now deeply conflicted. Babra's role as the key to the seals also remains a point of tension. Readers should watch for the reappearance of the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge and the consequences of the half-ancestors' decision. The moral stakes have been raised, and the next chapters will likely explore the aftermath of this revelation and the identity of the savior who appeared to restore balance.
