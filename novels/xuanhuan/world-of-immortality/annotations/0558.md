## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen ventures deeper into the cursed land and encounters a living but utterly decayed stone man, a former king of the Otherworld. Trapped beneath the ruins of a giant palace for countless ages, this once supreme being is reduced to a crumbling wreck. Yet even in his dying moments, he retains a fragment of his ancient arrogance and power. He attempts to forcibly imprint his legacy onto Xiao Chen through a self-destructive act, seeking to ensure his lineage and techniques survive through an unwilling vessel. After this tense encounter, Xiao Chen discovers a broken time-space channel guarded by a warning stone that points toward the celestial realm. The chapter balances visceral horror with strategic calculation, as Xiao Chen must decide how to handle the unwanted inheritance and whether to trust the forbidden path ahead.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen discovers a stone man's hand emerging from beneath the palace foundation, only to find the being is nearly decayed to dust. The stone man, a former Otherworld ruler, recognizes Xiao Chen as a descendant of his enemies and initially treats him with contempt. Xiao Chen responds by using the divine diagram to shatter the stone man's shoulder, demonstrating his superior position. The stone man then deliberately destroys his own remaining skull and body, releasing a final blast of yellow light that penetrates the divine diagram. Despite Xiao Chen's efforts to block it, the stone man's divine image and legacy force themselves into Xiao Chen's mind, leaving an indelible imprint. The stone man claims that only his descendants can cultivate this legacy, but Xiao Chen is skeptical and sees potential value in mastering it himself. After the stone man's complete annihilation, Xiao Chen explores further and finds a chaotic time-space channel. He deduces it may be an unfinished passage and begins to traverse it, eventually encountering a broken stone stele inscribed with ancient characters warning that the path leads to the celestial realm—a destination that sparks both curiosity and caution.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The stone man (石人) represents a pinnacle of physical cultivation in the world of immortality, where a being refines their flesh and bones into an indestructible stone-like substance. The 'stone man king' is a rank above ordinary stone men, comparable to an Ancestral God in combat power. The concept of forcibly imprinting one's legacy onto another—through self-destruction and mental projection—is a recurring xianxia trope, often used to ensure a lineage or technique survives despite the practitioner's death. The cursed land (废土) is a remnant of a destroyed civilization, filled with deadly blood light and resentful spirits, making it a classic 'forbidden zone' in cultivation stories. The broken time-space channel and the warning stone allude to the layered cosmology of the World of Immortality, where realms like the celestial realm (天界) are sealed off and accessible only through dangerous paths.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's new mental imprint raises important questions: Can he truly unlock the stone man king's legacy, or is there a trap? The stone man's claim that only his descendants can use it may hint at a bloodline restriction or hidden safeguard. Readers should watch for whether Xiao Chen attempts to study this legacy and whether it conflicts with his own Heavenly Stele-based cultivation. The appearance of the forbidden passage to the celestial realm sets up a major future journey. Given the warning and the chaotic nature of the channel, this path will likely lead to dangerous encounters or revelations about the upper realms. This chapter also marks a shift in Xiao Chen's power, as he now carries knowledge from a former Otherworld ruler—a potential double-edged sword.
