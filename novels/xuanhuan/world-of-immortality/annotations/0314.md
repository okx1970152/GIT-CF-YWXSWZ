## Chapter Overview

This chapter brings together the full might of the half-ancestor level powers from both the Eternal Continent and the Cultivation World as they converge on the Primordial Ancient Village. The atmosphere is tense and portentous: the Yellow Clay Altar and the ancient village hold a secret so terrifying that even half-ancestors fear for their survival. Xiao Chen remains a silent, scrutinized figure at the center, while the half-ancestors debate sealing the site. The chapter balances epic scale with introspective stillness, as Xiao Chen undergoes a profound cultivation transformation through the winter, breaking into the fiery Nirvana realm just as the new year dawns.

## Key Plot Points

The Three-Infant Grand Matriarch arrives with Ye Tian and confronts the Sun Knight over the death of Ye Tian's sister, revealing that Xiao Chen's hands bear the bloodstains. Ye Tian vows revenge but is struck down by the matriarch. The matriarch coldly explains that half-ancestors are united against Xiao Chen; only sealing the Yellow Clay Altar can protect him. She reveals the dire consequences of the village's secret. White Tiger Saint Emperor descends, confirming that earlier attempts to seal the site failed due to concealment by two powerful mortal-realm cultivators (Renwai Ren and Tianwai Tian). Original and Tongtian admit they failed to kill them; Tongtian's fury is palpable. More half-ancestors arrive: Zhunti, Jieyin, Da Yu, Hou Yi, and the Twelve Golden Immortals. Xing Tian and Chi You appear, with Chi You accepting that he may die. He tells Xiao Chen not to listen to the matriarch but to follow his own heart. The half-ancestors predict a great conflict within three years. Most depart, leaving Xiao Chen alone in the village with the sleeping villagers. Xiao Chen enters deep cultivation, meditating on the sound from the Yellow Clay Altar. Through autumn and winter, he crosses into the Nirvana realm, having passed the first heaven's death tribulation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Three-Infant Grand Matriarch's cultivation method of cultivating three golden pellets to birth three nascent souls parallels the Daoist principle of 'One Qi transforms into Three Purities' but is more sustained. She is a rare female powerhouse equal to the Blue Lotus Celestial Maiden. The Nirvana realm is the fourth great realm in the World of Immortality's cultivation system, characterized by teetering between life and death. Xiao Chen's months of motionless, death-like cultivation was a manifestation of Nirvana's first death tribulation. The Yellow Clay Altar is a mysterious relic from the primordial age, possibly connected to the Ancestral Gods. The half-ancestors' fear suggests it may contain power to kill even them. The 'two people half-sealed in the mortal world' likely refer to Renwai Ren and Tianwai Tian, who concealed the altar's true nature.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major countdown: a great calamity within three years that will threaten even half-ancestors. The Primordial Ancient Village and the Yellow Clay Altar are clearly central to what is to come. Xiao Chen's breakthrough to Nirvana marks a significant milestone, but his status as a target for the half-ancestors has escalated. Keep an eye on how he navigates their hostility. The matriarch's claim that Xiao Chen killed Ye Tian's sister is a lingering conflict from earlier events; it may resurface. The alliance of half-ancestors, despite their internal rivalries, signals a crisis of world-shaking proportion. Watch for Xiao Chen's role in the unfolding disaster.
