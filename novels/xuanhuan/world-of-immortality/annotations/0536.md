## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers an apocalyptic battle in the Curse Realm, where a primordial formation turns the entire realm into a weapon against eight Otherworld Ancestral Gods. Despite the formation's devastating power, three Supreme Ancestral Gods intervene with legendary artifacts, rescuing most of their kin. However, one Ancestral God is slain, marking a significant loss. The survivors flee into a black lake leading to the Death World, where they perform a dark sacrifice to power their passage. A terrifying, mindless Yellow‑Earth Giant pursues them, only to be confronted by a mysterious half‑petrified being wielding a stone spear in a ruined city beneath the Nether River. The chapter blends cosmic horror with mythic grandeur, advancing the desperate struggle of the Nine Provinces' last defenders.

## Key Plot Points
The Curse Realm's great formation activates, sanctifying all matter into killing weapons. Eight Otherworld Ancestral Gods are injured, and one is killed by an unknown attack within the formation's black mist. The three Supreme Ancestral Gods—wielding the Demon Blade, Immortal Heavenly Shield, and Thirty‑Three‑Story Divine Tower—retrieve the survivors. A yellow‑earth giant with a petrified head attacks a Heavenly Stele in a black lake, then pursues Xiao Chen's group as they enter a black‑hole passage. Inside, they sacrifice a captured Ancestral God's flesh and blood to fuel their escape. They emerge in the Death World's Nether River, beneath which lies a ruined city. At its center stands a stone spear. A horrifying being—half‑petrified, half‑decayed skeleton—climbs from a well and seizes the spear, engaging the yellow‑earth giant in a brutal, world‑shaking duel. The battle suggests deeper secrets: the stone spear resonates with the half‑skull Xiao Chen carries, and the yellow‑earth giant appears to be a mindless weapon of the Otherworld. The chapter ends with the two colossi locked in combat, leaving the fate of Xiao Chen's party uncertain as they observe from a distance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter expands the cosmology of the "Four Realms" by featuring the Curse Realm, one of the four great worlds alongside the Martial Realm, Qi Realm, and Soul Realm. The great formation that sanctifies all matter into weapons is a classic trope of xianxia: a ritual array that turns an entire domain into a killing field, capable of harming even Ancestral Gods. The concept of "Supreme Ancestral God" introduces a tier above ordinary Ancestral Gods, beings of near‑absolute power who command legendary artifacts like the Demon Blade and Immortal Heavenly Shield. The yellow‑earth giant, with a petrified head and body of clay, evokes the "Stone Man" cultivation path—a rare, body‑hardening technique that leads to near‑immortality but may degrade intelligence. The mysterious half‑petrified being from the well resembles a failed or incomplete Stone Man, hinting at ancient experiments or battles.

## Reading Guide
Readers should pay close attention to the ruined city and the stone spear—both are clearly significant artifacts, possibly connected to the Stone Man and the War Swords. The half‑petrified being's appearance raises questions about the origin of the yellow‑earth giant and the true nature of the "Stone Man" path. Xiao Chen's half‑skull may be a key to understanding these relics. The sacrifice of the captured Ancestral God foreshadows the desperate measures the defenders will take. The chapter sets the stage for an upcoming confrontation with the Supreme Ancestral Gods, who now suspect the Nine Provinces hide greater power. The battle between the two giants is likely a setup for Xiao Chen to acquire the stone spear or learn its secrets. The introduction of the Curse Realm's formation also suggests that the other realms may have similar hidden weapons.
