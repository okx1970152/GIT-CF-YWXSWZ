## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges deeper into the heart of Dragon Island's ancient city, where the precarious balance between life and death is shattered by the intervention of a Lion King Dragon. The beast's golden radiance clears the immediate threat of demonic spirits, but it also heralds the arrival of far more terrifying forces: ghost soldiers and heavenly soldiers, marching with ancient, unwavering purpose. Amidst the chaos, a mysterious woman veiled in colored mist emerges, accompanied by the Purple Dragon King—the first companion dragon king discovered on the island. Her unexpected bond with the draconic prince offers a fragile hope to the trapped cultivators, steering them toward the very heart of the city's mystery. The chapter crackles with desperate flight and mythic dread, as Xiao Chen and the others must trust a stranger and a legendary beast to survive the onslaught of the dead.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with a Lion King Dragon, a creature of golden light, effortlessly dispatching a thirty-meter-tall demon and driving off the skeletal birds that menaced the cultivators. This display of power underscores the hierarchy of beings in the city. However, the dragon's attention soon shifts, and the survivors are confronted by a disciplined army of ghost soldiers and heavenly soldiers, their eyes clear and menacing. Xiao Chen notes that these are far stronger than the individual ghost soldiers they had encountered earlier. The group flees through the ancient streets, but more threats close in: skeletal birds regroup, black-misted evil spirits pour from side alleys, and the central divine stele trembles, causing doors of ancient houses to creak open, hinting at even greater horrors. Just as despair sets in, a young Purple Dragon King appears, flying toward a mysterious woman in colored mist. To everyone's shock, the dragon king nuzzles her affectionately, revealing she has befriended it rather than subdued it. The woman, speaking with a celestial voice, leads the group. The dragon king guides them toward the area of the divine stele, a region so dangerous that most evil spirits halt their pursuit. Xiao Chen, ever analytical, observes the woman's behavior and finds it familiar, while the others question how such a bond could be formed without force. The chapter ends with the group approaching the vicinity of the divine stele, where other beings, including Buddha's Dharma wheel and bronze trigrams, are still assailing it, and numerous barbarian dragons circle above.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Ghost soldiers (阴兵) and heavenly soldiers (天兵) are classic elements in Chinese mythology, representing the armies of the underworld and the celestial realm, respectively. Here, they are depicted as ancient, disciplined warriors with clear intelligence, unlike mindless undead. The Purple Dragon King (紫龙王) is a companion dragon king (伴生龙王), a supreme dragon born alongside the Ancestral Dragon, destined to be a rival or successor. Its appearance here is significant because it represents untamed power that has chosen to ally with a human not through combat, but through friendship—a rare concept in the ruthless cultivation world, mirroring the earlier bond between Xiao Chen and the celestial horse Keke. The mysterious woman's philosophy of treating the dragon as a friend rather than a tool reflects a deeper, more harmonious path of cultivation that contrasts with the aggressive greed of other practitioners.

## Reading Guide

This chapter introduces a pivotal new character whose identity remains shrouded. Readers should watch for clues about the mysterious woman: her unordinary bond with the Purple Dragon King, her gentle yet authoritative tone when rebuking the swordsman Dugu Jianmo, and Xiao Chen's sense of familiarity. The dynamic between her and the dragon king may offer a new perspective on coexisting with divine beasts. Additionally, the approaching conflict at the divine stele sets the stage for major revelations about Dragon Island's secrets. The ghost soldiers and heavenly soldiers add a layer of military undead to the city's horrors, raising questions about who commands them. Xiao Chen's concern for Keke hints that the little beast may reappear soon, possibly with a role to play in this escalating crisis.
