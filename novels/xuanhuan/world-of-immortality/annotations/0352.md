## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges into the desolate world of the dead, where Xiao Chen, having traded purple diamond skeletons to accelerate his evolution, is ambushed by the Seven Kills City Master and his minions. After a narrow escape using his specially crafted divine wings, he resumes his solitary hunt for fire seed beings, needing only one more to complete his current transformation. The narrative shifts from evasion to active predation under a moonlit wasteland. The tension culminates in a duel with a red diamond skeleton who calls himself a wandering poet—a being whose personality and memories suggest he is far more than a mere undead. The stakes are personal: this encounter may reveal a connection to the lost world of the Nine Provinces, bridging the gulf between the living and the dead.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen departs Seven Kills City after an ambush by Du Si and five other purple diamond skeletons working for the city master. He kills Du Si with a swift decapitation, then exposes his divine purple wings to escape the flying Seven Kills City Master—a shocking revelation that the city master can fly without wings, forcing Xiao Chen to reconsider his intelligence. He continues his hunt across the barren plains, capturing twenty-nine fire seeds from same-tier foes through battles in three ancient castles. Then he encounters a red diamond skeleton standing alone on a rocky hill—a lone hunter like himself. The duel is intense and evenly matched, with both wielding bone blades, but Xiao Chen lands a decisive blow, breaking the skeleton's chest bone. The red diamond skeleton flees, begging and bargaining in a flamboyant, almost theatrical manner, claiming he can provide many fire seeds and even offers to be Xiao Chen's disciple. Their chase continues until Xiao Chen learns that this peculiar undead recalls a past life and claims to be from the Nine Provinces. The chapter ends with this revelation, setting up a profound link between the world of the dead and the mortal homeland.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The world of the dead in *World of Immortality* operates on a unique logic. Fire seed beings (火种生物) are the local equivalent of cultivators; they evolve by absorbing the concentrated soul-fire (神火) of their peers. Bone color indicates rank—white, purple, red, and beyond—each tier representing a significant leap in power and intellect. The fact that some beings retain memories of past lives hints at a cycle of reincarnation that transcends both the mortal and dead worlds, a concept that will become crucial later. Xiao Chen's purple wings were crafted by God Race elders and inscribed with ancient formations granting supreme speed—a rare asset in a realm where flight is uncommon. The Nine Provinces (九州) is the cultural heartland of the mortal world, now a lost homeland that echoes even in death. The flamboyant red diamond skeleton's self-identification as a "wandering poet" suggests a degree of self-awareness and individuality that challenges the stereotype of mindless undead.

## Reading Guide

This chapter introduces a character of great potential: the red diamond poet Chang Sheng. His flamboyant personality and claim of past-life memory from the Nine Provinces make him a unique figure in the dead world. Readers should watch for whether he tells the truth and what role he might play in Xiao Chen's quest to gather thirty fire seeds and evolve further. The ambush by the Seven Kills faction also plants seeds for future conflict—Xiao Chen has now marked these cities as future hunting grounds for rapid advancement. Additionally, the reference to God Race elders and the divine wings deepens world-building; the God Race appears to be an ally with hidden capabilities. The next chapter will likely continue the chase or the conversation, and may reveal more about the connection between the dead world and the living one. The tension between Xiao Chen's cold efficiency and the poet's theatrical survival strategy promises engaging dynamics ahead.
