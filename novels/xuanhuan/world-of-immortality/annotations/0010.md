## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen, having been captured by Wang Zifeng and Liu Yue, endures their contempt as they roost on a towering cliff. The night brings a terrifying presence—an ancient beast whose roar shakes the very mountain and disorients all who hear it. In that fleeting window of vulnerability, Xiao Chen strikes with savage precision, killing Liu Yue instantly and maiming Wang Zifeng. This chapter is a brutal showcase of Xiao Chen's survival instinct: he is not a hero waiting for rescue but a predator who exploits every opening. The subsequent chase through the jungle and the cliffside duel highlight the unforgiving nature of Dragon Island, where hesitation invites death and mercy is a luxury no one can afford.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter begins with the trio deciding to camp on a hundred-meter cliff to avoid the nocturnal dangers of the primeval forest. As darkness falls, a massive ancient beast—the same one that once fought the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon—roars in the distance, its voice splitting the clouds and shaking the cliff. Wang Zifeng and Liu Yue quickly seal their hearing, momentarily distracted by the overwhelming sound. Xiao Chen, who had been silently waiting for the perfect moment, acts immediately. He drives his hand like a blade into Liu Yue's back, then snatches her sword and severs her head in one clean stroke. Spinning, he cuts off Wang Zifeng's right arm before the latter can fully react. Wang Zifeng, though grievously wounded, hurls a scroll artifact that explodes with deadly radiance, forcing Xiao Chen to jump off the cliff to escape. Both survive the fall into the dense jungle. Wang Zifeng, blood-soaked and enraged, pursues. In the ensuing melee, his palm technique shatters Xiao Chen's sword, leaving him with only the hilt. Unfazed, Xiao Chen pushes forward with both palms, unleashing a blinding light that engulfs the forest. The chapter ends with the two locked in a final, decisive clash. This sequence establishes Xiao Chen's tactical ruthlessness—he sacrifices the chance to gloat in favor of swift, efficient murder. It also introduces the scroll artifact as a high-level trump card, hinting at the broader martial culture of the Otherworld. The cliffside duel reinforces the brutal reality of Dragon Island: strength alone is not enough; one must also seize the moment.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the concept of "scroll artifacts" (卷轴), one-time-use magical tools that contain pre-sealed energy attacks. In xianxia lore, such artifacts are common among wealthy or well-connected disciples, serving as life-saving trump cards. Wang Zifeng's scroll emits a devastating red light, nearly killing Xiao Chen despite his higher realm. This demonstrates that even a weaker cultivator can threaten a stronger one with the right equipment. The ancient beast that roars in the distance is a primordial monster—a remnant from Dragon Island's prehistoric age. Its roar is so powerful that it disrupts the senses and shakes the earth, a common trope in cultivation novels used to emphasize the vast gap between mortal and primal power. Xiao Chen's cold-blooded killing of Liu Yue, a beautiful woman, underscores a key xianxia theme: in the path of cultivation, attachment and sentiment are weaknesses. Beauty and gender offer no protection. The chapter also highlights the importance of opportunistic timing; Xiao Chen's success depends entirely on exploiting a momentary lapse of attention.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a fundamental shift in Xiao Chen's approach: from passive survival to proactive elimination. The scroll artifact scene sets up an important world-building element—magical tools can level the playing field. Readers should remember that such artifacts exist; they may appear again in the hands of future enemies. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen and Wang Zifeng locked in a life-or-death struggle, leaving the outcome uncertain. The next chapter will likely resolve this duel and reveal whether Xiao Chen can eliminate this threat before reinforcements arrive. One intriguing detail is the ancient beast's roar—it not only provided the distraction but also hints at the terrifying depths of Dragon Island's wilderness. The beast itself may become a future plot point. Additionally, Wang Zifeng's scroll came from his sect; this implies that the Disciples of the Evil King carry dangerous tools, setting up potential future conflicts with that organization. The chapter reinforces the law of the jungle: trust is a liability, and only the paranoid survive.
