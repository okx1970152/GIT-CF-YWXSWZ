## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen ventures into the lair of a pair of ancient winged dragons, a journey that begins with a false accusation of theft and ends in a profound exchange. The dragon couple request his help in passing down legendary battle techniques to their headstrong child, offering in return invaluable guidance on the Undying Wings technique—a cornerstone of the Undying Sect's power. What follows is a masterclass in negotiation set against a backdrop of glittering treasure, leading to a cultivation breakthrough that propels Xiao Chen to the Eighth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding and brings the Undying Wings to near-completion.

## Key Plot Points

The winged dragon couple reveal they have been watching Xiao Chen since his return from Dragon Island, and their request involves the little defiant dragon—a creature whose hidden potential they clearly recognize. Xiao Chen learns that mastering the Undying Wings requires opening a staggering eighty-one 'Flying Acupoints' (飞天穴) along the spine, a detail absent from the sect's terse scriptures. With their thousand years of experience, the dragons provide the missing context, allowing Xiao Chen to bypass years of blind probing. 

Later, in a solitary cultivation session, Xiao Chen achieves a remarkable double breakthrough: first into the Eighth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding, then nearly completing the Undying Wings as the final acupoints surge open. The breakthrough is accompanied by a physical fragrance and a sense of bodily refinement, marking a significant leap in both power and comprehension. Crucially, Xiao Chen reaffirms his commitment to his original cultivation method—the Heavenly Stele Profound Art—treating other techniques as mere skills rather than fundamental paths.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Undying Wings (不死天翼) exemplify the Xianxia trope of a 'sect-defining technique' that is nearly impossible to master without personal instruction. The eighty-one 'Flying Acupoints' are not part of standard acupoint charts, reflecting the esoteric nature of high-level cultivation arts. The winged dragons' ability to study and adapt human techniques over a millennium underscores the porous boundary between species in the World of Immortality—dragons are not mere beasts but intelligent beings with their own cultivation traditions. The mention of 'pair cultivation' (阴阳合气) as a minor note hints at the broader Daoist principle of harmonizing opposites, though here it serves the dragons' playful threat.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point for Xiao Chen's mobility: with the Undying Wings nearly complete, his combat options will expand dramatically. The little defiant dragon's growing reputation among ancient beings like the winged dragon couple raises its narrative stakes—is it a future Ancestral Dragon, or something else? The breakthrough to Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven brings him closer to the Spirit-Treasury realm and the legendary powers of the Dao. Watch for how Xiao Chen balances his new techniques with his core cultivation, and whether the dragon couple's request will lead to deeper involvement with the little defiant dragon's fate.
