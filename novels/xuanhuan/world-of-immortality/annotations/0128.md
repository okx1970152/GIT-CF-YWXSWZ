## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a seismic lore revelation, unveiling the true identity of the terrifying Devil that has haunted Dragon Island. Through the three sets of shed skin brought by Xiao Chen, the Undying Sect Leader recognizes them as the mortal remains of the legendary founder of the Demon Sect—a figure even more ancient and powerful than the sect's own ancestor, the Evil King. The chapter balances mythic weight with domestic comedy as Keke, the mischievous white beast, pilfers the sect's most sacred cultivation manual, the Undying Demon Art. Xiao Chen finds himself in an uneasy sanctuary: protected by the sect leader out of reverence for the Demon Sect founder, yet targeted by internal factional enemies. The stage is set for a quiet period of cultivation overshadowed by hidden dangers and a stolen treasure that could change everything.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen presents the three Devil skins to the Undying Sect Leader, who becomes visibly shaken. The leader leads him to a secluded hall where a painting of a serene elderly man hangs—identical to the Devil's aged form. The painting was drawn by the Evil King himself, depicting the founder of the once-great Demon Sect from the Mortal World. The Demon Sect founder created techniques like the Shattering Demon Seed and Divine and the Heavenly Demon Art, and vanished from history millenia ago. Now it is revealed he was trapped on Dragon Island, escaping only as the Devil we know. The Undying Sect Leader, out of respect for this founder and Xiao Chen's connection to him, decides not to punish Xiao Chen for Wang Hao's death, instead granting him sanctuary within the sect. However, internal strife arises when Qin Changfeng, father of the deceased Qin Yi, returns and demands justice. Xiao Chen is officially confined to a scenic area for one year, which he accepts as a chance for undisturbed cultivation. In a lighter yet tense scene, Keke presents Xiao Chen with a stolen copy of the Undying Demon Art, the sect's most guarded secret, leaving him both awed and alarmed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Demon Sect (魔教) is introduced as the original "number one sect under heaven" from the Mortal World, later splintering into many lineages. The Undying Sect is one such branch, founded by the Evil King (邪王) who inherited part of the Demon Sect's teachings before creating his own Undying Demon Art. The Demon Sect founder's techniques, such as the Shattering Demon Seed and Divine (碎魔种神), represent a pinnacle of ancient martial creativity, emphasizing the theme that true masters create rather than merely inherit. The reverence shown by the Undying Sect Leader toward the founder's painting reflects the deep cultural importance of lineage and origin in Xianxia—even centuries later, the founder's authority remains unchallenged. The Undying Demon Art is a forbidden manual, and Keke's theft of it is both a grave transgression and a potential game-changer for Xiao Chen, who now holds the key to a legendary cultivation path.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a transition from the constant action of Dragon Island to a more politically charged and introspective phase on the mainland. Xiao Chen now possesses a stolen treasure that could provoke even more enemies, but also offers a shortcut to power. Readers should watch for how the Undying Sect handles the internal conflict between the sect leader's protection and Qin Changfeng's thirst for revenge. The fate of the Demon Sect founder—the Devil—remains unresolved; will he complete his rebirth and return to the world? Keke's theft hints at her growing intelligence and loyalty, but also her reckless disregard for consequences. Xiao Chen's decision to either use or hide the Undying Demon Art will shape his cultivation direction. The groundwork is laid for alliances, betrayals, and the opening of a new chapter in Xiao Chen's journey.
