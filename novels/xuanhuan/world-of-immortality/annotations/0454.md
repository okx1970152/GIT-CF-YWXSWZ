## Chapter Overview

This chapter balances the epic aftermath of a Half-Ancestor's ascension with intimate vengeance. Lan Nuo's rise to the Half-Ancestor realm and forging of the Wisdom Sword stands as a pivotal cosmic event, but Xiao Chen's return to the Nine Provinces shifts the focus to a personal vendetta that spans a century and a half. The Tiger clan's cruel bait—an unnamed woman used to trap him—draws Xiao Chen into a ruthless assault on Tiger Castle, where he cuts down experts without mercy. Meanwhile, Jin Sanyi's comic antics with a captured Western holy figure provide a jarring but welcome counterpoint, reminding us that even in a world of gods and Half-Ancestors, human mischief endures.

## Key Plot Points

Lan Nuo completes her ascension to Half-Ancestor after absorbing the essence of thousands of stars, forging the Wisdom Sword in the sun's core. Xiao Chen's brutal outer-heavens battle with the Asura Half-Ancestor and Skeleton King ends inconclusively; both sides suffer severe wounds, with Xiao Chen saved by his Ancestral God War Armor but his stone giant destroyed. He recovers in the Death World's God Village among allied sovereign kings. Upon returning to the mortal world, Xiao Chen flies west to Tiger Castle, the Tiger clan's fortress. He demands the release of a woman used as bait over 150 years ago—a plot echoing earlier schemes. When his demand is deflected, he slaughters dozens of guards, including five Fish-Leap realm experts and a Perfected Man elder. The Tiger Second Elder triggers a vial of Ancestral God Corpse Poison, a suicidal trap that forces Xiao Chen to shatter a sealed wall and escape. The elder himself dissolves into corpse fluid. Xiao Chen then declares coldly, "I will kill until you tremble," and turns his gaze toward the Sun God Church. In parallel, Jin Sanyi and Sha Polang entertain themselves by extorting a captured Western holy figure—a chubby, benevolent deity reminiscent of Santa Claus—demanding gifts for friends and an introduction to Cupid's arrows. Their antics provide dark comedy against the grim violence.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Ancestral God Corpse Poison (祖神尸毒) is one of the deadliest substances in the world, derived from the decaying body of an Ancestral God. Even a wisp can kill Half-Ancestors. The Tiger elder's willingness to use it shows the depth of their desperation. Xiao Chen's Ancestral God War Armor, obtained from the Death World sovereign, likely saved him from worse injuries. The Half-Ancestor realm marks a being on the cusp of Ancestral Godhood, commanding cosmic authority. The captured Western deity—called "Santa" or "Christmas old man"—is a lighthearted reference, showing the novel's syncretic mythological worldbuilding, where figures from various pantheons coexist.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's turning gaze toward the Sun God Church preludes a major confrontation with the church's ancient battle formation and Half-Ancestor allies. The woman used as bait remains a mystery; her identity and fate could reveal older conspiracies. Jin Sanyi's quest for Cupid's arrows may lead to amusing entanglements or unexpected alliances. The Ancestral God Corpse Poison is now a known weapon in the enemy's arsenal, likely to reappear. With Lan Nuo now a Half-Ancestor and the Tiger clan reeling, the power balance in the Nine Provinces is shifting. Watch for the tiger's hidden masters to surface.
