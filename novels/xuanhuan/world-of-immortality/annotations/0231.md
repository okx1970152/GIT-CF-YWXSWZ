## Chapter Overview
The chapter returns to Yindu's Moon Lake, where the prestigious Book Sword Tea Meeting has gathered the continent's most talented young elites. The recent exploits of Dugu Jianmo have set the stage for a new confrontation. Chu Xingkuang, one of the legendary Four Talents of Yindu, returns and vows to cut down Dugu Jianmo in eighty moves, but his attention soon shifts to a mysterious outsider introduced as Xiao Shishui. The atmosphere crackles with tension as the crowd of nobles, princesses, and warriors watches the unfolding drama. This chapter blends martial spectacle with political maneuvering and introduces a fresh wave of conflict from the deadly Tiger clan of the Central Plains.

## Key Plot Points
At the Moon Lake gathering, Dugu Jianmo's silent testimony elevates Xiao Shishui above the assembled elites, claiming that he is even stronger than the peerless swordsman himself. Chu Xingkuang, prideful and contemptuous, publicly dismisses the Southern Wastes and declares he will behead this foreign challenger. Xiao Chen, under his alias, initially refuses combat, but Chu Xingkuang forces the issue with a sudden attack. A single use of the Lingxi Sword Wave—a forty-meter ring of destruction—instantly repels Chu Xingkuang and destroys his sleeve, shocking the onlookers. The brief exchange showcases Xiao Chen's terrifying power and mastery of rare techniques.

The duel escalates as Xiao Chen deploys the Fearless Lion Seal, manifesting a phantom of the three-headed Golden Lion King, and then the Unmoving Wisdom King Seal, a towering avatar that smashes through Chu Xingkuang's defenses. However, the fight is interrupted by a military dispatch, and before Chu Xingkuang can resume, two members of the fearsome Tiger clan—Hu Zitong and Hu Zifeng—intervene. They reveal their grudge against the true identity behind Xiao Shishui: the original Xiao Chen who killed their kin three years ago. With cold ruthlessness, Xiao Chen annihilates Hu Zitong with the Lingxi Sword Wave and the Divine Charm Flower Seal, reducing his body to a bloody mist. Hu Zifeng charges in only to meet the same fate. The crowd erupts in shock, and even Chu Xingkuang is forced to reassess the situation, realizing the trouble this will bring from the Tiger clan's powerful elder lurking in the city.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Book Sword Tea Meeting is a traditional gathering where young cultivators showcase their martial arts and literary refinement, often serving as a stage for political alignment and reputation building. The Four Talents of Yindu are the city's most celebrated young fighters, and Chu Xingkuang's haughty demeanor reflects the entrenched arrogance of the Central Plains nobility toward the so-called barbaric Southern Wastes. The Tiger clan of the Central Plains is a fearsome lineage said to carry the blood of the White Tiger, a sacred beast. Their members are known for their devastating lightning abilities and defensive white tiger avatars. Xiao Chen's repertoire of seals—the Fearless Lion, Unmoving Wisdom King, and Divine Charm Flower—are drawn from Buddhist and ancient forest spirituality, each embodying a specific essence: overwhelming power, immovable defense, and elegant destruction.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a significant turn in the Yindu arc, as Xiao Chen's alias is shattered and the Tiger clan becomes a direct enemy. The killing of Hu Zitong and Hu Zifeng will surely draw the attention of the legendary 'Old Tiger' in the city—a half-step demigod whose wrath could end Xiao Chen's mission early. Readers should watch for how Chu Xingkuang and other factions react to this bloodshed. The interruption of the duel by military news hints at larger events beyond the city walls, possibly connected to the celestial palace or the lingering threat of the Southern Wastes' number one. Xiao Chen's cold efficiency reveals his still-festering grief over Keke's death, and his willingness to slaughter anyone connected to that tragedy sets a grim tone for the coming chapters.
