## Chapter Overview

This chapter descends into the heart of the Death World's ancient terror. The long‑feared sovereign of the western region finally reveals himself: the Great Might Nether King Ruixiao, a being who was sealed five millennia ago by the master of the Celestial Palace. His resurrection is not a miracle—it is a hunger. Cold, beautiful, and utterly overwhelming, he demands six kings as a living sacrifice to complete his ascent to the Ancestor King realm. The atmosphere is one of suffocating dread, where even half‑ancestor experts are shattered like glass against his mere presence. The chapter establishes the hopelessness of the situation, as the six trapped kings are gradually consumed over decades, their spirits broken but not released into death.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the arrival of Ruixiao, a pale, beautiful youth in black nether armor whose footsteps cause celestial palaces to tremble. Thirty‑plus kings kneel before him. Ruixiao demands six kings for a blood sacrifice to break his final barrier into the Ancestor King realm. The hero Tianwaitian and Renwairen attack him but are both crippled instantly, their arms shattered by the impact. Even a drop of Ancestor King blood, secretly stolen and used as a weapon, cannot harm Ruixiao; it only exposes a layer of mysterious black iron armor beneath his nether robes. Ruixiao reveals that he was sealed within the Celestial Palace five thousand years ago by the Ancestor King, but he survived by devouring half of that Ancestor King's corpse—a mad, victorious act. He then surrenders the six kings (the three Tree‑Men, English Bear, Tianwaitian, and Renwairen) to a slow, century‑long sacrifice. Each day a portion of their flesh and soul is burned to fuel his ascension, while the rest regenerates, trapping them in endless agony. Xiao Chen, as a soul body without a king‑level cultivation, can only witness this horror. Over fifty‑five years, the six kings are reduced to shadows of their former selves, their wills nearly broken. At the chapter’s end, a withered, silent figure appears in the darkness behind Ruixiao, hinting at a possible turning point.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Great Might Nether King Ruixiao represents a peak of undead cultivation in the Death World: a being who has grasped the 'momentum' of heaven and earth, such that his will becomes law. The concept of a 'sacrifice of kings' echoes ancient ritual magic where supreme beings consume the essence of lesser powerhouses to break through cultivation bottlenecks. The thirty‑plus kings who kneel to Ruixiao are 'death marionettes'—mindless puppets created by the original Ancestor King. The Ancestor King (祖君) is a title for a cultivator who has transcended the Kingly Sovereign (君王) realm; it is a state only approached by half‑ancestors and above. The 'Doomsday Prophecy' mentioned by Ruixiao is an ancient inscription on bone tablets, said to foresee the fate of the world; Ruixiao claims to have read it, but refuses to reveal its contents. The scene where Tianwaitian and Renwairen attack Ruixiao and fail demonstrates the vast gap between half‑ancestor and near‑Ancestor King levels. The slow sacrifice over a century—burning flesh and soul daily while forced to regenerate—is a uniquely cruel method of cultivation alchemy, mirroring the 'cauldron furnace' concept in Daoist alchemy but inverted to feed the sacrificer.

## Reading Guide

The chapter ends on a note of bleak despair, but the arrival of a mysterious figure in the shadows suggests that the situation may yet shift. Readers should watch for the identity of this watcher—could it be an ancient power independent of Ruixiao? The Doomsday Prophecy lingers as an unresolved thread; its full contents may be crucial to understanding the broader conflict between the Death World and the Eternal Continent. The six kings’ agony is setting up a potential reversal: if they can endure the hundred years, or if outside help arrives, the sacrifice may be turned against Ruixiao. The chapter also reinforces that Xiao Chen, despite his growth, is still not at the level to directly challenge such a monster. His observation of the sacrifice will likely fuel his next phase of training. The mention of Ruixiao devouring an Ancestor King’s corpse is a reminder that in this world, even the gods can be consumed.
