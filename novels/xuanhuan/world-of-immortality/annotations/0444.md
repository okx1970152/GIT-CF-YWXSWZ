## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges us into the oppressive silence of a Death World palace, where a desiccated corpse stands motionless at the entrance, radiating an aura of absolute authority. The Great Nether King Resio, a being steps away from Ancestral Sovereign status, finds himself utterly outmatched as the dry corpse effortlessly neutralizes his attacks, claims dominion over everything in the hall, and proceeds to dismantle his entire retinue of king-level fire seed beings. The chapter is a masterclass in power scaling, demonstrating the chasm between mere kings and those who have touched the Ancestral realm. The eerie stillness, the casual destruction, and the mysterious identity of the corpse create a tension that permeates every exchange.

## Key Plot Points
The silent dry corpse handily deflects Resio's probing attacks, absorbing two divine beams without effort. When Resio attempts to use the fallen Ancestral Sovereign's blood-touched broken swords, the corpse catches all three in one hand and flings him away. Tianwaitian and his companions offer their oracle bone diagrams to the entity, correctly identifying it as an Ancestral Sovereign. Resio, realizing resistance is futile, also offers his diagrams and even his black iron armor, but the corpse refuses the armor, stating that everything here is already his. Enraged beyond endurance, Resio summons his Tianhuang Nether Realm and thirty-plus king-level warriors to battle the corpse. The ensuing fight is brutally one-sided: the dry corpse tears through kings with bare hands, shattering their bodies and even destroying the regenerating palace structures. The battle culminates in Resio fusing with his Nether Realm to unleash a 'Tianhuang Extinction Nether World Cycle'—a cyclical annihilation technique that traps the corpse for over an hour, leaving the outcome uncertain as the chapter closes.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Death World operates on a strict hierarchy where fire seed beings can absorb one another's essence to evolve, but advancement becomes exponentially harder near the top. An 'Ancestral Sovereign' (祖君) represents the pinnacle of power in this realm, a being whose will shapes reality around them. The Tianhuang Nether Realm is a supreme spatial technique unique to the Great Nether King, a pocket world that can trap and grind down enemies through cyclical destruction and rebirth. The oracle bone diagrams (甲骨图) are ancient relics coveted by top cultivators, said to contain the secrets of heaven and earth; their presence here indicates the depth of the Death World's lost civilizations. The ability of the palace and the destroyed kings to regenerate from fatal damage reflects the unique nature of fire seed beings—they are not easily permanently killed unless their soul-flame is completely extinguished.

## Reading Guide
This chapter raises crucial questions: Who is this mysterious dry corpse, and why does he claim everything in the palace as his own? His identity may connect to the lost history of the Death World's inner continent, possibly even to the vanished Ancestral Gods of the Nine Provinces. Resio's fate remains uncertain—if he survives, he will be a deeply humiliated and vengeful enemy. The oracle bone diagrams have now been collected by the corpse, setting up a potential treasure hunt for Xiao Chen or other explorers. Watch for how this power shift affects the balance of forces in the Death World, especially as Xiao Chen himself is heading toward the female sovereign's territory. The casual, almost bored manner in which the corpse defeats kings suggests an order of magnitude difference that will have ripple effects across all factions.
