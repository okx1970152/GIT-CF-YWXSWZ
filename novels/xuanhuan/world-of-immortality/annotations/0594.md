## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen ventures deep into the Death World through the three black holes beneath the Demon Plain, seeking the missing Heavenly Stele. He confronts decaying Stone Men within Primordial Demon Cities, using the power of the Eight Origin Sounds and the forbidden Holy Sacrifice to empower his companions. However, the third city proves to be a deadly trap laid by a powerful being known as Meng Degang, forcing a hasty retreat. Back in the Nine Provinces, Xiao Chen faces the grim reality that he needs more Heavenly Marks to counter such enemies, and unexpectedly discovers that the Heavenly Stele itself has shrunk to a mere meter and ended up in a mortal auction house. The chapter weaves together intense combat, strategic cultivation, and a shocking twist that recontextualizes the nature of the divine steles, advancing the narrative toward a crucial phase of acquisition and confrontation.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and his party—the Little Defiant Dragon, Sha Polang, and the Nine-Headed Serpent—enter the three black holes beneath the Demon Plain, each leading to a Primordial Demon City in the Death World. The first city contains a decaying stone man whose life fire is nearly extinguished. Xiao Chen engages it in direct combat using the Eight Origin Sounds, shatters the city, and performs a Holy Sacrifice on the stone man to channel its essence into the Little Defiant Dragon, propelling him to the Seventh Heaven of War Ancestor and granting him an exceptionally durable body. The second city holds a stone man already on the verge of death; Xiao Chen sacrifices it to boost Sha Polang and the Nine-Headed Serpent, raising them to King Seven Heaven and Half-Ancestor Eighth Heaven respectively. However, the third city is a deliberate ambush. The being within, proclaiming himself 'King of Ten Thousand Ages'—Meng Degang—has been waiting for Xiao Chen. His power rivals that of the female Stone Man king encountered earlier, and he seals the exit with a black hole. Xiao Chen wisely chooses to retreat through the Death World and return to the Nine Provinces using the remnants of the Ancestral Lord Warship. Afterward, Xiao Chen realizes he needs two more Heavenly Marks to counter such enemies, but his search for the Heavenly Stele at the Heavenly Burial Valley yields nothing. Back in Yan Capital, Sha Polang reports seeing a peculiar one-meter stone tablet at an auction house. Xiao Chen investigates and is astonished to discover it is none other than the Heavenly Stele itself, inexplicably shrunk and held by mortals.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Origin Sounds (本源八音)—described as 'A-o-m, Ke, Ah, Hou, Xiao, Duo, Zao, Dao'—are primordial sonic vibrations tied to the Yellow Clay Altar and the Martial Ancestor's legacy. They are a supreme technique that can shatter even a demon city and suppress a stone man. The Holy Sacrifice (圣祭) is a forbidden ritual from the Martial Ancestor's heretical chapter, allowing the caster to extract and transfer the essence of a powerful being to another, here used to accelerate the Little Defiant Dragon's cultivation. The concept of a 'Primordial Demon City' (太古魔城) is central to the Stone Man cultivation path: these cities are both a residence and a weapon, formed from the remains of countless beings. A stone man's 'final step' is the ultimate transformation into a complete stone being, requiring immense accumulation of power and often involving such cities. The Heavenly Mark (天痕) is an imprint left on a cultivator's divine source by a Heavenly Stele, serving as a key to access deeper mysteries. Meng Degang's title 'King of Ten Thousand Ages' echoes the ancient monarchs of the Celestial Realm, suggesting he is a survivor from a previous civilization era.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's strategy of gathering Heavenly Marks to counter supreme enemies sets the stage for the next narrative arc. The discovery of a shrunken Heavenly Stele in a mortal auction house is a pivotal twist: it implies the steles are not merely divine artifacts but possess a will of their own or are subject to forces beyond comprehension. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will attempt to recover or interact with this miniature Stele, and whether it will grant him the needed Heavenly Marks. The ambush by Meng Degang indicates that other ancient powers are actively monitoring Xiao Chen's movements, raising the stakes of his quest. The empowerment of the Little Defiant Dragon and Sha Polang suggests they will play increasingly significant roles in the coming conflicts. The chapter also reinforces Xiao Chen's preference for cultivation over reckless battle—he retreats when outmatched, a sign of his maturation. The unresolved mystery of the other two stone men's fates (the ambusher and the location of the missing Heavenly Stele) will drive the plot forward, building toward a confrontation with the Otherworld's supreme beings.
