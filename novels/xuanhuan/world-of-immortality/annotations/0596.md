## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen and his companions delve into the underground ruins of a fallen castle, where they gather massive stone blocks stained with Ancestral God blood and refine them into his miniature demonic city. The excavation reveals the central palace, sealed by a stone door that exudes overwhelming pressure. Within stands a Stone Man puppet—the corpse of a Stone Man King, animated as a soulless guardian. Xiao Chen uses his spatial techniques to bypass the puppet and enters the second hall. There, they discover a dragon-skin map marking legendary locations: the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, the Yellow Clay Altar (here called the Ancestral Dragon Platform), the Divine Diagram, the Ninety-Nine Heavenly Palaces, the Nine Ancient Lamps, and at least seven Heavenly Steles, with hints of an eighth and ninth. This map points toward the ultimate secrets of the universe and the path to transcendence. The tone is tense, mythic, and laden with mystery, as the group stands on the brink of world-shaking discoveries.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen absorbs the blood essence of fallen Ancestral Gods from the castle stones and uses the materials to strengthen his miniature demonic city, nearly transforming it into a king's divine city. The group discovers sealed stone chambers containing desiccated corpses—one stone, one bone, one flesh—which crumble upon touch, leaving only concentrated essence pills and a stone arm. The arm is identified as that of a Stone Man King, invaluable for city construction. After clearing debris, they uncover the central palace, which radiates an invisible pressure that blasts even Half-Ancestor-level experts away. The Little Defiant Dragon forces the stone door open, revealing a motionless Stone Man puppet—a dead Stone Man King repurposed as a guardian. Xiao Chen recognizes that no one could enslave such a being; it must be a corpse reanimated. Using his eight-aspect worlds and sacred artifacts, he leads the group past the puppet into the second hall. There, a dragon-skin map shows major goals: the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, the Yellow Clay Altar (labeled Ancestral Dragon Platform), the Divine Diagram, Ninety-Nine Heavenly Palaces, Nine Ancient Lamps, and seven clearly marked Heavenly Steles. Additionally, an eighth stele is faintly visible, and a ninth is barely discernible. The map consolidates the quests that Xiao Chen must undertake. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen at a crossroads—whether to commit to the Stone Man path or pursue the war-god path, aware that the Stone Man path requires immense epochs and often ends in tragedy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Stone Man path is one of the highest cultivation roads in the World of Immortality. A practitioner slowly petrifies their body into an indestructible stone form, becoming a Stone Man—and eventually a Stone Man King, whose power rivals the Ancestral Gods. However, this path requires endless ages of meditation and often results in death or stagnation. The Stone Man puppet encountered here is the corpse of such a being, its consciousness destroyed but its body retaining immense force. Reusing it as a guardian indicates the original builder's terrifying capabilities. The dragon-skin map, made from Ancestral Dragon hide, is a supreme artifact in itself. The locations marked are pivotal to the story: the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge connects worlds; the Yellow Clay Altar is a sacred relic of Suiren; the Divine Diagram is a fragment of the Immortal-Slaying Formation; the Ninety-Nine Heavenly Palaces may correspond to the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps guarded by the three Supreme Ancestral Gods; the Nine Ancient Lamps are buried beneath the Nine Provinces; and the seven (or nine) Heavenly Steles are primordial tablets containing the secrets of creation. This map is a roadmap to the ultimate treasures and dangers of the cosmos.

## Reading Guide

This chapter presents a pivotal decision for Xiao Chen: choosing his ultimate cultivation path. The discovery of the dragon-skin map provides a clear sequence of objectives. Readers should note the possible eighth and ninth Heavenly Steles, which have only been hinted at before—they may be the key to the final step. The Stone Man puppet is a significant obstacle but also a potential source of materials for building a divine city. The map's inclusion of the Ninety-Nine Heavenly Palaces suggests that Xiao Chen will eventually need to confront the Supreme Ancestral Gods guarding them. In the coming chapters, the group may venture to one of these sites, likely with extreme danger. The tension is high, and the stakes are cosmic. Xiao Chen's cultivation at Half-Ancestor Ninth Heaven means he must soon make his choice, and every action now carries immense weight. The chapter also deepens the lore of the castle's fall with a Heavenly Stele, implying that the castle was once a critical location.
