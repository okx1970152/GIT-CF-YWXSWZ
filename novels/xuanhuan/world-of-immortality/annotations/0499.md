## Chapter Overview

This chapter presents a breathtaking cosmic journey as Xiao Chen, Keke, and the mysterious old farmer ascend a luminous pathway toward a colossal gate that may lead to the fabled Primordial Celestial Realm. The gate, surrounded by a condensed universe of stars, emits overwhelming pressure that causes even the old farmer—wielder of the Great Annihilation War Spear—to hesitate. The discovery of Keke's mother's wooden box adds a poignant personal dimension to the quest. The chapter balances awe-inspiring worldbuilding with high-stakes action as the farmer unleashes the full power of his spear to shatter the gate, raising questions about what lies beyond and whether the journey to the Celestial Realm is truly attainable.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, Keke, and the old farmer travel up a strange light channel that may connect to the Primordial Celestial Realm. The atmosphere is tense as the energy in the channel threatens to become violent at any moment. A great voice emanates from the far end of the light path, sounding like thousands of Buddhas chanting or demons roaring, attempting to force the group to kneel in submission. The old farmer defiantly brandishes the Great Annihilation War Spear and declares 'Open!' The spear's killing intent surges toward the voice, causing the channel to tremble violently. A massive hundred-meter gate appears at the end of the path, surrounded by a condensed universe of stars—a cosmic barrier that shocks the old farmer, who believed the Celestial Realm to be illusory. Keke's wooden box from her mother is examined but cannot be opened beyond a crack; nevertheless, its presence seems to facilitate the gate's accessibility. Deciding to breach the gate forcefully, the old farmer unleashes the full power of the spear, uttering 'Great—Annihilation!' The gate trembles, stars are extinguished in waves, and a significant portion of the cosmic barrier is annihilated. The gate develops cracks and ultimately collapses under the legendary spear's might, opening the mysterious door.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of the Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界) is a classic element in Chinese mythology and xianxia fiction, representing the highest divine realm beyond the mundane cosmos. Here, it is portrayed as a real but sealed place, connected to the Nine Provinces through a gate guarded by a condensed universe—a powerful symbol of the vast barriers between mortal and immortal realms. The scene echoes ancient legends of heroes breaking through heavenly barriers to ascend to godhood. The Great Annihilation War Spear (大破灭战矛) is an Otherworld Ancestral God weapon capable of piercing worlds, and its use here to shatter the gate underscores the power of supreme artifacts in xianxia. The gate's protective stars being extinguished like fireworks highlights the transience and fragility of even cosmic defenses against overwhelming martial will. Keke's mother's box, a mysterious artifact from the Lost Paradise, serves as a key to this heavenly passage, adding a layer of ancestral inheritance to the journey.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pivotal reveal that the Primordial Celestial Realm may indeed be real and accessible, setting up major cosmological revelations for the coming arcs. Xiao Chen's journey is no longer just about personal power but about unlocking the highest mysteries of the universe. The breach of the gate suggests that the world's foundations are being shaken, and other powers—Otherworld Ancestral Gods, Death World sovereigns—will undoubtedly be drawn to the opening. Readers should watch for the consequences: what lies beyond the gate? How will other half-ancestors and gods react? Additionally, Keke's mother's box remains a key artifact with untapped potential; its full purpose may yet be revealed. The old farmer's words about 'spending his remaining heat' hint at a sacrifice or depletion, raising the emotional stakes. This chapter sets the stage for a dramatic shift in the cosmological landscape of World of Immortality.
