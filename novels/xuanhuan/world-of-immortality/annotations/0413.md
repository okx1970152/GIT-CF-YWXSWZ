## Chapter Overview
This chapter erupts into a full-scale war as Xiao Chen and his allies engage the Four Heavenly Kings—Leishen King, Haishen King, Dadi King, and the fearsome Yecha Queen—in a battle that shakes heaven and earth. The combat is brutal and unrelenting, with each king commanding devastating elemental powers. Xiao Chen, having reached the Perfected Man realm, displays his mastery of the Four Killing Moves, culminating in the spectacular destruction of Leishen King. The battlefield becomes a chaos of clashing divine arts, with Snow Dance matching the Yecha Queen blow for blow, while other warriors from the Divine Race and the Hundred Races join the fray. Just as the tide turns, the appearance of a half-ancestor threatens to upend the entire conflict, but a mysterious, overwhelming presence answers the challenge, leaving both kings and readers in awe.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen immediately targets the Four Heavenly Kings, using his Eight-Aspect Worlds to evade their combined assault. He unleashes the Four Killing Moves—Slay God, Collapse, Reverse Chaos, and Suppress Demon—which manifest as monumental stele shadows that radiate world-ending power. Leishen King is cornered and crushed by the simultaneous impact of all four steles. Elsewhere, Snow Dance engages the Yecha Queen in a duel of peerless warriors, calling down a mountain-sized seal and transforming a river into a weapon. Seven holy dragons attempt to intervene but are first stunned by the Yecha Queen's roar and then obliterated by Snow Dance's Phoenix Cry technique. The remaining kings are held at bay by a coalition: the Divine Race Seven Experts, Chen Hangjin, Hai Yuntian, and others suppress Haishen King, while Niu Ren, Jin Sanyi, Liu Mu, and others lock down Dadi King. The Black Dragon King and the Inverse Dragon King stand ready as reserves. After Leishen King's death, Xiao Chen turns toward the Yecha Queen, but an old Yecha half-ancestor materializes, claiming the trial battle has been moved to next month. Another even more imposing figure appears in the sky, causing the half-ancestors to retreat in shock. Keke joyfully runs toward the newcomer, indicating a powerful ally has arrived.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Four Heavenly Kings (Leishen King, Haishen King, Dadi King, Yecha Queen) are named after elemental and mythological concepts—Thunder, Sea, Earth, and Night/Terror—each representing a domain of power. In xianxia lore, such kings often serve higher cosmic forces, but here they are independent sovereigns of the Hundred Races. The Perfected Man (至人) realm is a major cultivation threshold reached after the Nine Heavens of Mortal-Shedding and the Fish-Leap transformation; it denotes a being whose body and spirit have been perfected and can wield laws of heaven and earth with devastating effect. The trial battle system is a formalized conflict mechanism among the Hundred Races, overseen by half-ancestors, who are just below Ancestral God in cultivation. The arrival of a half-ancestor signals a shift from mortal combat to a contest of supreme powers, and the intervention of an even stronger being suggests the involvement of figures that dwell in the Primordial Celestial Realm. Snow Dance's Phoenix Cry technique is a sonic attack that surpasses the standard Lion's Roar, capable of shattering dragons at the peak of their power.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's re-emergence as a force to be reckoned with. By killing a second Perfected Man–realm king, he has cemented his reputation as a nightmare of the Hundred Races. However, the intervention of half-ancestors and the mystery of the final figure raise the stakes dramatically. Who is the being that makes even a half-ancestor step back? The answer will likely reshape the power dynamics in the Nine Provinces. Readers should watch for the consequences of Xiao Chen's escalating violence: the Yecha race will surely seek vengeance, and the trial battle may become a showdown. Keke's excitement at the mysterious figure hints at a reunion with an old protector, possibly from the Primordial Celestial Realm. The chapter also showcases the growth of many supporting characters, from Snow Dance's divine prowess to the coordinated teamwork of the coalition against the kings.
