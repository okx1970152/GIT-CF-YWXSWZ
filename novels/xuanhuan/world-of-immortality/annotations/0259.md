## Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces a terrifying new element from another world: a tiny, crystalline sword that moves with impossible speed and precision. It attacks and gravely wounds several of the most promising young cultivators from the Roman Empire, the Buddhist lands, and the Great Shang Empire, all with seemingly effortless strikes. The sword does not kill, but its intent is clear: it is clearing a space on the Demon Plain. The arrival of this blade sends a shockwave through the gathered elites, and even the usually unflappable Yi Zhen turns pale, recognizing the weapon's origin as something from beyond their world. The chapter builds an atmosphere of ominous revelation, culminating in the emergence of twenty-four colossal sword beams that may herald the coming of the legendary Twenty-Four War Swords. Xiao Chen's sacred tree reacts, and Keke appears with news that the first seal of the Lost Paradise is about to break, setting the stage for a convergence of world-shattering events.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with a sense of dread as a small, brilliant flying sword descends upon the Demon Plain, where a gathering of young elites from multiple empires has been locked in conflict. The sword, only a palm’s length, moves like lightning, tearing through space and piercing through the defenses of the most powerful practitioners. It wounds a Divine Maiden of the Sword and Compassionate Sword Sect, a golden-haired Roman champion, a Buddhist master wielding a heavy demon-subduing staff, and three more experts, all within moments. The observers realize they are outmatched and scatter. Yi Zhen, watching from a distance with Xiao Chen, reveals that this sword – and its unseen master – likely originate from another world. He recounts a legend passed down through his sect: a being has been searching the Demon Plain for generations, seeking the Twenty-Four War Swords, treasures believed to have fallen from another realm. As if in confirmation, the sword carves a vast, intricate pattern on the plain, and then twenty-four titanic columns of sword light erupt from the earth, piercing into the heavens. The atmosphere becomes thick with murderous intent. Simultaneously, Xiao Chen's Seven-Colored Sacred Tree within his body begins to resonate with the sword beams, emitting seven beams of light that clash and harmonize. Before the tension can mount further, Keke reappears, her voice excitedly reporting that the first seal on the passage to the Lost Paradise is beginning to break, adding yet another layer of cosmic significance to the unfolding chaos.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The appearance of a 'sword from another world' draws upon a rich trope in xianxia literature where higher or parallel realms intersect, bringing artifacts and beings of incomprehensible power. The 'Twenty-Four War Swords' are introduced as legendary relics sought by a mysterious figure who has stalked the Demon Plain for millennia. This echoes themes of ancient treasures and the eternal quest for ultimate weapons. The Demon Plain itself is a cursed, desolate landscape in the World of Immortality, a fitting stage for such apocalyptic events. The reaction of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree—resonating with the sword energy—deepens the mystery surrounding Keke and her origins. The mention of a 'lost paradise' and seals breaking suggests a multi-layered cosmology, with sealed realms gradually opening, a common device in xianxia to escalate narrative stakes and introduce new arenas of conflict.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the character of the sword's master, who remains unseen but whose influence is absolute. This figure is clearly a major power on the cusp of obtaining the Twenty-Four War Swords, and their acquisition will likely shift the balance of power on the Eternal Continent. The reaction of the sacred tree hints that Xiao Chen may have an unexpected connection to these events, possibly drawing unwanted attention from forces beyond his current level. Meanwhile, Keke's news about the Lost Paradise suggests a parallel plotline that may soon converge with the main story. The next few chapters will likely focus on the fallout from the emergence of the twenty-four sword beams, the reaction of the great empires, and Xiao Chen's navigation of this newly volatile landscape. Will he seek to claim one of the swords? Or will he be drawn into the mystery of the otherworldly visitor?
