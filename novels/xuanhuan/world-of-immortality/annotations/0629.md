## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a pivot point in the latter half of the World of Immortality saga. It gathers seven of the most terrifying beings in the cosmos—Stone Man Kings and their corpse counterparts—under the shadow of a legend thought long dead: the Little Stone Emperor. What begins as a solemn philosophical discourse on the nature of the Dao quickly reveals itself as a meticulously laid trap. The Little Stone Emperor, who allegedly destroyed himself eons ago, unveils a grand scheme to use the power of these seven kings for his own rebirth and breakthrough. The atmosphere is thick with tension and awe, as the text oscillates between profound Daoist reflection and the cold mechanics of a transcendent prison. The chapter closes with the emergence of a half-flesh, half-stone body from the central tomb, and a cryptic unease from the mysterious old Daoist at Xiao Chen's side, setting the stage for a seismic shift in the power dynamics of the Celestial Realm.

## Key Plot Points

The Little Stone Emperor presides over a gathering of seven Stone Man Kings (Shi Shi, Xuan Kong, Li Shi Beast, Three-Eyed Stone Man King, Lu Zhan, Dan Jun, and one unnamed) and seven corpse shadows from the Chaos Land. He rewards those whose Dao insights resonate with him by bestowing legendary stone weapons: the Lunar Shadow Magic Blade, the Emperor Stone Sword, and a great seal of his own forging. Then, with chilling calm, he reveals that he destroyed himself long ago to pursue a path of rebirth, but failed to achieve final transcendence. He declares that the entire tomb complex—a network of seven grand tombs, each containing seven deadly formations (forty-nine in total)—was built precisely for this moment. One by one, he forces each king into a tomb: Li Shi Beast resists first, then Three-Eyed Stone Man King attempts to flee with his third eye, and Xuan Kong tries to attack, but all are subdued by the formations. Lu Zhan, Dan Jun, and the others submit. The corpse shadows are directed into position above the tombs. The Little Stone Emperor then tears open the central ground, revealing a half-flesh, half-stone body—his nascent rebirth vessel. The seven tombs blaze with divine light, channeling power into this body. Xiao Chen and Keke watch in shock, while the old Daoist beside them grows visibly disturbed, as if sensing an even deeper plot.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Little Stone Emperor' (小石皇) is a legendary figure in the novel's lore, once undefeated among the Stone Man Kings. The Stone Man path (石人路) is a perilous cultivation route that gradually petrifies the practitioner, culminating in a 'final step' that transforms one into an eternal stone being. The 'discourse on corpses and the Dao' (谈尸论道) reflects a tradition among deathless beings to debate the ultimate nature of reality, often drawing on Daoist philosophy. The number forty-nine (from the Great Derivation 大衍之数) holds deep significance in Chinese cosmology: it represents the totality of the manifest world, with the 'missing one' being the unmanifest. The Little Stone Emperor's forty-nine formations thus symbolize a near-perfect imprisonment. The 'Crown of the Three Realms' (三皇镜) is mentioned as a peerless treasure ranked third in the primordial age. The seven tombs each correspond to a cardinal direction and the center, forming a Big Dipper (北斗) arrangement, a common motif in Chinese mystical geomancy. The emergence of the half-flesh, half-stone body echoes the classical xianxia trope of a perfected being shedding its mortal shell.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is dense with lore and sets up a major arc that will likely span many chapters. The key tension is whether the Little Stone Emperor's gamble will succeed, and what the imprisoned kings will do in response. Readers should pay close attention to the old Daoist's subtle reactions—his discomfort suggests he may have an unexpected connection to the scheme. The fate of the seven Stone Man Kings hangs in the balance; their power, if fully absorbed, could elevate the Little Stone Emperor to an unprecedented level. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen and Keke remain observers for now, but their presence in this tomb complex suggests they will be drawn into the conflict. The next few chapters will likely reveal the first fruits of the Little Stone Emperor's plan and perhaps the true nature of the old Daoist. Brace for a dramatic escalation in the war between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld.
