## Chapter Overview

This chapter witnesses the awakening of the primordial demon city deep in the Death World. Five Otherworld ancestral gods, accompanied by three supreme ancestral god weapons, launch a catastrophic assault on the city's foundations. But the city is no mere fortress—it is a living entity, and from its core emerges a colossal stone statue whose hair is nine immortal life vines. The vines counterattack with devastating efficiency, killing two ancestral gods, severely wounding the remaining three, and destroying the spiritual avatars of the three supreme weapons. The survivors flee in terror, leaving half the half-ancestor army annihilated. The chapter emphasizes the terrifying power of ancient beings and hints at Keke's mysterious connection to this primordial relic.

## Key Plot Points

The five ancestral gods—Lu Lupei, Lan Yufeng, Ai'er, and two unnamed—initiate a coordinated attack on the giant city using supreme techniques: Lu Lupei's nine great seals, Ai'er's thirty divine kings, Lan Yufeng's dimensional blade, and the summoning of a star river. The thirty-three layered pagoda, a supreme ancestral god weapon, also strikes. The city's nine life vines retaliate, instantly piercing all five ancestral gods and binding the pagoda. The ancestral gods regenerate but are repeatedly wounded; their souls are damaged by the divine flowers on the vines. Meanwhile, the city's core swells, and a hundred-zhang stone statue rises from within, its hair formed by the nine vines. Three shadowy figures emerge from the supreme weapons, opening a black passage to retreat, but the vines pursue, killing two ancestral gods outright and destroying the shadow figures. The three surviving ancestral gods escape with half their half-ancestor force, while the supreme weapons flee to their distant owners, each receiving a drop of blood. The chapter closes with Keke approaching the city as a vine extends to her, suggesting a personal bond.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter draws on classic xianxia tropes of ancient, living weapons and cities that are themselves cultivation entities. The primordial demon city is a 'living artifact' that has grown a stone statue—a being that may have achieved the 'Stone Man' path, a legendary cultivation route where the body petrifies into indestructible stone. The nine life vines are reminiscent of the 'Ten Thousand Year Green Vines' often found in immortal realms, representing vitality and longevity. The supreme ancestral god weapons (demon blade, immortal shield, pagoda) are each sentient and tied to their masters through blood essence. The concept of '上祖神' (Supreme Ancestral God) indicates a hierarchy above ordinary ancestral gods, reflecting a deep cultivation structure. Keke's reaction hints at her origin from a lineage that controlled such primordial powers.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point in the Death World arc, revealing that the primordial demon city is not just a location but a character with agency. The destruction of two ancestral gods and the wounding of three others drastically shifts the balance of power in the Otherworld invasion. Readers should pay attention to Keke's recognition of the city—this may foreshadow her true heritage and the role of her parents, who were mentioned earlier. The vine extending to her at the end suggests she may be able to communicate with or even control the stone statue. The three supreme weapons have returned to their owners, who are wounded (blood from forehead), indicating they will be a threat later. The survival of Lu Lupei, Lan Yufeng, and Ai'er means the enemy still has three powerful ancestral gods.
