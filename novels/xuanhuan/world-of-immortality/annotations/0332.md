## Chapter Overview

The Dead City, an ancient fortress of dread and power, descends fully into the mortal world, shaking the foundations of the Eternal Continent. Xiao Chen and Qingqing find themselves atop its black walls, carried there by the mysterious Yellow-Clay Altar. Before them, the armies of the Sun Religion and the great powers of the land launch a desperate assault, only to be annihilated by the city's ancient guardians—the Celestial Soldiers and Underworld Soldiers. In the blood-soaked aftermath, a strange mark appears on Xiao Chen's forehead, and the ancient soldiers bow to him as their lord. But the city's depths hold darker secrets: a towering figure draped in the Ancestral Suiren's burial shroud watches from the shadows.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Dead City's full manifestation, its hundred-meter-high walls of black, blood-stained stone dominating the landscape. Xiao Chen and Qingqing stand upon the battlements, gazing down upon a sea of cultivators. Sun Saintess Zhao Lin'er's forces lead the charge: six thousand Iron Cavalry, reinforced by Sun Knights, attempt to breach the gates using a secret battle formation that channels their collective spirit. But the city's power suppresses flight and repels their attacks—over a thousand cavalry die from energy depletion alone. Then the ancient guardians emerge: Celestial Soldiers and Underworld Soldiers in rusted bronze armor, wielding corroded weapons. They move with terrible efficiency, slaughtering the five hundred Sun Knights and many other attackers in a brutal display of power. Xiao Chen discovers he now commands these soldiers, but a chilling realization dawns—he did not choose this. A mysterious mark burns onto his forehead, an ancient character that gives him an oppressive aura. Inside the city, the Heavenly Stele quakes, transmitting a pattern to the stone man within Xiao Chen. Yet the greatest shock comes at the chapter's end: a tall, shadowy figure wearing the burial shroud of Ancestral God Suiren stands deep in the city, watching. The shroud, which should be hidden in Xiao Chen's acupoint space, is gone.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Dead City is a recurring artifact of immense power in the World of Immortality, first seen during the Dragon Island arc. It is sealed by a Heavenly Stele and houses countless undead beings. The Celestial Soldiers (天兵) and Underworld Soldiers (阴兵) are ancient warriors bound to the city; their strength appears to have grown dramatically since their first appearance. Xiao Chen's forehead mark is an ancient divine script (太古神文), long forgotten even by the sages, suggesting a covenant or curse. The Yellow-Clay Altar (黄泥台), which transported Xiao Chen and Qingqing to the city wall, is a sacred relic of the Ancestral Gods. The burial shroud of Suiren (燧人氏裹尸布) is a legendary yin-aligned artifact capable of protecting against the Forbidden Sea's deadly energies—its sudden displacement hints at a powerful entity who can manipulate spatial treasures.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen's fate becomes intertwined with the Dead City, setting up a dangerous dynamic with the half-ancestors who fear the city's power. Readers should watch for the development of Xiao Chen's new mark and his connection to the ancient soldiers—will he truly become their master, or a puppet? The reappearance of the burial shroud on a mysterious figure raises urgent questions: who is this being, and what plans does he have for the shroud? Additionally, the Heavenly Stele's imprint on the stone man deepens the mystery of Xiao Chen's cultivation path. Tensions with the Sun Religion, the Tiger Clan, and the half-ancestors will likely escalate. Xiao Chen's priority may shift from personal vengeance to surviving the machinations of the Dead City.
