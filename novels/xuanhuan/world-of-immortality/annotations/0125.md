## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a profound transition, both in setting and in the bonds between key characters. The King Ship and the Divine Ship pass in a cataclysm of opposing energies, a spectacle that underscores the mythic scale of Dragon Island's final mystery. Amidst the chaos, the Little Defiant Dragon stands unyielding, and Keke is bathed in divine light—hinting at hidden depths. The chapter then shifts to the vast sea journey, carrying Xiao Chen, Yan Qingcheng, and the lingering tension of unresolved enmities. The core of the chapter is the desperate negotiation between Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng, culminating in a perilous pact: the Shatter Demon, Plant God technique, binding their fates together. This is a chapter of compromises, where survival forces old enemies into an uneasy, irreversible alliance.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the dramatic convergence of the King Ship and the Divine Ship, their energies clashing in a world-shaking confrontation. The Little Defiant Dragon stands its ground, facing the divine light without fear, and Keke's Sacred Tree is enveloped by a protective radiance—neither harmed. The ships separate without catastrophe, and the King Ship begins its long voyage across the Forbidden Sea, leaving Dragon Island behind. Days later, as they approach the legendary mainland of immortality, Xiao Chen confronts Yan Qingcheng, demanding a way to trust her. After hesitation, Yan Qingcheng proposes the Shatter Demon, Plant God technique—a legendary and extremely dangerous cultivation method that requires a cauldron. She offers to become the dependent party, linking her fate to Xiao Chen's: if he dies, she suffers; if she dies, he is unaffected. Xiao Chen, calculating the risks, agrees. The ritual is performed, and as the connection forms, the vast continent of the World of Immortality appears on the horizon. This pact transforms their relationship from captor-captive to a symbiotic bond, setting the stage for future developments.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Shatter Demon, Plant God technique (碎魔种神) is a rare and perilous cultivation method in the World of Immortality. Unlike typical cultivation that relies on one's own efforts, this technique uses a "cauldron" (鼎炉)—a living person who hosts a fragment of the cultivator's consciousness. If the cultivator's power is lower than the cauldron's, the cauldron gains dominance over the cultivator. This creates a dangerous interdependence. Historically, only a handful have successfully mastered it. The technique's name reflects its dual nature: it shatters the demonic (negative, chaotic) aspects within and plants the seeds of divinity (神), aiming for a rebirth of pure power. The King Ship (君王船) is another legendary vessel, distinct from the Divine Ship, capable of crossing the sealed Forbidden Sea without needing the nine dragon kings. Its appearance underscores that ancient powers still hold secrets to leaving Dragon Island—secrets that even the demon respected.
