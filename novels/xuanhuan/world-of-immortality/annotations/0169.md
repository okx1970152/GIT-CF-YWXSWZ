## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a spectacular battle between Xiao Chen and Yu Wenfeng, the "Southern Wastes' Mad Demon" who has cultivated a true domain—the Crimson Beast Domain—a power that normally belongs to the Spirit-Treasury realm. But Xiao Chen, still in Mortal-Shedding, refuses to accept the gap. Through the resonance of his eight divinely transformed acupoints connected by golden lines, he manifests a simulated domain that mimics the properties of a true domain, clothing himself in a Golden Divine Armor. The chapter is not merely a fight scene; it marks the first time Xiao Chen uses all four of his supreme killing techniques in a concentrated strike, and it reveals a new dimension of his cultivation path: the interlinking of acupoints as a foundation for a unique, self-created fighting style. The tone is raw, desperate, and triumphant—yet shadowed by the antagonist's escape and his vow to grow stronger.

## Key Plot Points
Yu Wenfeng activates his Crimson Beast Domain, a field that amplifies his power while suppressing Xiao Chen's. He mocks Xiao Chen, claiming he cannot fight without a domain. Xiao Chen is initially wounded but his fighting spirit ignites. He realizes that the eight divine acupoints—Baihui, Shangqiu, Jianjing, Laogong, Zhongting—are now linked by visible golden lines, forming a circuit of life essence. The golden lines create a simulated domain that merges with the Big Dipper Light Screen, producing an almost tangible Golden Divine Armor that neutralizes the domain's suppression. Xiao Chen unleashes all four killing moves—Slay the Gods, Suppress Demons, Collapse, and Reverse Chaos—in a single, pointed attack aimed at Yu Wenfeng's throat. Despite Yu Wenfeng's domain, the concentrated blast forces him to defend and both suffer injury. Xiao Chen's simulated domain allows him to fight on equal footing. He adopts Yu Wenfeng's own brutal style, overpowering him through raw force and winning the psychological battle. In the final exchange, Yu Wenfeng's fingers break; Xiao Chen's palm also cracks, but he presses the attack. Yu Wenfeng flees, hiding in river mud, then escapes. He later vows to become stronger, hearing dragon roars from the palace underground. The chapter ends with the line "Next, Dugu Jianmo."

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of "域" (domain/field) is a hallmark of higher cultivation in this novel, typically unlocked only at the Spirit-Treasury realm. It creates a localized reality where the user's power is amplified and the opponent's is suppressed, functioning almost like a separate dimension. Xiao Chen's breakthrough—linking eight acupoints with golden lines—is a unique innovation stemming from his cultivation of the Heavenly Stele Profound Art and the absorption of dragon essence. It echoes the Daoist idea of "body as a microcosm," where the acupoints become stars and the connecting lines form a celestial network. The four killing moves (Slay God, Suppress Demon, Collapse, Reverse Chaos) are derived from the divine stele's diagrams. Using them together in one strike is an extreme strain, but it demonstrates Xiao Chen's deep understanding and control. Yu Wenfeng's Crimson Beast Domain is tied to his cultivation of the Ten Thousand Beast Demon Art, which he practiced deep in the Southern Wastes by fighting ancient beasts. His power comes from a bestial, primal source.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's combat ability: he has now developed a method to counter domain-using opponents before reaching the Spirit-Treasury realm. Readers should watch for how this golden-line network evolves in future battles—it may become the foundation for a unique cultivation path. Yu Wenfeng's escape and his vow to grow stronger suggest he will return as a more dangerous foe. The final mention of Dugu Jianmo sets up the next major confrontation; Dugu is a swordsman of legendary reputation, and Xiao Chen's brush with domain power will be tested against a different kind of mastery. Also pay attention to the dragon roars from the underground of the Drunken Beauty Palace—this hints at the hidden dragon king secret that could be a key plot driver.
