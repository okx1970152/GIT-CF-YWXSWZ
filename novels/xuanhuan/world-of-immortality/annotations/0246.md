## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal turning point in Xiao Chen’s journey through the Eternal Continent. After three years of hiding under the alias Xiao Shishui, his true identity is publicly exposed during the grand youth tournament in Yin Capital. The revelation sends shockwaves through the assembled crowds, attracting the attention of legendary figures, ancient families, and hostile powers alike. Framed by the political tension between the Great Shang Empire and the Roman Empire’s Sun Religion, the chapter unfolds as a raw, confrontational display of Xiao Chen’s grief-fueled hatred for the Tiger Clan. His rage, still burning from Keke’s death three years prior, erupts in a barrage of coarse defiance and brutal violence. Two mysterious little girls add an element of eerie wonder, demonstrating power far beyond their innocent appearance. The chapter crackles with the energy of a hero unbound, no longer content to hide, and ready to carve a bloody path through his enemies.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the electrifying confirmation that the mysterious young expert Xiao Shishui is none other than Xiao Chen, the legendary figure who fought demigods three years ago. The news spreads like wildfire through the arena, drawing sharp reactions from all sides. Princess Yin Ying of Great Shang immediately moves to recruit him, while Zhao Lin’er, now the Sun Religion’s Saintess, watches with calculated intent. The Tiger Clan, still seething over the loss of their demigod at Xiao Chen’s hands, confronts him in the sky above the arena. Xiao Chen, his voice dripping with contempt, tears into their honorless history, reminding them of their past ambush with six demigods. He challenges their young generation to a duel, and when given permission by Princess Yin Ying, he executes the first Tiger youth with cold efficiency—severing his head after destroying his three incarnations. He then wounds a second Tiger female, attempting to press his advantage even under the noses of four Tiger demigods. His speed, honed by the Eight Aspect Worlds, proves almost impossible to counter. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen retreating after being blocked, but the message is clear: he will not stop until the Tiger Clan bleeds.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Tiger Clan (虎家) in this world claims descent from the White Tiger Saint Emperor (白虎圣皇), a primordial being powerful enough to challenge Buddhist ancestors. Their body cultivation includes the use of divine patterns (神纹) and the ability to incarnate destructive beasts. The term 'half-god' (半神) is used here as a colloquial equivalent of the Sky-Mastery realm (御空), indicating cultivators who can fly and command elemental forces. The Eight Aspect Worlds (八相世界) technique is derived from Fuxi's Eight Trigrams, manifesting eight illusory realms of extreme speed and spatial manipulation. The Slay God technique (戮神式) is one of the Four Killing Moves from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art, first learned on Dragon Island. The appearance of two seven- or eight-year-old girls who can fly, teleport, and block a demigod's attack highlights a motif in Xianxia where ancient reclusive families rear child prodigies with terrifying power. Their line "We have a few big white cats at home" is a coded threat that they keep genuine White Tigers as pets, implying lineage beyond the Tiger Clan.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up multiple threads that will intensify in the coming chapters. First, the Tiger Clan is now publicly committed to Xiao Chen's death, and their demigods will likely hunt him without restraint. Second, the two little girls, Linglong and Tutu, have taken a clear interest in Xiao Chen—whether as allies or captors remains ambiguous. Their claim to have white tigers as pets suggests a transcendent background that may even overshadow the Tiger Clan. Third, Princess Yin Ying's open favor and Zhao Lin'er's secret calculations weave a political web around Xiao Chen. His refusal to be bound by anyone will only deepen these entanglements. Finally, Xiao Chen's rage, though cathartic, signals a dangerous recklessness. Readers should watch for how his grief for Keke and his desire for vengeance collide with his survival instincts. The stage is set for an explosive confrontation that may draw in powers far beyond the young generation.
