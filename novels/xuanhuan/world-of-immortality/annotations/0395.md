## Chapter Overview

This chapter throws Xiao Chen into the heart of an escalating war among the foreign kings of the Hundred Races. Now a Yellow Diamond skeleton with one foot across the threshold of the Perfected Man realm, he stands atop a blood-drenched altar in the collapsed underground palace, his restored techniques blazing with renewed fury. The emotional tone is one of cold, relentless determination tinged with the desperation of a man who knows he cannot hold off an entire royal assembly forever. Yet with Keke at his side and the Paradise Lost as a final refuge, Xiao Chen is ready to paint the ancient ruins red.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's true identity remains hidden under the guise of a usurping skeleton king, but his actions quickly betray him. He annihilates ten skeleton generals and hundreds of fire-seed creatures with a single Lingxi Sword Ripple, drawing the War Clan's attention. When eight War Clan elites accuse him of murdering their king, he kills them all barehanded, using the Eight Aspect Worlds to move with fatal precision. The Scorpion clan ambushes him, but the Origin Eight Sound (Weng) reduces dozens to blood mist. The Bat Queen and Goblin King are the first foreign kings to fall—the Goblin King crushed by the Hand of the Supreme Being, the Bat Queen torn apart after a fierce sonic duel. The Death King and Zombie King, who had been allied with Xiao Chen, turn on him when the promised Bone King Banner fails to materialize. They lead six kings in a coordinated assault, but Xiao Chen uses the Eight Aspect Worlds to isolate and kill four of them, leaving only the wounded Death King and Zombie King. The Asura King intervenes, rescuing them with a single palm strike that tears through dimensions. The Golden Lion King, having recovered his strength, recognizes Xiao Chen and attacks with the Basde Ancient Spear. As the Asura King and Golden Lion King unite, nearly twenty foreign kings close in, forcing Keke to deploy Paradise Lost. The chapter ends with the Nightmare King's ominous approach, shaking the very sky with his footsteps.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The cultivation ladder in the death world mirrors the mortal realms: Yellow Diamond corresponds to the Fish-Leap realm, and the Perfected Man realm (至人境界) represents a qualitative leap where the body and soul achieve near-perfection. Xiao Chen has one foot into this realm, granting him a decisive edge against ordinary kings. The Lingxi Sword Ripples (灵犀剑波) is a sound-based area technique inherited from the Rhino Elder, capable of shattering multiple enemies simultaneously. The Origin Eight Sounds (本源八音) are even more profound; the 'Weng' sound governs life and death, and at the Perfected Man level, its killing power becomes terrifying. The Eight Aspect Worlds (八相世界) is Xiao Chen's signature spatial ability, allowing him to trap enemies in isolated elemental realms and crush them with combined forces. The Complete Vase Seal (圆满宝瓶印) from the Pure Land can absorb and purify even deathly aura.

## Reading Guide

The battle is far from over. The Asura King's arrival signals that the most powerful foreign kings are now actively targeting Xiao Chen. The Golden Lion King's recognition means his identity will spread, drawing even more attention. Keke's Paradise Lost is a powerful defense, but it cannot hold indefinitely against so many foes. The Nightmare King's footsteps foretell a confrontation that may push Xiao Chen to fully cross into the Perfected Man realm. Readers should watch for Xiao Chen's next move: will he break through under pressure, or will he use the chaos he has sown to escape and regroup? The fate of the Bone King Banner and the collected king corpses also promises further evolution.
