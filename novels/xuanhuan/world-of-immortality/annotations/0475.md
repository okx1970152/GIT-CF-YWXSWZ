## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges into the heart of the Valley of Buried Weapons (葬兵谷), a cursed realm where sentient ancestral god weapons clash for supremacy. The Martial Imprint, merged with the primordial divine tree, unleashes staggering power against the bronze bagua, chaos war halberd, and a mysterious war-sword wielder. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen finds himself ambushed by an old ally—the Golden Divine Halberd—disguised as a golden dragon. What begins as a tense confrontation turns into a hurried attempt to escape the valley as the world's power reasserts itself, sealing the only hidden passage. In the chaos, Xiao Chen makes a stunning discovery: half a stone skull, unmistakably from the same mysterious stone man whose fragments he has been collecting. This find reignites his determination to uncover the truth behind the stone man and its connection to the ancestral gods.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Martial Imprint merging with the three-hundred-zhang divine tree, becoming a fully awakened ancestral god weapon with a will of its own. Despite its boastful demeanor, it is immediately besieged by multiple ancestral god weapons: the bronze bagua's eight mystic symbols, the chaos war halberd, and the war-sword wielder, aided by two colossal beasts—an ink qilin and a three-headed nine-tailed dragon. The battle is fierce, with the Martial Imprint holding its own but slowly being pushed back. Then, a stone drill strikes it from the shadows, dealing a heavy blow. The stone weapons are revealed to be of the same origin as the stone man fragments.

In the second valley, Xiao Chen is stealthily attacked by a golden dragon-like creature—revealed to be the Golden Divine Halberd in disguise, accompanied by the Dark-Iron Seal. They claim they only wanted to help Xiao Chen and Bai Qi escape, having evolved to half-ancestor level over a century in this valley. However, before they can leave, the secret passage they know is sealed by the valley's world power. Xiao Chen, searching the debris, picks up a small stone that turns out to be half a stone skull—unmistakably from the stone man. This discovery suggests that the stone man's fragments are deliberately scattered across the most dangerous places. The chapter ends with the valley's mysteries deepening, and Xiao Chen more determined than ever to explore further.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'Valley of Buried Weapons' (葬兵谷) is a classic xianxia trope: a sealed land where legendary weapons are imprisoned or lie dormant, often containing the remnants of ancient battles between supreme beings. Here, the valley actually houses sentient ancestral god weapons—objects that have developed consciousness and the ability to cultivate, reaching half-ancestor realm. The Martial Imprint's merger with the divine tree mirrors the concept of a 'tool spirit' or 器灵, where a weapon gains a soul. The stone drill and stone man fragments connect to a broader mystery: the nine fragments of a stone man were divided among ancestral gods, suggesting a being of tremendous power. The discovery of half a stone skull here indicates that this valley may hold more such fragments. The golden divine halberd and dark-iron seal's evolution from mere weapons to half-ancestor beings showcases the potential for artifacts to attain near-divine status through accumulation of power in a spiritually rich environment.

## Reading Guide
Readers should pay close attention to the stone man mystery—this is clearly a major arc accumulating momentum. The half stone skull is the first cranial fragment found, and its location hints that other fragments may be hidden in similarly dangerous, sealed realms. The Martial Imprint's battle also sets the stage for potential conflict between the martial path and the weapon spirits. Xiao Chen's relationship with the Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal remains ambiguous: they are allies but their motivations are self-serving. The sealing of the secret passage forces Xiao Chen deeper into the valley, promising more confrontations and discoveries. The upcoming chapters are likely to explore the origin of the valley's world power and the full purpose of the stone man. Keep an eye on the bronze bagua and chaos war halberd—they may become future enemies or unexpected sources of information.
