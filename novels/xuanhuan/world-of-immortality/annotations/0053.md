## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, one-sided duel that cements Xiao Chen’s reputation as an unstoppable force, while simultaneously exposing the political machinations churning beneath Dragon Island’s surface. The battle between Xiao Chen and Kaeo is less a contest than an execution of dominance, with Xiao Chen systematically dismantling his opponent’s body and pride. Yet the real conflict simmers in the sidelines: Yaluode’s shameless attempt to stack the odds, Zhao Lin’er’s cold arrival with the Treefolk Valley alliance, and the emergence of new players like the Flying Cloud Alliance. The chapter deftly balances visceral combat with the growing web of alliances and enmities that define life on Dragon Island, reminding readers that in this world, strength alone determines survival, but politics can poison the well.

## Key Plot Points

Yaluode arrives just before the duel to interrupt, hoping to save Kaeo from certain humiliation. He orchestrates a despicable move: having a spellmaster from the Natural Alliance, Dimansi, volunteer to fight alongside Kaeo against Xiao Chen, twisting Xiao Chen’s own words against him. The shamelessness of this tactic draws jeers from the crowd, but the Natural Alliance, Treefolk Valley (led by Zhao Lin’er), and the newly introduced Flying Cloud Alliance (led by Li Miao) all formally consent to allow the two-on-one fight. However, Kaeo, driven by pride, roars at Dimansi to back down, threatening to kill him if he interferes. The farce ends, and the true duel begins. Kaeo unleashes his beast-soul manifestation—a massive energy wolf and tiger—and charges with savage fury. Xiao Chen meets him with cold precision, matching his power blow for blow. Though Kaeo fights with desperation, Xiao Chen’s superior strength and technique quickly dominate. He flings Kaeo repeatedly, breaking his arms and then his legs in a methodical, almost surgical dismantling. The battle ends with Kaeo crippled on the ground, a stark message to all watching: Xiao Chen is not to be challenged lightly, and his enemies will pay in flesh.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases the “beast-soul warrior” archetype common in xianxia, where practitioners fuse with ancient beast spirits to gain immense physical power and energy projections. Kaeo manifests a wolf and tiger, representing ferocity and strength. The energy beasts are not mere illusions but tangible manifestations of his refined beast-souls. The duel also highlights the importance of “face” in cultivation societies. Yaluode’s attempt to gang up on Xiao Chen is seen as shameless because it violates the implicit honor code of single combat. The crowd’s jeers show that even in a survival-of-the-fittest world, reputation matters. Furthermore, alliances like the Natural, Treefolk Valley, and Flying Cloud are not permanent blocs but fluid coalitions driven by self-interest. Zhao Lin’er’s support of the underhanded tactic reveals her continued vendetta against Xiao Chen, using political channels rather than personal combat.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for escalation. Xiao Chen’s decisive victory will undoubtedly draw more attention—both respect and envy. The alliances now see him as a primary threat or a valuable asset. Yaluode’s humiliation may push him toward more desperate schemes. Zhao Lin’er’s open alignment with the Natural Alliance signals that she intends to use political power to destroy Xiao Chen rather than face him directly. The mysterious Li Miao and the Flying Cloud Alliance bear watching: are they genuine allies of Yaluode, or do they have their own agenda? Xiao Chen’s path grows more dangerous as his fame spreads. Readers should anticipate that the next confrontation will not be a simple duel but a coordinated assault. Also, the condition of Kaeo—crippled but alive—may lead to a future revenge arc.
