## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a quiet interlude of wonder and revelation between storms. After the bloody battles and political scheming of recent chapters, Xiao Chen and his allies witness the birth of two Companion Dragon Kings: the Azure Dragon King from the Storm Dragon egg and the Verdant Dragon King from the Sword Dragon egg. The event is both a reward for earlier risks and a harbinger of new power dynamics. The chapter also underscores Xiao Chen's mature generosity: he deliberately offers these mythical beings to his allies Liu Mu and Yizhen, strengthening their bonds while ensuring the young dragons are raised by capable masters. Meanwhile, the ever-curious Keke treats the newborn dragon kings as playthings, asserting her own enigmatic superiority. The chapter closes with a somber observation of the distant Bone Sea, where a cataclysmic battle still rages, reminding readers that the island's deeper mysteries remain unresolved.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen reveals that he had hidden three dragon eggs in the snow: a Storm Dragon egg, a Sword Dragon egg, and a Thunder Dragon egg. To his surprise, the Storm and Sword eggs emanate strong life force, indicating imminent hatching. Liu Mu and Yizhen enter the ice cave to witness the births, each emerging with a newly hatched dragon king. The Azure Dragon King (from the Storm Dragon egg) bonds with Liu Mu, while the Verdant Dragon King (from the Sword Dragon egg) bonds with Yizhen. Both young dragons display unmistakable signs of kingly lineage: their dragon heads resemble the Ancestral Dragon, and they immediately absorb vast quantities of spiritual energy. The Thunder Dragon egg, however, proves lifeless—likely its essence was siphoned by the two emergent dragon kings—disappointing Liu Ruyan, who had hoped to claim one. Keke, unfazed, playfully leaps onto the dragon kings, annoying them with her superior speed and agility. The party then leaves the snowy mountains and gazes toward the distant Bone Sea, still shrouded in blood-red light and the echoes of an earth-shaking battle.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The birth of a Dragon King is a pivotal event in the mythology of 'World of Immortality'. In xianxia tradition, dragons are not merely beasts but primordial entities tied to the laws of heaven and earth. A Companion Dragon King (伴生龙王) is born alongside the Ancestral Dragon and is its natural rival; such a creature is nearly as powerful as the Ancestor itself. The process of hatching here highlights the concept of 'first impression imprinting': the first being a newborn dragon sees becomes its lifelong companion or master. This echoes folkloric motifs where a creature imprints upon its caretaker. Additionally, the absorption of boundless spiritual energy upon birth is a common trope in Chinese cultivation fiction, signifying a being's innate connection to the cosmos. The failure of the Thunder Dragon egg to hatch is attributed to the other two dragon kings competing for the same ambient energy, illustrating the ruthless survival logic of the xianxia world—only the strongest thrive.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major power shift on Dragon Island. Liu Mu and Yizhen now possess infant dragon kings that, if raised well, will become devastating assets. Xiao Chen's decision to give away these treasures enriches his alliances and reinforces his shrewd, generous nature. Readers should watch for how the dragon kings develop and whether the mysterious figure who bonded with the Purple Dragon King will become a future ally or rival. The unresolved battle at the Bone Sea looms as a threat and a source of answers: what are the gods and dead city fighting over? The empty Thunder Dragon egg also leaves a question mark—did the two dragon kings drain it, or was it always weak? Finally, Keke's effortless dominance over the young dragons hints at her own far greater origins, a thread that will surely be explored.
