## Chapter Overview
This chapter shifts the focus from Xiao Chen's relentless night raid to an explosive confrontation at the beast-fighting arena, where Keke faces a relentless stream of powerful opponents. What begins as a comedic romp—Keke tossing an arrogant old crone like a ragdoll—quickly escalates into a mythic clash of divine proportions. The Golden Lion King, a sacred beast of terrifying pedigree, unleashes a forbidden ritual to open its third eye, triggering an ancient sacrificial ceremony that threatens to annihilate everything. In response, Keke undergoes a startling transformation, casting off its playful demeanor to reveal a solemn, godlike presence wielding dual beams of black and white light. The chapter masterfully balances crowd-pleasing spectacle with deep lore revelations, hinting at Keke's origins and the cosmic forces at play in the World of Immortality.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a crone (虎奴) descending from the sky on thunder and lightning, assumed to be a Sky-Mastery expert. She immediately attacks Keke with nine crescent-shaped light blades, but Keke retaliates with twenty-nine beams of seven-colored radiance, shattering the blades and sweeping the crone out of the sky. Keke then uses a dragon race divine art—binding, sweeping, and killing—to slam the crone into the earth repeatedly, humiliating her before the crowd. The six arena guardians finally intervene, having deliberately let the crone suffer for her arrogance. The crowd is electrified, and observers—including Dugu Jianmo, Yan Qingcheng, Zhuge Liang, Kanasi, and others—are stunned by Keke's power, which they had previously underestimated.

In the second half, the Golden Lion King takes the stage. Refusing to leave, it prepares a drastic measure: it seals its Destruction Eye and Reincarnation Eye, transferring their accumulated power to its third, unopened eye. As the third eye begins to crack open, ancient chanting, prayers, and sacrificial sounds echo through the arena, creating an eerie ritualistic atmosphere. Keke becomes dazed, as if the ancient chants unlock something within it. Suddenly, Keke's demeanor shifts to profound solemnity, and its eyes emit two beams: one pitch-black like underworld light, the other blazing white like stellar radiance. The two beams tear through space, heralding a mysterious awakening. The chapter ends with the Golden Lion King's third eye possibly fully opened, but Keke's own eyes have become terrifying, suggesting a clash of primordial powers.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter provides a deeper look at the nature of divine arts and cultivation realms. The crone is assumed to be at the Sky-Mastery (御空) realm, the third of the Five Great Realms, allowing flight and control over natural forces. However, her defeat by Keke, who is still in the Spirit-Treasury realm, illustrates the power of unique inherited divine arts. As an old observer notes, rare bloodline-based divine arts can allow a weaker cultivator to challenge a higher realm opponent—this is the only way to fight across levels. The Golden Lion King's three eyes draw on two distinct divine powers: Destruction (毁灭) and Reincarnation (轮回). The sacrifice of sealing these eyes to open the third is reminiscent of ancient sacrificial rites, where offerings and ritual chants summon primordial forces. The chanting and prayers heard during the third eye's emergence suggest the Golden Lion King's race may have once been worshipped as gods, tying into the broader mythology of the World of Immortality. Keke's awakening with black and white beams evokes the dualistic cosmic forces of yin and yang or creation and destruction, hinting at its own celestial origin.

The Golden Lion King's third eye is a wildcard—its full opening could bring catastrophic consequences. Additionally, the reactions of Dugu Jianmo, Yan Qingcheng, and other familiar faces hint at how Keke's display will affect the political landscape of Heavenly Emperor City. The upcoming beast-fighting tournament may be upended by this revelation. Expect a continuation of high-stakes combat intertwined with mythic lore.
