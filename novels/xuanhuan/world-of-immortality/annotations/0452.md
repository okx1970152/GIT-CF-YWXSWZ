## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, high-stakes battle as Xiao Chen faces an orchestrated ambush. The Sun Religion’s five hundred Solar Knights and the Tiger Clan’s five hundred elite warriors activate ancient divine formations, merging their collective will into two towering war god avatars—a golden solar giant and a silver-white tiger-human hybrid—each comparable to a Half-Ancestor. The confrontation shatters Xiao Chen’s iconic stone statue, a cornerstone of his power since the Dragon Island era, but he immediately reveals a new asset: the Iron Battle Armor gifted by the Dead World sovereign. What follows is an explosive counterattack that demonstrates Xiao Chen has not weakened, but grown stronger, cutting down the war god avatars and even grievously wounding the Asura Half-Ancestor in a single, devastating exchange. The atmosphere is one of desperate fury and calculated aggression—Xiao Chen is no longer fleeing; he is reclaiming his status.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with five hundred Solar Knights forming the Golden War God, its spear splitting the sky. Xiao Chen meets the attack head-on, severing the god’s weapon with twenty-seven War Swords. The Tiger Clan then unveils its own divine formation, creating a silver-white Tiger-Man war god, and together the two giants pin Xiao Chen in a pincer. In the prolonged exchange, Xiao Chen’s stone statue—long his ultimate weapon—suffers catastrophic damage and finally crumbles to dust. At this crucial moment, the Asura Half-Ancestor and the Skeleton King descend to join the kill, confident of a swift victory. But Xiao Chen, now clad in the Iron Battle Armor, charges the Asura Half-Ancestor and nearly bisects him with a single sword stroke, shocking all observers. He then turns on the weakened war god avatars, dissolving them with pure killing intent and proceeding to decimate the Solar Knights and Tiger Clan elites, slaying two hundred before the remaining Half-Ancestors can react. This chapter marks the destruction of a treasured relic but also establishes the Iron Battle Armor as a formidable replacement and reasserts Xiao Chen’s combat dominance after a century and a half of absence.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of combining multiple cultivators into a single giant avatar via an ancient formation is a classic trope in xianxia, symbolizing the power of unity and collective will. The five hundred Solar Knights originate from the Solar Church, a major Western cult in the World of Immortality. The Tiger Clan’s formation mirrors this, indicating a cross-faction alliance. Half-Ancestors (半祖) are beings just below Ancestral God level; their intervention here reveals the deep enmity against Xiao Chen. The destruction of the stone statue—long associated with Xiao Chen’s comatose state on Dragon Island—carries heavy symbolic weight, signaling his transition from reliance on ancient relics to independent Martial Body strength. The Iron Battle Armor (冥铁战衣) is a relic from the Dead World’s sovereign, embodying deathly yet supremely durable power. This chapter also showcases the martial pinnacle of the ‘Hand of the Supreme Being’ and the ‘Eight Origin Sounds’ integrating with Xiao Chen’s unique path.

## Reading Guide

With the stone statue gone and the Iron Battle Armor proven effective, Xiao Chen’s arsenal has shifted dramatically. The Asura Half-Ancestor and Skeleton King remain formidable threats—while Xiao Chen wounded Asura, he did not kill him, and both are likely to return with reinforcements. The Tiger Clan and Sun Religion have suffered major losses but will not abandon their vendetta. The chapter ends with tension unresolved, setting the stage for further escalation. The next chapters may reveal more about the Half-Ancestors’ coalition, or perhaps the arrival of other powers intrigued by Xiao Chen’s return. Additionally, Keke, the Inverse Dragon King, and other allies remain off-stage; their reactions to this battle could reshape the conflict. This battle also marks Xiao Chen’s open declaration of war against the entire Hundred Races coalition—a gamble that will test his newfound strength to its limits.
