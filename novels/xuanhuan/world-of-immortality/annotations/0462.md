## Chapter Overview

In this harrowing chapter, Xiao Chen battles a monstrous fire‑seed creature in the underground palace of the Death World, a vast cavern dominated by a roaring river of yellow corpse‑water. The monster, a four‑armed, scorpion‑tailed skeleton, has been guarding the palace and answers to a far more terrifying presence upstream. When that dread entity falls into slumber, the monster grows bolder, unleashing its full arsenal—thousands of illusory bodies and a nascent Grand Extinction Spear. Xiao Chen counters with thirteen War Swords and the Seven‑Colored Sacred Tree, but the monster's mental attack nearly overwhelms him. At the climax, a second will seizes the monster's body: the Otherworld Ancestral God Babra has awakened from his ancient seal, possessing the creature and mocking Xiao Chen as a mere insect. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen's burning hatred, as he learns that Babra was responsible for the death of the human Ancestral God Wu Zu.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the monster summoning the power of the corpse river to trap Xiao Chen, but a sudden stillness signals the sleeping entity upstream. Relieved, the monster focuses entirely on killing Xiao Chen. It uses 'Thousand Illusions Myriad Manifestations' to generate hundreds of identical copies, each a blood‑drop projection. Xiao Chen's thirteen War Swords sweep through them, but the monster's true body lunges with four bone spears and a scorpion tail. The Sacred Tree's light deflects the spears, yet the monster's mental attack—two green beams from its eyes—pierces Xiao Chen's divine consciousness, causing searing pain. Xiao Chen wields the Sacred Tree to repel the mental assault and unleashes the Ancestral God essence stored within, forcing the monster back. In that moment of vulnerability, a hidden soul emerges: the Otherworld Ancestral God Babra, whose residual will had been sealed within the monster. Babra takes control, laughing at the carnage, and reveals that he once grievously wounded the Ancestral God Youchao and orchestrated the death of Wu Zu. Xiao Chen, learning that Wu Zu fell because of Babra's schemes, is consumed by vengeful fury.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces a new type of antagonist: an Otherworld Ancestral God (异界祖神), a being of supreme power from a hostile realm. In the novel's cosmology, Ancestral Gods (祖神) are the apex of cultivation, above even Half‑Ancestors. The human Ancestral Gods—Suiren, Youchao, Fuxi, Shennong—fought a cataclysmic war against the Otherworld to protect the Nine Provinces. Babra's claim that he wounded Youchao and caused Wu Zu's death ties directly into that ancient conflict. The 'Grand Extinction Spear' (大破灭战矛) is a legendary artifact said to be wielded by Otherworld Ancestral Gods; its phantom here shows its terrifying reach. Xiao Chen's War Swords and the Seven‑Colored Sacred Tree are his primary advantages, but even they strain against a true Ancestral God's will. The mental attack ('鼠目寸光') is a lowbrow yet deadly technique that binds the opponent's spirit, a reminder that in high‑level combat, spirit cultivation is as crucial as physical power.

## Reading Guide

The appearance of Babra raises the stakes for Xiao Chen's journey. Until now, he has fought Half‑Ancestors and kings; now he faces a remnant of an Ancestral God. This encounter deepens his personal vendetta, as Babra admits to engineering Wu Zu's fall—a hero Xiao Chen venerates. Readers should watch how Xiao Chen uses the stored Ancestral God essence from the Nether King; it may be crucial against Babra's remnant will. Additionally, the corpse river's upstream entity remains dormant but is clearly a major power; it could awake later to threaten both Xiao Chen and Babra. The chapter also reinforces the novel's theme of generational vengeance: Xiao Chen's hatred for the Otherworld mirrors the ancient war, and his resolve to 'skin Babra alive' signals a shift from defensive survival to active crusade.
