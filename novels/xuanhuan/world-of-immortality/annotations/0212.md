## Chapter Overview
This chapter thrusts readers into the heart of the Southern Wastes as Xiao Chen and Niu Ren approach the legendary Barbarian Sacred Mountain. Known as the Heavenly Crucible once forged by the Ancestral God Suiren, the mountain is a desolate yet awe-inspiring monument to a primordial age of divine artifice. The tone is one of mythic dread and sublime scale, as the travelers cross cursed plains and haunted mountains, witnessing firsthand the lingering dangers of ancient wars. At the same time, the chapter deepens Niu Ren’s character, revealing his cultivation path and his desperate need for a powerful beast soul to continue his growth. The journey culminates in a terrifying encounter with battling saint souls, forcing the pair to seek refuge in an eerily silent green oasis on the mountain’s flank, a sanctuary that exudes its own foreboding mystery.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a sweeping description of the Southern Wastes, a vast territory dotted with ancient tribes and rife with unknown dangers. Xiao Chen and Niu Ren travel for two days, visiting several old tribes and meeting with survivors from Dragon Island. The Black Dragon King carries them swiftly, passing the ominous Crimson Blood Plains—where Niu Ren once narrowly escaped a blood corpse ambush—and the Blue Mist Mountains, another forbidden zone. Upon reaching the Barbarian Sacred Mountain, Xiao Chen is struck by its impossible scale and contradictions: it radiates colorful light, yet up close it is barren and bloodstained, with patches of golden glow, black abysses, and green oases. Niu Ren explains the mountain is a failed crucible forged by Suiren, later becoming a haven for powerful saint souls. They ascend carefully, but soon a maelstrom of dark energy descends as several immense saint souls battle, forcing them to flee sideways. The Black Dragon King bolts into a silent, ancient forest—a green oasis that repels the dark waves. This sanctuary, however, is unnaturally quiet, and the saints souls avoid it, raising new questions about its nature. Xiao Chen resolves to remain cautious, but the chapter ends with them trapped in this eerie grove, aware that greater dangers lie ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Sacred Mountain is central to the novel’s mythic backdrop, echoing the Chinese legend of the Divine Farmer and other Ancestral Gods. The concept of a “Heavenly Crucible” that can refine the world is reminiscent of alchemical traditions in Chinese mysticism, where cauldrons and furnaces are used for spiritual transmutation. The beast soul cultivation method of the barbarian tribes differs from mainstream cultivators; Niu Ren’s fusion of multiple beasts souls into one is a risky but powerful path. The Crimson Blood Plains and Blue Mist Mountains draw on folklore about battlefields polluted by divine blood or residual malice, requiring taboos like avoiding travel at night. The presence of saint souls—residual consciousnesses of ancient powerhouses—driven to the mountain echoes the Buddhist concept of ‘residual karma’ and the Taoist idea of ‘incorruptible souls’ seeking a sacred vessel. This chapter grounds the world in a rich tapestry of mythological influences, from ancient engineering to spectral possession.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a major exploration arc within the Barbarian Sacred Mountain, which holds the potential for both immense treasures and lethal threats. Keep an eye on the green oasis: its ability to repel the saint souls suggests it is either an even more formidable stronghold or a tomb for an entity that surpasses them. Niu Ren’s quest for a prime beast soul will likely drive him deeper, and his connection to the Black Dragon King may prove crucial. Xiao Chen’s observations of the mountain’s structure—its patches of gold, black voids, and bloodstains—hint at the crucible’s incomplete refining process, possibly hiding sealed artifacts or remnants of Suiren’s power. The mention of other tribes and the Rigen family’s involvement from previous chapters may also come into play. Readers should anticipate a harrowing mix of survival, tactical cunning, and mythic revelation as the duo probes the heart of this ancient forge.
