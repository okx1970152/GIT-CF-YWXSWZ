## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges readers into the heart of Dragon Island's greatest mystery—the Sacred Mountain. The atmosphere is thick with dread and uncanny allure as Xiao Chen, the three skeleton kings, and Keke stand at the foot of a mountain that swallows beings without a trace. The Lion King Dragon's vanishing act sets the tone: power means nothing against this place. Xiao Chen's curiosity nearly costs him his life when he steps into an invisible abyss, saved only by Keke's quick divine intervention. The chapter balances awe and terror, reminding us that even the strongest can be humbled by the unknown. The reappearance of the debilitated Lion King Dragon only deepens the enigma, hinting at forces beyond mortal comprehension.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the eerie disappearance of the Lion King Dragon into the Sacred Mountain, a sight that unsettles even the skeleton kings, whose lotus brands flare in warning. Keke's instinctive rush toward the mountain and her unusual stillness at its base signal that something profound awaits. Xiao Chen discovers massive stone steles carved with dragon images at the foot of the mountain, each depicting a different dragon king lineage. His rational mind sets a boundary, but the mountain's pull overcomes him—he advances further, drawn to an ancient palace. Twice he experiences a terrifying sensation of falling into a lightless abyss, as if descending into hell itself. Each time, Keke's radiant divine light drags him back to safety. Faced with this inexplicable danger, Xiao Chen makes the prudent decision to retreat, acknowledging that the Sacred Mountain is a place of death and mystery beyond his current ability to explore. The Lion King Dragon then reappears, weakened and fleeing, confirming the mountain's draining power. Keke's fearless reaction to the dragon demonstrates her extraordinary nature.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Sacred Mountain in this chapter draws on ancient Chinese myths of forbidden mountains that serve as gateways to the underworld or prisons for divine beings. The sensation of falling into an abyss is reminiscent of descriptions of 'Nine Serenities' or the 'Nine Netherworlds' in Daoist cosmology—realms of death and darkness. The stone steles carved with primordial dragons evoke the tradition of stele inscriptions used to seal or record cosmic events. In the world of xianxia, such mountains often contain ancient formations or seals that restrain powerful entities. The fact that the Lion King Dragon loses vitality after entering suggests the mountain acts as a drain on life force or suppresses divine power, similar to the island's overarching seal on dragon abilities. Keke's immunity to the mountain's pull and her ability to rescue Xiao Chen with divine light mark her as a being of extraordinary origin, possibly connected to the Cosmic Tree and the primal forces of this world.

## Reading Guide
This chapter raises more questions than it answers, setting up the Sacred Mountain as a central mystery for future arcs. Readers should also note the dragon steles' carvings—they may encode historical knowledge about the island's sealed dragons. As Xiao Chen wisely retreats, the tension builds: he must grow stronger before he can unlock the mountain's truths. The following chapters are likely to focus on his cultivation and preparation for a return.
