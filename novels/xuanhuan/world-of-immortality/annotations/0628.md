## Chapter Overview

The inscription on the tombstones—'Discuss the Dao in life, contend for supremacy under heaven; discuss corpses in death, claim dominance in the tomb'—becomes stunning reality as ancient kings and corpses gather at the seven tombs of the Little Stone Emperor. This chapter brings together stone man kings, Otherworld ancestors, and notorious powerhouses like Lishishou, Shishi, and Xuan Kong to debate the highest mysteries of cultivation with the lingering imprint of the legendary Little Stone Emperor. For Xiao Chen, Keke, and the old Taoist, this is a rare and transcendent opportunity to listen to the inscrutable principles of the Dao. The tone is solemn and awe-inspired, emphasizing the grandeur of the moment and the profound implications for Xiao Chen's cultivation path.

## Key Plot Points

The seven tombs of the Little Stone Emperor manifest their peculiar power as the inscribed words come true: the five stone man kings of the Chaotic Land, Otherworld ancestor Lu Zhan, and other ancient corpses appear, seated on green stones behind the tombs. The three aggressive powerhouses—Lishishou, Shishi, and Xuan Kong—attempt to seize the tombs' treasures but are each repelled by powerful stone weapons (the Moon Shadow Magic Blade, an emperor stone sword) that fly out of the tombs. Finally, the Little Stone Emperor's Three Sovereign Mirror emerges, surrounded by his seven innate demon stars, causing even the strongest to hesitate. A formal Dao discussion begins with the seven kings and seven corpses, while Xiao Chen, Keke, and the old Taoist immerse themselves in the profound principles. The discussion covers the nature of the Dao—whether it is formless or tangible—allowing Xiao Chen to contemplate his future direction. He stands at a crossroads between the Stone Man path and the Supreme Ancestral God path, and the insights gained here will be decisive.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of 'discussing corpses' (论尸) after death is a striking xianxia motif: even beyond the grave, the contention for supremacy continues. The Little Stone Emperor is a legendary figure who created the Three Sovereign Mirror, one of the three supreme divine artifacts of the world, and his seven demon stars are innate to his left palm. The gathering of stone man kings and Otherworld ancestors underscores the high stakes of the Stone Man path—a route to near-invincibility that requires immense sacrifice. The Dao discussion itself reflects real philosophical debates in Chinese thought: Is the Dao formless and elusive, or tangible and patterned? These differing views are essential for understanding cultivation breakthroughs in the World of Immortality. The presence of the 'five stone man kings of the Chaotic Land' also hints at a deeper history of fallen civilizations.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a major turning point for world-building and philosophical depth. Pay close attention to the various perspectives on the Dao presented by the kings; they will inform Xiao Chen's eventual choice between the Stone Man and Supreme Ancestral God paths. The appearance of the Three Sovereign Mirror and the seven tombs hints at future conflicts and treasures. Note that the ancient powers seem unaware of Xiao Chen and his group, suggesting the mysterious, isolated nature of the discussion space. The chapter sets up major revelations to come—watch for how these insights affect Xiao Chen's cultivation and his ability to confront the Otherworld threats. Also, the Little Stone Emperor's imprint is shown to be immensely powerful even in death, raising questions about his true fate and the nature of the 'final step.'
