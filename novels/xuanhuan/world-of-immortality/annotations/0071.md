## Chapter Overview

This chapter strikes a rare emotional chord in the desolate mythic tapestry of Dragon Island. Xiao Chen and his skeleton companions follow the little white beast Keke back to its birthplace — the colossal stump of the Cosmic Tree. What begins as a homecoming quickly turns into a revelation of loss. Keke, normally playful and mischievous, experiences a pang of sorrow as it realizes its parents are absent. The chapter balances this poignant vulnerability with the awe-inspiring discovery of Keke's origins: it hatched from a rainbow divine jade egg atop the severed World Tree. Xiao Chen, ever the pragmatic survivor, shows genuine tenderness as he comforts Keke and collects the eggshell fragments as keepsakes. Meanwhile, the nearby presence of three king-lineage dragons — a Tyrant Dragon, a Lion King Dragon, and a Sword Dragon — underscores the danger of this primal territory, adding tension to the emotional core.

## Key Plot Points

Keke's return to the Cosmic Tree stump is the central event. The little beast, usually a terror to skeletons and a glutton for treasures, curls into a whimpering ball upon finding its home empty of parents. This display of vulnerability deepens Xiao Chen's bond with Keke, as he consoles it and declares them family in a world where both are orphans. Through gesture and deduction, Xiao Chen learns that Keke hatched alone from a rainbow egg, survived by drinking dew and feeding on the leaves of the Sacred Sapling — which itself is a new shoot from the Cosmic Tree's root. The discovery of the broken eggshells, described as translucent divine jade shimmering with multicolored light, confirms Keke's extraordinary origin. Additionally, the chapter introduces the geography of the inner island: the Cosmic Tree stump is surrounded by a den of ferocious beasts, including three apex dragons that coexist in uneasy peace. Their roars shake the mountains, a stark reminder of the power that guards this place. Xiao Chen's decision to collect the eggshells as a keepsake shows his growing attachment, while his cautious observation of the dragons signals future challenges.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Cosmic Tree (通天神树) is a staple of Chinese mythological geography, often described as a pillar connecting heaven and earth. Its depiction here as a severed stump hints at a catastrophic event in Dragon Island's ancient past — possibly related to the divine stele's sealing power. The Sacred Sapling growing from its root embodies the xianxia motif of rebirth from decay, a form of nirvana or metamorphosis. Keke's birth from a rainbow jade egg places it in the lineage of divine beasts like qilin or phoenixes, creatures born from elemental essences rather than mammalian reproduction. Its use of the sapling's leaves as food is reminiscent of the mythical 'divine bamboo' or 'jade fruit' that sustains immortals. The three dragons glimpsed at the chapter's end — the Tyrant Dragon (暴龙), Lion King Dragon (狮王龙), and Sword Dragon (剑龙) — are not mere monsters but represent specific dragon lineages with distinct powers. The Lion King Dragon, with its bone-spike mane and crocodile tail, is a rare and potent breed. Their peaceful cohabitation suggests they are bound by some greater law or protection, possibly as guardians of the Cosmic Tree or Keke itself.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as an emotional pivot, revealing the hidden depth of Keke's character and the mystery of its lineage. Readers should pay attention to the eggshells — they may become a plot device later, as Xiao Chen carefully preserves them. The revelation that the Sacred Sapling is a sprout from the Cosmic Tree elevates its significance beyond a mere treasure plant; it may hold keys to Dragon Island's history and the sealing of the dragons. The introduction of three king-lineage dragons in close proximity sets the stage for future conflict or alliance. Xiao Chen's growing attachment to Keke introduces a potential vulnerability, but also a powerful ally as Keke's true nature unfolds. The question remains: why were Keke's parents absent, and what happened to the Cosmic Tree? These threads portend a deeper exploration of the island's primordial war and the fate of its divine inhabitants.
