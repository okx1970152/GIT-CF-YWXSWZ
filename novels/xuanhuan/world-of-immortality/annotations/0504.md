## Chapter Overview

This chapter brings a dramatic confrontation between Xiao Chen and the Otherworld Supreme Ancestral Gods, a tier of power far beyond the Half-Ancestors. Fleeing through the future world of the Eternal Continent, Xiao Chen wields the newly manifested broken formation diagram from the Heavenly Stele, a fragmentary artifact that can block even a Supreme Ancestral God's assault. Despite his immense growth, he is brutally wounded, saved only by the sacred Hope passage and a strand of Eternal Light. The chapter balances epic-scale cosmic combat with Xiao Chen's sobering realization of the gap between him and the highest powers. It sets a new benchmark for the series' power scale and Xiao Chen's determination.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the broken formation diagram manifesting, causing tremors across the Eternal Continent and stunning the three supreme ancestral weapons. The Emperor City warns Xiao Chen not to confront the three Supreme Ancestral Gods directly, as they are above even normal Ancestral Gods. The city attempts to flee with Xiao Chen across the Ancient God Desert and the Western Mountains, but the three weapons pursue relentlessly. Xiao Chen decides to summon the "Hope" gate using Keke's mother's method to leave that future timeline. However, one of the Supreme Ancestral Gods temporarily freezes the gate, intending to kill Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen uses the broken diagram to defend himself, and it withstands the first blow from the Demonic Blade. The Supreme Ancestral God then gathers the soul power of countless stars to empower his weapon, but Xiao Chen supplements the incomplete diagram with his own body, fusing with it. The collision shatters Xiao Chen's physical form, but the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree and a strand of Eternal Light protect his flesh and spirit. He barely escapes through the Hope gate as it breaks free. Back in the mortal world, Xiao Chen reassembles his body, now partially merged with the Eternal Light and the formation diagram. He resolves to break through to the Eighth or Ninth Heaven of Half-Ancestor, realizing the immense gap between him and the Supreme Ancestral Gods.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of "Supreme Ancestral Gods" (上祖神) is introduced here, a tier above ordinary Ancestral Gods, comparable to the primordial powers that once battled the ancestors like Suiren and Fuxi. The "broken formation diagram" (残破阵图) appears to be a fragment of a greater primordial artifact, possibly linked to the Heavenly Steles or the creation of worlds. Its ability to block a Supreme Ancestral God's attack underscores its legendary origin. The "Hope" gate (希望之门) is a spatial-temporal passage taught by Keke's mother, allowing travel between different timelines or realms. "Eternal Light" (永恒之光) is a legendary substance formed from the death of multiple Ancestral Gods; here, it merges with Xiao Chen to protect his soul and begin fusing with the formation diagram. The chapter emphasizes that even at Half-Ancestor level, the gap to the highest beings is vast, and only through supreme treasures and fusion can a Half-Ancestor survive.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen has finally encountered beings that dwarf the half-ancestors he has been fighting. The broken formation diagram is now tied to him, and his body begins to act as a supplement to it, hinting at a future where he may become the complete diagram or wield its full power. His resolve to reach Half-Ancestor Eighth or Ninth Heaven sets up the next phase of cultivation. The reappearance of the Eternal Light and its fusion with him suggests a path to power that may allow him to truly threaten the Otherworld Supreme Ancestral Gods. Readers should watch for how this fusion affects his cultivation and the formation diagram's gradual growth. The chapter also shows that the Emperor City is still active, a potential ally or resource for the future.
