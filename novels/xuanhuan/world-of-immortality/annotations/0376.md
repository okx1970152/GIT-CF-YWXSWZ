## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal shift in the Death World arc. Xiao Chen, now a Purple Diamond skeleton, abandons subtlety and unleashes a ferocious, targeted assault on the Yecha (Night Shura) clan to draw out their king. The tone is one of grim determination and brutal efficiency, as Xiao Chen methodically eliminates every Yecha warrior he encounters. The chapter also expands the scope of the Hundred Races awakening, revealing the staggering scale of the overseas powers that have settled in the Nine Provinces. The stakes escalate dramatically: Xiao Chen's friends are being hunted, and he must act decisively to protect them.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen learns that the Yecha King, calling himself the 'Heavenly King', has been hunting his allies—Liu Mu, Jin Sanyi, Niu Ren, and the Inverse Dragon King—after escaping from the God Domain. The group is missing, possibly killed. Xiao Chen decides to draw out the Yecha King by annihilating his clan's foothold in the ancient capital of Luoyang. He dons iron armor over his skeletal frame and flies to the city. Once there, he walks through the streets and systematically slaughters every Yecha he finds, starting with gate guards and progressing to a general and three high-ranking Yecha generals. The massacre is swift and clinical, with Xiao Chen using his upper hand technique to dismember and decapitate his enemies. He spares one general to deliver a message but then pursues him to a mountain hideout where three powerful Yecha generals are stationed. A fierce battle ensues, lasting half an hour, ending with the Yecha forces completely wiped out. One general uses his dying life force to communicate the event to the Yecha King, which is exactly what Xiao Chen wanted—to force the king to abandon his hunt. News of the massacre spreads, and speculation points to the War God King (Douzhan Wang), who denies involvement, leaving Xiao Chen as the prime suspect. Unsatisfied, Xiao Chen decides to escalate further by heading overseas to find the Yecha King's home island. There, he discovers a breathtaking array of colossal islands and floating cities—the true strongholds of the Hundred Races, revealing the immense power arrayed against the Nine Provinces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Yecha (夜叉) are a race from Buddhist and Hindu mythology, often depicted as fierce, demonic beings. In this novel, they are one of the many 'Hundred Races' emerging into the Nine Provinces. The title 'Heavenly King' (天王) is a classical Chinese epithet for a supremely powerful ruler, often used in Buddhist cosmology for the four guardian kings. The upper hand technique (上苍之手) is a supreme martial art derived from the Heavenly Stele, emphasizing pure physical power over magical artifacts. The Desolate (Death) World's bone-body hierarchy (purple diamond, red diamond, etc.) continues to be relevant, as Xiao Chen's skeletal form is still his primary vessel. The concept of 'life source liquid' (生命源液) and 'fire seeds' (火种) are central to power progression in the Desolate World. The appearance of dozens of island-sized territories overseas, each with its own overlord, sets up the vast geopolitical landscape of the Hundred Races, reminiscent of the ancient 'Ten Thousand Kingdoms' era in Chinese mythology.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a direct confrontation between Xiao Chen and the Yecha King, who remains off-screen but is now clearly aware of Xiao Chen's actions. The massive overseas infrastructure suggests that many more powerful enemies will emerge. Readers should watch for whether Xiao Chen's plan to divert the Yecha King succeeds, and how his friends will respond if they survive. The mention of the War God King (Douzhan Wang) as a potential ally (or rival) is significant. Xiao Chen's next move—traveling overseas—promises new territory and new dangers. The scale of the Hundred Races is now fully apparent, and Xiao Chen's quest to reclaim his flesh-and-blood body becomes even more urgent.
