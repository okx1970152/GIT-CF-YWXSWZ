## Chapter Overview
This chapter opens with the aftermath of Xiao Chen's brutal killing of the golden boy, now revealed to be a clone of the fearsome Golden Lion King. The crowd is divided—some call him a butcher, others realize the boy's true nature. Zhao Lin'er's Sun Religion is furious, but the Third Princess of Great Shang subtly supports Xiao Chen. The political tension escalates as a new Sun Knight, Puder, challenges Xiao Chen. After initial reluctance, Xiao Chen swiftly executes him when Puder resorts to a dishonorable sneak attack. The chapter closes with Zhao Lin'er personally inviting Xiao Chen, addressing him by his true name, signaling that his disguise may be wearing thin. This chapter balances intense combat with political maneuvering and sets the stage for greater conflicts among the young elites.

## Key Plot Points
1. The golden boy's head transforms into a lion's head, confirming it was a clone or incarnation of the Golden Lion King, shocking the spectators who had felt sympathy for the 'child'.
2. Zhao Lin'er coldly rebukes the Great Shang party, warning that killing the clone will bring the Golden Lion King's main body seeking revenge.
3. Niu Ren boasts that the white beast (Keke) once defeated the Golden Lion King, but the crowd remains skeptical.
4. Sun Knight Puder, the ninth expert of the Sun Religion, challenges Xiao Chen to a duel. Xiao Chen initially dismisses him but accepts after persistent provocation.
5. Xiao Chen uses the Fearless Lion Seal to summon a golden lion phantom, clashing with Puder's golden spear. He then uses the Eight Aspect Worlds for speed and the Perfect Vase Seal to neutralize Puder's techniques.
6. After disarming Puder, Xiao Chen spares him and walks away, but Puder throws his broken spear in rage. Xiao Chen turns back, bisects Puder and his bear mount with an energy sword, then uses the Lingxi Sword Wave to obliterate the remains.
7. The Sun Religion's third expert arrives too late.
8. The next day, Zhao Lin'er invites Xiao Chen to a private meeting and addresses him by his real name, Xiao Chen, implying she has seen through his alias.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of an 'incarnation' or 'clone' (分神/分身) of a powerful being is a staple in xianxia. Here, the Golden Lion King's clone is a separate entity that can be killed without destroying the main body, but doing so invites the anger of the true body. The Fearless Lion Seal technique, which Xiao Chen uses, is derived from the essence of the Golden Lion King—a Buddhist-associated beast. This aligns with the theme of Buddhist symbolism in the novel. The Sun Religion and its Knight Order evoke Western religious orders, blending Eastern and Western mythologies. The honor code of knights is contrasted with Xiao Chen's pragmatic ruthlessness; when Puder attacks from behind, he loses any claim to honorable treatment, and Xiao Chen's killing is seen as justified within the world's logic.
