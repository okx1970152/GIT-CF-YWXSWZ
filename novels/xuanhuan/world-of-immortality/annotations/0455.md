## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen, now a Perfected Man able to challenge Half-Ancestors, storms the Tiger Clan fortress and the Sun God Palace in a desperate search for Su Ying, the childhood friend who was taken hostage over a century ago. The chapter blends brutal, efficient combat with deeply personal grief, as Xiao Chen learns that Su Ying has been missing for decades. The revelation that his doppelgänger, Wu Zhanhun, rescued her and erased her painful memories brings a bittersweet reunion, forcing Xiao Chen to confront his past and choose his future path.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen arrives at the Tiger Clan fortress and demands Su Ying and Tiger Ben. He destroys the fortress with a single sword strike, killing several Fish-Leap and Perfected Man experts. The Tiger Clan elite have already fled. He then travels to the Sun God Palace, where he defeats the golden-haired Sun Knight champion, a former rival from the Roman Empire. After destroying the palace, he learns from the memories of captives that Su Ying disappeared over 150 years ago. In a snowstorm, Su Ying appears with Wu Zhanhun and the Divine Tribe youths. Her memory has been erased, and she greets Xiao Chen as a stranger. Xiao Chen realizes his clone gave her a new life. Despite his grief, he smiles and wishes her happiness, then departs with renewed determination to become stronger. Wu Zhanhun protests, but Xiao Chen has already made his decision.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter references several cultivation realms: Fish-Leap (鱼跃), Perfected Man (至人), and Earth-Penetrating (彻地). The Perfected Man realm is a major threshold, granting near-immortal life and immense power. Xiao Chen's twenty-seven War Swords are legendary weapons tied to the Heavenly Stele, each capable of cleaving mountains. The Dark-Iron Battle Suit is an Ancestral God-level defensive artifact from the Death World. The practice of erasing memories to grant a peaceful life is a profound act of mercy in xianxia, reflecting the harshness of the cultivation world. Su Ying's fate—being taken as a hostage and later rescued by a clone—highlights the interconnectedness of fate and the consequences of past actions.

## Reading Guide

This chapter closes one of Xiao Chen's longest-running emotional arcs. Readers should note how Xiao Chen's grief over Su Ying fuels his resolve to become stronger, setting the stage for his next confrontation with the Half-Ancestors. The appearance of Wu Zhanhun (the clone) continues to blur the line between Xiao Chen's past self and his current identity. The disappearance of Tiger Ben and Ao Lituo means Xiao Chen's revenge is unfinished, and his vow to hunt them down will likely lead to a direct clash with the Tiger Clan and Sun God Church's Half-Ancestors. Additionally, Xiao Chen's decision to let go of the past may mark a turning point in his psychological journey.
