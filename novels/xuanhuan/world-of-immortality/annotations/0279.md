## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal return to the mortal world as Xiao Chen, now a terrifying demi-god, confronts a group of cultivators who have been terrorizing the land and demeaning pure martial arts. The tone is one of cold, tragic fury, as Xiao Chen cuts through their arrogance with devastating precision. The Yellow River, a sacred symbol of Chinese civilization, becomes the stage for a clash between the old ways of the pure warrior and the new order of supernatural cultivators. Readers witness Xiao Chen's unyielding belief in the martial path, fueled by the memory of fallen ancestors and the burden of his own tragic journey.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen surrounded by a dozen cultivators, who mock him as a mere 'low-level warrior' from the mortal realm. Without hesitation, he unleashes the Lingxi Sword Wave, instantly annihilating two young cultivators. Two middle-aged leaders engage him, using artifacts like a Purple Gold Shield and flying swords. Xiao Chen counters with the Fearless Lion Seal—a golden lion manifestation—and then draws the Golden Divine Halberd, a cracked but fearsome relic that shatters their artifacts. When they accuse him of relying on treasures, Xiao Chen sets the halberd aside and proves his superiority through pure technique, severing their legs and breaking their skulls. Their infant souls (yuan ying) try to escape, but Xiao Chen chases one to a construction site for the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, where he kills the fleeing elder and nearby disciples, using Space Divine Law to annihilate them. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen continuing his rampage upstream, determined to eliminate all threats.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the concept of the 'infant soul' (yuan ying), a hallmark of Daoist cultivation where a cultivator can survive physical death by fleeing in a small, condensed spiritual form. It emphasizes the difference between pure martial artists—who rely solely on bodily strength and technique—and cultivators who develop supernatural powers (divine abilities). The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge (通天死桥) is a legendary structure from the novel's lore, a bridge that supposedly leads to death or transcendence. Xiao Chen's disdain for those who dismiss martial arts reflects a central theme of the novel: the tragic decline of warrior traditions in a world dominated by flashy, external power. The Yellow River, rich in myth and ancestral memory, serves as a powerful backdrop, linking Xiao Chen's battle to the legacy of the Nine Provinces.

## Reading Guide
Look ahead to Xiao Chen's continued assault on the forces that threaten the mortal world. His use of Space Divine Law—a rare and devastating ability—hints at further development of his own unique path. The destruction of the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge construction may draw the attention of greater powers. Watch for the reaction of the surviving cultivator factions and the possible intervention of even stronger opponents. This chapter solidifies Xiao Chen's identity as a relentless avenger who will not tolerate the oppression of the weak by arrogant cultivators. The philosophical clash between pure martial arts and supernatural cultivation will remain a key tension in the chapters to come.
