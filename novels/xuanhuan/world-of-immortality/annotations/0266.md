## Chapter Overview
The chapter opens in the blood-soaked aftermath of the Lost Paradise massacre, where demigods and legendary figures lie dead by Xiao Chen's hand. The air is heavy with grief and accusation, yet also whispers of support from hidden quarters. Xiao Chen walks among the fallen, his expression unreadable, as the Warrior's Imprint on the Heavenly Stele speaks to him, offering not power but insight. The sixteen War Swords fly into his body, merging with his flesh in a painful but profound transformation. Meanwhile, Keke, the little snow-white beast, is drawn by an inexplicable sorrow to a humble hut where the bones of its parents rest, unlocking the truth of the Lost Paradise's origin. This chapter is a poignant blend of brutal violence, cosmic reward, and deep emotional catharsis, setting up Keke's inheritance and Xiao Chen's new path.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen has slain all the demigods inside the Lost Paradise, including Tongtian, Primordial, Buddha, Laozi, and the Sun Holy God, leaving their bodies and treasures scattered. The Warrior's Imprint on the Heavenly Stele commends his decisiveness and, despite finding his aptitude mediocre, rewards him by fusing all sixteen War Swords present into his body, granting him a formidable but mysterious weapon. The Imprint also imparts the true method of martial cultivation—a revelation that transforms Xiao Chen's understanding of his path. Meanwhile, Keke, sensing a familiar calling, leads Xiao Chen to a remote hut where it finds the skeletal remains of its parents. A jade leaf from the bones delivers a final message: death is a new beginning, and the Lost Paradise now belongs to Keke. The external situation escalates as a figure (the owner of the embroidered sword) gloats over the demigods' deaths, only to find Wu Sheng Sun Wu appearing behind him, while the Lost Paradise begins to dissolve, revealing deep caves beneath.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of 'War Swords' (二十四战剑) is central to the novel's power system—ancient weapons of immense might, with sixteen now merging into Xiao Chen. The 'Warrior's Imprint' on the Heavenly Stele represents the ultimate martial legacy, a transcendental inheritance independent of personal power. The Lost Paradise is revealed as a divine domain belonging to Keke's parents, who were beings of incredible stature (possibly at the level of Ancestral Gods). The verse 'death is not the end but a new beginning' echoes the cyclical rebirth theme common in Xianxia, particularly for sacred beasts like Keke. The butterfly passing by alludes to Zhuangzi, symbolizing the highest spiritual freedom. The chapter also references the Four Immortal-Slaying Swords and the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, both classic artifacts from Chinese mythology (Fengshen Yanyi), grounding the narrative in a rich mythological tradition.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a crucial turning point: Xiao Chen gains an unprecedented weapon set and the core principles of the martial way, but he does not receive a direct power boost—suggesting that future growth will be earned through application. Keke's inheritance of the Lost Paradise opens immense potential for her development, possibly including control over that realm. The disappearance of the Lost Paradise and the appearance of caverns hint at deeper secrets beneath the divine island, possibly the next major conflict zone. Externally, the confrontation between the embroidered sword owner and Wu Sheng Sun Wu foreshadows an escalating war between realms. Readers should watch for Xiao Chen's new sword techniques, Keke's awakening of Lost Paradise powers, and the hunt for the remaining eight War Swords.
