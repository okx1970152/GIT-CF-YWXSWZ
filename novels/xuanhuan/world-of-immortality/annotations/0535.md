## Chapter Overview

This chapter escalates the cosmic conflict as the Heavenly Stele, a primordial artifact, rises from the abyss to confront nine Otherworld Ancestral Gods and three supreme treasures. The battle hinges on Stone-Man blood, which suppresses the stele. In a brilliant counter, the stele's inscriptions capture one Ancestral God, and the allied powerhouses quickly dismember him. The group then enters a black death lake, guided by the Old Stone Turtle, who reveals that the lake leads to a path requiring a sacrifice—the captured god—to survive.

## Key Plot Points

The Heavenly Stele expands to mountain-like proportions, forcing back nine Ancestral Gods. The three supreme artifacts (Demon Blade, Immortal Shield, 33-layer pagoda) shed Stone-Man blood, which causes the stele to retreat. Old Stone Turtle explains the stele avoids self-harm. The inscriptions form divine light that captures an Otherworld Ancestral God. Xiao Chen uses his two Heavenly Marks to help pin the god. The group quickly wounds and separates the god's body. Old Stone Turtle directs them to enter the black death lake, which leads to a dangerous but promising path—they must sacrifice the captured Ancestral God to proceed. The captured god is intended as a sacrifice for the journey. The chapter ends with the group entering the lake, with a hint about the Curse Realm's great formation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Explain the significance of Stone-Man blood and the Heavenly Stele. Stone-Man blood refers to the blood of a primordial Stone-Man, a being of immense power from a previous era. The Heavenly Stele is one of the legendary steles that seal and govern the world. The concept of 'sacrifice' here is not mere ritual but a practical necessity to travel through the death lake, which is likely a dimensional passage requiring a powerful life force to open. The Old Stone Turtle is a wise ancient being, possibly connected to the turtle that carries the world in Chinese mythology (the black turtle of the north). The capture of an Ancestral God is a monumental feat, showing the allied forces' growing ability to fight back.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a critical shift: the group now has a captured Ancestral God as a sacrifice for traversal through the death lake. Readers should watch for the nature of the death lake and what lies beyond. The mention of the Curse Realm's great formation suggests that the Otherworld has its own defenses. The Old Stone Turtle's cryptic words about the sacrifice potentially yielding a Stone artifact hint at a reward for their risk. Xiao Chen's Heavenly Marks continue to prove crucial, linking him to the Heavenly Stele prophecy. The chapter balances epic battle with strategic retreat, maintaining the tragic, mythic tone.
