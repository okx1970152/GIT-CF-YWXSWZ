## Chapter Overview

In this devastating chapter, Xiao Chen's pent-up grief and rage erupt into a relentless campaign of vengeance against the Otherworld Ancestral Gods. He opens by coldly executing an Ancestral God with the broken Great Annihilation War Spear, then uses the Primordial Demon City to crush a second. The emotional core arrives when he seizes the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd and receives a haunting vision of his fallen friend Jin Sanyi's final moments. Xiao Chen's fury culminates in a brutal dismemberment of a Supreme Ancestral God. The action then shifts to the Eternal Unknown, where Keke's father wages a transcendent battle against the highest powers, killing one Supreme Ancestral God and wounding another—a display of power that reshapes the cosmological stakes of the war.

## Key Plot Points

1) Xiao Chen kills an Otherworld Ancestral God by impaling him with the broken Great Annihilation War Spear and detonating its destructive power.
2) He uses the Primordial Demon City to immobilize and decapitate a second Ancestral God, brutally tearing him apart.
3) A Giant Ancestral God wielding the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd confronts Xiao Chen, but Xiao Chen seizes the weapon and learns of Jin Sanyi's death through its memory echo. This triggers a savage, near-animalistic retaliation: he tears off the Giant's limbs, stomps his chest, and punches his head apart.
4) Xiao Chen reaches the Eternal Unknown, where he witnesses Keke's father fighting dozens of Ancestral Gods and Supreme Ancestral Gods. Keke's father demonstrates supreme temporal and spatial abilities, killing a Supreme Ancestral God and badly wounding another.
5) The two fallen Supreme Ancestral Gods are left on the stone steps. Xiao Chen, using the Pangu Stone Token to hide, retrieves both bodies for his own purposes, preventing their potential resurrection.
6) The chapter closes with Xiao Chen haunted by the memory of Keke's innocent voice, reinforcing his personal loss and resolve.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces or deepens several key concepts. The Eternal Unknown is revealed as a realm outside normal space-time where the highest Otherworld powers reside, guarding the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps—a staircase that leads to supreme transcendence or the Ancestral God final step. Keke's father's abilities show mastery of time and space at a level that surpasses ordinary Ancestral Gods: he can walk through frozen time, reverse time, and even cause time to 'collapse.' The Great Annihilation War Spear and Doomsday Heavenly Halberd are legendary weapons—the former once belonged to the Martial Ancestor, the latter to the Otherworld's supreme beings. The chapter also confirms that a slain Supreme Ancestral God can potentially be revived, hence Xiao Chen's seizure of their bodies. This underscores the high-stakes nature of the war: even death may not be permanent for the highest powers.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen's personal vengeance reaches its peak, and the war's scope expands dramatically with Keke's father entering the Eternal Unknown. Readers should watch for the implications of Xiao Chen's collected Supreme Ancestral God bodies—he intends to use them, likely for cultivation or as bargaining chips. The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps remain a central mystery; the surviving Supreme Ancestral God has ascended them, possibly to summon even greater powers. Keke's father's involvement may shift if he learns of Keke's status. Additionally, the loss of Jin Sanyi and other friends deepens Xiao Chen's bitterness—his next moves will likely be even more ruthless. The Pangu Stone Token's concealment powers will be crucial going forward.
