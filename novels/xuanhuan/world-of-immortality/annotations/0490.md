## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers one of the most emotionally resonant and lore-dense sequences in the arc. Xiao Chen and his allies finally breach the Most Evil Place, the legendary burial ground of Ancestral Gods, to reach the Eternal Light—a fount of primordial power that can mend divine weapons and elevate beings to godhood. But the treasure is guarded by a chilling fusion of war technologies from two lost civilizations: a metal titan armed with Ancestral God weapons, Killing Light, and antimatter cannons. More significantly, the chapter unveils the true nature of the cool little beast: a residual imprint of Keke's father, an entity of staggering power and nonchalance. The reunion is bittersweet, as the imprint fades after guiding Keke to her inheritance. The Eternal Light itself has a consciousness, and the chapter closes with the sudden, ominous arrival of the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou—and the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge rising to meet him.

## Key Plot Points

The group's journey to the Eternal Light nearly ends at the hands of a metal guardian—a fusion of Ancestral God weapons, Killing Light, and antimatter weapons—but the cool little beast's inscribed stone tablet forces it to retreat. They reach the Eternal Light, a radiant sphere that has grown conscious and speaks. The cool little beast reveals that he is a mental imprint of Keke's father, who once 'bathed' in the Eternal Light. He uses the light to repair Xiao Chen's three broken War Swords, bestow beams of Eternal Light upon Bai Qi, the two rogue artifacts (Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal), and even the Martial Imprint. Keke's father also inscribes a playful epitaph on a stone tablet, jesting about his own death. His imprint fades after creating a Star Gate to send the group home, leaving Keke in tears. The Eternal Light confirms that the female sovereign and the three skeleton kings (Qin Guang, Yan Luo, Lun Hui) have descended into the deepest part of the Death World. Upon exiting the Star Gate, the group feels the overwhelming killing intent of the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou—but the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge spans the sky to block him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Eternal Light is a legendary phenomenon born when ten or more Ancestral Gods are buried together, their souls burning into a single, inextinguishable radiance. It can elevate a Half-Ancestor to full Ancestral God status, repair supreme weapons, and grant unparalleled power. Killing Light is a primordial beam of absolute destruction, not a technique but a natural law that manifests during apocalyptic times. Ancestral God Killing Thoughts are the pure malice left behind by a slain god, capable of piercing even Ancestral God-level defenses. The metal guardian is a horrifying union of technologies from two eras: it wields Ancestral God weapons, emits Killing Light from its eyes, and carries a chest cavity of antimatter weaponry—a design meant to kill gods. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge is a legendary artifact that connects realms and has appeared before in the story to intervene at crucial moments.

## Reading Guide

The reappearance of Li Huiyou signals that the Otherworld Ancestral Gods are no longer mere legends—they are actively mobilizing. The Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge's intervention suggests that some cosmic force or Ancient Ancestral God is still protecting the Nine Provinces. Keke's grief over her father's imprint lingering will surely drive her character growth. The Eternal Light's mention of the female sovereign and the three skeleton kings entering the Death World's deepest parts hints at an upcoming convergence. Xiao Chen now possesses the fully repaired War Swords, the Martial Imprint has been gifted Eternal Light, and the rogue artifacts have regained their former glory. The stage is set for a massive confrontation that may determine the fate of the Nine Provinces—or perhaps the entire world.
