## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen deep into the ancient battlefield prohibition zone, a realm of blood rain, corpse wind, and malevolent lightning. After glimpsing the combined Oracle Bone Diagram—a key that once revealed the future to the master of the Heavenly Palace—Xiao Chen pushes forward against immense pressure. He seizes the opportunity to temper himself, unleashing the Eight Origin Sounds to refine the deadly blood lightning into pure essence, honing his already formidable War Ancestor body. But the true threat lies ahead: two of the Otherworld's most fearsome powerhouses, the Eternal King Meng Degang and the True Wood Female Saint Zhenmu Nüsheng, have gathered at a ruined Primordial Demon City. In a desperate ritual, they sacrifice their own blood and soul force to summon an ancient Saint Ancestor from a sealed realm. Xiao Chen must decide whether to break their summoning or let the ancient horror emerge.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen’s trek into the forbidden zone is a trial of both body and spirit. The gravity of the battlefield nearly crushes him; every step drives him knee‑deep into the cold earth. Yet he uses this crucible to his advantage, chanting the Eight Origin Sounds to absorb the destructive power of the blood lightning. This not only strengthens his physical form but also deepens his mastery of the Sounds, pushing his martial foundation closer to the next threshold. At the same time, he encounters the fleeting image of the completed Oracle Bone Diagram—a mosaic of all its scattered fragments—confirming its origin as the sacred bone of the Heavenly Palace and its power to glimpse fate. This sight stirs both hope and dread. The chapter’s central action unfolds when Xiao Chen locates Meng Degang and Zhenmu Nüsheng. The two Otherworld titans have set up a sacrificial array before a rotting Primordial Demon City. By igniting the city and pouring out their own stone‑blood, they activate an ancient altar that begins to open a portal. A voice from beyond declares that a projection of the Saint Ancestor shall descend. Xiao Chen, hidden nearby, recognizes the danger. With the True Heavenly Stele in hand, he makes his move: a tremendous strike aimed at the altar, the linchpin of the summoning. The chapter ends at the moment of impact, leaving the outcome uncertain.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Oracle Bone Diagram (甲骨图) is a relic of great divinatory power. In Chinese culture, oracle bones were used in Shang dynasty rituals to communicate with ancestors and predict the future. Here, the diagram is elevated to a cosmic artifact that can reveal the fates of worlds. Its fragments have been scattered, but reuniting them promises profound insight—or danger. The Eight Origin Sounds (本源八音) are a set of primordial tones drawn from Daoist and Buddhist mantra traditions. Chanting them resonates with the fundamental energies of creation; here Xiao Chen uses them to refine destructive lightning into life force. The concept of a 'Saint Ancestor' (圣祖) being summoned from a sealed realm echoes the classic xianxia trope of ancient beings returning to reshape the world. The sacrificial ritual—burning a demon city and offering one's own blood—parallels alchemical and shamanic practices of transmutation and invocation. The 'stone man' path (石人路) is a unique cultivation system in World of Immortality, where the practitioner's body gradually petrifies into an immortal stone form; Meng Degang and Zhenmu Nüsheng are practitioners of this path at an advanced stage.

Xiao Chen’s cultivation is at a bottleneck; his use of the battlefield pressure to train indicates he may be on the verge of a breakthrough. Finally, Keke’s faint warning from within the Lost Paradise reminds us that even these powerful allies are anxious. The next few chapters will likely focus on the aftermath of the attack and the true nature of the Saint Ancestor.
