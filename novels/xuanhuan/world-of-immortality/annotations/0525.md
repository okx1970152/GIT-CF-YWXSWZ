## Chapter Overview

Chapter 525 delivers a devastating blow to one of the most storied martial lineages in the World of Immortality, as the Dugu clan is annihilated by an Otherworld Ancestral God. The chapter opens with Dugu Jianmo's final descent into madness and the extinguishing of the Dugu name, a tragic echo of the wider war's merciless nature. Against this backdrop, Xiao Chen clashes with the curse-wielding Ancestral God Leng Xingyi in the Curse Realm, a domain where spellcraft reigns supreme. The battle is a grueling test of Xiao Chen's martial prowess versus an opponent who commands world-shattering curses. The chapter also reunites Xiao Chen with many old faces who chose to remain behind, from Wu Ming and his son to Bo Shi and a curious little monk. Yet the true mystery emerges when Xiao Chen steps into a forbidden region and plunges into a shadowy abyss, glimpsing a giant stele and colossal creatures below, hinting at the Curse Realm's own hidden cataclysm. This chapter balances epic combat with poignant loss and the looming unknown.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter's most harrowing event is the complete destruction of the Dugu clan. Dugu Jianmo, the peerless swordsman, cycles through seven episodes of madness in a short time before leaping into a legendary 'thousand-man pit' and vanishing. The clan's hundred-plus heroes are extinguished before a single Ancestral God, a sobering reminder of the power disparity in this war. Xiao Chen, having witnessed this, carves a towering monument carved with the characters 'Dugu' before pressing on.

His confrontation with Leng Xingyi is a highlight of the chapter. Leng, a master of curses who has comprehended laws of myriad worlds, unleashes a barrage of legendary techniques: 'Starry Sky Radiance,' 'Draw the Circle as a Prison,' 'Heavenly Fire Refines the Spirit,' 'Death Nether Strike,' and the ultimate 'Soul-Flesh Separation.' Xiao Chen initially struggles, his incomplete formation diagram and physical body barely matching Leng's curse arsenal. The tide turns when Keke emerges from the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, wielding the jade bowl infused with Ancestral God essence and Eternal Light, buying Xiao Chen enough time to reunite his soul and body. With renewed force, Xiao Chen uses his formation diagram to shatter Leng's physical form, forcing the Ancestral God to flee with only his head intact.

After the battle, Xiao Chen explores deeper into the Curse Realm and meets old acquaintances who also remained behind. Wu Ming and his son Wu Xiaoshi were dragged off the Ancestral Dragon Ship by the Martial Imprint, much to Wu Ming's fury. Others include Snow Dance, Zhao Zhongyang, Linglong and Tutu, Totti, Bo Shi (now with a wife and a young 'little monk' son), and more—Chu Xingkuang, Jue Dao, Wu Xingfeng, Yao Yao—all have gathered in the Curse Realm as a temporary refuge.

But the chapter closes on an unsettling note. Xiao Chen steps into a central area of the realm that affects Ancestral Gods—a place of immense formation power. The ground collapses into a bottomless abyss, and as he falls, he glimpses a colossal stele and giant beings moving in the darkness, suggesting the Curse Realm itself hides a terrible secret.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Curse Realm (咒界) is one of the four worlds (Martial, Qi, Curse, Soul) in the World of Immortality's cosmology. It is a realm analogous to the power of curses and incantations. The chapter emphasizes that this realm contains ancient formations that can seal or influence Ancestral Gods—a key plot point when Xiao Chen is pulled into the abyss. The 'thousand-man pit' (千万人坑) Dugu Jianmo leaps into is a legendary mass grave from ancient battles, often associated with intense death energy that can drive cultivators mad or become a training ground for death arts.

Leng Xingyi's curse techniques draw on the concept of 'comprehending the myriad realms' (万界神则), meaning a top-tier curse master can borrow the power of worlds and laws from across existence. The 'Soul-Flesh Separation' curse (灵肉分离) is a classic trope in xianxia: a highly dangerous technique that separates the soul from the body, leaving the victim vulnerable. Xiao Chen's reliance on his physical strength as a Martial Body practitioner contrasts with Leng's curse mastery, highlighting the tension between pure martial dao and external divine arts.

Keke's jade bowl (七彩玉碗) is a potent artifact refined with Eternal Light and Ancestral God essence, able to counter Ancestral God-level attacks. The bowl's power to 'melt heaven and earth' symbolizes the peak of artifact refinement—a fusion of primordial materials and supreme will.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up several threads that will become critical in the coming arcs. First, the Curse Realm's hidden abyss and the giant stele Xiao Chen glimpses likely connect to the Heavenly Stele mysteries and the primordial seal of the Nine Provinces. The fact that this place specifically targets Ancestral Gods suggests it may be a weapon or prison left from the age of the Ancestral Gods. Readers should pay attention to the description of the stele and the giant creatures—these may be remnants of the previous civilization or sleeping entities tied to the Martial Ancestor's legacy.

Second, the survival of so many familiar faces in the Curse Realm indicates that this realm will become a staging ground for the resistance. Characters like Wu Ming, Bo Shi, and the young elites represent the next generation of power. Their interactions with Xiao Chen will shape his strategy going forward.

Third, Dugu Jianmo's disappearance into the 'thousand-man pit' is left ambiguous—he may have been destroyed, or he may be undergoing a transformation in a place of extreme death energy. Sword cultivators often use such deadly locales to achieve breakthroughs. His fate remains an open question that could influence the war with the Otherworld.

Finally, Leng Xingyi's escape with only his head ensures he will return, likely with reinforcements. Xiao Chen has proven he can wound an Ancestral God, but killing one entirely may require the full forty-nine War Swords and complete formation diagram. The chapter deepens the sense that the war is far from over, and the Curse Realm may hold the key to turning the tide.
