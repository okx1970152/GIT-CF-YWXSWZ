## Chapter Overview
This chapter reunites Xiao Chen with Keke, who had been lost on Dragon Island. The joyful reunion quickly deepens into mythic revelation as Xiao Chen encounters an ancient, decrepit dragon on the Sacred Mountain. The dragon, keeper of forgotten history, gifts a stone arm that completes the Stone Man's limbs, forging a faint connection. Through fragmented memories, the dragon unveils the catastrophic 'Sin City' disaster that claimed countless gods, dragon kings, and ancestral figures like Suiren and Youchao. The chapter also reveals the white turtle's true dragon nature and ends with Keke astonishingly summoning the Divine Ship alone, hinting at her primordial power.

## Key Plot Points
Keke's joyful reunion with Xiao Chen after months of separation on the Sacred Mountain. The ancient dragon, on the verge of death, gifts a stone arm to Xiao Chen, completing the Stone Man's limbs and creating a subtle link between them. The dragon reveals that the stone arm was left by an ancestral god. In a trance-like state, the dragon recounts the Sin City catastrophe: untold numbers of beings perished, including two generations of dragon kings, Chiyou's evil form, Buddha's evil incarnation, and Xuanyuan (nearly killed). Only Shennong escaped with a handful of survivors. The world itself may be illusory, and Dragon Island's sealing was collateral damage. The jade bracelet from Ancestral Dragon Village contains an ancestral dragon essence that revitalizes the old dragon. The dragon also exposes that the white turtle is actually a dragon, sealed by its brother (the Southern Wastes dragon king), and gives a small dragon horn to help it recover. Finally, Keke single-handedly summons the Divine Ship, a feat normally requiring nine dragon kings or a young Ancestral Dragon, astonishing Xiao Chen.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter plunges into the novel's foundational mythology. The 'Sin City' (罪恶深渊) refers to the Dead City or the demon well beneath the Heavenly Stele, a site of ancient catastrophe where many legendary figures from Chinese myth fell—Suiren (燧人氏, the fire-drill ancestor), Youchao (有巢氏, the nest-builder), Chiyou, and even a dark incarnation of the Buddha. The concept that the Eternal Continent might be an illusion echoes Daoist skepticism about reality. The Divine Ship, built from Ancestral Dragon bone and World Tree wood, traditionally requires nine dragon kings to summon; Keke's solitary success parallels the power of a young Ancestral Dragon, underscoring her mysterious lineage. The old dragon's mention of Shennong sacrificing his body to neutralize otherworldly poison connects to earlier hints of a larger cosmic war.

## Reading Guide
This chapter resolves Keke's disappearance and opens several major plot threads: the secrets of Dragon Island's curse, the Stone Man's purpose, and the true nature of Keke. The old dragon's fragmented recollection of the Sin City disaster introduces key names and events that will shape the story's mythology. The revelation that the white turtle is a dragon—and that a powerful dragon king sealed it—sets up future conflict. Keke's enhanced ability to summon the ship shows her power is growing, hinting at her role in the coming struggles. Readers should pay attention to the old dragon's warnings and the enigmatic declaration that the world may be an illusion, as these themes will deepen in later arcs. The chapter marks Xiao Chen's departure from Dragon Island, beginning a new phase on the Eternal Continent.
