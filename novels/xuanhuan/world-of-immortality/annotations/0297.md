## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks the grand exodus from the World of Immortality as half-ancestors and countless cultivators prepare to enter the Mortal World via the Ancestral Dragon Ship and the King Ship. The tone is a mix of solemn departure, mythic scale, and underlying tension. Xiao Chen, having returned from the Ancient God Wasteland, reunites with the Little Defiant Dragon and the Azure Dragon King, while the headless monk Shenxiu makes a haunting appearance on the Forbidden Sea. The chapter culminates in a shocking discovery at Dragon Island: the divine stele is under attack by Yuanshi and Tongtian, signaling a crisis that may force even the most reclusive half-ancestors to act.

## Key Plot Points
The Ancestral Dragon Ship arrives with a massive rainbow bridge; half-ancestors like Buddha, Yuanshi, and Tongtian use their powers to store their followers in small worlds, demonstrating their immense cultivation. Xiao Chen, along with Chiyou, Buddha, and Laozi, remains behind to coordinate a second wave via the King Ship, as there are far too many cultivators for one vessel. Xiao Chen travels to the Southern Wastes to retrieve the Little Defiant Dragon and the Azure Dragon King, with Chiyou opening a spatial passage. The Southern Wastes old dragon reluctantly releases them after seeing the dragon horn artifact. The King Ship is summoned using the Nine Abyss Heaven-Reaching Formation; numerous cultivators board, including Hai Yunxue and Zhao Lin'er, who are shocked to see Xiao Chen alive. The headless monk Shenxiu appears on the Forbidden Sea, reciting his famous gatha; Buddha subdues him with the Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform. The King Ship reaches Dragon Island, where they discover the island shaking—Yuanshi and Tongtian are attacking the divine stele, and the bone sea and Death Castle reappear overhead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The poem '身是菩提树, 心若明镜台' is traditionally attributed to Huineng, but here it is tied to Shenxiu, reflecting the historical rivalry between these two great Buddhist figures. In xianxia, such legendary monks often transcend death, and Shenxiu's headless corpse walking the Forbidden Sea embodies both tragedy and power. The King Ship (君王船) is a yin-aligned vessel used by Chiyou, contrasting with the yang-aligned Ancestral Dragon Ship; its summoning involves ghostly wails and black mist, fitting its deathly nature. The divine stele (神碑) on Dragon Island is a key artifact that seals the island and suppresses dragons; its destruction could unleash catastrophic forces. The half-ancestors (半祖) are beings approaching the level of Ancestral God, such as Buddha, Yuanshi, and Tongtian, and their actions here hint at a cosmic shift.

## Reading Guide
Pay attention to the political dynamics: the exodus from the World of Immortality is not just a migration but a strategic retreat. The half-ancestors are preparing for a potential apocalypse, and the presence of hidden half-ancestors among the refugees suggests layers of intrigue. The appearance of Shenxiu hints at deeper Buddhist lore and the fate of powerful figures who died but continue to exist in death—his gatha carries weight. The attack on the divine stele at Dragon Island sets up a major conflict: will the stele break? What will happen to the island and the dragons? Xiao Chen's reunion with the Little Defiant Dragon and the Azure Dragon King is heartening, but the skeletons are still missing, leaving a thread unresolved. The final line from Buddha—'恐怕我们几人也不得不出手'—indicates that even the peaceful half-ancestors may have to fight, raising the stakes for the next arc.
