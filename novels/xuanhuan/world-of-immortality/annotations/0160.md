## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a quiet but significant inflection point in Xiao Chen's journey. After a period of intense meditation, he stands at the very edge of the Mortal-Shedding realm, gazing across a vast, flat plain that separates him from the Spirit-Treasury—a barrier that cannot be crossed by force alone. The tone is contemplative and almost philosophical, before a sudden, brutal disruption shatters the calm. The abduction of Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon by a mysterious black whirlwind injects a surge of primal urgency into the narrative. It is a chapter that balances the slow, deliberate progress of cultivation with the raw, unpredictable dangers of the World of Immortality.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen emerges from eleven days of secluded cultivation, his divine sense and physical form having grown markedly stronger. He has reached the Ninth Heaven of Mortal-Shedding and now confronts the legendary 'great plain' that stretches between this realm and the Spirit-Treasury—a daunting horizontal expanse that requires far more than simple power accumulation to cross. This realization does not discourage him; instead, he finds deeper satisfaction in the gradual refinement of his spirit and body. He locates Keke buried in a hoard of spirit herbs, asleep and content, and finds the Little Defiant Dragon wounded from grueling wilderness training orchestrated by the fat merchant Zhuge Liang. Returning to Heavenly Emperor City, Xiao Chen learns that many cultivators have departed for the southern wilds following a rumored Divine Palace ruins, said to be guarded by two sacred beast cubs of supreme lineage. Despite the temptation, Xiao Chen resolves to merely observe. Upon arriving at the floating island ruins, a terrifying black whirlwind erupts from the debris, seizing both Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon with impossible speed. Xiao Chen gives chase with his Undying Wings but the whirlwind vanishes, leaving him furious and helpless, catching only a glimpse of a massive, black-scaled beast within the storm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter introduces a critical cultivation concept: the 'great realm plain' (大境界平原). In traditional Chinese xianxia, each major realm (e.g., Mortal-Shedding, Spirit-Treasury) contains nine minor heavens, each representing a climb from one peak to the next. But crossing between major realms is not a vertical ascent—it is a horizontal trek across an enormous plateau, requiring not just raw power but profound transformation of spirit and body. This metaphor vividly captures the immense difficulty of breakthrough. The Divine Palace ruins and the twin sacred beast cubs tap into the lore of celestial beings ('gods' or 天神) that are not necessarily humanoid; any creature of sufficient power can be called a 'god' in this world. The mention of races that can rival dragons recalls earlier hints about Keke's origins, deepening the mystery around her. The black whirlwind abduction echoes the island's ancient, predatory forces, reminding readers that even on the mainland, the laws of survival are harsh and merciless.

## Reading Guide

This chapter raises several urgent questions. Who or what is the black-scaled beast that snatched Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon? Its speed and intelligence suggest a primordial creature of high intelligence, possibly a guardian of the Divine Palace or a rogue agent from the southern forbidden zones. Xiao Chen's inability to catch it even with the Undying Wings underscores that he has encountered a threat far above his current level. The fates of his two companions are now unknown, and his next steps will likely involve tracking the beast through the wilds or seeking allies who understand the region's secrets. The Divine Palace ruins themselves may hold clues about the beast's origin. Additionally, Xiao Chen's cultivation is at a threshold—the Spirit-Treasury realm would give him new powers, but progress will be slow. Readers can anticipate a shift from urban political intrigue back to raw survival, as Xiao Chen plunges into the untamed depths of the Southern Wastes to rescue his bonded friends, possibly uncovering greater mysteries about the ancient powers that linger there.
