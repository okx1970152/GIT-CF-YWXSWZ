## Chapter Overview

This chapter is the climactic pivot of the Otherworld invasion arc, centering on the Yellow Clay Altar's unleashed fury as it channels the Eight Origin Sounds in devastating sequence. The narrative oscillates between Xiao Chen's desperate plight aboard the altar and the cosmic duels of stone men and ancestral gods. The tone is one of apocalyptic finality and tragic sacrifice: the Death World's dying stone man gives his last ember of life to thwart the Otherworld's final step, while the Martial Ancestor stages a stunning reversal at the Eternal Unknown. The chapter crackles with the terrible grandeur of gods falling, of primordial weapons shattering, and of a single mortal riding the precipice of annihilation.

## Key Plot Points

The Yellow Clay Altar, now embedded in the stone skull seized from the Dead City, unleashes the Eight Origin Sounds one after another—Weng, Duo, Ao, Hao, Xiao, Duo, Zao, Dao—each sound piercing the defenses of the Otherworld Ancestral Gods and shattering the colossal stone hands that reach for it. Several Ancestral Gods are killed outright, and the very sky of the Otherworld is torn. The stone man within the divine city, on the verge of completing his 'final step' to become a perfect Stone Man, finds his accumulated power instead absorbed by the city walls, where a second stone man—the one from the Death World's Primordial Demon City—emerges to steal his nourishment. A desperate battle erupts between the two stone beings. The Death World stone man, his life-fire already guttering, fights with suicidal fury but is ultimately torn apart and reduced to yellow dust, too weak after eons of slumber to withstand a being on the cusp of transcendence. Xiao Chen, using the incomplete formation diagram, two Heavenly Marks, and the stone knife, barely escapes the Otherworld stone man's grasping hand, but the puppet body of Chou Tian is destroyed in his place. The enraged stone man then pursues Xiao Chen across the Nine Provinces, the Soul Realm, the Longevity Realm, and finally into the Curse Realm, where an ancient formation briefly stalls him. Meanwhile, at the Eternal Unknown, the Martial Ancestor stages a masterful deception: after appearing to be defeated by the Supreme Ancestral Gods, he explodes with renewed vigor and smashes one of them into paste with his bare hands.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Origin Sounds (本源八音) are primordial syllables that predate even the Buddhist six-syllable mantra, representing the fundamental vibrations of existence. In this chapter, we witness their full might: each sound corresponds to a different cosmic force—time, space, yin-yang, and more—and when linked, they can override the defenses of Stone Men and kill Ancestral Gods. The 'Duo' sound (咄) governs duality, while the later sounds ('Ao', 'Zao', 'Dao') tap into even more esoteric realms. The Yellow Clay Altar, formed from the flesh of fallen Ancestral Gods, serves as the perfect resonator for these sounds. The 'final step' of the Stone Man path is a moment of ultimate transformation where a Stone Man becomes a self-contained universe of power, capable of rivaling the Ancestral Gods themselves. To be thwarted at this juncture is the deepest tragedy for such a being, as seen in the Otherworld stone man's uncontrollable rage. The Eternal Unknown is a realm outside normal space-time, where the three Supreme Ancestral Gods dwell; the Martial Ancestor's assault there signifies a desperate, last-ditch gambit to alter the course of the war.

## Reading Guide

The chapter leaves Xiao Chen in a desperate chase across worlds, with the stone man's personal vendetta now driving him into the Curse Realm. Pay close attention to the Curse Realm's ancient formation—it may be a key to trapping or even destroying the stone man in future chapters. The sacrifice of the Death World stone man should not be forgotten; his residual essence may have left traces that could aid Xiao Chen later. The stone knife and the incomplete formation diagram are now Xiao Chen's primary weapons against the Stone Man threat. The Martial Ancestor's resurgence at the Eternal Unknown signals that the human side still holds hidden trump cards; the fate of the three Supreme Ancestral Gods after his strike will shape the larger war. Also, note that Keke has been absent throughout this arc—her transformation into a seven-colored Ancestral Dragon may prove pivotal when she reappears. The chase is far from over, and the next chapters promise a desperate game of cat and mouse across the most dangerous corners of the cosmos.
