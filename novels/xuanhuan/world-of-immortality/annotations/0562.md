## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a high-stakes confrontation where Xiao Chen's past battles with the Otherworld's Stone Man King resurface in the most dangerous way. Two powerful enemies—a War Ancestor and a young Ancestral God—attempt to humiliate him, only to discover that his strength far exceeds their assumptions. But the true crisis lies within: the imprint of the enemy Stone Man King stirs, seeking to merge with the young Ancestral God and replicate a supreme terror. Xiao Chen must simultaneously fight external foes and suppress an ancient, malevolent legacy that could shift the balance of the war.

## Key Plot Points

- Xiao Chen effortlessly destroys the eight beasts sent to trample him, then shatters the two divine chariots with a single application of the Heavenly Mark, displaying his overwhelming physical power.
- The middle-aged War Ancestor and the young Ancestral God recognize that Xiao Chen is no ordinary Half-Ancestor; his body has been refined through Holy Sacrifice and Living Sacrifice of an Ancestral God, giving him Ancestral God-level physique.
- The young Ancestral God traps Xiao Chen in chaotic spacetime, but the enemy Stone Man King's imprint within Xiao Chen begins to manifest, threatening to escape and merge with the young god.
- Xiao Chen uses the ancient scroll (古卷) to temporarily seal the imprint, but the enemy imprint is powerful and nearly overcomes the seal.
- The War Ancestor uses Purgatory Soul-Locking Pillars (炼狱锁神桩) to trap Xiao Chen, but Xiao Chen's thirty-three War Swords from the incomplete formation diagram destroy the pillars.
- The young Ancestral God unleashes the devastating Eternal Suns Setting (千古悠悠日西坠) technique, combining a river of time and 999 blazing red suns. Xiao Chen counters with the formation diagram and Heavenly Marks, redirecting the time river and shattering the suns.
- Just as the enemy imprint is about to merge with the young Ancestral God, five imprints of the ancestors (representing the five great ancestors of the Nine Provinces) emerge from Xiao Chen's body and violently suppress the enemy king imprint, crushing it underfoot.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of War Ancestor (战祖), a cultivation level where the physical body and divine sense match an Ancestral God, but the practitioner lacks comprehension of universal origin divine laws. Xiao Chen achieved this through the forbidden Holy Sacrifice (圣祭) technique, which burns the essence of a fallen Ancestral God to refine one's own flesh. The Purgatory Soul-Locking Pillars is a classic sealing technique that summons nine divine beast phantoms to grind the target. The Eternal Suns Setting is a dual-attribute time and solar attack that has claimed many Ancestral Gods. The five imprints correspond to the five ancestors from the earlier arcs (likely Fuxi, Suiren, Shennong, etc.) whose spiritual remnants still oppose the Otherworld's Stone Man King.

## Reading Guide

The appearance of the five imprints suppressing the enemy Stone Man King's imprint is a major revelation that will likely have far-reaching consequences. Who controls these imprints? Are they independent or linked to Xiao Chen's destiny? The young Ancestral God and War Ancestor are defeated but not killed; their survival sets up future conflicts as they will certainly report Xiao Chen's abilities and the appearance of the imprints. Additionally, Xiao Chen's mastery of the incomplete formation diagram and Heavenly Marks continues to grow, but his internal struggle with the enemy imprint is far from over. Readers should watch for how the five imprints interact with Xiao Chen's own cultivation and whether the enemy king's legacy can truly be suppressed.
