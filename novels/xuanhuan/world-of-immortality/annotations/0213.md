## Chapter Overview

This chapter weaves discovery, loss, and transformation into a somber yet hopeful tapestry. The Black Dragon King leads Xiao Chen and Niu Ren deep into a dead forest, where they encounter the skeletal remains of a primordial dragon king—nearly an Ancestral Dragon. The dragon king's bones yield a sacred sword, a relic of ancient battles, before revealing a hidden aperture pulsing with the legendary Earth's Bloodline. For a full month, the trio cultivates in this primordial essence, and the Sacred Tree grows a fifth leaf of crimson jade. Yet the forest's very trees hungered for souls, and only the tree's protection saved them. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen and Niu Ren locating a ruined altar, ready to attempt a desperate summoning of Keke's spirit. It is a chapter of awe, grief, and relentless forward motion.

## Key Plot Points

The Black Dragon King, driven by instinct, leads the group to a colossal dragon king skeleton coiled around an ancient tree. The skeleton, nearly identical to an Ancestral Dragon but lacking two hind claws, is clearly a warrior who fell in battle. With a mournful roar, the Black Dragon King breathes black flames upon the bones, which shrink and transform into a crystalline sacred sword. The sword, a holy relic of the dragon race, merges into the Black Dragon King's spine. Beneath the remains, a dark hole spews rich blood-colored energy—the Earth's Bloodline, a vein of pure primal essence. Xiao Chen plants the Sacred Tree beside the hole, and for a month, he, Niu Ren, and the Black Dragon King absorb this energy. The Sacred Tree's fifth leaf, a Red Jade leaf, matures. Niu Ren's fused beast-soul grows to nearly twenty meters, and he breaks through to Spirit-Treasury Third Heaven. The Black Dragon King grows half a meter. Xiao Chen's cultivation fluctuates wildly between Spirit-Treasury First and Third Heaven, but he divinizes seven new acupoints, bringing his total to sixteen and further developing his golden 'divine vein' network. The chapter concludes with their cautious journey to a ruined altar, where they prepare a ritual to summon Keke's soul.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Earth's Bloodline (大地血脉) is a legendary concentration of the world's life force, often found where a great being or treasure once interacted with the land. It granted immense purification and might to those who could absorb it. The Sacred Tree's fifth leaf, now of Red Jade, marks a significant milestone; each leaf embodies a different elemental or essence type—ink, white, emerald, gold, and now red—and their full growth promises unfathomable power. Xiao Chen's fluctuating cultivation between Spirit-Treasury tiers echoes the instability typically seen only in the Nirvana realm, hinting at his unique path. His divinized acupoints form a nascent 'divine vein' system that bypasses conventional meridians, a path that may lead to a breakthrough far beyond normal realm progression. The dragon race's sacred sword, formed from the marrow of a peerless dragon king, is a weapon of profound legacy, carried only by kings.

## Reading Guide

The stage is set for Keke's possible return, but the ritual's outcome remains uncertain. Xiao Chen's unstable cultivation base is both a weakness and a hidden advantage—his fluctuating power may allow him to surprise enemies one moment and struggle the next. The Black Dragon King now wields a sacred sword, dramatically increasing his combat effectiveness. Meanwhile, the dead forest's trees are shown to feed on souls, and the Earth's Bloodline may have attracted attention. Readers should watch for further developments in Xiao Chen's acupoint system, the Sacred Tree's evolution, and whether the summoning succeeds or draws greater danger. The tension between Xiao Chen's grief and his renewed determination sets the emotional tone for the next arc.
