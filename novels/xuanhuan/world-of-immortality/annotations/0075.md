## Chapter Overview

This chapter pivots from the high-octane theft of dragon eggs to the tense, political aftermath spreading through Dragon Island. Xiao Chen and his unlikely party—the three skeleton kings and the divine beast Keke—flee west into a surreal range of snow-capped mountains, a stark climatic anomaly on the tropical island. Meanwhile, the alliances left reeling from Xiao Chen's brutal night raid learn of his deeds. Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, and Wang Tong return from a failed pursuit of the Purple Dragon King, only to discover their home bases annihilated. The emotional register shifts from desperate flight to cold fury and scheming. The chapter sets up a new sanctuary for Xiao Chen and a brewing storm of vengeance among the elite practitioners.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's decision to head for the snow mountains proves strategic: the remote, icy terrain offers both concealment from the rampaging dragons and distance from the bone sea where cultivators lurk. Keke's innocent delight in the snow contrasts sharply with the grim necessity of their flight. The narrative then cuts to the alliance encampments, where tension simmers. Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, and Wang Tong return from a disastrous expedition against the infant Purple Dragon King—a creature so formidable that even three Mortal-Shedding fifth- and sixth-heaven experts failed to capture it. Worse, they learn that Xiao Chen has wiped out their remaining forces in a single night. Rand, the golden-haired spellmaster who escaped Xiao Chen's purge only by hiding in a flower valley, delivers the news. Yan Qingcheng's fury shatters the flowers around her—a rare display of raw emotion from the usually composed beauty. The chapter closes with Kailuo and Wang Tong howling in rage, their reputations and strength dealt a heavy blow. This sets the stage for an inevitable, larger confrontation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of an 'Alliance' (联盟) on Dragon Island is a survival mechanism born from the island's sealing—no one can leave, so power must be consolidated through mutual protection and resource sharing. The ten-person cap prevents any single faction from dominating, creating a fragile balance. Yan Qingcheng's line about the Purple Dragon King—'When they mature, even gods tremble'—reinforces the xianxia trope of young, supreme creatures with terrifying potential. The snow mountain range appearing next to tropical jungle is a classic xianxia device: unnatural geography created by ancient formations or cosmic imbalances. It hints that Dragon Island is not a natural land but a constructed prison or arena. Also noteworthy is the way injuries and defeats are treated: pride is as valuable as life. Rand's humiliation and Yan Qingcheng's rage stem as much from loss of face as from tactical setbacks.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pacing breather after the heist, but the tension remains high. Keep an eye on Yan Qingcheng: her composure is cracking, and she is likely to become more aggressive. The Purple Dragon King remains a wild card—still free, still powerful, and now with added grudges from both the alliance and Xiao Chen. The snow mountain destination will be Xiao Chen's new training ground; note how the environment may aid his cultivation or conceal him. Keke's growing attachment to Xiao Chen is also subtle character development—her playful revenge on the skeletons shows loyalty. Finally, the trio of Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, and Wang Tong represent the top tier of young geniuses on the island. Their coordinated failure against the dragon and their rage against Xiao Chen suggest future team-ups against him. The reader should anticipate a coalition forming to corner Xiao Chen in the snow mountains.
