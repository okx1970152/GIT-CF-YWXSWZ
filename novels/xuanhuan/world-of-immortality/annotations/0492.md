## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the narrative from personal vengeance to cosmic-scale confrontation. The Dead City's murderous light guards secrets older than the Nine Provinces, while the Ancestral Dragon Ship descends bearing a mysterious Ancestral God who confronts the Otherworld's divine gates. The long-dormant threat of the Otherworld Ancestral Gods crystallizes into an immediate crisis: their descendants begin to pour through black portals across the Nine Provinces and the Four Worlds. Xiao Chen, having gathered allies from the Death World—including Tianwaitian, Renwairen, and the ancient general Bai Qi—prepares for a war that will define the age. The chapter balances epic spectacle with intimate character moments: the return of Mu Zi carrying Yizhen's sarira, the playful reappearance of Keke, and the chilling declaration from Li Huiyou that his true body will soon descend. The tone is one of grim inevitability, a prelude to the great calamity foretold by the Doomsday Prophecy.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Dead City wreathed in annihilating light, a barrier that even half-ancestors dare not cross. Xiao Chen reunites with Mu Zi, who has awakened from a two-century slumber in the Ancestral Dragon Village bearing the sarira of the fallen monk Yizhen—a relic that may be the seed of a new Buddha. The Ancestral Dragon Ship, long vanished beyond the heavens, returns to the outskirts of the Primordial Ancient Village, bringing a shadowy Ancestral God who uses the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge to shatter a vast Otherworld gate. Two Otherworld Ancestral Gods—Li Huiyou and an unnamed ally—are forced back, but they promise that their descendants will soon pour through countless smaller portals to "train" in the human world. As black divine gates rip open across the sky, Xiao Chen mobilizes his forces. He descends into the Death World to recruit the six half-ancestors of the Divine Village, including Tianwaitian, Renwairen, and the berserk bear Yingxiong. By the time they return, a female warrior in silver armor has surrounded the Primordial Ancient Village with a host of foreign elites, having scarred the land in a failed assault on the village itself. The battle lines are drawn: the age of the Hundred Races and the Otherworld invasion has truly begun.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Primordial Ancient Village (also called Ancestral Dragon Village) is revealed to be far more than Xiao Chen's birthplace—it is a nexus connected to the legendary Primordial Celestial Realm, a plane above the Four Worlds that even half-ancestors struggle to comprehend. The Ancestral Dragon Ship, built from the bones of an Ancestral Dragon and the wood of the World Tree, is the only vessel capable of crossing the sealed Forbidden Sea; its reappearance signals the breaking of ancient taboos. The concept of "killing light" introduced here is a force born from the will of heaven and earth themselves, capable of piercing even Ancestral God-level beings—a stark reminder that the cosmos has its own terrible defenses. The Great Derivation number is evoked through the War Swords and the buried Ancestral God weapons, tying Xiao Chen's accumulated power to a cosmic pattern of incompleteness. Yizhen's sarira being hailed as a potential "Buddha above Buddha" points to the xianxia trope of spiritual relics transcending death, a nod to Buddhist traditions of relic veneration.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for the final major arc of the novel. Readers should pay close attention to the geopolitics of the Otherworld invasion: the descendants of the Ancestral Gods are not mere cannon fodder—they are elite warriors sent to weaken the Nine Provinces before the true Ancestral Gods descend. The reappearance of the three skeleton kings as disciples of Ancestral God Suiren in the chaotic spacetime vision hints that they may hold the key to countering the Otherworld's overwhelming numbers. The tension between the mysterious Ancestral God on the ship and Li Huiyou suggests that not all Ancestral God-level beings are aligned with the invading forces; some may be acting in humanity's interest. The failure of the half-ancestors to break into the Primordial Ancient Village despite devastating the surrounding land implies the village itself is protected by forces beyond the current era of combatants. As Xiao Chen gathers his allies, the question remains: can a coalition of half-ancestors and a single rising Earth-Penetrating realm warrior stand against the full might of an Otherworld that has been preparing for eons? The chapter ends with the sense that the real war has just begun.
