## Chapter Overview

This monumental chapter marks the culmination of Xiao Chen's long ordeal in the death world. After months of cultivation and absorbing life source liquid from fallen kings, he evolves from a Yellow Diamond skeleton into a Five-Colored Skeleton, breaking into the Perfected Man realm. The chapter opens with an earth-shattering evolution and then settles into a quiet, miraculous phase where the death-tainted land around him begins to bloom with life, a direct consequence of his flesh-and-blood rebirth. After seven months, Xiao Chen reconstructs his physical body, first in a trial incarnation, then perfectly using the Dragon Sacred Tree. The emotional weight of finally regaining flesh and blood, of being able to speak and laugh again, is palpable. The reunion with Keke is tender and joyful. Yet the chapter ends on a note of righteous fury: Xiao Chen immediately sets out for Giant City to settle old scores with the Giant King, who has been humiliating his allies.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's evolution to a Five-Colored Skeleton triggers a massive energy wave that shatters the ancient battlefield. The Purple Diamond Skeleton Sha Polang returns with news that the Giant King has unified the eastern death world and is using a Yellow Diamond skull as a pet's drinking vessel—likely the skull of their friend Old Yellow. Keke sleeps and plays in Paradise Lost, now owning a vast treasure garden. Xiao Chen spends seven months in deep cultivation, absorbing king blood essence and life source liquid. As his rebirth progresses, grass and trees sprout from the dead red earth, a testament to the life force he is generating. On the final day, he bursts from the ground with a new body, then immediately self-destructs it to rebuild a superior physique with the help of the Dragon Sacred Tree. The perfected body is described as radiant and brimming with vitality. He then leads Sha Polang to Giant City, lifting a two-thousand-meter dead mountain to hurl at the city as an opening salvo.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The death world's skeleton hierarchy is defined by bone color: from common white to blue, purple, yellow, and finally the five-colored diamond tier, which corresponds to the Perfected Man realm (至人境界). Life source liquid, refined from the blood essence of powerful beings, is used to purify the soul and enable flesh-and-blood rebirth—a near-mythical achievement that even death world kings rarely witness. The Dragon Sacred Tree, originally Keke's treasure, plays a key role in perfecting the new body, its life essence integrating with the Eight Origin Sounds to create an optimal vessel. The scene of grass growing from dead soil echoes the Daoist principle of death giving birth to life, a core theme of the novel's underworld arcs. Xiao Chen's self-destruction and reconstruction is a deliberate act of perfectionism, refusing to accept a merely adequate rebirth.

## Reading Guide

With Xiao Chen fully restored and at the pinnacle of the Perfected Man realm, his target is the Giant King—a super-evolutionary who has been oppressing the eastern death world. This revenge will likely draw the attention of the mysterious sovereign of the inner continent, setting up a larger conflict. The doppelgänger Xiao Chen on the sea still has unfinished business, and the Hundred Races war is far from resolved. Keke's sacred tree now has six leaves, hinting at further evolution. The power balance in the Nine Provinces is about to shift dramatically with Xiao Chen's return to the mortal world. Readers can expect a series of spectacular confrontations as the once-dead protagonist reclaims his place.
