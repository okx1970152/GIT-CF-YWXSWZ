## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with an apocalyptic vision: the Five Demon Kings, incarnations of the millions of slain evil spirits Xiao Chen had absorbed in the Dead World, manifest over the capital city Yindu. Their combined death aura blots out the sky, a declaration of terrifying power. Wu Ming, the cunning artifact-refiner from the Cultivation World, counters with a demonic array formed from eighteen ancient skulls, summoning reapers and unleashing his legendary Heavenly Thunder Hammer. The clash is not merely magical but strategic: Xiao Chen’s demonic techniques against Wu Ming’s artifact-laden body. As the battle reaches a fever pitch, Xiao Chen reveals himself, shifting the confrontation from mass slaughter to a high-stakes negotiation. The two former enemies acknowledge their past bloodshed and the impossibility of true reconciliation, yet they also recognize their common enemy—the Five Element Heavenly Kings. A fragile understanding emerges: they will test each other’s power in the Taihang Mountains, a duel that will determine the terms of any future cooperation.

## Key Plot Points

Wu Ming begins by deploying eighteen ancient skulls that form a demonic giant, countered by Xiao Chen’s hidden reapers with their deathly scythes. The reapers tear through the giant, but Wu Ming recalls his scattered artifacts and charges forward with the Undying Imperial Heavenly Bell and the Heavenly Thunder Hammer. His technique of fusing artifact souls into his body makes every strike devastating. Xiao Chen’s Five Demon Kings, embodiments of his accumulated death aura, are scattered by the thunder hammer but reform, showing their resilience. Wu Ming attempts to destroy the central shadow behind the Demon Kings, but Xiao Chen at last reveals his true form, a direct challenge. Their ensuing dialogue is a masterclass in political veiled threat: Wu Ming proposes a temporary alliance against the Five Element Heavenly Kings, citing their shared hostility. Xiao Chen, ever cautious, agrees to a duel in the Taihang Mountains to establish hierarchy. The duel is a showcase of peak Perfected Man combat: Wu Ming’s five-element artifact array, his body bristling with one thousand artifact souls, and the defensive might of the Undying Bell; Xiao Chen counters with the Origin Eight Sounds—especially the Duo sound of Yin-Yang—and the ultimate weapon, a War Sword that tears through space. The climax comes when Xiao Chen’s War Sword pierces the Undying Imperial Heavenly Bell, a feat that shocks both combatants and seals the provisional truce.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Origin Sounds are primordial vibrations more ancient than the Buddhist six-syllable mantra. The Duo sound, which Xiao Chen has mastered, governs the interplay of Yin and Yang—the cycles of life and death, creation and destruction. When deployed, it can shatter spiritual weapons and disrupt the fabric of reality. Wu Ming’s Undying Imperial Heavenly Bell is a half-ancestor-level treasure, forged to be nearly indestructible, yet the War Sword—one of the legendary forty-nine swords of the Great Derivation—proves its superior edge. The concept of body-refining by fusing artifact souls into one’s frame is a rare path that turns the cultivator’s flesh into a living weapon. The Taihang Mountains, also known as Nüwa Mountain, are sacred to the goddess Nüwa and are one of the Nine Provinces’ cardinal spiritual sites. The Five Element Heavenly Kings command the powers of wood, fire, earth, metal, and water, and they are backed by half-ancestors like the Yasha and Asura elders. Their threat is so great that even sworn enemies like Xiao Chen and Wu Ming consider an alliance against them.

## Reading Guide

The duel’s unresolved ending—the bell is pierced but not shattered, and Wu Ming retreats with his forces—leaves the reader wondering about the true depth of their tentative alliance. Will Wu Ming honor the ceasefire, or is he already plotting his next betrayal? Xiao Chen’s mastery of the Duo sound and his War Sword give him a decisive edge in single combat, but he faces a web of half-ancestor plots. The trial war is imminent, and the Five Element Heavenly Kings are only the most visible threat. Keep an eye on the artifact-refining sect’s movements and the reaction of the foreign kings. As Xiao Chen returns to the Mortal World, the stage is set for a multi-front conflict where temporary alliances may be the only path to survival.
