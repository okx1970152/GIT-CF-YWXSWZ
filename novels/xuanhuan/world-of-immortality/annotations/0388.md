## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen deeper into the brutal politics of the Death World, where survival hinges on ruthless alliances and tactical slaughter. Now a Yellow Diamond skeleton, he teams with two equally monstrous kings—a zombie king and a death spirit king—to hunt and kill the rulers of rival races within an ancient ruin. The tone is grim, tactical, and punctuated by dark humor from the zombie king's greedy complaints. The chapter underscores the Death World's law of the strong, where even kings must band together to eliminate threats. But the hunt is far from easy: while the demon ape king falls swiftly, the scorpion queen proves a venomous adversary capable of piercing even Xiao Chen's yellow diamond bones, forcing the trio to unleash their most desperate techniques. A subplot introduces familiar faces from the mortal world—Yan Qingcheng, Princess Ying, and the Peacock King—hinting at a convergence of the living and the dead.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, the zombie king, and the death spirit king plan to lure and kill the war king of the War Clan, but first they eliminate lesser kings to test their cooperation. They successfully bait the demon ape king: the death spirit uses a secret technique to provoke it, and the three overwhelm it in a short battle. The ape king's death is swift thanks to the death spirit's Heroic Spirit Space, which offers it to ancestral spirits. Next, they target the scorpion queen, a far more dangerous foe. Her invisible scorpion tail fires venomous rays that can damage souls and even bore holes in Xiao Chen's yellow diamond bones. The zombie king is badly poisoned, and the death spirit's soul body is punctured. Only by combining their ultimate attacks—Corpse Shocks the World, Nether Soul Swallows the Sky, and the Weng Origin Sound—do they finally kill the scorpion queen. Afterward, the zombie king and death spirit demand greater rewards for their injuries, leading to tense negotiations. Xiao Chen agrees to provide compensation but refuses the most extreme demands. As they plan the next attack on the war king, they spot Yan Qingcheng accompanied by a young winged dragon girl, Princess Ying with Gold (the golden ape now transformed into a demon ape king), and the Peacock King flying overhead, indicating that powerful cultivators from the Eternal Continent have entered the same ruins.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Death World in this chapter operates on a hierarchy of 'fire seeds' and 'diamond bone bodies.' Xiao Chen's yellow diamond bones place him above purple diamond but still vulnerable to certain innate abilities, such as the scorpion queen's 'Scorpion Ray'—a talent unique to the Scorpion Clan that directly attacks both flesh and soul. The zombie king's 'Corpse Shocks the World' and the death spirit's 'Undead Roar' are examples of death-attribute techniques passed down through their lineages. The death spirit's 'Heroic Spirit Space' connects to an ancestral realm where fallen heroes of his clan reside, and he can offer sacrifices to them—a practice rooted in Chinese ancestor worship but adapted to the death world's undead cosmology. The War Clan, mentioned as the next target, is a race of inveterate warriors; its king wields the 'Blood-Red Spear' stained with half-ancestor blood, making it a terrifying weapon. The chapter also introduces the concept of 'foreign race kings' (异族王), rulers of non-human tribes that coexist in the ancient ruins, each with unique racial talents. Xiao Chen's yellow diamond body is a significant achievement, but the scorpion queen's ability to damage it shows that even high-tier skeletons have weaknesses against specialized attacks.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes the dangerous calculus of Xiao Chen's survival in the Death World: alliances of convenience with unpredictable allies, where every victory comes at a cost and negotiations follow combat. The zombie king and death spirit king are not to be trusted beyond the current campaign, and their greed will likely cause friction later. The appearance of Yan Qingcheng, Princess Ying, Gold, and the Peacock King signals that the ancient ruins are becoming a nexus for powers from the living world. Their presence may intersect with Xiao Chen's plans—whether for good or ill remains to be seen. The upcoming battle against the war king, who wields a half-ancestor weapon, will test whether the trio's synergy can overcome a truly superior foe. Xiao Chen's demonic seal (from the Dead City) has not been activated yet, but it may become crucial. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen balances his need for power with the risk of overreliance on these death-world kings.
