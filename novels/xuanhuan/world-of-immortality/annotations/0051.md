## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts from Xiao Chen's personal survival to the broader political landscape of Dragon Island. The Forbidden Sea's sealing traps everyone, triggering a desperate, bloody reshuffling. The narrative broadens to show the chaos and the formation of alliances, introducing new factions and power dynamics. The tone is grim and panoramic, emphasizing the law of the jungle and the fragile emergence of order. Xiao Chen chooses to hide during the initial chaos, demonstrating strategic patience, while other characters rush to consolidate power.

## Key Plot Points

The Forbidden Sea's sealing event is complete: even demi-gods and Fallen Angels cannot escape, confirming that everyone on Dragon Island is permanently trapped. This realization sparks three days of mass slaughter, orchestrated by hidden puppet masters, reducing the island's population by nearly half. The Treefolk Valley, defended by a mysterious green-haired youth of immense power, survives and becomes a model for alliance formation. In the aftermath, numerous alliances spring up, including the Nature Alliance, Damo Alliance, Undying Alliance, Light Alliance, and Blue Rain Alliance. To prevent a single dominant faction, a rule is adopted limiting alliances to ten members each—a shrewd political compromise that reshapes the island's future. Xiao Chen remains hidden, avoiding the bloodshed, while Zhao Lin'er and the tree-men endure attacks thanks to the green-haired youth's intervention.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of "Half-God" (半神) as a practical term for Sky-Mastery realm practitioners, showing that in the World of Immortality, even beings who can fly and rival ancient races are powerless against the island's sealing. The "Great Reshuffling" reflects a common xianxia trope: when powerful individuals are trapped together, chaos and elimination follow until a new order emerges. The ten-person cap rule is a classic political mechanism to prevent tyranny, reminiscent of ancient Chinese checks on warlord power. The green-haired youth's sudden appearance from Treefolk Valley hints at hidden masters on the island, a staple of the genre. The alliance names—Natural, Damo, Undying, etc.—carry symbolic weight, reflecting each founder's philosophy or lineage.

## Reading Guide

Readers should note the names of the newly formed alliances, as they will be major players in the coming arc. The green-haired youth is a significant unknown; his motivations and origins will likely be explored. Xiao Chen's decision to hide during the chaos shows his strategic patience, setting him apart from the rush to power. The ten-person cap rule will force constant negotiation, betrayal, and shifting allegiances. Keep an eye on the Undying Alliance—Yan Qingcheng and Rand are now a formal faction. The Damo Alliance, led by the monks Yizhen and Yichi, may prove a more neutral or trustworthy group. This chapter marks a turning point from isolated survival to organized faction politics on Dragon Island.
