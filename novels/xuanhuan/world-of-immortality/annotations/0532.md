## Chapter Overview

Chapter 532 delivers one of the most intense and game-changing battles in the Soul World arc. Xiao Chen, having reached the Seventh Heaven of Half-Ancestor, faces Leng Xingyi—an Otherworld Ancestral God who refuses to die even after his physical form is shattered. The chapter opens with a cataclysmic clash between two Heavenly Steles and a river of stars summoned by the Ancestral God. Xiao Chen's unbeatable martial body allows him to break through curses and blood magic, but the true test comes when Leng Xingyi unleashes an ancient 'sacrificial burning' ritual that threatens to consume the entire world. Xiao Chen's response—a daring reversal using the forbidden technique of live sacrifice—marks a turning point in his journey from survivor to executioner of the divine. The tone is one of grim triumph and foreboding, as power is seized through ultimate risk.

## Key Plot Points

Leng Xingyi proves nearly impossible to kill. His blood transforms into a rain of lethal blades and triggers a sacrificial curse that links his life force to the whole world. Xiao Chen, protected by the Heavenly Steles and the remnants of the Immortal-Slaying Formation Diagram, withstands the assault but recognizes that brute force alone cannot end the battle. In a moment of clarity, he recalls the secret of 'living sacrifice' from the Martial Ancestor's teachings—a technique that turns an enemy's own sacrificial magic against them. By merging his spirit with the Heavenly Steles and using the Formation Diagram as a conduit, Xiao Chen initiates the live sacrifice of an Ancestral God. The chapter climaxes with Leng Xingyi's horrified realization that he is being consumed by his own ritual. This incredible feat solidifies Xiao Chen's reputation as a being who can kill gods, but it also demonstrates the enormous cost: the Ancestral God's power and essence are not destroyed but absorbed, binding Xiao Chen to a higher plane of conflict. The victory is decisive yet sobering, hinting that the war with the Otherworld is just beginning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'live sacrifice' technique (活祭) is a rare and dangerous inversion of the sacrificial arts. Traditional xianxia ritual sacrifice involves offering one's own life or power to a higher entity. Here, Xiao Chen exploits the fact that Leng Xingyi's sacrificial curse already connects him to the world's energy; by inserting his own will and the Heavenly Stele's authority, he turns the Ancestral God into the offering. This reflects the Daoist principle of using the opponent's force against them. The Heavenly Steles are not mere weapons but fragments of the primordial order—their presence allows Xiao Chen to command cosmic laws. The chapter also references the 'Martial Ancestor's heretical chapter,' suggesting that forbidden knowledge from ancient times is required to defeat beings that transcend normal hierarchy. The concept of an Ancestral God's blood permeating a world and being able to destroy it with a thought underscores the terrifying scale of these beings.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets a new benchmark for Xiao Chen's combat capability. He has now killed an Ancestral God—albeit a weakened one—without having reached that realm himself. The absorption of Leng Xingyi's life essence may have long-term consequences on his cultivation and his soul. Readers should watch for how this live sacrifice affects Xiao Chen's aura and whether it draws unwanted attention from even greater powers. The collapse of the curse world (咒界) also signals that the boundaries between realms are fraying. Meanwhile, the fate of other captured cultivators (Yan Qingcheng, the Undying Sect Leader) remains unresolved, hinting that the war is far from over. Expect the next chapters to explore the aftermath of this titanic clash and the emergence of new threats from the Otherworld.
