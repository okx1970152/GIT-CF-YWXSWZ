## Chapter Overview
In this gripping chapter of the death world arc, Xiao Chen executes a daring ambush against the Giant King’s top fire‑seed warriors. Still recovering from previous battles, he gambles on a reverse‑strike strategy, knowing that every kill brings him closer to the yellow‑diamond third rank. The atmosphere is tense and desolate, lit by moonlight and starlight over the red earth of the dead world. Xiao Chen’s calculated ruthlessness is on full display as he picks off the returning experts one by one, using the Heaven’s Hand and the Origin Eight Sounds. But the Giant King, a terror king with overwhelming power, arrives sooner than expected, forcing Xiao Chen into a desperate flight westward. The chapter balances savage combat with tactical cunning, setting the stage for an even greater confrontation.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen lies in ambush on the road to Giant City, having already slain three of the ten experts the previous night. As the remaining seven return, he strikes from hiding, instantly killing one with the Heaven’s Hand. Proving his mastery of the Origin Eight Sounds, he focuses the ‘Weng’ sound on a single enemy, shattering them while enduring strikes from the other five. Despite heavy damage to his yellow‑diamond bones, he methodically eliminates three more, reducing the seven to three. Before he can finish, the Giant King arrives, shrouded in demonic clouds and wielding a huge bone claw that tears the sky. Xiao Chen, exhausted from a day and a night of continuous battle, chooses flight over folly. He leads the Giant King on a four‑day chase across the eastern lands and into the western territory ruled by the Angel King. Cornered, Xiao Chen taunts the Giant King by shouting that he is coming to attack the Angel City. The Giant King, terrified of the Angel King’s power, retreats. Xiao Chen collapses from exhaustion but has secured seven powerful fire seeds. He resolves to reach yellow‑diamond third rank before returning to settle the score.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter illuminates the hierarchical cultivation system of the death world. Fire‑seed beings are ranked by bone quality: white, blue, purple, yellow, and diamond. Xiao Chen, at yellow‑diamond second rank, can shatter lower‑tier enemies with the Heaven’s Hand and the Origin Eight Sounds, but the Giant King, likely at a higher tier, commands weather‑altering powers—a sign of approaching transcendence. The death world’s politics are brutal: regional kings like the Giant King and the Angel King rule through fear, and territories are strictly observed. Xiao Chen’s tactic of baiting the Giant King into the Angel King’s domain exploits this fear, a classic xianxia trick of using a stronger enemy’s rival to escape. The ‘Weng’ sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin is shown here as a concentrated soul‑shaking attack, effective against single foes but less so in group battles.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen’s victory is not final; he has only delayed the inevitable. His decision to flee westward plants a seed: the Angel King is now aware of the Giant King’s incursion, and future conflicts between them may create opportunities. The seven fire seeds he collected will fuel his breakthrough to yellow‑diamond third rank, bringing him closer to true parity with the terror kings. Readers should watch for his return to the eastern death world and the revenge he has promised. The Angel King, mentioned but not yet seen, looms as a potential ally or deadly foe. Xiao Chen’s journey in the death world is far from over; every step is a gamble for survival and power.
