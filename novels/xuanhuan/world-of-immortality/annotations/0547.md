## Chapter Overview

Set ten years after the cataclysmic battle, the chapter depicts the survivors settling into a rhythm of training in the Death World, with familiar faces providing moments of levity. But beneath the surface calm, Xiao Chen confronts the terrifying scope of the Otherworld's hidden power. Heavy revelations about the first Stone Man who took the final step, the Heavenly Emperor’s failed attempt, and the ninety-nine stone steps reshape his understanding of the war. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen deciding it is time to move and collect the remaining Heavenly Marks, setting the stage for his next decisive actions.

## Key Plot Points

Ten years have passed in the Death World. The survivors from the Nine Provinces and other worlds have carved out a semblance of normalcy, with cultivators engaging in friendly sparring and daily banter. Characters like the stuttering Jin Sanyi, the roguish Totti, and the ever-hungry Zhuge Pangzi provide comic relief, even as they continue to train and grow stronger. Keke awakens from a decade-long sleep, having advanced to Half-Ancestor Sixth Heaven through its unique dreaming cultivation method—a stark contrast to the grueling efforts of others. Golden Lion King confronts Xiao Chen, revealing that he stayed behind to give his son a chance to escape; he has reached Half-Ancestor Fifth Heaven through sheer talent. Xiao Chen demonstrates his solidified power: after years of refining his body, he has fully achieved a War Ancestor physique, with physical strength rivaling an Ancestral God. His focus has now shifted to elevating his spiritual cultivation, which remains at Half-Ancestor Seventh Heaven. A poignant exposition from the Heavenly Human elder reveals the horrifying depth of the Otherworld's power—accumulated over multiple civilization eras, with ancient monsters lying dormant. The first Stone Man, who successfully took the final step, was a supreme genius who could have led the Nine Provinces to victory, but he was broken—possibly by the ninety-nine stone steps or the entity they guard. The Heavenly Emperor also attempted that path but ultimately perished or vanished. Now, three supreme ancestral gods guard the ninety-nine stone steps at the Eternal Unknown. Xiao Chen holds the half skull of that first Stone Man, a key to understanding. The chapter ends with his decision to venture forth and collect the remaining Heavenly Marks.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces foundational lore for the Stone Man cultivation path. The 'final step' refers to the ultimate transformation into a complete Stone Man, a being of near-invincible power, often pursued as an alternative to becoming an Ancestral God. The 'ninety-nine stone steps' are a metaphysical trial at the Eternal Unknown, guarded by three supreme ancestral gods—a concept borrowed from the idea of heaven’s stairway in Daoist cosmology. The 'Heavenly Marks' are imprints left by the Heavenly Steles on a cultivator's soul, acting as keys to unlock deeper mysteries. The chapter also expands the concept of 'civilization eras': each cycle of destruction leaves behind hidden experts and relics, creating immense accumulation of power for the Otherworld. Understanding these layered histories is crucial to grasping the true scale of the conflict.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a pause before the storm, offering crucial world-building that recontextualizes everything. Readers should pay close attention to the lore about the first Stone Man and the ninety-nine stone steps, as it will likely define the endgame. Xiao Chen's decision to seek the remaining Heavenly Marks signals that the next arc will be one of active collection and confrontation. The revelation that the Otherworld's true depth is unknown even to the Nine Provinces' oldest allies sets a grim, epic tone. The cheerful character moments provide contrast, but the underlying dread is palpable. Prepare for Xiao Chen to push into uncharted territory, both physically and philosophically.
