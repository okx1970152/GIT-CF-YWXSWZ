## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a relentless, multi-opponent battle that showcases Xiao Chen's brutal combat philosophy and the devastating power of his Eight-Aspect Worlds. Ambushed by three top-tier cultivators—the seductive Heavenly Demon Palace fairy, the ethereal Cihang Jianzhai sword fairy, and the Roman Empire's scripture-wielding master—Xiao Chen fights with calculated fury. The tone is desperate and mythically charged, as he uses body-sacrifice tactics and his unique regeneration ability to wound each enemy in turn. The chapter crescendos with his escape, only to face an even greater threat: the Golden Lion King, now manifested as a silent, powerful boy with a third eye, standing on the Demon Plain like an omen of doom.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen engages the three experts in a chaotic sky battle. He seizes the Heavenly Demon fairy's feet, using her as a shield against the Cihang fairy's heart-sword, taking a wound through his shoulder. He retaliates by smashing his elbow into the Cihang fairy's chest, shattering her mirror and breaking ribs—a dire humiliation. Using Lingxi Sword Waves, he severs the Heavenly Demon fairy's feet, but she regenerates with the Heavenly Demon Rebirth Art, shocking onlookers. The Rome expert's ancient sacred book and a golden-armored woman join the assault. Xiao Chen unleashes his Eight-Aspect Worlds, transforming into a mass of chaos light, and with Four Symbols rotating, he strikes all four enemies simultaneously, forcing them to retreat injured. The Little Li Flying Dagger inheritor holds back, unwilling to stain his sacred blade. As Xiao Chen recovers, he senses the Golden Lion King's overwhelming presence on the Demon Plain—a being that required demigods three years ago, now unfathomably powerful.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight-Aspect Worlds (八相世界) are Xiao Chen's signature divine art, derived from Fuxi's eight trigrams. Each world embodies a primal element—heaven, earth, water, fire, thunder, mountain, wind, swamp—and their rotation generates devastating chaos energy. This technique mimics the primordial chaos said to precede creation, though Xiao Chen is careful not to claim he can truly create a world, as that is a power reserved for Ancestral Gods. The 'Melting Weapons into the Body' (熔兵炼体) is a rare Spirit-Treasury ability that allows Xiao Chen to absorb divine weapons into his flesh, granting him near-indestructible limbs and rapid recovery. The Little Li Flying Dagger, a legendary technique from Li Xunhuan, is a mind-guided blade that never misses; the current inheritor wields a blood-stained version that can kill gods but is bound by a vow to only strike the truly evil. The Heavenly Demon Rebirth Art is a supreme regenerative technique that can regrow lost limbs, demonstrating the depth of demonic cultivation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major confrontation on two fronts. Xiao Chen has temporarily repelled three powerful enemies, but they are not defeated—they will regroup and likely seek revenge. The Little Li Flying Dagger inheritor's restraint suggests he may become an ally or a future opponent on better terms. The most significant development is the return of the Golden Lion King, now in human form. Three years ago, this beast could only be matched by demigods; now its power is unknown but clearly immense. Xiao Chen's escape is temporary, and the Demon Plain meeting promises an explosive clash. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's Eight-Aspect Worlds will evolve against an opponent of such mystery. Additionally, the political intrigue with the Great Shang Kingdom and the Sun Church is still simmering, waiting for its next spark.
