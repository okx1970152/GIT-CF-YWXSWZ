## Chapter Overview
This chapter shifts the epicenter of conflict to the Otherworld's First Ancient City, where a primordial entity from a past civilization cycle desperately attempts to refine the Seventh Heavenly Stele to absorb its origin power. The Ancient Lord's reckless efforts destabilize the entire realm, causing the Otherworld's Ancestral Gods—Lang Ya and Lu Zhan—to reconsider their alliance. Meanwhile, the Nine Provinces' coalition, led by Pangu King and Shi Zhongdi, breaches the Otherworld's ancient prison, freeing corrupted heroes. The chapter culminates in the dramatic appearance of Renmo Ge Qian, the Tenth Son of the Ancestral Dragon, who kills a red-haired Upper Ancestral God with a single stroke of his perfected divine principle, forcing the Ancestral Gods to retreat and summon their ultimate trump card—the Holy Ancestor.

## Key Plot Points
The First Ancient City's Ancient Lord, a being of immense but decaying power, subjects the Seventh Heavenly Stele to infernal flames and crushing force, yet the stele remains indestructible and even grows stronger. Lang Ya attempts to manipulate the Ancient Lord by offering the use of a divine weapon from the sealed stone gate, but the Lord slaps him away, asserting dominance. The explosion of the scientific-military zone (科武文明区) nearly annihilates the world, but Lang Ya and Lu Zhan contain it by tearing space and filling the void with chaos. However, this is revealed as a diversion: the Nine Ancient Lamps descend to shield the ancient prison (太古牢笼区), allowing Pangu King, Shi Zhongdi, and other Nine Provinces powerhouses to attack the prison and free its inmates—ancient heroes corrupted over eons. The Otherworld loses ten of the eighteen prison layers. Enraged, the four Ancestral Gods retreat to the Ancient Ancestral Forbidden Zone (古祖禁区) and begin a ritual to summon the Holy Ancestor. Renmo Ge Qian, arriving at the battlefield, challenges the red-haired prodigy who had participated in the killing of the original Ancestral Dragon. Using the divine principle he created with Ke Ke's father—"What exists is forgotten; what is past is remembered"—he annihilates the Upper Ancestral God in an instant, demonstrating his near-perfect cultivation. The Ancestral Gods, seeing no other option, invoke the highest power of their realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of "Nine Ancient Lamps" (九盏古灯) appears in a new context. Originally associated with the sealing of the Nine Provinces, they are now revealed as protective artifacts capable of shielding an area from even the attacks of a primordial Stone Man King. Their sacred light seems to specifically counter the deathly marks of corrupted beings, making them a key tool in the liberation of the prisoners. The "Holy Ancestor" (圣祖) is the venerated founder of the Otherworld's cultivation system, a being of supreme authority who has presumably reached the absolute pinnacle of the Stone Man or Upper Ancestral God path. The summoning ritual hints at a desperate measure, as the Ancestral Gods are willing to pay an immense price to call upon this entity. The divine principle of Ge Qian and Ke Ke's father is a philosophical expression of the interplay between memory and reality, rooted in Daoist concepts of existence and illusion. Ge Qian's mastery of this principle brings it to near-perfect realization, allowing him to erase an Upper Ancestral God from existence—a testament to the power of combined insight and will.

## Reading Guide
The summoning of the Holy Ancestor sets the stage for the final confrontation in the Otherworld arc. This ancient being is likely the ultimate antagonist behind the war, and its awakening will force the Nine Provinces' heroes to unite as never before. Xiao Chen remains trapped inside the Seventh Heavenly Stele within the First Ancient City, undergoing a metamorphosis. His eventual emergence may coincide with the Holy Ancestor's arrival. The liberation of the corrupted prisoners from the ancient prison adds a wave of powerful but unstable allies to the Nine Provinces' cause. The reader should note Ge Qian's emotional state—his vengeance against the red-haired prodigy is personal, tied to the death of the original Ancestral Dragon. His story may reach its climax soon. The Nine Ancient Lamps now serve as a defensive linchpin; their fate will be critical. This chapter balances large-scale cosmic destruction with intimate moments of revenge and sacrifice.
