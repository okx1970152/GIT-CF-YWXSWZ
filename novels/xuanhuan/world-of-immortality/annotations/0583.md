## Chapter Overview

This chapter presents a profound and tragic confrontation between two of the most powerful beings in the World of Immortality: the Old Stone Man King, an ancient overlord from the Otherworld, and the Human Demon Ge Qian, a melancholic figure who has walked the lonely path of a Stone Man. The battle is not merely a clash of raw power but a philosophical struggle, with Ge Qian embodying the despair of one abandoned by heaven and earth. The chapter also marks the reappearance of the mischievous yet ancient Old Stone Turtle, whose irreverent humor cuts through the epic tension. Xiao Chen and Keke's father make a brief appearance, reminding readers of the larger war unfolding. The duel leaves both combatants scarred, setting the stage for future conflicts and deepening the tragic, desolate atmosphere that defines this arc.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Old Stone Man King confronting Ge Qian in a shattered, chaos-bound realm. The Old Stone Man King wields a wolf-tooth stone mace, a weapon that can shatter worlds, yet Ge Qian proves unexpectedly resilient. Ge Qian recites his mournful mantra—'Heaven abandons me, I can deceive heaven; the world forsakes me, I shall destroy the world'—which resonates through the cosmos and seems to weaken the Old Stone Man King. The battle escalates: both suffer grievous wounds. Ge Qian tears away the last remnants of flesh from the Old Stone Man King's skull, but the Old Stone Man King counters by crushing Ge Qian's body and attempting to refine his soul with the power of all heavens. However, Ge Qian survives, emerging from the darkness as a more melancholy but indomitable figure. Xiao Chen and Keke's father arrive briefly; Xiao Chen asks Ge Qian why he hides his power, and Ge Qian reveals that the more he knows, the more despairing he becomes, admitting he has betrayed the Nine Provinces. The Old Stone Man King then flees deeper into the chaos toward a great ancient city where the Old Stone Turtle awaits. The turtle greets him mockingly, calling him 'firewood,' and reveals that he has been protecting a human—implied to be the Martial Ancestor or someone of great importance. The Old Stone Man King threatens the turtle, but the turtle remains unafraid, hinting at his own hidden strength and connection to the Heavenly Steles.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Old Stone Man King and Ge Qian are both practitioners of the Stone Man path, a legendary and nearly suicidal cultivation method that involves gradually petrifying the body to achieve ultimate power. The 'final step' refers to a Stone Man's ultimate transformation into a complete, eternal stone being. This path is considered heaven-defying and brings immense power at the cost of gradual loss of humanity. Ge Qian's mantra—'Heaven abandons me, I can deceive heaven; the world forsakes me, I shall destroy the world'—reflects a philosophy of total rejection of cosmic order, reminiscent of the 'Heaven-Defying' mindset common in xianxia. The Old Stone Turtle is a recurring enigmatic figure associated with the Heavenly Steles; his seemingly foolish demeanor hides immense age and knowledge, and he often dispenses cryptic wisdom. The reference to '柴火' (firewood) is a derogatory term for the Old Stone Man King's dried-out appearance, highlighting the turtle's audacity.

## Reading Guide

His confession of betraying the Nine Provinces suggests a complex backstory that may be explored in future chapters. Xiao Chen's brief interaction with Ge Qian also hints at a possible alliance or future confrontation. The battle leaves both the Old Stone Man King and Ge Qian wounded, setting the stage for a continued power struggle. The overarching war against the Otherworld is reaching a climax, with all major players converging on the ancient city.
