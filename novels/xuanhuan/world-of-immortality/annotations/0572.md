## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen and his dubious ally, the old goat, into the heart of the Sun Star Palace, where celestial kings like the Stone Corpse, Lotus King, and Sharpening Beast are vying for a legendary Heaven-defying War Treasure. The atmosphere is thick with tension as these ancient, near-godlike figures assert dominance, shattering a divine bell to signal their intent. Rather than engage directly, the old goat concocts an audacious plan to steal the treasure using a mysterious formation and Xiao Chen's divine diagram. The tone balances epic dread with the old goat's irreverent, almost farcical humor, creating a unique tension between cosmic stakes and mundane deception.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the sound of a bell being destroyed by the Stone Corpse, signaling that the celestial kings have begun their contest. The old goat grumbles about these 'tyrants' and expresses his intention to seize the treasure by trickery rather than force. He reveals knowledge of Xiao Chen's divine diagram and an ancient scroll, proposing to use them in conjunction with his self-proclaimed 'First Forbidden Formation' to pluck the treasure from under the noses of the kings. Xiao Chen is wary but reluctantly agrees.

As they approach the central region, a new threat emerges: a mysterious strongman from the foreign school, described as a 'mud man' with a wooden, unreadable face and one terrifying eye. He confronts them, but a disheveled 'lost soul' carrying a stone sword appears, having just feasted on the arm of a White Ancestral God. The old goat uses a strange, ancient language to direct the lost soul against the mud man, and the two clash with devastating power, destroying parts of the palace.

Escaping the chaos, Xiao Chen and the old goat find a hidden spot to lay the formation. The old goat scratches what looks like random scribbles on rocks and trees, claiming it is the 'First Forbidden Formation.' Despite Xiao Chen's skepticism, the old goat insists and eventually coaxes the divine diagram out of Xiao Chen. After several failed attempts—first snatching the Stone Corpse's coffin plank, then the Lotus King's sleeve—the formation finally produces a small stone box, the intended Heaven-defying War Treasure.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'First Forbidden Formation' concept, with its deliberate incompleteness, references the Daoist principle of 'the missing one' (遁去的一) from the I Ching. The old goat explains that perfection cannot endure, so he introduces a flaw—a pebble—to make the formation eternal. This reflects the philosophical idea that completeness leads to stagnation, and that true power lies in embracing imperfection.

The Celestial Divine Language (天界神语) used by the old goat to command the lost soul suggests a lost lore of sound-based divine commands. This connects to earlier mentions of the Eight Origin Sounds, reinforcing that spoken words and tones carry immense power in this world.

The Sun Star Palace itself is a realm of contradictions—a blazing star transformed into a serene, beautiful landscape with divine springs and cranes. This juxtaposition of destruction and tranquility is a hallmark of the xianxia genre, where the most dangerous places often hide the most exquisite treasures.

## Reading Guide

This chapter pays off the old goat's mysterious buildup. His ability to snatch items from celestial kings raises huge questions: Who is he really? Is he truly a reborn celestial king, or just a lucky fraud? Readers should watch how his actions affect the broader power balance, as stealing from these kings will not go unnoticed. The stone box is now in Xiao Chen's hands, but its nature remains unclear—will it be a weapon, a key, or a curse?

The tension with the foreign school's mud man is unresolved; he survived the lost soul's attack and will likely pursue Xiao Chen. Meanwhile, the celestial kings' reactions to the theft are sure to escalate the conflict. The chapter also plants seeds of the old goat's deeper knowledge: he knows about Xiao Chen's divine diagram and the ancient scroll, hinting at a broader conspiracy. The coming chapters should reveal whether this partnership will prosper or self-destruct.

Overall, this chapter is a masterclass in blending high-stakes divine intrigue with character-driven comedy, setting the stage for a treasure that could tip the scales of the war against the Otherworld.
