## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with the Martial Ancestor at the peak of his power—a being at the Ninth Heaven of the Half-Ancestor realm, crushing three Ancestral God-level remnants with contemptuous ease. His appearance reshapes the battlefield: he captures dozens of Half-Ancestors and Death World monarchs, seeking information about the enigmatic Oracle Bone Diagram. The tone is one of tragic inevitability and restrained grief: the Martial Ancestor has just regained his full memories and life's purpose, yet his final words and actions carry the weight of a one-way journey. He bestows gifts upon his followers, completes the Martial Imprint by passing the complete martial tradition, and then strides toward the Eternal Light, a decision that feels both triumphant and funereal. This chapter marks the resolution of the Martial Ancestor's arc, elevating him from a distant legend to a poignant, almost paternal figure who sacrifices himself for a greater cause.

## Key Plot Points

The Martial Ancestor's reappearance is devastatingly absolute: he grinds three Ancestral God-level remnant souls into pure soul force, captures every Half-Ancestor and Death World monarch present, and interrogates them about the Oracle Bone Diagram. Learning that the deepest part of the Death World may hold the answers, he decides to seek it after first using the Eternal Light to resolve his own body's issues. The Martial Imprint, now a complete soul, begs to follow but is refused—the Martial Ancestor instead transmits the full martial technique to it, declaring it independent. He then bestows techniques upon several characters: a mysterious martial art to Totti, a gluttonous cultivation method to Zhuge Liang (linking power to consumption), and martial heart techniques to Wu Ming and his son. Keke is offered a supreme artifact—a dark grinding wheel that surpasses even the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd—but pouts because it is ugly, flatly rejecting it in a moment of lightheartedness. The Martial Ancestor then completes Xiao Chen's Martial Ancestor True Scripture, whispering something about Keke's future. Finally, he marches toward the Eternal Light, leaving behind a solemn warning that they may never meet again. The closing imagery is deeply melancholic, evoking the feeling of a hero embarking on a doomed quest.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Martial Ancestor's 'Nine Heavens' (九重天) denotes the absolute peak of the Half-Ancestor realm, a state just one step from true Ancestral Godhood. His ability to crush Ancestral God-level remnants despite not being a full Ancestral God himself underscores the fiercely hierarchical nature of this cultivation world—where realm differences, even within the same tier, can be vast. The 'Eternal Light' (永恒之光) is herein presented as a tool for self-transformation, not merely a treasure to be seized, hinting that it may be central to transcending the Half-Ancestor bottleneck. The 'Oracle Bone Diagram' (甲骨图) continues to be a thread connecting the deepest mysteries of the Death World, possibly to the origins of the cosmos itself. The 'Doomsday Heavenly Halberd' (末日天戈) is confirmed as a supreme Ancestral God weapon, and the dark grinding wheel offered to Keke is implied to be even stronger—yet Keke's dismissal of it due to its appearance is a delightful reminder that even divine beings have personalities beyond their power levels.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a bittersweet farewell to one of the most powerful figures in the series. The Martial Ancestor's final scene—striding toward the Eternal Light with captured Half-Ancestors in tow—sets up a potential future arc where he may reappear either transformed or as a martyr. His gifts to the side characters (Totti, Zhuge Liang, Wu Ming) are likely to bear fruit in later chapters: Zhuge Liang's 'eat to become stronger' technique, in particular, is both absurd and ominous. The incomplete transmission to Xiao Chen and the cryptic whisper about Keke suggest that the protagonist's path is still unfolding around the clues left by the Ancestral Gods. The Martial Imprint, now a complete soul with the full martial tradition, may become a key independent ally or a wandering sage. The deepest part of the Death World is now a looming destination, and the reader should brace for revelations about the Oracle Bone Diagram and the fate of the fallen Ancestral Gods. Expect a tonal pivot from this chapter's elegiac majesty to more grounded, personal conflicts in the chapters ahead.
