## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks the triumphant conclusion of the beast-fighting tournament and the explosive beginning of a new arc. Keke, the snow-white divine beast, dominates the final matches, defeating five sacred beasts to claim the title of Southern Wilds Holy Beast King—the youngest in history. Yet the celebration is overshadowed by momentous news: a mysterious floating celestial palace has appeared in the neighboring Tianluo Kingdom, releasing four legendary divine weapons that shake the Eternal Continent. Xiao Chen, having revealed his bond with Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon, chooses not to rush to the celestial palace. Instead, he tends to the critically wounded Little Defiant Dragon, using a precious Buddha relic and the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree to spark its rebirth. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen's deepening introspection on cultivation, recognizing that to face such a world he must forge his own unique path. The balance of closure and new mystery drives the narrative forward.

## Key Plot Points
Keke enters the tournament's decisive round and one by one defeats the five remaining sacred beasts: the White Tiger (shattered to death), the golden monkey Gold (losing eight of its nine lives), the Five-Colored Peacock King (near death, saved by a cyan feather), the Golden Lion King, and the Three-Eyed Golden Lion. The white beast is unstoppable. It is invited onto the ancient Holy Beast King platform, where it playfully leaves a paw print beside the engraved names—a print that later cracks the stone. Keke receives a Buddha sarira from the organizers. To everyone's shock, it nibbles a piece off like candy and hides the rest for the Little Defiant Dragon, all without any harm. Xiao Chen, exposed as the owner, retrieves Keke and the dragon and leaves. The next day, news erupts that a floating celestial palace has appeared in Tianluo, and from its first seal four legendary artifacts have been unearthed: the Dragon-Slaying Blade, the Demon-Sealing List, the God-Worshipping Altar, and the Eternal Tree. Each has mythic pedigree—the altar was forged by Laozi and the Buddha to contact ancestral gods; the tree grants immortality. Practitioners flood toward Tianluo. Xiao Chen remains, using the sarira and the sacred tree to heal the Little Defiant Dragon. The dragon's shattered organs regenerate, undergoing a rebirth that promises greater strength. Meanwhile, the broken black blade Xiao Chen carries suddenly flies off toward the celestial palace. Xiao Chen reflects on the innate powers of sacred beasts like Keke and the Golden Lion King, realizing he must develop unique abilities to grow. He resolves to find his own path, not merely follow others.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Holy Beast King (圣兽王): A prestigious title from the Southern Wilds' beast-fighting tradition, inscribed on an ancient stone platform. The title elevates the beast to a legendary status. Keke's victory is unprecedented for its youth. Buddha's Relic (舍利子): In Buddhist mythology, sarira are crystalline remains left after an enlightened being's cremation, containing immense spiritual energy. Here it comes from a sacred beast that once served the Buddha, so it holds powerful Buddha essence. Keke's ability to eat it raw showcases its extraordinary constitution. Four Divine Weapons: Each has distinct lore. The Dragon-Slaying Blade has drunk dragon blood across ages; the Demon-Sealing List seals demons and gods; the God-Worshipping Altar, made by Laozi and Buddha, can call upon the lingering spirits of primordial ancestors like Youchao; the Eternal Tree is a black iron tree that grants deathless longevity to mortals and boosts cultivators. Floating Celestial Palace (长生殿堂): Appearing in the sky, such realms are common in xianxia as both treasure troves and death traps. The fact that four major artifacts emerged from just the first seal hints at even greater treasures within.

## Reading Guide
This chapter closes the tournament arc and opens a new world-level event. The celestial palace in Tianluo is clearly a major destination for all strong cultivators, and the flight of Xiao Chen's broken blade suggests his destiny is tied to it. However, Xiao Chen's decision to stay behind shows his strategic discipline; he recognizes he is not yet ready for a power scramble. The Little Defiant Dragon's rebirth with a Buddha relic hints at future power gains. Readers should note the parallels: the sacred beasts' innate abilities far surpass human cultivation at the same realm. Xiao Chen's internal resolution to forge a unique path will likely influence his breakthrough to the Spirit-Treasury realm. Watch for the broken blade's connection to the celestial palace, and the inevitable pull that will draw Xiao Chen north. The contrast between bustling Tianluo and Xiao Chen's quiet recovery creates dramatic tension.
