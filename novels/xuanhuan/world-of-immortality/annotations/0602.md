## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen and his allies into the eerie depths of the Death World's celestial palace, where an invisible horror stalks them. A pale, blood-stained face appears and vanishes, causing even the Great Might Nether King to lose his mind. The revelation that the palace's original owner has become a 'resentment' entity—a formless consciousness born from hatred and decay—raises the stakes. This entity, gestating within the Nether King after he consumed its corpse, proves nearly undetectable. Xiao Chen must rely on his ancient relics and the stone-man puppet to survive. The chapter culminates in a shocking discovery: the Heavenly Emperor himself killed this ancient being and created the Most Evil Place. With the entity absorbed by a mysterious stone jar, the group presses deeper into the Death World, arriving at seven dead cities arranged like the Big Dipper, where Keke is imprisoned. The presence of multiple Otherworld Ancestral Gods sets the stage for a desperate confrontation.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's stone-man puppet searching the Death Palace for an unseen observer. A pale face with blood appears to Sha Polang and the Little Defiant Dragon, terrifying them. The Great Might Nether King, his mind already damaged, awakens and screams in terror, identifying the face as the 'soul reaper'—the palace's original owner returned. The resentment entity emerges not from the gray mist but from within the Nether King's own body, revealing that it was gestating there after he devoured its corpse. This entity claims to be an 'upper ancestral god' and attempts to seize Xiao Chen's stone-man puppet as a new vessel. However, a stone jar from Xiao Chen's possession autonomously absorbs the resentment entity and the surrounding gray mist, purifying the area. Afterward, Xiao Chen refines the Nether King's immense residual soul power into a new body for the weakened spirits of Tianwaitian and Renwairen. Guided by their directions, the group travels for over a month to the deepest part of the Death World, where they find seven dead cities in a Big Dipper formation, each wreathed in killing light. Keke is imprisoned there, but Otherworld Ancestral Gods still guard the area. Xiao Chen hides using the Pangu Stone Token and prepares to strike.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of 'resentment' (怨) in this chapter draws on Chinese folk beliefs that a powerful being's lingering hatred can coalesce into a malevolent spirit even after death. This entity is particularly dangerous because it is not a traditional ghost or undead but a formless consciousness that can infiltrate minds and bodies. The Big Dipper (北斗七星) formation is a common motif in Daoist cosmology and xianxia literature, often used for sealing or destructive arrays. Here, seven dead cities are arranged to imprison Keke, with each city radiating 'killing light'—a term that evokes primordial, world-ending energy. Additionally, the Heavenly Emperor (天帝) is a figure in Chinese mythology often associated with the supreme ruler of heaven, but here he is implicated in an ancient murder and the creation of the Most Evil Place, adding layers of moral complexity to the cosmic hierarchy. The stone jar that autonomously absorbs the resentment entity suggests that Xiao Chen carries artifacts of profound, untapped origin.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major confrontation: Keke is held in a formidable prison of seven dead cities, and Xiao Chen must contend with both the cities' deadly properties and the surrounding Otherworld Ancestral Gods. The revelation that the Heavenly Emperor killed the Death Palace's original owner hints at a deeper conspiracy within the Celestial Realm. Readers should note the stone jar's mysterious behavior—it may hold significance for future events. The Big Dipper formation of cities mirrors earlier tales of seven steles or seals, suggesting a recurrent pattern in the world's design. In the next chapters, expect Xiao Chen to devise a plan to infiltrate or break the cities while avoiding the Ancestral Gods. The Little Defiant Dragon and the revived Tianwaitian and Renwairen will likely play key roles. The chapter also deepens the lore of the 'resentment' entity, which may become a recurring threat or tool.
