## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal turning point in Xiao Chen’s cultivation journey and his psychological readiness for the trials ahead. After a moment of unexpected abundance—a heavenly five-colored ginseng—he undergoes a profound physical transformation, divinizing three more acupoints and forging a connection between eight divine nodes. His body becomes a treasure of near‑invulnerable flesh and boundless life energy. Yet the real shock comes at the end: instead of consolidating his gains, Xiao Chen coldly declares a campaign of ten consecutive life‑or‑death battles against the strongest young experts on the Eternal Continent. The tone here is a blend of serene harvest and grim resolve. There is no triumph, only the weight of necessity.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Keke clutching a rare violet‑gold ginseng, its flesh dissolving into a rainbow‑colored liquid. Xiao Chen, Keke, and the Little Defiant Dragon share this heavenly treasure, but the process is far from peaceful. A massive pull from Xiao Chen’s Baihui acupoint (the crown of the head) greedily absorbs the life essence, triggering a new round of acupoint divination. The process repeats for both Laogong acupoints (palms), devouring most of the remaining ginseng until only a third remains for Keke and the dragon. The three newly divinized acupoints join the five already perfected, and suddenly a hidden network of energy pulses between all eight: left and right Shangqiu, Zhongting, left and right Jianjing, left and right Laogong, and Baihui. This fusion of life essence grants Xiao Chen an almost superhuman physique, with flesh as tough as steel and vitality that can heal wounds in moments. The narrative then shifts from cultivation to resolution. Xiao Chen reveals that he has glimpsed a new realm beyond Mortal‑Shedding—the Spirit‑Treasury—and that only extreme combat can force the breakthrough. He asks Hai Yuntian and the portly merchant to locate the strongest young experts, including Dugu Jianmo, Kanasi’s monstrous brother, and others. Then comes the chilling declaration: he will fight them all, one after another, ten battles in a single night, returning either wounded or dead. He orders Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon to guard the house and kill any intruder.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The core concept here is acupoint divination (穴道神化), a unique cultivation path in which a practitioner transforms ordinary acupoints into condensed nodes of light, each holding vast life essence. The human body has 365 main acupoints; Xiao Chen has now completed eight. This is distinct from the usual realm‑driven progression and reflects his foundational method from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art. The five‑colored ginseng is described as a “dragon ginseng” (龍參)—a legendary herb guarded by dragons, far more potent than ordinary dragon eggs. Its power derives from millennia of absorbing natural essence. The Spirit‑Treasury realm (识藏) is the next major stage after Mortal‑Shedding, where a cultivator opens the inner treasures of the body; it is said to be a door to unlocking human potential. Xiao Chen’s decision to pursue extreme combat as a catalyst for breakthrough is a classic xianxia trope: peril and crisis refine the spirit faster than peaceful cultivation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter closes the “city‑life” arc and signals a return to high‑stakes conflict. The upcoming battles will test Xiao Chen’s new physical limits and reveal the hidden capabilities of the eight divinized acupoints. The vow to fight ten top experts in one night creates an almost fatalistic tension: will any single opponent be enough to push him over the edge into the Spirit‑Treasury, or will the cumulative damage prove too great? Watch for the involvement of Kanasi’s brother and Dugu Jianmo, already established as near‑peerless. The reader should also note the continued mystery of the white‑shelled turtle (Xiao Zei) and the sleeping power of Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon. The coming chapters promise a storm of violence, sacrifice, and possibly the first true taste of Xiao Chen’s new mastery.
