## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers on the promise of a blood-soaked reckoning. Xiao Chen, having publicly challenged the Six Heroes of the Cultivation World to a duel in Chang'an, faces the final three: Lingxu Yan, Fang Tianqi, and Ming Ren. The battle is fierce and protracted, with Xiao Chen using his Martial Body and the War Sword to hold his own against three Spirit-Treasury peak opponents. But his true ambition is far greater: using the stone drill and stone ball—mysterious relics from the stone man within him—he strikes at the hidden demigod-level enemies, the White Tiger's third son Hu Ben and the Sun God's son Oulituo, gravely wounding both. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen hunting down and killing the remaining three heroes, fulfilling his vow. The night ends with Xiao Chen's name carved into legend as a slayer of the young generation's elite and a crippler of ancient bloodlines.

## Key Plot Points

The opening confrontation sets a desperate tone as the three surviving heroes—Lingxu Yan, Fang Tianqi, and Ming Ren—surround Xiao Chen. Lingxu Yan wields the Asura Umbrella, a treasure that channels chaotic sword energy. Fang Tianqi commands the Nine Tribulations Divine Palace, a mountain-like artifact that crashes down with crushing force. Ming Ren fights without artifacts, his body itself a peerless weapon, his fingers releasing beams that tear through space. Xiao Chen uses the War Sword and Reverse Dragon Seven Steps to counter, eventually cleaving Fang Tianqi's Divine Palace in two, wounding him severely. This victory is crucially timed: Keke secretly informs Xiao Chen that the real threats, Hu Ben and Oulituo, are watching from afar. Xiao Chen then deploys his hidden killer—the stone drill and stone ball from the stone man within him. These weapons, devoid of energy fluctuations, strike with devastating power, blasting off Hu Ben's tiger leg and shattering half of Oulituo's body. Both flee, grievously wounded. Xiao Chen then pursues and decapitates Fang Tianqi. Keke engages Lingxu Yan, stripping him of the Asura Umbrella with the Lost Paradise's binding light. Xiao Chen uses the 'Weng' sound technique to obliterate Lingxu Yan's blood-shadow clone. Finally, Xiao Chen chases Ming Ren north for over two thousand li. Ming Ren fights fiercely but is ultimately shattered; in his destruction, he reveals that the Six Heroes' Ming Ren was merely an incarnation of a far greater being, one who vows to confront Xiao Chen directly in the future. With that, all six are dead, and Xiao Chen's reputation is secured.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The duel in Chang'an echoes the classic 'Longmen Inn' tension of a neutral city hosting a battle to the death between opposing forces. The Six Heroes represent the cream of the young generation from the 'Cultivation World' (修真界), a realm distinct from the Mortal World and the World of Immortality. Their techniques—the Asura Umbrella, the Nine Tribulations Divine Palace—are typical of the 'artifact-based' combat style of that world, where cultivators refine their treasures to immense power. In contrast, Ming Ren's approach of forging his own body into a weapon aligns with the 'Treasure Body' or 'Martial Body' path that Xiao Chen himself walks. The 'Weng' sound technique is a form of primordial syllable cultivation, tapping into the eight primal sounds that can resonate with the soul itself. The stone drill and stone ball are relics of the Ancestral God Suiren, the primordial fire-bringer; their power lies in their absolute simplicity and connection to the origins of the world. Hu Ben and Oulituo are demigod-level scions of the White Tiger and Sun God lineages, representing the entrenched aristocratic powers that view mortals as ants. Their defeat by Xiao Chen is a stunning reversal that shakes the foundations of the Cultivation World's hierarchy.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks the climax of Xiao Chen's long-prepared reckoning with the Cultivation World's young elites. The narrative tension has been building for chapters, and the payoff is brutal and decisive. Readers should note that Xiao Chen's hidden weapons—the stone drill and stone ball—are now revealed to be his ultimate trump card, but their use depletes his spiritual energy entirely. This sets up a vulnerability for future conflicts. Ming Ren's revelation that he is but an incarnation of a far greater being introduces a new, hidden enemy who may appear later at full power. The survival of Hu Ben and Oulituo, though crippled, ensures that their families—the White Tiger Clan and the Sun God's followers—will burn with vengeance. Xiao Chen has made his name as a demigod-slayer, but he has also painted a target on his back. The next chapters are likely to follow his flight from the inevitable retaliation, and his continued search for ways to rescue Qingqing and face the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden. The emotional weight of his severed ties with Ruoshui lingers beneath the surface.
