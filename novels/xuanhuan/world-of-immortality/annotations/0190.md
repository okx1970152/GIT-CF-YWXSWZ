## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a double-edged spectacle: Keke's astonishing combat prowess and its shrewd, almost human-like negotiation to claim the Beast King Relic. The tournament atmosphere crackles with excitement as the crowd witnesses the little white beast's phenomenal power—first defeating the Golden Lion King and the golden ape Gold, then countering the Peacock King's fabled five-colored light. But the true highlight is Keke's masterful manipulation of public sentiment. It plays the crowd perfectly, feigning reluctance and then demanding the prize directly, showing a level of cunning that far exceeds a typical spirit beast. The chapter balances high-octane action with clever tactics, deepening Keke's character as both an adorable trickster and a formidable force.

## Key Plot Points

- Keke enters the beast-fighting arena as a mysterious seventh participant, immediately stunning everyone by defeating the Golden Lion King with a golden binding light that negates its destruction eye. This proves Keke is no ordinary beast.
- The golden ape Gold confronts Keke, but Keke easily evades its full-bodied assault, even teasingly imitating Gold's golden light by hiding its own seven-colored radiance. Gold is humiliated.
- The Peacock King intervenes with its five-colored divine light, but Keke surprisingly replicates the five-colored light and defeats the Peacock King, astonishing the crowd and establishing its ability to learn and counter divine arts.
- Six ancient experts attempt to forcibly remove Keke from the arena, but the 50,000-strong crowd erupts in support of the little beast, forcing the tournament organizers to allow it to continue.
- Keke cleverly feigns reluctance and negotiates for the Buddha Beast Relic (the primary prize) before agreeing to fight again, showing strategic thinking.
- The chapter ends with Keke facing all four remaining sacred beasts simultaneously and dominating them, targeting the third eye of the Golden Lion King—the most dangerous aspect of its opponent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The 'five-colored divine light' (五色神光) is a famous technique from the Chinese myth of Kong Xuan, a peacock demon who could sweep away any opponent. Its appearance here ties the Peacock King to a legendary lineage.
- The 'Beast King Relic' (兽王舍利) is a Buddhist concept—a crystal-like bead left after the nirvana of a holy beast, containing immense spiritual power. Such relics are often sought for cultivation enhancement.
- Keke's ability to mimic the five-colored light hints at its primordial, rootless nature: as a being that can learn any divine art, it transcends fixed lineages. This mirrors the concept of a 'primordial beast' that predates modern cultivation systems.
- The tournament setting (斗兽大赛) is a common xianxia trope where beast masters compete for prestige and treasure. The crowd's reaction drives the plot, as spectator support can override even powerful experts.

## Reading Guide

- Watch for Keke's tactical brilliance: it deliberately obscured its true seven-colored light to mimic Gold's gold light, both to taunt and to hide its full capacity. This may be a prelude to more calculated moves.
- The Golden Lion King's third eye has been a recurring threat—Keke's decision to target it directly suggests the eye holds a trump card that could endanger Keke. The outcome of this confrontation remains unresolved.
- The tournament prize negotiation sets up a future goal. Keke's desire for the relic will likely align with Xiao Chen's cultivation needs, and the relic's nature may connect to Keke's mysterious origins.
- Xiao Chen's presence in the background reminds us that Keke's actions are not entirely independent; the relationship between the two will deepen as Keke demonstrates both loyalty and independent will.
