## Chapter Overview

This chapter takes a turn from direct combat into deep lore and mystery. Xiao Chen, along with the Lone Sword Demon and a mysterious woman, tracks a seemingly harmless fat boy who carries the unsettling aura of his old enemy Kailo. Their pursuit leads them to a hidden village on Dragon Island—a place that feels like a breath of the mortal world, yet is steeped in an ancient curse. Here, a frail old woman reveals the truth: Kailo was killed and his beast souls were transplanted into her grandson. More importantly, she unveils the island's greatest secret—a way to leave the sealed realm, but only by gathering nine Companion Dragon Kings or a single Primordial Ancestral Dragon. The chapter is laden with tragic, desolate undertones, hinting at the immense cost of freedom.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's keen observation: the fat boy possesses Kailo's scent but a different body. Suspecting possession or resurrection, Xiao Chen follows him into the southern forests, accompanied by the Lone Sword Demon and a veiled woman. Their shared suspicion confirms that something sinister is afoot. The boy eventually leads them to a hidden village, a rustic settlement of about seventy households, complete with roosters and dogs—a surreal contrast to the savage wilderness of Dragon Island.

An old woman appears like a ghost, confronting the trackers. Xiao Chen straightforwardly states his intent: he sensed a familiar enemy's aura in her grandson. To his surprise, the old woman admits she killed Kailo and extracted his ancient beast souls, implanting them into her own grandson, who is also a barbarian descendant. She explains that her people are descendants of cultivators stranded on the island long ago, and they are cursed to be unable to leave. However, legend holds that nine Companion Dragon Kings working together can summon a divine ship—built from Ancestral Dragon bones and World Tree wood—that can cross the Forbidden Sea. An alternative is a single Primordial Ancestral Dragon, which equals the power of nine dragon kings.

This revelation redefines the stakes on Dragon Island. The earlier pursuit of the Purple Dragon King gains new significance. Xiao Chen, the Lone Sword Demon, and the mysterious woman now have a clear, daunting goal: capture nine dragon kings or find the ancestral dragon to escape the sealed realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of beast soul transplantation, a dark art where ancient beast souls sealed within barbarian bloodlines can be forcibly extracted and implanted into another host. This echoes the xianxia trope of soul theft and body snatching, but is grounded in the racial lore of the barbarian tribes. The old woman's casual mention of this act underscores the harsh morality of Dragon Island—survival justifies any means.

The 'Companion Dragon King' (伴生龙王) is a dragon born from a dragon egg alongside its mother, possessing kingly power from birth. The 'Primordial Ancestral Dragon' (祖龙) is the supreme dragon sovereign, said to evolve from any dragon lineage through endless combat. Both are key to escaping the island, but their acquisition is nearly impossible.

The divine ship itself—made from Ancestral Dragon bone and World Tree wood—is a classic xianxia motif: a artifact of primordial materials that can traverse forbidden realms. The World Tree (通天神木) here is the same cosmic tree Keke's eggs were found near, tying the lore together.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes the primary long-term objective for Xiao Chen and other practitioners on Dragon Island: gather nine Companion Dragon Kings or find the Primordial Ancestral Dragon to summon the divine ship. Watch for how this new goal reshapes alliances and conflicts. The old woman and her cunning grandson may become important allies or hidden threats. The mysterious woman in colored mist remains unnamed—her identity and intentions are clearly set up for future revelation. Additionally, the curse on the villagers hints that even if Xiao Chen gathers the dragon kings, there may be a price to pay. The chapter ends with a glimmer of hope, but the path ahead is fraught with titanic battles and strategic scheming.
