## Chapter Overview
This chapter opens with a chilling confrontation: a powerful woman who bears the exact likeness of Xiao Chen's lost love Ruo Shui appears, demanding to take Qingqing. Despite Xiao Chen's desperate resistance using the Black Iron Seal and Golden Divine Halberd, he is easily brushed aside. Qingqing, showing maturity and sacrifice, agrees to go with the woman to protect Xiao Chen, leaving him a tiny crystal tower—the legendary Haotian Tower—to contact her master. After their departure, Xiao Chen is challenged by Deng Yu of Shu Mountain, who exploits Qingqing's absence to attack. However, Deng Yu's Nirvana realm power fluctuates, and Xiao Chen turns the tables using the Haotian Tower. He defeats Deng Yu and a middle-aged cultivator, then deliberately spreads news of the tower to attract the old man. Half a month later, the old man arrives, and they journey through a space gate into a mysterious dimension where they discover the woman's true purpose: she is guarding the Tongtian Death Bridge and using a growing Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform to nurture the four legendary War Swords.

## Key Plot Points
- Xiao Chen and Qingqing are confronted by a woman who is a pure essence projection from a lotus platform; she resembles Ruo Shui but carries ancient, cold authority.
- Qingqing voluntarily leaves with the woman to prevent further violence, handing Xiao Chen the Haotian Tower to summon her master.
- Deng Yu, a Shu Mountain cultivator at the Nirvana realm, attacks Xiao Chen now that Qingqing is absent, taunting him.
- Despite being outmatched in realm, Xiao Chen uses the Haotian Tower to fight back, but initially struggles due to inexperience.
- Deng Yu's Nirvana realm power fluctuates unpredictably, and he drops drastically in strength, allowing Xiao Chen to brutally beat him and finally cripple him.
- Xiao Chen then captures a middle-aged cultivator with the tower and lets another flee to spread news of the Haotian Tower, hoping the old man will come.
- After half a month, the old man appears, easily deducing the situation and taking Xiao Chen through a space gate to the woman's dimension.
- There, they see the Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform, still growing and thus even more precious than the Buddha's lotus treasure, and the four legendary War Swords being nurtured within it.
- The old man reveals the woman is guarding the Tongtian Death Bridge, which connects the cultivation world and the mortal world, and that she intends to use the lotus and swords for some purpose.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Nirvana realm (called Jiehuo by cultivators) is a volatile stage where one's power oscillates between peaks and troughs, representing the 'burning' of karma before rebirth. This chapter dramatizes that instability: Deng Yu's sudden drop in strength is a key feature of the realm, making it both dangerous and unpredictable. The Haotian Tower is a legendary artifact from Chinese mythology, associated with celestial power. The Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform is a Buddhist sacred object; a growing platform is even more extraordinary, implying it has not yet reached full maturity. The Tongtian Death Bridge is a liminal space connecting worlds, guarded by ancient powers. The four War Swords are legendary weapons from another world, now being refined by the mysterious woman.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point: Qingqing is taken away, and Xiao Chen gains a powerful artifact and an unexpected ally in the old man. The revelation that the woman is guarding the Tongtian Death Bridge and nurturing the War Swords suggests a larger conspiracy involving the connection between realms. Readers should watch for the old man's true strength and the woman's ultimate goal. The Haotian Tower's full capabilities have yet to be revealed, and the four War Swords hint at future conflicts. The power dynamics are shifting, and Xiao Chen is being drawn into a war on a cosmic scale. The next chapter likely continues the confrontation with the woman and her ancient agenda.
