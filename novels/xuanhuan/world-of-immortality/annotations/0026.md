## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen into a harrowing game of survival against overwhelming numbers and supernatural terror. After his earlier setback, Gu Luo and Zhao Lin'er renew their relentless pursuit, this time armed with sacred protections that neutralize the corpse miasma. Xiao Chen is forced to retreat deeper into the Death Swamp, where an even greater horror awaits: the legendary Undying King. The chapter balances tactical skirmishes with escalating dread, as the hunters become the hunted. The mood is one of desperate flight, punctuated by moments of brutal violence and the crushing weight of an ancient entity that treats mortal cultivators as mere insects.

## Key Plot Points

Gu Luo uses a precious Sacred Purification scroll to save his poisoned men, then activates a Sacred Protection scroll that renders the entire group immune to the swamp's deadly miasma. Empowered, they resume the chase, forcing Xiao Chen and the three skeletons into a frantic retreat. Xiao Chen, realizing he cannot outrun them, deliberately leads the pack toward the swamp's central region, where the most malevolent presence dwells. The Undying King—a colossal, rotting corpse with immense wings—rises and slaughters Gu Luo's soldiers effortlessly, its death energy extinguishing even the sacred light. Xiao Chen barely escapes with the help of the three skeletons, who drag him through the darkness. The hunters lose most of their remaining forces, and Gu Luo is left pale and horrified, his resources depleted.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Corpse Miasma (尸瘴) is a toxic vapor born from accumulated death and decay, common in cursed or battle-scarred lands. In xianxia, it often serves as a natural barrier that only the well-prepared can cross. Sacred Purification and Sacred Protection scrolls are consumable artifacts imbued with holy energy, often created by high-level cultivators or blessed by divine beings. Their use here highlights the resourcefulness of Gu Luo's faction and the disparity in wealth between him and the isolated Xiao Chen. The Undying King (不死之王) is a rare and terrifying undead sovereign; in the novel's cosmology, such entities are akin to natural disasters, and a mature Undying King can rival gods. Its sudden appearance underscores the ancient, untamed dangers of Dragon Island and foreshadows deeper horrors.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point in the pursuit arc: Gu Luo's losses are crippling, and Zhao Lin'er's resources are dwindling. Yet Xiao Chen's escape is far from clean—he now knows that the Death Swamp harbors a being far beyond his current power. The three skeletons demonstrate increasing loyalty and intelligence, hinting at their potential evolution. Readers should watch how Gu Luo adapts to his diminished forces, and whether Zhao Lin'er will take more direct action. The Undying King's presence also raises questions about the swamp's secrets and other ancient entities slumbering on the island. The next chapter likely explores the immediate aftermath and Xiao Chen's next survival strategy.
