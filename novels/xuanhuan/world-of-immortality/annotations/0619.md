## Chapter Overview
This chapter opens with the Stone Crow—a primordial calamity star of terrifying power—awakening from his sealed tomb, his gaze burning with revived flame. The tension between his irascible nature and the roguish provocations of Sha Polang provides dark comic relief, but beneath the surface lies a tide of unease. The Stone Crow and the ancient Skeleton King exchange cryptic warnings about an imminent catastrophe, hinting at forces beyond even their unfathomable reach. Xiao Chen, ever the analytical survivor, presses for deeper truths about the Divine Diagram and the mysterious boundary between the Celestial Realm and the World of the Dead. The chapter closes with a chilling revelation: Lu Zhan, a legendary Arch-Enemy of the Otherworld, has set foot in the Celestial Realm for the first time, a move that foreshadows a cataclysm that may engulf all worlds. The mood is one of creeping dread overlain with a veneer of banter and reunion.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter begins with the Stone Crow returning to life after absorbing a strand of fire source, the oppressive yin aura dispelled. The Stone Crow immediately confronts Sha Polang about disrespectful remarks, inflicting comical misfortunes as retribution—a display of his terrifying control over bad luck curses. The Skeleton King then draws the Stone Crow into a secret conversation, revealing the latter's self-immolation as a desperate gamble to survive an impending great calamity. They speak in muffled tones of a 'peerless figure from the past' who may be returning. Xiao Chen seizes the opportunity to question the Stone Crow about the Divine Diagram and the rumored end of all worlds. The Stone Crow confirms that a mysterious unknown realm exists at the convergence of the Celestial Realm and the Death World, a domain that few have ever entered and none have returned from. He also reveals that the Divine Diagram was born from a Heavenly Bone and refined by the Nine Sacred Lamps, carrying secrets that could push extremecultivators toward the Final Step. Later, Xiao Chen ventures to an ancient city to spy on the Otherworld expert who had come to borrow the Chaotic Plantain. But his astonishment peaks when he sees Lu Zhan, one of the legendary Otherworld Ancestral Gods, seated motionless atop a primordial mountain—a being who was never supposed to ascend to the Celestial Realm. This single image suggests that the balance of powers is about to be overturned, and a terrifying era of conflict looms.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter deepens the lore of the Divine Diagram (神图), a relic that has appeared throughout earlier arcs as the incomplete formation diagram composed of War Swords. Here, it is revealed that the diagram originated from a Heavenly Bone imbued with the patterns of the Nine Sacred Lamps—a concept drawn from the Daoist principle of the 'Great Derivation' (大衍), where the number fifty is reduced to forty-nine, leaving one as the 'missing one' (遁去的一). The Stone Crow also alludes to the 'Final Step' (最后一步), the elusive transcendence that Stone Men seek, a path fraught with self-destruction and nirvana. The mention of Lu Zhan—one of the four Otherworld Ancestral Gods—ties into the larger mythology of the Nine Provinces' ancient enemies. For readers familiar with Chinese mythological cycles, the appearance of a primordial arch-foe in a realm he was forbidden to enter signals a tectonic shift in the cosmic order, reminiscent of the collapse of ages in classical Xianxia narratives.
