## Chapter Overview

This chapter escalates the stakes on Dragon Island as a second Companion Dragon King emerges—a black-scaled beast born from the same lineage as the Purple Dragon King. The appearance of the Black Dragon King confirms that the competition for the Ancestral Dragon's throne is now a two-way race. But the chapter's most compelling tension comes from the demon's return. Wounded but unbroken, the pale-faced entity offers a stark choice: surrender Keke's sacred tree, and he will leave Dragon Island in peace. This ultimatum forces Xiao Chen and the survivors to weigh survival against loyalty, while the myth of the legendary Spirit Boat—the only vessel capable of crossing the sealed Forbidden Sea—offers a fragile ray of hope.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Black Dragon King's introduction—a powerful young dragon with jet-black scales, slightly smaller than the Purple Dragon King but equally proud. The two dragons immediately bristle with hostility, foreshadowing the inevitable battle between Companion Dragon Kings for the Ancestral Dragon's throne. Keke, ever perceptive, detects the demon's approach before anyone else, using the dragon's blood to wound him further. The demon reveals that his blood has been contaminated by dragon blood, showing a vulnerability. He returns not to kill but to bargain: he will spare everyone if he receives the sacred tree from Keke's head. This demand splits the group: the elderly couple, fearing for their returning kin, lean toward acceptance, while Xiao Chen flatly refuses. The demon claims he is not a demon but a human undergoing rebirth to revive as a living being. The chapter also reveals the existence of a Spirit Boat made from Ancestral Dragon bone and World Tree wood, which can sail the Forbidden Sea. To assemble it, nine Dragon Kings must be gathered. This sets a new objective: unite all dragon kings to escape the island.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens the lore of Companion Dragon Kings—beings born from the same lineage as the Ancestral Dragon, destined to fight for supremacy. This mirrors the Chinese mythological theme of the dragon as a symbol of imperial power and ceaseless struggle. The demon's claim of being a 'person undergoing transformation' (还阳再生) echoes Taoist concepts of resurrection and refinement, where a deceased entity can regain flesh through cultivating yin energy. Keke's sacred tree (圣树) is now confirmed as a treasure coveted by even ancient evils, suggesting it originates from the World Tree (通天神木) that once connected realms. The Spirit Boat made from Ancestral Dragon bones and World Tree wood recalls Chinese legends of magical vessels used by immortals to traverse dangerous seas. The political tension among cultivators—elder hidden experts versus young practitioners—reflects the stratified cultivation society where older generations often manipulate younger ones.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up multiple converging threads: the race to gather nine Dragon Kings, the demon's lingering threat, and the sacred tree's importance. Readers should pay attention to Keke's mysterious perception and fearlessness toward the demon—she may hold the key to more than just the tree. The Black Dragon King's introduction hints that more Dragon Kings may appear, each with unique attributes. Xiao Chen's steadfast refusal to sacrifice the tree shows his growing moral code and bond with Keke. The demon's promise not to harm others if given the tree is a classic devil's bargain—whether he will keep his word remains an open question. Keep an eye on the returning elder cultivators, as they may become either vital allies or new obstacles. The Spirit Boat lore suggests the island's ultimate escape mechanism is tied to the dragons themselves, raising the stakes for every future confrontation.
