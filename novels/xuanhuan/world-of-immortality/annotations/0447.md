## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a somber return to the Dead City, where Xiao Chen confronts the eerie seeping of the Yellow Clay Altar—a sign that the Demon Well's seal may be weakening. After years of toil in the Death World, he finds his connection to the ancient city restored, yet the discovery hints at a greater calamity. Moving to the Mortal World, he visits Chang'an's Misty Rain Pavilion, where the legend of Wu Ming and the current power structure of the Hundred Races unfold. The chapter blends introspective dread with vibrant political stage-setting, as old rivals and new pawns alike sense his return.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen stands atop the Dead City, his body resonating with its will and the twenty-seven War Swords. He dismisses his companions and plunges into the city alone, walking its desolate streets and recalling the sacrifices of the Ancestral Gods. At the sealed Demon Well, he notices the Yellow Clay Altar seeping blood—an alarming anomaly he cannot explain. After days of observation, he leaves unstoppered and heads to the Mortal World. In Chang'an, he visits the Misty Rain Pavilion, now a legendary venue frequented by the Hundred Races. Overhearing conversations, he learns of his old foe Wu Ming, who has risen to become a Heavenly King feared across the world. The purple diamond skeleton Sha Polang provides comic relief, but the atmosphere turns tense when Wu Ming arrives to negotiate a marriage with the Tiger Clan. Xiao Chen projects his voice, and Wu Ming, recognizing the threat, flees in absolute terror. The chapter ends with the enemies of Xiao Chen now aware of his return.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Dead City is one of the most feared locales in the Nine Provinces, a relic of the age of Ancestral Gods where countless beings perished. The Yellow Clay Altar, formed from the flesh of Suiren and other ancestor gods, seals the Demon Well—a gateway to an underworld that once contained the illusory half-ancestors. Its bleeding suggests the seal is failing, a catastrophic omen. The 'Eight Syllables of Origin' are foundational sounds of the cosmos; Xiao Chen wields the first two. The Misty Rain Pavilion has become a neutral ground where Heavenly Kings—the top-tier Perfected Man realm experts of the Hundred Races—gather and clash. Wu Ming, once a prodigy, now wields the Undying Imperial Heavenly Bell and the Heavenly Thunder Hammer, symbolizing the political and martial complexity of the post-Half-Ancestor era.

## Reading Guide
This chapter acts as both a grim foreshadowing and a re-establishment of Xiao Chen's legend. The bleeding altar is a mystery that will likely drive the coming arc—what could be strong enough to crack an Ancestral God's seal? Pay attention to Xiao Chen's repeated connection to the Dead City; it may hold a key to his future. His silent intimidation of Wu Ming shows that even after 160 years, his name still carries weight. Upcoming conflicts will likely involve the Tiger Clan and the half-ancestors who still hunt him. The reappearance of characters like Sha Polang suggests that the Death World's politics will interweave with the Mortal World's affairs. Keep watch for signs of the female sovereign and the three skeleton emperors who may soon emerge.
