## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges deeper into the Death World, a desolate realm of skeletal beings and conflicting fire seeds. Xiao Chen, now a fearsome Yellow Diamond (黄钻) skeletal king, allies with his old comrade, the Purple Diamond Skeleton (now named Sha Polang/杀破狼), to eliminate spies from the eastern lands. The narrative escalates into a direct confrontation with the terrifying Broken Sword King (断剑王), who has evolved into a Yellow Diamond second-rank powerhouse wielding a fragment of Zhunti's holy sword. A mysterious ally appears wielding the legendary Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree (七宝妙树), turning the tide and allowing Xiao Chen to claim a Yellow Diamond fire seed. The chapter culminates in Xiao Chen's ascension to Yellow Diamond second rank, his golden bones blazing like a sun, signaling a major leap in his power within the Death World.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen reunites with the Purple Diamond Skeleton, now a regional ruler frustrated by spies from the eastern giant city. Together, they plan to eliminate the lurking experts, suspected to be agents of the Broken Sword King. Xiao Chen demonstrates overwhelming power by instantly killing five powerful skeletal beings with his Supreme Hand (上苍之手), shocking all witnesses. This establishes his authority and confirms that his Yellow Diamond form allows him to dominate lower-rank fire seed creatures with ease.

The Purple Diamond Skeleton, emboldened, names himself Sha Polang after the cities of Seven Kill, Broken Army, and Greedy Wolf. The Broken Sword King, having sensed the loss of his agents, leaves the safety of his giant city to confront Xiao Chen directly. Xiao Chen intercepts him on a desolate plain, now confident in his Yellow Diamond first-rank power but wary of the Broken Sword King's second-rank strength and deadly rainbow-colored sword. The battle is tense, with Xiao Chen relying on the Weng sound (嗡字天音) of the Eight Primordial Sounds to wound the Broken Sword King's soul and seize part of his fire seed.

A hidden Yellow Diamond second-rank ally emerges from the Broken Sword King's retinue, forcing Xiao Chen to flee. However, a third Yellow Diamond skeleton rushes to Xiao Chen's aid, wielding the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree with brutish force—swinging it like a club rather than using its divine abilities. This surprising ally draws off the hidden expert, allowing Xiao Chen to defeat the Broken Sword King with Supreme Hand strikes and the Weng sound. Together, Xiao Chen and the mysterious ally destroy the hidden second-rank expert as well. Xiao Chen then receives a Yellow Diamond fire seed, absorbs it completely, and breaks through to Yellow Diamond second rank, his golden bones radiating power across the eastern lands.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter delves into the unique cultivation system of the Death World (死者世界), where beings evolve by absorbing fire seeds (火种) from defeated skeletal creatures. The hierarchy runs from white bones to colored diamond ranks: purple diamond (紫钻), yellow diamond (黄钻), and beyond. Each ascent dramatically amplifies combat power; Xiao Chen's new Yellow Diamond second rank places him among the elite of this realm. The chapter also reintroduces Zhunti's Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree (七宝妙树), a legendary artifact from Fengshen Yanyi canon, known for its ability to sweep away enemies with five-colored divine light. Here, it is used crudely as a bludgeon, providing darkly comic commentary on the ignorance of Death World beings toward the artifact's true power. The Weng sound (嗡字天音) is part of the Eight Primordial Sounds (本源八音) from the Yellow Clay Altar, a direct soul attack effective against fire seed entities.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is pivotal for Xiao Chen's Death World arc: his advancement to Yellow Diamond second rank significantly boosts his strength, enabling him to contend with greater threats and plan his return to the Nine Provinces. The mysterious ally with the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree raises questions: Is he connected to Zhunti? Does he know the tree's true value? His brief appearance hints at deeper factions within the Death World. The Broken Sword King's defeat leaves the eastern giant city vulnerable, but Xiao Chen's aggressive pillaging may provoke retaliation from even stronger beings. Readers should watch for the deployment of the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree in future conflicts, and whether the ally's rough treatment of it will change once its true power is understood. Xiao Chen's growing mastery of the Weng sound and Supreme Hand also suggests he is weaving together his martial and divine arts into a terrifying whole.
