## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a potent blend of mythic artifact discovery and strategic pivot, as Xiao Chen and Keke confront the legendary Golden Divine Halberd—an ancient sacred weapon that once attacked the very stele sealing Dragon Island. The tension crackles as Keke's impulsive grab triggers a devastating golden light, only for the little beast's sacred tree hat to absorb the attack, saving them. The halberd's mysterious disappearance afterward leaves an eerie sense of residual presence. In parallel, the news that the Winged Dragon King has been tamed by the gentle yet formidable Lan Yu shifts the island's power landscape, accelerating the countdown to escape. The chapter balances awe-inspiring artifact lore with grounded political calculation, as Xiao Chen weighs trading his sacred tree with the Demon to secure a way off the island.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen recognizes the Golden Divine Halberd as one of the ancient sacred artifacts that previously bombarded the divine stele sealing Dragon Island, linking it to the Buddha Dharma Wheel and Ancestral Dragon Horn. The Demon warns them to stay away, citing previous deaths caused by the halberd's killing aura. Keke, fascinated by the gleaming weapon, dashes forward despite Xiao Chen's call, triggering a devastating golden light storm. The sacred tree hat worn by Keke absorbs the lethal energy, saving everyone. Keke then manages to seize the halberd, but after a violent explosion, the halberd vanishes without a trace, astonishing the Demon and leaving only a faint lingering sensation for Xiao Chen. Later, news arrives that the Winged Dragon King has been voluntarily delivered to the female cultivator Lan Yu by its parent Light Sacred Dragon, signifying that the conditions for leaving Dragon Island (nine dragon kings) are nearly met. This forces Xiao Chen to confront his own dilemma: he must trade his sacred tree with the Demon to secure his escape, as he lacks a dragon king of his own. Conversations with Liu Mu and Yizhen reveal the current top fighters, including the fearsome Dugu Jianmo at Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven, and the time-controlling woman who is his only peer.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Golden Divine Halberd exemplifies the concept of an "ancient sacred artifact" (上古圣器), a class of mythic weapons that possess their own will and cosmic power, often linked to primordial battles. Such artifacts are so potent that even the sealing force of Dragon Island cannot fully suppress them, as seen when the halberd once attacked the divine stele. The taming of the Winged Dragon King by Lan Yu illustrates a recurring xianxia trope: a powerful beast choosing its master based on purity or destiny, bypassing brute-force capture. The requirement of nine dragon kings to open a passage off the island harkens to the idea of gathering rare, spiritually charged beings to break a supernatural lock. The Demon remains an enigmatic figure—neither ally nor enemy, bound by his own mysterious purposes—evoking the classic archetype of a wise (or treacherous) hermit in a sealed realm.

## Reading Guide

As Xiao Chen prepares to negotiate with the Demon, readers should watch for the cost of this transaction—will the sacred tree be permanently lost, or will it return in a new form? The disappearance of the Golden Divine Halberd feels deliberate, perhaps hinting that the weapon has bonded with someone (Keke? Xiao Chen?) despite its physical vanishing. The emergence of top-tier fighters like Dugu Jianmo and the time-controlling woman signals that the final days on Dragon Island will be shaped by clashes between these apex cultivators. With the nine dragon kings assembled, the path to escape is opening, but Xiao Chen's own journey is far from resolved. The chapter closes on a note of calculated risk, setting the stage for a high-stakes trade that could redefine his power set.
