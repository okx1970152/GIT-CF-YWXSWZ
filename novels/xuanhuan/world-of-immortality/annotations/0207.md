## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers one of the most devastating blows in the entire Dragon Island arc: the apparent death of Keke, the little white divine beast. The heavens themselves mourn with falling flower petals, ancient chants, destruction hymns, and sacrificial sounds—a sign that a being of near-demigod status has fallen. Xiao Chen, on the verge of death from his battle against six demigods, is saved when the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree enters his body, fusing with his nine divine acupoints and granting him Keke's dragon clan divine abilities. But the cost is immeasurable: Keke is gone, and Xiao Chen's grief transforms into a cold, burning resolve for revenge. The chapter balances despair with newfound power and a vow that will shape the entire next phase of the story.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with cosmic lamentation—falling flower petals, eight-syllable origin chanting, destruction hymns, and sacrificial sounds—marking Keke's death. Xiao Chen's vow echoes: 'Willing not to seek eternal life, only to seek eternal coexistence…' as he holds the Sacred Tree. The tree absorbs Keke's seven strands of essence and merges into Xiao Chen's body, connecting to his nine divinized acupoints and implanting like a spiritual root. Xiao Chen instantly gains Keke's two major dragon clan divine arts: 'Binding' and 'Sweeping.'

The six demigods (Tiger family's Hu Nu and Hu Shi, plus four others) surround Xiao Chen with their domain field, intending to kill him. Xiao Chen fights but cannot break the domain. Suddenly, a sword strike from an unknown flute player tears through the domain, allowing Xiao Chen to escape into the Southern Wastes. Instead of fleeing far, Xiao Chen circles back to Heavenly Emperor City, launching a vicious counterattack: he kills over twenty Hai family members, including a demigod. He wounds Hu Nu, shattering her arm and jaw. However, he is forced to retreat again when Hai Fan Yun and another elder pursue him. Xiao Chen then hears two demonic voices tempting him with power in exchange for his soul, offering to let him massacre Heavenly Emperor City. The chapter ends with this moral dilemma.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The heavenly phenomena—falling flowers, ancient chants, destruction hymns, and sacrificial sounds—are classic xianxia indicators of a being of great significance dying, often a demigod or sacred beast. It is the universe mourning a lost legendary creature. The integration of the Sacred Tree into Xiao Chen's body is a profound cultivation event: the tree's roots connect to his nine acupoints, establishing a symbiotic life link. This grants him the dragon clan's innate divine abilities originally belonging to Keke, such as the Binding and Sweeping arts. The 'domain field' used by the six demigods is a spatial technique that suppresses enemies within a bounded territory, making it nearly impossible to escape without superior power or outside intervention. The mysterious flute player who cuts through the domain with a sword is a classic 'hidden master' trope, setting up a future ally or rival.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a watershed moment. Keke's apparent death—though the Sacred Tree retains her seven essence strands—leaves her fate ambiguous. Xiao Chen's fusion with the tree gives him immense power, but at what cost? The demonic voices tempting him toward a bloody path introduce a new internal conflict. Will he succumb to darkness to gain the strength to eliminate his enemies? The identity of the flute-playing swordsman, likely a woman given the context of previous chapters, remains a mystery—watch for her to reappear. The Tiger Family demigods are not finished; they will seek revenge, and Xiao Chen's vow ensures further bloody confrontations. Also, Xiao Chen's ability to kill a demigod despite being at Mortal-Shedding shows his rising threat level. The balance of power in Heavenly Emperor City is shifting. The next chapters will likely explore Xiao Chen's training to consolidate his new powers and the fallout from Keke's death.
