## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers the long-awaited, earth-shattering clash between two primordial stone-man kings, Pangu King and the decrepit old stone man king. The battle transcends time and space, with Pangu King manifesting as a temporary, devastating projection, while the old king, on the verge of annihilation, summons the battle soul of his prime through a forbidden ancient chant. The emotional tone is one of tragic desperation and mythic scale: a once-mighty being clawing back from the brink, and a group of allied warriors — Xiao Chen, the old stone turtle, the human demon Ge Qian, and the father of Keke — struggling to contain a threat that could undo all their progress. The stakes are nothing less than the survival of the Nine Provinces' hope.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Pangu King overwhelming the old stone man king, cleaving his arms and splitting his torso with the divine stone axe. Just as annihilation seems certain, the old king recites a soul-summoning verse that reconnects him with his ancient battle will, allowing his shattered remains to regenerate. Pangu King's form fades as he is revealed to be an ephemeral projection. The revived old stone man king, now nearly at his peak, crushes everyone in his path, including Ge Qian and Keke's father. Meanwhile, at the Eternal Unknown, Yan Huang and the War Ancestor shatter three of the ninety-nine stone steps, achieving their primary objective. The old stone turtle attempts to fuse Xiao Chen with the broken stone man, but the old king breaks the fusion. In a moment of brilliance, Ge Qian and Keke's father independently create complementary divine principles — 'existence forgotten, myriad worlds vanish' and 'the past remembered, an eternal moment in the heart' — which together stagger the old king. A new complete stone man finally emerges from the divine city, and the shattered stone steps are seized by the retreating forces. The old king, though wounded, warns of even greater threats.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'stone man' cultivation path is a central concept: cultivators petrify their bodies over eons, seeking the 'final step' to achieve a perfect, eternal stone form. The ninety-nine stone steps at the Eternal Unknown are a trial for stone men to ascend, representing heaven's obstacle. Pangu is drawn from the primordial Chinese myth of the creator who separated heaven and earth; here he is depicted as a supreme stone warrior. The forbidden technique '贯古通今' (pierce the ages) allows call upon one's past peak power, a high-risk resurrection art. Ge Qian's divine principle '存在的忘记了' parallels Zen-like paradoxes, blending the martial with the philosophical. The divine city within the sun palace is a crucible for stone men rebirth, a key location in the treasure hunt.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is the explosive finale of the stone man arc, where long-built tensions erupt. Pay close attention to the new complete stone man who emerges from the divine city — his identity and allegiance will shape the next phase. The stolen stone steps are not merely a prize but a key artifact that may unlock ancient secrets. Ge Qian and Keke's father undergo a subtler transformation, developing unique divine principles that mark them as geniuses among their peers. Xiao Chen's attempted fusion with the broken stone man is a failed but significant experiment — it hints at his future path. The old stone man king, though beaten back, warns that even a being who has taken the final step may not be able to change the outcome, raising the central mystery of the ninety-nine stone steps and the power behind them.
