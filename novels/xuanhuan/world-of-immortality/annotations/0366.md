## Chapter Overview
In this chapter, Xiao Chen—now a formidable Yellow Diamond second-rank skeleton—goes on an unstoppable rampage across the eastern Death World. His golden bones radiate an oppressive might that sends fire seed creatures into panic. He demolishes six major cities, killing their lords, before turning his sights on the towering Giant City, the seat of the Giant King. The chapter opens with a poetic allusion to Li Bai's line "Slay one man every ten steps," perfectly capturing Xiao Chen's merciless, hundred-league pursuit. He smashes through city walls, clears the city lord's mansion with devastating force, and secures a cache of ten large bottles of Life Source Liquid—a rare resource for soul purification. But his ambition does not end there. Rather than retreat, he formulates a daring plan to intercept the seven powerful experts returning to the Giant King, hoping to absorb their fire seeds and break through to Yellow Diamond third rank. The chapter is a masterclass in Death World progression, blending raw power with strategic cunning.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen begins his conquest by storming through the northeastern Death World, sacking six large cities in a single day. Each city lord falls to his Hand of the Supreme Being, their fire seeds consumed to fuel his growth. As he approaches Giant City, the ruling seat of the Giant King, a failed evolver—a Purple Diamond skeleton with Yellow Diamond-level power—attempts to flee into the city. Xiao Chen pursues, using it as a guide, and executes it at the city gates, sending a clear message to the defending forces. When the city's defenders emerge to assist the evolver, he slays it before their eyes, causing them to flee in terror. Rather than enter through the gate, Xiao Chen channels the chapter's thematic efficiency: he demolishes a three-hundred-meter section of the ancient wall using the Hand of the Supreme Being, then storms the city center. There, he faces four guardian experts. Using the Om Sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin, he creates a ten-zhang zone of annihilation, sweeping through the fire seed armies. Three guardians die; one escapes. In the underground stronghold of the city lord's mansion, Xiao Chen uncovers ten large bottles of Life Source Liquid, a treasure that will aid his soul refinement. After reducing the city's core to rubble, he senses the Giant King's return and vanishes into the wilderness—not to flee, but to set a trap. He plans to intercept the seven late-returning experts who accompany the Giant King, hoping to harvest their fire seeds and ascend to Yellow Diamond third rank, even at great personal risk.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Death World operates on a clear hierarchy of bone ranks: White, Blue, Purple, Yellow, and higher, each reflecting a skeleton's cultivation level and power. Xiao Chen has attained Yellow Diamond second rank, his bones now golden and near-indestructible. The chapter's title, "Slay One Man Every Ten Steps," is a direct reference to Li Bai's poem "The Swordsman," which romanticizes the lone warrior's total efficiency in combat. This aligns with the xianxia trope of "conquering cities to advance." The Hand of the Supreme Being is a pure martial technique that shatters artifacts and defenses. The Om Sound is the first of the Eight Syllables of Origin, a primordial sound technique that can disintegrate weaker beings en masse—ideal for army clearing. Life Source Liquid is a rare fluid that purifies the soul and fire seed, crucial for Xiao Chen's plan to achieve true resurrection from the Death World.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a major power leap: if Xiao Chen succeeds in absorbing the fire seeds of the seven returning experts, he could break through to Yellow Diamond third rank, significantly closing the gap with the Giant King. The acquisition of Life Source Liquid is a strategic boon—it will allow him to cleanse his soul and potentially manifest in the mortal world for longer periods. However, the Giant King is now furious and alert, raising the stakes. The ambush is a high-risk gamble; if the Giant King catches wind, Xiao Chen's progress could be undone. Readers should watch for how the clash between the Golden Lion King and the War Clan prodigy Mabao foreshadows the emerging competition among the ancient races, which will soon redefine the balance of power in the Nine Provinces. Xiao Chen's time in the Death World is nearing a critical turning point.
