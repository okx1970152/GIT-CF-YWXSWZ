## Chapter Overview
This chapter brings one of the most electrifying revelations in the Eternal Continent arc: the desiccated corpse that has been stalking the Burial Valley is none other than the Martial Ancestor himself. What begins as a desperate three-way assault by Otherworld and Death World ancestral gods quickly becomes a triumphant display of absolute power when Li Huiyou’s time-reversal technique inadvertently restores the Martial Ancestor to his legendary peak—the ninth heaven of the Ancestral God realm. The tone is one of awe and catharsis, as the supreme martial god of humanity sheds his withered form and annihilates his enemies with effortless contempt. Readers witness the full terror of a being who once killed two Otherworld ancestral gods and wounded two more in a single battle, now reborn and untouchable.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the shocking revelation that the evil corpse is the Martial Ancestor. Li Huiyou, the Red Robe Sword Ancestor, and the White Bone Ancestor Lord coordinate a three-pronged assault: time reversal to weaken him, a star-destroying sword domain, and a world-sinking decay technique. The Martial Ancestor stands motionless, allowing Li Huiyou's time power to wash over him. Instead of regressing to childhood, he reverses to his prime—a towering, black-haired warrior with gleaming bronze skin and a gaze that shatters souls. He then proceeds to kill each enemy with devastating simplicity: he crushes Li Huiyou’s soul with a single grasp, smashes the Red Robe’s star domain with a palm strike and extinguishes his soul, and finally tears the White Bone Ancestor Lord’s fire seed from his skull. The chapter closes with the onlookers in stunned silence, witnessing the return of a terror that even half-ancestors cannot fathom.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Martial Ancestor embodies the pinnacle of pure martial cultivation—a path that scorns reliance on divine artifacts and spells and achieves supremacy through the body alone. His return to the ninth heaven of the Ancestral God realm is a monumental event, as that level of power has not been seen since the ancient wars between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld. The concept of “nine heavens” within a major realm (here Ancestral God) signifies the absolute peak; the difference between the eighth and ninth heaven is often described as the gap between heaven and earth. Li Huiyou’s mistake—attempting to weaken the Martial Ancestor with time reversal—echoes a classical xianxia irony: interfering with a supreme cultivator’s timeline can backfire catastrophically. The chapter also reinforces the sacredness of the Martial Ancestor’s legacy, connecting him to the stone-man path and the primordial sacrifices of the Nine Provinces’ ancestral gods.

## Reading Guide
This chapter resets the power scales of the narrative. With the Martial Ancestor restored, the Otherworld’s invasion plans face a titanic obstacle. Readers should watch for how this affects the balance among the half-ancestors: the Skeleton King, Asura Half-Ancestor, and Angel Holy God have all been actively scheming in the Soul World. The Martial Ancestor’s return may drive them to desperate measures. Additionally, Xiao Chen’s own martial path—the Heavenly Stele Profound Art and the Martial Imprint—gains new context: he is pursuing a legacy that has now proven it can rival and surpass the highest ancestral gods. The search for Eternal Light and the secrets of the Burial Valley remain open threads. This chapter also sets the stage for a potential confrontation or alliance between Xiao Chen and the resurrected Martial Ancestor.
