## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen returns to the mortal world in his new form as a red-diamond skeleton, only to find that years have passed and his former enemies are plotting against his beloved companion, the Little Defiant Dragon. The chapter weaves between the desolate beauty of the World of the Dead and the familiar landscapes of the Nine Provinces, now transformed by the rise of the National Religion and the machinations of Zhao Lin'er. As Xiao Chen desperately searches for his old friends, he overhears a deadly conspiracy that forces him to rush to the Eastern Sea, where an astonishing revelation awaits—a colossal stone statue of himself, pierced by twenty-four war swords, rising from the ocean depths.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen successfully purifies a powerful fire-seed and advances to the red-diamond level, dominating the surrounding fire-seed creatures. He discovers a jade bottle of Life Source Liquid, a rare treasure that can purify fire-seeds, and decides to save it for later use. He returns to the God Village, where he tasks the red-diamond skeleton king with trading colored-diamond bones for higher-level fire-seeds, hoping to accelerate their evolution together. Then, Xiao Chen re-enters the mortal world through the bone well, now a red-diamond skeleton. He moves cautiously, avoiding daylight and humans. He overhears Zhao Lin'er and the Nine-Headed Snake plotting to kill the Little Defiant Dragon, who has become a feared figure known as the Defiant Dragon King. Zhao Lin'er wants to eliminate the dragon before it can ally with the mysterious crying little beast (Keke). Xiao Chen, enraged, speeds toward the Eastern Sea, where he witnesses a standoff between the Little Defiant Dragon and the Nine-Headed Snake. But the main event is the sudden emergence of a mountain-sized stone statue of Xiao Chen himself, pierced by twenty-four giant stone swords—a monument to his past sacrifice. The onlookers recognize him, and shockwaves ripple through the crowds.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens the cosmology of the World of the Dead, where fire-seeds are the essence of undead beings. Colored-diamond skeletons (red, purple, yellow) represent tiers of power, akin to cultivation realms in the living world. Life Source Liquid is a rare substance that can purify a fire-seed, removing impurities and increasing potential. The red-diamond king's reaction highlights the value of colored-diamond bones, which can be used by lesser undead to evolve. The bone well serves as a portal between the World of the Dead and the mortal realm. Zhao Lin'er's mention of the 'crying little beast' refers to Keke, one of Xiao Chen's divine companions. The stone statue rising from the sea is a physical manifestation of Xiao Chen's legendary sacrifice, now a landmark that evokes awe and dread.

## Reading Guide

The appearance of Xiao Chen's stone statue, pierced by twenty-four swords, is a powerful callback to his ordeal at the end of the Dragon Island arc. Readers should recall that the Twenty-Four War Swords were involved in sealing a spatial rift. The return of this image hints that deeper mysteries about the statues, the war swords, and Xiao Chen's fate are about to be unraveled. The conspiracy against the Little Defiant Dragon sets up an imminent clash between the Defiant Dragon King and the combined forces of Zhao Lin'er and her allies. Additionally, the mention of Keke having been spotted half a year ago suggests that the beloved white beast may soon reappear. The chapter ends with a massive cliffhanger, as the stone statue's appearance will surely draw the attention of all major powers.
