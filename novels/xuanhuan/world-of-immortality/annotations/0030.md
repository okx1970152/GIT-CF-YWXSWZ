## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a deep, mythic lore-dump that reshapes the reader's understanding of Dragon Island and its ultimate prize. Xiao Chen sits with the monk Yizhen and learns the brutal truth about the Ancestral Dragon—a creature not born but forged through endless combat, a Darwinian nightmare of dragon evolution. The tone is solemn and weighty, carrying the dread of a world where gods themselves are stripped of power upon entering the island. The arrival of three foreign cultivators, including the breathtaking Yan Qingcheng, shifts the atmosphere from philosophical discussion to tense political intrigue. The stage is set for a clash not only of martial strength but of civilizational legacies.

## Key Plot Points

Yizhen reveals that the Ancestral Dragon is not a fixed species but any dragon that defeats ten thousand rivals and metamorphoses into a five-clawed sovereign. Only one Ancestral Dragon exists at a time, and its birth triggers the emergence of several Companion Dragon Kings—nearly as powerful, destined to be both rivals and eventual usurpers. Xiao Chen learns that Dragon Island was sealed by a divine stele, suppressing all divine abilities within its boundaries, even for visiting gods. The Forbidden Sea surrounding the island becomes passable only once every few hundred years, which explains the current influx of practitioners. Four new figures arrive on the beach: the beautiful and dangerous Yan Qingcheng, heiress of the Undying Evil King lineage; the towering barbarian Kailai, with wolf ears and a tiger tail, whose body houses an ancient beast-soul; the forest-clan youth Yalode, who can command plants with his spirit power; and a fourth, less distinct practitioner. Xiao Chen immediately recognizes Yan Qingcheng as a potential enemy, given her likely connection to Prince Zifeng, whom he killed earlier. The chapter closes with the group wary of the island's power-suppressing effect, hinting at the unique challenges ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ancestral Dragon (祖龙) is a foundational myth in Chinese culture, often depicted as the progenitor of all dragons. In Chen Dong's world, this myth is reimagined as a cruel process of natural selection among dragons: any dragon can become the Ancestor by defeating all others, making it a title earned through slaughter rather than birthright. The term 'Companion Dragon King' (伴生龙王) introduces a fascinating counterweight—these dragons are born alongside the Ancestor, nearly equal in power, and serve as its ultimate test. The barbarian Kailai belongs to the 'War Clans' (战族), a race said to have ancient beast-souls sealed within their bloodlines, granting them supernatural strength. The forest tribe's ability to manipulate plants echoes the 'wood element' control found in many Chinese cultivation systems. The Undying Evil King (不死邪王) lineage Yan Qingcheng represents hints at a demonic cultivation path that defies death itself, adding a layer of moral ambiguity to the alliance dynamics.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is foundational for understanding the central conflict of the Dragon Island arc. Pay close attention to the rules of the Ancestral Dragon—they will drive the decisions of every major character going forward. Yan Qingcheng's introduction is laden with portent: her beauty masks a likely deadly enemy, and readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's murder of Prince Zifeng will catalyze their confrontation. Kailai's beast-soul and Yalode's plant manipulation are early glimpses into the diverse combat systems of this world, hinting at battles that blend raw power with esoteric abilities. The revelation about the island suppressing divine abilities creates a level playing field where physical strength and cunning matter more than borrowed godly power. The next chapters will likely test Xiao Chen's ability to navigate this new social order while surviving the island's primordial threats.
