## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a cataclysmic turning point in the war between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld's ancient powers. Four primeval sovereigns—led by the Illusory Sovereign and the Cruel Sovereign—decisively obliterate the nine clones of the ancient saint sovereigns, seizing control of the path to the Sole True Realm. As Xiao Chen and Shi Zhongdi watch in desperate frustration, a white-robed woman appears: the long-lost Ancestral Goddess Nuwa, merging with her stone corpse body and wielding the Three Sovereign Mirror and Five Emperor Tower. Though gravely wounded by the four sovereigns' combined might, her arrival signals the return of hope. Meanwhile, the Martial Ancestor stands before the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, casting oracle bone fragments into the sky and summoning the ancestral souls of humanity's greatest heroes across time. The chapter balances breathtaking ancient combat with a solemn struggle for the fate of all worlds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the four ancient sovereigns effortlessly crushing the nine clones of the Primordial Saint Sovereigns, tearing them apart and refining their essence into nine sources of Dao power. The eight broken ancient lamps and the eight Heavenly Steles briefly suppress the raging destruction of myriad worlds, but the sovereigns overpower them and advance toward the gate of the Sole True Realm. They mock the futile efforts of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, declaring that only they will rule the eternal paradise. Xiao Chen prepares to sacrifice himself by flying up to activate the Heavenly Steles, but Shi Zhongdi stops him. At this critical moment, a woman in pure white appears, her face shifting through forms before settling on the visage of Nuwa. The last unbroken ancient lamp flies to her, the Three Sovereign Mirror glows before her, and the Five Emperor Tower descends. She merges with the beautiful female corpse that Xiao Chen had carried from the Otherworld stone gate, becoming a true combatant. However, her divine body is cracked from ancient wounds, and even with three supreme treasures, she is overwhelmed by the four sovereigns and severely wounded. Martial Ancestor then casts the oracle bone fragments he collected across the Death World, chanting a powerful summons to bring back the souls of fallen ancestors from the battlefield of eons past. His call shakes heaven and earth, causing even the four sovereigns to pause.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter draws heavily on Chinese mythological references. The three sovereigns and five emperors (三皇五帝) are legendary culture heroes and rulers from the dawn of Chinese civilization—figures like Nuwa, Fuxi, Shennong, the Yellow Emperor, and the Flame Emperor. Their names carry immense cultural weight, representing humanity's golden age. The 'oracle bone fragments' (甲骨图) recall the ancient practice of divination using turtle shells and animal bones, a key element of Shang dynasty ritual. The Martial Ancestor's summoning incantation, calling to 'ancestral souls across the barrier of eons,' echoes shamanic traditions of ancestral worship. The 99 stone steps (九十九重石台阶) are a recurring cosmic trial, each step testing body and soul, leading to the 'Sole True Realm' (唯一真界)—a concept paralleling Daoist notions of the One True Source behind all phenomena. The battle also incorporates the Daoist theory of yin-yang and taiji, as chanted by the ancient lamps: 'extreme emptiness yields qi, qi yields the taiji, taiji moves yin and yang, yin and yang divide heaven and earth.'

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a climactic escalation: the four ancient sovereigns are revealed as nearly unstoppable, having absorbed the power of the nine clones. Readers should pay close attention to the fate of the female corpse—Nuwa's body—which is now cracked and may not survive another onslaught. The Martial Ancestor's oracle bone summoning is a long-anticipated payoff; what ancestral souls will answer? Xiao Chen's role remains that of a witness forced into inaction, but his connection to the Heavenly Steles and his own unfinished Stone Man path may soon become decisive. The nine ancient lamps and the steles are being gathered; their true purpose is unfolding. The chapter sets up a desperate last stand, with the four sovereigns closing in on the gate of the Sole True Realm. Look for the return of other ancient heroes and the possible sacrifice of Nuwa or the Martial Ancestor to buy time.
