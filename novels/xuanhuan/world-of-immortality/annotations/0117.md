## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a devastating twist: just as escape from Dragon Island seems within reach, Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng are betrayed by a spatial scroll thrown by the Undying Alliance elder Wang Hao. The Ancestral Dragon Ship sails away, stranding them on the island with only Keke and the three skeletons for company. The tone shifts from the solemnity of departure to raw frustration, then to a grimly comic standoff between Xiao Chen and his reluctant companion. It is a chapter of forced endurance and dark humor amid betrayed hopes, where the protagonist must choose between despair and a coldly pragmatic outlook.

## Key Plot Points
As the Ancestral Dragon Ship prepares to depart, the beach is filled with desperate cultivators. Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng are suddenly caught in a spatial scroll that transports them ten li inland, causing them to miss the boarding. Keke, enraged, drags the three perpetrators—Wang Hao and two elderly spellmasters—into the teleportation as well. Xiao Chen rushes back to the shore but arrives just as the ship leaves; the rainbow bridge is withdrawn, and the Forbidden Sea's power prevents any crossing. In a calculated display of vengeance, Xiao Chen uses Keke's binding light to channel his blade energy, stripping the flesh from the three attackers' right arms, a technique he calls "Slay the Gods." The Ancestral Dragon Ship disappears over the golden sea, severing the last hope of escape. Later, the three villains are thrown into the Forbidden Sea and instantly destroyed. Xiao Chen reveals to Yan Qingcheng that he knows of another vessel—a "King Ship"—that may offer another chance. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen teasing the frightened Yan Qingcheng, using her as a foil against loneliness, while they head toward the snowy mountains with Keke and the skeletons.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the Ancestral Dragon Ship (祖龙神船), a vessel of immense power that can cross the sealed Forbidden Sea. Its sailing is triggered by the arrival of the eleven Companion Dragon Kings, an irreversible event. The Forbidden Sea is a death zone filled with sealing power from the divine stele, which disintegrates anything that tries to cross without protection. Spatial scrolls (空间卷轴) are rare artifacts that can teleport victims, but their precision is imperfect, as shown by the elders being caught in their own trap. The "King Ship" (君王船) is a mysterious alternate escape route, hinting at deeper island lore. The technique Xiao Chen uses, "Slay the Gods" (戮神), is a display of his growing mastery over his Qi-Refining Diagram, able to compress hundreds of blade strikes into a single, horrifically precise attack. Keke's binding ability proves instrumental once again, showing her versatility beyond mere defense.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a new survival arc on Dragon Island. The departure of the Ancestral Dragon Ship forces Xiao Chen to rely on the mysterious King Ship, which likely requires dangerous conditions to activate. The uneasy alliance with Yan Qingcheng offers both humor and tension; her fear of being alone makes her pliable, but her lineage as an Undying successor could become a liability. Keke's growing power and protective nature are emphasized, hinting at her potential as a key player in future conflicts. The three skeletons remain silent but loyal, their lotus brands a reminder of their evolution. Readers should watch for the psychological toll of isolation and whether Xiao Chen's dark amusement masks deeper wounds. The mention of the King Ship also raises questions: what is it, who built it, and what price must be paid to use it?
