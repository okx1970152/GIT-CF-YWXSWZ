## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a turning point in the power dynamics of the World of Immortality as the enigmatic corpse that once sailed the King Ship emerges as a being of overwhelming might. The corpse, long shrouded in mystery, demonstrates the crushing supremacy of pure martial arts against two otherworld Ancestral Gods. His arrival shatters the preconception that divine abilities are superior, echoing the Martial Imprint's earlier defense of martial arts. The chapter is both a vindication of the martial path and a revelation of the corpse's true identity.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the corpse suddenly appearing in the heart of the Most Evil Place, where the White Bone Ancestral Lord and Red Robe Sword Ancestor were confronting Xiao Chen and the Martial Imprint. Without a word, the corpse attacks, sending both Ancestral Gods flying with sheer physical force. The Red Robe Sword Ancestor, revealed to be the one who was wounded in chaotic spacetime, attempts to use his ultimate technique, calling upon billions of stars to rain sword beams. The corpse, however, demonstrates the weakness of such grand divine arts: by focusing all his martial power on the caster, he breaks through the star swords and tears the Ancestral God's chest open with Heaven-Fault Finger and Hand of the Supreme Being. The White Bone Ancestral Lord then joins, using a soul-binding divine art that summons enormous chains to trap the corpse, but the corpse breaks free with pure strength. The chapter ends with the corpse revealing his hand signs and the Martial Imprint shouting, "Martial Ancestor! You will die!"—implying the corpse is the legendary Martial Ancestor himself, a being who cultivated only the physical body.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter reinforces a core theme of the novel: the conflict between pure martial cultivation and the path of divine powers. The Martial Ancestor represents the ancient, nearly lost tradition of training the body to the point of becoming a weapon that can tear through space and nullify divine law. The Heaven-Fault Finger technique, first seen used by the War King Mabao, derives from the War Clan's principle that imperfection is the essence of true power. The Hand of the Supreme Being is a martial technique that Xiao Chen himself had cultivated, now shown in its ultimate form. The chapter also introduces the concept of soul-binding chains as a death world divine art, a counterpoint to the straightforward physical approach.

## Reading Guide

The identity of the corpse as the Martial Ancestor is a monumental revelation that will have far-reaching consequences. His relationship with the Martial Imprint becomes clearer, and his disdain for the otherworld Ancestral Gods suggests he may lead a counter-offensive. Xiao Chen, who has been collecting fragments of the stone man and seeking eternal light, now faces a new paradigm: the martial path he follows has a living paragon. Readers should watch for the Martial Ancestor's next moves and how Xiao Chen will integrate these insights into his own cultivation. The temporary arrival of Keke and the others through the star gate also hints at impending convergence of major characters.
