## Chapter Overview

In this monumental chapter, the White Bone Ancestral Lord descends upon the outer regions of the Death World, his presence shaking heaven and earth. He begins to refine the colossal black bones buried beneath the plains, summoning the King Ship and reshaping it into a true vessel of war. As he forges the skull gate into the ship, ancient images flicker across the polished bone—visions of a distant past that unravel long-held mysteries. We witness Keke's father, a cool white beast, carrying the corpse of the Martial Ancestor across the Death World, battling an Otherworld Supreme Ancestral God, and later entrusting the body to two shadowy figures. The chapter is a profound historical unveiling, connecting threads from the Martial Ancestor's death to the origins of the Fiendish Corpse, and setting the stage for the closed path between worlds.

## Key Plot Points

The White Bone Ancestral Lord, a supreme being of the Death World, arrives with overwhelming power. He tears open the earth, extracting mountain-sized black bones that had long been buried, and begins to reforge the King Ship into a black war vessel. A massive piece of forehead bone, once used as a gateway between the World of Immortality and the Death World, is also drawn out and fused into the ship. As the Ancestral Lord refines these materials, the forehead bone reveals stunning visions of the past. The first shows Keke's father—a small, confident white beast with a straw in his mouth, carrying the limp body of the Martial Ancestor across the desolate land. He engages an Otherworld Supreme Ancestral God in a battle that lasts three hours, emerging victorious. Two indistinct figures then appear, and together they slay a black giant Ancestral Lord who came to seize the fallen god's soul. The vision ends with the white beast handing the Martial Ancestor's corpse to the two figures before departing. The chapter concludes with the Ancestral Lord also extracting the Bone Well and fusing it into the ship, seemingly sealing all passages between the Death World and the mortal realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter delves into the deep lore of the Death World and its hierarchy. The White Bone Ancestral Lord is a 'zu jun'—a supreme fire-seed being equivalent to a true Ancestral God in power. The King Ship, originally a ghostly vessel from the Forbidden Sea, serves as the basis for a new war chariot, imbued with the essence of countless black bones. The forehead bone was once a 'world gate,' a stabilized spatial passage, now reforged into part of the ship. The visions confirm that the Fiendish Corpse (邪尸) was none other than the remains of the Martial Ancestor (武祖), a legendary human figure who killed Otherworld Ancestral Gods in the ancient war. Keke's father is depicted as a transcendent being, casually carrying the Martial Ancestor's body and defeating an Otherworld Supreme Ancestral God. The two shadowy figures are implied to be the ancestors of the Divine Tribe, possibly Fuxi and Nüwa, who sealed the Death World's sovereign in ages past. The closing of the Bone Well hints that Xiao Chen's link to the Nine Provinces may be severed, raising the stakes for his return.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the historical images, as they resolve many long-standing questions about the Martial Ancestor, the Fiendish Corpse, and Keke's origins. The portrayal of Keke's father provides a benchmark for Keke's own latent potential. The White Bone Ancestral Lord's actions—refining the ship and sealing the passage—suggest he has a grand plan that may involve the Martial Ancestor's revived body or the Eternal Light. The closing of the Bone Well creates a sense of isolation, but also implies that the Death World's deeper secrets are now more accessible. How Xiao Chen will navigate this new situation remains to be seen. The chapter masterfully blends epic world-building with poignant history, reminding us of the cost of ancient wars and the enduring legacy of heroes.
