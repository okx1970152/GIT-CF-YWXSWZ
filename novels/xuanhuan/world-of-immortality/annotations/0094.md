## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges deeper into the horrors of the Dead City as Xiao Chen and his companions face a ghoul empowered by a cursed shroud. The battle is a test of strategy and teamwork, revealing that the ghoul's terrifying strength comes not from its own cultivation but from the grudge-soaked cloth it wears. The three skeletons, often underestimated, play a decisive role with their soul-light abilities, marking their evolution as cunning and powerful allies. More disturbingly, the chapter ends with a sight that shakes Xiao Chen to his core—a legendary immortal from the mortal world, now reduced to a deathly figure lurking in the Dead City. This revelation hints at the city's connection to ancient, corrupted histories and sets a tone of mounting dread.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and Liu Mu coordinate an attack on the ghoul alongside Yan Qingcheng and Liu Ruyan, but the ghoul proves overwhelming due to its shroud. The three skeletons, who had been observing, suddenly intervene, tearing at the shroud and preventing Xiao Chen from being fully entangled. Liu Mu uses spatial abilities to restrain the ghoul, while Xiao Chen, realizing the shroud is the source of its power, focuses on stripping it away. The skeletons disassemble their bodies to evade the ghoul's counterattack, then launch a soul-light assault from their skulls, striking the ghoul's eyes and causing a fatal vulnerability. Xiao Chen decapitates the ghoul, but even headless it continues to attack until he uses a new technique—Slay God Dispersing Hand—learned from the stele patterns near the Divine Stele. After the battle, the shroud is examined and buried, but a mysterious figure in ancient clothing retrieves it. Xiao Chen recognizes this figure as a legendary immortal from the mortal world, now devoid of spirit and radiating only deathly aura, deeply unsettling him. They then join Yizhen, Dugu Jianmo, and a mysterious woman in a battle against an elite yin soldier.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The shroud (裹尸布) is a classic xianxia trope of a cursed artifact that amplifies the power of its wielder, especially undead or demonic entities. Its power comes from being soaked in the blood of powerful beings, a common motif for evil relics. The technique Xiao Chen uses, Slay God Dispersing Hand (戮神散手), is a legendary lost art—one of the many ancient techniques inscribed on the railings surrounding the Divine Stele. This emphasizes the importance of the stele as a repository of lost martial knowledge. The yin soldiers (阴兵) are elite undead warriors, often bound to ancient battlefields, and their presence in the Dead City signifies a deeper layer of danger. Finally, the appearance of a figure from mortal mythology (likely a Daoist immortal) as a hollow, feral entity in the Dead City suggests that even the most exalted can fall and be corrupted in this grim world—a reminder of the novel's tragic and desolate tone.

## Reading Guide

The disturbing revelation of a legendary immortal reduced to a deathly creature raises profound questions about the nature of the Dead City and the fate of those who sought immortality. Readers should pay close attention to how this shapes Xiao Chen's perception of cultivation and the ultimate cost of power. The shroud, now in the hands of this corrupted figure, may resurface as a future threat. Meanwhile, the ongoing battle with the yin soldier showcases the growing alliances among the younger generation of cultivators—Dugu Jianmo, Yizhen, and the mysterious woman all demonstrate formidable abilities. Watch for how Xiao Chen's new techniques evolve and how the skeletons' soul-light develops. The Dead City's secrets are only beginning to unravel, and the foreshadowing of greater horrors looms over every shadowed alley.
