## Chapter Overview

This chapter strikes a somber and desperate chord, blending cosmic-scale confrontation with the tragic annihilation of one of the mortal world's most revered sword lineages. Xiao Chen, fresh from his retreat into the Death World, returns to find the Nine Provinces under the heel of the Otherworld. The opening battle beneath Luoyang against a nightmare entity from the distant past sets a tone of barely contained cataclysm. But the true heartbreak comes in the Curse Realm, where the entire Dugu clan—a family that lived for the sword and died by it—is methodically erased by the cold, arrogant power of Ancestral God Cold Star Wing. Xiao Chen's fury erupts in a lightning ambush, but the chapter closes on a note of madness and grief as Dugu Jianmo, the last of his bloodline, shatters under the weight of his loss.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens in the underground palace of Luoyang, where a colossal yellow hand reaches through a temporal rift, threatening Xiao Chen and his companions. A massive vine intervenes, dragging the hand away, but a second hand seizes the chance to snatch the broken formation diagram. Xiao Chen's half stone skull activates for the first time, repelling the hand and shattering the spatial passage. Retreating to the Death World, Xiao Chen discovers the half skull has entered his body, causing partial petrification that he can control. After a brief stay, he returns to the Nine Provinces to find the Curse Realm under siege. There, he witnesses the destruction of the Dugu clan: over a hundred gray-robed swordsmen, wielding the legendary Dugu Nine Swords, are crushed by Ancestral God Cold Star Wing. Their final, desperate sword intent—a brilliant bloom of collective will—escapes into the wilderness, finding the broken spirit of Dugu Jianmo, who has been in hiding. Overcome by the vision of his family's slaughter, Dugu Jianmo goes mad, weeping blood and plunging into the legendary 'ten-thousand-man pit.' Xiao Chen, enraged by the wanton massacre, ambushes Cold Star Wing with the broken formation diagram, severing one of his arms before the Ancestral God retreats.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Curse Realm (咒界) is one of the four great worlds of the Eternal Continent, alongside the Martial, Qi, and Soul Realms. It is known for its ancient, world-spanning formations that can trap even Ancestral Gods—hence the Otherworld powers have been slowly dismantling them for decades. The Dugu family is a legendary sword lineage, their 'Dugu Nine Swords' representing the pinnacle of mortal swordsmanship. Their annihilation here underscores the vast power gap between Ancestral Gods and all lesser cultivators. The half stone skull, a fragment of the mythical Stone Man, is a recurring mysterious artifact; in this chapter it finally shows active power, entering Xiao Chen's body and granting him a stone armor form. The broken formation diagram—a fractured remnant of the Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation—is Xiao Chen's most potent weapon, allowing him to wound an Ancestral God despite being far below that realm.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up two major threads: Dugu Jianmo's mad descent and potential future rebirth from the 'ten-thousand-man pit,' and Xiao Chen's growing reliance on the half stone skull and its mysterious properties. The ambush of Cold Star Wing signals that Xiao Chen is now able to threaten Ancestral Gods directly, but the cost of that power—his petrification episodes—remains a looming danger. The Curse Realm's ancient formations may become a crucial battlefield or hiding ground. Watch for signs of Dugu Jianmo's eventual return, transformed by the collective sword-intent of his clan. The Otherworld's relentless conquest will force Xiao Chen to make increasingly desperate choices.
