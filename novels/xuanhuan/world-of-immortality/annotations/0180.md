## Chapter Overview
The chapter opens with a serene depiction of Heavenly Emperor City at dawn, a stark contrast to the previous night's violence. Xiao Chen's daily routine of breakfast at Beidou Academy and meditation is interrupted as he visits the Primordial Beast Fighting Palace to observe the final qualifying rounds of the beast tournament. The focus narrows on a seemingly ordinary white tiger cub that displays extraordinary combat skill, defeating two formidable opponents with technique that suggests a hidden heritage. The calm, observational tone sets the stage for a deeper exploration of the world's beast culture and the mysteries surrounding this unassuming fighter.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter is largely descriptive and observational. After a civilized morning, Xiao Chen enters the Primordial Beast Fighting Palace, one of the three great arenas. He observes a white tiger cub that, despite its ordinary appearance, possesses remarkable speed and battle instinct. Its first opponent, a golden eagle with divine light, is swiftly dispatched when the tiger uses an unexpected feint and instant kill. The second opponent, a purple wolf cub that can fly and conjure lightning, presents a greater threat but is ultimately eviscerated by the tiger's precise claws. Xiao Chen notes that the white tiger's movements are not merely instinctual but follow a refined ancient combat art, reminiscent of the Little Defiant Dragon's dragon technique. This suggests the tiger may have inherited a sacred beast lineage. The chapter ends with the white tiger's victory and the crowd's astonishment. No direct confrontation or new alliances occur; instead, the chapter plants a seed of intrigue about the white tiger's owner and origin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The beast fighting tournament is a major institution in the World of Immortality. Facilities like the Primordial Beast Fighting Palace are lavish, with spherical crystal screens provided by Spiritists to broadcast the combat. The tournament structure involves qualifying rounds where beasts fight seven consecutive battles to become a “Beast King,” then the Beast Kings compete in a final “King War.” The white tiger's technique may be a form of ancient beast combat art, a tradition passed down among certain sacred bloodlines. This mirrors the Little Defiant Dragon's innate dragon combat arts, emphasizing that in this world, both humans and beasts can inherit powerful martial legacies. Such techniques grant even unremarkable-looking creatures devastating effectiveness.
