## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks the long-awaited departure from Dragon Island, a moment both triumphant and bittersweet. Lan Nuo mediates a tentative peace between Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er, while the unprecedented gathering of eleven Companion Dragon Kings collectively roars across the Forbidden Sea, summoning the legendary Ancestral Dragon Ship. Keke, the whimsical white beast, surprisingly leads the dragon kings in their ritual, her voice cutting through the cacophony with inexplicable clarity. The mood shifts from tense anticipation to awe as the immense, rainbow-hued ship appears on the horizon, and finally to poignant farewell as the adult dragons gather to send off their young with mournful cries. It is a chapter of closure and new beginnings, steeped in mythic grandeur and undercurrents of unresolved grudges.

## Key Plot Points

Lan Nuo assures Xiao Chen of her safe return and confident cultivation, and she single-handedly reconciles the bitter feud between Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er, though both parties remain fundamentally unwilling. Xiao Chen's private reflections on Lan Nuo's legend reveal his deep admiration and conflicted feelings, grounding the epic events in human emotion. The eleven young Companion Dragon Kings, now including four newly arrived from older experts, gather on the beach and begin a coordinated roar that shakes heaven and sea—a ritual to summon the ancestral ship. Keke mischievously runs to the front of the dragon kings and joins the chorus, her infant-like cries somehow rising above the thunderous roars, marking her as far more than a simple pet. The forbidden sea parts as the Ancestral Dragon Ship appears, an enormous vessel built from dragon bone and divine wood, radiating seven-colored light. As the ship docks via a rainbow bridge, the adult dragons—tyrants, lion-kings, sword-dragons—gather behind the beach, roaring in grief-filled farewell to their departing young. The eleven young dragons run back to their elders in a heartbreaking display of attachment before the journey begins.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The ritual of summoning the Ancestral Dragon Ship is a key piece of Dragon Island's lore, echoing ancient Chinese myths of dragon boats and celestial conveyance. The ship is said to be constructed from the bones of a fallen Ancestral Dragon and rare divine wood, and it can cross the otherwise impassable Forbidden Sea—a deadly barrier that has trapped all cultivators on the island. The traditional belief held that nine Companion Dragon Kings were required for the summoning, but here eleven have gathered, breaking the expected pattern and hinting at greater forces at work. Keke's inexplicable ability to lead the dragon kings and have her voice heard above theirs suggests she possesses a sacred or primordial status that even the dragon bloodlines respect. The scene of adult dragons bidding farewell to their young injects a note of primal emotion into the mythic framework, reminding readers that even ancient beasts feel love and loss.

## Reading Guide

As the party prepares to board the Ancestral Dragon Ship, several threads remain unresolved. Zhao Lin'er's reconciliation with Xiao Chen is fragile and likely to fray once away from Lan Nuo's influence. The journey across the Forbidden Sea may bring new dangers or revelations, especially given the appearance of the ship and the mention of a 'Demon Plain' beyond. Keke's true nature grows ever more enigmatic—her authority over dragon kings hints at a lineage tied to the original Ancestral Dragon or even the World Tree itself. The adult dragons' mournful cries suggest deeper bonds that may affect the young dragon kings' loyalty later. Readers should watch for how the dynamics shift aboard the ship, especially among the rival alliances, and whether the fragile peace holds. The next chapter promises the first true exploration of the world beyond Dragon Island, a realm of ancient gods and greater perils.
