## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers into a maelstrom of ancient powers as the Heavenly Burial Valley becomes the stage for a convergence of half-ancestors. A mysterious woman bearing Qingqing's exact likeness emerges from a churning black abyss, her identity and intentions shrouded in enigma. As she reaches for a sealed world called the Deep Prison Abyss, half-ancestors from the Cultivation World, the Night Yaksha, the Asura, the Angel Holy God, and the Skeleton King all arrive, each determined to claim the prize. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen finds himself cornered by four vindictive heavenly kings—War King Mabao, Earth King, Sea God King, and the Night Yaksha Queen—who have used his own ambitions to trap him. Betrayed by Wu Ming, who manipulates the chaos for his own elemental refinement, Xiao Chen must fight for his life in a space sealed by the Heavenly Stele, cut off from his death-world escape. The chapter crackles with tension as long-buried secrets and ruthless schemes collide.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the stunning appearance of a woman identical to Qingqing, striking down a hundred-zhang flying sword with a single finger. Two Cultivation World half-ancestors confront her, but their attack is intercepted by a massive armored claw from one of the valley's mysterious caves, revealing a terrifying scaled demon. Soon, more half-ancestors descend: a gaunt Night Yaksha elder, a blood-red Asura, a radiant Angel Holy God, and a titanic Skeleton King. They seal the deepest part of the valley and begin contesting control of the Deep Prison Abyss, a small world said to be comparable to the Lost Paradise.

Forced back into the forty-nine ancient palaces, Xiao Chen is immediately identified by War King Mabao, whose innate power sees through all disguises. The four heavenly kings—Mabao, Earth King, Sea God King, and Night Yaksha Queen—surround him, revealing their plot to lure him here by exploiting his knowledge of the Heavenly Stele. As Xiao Chen prepares to flee via the death world, Mabao coldly informs him that the stelesealed ground blocks all such escape. At that moment, Wu Ming strikes from behind, smashing the Indestructible Imperial Heavenly Bell into Sea God King’s back, attempting to harvest his water soul. A chaotic battle erupts: Xiao Chen engages Earth King and Mabao, while Wu Ming presses his assault despite the Night Yaksha Queen’s sword-fire. The situation grows more tangled when Ye Jing and her sect sisters arrive, demanding the return of the bell that rightfully belongs to her clan. Wu Ming, desperate to avoid additional enemies, shouts Xiao Chen’s name to deflect attention, hoping the assembled cultivators will turn on him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Half-ancestors represent the pinnacle below true Ancestral Gods, beings who have partially transcended the mortal limit and can command the laws of heaven and earth. Their arrival instantly renders all lesser cultivators irrelevant, as the treasures and secrets of the Heavenly Burial Valley become forbidden fruit. The Deep Prison Abyss is introduced as a legendary sealed world, paired with the Lost Paradise that Keke inherited, hinting at a larger cosmology of hidden realms. The Heavenly Stele sealing power is demonstrated here: in areas as close to the divine stele, even the death-world recall technique fails, cutting off Xiao Chen’s lifeline. War King Mabao’s ability to see through void and essence is a rare innate talent of the War Clan, making him a particularly dangerous tracker. Wu Ming’s body refinement art, which fuses countless artifacts into his flesh, illustrates an alternative path to power—one that requires elemental souls for completion.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation in the Heavenly Burial Valley. The identity of the Qingqing-like woman remains ambiguous; while she wears a crown and armor befitting a sovereign, her power and actions suggest she may not be the gentle girl Xiao Chen once knew—or perhaps she has been transformed by her time in the abyss. The Deep Prison Abyss is certain to be a central prize, and the half-ancestors’ conflict over it will likely escalate. Xiao Chen is now trapped and publicly named, with enemies on all sides. Will the old allies who once fought beside him, like the Incorruptible Heavenly Bell’s true owner Ye Jing, side with him or against him? Watch for the appearance of the three skeleton kings who peeked from the death-world cave earlier—they may yet play a role. The tension is at a breaking point, and the next chapters promise explosive combat and stunning revelations.
