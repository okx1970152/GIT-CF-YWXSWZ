## Chapter Overview

Chapter 63 delivers a concentrated duel of will and esoteric power, pitting Xiao Chen against one of the rarest and most dangerous types of practitioners: a Space Spiritist. Set in the eerie, mist-shrouded heart of Dragon Island near the ancient stele, the chapter strips away the sprawling politics of the alliances and focuses on a raw, personal confrontation. The tone shifts from the epic scale of mob battles to an intimate, almost claustrophobic struggle within a sealed pocket dimension. Here, brute force and resilience are tested against the absolute authority of spatial control, creating a tension that feels both primal and cosmic.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen ventures deeper into the forbidden zone around the divine stele and encounters a seemingly lifeless figure shrouded in black mist. This figure, Liu Mu, is not a corpse but a Space Spiritist so deeply immersed in cultivation that his life-signs had slowed to near stillness. Liu Mu, irritated by the increasing number of intruders near his training ground, confronts Xiao Chen. He traps Xiao Chen within his personal sealed space—a 100-square-meter domain of green light where Liu Mu holds absolute dominance. Within this space, spatial forces crush Xiao Chen's body, threatening to shatter his bones. Yet Xiao Chen refuses to submit. He draws on his profound physical cultivation and the power of his divinely transformed acupoints, forcing his body to move step by agonizing step toward Liu Mu. This display of sheer willpower surprises the Space Spiritist, who begins to exhaust himself maintaining the compression. Xiao Chen then unleashes a new technique—Big Dipper Sealing the Divine—using seven points of starlight to form a protective screen that absorbs Liu Mu's spatial attacks and redirects the energy back at him. The sealed space cracks, and Liu Mu suffers a backlash. Though Xiao Chen ultimately gains the upper hand, he chooses not to kill Liu Mu, and the two part with mutual respect, even exchanging information about the stele and the island's dangers. This encounter establishes Liu Mu as a powerful but honorable figure who may become an ally.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces one of the most celebrated archetypes in xianxia fiction: the Space Spiritist (空间灵士). In Chinese cultivation lore, innate abilities are rare and often define the practitioner's entire path. Spiritists (灵士) are born with a natural affinity for a specific element or law; space manipulation is considered among the most formidable, as it governs the very fabric of existence. The text explicitly notes that space spiritists are "kings among same-level cultivators" because controlling a sealed space effectively places an opponent's life in their hands. Liu Mu's pale, sickly complexion is a common trope for those who train in isolation, away from sunlight, and who channel vast energies through fragile bodies. Xiao Chen's counter-technique, Big Dipper Sealing the Divine (北斗封神), draws on the Big Dipper constellation—a powerful symbol in Daoist cosmology representing authority over fate and cosmic order. The seven stars of the Dipper are often used in protective and sealing arts, making this technique a perfect thematic counter to spatial locking. The chapter also reinforces the idea that even the strongest innate advantages can be overcome by unyielding will and the right technique—a core theme of the genre.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a crucial character-building moment for Xiao Chen, demonstrating that his growth is not solely about acquiring external treasures but about refining his inner resolve and developing new applications of his existing power. The Big Dipper Sealing the Divine technique, which he first used instinctively against the Tyrand Dragon King's aura, is now consciously mastered. Readers should watch for how this technique evolves in future battles, as it may become a signature defense. The introduction of Liu Mu adds a new kind of ally to the story—one who is detached from the island's factional wars but possesses immense power. His knowledge of the divine stele and the deeper secrets of Dragon Island could be pivotal. The chapter also subtly raises the stakes: if a Space Spiritist finds it hard to cultivate peacefully near the stele, what other dangers and competitors are lurking in the island's core? The note that others have already entered the deep region suggests that Xiao Chen is about to encounter even more formidable opponents—and potential allies—as the arc progresses.
