## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers the long-anticipated confrontation between Keke's father—the white beast reborn as a human warrior—and the ancient Old Stone-Man King, a primordial titan who once battled the five Stone-Man Kings of legend. The battle takes place on the terrifying Ninety-Nine Stone Steps within the Eternal Unknown, a realm beyond mortal and god alike. The tone is one of tragic grandeur: Keke's father fights with desperate brilliance, knowing he faces an opponent who has lived through eons. The stakes could not be higher—not only does this duel determine the fate of the Nine Provinces' last hope, but it also reveals the true power of the Stone Man path and the hidden reserves of the Heaven-Defying Clan. The emotional weight is immense, as Keke's father sacrifices his body yet proves his indomitable will.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Old Stone-Man King displaying his terrifying power: his withered stone body has sprouted strands of gray hair, a sign that he has begun walking the Ancestral God path while retaining his Stone-Man foundation. Keke's father refuses to back down, declaring he will take half the king's life. The battle escalates rapidly as the Old Stone-Man King employs the 'Turning Heaven and Earth' divine art, crushing space itself. Keke's father counters with the 'Dragon Travels the World' technique and a King-level divine city, forcing the old king to fall back three steps and reopen ancient wounds. In a final, desperate exchange, the old king unleashes a soul-shaking killing curse, and Keke's father counters with a spontaneous enlightenment: 'Great sound is silent, great image is formless.' The clash erases stone steps and leaves Keke's father a bloody smear on the stone. But in a shocking reversal, he resurrects from a single drop of holy blood he had hidden earlier. The ancient scroll (Reverse Heaven War Treasure) flies to him, and he confronts the old king once more. The Old Stone-Man King, enraged, seals the Eternal Unknown and turns his attention toward Xiao Chen, sensing the presence of the Stone-Man King's imprint within him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps represent the trial path for a Stone Man attempting the 'Final Step'—the ultimate transformation into a complete stone being of supreme power. The Old Stone-Man King's dual cultivation of the Stone Man and Ancestral God paths is a rare and profound achievement. The Heaven-Defying Clan (Keke's lineage) is a cursed bloodline that once rivaled the mightiest ancestors; Keke's father's ability to wound a Stone-Man King emphasizes their legendary ferocity. The concept of 'Great sound is silent, great image is formless' derives from Daoist philosophy, reflecting the highest realm of martial and spiritual unity. The Reverse Heaven War Treasure (ancient scroll) is a legendary artifact that once belonged to the five fallen Stone-Man Kings; its appearance signals a major shift in the cosmic power balance.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for the final act of the war. Xiao Chen now carries the imprint of an Otherworld Stone-Man King within him, and the Old Stone-Man King has sealed the Eternal Unknown to prevent his escape. The scroll now in Keke's father's hands may be the key to rallying the Nine Provinces' forces. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen uses the ancient scroll and the Stone-Man King imprint in the coming conflicts. The resurrection technique demonstrated by Keke's father hints that many seemingly fallen heroes may still have hidden aces. The next chapters will likely focus on Xiao Chen's escape from the sealed realm and the inevitable clash with the Old Stone-Man King's followers.
