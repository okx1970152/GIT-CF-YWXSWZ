## Chapter Overview

After a century and a half in the Death World, Xiao Chen and his companions return with treasures and scars from their expedition into the deepest reaches. The narrative shifts from relentless battle to a warm reunion at the Divine Village, where old friends have aged and new generations have risen. Yet beneath the surface of celebration, the fates of key figures—especially the mysterious female sovereign who resembles Qingqing—hang in the balance. The chapter closes with an electrifying moment at the Dead City, where Xiao Chen's forehead mark flares to life, suggesting that his time in the mortal world is once again intertwined with the ancient power of the underworld.

## Key Plot Points

- Over 150 years have passed since Xiao Chen and his party entered the depths of the Death Continent. They return with Ancestral God blood, a black iron battle suit, and three broken War Swords. The damaged condition of the swords casts doubt on completing a perfect sword formation.
- At the Divine Village, they are reunited with old allies: the ever-rakish Jin Sanyi, the now-married Divine Tribe warrior Qi'er, and the grown Tianya. Binglan and Xuemeng remain as lively as ever, but their romantic interest in Xing Tian and Chi You leaves Jin Sanyi in comic despair.
- Critical intelligence is received about the female sovereign—a woman identical to Qingqing—who ventured deep into the Death Continent with Yi Tianzhong and other kings. After a century trapped in an ancient ruin, she chose to continue onward with Yi Tianzhong, while one wounded king escaped to report the news.
- Xiao Chen decides to return to the Nine Provinces via the Dead City. Upon stepping before the ancient city, his forehead mark (the Dead City sigil) erupts with blinding light, hurling his companions back and sending him soaring to the city wall in a transcendent moment of power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The concept of 'Ancestral God blood' (祖君血液) is introduced as a supreme cultivation resource. A single drop can enhance artifact refinement and aid in breaking through bottlenecks, underscoring the value of even a small amount of such essence.
- The broken War Swords are tied to the 'Great Derivation' (大衍之数) cosmology: forty-nine swords form a complete array, but damage to the internal sword formations may render them useless. This reflects the xianxia trope of items requiring perfect matching to unlock their full potential.
- The Dead City mark on Xiao Chen's forehead is a recurring motif, linking him to the underworld. Its activation at the chapter's end signals that his identity is increasingly bound to the Death World's sovereignty, a classic cultivation theme of dual-world inheritance.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a breather after intense action, focusing on narrative consolidation and emotional reunion. Pay attention to the news about the female sovereign: her journey with Yi Tianzhong into the deepest Death Continent is a major thread that will likely reconnect with Xiao Chen in future arcs. The three broken War Swords may become a key objective—possibly requiring a trip to the Artifact Refining Sect of the Cultivation World. The cliffhanger at the Dead City with Xiao Chen's mark blazing suggests an imminent power-up or confrontation, so readers should watch for developments regarding the Dead City's secrets and their connection to Xiao Chen's cultivation path.
