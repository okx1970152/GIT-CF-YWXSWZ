## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a stunning tactical victory for Xiao Chen, as he combines the full might of his Eight Aspect Worlds with an array of divine artifacts—the stone man puppet, the divine diagram, the ancient scroll, and heaven-mark manifestations—to humble the legendary Eternal King Meng Degang. Already weakened by the theft of his lower body by the three skeleton kings, Meng Degang finds himself trapped in a fusion of eight illusory worlds, each packed with a supreme treasure. The battle is a masterpiece of strategic layering: Xiao Chen, assisted by Keke’s nine-colored divine light and the overwhelming presence of the stone man puppet, systematically breaks down a being who has survived eons. But the chapter closes on a note of mystery, as the three skeletons deliver their stolen prize—half of Meng Degang’s stone body—to an ethereal maiden through an ancient summoning formation. The victory is decisive, but new shadows gather.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Meng Degang already in a dire state: his lower stone body was taken by the three skeleton kings earlier, and now the stone man puppet smashes his right arm with a single blow. Xiao Chen takes advantage of the chaos, transforming his body into the divine diagram and slashing at Meng like a blade of pure light. Meng retaliates with ten finger beams that tear space, but Xiao Chen’s speed and the puppet’s pressure keep him on the defensive.

Keke enters the fray, unleashing a nine-colored divine light that binds Meng and drains a tenth of his divine power instantly. Though Meng breaks free, his confidence is shaken. Xiao Chen then deploys the ancient scroll, using the stone spear—taken from Xutian—to parry Meng’s golden soul-attack. The clash of the spear’s full stone-man-king resonance against Meng’s divine thought nearly shatters his will to fight.

Meng attempts to flee, but Xiao Chen opens the Eight Aspect Worlds, trapping him in a dimensional cage. Each world hosts a supreme treasure: the stone man puppet in one, the divine diagram in another, the heaven-marks and the king-city in others. The worlds rotate and converge, and Meng is crushed beneath simultaneous assaults from all eight directions. His stone arms shatter, his consciousness is battered. He tries to break out through the Heaven Aspect, but the heavenly mark transforms into a massive stele and slams into him, amplified by the combined force of all eight worlds.

In a final, desperate gambit, Meng charges at Xiao Chen’s position in the Marsh Aspect. Xiao Chen responds by merging the eight worlds into one, condensing their primordial force into the stone spear. The spear tears through space and pierces Meng’s forehead, pinning his skull from front to back. Meng’s divine consciousness erupts in a fury, but unable to face the combined might of the puppet and artifacts, he flees, screaming vengeance.

Xiao Chen begins a holy sacrifice on the captured stone body, extracting its essence. Meanwhile, the three skeletons, having carried the lower half of Meng’s body across treacherous Death World terrain, reach a hidden mud lake. They activate an ancient summoning formation, and a transcendent, ethereal maiden manifests to receive the prize—her beauty described as otherworldly, but her true nature unknown.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Aspect Worlds are derived from Fuxi’s Eight Trigrams, representing fundamental elements: Heaven, Earth, Water, Fire, Thunder, Wind, Mountain, and Marsh. Xiao Chen’s ability to merge them into a single unified world demonstrates his mastery of this complex divine art. The technique’s strength lies in synergy—each world amplifies the others, so an attack on one aspect carries the force of all eight.

Holy sacrifice (圣祭) is a taboo technique from the Martial Ancestor’s heretical chapter. It allows a cultivator to extract the life essence from a powerful being’s corpse to strengthen themselves. Its use here on a stone man king’s body marks a significant step in Xiao Chen’s cultivation.

Stone men are a unique cultivation path in the World of Immortality, where the practitioner’s body becomes petrified stone, granting immense physical power but often at the cost of flexibility. Meng Degang is an Otherworld stone man king, a being who has survived multiple civilization cycles. His defeat by a coalition of lower-cultivation beings (Xiao Chen at Half-Ancestor, Keke at Upper Ancestral God) underscores the power of artifact combinations and tactical genius over raw realm.

The three skeleton kings—Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, and Lunhuiwang—were originally disciples of Sovereign Suiren. Their reappearance here, working for a mysterious maiden, suggests they serve a hidden power deeper in the Death World.

## Reading Guide

This chapter showcases Xiao Chen’s growth in tactical artifact manipulation; he is no longer merely relying on raw power but orchestrating a symphony of treasures. Pay attention to how each artifact interacts with the Eight Aspect Worlds—the stone man puppet provides offense, the ancient scroll and divine diagram offer defense and suppressive force, and the heavenly marks anchor the dimensional cage.

The mysterious maiden at the chapter’s end raises several questions: Who is she? Why does she want a piece of a stone man king’s body? Her ethereal, immortal-like appearance may be a disguise or a projection from an even greater being. The three skeletons’ silent obedience hints that they are still serving a larger cause.

Meng Degang’s escape of his divine consciousness means this is not the last we’ll see of him. A stone man king’s will is not so easily extinguished, and he will likely seek revenge against Xiao Chen once he recovers a new body.

Finally, note that Keke has stepped onto the path of an Upper Ancestral God, and her nine-colored light is now a serious threat to stone man level enemies. Her bond with Xiao Chen remains strong, and she continues to grow in power and cunning.
