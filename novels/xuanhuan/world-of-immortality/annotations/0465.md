## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a shift from open battle to shadowy intrigue beneath the earth. Xiao Chen, having barely survived his clash with the Otherworld Ancestral God Babura, recovers in a primeval forest and studies a mysterious jade book. The book contains the lethal diagram of the Great Destruction Spear, a technique that nearly overwhelms his spirit. Meanwhile, the half-ancestors Asura, Skeleton King, Yaksha, and Angel Holy God seal off the region with a terrifying bone banner formation, intending to monopolize the Eternal Light said to lie deep underground. Xiao Chen, still hiding his identity, watches as several half-ancestors and even the feared Babura breach the formation and descend into the subterranean corpse river. The atmosphere is one of mounting dread and calculated caution; the underground world holds secrets that may empower or annihilate all who enter.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen spends several days healing from the devastating battle with Babura. While reading an indecipherable jade book, he discovers two diagrams—one of a war spear—that contain the essence of the Great Destruction Spear technique. He successfully grasps its meaning through spiritual resonance, condensing the spear's terrifying aura. Outside, the half-ancestors complete a massive bone banner formation to seal the area and block rivals. Several half-ancestors, including Babura, manage to enter the seal. Xiao Chen, along with three other half-ancestors, forces his way through and descends a deep shaft into the corpse river. There, he witnesses the Skeleton King enraged after losing his left lower leg bone to a mysterious attacker. The half-ancestors press deeper toward an immense yellow corpse sea, where a colossal island glows with divine light—the rumored source of Eternal Light. Xiao Chen realizes Babura, despite his fear of the slumbering 'Great One' deeper in the river, is driven by the prospect of Eternal Light, prompting Xiao Chen to follow urgently.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens the mythology of the World of Immortality. The Great Destruction Spear represents a peak offensive technique that embodies pure annihilation; understanding such a technique does not require written instructions but a spiritual communion with its intent—a hallmark of high-level cultivation in xianxia. The corpse river (尸河) and yellow corpse sea are classic symbols of death and impurity in Chinese mythology, often associated with the underworld and undead. The 'Eternal Light' (永恒之光) is a legendary substance formed when ten or more Ancestral Gods are buried together; it can elevate a half-ancestor to a true Ancestral God, explaining why even the cautious half-ancestors risk the descent. The bone banner formation (白骨大幡) combines necromantic and sealing arts, showing the half-ancestors' cunning—they use the Skeleton King's undead magic to create a barrier that also amplifies death energy. The image of a half-ancestor losing a bone to an unseen assailant underscores the lawless, lethal nature of the death world and the desperation of those seeking supreme power.

## Reading Guide

Readers should note the careful power dynamics at play: Xiao Chen is still below half-ancestor level but can now threaten them with the Great Destruction Spear. His decision to hide his identity and observe rather than act shows his growing strategic patience. The Skeleton King's missing leg bone is a clue to a new, hidden player—possibly a being that rival the half-ancestors. The underground corpse sea and the glowing island suggest that the Eternal Light may be real, but the sleeping 'Great One' (the '大人') that even Babura fears could be awakened. The chapter ends with all major parties converging on the island, setting up a confrontation that could involve multiple half-ancestors, Babura, and the slumbering entity. The question of whether the half-ancestors will fight each other or cooperate remains open.
