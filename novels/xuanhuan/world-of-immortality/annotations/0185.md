## Chapter Overview
In Chapter 185, Xiao Chen arrives at the Undying Sect's branch in Heavenly Emperor City to confront Yan Qingcheng about the rumored betrothal that nearly cost him his life. Their interaction oscillates between tense provocation and playful mockery, revealing the unresolved friction between them. The chapter then shifts to the beast tournament, where Keke’s oversleeping leads to disqualification, and the audience witnesses a shocking duel between the Golden Lion King—a three-headed beast with piercing vertical eyes—and a Xuanwu, a direct descendant of the Black Tortoise. The Lion King’s devastating secret weapon instantly obliterates the Xuanwu, marking it as the tournament’s most terrifying contender.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen seizes Yan Qingcheng’s hand and flies toward the Undying Sect’s hall, ignoring her protests. Inside, he pays respects to the statue of the Evil King, then delivers a bold speech about his own path, drawing a contrast between his ambition and the legendary figure. Elder Liu Qingfeng, Yan Qingcheng’s master, receives them. When Xiao Chen broaches the betrothal, Liu Qingfeng surprisingly does not reject it, only suggests delaying until Yan Qingcheng completes her "Shatter Demon, Plant God" technique. Xiao Chen, who came to provoke Yan Qingcheng, achieves his goal but is himself angered by her disdainful rejection. He leaves with a playful threat that she may end up as a concubine if she hesitates too long. Separately, Keke oversleeps and loses its beast tournament slot. Later, the fifth day’s battle pits the Golden Lion King against a Xuanwu. The Xuanwu proves formidable, with its indestructible shell and powerful earth energy, but the Golden Lion King opens one of its three vertical eyes, releasing a blood-red beam that shatters the Xuanwu entirely, shocking the crowd. Xiao Chen realizes that even he might not withstand such an attack.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Undying Evil King is the legendary founder of the Undying Sect, worshipped for his peerless martial power and solitary arrogance. His statue in the temple embodies the sect’s ideal of invincibility. The betrothal negotiation, though seemingly frivolous, reflects real cultivation-world politics where marriage alliances can strengthen ties between powers. The "Shatter Demon, Plant God" technique is a legendary method that uses a "cauldron" (a practitioner) to refine the user’s heart and spirit, explaining why Yan Qingcheng must stay near Xiao Chen despite her hatred. In the beast tournament, the Xuanwu is a direct descendant of the Black Tortoise, one of the Four Sacred Beasts, known for supreme defense. The Golden Lion King’s three heads and vertical eyes evoke mythical creatures like the "Three-eyed Golden Lion" said to possess a destructive gaze. The destruction of the Xuanwu underscores the Lion King’s terrifying potential, and such vertical eyes are often associated with divine powers in Chinese mythology.

## Reading Guide
This chapter advances two ongoing threads: Xiao Chen’s fraught relationship with Yan Qingcheng and the escalating stakes of the beast tournament. Xiao Chen’s visit to the Undying Sect shows a softening in the elder’s stance, but the delay hints at future complications. The Golden Lion King’s devastating eye beam introduces a new major threat in the tournament—will Xiao Chen have to face it with the Little Defiant Dragon? Keke’s laziness may lead to more problems; readers should watch for its reaction to missing the match. Additionally, the mention of Yan Qingcheng’s cultivation technique and her dependency on Xiao Chen suggests that their entanglement is far from over. The chapter’s tone shifts from lighthearted banter to grim spectacle, reminding us of the world’s brutal edge.
