## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with the eerie dried corpse (邪尸) using its immense power to tear open a portal to the Dead World, demonstrating a level of strength that unsettles even half-ancestors. The Ancestral Dragon Ship arrives, and Xiao Chen, connected through the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, uses the ship to ram and injure three half-ancestors. A chaotic dimensional battle ensues, where the Mad Monk Jigong employs the Great Prophecy Art to transform several half-ancestors into pigs, a comedic yet humiliating defeat. Xiao Chen emerges from the battle as a figure now recognized as a peer of half-ancestors. He then leaves for the Dead World with Tianwaitian and Renwairen, seeking to observe the dried corpse’s influence there.

## Key Plot Points

The dried corpse opens multiple spatial rifts, including a direct portal to the Dead World, showcasing its terrifying mastery over space. The Ancestral Dragon Ship, drawn by Xiao Chen’s Sacred Tree, arrives and he uses its power to strike the Asura, Angel Holy God, and Skeleton King half-ancestors, shattering their defenses. The four humiliated half-ancestors retreat into a dimensional battlefield, where Jigong uses a single syllable to turn the Yaksha, Asura, Skeleton King, and Angel Holy God into pigs—a devastating blow to their pride. The half-ancestors attack Jigong, but he escapes via the Bone Well. Xiao Chen now possesses twenty-seven War Swords and is acknowledged as a force capable of contending with half-ancestors. He decides to retreat to the Dead World with Tianwaitian and Renwairen, inviting them to witness the chaos the dried corpse may cause there.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The dried corpse (邪尸) is revealed to be from the Dead World and strong enough to force open its gate, a feat that implies it may be a transcendent being beyond half-ancestor. The Ancestral Dragon Ship is a legendary vessel from Dragon Island that responds to the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, a relic of Keke's parents. Jigong's Great Prophecy Art is a comic yet powerful divine ability that can transform beings by uttering single words; the 'zhu' syllable turns targets into pigs, a humorous reference to the classic Journey to the West. This chapter also shows that half-ancestors, despite their godlike status, can be humbled by unconventional techniques, emphasizing the xianxia theme that no one is truly invincible.

## Reading Guide

This chapter resolves the immediate standoff at the Forbidden Sea and marks Xiao Chen's departure to the Dead World, setting up his next major arc of cultivation and exploration. The half-ancestors who were turned into pigs will likely harbor deep grudges, and their humiliation may provoke future conflicts. The dried corpse's return to the Dead World hints at major changes there—readers should watch for how it stirs up the inner continent. Xiao Chen's cultivation in the Dead World, aided by the experience of Tianwaitian and Renwairen, will be crucial for his growth toward the Che Di realm. The reappearance of the three skeleton kings as battle emperors is expected soon.
