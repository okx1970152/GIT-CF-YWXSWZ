## Chapter Overview

This chapter weaves two parallel threads of terror and triumph. Within an ancient, forbidden temple, Xiao Chen confronts a chilling mystery: the corpse of a breathtakingly beautiful woman, pierced by stone weapons and sealed for eons. An invisible force compels his hands to remove the artifacts, raising questions of resurrection and ancient grudges. Simultaneously, the long-simmering war against the Otherworld erupts into open conflict. Pangu King, wielding his divine stone axe, leads a coalition of ancient powers—Keke's Xuanzu, the baleful Stone Crow, the resurrected head bone of the Little Stone Emperor, and the Martial Ancestor—in a devastating assault on the Otherworld's heart. The holy sacrifice performed by the Martial Ancestor threatens to annihilate an entire realm, forcing the four Otherworld ancestors to retreat and seal their world.

## Key Plot Points

In the temple, Xiao Chen discovers a female Stone Man corpse, her eyes pierced by stone arrows, her body pinned by a stone axe, a stone sword, and four stone daggers. An unseen will drives him to remove these weapons one by one. Each extraction reveals a hint of life: blood beads from the eyes, a healed heart wound, and a flicker of vitality. The corpse is a Stone Woman who attempted flesh-and-blood rebirth but was slain by supreme beings. The involuntary compulsion suggests her residual consciousness or a higher power is acting through Xiao Chen.

Meanwhile, the Otherworld faces an unprecedented invasion. Pangu King appears with his world-splitting axe, joined by Keke's great-grandfather (Xuanzu), the Stone Crow, and the Martial Ancestor. The four Otherworld ancestors—Lu Zhan, Lang Ya, and two others—are targeted. The Martial Ancestor begins a holy sacrifice of the entire world, a forbidden technique that drains life essence. Lu Zhan deduces that the Little Stone Emperor's head bone has survived and orchestrated this attack. The alliance systematically dismantles the Otherworld's defenses, forcing the ancestors to order a full retreat and seal their realm behind the stone door.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'zhentao' (镇尸) technique, or 'corpse suppression', is a legendary method used by ancient supreme beings to prevent powerful cultivators from resurrecting after death. By piercing the body with stone weapons—each representing a specific law or curse—they trap the soul and seal the flesh. Xiao Chen's involuntary removal of these weapons may be undoing such a seal, potentially resurrecting the Stone Woman or unleashing a calamity.

The 'holy sacrifice' (圣祭) is a forbidden art of the Martial Ancestor's heretical chapter, capable of sacrificing an entire world to empower the user or destroy enemies. Its activation signals the Martial Ancestor's ultimate commitment to wiping out the Otherworld, even at the cost of countless lives. The Little Stone Emperor's surviving head bone represents the indomitable will of a peak Stone Man King, who can persist beyond bodily destruction.

## Reading Guide

This chapter dramatically escalates the conflict. Xiao Chen's temple encounter may be a crucial key—could the Stone Woman be an ally or a new catastrophe? His acquisition of the stone weapons (though not yet claimed) could boost his power significantly. The war is now total, with ancient legends returning to fight. Readers should watch for the consequences of the Martial Ancestor's sacrifice, the four ancestors' counterattack, and Xiao Chen's exploration of the temple's deeper secrets. The reappearance of the Little Stone Emperor's will suggests that past heroes are not entirely gone, and the final battle for the fate of all worlds is drawing near.
