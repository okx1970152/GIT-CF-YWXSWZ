## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal shift in strategy for Xiao Chen as he forges a risky alliance with a former enemy, the Otherworld Ancestral God Babula. Having recovered to full power after eons, the spider-like god of war proposes a mutual goal: infiltrate the heart of the Otherworld and assassinate key ancestral gods. The focus narrows to one of the most terrifying—the True Wood Saintess, a female ancestral god on the verge of taking the final step. The chapter balances tense negotiation with elaborate preparation, as Xiao Chen borrows half of Babula's soul-source in exchange for the Primordial Demon City. The final act sees Xiao Chen and the Little Defiant Dragon marching openly toward the floating demon city where the Saintess's forces have hung the bodies of fallen heroes, signaling that a desperate showdown is imminent.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Sha Polang enjoying mortal luxuries in a nightclub, where he is discovered by Babula, a pale, sickly-looking young man who is revealed to be an Otherworld Ancestral God. Despite initial hostility, Babula states he is no longer aligned with the Otherworld, seeking revenge for the desecration of his wife's corpse. He takes Sha Polang to an isolated sea to interrogate him, but Xiao Chen arrives. Babula recognizes Xiao Chen from their earlier battle in the Corpse River and proposes an alliance to invade the Otherworld and kill powerful ancestral gods. He specifically targets the True Wood Saintess and others.

Xiao Chen, while wary, sees the opportunity. He negotiates to borrow half of Babula's soul-source power—his very life essence—to merge with the Otherworld's Dao imprint that Babula carries as a descendant. In exchange, Xiao Chen pledges the Primordial Demon City as collateral. Babula reluctantly agrees, and Xiao Chen spends ten days refining the soul-source into his own cultivation.

The chapter then jumps to the moonlit sea. Xiao Chen, the Little Defiant Dragon, Sha Polang, and the Nine-Headed Snake arrive at the True Wood Saintess's domain—a vast floating demon city hung with the bodies of the old farmer and Mo Xiaowang, among others. The Saintess herself, a towering stone woman, speaks from the city, mocking them. Xiao Chen and the Little Defiant Dragon do not hide their presence; they openly challenge her. When the Saintess uses Mo Xiaowang's corpse as a puppet to demonstrate dragon combat arts, she enrages the Little Defiant Dragon. Xiao Chen activates three Heavenly Marks and the formation diagram, preparing for a decisive battle.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'True Wood Saintess' (真木女圣) is an archetype in xianxia: a female cultivator at the apex of power, often associated with the Wood element, and on the verge of a major breakthrough—the 'final step' from Half-Ancestor to Stone Man or Ancestral God. Her ability to puppet the dead bodies of powerful beings like the old farmer and Mo Xiaowang reflects a common Daoist necromantic trope, where controlling a corpse is the ultimate insult and a display of dominance. Babula's spider-like limbs (eight spears and a scorpion tail) evoke the image of the Otherworld race of spider/scorpion ancestral gods, tying to the earlier arc where he fought Xiao Chen in the Corpse River. The 'Ancestral Dragon Leather Scroll' is a reference to the ancient maps and secrets Xiao Chen has been collecting—a recurring artifact that holds the key to understanding the full scope of the war between worlds. The exchange of the Primordial Demon City for soul-source highlights a key xianxia concept: tangible divine treasures as collateral for intangible power, a testament to the value of both.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a high-stakes assassination attempt. Readers should pay close attention to the details of Babula's proposal and the terms of the exchange—the half soul-source is Xiao Chen's tool to control the Otherworld Dao imprint, which he will later use to infiltrate the Otherworld's core. The scene with the floating demon city and the Saintess's puppetry of the old farmer and Mo Xiaowang recasts the emotional stakes: these were allies and heroes from earlier arcs, and their desecration fuels Xiao Chen's fury. The upcoming confrontation with the True Wood Saintess will test whether Xiao Chen's gambit—borrowing power from a treacherous ally—pays off. Also watch for the Nine-Headed Snake and the Inverse Dragon King to play key roles in the battle. The mention of Meng Degang (Peerless King) and other powerful ancestral gods hints that reinforcements may arrive, complicating the plan.
