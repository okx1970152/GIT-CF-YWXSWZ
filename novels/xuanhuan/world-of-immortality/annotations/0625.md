## Chapter Overview
This chapter pivots from the desperate flight of the Little Stone Emperor's tomb to a catastrophic descent that swallows all who pursued it. The tomb, a self-aware sepulcher of a legendary supreme being, evades attacks from multiple Celestial Realm giants and Stone Kings through cunning movement and ancient defensive arrays. The intervention of the ancient Shi Corpse with black demon blood and the partial success of the mysterious Shan Jun, who uses the deceased emperor's own hair to fish out a broken stone knife, only delay the inevitable. The alliance of five Stone Kings finally breaches the tomb's defenses, but their victory is short-lived as the entire ancient land collapses into a bottomless black hole, dragging everyone—including Xiao Chen and his companions—into the unknown depths. The tone is one of mounting tension and awe, blending the epic scale of powerhouses clashing with the eerie mystery of the tomb's final gambit.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with two Celestial Realm giants attacking the tomb, only to have their strikes neutralized by seven black demon stars that protect the tomb. The tomb then vanishes, moving underground. Several Stone Kings attempt to intercept it, including one who uses a third eye to try to open a sacred portal, but the tomb's killing formation shatters it. The ancient Shi Corpse emerges, throwing black demon blood from a fallen ancient king to immobilize the tomb; yet a hand emerges from the tomb to burn away the blood. Shan Jun appears, wielding a strand of hair that once belonged to the Little Stone Emperor—this hair enters the tomb unopposed and retrieves a broken stone knife, but when he tries again, the hair catches fire. Five of the strongest Stone Kings then join forces, using a five-element formation to seal the tomb and break in. However, as they and many others rush inside, the entire region collapses into a massive black hole, sucking everyone down. Xiao Chen, Keke, the Stone Crow, the old skull, and others are pulled into the tomb, which now feels like an endless world. The old Daoist remains suspiciously calm, arousing the Stone Crow's suspicion.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Little Stone Emperor (小石皇) is a legendary figure in the World of Immortality, a supreme being whose power was so immense that even his tomb can resist attacks from multiple Stone Kings. Stone Kings (石人王) are beings who have cultivated the Stone Man path, a difficult and perilous road toward petrification and ultimate power. The black demon blood used by Shi Corpse is a taboo substance—the blood of a fallen ancient king, said to be able to corrupt and break even the strongest divine formations. The concept of using a deceased cultivator's own body parts (here, hair) to bypass defenses is a recurring theme in xianxia, as such items retain the original owner's aura and can bypass formations keyed to that aura. The collapse of the ancient land into a black hole and the tomb becoming a small world inside reflects the idea that supreme beings often create pocket dimensions as tombs or training grounds.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major turning point: Xiao Chen and many other powerful beings are now trapped inside the Little Stone Emperor's tomb. The fate of the five Stone Kings who first entered is unknown, as is the nature of the tomb's interior. Watch for the old Daoist, whose unnatural calm suggests he may have a connection to the Little Stone Emperor himself—possibly as a descendant or reincarnation. Xiao Chen's main concern is to protect Keke and avoid exposing his own treasures (the Divine Diagram, ancient scroll) to the other powerhouses inside. The tomb is described as a world unto itself, hinting at hidden dangers and possibly the tomb owner's legacy. This arc may reveal more about the Little Stone Emperor's past and his relationship with the Stone Emperor and other ancient figures. The alliance of the five Stone Kings may fracture now that they are trapped, leading to conflict.
