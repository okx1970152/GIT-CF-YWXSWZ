## Chapter Overview

In this taut, action-driven chapter, Xiao Chen steps directly into the trap laid by Hai Yunxue and her allies. The peaceful setting of the Bright Moon Garden transforms into a slaughterhouse as Xiao Chen, accompanied by Keke, confronts the accusations leveled against him. The chapter crackles with tension, blending political slander with brutal, decisive combat. Xiao Chen’s cold, unflinching resolve to cut down those who stand against him is on full display, as is the growing web of half-ancestor manipulation. The chapter ends on a chilling note: the emergence of the Tongtian Death Bridge and three War Swords, signaling that the powers behind the scenes have finally moved to ensnare him.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen overhears Hai Yunxue publicly denouncing him as the 'enemy of all under heaven,' a global threat who must be eliminated. Instead of retreating, he confronts her directly, asserting that half-ancestors who oppress the masses deserve no reverence. The first challenge comes from a female cultivator, Shuifuyun, who unleashes a swarm of venomous golden centipedes; Xiao Chen annihilates them with a single 'Weng' syllable technique and kills her with the Hand of the Supreme Being. Next, Ye Tian attacks with his Golden Bell, but Xiao Chen shatters it with a single blow, though Ye Tian escapes via the regenerating bell. Xiao Chen then slaughters dozens of Hai Yunxue’s assembled forces—Tiger family experts, Sun Knights, and disciples of the Three Baby Grandmothers—using the War Sword and Keke’s Paradise Lost. The bloodshed triggers the emergence of the Tongtian Death Bridge from beneath the garden, its three War Swords rising to seal the space. The bridge’s appearance confirms that half-ancestors are actively orchestrating Xiao Chen’s doom.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Tongtian Death Bridge (通天死桥) is a legendary structure from Chinese mythology, here depicted as an ancient stone bridge connected to the underworld and capable of trapping supreme beings. Its appearance alongside War Swords reinforces the novel’s theme of cosmic conspiracy. The Hand of the Supreme Being (上苍之手) is a pure martial technique, emphasizing Xiao Chen’s cultivation path as a martial artist who rejects reliance on divine abilities. The 'Weng' word technique echoes Buddhist mantra practices, where specific syllables can resonate with the soul and cause destruction. The Golden Bell (皇天神钟) is a classic defensive artifact in xianxia literature, often portrayed as indestructible; its regeneration here subverts that expectation. Hai Yunxue’s manipulation of public opinion through a garden party reflects the political intrigue common in cultivation worlds where strength and reputation are intertwined.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen moves from being on the defensive to actively striking at his enemies' power bases. However, the appearance of the Tongtian Death Bridge suggests that the half-ancestors are coordinating a far-reaching trap. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will escape this spatial seal—will his allies, such as Liu Mu’s spatial coordinate, or Keke’s Paradise Lost, provide an out? The mention of the Three Baby Grandmothers (三婴太君) hints at a new enemy faction. Ye Tian’s escape with the Golden Bell signals that he will return as a recurring rival. Hai Yunxue’s cold, calculating demeanor and her willingness to sacrifice pawns show that she is a dangerous strategist, not merely a scorned woman. This chapter sets the stage for a larger confrontation with the half-ancestors and the true purpose of the War Swords.
