## Chapter Overview
Chapter 674, Nine Emperor Projections Invade, delivers a crushing blow to the hopes of the Nine Provinces. Nine Imperial Sovereign projections descend, their mere presence threatening to collapse worlds. They effortlessly annihilate the Spirit Demon Saint Shadow, a half-ancestor who dared to oppose them, demonstrating the absolute gulf between mere kings and true emperors. Then they turn their attention to the ancient lamps—the sealed tombs of the legendary Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors. Shattering eight of the nine lamps, they expose the enduring but weakened souls of those primordial heroes. The Sovereigns and Emperors, merged with their lamp-tombs, rise to fight but are clearly outmatched. Before the final blow can fall, a hidden emperor, the Void Emperor, emerges from the Death World, having crippled his own cultivation and hidden for eons. Three more ancient emperors, who sealed themselves in chaotic void regions, also appear. The four offer to ally, turning the contest for the Initial Sacred Land into a struggle among true emperors. Xiao Chen, watching from Paradise Lost, can only despair as his side possesses no emperor-level power. The tragic, desolate tone is one of overwhelming odds and fading hope.

## Key Plot Points
The nine Saintly Shadows, born from the Sole True Realm as innate deities, first destroy the Spirit Demon Saint Shadow—a half-ancestor who had slain multiple Stone Man Kings—with a single combined strike. They then focus on the mysterious path guarded by the ancient lamps. Each lamp is revealed to be the tomb of a Sovereign or Emperor: Suiren, Fuxi, Shennong, Yellow Emperor, Zhuanxu, Diku, Yao, Shun. Pangu King holds a ninth. The nine Shadows shatter eight lamps; the Sovereigns' undying souls rise, merging with the lamp-fire. But the Shadows declare them too weak to resist and prepare to refine their souls. They claim the Sovereigns and Emperors had schemed across ages to open the Initial Sacred Land, but all their plans are now fruitless. Then the Void Emperor, a being of pure illusion who had hidden near the eighth Heavenly Stele, reveals himself. He deliberately broke his own emperor cultivation and rebuilt it in secrecy to avoid entering the Sole True Realm, waiting for this moment. Three more hidden emperors, who sealed themselves in the chaotic void, also appear. They had survived the Age of Emperors by extreme self-isolation. The four agree to ally: they will cooperate against the nine Saintly Shadows to seize control of the Initial Sacred Land and dominate the Sole True Realm. However, their alliance is brittle and based on mutual convenience. The Saintly Shadows, though surprised, remain confident. Xiao Chen sees no hope: his side lacks any emperor, and the struggle has now escalated beyond half-ancestors. The chapter ends with his bitter contemplation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces several key cultivation concepts. The Sole True Realm (唯一真界) is the ultimate reality beyond the layered illusory worlds; only emperors (皇者) can reside there. The Initial Sacred Land (初始圣地) is the gateway. The ancient lamps are not mere treasures; they are the sealed tombs of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, who transformed their bodies and souls into these vessels to preserve their existence across eons. The distinction between 'innate' (先天) and 'acquired' (后天) cultivators is central: the nine Saintly Shadows claim to be innate deities born directly from the Sole True Realm, while the Sovereigns and Emperors are acquired beings who rose through cultivation. The Void Emperor's method—crippling his own cultivation to remain in the illusory worlds—is a rare survival tactic, showing that emperor-level beings cannot normally exist outside the Sole True Realm for long. The four hidden emperors represent those who avoided the final war. Their appearance upends the power balance, as emperor-level combat can destroy worlds. The concept of 'emperor' here is the pinnacle, above Stone Man Kings and half-ancestors.

## Reading Guide
The sudden emergence of four hidden emperors transforms the battlefield. Readers should watch how the alliance between the Void Emperor and the three others holds—their goals conflict, and betrayal seems inevitable. The nine Saintly Shadows are only projections; their true bodies remain in the Sole True Realm, indicating even greater power. Xiao Chen, currently lacking emperor-level strength, must find a way to ascend or forge alliances. The destruction of the lamp-tombs and the scattering of the Sovereigns' souls may provide an opportunity: the five imprints within Xiao Chen (from the Five Ancestral Gods) might resonate with the remaining fragments. The Initial Sacred Land is now contested by multiple emperor factions; Xiao Chen's window to act is narrow. The coming chapters will likely focus on his desperate attempt to reach the emperor realm before the four hidden emperors consolidate or the nine Saintly Shadows summon their true bodies. The tragic tone suggests heavy sacrifices ahead.
