## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges Xiao Chen into the heart of the overseas territories dominated by the Hundred Races. Seeking traces of the Yasha King, he instead discovers Skeleton Island, a deathly realm eerily reminiscent of the World of the Dead. Dressed in his yellow-diamond bone body, he infiltrates the island and encounters a seemingly crippled old skeleton of immense age and hidden power. The chapter masterfully blends tense infiltration with sudden explosive conflict, culminating in a shocking revelation about the old skeleton's former status and a catastrophic aftermath that ripples across the entire overseas domain. The tone is one of grim discovery and escalating dread, as Xiao Chen inadvertently triggers a continental-scale upheaval.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, having advanced to the yellow-diamond rank, ventures into the perilous overseas islands now controlled by the Hundred Races. He bypasses numerous vibrant island territories before arriving at Skeleton Island, a barren, death-enshrouded land reminiscent of the death world. There, he witnesses a yellow-diamond skeleton receiving instruction in soul cultivation techniques from an unremarkable white-boned skeleton – a being that exudes an ancient, fossil-like presence. The old skeleton, admitting it is crippled, still possesses terrifying power. It detects Xiao Chen and unleashes a devastating soul attack that nearly destroys the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, a half-ancestor-level artifact. Xiao Chen survives and retaliates, killing the old skeleton, whose bones prove indestructible even in death. The old skeleton's final words hint at a path from the death world's depths to the Primordial Celestial Realm, a lost opportunity. Upon leaving, Xiao Chen witnesses an unprecedented uproar among the foreign races: kings such as the Yasha King, Skeleton King, and War King abandon the Nine Provinces to rush back overseas. Xiao Chen realizes he may have killed a crippled king-level being, triggering a 'great earthquake' among the Hundred Races. He decides to exploit this chaos further.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces the hierarchy of skeletal beings in the death world and its overseas extensions. The 'bone body' ranks – white, blue, purple, yellow, and diamond – reflect the evolutionary path of fire-seed creatures. Yellow-diamond is a high tier, placing Xiao Chen among the regional powers. The old skeleton's claim to have once been a 'king' suggests a level beyond ordinary skeletons, likely equivalent to a half-ancestor or higher. His indestructible bones align with the 'king' classification. The 'Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree' is a legendary Buddhist artifact; its near-destruction here underscores the ancient being's residual might. The mention of a 'Primordial Celestial Realm' above the four worlds adds depth to the cosmology, tying the death world's deepest regions to the highest realms. The old skeleton's connection to the 'soul world' links the Hundred Races back to the death world's origins.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point for Xiao Chen in the overseas campaign. Accidental as it seems, the death of a deposed king radically shifts the balance of power among the Hundred Races, forcing many kings to withdraw from the Nine Provinces. Readers should watch for how this 'earthquake' affects Xiao Chen's plans and the ongoing conflicts in the mainland. The old skeleton's cryptic mention of a path from the death world to the Primordial Celestial Realm will likely become a major plot thread. Additionally, the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree's damage raises questions about its future viability. Most importantly, Xiao Chen's realization that he can potentially assassinate crippled but high-ranking beings opens a new strategy: deliberately hunting such 'kings' to cause further disruption and buy time for his allies.
