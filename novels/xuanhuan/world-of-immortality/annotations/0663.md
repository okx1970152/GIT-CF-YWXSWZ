## Chapter Overview

Chapter 663 is a monumental chapter in the *World of Immortality* saga, thrusting Xiao Chen into a crucible of unimaginable duration and intensity. For thousands of years, he sits before the Seventh Heavenly Stele in the Curse World, his mind and soul battered by the stele's awesome power. This is not a battle of fists but of spirit and endurance. The chapter masterfully conveys the agony of prolonged mental refinement, the sheer weight of time, and the ultimate crystallization of his will. Yet even as he emerges transformed, embracing a unique hybrid path between the Stone Man and Upper Ancestral God roads, a far greater threat looms. The dread entity of the First Ancient City, from the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, reaches across worlds to seize the stele itself, dragging it into the Otherworld's heart. The chapter crescendos with Xiao Chen trapped inside the stele, engulfed by demonic flames, but a spark of defiant hope remains—he intends to borrow that very fire for his own rebirth.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's cultivation at the Seventh Heavenly Stele spans tens of thousands of years, a period of intense trial. The stele's fierce divine consciousness repeatedly assaults his own, nearly destroying him, yet each iteration tempers his divine sense to a level rivaling Stone Kings—a core achievement of the Upper Ancestral God path. He achieves true comprehension (通透) of the stele's mysteries, understanding that the Seventh Stele is the synthesis of all previous six. After this enlightenment, he faces a critical choice: accept the Stone Man path's complete petrification, or forge his own way. He rejects full petrification, instead allowing a meter-thick stone shell to encase his living flesh, forming a miniature divine city around himself. This hybrid citadel, neither a true King's Divine City nor a Primordial Demon City, is a testament to his individuality. The peace is shattered when the entity within the First Ancient City—a colossal, ancient power that has survived since primordial times—reaches across the dimensional barriers. It seizes the Seventh Heavenly Stele, uproots it from the Curse World, and draws it to the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. Demonic divine flames envelop the stele as the entity attempts to refine it, discovering the stone shell within. Meanwhile, Otherworld Ancestral Gods Lu Zhan and Lang Ya observe from a distance, acknowledging the ancient city's power. Xiao Chen, semi-conscious within his stone shelter, whispers a declaration: he will use this divine fire to fuel his own ultimate ascension.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter is steeped in xianxia cultivation philosophy. The Seventh Heavenly Stele represents the culmination of the first six steles, acting as the 'final formula' that unlocks the deepest secrets of the Heavenly Stele Profound Art. Xiao Chen's process of achieving 'true comprehension' (通透) echoes the Daoist concept of sudden enlightenment after prolonged hardship. The 'stone shell' he constructs is a brilliant narrative twist on the classic Stone Man path: rather than letting petrification consume his flesh, he externalizes it as armor, preserving his organic vitality. This hybrid approach mirrors the 'immortal body' concept where the physical form can be both imperishable and alive. The First Ancient City (第一古都) is a remnant of a previous cosmic cycle, its dweller a 'living fossil' that has survived by anchoring its essence within a divine city. The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps are a liminal space between worlds, often guarded by supreme beings. The entity's method of refining a Heavenly Stele through divine flames is an extreme form of 'refining by fire,' a common motif in xianxia where external forces are used to forge inner strength. Lu Zhan and Lang Ya's observing presence reminds us that the Otherworld's highest powers are still active, viewing this ancient entity as both a tool and a potential rival.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen has been thrust into the lion's den: the First Ancient City is the seat of one of the oldest and most terrifying powers in the Otherworld hierarchy. His stone shell is now suspended in a sea of refining flame, but rather than despair, he sees opportunity. The chapter sets up a classic reversal: the enemy's attempt to destroy him may become the very catalyst for his final breakthrough. Readers should watch for Xiao Chen's next move—will he truly absorb the ancient entity's power? The reactions of Lu Zhan and Lang Ya also hint at deeper political currents among the Otherworld's ancient rulers. They seem content to let the city's entity act as a 'blade,' suggesting they have their own plans. This chapter marks a turning point where Xiao Chen moves from passive cultivation to an active confrontation with a primordial foe. The stage is set for a battle that could reshape the cosmos.
