## Chapter Overview

This chapter blends the lighthearted domestic chaos of Keke’s latest raid with a profound cultivation breakthrough. The white beast returns from the Southern Wastes Spirit Hall laden with a grotesque sack of treasures, plunging Xiao Chen and Fatty Zhuge into a crisis of ethics and practicality. The tension is deftly balanced by Keke’s unabashed glee, forcing Xiao Chen to negotiate a compromise that satisfies the little beast’s greed while averting open conflict with a powerful merchant guild. Amidst this comedy, a rare Nine-Grade Lotus Platform yields four jade seeds that fuel Xiao Chen’s next acupoint divinization and heal the Little Defiant Dragon’s deep wounds. The chapter ends with a quiet but charged visit to the Undying Sect, where old grudges with Yan Qingcheng simmer beneath polite greetings.

## Key Plot Points

Keke drags a bag larger than itself packed with blinding spirit herbs into Xiao Chen’s house. Fatty Zhuge immediately recognizes the haul as a complete looting of the ‘First Grade Hall’ of the Southern Wastes Spirit Hall, a theft that would trigger massive repercussions. Xiao Chen insists on returning everything, but Keke stubbornly guards its treasure. After lengthy coaxing and promises of future compensation, Keke reluctantly returns the main sack—but keeps the Nine-Grade Lotus Platform and a small bundle of choice delicacies. It then disappears for half an hour and returns with a full belly, having eaten its fill at the very store it robbed.

The Lotus Platform, a naturally formed divine material linked to Buddhist lore, is cracked open. Four emerald-like seeds emit fragrance and pure spiritual energy. Xiao Chen distributes one each to Keke, the Little Defiant Dragon, and the Azure Dragon King, consuming the fourth himself. The seed transmutes into a torrent of life essence that divinely transforms his ‘Central Pivot’ acupoint on the spine, momentarily connecting multiple divinated points with golden lines. The crushed lotus shell is used to dress the Little Defiant Dragon’s wounds, accelerating its healing. The Azure Dragon King, recovering from its corruption, accepts the seed silently. Evening reports from the Beast-Fighting Palace confirm two beast kings advanced in the tournament.

Xiao Chen then visits the Undying Sect’s sprawling gardens. There he meets Yan Qingcheng, still bitter but bound by the Demon-Seed Spirit Art to remain in the same city. Their exchange is a barbed dance of pride and necessity—Xiao Chen’s lazy provocations clash with Yan Qingcheng’s simmering fury, ending with her threatening to call for help as he teases her with talk of “taking her home.”

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nine-Grade Lotus Platform is a direct nod to the Buddhist lotus throne, a sacred artifact of immense spiritual power. In this world, it is a naturally formed treasure of jade-like lotus, containing four concentrated seeds that can elevate a cultivator’s physique and unlock acupoints. The description of the lotus platform’s origin—a mountain lake atop a peak with Buddhist relics—links it to ancient divine inheritance typical of Xianxia world-building: mundane locations hide imprints of past gods.

The acupoint divinization Xiao Chen undergoes continues his unique path, skipping standard realm progression. The ‘Central Pivot’ acupoint on the spine is a major energy hub; its activation reinforces his emerging ‘golden line’ network, drawing him closer to a simulated domain. This approach, using external treasures to saturate acupoints, contradicts orthodox cultivation teaching, but Xiao Chen persists in his heretical method.

The Undying Sect’s garden-like grounds reflect the vast wealth and influence of major sects in Heavenly Emperor City. The tension between Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng stems from her forced cultivation of the ‘Shattering Demon, Planting God’ art, which ties her life-force to his, a precarious interdependence that leaves her proud but vulnerable.

## Reading Guide

This chapter delivers a breather after intense battles, but the seeds of future conflict are sown. Keke’s theft may not be fully forgiven by the Spirit Hall’s patrons, and Xiao Chen’s public association with the ‘First Grade Hall’ incident puts him further in the spotlight. The healing of the Little Defiant Dragon suggests it will soon be ready for the beast tournament, while the Azure Dragon King’s recovery hints at deeper loyalties to Xiao Chen’s group. Yan Qingcheng’s reluctant dependence on Xiao Chen will continue to evolve: will she find a way to break the technique, or will their forced alliance shift into genuine partnership? Keep an eye on the approaching beast tournament, as the gathered young experts—including the dangerous Qilao and the ever-watchful Dugu Jianmo—are preparing their moves.
