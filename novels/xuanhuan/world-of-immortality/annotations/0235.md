## Chapter Overview
This chapter opens on a note of farewell, as the young elites who gathered in Heavenly Emperor City prepare to scatter across the Eternal Continent. The mood is bittersweet, deepened by the ever-present dangers of the cultivation world. Yet before they can depart, the Third Princess of Great Shang, Ying Ying, demonstrates her political mastery by inviting the entire group to her lakeside pavilion. Through carefully chosen gifts and information—from news of the legendary Lan Nuo to Zhang Sanfeng's sword-carved stones—she binds each visitor to her favor. The chapter closes with a dark edge: Xiao Chen receives a death threat from the Tiger Clan, and his defiant response sets the stage for a clash that could shake Yindu.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter begins as Xiao Chen, Dugu Jianmo, Yan Qingcheng, and their companions prepare to part ways in Heavenly Emperor City. Outside the private room, they are intercepted by the city's noble scions, including Bo Shi, Chen Hangjing, and Yin Feng, all eager to curry favor. Prince Chu Xingkuang and Yan Lingkong also appear—the former composed despite his earlier defeat. The Third Princess's deputy commander Yu Tong arrives bearing an invitation, and after revealing that the princess has already defused the Tiger Clan's 'strong tiger' threat, Xiao Chen agrees to attend. At the princess's villa, Ying Ying deploys her arsenal of enticements: news of Lan Nuo for Qilao, a rare Beast Soul for Yuwen Feng, the Fire Cloud Divine Pearl for Huo Niao, youth-preserving herbs for A Bing, a complete water-splitting technique for A Shui, and the legendary Zhang Sanfeng sword intent for Dugu Jianmo. She offers Xiao Chen nothing but her goodwill, having already aided him. Xiao Chen understands that such generosity comes with invisible bonds, but he accepts the debt. Later, as he leaves the princess's estate, a messenger delivers a death threat from the Tiger Clan, demanding he leave Yindu or die. Xiao Chen laughs coldly and declares he will behead the 'strong tiger' within ten days—inside the city itself.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The term 'strong tiger' (壮老虎) is a colloquial epithet for a powerful expert from the Tiger Clan, a faction feared in the Eastern Continent. The Third Princess's strategy exemplifies 'political cultivation'—using favors and connections instead of force. Her mention of Zhang Sanfeng, the legendary Daoist immortal, ties the world to real Chinese mythology. The 'sword intent' left on stone by Zhang Sanfeng is a classic xianxia trope: a master's will carved into matter for future generations to comprehend. The 'Fire Cloud Divine Pearl' and 'Water-Splitting Divine Technique' are typical xianxia treasures and skills. Additionally, the concept of 'refining the heart through worldly experience' (入世炼心) is a common cultivation philosophy—practitioners immerse themselves in mortal society to temper their spirit. The Great Shang Empire is one of the five hegemonic states, holding sixteen percent of the Eternal Continent's territory, showcasing the scale of this world.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's bold declaration sets up a direct confrontation with the Tiger Clan, a powerful force in Yindu. Readers should watch for the 'strong tiger'—a demigod-level expert—and whether Xiao Chen can truly behead him within ten days. The Third Princess's network of favored experts may become crucial in future conflicts. Meanwhile, Dugu Jianmo is now bound to the princess by the sword-carved stones; will his pursuit of the sword interfere with his friendship with Xiao Chen? Yan Qingcheng remains in Yindu, her feelings toward Xiao Chen still ambiguous. The chapter also hints at Lan Nuo's whereabouts—a major narrative hook. Finally, the threat of the Tiger Clan may force Xiao Chen to reveal more of his Spirit-Treasury realm abilities, including his newly mastered 'Smelting Weapons into the Body' divine art.
