## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the long-anticipated conclusion of the Little Stone Emperor (Xiao Shi Huang), a legendary figure who chose to burn brightly for a single instant rather than fade into obscurity. His death is not a defeat but a transcendent peak, illuminating the ages. In the aftermath, eight Stone-Man Kings erupt into a savage free-for-all over the supreme artifact he left behind: the Three Sovereign Mirror. Amid the chaos, Xiao Chen and Keke engage in a desperate scavenge of the seven great tombs, searching for the ninth Heavenly Stele and collecting whatever treasures they can. The chapter masterfully juxtaposes solemn tragedy with frenzied avarice, and closes with the terrifying Stone Corpse swallowing Xiao Chen and Keke, only for Xiao Chen to threaten him with the indestructible Heavenly Stele from within.

## Key Plot Points

- Xiao Shi Huang, the young stone emperor, ascends in a blaze of glory, leaving behind his seven innate demon stars which sink into the seven tombs. His body crumbles to ash, and a ghostly vision of a rough hand leading a small figure fades into the sunset.
- Lu Zhan and the Stone Crow immediately break the mournful silence, charging after that ethereal hand and figure, while others like Xuan Kong Old Ancestor and the Three-Eyed Stone-Man King lunge for the Three Sovereign Mirror.
- The Old Turtle skull withdraws from the mirror, having paid respects to Xiao Shi Huang, and avoids the coming storm.
- Xiao Chen and Keke frantically search the tombs for the ninth Heavenly Stele, but find no trace. Keke instead begins looting every object in sight, from stone beds to incense burners, collecting them into Paradise Lost.
- The Old Turtle skull reveals that a clump of ordinary-looking grass is actually Life-Reviving Grass, cultivated with Stone-Man King blood, prompting Keke to dig up everything.
- Xiao Chen realizes he has two of the seven stone weapons from the Five Kings of the Chaotic Land (now in his ancient scroll) and seeks the others. He also desires the seven demon stars, but they remain hidden.
- The five kings who fought over the illusionary imprint of Xiao Shi Huang return empty-handed, and the real battle for the Three Sovereign Mirror intensifies among all eight Stone-Man Kings.
- Lu Zhan and the Lishi Beast fight fiercely; the Stone Crow tears out the Three-Eyed King’s third eye; Shan Jun and Xuan Kong Old Ancestor exchange heavy blows.
- The Stone Corpse and another king bypass the central battle and corner Xiao Chen and Keke in a tomb. Keke tries to trap him with her nine-colored jade bowl, but he swallows both of them.
- Xiao Chen, protected by the ancient scroll and divine diagram, holds up the genuine Heavenly Stele inside the Stone Corpse’s belly, threatening to burst him open.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The Little Stone Emperor’s philosophy of pursuing the “one that escapes” (the missing fifty-first part of the Great Derivation) is rooted in the I Ching: “The Great Derivation number is fifty, its application is forty-nine.” Xiao Shi Huang chose to chase the one that flees, seeking perfection, and ultimately achieved a transcendent death.
- The Three Sovereign Mirror is the third most powerful war treasure in the world, after the Stone King puppet and the Heaven-Overturning Seal? Its value draws all eight kings into a bloodbath.
- The demon stars (seven innate stars) of Xiao Shi Huang return to the seven tombs. Such innate spiritual objects are extremely rare and signify a being born with a cosmic destiny.
- Life-Reviving Grass (回命草) is a peak healing herb that requires Stone-Man King blood to cultivate. It can regenerate a shattered body and stabilize a near-destroyed soul, making it nearly invaluable.
- The Five Kings of the Chaotic Land are ancient supreme beings now bound within the ancient scroll. Their presence strengthens the scroll, and Xiao Chen’s possession of two stone weapons (given to them by Xiao Shi Huang) further enhances his battle treasure.

## Reading Guide

- Pay close attention to the aftermath of Xiao Shi Huang’s death: the rough hand and small figure may hint at a reincarnation or a hidden continuation of his legacy.
- The eight Stone-Man Kings are now locked in a bloody contest for the Three Sovereign Mirror. With each wound they inflict, the balance of power shifts, and Xiao Chen may yet exploit the chaos to seize the mirror or find the missing ninth Heavenly Stele.
- Xiao Chen’s threat to use the Heavenly Stele from inside the Stone Corpse is a high-risk gambit. If he succeeds, he could gain the Stone Corpse’s resources or even turn him into an unwilling ally.
- The seventh demon stars remain unaccounted for. They may resurface later as a key to unlocking Xiao Shi Huang’s true inheritance.
- Keke’s indiscriminate looting may have netted some cursed or sealed items. Keep an eye on the Paradise Lost for unexpected consequences.
