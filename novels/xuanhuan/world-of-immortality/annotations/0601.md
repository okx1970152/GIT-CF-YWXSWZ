## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a significant return to the Death World as Xiao Chen, now at the Half-Ancestor Ninth Heaven, confronts an old enemy—the Grand Dread Nether King Ruixiao. The discovery of hundreds of Nine Provinces cultivators' skeletons aboard the broken Ancestral Lord Warship sets a somber tone, revealing the scale of past tragedies. Xiao Chen's effortless dominance over Ruixiao, who has ascended to Ancestral Lord Seventh Heaven, showcases his tremendous growth. However, the chapter takes a mysterious turn when a prophecy vanishes and an unseen presence makes itself known, while Tianwaitian's fading soul warns that Keke is in grave danger, shifting the focus to an urgent rescue mission.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and his companions examining the ruined Ancestral Lord Warship, finding hundreds of skeletal remains of Nine Provinces cultivators who died from battle wounds inflicted by supreme beings. This grim discovery fuels Xiao Chen's resolve. They then enter the Death Heavenly Palace, where they confront Ruixiao, the former Grand Dread Nether King who had tormented them in the past. Ruixiao has evolved to Ancestral Lord Seventh Heaven, but Xiao Chen, now at Half-Ancestor Ninth Heaven, easily defeats him with contemptuous ease, reversing their former power dynamic. Xiao Chen interrogates Ruixiao about the fates of Tianwaitian, Renwairen, and Yingxiong, learning that Ruixiao nearly absorbed them but left their souls intact in an attempt to change his prophesied death. Xiao Chen extracts their remnant soul sparks. He then attempts to read the prophecy from Ruixiao's memory, but it mysteriously vanishes, erased by an invisible force. Xiao Chen senses a terrifying presence nearby. Tianwaitian's weakened soul warns him that Keke is in danger, adding urgency. The chapter ends with an oppressive, chilling atmosphere as an unseen entity lurks.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of a 'prophecy book' (预言簿册) that records one's fate is a common xianxia trope, often tied to karma and the struggle against destiny. Ruixiao's attempt to change his fate by not fully devouring his victims reflects the central theme of defying heaven's will. The 'Death Heavenly Palace' (死亡天宫) is a typical xianxia location—a sealed realm of a powerful being, filled with traps and remnants of past battles. The power structure is clear: Half-Ancestor and Ancestral Lord are tiers above ordinary Ancestral Gods, with Xiao Chen now matching the highest levels. The vanishment of the prophecy at a crucial moment introduces a mysterious, almost cosmic opponent capable of manipulating fate and memory.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major confrontation with an unknown, terrifying entity that can manipulate prophecies and minds. The revelation that Keke is in danger will likely drive the next arc. Readers should note the ominous presence that erased the prophecy—this may be a new supreme antagonist or a previously hinted cosmic force. Xiao Chen's power is now at a peak, but the mystery suggests he is not yet at the top. The fate of Tianwaitian and the others hangs in the balance. The chapter also reinforces the theme of defying fate, as even a prophecy can be altered. Look for foreshadowing of the entity's identity in future chapters.
