## Chapter Overview

This chapter takes us deep into the Celestial Realm, where Xiao Chen resumes his shadowy reconnaissance of the Otherworld faction. The narrative unfolds with a tense atmosphere of political maneuvering among Stone Man Kings, revealing a conspiracy that stretches back to the primordial age of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors. The key revelation is that the Purple Cloud Stone King Zixiao Wang, long thought dead, is alive and identical to the being known as Hongjun from the Death World. This discovery reshapes the understanding of ancient powers and their secret agendas. The chapter then shifts abruptly into high-octane action as Xiao Chen, allied with Renmo Ge Qian and Shi Zhongdi, launches a precision ambush on a young Otherworld prodigy who carries two precious Heavenly Marks. The ambush is a calculated risk against overwhelming numbers, and the successful theft of the Heavenly Marks sets the stage for a dangerous pursuit by multiple Stone Man Kings.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen observing the Otherworld faction's mountain stronghold. He learns that Lu Zhan and Lang Ya have summoned two Stone Man Kings for a critical discussion: Stone Corpse and a mysterious other. Inside the council, they debate the emergence of an 'ancient enemy of the Five Emperors,' a primordial being now dwelling behind the stone gate at the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. This entity is hostile to the Five Emperors' lineage and has survived through eons by being exiled to the void. Lu Zhan brings a young prodigy who has been personally groomed by this ancient being and infused with two Heavenly Marks, intended to serve as a vessel for the enemy's will. The council then visits the hidden Zixiao Wang, who is revealed to be the same entity as the Death World's Hongjun. Zixiao Wang admits that he has been secretly refining a single-layer Five Emperor Tower beneath his palace. Xiao Chen, with the help of Renmo Ge Qian (using the ancient lamp) and Shi Zhongdi (using the 'Supreme Tortoise Fist'), seizes the opportunity to ambush the young prodigy outside Zixiao Palace. The ambush is swift: the Five Emperor Tower crushes the prodigy, and Xiao Chen captures the two Heavenly Marks before the Stone Man Kings can react. The ensuing chaos allows the group to escape, though they are now hunted by Lu Zhan, Lang Ya, and Stone Corpse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter heavily references the legendary 'Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors' (三皇五帝) from Chinese mythology, treating them as historical beings of immense power who once ruled the myriad worlds. Their 'ancient enemy' is a classic trope: a primordial evil sealed away or exiled, now returning to threaten the present order. The Heavenly Marks (天痕) are imprints left by the Heavenly Steles, which are themselves monumental artifacts tied to the world's creation and laws. Collecting these marks allows a cultivator to approach the ultimate Dao. The Five Emperor Tower (五帝塔) is a supreme artifact, here used both as a defensive city and as an offensive weapon, demonstrating its versatility. The ambush scene showcases the importance of timing and tactical alliance, common in xianxia warfare, where even supreme beings can be caught off-guard by a perfectly coordinated strike. The 'Supreme Tortoise Fist' (至尊王八拳) is a humorous yet powerful technique, blending mythic gravitas with a touch of irreverent humor.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a major lore dump and a setup for future conflicts. Pay close attention to the political dynamics among the Stone Man Kings—not all are loyal to Lu Zhan's faction, and Zixiao Wang's hidden agenda may prove significant later. The ancient enemy of the Five Emperors is now a clear antagonist, and Xiao Chen's possession of two Heavenly Marks makes him a primary target. The ambush also deepens the alliance between Xiao Chen, Renmo Ge Qian, and Shi Zhongdi, setting the stage for a united front against the Otherworld. The appearance of the young prodigy suggests that the ancient enemy is actively grooming avatars; more such vessels may appear. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen on the run, but the stolen Heavenly Marks are a crucial resource. Expect pursuit and possibly a confrontation with Zixiao Wang, who may not be willing to let the Five Emperor Tower and the Marks slip away so easily.
