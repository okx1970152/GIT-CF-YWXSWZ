## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen, after countless trials in the Death World, finally begins to control the ancient Dead City. With the aid of the Old Ancestral Dragon, Yan Huang, and the Martial Imprint, he tests the city's power against the Old Farmer wielding the Great Annihilation War Spear. Though the city's might is still below absolute dominance, the discovery that the Dead City yet harbors a dim light of life beneath its core—a broken stone man statue—points to a deeper potential. Returning to the Nine Provinces, Xiao Chen uses his puppet, the Otherworld Ancestral God Chou Tian, to slip past enemy lines and rescue Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect Leader. In doing so, he kills a Ninth Heaven Half-Ancestor with brutal efficiency, signaling that he is ready to intervene directly. The chapter builds to a grand stage: a ten-thousand-year Otherworld assembly, where Xiao Chen intends to shatter the enemy's foundations. The tone is a mixture of calculated menace, desolate power, and the first stirrings of a counteroffensive that will shake the heavens.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, with the support of the Old Ancestral Dragon, Yan Huang, and others, pushes the Dead City to its limits. He causes his own body to petrify by merging with the half stone skull, achieving a degree of unity with the city. The city's performance is tested by suppressing the Old Farmer, and though the pressure nearly kills him, the trial reveals that the Dead City has a hidden vitality—a broken stone man beneath its center, wrapped in a faint radiance. This discovery hints that the city may not be a dead relic but a sealed artifact of immense power.

Returning to the Nine Provinces, Xiao Chen locates Chou Tian, the otherworld ancestral god he had earlier transformed into a puppet. Using this puppet as cover, Xiao Chen enters the pocket realm where Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect Leader are held. Two events reveal his ruthlessness: he orders Chou Tian to kill four of the Ancestral God's descendants, and he himself executes an Eighth Heaven Half-Ancestor by crushing his soul with the imprint of a Heavenly Stele. The rescue is clean, but Xiao Chen's cold demeanor shocks Yan Qingcheng, who must reconcile the youth she once knew with this ancient-style slayer of gods.

The chapter ends with a harbinger: the Otherworld's ten-thousand-year grand meeting is announced. Xiao Chen, knowing his advantage with Chou Tian is temporary, prepares to strike at the heart of the enemy's power. The stage is set for a storm that will engulf the Nine Provinces and the Death World alike.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Dead City (死城) is a recurring trope in xianxia—an ancient, massive structure that can serve as both a fortress and a weapon. In this story, it is a failed creation of Ancestral God Suiren, but still retains terrifying power. The "half stone skull" that Xiao Chen uses to merge with the city is a fragment of the primordial Stone Man, a path of cultivation that leads to kingly or imperial sovereignty. The act of "living sacrifice" (活祭) is a dark ritual where a cultivator's soul and power are refined into another, leaving only a puppet shell—a technique Xiao Chen uses on Chou Tian. The "Heavenly Stele" (天碑) is a recurring symbol of ultimate law and martial truth; Xiao Chen's palm strike forming a heavenly stele imprint shows his mastery of this primordial force.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's control over the Dead City and the puppet Chou Tian is a fragile advantage. The imminent Otherworld grand meeting will likely draw out the three Supreme Ancestral Gods, and Xiao Chen's forces—the Old Dragon, Yan Huang, the Old Farmer—must plan carefully. The broken stone man at the city's core may be a key to unlocking the city's full power, but it could also be a slumbering terror. With Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect Leader rescued, Xiao Chen has removed a distraction, but the war is far from over. Readers should watch for how the ancient weapons (the Ancestral Dragon Heaven Drum, the formation diagram) will be deployed, and whether the Old Farmer's cryptic warnings about a "trap" will come true. The coming chapters promise high-stakes scheming and battles that will decide the fate of the Nine Provinces.
