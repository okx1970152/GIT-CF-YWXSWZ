## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the long-awaited culmination of the great war as Xiao Chen unleashes the combined might of the seven Heavenly Steles. The Nine-Nine Stone Steps shatter under their devastating weight, and the blood altar at their peak is obliterated. The Otherworld elite are thrown into disarray as the ancestral gods of the Nine Provinces—Suiren, Fuxi, and Shennong—return with vast armies of ancient tribes. Simultaneously, a white bone army from the Death World invades, turning the battlefield into a cataclysm of multiple realms. Amid the chaos, Lu Zhan orders the summoning of the legendary 'First Sacred City,' hinting at an even greater escalation to come.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, though severely weakened after controlling the steles, receives support from Shi Zhongdi and gradually recovers. The stone king Xutian attempted a surprise assassination but was repelled by Xiao Chen's stone tower and Shi Zhongdi's intervention. Xiao Chen cleverly used the ancestral stone king imprint to lure enemy cultivators into a trap, then crushed them with the seven steles, annihilating more than a dozen powerful opponents including several stone men and upper ancestral gods. The twin stone kings tried to turn karmic resentment against Xiao Chen, but he inverted their technique, causing them to flee wounded by Ge Qian's timely strikes. The ancestral gods Fuxi, Suiren, and Shennong appear with an enormous army of ancient tribes. Fuxi, riding a fire-red dragon horse, wields the copper eight trigrams and stone Taiji diagram. The Yellow Emperor and the other four emperors also arrive with their forces, including many legendary figures such as Cangjie and Feng Hou. Lu Zhan, enraged, declares that the ultimate weapon will be deployed—the First Sacred City.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The seven Heavenly Steles are primordial artifacts of immeasurable power; using them together is an almost impossible feat that can kill even stone kings. The Nine-Nine Stone Steps represent the path to transcendence and are a focal point of the Otherworld's power. The return of Chinese ancestral gods—Suiren (discoverer of fire), Fuxi (creator of the eight trigrams), Shennong (divine farmer), and the Five Emperors (Huangdi, Zhuanxu, Diku, Yao, Shun)—parallels Chinese foundational mythology. Their appearance with massive armies evokes the epic cycles of classical xianxia where the 'investiture of the gods' style battles unfold. The 'First Sacred City' is a legendary fortress that can suppress the steles; its summoning signals a new phase of the war.

## Reading Guide

The summoning of the First Sacred City raises the stakes: will Xiao Chen's coalition be able to resist this ultimate weapon? The retreat of the twin stone kings may be temporary—they possess hidden reserves. The white bone army's invasion introduces a new faction that could tip the balance. Pay attention to the roles of the returning ancestral gods—each has unique abilities (Fuxi's Bagua, Suiren's fire, Shennong's life power). The next chapters will likely focus on the clash with the Sacred City and the final fate of the Otherworld. Xiao Chen's depleted state may lead to a breakthrough or sacrifice.
