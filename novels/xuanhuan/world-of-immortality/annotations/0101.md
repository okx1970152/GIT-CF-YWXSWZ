## Chapter Overview

After the harrowing ordeal of the Dead City, this chapter offers a moment of serene respite—but only a moment. Xiao Chen achieves a breakthrough to the Seventh Heaven of Mortal-Shedding, and more importantly, awakens a rare precognitive perception, a sign of the Spirit-Treasury realm manifesting early. The tranquil spirit mountain seems a world away from the blood-soaked Bone Sea, yet the horrors of that night linger. The Dead City vanishes after seven days, and as the survivors gather, a new, far more ancient threat emerges from the shadows: a pale youth whose presence is linked to the three ice coffins and the shed skins discovered months ago. The calm does not last; the island holds deeper, older secrets.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen meditates in a crystal-clear lake, unknowingly entering a state of unity with nature. He spends the entire night submerged, emerging at dawn with a new ability: he can sense the presence and intention of beings over a hundred meters away without sight or sound. This power astonishes Liu Mu and Yizhen, as it is a hallmark of the Spirit-Treasury realm, which Xiao Chen has not yet reached. Over the following days, the group trains on the spirit mountain. Yizhen reveals that the “Death God Severing” technique Xiao Chen practices is a legendary killing art, but only effective when channeled through the Heavenly Stele Profound Art. On the seventh day after the Dead City's appearance, the blood sea recedes and the city itself fades from the Bone Sea, leaving only the towering divine stele. All the ancient dragons that had regained flight during those seven days lose their power and crash to the ground. Dozens of survivors—including the sword demon, the mist-veiled woman, and others who entered the Dead City—gather to investigate. Among them, Xiao Chen detects a pale, unhealthy-looking youth. His precognition identifies a red birthmark on the youth's wrist, exactly matching the three shed skins Xiao Chen had excavated from ice coffins in the snowy mountains. This confirms the existence of a centuries-old “ghost” who has been living on Dragon Island. Worse, Xiao Chen also senses a figure with Kael’s aura but a different appearance, hinting at further deception.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of the Five Great Realms: Mortal-Shedding, Spirit-Treasury, Sky-Mastery, Nirvana, and Deathless Longevity. Xiao Chen’s new perception ability is a typical divine art of the Spirit-Treasury realm, where a cultivator opens the hidden treasury of inner consciousness and gains supernatural senses. That he manifests such an ability while still in the Mortal-Shedding Seventh Heaven is extraordinarily rare, underscoring his unique path. The technique “Death God Severing” (戮神散手) is a supreme killing move supposedly capable of slaying gods when mastered. It is intrinsically tied to the Heavenly Stele Profound Art, Xiao Chen’s foundational method derived from the Yellow River Divine Stele. The seven-day cycle of the Dead City and the temporary restoration of the dragons’ divine powers evokes a ritualistic, almost scriptural pattern—the number seven holds significant mythological weight in Daoist and cosmic cycles, often representing completeness or transformation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up the next major arc with the introduction of the ancient “ghost” from the ice coffins. He is dangerous and deliberate, and Xiao Chen’s encounter with him is only a first glimpse. Keep an eye on how the pale youth interacts with other powerful survivors. The mystery of the Dead City’s cycles—seven days of chaos, then disappearance—suggests a deeper, possibly periodic mechanism tied to the island’s sealing. The presence of a shape-shifter or mimic using Kael’s aura adds another layer of paranoia. Xiao Chen, Yizhen, and Liu Mu will need to decide whether to confront these threats or avoid them. Meanwhile, Keke continues to cause mischief with the little dragon kings, providing lighthearted contrast. The next chapters will likely deepen the lore of the ice coffins and the immortal’s scheme.
