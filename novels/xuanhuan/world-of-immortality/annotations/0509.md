## Chapter Overview

This chapter ascends to the highest registers of power as Ancestral Gods and Half-Ancestors wage war across the starfields. The petty squabbles of the mortal world are forgotten; here, beings who command the laws of the cosmos clash with weapons that have slain gods for epochs. Yan Huang strides through the void with a stone weapon that shatters stars. Mo Xiaowang, the fused essence of two War Ancestors, fights with primal fury. The old farmer wields the Great Annihilation War Spear in a way that proves its legendary reputation. And the old Ancestral Dragon unleashes the Six Paths of Reincarnation, a technique that can grind Ancestral Gods to dust. The tone is one of mythic war—tragic, grandiose, and filled with the weight of ages.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Yan Huang forcing the Otherworld Ancestral God Lu Lupei to retreat, demonstrating the power of the stone soldier artifact. Mo Xiaowang, half-tiger and half-serpent, dominates his opponent but is nearly undone when a second Ancestral God strikes him with a soul attack called 'Heart Flower Withering.' He recovers by trapping an enemy in a black hole and fighting on. The most decisive duel occurs between the old farmer (wielding the Great Annihilation War Spear) and Lan Yufeng, an Otherworld Ancestral God from the previous civilization. The farmer shatters Lan Yufeng's divine shield, pierces his body with killing intent, and leaves him riddled with wounds. Meanwhile, the old Ancestral Dragon is surrounded by three enemies. He uses the Ancestral Dragon Platform and the dragon-clan binding art to isolate one, then unleashes the Six Paths of Reincarnation to kill that foe. However, the two remaining Ancestral Gods nearly slay him in return. The Martial Imprint intervenes with a divine tree, saving the dragon but revealing the battle's cost.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Six Paths of Reincarnation (六道轮回) is a supreme dragon-clan divine art that can cycle through realms of existence, grinding the target's soul and body into nothing. It is most effective when focused on a single opponent; spreading its power dilutes the effect. The Great Annihilation War Spear (大破灭战矛) embodies the principle of total destruction—its wielder can project killing intent across vast distances, piercing defenses without physical contact. The fusion of two War Ancestors into a single entity, like Mo Xiaowang, is a rare phenomenon from the primordial age, granting the composite being the strengths of both original forms. The 'Heart Flower Withering' technique is an esoteric soul attack that targets the enemy's consciousness directly, bypassing physical defenses.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes the power hierarchy among the supreme beings fighting for the mortal realm. The old farmer's victory shows that conviction and unity with one's weapon can overcome even an Ancestral God of an older civilization. The old Ancestral Dragon's near-fatal encounter sets the stage for deeper involvement from the Martial Imprint and hints that the war is far from over. The mysterious figure observing from the black door at the Ancestral Dragon Village suggests that the Otherworld has reserves yet unrevealed. Readers should watch for how these battles shape the next moves of the divine participants and whether any will fall permanently.
