## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks Xiao Chen's first major public appearance in Heavenly Emperor City, as he steps into the hallowed grounds of the Beidou Academy. What begins as a mischievous scheme by the capricious young lady Kanasi quickly escalates into a high-stakes confrontation. Xiao Chen, calm and unyielding, faces not one but four of the academy's most formidable experts, including the eighth-heaven powerhouse Lumuda. The chapter crackles with tension as Xiao Chen delivers a stunning display of martial mastery, using the Undying Sect's legendary Indestructible Seal to topple his first opponent in a single, devastating blow. It is a moment that establishes his reputation and sends shockwaves through the academy.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, accompanied by the portly merchant Zhuge Liang and Hai Yuntian, is led into the Beidou Academy by the imperious Kanasi of the Mand family. Kanasi, still smarting from her earlier humiliation, loudly announces a duel to avenge her honor, drawing a large crowd. She expects her fearsome older brother to be the opponent, but instead presents Lumuda—a twenty-nine-year-old cultivator at the Mortal-Shedding Eighth Heaven—along with three other academy experts. Xiao Chen, unfazed, declares they should all attack him at once. The first challenger, Wang Ming, a sixth-heaven expert, unleashes the Blood Transformation Heavenly Net Blade, a technique that fills the arena with a storm of crimson energy. Xiao Chen counters with the Indestructible Seal, an arcane art from the Undying Sect that nullifies the attack and shatters Wang Ming's blade, sending him flying with a single blow. The crowd erupts in shock, and Xiao Chen's name spreads like wildfire through the academy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Beidou Academy is one of the most prestigious institutions in the Southern Wastes of the Eternal Continent, founded eight hundred years ago by unaffiliated rogue cultivators as a collective to rival established sects. It has since developed a rich tradition of eclectic cultivation methods, absorbing and refining techniques from many schools. The Mortal-Shedding realm is the first true threshold of the immortal path, and reaching the Eighth Heaven is a mark of exceptional talent among the younger generation. The Indestructible Seal, a core technique of the Undying Sect, is famed for allowing a weaker cultivator to overcome a stronger opponent through an economy of power. It embodies the principle that a focused, controlled attack can defeat a more diffuse display of force. This chapter also highlights the academy's culture of honor and challenge, where public duels are a way to settle disputes and build reputation.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for Xiao Chen's life in Heavenly Emperor City. His decisive victory over Wang Ming ensures he will be watched by both allies and enemies. The Rigen family, already nursing a grudge over the death of their young master, will certainly take note of his arrival. Meanwhile, the Undying Sect's techniques are now public knowledge, tying him to Yan Qingcheng and her lineage. Pay attention to the political undercurrents—Kanasi's family, the Mand family, and the academy's internal dynamics may surface again. The calm before the storm is brief; Xiao Chen's path forward will be anything but quiet. The contrast between the serene, elegant academy and the brutal efficiency of his martial arts underscores the dual nature of this world.
