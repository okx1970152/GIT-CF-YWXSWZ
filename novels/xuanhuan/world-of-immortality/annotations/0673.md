## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers one of the most pivotal revelations in the entire saga: the Sole True Realm—the legendary origin of all worlds—is finally breached. The demon shadow, blasted by sacred light, plummets from the ninety-nine stone steps as the assembled sacred artifacts realign into a coherent passage. But this is no open gate; it is a deadly trial that even a Stone King cannot survive. The ancient heroes of the Nine Provinces—Pan Gu, Suiren, Fuxi, Shennong, the Yellow Emperor—charge forward, hoping to claim the Primordial Source before the Otherworld powers can seize it. Yet their path is blocked by the sudden manifestation of nine projections of primordial deities, each radiating a power that makes the very heavens tremble. The chapter is a masterclass in escalating tension: revelation, ambition, horror, and the crushing weight of cosmic authority.

## Key Plot Points

The demon shadow, grievously wounded by the sacred flower rain, collapses from the ninety-nine stone steps, its form cracking under the purifying light. Meanwhile, the scattered sacred artifacts—the Heavenly Stele, the ninety-nine stone steps, the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge, the nine ancient lamps—rearrange themselves into a single, coherent miniature world, a passage to the fabled Sole True Realm. This stunning sight causes chaos: the Otherworld stone kings and their armies pray for their Saint Ancestor to descend, but receive no response. The heroes of the Nine Provinces seize the moment, ascending the steps and crossing the death bridge. The stone corpse, a Stone Man King, attempts to follow but is instantly reduced to ash by the sacred lamps. This death shocks all present, confirming that the path is reserved for true kings. Just as the ancient heroes press forward, the sky splits open and nine projections of primordial deities appear, each a law unto itself, and declare that no one may trespass into the Primordial Source. The demon shadow, now barely conscious, identifies them as projections of the Primordial Saint Emperors. The chapter ends with the nine projections preparing to annihilate the intruders, while the stone kings of all worlds watch in terror.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of the Sole True Realm (唯一真界) draws from Chinese mythological ideas of a primordial origin that underlies all creation. In this cosmology, the Sole True Realm is not merely a higher dimension but the very source from which all worlds emerged. The Primordial Source (初始源地) within it represents the ultimate origin, a place of absolute power and knowledge. The nine ancient lamps (九盏古灯) and the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge are artifacts deeply tied to the Zhou dynasty era and the legendary Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors. The projections of the Primordial Deities (先天神祗) are beings that predate even the current cycle of existence—their appearance signals that the struggle for the Sole True Realm has drawn the attention of the oldest powers in the cosmos. The death of the stone corpse (石尸) is a stark reminder of the xianxia principle that realms are guarded by tribulations that cannot be bypassed without surpassing one's own cultivation ceiling. The sacred light that purifies demons and shatters souls is a classic manifestation of the Dao's judgment on those who are not in harmony with the true path.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for the final confrontation over the Sole True Realm. Pay close attention to the arrangement of the sacred artifacts—they are not random but form a 'true world' that mirrors the ancient path to origin. The nine primordial deity projections are not mere power displays; their presence suggests that the attempt to enter the Primordial Source has awakened forces that have been slumbering since before recorded history. The death of the stone corpse is especially significant: it demonstrates that even the mightiest beings cannot bypass the strictures of this path without the proper destiny or cultivation. Meanwhile, the heroes of the Nine Provinces are still advancing, hinting that they may possess the necessary legacy to survive. Watch for how the demon shadow's weakened state will affect the balance—its alliance with the Otherworld is now fragile. The next chapters will likely test the resilience of Pan Gu's party and reveal more about the nature of the nine projections and the ultimate prize they guard.
