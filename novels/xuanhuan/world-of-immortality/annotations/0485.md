## Chapter Overview

The chapter opens with the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou wielding the dreaded Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, his murderous intent fixed on the little beast Keke. But when his first strike is blocked by two ordinary-looking green stones scrawled with crooked characters, the tables turn dramatically. From those stones rises a projection of Keke's parent—a cocky, sunglasses-wearing 'cool little beast' that exudes an air of absolute confidence. What follows is a one-sided beatdown: the projection dismantles Li Huiyou with pure physical force, mocking his divine techniques and ancient weapons. The chapter contrasts epic mythic conflict with absurd humor, as the beast declares 'Life is truly as lonely as snow' while standing on the Ancestral God's head. Meanwhile, Keke watches with stars in her eyes, inspired by the raw martial power on display.

## Key Plot Points

Li Huiyou, an Otherworld Ancestral God, attacks the group with the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd, a weapon said to slaughter civilizations at the end of ages. His first thrust at Keke and the green stones is deflected by the crooked inscriptions lighting up. Annoyed, he strikes again, shattering the stones—but the runic characters detach and circle around the emerging projection of Keke's parent. The projection, appearing as a white beast with sunglasses and a straw in its mouth, yawns self-importantly. When Li Huiyou presses the assault, the beast moves faster than light, punching him a hundred leagues into the sky and then driving him headfirst into the earth. The Ancestral God tries to turn back time to send the projection to the primordial era, but the beast brushes it off, claiming none can match its divine law mastery. The scene ends with the humiliated Li Huiyou climbing out of the ground, while every half-ancestor and sovereign looks on in disbelief. The chapter reinforces the terrifying potential of Keke's bloodline and sets up the projection as a temporary but overwhelming protector.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Doomsday Heavenly Halberd' (末日天戈) echoes apocalyptic myths common in Chinese fantasy, where divine weapons appear during ages of annihilation to cull civilizations. Its name and description evoke the inevitability of cosmic cycles of destruction. The projection of Keke's parent is formed from 'eternal battle will' combined with 'Eternal Light' (永恒之光)—a legendary substance said to exist where ten or more Ancestral Gods fell, used to forge indestructible passages or, here, to give form to a lingering consciousness. This blends the tropes of undying will with the sacred light of fallen gods. The beast's line 'Life is truly as lonely as snow' (人生真是寂寞如雪) is a classic boast of xianxia peak experts, conveying absolute dominance and isolation at the top. The crooked inscriptions on the green stones suggest a primeval writing system that predates even the Ancestral Gods, hinting at lost ages of power.

## Reading Guide

This chapter provides a glimpse of the latent power within Keke's bloodline—a terrifying omen for her enemies. Her parent's projection, though temporary, demonstrates that pure martial strength can overwhelm even Ancestral God-level artifacts and techniques. Keke's reaction—fascination with the beast's fist-based combat—may foreshadow her future growth in a new direction. Li Huiyou's humiliation will not be forgotten; he may seek revenge or retreat to plot. The projection's ultimate fate remains unclear—will it fade, or can it be summoned again? Look for subsequent chapters to explore the origin of the two green stones and the deeper connection between Keke and the Eternal Light. The reappearance of the cool little beast also raises questions about where Keke's parents truly are, and whether more projections may manifest in times of crisis.
