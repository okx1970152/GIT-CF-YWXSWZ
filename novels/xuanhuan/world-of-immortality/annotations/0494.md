## Chapter Overview

This chapter depicts the climax of the half-ancestor war, with Xiao Chen and the Ancestral Gods making a final stand. The battle is desperate; Xing Tian and Chiyou slay enemies, but the Otherworld's eight-heaven female half-ancestor escapes with a magic chart. The two Ancestral Gods from the Ancestral Dragon Ship descend to engage in targeted genocide, but the seven magic charts seal the Primordial Ancient Village, cutting off hope from the Primordial Celestial Realm. In a heart-wrenching proclamation, the Ancestral Gods declare that they will send some cultivators away as seeds, while the rest fight a final doomed battle. The chapter ends with a poignant farewell to the homeland.

## Key Plot Points

The battle in the celestial realm reaches a fever pitch. Xing Tian and Chiyou dominate, killing multiple half-ancestors. The female half-ancestor at eight heavens is severely wounded and forced to retreat using a magic chart that collects all retreating enemy forces. The group, including Xiao Chen, Keke, and others, return to the Nine Provinces and discover war raging across all worlds. The Ancestral Dragon Ship arrives with two Ancestral Gods, who begin systematic annihilation of enemy forces on the main battlefield. However, the Otherworld had already deployed seven magic charts over the Primordial Ancient Village. These charts are fully activated by multiple Otherworld Ancestral Gods, sealing the passage to the Primordial Celestial Realm and blocking any further aid. The two Ancestral Gods give a grim report: the hopes of calling for reinforcement are dead. They decide to select certain promising cultivators to be sent away to a mysterious world as seeds for the future, while the rest will fight to the last. They deliver a moving speech, asking the departing ones to remember their homeland and fallen comrades.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Primordial Celestial Realm' (洪荒天界) is introduced as a higher realm that contains remnants of the Ancestral Gods. It is connected to the Primordial Ancient Village via a passage that is now sealed by seven magic charts (魔图). These charts are artifacts refined by Otherworld Ancestral Gods, capable of transmitting their power across worlds. This echoes themes of sealed realms and lost heavens common in Chinese mythology. The concept of 'sending seeds' (留下火种) is a classic trope in apocalyptic xianxia narratives: the strongest preserve promising juniors to ensure the continuation of their civilization. The Ancestral Gods' farewell emphasizes the burden of memory and the hope of return. The battle dynamics involve multiple worlds: the Celestial Realm, Nine Provinces, Soul Realm, Curse Realm, etc. Each world has its own cultivator types (e.g., curse masters in the Curse Realm) and contributes to the total war effort.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point from desperate resistance to acceptance of defeat. The sealing of the Primordial Celestial Realm path is a major plot development that removes the possibility of external salvation. Readers should pay attention to the selection of 'seeds': those who are chosen will likely form a new generation of protagonists in future story arcs. The fate of characters like Xiao Chen, Keke, and others may hang in the balance. The Ancestral Gods' calm, despairing proclamation sets the stage for a final, tragic stand. The following chapters will likely focus on the last battles, sacrifices, and the departure of the chosen ones. Note that the seven magic charts (associated with the female half-ancestor earlier) are now fully deployed, hinting that the Otherworld's plan has been long in motion.
