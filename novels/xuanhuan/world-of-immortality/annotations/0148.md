## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a high-voltage clash between two of the World of Immortality's most formidable young talents. Xiao Chen, fresh from his victories in Heavenly Emperor City, faces an opponent who matches his ruthlessness and surpasses his reputation: Wu Xingfeng, the legendary youth of the northern Tianluo Kingdom. The tension is palpable from the moment Wu Xingfeng steps forward—his bearing is that of a man who has never tasted defeat, and his gaze is calm, absolute. The fight erupts with the raw power of the Five Elements Fist against Xiao Chen's Slay God and Undying Seal techniques. The stakes extend beyond personal pride; this duel is a proxy for the imperial ambitions of the Tianluo Kingdom, which seeks to dominate the Southern Wastes through its younger generation. Xiao Chen's blood burns as he meets overwhelming pressure with indomitable will. This is not merely a battle of techniques—it is a collision of two unyielding philosophies.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a commentary on the nature of genius in cultivation: true prodigies reveal themselves young, and the world watches the rising stars of each generation. The narrative then zooms in on Wu Xingfeng, the undefeated youth of the northern lands, whose fame has reached even the Southern Wastes. Prince Sun Miao of Tianluo brings Wu Xingfeng to Heavenly Emperor City, and the intent is clear—to display overwhelming superiority and send a political signal. Wu Xingfeng approaches Xiao Chen directly, stating he has heard of him. Xiao Chen's blunt reply—"I haven't heard of you"—sets the tone for the confrontation. Wu Xingfeng launches the first attack: a slow, pressurized Gold Fist that seems to seal all escape. Xiao Chen meets it head-on with the Slay God technique, unleashing a storm of golden blades. The collision shatters the ground and sends shockwaves through the crowd. Wu Xingfeng then shifts to Earth Fist, a heavy, crushing force that forces Xiao Chen to retreat, using the Undying Seal to deflect but unable to counter. Next comes Wood Fist, vibrant with life energy but devastating in impact. Xiao Chen is pushed back again, yet his fighting spirit only intensifies. The chapter ends with the battle still raging, Xiao Chen's eyes burning with a defiance that promises escalation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Five Elements Fist (五行拳) is a profound martial system rooted in the Chinese cosmological concept of the Five Phases (Wu Xing): Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth. In xianxia lore, mastering these elements grants a cultivator near-boundless versatility and power. Wu Xingfeng's family is clearly one of the ancient lineages that have preserved the highest secrets of this art—each fist-style manifests as a tangible elemental force, not mere energy. Xiao Chen's Slay God (戮神) technique, derived from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art, is the opposite: a condensed, single-purpose killing move that channels countless blades into one overwhelming strike. The Undying Seal (不灭印) from the Undying Sect focuses on minimal-force redirection, a sophisticated defensive approach. The chapter also touches on the unwritten rule that elder-generation experts generally do not intervene in youth duels—a crucial social contract that allows young geniuses to rise without being crushed prematurely. This rule is invoked by the Prince's use of Wu Xingfeng: a way for a kingdom to exert pressure without triggering open war.
