## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen and his allies execute a meticulously planned night ambush against the Tiger Servant, a demigod of the Hu family. The chapter crackles with tension as four young masters—each possessing unique, terrifying abilities—coordinate their assault. From Jin Sanyi's unorthodox kitchen knife attacks to Dugu Jianmo's sword-body fusion, every strike undermines the demigod's pride and confidence. The Tiger Servant's transformation into a white tiger with divine patterns raises the stakes, but the team's relentless pressure forces him into a desperate final technique. The chapter balances brutal combat with moments of dark humor, notably Jin Sanyi's greed for the tiger pelt. A shocking revelation of the Longevity Art and a costly space scroll escape set the stage for Xiao Chen's lethal finishing move, only to end with a surprise appearance by Zhao Lin'er, hinting at new complications.

## Key Plot Points
The ambush unfolds with careful precision: Jin Sanyi accurately predicts the Tiger Servant's arrogant nature, ensuring he will follow Xiao Chen's bait despite the risk. Once lured to the designated mountain wilderness, Dugu Jianmo and the Crimson Dragon King deliver a devastating combined strike, forcing the demigod to acknowledge the threat. Niu Ren follows with a bull-charge that punches bloody holes in the white tiger's side. Jin Sanyi's comedic yet effective 'strikes'—aiming at head, belly, hooves, and crotch—reveal a deeper truth: his kitchen knife, when used with his unique technique, can penetrate a demigod's defenses. The Tiger Servant's transformation into a ten-meter white tiger with sacred body patterns amplifies his power but also exposes his pride. During a fierce exchange, Jin Sanyi appears torn apart—but his Longevity Art allows instant reconstruction, shocking everyone. Cornered, the Tiger Servant unleashes 'White Tiger Annihilation', a catastrophic area attack. Jin Sanyi deploys a precious space scroll to evacuate himself, Niu Ren, and Dugu Jianmo, saving them from the blast. With the demigod exhausted, Xiao Chen delivers the killing blow using the perfected Slay God technique, cutting the white tiger in two. Post-battle, they discover the Tiger Servant was only a half-blood, explaining his relative weakness. The chapter closes with Zhao Lin'er's dramatic entrance leading a troop of Western golden-haired warriors, hinting at her rising status.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases several rare divine arts. 'Longevity Art' (长生术) is a supreme-level ability that makes the practitioner nearly impossible to kill by peers; it even surpasses the 'Twin Destiny' technique in survivability. The 'White Tiger Annihilation' (白虎灭世) technique taps into the sacred patterns (神纹) inherent in the white tiger bloodline—these patterns are considered ancient divine script, capable of translating into lethal power. The 'Space Scroll' (空间卷轴) is a rare artifact that can teleport groups, indicating Jin Sanyi's wealth or connections. Also notable is the concept of 'half-tiger form' (半虎身)—not all sacred beast clans are pureblood; a half-blood carries diluted bloodline and weaker innate abilities. The white tiger's sacred patterns on its body are often seen as a gift from heaven, granting enhanced abilities but also serving as a valuable resource for alchemy or artifact forging, explaining Jin Sanyi's obsession with the pelt.

## Reading Guide
This battle cements Xiao Chen's reputation among the younger generation of the Eternal Continent, as he personally slays a demigod. The appearance of Zhao Lin'er at the chapter's end raises immediate questions: what has she been doing since Dragon Island? Why is she leading Western clan warriors? This suggests a new power faction entering Heavenly Emperor City. Additionally, the alliance of convenience between Xiao Chen, Dugu Jianmo, Niu Ren, and Jin Sanyi may solidify into a more permanent bond, as they have proven reliable in combat. The Hu family will undoubtedly seek revenge for the death of the Tiger Servant, and the Hai family's intentions remain uncertain. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's new allies, especially the cunning Jin Sanyi, will influence his next moves. The mysterious space scroll and Longevity Art hint at deeper backgrounds for the supporting cast.
