## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a shift from open battle to stealth and strategy. Xiao Chen, now a Yellow Diamond skeleton with flesh-and-blood rebirth, infiltrates an Otherworld Ancestral God's domain in the South China Sea. He witnesses the humiliation of Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect leader, who are selected for forced beauty contests and gladiatorial combat. Rather than intervene immediately, Xiao Chen restrains himself and formulates a daring plan: to assassinate the Ancestral God who is in a nirvana-like dormant state, having cultivated the Demon-Seed God-Sowing Art. The chapter builds tension through Xiao Chen's cold observation, internal debate, and eventual resolve to strike at the highest level.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen follows the captors to the Ancestral God's floating palace. He learns from the Undying Sect leader via telepathy that the Otherworld Ancestral God has adopted the "Shattering Demon Planting God" technique, putting himself into a death-like meditative state to break through. Xiao Chen reveals he has already killed a dying Ancestral God before and estimates a 60% chance of success if the god is truly dormant. He decides to postpone rescuing Yan Qingcheng and the sect leader to avoid alerting the enemy. He discovers that the Ancestral God is not in the secondary space but in a hidden chamber guarded by a Ninth Heaven Half-Ancestor. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen resolved to "lift the butcher's knife to the Otherworld Ancestral God" that very day, foreshadowing a major assassination attempt.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The "Shattering Demon Planting God" (碎魔种神) technique is a legendary cultivation method that uses a cauldron (鼎炉) to break through limits through inner conflict. Here, the Ancestral God uses himself as the cauldron, risking eternal stagnation for a chance at supreme power. This mirrors the concept of "Nirvana" (涅槃) in xianxia, where a cultivator undergoes a death-like state to be reborn stronger. Xiao Chen's ability to sense the Ancestral God's dormant state is due to his own experiences with near-death and rebirth in the Death World. The chapter also introduces the "Wan Nian Yi Yu" (万年一遇) grand competition among Otherworld gods, where they pit their slaves and disciples against each other as a form of power display.

## Reading Guide
The tension is palpable as Xiao Chen prepares to assassinate an Ancestral God. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will overcome the Ninth Heaven Half-Ancestor guardian and whether the Ancestral God is truly dormant or feigning. The fate of Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect hangs in the balance. This chapter sets up a high-stakes infiltration arc that will likely span several chapters. Pay attention to Xiao Chen's new abilities from the Death World and how he uses the Half Stone Skull and Ancestral God blood droplets to punch above his weight class. The upcoming assassination will have massive repercussions across the Otherworld and the Nine Provinces.
