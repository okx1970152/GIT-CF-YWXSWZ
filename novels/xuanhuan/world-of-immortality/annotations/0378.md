## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a high-stakes game of cat and mouse as Xiao Chen, now a formidable Yellow Diamond skeleton, executes a daring assassination on Sacred Dragon Island. He not only slays a powerful Sacred Dragon—a candidate Dragon King—but also uses the resulting chaos to cover his escape. However, his planned infiltration of Yecha Island backfires when three kings ambush him, forcing a desperate flight. The chapter balances Xiao Chen's tactical cunning with raw displays of power, as he employs the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree and the Eight Origin Sound against his pursuers. The tension escalates into a narrow escape toward the neutral territory of the Combat God Clan, leaving the broader political situation more volatile than ever.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen continues his one-man campaign to destabilize the foreign races. After slaying a former king (a crippled old sovereign), he sets his sights on Sacred Dragon Island. He infiltrates the cave of a Sacred Dragon—a candidate Dragon King—and engages in a close-quarters ambush. Despite the dragon's initial taunts and powerful counterattack using the 'Glorious Years' technique, Xiao Chen leverages the confined space and the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree to brutal effect, shattering the dragon's body and soul. The kill sends shockwaves through the island, drawing massive aerial pursuit. Xiao Chen escapes into the sea and resurfaces miles away. He then attempts to infiltrate Yecha Island, believing the chaos would distract his enemies. However, the Yecha King, War King Jing Pu, and a powerful Skeleton King have anticipated his move and set an ambush. Xiao Chen is overwhelmed by the Yecha King's power—his 'Break All Laws' technique neutralizes the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree's divine light. Forced to retreat, Xiao Chen makes a risky decision: he charges toward the island of the Combat God Clan, a race as formidable as the War Clan, hoping to drag his pursuers into a new conflict. This chapter advances the plot by deepening the racial tensions and showing Xiao Chen's strategic gambits backfiring, yet also reinforcing his resilience and cunning.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter showcases several classic xianxia conventions. The Sacred Dragon's ambush mirrors the 'beast-slaying in a cave' trope, where the protagonist uses the environment to negate a larger foe's advantages. The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, a legendary artifact from the Daoist sage Zhunti, is used here as a blunt weapon and a source of divine light—a nod to the chaotic, reimagined mythological items in Chen Dong's world-building. The Yecha King's 'Break All Laws' technique is a potent example of the 'law-crushing' ability often seen in high-level cultivators, nullifying weaker divine powers. The trilogy of kings—Yecha, War, and Skeleton—converging on Xiao Chen highlights the 'hero pursued by many' archetype, raising stakes through multiple powerful adversaries. Finally, Xiao Chen's flight toward the Combat God Clan introduces a new faction, a classic xianxia device to expand the world and introduce potential allies or fresh threats.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up several threads for the near future. Xiao Chen's gambit to drag the Combat God Clan into the conflict is extremely dangerous—will they side with him or attack him on sight? The ambush by the three kings suggests that Xiao Chen's earlier plans to create discord have been partially seen through, forcing him to adapt. The power of the Yecha King is clearly established as a major threat; Xiao Chen will need to grow stronger or find a strategy to overcome him. The Sacred Dragon's death will not go unavenged, and the Sacred Dragon Island faction is likely to escalate its hunt. Additionally, the fate of Niu Ren, Liu Mu, and others remains unresolved, adding emotional weight to Xiao Chen's actions. Readers should watch for the reaction of the Combat God Clan and whether Xiao Chen's gamble pays off or leads to an even deadlier trap.
