## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen, fresh from his trials in the Death World, makes his presence known in Yan Capital of the Cultivation World. His target is the legendary Multitude of Treasures Heavenly King, Wu Ming—a master of countless divine artifacts and one of the most feared Perfected Man realm experts. This chapter delivers a high-stakes opening salvo as Xiao Chen seizes Wu Ming's niece to draw out his opponent, then engages in a terrifying spiritual duel that shakes the very foundations of the capital. The tone is one of cold, calculated provocation mixed with breathtaking displays of power, as Xiao Chen tests his reborn martial body against a rival who wields the Indestructible Imperial Bell and the Heavenly Thunder Hammer. The chapter also features a rare moment of domestic warmth as Xiao Chen and Keke indulge in mortal delicacies, a brief respite before the storm.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen locates Wu Ming's niece and forcibly takes her golden bell, crushing the protective artifacts of her escorts with casual ease. He dismisses the woman's threats and seals her in the bell, demanding that Wu Ming come to face him. The act sends shockwaves through Yan Capital, where Wu Ming is regarded as nearly invincible. After a leisurely meal with Keke, Xiao Chen refines his cultivation—nearly all 365 major acupoints have now been divinely transformed, forming an entirely new divine meridian network that channels power directly from the Heavenly Stele diagrams. Wu Ming's arrival triggers a colossal spiritual clash: their divine consciousnesses collide like two worlds, shattering the sky above the capital. When Wu Ming unleashes his full arsenal—five immortal swords, a replica of the Haotian Tower, a black dragon rope, and more—Xiao Chen counters by manifesting the Five Great Kings: celestial guardians that seem to blur the line between Buddhist enlightenment and demonic ferocity. The battle is both a test of will and a spectacle of power, revealing that Xiao Chen's cultivation has reached a level where he can stand as equals with the mightiest Heavenly Kings.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The term 'Heavenly King' (天王) is a title for a supreme cultivator at the Perfected Man realm (至人) or above, often commanding a region or sect. Wu Ming's epithet 'Multitude of Treasures' (多宝) reflects his mastery over a vast collection of divine artifacts—a hallmark of the Artifact Refining Sect. The Five Great Kings (五大明王) are a distinct concept in Chinese Buddhist and esoteric lore: wrathful manifestations of Buddhas and Bodhisattvas who subdue demons and protect the Dharma. Xiao Chen's ability to manifest both the Buddhist 'Bright Kings' (明王) and then the demonic 'Dark Kings' (魔皇) showcases his unique fusion of pure martial arts and death-world cultivation, symbolizing his rejection of fixed categories. The Complete Vase Seal (圆满宝瓶印) is a technique from the Pure Land, representing absorption and containment—used here to counter divine swords. The ongoing transformation of Xiao Chen's 365 acupoints into a divine meridian network is a unique path derived from the Heavenly Stele diagrams, setting him apart from traditional cultivators.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation between Xiao Chen and a top-tier Heavenly King. Wu Ming is not only a powerful opponent but also a plot catalyst: he is one of the key players in the Cultivation World's emerging order. Readers should note that Xiao Chen's cultivation has reached a critical inflection point—the near-completion of his acupoint transformation suggests an impending breakthrough to an even higher realm. The duel is intentionally drawn as a proxy battle; Wu Ming's true strength remains hidden behind his army of treasures. Additionally, the reappearance of the Five Great Kings hints at a larger mythic framework—are these manifestations original to Xiao Chen, or are they connected to the lost scriptures of the Buddhist and Daoist pantheons? The next chapter promises to escalate the conflict, potentially bringing in other Heavenly Kings and even Half-Ancestors who watch from the shadows.
