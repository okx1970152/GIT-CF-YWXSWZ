## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a profound revelation that reshapes Xiao Chen’s understanding of the cosmos. Hidden deep beneath the earth, a mysterious guardian reveals the truth of Pangu, the primal ancestor of the Nine Provinces, and the catastrophic nature of the Celestial Realm. The Celestial Realm, far from being a paradise, is a battleground of ancient powers and a graveyard of destroyed civilizations. Xiao Chen learns that no external help will come, forcing him to confront the grim reality that only self-reliance can save his world. The chapter blends epic lore with poignant despair, setting the stage for a solitary struggle.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter begins with Xiao Chen’s shock at hearing 'Pangu's Ancient Land.' The mysterious voice explains that Pangu was a real being, the last king of the 'waste land' (the ruined Nine Provinces), who held back extinction. The voice reveals the Celestial Realm is not a peaceful paradise but a gathering of the mightiest survivors from countless destroyed worlds, a place of constant conflict and shifting alliances. Most of Jiuzhou's Ancestral Gods who ascended have fallen. The traditional allies of Jiuzhou are now powerless or have changed allegiance. Xiao Chen realizes the futility of seeking aid in the Celestial Realm; his only path is self-reliance. The voice provides a Pangu Stone Token to enter the protected Pangu Ancient Land and teaches him to conceal his traces from enemies. Finally, the guardian manifests as a colossal stone statue, identified as Keke's great-grandfather (Zu of Ke). He reveals his remnant consciousness is protecting a female Saint of their family. This connection ties Xiao Chen's companion Keke to a lineage of immense power and tragedy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter draws on the Chinese creation myth of Pangu, traditionally the first being to separate heaven and earth. Here, Pangu is reimagined as a primordial king and protector of the Nine Provinces. The Celestial Realm (天界) is depicted not as a utopia but as a harsh arena where the strongest from extinct civilizations congregate—a concept that deepens the novel’s dark fantasy worldbuilding. The 'stone man' path (石人王者) is a supreme cultivation trajectory, and the 'final step' (最后一步) refers to a critical breakthrough that risks utter annihilation. The voice’s reference to 'Pangu Stone Token' (盘古石令) echoes the use of talismans in Daoist and xianxia traditions to access forbidden zones.

## Reading Guide
This chapter closes a major arc: Xiao Chen’s hope for celestial aid is extinguished, hardening his determination to rely on his own strength. Readers should note the implications of Keke’s lineage—her great-grandfather is a stone statue of immense power, hinting at a hidden war and a protected saint. The Pangu Stone Token becomes a crucial artifact for future sanctuary. Pay attention to Xiao Chen’s cultivation of the 'Heavenly Mark' (天痕) and his mastery of the Eight Origin Sounds, which will be his weapons in the coming struggle. The next chapters likely shift focus to his return to the lower realm and the confrontation with the Otherworld, carrying the weight of this tragic knowledge.
