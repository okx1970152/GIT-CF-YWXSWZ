## Chapter Overview
This cataclysmic chapter delivers one of the most shocking sequences in the entire arc. Xiao Chen, with the help of Keke's soul merged into the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree, turns the tables on the supreme beings who thought themselves untouchable in the Lost Paradise. Stripped of divine power and reliant only on martial skill, the Half-Ancestors are reduced to mortal fragility. Xiao Chen's cold fury, born of seeing Keke impaled on a war sword, drives him to execute Original, White Tiger Saint Emperor, and the Sun Holy God in rapid succession—each beheaded with the same blade that held Keke. The chapter crescendos with Tongtian also slain, and Xiao Chen advancing on Laozi and Buddha. The tone is one of bleak apocalypse; the world trembles as thunder heralds the fall of each half-ancestor.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a mysterious voice declaring its intention to incite Xiao Chen to kill half-ancestors—signaling a hidden force acting through the Heavenly Stele's imprint. Within the Lost Paradise, where no divine power functions, Original duels Xiao Chen with raw martial skill and initially wounds him. Keke, pierced on Original's sword, is dying. In a desperate act, Keke's soul leaves its shattered body and merges with the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree inside Xiao Chen. This fusion breaks the rules of the Lost Paradise: the Sacred Tree can wield its divine power—binding and slowing rays—even here. Keke's first binding freezes Original, allowing Xiao Chen to sever his arm and then decapitate him. The White Tiger Saint Emperor and Sun Holy God attempt to flee, but Keke's radiance traps them, and Xiao Chen executes them with the same cold precision. Tongtian, the Jie Sect master, is also killed. The crowd outside the Lost Paradise is convulsed in disbelief; three half-ancestors have fallen in minutes. Xiao Chen, unyielding, now walks toward Laozi and the Buddha, determined to extinguish every half-ancestor on the continent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Lost Paradise (失乐园) is a unique realm within the World of Immortality, separate from the external mysterious world. In the outer mysterious world, half-ancestors retain partial divine power; but in the Lost Paradise proper, all supernatural abilities—spells, domain control, flight—are nullified. Only raw physical prowess and martial arts remain. This explains why Original, a supreme being, can be wounded by Xiao Chen's sword. The Seven-Colored Sacred Tree is not native to the Lost Paradise; it originally came from Keke's homeland, which is linked to the same chaotic forces that govern the paradise. Thus, when Keke's soul merges with it, the tree can channel her will and use its divine light even in this power-suppressed zone. This is a major lore reveal: the Sacred Tree is a key to resisting or sidestepping the Lost Paradise's laws. The thunder rolling across the Eternal Continent after each half-ancestor death is a cosmological signal—the world itself mourns the loss of such pinnacle beings. Each half-ancestor's extinction shakes heaven and earth.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks the apex of Xiao Chen's revenge arc for Keke's suffering and the long-building hatred toward the half-ancestors who hunted him. The mysterious voice from the Heavenly Stele imprint remains a critical unanswered question: who is this vanishing imprint, and why does it favor Xiao Chen's carnage? Pay attention to the author's use of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree as both a weapon and a narrative device—it will likely return as a major plot point. The deaths of these three half-ancestors will have enormous repercussions: their disciples, sects, and aligned powers across the Eternal Continent will now hunt Xiao Chen relentlessly. The final image of Xiao Chen advancing on Laozi and the Buddha suggests the slaughter is not yet finished. This chapter also deepens the emotional bond between Xiao Chen and Keke, now fused at the soul level. The coming chapters will explore whether Xiao Chen can truly escape the Lost Paradise and what the Heavenly Stele's imprint truly intends.
