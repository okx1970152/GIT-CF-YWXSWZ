## Chapter Overview

In this tension-laden chapter, the legendary and feared Heavenly King Wu Ming is reduced to a panicked flight by two simple sentences. The mystery deepens as the same unseen force later peacefully coaxes him back, revealing an intricate game of power and survival in the Eternal Continent. Meanwhile, the irrepressible Purple Diamond Skeleton – Sha Polang – stirs chaos in the Misty Rain Pavilion with his provocative orders for tiger-themed dishes, deliberately baiting the Tiger clan. When the young Asura king steps in to assert dominance, he is humbled by a single, effortless palm stroke from a seemingly ordinary man. The chapter masterfully weaves together Xiao Chen’s quiet return, the cunning calculations of Wu Ming, and the reckless humor of Sha Polang, all while building a thick atmosphere of political intrigue and imminent collision.

## Key Plot Points

Wu Ming, one of the most feared Heavenly Kings in the cultivation world, is terrified when he hears two sentences directed at him in the Misty Rain Pavilion. He immediately retreats, to the astonishment of all present. Later, after being reassured by the man (Xiao Chen) that he means no immediate harm, Wu Ming returns to keep his appointment with the Tiger clan family. The Tiger clan’s third-generation leader, Tiger King Hu Xifeng, is hosting Wu Ming to discuss a marriage alliance. Meanwhile, Sha Polang – the purple diamond skeleton ruler of three cities in the death world – deliberately orders dishes made from tiger parts, knowing full well it will enrage the Tiger clan. Xiao Asura, a recently risen Perfected Man realm king, attempts to punish Sha Polang but is effortlessly repelled by Xiao Chen, who reveals himself with a casual display of overwhelming power. Xiao Chen then ascends to the top floor where the Tiger clan and Wu Ming are meeting, setting the stage for a direct confrontation with the hidden powers of the Eternal Continent.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces several key concepts of the late-stage cultivation hierarchy. ‘Heavenly King’ (天王) is an informal title given to cultivators at or near the Perfected Man realm (至人境界). Wu Ming is said to have fused with the Indestructible Imperial Heavenly Bell, granting him an undying body and soul, and he has survived wars against Five Element Kings and respected Tieren elders. The Tiger clan is an ancient royal lineage, known for its secret strength; their third-generation king, Hu Xifeng, is rumored to be approaching the Earth-Penetrating realm (彻地境界) and has even defeated a Tieren opponent of the same level. The Tieren race is famed for its genius-level talents, so defeating one proves extraordinary combat power. The mention of ‘Tieren’ ties into earlier arcs from the Eternal Continent. Xiao Asura is a newer king who trained under the legendary Cold Frozen, the first-ranked king of his generation. The Perfected Man realm grants a cultivator the ability to merge with heaven and earth, producing a natural domain, but Xiao Chen’s casual defeat of Xiao Asura shows his far superior cultivation.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the delicate balance Xiao Chen maintains: he has not lost his cunning but now moves with a calm confidence that unnerves even the most powerful figures. Wu Ming’s fear and his later careful diplomacy hint at a potential alliance or conflict, but Xiao Chen’s ultimate goal remains mysterious. Sha Polang’s deliberate provocations may serve a deeper purpose, perhaps to test the Tiger clan’s response or to create a diversion. The sudden arrival of Xiao Asura and the quick suppression of his challenge signal that Xiao Chen is not merely hiding – he is actively asserting his presence in the Eternal Continent. The conclusion of the chapter, with Xiao Chen ascending to the top floor where a major power alliance is being negotiated, suggests a direct encounter with the Tiger clan leadership and possibly the beginning of a larger power struggle. The mention of the ‘Demon King’ in the crowd’s speculation also foreshadows how Xiao Chen’s legendary reputation will shape events."
