## Chapter Overview

This chapter masterfully escalates from a tense negotiation over ancient artifacts to a full-scale clash of celestial powers, culminating in a staggering identity revelation. Xiao Chen’s group encounters a mysterious Taoist who claims knowledge of the Little Stone Emperor’s sealed tomb. After failing to trade for a Heavenly Stele, the Taoist follows the party, offering crucial intelligence in exchange for safe passage. The hunt for the tomb’s sole witness draws the attention of three Celestial Realm Stone Man Kings, who tear up eight hundred li of land to capture the disciple. In the chaos, the ancient powerhouse Lu Zhan intervenes, snatching the disciple and extracting the tomb’s location: the Cracked Heaven Valley. This shocking twist sets the stage for a desperate race. The chapter ends with an even more stunning turn: the old stone crow recognizes the Taoist as the Little Stone Emperor himself, alive after millennia presumed dead. The tone is mythic, frantic, and layered with ancient deception.

## Key Plot Points

- A decayed Taoist tries to exchange for the Heavenly Stele brick, offering any price, but Xiao Chen refuses. The Taoist then offers information: the Little Stone Emperor’s tomb was discovered by a disciple of Floating Sky Island but vanished when approached. After a Celestial Realm giant captured that disciple, he escaped with a precious treasure and has been hunted ever since.
- Xiao Chen tracks a 9th-heaven Otherworld Ancestral God who is also searching for the disciple. They follow the god’s trail for thousands of li.
- Three gigantic stone hands descend from the sky, each covering hundreds of li, to seize the entire region containing the disciple. Xiao Chen barely escapes with his Eight Aspect Worlds speed.
- Lu Zhan, a living fossil from the pre-primordial age, erupts in a black mist, tears open one hand, extracts the disciple, and reads his memory. He learns the tomb is in the Cracked Heaven Valley, then destroys the disciple and flees, evading the pursuing hands.
- Xiao Chen visits the old stone crow and skull to recruit their aid. The old stone crow immediately recognizes the Taoist as the Little Stone Emperor, who has been hiding and persecuting them for thirteen thousand years.
- The chapter clarifies the stakes: the tomb requires mutated dragon grass (which Keke has) as a key, and Celestial Realm giants are now aware of the location.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Little Stone Emperor (小石皇) is a legendary figure in the World of Immortality, a supreme cultivator who once left a sealed tomb that supposedly holds unimaginable treasures and secrets. His supposed death and the existence of his tomb have been a major myth for ages. This chapter reveals he faked his death, adding layers to the world's political and power dynamics. The Celestial Realm giants are Stone Man Kings, beings who have taken the final step of the Stone Man path, making them nearly invincible. Their ability to uproot hundreds of li of land demonstrates their cosmic authority. The mutated dragon grass (龙草) and its fruits are formed from Ancestral Dragon essence, making them not only rare spiritual treasures but also indispensable keys to unlocking the Little Stone Emperor's tomb. Lu Zhan (陆战) is introduced as a living fossil from a primordial era, a being of such ancient power that even Stone Man Kings cannot stop him. This chapter illustrates the intricate web of ancient rivalries and hidden agendas that drive the conflict.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to the Taoist—his identity is now the central mystery. Why did the Little Stone Emperor fake his death, and what is his purpose in following Xiao Chen? The location of the tomb (Cracked Heaven Valley) is now known to multiple factions, including Celestial Realm giants and Lu Zhan. The mutated dragon grass in Keke's possession has become a critical asset; expect it to draw more attention. The old stone crow and skull have personal vendettas against the Little Stone Emperor, making them volatile but powerful allies. Future chapters will likely involve a race to the tomb, confrontations with Lu Zhan, and revelations about the Little Stone Emperor's schemes. Xiao Chen's decision to involve the two ancient beings was wise, but he must now navigate the complex loyalties between the broken stone man, his stone turtle mentor, and the newly revealed emperor.
