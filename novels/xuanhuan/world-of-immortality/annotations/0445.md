## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a monumental turning point in the Death World arc. The mysterious corpse, a being of unfathomable power, effortlessly dominates the Great Power Dark Sovereign, a half-ancestor who commands thirty‑odd kings. With a single move, the corpse extracts a strand of Ancestral God essence and shatters the Dark Sovereign's realm, leaving him broken. The survivors—Xiao Chen, Tianwaitian, Renwairen, Yingxiong, and three Tree Emperor warriors—finally escape the gray mist that had trapped them for a hundred and fifty years. The tone is tragic and desolate, underscoring the immense passage of time and the fragility of even the mightiest civilizations. The chapter closes with the group's bitter realization that Yingxiong's kingdom has long turned to dust, and that the mortal world they once knew has been transformed beyond recognition.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Great Power Dark Sovereign wielding the Heaven‑Earth Millstone, a supreme artifact that grinds all matter and spirit into nothing. He proclaims his intent to dominate the Death World and beyond. But the corpse—ancient and withered—turns the tables, revealing that it was the true force behind the millstone. The corpse tears the millstone from the Dark Sovereign's realm, causing the entire plane to collapse. In seconds, the Dark Sovereign is reduced to a blood‑soaked wreck, his kings scattered. The corpse extracts a single strand of Ancestral God essence from him, then spares him with a cryptic warning about balance. The seven survivors, freed from their bonds, scramble to escape through a rapidly closing passage in the gray mist. They barely make it out, emerging into the familiar red‑earth landscape of the Death World. Once safe, they learn the bitter truth: Yingxiong's once‑mighty Devil Kingdom fell ten thousand years ago. The century and a half they spent in the gray mist has erased the world they left behind. Xiao Chen feels the weight of generations lost. The group decides to seek shelter in God Village before attempting to return to the Nine Provinces, knowing that their weakened state—all but Xiao Chen have fallen from the half‑ancestor realm—makes them vulnerable.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter introduces the concept of the Heaven‑Earth Millstone, a primordial artifact that embodies the cycle of cosmic destruction and rebirth. In xianxia cosmology, such artifacts often represent the ultimate law of entropy. The half‑ancestor realm (半祖) is the level just below Ancestral God, granting near‑immortality and domain control. The Great Power Dark Sovereign, despite being a half‑ancestor, is helpless before the corpse, which is implied to have touched the Ancestral God level. Ancestral God essence (祖君精气) is the concentrated life force and authority of a supreme being, used for healing or empowering artifacts. The gray mist is a manifestation of a fallen Ancestral God's residual yin soul—a deadly trap that can imprison even kings for millennia. The passage of time in the Death World is erratic; a hundred and fifty years inside the mist felt like mere days to the survivors, highlighting the fluid nature of time in cultivation realms.

## Reading Guide

The chapter sets up several critical threads. The corpse's actions—sparing the Dark Sovereign but taking only one strand of essence—suggest a long‑term plan; this half‑ancestor may become a future ally or a recurring threat. The survivors' severely depleted power means they cannot immediately challenge the upper echelons of the Death World, so the next arc will focus on recovery and intelligence gathering. Xiao Chen's realization that an entire mortal generation has passed deepens his personal stakes: old friends may have died, and the political landscape of the Nine Provinces has likely shifted. Readers should watch for the fate of the three skeleton kings (Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, Lunhuiwang), who are hinted to have reached Earth‑Penetrating realm under a mysterious female sovereign. The group's journey to God Village will likely bring them into contact with familiar figures like Li Mu and the Divine Tribe, setting the stage for a return to the mortal world at a time of great change.
