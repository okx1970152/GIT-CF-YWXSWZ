## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a poignant blend of grief, reunion, and the dawning of a new era. Keke, mourning Xiao Chen for over a decade, performs a soul-summoning ritual outside the Dead City, her childish cries tearing at the heart. Xiao Chen, now a yellow-diamond skeleton, watches from the shadows, deeply moved but unable to reveal himself. When assassins approach, Keke's overwhelming power—a single sweep of seven-colored light—sends everyone, including Xiao Chen, tumbling into the Dead City. The chapter then shifts from intimate sorrow to the brutal reality of the Dead City's horrors and, finally, to the broader stage of the Nine Provinces, where the Age of Kings has fully arrived. Xiao Chen walks among mortals who now accept the hundred races, and he learns of the epic battles between legendary figures: the Golden Lion King, the War Clan, the Lili King, and more. The emotional anchor remains Keke's loneliness, but the narrative surges forward with the promise of conflict and Xiao Chen's determination to reclaim his place.

## Key Plot Points

Keke’s grief is laid bare: she sets out candles and food for Xiao Chen, weeping and calling his name with a haunting, childish rendition of the soul-summoning song. This moment crystallizes her decade-long isolation and her refusal to accept his death. Xiao Chen, as a yellow-diamond skeleton, feels an overwhelming urge to comfort her but holds back when he senses assassins. Keke, despite her sorrow, retains her vigilance: she detects the ambush and, without turning, unleashes a rainbow expanse of power that freezes everyone, including Xiao Chen, and hurls them into the Dead City. This act demonstrates her immense growth (her fur even flickers through gold and emerald) and her continued connection to the Lost Paradise. Inside the Dead City, the trapped cultivators panic; they reveal that the Lili King and Yasha King had hired them to track Keke. The Dead City proves its lethal nature: spectral corpses emerge from ancient houses, killing all but Xiao Chen, who fights them off. He scales the city walls and escapes, exhausted. Outside, he finds Keke’s altar and resolves to find her with treasures. His journey through the mortal world shows the new normal: commoners casually mention seeing a hundred-zhang sacred dragon, an ant tribe, and even skeletons; Xiao Chen is mistaken for a walking gold source. He finally reaches Chang’an, the imperial capital, where he meets three ordinary skeletons who bow to him as a superior. From them, he learns of the new order: the War Clan, the Golden Lion King, the Lili King, the Yasha King, and the emergence of the Giant Island’s golden-blooded royal clan. The chapter ends with the bitter note that humanity has declined, with many experts submitting to other races.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Dead City (死城) holds immense lore: it is the final resting place and prison of countless half-ancestors, a cursed zone where even the strongest fear to tread. The soul-summoning song (招魂曲) that Keke chants is a traditional Chinese funerary practice intended to call the spirit of a lost one back to the body. In the context of the novel, it also echoes the ancient battle chants of the ancestors. The hierarchy of skeletons—white, blue, purple, yellow, diamond—reflects the cultivation path in the Death World, where fire seeds (火种) are the currency of evolution. The emergence of the hundred races on the Nine Provinces marks the culmination of the ‘return to reality’ arc: the illusory half-ancestors have fallen, and true races like the War Clan, the dragon-blooded, and the sky-snake tribe now walk the land. The Age of Kings (王的时代) refers to the violent competition among these new powerhouses, each claiming dominion over territories. The Lili King (丽人王) and the Golden Lion King (黄金狮子王) are among the most prominent, their battles shaping the new political landscape.

## Reading Guide

This chapter resolves one of the longest-running emotional threads: Keke’s grief and Xiao Chen’s return to the living world. Their reunion is delayed, but the stage is set for a dramatic meeting—likely when Xiao Chen reveals his yellow-diamond form bearing a hoard of spiritual treasures. The Dead City remains a potent threat; Xiao Chen’s escape suggests he can navigate it, but its deeper secrets (including the Demon Well and the fate of the ancestral gods) may still await him. The introduction of Chang’an as a hub of racial conflict foreshadows a major confrontation. The Golden Lion King’s fourth duel with the War Clan’s Mabao, and the Yasha King’s massacre of human experts, signal that the mortal world is entering a period of chaos. Xiao Chen’s own status as a yellow-diamond skeleton places him among the top-tier undead, and his next logical step is to assert himself in this new world order. Readers should watch for the Lili King and the Yasha King as potential allies or enemies, and note that the human race’s decline may spur Xiao Chen to take on a leadership role. Keke’s unexpected power surge hints that she, too, is a force to be reckoned with in the coming age.
