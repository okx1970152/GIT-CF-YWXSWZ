## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a masterclass in tactical ambush and attrition warfare within the death world. Xiao Chen, alongside the Zombie King and Nether King, sets a chain of interconnected death arrays to trap the War King Jing Pu, a fierce War Clan warrior. The War King, wielding the legendary Ba Side Ancient War Spear and commanding the Time Divine Principle, fights desperately through curses, blood seas, and zombie hordes. Yet the relentless erosion of his power and the coordinated strikes from three cunning kings lead to his downfall. The chapter is a brutal, high-stakes engagement that showcases the strategic depth of the death world and the unyielding resolve of all parties involved.

## Key Plot Points
- The War King is lured into a region filled with multiple death traps—spatial curses, soul-draining mists, blood seas, and zombie legions—carefully prepared by the Zombie King and Nether King.
- Each trap systematically weakens the War King: his life force is drained, his body corroded, and his combat power reduced.
- Despite his formidable abilities, including time reversal that allows him to undo some damage, the cumulative effect of the curses proves too great.
- The three kings launch a coordinated assault: Nether King strikes from shadow, Zombie King sacrifices a puppet body, and Xiao Chen uses the Origin Sound and the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree.
- War King manages to kill one of Zombie King's puppets and grievously wound Nether King, but Xiao Chen seizes a critical opening to sever his neck.
- In the aftermath, the War King’s head is taken, and the Ba Side Ancient War Spear becomes a prize sought by others, including a mysterious golden hand from beyond the sky.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- The War Clan are innate battle sages, and their kings possess combat prowess that borders on the legendary. The Ba Side Ancient War Spear is a relic stained with the blood of half-ancestors, giving it an almost sentient killing aura.
- Death world curse techniques are highly varied and insidious: they drain life essence, corrupt the body, and summon undead minions. Crafting such a layered ambush requires deep knowledge of necromantic cultivation.
- Time manipulation is a rare divine principle; here it is used to reverse injuries and disruptions, showing its immense tactical value.
- The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, originally a Buddhist sage's artifact, is employed by Xiao Chen as a blunt weapon—its multi-colored divine light proves effective even against the ancient war spear.

## Reading Guide
- With the War King dead, the immediate threat is neutralized, but his clan may seek retribution. Keep an eye on the broader War Clan reaction.
- Xiao Chen’s use of the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree signals a new phase in his arsenal—this artifact may become a key tool in future battles.
- The fate of the Ba Side Ancient War Spear remains uncertain; it could empower a new enemy or ally.
- The alliance with the undead kings may fracture now that their common enemy is gone—watch for shifting loyalties.
- This chapter also deepens the lore of death curses and space traps, which may reappear in later conflicts.
