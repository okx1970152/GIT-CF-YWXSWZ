## Chapter Overview
This chapter sees the convergence of multiple factions on Xiao Chen's ancestral village, the site of the mysterious Huangni Altar. Despite the departure of the three eccentric guardians (Shanwaishan and his peers), the village proves far from defenseless. A prophecy of doom is fulfilled when over a thousand cultivators enter and are instantly annihilated by surging Xuanhuang qi, leaving only Xiao Chen and the villagers unharmed. The arrival of Zhao Lin'er with the formidable Sun Knights and the appearance of a half-ancestor elder raise the stakes to a mythological scale, transforming the quiet rural setting into a crucible of cosmic conflict.

## Key Plot Points
- Hai Yunxue orchestrates an ambush with Tiger family experts, but Xiao Chen escapes using his Eight Aspect Worlds and is saved by Shanwaishan's intervention, which effortlessly destroys the 'shadow demons' prepared for him. 
- The three guardians (Shanwaishan, Tianwaitian, Renwairen) sense an ominous fate for the village and abruptly depart, leaving Xiao Chen to defend it alone.
- Emboldened by their departure, over a thousand cultivators from both the Xiuzhen and Longevity worlds rush into the village to plunder. Before they can act, the altars power ignites: Xuanhuang qi rises, blood light erupts, and every intruder is reduced to ash a single, devastating moment.
- Zhao Lin'er arrives with the legendary Sun Knights, threatening to burn the village. Her demand that Xiao Chen cooperate is met with cold refusal.
- A half-ancestor, the Three-Infant Grandmother, appears summoned by the Xiuzhen elite Ye Tian. She studies the Huangni Altar with deep suspicion, hinting at even greater dangers ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
- 'Xuanhuang' (玄黄) refers to the colors of heaven and earth (black and yellow) and symbolizes primal chaos. In Daoist cosmology, Xuanhuang qi represents the original substance of the universe, often associated with creation and destruction. Its appearance here as a protective mechanism for the village parallels the legendary power of sacred sites guarded by ancient formations.
- The phrase 'Blood of the sky, essence of the earth, yin and yang in battle, weeping blood-dark Xuanhuang' echoes a classical Chinese incantation, evoking the cosmic scale of the conflict. It serves as both a prophecy and a warning, underscoring that even powerful cultivators are mere 'ants' before such fundamental forces.
- The Sun Knights are an elite order from the Western Roman Empire's Sun Church, historically said to be capable of challenging half-ancestors when united. Their golden armor and ancient steeds emphasize the transcontinental scope of the forces now converging on the humble village.

## Reading Guide
- Pay attention to the shifting power dynamics: Xiao Chens reliance on the villages innate defense rather than his own strength marks a temporary but crucial reprieve. However, the half-ancestors interest signals that the village will become a major battleground in the coming arcs.
- Note Kekes reawakening and the release of the two little girls (Linglong and Tutu). Their playful yet mysterious nature hints at their potential role in future events, possibly as allies or wild cards.
- The departure of the three guardians suggests that even those who approached half-ancestor level are unwilling to face the villages fate. This sets up a mystery: what exactly lies beneath the village, and why do ancient powers fear it?
