## Chapter Overview
This chapter delves into the mysteries of the dead world as Xiao Chen and the spirit race elders explore a bone well that connects to the Nine Provinces. The well is revealed to be a colossal spinal column of a being beyond normal kings. Xiao Chen returns to the mortal world as a phantom and learns the mechanics of his teleportation. He gathers valuable colorful diamond bones to trade for powerful fire seeds, advancing his cultivation. However, his frequent trades draw dangerous attention, forcing a strategic retreat.

## Key Plot Points
- Two spirit race elders, Li Mu and Zhao Ying, investigate a bone well and discover it is actually a massive spinal column, indicating a being of immense size and power, possibly surpassing the legendary kings.
- Xiao Chen enters the dead city again via the bone well, now able to briefly manifest in the mortal world. He learns that expending the instantaneous energy allows him to return to the dead world.
- He collects a vast amount of colorful diamond fragments from the bone well, which are extremely valuable in the dead world.
- Using these fragments, Li Mu helps Xiao Chen trade for a powerful fire seed at a city's lord mansion, successfully advancing him one cultivation rank.
- Over the next two weeks, Xiao Chen visits three cities (Seven Kill, Broken Army, Greedy Wolf) and trades for nearly thirty fire seeds, rapidly advancing his cultivation but attracting the attention of city lords and strong individuals.
- Sensing danger, Xiao Chen withdraws despite being only five fire seeds short of the next breakthrough. He is ambushed on his way back but manages to escape.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter introduces central cultivation concepts of the dead world. Fire seeds (火种) are the equivalent of cultivation pills; absorbing them allows a skeleton to upgrade its bone body from red diamond to purple diamond. Colorful diamond bones (彩钻碎骨) are precious trade goods that enable advancement. The bone well (骨井) is a rare spatial link between the dead world and the mortal Nine Provinces. The ability to manifest physically in the mortal world improves with cultivation level—Li Mu can persist for much longer than Xiao Chen. The dead world's cities have strict trading hierarchies: powerful fire seeds are only traded in the lord's mansion, requiring a strong backer like Li Mu. This mirrors real-world cultivation economies where rare resources are controlled by elites.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen's cultivation is accelerating, but his reckless trading will have consequences. The three cities' lords and the hunter Du Si are now aware of his riches. The bone well's secret is guarded by the spirit race elders, but its existence may draw even greater powers. Xiao Chen's ability to teleport between worlds will become a key asset, and the references to a being with a spinal column beneath the ground hint at ancient myths that may tie into the greater cosmology. Readers should watch for the arrival of the 'king' level enemies and the potential conflict with the three city lords. The next chapter likely involves Xiao Chen's escape and the aftermath of his rapid growth.
