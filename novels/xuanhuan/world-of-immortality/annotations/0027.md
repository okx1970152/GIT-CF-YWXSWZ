## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a decisive turning point in Xiao Chen's survival campaign on Dragon Island. After a harrowing escape from the Undying King of the Death Swamp, Xiao Chen pursues his wounded enemies through the moonlit forest, demonstrating a cold, calculated ruthlessness. The chapter juxtaposes the serene beauty of moonlight with the brutal reality of execution-style killings, reinforcing the desolate, mythic tone of the novel. As Xiao Chen corners Gulou and Zhao Lin'er, he shows curiosity about the mystical arts of this world, yet never wavers from his ultimate goal: eliminating all threats. The three skeletons rattle their jaws in the night, a chilling reminder of the forces Xiao Chen now commands.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and his three skeleton allies emerge from the Death Swamp, having narrowly avoided the Undying King's attention. They quickly catch up to the weakened Gulou and Zhao Lin'er, who are suffering from corpse miasma poisoning. A rear guard sacrifices themselves to allow the leaders to escape, but Xiao Chen and the skeletons massacre them in a swift, brutal fashion. The narrative emphasizes each killing with short, punchy sentences: "Blood sprayed. Heads rolled." Xiao Chen intercepts the main party again after the rear guard is annihilated, leaving only a single flame-blade guardian standing between him and his prey. Instead of immediate execution, Xiao Chen pauses to interrogate Gulou about the Flame Blade technique and the fusion of Eastern and Western magic—a rare moment of curiosity amidst the carnage. Gulou, surprisingly, answers the question, revealing that the flame blade is an application of a fused curse-art system. This confirms the depth of the World of Immortality's mysteries. Xiao Chen then offers Gulou a choice: kill yourself or be killed. The chapter ends with the three skeletons clicking their jaws in the moonlight, an eerie sound that underscores the finality of the situation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The "fusion of Eastern and Western curses" (古老的东西方咒语融合) is a significant worldbuilding revelation. It hints at a broader magical system that transcends cultural boundaries, which is rare in traditional xianxia. This fusion may be a precursor to later developments where practitioners from different backgrounds combine their arts. The Flame Blade (火焰刀) technique is a product of this fusion: it combines the elemental power of fire with the cutting edge of a blade, suggesting a practical, combat-oriented application of mixed traditions. The Undying King (不死之王) is a terrifying entity from the Death Swamp, reminiscent of Chinese mythology's kings of the dead. Here, it is a physical, mobile horror that spreads corpse miasma. Xiao Chen's interrogation of Gulou shows that even in desperate situations, knowledge gathering is a priority for the protagonist, a trait of a true survivor in the cultivation world.

## Reading Guide

The conflict with Gulou and Zhao Lin'er is reaching its climax. The question of whether Xiao Chen will execute them both hangs heavy, but given the narrative patterns of the novel, a dramatic intervention may be coming. Pay attention to any external factors that might interrupt. The three skeletons continue to evolve as characters; their clicking jaws could be a sign of communication or simply a habitual response. Watch for them to develop more personality. The revelation about fused magic points to a larger system of power that may explain some of the strange abilities encountered on Dragon Island. This could be a seed for future plot points. The moonlit atmosphere is a signature element of this arc; the contrast between peaceful natural beauty and violent death is a hallmark of the author's style. Expect this to persist.
