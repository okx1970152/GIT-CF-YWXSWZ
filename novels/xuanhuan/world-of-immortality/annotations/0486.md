## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers an awe-inspiring display of Ancestor God-level combat as the enigmatic 'cool little beast' – a projection of Keke's father – unleashes the legendary Three Realms Rotation technique against the Otherworld Ancestor God Li Huiyou. The battle transcends mortal comprehension: Li Huiyou attempts to reverse time itself, only to be countered with overwhelming confidence. The chapter also advances the search for Eternal Light, introducing the most evil place's inner sanctum and a mysterious protector who shields the Martial Imprint from two Ancestor God-level entities. The tonal contrast between cosmic destruction and Keke's innocent reunion with her father's echo is masterful.

## Key Plot Points

Li Huiyou initiates a time reversal technique to drag the cool little beast into the Primordial Era and kill it before it can achieve power. The little beast calmly walks through the chaotic spacetime, deflecting the Doomsday Heavenly Halberd with a single claw. It then invokes the Three Realms Rotation, a supreme divine art that splits heaven, earth, and the underworld into three separate worlds, grinding Li Huiyou's Ancestor God body to dust despite his armor and the Heavenly Halberd's power. Li Huiyou's divine mind escapes into the second veil of the most evil place, aided by an unknown Ancestor God-level being. The little beast reforms as a stone tablet, tames the Killing Light, and gives Keke's group safe passage. Inside the second veil, they glimpse Eternal Light. The Martial Imprint is attacked by two Ancestor God-level beings and scorned as an 'ant,' but a mysterious, gaunt figure appears, defends the Martial Imprint, and sends both Ancestor Gods reeling with a blast of power.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Three Realms Rotation (三界轮转) is a supreme technique that manipulates the three realms of heaven, earth, and the underworld, reminiscent of the cosmic cycles in Daoist cosmology. The chapter also references the Six Paths of Reincarnation (六道轮回), another legendary divine art from Buddhist mythology. Ancestor Gods (祖神) in this world possess near-absolute power; a single incarnation can annihilate Half-Ancestors with ease. The 'most evil place' (最邪之地) is a dangerous realm where fallen Ancestor Gods' remains and weapons converge, and Eternal Light (永恒之光) is a legendary substance sought to forge indestructible artifacts. The Killing Light (杀戮之光) is a prehistoric beam of pure destruction that can be tamed by supreme beings.

## Reading Guide

This chapter raises critical questions: Who is the mysterious protector who defends the Martial Imprint? His gaunt form and sudden appearance suggest a hidden Ancestor God ally. What is the cool little beast's true relationship to Keke? It is clearly a remnant of her father, but its power and knowledge are terrifyingly vast. The Eternal Light is now within reach, but the Death World Ancestor Lord and the mysterious being who aided Li Huiyou remain opposed. The Martial Imprint's fate and the battle for the Eternal Light will define the next arc. Pay attention to the tamed Killing Light – it may become a key weapon for Keke's group.
