## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts gears from the dragon-filled wildernesses to the political intrigues of the mainland. Xiao Chen, after months of survival on Dragon Island and the bone sea, now faces a new kind of threat: the entitled arrogance of a noble from the Ligen family of Heavenly Emperor City. The conflict over the Little Defiant Dragon is swift and brutal, showcasing Xiao Chen's hard-won power and his ruthlessness when provoked. It also introduces the bustling center of the Southern Wastes, setting the stage for a new arc of power struggles and city politics.

## Key Plot Points

- Cadyro of the Ligen family, a noble from Heavenly Emperor City, spots the Little Defiant Dragon and recognizes its potential as a dragon bloodline. He attempts to capture it with his retinue of a battle guard and three female spiritists.
- Xiao Chen intervenes, claiming the dragon as his own. Despite Cadyro's arrogance and the presence of a Mortal-Shedding sixth heaven battle guard, Xiao Chen does not back down.
- A fight ensues. Xiao Chen easily overwhelms the guard, shattering his sword and twisting Cadyro's arm into an unrecognizable spiral with a single punch.
- The three female spiritists try to flee, but Keke returns and binds two of them with her holy light. The third is shot down by the Little Defiant Dragon's horn beam.
- Cadyro uses a flight scroll to escape, but Xiao Chen gives chase using the Undying Sky Wings, a new flight technique, intercepting and beheading him mid-air.
- Xiao Chen eliminates all witnesses to prevent any trace back to him, killing the remaining spiritist and the already-wounded guard. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen hiding for two weeks before finally reaching Heavenly Emperor City.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces mainland cultivator society, contrasting with Dragon Island's beast-focused world. The Ligen family is a major clan in Heavenly Emperor City, controlling the “Beast Fighting Palace” (斗兽宫), a center for taming and fighting spiritual beasts. The hierarchy among cultivators is emphasized: Mortal-Shedding sixth heaven is considered high among nobles, but Xiao Chen at eighth heaven easily dominates. The Undying Sky Wings technique, derived from the Undying Demon Art, grants Xiao Chen flight—a privilege typically reserved for high-level practitioners or spiritists. This ability gives him a decisive tactical advantage. Additionally, the mention of spiritists as a distinct class highlights the diversity of cultivation systems.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a transition: Xiao Chen is no longer an isolated survivor but a player in a larger world. The arrival at Heavenly Emperor City is a major milestone. Pay attention to how Xiao Chen navigates this new environment: he has made enemies of a powerful local family. The presence of legendary figures like Dugu Jianmo and others mentioned hints at future power struggles. The introduction of the city opens opportunities and dangers alike. Xiao Chen's cultivation is on the edge of breaking through to the Spirit-Treasury realm, and his new techniques like the Undying Sky Wings will be crucial. Keke's mysterious abilities and the Little Defiant Dragon's growth also remain threads to watch.
