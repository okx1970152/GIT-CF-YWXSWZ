## Chapter Overview

This chapter brings a fierce confrontation as Zhao Lin'er finally tracks down Xiao Chen, only to find him sheltered among a troop of intelligent giant apes who have accepted him as a neighbor. The moonlit forest becomes a stage for brutal combat, where royal grace meets primal fury. The emotional tone is one of desperate survival and calculated vengeance, with Xiao Chen using every resource at his disposal—including unexpected allies—to turn the tide. The contrast between the serene image of apes absorbing moonlight essence and the sudden explosion of violence encapsulates the duality of this savage world.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's three-day recovery is interrupted when Zhao Lin'er breaks through the Tyrant Dragon's territory to find him. Rather than flee, Xiao Chen strategically retreats to the giant apes' habitat, knowing the apes have accepted him after days of sharing food. The Four-Armed Blood-Red Ape, leading a dozen giant apes, is in the midst of cultivating moonlight essence—a practice that reveals their intelligence and spiritual awareness. Zhao Lin'er hesitates but ultimately attacks, using the Rainbow Sword Art to lethal effect. She kills one of the giant apes, piercing its chest with a rainbow beam. This act enrages the Four-Armed Blood-Red Ape, which is nearly invulnerable to her swordsmanship due to its diamond-hard body. As Zhao Lin'er becomes cornered by the enraged apes, Xiao Chen joins the fray, emphasizing that only fists and action matter now. The chapter ends with a tense multi-party battle.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The giant apes' practice of absorbing moonlight essence (月华) is a classic trope in Chinese mythology, where beasts and spirits cultivate by drawing on celestial energies to achieve intelligence and power. This chapter explicitly references '妖怪' (yaoguai)—spirit creatures who 'swallow the essence of sun and moon' to attain spiritual awakening. The Four-Armed Blood-Red Ape's nearly invulnerable body and its ability to snap a divine sword illustrate the apex of bestial cultivation within the Mortal-Shedding tier. The Rainbow Sword Art (彩虹剑诀) is a high-grade divine technique from Zhao Lin'er's royal lineage, manifesting multicolored energy beams capable of piercing even well-tempered flesh. The chapter also underscores the law of the jungle: even an elegant cultivator like Zhao Lin'er must contend with feral intelligence and raw strength.

## Reading Guide

This chapter raises the stakes for the personal conflict between Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er, while also deepening the bond between Xiao Chen and the ape tribe. Readers should note the apes' capacity for friendship and vengeance—they will likely remain a recurring asset or threat on Dragon Island. Zhao Lin'er's struggle against the apes, and her narrow survival hint at a future escalation of her enmity. The fact that Xiao Chen deliberately uses the apes as a shield shows his growing tactical cunning. Watch for how the Four-Armed Blood-Red Ape may repay Xiao Chen's hospitality in the coming conflicts. The subtle implication that the apes are not mere beasts but '通灵异兽' (spiritually awakened creatures) suggests a deeper connection to the island's mysteries and the balance between humanity and the wild.
