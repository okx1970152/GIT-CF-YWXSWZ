## Chapter Overview

After days in the savage southern wilderness, Xiao Chen and his companions stumble into an unexpected sanctuary—a valley of breathtaking beauty where no fierce beasts roam. Here, they discover a phoenix nest woven from divine treasures, guarded by a single enormous parasol tree. The allure proves irresistible to Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon, who descend upon the nest with characteristic recklessness. What begins as a simple theft of spiritual herbs escalates into a far more serious crime when the little beasts inadvertently scoop up a Phoenix egg. The arrival of the Phoenix Saintess transforms the episode from a comedic interlude into a high-stakes confrontation with one of the world's most ancient and powerful races. This chapter balances lighthearted mischief with the weight of mythic consequence, showcasing the diversity of the living world beyond Dragon Island.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen marveling at the serene, untouched landscape—a stark contrast to the violence of Dragon Island. The phoenix nest, built from materials such as Blazing Fire Golden Lotus and Silver Thread Divine Grass, is depicted as a living treasure trove. Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon, driven by hunger and greed, immediately begin stuffing herbs into their bags. Xiao Chen's warning comes too late; they have already taken a Phoenix egg. The flight is tense but futile, as the Phoenix Saintess easily tracks them and brings them back to the nest. In the confrontation, the Saintess initially sounds cold and vengeful, but she softens upon recognizing that the beasts belong to sacred lineages—Keke appears to be a divine beast of unknown origin, and the Little Defiant Dragon is a dragon king. She takes back the stolen Phoenix egg and all the pilfered herbs, but goes further, confiscating the three packs of herbs that Keke had collected from the mountains over the past days. This double loss infuriates Keke, who rages against the injustice. The Little Defiant Dragon, for the first time, unleashes a beam of light from its horn, attempting to challenge the Phoenix, but she dismisses it with a laugh, suggesting that her niece will fight the dragon when born. The chapter ends with the two beasts frustrated and the Phoenix Saintess amused, having taught them a firm but fair lesson.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The phoenix (凤) occupies a position in Chinese mythology equal to that of the dragon (龙). In the world of immortal cultivation, phoenixes are not merely beasts but divine beings with sentience, speech, and profound magical power. A Phoenix Saintess like the one encountered here likely belongs to a high-ranking bloodline, entrusted with guarding the clan's sacred eggs and nesting grounds. The construction of the nest from spiritual plants such as 烈火金莲 (Blazing Fire Golden Lotus) and 银丝神草 (Silver Thread Divine Grass) reflects the cultivation world's belief that growth environments influence the quality of progeny. Phoenix nests are often built on 梧桐 (parasol trees), a tree species said to be the only one phoenixes deign to perch upon. The Saintess's treatment of Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon as "sacred clan" indicates that these races carry an ancient nobility that transcends mere power, earning her leniency. This chapter also subtly introduces the mainland's hierarchy: even a Mortal-Shedding cultivator must bow to a Phoenix, underscoring that the journey has only just begun.

## Reading Guide

This chapter offers a rare moment of levity before the next trial, but its consequences will echo. The confiscated herbs represent days of effort, and Keke's fury is unlikely to be forgotten—this may drive her to seek even greater treasures to compensate. The Phoenix egg's mention raises the possibility of a future encounter with a young Phoenix, who might become either a rival or an ally. The Little Defiant Dragon's new horn beam technique signals his maturation; readers should watch for further dragon abilities as his bond with Xiao Chen deepens. Additionally, the existence of the Phoenix Saintess confirms that the mainland is teeming with beings far beyond the Mortal-Shedding realm, setting the stage for escalated conflicts and alliances. Xiao Chen's diplomatic handling of the situation shows his growth in navigating complex social dynamics, a skill that will prove vital in the upcoming trials. Pay attention to the Phoenix's laughter—her amusement may indicate that she has taken an interest in the group, for good or ill.
