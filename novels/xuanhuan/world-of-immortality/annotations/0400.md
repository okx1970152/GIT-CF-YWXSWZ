## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal moment in the Dead World arc, as Xiao Chen transitions from a lone hunter to a faction leader forging alliances. The chapter opens with the aftermath of Xiao Chen's recent conquests: he rescues Old Huang (the yellow diamond skeleton) and consolidates control over the eastern region. But the real surprise comes when the legendary Angel King, a five-colored skeleton who has not appeared in two decades, arrives not as an enemy but as a proposer of an unprecedented alliance. The stakes are elevated when the Angel King reveals the existence of a sleeping sovereign beneath the external regions—a being whose awakening would require the annihilation of all fire seed beings. Xiao Chen's visit to the God Village also yields a new asset: a cadre of talented young God Tribe warriors eager to train in the Nine Provinces. The chapter balances tactical consolidation with the looming threat of a sovereign, setting the stage for Xiao Chen's next major decision.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen begins by locating Old Huang, the yellow diamond skeleton who was not killed but captured by the Giant King. After rescuing him and restoring his fire seed, Xiao Chen orders him to join Sha Polang in unifying the eastern Dead World. The unification effort proceeds rapidly, with Sha Polang and Old Huang sweeping across the region. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen and Keke visit the God Village, where Xiao Chen's partially restored flesh-and-blood body astonishes the young God Tribe members. Through conversation with the elders Li Mu and Zhao Ying, Xiao Chen convinces them to let the young God Tribe warriors train in the Nine Provinces, gaining a powerful new fighting force. Just as these plans are underway, news arrives that the Angel King from the western Dead World is leading thirteen elite skeletons into the east, prompting Xiao Chen to return to Hundred Battle City. The Angel King's envoy is arrogant and is swiftly humbled by Xiao Chen, but the Angel King himself proves to be a five-colored skeleton super-evolutionary at the Perfected Man realm. Instead of fighting, the Angel King proposes a secret alliance: together they must kill a sleeping sovereign in the underground abyss of the external region, a being whose awakening would spell doom for all fire seed beings. The proposal is shocking—killing a sovereign—but the Angel King is sincere, and Xiao Chen is left to consider this dangerous path.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The hierarchy of fire seed beings in the Dead World is based on bone color and quality, from common white to blue, purple, yellow, and five-colored diamonds. Achieving the five-colored diamond body is a sign of a super-evolutionary, a being that has broken through normal evolutionary limits to reach the Perfected Man realm. The Angel King is one such being, and his appearance with thirteen powerful skeletons underscores his authority. The concept of a 'sovereign' (君王) in this context refers to a being even higher than the Perfected Man realm, perhaps approaching the Ancestral God level. The mention of a sleeping sovereign in the underground abyss recalls earlier hints about the Dead World's deepest secrets—forgotten ancient cities and entities that even kings fear. Xiao Chen's flesh-and-blood rebirth is also noteworthy: he has now gained enough life source liquid to regenerate visible flesh, allowing him to eat and interact more naturally. The God Village, a safe haven of the God Tribe, provides a potential bridge between the Dead World and the Nine Provinces, as its young warriors can now travel between realms using the Bone Well and divine rings.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up multiple threads for future development. The alliance proposal with the Angel King is a double-edged sword: if successful, it could eliminate a catastrophic threat, but it requires confronting a sovereign that even the Perfected Man realm Angel King fears. Xiao Chen must weigh the risk against the potential reward—perhaps the sovereign's fire seed or treasure could accelerate his rebirth. Meanwhile, the young God Tribe warriors are about to enter the Nine Provinces, where they will likely clash with the Hundred Races and provide Xiao Chen with on-the-ground intelligence and fighting strength. The unification of the eastern Dead World under Sha Polang and Old Huang gives Xiao Chen a stable base and a potential army. Readers should watch for how the Angel King's proposal interacts with Xiao Chen's long-term goal of returning to the mortal world to confront his enemies. The sovereign's awakening timeline remains unclear, but the urgency is palpable. Will Xiao Chen trust the Angel King, or will he see treachery in this sudden alliance?
