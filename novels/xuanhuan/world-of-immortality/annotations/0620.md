## Chapter Overview
Chapter 620 shifts focus to a tense, psychological confrontation between ancient powers in the Celestial Realm. The peerless Stone Man King Lu Zhan, a living fossil from eons past, sits motionless on a demonic mountain, exuding an aura of absolute dominance. A ninth-heaven Otherworld ancestral god reports his probing of the legendary ‘stone crow’—a disaster-star-like figure feared since antiquity—and finds it seemingly dead in a stone coffin. Yet when Lu Zhan moves to investigate, the stone crow erupts with overwhelming black flames and delivers a ferocious ultimatum, forcing the suspicious king to retreat. Xiao Chen and Keke observe from the shadows, recognizing the crow’s weakness masked by bravado. The chapter weaves together the themes of deception, ancient grudges, and the gathering of lore—most notably the revelation of Little Stone Emperor’s tomb—while setting up a daring ambush plot against Lu Zhan. The tone is mythic and brooding, with undercurrents of scheming that recall the darkest episodes of the Death World. This is a chapter of whispered secrets and measured risks, where the might of a Stone Man King is momentarily outwitted by cunning and nerve.

## Key Plot Points
Lu Zhan, one of the four founding Stone Man Kings of the Otherworld, receives a report from a ninth-heaven ancestral god who dared to test the stone crow. The crow appears as a cold corpse in a flame cave, but when Lu Zhan tears space to investigate, the crow unleashes a startling display: it transforms into a black sun, wields a Chaos Plantain tree, and declares it will devour any foe who dares challenge it. Lu Zhan, ever cautious, is briefly seen but withdraws after the crow’s blast incinerates a mountain. Xiao Chen, hidden with the Pangu Stone Token, witnesses this exchange. He later observes the old skull reassembling a bone body and collaborating with the crow—revealing their bluff to scare away the ancient king. The crow admits its weakness and its need to relocate. It also discloses that the location of Little Stone Emperor’s tomb has been discovered, drawing immense interest across the Celestial Realm. Xiao Chen then proposes a counter-scheme: use the jade token carried by the ninth-heaven ancestral god to summon Lu Zhan into a prepared killing array, aiming to permanently eliminate one of the four supreme Otherworld guardians. The alliance between crow, skull, and Xiao Chen is solidified, setting the stage for a high-stakes ambush.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Stone Man Kings represent the pinnacle of the Stone Man cultivation path—beings whose bodies have petrified into near-indestructible divine forms, granting them combat power that surpasses even Upper Ancestral Gods. Lu Zhan is a prime example: he has existed for billions of years, his presence alone enough to intimidate the Celestial Realm’s giants. The concept of ‘bluffing’ is central here: even a weakened being of legendary status can project overwhelming force through sheer presence and provocative speech, because no one dares test whether the bluff is real. The jade token used by the ancestral god functions as a ‘soul beacon’—a common trope in xianxia where a master can instantly manifest at a disciple’s location when the token is destroyed. The stone crow’s mention of having survived the Little Stone Emperor’s three calamities ties into a deeper lore of immortal tribulations and the survival of ancient monsters through cyclical cosmic destructions. The Chaos Plantain tree, mentioned as the crow’s treasure, is a nod to Daoist mythology where plantain leaves symbolize chaotic primordial energy. Collectively, these elements emphasize the dual nature of cultivation: raw power is often secondary to strategy, reputation, and the art of deception.

## Reading Guide
This chapter builds critical momentum for the upcoming confrontation between Xiao Chen’s faction and the Otherworld’s foundational powers. Readers should watch for the execution of the ambush plan: will Lu Zhan fall for the jade-token trap, or will his ancient instincts detect the ruse? The stone crow and old skull, though currently weakened, are now active allies—their full recovery could turn the tide. Also pay attention to the mention of Little Stone Emperor’s tomb; it is likely a major treasure site that will draw all Celestial Realm giants. Xiao Chen’s decision to target a Stone Man King head-on marks a shift from defensive survival to aggressive counterstrike. The dynamics between Keke’s tracking of the ninth-heaven ancestor and Xiao Chen’s coordination with the ancient powers create layered suspense. Finally, note Lu Zhan’s cautious nature—he is powerful but not reckless, making him a dangerous but potentially outmaneuverable foe. The next chapters promise a clash of wits and wills, with the fate of the Nine Provinces’ remnant forces hanging in the balance.
