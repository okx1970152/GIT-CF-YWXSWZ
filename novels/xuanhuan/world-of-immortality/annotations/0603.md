## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a powerful emotional turning point after over a hundred millennia of separation. Xiao Chen and his allies track Keke to the seven demon cities in the Death World, only to discover that the little beast has been trapped there for ages, repeatedly attempting escape and suffering grievous wounds each time. The narrative shifts from grim reconnaissance to a furious assault, driven by Xiao Chen's rage upon seeing a vial of Keke's blood. The reunion that follows is both heart-wrenching and triumphant, as Keke emerges from the demon cities to embrace her old friends. The chapter balances intense combat against a stone man and a super-ancestral god with tender moments of tears and laughter, highlighting the enduring bond between the protagonist and his loyal companion.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen captures an Otherworld half-ancestor near the seven demon cities and forcibly searches his memories. He learns that Keke has been sealed in that region for over a hundred thousand years, with seven failed escape attempts that each nearly killed her. Otherworld forces, including a stone man and a newly ascended super-ancestral god, have been guarding the area, hoping to claim the demon cities themselves. When Xiao Chen sees a jade bottle containing Keke's blood, he attacks in cold fury. He deploys the stone man puppet to engage the enemy stone man and activates the ancient scroll to severe the super-ancestral god's arm. At that moment, Keke appears from within the demon cities, warning of traps. A stone woman, True Wood Saintess, and another stone man king, Meng Degang, ambush him, but Xiao Chen charges through to Keke. Together with the stone man puppet, they fend off the enemies. Keke, now a super-ancestral god herself, casually seals the captured super-ancestral god. The chapter ends with a tearful reunion, as old friends gather around Keke, whose innocence remains intact despite her suffering.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The seven demon cities are a cluster of Primordial Demon Cities used by Stone Men for nirvana and rebirth. The Stone Man path is a grueling cultivation route that leads to a supreme stone body, and those who take it are among the most feared beings. The super-ancestral god (上祖神) is a tier above ordinary Ancestral God, representing a peak of power just below a true Stone Man. Keke's blood is shown to have immense value, used by the Otherworld to aid their own cultivators' breakthroughs. The ancient scroll is a relic from a previous civilization era, capable of trapping and annihilating even super-ancestral gods. The stone man puppet Xiao Chen carries is a refined corpse of a stone man king, a devastating weapon. The 100,000-year timeline underscores the aeons-long war between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld, with Keke enduring captivity for most of that span.

## Reading Guide

This reunion is only the beginning; the Otherworld forces remain in force around the seven demon cities. The stone woman True Wood Saintess and King Meng Degang are major threats who will likely return. The sealed super-ancestral god may be interrogated for intelligence. Keke's newfound power as a super-ancestral god will be crucial, but her childlike personality means she still needs protection. Expect future battles to involve the seven demon cities themselves, perhaps as a strategic asset. Xiao Chen's rage over Keke's suffering will drive him to more aggressive tactics. The ancient scroll and stone man puppet are now key weapons in his arsenal. Readers should also watch for the possible intervention of the heaven-defying clan elders, as Keke's awakening may draw their attention.
