## Chapter Overview
This chapter thrusts Xiao Chen into a desperate chase as three colossal Cyan Treants, commanded by the relentless Zhao Lin'er, pursue him across Dragon Island. Bloodied and battered, he barely escapes into a river, only to emerge into a world of new dangers: lurking cultivators eager to strike a weakened foe. His skeleton allies arrive just in time to drive off the predators, but a far stranger encounter awaits. Near the edge of the Death Swamp, Xiao Chen discovers a tiny, snow-white creature that seems impossibly innocent and pitiable. Yet when the three skeletons lay eyes on it, they are seized by primal terror and flee, abandoning Xiao Chen to face the unknown alone. This chapter marks the first appearance of the mysterious little beast who will become Keke, hinting at a power that even the undead fear.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen is relentlessly hunted by three Cyan Treants — ancient trees that have awakened into towering, green-glowing giants with immense strength. Despite his breakthrough in cultivation, he is overwhelmed by their combined natural force, sustaining serious injuries. He exploits the river to escape, but Zhao Lin'er halts the pursuit at the border of a Tyrant Dragon's domain, unwilling to provoke the creature. After surfacing, Xiao Chen senses hostile cultivators closing in. He puts on a show of strength, but the danger remains until the three skeletons — Qin Guang Wang, Yan Luo Wang, and Lun Hui Wang — arrive, tearing apart an ambushing tiger and scaring off the watchers. Grateful but exhausted, Xiao Chen lets the skeletons carry him toward the Death Swamp. On the way, he spots a small white beast with black gem-like eyes, making soft baby-like sounds. It appears orphaned and hungry, stirring his compassion. But when the skeletons see it, they panic, drop him, and flee into the swamp to play dead. Their reaction reveals that this seemingly harmless creature is something far more terrifying to the undead — a first hint of the beast's later significance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Treants (树人) are a classic mythological entity in many cultures, but in the context of World of Immortality, they are formed when ten-thousand-year-old ancient trees undergo a metamorphosis, awakening consciousness and gaining control over natural forces. Their strength is tied to the forest, making them formidable on their home ground. The Tyrant Dragon (暴龙) is described as one of the most aggressive dragon-king lineages, capable of challenging gods — a reminder that Dragon Island is full of hidden apex predators. The little white beast's appearance echoes the xianxia trope of powerful divine beasts disguising themselves in cute forms. Its cry of "ya-ya" (咿呀) resembles a human infant, a common device to evoke empathy and signal high intelligence. The skeletons' instinctive terror suggests that this creature may be a natural nemesis of undead beings, possibly possessing pure yang or sacred attributes.

## Reading Guide
This chapter introduces the character who will become one of Xiao Chen's closest companions — Keke. Pay close attention to the contrast between her adorable exterior and the fearful reaction of the undead; it foreshadows a latent power that will prove critical in later conflicts. Zhao Lin'er's alliance with the Treants shows she is building strength and will remain a persistent threat. Xiao Chen's injuries and narrow escape highlight his vulnerability and the need for greater power. The journey toward the Death Swamp sets the stage for deeper exploration of the island's secrets. Readers should also note the growing attention from other cultivators — Xiao Chen's reputation is spreading, and his enemies are multiplying. The meaning behind the skeletons' terror will soon be revealed, so stay watchful.
