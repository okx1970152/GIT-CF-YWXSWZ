## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal turning point in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey, set against the backdrop of an ominous storm that shrouds Heavenly Emperor City in an almost supernatural stillness. The atmosphere is thick with dread, evoking memories of the Dead City on Dragon Island. In this charged environment, Xiao Chen's long-dormant broken blade awakens, guiding him through a mystic sword dance that unlocks fragments of ancient battles. Keke's Sacred Tree unexpectedly reveals two legendary artifacts—the Golden Divine Halberd and the Black Iron Seal—that once terrorized Dragon Island. The chapter culminates in Xiao Chen's full breakthrough into the Spirit-Treasury realm, where he awakens his first divine ability: Melting Weapon into Body, a power that sets him on a path of unparalleled physical transformation.

## Key Plot Points

As the storm descends, Heavenly Emperor City falls into an uncanny silence, and Xiao Chen's broken black blade begins to vibrate violently, reacting to the city's ancient energies. Seized by an irresistible urge, Xiao Chen steps into the rain and performs a spontaneous sword dance, channeling a profound sword intent that cuts through the darkness. The storm's flashes reveal ghostly figures of ancient warriors battling in the sky, linking the city to a forgotten epoch. Keke, excited by the turmoil, causes the seven-colored Sacred Tree to release two sacred weapons: the Golden Divine Halberd and the Black Iron Seal, which soar into the sky and dispel the phantoms. The oppressive pressure lifts, and Xiao Chen experiences a physical and spiritual metamorphosis. His bones creak and flesh writhes as he finally stabilizes and elevates his cultivation to the full Spirit-Treasury realm. The breakthrough manifests his first innate divine ability—Melting Weapon into Body—which allows him to dissolve the broken blade into his flesh, merging its essence with his own. This not only marks his entry into a new realm but also sets a foundation for his future path as a 'divine treasure body' cultivator.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In the world of *World of Immortality*, cultivation is divided into major realms, with the Spirit-Treasury (识藏) realm being the second after Mortal-Shedding. This realm focuses on 'opening the body's treasure', unlocking latent divine abilities that are unique to each practitioner. These abilities are not learned but emerge naturally upon breakthrough, reflecting one's innate path and cultivation foundation. Xiao Chen's ability, Melting Weapon into Body, is an extremely rare and coveted divine ability that allows a cultivator to fuse divine weapons into their own flesh, effectively turning their body into a living artifact. This echoes the 'treasure body' (宝体) concept in Chinese mythology, where physical cultivation leads to near-indestructibility and the power to mimic or absorb other treasures. Additionally, the chapter reinforces the connection between Heavenly Emperor City and the Dead City—both are ancient cities from the same primordial age, hinting at a deeper cosmic design. The appearance of the Golden Divine Halberd and Black Iron Seal, which were sacred artifacts in the Dead City, further ties the two locations together and suggests that Keke's Sacred Tree may be a vessel for such relics.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets the stage for a new phase in the story. Xiao Chen's breakthrough to the full Spirit-Treasury realm dramatically increases his power and unlocks his first unique ability, which will likely become a cornerstone of his combat style. The Melting Weapon into Body ability raises intriguing questions: can he fuse other legendary weapons into his body? Could he eventually surpass even the God-Slaying Spear or the Golden Divine Halberd? Meanwhile, the reappearance of the two sacred artifacts signals that they are not lost but hidden within Keke's tree, hinting at a larger purpose or a future confrontation when they are needed. The storm's connection to the ancient city also foreshadows that Heavenly Emperor City holds secrets that may soon erupt into the open. The mysterious phantom battle and the city's oppressive aura suggest that the city itself is alive with residual power. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's reputation grows now that he has firmly entered the ranks of true realm-cultivators, and how the nobles and experts of the city will react to this new power player.
