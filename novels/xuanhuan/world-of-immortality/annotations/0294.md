## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal emotional turning point in Xiao Chen's journey. After years of separation, he returns to the perilous Dragon Island, only to be confronted with what appears to be irrefutable evidence of Keke's death. The desolate atmosphere of the Forbidden Sea and the island's deathly stillness mirror Xiao Chen's inner turmoil. Yet, in a sudden reversal, the chapter delivers one of the most poignant reunions in the story, as Xiao Chen finds Keke alive, sleeping safely atop an ancient, decayed dragon. The tone shifts from crushing despair to overwhelming relief, all while maintaining the mythic, tragic register that defines the novel.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen crosses the Forbidden Sea alone, landing on Dragon Island after years away. He immediately searches for Keke, using his enhanced speed and spatial abilities to navigate the treacherous landscape. Along the way, he encounters and slays the same silver centipede that once terrorized him, a stark reminder of how far he has grown. His search grows increasingly desperate as he finds only ash piles and signs of another presence on the island. The emotional climax comes when he discovers a blood-stained patch of white fur clinging to a thorn bush—fur that unmistakably carries Keke's scent. Convinced of her death, Xiao Chen experiences a rare moment of emotional collapse, his grief so intense that his blood vessels burst from the strain. He is then attacked by a massive sword dragon and a silver winged dragon, forcing him to flee. Wandering aimlessly, he arrives at the base of the Sacred Mountain, where his broken stone man suddenly points the way. Guided by the relic, Xiao Chen ascends the mountain and finds a hidden oasis containing two young dragon kings and, astonishingly, an ancient, withered dragon. On that dragon's neck, he sees a sleeping ball of white fur: Keke. His desperate call and promise of treasures finally rouses the little beast, and the two are joyfully reunited. The chapter ends with Keke flying into his arms, the terror of loss vanquished.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Dragon Island remains one of the most mythically rich and dangerous locations in the World of Immortality. The Forbidden Sea that encircles it is a golden, lifeless expanse that completely seals the island, and only legendary vessels like the Ancestral Dragon Ship or the King Ship can cross it. The dragon clan's evolutionary struggle is vividly depicted here: young dragons—including kings—engage in brutal combat from birth, winnowing the weak to strengthen the bloodline. This Darwinian harshness is the root of dragon supremacy. The Sacred Mountain, blood-red and desolate, is a place of ancient mystery and lethality; its palaces connect to the underworld, and only the stone man's intervention allowed Xiao Chen to approach safely. The stone man itself, a fragment of a primordial being, demonstrates its first true power by shifting the palace ruins aside—a hint at the vast, dormant potential within Xiao Chen's body. Keke's survival on the back of an ancient dragon suggests that even the most fearsome beasts on the island recognize the little creature's extraordinary nature.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets several important threads in motion. Xiao Chen's reunion with Keke will undoubtedly reinvigorate his resolve. The presence of a new generation of dragon kings—hidden reserves kept from the Ancestral Dragon Ship—signals that Dragon Island still holds untapped power. The ancient dragon who sheltered Keke is a character of obvious significance; its age and wisdom may hold keys to the island's deepest secrets. Meanwhile, the stone man's awakening points toward an impending revelation about its origin and its connection to the ancestral gods. Readers should watch for how Keke's safe return affects Xiao Chen's emotional state and whether the mysterious 'other person' still lurking on the island will soon make a move. The search is not over—only the first and most precious piece has been found.
