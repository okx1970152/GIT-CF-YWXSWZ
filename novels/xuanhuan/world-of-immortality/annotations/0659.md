## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks Xiao Chen's first deep infiltration into the Otherworld, the forbidden home of his enemies. The atmosphere is one of tense reconnaissance as he navigates a civilization built upon countless conquered realms. The discovery of a primordial, decaying beast living at the end of the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps reshapes the entire power structure of the conflict. This is not a chapter of direct confrontation, but of dread and revelation. The stakes are raised immeasurably as a being from before the age of the Five Emperors plots to survive by devouring the essence of the Heavenly Steles.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen crosses into the Otherworld, using the Five Emperor Tower to mask his presence. He quickly learns of the continent's four distinct regions: the Divine Demon Region, the Science and Civilization Region, the Ancient Prison Zone, and the Ancient Ancestor Restricted Area. The capital city itself, vast beyond any demon city he has seen, unsettles him with its natural, oppressive pressure. After days of surveillance, he identifies a young man who has absorbed two Heavenly Marks. Following him, Xiao Chen witnesses a cyan-scaled, mountain-sized beast emerging from the end of the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps, claiming the young man as its tool. Overhearing a conversation between Lu Zhan and the young man, Xiao Chen learns that the beast is an entity from the primordial era, possibly an enemy of the Five Emperors. Its body is decaying, and it sought refuge in the Otherworld, using the young man as a potential vessel. The beast is collecting Heavenly Marks, a scheme that terrifies even Lu Zhan. Xiao Chen realizes he cannot act while the beast is so close; he decides to follow Lu Zhan and the young man to the Celestial Realm to find an opening.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps are a recurring symbol of ultimate power and taboo in the *World of Immortality* cosmology, previously the site of the ancient Otherworld gate and the imprisonment of Stone Man Kings. Here, they serve as a lair for a being from before the age of the Five Emperors—a time even more ancient than the Ancestral Gods. The beast's method of 'nurturing' a vessel by absorbing Heavenly Marks echoes earlier cultivation techniques like the 'Shatter Demon, Plant God' art, but on a far more sinister scale. The Five Emperor Tower, a legendary artifact capable of suppressing even the Source of All Evil, now serves as Xiao Chen's indispensable tool for concealment, highlighting its status as one of the world's supreme treasures.

## Reading Guide

This chapter lays the groundwork for the next major arc: the hunt for the Heavenly Marks and the confrontation with an enemy from the forgotten past. Pay close attention to the descriptions of the beast's decay and its fear of the Five Emperors. The young man's anxiety is a sign of weakness that Xiao Chen may later exploit. The impending journey to the Celestial Realm promises answers about the beast's origins, but also new dangers. Will the Celestial Realm giants ally with this ancient horror, or will they also see it as a threat? Xiao Chen's role as a phantom observer is about to end; the hunter will soon become the hunted.
