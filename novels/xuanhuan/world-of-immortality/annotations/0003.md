## Chapter Overview
This chapter bridges Xiao Chen's mortal past with his new reality on Dragon Island. Through a flashback, we learn of his decade-long cultivation of the Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram and his violent confrontation with the royal guards of Zhao Lin'er. The tone is one of desolate determination—Xiao Chen is not a triumphant cultivator but a reluctant fugitive burdened by incomplete knowledge. The chapter's centerpiece is the awe-inspiring arrival of the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon, a primeval beast that dwarfs all mortal conceptions of power. The contrast between the tranquil island evening and this monstrous apparition sets the grim, mythic tone for his journey.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen's ten-year cultivation of the mysterious stone carving is validated through his recollection of a brutal battle in the Kunlun Mountains, where he killed thirteen royal guards and the personal maid of the Royal Celestial Maiden Zhao Lin'er to escape persecution. This flashback establishes the deep enmity between them and reveals that his cultivation method, while genuine, feels incomplete. On the island, he heals steadily by absorbing natural essence. As dusk falls, the legendary Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon emerges from the sea—a fifty-meter-long silver leviathan with eight clawed arms, a single horn, and rows of bone spikes. Its earth-shaking roar terrifies the island's wildlife. Xiao Chen, displaying rationality, retreats deeper into the forest to observe from safety. The dragon basks on the beach under the moon and departs at dawn. Xiao Chen is left stunned by the scale of power in the World of Immortality, understanding that his journey has only begun.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon draws on Chinese mythological traditions of primordial beasts (上古凶兽) that rival gods and command natural forces like storms and tides. Such creatures often guard ancient secrets or territories, and their presence signals the elevated danger of the cultivation world. The Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram from the Yellow River Divine Stele is a classic xianxia trope: a mysterious inheritance from a divine artifact that grants extraordinary power but may be incomplete, driving the protagonist toward further exploration. Xiao Chen's method of absorbing sunlight, moonlight, and wood essence (草木精气) reflects the Daoist practice of refining natural energies into personal power, a foundation of cultivation lore.

## Reading Guide
The appearance of the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon establishes that Dragon Island is a land of primordial horrors, not merely a paradise of spiritual treasures. Xiao Chen's suspicion that his cultivation technique is incomplete will likely propel him to seek other divine steles or ancient inheritances. His enmity with Zhao Lin'er, now implied to have entered this world as well, foreshadows a future confrontation. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen adapts to the island's brutal ecosystem—his caution and rationality are his greatest assets. The dragon's nightly return hints that this beach is its territory, a detail that may become crucial.
