## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal turning point for Xiao Chen as he weathers a night of relentless assassins and duels while undergoing a profound physical and spiritual metamorphosis. The atmosphere is thick with tension and dread, as multiple factions test his strength and resolve under the cover of darkness. Xiao Chen’s cold efficiency in killing and his refusal to be cowed by noble arrogance or hidden threats set the tone for a chapter that balances brutal combat with the sacred radiance of cultivation breakthrough. The reappearance of the three skeletons and the intervention of ancient powers hint at deeper political currents in Heavenly Emperor City.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with four attackers of the Mortal-Shedding Sixth Heaven being swiftly slain by Xiao Chen wielding the Basilisk Ancient Spear, a weapon steeped in divine blood. The Azure Dragon King, visibly corrupted and hostile, charges at the Little Defiant Dragon, igniting a fierce battle that showcases the little dragon’s mastery of ancient dragon combat arts. Despite the Azure Dragon King’s superior divine abilities, the Little Defiant Dragon defeats it using pure martial skill, tearing off a chunk of flesh and finally downing it with a beam from its horn. Meanwhile, a mysterious woman shrouded in black mist observes, accompanied by a golden-furred monkey that proves strong enough to briefly resist Keke’s rainbow light—a first for any opponent. Keke ultimately overwhelms the beast, and the woman reveals she came only to test her pet. A Mortal-Shedding expert from the Ligen family is captured and interrogated, then executed despite his confession, cementing Xiao Chen’s ruthless streak. The three skeleton kings return with an old spiritist, who together with a shadowy figure under the grape arbor—likely a Spirit-Treasury realm expert—engage in a tense standoff. The shadowy figure retreats when Xiao Chen claims ties to a being from the deepest Southern Wastes. Xiao Chen spends the entire night standing in the courtyard, bathed in transformative radiance, taking his first step toward the Spirit-Treasury realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Spirit-Treasury realm (识藏) is the second major cultivation threshold after Mortal-Shedding, where the practitioner opens the inner treasure of divine consciousness and begins to unlock the mysteries of the soul. This chapter illustrates the brutal politics of Heavenly Emperor City, where powerful families like the Ligen clan and the Mande family vie for influence, and young elites are tested through open combat and assassination. The Basilisk Ancient Spear (巴斯蒂古矛) is a divine artifact stained with the blood of defeated gods, its inscriptions glowing with ancient power. The golden monkey’s ability to resist Keke’s rainbow binding art highlights the diversity of sacred beasts in this world—descendants of gods or primordial entities. The three skeleton kings, now under the tutelage of the old spiritist, continue their evolution toward kingship, their lotus brands a mark of divine transformation. The appearance of a Phoenix feather as a protective token reinforces the sacred status of the Phoenix race and their role as guardians of hidden secrets.

## Reading Guide
Xiao Chen’s breakthrough into the Spirit-Treasury realm is imminent, setting the stage for a major power leap. The suspicious corruption of the Azure Dragon King foreshadows a deeper conspiracy involving Liu Mu’s disappearance and possibly the influence of an ancient evil. The shadowy figure under the grape arbor—likely a powerful patriarch of the Ligen family—has been temporarily deterred but will surely seek revenge, especially after the death of five of their Spirit-Treasury experts. The invitation from the mysterious woman to journey north remains an open thread, hinting at greater opportunities and dangers beyond the Southern Wastes. The old spiritist’s cryptic return of the three skeletons suggests they are being groomed for a significant role. Keke’s growing power and the golden monkey’s resistance raise the possibility of more divine-beast conflicts in future tournaments. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen’s newfound cultivation level and his reputation as a ruthless killer will affect his standing in Heavenly Emperor City’s volatile power structure.
