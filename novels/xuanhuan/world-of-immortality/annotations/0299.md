## Chapter Overview

Chapter 0299 delivers a seismic shift in the narrative, unveiling the secret that the Buddha and Jieyin Daoren are two halves of a single being through the legendary Fate Twins divine ability. This revelation reshapes the understanding of the dead city and its monumental history. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen demonstrates his terrifying growth by instantaneously killing a Tiger demigod, solidifying his status as a force to be reckoned with. The chapter also introduces an ancient, decrepit dragon that dares to lift the entire dead city and the heavenly stele itself, threatening to unearth secrets buried for eons. As half-ancestors from various traditions scramble to claim ancient weapons beneath the city, the stage is set for a cataclysmic confrontation that could rewrite the world's balance.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen faces an ambush by Hai Yunxue and a Tiger half-god near the dead city. With cold efficiency, he uses the Eight-Aspect Worlds and the Supreme Hand to sever the half-god's head in one blow, shocking the attackers. Hai Yunxue and her forces retreat when the Pure Land's Xuanwu ancestor arrives to counter the pressure of the White Tiger Saint Emperor. The spatial bracelet Xiao Chen possesses is confirmed as the key to accessing the mortal world, making him a target for those desperate to leave Dragon Island.

On the dead city wall, the Buddha confronts a sinister, disheveled Daoist: Jieyin Daoren. Through a series of exchanges and a magical clash involving the Twelve-Grade Lotus Platform and the massive Dharma Wheel, it becomes clear that the two are linked by the Fate Twins technique. Jieyin Daoren is the 'evil child,' revived when Buddha drew near the weapon, and he chooses to separate from Buddha, asserting his independent identity. This revelation forces a radical reinterpretation of the Buddha's nature and the history of the Western teachings.

Simultaneously, Primordial, Tongtian, Zhunti, Chi You, and Laozi furiously dig at the foundation of the dead city, uncovering sealed weapons: a seven-colored evil sword, an ancient staff, an iron dagger, and a shield. Their excitement is palpable until an ancient, nearly dead dragon emerges, shouldering the entire city wall and the heavenly stele in a desperate attempt to overturn them. The dragon's power rivals that of an Ancestral God, shocking all onlookers. However, the sudden manifestation of multiple heavenly stele projections forces the dragon to relent, and the city and stele settle back down, leaving the half-ancestors frustrated but with the artifacts they unearthed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Fate Twins (命运双生子) is a rare divine art that splits a cultivator into two independent beings—one good, one evil. It is nearly impossible to kill the practitioner as long as either avatar exists. The revelation that the Buddha and Jieyin Daoren are such a pair is momentous, as Jieyin Daoren is a figure from the classic novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods), where he is the first patriarch of Western Buddhism. His resurrection here as the evil child of Buddha recontextualizes ancient scripture and provides a new layer of depth to the power structure of the World of Immortality.

The dead city is revealed to be a prison for the dragon clan, with a ghost dragon still alive beneath it. This dragon's ability to lift a mountain-sized wall and a supreme heavenly stele demonstrates the terrifying potential of the sealed race. The half-ancestors' excavation of weapons—such as the Zhuxian swords and unknown artifacts—echoes the mythological story of a primordial war where such weapons were buried. The dragon's resistance against the steles suggests a cosmic order that prevents the dead city from being fully overturned.

## Reading Guide

Watch for the implications of the Fate Twins on Chu Xingkuang and other characters who possess this ability. The separation of Jieyin Daoren from Buddha may lead to a new faction or conflict in the West. The ancient dragon's appearance hints at a deeper rebellion among the sealed dragons, and its failure today may foreshadow a larger uprising. The weapons the half-ancestors excavated (the seven-colored sword, the staff, the dagger, and the shield) are likely to become major artifacts in the coming battles. Xiao Chen's spatial bracelet is now a key to the mortal world, making him a target for anyone desperate to leave Dragon Island. His swift kill of the half-god shows his combat prowess is beyond standard expectations.

The philosophical debate between Buddha and Jieyin about independent vs. part of a whole echoes the novel's ongoing themes of self and destiny. Pay attention to how the half-ancestors' alliance shifts as they secure these ancient weapons.
