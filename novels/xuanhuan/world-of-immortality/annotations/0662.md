## Chapter Overview

Chapter 662 is a chapter of profound revelation and sweeping time. The ancient enemy of the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps is driven back by the nine ancient lamps, their attack repulsed. Then, thousands of years pass in a single breath. The legendary heroes of the Nine Provinces—Chi You, Laozi, Xing Tian, Zhuangzi, Yao, and Shun—return from their long absence, signaling a potential counteroffensive. But Xiao Chen remains apart, devoting himself entirely to the comprehension of the six Heavenly Steles he has gathered. His meditation culminates in a startling breakthrough: the six steles merge into a wheel that echoes the Six Paths of Reincarnation, yet his insight transcends that, revealing the true cycle of material transformation. This enlightenment carries him to the seventh Heavenly Stele, where deeper secrets stir.

## Key Plot Points

The ancient being who attacked the Nine Provinces is repelled by the nine lamps, shedding blood and losing a claw, before retreating with the stone steps. Over the following millennia, the nine lamps protect the realm, and Xiao Chen continues his solitary cultivation. After ten thousand years, the peace is broken when the stone steps and that being return for another assault, but they are forced back again. Xiao Chen learns that the entity has preserved itself by refining the Otherworld's first divine city. In the subsequent eras, more ancient heroes return: Chi You, Laozi, Xing Tian, Zhuangzi, Yao, Shun, and others, all having achieved great power. Ge Qian (the Human Demon) attacks Lang Ya in the Celestial Realm, sparking a war that severely damages the Otherworld before it stalls. Xiao Chen remains focused on his cultivation, finally achieving true comprehension of the first six Heavenly Steles. The six Heavenly Marks manifest as six miniature steles, each bearing a foundational image: a celestial palace, a hell gate, a human world of toil, a scene of gods and beasts, a blood-sea, and a disaster realm. They merge into a stone disc resembling the Six Paths of Reincarnation, but Xiao Chen realizes the true meaning is not about soul-transmigration but about the cycle of matter—life to death to new life, a natural regeneration. This enlightenment allows him to understand the seventh Heavenly Stele, which summarizes and transcends the first six. Dugu Jianmo, who has been sealed in the Curse Realm, emerges as an iron sword and departs into the void. The seventh stele reveals that the steles themselves contain flesh, blood, Dao imprints, and tombs, hinting at vast mysteries within.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nine Ancient Lamps are a key defensive artifact, first appearing early in the novel as seals beneath the Nine Provinces. They are attuned to the realm and can harm beings that exceed the world's limits, like the ancient being. The Heavenly Steles are the central mystery of Xiao Chen's cultivation path. Each stele contains a foundational diagram representing a different aspect of existence—heaven, hell, human society, primordial beasts, disasters, and the cycle of life. This echoes Buddhist cosmology with its Six Realms (deva, asura, human, animal, hungry ghost, hell), but the novel deliberately subverts this by having Xiao Chen reject literal reincarnation in favor of a material cycle: flesh becomes soil becomes plants becomes flesh. This is a more Daoist perspective rooted in natural transformation. The return of Chi You, Laozi, Xing Tian, and Zhuangzi—figures from Chinese mythology—signals that the Nine Provinces' greatest talents have been secretly accumulating power, preparing for the final confrontation.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's breakthrough is not the end but a beginning. The seventh Heavenly Stele hints that the steles are more than scriptures—they are containers. What lies sealed within them? The eighth stele's speculations (mentioned earlier) seem more plausible now. Dugu Jianmo's transformation into an iron sword and his departure leave his fate open; he may reappear in a critical moment. The war with the Otherworld has settled into a tense peace, but the ancient enemy remains active, and Ge Qian's gambit shows the conflict is far from over. With his new understanding, Xiao Chen may now seek the deeper secrets of the steles, perhaps even the eighth stele itself. His next move could change the balance of power. The return of ancient heroes also raises the stakes—will they unite with Xiao Chen, or do they have their own plans?
