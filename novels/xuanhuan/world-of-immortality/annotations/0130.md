## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a significant transition in Xiao Chen's journey on Dragon Island. After half a year at the Undying Sect, he has made substantial progress in mastering the Four Killing Hands and developed a new technique—the Imperishable Seal. Yet, his cultivation is punctuated by humorous interludes (a prank involving aphrodisiacs) and the ever-present tension with Yan Qingcheng. The chapter's emotional core lies in the Little Defiant Dragon's relentless battles against a Winged Dragon, and Xiao Chen's own desperate attempt to decipher flight techniques from the enemy. The culmination is a unexpected confrontation with two adult Winged Dragons, who propose a conditional bargain that ties Xiao Chen's fate to the stubborn little dragon's ancient combat arts.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen starts the chapter by playing a practical joke: he laces the food and tea with an aphrodisiac, causing chaos among the elderly recluses and Yan Qingcheng, who storms his residence in embarrassment. The prank backfires when Qin Yi's father discovers the tampered tea and punishes his son. This light episode contrasts with the serious cultivation that follows. Over four months, Xiao Chen masters the Slay God and Reverse Chaos techniques, and pioneers the Imperishable Seal—a condensed version of the Undying Sect's divine scripture. However, his effort to cultivate the Undying Heavenly Wings (不死天翼) remains frustratingly slow, producing only faint purple mist on his back. Meanwhile, the Little Defiant Dragon grows visibly: its single horn fully emerges, and it fights a young Winged Dragon each day, gaining strength through near-death injuries. Xiao Chen, inspired by observing the Winged Dragon's flight, captures the young beast to study its wing structure. This rash act is discovered by the adult Winged Dragons—both demigod-level beings—who transform into human form and confront him. Instead of punishment, they propose a deal: make the Little Defiant Dragon teach their child ancient dragon combat arts. Xiao Chen has no choice but to agree, setting up a new dynamic between the two factions.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter delves into the mechanics of cultivation and dragon lore. The 'Undying Heavenly Wings' is a rare flight technique guarded by the Undying Sect, requiring the practitioner to open back meridians. Its difficulty mirrors the rarity of flight among practitioners—even at the Mortal-Shedding realm, few can fly. The Winged Dragons' ability to assume human form (化形) is a hallmark of demigod-level (半神) beasts, a common trope in Chinese mythology where powerful monsters can take on human appearances after reaching a certain cultivation threshold. The Imperishable Seal (不灭印) is derived from the 'Divine Demon Imperishable Seal' (神魔不灭印), a pinnacle scripture of the Undying Sect. The Four Killing Hands (散手) Xiao Chen previously learned from the divine stele are now supplemented by this original creation, showing his growth as a cultivator who synthesizes multiple traditions.

## Reading Guide

This chapter plants seeds for future conflicts and alliances. The bargain with the Winged Dragons will likely force Xiao Chen to invest more in the Little Defiant Dragon's training—possibly unlocking deeper secrets about its unyielding nature. Yan Qingcheng's cultivation of the 'Demon-Shattering Divine Art' (碎魔种神大法) is nearly complete, which may either turn her into a powerful ally or a deadly foe given their complicated history. Xiao Chen's own progress on the Undying Heavenly Wings lags, but the direct observation of dragon flight may provide the breakthrough he needs. Readers should also note the growing reverence the Undying Sect elders show toward the Little Defiant Dragon—its survival through impossible odds hints at a hidden lineage. The stage is set for the next phase: Xiao Chen must negotiate the demands of dragons while managing his own cultivation ambitions and the looming threat of the Green-Haired Devil.
