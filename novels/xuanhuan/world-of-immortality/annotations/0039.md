## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a quiet but significant milestone in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey. After absorbing a second Snake-Elephant Dragon Egg, he successfully achieves the divinization of his third acupoint, the Central Court Acupoint, linking it with the two Shangqiu acupoints on his feet. This new harmony within his body visibly enhances his vitality, physical strength, and speed, bringing him to a new level of power within the Mortal-Shedding realm. Meanwhile, the mysterious Sacred Sapling demonstrates an unexpected autonomy—it moves on its own to draw essence from the remaining dragon egg, hinting at its sentient nature. Xiao Chen then sets out to resolve his lingering vendetta, seeking the Crimson Demon Ape for clues about Zhao Lin'er. The ape leads him to a mountain range but refuses to go further, suggesting dangers beyond Xiao Chen's current experience. The chapter balances personal growth with unfolding external mysteries.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen completes the refinement of the second Snake-Elephant Dragon Egg, fully divinizing the Central Court Acupoint (Zhongting) on his chest. The three divinized acupoints—both Shangqiu acupoints on his feet and the Central Court—begin to resonate, connecting through strands of life energy that strengthen his overall vitality. He senses a tangible improvement in his regeneration ability, speed, and strength; his cultivation level is now estimated at either the third or fourth heavenly tier of Mortal-Shedding. The Sacred Sapling, previously rooted in a host tree, is discovered by Xiao Chen's skeleton companions to have moved onto the last dragon egg on its own, absorbing its essence until the egg shatters empty. This suggests the sapling possesses a degree of will and self-directed purpose, making it far more than a passive treasure. Afterward, Xiao Chen allocates all future common beast crystals to the three skeletons for their evolution, reserving only dragon-grade crystals for his own acupoint path. He then visits the giant ape stronghold and, through careful gesturing, secures the aid of the Crimson Demon Ape to track Zhao Lin'er. The ape leads him westward across several mountain ranges but halts at a dangerous boundary, refusing to proceed, indicating formidable threats ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of "acupoint divinization" (穴道神化) is a unique cultivation method in Chen Dong's world, distinct from the more common realm-based progression. Instead of breaking through broad cultivation thresholds, Xiao Chen systematically infuses his 365 major acupoints with concentrated life essence, turning each into a "divine light point." This process grants him incremental but cumulative enhancements to his vitality, physical prowess, and healing capacity. The linking of three divinized acupoints suggests a network effect, hinting at a hidden potential yet to unfold. The Snake-Elephant Dragon Egg is a type of rare dragon egg, prized for its immense life essence; Xiao Chen's method of absorbing it whole and guiding the energy into specific acupoints demonstrates a pragmatic adaptation of his inherited cultivation art from the Yellow River Divine Stele. The Sacred Sapling's autonomous movement is a key moment—it implies that this divine tree is not merely a biological plant but possesses a form of consciousness, a common trope in xianxia where high-level spiritual treasures have nascent intelligence. The Crimson Demon Ape's refusal to proceed into the mountain range reinforces the theme of the World of Immortality as a place where even powerful local creatures have their territories of terror.

## Reading Guide

With the third acupoint divinized, Xiao Chen's personal power curve is clearly accelerating, but his external threats remain unresolved. Zhao Lin'er and Gu Luo are still at large, and the mountain range that the Crimson Demon Ape refused to enter likely holds a significant danger or secret—perhaps a powerful beast territory or a hidden relic of Dragon Island. Readers should pay attention to the Sacred Sapling's behavior; its self-directed movement hints at a deeper connection to the cosmos or to Keke, the divine beast who originally owned it. The skeletons' gradual improvement under the sapling's light also implies they may evolve into more formidable allies. The coming chapters promise a confrontation, but also the revelation of more about the island's hierarchy of powers. Keep an eye on Xiao Chen's strategic decisions: he is now confident enough to seek out his enemies directly, but the world keeps reminding him that there are always greater forces looming.
