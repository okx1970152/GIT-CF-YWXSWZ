## Chapter Overview

This chapter delves into the ancient legacy of Keke's mysterious clan. Guided by a mobile suit guardian, Keke confronts a towering stone statue—her great-great-grandfather—who exists in a liminal state between life and death. The chapter reveals that Keke's race is a 'cursed lineage,' pursued by the jealousy of heaven. Amidst this solemn revelation, Keke begins forging her own divine artifact, the 'Wan'er' bowl, using her Seven-Colored Jade Eggshells and the immense divine source provided by the clan's ancient city. The forging process is interrupted by a devastating blood clone sent by three Supreme Ancestral Gods, wielding legendary weapons.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Keke being guided by a green divine vine into the ancient city where a hundred-zhang stone statue stands. Keke's innocent questioning reveals that the statue is her Xuanzu (great-great-grandfather), a being in a state of suspended animation. A mobile suit figure explains that Keke's race was originally human but transformed into beast forms due to a curse of heaven's jealousy, with only a handful of members remaining. Keke then decides to forge her own divine artifact using the Seven-Colored Jade Eggshells from her birth, which are summoned across spacetime by the statue's nine vines. After rejecting comedic names, she settles on 'Wan'er' (little bowl) for her shape-changing artifact. However, the forging is interrupted by a blood clone dispatched by three Supreme Ancestral Gods from the Otherworld, accompanied by the Demon Blade, Immortal Shield, and Thirty-Three-Story Pagoda. The nine vines strike back, simultaneously attacking the three Supreme Ancestral Gods in the eternal void, causing their foreheads to crack and bleed. The enemy Ancestral Gods acknowledge the power of Keke's ancestor, but hint that someone exists who can counter him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In Chinese mythology and xianxia, the concept of a 'cursed race' or 'heaven's jealousy' (天妒) often applies to beings of extraordinary talent or bloodline who suffer calamities to maintain cosmic balance. Keke's clan being originally human but transformed into beast forms echoes themes from legends where divine punishment alters one's form. The forging of a personal artifact using materials from one's own birth (like the Seven-Colored Jade Eggshells) is a common motif—the natal item carries innate resonance with the owner. The Wan'er bowl, despite its humble name, suggests a vessel of containment akin to 'Paradise Lost,' capable of storing worlds. The mobile suit guardian (机动战士) introduces a sci-fi element fused with xianxia, indicating the clan's technology transcends eras.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes critical lore about Keke's origins and the power of her ancestor—a being who can wound Supreme Ancestral Gods from across spacetime. The 'cursed race' revelation deepens Keke's personal stakes, while her forging of the Wan'er bowl sets up a new artifact for her growth. Readers should pay attention to the interplay between Keke's childish innocence and the grim cosmic reality surrounding her family. The blood clone's attack is only the beginning; the Supreme Ancestral Gods' hint that someone can counter the stone statue suggests an even greater antagonist. The upcoming confrontation will likely test Keke's newly forged artifact and Xiao Chen's path as he prepares to return to the Nine Provinces.
