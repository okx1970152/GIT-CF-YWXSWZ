## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges the group into the depths of Xiao Shihuang's tomb, a labyrinthine construction of world nodes and deadly traps. The most shocking revelation is the projection of the Ancestral Dragon Ship, which the Little Defiant Dragon and Keke confirm as genuine—a phantom from the legendary vessel that carried the seeds of Nine Provinces civilization across worlds. The source of this projection is the Three Emperor Mirror, the third-ranked stone weapon of all heavens, capable of replicating any attack and projecting scenes from the past across time and space. The mirror's presence raises the stakes enormously, as multiple stone man kings have entered the tomb seeking it. The stone corpse appears as a direct threat, recognizing Xiao Chen and coveting his treasures. Amidst the chaos, the group navigates world nodes, encounters a suspicious old Daoist, and discovers a fragmentary map that may lead to the main burial chamber. The chapter masterfully balances cosmic lore with tense tomb exploration.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Little Defiant Dragon and Keke identifying the Ancestral Dragon Ship from a phantom projection within the tomb, confirming that the ship is not physically present but is a reflection captured by the Three Emperor Mirror. The stone crow and old skull explain the mirror's terrifying capabilities: it can reflect all attacks and even replicate them, and it can project images from the distant past across the void. This establishes the mirror as possibly the most dangerous artifact in the tomb. The group then faces the stone corpse, a powerful undead being from the Celestial Realm who recognizes Xiao Chen and threatens to claim his treasures. The stone corpse retreats when Xiao Chen activates the divine diagram with aid from the stone crow and old skull. The group proceeds through multiple cross-shaped world nodes, witnessing stone man kings fighting and the terrible aftermath of their battles—dozens of cultivators lie dead. A world node collapses, scattering the group, and Xiao Chen ends up with only Keke and the old Daoist. The old Daoist guides them to a hidden chamber containing a map fragment, claiming it leads to the main tomb. The old Daoist's behavior grows increasingly suspicious, as he seems to know the tomb's layout intimately. The chapter ends at a black river with a massive stele on the far bank, signaling that they are nearing the heart of the tomb.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Three Emperor Mirror is a legendary 'stone weapon' (石兵), a class of artifacts forged from primordial stone materials that rival the power of the Heavenly Steles. Its rank as third among all such weapons underscores its near-absolute defensive and replicative capabilities. In xianxia cosmology, such artifacts often have wills of their own and can project past events—a concept that blurs the line between memory and reality. Xiao Shihuang's tomb is itself a marvel of construction, using 'world nodes' (世界节点) that are actually pocket worlds or sealed dimensions used as corridors. This technique requires staggering power and knowledge of spatial laws, reflecting the Little Stone Emperor's status as a supreme being who once nearly achieved the final step. The map fragment found in a side chamber echoes classic tomb-raiding tropes, where adventurers must piece together clues to reach the central treasure, all while dodging traps and rival cultivators.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a classic dungeon-crawl dynamic within a xianxia framework. Readers should watch the old Daoist carefully—his intimate knowledge of the tomb and his calm demeanor amidst chaos mark him as a potential hidden agent, possibly linked to Xiao Shihuang himself or another scheming power. The stone corpse's retreat is temporary; he will return with allies or at a more opportune moment. The map fragment suggests that the main burial chamber contains the Three Emperor Mirror, but the path will be fraught with world node shifts and possibly other stone man kings lying in ambush. The absence of the stone crow and old skull after the node collapse leaves their fates open, but their lore about ancient weapons will be vital. Most importantly, the Ancestral Dragon Ship's phantom raises questions: was the original ship destroyed, or does it still sail somewhere beyond the void? The answer may connect to Keke's origins and the lost legacy of the Nine Provinces.
