## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks Xiao Chen's long-awaited breakthrough into the Spirit-Treasury realm, unlocking a profound and rare divine art: the ability to melt and refine weapons directly into his own flesh and blood. The narrative balances intimate cultivation detail with a grand, mysterious celestial event over Heavenly Emperor City, where sacred artifacts clash with ancient phantoms. The tone is both reverent—as Xiao Chen discovers the terrifying potential of his new power—and foreboding, as the city reveals depths of ancient power equal to Dragon Island's City of the Dead. The chapter also deepens the mystery of the Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal, two primordial weapons that now reside within Xiao Chen's hands.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen's breakthrough into the Spirit-Treasury realm triggers his first sealed divine ability: 'Refining Weapons into the Body' (熔兵炼体). This allows him to absorb and integrate weapons into his own corporeal form, transforming parts of his body into living armaments. He immediately tests the power by absorption of an ordinary dagger and some blades, finding his right hand takes on the cutting properties of the steel. The stakes escalate when a violent thunderstorm erupts over Heavenly Emperor City, accompanied by spectral visions of ancient armored warriors. The Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal, two of the sacred artifacts that once attacked the divine stele on Dragon Island, descend toward Xiao Chen's courtyard. He daringly attempts to refine them into his hands, succeeding only partially: the artifacts embed themselves as palm-sized marks but cannot be fully melted or controlled. The process shatters his previous refined weapons inside his body, and the Dark-Iron Seal even expels the black broken sword he carried. Finally, he discovers that Suiren's Fire-Drill, a seemingly ordinary stone tool, can also be refined alongside the two great weapons, revealing its true nature as a peerless sacred artifact. The chapter closes with the storm and phantoms vanishing, leaving only starlight and the lingering sense that Heavenly Emperor City holds secrets as formidable as any place on Dragon Island.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of 'Spirit-Treasury' (识藏) is central to the cultivation system in the World of Immortality. It represents the second major realm, where the practitioner begins to unlock the hidden treasures and divine abilities sealed within their own body. Unlike the Mortal-Shedding realm, which focuses on purifying the physical form, the Spirit-Treasury realm opens the 'sealed gates' of the body, granting unique supernatural powers. Xiao Chen's ability to refine weapons into his flesh is presented as a rare and extreme version of this concept, allowing him to transform his body into a walking arsenal. The 'Refining Weapons into the Body' technique (熔兵炼体) is a specific divine art that allows a cultivator to melt and absorb weapons into their flesh, granting the body the properties of those weapons. It can also theoretically absorb entire mountains or rivers at higher levels. The chapter also touches on the idea that sacred artifacts like the Golden Divine Halberd and Dark-Iron Seal are sentient to a degree, seeking out places of power and reacting to ancient energy. The appearance of phantoms of ancient warriors during the storm mirrors the phenomena on Dragon Island, suggesting a deep connection between the two locations.

## Reading Guide
This chapter serves as a major power-up for Xiao Chen, establishing a unique combat style that will likely define his fighting ability for a long time. Pay attention to the limitations: he cannot yet control the two sacred weapons properly, and they reject all lesser armaments. This sets up future challenges—how will he develop this art, and what will it cost him to master these dangerous relics? The mention that even the Basder Ancient Spear might not pierce his hands after this refinement hints at his growing physical invulnerability. The celestial phenomena and the appearance of ancient phantom soldiers over Heavenly Emperor City should not be overlooked; the narrative explicitly compares its hidden power to the City of the Dead. This suggests that the city itself is an ancient, living entity with a will of its own, and its secrets may intertwine with Xiao Chen's destiny. The successful absorption of Suiren's Fire-Drill—a seemingly humble object—alongside the two great weapons is a strong hint that the Fire-Drill is far more than a simple tool. Readers should wonder about its true power and its connection to Suiren, the Ancestral God of the Nine Provinces. The growing trust between Xiao Chen, Keke, and the Little Defiant Dragon is also evident in the casual negotiation over the artifacts.
