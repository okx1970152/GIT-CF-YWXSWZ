## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges the reader into a night of unprecedented violence and desperate ambition. Xiao Chen, driven by the crushing weight of his forty-five-year deadline to reach Deathless Longevity, makes a mad decision: instead of issuing a formal challenge, he will launch a solo assault on the Prince's Mansion, hunting down every one of his enemies. The tone is urgent, tragic, and resolute—a martial epic compressed into a single night. The opening contrasts the serene twilight of Xiao Chen's courtyard with the iron resolve behind his plan. A comic interlude with the white-shelled turtle lightens the mood briefly, but the narrative quickly accelerates into a brutal aerial confrontation between Xiao Chen and the Tianluo elites Li Daoyuan and Liu Ziliang. Every strike carries the weight of mortality; every technique is a gamble for transcendence.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen readies himself for ten consecutive battles, fully aware that he may perish—or break through. The white-shelled turtle, previously purchased at market, reveals extraordinary speed and sentience, beating both Zhuge Liang and Hai Yuntian with boxing-like strikes before fleeing. This brief absurdity underscores the world's hidden mysteries. At nightfall, Xiao Chen flies toward the Prince's Mansion. Using intelligence from Zhuge and Hai families, he finds his first target: Li Daoyuan, a famed spiritist of the Tianluo state. Their battle opens with a clash between Xiao Chen's Collapse technique and Li Daoyuan's Destruction Light—a deathly beam that drains life force. Li Daoyuan then transforms into a 'Hell Corpse,' channeling black mist and becoming a corpse-like being with superhuman durability. Xiao Chen counters with the Slay God technique, penetrating the darkness. A second enemy, Liu Ziliang, attacks from behind with lightning magic. Xiao Chen escapes the combined assault but has only begun his night of slaughter.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Li Daoyuan's 'Hell Corpse' (地狱邪尸) technique is a forbidden spiritist art that channels energy from the underworld. In xianxia cosmology, spiritists commune with elemental or metaphysical forces; this rare variant draws upon death qi, granting near-immunity to pain and flesh-like hardness but at the cost of human warmth. The technique borrows imagery from Chinese ghost lore—corpse rigidity, black mist, blood-red eyes—to create a figure that is more monster than man. Xiao Chen's Undying Wings (不死天翼) are a flight technique from the Undying Sect, manifesting as radiant energy wings that grant extreme speed and aerial combat ability. His reliance on the four Divine Stele techniques (Slay God, Reverse Chaos, Suppress Demon, Collapse) reflects his foundational path, which prioritizes direct, overwhelming power over esoteric arts. The 45-year deadline is a critical anxiety: in the World of Immortality, cultivation slows drastically after youth, and Xiao Chen's urgency is both tragic and practical.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes the first act of Xiao Chen's desperate offensive. The reader should watch for the consequences of his raid—the Prince's Mansion will demand retribution, and Li Daoyuan's death is a direct challenge to the Tianluo Kingdom. The white-shelled turtle's appearance is no mere comedy; its speed and sentience suggest a hidden lineage, possibly draconic, that may become significant later. Xiao Chen's survival against both Li Daoyuan and Liu Ziliang proves his rising power, but the night is far from over. The question lingers: can he sustain this pace across ten battles without fatal injury? The coming chapters will test both his combat mastery and his strategic foresight.
