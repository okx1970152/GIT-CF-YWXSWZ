## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers the long-awaited payoff of Xiao Chen's year of secluded cultivation. He faces Lumuda, an eight-heaven Mortal-Shedding expert wielding the legendary Yin-Yang Annihilation Hand, a technique that conjures a phantom hell realm. Xiao Chen counters with the Slay God technique—a divine imprint from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art—unleashing thousands of miniature celestial blades that shatter the illusion. The battle is a masterclass in esoteric combat, where two rare arts clash under the gaze of three great academies. Xiao Chen emerges victorious, but the victory comes at a cost: he is slightly wounded, and his growing reputation draws unwanted attention from noble families and royal soldiers.

## Key Plot Points

Lumuda of the Mande family opens with his family’s treasure technique, Yin-Yang Annihilation Hand. A dark and pale palm divide the sky, generating a vision of an ancient hell palace with a black door that seeks to devour the opponent. The technique is so powerful that it shakes the training grounds of Beidou Academy and is reputed to make even time and space spiritists retreat when used at the same cultivation level. However, Xiao Chen responds with the Slay God technique, the first of the four killing moves from the Divine Stele. This "divine imprint" manifests as countless tiny swords and blades of ancient design, each one a condensed strand of the Dao. They tear through the hellish vision in a cascade of light. After dozens of collisions, both fighters are wounded, but Lumuda falls from the sky, admitting defeat and acknowledging Xiao Chen’s mercy. The crowd erupts—Xiao Chen has now defeated four consecutive opponents, rewriting the youth rankings of the Southern Wastes. Afterwards, Zhuge Liang and Hai Yuntian urge an immediate retreat to avoid political fallout. Later, soldiers belonging to a prince attempt to harass Xiao Chen under a flimsy pretext. Xiao Chen does not back down, his murderous intent barely restrained. The chapter ends with unresolved tension, as Xiao Chen’s patience begins to wear thin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Yin-Yang Annihilation Hand is a classic example of a "clan treasure technique" in xianxia: a unique martial art that defines a family’s power and status for generations. Its ability to manifest a phantom realm reflects the concept of "yi jing" (artistic conception) in Chinese martial arts fiction—a technique that goes beyond physical movement to create a mental or spiritual reality that traps the enemy. The Slay God technique, on the other hand, represents the pinnacle of "divine imprint" cultivation: the idea that the highest arts are not techniques at all, but tangible marks of the Dao itself, requiring the cultivator to fuse their soul with the principle of the move. The chapter also highlights the formalized competition among academies in the World of Immortality. Beidou Academy serves as a neutral ground for young elites to test their strength, but also as a stage for political maneuvering among noble families. The intervention of soldiers from a prince signals that Xiao Chen’s actions have national-level implications.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen’s victory places him squarely on the map of the Southern Wastes’ powerful families. The Mande family will likely seek revenge, and the prince who sent the soldiers may become a recurring threat. The Slay God technique has been witnessed by many, and rumors of Xiao Chen’s power will attract both allies and predators. Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon remain his silent, mysterious companions; their own roles in the coming conflicts are yet to be revealed. Yan Qingcheng and the Undying Sect are still in the background, waiting to re-enter the narrative. Readers should pay attention to the subtle foreshadowing of larger forces at play: the mention of "Sword Demon Dugu" and other top-tier geniuses suggests that Xiao Chen has not yet faced the true apex of his generation. The next chapters may shift from open duels to shadowy conspiracies as the political landscape of Heavenly Emperor City adjusts to this new power.
