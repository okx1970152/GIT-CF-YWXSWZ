## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges Xiao Chen deeper into the abyss of ancient secrets as he is forcibly controlled by the mysterious demonic shadow. Forced to witness a primordial vision of a sovereign wielding the Three Sovereign Mirror, he learns that the perfect female corpse was cast out from the ultimate destination itself—the fabled end-point of the Ancestral Dragon Skin Map. The demonic shadow, born from the corpse's own black smoke, now seizes control and uses the stone jar to absorb the power of a colossal guardian beast. In a chilling climax, both the female corpse and Xiao Chen are thrown into the stone jar, where the shadow intends to refine a "sole dominating divine body"—a supreme physique that could reshape the heavens. The atmosphere is thick with horror and revelation, setting the stage for a transformative ordeal.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter begins with Xiao Chen's body acting against his will—his hand pulls a stone arrow from the female corpse's eye, then a stone dagger from her palm, each movement restoring her unearthly beauty. Struggling to resist the overmastering force, Xiao Chen is overwhelmed and plunged into a vision of the primordial age: a crowned sovereign riding a divine chariot, the Nine Lamps, the Heavenly Steles, and the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps all pointing to a mysterious ultimate realm. He learns that the female corpse was cast out from this very destination, her body pinned by the Corpse-Sealing Technique. The demonic shadow emerges from her as black smoke, then takes possession of Xiao Chen's body. Using the stone jar, the shadow journeys to a dark abyss guarded by an immense beast, siphoning torrents of divine power into the jar. Returning to the ancient hall, the shadow removes every stone weapon from the female corpse and throws her into the stone jar, then seals Xiao Chen inside as well—declaring his intent to cultivate the strongest physique and create a "unique divine body". The chapter ends with Xiao Chen falling into a darkness filled with thunder and clanking chains, his fate sealed alongside the ancient corpse.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Corpse-Sealing Technique (镇尸大法) is a forbidden art used to pin powerful corpses with stone weapons, suppressing their residual power or preventing resurrection. Here, the female corpse is bound by multiple stone weapons, indicating her extraordinary origin—she was thrown from the ultimate destination, the world at the end of the Ancestral Dragon Skin Map. That map, made from Ancestral Dragon hide, charts a path through sacred objects like the Nine Lamps and Ninety-Nine Stone Steps to a primordial realm beyond. The stone jar is more than a vessel—it acts as a cauldron for refinement, absorbing power from a beast that breathes star-destroying energy. The demonic shadow's goal of forging a "sole dominating divine body" (唯我独尊之躯) suggests a pinnacle path of physical cultivation, surpassing even the Stone Man or War Ancestor roads. This chapter reinforces the novel's recurring theme of ancient, hidden worlds and the manipulation of fate by beings from beyond known time.

## Reading Guide
As Xiao Chen enters the stone jar, prepare for a grueling trial of refinement—the jar has amassed immense power from an abyss guardian, and the demonic shadow intends to merge Xiao Chen with the mysterious female corpse. Can Xiao Chen resist becoming a vessel for someone else's ambition? Keep an eye on the visions: the sovereign with the Three Sovereign Mirror may be the Little Stone Emperor or another ancient king, and the ultimate destination may connect to the Primordial Celestial Realm. This chapter also deepens the mystery of the stone jar's origin and its connection to the Corpse-Sealing Technique. Pay attention to any future references to "sole dominating divine body"—it could signal a new cultivation goal for Xiao Chen or a threat to overcome. The coming chapters will likely focus on the internal struggle within the jar.
