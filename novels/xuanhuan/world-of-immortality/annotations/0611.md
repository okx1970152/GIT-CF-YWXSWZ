## Chapter Overview

This chapter shifts the scene to the Pure Land, a serene haven bordering the cursed Ancient Battlefield of the Death World. Here, Xiao Chen and his companions—Qingqing, the three Skeleton Kings, Keke, the Little Defiant Dragon, and Sha Polang—confront a new layer of danger: a malevolent entity has been using cursed forces and residual soul energy to subtly attack them during their cultivation. The chapter centers on the mysterious stone jar, a treasure Xiao Chen has acquired that proves capable of absorbing curses and soul power. When it nearly pulls Keke's soul out along with its curse, the group realizes the depth of Keke's ancient, double curse—a legacy from both her Heaven-Defying clan and her Ancestral Dragon mother. The revelation deepens the emotional stakes and pushes the group to venture into the Ancient Battlefield itself, a realm of ghostly perils and lost treasures, in search of opportunities and answers. The tone is tense and foreboding, blending cosmic horror with poignant character moments.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with a discussion among the Skeleton Kings about the nature of the Ancient Battlefield—a place where the strongest beings of the old era fell, its dark energies still potent. An attacker has been sending endless bone armies and cursing the Pure Land, hoping to steal the 'Dream Flower' (梦想之花) that Qingqing has merged with. The Skeleton Kings explain that the curse is a slow, insidious method left behind by a powerful remnant soul from the battlefield. Suddenly, Xiao Chen's stone jar vibrates and begins absorbing dark energies from the surroundings, revealing that the attacker had been leaving curse-imbued spiritual imprints. The jar also attempts to absorb Keke's soul when it senses the curse within her, forcing Keke to still its power. The Skeleton Kings recognize the curse as one of the most powerful from the previous epoch, inextricably linked to Keke's soul. They conclude that only by reaching the Stone Man King realm could Keke purge it herself. The jar's surface bears countless overlapping marks, and Keke's paw print accidentally matches a hidden 'plum blossom' imprint, activating a devastating curse beam that nearly destroys the dimension. Through careful planning, the group decides to enter the Ancient Battlefield. They glean that it holds remnants of Stone Men and a Heavenly Stele. Upon entering, they immediately flee from a floating ghost fire that is the remnant will of a Stone Man King, and eventually sense the presence of a Heavenly Stele far in the distance, setting up the next leg of their journey.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Death World (死界) is revealed to have been the center of all realms billions of years ago, a hive of power from which countless experts built their Primordial Demon Cities. The Ancient Battlefield is the site of the final war that shattered that age, and even today, only the strongest dare enter. The stone jar (石罐) proves to be a potent artifact that absorbs soul essence and curses. Curses in this setting are a forbidden, nearly extinct art, but can be left behind as 'thought residue' that attacks over time. Keke's curse is identified as one of the top five most powerful curses from the previous era—a legacy of the Heaven-Defying Clan (逆天家族) that was nearly wiped out by it. The curse has merged with Keke's soul, making it inseparable. The Skeleton Kings, having dabbled in curse arts, explain that such curses can only be broken by achieving Stone Man King level. The ghost fire (石人王鬼火) encountered in the battlefield is a semi-sentient emanation of a fallen Stone Man King, powerful enough to annihilate even Ancestral Gods instantly. This demonstrates that true Stone Man King-level power remains a realm of near-immortal danger.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up several threads for the future. The stone jar's connection to Keke's curse and the hidden plum blossom imprint suggest that the jar may be a key to understanding or even breaking the curse, but its powers are unpredictable and dangerous. The Ancient Battlefield will be a major setting—one that holds both tremendous rewards (like remnants of Stone Men and a Heavenly Stele) and extreme perils (like the ghost fire). Xiao Chen's decision to enter this realm indicates his growing desperation for power and answers. Keep an eye on the 'Dream Flower' and Qingqing's role; she remains behind but her treasure is clearly a target. The mention of a Heavenly Stele far in the battlefield hints that Xiao Chen may need to collect all nine Heavenly Marks to complete his path. The chapter also deepens Keke's character, revealing the extent of her suffering and the heavy burden of her bloodline. Readers can look forward to tense exploration, discovery of lost artifacts, and likely a confrontation with the remnant soul that has been hunting them.
