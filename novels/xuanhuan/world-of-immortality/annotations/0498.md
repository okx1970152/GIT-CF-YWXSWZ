## Chapter Overview

The chapter plunges readers into an epic confrontation as the Otherworld Ancestral God Li Huiyou attempts to force his way through the black divine gate. His sheer presence overwhelms even the Dragon Clan War God King, a peak Half-Ancestor, who is wounded despite using the sacred dragon horns. The scene is one of oppressive power and desperation — until a seemingly ordinary old farmer appears. With a humble hoe and mud-stained clothes, he effortlessly seizes the Great Annihilation War Spear and turns the tide, revealing that this war spear’s true wielder is far older and more mysterious than Li Huiyou. The farmer also unlocks Keke's wooden box, sending her hurtling into a radiant passage that may lead to the Celestial Realm. The chapter balances mythic horror with a touch of absurdity, reminding us that in the World of Immortality, the greatest powers often hide in the most unassuming forms.

## Key Plot Points

Li Huiyou, an Otherworld Ancestral God, emerges through a black divine gate with only his head at first, but his killing intent alone is enough to wound the Dragon Clan War God King. The War God King retaliates using the seven dragon horns — a sacred dragon clan weapon formed from the essence of seven Ancestral Dragons — and manages to strike Li Huiyou across the face. Keke's wooden box, emitting seven-colored light, also strikes Li Huiyou, adding to his humiliation. Enraged, Li Huiyou fully manifests in his spiked armor, wielding the Great Annihilation War Spear, and commands his descendants to hunt the gathered cultivators. Just as disaster looms, a mysterious old farmer appears, armed with a hoe. He nonchalantly sits on the divine gate, seals it with mud, and seizes the Great Annihilation War Spear from Li Huiyou. Demonstrating the true power of the spear, he wounds Li Huiyou twice — once through the heart and once through the throat — forcing the Ancestral God to flee. The farmer then examines Keke's box and opens it, unleashing a beam of seven-colored light that draws Keke into a cosmic passage, possibly the legendary Celestial Realm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

Ancestral Gods (祖神) are beings of supreme power in the World of Immortality's hierarchy, ranked above Half-Ancestors. Li Huiyou is introduced as a powerful Otherworld Ancestral God, but the chapter emphasizes that even his first incarnation is formidable. The Great Annihilation War Spear (大破灭战矛) is an Ancestral God weapon that was previously seen in the hands of Babra, another Otherworld Ancestral God. However, this chapter reveals that the spear's ultimate combat potential — the "True Meaning of Great Annihilation" — can only be unlocked by its original master, the old farmer. This farmer is implied to be a survivor from a previous cosmic era, a common trope in xianxia where ancient beings transcend the destruction of worlds. The divine gate through which Li Huiyou attempted to cross is a channel between worlds, and the farmer's mud seals it with the power of "Divine Mud" (神泥), a technique reminiscent of Nüwa's creation myth. Keke's box, which has been a mystery since Dragon Island, is finally opened, hinting at a connection to the Celestial Realm (天界) — a higher plane beyond the four known worlds.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a turning point for Keke's storyline. Her wooden box, teased for so long, is now open, and she is pulled into a beam of light. Readers should pay close attention to where she ends up — the old farmer suggests it may be a path to the Celestial Realm. Li Huiyou is driven off but not killed; two human Ancestral Gods from the Nine Provinces pursue him, so his fate remains open. The mysterious old farmer is a character to watch: he knew the Great Annihilation War Spear's true master and claims to have died long ago, yet still acts. His comment about "that little freak" regarding Keke's box hints that Keke's origins are even more extraordinary than previously thought. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen and the others are left in the Soul World, with the divine gate sealed and Li Huiyou temporarily dealt with. The chapter raises questions about the deeper layers of the Death World's history and the existence of beings from previous eras. Expect Keke's journey to reveal new powers or connections, and look for the old farmer to reappear when least expected.
