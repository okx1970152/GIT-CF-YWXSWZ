## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a powerful convergence of myth and emotion. Xiao Chen and his allies encounter the living Buddha Damo, a legendary figure who effortlessly destroys three otherworldly demi-saints through sheer spiritual force. The scene balances awe with light humor when Damo forces Bo Shi to confront his inner burdens. The emotional heart of the chapter belongs to Keke, who finally receives a keepsake from her long-lost mother. The box's multicolored light streaks across the heavens to clash with the Seven Devil Diagram, while an otherworldly ancestral god begins his descent. The chapter sets the stage for an imminent confrontation of cosmic proportions, blending personal grief with the gathering storm of war.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen's group finds three otherworldly demi-saints pinned in a decaying temple. The culprit is the ancient sage Damo, who has been meditating inside the main Buddha statue. Using only his spiritual pressure, Damo kills the three demi-saints without moving a finger. He then turns to Bo Shi, forcing him to shave his head and leave behind his guilt over his brother Yizhen's death. Bo Shi is left in the temple for cultivation. Damo identifies Jin Sanyi as a 'true Buddha' walking the world, causing the stuttering assassin to flee in panic. The group then visits Dragon Island, where the Dragon Clan War God King presents Keke with a small box left by her mother. Keke cannot open it by herself, but when she channels Paradise Lost, the box emits a dazzling multicolored light that soars across the world and crashes into the Seven Devil Diagram covering the Primordial Ancient Village. Simultaneously, an otherworldly ancestral god begins to emerge through a black divine gate, his presence shaking the sky for ten thousand li.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Damo (Bodhidharma) is a historical figure venerated in Chinese Buddhism as the founder of Chan (Zen) Buddhism. Legend holds that he meditated facing a wall for nine years, cutting off his eyelids to stay awake. In the novel's cosmology, Damo is a peak half-ancestor whose cultivation of the 'Great Prophecy Art' or similar supreme techniques allows him to kill through spiritual essence alone. The concept of using 'divine sense' (shenshi) as a weapon is a common trope in xianxia: a powerful cultivator can project their consciousness to attack an opponent's soul, and the loser often suffers complete annihilation. The Seven Devil Diagram is a foreign artifact used by otherworldly invaders to weaken the Nine Provinces' defenses, and Keke's mother's light seems to counter it directly, hinting at a primordial alliance or bloodline that opposes the otherworld.
