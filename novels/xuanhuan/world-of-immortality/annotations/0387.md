## Chapter Overview

In the desolate reaches of the Death World, Xiao Chen executes a cunning plan to protect Keke and seize the Ancestral God corpse poison. Having slain the Yellow Diamond Skeleton King, he dons the mantle of a new-born skeletal powerhouse and approaches the Zombie King and Wraith King. This chapter unfolds as a masterclass in deception, where Xiao Chen weaves a false narrative of betrayal by the War King to recruit two fearsome allies. The atmosphere is thick with the ancient malice of death-aligned beings, and the political maneuvering among undying races gives a glimpse into the broader conflicts of the Hundred Races awakening. Xiao Chen's strategic mind is on full display as he turns potential enemies into tools for his revenge.

## Key Plot Points

- Xiao Chen intercepts and kills the Yellow Diamond Skeleton King, claiming the Ancestral God corpse poison and the skeleton's spatial ring containing Life Source Liquid. He then impersonates a newly awakened skeleton from the Holy Bone Altar, using a fabricated 'flesh regeneration' technique to lower the Yellow Diamond King's guard before delivering a lethal blow with the 'Weng' primordial sound.
- After disposing of the corpse, he flies to the stronghold of the Zombie King and Wraith King, two powerful death-aligned monarchs. He introduces himself as a brother skeleton and proposes an alliance against the War King, claiming that the War King betrayed him and covets a sacred treasure—the White Bone Sovereign Banner.
- The Zombie King and Wraith King, despite their initial wariness, are lured by the promise of a half-ancestor-slaying artifact. They agree to join forces with Xiao Chen, but only after he convinces them that the War King must be eliminated first to secure the treasure. He also suggests hiding their identities during the assassination to avoid potential backfire.
- To solidify the alliance and prevent the War King from gaining allies, Xiao Chen proposes a preemptive strike against the Monkey King and Scorpion King, who are aligned with the War King. The three death kings set out to kill these two monarchs first, deepening their cooperation and eliminating threats before the main assault.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The Death World hierarchy is based on 'bone body' cultivation tiers: white, blue, red, purple, yellow diamonds. Xiao Chen is currently a Yellow Diamond skeleton, a rank that commands respect and fear. The 'Holy Bone Altar' on Skeleton Island is a sacred site where legendary warriors' bones are interred, capable of birthing new powerful skeletons.
- 'Ancestral God corpse poison' is a lethal substance sealed within a black skull, highly sought after for its ability to kill even half-ancestors. Xiao Chen's acquisition of it is a major power gain.
- The 'White Bone Sovereign Banner' is an artifact mentioned in the Yellow Diamond King's memories—capable of destroying half-ancestors. Xiao Chen uses this as bait to manipulate the Zombie King and Wraith King, though its actual location in the ancient ruins is uncertain.
- The Zombie and Wraith races are natural allies of the skeleton race, all originating from the 'Soul World' (魂界) of the Four Worlds. Their cooperation is based on ancient lineage and shared death-based cultivation, making Xiao Chen's deception plausible.

## Reading Guide

- This chapter sets up a multi-layered assassination plot: Xiao Chen first eliminates the Yellow Diamond King, then manipulates the Zombie and Wraith Kings into helping him kill the War King and his allies. Readers should watch for the inevitable confrontation between the death kings and the War King, where Xiao Chen may reveal his true purpose.
- Keke's safety is the central motivating force—the War King and his network of death monarchs are hunting the white beast. Xiao Chen's ruthless efficiency underscores his determination to protect her.
- The alliance is built on lies, and the Zombie King and Wraith King are not trustworthy. Their greed may lead to betrayal when the treasure doesn't materialize. Xiao Chen's contingency plans and his possession of the Ancestral God corpse poison could play a pivotal role in future conflicts.
- The upcoming battles against the Monkey King and Scorpion King will test the strength of this temporary alliance and reveal more about the politics of the Death World.
