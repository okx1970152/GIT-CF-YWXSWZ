## Chapter Overview
This chapter opens with a haunting scene: the Demon, a fearsome undead being, is moved to tears under the moonlight, revealing a vulnerable, melancholic side that belies his terrifying reputation. This intimate moment contrasts sharply with the brutal violence that often defines his existence. Xiao Chen, too, is caught in a rare moment of homesickness, longing for his family and the mortal world. However, the narrative swiftly shifts to the broader island politics: alliances are forming, and the search for the fabled Dragon Kings intensifies. The chapter masterfully balances emotional depth with strategic intrigue, showing the cost of isolation and the weight of memory on both mortal and immortal alike.

## Key Plot Points
The Demon confesses to Xiao Chen that the moonlight evokes a deep pain, as if he lost something precious in aeons past. He wipes away tears, acknowledging his growing humanity, but then coldly gives Xiao Chen a seven-day ultimatum. Xiao Chen, alone, allows himself to grieve for his family and the life he left behind, before steeling himself and resuming cultivation. The next day, he returns inland and meets Liu Mu, Yizhen, and their group, which now includes the captive Yan Qingcheng supervised by Liu Ruyan. They reveal that the island's cultivators have mobilized to find Dragon Kings, hoping to summon a divine ship and escape. They have already located one Dragon King. Xiao Chen shares that he spotted two in the dragon sacred mountain area. Yizhen then recounts how Dugu Jianmo, a powerful swordsman, cunningly tamed the Red Dragon King by saving it from a trap and then betraying his own allies. This exposes the ruthless pragmatism of the island's power games. Suddenly, Dugu Jianmo arrives with the Red Dragon King. The Red Dragon King is aggressive, and Keke hops onto its head, stomping and mocking it, immune to its attacks. Xiao Chen deduces that Keke is avenging a injured dragon from a previous night. The chapter ends with Dugu Jianmo's irritation and the others' satisfaction.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of 'Dragon Kings' (龙王) here refers to a specific lineage: the Companion Dragon Kings (伴生龙王) that accompany the Ancestral Dragon. They are not mere beasts but intelligent, powerful beings with their own pride and strength. The 'Divine Ship' (神船) is a key plot element: it is said that gathering nine Dragon Kings can summon this ship to leave Dragon Island, which is otherwise sealed. The demons and undead in this world often retain fragmented memories of their past lives, a common trope in xianxia where powerful beings may be reincarnated or accumulate karma over aeons. The Demon's tears evoke this theme of lost identity and the burden of immortality. Also, the rivalry between Dragon Kings reflects the hierarchical and competitive nature of the beast kingdom in this world. Keke's immunity to attack highlights her mysterious divine origin, previously hinted at in the Sacred Tree arc.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up major developments: the seven-day ultimatum from the Demon adds immediate time pressure, suggesting that Xiao Chen must either find a way to escape or confront the Demon soon. The gathering of Dragon Kings accelerates the plot toward the island's escape mechanism. Pay attention to Dugu Jianmo; his ruthless method of taming the Red Dragon King reveals him as a cunning and ambitious rival. Keke's continued defiance of Dragon Kings underscores her immense potential—she may be key to future conflicts. Also, Yan Qingcheng's captivity and Liu Ruyan's supervision add a layer of political tension. As alliances shift and personal grudges fester, readers should watch for signs of betrayal or unexpected cooperation among the young elites.
