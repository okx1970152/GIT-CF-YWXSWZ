## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a somber, lore-heavy revelation that reshapes the reader’s understanding of the war between the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld. Xiao Chen, now fused with the divine diagram, witnesses through ancient visions the cataclysmic fate of another world—one that was labeled “Chaotic Land” and its people “Sinners” by the Otherworld. The tone is one of deep tragedy and bitter inevitability, as Xiao Chen sees stone man kings sacrifice themselves to forge the very divine diagram he now carries. The chapter balances epic violence with heartbreaking pathos, showing the Otherworld’s relentless predatory nature and the desperate heroism of those who opposed them.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen absorbing the tragic truth that the world he wanders was once a prosperous realm reduced to a blood‑soaked wasteland by the Otherworld. A water‑blue giant (an Otherworld ‘killing tool’) battles a black wind that wields over a dozen ancestral god weapons. The giant’s body shatters, but its stone skull survives and charges into the blood‑colored light screen, only to be annihilated by the residual power within the ancient palace. The three Supreme Ancestral Gods in the Eternal Unknown sense the destruction and remark on the difficulty of conquering this place. An Otherworld Ancestral God is drawn into the light and utterly destroyed, his dying怨念 (resentment) confirming the Otherworld’s hand in creating this “waste world.” Through the divine diagram, Xiao Chen then sees visionary flashbacks: the ancient battle where stone man kings fought the Otherworld stone men, the failed attempt to complete the divine diagram, and the kings’ desperate decision to “use their bodies to fill the diagram”—immolating themselves to merge with the nascent artifact. The resulting explosion shattered the ninety‑nine stone steps and scattered the diagram across the cosmos, leaving only a fragment of the original world behind.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces the concept of “Chaotic Land” (亂地) and “Sinners” (罪人) as the Otherworld’s justification for conquest—a label applied to worlds they prey upon. The stone man path is central to the cultivation system: a stone man is a being whose body becomes increasingly petrified, and the “final step” is ascending the ninety‑nine stone steps to become a complete stone man, a near‑invincible sovereign. The divine diagram is not merely a weapon but a living entity born from the sacrifice of multiple stone man kings, making it a relic of immense emotional and historical weight. The three Supreme Ancestral Gods, first mentioned in earlier arcs, are now confirmed as ancient beings who have guarded the Otherworld’s highest secrets for eons, adding depth to the cosmic hierarchy of the novel.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a crucial turning point in the long war narrative. The tragic history of the Chaotic Land mirrors the Nine Provinces’ current plight, foreshadowing the stakes of the final battle. Xiao Chen’s deepening connection to the divine diagram—and his witnessing of its birth through sacrifice—will likely give him new insights into how to wield it against the Otherworld. Pay attention to the three Supreme Ancestral Gods and the ninety‑nine stone steps; these elements are not background lore but active threats that will resurface. The emotional weight of this chapter also reinforces Xiao Chen’s resolve and his growing understanding that some battles must be fought to the death, with no hope of retreat or mercy.
