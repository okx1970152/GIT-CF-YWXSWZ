## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers into the heart of a civilization-ending confrontation. The Otherworld's most powerful being, on the cusp of taking the ultimate 'final step' toward becoming a full Stone Man, is brutally interrupted by a mysterious stone-wielding figure. The attack shatters centuries of preparation and triggers a cascade of chaos that pulls in Ancestral Gods from both the Otherworld and the Nine Provinces. The Dead City—an ancient artifact of terrible power—maneuvers to block the Otherworld Ancestral Gods from returning, while Xiao Chen, seizing the moment, unleashes a second demon city from the Death World. The tone is one of apocalyptic urgency, tragic sacrifice, and the bitter cost of a single step between failure and transcendence.

## Key Plot Points

The opening thunderclaps signal a disaster of cosmic scale: the Otherworld's champion, already halfway into stone transformation, is attacked by a rival Stone Man wielding a colossal stone knife. The rival has willingly destroyed his own chance at the final step to prevent his enemy from achieving it first. The interruption is a devastating blow—the champion's incomplete form cannot be sustained, and all the accumulated power of the ancient city begins to dissipate. The Otherworld Ancestral Gods, enraged, attempt to return home but find their path blocked by the Dead City, which has flown across worlds to seal the route. This delay allows Xiao Chen, who has been following the battle, to act decisively. He drives the ancient demon city from the Death World into the midst of the stranded Ancestral Gods, grinding three of them into nothingness before the city itself begins to crack from the strain. Meanwhile, in the Otherworld, the stone attacker is being overwhelmed by combined enemy forces, his body shattered multiple times. Yet he holds on just long enough to ensure his rival cannot complete the step. The chapter ends with the three supreme Ancestral Gods of the Otherworld—dormant for ages—being attacked and beginning to stir, their awakening promising an even greater escalation of the war.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Stone Man' path is one of the most profound and dangerous cultivation methods in the World of Immortality. It involves a gradual petrification process that culminates in a final step where the cultivator becomes a being of indestructible stone, wielding the power of the earth itself. Interrupting this step is not merely a physical wound but a spiritual one—the practitioner may never be able to attempt it again, and the backlash can shatter his will. The Dead City, once a prison for the dead, now serves as a weapon of cosmic reach. Its ability to cross worlds and block Ancestral Gods demonstrates that even places of death can be turned into instruments of defiance. Xiao Chen's use of the second demon city from the Death World shows how far he has come: he now commands the remnants of an ancient civilization's power. The mention of 'borrowing the corpse to return the soul' (借尸还魂) hints at the possibility that a powerful being has taken over the Dead City's consciousness, raising questions about who truly controls it.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point in the war between worlds. The Otherworld's greatest hope has been crippled, but their supreme Ancestral Gods are now awakening, setting the stage for a final, desperate struggle. Readers should pay close attention to the fate of the stone attacker—his sacrifice may have bought precious time, but his survival is uncertain. Xiao Chen's ability to wield the demon city gives him new strategic options, but the city's cracking indicates that even such weapons have limits. The identity of the mysterious being who may have possessed the Dead City is a thread to watch. With the supreme Ancestral Gods entering the fray, the next chapters promise battles that will shake the very foundations of the cosmos. The clock is ticking, and every decision matters.
