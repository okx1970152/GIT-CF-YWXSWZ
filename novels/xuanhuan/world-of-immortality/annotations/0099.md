## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a dramatic pivot from the suffocating terror of the Sacred Mountain to the stark, hopeful wilderness of the western snows. The group's desperate gamble with an ancient blood altar pays off, transporting them to relative safety, but not without lasting cost. The central thread is Xiao Chen's personal evolution: he emerges from the ordeal not only alive but spiritually reborn, his cultivation foundation completed by acquiring the full Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram and mastering the first of the four legendary killing moves. The chapter balances collective survival with individual growth, setting the stage for the next phase of his journey.

## Key Plot Points
Discovery of a ten-meter-tall black-and-brown stone altar in the Sacred Mountain's desolate outskirts sparks a life-or-death gamble. With no other escape route, Xiao Chen and the survivors elect to perform a blood sacrifice, cutting their palms to activate the altar. The ritual triggers visions of the City of the Dead, the blood sea, the bone mountain—scenes carved into the Divine Stele—confirming that those engravings depict a real, coexisting realm. The altar's glow transports the group across space to the snowy mountains west of the Bone Sea, a region tied to the Sacred Mountain and the central Bone Sea in a mysterious three-point alignment. Once safe, the survivors part ways: Kailuo flees to avoid certain death at Xiao Chen's hands, Dugu Jianmo and others leave with their own agendas, while Yan Qingcheng remains captive under Liu Ruyan's watch. Xiao Chen immediately seizes the opportunity to practice the four killing moves—Slay the Gods, Reverse Chaos, Seal Demons, and Collapse—derived from the Divine Stele Heaven Diagram. He finds them immensely powerful but draining; after completing the first two, his incomplete qi method reveals itself, confirming the vital need for the lost sequel. Integrating the full Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram he obtained in the dead city, Xiao Chen achieves a harmonious breakthrough to Mortal-Shedding Seventh Heaven, his body suffused with divine light. The chapter closes on a quiet morning of shared roasted rabbit under the snowy sun, a brief respite after relentless danger.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Blood sacrifice is a recurring motif in Chinese mythology, often used to awaken ancient artifacts or altars. Here, the altar's activation through self-cutting underscores a belief that blood—as life essence—carries spiritual potency. The visions of the blood sea, bone mountain, and dead city directly echo the carvings on the Divine Stele, reinforcing the idea that the stele is not mere decoration but a 'map' of a real multiverse layer. The four killing moves—Slay the Gods (戮神), Reverse Chaos (逆乱), Seal Demons (镇魔), Collapse (崩裂)—are drawn from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art, Xiao Chen's signature cultivation method. Their names evoke concepts of cosmic order and divine combat. Mortal-Shedding is the first true cultivation realm in the Five Great Realms system, subdivided into Nine Heavenly Tiers. Xiao Chen's breakthrough to the Seventh Heaven places him securely among the upper echelons of his generation, while the complete Qi-Refining Diagram provides a stable foundation for future progress.

## Reading Guide
The chapter sets up multiple threads: the mysterious three-point alignment of Sacred Mountain, Bone Sea, and Snowy Mountains hints at deeper secrets still locked within Dragon Island. Xiao Chen's new cultivation plateau and potent killing moves promise accelerated growth, but his incomplete understanding of the moves' full power suggests a steep learning curve. The survivors' dispersal leaves alliances fragile—Yan Qingcheng remains prisoner, Liu Ruyan's loyalty is uncertain, and Kailuo's escape guarantees future conflict. Yizhen and Liu Mu remain allies, but Liu Mu's newfound space-cultivation techniques may be a wild card. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen integrates the four killing moves into his combat style, whether the stele carvings will guide further exploration, and what role the playful but perceptive Keke will play in the trials ahead.
