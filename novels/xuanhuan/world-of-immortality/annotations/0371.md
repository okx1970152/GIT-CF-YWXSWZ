## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen, now a Yellow Diamond skeleton from the Death World, enters Chang'an—the ancient capital of the Nine Provinces—and witnesses the plight of humanity under the newly ascended Hundred Races. The chapter contrasts the serene, moonlit splendor of Chang'an with the simmering despair of human cultivators, who find themselves oppressed by beings such as the Yaksha King and the arrogant sons of powerful non-human clans. Xiao Chen's presence as a disguised undead monarch adds a layer of tragic irony: a former human champion now exists between worlds, unable to fully rejoin his people yet still stirred by their suffering. The chapter crackles with tension as a drunken human, Old Xu, speaks truth to power and is nearly executed, only to be saved by an invisible force—Xiao Chen's wielded soul power. This sets the stage for Xiao Chen's ambiguous role as both observer and potential protector in a world that no longer belongs to humanity.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen, having reached the Yellow Diamond extreme realm, can now remain in the mortal world for extended periods. He disguises himself in iron armor and cloth to hide his skeletal form and seeks information at Chang'an's famous Misty Rain Tower. The tower is a gathering place for both human and non-human cultivators, its walls scarred by the techniques of powerful beings. He overhears a conversation about the dire state of humanity: the Yaksha King has slaughtered twenty-three human experts in a single night, and many human cultivators have defected to the non-human kings. A drunk man, Old Xu, publicly denounces the situation, calling the current age a 'golden age for beasts.' The First Prince of the Nine Provinces—son of the Nine-Headed Divine Snake—orders his guards to kill Old Xu. A young human cultivator intervenes and fights the prince to a standstill, but two Yaksha strongmen then appear and wound him. Xiao Chen, hidden in his disguise, unleashes a burst of soul power—the 'Weng' sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin—striking down the Yaksha without revealing himself. Meanwhile, other powerful beings observe: a Golden Dragon King and a War Clan king are present in adjacent rooms. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen's intervention unknown to all, preserving his anonymity while saving the humans.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'Hundred Races' refers to the myriad non-human species and bloodlines that have awakened across the Nine Provinces after the world's seal weakened. These include the Yaksha clan (fierce demonic warriors), the War Clan (an ancient battle lineage), the Divine Dragon clan, and many others. The Misty Rain Tower is modeled after the traditional Chinese 'literati pavilion' where poetry and martial arts meet—a common trope in xianxia. Xiao Chen's 'Yellow Diamond' skeleton form represents the highest tiers of cultivation in the Death World, where skeletons refine their bones through absorbing 'fire seeds.' His ability to use the 'Weng' sound—a primordial syllable from the Eight Syllables of Origin—demonstrates how Death World cultivation can synergize with his original divine arts. The chapter also highlights the concept of 'spiritual energy absorption' across worlds: Xiao Chen can now refine the natural essence of the mortal realm despite being an undead being, a breakthrough that allows him to train in both realms.

## Reading Guide
This chapter deepens Xiao Chen's emotional conflict: he is a dead being with a living heart, torn between his past humanity and his current nature. The introduction of the Hundred Races and their dominance sets up a broader conflict for the coming arcs. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's dual existence—as a Yellow Diamond skeleton and a former human champion—will allow him to operate as a hidden protector or a cold observer. The appearance of the War Clan and Dragon King hints at future alliances or confrontations. The 'Weng' sound is a reminder of the Eight Syllables of Origin, which may become crucial in his cultivation. The political situation in Chang'an, with the First Prince and his mother Empress Zhao Lin'er, suggests that human royalty is compromised, and Xiao Chen's next moves could shatter the fragile balance.
