## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen walks through the bustling streets of Yanjing, observing the vibrant mortal world after his long absence. He meets his old friend Fatty Zhuge, who has prospered in the garden business. At a reserved viewing platform, he overhears a group of young foreign elites boasting about killing him. When a servant attacks him, Xiao Chen casually defeats him. Wu Ming, the Multi-Treasure Heavenly King, arrives and publicly shows deep respect to Xiao Chen, forcing his son and the other young masters to address Xiao Chen as 'uncle', humiliating them. The chapter closes with Wu Ming mentioning the 'Martial Ancestor True Sutra', a revelation that shocks Xiao Chen and promises world-shaking developments ahead.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's arrival in Yanjing is marked by a contrast between the peaceful mortal life and the simmering tensions of the Hundred Races era. Fatty Zhuge's success shows the enduring bonds of old friendships in a changing world. The encounter with the young elites—scions of powerful foreign kings slain by Xiao Chen—illustrates how his legend looms over the new generation. Their boasting rings hollow when faced with the reality of Xiao Chen's presence, and Wu Ming's calculated deference forces them into a position of impotent humiliation. This public display reinforces Xiao Chen's status as a living myth, while also revealing the deep grudges that still burn in the younger generation. Wu Ming's mention of the Martial Ancestor True Sutra is a crucial exposition, hinting at a major conflict that will draw in ancient powers and possibly reveal more about the true history of the Nine Provinces. The chapter establishes that Xiao Chen's return will not be quiet; the past is not forgotten, and new challenges are already forming.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The term 'Martial Ancestor' (武祖) refers to a legendary figure who reached the pinnacle of mortal cultivation (祖神九重天顶峰). In the cosmology of the World of Immortality, Ancestral Gods are the ultimate symbols of power and civilization. Learning that the Martial Ancestor's true sutra has appeared indicates that one of the most profound inheritances has resurfaced, potentially changing the balance of power among the Hundred Races. The sequence in the garden also highlights the importance of 'face' in this cultivation society: Wu Ming's public deference to Xiao Chen is a calculated act that simultaneously elevates Xiao Chen's status and protects his own family. The young elites' humiliation is not just personal but political, damaging their families' prestige. Such social dynamics are central to the political maneuvering in the novel.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a bridge between Xiao Chen's return to the mortal world and the next major arc. The mention of the Martial Ancestor True Sutra signals that a significant treasure or revelation is about to emerge, likely drawing in half-ancestors and ancient powers. Readers should watch for how the young elites' grudges may fuel future conflicts, and how Wu Ming's alliance will play out. The balance between Xiao Chen's personal journey and the larger cosmic struggle is about to shift, and the next chapters will likely accelerate toward the conflict over the Martial Ancestor's legacy.
