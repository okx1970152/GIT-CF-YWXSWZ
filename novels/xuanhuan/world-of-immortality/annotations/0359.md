## Chapter Overview
This chapter sees Xiao Chen wielding devastating power in the Dead World, erasing an entire city with a single strike and advancing his cultivation through the mystical Eight Syllables of Origin. Yet the true shock comes when he returns to the Mortal World and confronts a double—someone identical to him standing atop his own petrified statue. The chapter balances explosive action, a personal breakthrough, and a chilling mystery that recontextualizes Xiao Chen's journey. The dead world trembles at his rise, but the mortal world greets him with an enigma that threatens everything he knows.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen uses a mysterious ancient artifact to utterly destroy Qisha City, reducing it to rubble and killing nearly all fire-seed beings within. The city lord, a three-tier Violet Diamond expert, barely escapes with half a body but is subsequently captured and his residual fire seed absorbed by Xiao Chen, propelling Xiao Chen from Violet Diamond first rank to second rank. Over the following year, Xiao Chen cultivates the 'Weng' sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin, strengthening his soul fire without absorbing external fire seeds. He reaches Violet Diamond third rank and even touches the threshold of Yellow Diamond. With this power, he assaults Pojun City, single-handedly killing its lord and three subordinate strongmen, taking the city. He endows his companion, the now Violet Diamond skeleton, with the Pojun lord's seed to continue his evolution. Xiao Chen then returns through the bone well to the Mortal World. But upon reaching the Eastern Sea, he finds his stone statue occupied by a figure bearing his exact likeness, laughing with world-shaking arrogance, while the Nine-Headed Serpent and Ye Jiuzhong nurse wounds before it. The appearance of this doppelgänger raises dire questions about Xiao Chen's true nature and the events unfolding in the Mortal World during his absence.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Eight Syllables of Origin (本源八音) are primordial sounds that predate Buddhist mantras. The 'Weng' (嗡) sound, taught by the Yellow Clay Altar, controls the power of life and death—an ability extremely rare in the Dead World where beings rely on fire seeds for survival. Cultivating soul fire without absorption marks Xiao Chen as having inherited a supreme technique usually reserved for the most powerful kings of the Dead World's interior. The dead world's hierarchy is built on fire seed ranks: Red Diamond, Violet Diamond, Yellow Diamond, etc. Absolute destruction of an entire city like Qisha by a single blow is a feat only legendary kings have accomplished, instantly elevating Xiao Chen's reputation. The appearance of a doppelgänger is a classic xianxia trope often signifying identity theft, dual bodies, or time paradoxes—here it suggests that Xiao Chen's stone statue has been inhabited by a sentient being replicating his form and power.

## Reading Guide
This chapter closes a major arc in the Dead World and opens a new, deeply personal conflict in the Mortal World. Xiao Chen's double is an ominous development that demands immediate investigation. Who is this being, and why is it using Xiao Chen's image? The Nine-Headed Serpent and Ye Jiuzhong's injuries indicate that even top experts of the Mortal World are helpless against this new threat. Readers should note that Xiao Chen's self has been split—his sealed soul in the stone statue may have awakened independently, or a malicious entity has taken his place. The coming chapters will likely explore the nature of identity, reality, and illusion, themes already foreshadowed by the fortune teller's warnings about the reversal of illusion and reality. Xiao Chen's ascension in the Dead World may be mirrored by a crisis in the Mortal World that tests his very sense of self.
