## Chapter Overview
This chapter pivots from intense combat to a lore-heavy quest for the legendary Eternal Light—a phenomenon formed from the souls of ten or more fallen Ancestral Gods. Xiao Chen, having mastered the War Swords and the Origin Sounds, now seeks a way to repair his damaged divine weapons. The chapter carefully lays out several potential burial grounds, each tied to a Heavenly Stele, deepening the mythic geography of the World of Immortality. The tone is one of solemn investigation and mounting tension, as Half-Ancestors and younger prodigies alike converge on the same secret. The Martial Imprint's irreverent commentary provides a rare moment of dark humor, but the stakes are unmistakable: the discovery of Eternal Light could elevate a being to Ancestral Godhood.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen infiltrates the Artifact Refining Sect and compels its elders to search ancient texts for clues about Eternal Light. The sect's records identify several prime candidates: Dragon Island's Sacred Mountain, the Demon Plain of the Eternal Continent, the Heavenly Burial Valley of the Cultivation World, the Dead City of the Nine Provinces, and the most evil place in the Soul Realm. Wu Ming nearly reveals the same conclusions but is outmaneuvered. Returning to Yan Capital, Xiao Chen witnesses the Martial Imprint's brutal evaluations of countless prodigies, including Half-Ancestors like the Yaksha Half-Ancestor and the Angel Holy God, whom it dismisses as unworthy. The Martial Imprint then publicly blurts out that Eternal Light exists and names the possible locations, sending Half-Ancestors racing across the realms. The chapter culminates with Xiao Chen himself becoming trapped in a mysterious ancient formation in the Soul Realm, facing a broken stele that records the slaying of an Otherworld Ancestral God.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Eternal Light (永恒之光) is a legendary substance that can repair supreme divine weapons and may even allow a cultivator to become an Ancestral God. Its formation requires the souls of ten or more Ancestral Gods to burn together in one place—an event nearly impossible across multiple civilization epochs, as Ancestral Gods are beings of limitless lifespan who can only be killed in battle and typically annihilate their own bodies and souls. The Artifact Refining Sect is depicted as an ancient repository of forgotten knowledge, possessing 'sacred scriptures' that record such esoteric data. The Martial Imprint (武之印记) is a remnant consciousness of the Martial Ancestor, a pure martial artist who achieved near-Ancestral God status. It evaluates cultivators with caustic honesty, claiming that only those with potential to become an Ancestral God are worthy of its teachings. The Soul Realm (魂界) is introduced as a world of perpetual yin energy, filled with ancient battlefields and sealed tombs.

## Reading Guide
The chapter sets up a continent-spanning treasure hunt that will define the next major arc. Readers should pay close attention to the five potential locations for Eternal Light, as each is tied to a Heavenly Stele and will likely be explored in sequence. The Martial Imprint's careless revelation has now alerted every Half-Ancestor in the world, turning the search into a race. Xiao Chen's encounter with the broken stele in the Soul Realm hints at a direct link to Otherworld Ancestral God warfare, suggesting that the 'most evil place' may indeed hold the key. The interaction with Wu Ming also reminds us that old rivals are still active and resourceful. The tone balances scholarly investigation with the looming threat of Half-Ancestor intervention, creating a tense, exploratory mood.
