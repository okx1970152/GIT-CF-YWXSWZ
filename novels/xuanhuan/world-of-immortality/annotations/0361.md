## Chapter Overview
This chapter unfolds the stunning confrontation between two iterations of Xiao Chen—the flesh-and-blood self born from residual soul fragments and the true body sealed within the colossal stone statue. The battle is a collision of worlds: the new Xiao Chen wields spatial and temporal divine arts, while the stone figure commands the absolute power of the Twenty-Four War Swords. After this fierce clash, a pair of Sacred Dragons descends from the hidden Sacred Dragon Island, leading to a legendary dragon hunt that shocks the Nine Provinces. The chapter balances epic combat with the grim return of ancient races, setting the stage for the Hundred Clans contention.

## Key Plot Points
The flesh-and-blood Xiao Chen opens with a daring assault, his left hand wielding space and his right hand time, striking the stone giant's head. The stone figure, a relic that once suppressed a spatial rift and resisted Ancestral Gods, effortlessly repels him with a wave of its sword, sending him bleeding across the sea. The new Xiao Chen then unleashes his Eight Aspect Worlds, expanding them to trap the giant, but the stone figure summons ten thousand bolts of purple lightning, shattering the illusion and wounding its opponent severely. A desperate "Return to Origin" technique nearly inverts reality, yet the stone giant's overwhelming might obliterates the divine art. Fleeing, the new Xiao Chen vows eternal supremacy over the war swords. Meanwhile, two Sacred Dragons—from a second Dragon Island—arrive and pursue the wounded clone, leading to a climactic battle near the capital. The clone ambushes and slays one Sacred Dragon, bloodying the sky and earning fame throughout the mortal world. The purple-bone Xiao Chen, newly emerged from the dead world, observes the battlefield and later encounters a War Clan youth who can see through his deathly essence.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter deepens the xianxia themes of selfhood and legacy. The flesh-and-blood Xiao Chen, born from a fragment of the true Xiao Chen's soul, represents a new person with the same foundation but a distinct will—a common trope in cultivation novels exploring identity. The "Return to Origin" technique (返本还源) draws on Daoist ideas of reverting to a primordial state, here weaponized to reverse time and space. Sacred Dragons are introduced as the unsealed dragon lineage from a hidden island, distinct from Dragon Island's sealed dragons; their existence parallels the Western myth of divine dragons and signals the coming of the Hundred Clans. The War Clan (战族) is an ancient race of warrior-sages, now emerging from seclusion, further thickening the world's mythos.

## Reading Guide
With the flesh-and-blood Xiao Chen wounded but triumphant over a Sacred Dragon, and the true Xiao Chen now actively observing from the dead world, the stage is set for a reckoning between the two selves. Will they unite or clash? The emergence of the War Clan, Sacred Dragons, and other ancient races indicates that the Nine Provinces are entering a brutal era of contention—the Hundred Clans tournament. Readers should watch for how the real Xiao Chen, now in a purple-bone form, will regain his strength and whether he will reclaim his legacy from his clone. The dragon slaying also ensures that the Sacred Dragon Island will retaliate, raising the stakes for all cultivators on the Eternal Continent.
