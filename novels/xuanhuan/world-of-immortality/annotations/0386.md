## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen strikes from the shadows of the Death World, returning to the Nine Provinces with a single purpose: to weaken the alien kings. With cold precision, he slays the Ant King in his own stronghold, using the Eight Aspect Worlds and the Weng sound to ensure utter destruction. Yet the victory is shadowed by a far darker revelation—a plot to murder Keke with ancestral god corpse toxin. The chapter balances the triumphant feel of a successful assassination with the creeping dread of a poison that could kill even a half-ancestor.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen infiltrates the Ant King's valley, his Yellow Diamond bone body now faintly threaded with blood, allowing him to merge with nature and evade detection. He waits patiently through the night, ignoring the arrival of War King and the Yellow Diamond Skeleton King who set an ambush. At dawn, when the Ant King is most relaxed, Xiao Chen strikes: Eight Aspect Worlds brings him within range, the Weng sound neutralizes the Ant King's defenses, and the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree cleaves off his head. He collects the body and refines its life essence, integrating it into his own skeleton. Returning to the Death World, he experiments with soul seeds to animate nearby bones as autonomous spies. Back in the mortal world, he uses animal vessels to gather intelligence and uncovers the conspiracy. War King and the Yellow Diamond Skeleton King intend to lure Keke with a spirit fruit laced with ancestral god corpse toxin, then have several kings attack together to ensure her death. Enraged beyond measure, Xiao Chen decides to shed his disguise and confront them directly.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Syllables of Origin are a recurring motif: the 'Weng' sound in this chapter functions as a devastating sonic attack that can counter divine abilities. The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree of Zhunti Daoist is here used simply as a heavy blade—a reminder that in Xiao Chen's hands, even a supreme artifact becomes a tool of raw violence. The ancestral god corpse toxin is a chilling concept: the lingering miasma from a dead Ancestral God, potent enough to kill a half-ancestor. The War Clan's Basde Ancient Spear is also unveiled as a true artifact, dripping with the blood of past victims, underscoring the apocalyptic scale of the Hundred Races conflict. The process of refining a king's life essence into a vial of liquid vitality is a grim but practical method of cultivation in this world, turning an enemy's flesh into one's own power.

## Reading Guide

The hidden blade that Xiao Chen sets into motion—his disguise as a Yellow Diamond skeleton—promises a bloody reckoning. With the conspiracy to poison Keke, the stakes rise from political maneuvering to an intimate threat against Xiao Chen's last remaining bond. Readers should watch for the clash between Xiao Chen's covert infiltration and his inevitable full-frontal assault. The Basde Ancient Spear's appearance hints that the War King is now armed with a weapon of devastating power; this may become a direct threat to Xiao Chen later. The chapter also reinforces the unforgiving hierarchy of the Death World, where even a skeleton's advancement is measured by the fire seeds of the fallen.
