## Chapter Overview

Chapter 397 of *World of Immortality* is a brutal, transitional chapter that bridges a major battle on the mortal side with a return to the dead world, setting the stage for Xiao Chen's long-awaited flesh-and-blood rebirth. The chapter opens with a desperate, multi-front confrontation between Xiao Chen (in his yellow-diamond skeleton form) and a coalition of foreign kings, including the fearsome Yaksha King and the Asura King. Keke fights alongside him, using the Paradise Lost to devastating effect. However, the pull of the Death World forces Xiao Chen to withdraw. He discovers that Paradise Lost can block the death world's summons, a crucial revelation. He then uses the Fallen Angel King as a test subject to prove that living beings can cross between worlds via the Sacred Tree. Returning to the Death World, he finds his conquered cities destroyed by the Giant King and a mysterious, powerful skeleton wielding four legendary swords. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen resolving to undergo rebirth, his will unbroken despite the overwhelming odds.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter's central conflict is Xiao Chen's struggle against the foreign kings in the ancient ruins. He and Keke fight fiercely, killing several kings, but are overwhelmed by the arrival of five kings at the Perfected Man realm: the Yaksha King, Asura King, War God King, Sea God King, and Thunder God King. Xiao Chen is forced to retreat, but not before taunting them. A critical discovery occurs when Xiao Chen realizes that the Paradise Lost can sever the Death World's pull, allowing him to stay longer in the mortal world and potentially bring living beings across. He tests this by capturing the Fallen Angel King and successfully transporting her to the Death World via the Sacred Tree. This breakthrough is pivotal for his future plans. Upon returning to the Death World, Xiao Chen finds his domain in ruins. His loyal follower Sha Polang, nearly destroyed, reveals that the Giant King, now allied with a terrifying skeleton from the inner continent wielding the Four Swords of Zhuxian (Immortal-Slaying, Trap Immortal, Extinguish Immortal, Slay Immortal), has annihilated their cities. The chapter establishes this new, immensely powerful antagonist and sets up Xiao Chen's resolve to undergo flesh-and-blood rebirth to reclaim his strength.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The chapter deepens the lore of the Death World, a realm of fire-seed beings where strength is the only law. The hierarchy is clear: from ordinary fire-seed creatures to skeleton kings (yellow-diamond, etc.) to kings at the Perfected Man realm (至人境界), and beyond to the mysterious sovereigns of the inner continent. The four swords wielded by the skeleton are a direct reference to the legendary Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation of Tongtian Jiaozhu from the *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods), a classic novel. Their appearance here signals that this skeleton is connected to the 'illusory world' lore of half-ancestors, suggesting a powerful remnant of that age. The concept of 'flesh-and-blood rebirth' is a central theme for Xiao Chen—it represents the return from death to true life, a process requiring immense effort and rare resources like life-source liquid and the essence of fallen kings. The Sacred Tree's ability to transport living beings across worlds is a game-changer, hinting at the tree's nature as a treasure beyond half-ancestor artifacts.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a major turning point. Xiao Chen is now on the verge of a physical rebirth, which will dramatically change his capabilities. Readers should note that the alliance against him is growing, but his discovery of Paradise Lost's new function gives him a strategic advantage. The appearance of the skeleton with the Four Swords introduces a threat that connects directly to the mythology of the half-ancestors and the Investiture of the Gods. Watch for how Xiao Chen's rebirth will affect his power and his ability to confront these new enemies. Keke's role is also evolving; she is no longer just a cute companion but a formidable combatant and a key to Xiao Chen's plans. The next chapters will likely focus on the rebirth process and the looming confrontation with the Giant King and the sword-wielding skeleton.
