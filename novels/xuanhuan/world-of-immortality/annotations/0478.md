## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges Xiao Chen and his allies deeper into the perilous passage toward the 'most evil place.' They traverse a surreal prehistoric plain, confront the lethal Ancestral God Yin Fog and a spectral Tianren Death Knight, and debate the layered history of extinct civilizations. A chance discovery of a ruined city built from divine materials triggers a devastating prehistoric Killing Light, forcing them into chaotic space-time where they glimpse Qingqing and the three skeletons—now revealed as the lost disciples of Suiren. The chapter expands the cosmic lore while tightening the tension of the journey.

## Key Plot Points

The group crosses a vast plain teeming with bizarre prehistoric creatures that defy underestimation. The Martial Imprint warns that any survivor from a previous civilization is a threat. They encounter a Tianren Death Knight leading a tide of Ancestral God Yin Fog, which the Martial Imprint can outrun but cannot easily neutralize. The discussion of the three great racial cycles—Human, Dragon, and Celestial (Tianren)—frames the world's deep history. The discovery of a metallic city built from supreme forging materials hints at a lost, highly advanced civilization. When they attempt to excavate, they trigger a prehistoric 'Killing Light' that nearly annihilates them. Forced into chaotic space-time, they catch fleeting visions: Qingqing drifting ahead and the three skeleton kings following, whom the Martial Imprint identifies as the three disciples of Suiren—a revelation that recontextualizes their origins and purpose.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of 'Zushen Yin Fog' (ancestral god yin fog) is a deadly residue of a fallen Ancestral God, previously encountered by Xiao Chen when trapped for 150 years. The Tianren (Celestial) race is described as having 'same-level king' status, indicating they were once the dominant species before the dragons. The 'prehistoric Killing Light' is a beam of pure destruction, likely a weapon or trap from a bygone era. The chaotic space-time is a dangerous realm where time and space are distorted; glimpsing Qingqing and the skeletons there suggests they may be in a different time layer or dimension. The identification of the three skeleton kings as Suiren's disciples ties the death-world narrative back to the ancestral human myths, enriching the novel's tapestry.
