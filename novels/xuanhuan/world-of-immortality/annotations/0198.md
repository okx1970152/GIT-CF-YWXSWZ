## Chapter Overview
This chapter serves as a moment of profound introspection and external tension. Xiao Chen, having reached the peak of Mortal-Shedding, grapples with the overwhelming pressure to forge a unique path into the Spirit-Treasury realm. His seven-day meditation and subsequent enlightenment mark a crucial mental evolution: he learns to let go of rigid intention and embrace natural flow, a lesson that will define his future cultivation. Simultaneously, the appearance of a mythical Heavenly Palace in the southern Eternal Continent stirs the entire region, drawing cultivators into a frenzy for ancient treasures. Yet the chapter's most personal threat arrives in the form of Long Teng, a legendary figure from thirty years past who delivered crushing humiliation to the Ligen family. His visit brings dire news: Keke has killed a white tiger cub belonging to a fearsome entity, and retribution looms. Long Teng offers to take Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon to the Southern Wastes for safety, but both refuse unless Xiao Chen accompanies them—setting up a fateful crossroads. The chapter weaves cosmic wonder with intimate peril, balancing epic discovery with the fragility of bonds that must now be tested.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen's meditation arc dominates the first half: he sits in contemplation for ten days, struggling to conceive a revolutionary cultivation method. He reaches a critical insight—that forcing a new path is itself a form of bondage. Instead, he must walk countless existing roads to transcend them. This mental breakthrough does not elevate his realm but transforms his perspective, granting him a newfound clarity and calm. Meanwhile, the Heavenly Palace discovery galvanizes the Eternal Continent. The Tianluo Kingdom becomes a nexus for elites seeking relics like the Dragon-Slaying Blade, Demon-Sealing Tablet, Sacrificial Altar, and Longevity Tree. Bloody battles erupt around the floating palace. Back in Heavenly Emperor City, the Ligen family patriarch Slof encounters the legendary Long Teng on Beast-Fighting Street, triggering old wounds. Long Teng then visits Xiao Chen's home, revealing his identity and warning that Keke's killing of a sacred white tiger cub has enraged powerful beings. He insists the two beasts must flee to the Southern Wastes immediately. Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon adamantly refuse to leave without Xiao Chen, creating an impasse. Long Teng grants three days for a decision, warning them not to leave the city. The chapter ends with the weight of choice: safety for the beasts, or unity in danger.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The appearance of a 'Heavenly Palace' (长生殿堂) draws from ancient Chinese mythology of floating celestial abodes belonging to immortals or ancestral gods. Such structures often emit radiant light and gather star essence, serving as tombs or relic depositories. The treasures mentioned—Dragon-Slaying Blade, Demon-Sealing Tablet, Sacrificial Altar, Longevity Tree—are legendary artifacts tied to primordial epochs; each name hints at its purpose: sealing demons, sacrificing to gods, or granting immortality. Xiao Chen's meditation epiphany echoes Daoist principles of 'effortless action' (无为) and 'natural flow' (自然). He realizes that clinging to originality creates 'attachment' (执念), which blocks genuine insight. This aligns with the cultivation philosophy that wisdom arises from experiencing myriad paths rather than forcing uniqueness. Long Teng is a classic 'hidden expert' trope: a figure who appears youthful despite decades passing, suggesting advanced cultivation and sealed aging. His past humiliation of the Ligen family and subsequent destruction of their arena establish him as a force of nature. The 'Southern Wastes' (南荒) represents a mysterious, untamed region beyond normal civilization, often home to ancient powers. The white tiger cub's death invokes the 'big background' trope—killing a young sacred beast triggers retribution from its powerful lineage.

## Reading Guide
This chapter plants seeds for multiple story arcs. The Heavenly Palace will likely become a major treasure-hunting site, setting the stage for Xiao Chen to eventually participate. Long Teng's appearance introduces a powerful figure from the Southern Wastes who may become an ally or mentor, but his motives remain ambiguous. The three-day deadline creates immediate urgency: will Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon accept protection, or will they stay and face an unknown but formidable enemy? The white tiger cub's master is hinted to be a 'fierce woman' or a 'powerful being'—likely a future antagonist. Xiao Chen's mental breakthrough is a quiet but essential milestone: his new 'heart realm' will be the foundation for his eventual breakthrough to Spirit-Treasury. Watch for how he applies this mindset in coming conflicts. The chapter also darkly foreshadows that leaving the city may be fatal, but staying invites danger. The next chapter will test loyalties and resolve.
