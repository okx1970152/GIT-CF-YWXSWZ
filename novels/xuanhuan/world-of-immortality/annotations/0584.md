## Chapter Overview
This chapter presents a desperate confrontation between the Old Stone Man King of the Otherworld and the defenders of the Primordial Demonic City. The Old Stone Turtle, having shown only wit and evasion earlier, now unleashes hidden combat power and summons a broken Stone Man King—a legendary figure who once challenged the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps and was shattered into nine parts. As the battle reaches a fever pitch, a simultaneous assault on the Eternal Unknown draws the Old Stone Man King's attention: the Martial Ancestor, Yan Huang wielding the Eternal Light, and a lone stone arm (the missing half of the broken king) strike the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. The climax arrives when the divine city itself disgorges a colossal stone axe, and the voice of Pangu himself shakes the battlefield. The chapter crackles with mortal urgency and mythic scale.

## Key Plot Points
The Old Stone Turtle goads the Old Stone Man King into initiating his ultimate technique, "Open Heaven, Bury All," which attempts to create a new world to entomb all enemies. In response, the turtle summons a broken Stone Man King—a remnant of an ancient warrior who was shattered during a previous assault on the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. Xiao Chen supplies the half stone skull to complete the broken king's head. The broken king fights the Old Stone Man King to a standstill, but as a mere remnant, he cannot prevail alone. Just as the Old Stone Man King seems to gain the upper hand, a thunderous blow from the Eternal Unknown distracts him: the Martial Ancestor wielding a stone knife, Yan Huang blazing within the Eternal Light, and a lone stone arm (the missing limb of the broken king) all attack the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps. The Old Stone Man King's attention is divided, allowing the defenders to regroup. Finally, the divine city releases a titanic stone axe, and a towering figure—the legendary Pangu—strides forth, shattering the "Open Heaven, Bury All" technique and confronting the Old Stone Man King directly. The tables have turned.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The "Stone Man King" (石人王者) represents the apex of the stone man cultivation path—a being of such supreme physical and metaphysical power that they can shatter worlds and challenge the very laws of existence. The Ninety-Nine Stone Steps (九十九重石台阶) serve as the ultimate trial and gateway to transcendence, guarded by the three Supreme Ancestral Gods of the Otherworld. The technique "Open Heaven, Bury All" (开天辟地葬人) embodies the fusion of creation and destruction: the user opens a new universe and immediately uses it as a tomb for their enemies. Pangu (盘古) is the primordial creator of heaven and earth in Chinese mythology; here, he appears as a war ancestor, wielding the axe that separated yin from yang. His emergence signals that the most ancient powers of the Nine Provinces are awakening to counter the Otherworld invasion.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major turning point in the arc. The simultaneous attack on the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps will force the Otherworld Supreme Ancestral Gods to divide their attention, potentially opening a path for the Nine Provinces' forces to strike deeper. The appearance of Pangu suggests that even the most legendary figures are willing to enter the fray when the stakes are highest. Readers should watch for the fate of the Old Stone Man King—his dual-front engagement may be his undoing. Also note the significance of the broken Stone Man King; his missing limbs are being gathered across realms, hinting at a future full resurrection. The next chapter will likely reveal the outcome of the attack on the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps and the full extent of Pangu's power.
