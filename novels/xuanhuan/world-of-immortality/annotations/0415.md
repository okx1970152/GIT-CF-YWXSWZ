## Chapter Overview
This chapter takes Xiao Chen deep into the immortal mountains of the cultivation world, where a chance encounter with an old adversary forces a confrontation that quickly spirals into mayhem. The appearance of the Undying Imperial Heaven Divine Bell signals that enemies of the past have not forgotten him, while the sudden intrusion of otherworldly kings turns a quiet bamboo grove into a battlefield. Beneath the surface, a greater mystery beckons: a stele shadow has manifested in the legendary death valley, drawing the attention of all factions.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen stumbles upon Wu Ming (formerly Ming Shi) and several cultivators guarding three Blood-Tear Divine Bamboo stalks. Wu Ming, who now wields the fully awakened Undying Imperial Heaven Divine Bell, surprises Xiao Chen by declining to fight. He reveals that a giant stele shadow has appeared in the forbidden Sky Burial Valley, hinting at a connection to Xiao Chen's own Heavenly Stele arts. Before more information can be exchanged, the otherworld kings – including the Yaksha Queen, Sea God King, Land King, and Mabao – arrive in force, eager to annihilate Xiao Chen. Wu Ming and the other cultivators are caught in the crossfire. Xiao Chen exploits the chaos, deliberately drawing the kings' attention to the cultivators to sow confusion. Wu Ming fights fiercely with his divine bell, killing two kings before breaking out of the encirclement. Xiao Chen uses his Eight Aspect Worlds speed to slip away, heading directly toward Sky Burial Valley to investigate the stele phenomenon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Undying Imperial Heaven Divine Bell is a supreme artifact from the Central World, comparable to the Golden Divine Halberd and the Dark-Iron Seal. It produces soul-shaking sound waves that can level mountains. Wu Ming's cultivation path is unique: he refines captured artifacts into his own body, making his flesh an even deadlier weapon. The Blood-Tear Divine Bamboo is one of the ten divine spiritual plants, containing pure wood essence and sought by those seeking to complete their elemental cultivation. Sky Burial Valley is a legendary death zone in the cultivation world – no one who enters has ever been recorded as returning alive. The sudden radiance and stele shadow suggest something has changed within, attracting the attention of both cultivators and otherworld kings.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a new narrative arc for Xiao Chen: the exploration of Sky Burial Valley and the mystery of the stele. Wu Ming's reappearance confirms that old grudges remain dormant but not extinguished – he is a calculating rival who may either become a temporary ally or a future threat. The otherworldly kings are now active in the cultivation world, raising the stakes for Xiao Chen's every move. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's Heavenly Stele connection may tie into the valley's secrets, and whether he will meet more familiar faces from his past in the depths.
