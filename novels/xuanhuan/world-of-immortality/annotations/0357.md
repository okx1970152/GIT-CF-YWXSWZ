## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen returns to the Death World after his journey to the Mortal World, his mind stirred by memories of home. The chapter opens with a lighthearted scene of the Red Diamond Skeleton King courting a goddess-like Painted-Skin woman, but quickly shifts to the grim business of trading the precious Yellow Diamond skull for a breakthrough. Then a transcendent event shatters the monotony: a woman's heavenly voice rings across the dead continent—a song of longing and triumph, revealing that she has reversed death and achieved true resurrection. This supernatural occurrence galvanizes Xiao Chen's resolve. He finalizes the transaction plan and sets out for the legendary Giant City, a domain of towering fire seed beings. The chapter balances domestic humor in the Divine Village with the desolate, mythic grandeur of the Death World's power struggles, culminating in a tense encounter with a five-meter terror king and the shocking sight of an ancient half-ancestor weapon—the Seven-Colored Evil Sword of Zhunti Daoist.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen returns from the Bone Well burdened by memories of his mortal family and the Nine Provinces. He finds the Red Diamond Skeleton King amusing himself with poetry, pursued by a Painted-Skin girl. After distributing gifts to the younger fire seed beings, Xiao Chen learns that Red Diamond has arranged a buyer—a powerful fire seed being in the eastern Giant City, a major safe hub for trade. But before the journey, the Death World is shaken by an eerie phenomenon: a woman's singing drifts from the continent's depths, the lyrics a poignant poem about the moon and separation. The voice is not spiritual fluctuation but true sound, indicating a being has revived flesh and blood. This 'Heavenly Voice' signals the rise of a new overlord who has reversed death, sending awe and fear across the land. Xiao Chen, profoundly moved, hurries to complete the trade. He and Red Diamond travel to the colossal Giant City, passing the declining Seven Kills City. Inside, they meet a terrifying fire seed king—five meters tall, with bone limbs and a torso covered in cyan scales, wielding crushing pressure. The king tests them but agrees to examine the Yellow Diamond skull. As the king rises from his throne, Xiao Chen spots a broken sword embedded in the seat: the Seven-Colored Evil Sword, once wielded by the half-ancestor Zhunti Daoist, now lying in the Death World.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter deepens the lore of the Death World, a realm where fire seed beings evolve by strengthening their bone bodies. The tiers of power are marked by bone color: white, blue, purple, yellow, and the legendary diamond ranks. A Yellow Diamond skull is a supreme treasure that can elevate a purple-rank being to a new level. The true terror of the Death World is the possibility of 'authentic resurrection'—a fire seed being regaining flesh and blood, becoming a living being once more. The woman's song (a famous poem by Su Shi) signals that she has achieved this, making her akin to a demigod or half-ancestor. The appearance of the Seven-Colored Evil Sword, a peerless divine weapon from the Investiture of the Gods cycle, hints that the Death World holds relics of the living world's greatest powers, connecting the two realms of myth.

## Reading Guide
Pay close attention to the Seven-Colored Evil Sword—its presence implies that Zhunti Daoist's fate is linked to the Death World, and Xiao Chen may soon confront a half-ancestor's legacy. The mysterious singing woman, who has returned to life, may become a powerful ally or a terrifying foe. Xiao Chen's desperation to grow stronger is palpable; the Yellow Diamond trade is just the first step. Will the Terror King prove trustworthy? And what ancient secrets does the Giant City hold beyond its towering walls? The next chapters promise a confrontation between worlds—the living and the dead, and the weapons of the fallen gods.
