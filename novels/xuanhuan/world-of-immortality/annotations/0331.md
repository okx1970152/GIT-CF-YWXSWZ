## Chapter Overview

The long-awaited descent of the Dead City into the mortal world finally begins, casting a shadow of dread across the Nine Provinces. As the ancient deathly city gradually manifests, Xiao Chen and Keke stand guard at the primeval village, sensing the inevitable collision of worlds. Meanwhile, Zhao Lin’er ascends to the imperial throne and, allied with the disciples of fallen half-ancestors, orchestrates a sinister curse aimed at Xiao Chen using the Yellow Clay Altar. Yet the scheme is subverted by the clever and carefree Qingqing, who replaces the half-ancestor tablets with Xiao Chen’s name, turning the intended malice into a torrent of pure spiritual energy. This chapter balances the weight of cosmic calamity with a spark of cunning hope, showcasing how the people’s faith can be weaponized for either destruction or salvation.

## Key Plot Points

The Dead City’s gradual emergence from the mysterious lower space is the chapter’s central event. Over sixty days, the black-walled city rises from the earth, shrouded in mist, its oppressive aura threatening to break into the mortal world. In the imperial capital, Zhao Lin’er consolidates power after the old emperor’s death, becoming the sole female sovereign. She secretly erects temples across the land for the Sun Church, White Tiger Saint Church, Chan Sect, Jie Sect, and others—preparing a ritual to harness the people’s willpower. The coup-de-grâce is a curse targeting Xiao Chen’s Yellow Clay Altar, meant to strip him of the divine relic. However, Qingqing, wielding her master’s crystal pagoda, replaces the half-ancestor spirit tablets with Xiao Chen’s, inverting the curse into a blessing. A vast tide of pure spiritual energy floods the primeval village, elevating Xiao Chen’s cultivation to the Sixth Heaven of Nirvana and spiritually transforming over a dozen acupoints. Keke, lured by an immortal fruit, meets Qingqing, who charms the little beast. Finally, the Dead City fully descends, shaking the continent and drawing countless cultivators to witness the ancient fortress.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter dramatizes a core xianxia concept: the power of collective belief, or “min yuan” (民愿). In Chinese folk religion and cultivation lore, the heartfelt prayers of multitudes can manifest tangible force, either as blessings or curses. Here, Zhao Lin’er’s faction attempts to weaponize this faith through ritual, only for Qingqing to hijack it. The Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台) is a primordial artifact intimately tied to Xiao Chen, capable of absorbing and neutralizing such influences. The Nirvana realm (涅槃) is characterized by extreme danger and fluctuation; passing through its death-calamities grants leaps in power, but failure means utter annihilation. Xiao Chen’s breakthrough from the Fifth to the Sixth Heaven, along with the spiritual transformation of his acupoints, demonstrates his unique path of divine acupoint cultivation. The Dead City (死城), first encountered on Dragon Island, is a cursed realm that now manifests in the mortal world, heralding the arrival of half-ancestor remains and otherworldly domains.

## Reading Guide

With the Dead City now fully planted in the Nine Provinces, the stage is set for a chain reaction of ancient powers. Xiao Chen’s cultivation has surged, but his enemies—Tiger Ben, Sun God Son, and Ye Xiao—will surely respond to the failed curse. Qingqing’s bold intervention has made her a target, and her carefree demeanor may soon be tested. Keke’s new fondness for Qingqing hints at a deepening bond that could prove vital. The Dead City’s interior remains a deathly labyrinth; what secrets and half-ancestor remnants will emerge? Readers should watch for the half-ancestor disciples’ next move, the revelation of the city’s true purpose, and how Xiao Chen will balance his personal growth with the escalating threat from both the immortal world and the cults of the Nine Provinces. The convergence of realms is only just beginning.
