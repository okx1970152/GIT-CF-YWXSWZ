## Chapter Overview

This chapter thrusts the reader into the heart of a catastrophic war that engulfs the Eternal Continent. The fragile peace shatters as the Great Shang and Roman empires launch a coordinated offensive against the Fan Kingdom, only to fall into a devastating trap. The betrayal is absolute: the Great Zhou kingdom joins Fan, encircling the invading forces in the Demon Plain. What follows is a massacre of staggering proportions—over a million souls lost in a single month. The battlefield transforms into a realm of supernatural horror: blood rains fall, ghostly wails echo, and the very heavens seem to grieve. The chapter masterfully blends political chess with mythic terror, setting the stage for an epic conflict that will shake the foundations of the world.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the explosive outbreak of war between the five hegemonic nations. Great Shang and Rome, having secretly prepared, strike the Fan Kingdom and initially seize territory with alarming speed. However, this is revealed as a ruse. The Fan Kingdom had allied with Great Zhou, allowing the invaders to penetrate deep before springing the trap. The capture of Shanhe Pass is shown to be a deliberate lure. Once the combined Shang-Rome force is committed, Great Zhou's six million troops seal the pass, cutting off supply lines, while Fan's forces ambush the invaders on the Demon Plain. The ensuing battle is a bloodbath: of the eight million allied soldiers, only one million eight hundred thousand escape after a month of desperate fighting. The death toll reaches an unprecedented ten million, turning the Demon Plain into a cursed, supernatural zone. Blood rains, ghostly activity, and the appearance of legendary death-deities like Lü Bu and Bai Qi hint at deeper, cosmic forces at play. The vanishing of all the million souls overnight, attributed to an unknown power, raises terrifying questions. Xiao Chen, drawn by an inexplicable pull, enters the plain and feels a resonance with the ancient divine steles, suggesting a connection between the war, the underworld, and his own fate.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter draws heavily on Chinese myth and history. Lü Bu and Bai Qi are legendary figures from the Three Kingdoms and Warring States periods respectively, here reimagined as deathless war spirits drawn to the mass slaughter. Their presence signals the scale of the tragedy. The concept of blood rain and soul gathering ties into traditional beliefs about violent death creating malevolent spirits. The "Heaven-Earth Furnace" mentioned by the mysterious voice is a reference to the primordial artifact of Ancestral God Suiren, suggesting that the war may be part of a larger scheme to repair or reactivate this ancient object. The five hegemonic nations (Shang, Zhou, Fan, Rome, and Xia) represent a geopolitical map that dwarfs the Mortal World. The westernmost nations bring elements of Western religion (Solar Church, Sun Knights) blending with Eastern xianxia. The disappearance of the souls evokes the concept of "soul circulation" (轮回) and hints at higher powers intervening.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a major shift from localized conflict to continent-wide war. The political machinations will likely escalate, affecting all major factions. Xiao Chen's journey to the Demon Plain is not random; his connection to the steles (Yellow River, Dragon Island) suggests a link to the Heaven-Earth Furnace. Pay attention to the mysterious voice and the vanished souls—this may set up a new arc involving ancient gods or the underworld. The war also brings many characters into play: the princess of Shang and her retinue of young experts head to the front lines, while Zhao Lin'er reappears as a Sacred Maiden of the Solar Church. The combination of mortal warfare and supernatural horror creates a rich backdrop for Xiao Chen's cultivation and personal quest. Readers can anticipate more battles, both political and mystical, as the continent descends into chaos.
