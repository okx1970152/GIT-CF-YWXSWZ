## Chapter Overview

This chapter is a haunting meditation on sacrifice and the weight of history. Xiao Chen, barely alive after a devastating battle with the Otherworld Ancestral God Ge Qian, is drawn by an irresistible force toward a mysterious blood-red palace at the heart of a desolate world. There, he witnesses visions of fallen stone-men kings—peerless warriors who once ruled but were driven to a tragic, defiant end. The tone is one of profound sorrow and bitter indignation, as the chapter raises questions about the nature of the ‚Äòcursed lands' and the fate of those who fight against cosmic injustice. It is a lore-heavy chapter that expands the novel's mythology while deepening Xiao Chen's own sense of duty.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen is in critical condition, his body shattered and barely held together by divine force after clashing with Ge Qian. The destructive energy from that battle still corrodes his flesh and soul, and even the healing tones of the Eight Origin Sounds prove insufficient. In a desperate gamble, he merges his wounded body with the incomplete formation diagram from the War Swords, using its life-giving potential to expel the foreign power. This act also rouses the half stone skull within him, which devours the remaining deathly energy. As he convalesces, a black wind carrying shattered Ancestral God weapons scours the land, and Xiao Chen is caught in it, barely protected by the diagram. When night falls, three blood-red moons rise and a mournful song begins from the distant palace, exerting an irresistible pull on all living things. Xiao Chen cannot resist the call and is drawn into a sea of blood around a colossal palace. The palace recognizes the diagram as a kindred force and ceases its attack. Through the half-open door, he sees floating fragments of stone-man bodies—limbs, skulls, and shattered torsos. Then a vision unfolds: in the bloody sunset, several towering stone-men kings, grievously wounded and leaning on each other, cry out against fate, asking why their world is called a land of chaos and their people sinners. They walk away together on a road to nowhere, a road of no return. Xiao Chen realizes this may be the fate of his own civilization, and that these fallen kings might be his own ancestors.
