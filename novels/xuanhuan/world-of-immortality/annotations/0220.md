## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a transition from the grim underworld to the serene Pure Land, as Xiao Chen returns from the Yin World with a rare treasure and renewed purpose. The tone shifts from the oppressive, deathly atmosphere of the previous arc to a quieter, more reflective cadence. Xiao Chen's discovery of the Purple Diamond Yin Wood and its hidden fruit provides a tangible symbol of hope, especially for Keke's uncertain fate. Back in the Pure Land, the passage of time is marked by Xiao Chen's steady cultivation and the nurturing care of Qingqing, who emerges as a luminous presence. Yet the calm is undercut by news from the outside world: the failed marriage of Hai Yunxue, the resurgence of legendary figures like Lü Bu and Bai Qi, and the stirring of ancient powers. The chapter masterfully balances personal growth, heartfelt human connection, and the escalating cosmic tension that will soon draw Xiao Chen into the Central Plains.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and Niu Ren emerge from the Yin World's exit, finding themselves near a severed mountain. Xiao Chen spots a glimmering purple object and flies up using his Undying Wings, discovering a rare Purple Diamond Yin Wood—an extraordinary specimen that is only one zhang tall but spreads ten zhang horizontally, its roots yielding a hidden fruit. He uses the Melting Weapons into Body divine ability to absorb the wood and fruit, intending to save it for Keke's eventual rebirth. Returning to the Pure Land, they are greeted by Qingqing, whose ethereal beauty and kind heart provide a warm contrast to the underworld's horrors. Over the next year, Xiao Chen stabilizes his cultivation at the Third Heaven of the Spirit-Treasury realm and experiences the restorative power of community. However, news arrives that Hai Yunxue has been forced to marry into the Central Plains Tiger clan, and her husband is assassinated by the legendary organization "Bounty Three Billion." Xiao Chen suppresses his urge for revenge, recognizing his current limitations. Over three years, he continues his cultivation, his white hair turning black again, and decides to leave for the Central Plains as legendary figures like Lü Bu, Bai Qi, and the Demon Sect Founder begin to manifest in the mortal world. He leaves a portion of the Purple Diamond Yin Wood as a gift and departs, marking the end of his time in the Pure Land.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Purple Diamond Yin Wood (紫钻阴木) is a legendary spirit wood that grows in the Yin World, taking ten thousand years to reach one zhang in height. Its color deepens with age: blood-red at one zhang, purple diamond at three zhang. Xiao Chen's specimen, though short, has an unprecedented ten-zhang horizontal spread, making it a peerless treasure. The chapter also introduces the legendary assassin organization "Bounty Three Billion" (赏金三亿), a name that piques curiosity—it is an ancient, near-mythical guild that once dared to assassinate the White Tiger Saint Emperor. The reappearance of historical and mythical figures—Lü Bu, Bai Qi, Ran Deng Buddha, Confucius, Zhang Sanfeng, and others—signals a major shift in the World of Immortality. These are not mere legends but awakened beings walking the earth, suggesting a convergence of epochs and an impending cataclysm. Xiao Chen's decision to enter the Central Plains aligns with this gathering storm.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a serene interlude before the storm. Readers should savor the warmth of Qingqing's character and the tranquil beauty of the Pure Land, as these peaceful moments will likely become scarcer as Xiao Chen ventures into the Central Plains. The appearance of so many legendary figures is not casual—it sets the stage for a dramatic escalation in the novel's scope. Pay attention to the names: Lü Bu, Bai Qi, the Evil King, the Demon Sect Founder—each has a history in the novel's mythos. Xiao Chen's choice to leave the Pure Land is a pivotal character moment; he has healed, grown, and now must re-enter the world's conflicts. The unresolved threads with Hai Yunxue and the Tiger clan promise future confrontation. Additionally, Keke's fate remains uncertain, but the saved fruit hints at a possible resurrection. This chapter gently reminds us that even in a world of slaughter and intrigue, kindness and hope persist.
