## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a potent mix of emotional revelation and visceral combat. Xiao Chen comes face-to-face with the eccentric 'old madman' who claims to be Lan Nuo's master—a startling connection that reopens his ties to the mortal world. The old madman's coarse demeanor contrasts sharply with his transcendent wisdom, hinting at depths behind the chaos. The chapter then pivots to a confrontation with ten cultivators from the opposing faction, where Xiao Chen unleashes the legendary 'Hand of the Supreme Being,' a pure martial technique that shatters artifacts and bodies alike. The battlefield becomes a stage for the clash between ancient martial arts and magical cultivation, with Xiao Chen emerging as an unstoppable force. The emotional undercurrent of the bracelet discovery at the end grounds the mythic violence in personal loss.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the old madman laughing and revealing himself as the master who gave Lan Nuo the 'Divine Guidance' technique, which nearly killed her but ultimately enabled her ascension. He confirms he witnessed Xiao Chen's departure to the World of Immortality and recognizes his soul signature. Xiao Chen learns that the old madman tried to stop Lan Nuo's ascension, suggesting hidden dangers in that realm. Then, at the old madman's urging, Xiao Chen steps forward to face ten cultivators alone. He demonstrates terrifying efficiency: crushing a sand-based attack with a single palm, shattering a thirteen-sword formation barehanded, decapitating a female cultivator, and tearing through three more opponents with ruthless precision. His technique is identified by the observers—Tianwai Tian, Renwai Ren, and the old madman (Shanwai Shan)—as the 'Hand of the Supreme Being,' a lost ancient martial art that can destroy even divine artifacts. The elders admit that Xiao Chen has truly restored this legendary technique. The chapter ends on a jarring note: Xiao Chen's friend Chen Fang points out that one of the fleeing cultivators wears a jade bracelet that Xiao Chen had given to Ruoshui, his beloved from the mortal world.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The 'Hand of the Supreme Being' (上苍之手) is a legendary martial technique that predates and counters the reliance on magical artifacts and spells. In the World of Immortality's cosmology, pure martial arts (古武战技) are considered the primordial path, capable of breaking divine laws. The old madman and his peers—Tianwai Tian, Renwai Ren, and Shanwai Shan—are half-ancestor level beings who serve as guardians of the mortal world's orthodox lineage. Their colloquial speech and eccentric behavior disguise their immense power. The 'Divine Guidance' (神引) technique is a profound fusion of martial and sorcerous principles, demonstrating that even the most transcendent cultivation has mortal roots. The battle scenes highlight a fundamental conflict in xianxia: physical cultivation versus magical cultivation—a tension central to this novel's philosophy.

## Reading Guide
This chapter advances Xiao Chen's personal journey on multiple fronts. The reunion with the old madman provides crucial context about Lan Nuo's past and raises questions about the true cost of ascending to the World of Immortality. More urgently, the bracelet revelation plunges Xiao Chen into a new conflict: Ruoshui, his mortal-world beloved, may be in danger, and the cultivators who hold her bracelet are clearly enemies. Readers should note that this revelation ties the current battle directly to Xiao Chen's deepest emotional wounds and will likely drive his actions in the coming chapters. Additionally, the full confirmation of the 'Hand of the Supreme Being' technique marks a major milestone in Xiao Chen's cultivation, establishing him as a peer to the ancient martial masters. The chapter sets up an inevitable clash between the mortal world's guardians and the otherworld cultivators, with Xiao Chen at the center.
