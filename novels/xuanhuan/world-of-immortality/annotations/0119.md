## Chapter Overview

The little defiant dragon, true to its stubborn nature, returns bearing a remarkable gift: a blood-stained cloth that may be the burial shroud of the primordial ancestor Suiren himself. This relic, once glimpsed in the City of the Dead, now proves central to the plan for escape. The demon emerges from seclusion, returns the Little Sacred Tree to a grateful Keke, and begins a seven-day ritual to summon the King Ship—a legendary vessel capable of crossing the sealed Forbidden Sea. As the demon carves an ancient formation on the beach, the sky darkens with nether energies and the air fills with ghostly wails. Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng must shelter under the shroud, which alone can protect them from the lethal yin aura. The chapter balances discovery, preparation, and mounting dread, setting the stage for the final departure from Dragon Island.

## Key Plot Points

The little defiant dragon demonstrates its intelligence and value by retrieving the mysterious shroud from the divine stele area, having learned of its location through its own instinct or communication with Keke. Keke acts as translator, confirming the cloth's origin. The demon identifies the shroud as likely genuine—either from Suiren or other great ancestors like Laozi or the Buddha—and notes it carries the scent of holy blood. He instructs Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng to mentally refine the shroud daily, as it will anchor their souls during the King Ship crossing. The demon returns the Little Sacred Tree to Keke, who rolls in the snow with joy. He then proceeds to the beach, where he inscribes a complex formation and plants dozens of tattered ancient flags, summoning wild war souls from across the island. The gathering of these spectral remnants, drawn by the Nine Abyss Heaven-Traversing Formation, creates an apocalyptic scene of dark clouds, blood-red lightning, and ghostly roars. Xiao Chen and Yan Qingcheng can only shelter under the shroud. The demon warns the ritual will take seven days and eventually require someone's blood as a final catalyst. As the chapter ends, a massive crash from the island's depths hints at further disturbance, possibly linked to Keke's mysterious absences.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The burial shroud of Suiren is a potent relic tied to one of China's mythic culture heroes—Suiren, who discovered fire and taught humanity to cook. Such ancestral relics in xianxia often carry immense spiritual weight and can act as protective talismans. The requirement to 'heart-refine' the shroud (祭炼) involves imprinting one's spiritual sense into the object to form a bond, a common practice for controlling artifacts. The King Ship (君王船) is a lesser-known escape vessel from Dragon Island, distinct from the Ancestral Dragon Ship, and its summoning requires gathering the lingering souls of ancient battle dead. The Nine Abyss Heaven-Traversing Formation (九幽通天阵) is a legendary array said to have been created by a dark king; it channels netherworld forces and is powerful enough to call forth the ship. The demon's ability to open a spatial domain filled with skeletons and black fluid demonstrates that his power far exceeds the half-god level he claims, hinting at a deeper cultivation path.

## Reading Guide

This chapter builds toward the climax of the Dragon Island arc—the escape. But the seven-day countdown and the need for a blood sacrifice introduce new tension. Who will offer the blood? Keke's continued unexplained wanderings suggest she may play a crucial role in the ritual's success or failure. The shroud's connection to Suiren and the divine stele deepens the mystery of the island's ancient history and the demon's true identity. Readers should watch for signs of the demon's hidden agenda—his power seems too great for his stated goals. The little defiant dragon's evolving bond with Xiao Chen is also a thread to follow, as it may become a key ally. With the King Ship summoning in progress and the terror escalating, the next chapters will likely bring a final confrontation or twist before departure.
