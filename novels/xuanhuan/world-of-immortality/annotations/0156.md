## Chapter Overview
This chapter shifts the scene from wilderness survival to urban political theater as Xiao Chen confronts the two arrogant noble scions, Zhuge Kun and Huffman, in a hot spring resort. Rather than killing them outright, he chooses a more humiliating and profitable path: forcing them to pay reparations for the supposed death of the Little Defiant Dragon. The tone balances cold fury with dark comedy as Xiao Chen methodically strips them of pride and wealth. The chapter also unveils deep lore through the families' fearful speculation about Xiao Chen's origins, linking him to the mysterious deep Southern Wastes and a legendary figure from thirty years past.

## Key Plot Points
1. Xiao Chen slaps Zhuge Kun repeatedly, then forces him and Huffman to slap each other until both are bloodied and swollen. He declares himself done with physical punishment and moves to settlement. 2. Xiao Chen demands 100,000 gold coins each for the 'death' of the Little Defiant Dragon—a sum far beyond their personal means. They negotiate down to 80,000 each. 3. Xiao Chen sends their guards to fetch the gold from the Zhuge and Ligen families, testing the unwritten rule that elder experts do not interfere in youth conflicts. 4. Both families, upon hearing the demand and learning of Xiao Chen's unexplained one-month delay during his journey, speculate he may come from the deep Southern Wastes—the same origin as a notorious figure who terrorized noble houses thirty years ago. They decide not to intervene, telling their sons to handle the matter themselves. 5. The gold arrives in heavy coin sacks; Xiao Chen dismissively demands banknotes instead, causing the couriers to retreat. 6. News of the incident spreads through the resort, cementing Xiao Chen's reputation as a fearless outsider willing to flout conventions.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
1. **Unwritten Rules of Conflict**: Elder cultivators ('old monsters') typically avoid meddling in younger generations' disputes. Xiao Chen exploits these rules, but the families consider sending experts if he oversteps. 2. **The Deep Southern Wastes**: A mysterious, feared region said to house ancient 'kings' from a sealed race. The mention of a figure from thirty years ago who caused chaos among noble families establishes a rich backstory and hints at Xiao Chen's possible hidden connections. 3. **Gold Value**: Two gold coins buy a fine banquet; 80,000 is an enormous fortune for a non-heir noble. Holy beasts (圣兽) are priceless, making Xiao Chen's claim plausible. 4. **Economy**: The use of banknotes (金票) indicates a developed merchant economy within the cultivator society. 5. **Beast-Fighting Culture**: Holy beasts are highly coveted, and the Little Defiant Dragon's value is central to the negotiation.

## Reading Guide

His provocations test the limits of local norms and the patience of great families—a dangerous game that could backfire. Pay close attention to the families' fear of the Southern Wastes and the thirty-year precedent; this lore will likely resurface in future arcs. The Little Defiant Dragon, notably, is very much alive—its true significance remains a secret that will shake Heavenly Emperor City.
