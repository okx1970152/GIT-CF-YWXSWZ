## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a significant turning point in Xiao Chen's cultivation journey within the Death World. After discovering that his old skeleton companions—Qin Guangwang, Yanluowang, and Lunhuiwang—have evolved into War Emperors at the Che Di realm and ventured into the deepest reaches of the continent, Xiao Chen chooses to remain in the Junwang City to train. Under the guidance of the ancient skeleton king Lian Po, he deepens his understanding of the Duo celestial sound and divinizes over two hundred acupoints. However, his progress stalls at the Perfected Man realm, prompting him to undertake the ultimate trial: entering the legendary Demon Cave. This perilous underworld is a nexus of countless evil spirits and demonic entities, and Xiao Chen's two-year ordeal inside pushes him to the brink of death repeatedly. His eventual breakthrough to the Fourth Heaven of Perfected Man and the mysterious patterns etching themselves onto his skin—connected to the long-lost War Swords—set the stage for the next phase of his quest.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen learns that the Three Skeleton Kings, now styled as War Emperors, have departed with the female Junwang into the Death Continent's most dangerous region—a journey that has already claimed several sovereigns. The seven-colored skeleton king reveals his own identity as Lian Po, the legendary general from the Warring States period, and becomes Xiao Chen's mentor. Over a year of intensive cultivation, Xiao Chen masters the Duo celestial sound to seventy percent proficiency and divinizes over two hundred acupoints, yet cannot break the Perfected Man barrier. Desperate for progress, he enters the sealed Demon Cave beneath the Junwang's palace. His first year inside is an endless nightmare of combat: he kills an undead emperor, triggering an eternal vendetta from that race, and is beset by seven undead kings simultaneously. By the end of the second year, he has slain four of those kings and fled into another abyss, finally breaking through to the Fourth Heaven of Perfected Man. More importantly, he discovers that the legendary War Swords exist within the Death World, buried in the cave walls, and that the mysterious patterns appearing on his skin resonate with them. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen confronting the Flame Emperor, a sovereign-level being wielding hellfire, while two ancient War Swords hum in the stone behind him.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Perfected Man realm is described here as the absolute limit of a living creature's evolutionary potential—surpassing it requires crossing an insurmountable chasm. This chapter vividly illustrates the thesis that cultivation at these heights cannot rely solely on meditation; it demands life-or-death trials. The Demon Cave recalls the classic xianxia trope of the 'forbidden training ground' where cultivators face endless waves of enemies, forcing the body and soul to extremes. The appearance of Lian Po, a historical figure from the Three Kingdoms period, underscores the novel's syncretic approach to Chinese mythology, where legendary mortals can be reborn in other realms and retain their memories. The War Swords' presence in the Death World is particularly intriguing, hinting that the forty-nine Great Derivation swords are not confined to the living world but are scattered across all planes, perhaps as part of a larger cosmic design.

## Reading Guide
Readers should pay close attention to the bodily patterns etching themselves onto Xiao Chen. These are not merely cultivation marks but seem to be a physical bridge to the War Swords, suggesting that his unique path may lead him to wield or control multiple swords simultaneously. The Flame Emperor's domain holds two War Swords—this is a major clue that the Death World is a treasure trove of such artifacts, possibly rivaling the Nine Provinces. Xiao Chen's breakthrough to the Fourth Heaven of Perfected Man is a milestone, but the appearance of a sovereign-level Flame Emperor raises immediate stakes. Will he attempt to claim the War Swords, or will he be forced to flee again? The chapter sets up a high-tension confrontation that could redefine his power level and his relationship with the Death World's rulers. Additionally, the mention that the female Junwang and the Three Emperors went to the deepest continent implies that future arcs will involve alliances or conflicts at that scale.
