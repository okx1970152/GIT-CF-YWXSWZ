## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a turning point in the cosmic war. Xiao Chen commands the Ancestral Ancestor Warship into battle against Otherworld Ancestral Gods, supporting Yan Huang, the Old Farmer, and the Old Ancestral Dragon. After repeated ramming attacks, Xiao Chen uses the broken formation diagram from the War Swords to annihilate an Ancestral God—his first such kill. However, new enemy Ancestral Gods arrive, forcing a desperate retreat into the Death World. The leader of the enemy reveals himself as the son of an Ancestral God lost in the Heavenly Burial Valley, setting up a deeply personal conflict.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen uses the Ancestral Ancestor Warship to ram an Otherworld Ancestral God seven times, weakening him while the Old Farmer pins the god with the Great Destruction Spear. When the god tries to escape, the Old Farmer feints an attack on Blue Feather, then delivers a devastating killing blow that shatters the god's body. However, the god's soul begins reassembling. At this critical moment, Xiao Chen unleashes thirty War Swords and the broken formation diagram, grinding the lingering soul into nothingness—his first successful killing of an Ancestral God. Immediately, four more enemy Ancestral Gods emerge from the Mortal World, stepping into the starry battlefield. With Yan Huang, the Old Farmer, and the Old Ancestral Dragon exhausted from battle, retreat becomes the only option. The group boards the warship and flees into the Death World. The lead enemy Ancestral God reveals himself as the son of the Ancestral God who vanished in the Heavenly Burial Valley long ago, and he swears vengeance. This retreat effectively abandons the Nine Provinces and the Four Worlds to the enemy.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Ancestral Ancestor Warship is forged from the bone essence of a Death World Ancestral Ancestor and an Otherworld Supreme Ancestral God, condensed with Eternal Light, making it a supreme war vessel capable of withstanding and ramming Ancestral Gods. The broken formation diagram is an incomplete version of the legendary Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation, formed here from thirty War Swords. It can annihilate a weakened Ancestral God, but the full formation would require all forty-nine War Swords. Killing an Ancestral God is an unprecedented feat for someone below that level, marking a major milestone for Xiao Chen. The retreat to the Death World signifies a strategic withdrawal to a hostile dimension that may hold secrets and allies.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up the next major arc in the Death World. The group is now isolated and hunted, but the Death World holds potential allies like the three skeleton kings, who are Suiren's disciples. Xiao Chen has used his trump card: the broken formation diagram. Future usage depends on recovering more War Swords or repairing the diagram through Eternal Light. The son of the missing Ancestral God from the Heavenly Burial Valley is a new antagonist with a personal grudge; his father's fate may be connected to the secrets of the Burial Valley and the Eternal Light. Readers should note the shifting balance of power: the Mortal World seems lost, but the Death World may provide a second front for counterattack.
