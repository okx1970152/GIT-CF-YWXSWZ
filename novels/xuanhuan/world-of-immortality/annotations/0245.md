## Chapter Overview

This chapter thrusts readers into the heart of the legendary tournament between the Great Shang Empire and the Roman Empire's religious alliance. The stage is set for a clash of civilizations, with ten champions from each side displaying fearsome divine arts. Amidst the thunderous battles, Xiao Chen watches from the shadows, his identity concealed as Xiao Shishui. But the arrival of two enigmatic little girls threatens to unravel his secret, drawing the hostile attention of the Tiger family and the two Number One champions. The atmosphere crackles with tension as ancient techniques and forbidden powers take center stage.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the Tiger family's intimidating entrance, signaling their intent to avenge their slain kin. The tournament proper begins as the Roman Empire's Number One takes the field, his "God Says" technique effortlessly defeating Bo Shi's Purple Prison Space and later crushing Chu Xingkuang's Fate Twins through a six-pointed star formation that drains his power. The Great Shang's Number One, revealed as the Little Li Flying Dagger successor, demonstrates his own prowess by felling a knight with a glance. Both champions then publicly demand Xiao Shishui to fight. The tension escalates when two little girls latch onto Xiao Chen's arms, exposing his true identity with cries of "Xiao Chen!" This revelation triggers an uproar—Tiger family experts converge, and the two Number Ones fix their deadly gazes upon him. Xiao Chen, with nowhere left to hide, releases his transformation and stands revealed in his true form, ready to face the gathering storm.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The "God Says" technique embodies the Western divine art system, where spoken words channel absolute authority from a deity—a hallmark of faith-based cultivation. It contrasts sharply with Eastern Daoist and Buddhist techniques like the Fate Twins and the Little Li Flying Dagger. The latter is a legendary assassination method from the Mortal World, said to never miss, now inherited by a new disciple seeking revenge. The tournament itself serves as a proxy for geopolitical rivalry between the Central Plains (Great Shang) and the Roman Empire's religious bloc, mirroring historical tensions. The Tiger family, a lineage of White Tiger descendants, represents the ancient beast-blooded clans that command demigod-level power and relentless vendetta.

## Reading Guide

With Xiao Chen's identity now public, the next chapters promise a brutal confrontation. The Tiger family will surely strike, and the two Number Ones may join the fray—each has a personal grudge against Xiao Chen. The two little girls remain an enigma: their true allegiance and power are unknown, but they clearly recognize Xiao Chen and are not ordinary children. Keep an eye on the Roman Empire's divine arts; their system may introduce new rules of combat. Xiao Chen's Eight-Aspect Worlds and Lingxi Sword Wave have yet to be tested against such exotic techniques. The tournament may be interrupted by an all-out assault, so readers should brace for a chaotic clash of civilizations and personal vendettas.
