## Chapter Overview

This chapter weaves together two parallel threads that expand the mythic scope of the World of Immortality. On one side, Xiao Chen follows the Martial Imprint toward a primordial demonic city, where two devastating beams of light circle like harbingers of doom—forces so terrible even the Martial Imprint dreads them. On the other, Keke's innocent curiosity in the Soul Realm leads to the discovery of a mysterious stone tablet, triggering a world gate that releases half-ancestors fleeing a sealed nightmare. Yet the chapter's most puzzling revelation comes when a mirror-image of Keke appears—a cool, sunglasses-wearing white beast that speaks of ancient boredom and a safely carved path to the 'most evil place.' The tone shifts between cosmic dread, playful mystery, and dry humor, all while deepening the enigma of Keke's origins and the hidden history of the Soul Realm.

## Key Plot Points

The Martial Imprint identifies two devastating beams of light circling a primordial city, warning that even Ancestral Gods would be imperiled. Xiao Chen and the War Soul advance with extreme caution, sensing an inevitable confrontation. Meanwhile, in the Soul Realm, Keke, Wu Ming, Jin Sanyi, and others investigate a large bluestone tablet carved with childish characters. When Keke presses her paw onto a matching imprint, a world gate opens, and half-ancestors burst out, bloodied and terrified—but Keke's group is trapped inside after the gate closes. They then enter a burial valley where a second bluestone converts into a mirror, revealing a doppelgänger: a white beast sporting sunglasses and a lazy demeanor, claiming to have prepared a safe route to the 'most evil place.' This mirror beast exudes casual power and knowledge far beyond Keke's, suggesting a progenitor or future version of her race. The chapter ends with the group following this mysterious guide into the depths.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'Soul Realm' (魂界) is one of the four great worlds in the World of Immortality's cosmology—alongside the Qi Realm, Curse Realm, and Martial Realm—each representing a distinct path of cultivation. 'Half-ancestor' (半祖) denotes a cultivator on the cusp of Ancestral Godhood; their fearful flight underscores the lethal nature of the sealed domain. The 'Burial Valley' (葬兵谷) is a legendary resting place for ancestral divine weapons, where even half-ancestor-level artifacts reside. Keke's 'Paradise Lost' (失乐园) is a unique inherited realm that allows her to seal and transport beings, reflecting her parents' immense legacy. The mirror beast's mention of 'the most evil place' hints at a location tied to the primordial chaotic power that even half-ancestors avoid—possibly the seat of a fallen Ancestral God or the origin of the War Swords.

## Reading Guide

This chapter plants a compelling mystery: who is the cool, sunglasses-wearing white beast, and what is its relationship to Keke? Its casual mastery of the Burial Valley's secrets and its bored demeanor suggest an origin far older and more powerful than Keke herself. Readers should watch for hints that this doppelgänger may be a future incarnation or a progenitor of Keke's race, potentially holding the key to her true potential. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen's impending encounter with the ancient city's lethal beams foreshadows a high-stakes battle with forces that could threaten even half-ancestors. The chapter balances lighthearted humor (Jin Sanyi's drunken antics, Keke's embarrassed denial of the mirror beast's existence) with escalating tension, setting the stage for revelations about the Soul Realm's deepest secrets and the true nature of the world's primordial evil.
