## Chapter Overview

This chapter delves into the aftermath of the group's journeys through time (“回”). Key revelations include the existence of a missing formation diagram and five war swords that are lost in different historical eras, making collection seemingly impossible. The mysterious old farmer returns grievously wounded from his own time-travel, confirming the formation diagram’s power. Keke’s mother provides insights, and Keke herself returns, comically suffering from overeating spirit essences. The chapter balances mythic exposition with lighter moments, including Jin Sanyi’s tearful return due to a broken heart. The group prepares for imminent battle as the black divine gate threatens to reopen. The chapter ends with a chilling new presence in the Death World.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen reflects on the incomplete formation diagram that saved his life against the Ancestral God’s killing intent. The mysterious old farmer appears, wounded from his return to the past. He confirms witnessing one corner of the formation diagram surrounded by five war swords during the final battle of the previous civilization, but does not know who controlled them. Keke’s mother reveals that she also saw the same vision while traveling through time, and further states that Keke’s father theorized the war swords are distributed across different eras, making it impossible to gather all forty-nine. Xiao Chen realizes the grave difficulty. The old farmer spits blood from an ancient wound sustained by a terrifying Ancestral God weapon. Keke returns from the “回” portal, claiming to have resolved her regrets, but then admits her “regret” is now a stomachache from overeating. She gives Xiao Chen a bundle of spirit essences. Keke’s mother expresses affection. Jin Sanyi returns weeping, revealing a lost love and a broken heart, but quickly recovers his roguish demeanor. He mentions seeing five war swords and the formation diagram during the pseudo-god catastrophe. The group departs back to Ancestral Dragon Village, with the farmer warning that the black divine gate will reopen within days. Xiao Chen decides to send the group into the Death World’s God Village for safety. Upon arriving, they sense a terrifying Ancestral God-level presence—a white skeleton floating above the God Village.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The “回” (Return) is a mysterious technique that allows one to travel into the past, but it cannot change major historical outcomes. This concept reflects the xianxia trope of revisiting pivotal moments to witness truth, not alter fate. The formation diagram (阵图) and war swords (战剑) are legendary artifacts; here, the incomplete diagram paired with five swords is a recurring vision across time, suggesting a cosmic puzzle. The idea of items lost across different eras adds a temporal dimension to artifact collection, a theme seen in other cultivation novels. The “pseudo-god catastrophe” refers to the earlier major event in the story. Keke’s mother and the old farmer are both at the Ancestral God level, emphasizing the power scale. Jin Sanyi’s returning with a broken heart contrasts with his usual comic relief, giving depth to his character.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a crucial lore dump about the war swords and formation diagram, setting up future plot threads about gathering these artifacts. The revelation that some are lost in time suggests Xiao Chen may need to find a way to traverse different eras. The old farmer’s warning about the black divine gate reopening foreshadows an imminent battle. The appearance of a white skeleton Ancestral God at the Death World’s God Village signals new dangers. Readers should pay attention to the emotional vulnerability of Jin Sanyi, which may hint at his past and motivations. Keke’s mother and the mention of Keke’s father are important for Keke’s backstory. The chapter balances exposition with character moments, so enjoy the mythic revelations alongside the human (and beastly) touches.
