## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges the party deeper into the horrors of the Death City. The arrival of the Yin Soldier, a powerful armored undead, forces a combined tactical assault. The group discovers that the soldiers' ancient armor is the key to their immortality – without it, they degenerate into weaker Wraiths. The threat escalates rapidly as thousands of Skeleton Birds swarm from above and a colossal thirty-meter Giant Demon strides toward them, leaving little hope for escape. The atmosphere is one of crushing despair, punctuated by moments of desperate combat and a surprising act of cunning from the three skeletons.

## Key Plot Points
The group learns the secret of the Yin Soldiers: their armor sustains their life. Once shattered, the soldier decays into a weaker Wraith, which Xiao Chen quickly dispatches. This revelation sparks greed in some cultivators for the armor fragments, but before they can act, a massive tremor signals the Giant Demon's approach. They flee into a side alley, but the Demon's path shakes the city. The mysterious woman in colored mist warns them never to enter the ancient houses, as many have died within. Skeleton Birds attack, and the group fights a desperate rearguard action. The three skeletons disassemble themselves and feign death on the street, pointing toward the blood-red river. The party is forced to retreat toward the river, but a wounded cultivator who jumps in is instantly killed by a monstrous claw from the depths. Surrounded by birds and facing the Giant Demon, the cultivators are on the verge of annihilation when a golden dragon roar suddenly erupts.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Yin Soldiers (阴兵) are a classic element of Chinese ghost folklore – spectral soldiers guarding ancient tombs or cursed battlefields. Their armor possessing life-sustaining properties echoes concepts of talismanic objects and the dependency of undead on external seals. The Skeleton Birds (骷髅鸟) and the Giant Demon (巨型魔鬼) are drawn from the rich bestiary of xianxia, where power level corresponds to size and presence. The Giant Demon's description – thirty meters tall, covered in bone scales, with a crocodile-like tail – follows the tradition of mountain-sized monsters that represent the apex of yin corruption. The blood river and the mysterious claw beneath it evoke the Styx-like rivers of Chinese underworld mythology, where death is certain for those who fall in.
