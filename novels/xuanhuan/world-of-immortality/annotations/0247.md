## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen boldly attacks the Tiger family in broad daylight, defying four half-gods who surround him. He uses the Lingxi Sword Wave to cripple a female cultivator and forces another to escape via a precious space scroll. The chapter escalates dramatically when the assassin Jin Sanyi joins the fray, delivering a humiliating blow to one half-god's hindquarters. Xiao Chen then unleashes his Eight Aspect Worlds technique, trapping the wounded half-god and, after a desperate pursuit, merging the eight realms into one to annihilate him completely. The crowd erupts in shock and admiration. This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen proves he can kill a half-god, cementing his reputation as a fearsome combatant in the Central Plains.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen first eliminates two younger Tiger family members, one via the Lingxi Sword Wave and another by the Collapse technique. The four half-gods fail to protect them due to Xiao Chen's speed with Eight Aspect Worlds. Jin Sanyi, the stuttering assassin, appears in a terrifying killer guise and repeatedly attacks the same half-god's buttocks, causing severe injury and humiliation. Xiao Chen then focuses on the wounded half-god, trapping him in the Eight Aspect Worlds while evading the other three. Inside the worlds, the half-god's Star Nebula Chain technique is drained of power, and Xiao Chen seals him with the Yin-Yang Tenfold Seal. Ultimately, Xiao Chen invokes Eight Aspect Unity, collapsing all eight realms into one to utterly destroy the half-god. The crowd, including Zhao Lin'er, Princess Yin Ying, Yan Qingcheng, and Dugu Jianmo, watches in awe. The Tiger family's prestige is shattered.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Eight Aspect Worlds technique is derived from the Eight Trigrams (Ba Gua) of ancient Chinese philosophy, representing fundamental natural forces. Xiao Chen's mastery of this spatial divine art allows him to rival cultivators at higher realms. Half-gods (半神) are experts at the Sky-Mastery (御空) realm, typically far beyond Spirit-Treasury cultivators. Killing one at the Spirit-Treasury Seventh Heaven is extraordinary. The Lingxi Sword Wave is a rare sound-based attack, and the Star Nebula Chain is a Tiger family secret art. The assassin Jin Sanyi belongs to the legendary Bounty Three Billion organization, which has a long-standing feud with the Tiger clan. His tactics, while comedic, are deadly effective.
