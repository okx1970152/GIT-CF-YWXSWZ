## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a brutal, tragic conflict between Xiao Chen and the Otherworld invasion. The tone is one of relentless violence and sorrow, with Xiao Chen unleashing his full power to protect the weak. He single-handedly kills multiple half-ancestors and hundreds of cultivators, using his thirty War Swords and a damaged ancient formation diagram. The chapter contrasts epic combat with heartbreaking scenes of orphans weeping over their parents. Xiao Chen ultimately retreats to the Death World on the Ancestral Lord Ship, emphasizing the war's grim cost and the necessity of strategic withdrawal. The emotional weight of civilian casualties drives Xiao Chen to act with cold fury.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Otherworld overwhelming the Nine Provinces. Xiao Chen arrives at a scene of massacre and kills an Earth-Penetrating realm cultivator who impaled a young girl. He then confronts a half-ancestor, decapitating him and destroying his soul with the Duo sound. Three more half-ancestors attack, and Xiao Chen uses the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree's Dragon Species Immobilization technique to freeze them, then strikes them down with the Hand of the Supreme Being. Hundreds of enemies surround him; he responds by activating the thirty War Swords under his control and the damaged Zhuxian Formation Diagram. The formation's power annihilates nearly all enemies, including three seventh-heaven half-ancestors, leaving only two fifth-heaven half-ancestors who flee in terror. The massacre shocks both sides. Keke arrives with news of rescued survivors, including Chen Fang and many other cultivators. Xiao Chen decides to retreat to the Death World on the Ancestral Lord Ship to protect the rescued cultivators, knowing that Otherworld Ancestral Gods have already descended.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases the pinnacle of half-ancestor combat. Xiao Chen, now at the Seventh Heaven of the Half-Ancestor realm, demonstrates the power of the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree's Dragon Species divine art, which can immobilize even half-ancestors. The damaged Zhuxian Formation Diagram is a relic from the primordial age, originally created by lord Tongtian; even in its broken state, it can obliterate hundreds of cultivators. The Ancestral Lord Ship is a Death World vessel capable of crossing worlds, refined from the bone well and a sovereign's skull. The Duo sound from the Eight Syllables of Origin is used to destroy a half-ancestor's soul, showcasing its lethal power. The emotional tragedy of mortals suffering reflects the novel's central theme of the cost of civilization's survival against cosmic enemies.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen has become one of the most powerful beings in the current era, but the war is far from over. The Otherworld Ancestral Gods have descended, and Xiao Chen's retreat to the Death World signals a strategic relocation of survivors. The damaged formation diagram's power hints that it may be a key weapon against the Ancestral Gods if repaired. Readers should watch for whether Xiao Chen can fully reconstruct the formation, and how the Death World's fire seed kings will react to his presence with his new power. The fate of the rescued cultivators and Keke's role in seeking out allies will shape the next arc. The tragedy of civilian losses also deepens Xiao Chen's resolve.
