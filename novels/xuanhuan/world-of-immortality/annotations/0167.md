## Chapter Overview
This chapter is a raw, unrelenting combat sequence that showcases Xiao Chen at his most brutal and tactical. Fresh from his public duel at Beidou Academy, Xiao Chen now engages in a night of assassination and open warfare, tracking down three top-tier enemies in succession. The tone is one of grim efficiency: the narrative moves from one deadly confrontation to the next with barely a breath in between. Xiao Chen’s internal voice is cold and analytical, even as he pushes his body to its limits. The chapter underscores his willingness to endure injury to secure kills, his mastery of the Four Killing Hands, and the resilience granted by his eight divinely transformed acupoints. It is a demonstration of why Xiao Chen is feared across Heavenly Emperor City—not just for his power, but for his ruthless, calculating approach to battle.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen flying over the city, tracked by two assassins: the curse master Liu Ziliang and the hell corpse Li Daoyuan. They unleash nine lightning bolts together. Xiao Chen takes a calculated risk—he charges through the lightning, sustaining injury, to close distance on Liu Ziliang. Using the Slay God, Reverse Chaos, and Suppress Demon techniques fused into his blade, he severs Liu’s arm and later bisects him, killing the curse master despite the ongoing pursuit from Li Daoyuan. He then turns on Li Daoyuan, battering the undead warrior with multiple strikes until the hell corpse body begins to break and bleed, signaling its weakening. Wu Xingfeng arrives, rescues Li Daoyuan, and engages Xiao Chen in a fierce fist-to-fist battle. The two exchange over a hundred blows before both are injured and part ways in a draw. Xiao Chen immediately heads toward a new target: Yuwen Feng, who is resting at the Drunken Beauty Palace, signaling that the night of slaughter is far from over.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter demonstrates key elements of xianxia combat and cultivation logic. The 'hell corpse' (地狱邪尸) is a type of undead practitioner who has transformed their body into a nearly indestructible vessel—no blood flows, and even severe cuts are difficult to land. Breaking such a body requires techniques that bypass physical defense, which Xiao Chen accomplishes with the Four Killing Hands derived from the Heavenly Stele Profound Art. The 'curse master' (咒师) relies on spoken incantations and ritual power; losing an arm disrupts their focus and reduces combat effectiveness. Xiao Chen's 'eight divine acupoints' (八大神穴) are a unique cultivation foundation: he has transformed major acupoints into reservoirs of life essence, granting him rapid healing and the ability to fight through wounds that would cripple others. The 'Immortal Heavenly Wings' (不死天翼) grant him flight speed that rivals or exceeds that of specialized Spiritists and Spellmasters, allowing him to dictate engagement ranges.

## Reading Guide
This chapter establishes Xiao Chen’s capacity for extended, high-stakes combat against multiple elite opponents. Readers should watch how his tactical approach evolves: he targets the most dangerous opponent first, accepts deliberate damage to finish a kill, and conserves energy by using the Four Killing Hands sparingly. The appearance of Wu Xingfeng reintroduces a major rival whose Five Elements Fist has grown in power—their draw suggests both are still ascending toward the same peak. The mention of Yuwen Feng at the chapter’s end promises another confrontation, and given Xiao Chen’s current injuries, the next fight will test his endurance further. The chapter also hints at the political undercurrents: the curse master and hell corpse were sent by someone, and Wu Xingfeng’s involvement may signal deeper coordination among Xiao Chen’s enemies.
