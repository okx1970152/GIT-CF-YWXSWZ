## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges readers deep into the central-west Death Battlefield, where Xiao Chen—now a Yellow Diamond skeleton—joins forces with the formidable Five-Colored Angel King to excavate the remains of a being that surpassed the level of a monarch. The atmosphere is thick with dread and anticipation as they unearth colossal fingernails, finger bones, and eventually a vast skull that houses a spatial gateway to the Golden Forbidden Sea—the very sea where the King Ship drifts. The discovery not only sheds light on the ancient battle between Ancestral Gods and a transcendent evil but also deepens the mystery connecting the Bone Well, the Nine Provinces, and the King Ship. Amidst the tension, Keke's playful interference with the spirit treasures provides a necessary counterpoint, reminding readers of the fragile bonds of loyalty and greed that drive these characters.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen allies with the Five-Colored Angel King, a powerful being of the Perfected Man realm, to excavate a sleeping monarch beneath the burial grounds. The excavation team, including Sha Polang and the Angel King's thirteen warriors, first uncovers a massive black fingernail, then a finger bone like a mountain ridge, leading to the realization that these remains belong to a being that surpassed the monarch level—possibly an enemy of the Ancestral Gods. As the digging continues, they uncover a vast ribcage that encloses a treasure trove: purple diamond yin wood trees and yin jade, which Keke eagerly claims for her Paradise Lost garden. The excavation pattern shifts toward the direction of the Bone Well, confirming that the giant bones are linked to the spinal column that forms the well. Finally, they discover the floating skull, a dark mountain-like bone, that serves as a spatial portal to the Golden Forbidden Sea, where the King Ship is seen sailing. A warrior accidentally steps through the portal, confirming the connection between the Death World and the Eternal Continent's sealed sea. The chapter ends with a deepened sense of the cosmic scale of the conflict: the sleeping monarch may not be the only threat, and the ancient battle between Ancestral Gods and a transcendent evil has left these colossal relics as silent witnesses.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Death World hierarchy: fire seed beings evolve through bone color tiers—white, blue, purple, yellow, and five-colored (五彩). The Five-Colored Angel King is at the Perfected Man realm, equivalent to a high-tier king. The 'monarch' (君王) level is above that, and the being whose bones they find surpassed even monarchs, approaching the level of an Ancestral God. The Bone Well, previously discovered, is now confirmed to be the spine of this transcendent being, artificially refined to connect the Death World to the Nine Provinces, likely by Ancestral God Nuwa. Purple Diamond Yin Wood (紫钻阴木) is a rare death-world spirit plant that absorbs yin energy and produces fruits that enhance fire seeds; its value makes it a prime treasure for both the undead and living cultivators. The King Ship (君王船) reappears from earlier arcs, a ghostly vessel that can cross the Forbidden Sea, now seen drifting in the Golden Sea through the skull's spatial portal.

## Reading Guide

This chapter expands the Death World's lore and ties it directly to the Eternal Continent and the Nine Provinces, setting up future cross-world conflict. The discovery of the spatial portal hints that Xiao Chen may find a new path between worlds, potentially allowing him to return to the mortal world more permanently or to bring allies from the Eternal Continent. The Five-Colored Angel King is a new ally whose motives remain complex; readers should watch for potential betrayal or hidden agendas as the excavation continues. Keke's treasure hoarding continues to pay off, but her safety may be threatened if the sleeping monarch awakens—foreshadowing a major crisis. The missing skull of the transcendent being suggests that the most important part of the remains—perhaps containing its consciousness—is still at large, possibly in the Golden Sea or elsewhere.
