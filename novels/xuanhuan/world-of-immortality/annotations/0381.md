## Chapter Overview

Xiao Chen, now a yellow diamond skeleton in the desolate Death World, is suddenly confronted by a terrifying five-colored skeleton who demands his unconditional surrender. The newcomer serves a sovereign named Yi Tianzhong, who is purging all potential threats before a great war. Refusing to be a puppet, Xiao Chen attacks with the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree and begins a desperate flight across the eastern death world. The pursuit escalates when a second skeleton king of equal power joins the chase, forcing Xiao Chen to gamble everything on a return to the mortal world through the bone well. This chapter combines high-speed pursuit, brutal bone-shattering combat, and a climactic unleashing of the stone giant. The tone is urgent and grim, with Xiao Chen's survival hinging on his cunning and the terrifying power of his petrified former body.

## Key Plot Points

The five-colored skeleton announces Yi Tianzhong's ultimatum: serve or die. Xiao Chen refuses, strikes with the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, and flees. The five-colored skeleton demonstrates master-level fire seed manipulation, turning entire cities of fire seed beings into puppet explosives. Xiao Chen seeks aid from the Giant City king, who instantly submits rather than fight. The white skeleton joins the pursuit, making escape seemingly impossible. Xiao Chen leads both kings through the bone well into the mortal world near the Dead City. In the mortal realm, he directs the chase toward his stone giant statue in the Eastern Sea. The stone giant awakens, crushing the white skeleton to a shattered fire seed and severely wounding the five-colored skeleton, which is captured in the stone giant's hand. Xiao Chen's tactical use of the environment saves him, but his yellow diamond body is heavily damaged from the ordeal.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Death World's hierarchy is defined by bone color: white, blue, purple, yellow, and now the rare five-colored stage, which indicates an evolution approaching true resurrection. Fire seeds (火种) are the concentrated soul essence of undead beings; absorbing them allows growth. The five-colored skeleton's ability to instantly turn other fire seed beings into puppets reveals the pinnacle of fire seed manipulation as a form of divine art. The stone giant is Xiao Chen's former petrified body, a remnant of his sealing on Dragon Island. It now serves as a cross-world weapon, capable of crushing death world kings. The bone well is a spatial nexus formed from a supreme being's spine, bridging the Death World and the Nine Provinces. The larger conflict between Yi Tianzhong and the mysterious singing woman hints at a sovereign-level war within the death world's depths.

## Reading Guide

This chapter advances Xiao Chen's survival quest while unveiling the death world's internal politics. The shattered fire seed of the white skeleton is a valuable prize that Xiao Chen can collect to boost his cultivation. However, the captured five-colored skeleton may bring Yi Tianzhong's wrath upon the mortal world. The mysterious singing woman from the deeper death world emerges as a potential counterweight to Yi Tianzhong, and her connection to the sovereign war could affect Xiao Chen's future. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen uses the stone giant's victory to accelerate his evolution, and whether the death world kings' invasion will force the mortal world's hidden races to act. The bone well's location is now compromised, setting the stage for a possible cross-world incursion.
