## Chapter Overview

The final round of the sacred beast tournament arrives, pitting six extraordinary young creatures against one another in a battle that goes far beyond mere spectacle. The chapter opens with a detailed analysis of each contender – the overwhelming Golden Lion King, the savage Red Dragon King, the deceptive White Tiger, the god‑descended Gold and Peacock King, and the unyielding Little Defiant Dragon. The tone is one of mounting tension and anticipation, as crowds fill the city and every detail is scrutinized. The stage is set for a clash that will test not only raw power but also heritage, cunning, and will. The narrative then transitions into the first true combat exchanges, with the Peacock King and Golden Lion King locking into a dazzling aerial duel that showcases legendary abilities.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with an exhaustive breakdown of each sacred beast's strengths and rumored techniques, establishing their unique threat levels. The Golden Lion King is portrayed as the favorite, its single Destruction Eye already proven capable of shattering even the defensive White Turtle shell. The Red Dragon King is feared for its ruinous dragon arts, while the White Tiger’s incarnation technique and divine markings suggest hidden depths. The two god‑descended beasts, Gold and Peacock King, have been raised together and are expected to ally. Little Defiant Dragon is the beloved underdog, its history of rising through defeat earning it the analyst’s respect. When the battle begins, Little Defiant Dragon and Red Dragon King stride forward in mutual defiance, refusing to ally and reigniting their rivalry. Gold and Peacock King form a natural pact, floating above the arena. The Golden Lion King, unwilling to let the god‑descended pair dominate, leaps skyward and attacks the Peacock King directly. The Peacock King responds with its legendary Five‑Colored Divine Light, which sweeps away the Lion King’s Destruction Ray – a moment that shocks the audience and reveals a mythical power capable of neutralizing even the Lion King’s ultimate weapon.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter shines a spotlight on the concept of “sacred beasts” (圣兽, sheng shou) – creatures of extraordinary lineage whose power rivals cultivators of the Nirvana realm and beyond. In the World of Immortality, such beasts are never truly subjugated; they are venerated as equals or even ancestors, often integrated into powerful families through marriage or mutual respect. The Destruction Eye (毁灭之眼) of the Golden Lion King is a classic trope in Chinese mythology – a third, vertical eye that unleashes ruinous light, often associated with divine judgment or primordial chaos. The Peacock King’s Five‑Colored Divine Light (五色神光) carries even deeper mythological weight, invoking the legendary peacock of Ming dynasty fiction, whose five-colored tail feathers could brush away any weapon or magic. Here, it is presented as a supreme technique that counters even the Destruction Eye. The commentary also establishes that the adult forms of these beasts are so rare and powerful that they serve as deterrents – a note on the world’s power structure where ancient bloodlines are the ultimate currency.

## Reading Guide

This is a high‑water mark for the beast tournament arc, delivering both strategic analysis and explosive action. The outcome of the Peacock King – Golden Lion King clash will likely set the tone for the rest of the finale. Watch for the Little Defiant Dragon’s growth: its willingness to challenge the Red Dragon King directly hints at a larger destiny – perhaps tied to the ancient dragon mysteries from Dragon Island. The chapter also teases the other beasts’ hidden abilities: the White Tiger’s divine markings and the fact that both Gold and Peacock King have yet to display their full ancestral inheritance. As the tournament approaches its climax, tensions off the arena (the manipulative noble families, Xiao Chen’s unresolved conflicts, and the ever‑present threat of the demon) may surface. Keke’s restrained fury at being tied up also suggests it might yet barge into the fray, potentially upsetting the established order.
