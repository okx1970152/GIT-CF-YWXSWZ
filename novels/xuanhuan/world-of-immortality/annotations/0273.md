## Chapter Overview
This chapter escalates the conflict from half-ancestor skirmishes to a full-scale war between Ancestral Gods. The true nature of Shennong's sacrifice is revealed—he melted his physical body to neutralize a devastating Otherworld poison, preserving the Eternal Continent. Now, through Zhuangzi's spiritual vessel, he returns to confront an Otherworld Ancestral God. Meanwhile, the half-ancestors of the Eternal Continent unleash their most devastating techniques, including Tongtian's Zhuxian Sword Formation and Zhunti's Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, slaughtering two Otherworld half-ancestors. Xiao Chen, awed by the cosmic scale, is ordered to awaken Keke for the ultimate weapon: Paradise Lost.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Ancient God Wasteland in turmoil as an Otherworld Ancestral God descends, its mere energy fluctuations sending White Tiger Saint Emperor and the first Demon Lord of Heavenly Demon Palace flying. Shennong's identity is revealed when the Otherworld Ancestral God names him in shock. Zhuangzi's body serves as the vessel for Shennong's residual consciousness, shocking all half-ancestors. Tongtian Jiaozhu, aided by the Peacock King Kong Xuan, uses the Zhuxian Sword Formation to mortally wound one Otherworld half-ancestor, while Zhunti and Satan cooperate to kill another. Sun Wu employs a stone dagger to behead a third. Xiao Chen offers his stone artifacts to Shennong but is ordered by Sun Wu to remain and awaken Keke for Paradise Lost, which is needed to ensure the complete annihilation of the Otherworld half-ancestors' souls. The chapter ends with a warning from the spatial rift that another Ancestral God is about to descend.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter draws heavily on Chinese mythological figures from the Investiture of the Gods (Fengshen Yanyi) and Buddhist lore. Shennong, the Divine Farmer, is one of the Three Sovereigns, known for tasting herbs and sacrificing himself for humanity. His self-immolation to neutralize corpse poison mirrors Buddhist tales of compassionate sacrifice. Zhuangzi's role as a spiritual vessel reflects the Daoist concept of transcendence—he is not a combat powerhouse but a being whose consciousness nearly reaches the Ancestral God level. The Zhuxian Sword Formation, Tongtian's ultimate technique, is famous from Fengshen for its ability to kill immortals. Paradise Lost (shiyuan) is introduced as a domain that can trap and destroy half-ancestors, requiring Keke's unique power to activate. The stone dagger wielded by Sun Wu is revealed as an Ancestral God relic, capable of slaying even half-ancestors.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a climactic confrontation between Ancestral Gods. The appearance of Shennong raises the stakes, but the imminent arrival of another Otherworld Ancestral God suggests the battle is far from over. Xiao Chen's role now pivots from frontline fighter to key support—his connection to Keke and Paradise Lost may prove decisive. Pay attention to the strategic deployment of half-ancestors: Tongtian, Zhunti, Satan, and Sun Wu are the main offensive force, while figures like the Yellow Emperor, Laozi, and Chi You guard the spatial rift. The fate of the slain Otherworld half-ancestors' souls hangs on Keke's awakening. The next chapters will likely focus on Xiao Chen's urgent task and the confrontation between Shennong and the incoming Ancestral God.
