## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a pivotal transformation for Xiao Chen in the death world. Having recently absorbed the fire seed of the Broken Sword King, he evolves into a Yellow Diamond second-rank skeleton—a golden, sun-like being that radiates terror across the eastern lands. The emergence of a true Yellow Diamond king sends shockwaves through all fire-seed creatures, forcing even city lords to tremble. Yet Xiao Chen does not rest; driven by a desperate need to grow strong enough to return to the Nine Provinces, he deliberately provokes the mightiest power in the region: the Giant City King. The stage is set for a deadly duel that will determine whether Xiao Chen can seize the power he needs or be crushed by the established order.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen’s breakthrough to Yellow Diamond second rank is announced with earth-shaking authority: his golden bones blaze like a newborn sun, causing all lesser fire-seed beings to prostrate in awe. The accompanying Yellow Diamond skeleton, now wielding the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, watches with a mix of awe and relief, recognizing that Xiao Chen is not a predator who will turn on allies. In a gesture of trust, Xiao Chen presents the Broken Sword King’s weapon to the allied skeleton, who then eagerly trades his beloved but blunt divine tree for the sharper blade. Xiao Chen accepts the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, but quickly discovers it has lost its former efficacy—it can now only be used as a crude club. Unfazed, he stows it away and begins a deliberate march eastward, entering city after city, provoking panic among local lords. The pressure builds until the Giant City King, a Yellow Diamond third-rank powerhouse, issues a formal challenge to meet on the ancient battlefield outside Night Demon City in three days. Xiao Chen’s gamble has paid off: he has forced the hand of the region’s overlord, but he knows he remains vulnerable at only second rank. The chapter closes with the uneasy realization that the Giant City King is no ordinary foe—he is a super-evolutionary with strength far beyond Xiao Chen’s current level.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The death world’s hierarchy of skeletal cultivation is central here: from common white bones to blue, purple, and finally yellow diamonds—each tier representing a quantum leap in power and rarity. Yellow Diamond skeletons are considered ‘super-evolutionaries,’ beings that can dominate entire external regions. The fire-seed economy and politics of the death world are built on mutual predation; weaker beings flee or submit, while kings duel to absorb each other’s fire seeds for advancement. Xiao Chen’s tactic of openly walking through cities to provoke a confrontation is a classic xianxia strategy—using his reputation as bait to lure out the strongest opponent, forcing a decisive battle that can yield the greatest rewards. The Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree, a legendary artifact of the sage Zhunti, appears here in a diminished state, its divine light faded; its transformation into a blunt instrument is a wry commentary on how sacred relics can lose their original purpose in the hands of the unworthy or in a world where the rules of cultivation have changed.

## Reading Guide

This chapter builds tension toward an inevitable clash. Xiao Chen’s decision to challenge the Giant City King head-on is bold but risky: he is at Yellow Diamond second rank, while his opponent is third rank and commands a vast network of subordinate cities. Readers should watch for whether Xiao Chen will attempt to even the odds through strategy, allies, or some hidden trump card. The comedic Yellow Diamond skeleton—who now wields the broken sword—remains a loose ally; could he be drawn into the coming battle? Meanwhile, the Purple Diamond Sha Polang waits anxiously in the rear, ready to flee if the duel goes poorly. The impending fight will not only determine Xiao Chen’s survival but also shape the power balance of the eastern death world. Alliances, betrayals, and the true nature of the ancient artifacts may come to light. Keep an eye on the Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree: even as a club, it may hold secrets that Xiao Chen has yet to unlock.
