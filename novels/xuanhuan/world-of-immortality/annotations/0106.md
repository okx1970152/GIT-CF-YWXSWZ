## Chapter Overview
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen's quiet cultivation disturbed by the demon's sudden appearance. The demon, a pale-faced entity that escaped from the City of the Dead, unveils critical lore about the sacred tree and Keke's origins. He reveals that the tree once grew from the World Tree's stump and has taken 1000 years to develop three leaves. Keke itself was a seven-colored jade egg that incubated for a millennium. The demon wants to borrow the sacred tree but is hesitant, fearing Keke's elusive parents and the dragon clans. Xiao Chen, ever analytical, probes for weaknesses and later attempts to use the dragon sacred mountain as a shield. The chapter deepens the mythic mystery around Keke and the sacred tree while raising the stakes for Xiao Chen's survival.

## Key Plot Points
The demon's appearance in the midnight camp sets a tense atmosphere. He confesses his memory loss about the distant past but remembers the sacred tree from 1000 years ago when it had only one ink-jade leaf. He identifies the tree as a regeneration of the World Tree, the dragon races' sacred plant, which was transplanted to Dragon Island. The demon also reveals that Keke's birth was a thousand-year event, hatching from a seven-colored egg that sealed with the tree. Despite the threat, Xiao Chen is not intimidated. He infers that the demon's main advantage is fear of Keke's parents. The demon warns that his patience is limited and implies he might act if provoked. Later, Xiao Chen takes Keke to the dragon sacred mountain, hoping to find dragons with their divine powers intact to counter the demon. Disappointingly, even the upper dragons have lapsed back into beastly states without the holy powers they displayed in the City of the Dead. In the distance, he spots cultivators chasing a golden dragon—likely one of the nine companion dragon kings—heedless of the danger from surrounding primordial beasts.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter deepens the mythology of the World Tree, a classic xianxia motif representing the connection between heaven, earth, and dragon-kind. The sacred tree's slow growth over a millennium underscores the immense timescale of cultivation treasures. Keke's thousand-year incubation ties to the idea that powerful beings often require extended gestation to accumulate enough spiritual energy, especially on sealed lands like Dragon Island. The demon's memory loss is another recurring theme: beings from ancient times often have fragmented recollections due to vast eras, sealing, or interference. The dragon sacred mountain is a place of concentrated dragon essence and mystery, echoing the sacred mountains of Chinese myth. The hunt for the nine companion dragon kings reflects the desperate collective effort of cultivators to escape the sealed island by triggering an ancient formation.

## Reading Guide
The demon's patience is a ticking clock; Xiao Chen's next moves will be critical. Will he negotiate, fight, or find a third party to mediate? The appearance of the golden dragon and other king candidates suggests the race to gather nine dragon kings is accelerating—this plot thread will likely create opportunities and conflicts for Xiao Chen. The sacred mountain's mysteries remain unsolved: why do dragons lose their powers here? Is there a connection to the City of the Dead? Keke's emotional vulnerability about her parents may lead to new discoveries or alliances. Watch for how Xiao Chen uses his existing partnerships with the three skeletons, the monks, and Liu Mu to navigate the growing pressure.
