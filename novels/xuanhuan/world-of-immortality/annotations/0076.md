## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a pivotal shift from Xiao Chen's solo survival to becoming the central target of a large-scale conspiracy. The island's alliances, driven by greed for dragon-kind, converge on the snowy peaks where Xiao Chen cultivates in seclusion. The atmosphere is thick with scheming and anticipation, as both the major powers and the mob gather. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen achieves a significant breakthrough, divinizing his fifth acupoint and feeling a surge of life force that doubles his vitality. The chapter masterfully contrasts the serene, golden light of cultivation with the dark, churning motives of the approaching crowd, creating a tense and dramatic buildup.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the aftermath of the Purple Dragon King encounter. Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, and Wang Tong failed to capture the young Companion Dragon King, which triggers widespread excitement about the existence of such beings on the island and emboldens other cultivators to consider dragon-kind as obtainable prizes. Then, rumors spread that Xiao Chen has stolen four dragon eggs and is heading west, leading to the assumption that he is attempting to raise a Companion Dragon. In reality, Xiao Chen is consuming the eggs for cultivation, but the misunderstanding fuels the mob's greed. The mob, incited by figures like the Forest Tribe strongman Yalode and possibly the major alliance leaders, organizes a 'witnessing' expedition that is actually an excuse to seize the eggs. Xiao Chen remains unaware, deeply absorbed in cultivating the Lion King Dragon egg's essence. He successfully divinizes his right shoulder-well acupoint, bringing his total divine acupoints to five: both feet's Shangqiu, chest's Zhongting, and both shoulders' Jianjing. These five now resonate, greatly enhancing his life essence. The chapter ends with nearly a hundred cultivators gathering at the snowy mountains, with more arriving, while Xiao Chen remains in seclusion, oblivious to the impending storm. The narrative builds tension by juxtaposing the peaceful golden glow of cultivation with the greedy murmurings of the mob.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The cultivation breakthrough in this chapter illustrates the unique path of acupoint divinization, distinct from standard realm progression. Xiao Chen's five divinized acupoints now resonate with each other, greatly enhancing his life essence and vitality. This concept reflects the Chinese medical idea of acupoints as nodes of energy, here elevated to a divine transformation, giving Xiao Chen the feeling of having two lives. The chapter also highlights the political ecosystem on Dragon Island: alliances form around shared goals, rules are fragile, and figures like Yalode manipulate public sentiment. The 'Dragon King' concept is central – these beings are so powerful that even a juvenile can repel top cultivators, making them coveted assets. The 'Little Li Flying Dagger' legend is referenced, a classic wuxia trope from Gu Long novels, here adapted into the xianxia setting, adding a layer of rich intertextuality.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation. Xiao Chen is about to face a united front of enemies, driven by greed and grudges. The key question is whether he can survive this onslaught alone or if his allies—Yizhen, the three skeletons, possibly Keke—will intervene. The chapter also deepens the mystery of the dragon eggs: Xiao Chen is consuming them, not raising them, which subverts the expectations of both the mob and the reader. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's breakthrough affects his combat power and whether the mob will take action before he emerges. The manipulation by Yalode and the possible involvement of Yan Qingcheng, Kailuo, and Wang Tong adds layers of political intrigue to the purely physical threat. The next chapters promise explosive action and strategic maneuvering.
