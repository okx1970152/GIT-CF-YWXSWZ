## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a dramatic pivot in Xiao Chen's fortunes. The legendary Demon Sect Founder emerges from obscurity to publicly declare his support for Xiao Chen, an act that reshapes the political landscape of the Eternal Continent. The Tiger clan's demigods, sent to assassinate Xiao Chen, are annihilated in a coordinated assault by twenty of the most talented young experts from Great Shang and the Roman Empire—a display of overwhelming power that underscores the Founder's influence. Amid the chaos, two mischievous little girls attempt to capture the young experts with a damaged Three Realms Bag, and the roguish assassin Jin Sanyi schemes to profit from the chaos. The chapter blends epic violence with dark comedy, as Xiao Chen ultimately departs with the girls under the Founder's guidance, leaving the world reeling from the events.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Demon Sect Founder's shocking appearance, as he publicly sides with Xiao Chen and threatens the Tiger clan without fear of their White Tiger Saint Emperor. This act immediately deters any other parties who might have considered moving against Xiao Chen. The Tiger clan's three demigods, cornered and terrified, are set upon by a coalition of twenty young experts—including the ten strongest of Great Shang and ten from the Roman Empire—who have been promised the return of lost scriptures and treasures by the Founder. The assault is swift and brutal: the demigods are slain in moments as the young experts unleash their ultimate techniques. Among them, the Little Li Flying Dagger, Solar Divine Light, Nine Nether King Wheel, and other legendary arts converge to vaporize the half-gods. After the battle, two young girls (the Founder's nieces) try to capture the exhausted experts with a tattered Three Realms Bag, but only manage to trap a few. The stuttering assassin Jin Sanyi, in a bid to obtain the bag for himself, begins tricking people into it—including the prince Torit—but eventually gets kicked into the bag by Xiao Chen. Xiao Chen then takes the bag and follows the Demon Sect Founder to an unknown floating island. The chapter concludes with the news spreading across the continent, causing shock among major powers. In the Undying Sect, Yan Qingcheng's master smiles grimly; the Hai family patriarch freezes in dread; and the Tiger clan learns that three of their demigods have been lost, along with a cold-faced young woman who returns to report.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases the concept of 'face' and political backing in the cultivation world. The Demon Sect Founder's endorsement is a form of power projection that rivals even the threat of a Saint Emperor, demonstrating how reputation and alliances can shift the balance of power. The Three Realms Bag (三界袋) is a classic xianxia artifact, said to be capable of containing vast spaces; here it is damaged and used for comic effect. The Little Li Flying Dagger (小李飞刀) is a legendary technique from the mortal world that has crossed over into the World of Immortality, now wielded by a descendant. The joint attack by the ten strongest youths of two empires highlights the collaborative nature of high-level cultivation politics—they act not out of loyalty to Xiao Chen, but because the Demon Sect Founder has promised them rewards. The chapter also reinforces the brutality of the cultivation world: three demigods die in a heartbeat, their screams cut short, underscoring that even supreme beings can be overwhelmed when the right forces unite.

## Reading Guide
This chapter marks a turning point: Xiao Chen is no longer a hunted fugitive but a figure backed by one of the most terrifying beings in existence. The deaths of the Tiger demigods signal that an ancient force—the Demon Sect Founder—has returned to the world stage. Readers should watch for how this affects the Tiger clan, the Hai family, and the Undying Sect, all of whom receive the news with consternation. The appearance of the two little girls suggests that the Founder has a personal stake in Xiao Chen's journey, possibly grooming him for greater purposes. Meanwhile, the stuttering assassin Jin Sanyi, despite his comic demeanor, proves to be a resourceful and amoral player—his capture of Prince Torit hints at a subplot involving the secrets of Solar Divine Blood. The chapter ends ambiguously, with Xiao Chen vanishing to an unknown floating realm. The next chapter will likely explore the aftermath of this power shift and the Founder's plans for Xiao Chen and his captured companions.
