## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, Xiao Chen steps out of the shadows and into the full glare of the Dragon Island stage. Having accepted the Revenge Alliance's challenge, he proposes a closed valley as the battlefield—a choice that everyone misreads as a sign of weakness. Instead, it becomes a killing cage. From the very first clash, Xiao Chen shatters expectations by displaying the overwhelming power of Mortal-Shedding Fifth Heaven, instantly decapitating one opponent and then systematically dismantling the alliance's numerical advantage. The chapter crackles with brutal, efficient violence, but its true weight lies in Xiao Chen's calculated restraint: after that initial display, he deliberately conceals his full realm, feeding his enemies a false sense of hope even as he cuts them down one by one. This is not a berserker's charge; it is a predator's methodical hunt.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen's proposal of a closed valley is a masterstroke of psychological warfare. He frames it as a concession—not wanting a prolonged guerilla war—but the real purpose is to prevent any escape. Once inside, he immediately abandons the pretense and attacks at full power. His opening strike kills one enemy instantly, and within moments two more are dead, one ripped apart by the sheer force of Fifth Heaven energy. The crowd above gasps; the Revenge Alliance realizes they have been trapped. Xiao Chen then smoothly reduces his displayed power to Fourth Heaven, a deliberate misdirection that allows him to preserve an edge. He dodges devastating attacks from the spiritist Li Lingfeng and the spellmaster Carter, while relentlessly hunting down the weaker members. When Li Lingfeng attempts to immobilize Xiao Chen with his innate spiritual power, Xiao Chen's higher realm breaks the hold, causing a backlash that wounds the spiritist. In just under fifteen minutes, Xiao Chen kills six of the ten alliance members, leaving only Guluo, Li Lingfeng, Carter, and one fugitive hiding deeper in the valley. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen turning his full attention to Guluo, setting up the final reckoning. The strategic depth here is remarkable: Xiao Chen used the valley not out of fear, but to ensure a total victory that would send an unmistakable message to every watching alliance.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Mortal-Shedding realm is the first true threshold of cultivation in the World of Immortality, where practitioners shed their mortal frames and begin to channel the essence of heaven and earth. Within this realm, there are nine heavenly tiers, each representing a significant increase in power. Xiao Chen's Fifth Heaven status puts him two tiers above most of his opponents, granting him an overwhelming advantage. The gap between tiers is substantial—enough that a spiritist like Li Lingfeng, normally a king among same-level fighters, suffers backlash when trying to restrain a higher-tier foe. Spiritists (灵士) are practitioners with innate spiritual talents, honed into potent psychic abilities like telekinesis and immobilization. Spellmasters (咒师) wield power through incantations, calling down lightning and energy blades. The chapter also highlights a central dynamic of Dragon Island: alliances are temporary constructs, and individual strength can overturn numerical superiority. Xiao Chen's decision to fight alone against an entire alliance is a declaration that he no longer fears the island's political games.

## Reading Guide

The immediate tension is whether Xiao Chen will finish off Guluo, Li Lingfeng, and Carter—and what the hidden member of the alliance might do. But the broader implications are more significant: Xiao Chen has now publicly demonstrated power at the Fifth Heaven level, a realm that places him among the top young experts on the island. Every watching alliance will need to recalibrate their view of him. His enemies, like Yan Qingcheng and Zhao Lin'er, will learn of this display and may change their strategies. The chase is over; Xiao Chen has become a hunter. Readers should pay attention to the reactions of the major alliances, as they will shape the next phase of conflict. Additionally, the fact that Xiao Chen deliberately concealed his full power even after killing six opponents suggests he is saving something for the final confrontation with the three main foes. The chapter's title hints at a cage—but who is truly caged?
