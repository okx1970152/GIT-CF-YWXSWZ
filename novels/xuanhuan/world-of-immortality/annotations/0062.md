## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen steps away from the political chaos of Dragon Island and ventures into its central unknown. This chapter is a shift from combat-driven survival to mythic exploration, as he confronts the eerie Bone Sea and the colossal divine stele that seals the island. The atmosphere is thick with dread and mystery, as ancient horrors and celestial secrets lie buried beneath layers of white bones and black mist. It is a pause in the action to expand the world's cosmological depth, revealing that Dragon Island is not merely a wilderness but a prison crafted by forces beyond mortal understanding. The tone is solemn, desolate, and charged with foreboding, setting the stage for revelations that may reshape Xiao Chen's understanding of his own past.

## Key Plot Points
After his stunning victory over the Revenge Alliance, Xiao Chen becomes the most talked-about figure on Dragon Island, and the Undying Alliance led by Yan Qingcheng announces a 'death chase' against him. However, Xiao Chen has already slipped away, ignoring the commotion and heading straight into the island's central zone. He arrives at the vast Bone Sea—an endless field of skeletal remains ranging from two to a hundred meters in length, including humanoid bones, giant birds, ferocious beasts, and dragon skeletons. The air is filled with a murderous aura; no sound breaks the dead silence. The Three Skeleton Kings grow uneasy, their soul-flames flickering, while Keke seems curious. At the heart of the Bone Sea stands a towering stone monument—the divine stele that, according to the monk Yizhen, fell from the heavens in the primordial age to seal all dragon clans on Dragon Island, stripping them of their divine powers. Xiao Chen tries to approach but is stopped by swirling black mist that shreds bones within a radius of one li. The mist generates terrifying illusions: a five-meter skeleton, a snake-tailed woman, and a fifty-meter violet tyrant dragon, all of which ultimately shatter—perhaps echoes of ancient history. Xiao Chen makes a bold connection: this stele reminds him of the Yellow River Divine Stele from his childhood, which bore the inscription 'Ever-Pacify the Yellow River' and the Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram. He longs to see whether this stele also has a diagram on its back, but the life-forbidden zone bars his entry. As he circles the stele, he suddenly senses danger and discovers a fossil-like human figure standing very close within the black mist—this is no illusion.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Bone Sea (骨海) is a classic trope in xianxia and dark fantasy—a desolate landscape symbolizing the toll of ancient wars or divine punishments. The presence of enormous dragon skeletons reinforces the scale of the calamity that befell the dragon race. The divine stele (神碑) is a recurring mythological motif in Chinese literature, often representing heavenly mandates or seals. In this novel, the stele is a physical manifestation of cosmic law, used to suppress beings too powerful to be killed. The 'life-forbidden zone' (生命禁区) is a common xianxia concept: an area where survival is impossible for ordinary cultivators due to raw destructive energy. The illusions Xiao Chen witnesses may be residual spiritual imprints (神识残留) from the past, a way for the author to show historical flashbacks without breaking the narrative flow. Xiao Chen's suspicion that the Dragon Island stele and the Yellow River stele are connected adds a layer of foreshadowing, hinting at a larger cosmic design that ties his personal cultivation inheritance to the fate of Dragon Island.

## Reading Guide
This chapter is dense with worldbuilding and sets up a major mystery thread. Pay close attention to the description of the stele's black mist and the illusions—they may reveal secrets about the island's past. Xiao Chen's unanswered question about whether the stele bears a Qi-Refining Diagram on its reverse is the chapter's emotional core. The fossil-like figure at the end introduces a new character or entity; consider who this might be and why they are standing inside the life-forbidden zone. This discovery forces Xiao Chen to reassess his priorities—will he continue exploring or retreat for now? The tension between his curiosity and the lethal barrier is the hook that will drive the next chapters. Meanwhile, the Undying Alliance's 'death chase' remains unresolved, meaning that time is running out before his enemies catch up to his location.
