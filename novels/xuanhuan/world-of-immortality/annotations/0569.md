## Chapter Overview

This chapter masterfully blends treasure hunting with heartbreaking tragedy, as Xiao Chen finds himself deep within a Chaos Cave on the Sun Star. The tension is palpable from the start: a suspicious old goat latches onto him, Otherworld half-ancestors prowl the corridors, and every step could lead to an ambush. What begins as a competitive scramble for a sealed treasure quickly transforms into something far more profound when a mortally wounded Ancestral God of the Nine Provinces stumbles into Xiao Chen's path. The emotional core of the chapter is the warrior's fading plea to return to his homeland—a devastating reminder of the war's cost. The discovery of nine seemingly rotten arrows, the brutal dispatch of rival cultivators, and the desperate escape through the labyrinthine palace all serve to heighten the sense of loss. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen clutching a bow and nine arrows, vowing to honor a hero's last wish, making this one of the most poignant episodes in the arc.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen enters a sealed Chaos Cave with an eccentric old goat who claims to be the brother of Shi Zhongdi. Inside, a trio of cultivators (a male at Third Heaven, a female at Fourth Heaven, and their wounded companion) are trying to break a seal. The old goat proposes a temporary alliance to open the seal faster. Once breached, the group rushes for treasure: the female grabs a white jade vase, while the old goat and Xiao Chen snatch nine worn wooden arrows from the debris.

The revelation comes when the White-Haired Otherworld Half-Ancestor appears at the cave exit, mocking the group for taking the vase while ignoring the true treasure—the arrows, once used by a Stone Man King. He then kills the three vase-holders with invisible Soul-Severing Spears. Meanwhile, the old goat uses the combined power of the nine arrows to tear a passage through the chaos walls, and he and Xiao Chen flee into a new corridor.

As they navigate the maze-like palace, they encounter a dying Ancestral God of the Nine Provinces. His body is shattered and his consciousness is fading. In his final moments, he laments that all the Ancestral Gods of the Nine Provinces who came to the Celestial Realm have nearly been wiped out. His one wish is to return to his homeland. Xiao Chen promises to bring him back. The old goat gives Xiao Chen his three arrows to complete the set of nine, and Xiao Chen recovers a bow from the fallen hero. With grim resolve, he vows to use these arrows to slay enemies and then bury the bow in the Nine Provinces.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter introduces key elements of Stone Man lore. The 'Stone Man King' (石人王者) is a supreme being on the path of petrification, a rare cultivation route distinct from standard divine power. Their weapons are often simple stone or wood objects that contain unimaginable power. The nine rotten arrows are a perfect example: outwardly decaying, yet brimming with fire essence. The white jade vase that held them is also a valuable containment artifact.

The concept of 'Ancestral Gods of the Nine Provinces' fighting and dying in a distant Celestial Realm parallels the historical sacrifices of Chinese mythological heroes who defended their civilization beyond their borders. The dying warrior's line '我好想回到九州去看一看' (I long to return to the Nine Provinces to look upon it) echoes a common trope in Chinese literature: the unfulfilled desire of a exiled or fallen hero to see their homeland one last time. This adds a layer of tragic cultural resonance, as the Nine Provinces represent not just a place but the entire heritage and ancestry of the human race.

The old goat's comedic persona (talking incessantly, claiming to be Shi Zhongdi's brother, yet admitting he runs from fights) provides a classic xianxia contrast—a seemingly buffoonish but knowledgeable character who appears at critical moments.

## Reading Guide

This chapter ends with Xiao Chen in possession of the nine arrows and the bow, but he is still deep inside the Sun Star's palace, hunted by the White-Haired Half-Ancestor and possibly other Otherworld powers. The old goat remains an ambiguous ally—his true strength and motives are unclear, and his insistence on sticking with Xiao Chen may have ulterior purposes. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will use the Stone Man King arrows, especially since he now has all nine. The promise he made to the dying Ancestral God is a pledge that will surely drive his actions in the coming chapters. Pay attention also to the mysterious 'Soul-Severing Spears' used by the White-Haired enemy; they seem to be invisible and extremely deadly. The search for the legendary 'inverse celestial treasure' (逆天战宝) is still ongoing, and with more powerful enemies gathering, Xiao Chen's next moves will be critical. The emotional weight of this chapter sets the stage for a darker, more determined phase of the protagonist's journey.
