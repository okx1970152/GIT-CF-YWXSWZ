## Chapter Overview

This chapter presents a raw clash of philosophies and power on Dragon Island. Xiao Chen, having shed his earlier restraint, chooses to meet the barbarian Kaeo on his own terms—overwhelming brute force. The battle is not merely physical but psychological, as Xiao Chen aims to imprint an ineradicable shadow on his enemy's spirit. Yet beneath the surface of this primal confrontation, a darker game unfolds: a mysterious spellmaster deliberately obstructs the forest tribesman Yarode from aiding Kaeo, revealing that someone wishes to see the barbarian dead. The chapter crackles with tension not only from the fight itself but from the revelation that the island's politics have turned towards calculated assassination and alliance manipulation.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen seizes Kaeo by the legs and smashes him repeatedly into the ground, using the barbarian's own strength against him. Despite his intent to kill, the ancient beast-souls—a divine wolf and a fierce tiger—emanating from Kaeo form a protective barrier, preventing a lethal blow. Meanwhile, Yarode is intercepted by a nameless Spellmaster who stalls him until Kaeo is thoroughly beaten. When Yarode finally breaks free, he unleashes a torrent of vines and thorny plants, forcing Xiao Chen to wield Kaeo's body as a makeshift weapon. The three skeletons intervene with their dark netherfire, burning through the vegetation, but are soon subdued when Yarode regains his full power. Realizing they are outmatched and that hidden enemies are exploiting their weakness, Yarode persuades the furious Kaeo to retreat. Xiao Chen, calm and detached after the battle, reflects on the new reality: the island is descending into a law where the strong survive and the weak perish. He discovers signs of an earlier skirmish between Yarode and the unknown Spellmaster, confirming that someone is pulling strings from the shadows. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen and his skeletal companions entering a eerily quiet valley marked by meter-long footprints, hinting at even greater dangers ahead.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter illustrates a core tenet of Xianxia philosophy: the cultivation of the Dao is also a cultivation of the heart. Xiao Chen's decision to beat Kaeo with his own strength is not merely tactical—it is a deliberate attempt to crush his opponent's fighting spirit. In Chinese martial culture, such psychological dominance is considered as important as physical victory. The ancient beast-souls that protect Kaeo are a hallmark of the Barbarian Tribe's cultivation method: they seal primordial beast spirits within their bodies, which can emerge to defend their host. This reflects the Xianxia trope of 'spirit beasts' and the concept of merging with beast essence. The appearance of a mysterious Spellmaster who manipulates events from behind the scenes introduces the motif of ‘hidden hands’—a common device in Chinese web novels where powerful figures orchestrate conflicts to eliminate rivals without direct involvement. The term 'Spellmaster' (咒师) indicates a practitioner who uses incantations and curses, a distinct branch of cultivation from pure martial or qi-refining arts.

## Reading Guide

Readers should pay close attention to the political undercurrents introduced in this chapter. The unknown Spellmaster who blocked Yarode suggests that a third party is actively attempting to eliminate Kaeo and use Xiao Chen as a tool. This foreshadows deeper conspiracies on Dragon Island, where alliances are fragile and betrayal is commonplace. Xiao Chen's realization that 'the strong live, the weak die' marks a hardening of his worldview—he will no longer be a passive survivor but an active predator. The silent valley at the end, with its massive footprints, signals an imminent encounter with a being far more powerful than any human cultivator encountered so far. The skeletons' growing combat prowess with their dark netherfire is also worth noting, as it indicates they are evolving beyond ordinary undead. The next chapter will likely test Xiao Chen's new philosophy against a true apex predator.
