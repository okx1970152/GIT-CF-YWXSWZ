## Chapter Overview
This pivotal chapter transitions from Dragon Island's survival conflicts to the emotional and logistical preparations for departure. The Demon's request to borrow the Sacred Tree forces Xiao Chen into a painful dilemma, as Keke's silent sacrifice reveals the depth of their bond. A hidden ice palace yields ancient treasures, including a mysterious broken blade and the rare Divine Ice Jade, which Keke offers to nourish the tree before parting. The revelation of the King Vessel introduces an alternative exit from the island, while the looming summoning of the Divine Ship by the Nine-Headed Dragon Kings sets a ticking clock. The chapter moves between wistful farewell, mythic discovery, and the cold calculus of power.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Liu Ruyan teasing that she has trained Yan Qingcheng into a perfect female slave, though Yan remains aloof. The Demon appears, pressing Xiao Chen for the Sacred Tree, offering to borrow it for one year. Keke reluctantly hands the tree over after the Demon gifts a piece of Divine Ice Jade, which the tree roots into. Xiao Chen is deeply conflicted, feeling he has betrayed Keke's trust. The Demon then reveals the secret of the King Vessel—a ship independent of the Divine Ship that can also cross the Forbidden Sea—promising to return the tree within a year and swearing an oath by an ancient king. After the Demon seals himself in an ice cavern for ten days of transformation, Keke becomes listless and despondent. Xiao Chen consoles Keke by suggesting a trip to her birthplace near the sacred mountain. Meanwhile, the island's cultivators are gathering the nine dragon kings to summon the Divine Ship, with old experts making a final attempt to locate the young Ancestral Dragon. The departure is imminent within half a month. Xiao Chen and Keke visit the sacred mountain area, where Keke's sorrow deepens amid memories of her origin.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of a 'demon' (魔鬼) in this context does not necessarily mean evil; it refers to a being from the Death Realm who is seeking to purify death qi into life energy. The Demon's oath by an ancient king (上古君王) reflects the seriousness of promises in xianxia culture, where breaking a vow can lead to spiritual annihilation. The Divine Ice Jade (冰神玉) is a rare spiritual essence from snow veins, extremely cold and potent, used here to nourish the divine sapling. The King Vessel (君王船) is a legendary craft distinct from the Divine Ship, suggesting multiple layers of mystical transport tied to Dragon Island. The broken weapons from the ice palace are said to be ancient immortal implements, their damage hinting at cataclysmic battles in the island's past. The young Ancestral Dragon (小祖龙) is a singular being capable of summoning the Divine Ship alone, underscoring its supremacy among dragons.

## Reading Guide
With the Sacred Tree lent to the Demon, Keke's emotional state becomes a concern—this loss may affect her powers or bond with Xiao Chen. The Demon's King Vessel hints at his future reappearance in the main continent, potentially impacting the larger plot. The broken blade Xiao Chen chose may prove to be a significant artifact. Yan Qingcheng remains a sealed captive; her fate could swing between ally and enemy. The ongoing search for the young Ancestral Dragon may succeed or fail, altering departure conditions. As the Divine Ship approaches, the power dynamics among factions may shift. Keke's birthplace near the sacred mountain may hold secrets about her origin and the Cosmic Tree. Readers should watch for the Demon's return and the resolution of the Ancestral Dragon hunt.
