## Chapter Overview

This chapter takes Xiao Chen deeper into the underworld, to the ominous Nine Serenity Platform—a towering, blood-soaked mountain that serves as a conduit between the underworld and the dark domain. Here he meets a hideous crone who guards the platform for King Chu Jiang, and learns that the mysterious purple diamond yin wood message was not Keke's distress call but a deliberate summons from a manipulative old ghost. In a moment of revelation, Xiao Chen communicates through the sealed gate and discovers that Keke is not only safe but living in contented leisure, complaining only about the shortage of rare fruits. Meanwhile, the crone drags the protesting Niuren to receive the legendary Ox Head war soul, setting up a major power-up for his companion.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and Niuren arriving at the Nine Serenity Platform, a mountain-like structure that feels almost alive with malice. An ancient crone, claiming to be Niuren's ancestor, appears and reveals that she oversees the platform. She explains that the platform was once a passage to the dark domain and the Nine Abyss Eighteen Hells, with a sealed gate that still allows communication. Xiao Chen, believing the purple diamond yin wood message was Keke's cry for help, insists on descending into the abyss to reach the gate. Despite the danger from countless vengeful spirits, he leaps down. After a two-hour fall, he reaches the bottom and finds the sealed gate. A ghostly figure emerges and, after an hour of communion, enables Xiao Chen to sense Keke. To his surprise, Keke's mental voice is lazy and content, complaining that the local beings have only found eight purple diamond yin wood fruits in months and she hasn't had her fill. This reversal reveals that the message was a ruse to lure Xiao Chen to the underworld for a different purpose. Meanwhile, Niuren is forced to accept the Ox Head beast soul, a legendary but ignoble war soul that the crone insists is perfect for him despite his horror at the notion.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Nine Serenity Platform (九幽台) is a central location in Chinese underworld mythology, often associated with the deepest, most cursed levels of hell. Here it is reimagined as a sealed passage to the dark domain, a remnant of the primordial plans of Ancestral God Suiren to establish a proper underworld. The concept of the Ten Kings of Hell (十殿冥王) is drawn from Chinese folk religion, where each king judges souls in a specific court. The purple diamond yin wood (紫钻阴木) is a fictional treasure that grows only in the underworld and bears fruit only after ten thousand years, making it rarer than celestial divine fruits. The Ox Head (牛头) beast soul is a humorous twist on the traditional Ox-Head and Horse-Face guardians of hell, here reimagined as a barbarian war soul rather than a minor underworld functionary. This chapter also illustrates the xianxia trope of a "soul imprint" (灵魂印记) that cannot be destroyed, which is why Keke's body vanished but her essence persists.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a major emotional pivot. Xiao Chen's desperate rescue mission turns into a revelation that Keke is not only safe but living like a spoiled princess in the underworld, which adds a layer of bittersweet humor to the grim setting. Pay attention to the crone's ambiguous motives—she seems to serve King Chu Jiang but also acts independently. The sealed gate and the ghostly communicator hint at deeper underworld politics that may resurface. Meanwhile, Niuren's forced acceptance of the Ox Head soul is played for comedy but will likely grant him significant new power. Xiao Chen's bond with the sacred tree continues to evolve as he merges Keke's essence. The chapter sets up future conflicts: the old ghost who summoned Xiao Chen likely has an agenda, and the true purpose of the message remains partially obscured. Readers should watch for signs that the underworld's rulers are not entirely trustworthy.
