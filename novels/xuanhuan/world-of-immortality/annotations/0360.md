## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a stunning twist: a doppelgänger of Xiao Chen, long believed dead, has returned to the Mortal World and is wreaking havoc with overwhelming power. The false Xiao Chen, standing atop a colossal stone statue in the Eastern Sea, fights and defeats two formidable cultivators — the Nine-Headed Serpent and Ye Jiu — with a combination of ancient combat techniques that blend time and space. His arrogance is boundless; he declares that he will marry Zhao Lin'er, now empress of the Nine Provinces. Meanwhile, the real Xiao Chen watches from the sidelines, his body now a crystalline purple skeleton, reforged in the fires of Nirvana. The chapter builds toward a confrontation between the two, a clash of identity and destiny that echoes the novel’s central themes of illusion, reality, and the burden of power.

## Key Plot Points

- The false Xiao Chen, standing on a massive stone statue that has jutted from the sea for over a year, engages the Nine-Headed Serpent and Ye Jiu in combat. He demonstrates mastery of bizarre, dual-attribute techniques — his left hand radiates dark, space-warping energy, while his right hand shines with pure light imbued with the power of time. Despite their best efforts, both opponents are driven back, wounded, and forced to retreat.
- The doppelgänger then pronounces his intention to marry Zhao Lin'er, the current empress of the Nine Provinces, sending tremors through the cultivation world. Zhao Lin'er receives the news with cold calculation, preparing her ‘final weapons’ to deal with him.
- The real Xiao Chen — now a translucent purple skeletal figure — has been observing the doppelgänger from the sea for days. He realizes that this doppelgänger is a remnant soul from his past, one that escaped the sealing on Dragon Island and entered the Mortal World years ago. This soul has since repaired itself and grown into a separate entity, driven by ambition and the desire to claim the Twenty-Four War Swords sealed within the stone statue.
- When the doppelgänger senses Xiao Chen’s presence and attacks, Xiao Chen merges with the stone statue, wielding its massive stone sword against the false self. The doppelgänger is caught off guard, unable to harm Xiao Chen in his fused state, and a tense standoff ensues.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

- The Nine-Headed Serpent (九头神蛇) is a beast associated with the primordial chaos of the Eastern Seas; its ability to split into nine bodies and regenerate from a single remnant is a classic trope of powerful yaojing in Chinese mythology. Ye Jiu (叶九重) is a cultivator who wields an eighteen-story pagoda, a common symbol of Daoist refinement and spatial trapping in xianxia.
- The doppelgänger’s technique of wielding time and space simultaneously is a rare and feared ability, often considered the domain of near-Ancestral God beings. The Eight Aspect Worlds (八相世界), which Xiao Chen uses, are derived from the original eight trigrams of Fuxi, representing the fundamental forces of the universe.
- The stone statue is a recurring symbol — it once sealed the Twenty-Four War Swords, legendary weapons that have been scattered across the world. Its presence in the Mortal World signals a critical convergence of the novel’s major artifacts and power struggles.
- The theme of remnant souls (残魂) becoming independent entities is a recurring motif in xianxia, drawing on Buddhist and Daoist concepts of karmic imprints and the non-duality of self.

## Reading Guide

- This chapter sets up a direct conflict between Xiao Chen and his doppelgänger, a battle that will force Xiao Chen to confront his own former darkness. The doppelgänger is stronger than Xiao Chen’s current peak, but the stone statue may hold the key to victory or a devastating cost.
- Zhao Lin’er’s reaction — readying ‘secret weapons’ — suggests she is preparing for a war on multiple fronts. Her relationship with Xiao Chen remains ambiguous, but her political acumen is sharp.
- The chapter ends with the doppelgänger vowing to destroy the stone statue and seize the war swords. Readers should watch for the fate of the Twenty-Four War Swords, the nature of the remnant soul, and whether Xiao Chen can reclaim his former power from the false self.
