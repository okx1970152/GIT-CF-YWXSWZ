## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a decisive shift in Xiao Chen's approach on Dragon Island. No longer content to react to threats, he takes the offensive, launching a brutal nighttime raid against the Undying Alliance and its allied factions after learning of their conspiracy to ambush him. The chapter's atmosphere is soaked in rain, thunder, and blood, as Xiao Chen unleashes the full force of his Mortal-Shedding Fifth Heaven power. The tone is one of cold, calculated ruthlessness—Xiao Chen is no longer the hunted fugitive but the hunter, and the stormy night becomes both a literal and metaphorical backdrop for his righteous slaughter. The chapter also deepens the worldbuilding by introducing two deadly rivals: Kailo, a genius illusionist, and Wang Tong, the inheritor of the legendary Little Li Flying Dagger.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen learns from his allies, the monks Yizhen and Yichi, that the Undying Alliance, led by Rand, has allied with the Phantom Alliance (founded by Kailo) and the Flying Dagger Alliance (founded by Wang Tong) to eliminate him. Suspicious, he investigates and discovers that Kailo and Wang Tong have vanished along with key members of the Undying Alliance, likely hunting a Companion Dragon King—or waiting to ambush him. Instead of waiting to be attacked, Xiao Chen decides to preemptively destroy their base camps. That night, he leads his three skeleton kings to the Undying Alliance's camp. He cuts down their buildings with a single blade-light and leaves the remaining guards to the skeletons, who are close to the Third Heaven of Mortal-Shedding. When members of the Phantom and Flying Dagger Alliances attempt to intervene, Xiao Chen turns on them with overwhelming force. In a gruesome, rain-soaked massacre, he annihilates all three alliances' residual forces, cementing his reputation as a merciless and unstoppable avenger.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The chapter introduces two legendary lineages from Chinese literature. Kailo is described as a "Spiritist" (灵士) with innate illusion powers, a rare type of cultivator whose abilities blur reality and deception—a staple in xianxia where spiritual talents are often hereditary or born of unique constitutions. Wang Tong is the descendant of Li Xunhuan, the protagonist of Gu Long's wuxia classic "The Legendary Couple" (《小李飞刀》). Li Xunhuan's trademark technique, the Little Li Flying Dagger (小李飞刀), is said to never miss its target; in this novel's mythology, he even slew a god upon entering the World of Immortality. This intertextual reference enriches the world by incorporating legendary figures from Chinese martial arts fiction, treating them as historical figures who ascended to immortality. The chapter also reinforces the realm system: Mortal-Shedding Fifth Heaven is shown as an unstoppable tier against lower-realm opponents, with Xiao Chen's blade-light carving through enemies and terrain alike.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for an inevitable confrontation with the top-tier figures of Dragon Island. Xiao Chen has deliberately provoked the three strongest alliances, ensuring that Kailo, Wang Tong, and Rand will seek vengeance. Readers should pay close attention to the power scales introduced: Illusionist abilities (Kailo) and the one-shot kill threat of the Little Li Flying Dagger (Wang Tong) represent dangers that brute force alone cannot overcome. The skeleton kings are approaching a breakthrough, which may give Xiao Chen more tactical options in future battles. The rainy night massacre also solidifies Xiao Chen's reputation as a ruthless survivor, which will likely attract both enemies and potential allies. The next chapters promise either a grand confrontation or a strategic retreat into deeper training. The absence of Yan Qingcheng and her faction is notable—she may be planning something more subtle.
