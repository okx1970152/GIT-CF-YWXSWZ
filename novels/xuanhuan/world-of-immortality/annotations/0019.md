## Chapter Overview
This chapter marks a decisive shift in Xiao Chen’s survival strategy from passive evasion to proactive elimination. Armed with cold precision and a pragmatic ruthlessness that has become his hallmark, he stalks the encampment of the brown-haired newcomer—an ally of Zhao Lin’er—and systematically eliminates the enemy’s outer guard. The serene beauty of the island’s coconut grove and azure sea forms a stark, almost surreal backdrop to the brutal ambushes. Xiao Chen’s internal monologue is sparse; his actions speak volumes. Yet the chapter also introduces a new layer of threat: the brown-haired man’s mastery of magical scrolls, which can conjure serpentine energy constructs and volleys of ice spears. The final trap of eight scrolls encircling the bamboo hut signals that Xiao Chen has walked into a deliberate snare, raising the stakes for his next move.

## Key Plot Points
Xiao Chen begins by scouting the enemy encampment near a bamboo hut. He locates the brown-haired man (Guluo) and his servants—a male and female attendant, plus three hidden guardians. After waiting for an opportunity, he follows the two attendants as they depart to patrol the beach. In a swift ambush, he hurls bamboo spears at them, then engages in close combat. He breaks the male servant’s arms and crushes his chest, then pursues the female. She unleashes a scroll, releasing a three-meter-long energy serpent accompanied by a barrage of ice spears. Xiao Chen dodges but is forced to retreat momentarily. He kills her with a bamboo spear, staining the sand red. Returning to the bamboo hut, he attempts to eliminate the three hidden guardians. He nearly succeeds on the first, but the man senses him and conjures a flame blade, forcing a brief clash. The noise alerts the others and the brown-haired man emerges. Xiao Chen decides to retreat, but eight scrolls unfurl from surrounding trees, forming a trapping formation. The brown-haired man reveals his confidence, stating he had planned to hunt Xiao Chen anyway. Xiao Chen is now cornered, facing an unknown magical array.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
Magical scrolls (卷轴) are a recurring artifact in the World of Immortality, serving as portable spell repositories. They can be inscribed with images and incantations that, when activated, unleash elemental attacks—energy beasts, ice spears, flame blades, or sealing formations. In this chapter, the female servant’s scroll creates a hybrid attack: a living energy serpent plus a volley of solid ice spears. This fusion of Eastern talisman and Western grimoire concepts reflects the novel’s blending of martial cultivation with arcane magic. The brown-haired man’s eight-scroll formation echoes the Daoist eight-trigram array, but his control via chanting and hand signs suggests a unique sorcerous tradition. The bamboo spear, a simple weapon, contrasts with these high-tier artifacts, highlighting Xiao Chen’s resourcefulness and the world’s layered power systems.

## Reading Guide
With Xiao Chen trapped inside a circle of eight magical scrolls, the next chapter promises a desperate confrontation. The brown-haired man – whose name and origins remain unknown – exudes confidence and commands formidable artifacts. Xiao Chen must find a way to break the formation without sustaining further injury, especially since he is still recovering from earlier battles. The chapter also raises questions: How deep is this man’s alliance with Zhao Lin’er? What other scrolls does he possess? And will Xiao Chen’s unique acupoint cultivation give him an unexpected advantage? Readers should watch for tactical ingenuity from the protagonist, as direct force against unknown magic may prove foolhardy. The serene island setting continues to contrast with the violence, reminding us that beauty and danger coexist in this realm.
