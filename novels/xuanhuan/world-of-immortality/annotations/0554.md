## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a brutal, high-stakes confrontation as Xiao Chen makes a desperate bid to ascend to the Heavenly Realm. Brandishing the melting token, he fuses the Formation Diagram with thirty-three War Swords, knowing his body cannot bear the strain without the Yellow Clay Altar. Yet two overwhelming foes await: the enigmatic Human Demon Ge Qian, whose power rivals the peerless Stone Man, and a silent giant who breathes starry bodies — an Upper Ancestral God. The clash is catastrophic. Xiao Chen's body cracks like pottery, geysers of blood staining the sky, and in the end he is hurled into the fabled Land of Chaos, a wasteland that once witnessed the annihilation of a primordial race. The chapter is a tragic testament to the gulf between Half-Ancestor and those who guard the ultimate secrets.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen learns that his token will self-destruct after a single activation, forcing a do-or-die assault on the heavenly waterfall passage. Ge Qian intercepts him with calm, almost sorrowful authority, refusing to let him pass. The giant Upper Ancestral God then joins the fray, hurling twin celestial orbs as weapons. Xiao Chen responds by merging his body with the Formation Diagram and the War Swords, unleashing a devastating volley of sword-light. He wounds the giant's hand and even cracks Ge Qian's stone arm, but at a horrendous cost — his own form splinters under the backlash. Without the Yellow Clay Altar's protection, he cannot sustain the power. Ge Qian's final strike, a sweeping blow from his arm, sends Xiao Chen tumbling into the Land of Chaos. The token dissolves. The path to Heaven is sealed. Underlying the combat is Ge Qian's complex character: he speaks of duty and regret, calling Xiao Chen a potential ancestral-god-level being, yet he does not relent. The giant shows fear of Ge Qian, hinting at an even deeper hierarchy among the Otherworld's guardians.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The "Formation Diagram" is a fragment of the legendary Immortal-Slaying Sword Formation, originally belonging to the sage Tongtian. When activated, it can annihilate Ancestral Gods, but it drains the user's vitality to a dangerous degree. The "Upper Ancestral God" (上祖神) represents a tier above ordinary Ancestral Gods, beings who have assimilated the laws of entire worlds and can command starry essences as combat tools. The "Land of Chaos" (罪乱之地) is the ruined homeland of a vanished race that once shattered the Otherworld's foundation; it is a realm of unpredictable laws and lingering death, safe only for the truly strong or the utterly broken. The token used by Xiao Chen was a key to the Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界), a dimension believed to hold the power to turn the tide against the invading forces. Its self-destruction underscores the finality of this failed attempt.

## Reading Guide

The chapter ends with Xiao Chen cast into the Land of Chaos, a place of legend and danger. Readers should watch for how this setback might become a hidden opportunity—many a cultivator has grown stronger by surviving a near-fatal ordeal. Ge Qian's motives remain ambiguous: why does he block Xiao Chen with such personal investment? The giant Upper Ancestral God's deference to Ge Qian suggests layers of power yet unrevealed. Without the token, Xiao Chen's path to the Heavenly Realm is severed, but the Land of Chaos may conceal its own secrets—perhaps fragments of the destroyed civilization's power, or even a way to bypass the need for the Heavenly Realm altogether. The question of whether Ge Qian is an enemy, a tragic gatekeeper, or something else lingers. Next chapter should reveal the nature of this desolate new world and Xiao Chen's first steps within it.
