## Chapter Overview

This chapter thrusts the reader into the heart of political and martial intrigue in the Yin Capital of the Dashang Kingdom. A mysterious swordsman, wielding only an iron sword, declares himself not the top young expert of the Southern Wastes—yet proceeds to dismantle the assembled elite of the capital's noble families with contemptuous ease. The scene is a book-sword tea gathering, ostensibly a talent selection, but it quickly becomes a stage for raw power and the shifting tides of authority. The contrast between the calm, almost bored voice of the swordsman and the brutal, efficient carnage creates a tension that underscores the fragile nature of aristocratic dominance when faced with true martial prowess. This chapter is a showcase for a character whose name is already spoken with fear and respect in Heavenly Emperor City, and it sets the stage for the larger political games that will unfold.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the swordsman's chilling declaration that he is not the Southern Wastes' top youth expert, immediately unsettling the assembled nobles. Despite their attempts to dismiss him, several elite guards are sent to capture or kill him, only to be swiftly cut down by the iron sword. The methodical slaughter—from the first two guards bisected to the final group of spiritists and warriors—establishes the swordsman's absolute dominance. Xu Shan, a powerful spiritist from the Bo family, attempts to engage using the legendary Sleeve Universe technique, but his prodigious skill is shattered by the sword. The swordsman's energy form manifests, revealing that he fights with pure sword intent rather than a physical avatar. After defeating multiple challengers, including a second spiritist, the swordsman openly invites the remaining opponents to attack together. The scene ends with the noble families in shock and the Princess of Dashang taking an interest in recruiting this formidable figure. The chapter reinforces the theme that true power transcends family and political backing, and that a single unmatched warrior can destabilize the established order.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The 'book-sword tea gathering' (书剑茶会) is a classic xianxia trope—a social event where young cultivators display their talents in both literary and martial arts, often a pretext for noble families to scout for talent. The presence of the Princess of Dashang and her retinue highlights the intersection of cultivation and court politics. The Sleeve Universe technique (袖里乾坤) is a famous divine ability from Chinese myth, often associated with the Daoist immortal Zhang Guolao; here it is used by a spiritist and represents the pinnacle of spatial manipulation within the Mortal-Shedding realm. The swordsman's ability to fight with a purely energy-based form, condensing sword intent into a tangible shadow, reflects a high level of mastery where spirit and technique merge. The political fragmentation of the Yin capital into rival noble factions echoes historical Chinese dynastic struggles, adding a layer of realistic court intrigue to the mythological setting.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a dramatic showcase for Dugu Jianmo, a character who has been a lurking presence in the background of the arc. His cold, efficient combat style and his almost dismissive attitude toward the capital's elite reinforce his reputation as a peerless swordsman. Readers should pay close attention to the political ripple effects of his demonstration—the Princess's interest and the humiliation of the Bo family will likely have consequences. The chapter also subtly contrasts the 'polite' world of court politics with the brutal reality of cultivation, where strength speaks louder than lineage. Future chapters may shift focus to how the capital's powers react to this disruption, and whether Dugu Jianmo's actions will bring him into direct conflict with Xiao Chen, who is navigating similar political currents.
