## Chapter Overview

This chapter transitions from Xiao Chen's audacious night of challenges to the political and commercial machinations of Heavenly Emperor City. The sale of the Basde ancient spear nets Xiao Chen a fortune, but the relic's theft by its original owner throws the city into turmoil and conveniently shields Xiao Chen from lingering vendettas. Meanwhile, Yan Qingcheng's strained relationship with Xiao Chen is laid bare in a tense exchange, and the looming beast-fighting tournament sets the stage for a grand banquet at the Mande family's castle, where old rivals and new faces converge. The tone shifts from the raw intensity of personal combat to the subtle warfare of noble society, yet the undercurrents of power and ambition remain as sharp as ever.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with Yan Qingcheng's simmering resentment: she is bound to Xiao Chen through the Demon Seed Spirit Art, unable to strike at him and humiliated by his public claim that she is betrothed to him. Xiao Chen further provokes her by vowing to visit the Undying Sect to claim his 'wife'. This subplot reinforces their antagonistic, forced alliance. Next, the auction of the Basde ancient spear concludes at eighty thousand gold—a sum deemed too low due to collusion among major families including the Zhuge and Ligen clans. The families accept responsibility for the spear's past conflicts, relieving Xiao Chen of that burden. However, that very night, the spear is stolen by its original owner, a Western tribal powerhouse who gravely wounds an elder of one family before escaping. The theft becomes the talk of the city. Xiao Chen, now wealthy, entrusts Fatty Zhuge Ming with purchasing rare spirit herbs, including a legendary nine-petal lotus throne, to fuel his acupoint divinization. Finally, the chapter ends at the Mande family's grand banquet, where the beast-fighting tournament's participants and influential figures gather. Xiao Chen meets Kanasi, her mysterious 'deformed' older brother—a blue-haired youth of intimidating reputation—and finds himself once again under the hostile gaze of Hofman and Li Dongbo.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Basde ancient spear is a War Clan relic—a lineage said to descend from battle gods. Such weapons carry not only immense power but also historical and bloodline burdens, making them targets for both collectors and the original clans. The collusion among noble families to suppress the auction price reflects the entrenched power structures of Heavenly Emperor City, where wealth and influence are tightly controlled. The beast-fighting tournament, a major event drawing talents from across the Southern Wastes, serves as a proxy for clan rivalries and cultivator status. Spirit herbs like the nine-petal lotus throne are rare resources that can dramatically enhance cultivation; Xiao Chen's plan to spend eighty thousand gold on such herbs underscores his unique path of acupoint divinization, a method far more resource-intensive than standard realm cultivation. The 'deformed' epithet for Kanasi's brother is a xianxia idiom for a prodigy whose abilities are so extreme they appear monstrous—a sign of supreme talent.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets in motion several converging threads. The theft of the Basde spear may draw the original owner back into the narrative, potentially as a future ally or foe. Xiao Chen's investment in rare herbs signals an imminent cultivation breakthrough, which will be critical for the upcoming tournament. The Mande family banquet introduces Kanasi's brother, a powerhouse likely to be a major contender in the beast-fighting tournament, and the tension with Hofman and Li Dongbo hints at continued conflict. Yan Qingcheng's reluctant bond with Xiao Chen will likely evolve as she seeks to surpass him through the Demon Seed Spirit Art. Watch for how the tournament's political backdrop forces alliances and enmities to crystallize.
