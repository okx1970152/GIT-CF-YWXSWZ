## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a surprising turn of events as several former enemies approach Xiao Chen seeking reconciliation. The proud Spellmaster Rand, once arrogant, humbly admits his mistakes and asks for peace, having been thoroughly humbled by Xiao Chen's victory. More shockingly, Zhao Lin'er appears before him—not as a vengeful hunter, but as a guide. She leads Xiao Chen to the shore, where an ethereal figure awaits: Lan Nuo, the very cultivator whose void-shattering ascent transported Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er into the World of Immortality. The tension shifts from confrontation to mediation as Lan Nuo reveals her surprising presence on Dragon Island and her desire to heal the rift between the two. The chapter also deepens the cultivation lore, as Lan Nuo explains her weakened state: she is trapped in the dangerous Nirvana realm, her power rendered unstable by the island's ancient seal.

## Key Plot Points
Rand arrives at the clearing where Xiao Chen sits with his allies, openly confessing his former arrogance and acknowledging that Xiao Chen's severe beating taught him a necessary lesson. He points out that with their impending departure from Dragon Island, continuing their enmity would be meaningless. Xiao Chen, practical and tired of endless conflict, accepts the olive branch. Shortly after, Zhao Lin'er appears—shocking everyone—and announces she too wishes to end their feud. She leads Xiao Chen to the beach, where the legendary Lan Nuo stands beside a purple infant dragon. Xiao Chen is utterly stunned; he never expected the transcendent goddess who broke the void to be here. Lan Nuo explains that when she shattered the void, she saw Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er rushing in and tried to stop them, but could not reverse the cosmic force. She now wants to mediate, citing their shared origin from the Mortal World. After some tense back-and-forth, both Xiao Chen and Zhao Lin'er agree to swear an oath not to harm each other. The chapter then reveals Lan Nuo's condition: she entered the Nirvana realm just before her ascension. That realm causes her power to fluctuate between demigod-level and below-Spirit-Treasure, and Dragon Island's seal has further crippled her. She is now barely able to suppress a foe like Dugu Jianmo.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Nirvana realm (涅槃境界) is the fourth of the Five Great Realms in the cultivation hierarchy of World of Immortality. Practitioners in this realm hover on the edge of life and death, their power fluctuating wildly. At their peak, they can match a Deathless Longevity expert; at their lowest, they may fall below even a Spirit-Treasure cultivator. This makes Nirvana both a great opportunity and a deadly trap: those who understand the secret of life and death can transform and achieve true immortality, but those who remain stuck face total annihilation. Lan Nuo's situation also illustrates the unique suppression of Dragon Island. The ancient divine stele seals all divine powers, affecting even those in the Nirvana realm. This explains why a void-shattering goddess like Lan Nuo is now so weakened—she had to deliberately suppress her own cultivation to avoid being crushed by the island's seal.

## Reading Guide
This chapter resolves two major conflicts: Xiao Chen's enmity with Rand and his death-feud with Zhao Lin'er. However, the truce is fragile—Zhao Lin'er's oath may not erase her deep-seated bitterness. Meanwhile, the fate of Yan Qingcheng remains unresolved; Xiao Chen cannot easily release her without risking retaliation from the Undying Evil King lineage, yet killing her would bring even worse consequences. The appearance of Lan Nuo adds a new layer of mystery. She is clearly powerful in origin but currently crippled, which may become a vulnerability for her enemies. Finally, the mention of the flying dagger alliance and the illusionist alliance as still lurking threats suggests that not everyone will be as willing to forgive as Rand. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen balances these new peaceful ties with the inevitable dangers ahead.
