## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a breathtaking half-ancestor showdown as Laozi employs the legendary One Qi Transforms Three Purities technique against the formidable Green Lotus Celestial Maiden. The battle rages across sea and sky, with the maiden wielding four War Swords and a deadly Six Harmonies Extinction Sword Array. Xiao Chen and Keke seize a crucial opportunity to capture one of the legendary swords, but the victory is overshadowed by a devastating revelation: the soul of Ruoshui has been utterly annihilated, leaving Xiao Chen in a state of hollow numbness. The chapter masterfully blends epic martial action with profound emotional tragedy.

## Key Plot Points
Laozi unleashes the One Qi Transforms Three Purities, manifesting three half-ancestor-level Taoist incarnations—Shangqing, Yuqing, and Taiqing—each wielding distinct weapons. Together with his true body, they momentarily fight as four half-ancestors against the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden. The maiden proves her might, countering with her four War Swords and summoning the disastrous Six Harmonies Extinction Sword Array, which annihilates two of the purified incarnations. However, Laozi's strategy succeeds in securing one of her War Swords and throwing it to Xiao Chen, who, with Keke's Lost Paradise, permanently seizes the blade. The maiden's array is broken, and she is surrounded by eleven half-ancestors. In a moment of cruel triumph, she reveals that she has completely destroyed Ruoshui's soul—not killed, but annihilated beyond any possibility of recovery or reincarnation. Xiao Chen, his own soul incomplete from earlier sealing, finds himself unable to grieve, experiencing a profound and hollow emptiness.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The One Qi Transforms Three Purities is a classic Daoist technique from the Ming dynasty novel *Fengshen Yanyi* (Investiture of the Gods), attributed to the sage Laozi. In that story, Laozi splits his essence into three distinct Taoist lords—Shangqing, Yuqing, and Taiqing—each embodying a different aspect of the Dao. This chapter faithfully adapts that mythological feat, complete with their symbolic regalia: the Nine Cloud Crown, the Ruyi Crown, and the Nine Heavens Crown. The Six Harmonies Extinction Sword Array is an original creation for this novel, but it draws on the concept of "six directions" (up, down, east, west, north, south) used in many xianxia formations. The Lost Paradise, Keke's divine realm, continues to prove itself a threat even to half-ancestors, capable of trapping a legendary War Sword and neutralizing the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge. Ruoshui's absolute annihilation—erasing her soul imprint from the cosmos—is a tragic extreme in xianxia, surpassing mere death and closing all paths to revival.

## Reading Guide
The battle between Laozi and the Green Lotus Celestial Maiden showcases the peak of half-ancestor combat, with strategies that involve sacrifice and cunning rather than just raw power. Xiao Chen's capture of the War Sword via the Lost Paradise marks a major power gain, but his emotional disconnection signals a looming crisis: a half-soul cannot sustain true cultivation progress. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen will cope with this numbness, and whether Keke's Lost Paradise can be used to recover more treasures from the besieged maiden. The revelation of the Heaven-Reaching Death Bridge as a potential path to the Primordial Celestial Realm (洪荒天界) also sets up a new cosmic horizon. The next chapter will likely resolve the maiden's fate and explore the aftermath of Xiao Chen's hollow victory.
