## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers one of the most devastating and morally ambiguous moments in the Heavenly Burial Valley arc. Xiao Chen, cornered by an alliance of otherworld kings and cultivators from the Cultivation World, unleashes a forbidden weapon: the Ancestral God corpse poison sealed in a skull. The result is a massacre that kills over a thousand elite cultivators and nearly twenty Perfected Man realm kings in seconds. The tone is one of cold, brutal ruthlessness, highlighting Xiao Chen's transformation into a figure willing to use any means to survive. The chapter also showcases the cunning of Wu Ming, who manipulates the conflict to claim elemental souls, and the fragile nature of alliances when faced with mutual destruction.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Wu Ming falsely identifying Earth King as Xiao Chen, inciting the gathered cultivators to attack the otherworld kings. Xiao Chen plays along, brandishing the Bone King Banner to make the deception credible. A chaotic battle erupts, during which Wu Ming kills Sea God King with a hidden half-ancestor hammer, claiming his water-element soul. The Ye family, including the perceptive Ye Jing, begins to suspect foul play. Xiao Chen then reveals his true identity, shattering the pretense. Despite brief cooperation, Xiao Chen understands he is still the primary target. In a calculated move, he secretly negotiates with each faction to sow discord while preparing his true escape plan. When the leaders decide to eliminate him together, Xiao Chen unleashes the Ancestral God corpse poison from the skull. The poison spreads instantly, killing all but five individuals in the hall: Wu Ming, Yaksha Queen, War King Mabao, Earth King, and Xiao Chen himself. The action is rapid, with short, percussive sentences conveying the sudden devastation.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Ancestral God corpse poison is a legendary toxin derived from the decayed body of a fallen Ancestral God. It can kill even Perfected Man realm experts, who are normally nearly immune to mundane toxins. The skull containing the poison was originally obtained by Xiao Chen from a Yellow Diamond skeleton king in the Death World, and its power stems from the residual divine laws of a dead Ancestral God. The concept of 'ten thousand laws' immunity, as demonstrated by the Yaksha Queen, is a rare defensive ability that can resist most magical attacks but proves useless against such primordial poison. The Cultivation World (also called the Qi Realm) is one of the four worlds beyond the Mortal World, known for its emphasis on artifact refinement and sword cultivation. Wu Ming's family, the Artifact Refining Sect, is a major power skilled in forging divine weapons.

## Reading Guide
This chapter has massive implications for the political landscape of both the Cultivation World and the Eternal Continent. The near-total annihilation of the expeditionary force means that many factions will be leaderless and weakened, potentially triggering a scramble for power. Wu Ming has successfully gathered multiple elemental souls, setting him up as a major threat with a perfected divine body. Xiao Chen, meanwhile, has painted a massive target on himself by using such a forbidden weapon. The half-ancestors will likely intervene, and the fragile peace between the Hundred Races threatens to collapse. Readers should watch for the fallout among the surviving kings—Mabao and Earth King—and the regrouping of the Ye family. The fate of Keke and the other allies left behind also remains a pressing concern. The next chapter will likely deal with the immediate aftermath and set the stage for the final confrontation.
