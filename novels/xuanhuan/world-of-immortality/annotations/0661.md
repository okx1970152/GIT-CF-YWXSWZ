## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a breathtaking fusion of high-level combat and world-shattering revelation. Xiao Chen, having seized control of the Five Emperor Tower and the perfect corpse of a fallen Stone Man King, faces down multiple hostile Stone Kings in a desperate bid to escape the Death World. The female corpse's transcendent power exceeds the world's limits, but at a terrible cost: her form begins to crack and decay. After a narrow escape, the narrative pivots to an even more shocking scene: an ancient being from the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps descends upon the Nine Provinces, intent on destruction. In response, the nine ancient lamps rise, and their true nature is unveiled—they are the tombs of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors. The chapter thus merges pulse-pounding action with deep mythological lore, setting the stage for the final confrontations of the arc.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen remains inside the two-layer Five Emperor Tower, controlling the female corpse to wield the tower's power. He repels the Purple Xia King's attempt to reclaim the tower, using the Five Sovereigns' imprints to stabilize the structure. The female corpse's body begins to show severe cracks from the exertion. Xiao Chen seizes the moment to briefly enter the second tower layer and partially erase the Purple Xia King's spiritual imprints. With the help of Shi Zhongdi and Ge Qian, Xiao Chen escapes the encircling Stone Kings, tossing the twin tower to Shi Zhongdi for safekeeping. Meanwhile, the ancient entity at the end of the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps—the same being that once hunted Xiao Chen for the Heavenly Mark—attacks the mortal world with a giant claw. The Nine Provinces' nine ancient lamps ascend, and Ge Qian's own lamp flies back to join them. The lamps emit sacred flames that grievously burn the ancient being, forcing it to retreat while vowing to return. In a stunning revelation, the being declares that the lamps are the tombs of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors, who fell ages ago. Xiao Chen learns that the lamps are not merely protective artifacts but the eternal resting places of the primordial supreme powers.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Five Emperor Tower (五帝塔) is a legendary artifact rivaling the Three Sovereigns Mirror. Its layered structure—each floor representing an emperor—makes it a supreme tool for sealing and suppression. The concept of a 'perfect body' that exceeds the world's allowed power is central here: such a vessel cannot exist for long without collapsing, a reflection of the 'Heaven's Jealousy' principle seen earlier. The nine ancient lamps are now confirmed to be the actual graves of the Three Sovereigns (三皇) and Five Emperors (五帝), foundational figures in Chinese mythology. In the novel, these are not mere historical legends but ascendant beings whose residual power can still protect the Nine Provinces. Their appearance as lamps that burn the invading ancient being draws on the symbolism of light vanquishing darkness and of ancestral spirits guarding their homeland. The ancient being from the Ninety-Nine Stone Steps remains unnamed, but its connection to the Otherworld's deepest secrets suggests it is one of the primordial evils that the ancestors once fought.

## Reading Guide

The chapter leaves several threads dangling. The female corpse's body is nearly spent—will it be used again, or has it reached its limit? The ancient being is wounded but vows to return, and the lamps have now been exposed; their protection may not be permanent. Xiao Chen has relinquished the Five Emperor Tower to Shi Zhongdi, but the Stone Kings will surely continue to hunt for it. Meanwhile, Shi Zhongdi and the old stone turtle now hold a weapon capable of killing Stone Kings, which could shift the balance of power. Pay attention to Ge Qian's lamp—he lost it to the Nine Provinces, and his reaction is yet to be seen. Finally, the revelation that the lamps are tombs ties back to the earlier hints about the fates of the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors. This chapter sets up a major confrontation: the ancient being, the Stone Kings, and the remaining heroes all converging on the Nine Provinces. The next chapters should deal with the aftermath of this assault and Xiao Chen's next move to gather the Heavenly Steles.
