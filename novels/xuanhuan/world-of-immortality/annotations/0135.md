## Chapter Overview
This chapter shifts focus from the mythic horrors of the Dead City to the gritty, commercial world of beast fighting on the mainland. Xiao Chen, accompanied by the Little Defiant Dragon and Keke, enters a frontier town and encounters a shrewd but affable beast merchant named Fatty Zhuge. The core of the chapter is a series of trial fights that reveal the Little Defiant Dragon’s extraordinary combat potential—its blend of raw power, tactical cunning, and dragon lineage. The chapter also sets up a journey toward the great city of Heavenly Emperor City, but a detour into the Southern Wastes derails the plan, leaving Xiao Chen and his companions lost in a land of ancient legends and primordial beasts.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a meal where the two small beasts (Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon) express irritation at being treated like common pets. Fatty Zhuge, a beast-fighting entrepreneur, shows keen interest in the Little Defiant Dragon, recognizing its potential. They arrange a trial at the local beast arena. In the first bout, the Little Defiant Dragon intimidates a leopard with mere dragon aura, causing it to collapse. The second bout sees it defeat five lions with swift tail strikes. The third and most significant bout pits it against a six-meter white wolf that can fly and breathe fire. The Little Defiant Dragon initially struggles, forced on the defensive, but then feigns weakness before leaping to deliver a devastating blow to the wolf’s underbelly, winning the fight. Fatty is thrilled and agrees to provide the dragon with powerful opponents, envisioning a grand future. He then sets off on a personal mission into the Southern Wastes, promising to return within a week. Days stretch into twenty, and Xiao Chen, having waited, decides to head toward Heavenly Emperor City on his own. However, the roads are broken and overgrown, and soon Xiao Chen, Keke, and the Little Defiant Dragon find themselves lost in the wild Southern Wastes, where mysterious ancient energies and formidable beasts lurk. The chapter ends with Fatty finally returning to the town, only to learn Xiao Chen has left, and receiving news of a dragon king appearance in Heavenly Emperor City, prompting him to set out in pursuit.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The beast fighting industry, or 斗兽 (dòu shòu), is a major cultural and economic force in the World of Immortality. It is not mere entertainment but a deeply ingrained practice that tests the bloodlines and combat potential of rare creatures. The "试兽" (shì shòu) or trial process is a standardized method to evaluate a beast’s abilities before it enters formal competition. The arena described reflects a stratified society where beast owners, merchants, and gamblers all have stakes. The Southern Wastes (南荒) are steeped in legend, said to be the retreat of Sovereign Suiren (燧人氏), a primordial ancestor who taught humanity the use of fire. However, Xiao Chen’s possession of a burial shroud from the Dead City casts doubt on whether such gods still live. The region’s dangerous primordial beasts hint at the untamed wildness of this world, a stark contrast to the civilized cities.

## Reading Guide
This chapter serves as a bridge between the claustrophobic horror of the Dead City arc and the wide-open exploration of the mainland. Readers should watch how the Little Defiant Dragon’s growth accelerates through combat, and how its bond with Xiao Chen deepens. Fatty Zhuge’s reliability is questionable; his disappearance may signal further complications or an ambush in the Southern Wastes. The mention of a dragon king appearing in Heavenly Emperor City suggests that other Companion Dragon Kings from Dragon Island have also arrived, setting the stage for conflicts and alliances in the capital. The Southern Wastes are a wild card—full of ancient power and potential secrets that may intersect with Xiao Chen’s quest for the complete Qi-Refining Diagram and his own path to power.
