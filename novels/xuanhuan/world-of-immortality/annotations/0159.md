## Chapter Overview

This chapter serves as a pivotal transition in the Heavenly Emperor City arc, blending mystery, cultivation, and the quiet development of supporting characters. The tone shifts from the political and social tensions of the previous chapters to a more introspective and mythic register as Xiao Chen reunites with the white-shelled turtle and confirms its true nature as a sealed dragon. The chapter also sets in motion a major change for the three skeletons, who are taken by a mysterious old spiritist from Beidou Academy for training—a decision that may reshape their fates. Meanwhile, the looming beast tournament and the arrival of northern elites continue to build pressure, but Xiao Chen chooses to retreat into the wilderness for intense cultivation, preparing for the clashes ahead.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with a brief scene of three top young experts from the Southern Wastes ending their secluded cultivation and heading back to Heavenly Emperor City, drawn by the approaching beast tournament and the arrival of powerful northern practitioners. This establishes the escalating stakes. The focus then shifts to Xiao Chen's courtyard, where he discovers the white-shelled turtle—the 'Little Thief'—swimming playfully, displaying un-turtle-like agility. The turtle's black iron chain has come undone, and the three skeletons reveal that it drank the blood of the Little Defiant Dragon when the latter was gravely wounded, which apparently dissolved the seal. Xiao Chen confirms the turtle is indeed a sealed dragon through ancient characters it carves. An old, white-haired spiritist from Beidou Academy arrives, seeking the three skeletons. He reveals he sensed their powerful soul-flames and offers to teach them life spirit techniques, implying they may one day be reborn. The skeletons agree to go with him. Zhuge Liang and Hai Yuntian later identify the old man as an ancient figure from family portraits—a master of life spirit arts. They warn Xiao Chen that Hoffmann and Zhuge Kun, still suspicious of his identity, are plotting against him. Instead of confronting them directly, Xiao Chen takes Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon into the mountains near Heavenly Emperor City to cultivate under a ten-meter waterfall for ten days, tempering both body and mind before the inevitable battles.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The scene where the white-shelled turtle drinks dragon blood to break its ancient chain taps into a common xianxia trope: powerful bloodlines can unlock seals or grant transformations. The old spiritist's ability to conduct soul-level dialogue with the undead reflects an advanced form of spiritual cultivation unique to Spiritists, who manipulate life force and consciousness. His age—stretching back multiple generations—hints at the longevity of high-level cultivators and the deep history of institutions like Beidou Academy. The waterfall cultivation is a classic xianxia training method: using the crushing force of falling water to strengthen the physical body while simultaneously achieving mental stillness. This dual cultivation of body and mind is fundamental to the Mortal-Shedding realm, where the practitioner must shed mortal limits. The mention of ancient divine characters and the fact that only the character 'dragon' (龙) remains unchanged over eons highlights the continuity of dragon-civilization in this world.

## Reading Guide

This chapter plants several seeds for future arcs. The white turtle, now revealed as a sealed dragon, is likely to become a major companion or treasure. Its ability to move with extreme speed and its sly intelligence suggest hidden power. The three skeletons' training under the ancient spiritist may lead them to evolve into true undying kings—a development that could be crucial later. Xiao Chen's retreat into cultivation signals that he is stockpiling power before the storm. The warnings from Hai Yuntian and Zhuge Liang confirm that Hoffmann and Zhuge Kun are still active threats, and their schemes will likely come to a head when Xiao Chen returns to the city. The approaching beast tournament will bring together young elites from across the continent, making it a perfect stage for conflict and showcasing Xiao Chen's growth as a practitioner.
