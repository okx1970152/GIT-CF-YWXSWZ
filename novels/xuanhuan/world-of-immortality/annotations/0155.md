## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a decisive shift in Xiao Chen's approach to settling scores in Heavenly Emperor City. Rather than hunting down his enemies one by one, he forces them to come to him—and then dismantles their pride with cold, methodical brutality. The setting, a luxurious steam bath, is deliberately ironic: a place of relaxation becomes a stage for humiliation. Xiao Chen's interrogation of Hofmann and his cronies is not mere revenge; it is a calculated display of dominance designed to send a message to every noble family who participated in the attack on the Little Defiant Dragon. Meanwhile, off to the side, another kind of scheming unfolds as the women of the Mand family and Yan Qingcheng plot to make trouble for Xiao Chen through Keke. The chapter balances raw power politics with lighter, almost mischievous intrigue, showing both the heavy hand and the subtle threads of the world.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen begins by confronting Hofmann, a depraved noble responsible for numerous violent crimes. At the peak of Mortal-Shedding Ninth Heaven, Xiao Chen effortlessly overpowers Hofmann's Fourth Heaven cultivation, slapping him repeatedly and dragging him through the water like a puppet. Li Dongbo, who earlier tried to slap Xiao Chen, receives similar treatment. Keke returns from its wanderings and, at Xiao Chen's command, uses its binding light to freeze all of Hofmann's guards in place, submerging them in the hot spring. Xiao Chen's dialogue is a series of calculated humiliations: he forces each man to approach, then slaps them down again. He shows no mercy, not because of cruelty, but because he knows these men have committed murder, rape, and robbery without consequence. The scene establishes that Xiao Chen will not be bound by the usual social hierarchies of Heavenly Emperor City. In the adjacent bath, Kanasi, Hai Yunxue, and Yan Qingcheng discuss the adorable white beast (Keke) and decide to go find it, planning to cause trouble for Xiao Chen. Yan Qingcheng silently approves, eager to get back at Xiao Chen for past humiliations.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The steam bath culture depicted here is a common trope in Chinese web novels—a neutral, boundary-blurring space where cultivators relax, scheme, and sometimes fight. The hot spring setting removes overt weapons and armor, placing emphasis on raw cultivation power. Xiao Chen's Mortal-Shedding Ninth Heaven is the peak of the first realm, just a step from Spirit-Treasury, making him virtually untouchable to Fourth or Sixth Heaven opponents. The Mand, Rigen, and Zhuge families are examples of powerful clans that operate Beast-Fighting Palaces—arenas where spirit beasts are forced to fight for gambling and prestige. These families are the true political powers in the Southern Wastes, and challenging their scions is a direct assault on their authority. Keke's binding art, a rainbow light that immobilizes enemies, is a unique divine ability that hints at its primordial lineage. The chapter also reinforces the strict hierarchy in the cultivation world: higher realm cultivators can humiliate lower ones with impunity, and face is a currency more valuable than gold.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a palate cleanser after the tension of Dragon Island—a moment of catharsis where Xiao Chen takes control of his fate in the city. Watch for how the power dynamics shift: Xiao Chen's open defiance of the Rigen family will almost certainly bring retaliation. The women's plot involving Keke introduces a new form of conflict—social and political rather than direct combat. Kanasi, though defeated earlier, is not broken; her alliance with Yan Qingcheng suggests that Xiao Chen's enemies are consolidating. The chapter ends on a note of impending mischief rather than violence, setting the stage for a more playful but still dangerous confrontation. Pay attention to how Xiao Chen's reputation as the 'madman' spreads; his methods are crude but effective, and they establish a pattern that will either attract powerful allies or bring down overwhelming force.
