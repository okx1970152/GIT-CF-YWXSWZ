## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a harrowing turning point in Xiao Chen's journey. Stricken by grief over Keke's apparent death, he is confronted by two ancient divine weapons — the Dark-Iron Seal and the Golden Divine Halberd — which tempt him with offers of immense power. Refusing to sacrifice the Seven-Colored Sacred Tree that holds Keke's essence, Xiao Chen instead trades two hundred years of his own lifespan for a single night of demigod-slaying strength. What follows is a brutal, cathartic rampage as he faces five demigods in the skies over Heavenly Emperor City. The chapter weaves together profound philosophical temptation, the cost of vengeance, and the raw violence of a man with nothing left to lose.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter opens with the two sacred artifacts speaking directly to Xiao Chen, offering him the power to annihilate his enemies. They claim a fearsome pedigree — the Dark-Iron Seal once crushed the past life of Laozi into mortal pulp, and the Golden Divine Halberd pinned the Buddha's past life to a peak for forty-nine days until he bled out. Their goal is to obtain the seven-colored radiance within the Sacred Tree, which holds Keke's soul. Xiao Chen refuses, so they propose an alternative: trade two hundred years of his life for half a night of transcendent power. With the lifespan calculations laid bare — a Mortal-Shedding cultivator lives 160 years, a Spirit-Treasury cultivator 240 — Xiao Chen realizes he will be left with only nineteen years to live. He accepts without hesitation, his hair turning stark white as his life force drains away. Empowered by the weapons, he confronts five demigods: the Tiger Servant, Tiger Attendant, and three from the Hai family. In a furious assault, he cuts down the Hai family demigod, wounds the Tiger Servant, and impales the Tiger Attendant, carrying his body through the city as a symbol of his wrath. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen, white-haired and blood-soaked, screaming his defiance against the night.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter vividly illustrates the sacredness of lifespan in xianxia cultivation. Each realm grants additional years: Mortal-Shedding doubles a mortal's eighty-year span, Spirit-Treasury adds another eighty, and higher realms extend life further. Trading away centuries is a desperate, almost suicidal act, as it compresses the time available for future breakthroughs. The two weapons' claims about killing past lives of Laozi and Buddha tap into the trope of cyclical reincarnation and the ancient warfare among primordial beings. The Tiger family's "Ancestral Metamorphosis" technique allows a human practitioner to assume a white tiger form, drawing on the bloodline of a divine beast. Such bloodline-based arts are common in xianxia, where heritage grants access to unique powers. The weapons themselves are sentient, malevolent entities — a reminder that in this world, even artifacts have wills and agendas.

## Reading Guide

Xiao Chen's bargain leaves him with a ticking clock: nineteen years to reach Sky-Mastery or perish. This adds urgency to every future decision. Keke's fate remains ambiguous — the seven strands of essence within the Sacred Tree suggest a possibility of revival, which will likely become a driving goal. The Tiger family and Hai family have been dealt a severe blow, but their higher-level powers may now take notice. The two divine weapons remain sealed within Xiao Chen's hands, sleeping but potentially dangerous. Readers should watch for signs of the weapons' influence and for any hints of a way to recover Xiao Chen's lost lifespan or Keke's rebirth. This chapter intensifies the story's emotional stakes and sets the stage for a desperate race against time.
