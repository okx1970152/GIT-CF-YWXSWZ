## Chapter Overview

This chapter weaves together two threads: the Old Goat's recollection of two vanished Stone-Man Kings and a desperate escape from six Celestial Realm giants. The narrative opens with a nostalgic, tragic account of peerless beings who stepped through a mysterious door and never returned, linking their fate to the enigmatic "stone key." The tone shifts to high-stakes action as Xiao Chen and the Old Goat, bearing the stone key, are pursued by six titans of the Heavenly Realm—Stone Corpse, Lotus King, Sharpening Beast, and others. The chapter masterfully blends mythic lore with visceral combat, culminating in a narrow escape through a chaotic passage to the Land of Chaos, where the Old Goat is forcibly summoned away by the chanting of "Shi Zhongdi returns." The dual focus deepens the Celestial Realm's mystery while showcasing Xiao Chen's growing power and alliances.

## Key Plot Points

The Old Goat recounts how, eons ago, he witnessed two Stone-Man Kings play chess and later ascend through a heavenly window, never to return. Their disappearance coincides with the emergence of the Otherworld and the Nine Provinces as separate realms, hinting at a vast conspiracy. The key clue—"stone key"—was dismissed for ages until now. Xiao Chen and the Old Goat test the stone key against the fossilized root of a primordial tree, triggering a brief vision of a window opening and flower petals stained with blood. Six Celestial giants—Stone Corpse, Lotus King, Sharpening Beast, Water King Song Dafei, Suspended Ancestor, and the Rebel Heaven Warrior Shan Jun—converge, furious at being deceived about the treasure's nature. Xiao Chen and the Old Goat flee toward the chaotic passage to the lower realms. During the pursuit, Xiao Chen uses the ancient scroll to shatter two incomplete divine cities belonging to descendants of the Otherworld Stone-Man King lineage, grievously wounding their foundations. He sustains devastating blows from the six giants but reaches the Land of Chaos sanctuary. However, the six giants invoke a ritual to summon the Old Goat back, chanting "Shi Zhongdi returns," forcing him to fade away, declaring he will be reborn in thirty thousand years.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of "stone key" (石钥匙) resonates with the broader mythology of keys unlocking hidden realms, a common motif in Chinese xianxia. The Old Goat's reference to "falling flower petals stained with blood" as an omen of a Stone-Man King's death echoes the poetic tradition of using natural imagery to signal cosmic events—a hallmark of Chen Dong's style. The Celestial Realm (天界) is depicted as a vast, ancient domain where supreme beings like the Stone Corpse (石尸) and Lotus King (莲王) dwell; their titles evoke Buddhist and Daoist symbolism (the lotus representing purity, the corpse representing undying yin). The "Reverse Heaven Battle Treasure" (逆天战宝) is revealed to be the stone key itself, not a conventional weapon—this subverts expectations and emphasizes that true treasures are often misunderstood. The ritual summoning of the Old Goat by chanting "Shi Zhongdi returns" reflects the power of names and incantations in Daoist practice, where invoking a true name can compel a being's presence.

## Reading Guide

This chapter deepens the mystery of the Nine Provinces' ancient war and the Stone-Man Kings' fate. The stone key emerges as a pivotal artifact—likely connected to the ninety-nine stone steps and the Otherworld's supreme powers. Readers should note the Old Goat's sacrifice; despite his roguish demeanor, he willingly faces dissolution for Xiao Chen's sake, hinting at a deeper bond and the importance of the divine diagram. The two incomplete Stone Men whose cities Xiao Chen destroyed are descendants of the Otherworld's Stone-Man King lineage; this act cements his enmity with that faction and may trigger future retaliation. The Old Goat's claim that he will return in thirty thousand years leaves open the possibility of his reappearance, but for the immediate story, Xiao Chen loses a powerful ally. The next chapter will likely explore the aftermath in the Land of Chaos and Xiao Chen's next steps now that he is alone again.
