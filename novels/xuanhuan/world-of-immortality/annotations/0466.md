## Chapter Overview

This chapter plunges into the terrifying yellow sea of corpse fluid, a landscape of unimaginable horror. Xiao Chen follows the otherworld ancestor god Babulas, who seeks his lost divine weapon. The half-ancestors are drawn by rumors of 'Eternal Light', a treasure said to elevate a half-ancestor to ancestor godhood. What they find is an ancient seal, and the desperate scramble for what lies beneath threatens to awaken a sleeping horror. The tone is desolate and suspenseful, blending epic greed with the dread of cosmic consequences.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen arrives at the corpse sea and observes Babulas searching the seabed. He deduces that Babulas is looking for the Great Annihilation Spear, a weapon formidable enough to match the Heavenly Stele. Soon, a group of over a dozen half-ancestors converge on a massive island where divine light pulses, believing it to be Eternal Light. They break an ancient seal, suspecting it to be of otherworld origin. Babulas seizes a jade box from beneath the seal, but Xiao Chen ambushes him, stealing the box. The jade box is revealed to be the central formation flag of a much larger sealing array—removing it weakens the entire seal and endangers the region. Xiao Chen flees with all half-ancestors in pursuit, and as he escapes, he discovers a second island with an even stronger sacred light, hinting at a grand formation across the entire corpse sea.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The corpse sea is formed from the fluids of countless dead powerful beings—a concept drawn from the immense scale of death typical in xianxia worlds. 'Eternal Light' (永恒之光) is a legendary substance believed to allow a half-ancestor to transcend to the level of an ancestor god (祖神). The seal uses otherworld runes, emphasizing the cosmic conflict between realms. The Annihilation Spear (大破灭战矛) is a weapon that killed gods; its mere artifact soul was devastating in earlier battles. The jade box serving as a formation flag reminds readers that ancient seals are often complex arrays requiring specific keys, and tampering with them can have catastrophic consequences.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a major conflict: Xiao Chen now possesses the key component of the corpse sea's sealing formation, making him the target of every half-ancestor present. The second island suggests that the 'Eternal Light' may be a lure or part of a separate seal. The sleeping horror in the depths has not yet awakened, but the removal of the first seal may accelerate its emergence. Readers should watch for Babulas's next move—he will not give up his spear easily. The tension among the half-ancestors will likely erupt again as they are forced to cooperate or compete for the jade box.
