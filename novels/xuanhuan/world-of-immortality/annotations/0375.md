## Chapter Overview

This chapter delivers a spectacular clash between ultimate techniques and introduces the terrifying Yasha King. Wu Zhanhun, the flesh-and-blood clone of Xiao Chen, unleashes his "Return to the Origin" to overcome the Holy Dragon King's incomplete "Glorious Years," a victory that comes at great cost. But there is no respite: the Yasha King arrives, overwhelming in presence and power, forcing the allied group to resort to forbidden weapons from a lost civilization to buy time. Xiao Chen, observing from the sidelines, is pulled back to the Dead World before the battle's outcome. When he returns a day later, he finds only devastation and hears that the Yasha King survived and the allies were broken, with many captured or killed.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter begins with Wu Zhanhun and the Holy Dragon King locked in an apocalyptic exchange: "Return to the Origin" versus "Glorious Years." The battle tears a black hole in the sky, and the Holy Dragon King, having only mastered the "Light" aspect of Glorious Years without the "Time" component, is ultimately outmatched and disintegrates. War King Jing Pu notes the dragon's incomplete mastery, hinting at a greater threat. Immediately, the Yasha King arrives—a beautiful but dreadfully powerful figure who killed Qi Liao and Kanasi in five moves. He announces his intention to slaughter all who killed his allies. Liu Mu, Niu Ren, Jin Sanyi, and the Azure Dragon King use a precious pearl and forbidden weapons from a destroyed civilization to seal the Yasha King in a different dimensional space with devastating energy. They then retreat as massive enemy forces—skeleton kings, Yasha legions, holy dragons—converge. Xiao Chen, weakened, is forced back to the Dead World via the Bone Well. After a day, he returns to learn that the Yasha King survived the sealing and that many allies were captured or killed.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

"Return to the Origin" (返本还源) is a supreme technique that reduces all things to primordial nothingness, while "Glorious Years" (光辉岁月) is a divine dragon ability that channels light and time. The Holy Dragon King's incomplete grasp of only "Light" (光辉) without "Time" (岁月) proved his undoing—a reminder that even legendary skills require full mastery to be invincible. The Yasha King is a rare, refined Yasha—beautiful yet demonic, with green hair and glowing eyes, embodying the dangerous allure of the demon race. The forbidden weapon used against him is a relic of a lost civilization, described as too terrible even for that world to deploy; its appearance hints at forgotten cataclysmic powers that still linger, blurring the line between martial arts and apocalyptic technology.

## Reading Guide

This chapter establishes the Yasha King as an almost invincible antagonist—he survived a direct hit from forbidden weapons and will require a greater strategy to defeat. The incomplete "Glorious Years" foreshadows a Western Sacred Dragon who has mastered both light and time, a future threat of immense magnitude. Xiao Chen's limitation of one day per journey to the mortal world from the Dead World forces him to choose his moments carefully; his allies are now scattered and captured, deepening his resolve to advance in the Dead World's hierarchy. The presence of forbidden weapons suggests a larger, older conflict that may reshape the Hundred Races era. Readers should brace for Xiao Chen's next breakthrough and the escalating war.
