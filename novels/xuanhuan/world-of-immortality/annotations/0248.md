## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a critical turning point for Xiao Chen. After publicly killing a Tiger family demigod with his Eight Aspect Worlds technique, he becomes the center of attention in the capital. But the real shock comes when the legendary Demon Sect Founder reveals his protection over Xiao Chen, causing twenty top young experts from both the Great Shang Empire and the Roman Empire to side with him against the remaining Tiger demigods. The chapter pulses with high-stakes combat, political intrigue, and the unveiling of a mysterious ally that will reshape Xiao Chen's path.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen, having recently broken into the Spirit-Treasury realm, uses his Eight Aspect Worlds to kill a Tiger family demigod in front of thousands. This feat alone would cement his reputation, but he immediately engages in two restrained duels—one with the golden-haloed curse master of the Roman Empire and one with the legendary Little Li Flying Dagger heir. Both duels end in draws, demonstrating Xiao Chen's ability to stand against the most feared techniques of his generation. The true drama unfolds when the Tiger demigods attempt to encircle Xiao Chen: twenty young experts, including the Little Li Flying Dagger heir, the demonic princess of the Tianmo Hall, and the sacred maiden of the Cihang Sword Sect, all rise to surround the Tiger demigods. They reveal they are acting on orders of the Demon Sect Founder, a primordial figure who has returned to the Eternal Continent and declared Xiao Chen his friend. The revelation stuns the crowd and leaves the Tiger family helpless.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Eight Aspect Worlds technique is derived from the Eight Trigrams of Fuxi, one of the Ancestral Gods of Chinese mythology. Xiao Chen's mastery of this technique allows him to manipulate eight elemental realms—Heaven, Earth, Water, Fire, Thunder, Mountain, Wind, and Swamp—making it a uniquely versatile and powerful divine art. The Little Li Flying Dagger is a legendary technique from the mortal world, said to have killed gods when Li Xunhuan broke into the World of Immortality. Its wielder here is a descendant of that lineage. The Demon Sect Founder is an ancient figure who once rivaled the Evil King; rumors claim he is a reincarnation of Chiyou, the mythical war god. His reappearance after millennia signals a seismic shift in the power dynamics of the Eternal Continent. The term 'demigod' (半神) here refers to cultivators at the Sky-Mastery realm, capable of flight and commanding elemental forces.

## Reading Guide

This chapter sets up a profound change in Xiao Chen's status: he now has the backing of one of the most terrifying figures in history. However, such protection comes with obligations and may draw even greater enemies. The two mysterious little girls who call the Demon Sect Founder 'uncle' hint at deeper ties and future reveals. Watch for how Xiao Chen navigates this new power and whether the Tiger family will dare to strike despite the Founder's warning. The duels with the Roman curse master and the Little Li Flying Dagger heir were only opening moves; their full strength remains concealed. The stage is set for an explosive confrontation between the young elites of East and West.
