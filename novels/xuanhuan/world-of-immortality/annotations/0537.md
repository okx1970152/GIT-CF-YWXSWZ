## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges the reader into a brutal battle between two primordial stone beings within a dead city at the bottom of a black river. Their mutual annihilation is so devastating that it kills an Ancestral God who tries to intervene. The aftermath witnesses the descent of a new ancient demon city, which absorbs the residual power, while a stone spear escapes. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen and the ancestral gods return to the God Village to discuss the larger strategy: creating a diversion to allow the Martial Ancestor to ascend the ninety-nine stone steps unnoticed by the Otherworld's three supreme ancestral gods. This chapter blends visceral combat with strategic lore, advancing both the immediate action and the grand cosmic conflict.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with a stone giant (the "upper yellow giant") and a strange skulled creature fighting in a dead city under the black river. Their battle is catastrophic: each strike shatters the city and shakes the death world. The yellow giant's stone head is pierced by a stone spear, and they both eventually self-destruct, burning up completely. A white-bone ancestral lord arrives, seeking to seize the stone spear, but the two dying beings use their residual power to drag him into their fiery death, annihilating him entirely—showing the terrifying potency of stoned beings. New twists: a new ancient demon city flies in from deep in the death continent, suppresses the chaotic spacetime, and absorbs the remaining divine radiance from the destroyed beings. The stone spear, however, escapes, tearing through spacetime, evading even the city's attempt to capture it. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen's half skull absorbs two small stones that were the essence of the two destroyed beings. After returning to God Village, Yan Huang and the Old Ancestral Dragon reveal that the plan is to make the Otherworld's three supreme ancestral gods focus on the Nine Provinces and the four worlds, so that the Martial Ancestor can silently penetrate the ninety-nine stone steps without detection. This sets up the next phase of the war.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The concept of the "Ancient Demon City" is a recurring element in the death world. These cities are not merely structures but contain residual power and consciousness; they are drawn to absorb the essence of destroyed powerful beings. The "ninety-nine stone steps" referenced at the end allude to a legendary path leading to a higher realm or transformation, often guarded by supreme entities. The "half skull" and the "small stones" Xiao Chen acquires are fragments of the stone man path, a cultivation method that petrifies the body to achieve near-indestructibility. The mutual annihilation of the two stone beings and their ability to kill an Ancestral God in the process highlights the extreme danger of engaging with entities that have partially achieved the stone man transformation. The strategy of distraction to allow the Martial Ancestor to ascend the steps mirrors classic xianxia tactics where allies create a commotion to mask a crucial move.

## Reading Guide
This chapter resolves the short arc of the stone giant battle and provides new artifacts (the small stones) and strategic direction (the distraction plan). Readers should note that the stone spear has escaped and may reappear later as a major weapon. The absorption of the small stones into Xiao Chen's half skull suggests his stone man cultivation will advance. The discussion about the three supreme ancestral gods and the ninety-nine steps foregrounds the coming conflict: the Martial Ancestor's attempted breakthrough is likely to be a climactic event. The next chapters may show the execution of the diversion, possibly involving large-scale battles in the Nine Provinces or other worlds. Keep an eye on the Martial Imprint's role and the fate of the ancient demon city.
