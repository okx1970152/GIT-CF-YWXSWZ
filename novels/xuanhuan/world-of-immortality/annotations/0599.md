## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks the climax of Xiao Chen's bold campaign against Zhenmu Nüsheng, the Female Stone Saint, a being whose power rivals that of a reborn Ancestral God. With a cunning strategy backed by the control of a Stone Man puppet, Heavenly Steles, an ancient scroll, and a divine diagram, Xiao Chen manages to seal her demonic city and sever the very foundation of her cultivation. The battle is a fierce testament to Xiao Chen's growth from a half-ancestor into a figure who can challenge the upper echelons of the Otherworld. The tone is one of desperate triumph, as Xiao Chen achieves a symbolic victory but knows the war is far from over.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen activates the stone jar to control a powerful Stone Man puppet, which becomes the centerpiece of his assault. The puppet, a mindless but supremely powerful fighter, engages Zhenmu Nüsheng directly while Xiao Chen uses Heavenly Steles, the ancient scroll, and the divine diagram to seal her city. The Female Stone Saint fights back with immense power, nearly breaking free, but the Stone Man puppet's martial domain proves decisive. In a desperate move, Zhenmu Nüsheng abandons her city, sacrificing her stone foundation to escape. Xiao Chen then refines the shattered city into a king-level divine city, a prize that holds immense potential. Babulas, an Otherworld Ancestral God, watches the battle in disbelief, realizing that Xiao Chen has dealt a major blow to one of the Otherworld's most formidable warriors.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

In the cultivation cosmology of World of Immortality, a Stone Man's foundation (石人根基) is the existential base for a being walking the Stone Man path. Severing that foundation is akin to shattering their potential for the final step to become a true Stone Man King. The stone jar is a rare artifact that controls the puppet, a relic from a previous era that gives Xiao Chen leverage against supreme enemies. The battle showcases the interplay of multiple divine artifacts—Heavenly Steles represent order, the ancient scroll holds the wills of fallen kings, and the divine diagram is a fragmentary supreme weapon. Together, they create a sealed domain that even a ninth-heaven half-ancestor cannot easily escape.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a significant turning point: Xiao Chen has demonstrated he can challenge and even cripple an upper-tier Ancestral God. However, Zhenmu Nüsheng vows to return on the Upper Ancestral God path, a clear warning that the victory is temporary. The acquisition of the king-level divine city opens new possibilities for Xiao Chen's own cultivation. Readers should watch for how this resolved conflict reshapes the balance of power in the Nine Provinces and the Otherworld's response. The chapter also teases the nature of the stone jar and its origins, hinting at deeper mysteries surrounding the Stone Man path and the primordial wars.
