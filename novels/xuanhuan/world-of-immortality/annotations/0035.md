## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen’s understanding of the cultivation world. After a tense encounter with the golden-haired Spellmaster Rand and a glimpse of Yan Qingcheng’s ambition, Xiao Chen meets the monk Yizhen once more. Through Yizhen’s patient explanation, the novel’s cultivation hierarchy—the Five Great Realms—is laid out with breathtaking clarity. The tone is solemn and expository, a pause in the relentless violence of Dragon Island that nevertheless deepens the reader’s sense of scale and stakes. Xiao Chen, humiliated but resolute, absorbs this knowledge like dry earth drinking rain, already calculating his next move.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen’s confrontation with Rand ends not in battle but in a calculated retreat. He feels the sting of being dismissed as weak, yet suppresses his anger with iron discipline. This moment crystallizes his determination: he must grow stronger, and soon. Moving through the forest, he discovers fresh corpses—evidence that more cultivators have landed on Dragon Island. The isolation of the early chapters is giving way to a crowded, dangerous arena. Then Yizhen appears, walking on treetops with effortless grace. Their conversation reveals the core of the chapter: the Five Great Realms of cultivation—Mortal-Shedding, Spirit-Treasury, Sky-Mastery, Nirvana, and Deathless Longevity. Yizhen explains that Rand and Yan Qingcheng are at the third or fourth heavenly tier of Mortal-Shedding, a level Xiao Chen must match or surpass. The three skeletons, who have been feigning stupidity, stand silently by, their true cunning hidden from the monk. Xiao Chen learns that the unified realm system exists to compare diverse cultivation paths across races—Spellmasters, Beast-Soul Warriors, Spiritists. His own path, based on the Yellow River Divine Stele, is not yet measured by these standards. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen parting from Yizhen, his mind already focused on the gap he must close.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Five Great Realms introduced here are the backbone of power scaling in *World of Immortality*. Unlike many cultivation novels that use a long ladder of realms, Chen Dong compresses strength into five wide thresholds. Mortal-Shedding (蜕凡) is the first true step—shedding mortality to commune with heaven and earth’s primal energy. Spirit-Treasury (识藏) opens the inner divine consciousness. Sky-Mastery (御空) grants flight and command over natural forces, earning the title ‘demigod’. Nirvana (涅槃) is a dangerous rebirth—cultivators oscillate between immense power and near-annihilation. Deathless Longevity (长生) is the ultimate goal, true immortality. Each realm is divided into nine heavenly tiers, allowing granular comparison. This system unifies disparate paths like Spellmasters (咒师), who borrow divine power, Beast-Soul Warriors (兽魂战士) with sealed ancient beasts, and Spiritists (灵士) with innate psychic abilities. The chapter emphasizes that most practitioners never even reach Mortal-Shedding—it is a threshold that separates the true cultivator from the crowd.

## Reading Guide

This is a lore-heavy chapter, but it plants critical seeds for the conflicts ahead. Xiao Chen now has a clear target: he must break into Mortal-Shedding’s higher tiers to challenge Rand and Yan Qingcheng. Pay attention to the three skeletons’ behavior—they are deliberately playing dumb, which will pay off later. Yizhen’s friendly demeanor and willingness to share knowledge marks him as a rare ally in this brutal world. Also note the mention of a young dragon’s tracks near the restricted area; the ‘companion dragon’ (伴生龙王) is becoming a central prize. As the number of cultivators on the island grows, the political landscape is about to shift from survival to open competition. Xiao Chen’s internal resolve is hardening—not with anger, but with cold purpose.
