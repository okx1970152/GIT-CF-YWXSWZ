## Chapter Overview

This chapter offers a rare moment of introspective calm amid the rising tensions of the Eternal Continent. Xiao Chen, having recently arrived at the stone tree where the Ancestral God Suiren once sat, experiences a strange nocturnal vision: he overhears fragmentary conversations between two primordial figures—Sui Ren Shi and You Chao Shi—discussing the forging of the Heaven-Earth Furnace and the division of the Stone Man. Initially frustrated by his inability to hear clearly, Xiao Chen soon realizes these are residual phantoms preserved by the environment, echoes of an age long past. The encounter stirs a deep philosophical struggle within him: should he follow the natural, peaceful dao of the ancestors, or carve out his own? In the end, he chooses the latter, forging a unique understanding that leads to a breakthrough to the Spirit-Treasury Seventh Heaven. The chapter closes with Xiao Chen heading to witness the clash between the young elites of Great Shang and the Western Roman Empire, a spectacle that promises both revelation and danger.

## Key Plot Points

The chapter begins in a moonlit stone forest, where Xiao Chen hears indistinct voices. Gradually, he recognizes two spectral figures—Ancestral Gods Suiren and Youchao—sitting just meters away, discussing the "Heaven-Earth Copper Furnace" and the "Nine Parts of the Stone Man." He watches them produce artifacts: stone dagger, stone ball, stone drill, and broken limbs, many of which he has encountered before. As dawn approaches, the phantoms fade, but their influence lingers. Xiao Chen realizes that simply imitating the ancestors' natural, effortless dao is not his path. In a moment of enlightenment, he visualizes a unique "green leaf" within him—a symbol of his own evolving truth—and lets go of the ancestral images. This inner breakthrough propels him from Spirit-Treasury Sixth Heaven to Seventh Heaven, strengthening his divine form and refining his techniques, especially the Melt Weapon into Body ability and the Eight Aspect Worlds. Afterward, he heads to the Royal Garden, where a massive tournament between Great Shang and the Roman Empire's young experts is underway. He observes familiar faces: Yan Qingcheng, Dugu Jianmo, Chu Xingkuang, and others, as well as the imposing presence of the Sun Knight Order. A lecherous stuttering character (Jin Sanyi) causes comic relief while also identifying targets. Finally, the arrival of several giant white tigers signals the involvement of the Tiger Clan, escalating the stakes.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter dives into one of xianxia's most compelling themes: the tension between following ancient wisdom and forging one's own path. The phantoms of Suiren and Youchao represent the pinnacle of primordial cultivation—beings who defined the natural order of the world. Their "natural dao" of harmony and effortless action is a classic Taoist ideal, echoed in the serene imagery of green leaves and morning light. However, Xiao Chen's rejection of wholesale imitation is a hallmark of the genre: the hero must internalize lessons but ultimately transcend his predecessors. The breakthrough scene, where a conceptual "green leaf" crystallizes within his body and then dissolves into divine light, illustrates the xianxia principle of "enlightenment" (顿悟) as a path to power. Additionally, the "Stone Man" fragments and the artifacts (Suiren's fire-drill, Youchao's stone weapons) tie into the ongoing mystery of the primal Stone Man—a being whose body, split into nine parts, holds cosmic significance. The chapter also introduces the Sun Knights and Western religions, blending Eastern and Western mythological flavor.

## Reading Guide

This chapter is a breather after intense action, but it sets up major future developments. Pay attention to the discussion of the "Heaven-Earth Copper Furnace" and "Stone Man Nine Parts"—these are key plot threads that will likely resurface in later arcs. Xiao Chen's breakthrough to Spirit-Treasury Seventh Heaven marks a significant power increase, but more importantly, his philosophical resolution to walk his own path signals a maturation of character. The tournament scene at the end introduces multiple threads: the Sun Knights and Zhao Lin'er's reappearance, the lecherous assassin Jin Sanyi (whose stutter masks deadly skills), and the Tiger Clan's arrival. The interaction between the Great Shang heroes and the Western experts foreshadows a larger conflict. Keep an eye on the "Three Billion Bounty" assassin organization and the mysterious child with the third eye from the previous chapter—they are likely to play a role in the coming battles. The next chapters promise high-octane clashes and political maneuvering.
