## Chapter Overview
This chapter pivots between absurd comedy and grim escalation. The half-ancestor of the Yaksha race sends his most powerful emperor to assassinate Xiao Chen, only for the monk Jigong to transform the legendary warrior into a terrified white pig—a humiliating reversal that shocks even the half-ancestor. The incident is a masterstroke of dark humor, demonstrating that the mortal world possesses unpredictable trump cards. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen's allies continue to assemble: a Yazi emperor descended from the Ancestral Dragon, a female assassin from an ancient organization, the phoenix clan, and the terrifyingly powerful skeleton emperor Lian Po all make their presence felt. The chapter closes with the announcement that Xiao Chen will face the Yaksha half-ancestor in single combat aboard the King Ship, a duel that will determine the fate of the Nine Provinces.

## Key Plot Points
The Yaksha half-ancestor, enraged by Xiao Chen's survival, dispatches the legendary emperor Cold Ice, a Che Di realm warrior who peaked in antiquity but never broke through to half-ancestor. Jigong the drunk monk greets him with a hearty laugh and transforms Cold Ice into a pig using a curse that the half-ancestor identifies as the Grand Prophecy Art—a lost technique blending Buddhist and curse-path powers. The half-ancestor cannot reverse the transformation, a devastating loss of face. Meanwhile, a female assassin from the Bounty Three Billion organization negotiates for the White Bone King Banner. The Yazi emperor, a descendant of the Ancestral Dragon, arrives and fights two Yaksha emperors to a standstill. Lian Po, the seven-colored skeleton king, effortlessly dismantles a skeleton emperor, revealing himself as a cultivator who nearly reached half-ancestor. The Yaksha emperor and the skeleton emperor both flee. With the protection of Tianwaitian and Renwairen, Xiao Chen formally challenges the Yaksha half-ancestor to a duel aboard the King Ship in the Forbidden Sea, setting the stage for an unprecedented confrontation between a mortal and a half-ancestor.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Grand Prophecy Art (大预言术) is a rare divine ability that ties into Buddhist and curse-path traditions, capable of altering reality through spoken words. Jigong's use of it to turn a Che Di realm emperor into a pig is a satirical twist on the Buddhist vow to liberate all beings. The Yazi (睚眦) is a mythical beast, the second son of the Ancestral Dragon, famous for its vengeful nature and association with wrath. Its presence here signals the involvement of primal dragon lineage forces. Lian Po, the seven-colored skeleton king, is a reincarnation of the famous Warring States general, blending historical Chinese lore with the death world hierarchy. The duel's venue—the King Ship on the Forbidden Sea—recalls the novel's earlier arcs and carries connotations of death and undead power, making it an appropriate stage for a fight between a half-ancestor and a practitioner who has already died and been reborn.

## Reading Guide
The duel between Xiao Chen and the Yaksha half-ancestor is not just a personal vendetta; it will likely determine the balance of power among the Hundred Races. Watch for the involvement of Jigong—his power seems to surpass even the half-ancestors' understanding. The Yazi emperor and Lian Po have both demonstrated that they can match or exceed Che Di realm opponents, raising questions about what other hidden forces will emerge. The King Ship venue introduces the possibility of interference from the death world, and Xiao Chen's own cultivation path—especially his mastery of the Eight Origin Sounds and his death world rebirth—may give him unexpected advantages against a being who has never truly died. Finally, keep an eye on the female assassin and the White Bone King Banner, as that artifact may play a role in the broader conflict.
