## Chapter Overview
After three decades of silent cultivation in the Death World, Xiao Chen returns to the Nine Provinces to find his homeland under the heel of the Otherworld. Luoyang, the ancient sacred city, has become a staging ground for an unprecedented assembly of half-ancestors. Dozens of powerful beings, including multiple ninth-heaven experts and an eighth-heaven female warrior, gather to break open the underground realms beneath the city. Their goal: to retrieve a treasure that was stolen from their world in ages past. Xiao Chen quickly learns that this treasure is intimately connected to Keke's mysterious family, whose legacy proves far more extraordinary than anyone imagined. The chapter masterfully blends the tension of an overwhelming enemy presence with the promise of hidden powers still lurking in the mortal realm, all while Keke's innocent greed for treasure adds a light counterpoint.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with Xiao Chen and his trio of comic companions – Sha Polang, Jin Sanyi, and Zhuge Liang – arriving in Luoyang after three decades away. Almost immediately, they witness a massive mobilization of Otherworld elites: over sixty half-ancestors converge on the city, including an eighth-heaven female half-ancestor and the grandson of the Otherworld ancestral god Li Huiyou. The half-ancestors openly discuss their mission: to open the underground world and recover an artifact that was supposedly stolen from their civilization long ago. They also reveal their underlying fears – that ancient horrors like a surviving Tianren clan elder and the mythical Heavenly Emperor may still be alive. Xiao Chen follows them underground, shielded by the half stone skull. There, they encounter a terrifying guardian: a metal humanoid constructed from fused ancestral god weapons. This entity instantly vaporizes a half-ancestor with a beam of light, then stands motionless before a stone gate. Before Xiao Chen can explore further, an Otherworld ancestral god arrives above the city, forcing him to retreat into hiding. The chapter ends with Xiao Chen weighing his options, the prize – Keke's family treasure – still out of reach.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter deepens the lore of the Otherworld's long history with the Nine Provinces. The term 'half-ancestor' (半祖) denotes beings below true ancestral gods but still capable of world-shaking power; ninth-heaven half-ancestors are only one step from godhood. Luoyang's status as an ancient sacred site parallels Ancestral Dragon Village, both being focal points of primordial power. The metal guardian is a chilling example of ancient military technology – a construct of fused ancestral god weapons with no independent life, yet capable of slaying half-ancestors with a single strike. It demonstrates the terrifying martial prowess of the civilizations that preceded the current era. The half stone skull (from the Stone Man) continues to prove its worth, masking Xiao Chen's aura even from an ancestral god's perception. The chapter also reinforces the theme that the Otherworld's confidence is not absolute: even they suspect that powerful beings from the mortal world's past may still survive, hinting at a deeper layer of the conflict.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for a major confrontation beneath Luoyang. The treasure that the Otherworld seeks represents a critical plot point – it was stolen from them, implying it is a weapon of immense significance. Keke's family connection to Luoyang suggests that her parents were involved in that theft, and Keke's vague sense of the treasure may prove crucial. The arrival of an ancestral god raises the stakes dramatically; Xiao Chen cannot fight such a being directly, but his concealment ability and tactical patience give him an edge. The half-ancestors' own fears of ancient powers like the Heavenly Emperor and the Tianren elder indicate that the mortal world may have hidden defenders yet to reveal themselves. Readers should watch for Keke's potential awakening to her family's power, the emergence of these ancient guardians, and Xiao Chen's strategic moves to seize the treasure from under the enemy's nose.
