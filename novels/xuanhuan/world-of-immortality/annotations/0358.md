## Chapter Overview

The Dead World reveals its harshest law: power alone dictates justice. In this chapter, Xiao Chen ventures into Giant City to exchange a rare yellow diamond skull for a purple diamond fire seed—a transaction that almost costs him his freedom. The Broken Sword King, a towering skeleton wielding an ancient broken sword, embodies the predatory nature of the dead realm's elite, attempting to seize the goods and enslave the traders. Only the timely intervention of the divine tribe elders, wielding mysterious bone knives, forces the trade to proceed. Yet the danger is far from over. On the return journey, the Seven Kills city lord—a cunning old foe—ambushes Xiao Chen with a phalanx of faux purple diamond skeletons, nearly slaying him and his red diamond companion. The chapter swells with tension, balancing the grim realities of survival in the dead world against the rare moments of fellowship and strategic wit.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen and the red diamond skeleton enter Giant City to complete a long-planned exchange: a genuine yellow diamond first-rank skull for a purple diamond first-rank fire seed. The Broken Sword King, a self-styled ruler of the city, immediately tests their resolve with bluster and threats, but two flying bone knives from the elders shift the balance, compelling a fair trade. After leaving, the Seven Kills city lord—a nemesis from earlier chapters—springs a trap with ten artificial purple diamond skeletons, all armed with bone spears. Xiao Chen sustains heavy injuries, his ribs cracked and his red diamond ally skewered through the pelvis. They escape only thanks to Xiao Chen's enchanted purple wings, a gift from the elders. Retreating to a bone battlefield, Xiao Chen spends weeks recovering and finally successfully refines the purple diamond fire seed, advancing to true purple diamond rank—a milestone that grants him far greater power. Nonetheless, when he returns to challenge the Seven Kills city lord, he discovers his foe may be at the third tier of purple diamond, still beyond his reach. Returning to Divine Village, Xiao Chen learns of the elders' recent journey to the Mortal World using the legendary Ring of Nuwa, which alone can transport physical objects across realms. He borrows the ring and attempts to carry the colossal stone giant statue from the eastern sea coast into the dead world—hoping to use it as an ultimate weapon—but fails, as the ring cannot contain such a massive entity. Undeterred, he receives from the defiant dragon a mysterious artifact that once belonged to Zhao Lin'er, said to contain immense destructive energy. Armed with this, Xiao Chen charges alone toward Seven Kills city and unleashes a cataclysmic explosion, blotting out the sky.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

This chapter deepens our understanding of the Dead World's social hierarchy. The classification of skeleton bone quality—red, purple, yellow, with each color having three tiers—mirrors the cultivation realm divisions in the living world. A 'fire seed' (火种) is the core of a dead being's consciousness and power; absorbing a higher-grade seed enables evolution. The Broken Sword King's broken sword, possibly the legendary Seven Treasures Wonderful Tree or a fragment of a primordial artifact, hints at the chapter's mythological subtext: the figure may well be an incarnation of the Daoist sage Zhunti, whose iconic weapon was a branch of the Seven Treasures Tree. This overlap between mythological figures and Dead World inhabitants enriches the novel's cosmic scale. The Elders' ability to transport living-world objects via Nuwa's ring demonstrates that certain divine relics can bridge the boundary between life and death—a rare and precious capacity that Xiao Chen desperately seeks to exploit. The weapon he acquires from the defiant dragon, a remnant of Zhao Lin'er's arsenal, suggests that even discarded divine tools retain catastrophic power, a reminder that in this world, every relic holds the potential for mass destruction.

## Reading Guide

This chapter marks a turning point in Xiao Chen's dead-world journey. He has achieved purple diamond rank, placing him among the region's true elite, but the encounter with the Seven Kills city lord proves that even at this level, he is not yet invincible. The cataclysmic weapon he just unleashed may have devastated the city, but we do not yet know the fate of the city lord—or the price Xiao Chen will pay for wielding such power. The failure to move the stone giant statue leaves that narrative thread dangling; its purpose may be revealed later. Readers should also keep an eye on the Broken Sword King, whose arrogance was only temporarily checked. The divine tribe elders remain enigmatic, and their relationship with the mortal world through Nuwa's ring may open further pathways for Xiao Chen's plans. With the dead world becoming an ever more dangerous battlefield, every new ally and every new artifact becomes critical.
