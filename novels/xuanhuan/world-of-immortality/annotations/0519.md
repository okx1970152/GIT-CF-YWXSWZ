## Chapter Overview
This chapter plunges readers deep into the heart of the Otherworld invasion as five Ancestral Gods converge on the underground labyrinth beneath Shen Du. Their goal is to retrieve a supreme treasure sealed behind an ancient stone door, but the path is guarded by an enigmatic metal construct—a fusion of Ancestral God weapons and lost technology. The tension escalates as they deploy the legendary Thirty-Three-Layer Pagoda, an artifact of an Upper Ancestral God, to shatter the barrier. However, beyond the door lies not a vault but a gateway to a primordial world where time itself flows backward. The chapter masterfully blends cosmic horror with archaeological mystery, as the invaders realize they are being lured into a trap set by a vanished civilization. Xiao Chen and Keke follow in stealth, using the half-stone skull to conceal their presence.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with five Otherworld Ancestral Gods—Lu Lopes, Lan Yufeng, Ai Er, and two unnamed—attempting to open the stone door in the underground labyrinth. A metal guardian, impervious to their attacks, briefly engages them before retreating. The Ancestral Gods then use the Thirty-Three-Layer Pagoda, an artifact of an Upper Ancestral God, to break the door, which had resisted their combined efforts. Beyond the door, they enter a lush, primeval world that seems to be a remnant of the ancient past. A half-ancestor from the Luo Hou race is killed by a giant ape as they pass. The Ancestral Gods realize that time is flowing backward; they are stepping into the remote prehistoric era. They attempt to use the pagoda to stabilize the world but cannot restore their path. The metal guardian reappears and delivers a cryptic warning: someone from the ancient times is waiting for them. Meanwhile, Xiao Chen and Keke follow stealthily, using the half-stone skull to hide their presence, observing the events unfold.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Thirty-Three-Layer Pagoda is a direct reference to Chinese mythological tower artifacts, often representing layers of heaven or accumulative power. Here it belongs to an 'Upper Ancestral God', indicating a tier above ordinary Ancestral Gods, a concept central to the novel's cultivation hierarchy. The terms 'Ancestral God' (祖神) and 'Half-Ancestor' (半祖) are fundamental to understanding the power dynamics in this world. The time-reversal world is a clever narrative device that blends xianxia cosmology with a sense of temporal displacement often seen in myths of sealed realms. The metal guardian is a fusion of 'mythical-era Ancestral God weapons' and 'advanced technology', suggesting a lost civilization that achieved a synthesis of magic and science—a hallmark of Chen Dong's worldbuilding. The repeated reference to a 'deformed clan' (变态的家族) hints at the legendary family behind these creations, possibly connected to the protagonists' lineage.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets up a major plot device: the time-reversal world that may connect to the ancient past and the lost civilization that created the metal guardian. Readers should pay close attention to the metal guardian's warning about 'someone in the ancient times waiting for them', hinting at a powerful ally or adversary awaiting the invaders—and perhaps Xiao Chen as well. Xiao Chen and Keke's stealthy following suggests they will witness or participate in events in this ancient world. The Thirty-Three-Layer Pagoda is established as a formidable weapon; its owner, the Upper Ancestral God, may become a key antagonist. The chapter deepens the mystery of the underground labyrinth and the treasure sealed within, which may be tied to Keke's family or the primordial civilization. The appearance of ancient beasts capable of killing half-ancestors also sets the stage for potential encounters with legendary creatures.
