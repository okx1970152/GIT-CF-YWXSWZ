## Chapter Overview
Xiao Chen, transformed by grief and fury, unleashes a merciless campaign of vengeance against the factions that slaughtered his village and killed his friend Yizhen. He single-handedly destroys the Tiger clan's stronghold at Xirong Mountain and the mountain stronghold of the Three Infants Tai Lord, leaving rivers of blood. Yet this is not mere slaughter; it is a calculated prelude. The chapter culminates in a public challenge to the Six Prodigies of the Cultivation World—a duel that will be fought without his greatest protective artifact, the Yellow Clay Altar. The stage is set for a confrontation that will decide the fate of the young generation.

## Key Plot Points
First, Xiao Chen arrives at Xirong Mountain, the Tiger clan's domain, and declares his intent. He uses the Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台) to protect himself while unleashing devastating attacks. He kills the son of the Tiger clan head and nine other experts, then wipes out the remaining defenders with the 'Om' divine sound technique. The entire mountain stronghold is annihilated. Next, he travels to Huashan, now held by the Three Infants Tai Lord's lineage. There, an ancient immortal formation traps him, but the Yellow Clay Altar shatters it within moments. He slaughters the sixty cultivators stationed there and loots the herb garden and alchemy room, taking all spiritual treasures. With these resources, he divinely transforms the remaining acupoints to complete the full set of eighty-one major acupoints, achieving a breakthrough in his cultivation. After a brief rest, he issues a challenge to the Six Prodigies of the Cultivation World: Ye Tian (wielding the Undying Emperor Heaven Bell), Fang Tianqi (with the Divine Palace), Ling Xuyan (with the Rakshasa Umbrella), Shao (with a divine bow and nine arrows), Xia Ding (with nine bronze cauldrons), and a mysterious sixth. He vows not to use the Yellow Clay Altar in this duel. The world watches in anticipation as the battle lines are drawn.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
The Yellow Clay Altar (黄泥台) is a mysterious artifact from Dragon Island, possibly linked to the Ancestral God Suiren, that provides near invincibility. Xiao Chen's completion of eighty-one divinized acupoints is a major milestone—his unique cultivation path based on the Heavenly Stele diagrams now forms a complete internal network, greatly enhancing his life essence and combat capability. The Six Prodigies wield legendary artifacts reminiscent of ancient Chinese myths: Shao's bow and arrows evoke Houyi, the divine archer who shot down nine suns; Xia Ding's nine cauldrons echo the Nine Tripod Cauldrons of Dayu, symbolic of imperial authority. These are not ordinary weapons; they carry the weight of cultural history. Xiao Chen's refusal to rely on the Yellow Clay Altar demonstrates his determination to prove his strength on his own terms, a common trope in xianxia where protagonists face overwhelming odds to grow.

## Reading Guide
This chapter sets the stage for what promises to be an epic duel. Readers should watch Xiao Chen's combat style without the safety net of the Yellow Clay Altar—will his Eight Aspect Worlds and Supreme Hand be enough? The Six Prodigies each have unique artifacts and likely multiple trump cards. Pay attention to the dynamics between them: Ye Tian has a personal grudge from earlier defeats, and the mysterious sixth prodigy may hold the greatest threat. Also note that Xiao Chen's actions have enraged multiple half-ancestor factions, and even if he wins the duel, more powerful enemies will likely emerge. The phrase "丧钟" (death knell) at the end suggests this is just the beginning of a larger conflict. Keke is still present but hasn't acted; her role in the duel remains uncertain.
