## Chapter Overview

This chapter unfurls one of the most profound revelations in the series, centering on Keke's true identity and the tragic legacy of the dragon race. Xiao Chen, while exploring the base of the Heavenly Stele, discovers an ancient stone turtle that has lain dormant for eons. The turtle's idle demeanor belies its vast knowledge. As Xiao Chen watches, Keke undergoes a startling transformation, shedding her familiar white-furred form to become a miniature seven-colored Ancestral Dragon. The stone turtle then unveils the secret history of the dragon race's curse, Keke's birth as a 'born Ancestral Dragon', and the immense burden she carries from two cursed lineages. The chapter is a masterful blend of mythic revelation and emotional depth, reshaping our understanding of one of the story's most beloved characters.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen detects a hidden presence at the base of the Heavenly Stele and discovers a gray-brown stone turtle that blends perfectly with its surroundings. The turtle acts with comical nonchalance, dismissing Xiao Chen's war sword as a 'toothpick' and ambling toward the transforming Keke. Keke, encased in a seven-colored light cocoon, undergoes a dramatic metamorphosis: her fluffy white body shifts to reveal gleaming seven-colored scales, her tiny horn buds grow into crystal-like dragon horns, and she takes the form of a miniature Ancestral Dragon. The stone turtle then reveals a decades-old secret: Keke is a 'born Ancestral Dragon' (天生小祖龙), conceived from the essence of the World Tree (通天神木) rather than born of dragon parents. This violates the dragon race's deep curse, which prevents more than one Ancestral Dragon from existing in the same era. The turtle explains that Keke's mother was also a born Ancestral Dragon, and her union with Keke's father—who carried his own family curse—compounded the tragedy. Their child, Keke, was sealed in a seven-colored jade egg for eons to prevent the curse from destroying the dragon race. The chapter culminates in Keke waking, distressed by her new form, and eventually stabilizing back to her original appearance, though the power of the Ancestral Dragon remains within her.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The concept of a 'born Ancestral Dragon' (天生小祖龙) is a unique twist within xianxia mythology. Normally, Ancestral Dragons are supreme beings that evolve through intense competition among dragon kings. Here, Keke is born fully formed as an Ancestral Dragon due to the accumulated essence of the World Tree and fallen Ancestral Dragons—a concept that elevates her origin beyond mere biology. The dragon race's curse, which triggers calamities when a second Ancestral Dragon appears, echoes themes of heavenly jealousy (天妒) common in Chinese fantasy. The World Tree (通天神木) serves as a sacred axis mundi, storing primordial dragon energy and acting as a vessel for the born Ancestral Dragon's gestation. This chapter also introduces the motif of dual family curses, a rare and devastating burden that foreshadows Keke's unique role in the coming conflicts. The stone turtle itself, a cryptic sage dwelling at the stele's foundation, invokes the archetype of the ancient tortoise as a keeper of wisdom—a figure seen in Daoist and Chinese folklore as a symbol of longevity and hidden knowledge.

## Reading Guide

This chapter shifts the narrative from immediate combat to mythic exposition, making it essential for understanding Keke's character arc. Pay close attention to the stone turtle's words—it hints at a previous visitor who also mastered the Heavenly Stele techniques, suggesting a lineage of practitioners tied to the stele. Keke's reaction to her transformation is telling: she despises the dragon form and forces herself back to her familiar shape. This refusal to accept her destiny may become a key character trait. The turtle's mention of a previous seeker who knew dragon secrets also plants seeds for future encounters. As the story progresses, watch for how Keke's curse and her dual heritage affect her relationships and her role in the war against the Otherworld. The revelations here also cast new light on earlier scenes—her ability to command dragon kings and summon the Ancestral Dragon Ship alone were hints of her true nature. This chapter deepens the tragedy of Keke's existence, making her one of the most layered characters in the novel.
