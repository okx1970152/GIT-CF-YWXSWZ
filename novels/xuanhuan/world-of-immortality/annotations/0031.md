## Chapter Overview

This chapter marks a crucial pivot from Xiao Chen's solitary survival to his reluctant entry into the social hierarchy of Dragon Island. The tone shifts from the intimate dread of the wild to the cold, political reality of power among cultivators. Xiao Chen is introduced to four elite young masters from different tribes—each dismissive of his origins, each radiating an authority that reminds him how far he still has to go. Yet before the sting of their indifference can settle, the sky erupts in battle. A Winged Dragon of demigod strength clashes with a Fallen Angel—an entity thought lost to the ages. The chapter balances the humiliation of being overlooked with the awe of witnessing true demigod combat, reminding both Xiao Chen and the reader that on this island, even the strongest mortals are still small.

## Key Plot Points

Yizhen the monk introduces Xiao Chen to the four young masters who have just arrived on Dragon Island: Rand, a golden-haired God-Gifted Spellmaster with the radiance of a sun; Kaeo, a three-meter Barbarian warrior whose fury is barely contained after losing his ship; and Arold, a quiet Forest Clan practitioner who seems unruffled by the disaster. Their reaction to Xiao Chen is telling—they are briefly stunned that he came from the Mortal World, but once they learn he did not break the void himself, they dismiss him entirely. Not a single word is exchanged. Xiao Chen's self-deprecating smile cuts deeper than any insult. Then the battle begins. The Winged Dragon, a fifteen-meter Sacred Light Dragon wreathed in lightning and holy fire, reveals the upper limits of this world's power. Against it stands a Fallen Angel—a figure of dark, terrible beauty wielding a ten-meter energy blade, her black wings dispersing the dragon's sacred flames. Arold's shocked identification of the Fallen Angel sets the stakes: these beings have not been seen in over a century, and their presence here signals that Dragon Island holds secrets worth more than mere survival.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Winged Dragon is the one dragon lineage not sealed by the ancient suppression on Dragon Island. As Yizhen explains, adult Winged Dragons possess demigod-level power, making them among the strongest beings on the continent. The term 'demigod' here corresponds to the Sky-Mastery realm, where cultivators can fly and command natural forces. The Fallen Angel is an even rarer entity—a dark-winged humanoid of immense power, worshiped as a god by lesser races before being hunted to near extinction a century ago. Her combat style combines lethal grace with overwhelming energy projection. Xiao Chen's observation that the dragon can freely use divine arts despite the island's seal is a key lore hint: the Winged Dragon line is exempt, suggesting either a different origin or a deliberate exemption in the ancient sealing. The God-Gifted Spellmaster, like Rand, draws power from the gods they worship, marking a distinct cultivation path from Xiao Chen's Qi-Refining or the beast-soul warriors' inheritance.

## Reading Guide

Pay close attention to how each of the four young masters carries themselves—their dialogue, their silences, and their combat roles will recur as the island's politics deepen. Rand's dismissal of Xiao Chen plants a seed of rivalry that will flower later. The Fallen Angel's sudden appearance raises urgent questions: why here? Why now? And what does her presence mean for the dragon hunting that is about to engulf the island? Xiao Chen's internal resolve, forged in humiliation, will drive him to prove his worth through action rather than words. Keep an eye on the Sacred Light Dragon—it is not merely a monster but a potential tool for Xiao Chen's survival if he can navigate the chaos. The next chapters will escalate toward direct confrontation, and the alliances formed here will fracture under greed and ambition.
