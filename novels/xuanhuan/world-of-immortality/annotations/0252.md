## Chapter Overview

Chapter 252 shifts from the political intrigue of Yin Capital to the brutal front lines of the Eternal Continent's hegemonic war. Xiao Chen, now hardened by months of battlefield experience, follows a band of elite young assassins from Great Shang and the Roman Empire as they venture into the infamous Demon Plain. This desolate, bone-strewn wasteland holds dark legends of ancient gods and sages meeting their end. The chapter culminates in a dramatic reunion when Xiao Chen discovers that the assassins' target is linked to his old friend Yizhen, the young monk who inherited the Buddha's Dharma Wheel on Dragon Island. Yizhen fights alongside his companion the Azure Dragon King against overwhelming odds, forcing Xiao Chen to intervene with his Eight Aspect Worlds technique.

## Key Plot Points

Xiao Chen has spent months on the battlefield, first as an observer and then as a common soldier, tempering his spirit amidst the slaughter. He witnesses the horrors of war: soldiers breaking mentally, half-god combat that levels landscapes, and the constant flow of blood and death. This experience has hardened his resolve without making him callous. When he spots thirteen young masters from the allied forces of Great Shang and Rome slipping out of camp at night, he follows them using his Eight Aspect Worlds technique, staying a cautious ten li behind. The group includes the successor of Little Li Flying Dagger, the demoness of the Devil Palace, and the maiden of the Sword Hut. Their mission is to assassinate a prince of the Fan Kingdom—a brilliant strategist who plans to trap the allied armies with a deadly formation on the Demon Plain.

The assassins encounter resistance from Fan Kingdom experts, and in the ensuing battle, Xiao Chen recognizes a familiar white-robed figure fighting against overwhelming odds: Yizhen. The young monk has advanced tremendously in the four years since Dragon Island, now wielding a massive Buddha hand technique that can crush opponents. His Azure Dragon King has also grown into a formidable fighter. But the combined assault of twelve elite enemies forces Yizhen and his allies onto the defensive. As the Little Li Flying Dagger successor prepares to unleash his lethal technique, Xiao Chen makes his move, stepping in to protect his friend.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes

The Demon Plain (魔鬼平原) is one of the Eternal Continent's most infamous cursed lands, steeped in legends of the violent downfall of supreme beings. The chapter references the original (原始) once moving the immortal mountain Kunlun here, only to see it collapse in a single night. More chillingly, it claims that the past incarnation of Laozi was crushed to a pulp here by a demonic artifact, and the past life of the Buddha was nailed to a peak and bled out over forty-nine days. These references draw from Chinese folk mythology, treating figures from Daoism and Buddhism as historical cultivation ancestors who suffered catastrophic defeats. The plain is now a burial ground for millions of soldiers from the great wars between the five hegemonic kingdoms. The mention of the 'Nine Heavens Ten Earths God-Slaying Demon-Exterminating Extinction Formation' (even an incomplete version) evokes the grand tactical formations common in xianxia, where a single battle formation can decide the fate of armies.

Yizhen's mastery of the huge Buddha hand technique stems from his fusion with the Buddha's Dharma Wheel in Dragon Island's City of the Dead. This connects to the Buddhist concept of 'direct transmission'—an inheritance of enlightenment and power directly from a Buddha or bodhisattva. The Azure Dragon King's transformation from a mere dragon cub to a powerful combatant demonstrates the rapid growth potential of Companion Dragon Kings, especially when bonded to a gifted cultivator.

## Reading Guide

This chapter serves as a turning point where Xiao Chen reconnects with a key ally from Dragon Island. Yizhen's reappearance brings both a powerful friend and a reminder of the friends Xiao Chen has lost or been separated from, particularly Keke and the Little Defiant Dragon. The war setting also introduces a new layer of scale: the conflict between the hegemonic kingdoms will likely draw in more legendary figures and force Xiao Chen to choose sides. The assassination plot against the Fan prince hints at a major battle in the near future, possibly involving the demonic formation. Readers should watch for how Xiao Chen's battlefield experience has changed his mindset—he is now more decisive and willing to engage directly. The presence of the Little Li Flying Dagger successor and other young masters signals that the younger generation's rivalries are accelerating, and Xiao Chen's growing reputation will invite challenges.
