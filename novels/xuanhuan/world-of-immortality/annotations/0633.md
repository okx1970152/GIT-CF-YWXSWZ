## Chapter Overview
This chapter delivers a spectacular chain of events centered on the chaotic clash of multiple Stone Man Kings over the legendary Three Sovereign Mirror. Xiao Chen, with Keke's aid, unleashes the full power of a genuine Heavenly Stele from within the Stone Corpse's body, causing the ancient undead king to self-destruct in a desperate gambit. The explosion ripples across the ancient battlefield, scattering the assembled kings and briefly clearing the path for Xiao Chen. Amid the frenzy, the young protagonist demonstrates both audacity and tactical restraint—while he cannot directly contend with the kings for the mirror, a fortunate deflection sends the Three Sovereign Mirror directly onto the Heavenly Stele in his arms. The chapter balances terrifying displays of peak combat with a stroke of luck, underscoring the volatility of cosmic treasure-hunting in the Primordial Celestial Realm.

## Key Plot Points
The chapter opens with the Stone Corpse, a long-lived Stone Man King of the Heavenly Realm, attempting to forcibly refine the divine diagram and ancient scroll within him, using his colossal demonic body to manifest supreme corpse-path laws. However, Xiao Chen has secretly brought a true Heavenly Stele—not merely a Heavenly Mark—into the Stone Corpse's abdomen. As Xiao Chen and Keke pour their power into the stele, it rapidly expands, growing so immense that it tears through the Stone Corpse from the inside. The Stone Corpse, realizing he cannot expel the stele, makes a savage decision: he lunges into the center of the gathered Stone Man Kings and shatters his own body, hoping to drag everyone down with him. The resulting explosion devastates the area, destroying the remaining ancient killing formations and punching multiple spatial tunnels to unknown worlds. When the dust settles, a genuine ten-meter Heavenly Stele falls from the Stone Corpse's remnants, stunning the kings who now understand what happened. Lu Zhan immediately tries to strike Xiao Chen, but Xiao Chen uses the ancient scroll and divine diagram to deflect the blow. The Stone Corpse's broken body tries to escape through the tunnels, but Xiao Chen captures one of his feet using the five Stone Man King imprints from the ancient scroll. Amid the ongoing struggle for the Three Sovereign Mirror, which amplifies and reverses attacks, the mirror is knocked flying toward Xiao Chen and Keke. Xiao Chen instinctively shields himself with the Heavenly Stele, and the mirror adheres to it. Seizing the opportunity, Xiao Chen grabs both stele and mirror and flees at maximum speed, pursued by the ten furious kings.

## Cultural / Xianxia Notes
This chapter showcases several core concepts of the World of Immortality's peak cultivation system. The Stone Man King represents the ultimate combat tier below the legendary Little Stone Emperor—a being so resilient that even self-destruction does not guarantee permanent death, as the Stone Corpse later attempts to escape via broken body parts. The Heavenly Stele (天碑) is distinguished from a Heavenly Mark (天痕); the former is a physical stele with overwhelming weight and power, while the latter is merely an imprint left on a cultivator after receiving a stele's recognition. The Three Sovereign Mirror (三皇镜) is an ancient artifact that can copy and redirect the most powerful attacks of its opponents, forcing the assembled kings to wound themselves with their own techniques. The concept of a 'World-Age' or 'Cosmic Cycle' is implied by the Stone Corpse's title as the longest-lived Stone Man King, suggesting that even peak beings have finite lifespans across eons. The 'Seven Great Tombs' (七座大墓) and their 'Demonic Stars' (魔星) are also introduced as valuable treasures born from a sovereign's innate nature.

## Reading Guide
Readers should pay close attention to Xiao Chen's evolving tactics: rather than relying on brute force, he uses the environment and his enemies' greed to create openings. His willingness to sacrifice the Stone Corpse's foot (rather than pursue total destruction) shows strategic prudence—he knows refining a Stone Man King's limb will take time but yield immense benefits. The sudden appearance of the Three Sovereign Mirror in his possession sets up a major shift in power dynamics. Now Xiao Chen holds both a genuine Heavenly Stele and a mirror that can reflect king-level attacks, making him a far more dangerous target. The pursuit by ten Stone Man Kings will undoubtedly lead to intense conflict in upcoming chapters. Additionally, the old skeleton (老头骨) reappears, having also secured a tomb and a demonic star, hinting that he remains a wildcard with his own agenda. The chapter's title refers to the Heavenly Stele's devastating emergence, but the true prize may be the mirror that 'coincidentally' landed in Xiao Chen's hands.
