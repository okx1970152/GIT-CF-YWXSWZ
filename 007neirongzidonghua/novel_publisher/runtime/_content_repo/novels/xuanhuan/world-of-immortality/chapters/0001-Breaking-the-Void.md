Who in this world can ever escape death?

No matter how brilliant your beauty, how peerless your grace, in the end you become but a skeleton in rouge and powder. No matter how favored you are by heaven, how vast the mountains and rivers under your rule, in the end you will dissolve into a handful of yellow earth.

Deathless longevity is the desire of all. But there is no ageless beauty, no immortal sovereign. The peerless and the common are no different—none can escape birth, aging, sickness, and death. No one can live forever in this world.

Yet the legend of deathless immortality has always endured.

Laozi, Zhuangzi, Bodhidharma, Chen Tuan, Zhang Sanfeng…

Names that have echoed through the ages, like ancient incantations spurring later generations onward, making people believe that deathless longevity is not utter absurdity—that some can indeed reach that realm.

But time is the most ruthless of all. As the years flow by, even the immortal legends are gradually worn away in the river of time.

Until, after endless ages of silence, a miracle erupts again from the mundane!

On the night of the fifteenth full moon, the peerless divine maiden Lan Nuo would sever all mortal ties on Red Dust Peak in the Kunlun Mountains and break the void to ascend. The world of practitioners was shaken; the talk of immortality blazed anew.

In recent days, over a hundred thousand people had surged into Kunlun—from nobles and royalty to peddlers and commoners, spanning every walk of life. They all shared one purpose: to witness a divine miracle unseen for millennia.

At last, the day of the full moon arrived. The majestic Kunlun range, vast and splendid, seemed veiled in a hazy gauze under the silver moonlight, making this sacred mountain appear as ethereal as a celestial realm.

In that moonlit night, Xiao Chen ran like the wind, his body drenched in blood, even his jet-black hair stained crimson. But his chiseled, handsome face bore an expression of unyielding defiance, and his bright, star-like eyes shone with a resolute light.

He was fleeing for his life!

The Royal Celestial Maiden Zhao Lin'er had sworn to destroy him and led dozens of practitioners in a four-direction siege. Her face hidden behind a light veil, her figure graceful and curves exquisite, her eyes like autumn waters, she moved as lightly as a startled swan, as fleeting as a shadow, as transcendent as an immortal descending to the mortal world.

With no escape, Xiao Chen charged toward Red Dust Peak!

Under the moonlight, the area around Red Dust Peak was a sea of people. The mountains and valleys were covered with countless figures. Yet, though over a hundred thousand had gathered, they were so silent—all of them quietly gazing up at the white-clad woman on the peak.

At the summit, Lan Nuo was dressed in white, pure as snow. Under the moonlight's embrace, her immortal form seemed to emit a faint, sacred radiance. Her white robes fluttered in the wind. She was truly like the goddess of the moon, untouched by worldly dust.

Over the past half month, she had twice attempted to break the void, but both times, at the very moment of success, she had withdrawn the foot she had stepped forward.

A single step away, and she would live eternally between heaven and earth!

But if she took that step, the vast red dust of the mortal world would forever leave her, and all mortal ties would be severed forever!

To sever mortal ties with the sword of wisdom required immense courage. Because after taking that step, in the endless years of deathless longevity that awaited her, perhaps there would only be infinite solitude.

The heart of heaven is unfathomable; the emotions of immortals are as cold as frost.

Today, she had stood here from dawn till now, all the experiences of the mortal world passing through her heart one by one. Finally, the time had come to bid farewell to the world. Blindingly brilliant divine light suddenly burst forth at the summit, and the entire mountaintop was enveloped in an incredibly sacred radiance.

Lan Nuo's form was ice and jade, pure and noble. In the holy glow, she was so otherworldly and lofty. Under the gaze of the multitude, the void shattered, and she took a firm, resolute step forward.

At the final moment, she looked back upon the mortal world one last time. That dreamlike, ethereal countenance would forever remain in the hearts of all who witnessed it. A hundred thousand voices cried out Lan Nuo's name in unison.

But the orderly shouts soon scattered into chaos as people saw two figures racing swiftly across the summit, following Lan Nuo through the shattered void!

The chronicles of the Nine Provinces record that in the year 7316, the peerless divine maiden Lan Nuo broke the void through martial prowess. The Royal Celestial Maiden Zhao Lin'er was blessed with a celestial connection and followed her into the World of Immortality.

As for Xiao Chen, fate did not grant him a place in the annals of history.

In the moment the void shattered, Xiao Chen was stunned beyond measure!

He had never imagined that such a day would come, that he would enter the World of Immortality in this manner. In that instant, his mind raced with countless thoughts—his family, his friends… all would be parted from him forever. He would leave this mortal world behind forever.

A desperate flight for his life had ended in such a result. For many, breaking the void and entering the World of Immortality was an eternal glory. But Xiao Chen would have gladly forfeited this chance. He was so reluctant to leave this world—his parents, his loved ones… forever! Wordlessly, he bid farewell to the red dust.

Xiao Chen did not know that the Royal Celestial Maiden Zhao Lin'er had also broken through the void and departed alongside him.
