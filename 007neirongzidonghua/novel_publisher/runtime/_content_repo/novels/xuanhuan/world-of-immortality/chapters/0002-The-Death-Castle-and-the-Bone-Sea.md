Rolling black clouds blotted out the light of heaven and earth in an instant. Endless darkness descended like a shroud of death, and waves of chilling, sinister energy flooded the world.

Within the surging death-clouds, a towering, ancient castle flickered into view at the edge of the sky. Rivers of blood surged up from within the castle, casting a mournful red glow against the black mist. A terrifying silence descended—not a single sound, a stillness like the grave.

The ancient castle bore the weight of countless ages, as if it had torn through time itself from a primordial era. It hung like a vortex of death, drawing the deathly energy of the ten directions slowly toward it, swallowing the roiling black clouds.

The dark mist gradually faded, but the high heavens grew only more sinister. Around the castle floated an endless expanse of white bones, a vast sea of death. The grim castle stood suspended above that snowy field of remains, a vision of unspeakable horror and desolation.

Several gray-winged humanoid skeletons flew out from the castle, their deathly aura spreading across the sky as they gazed down upon a lone island far below, deep in the vast ocean.

It was a solitary island in the heart of the boundless sea.

On the island, forests stretched wild and dense, with the cries of apes and the roars of tigers rising through the trees. Ninety percent of the land was covered in primeval forest, where ancient trees blotted out the sun, and ferocious beasts and birds of prey raced and screamed—a scene of primordial savagery.

But at that moment, a wave of icy, deathly energy swept in from the distant sky. The roars of the wild beasts gradually fell silent.

From deep within the island came a few muffled, angry bellows, as if some ancient monster stirred. Apart from that, there was no sound at all.

Xiao Chen, unconscious on the beach, seemed to sense the bone-piercing cold. His body twitched several times involuntarily.

After a long while, the deathly aura slowly receded. The pale bone sea in the distance, bearing that ancient castle, gradually vanished over the endless ocean.

Only then did the world begin to revive. Several dozen roars like dragon cries sounded from deep within the island, and life gradually returned.

The sun blazed overhead. He did not know how long he had lain there before waking. The sound of waves reached his ears. Slowly opening his eyes, he saw the blue-green sea surging before him. He was lying on the hot, golden sand.

The scorching sunlight left Xiao Chen dizzy and lightheaded. His body burned with pain, his mouth was parched, and his lips were cracked. Struggling to sit up, he surveyed this unfamiliar environment.

The hot air carried a salty, fishy sea breeze. Flocks of seabirds soared above the green sea, and giant fish leaped from the waves, stirring up great splashes.

From deep within the island, the roars of savage beasts thundered like heavenly drums. Xiao Chen felt as if he had stepped back into the age of primal chaos.

By the shore, a grove of verdant coconut trees cast a patch of cool shade. Clusters of ripe, brownish-yellow coconuts hung heavily among the green leaves, utterly tempting.

His dry mouth began to moisten. With effort, he stood and staggered forward.

The scorching sun was blocked, and the burning pain finally eased.

A slight sea breeze stirred the green leaves, and Xiao Chen finally felt a hint of coolness.

Ripe coconuts occasionally fell to the ground with a thud.

It felt as though flames were leaping in his throat, smoke rising from his mouth. He was unbearably parched. His body ached all over. Stumbling, he gathered a pile of coconuts and then sat down heavily.

With a pulse of his qi, he split one open and drank deeply of the sweet, cool milk. It was such a relief. For someone burning with thirst, it was like nectar.

So he had come to the World of Immortality, after all!

A thousand thoughts flooded Xiao Chen's mind. Where had Lan Nuo gone? And those legendary figures immortalized through the ages—did they truly live in this world? He was full of questions. Of course, he did not know that the Royal Celestial Maiden, Zhao Lin'er, had also entered this realm.

Finally, he knelt silently on the ground, kowtowing to his parents across the boundaries of space and time. From this day forth, the paths of immortals and mortals would never cross again.

He was a decisive man. Forcing himself to set aside his grief, he began to consider how to survive in this strange world.

Sitting quietly on the sand, his back against a thick coconut tree, Xiao Chen's skin began to gleam with a faint luster. His body seemed wrapped in a hazy radiance as the essence of heaven and earth converged around him. Within the grove, spiritual energy swirled, and a faint, colorful mist drifted.

The roars of beasts continued. This was no peaceful land. His first task was to recover his strength. Xiao Chen circulated his cultivation technique, drawing on the primal energy of plants to heal his wounds.

His method of cultivation originated from a mysterious stone carving.

He had grown up on the banks of the Yellow River. When he was seven years old, a severe drought struck. The river in front of his village nearly dried up, revealing a colossal stone stele. When the mud was cleared away, four ancient characters were carved on its front: "Eternally Settle the Yellow River!"

An old legend had always been told along the Yellow River: during the time of Yu the Great's flood control, a divine stele had descended from the Nine Heavens to settle permanently in the river, ensuring that the floods would never return.

Thus, when the stele emerged from the riverbed, the villagers immediately thought of the ancient legend. They washed the mud from its surface and burned incense in worship day after day.

Once it was cleaned, the back of the stele also revealed carvings—a mysterious and complex diagram. The villagers could not understand it and declared it a heavenly script.

The talk of a "heavenly script" filled the young Xiao Chen with endless wonder. Every day he would go and stare at the stele, until two months later, when heavy rains returned and the river rose, burying the stele forever in the depths.

But by then, that mysterious and intricate diagram had been deeply imprinted on young Xiao Chen's mind. It was only later, when he began to study martial arts and walk the path of cultivation, that he understood what that stone carving truly was.

It was an ancient and mysterious Heaven-Bestowed Qi-Refining Diagram!

Xiao Chen never knew whether that mysterious carving could compare with the legendary divine scriptures and precious tomes. But from the age of ten, he had begun to practice it without cease. For the next ten years, he learned techniques and combat moves from others, but he never changed his fundamental cultivation method.
