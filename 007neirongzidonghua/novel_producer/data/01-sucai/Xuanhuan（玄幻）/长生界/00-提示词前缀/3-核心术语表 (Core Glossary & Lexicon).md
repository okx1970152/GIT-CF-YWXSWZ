# Core Glossary & Lexicon for "World of Immortality" (长生界)

**Translation policy used here:** personal names stay in Pinyin without tone marks; mythic-civilizational titles are translated when the title carries ritual weight, especially 氏, 祖, 皇, 帝, 王. Realm names are rendered as formal High Fantasy terms, not generic xianxia equivalents such as “Foundation Establishment” or “Golden Core.”
