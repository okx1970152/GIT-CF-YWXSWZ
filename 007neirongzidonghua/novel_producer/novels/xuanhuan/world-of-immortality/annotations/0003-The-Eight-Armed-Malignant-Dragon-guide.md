title: "World of Immortality Chapter 3: The Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon"
---

This chapter delivers one of the novel's first truly mythic set-pieces: the emergence of a legendary sea monster, the Eight-Armed Malignant Dragon. The beast is described in staggering physical detail—fifty meters long, armored in silver scales, eight clawed arms, bone spikes like lances—and its roar alone swells the sea and drops birds from the sky. It’s a primeval creature straight out of folklore, a force of nature that even gods would flee. And Xiao Chen? He doesn’t charge in with sword drawn. He watches, retreats, and survives. That’s the core of his character here: pragmatic caution over reckless pride. He’s been cultivating for ten years from the mysterious stone carving, but he already senses his technique is incomplete. That subtle thread—the incomplete stele, the lost divine scriptures—becomes a long-term driver. But the real star is the dragon. Chen Dong’s prose turns the beach into a stage for sublime horror, contrasting peaceful sunset and coconut groves with a monster that could flatten a warship. It’s a perfect tone-setter: the World of Immortality is beautiful, vast, and utterly lethal.
